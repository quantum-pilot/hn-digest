# How China built its ‘Manhattan Project’ to rival the West in AI chips

- Score: 158 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46316907) | Link: https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2025/12/18/tech/china-west-ai-chips/

### TL;DR

Anonymous-source reporting says a secret Shenzhen program produced a crude prototype that generates extreme-ultraviolet light, using former ASML engineers and salvaged components. It has not made working chips, and precision optics remain a major obstacle. Huawei reportedly coordinates a large supplier and research network, while officials target 2028 and sources consider 2030 more realistic. The account suggests export controls slowed China but also intensified its push for semiconductor independence; company and government confirmations are limited.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters stressed that generating EUV light is far short of reliable high-volume chip production.
