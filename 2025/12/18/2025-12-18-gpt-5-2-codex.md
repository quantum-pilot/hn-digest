# GPT-5.2-Codex

- Score: 327 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46316367) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2-codex/

### TL;DR

OpenAI released GPT-5.2-Codex to paid Codex users, with API access planned later. The company claims stronger long-context work through native compaction, more reliable tool use, large refactors and migrations, Windows support, vision, and leading SWE-Bench Pro and Terminal-Bench 2.0 performance. Cyber capability also increased but remains below OpenAI’s “High” threshold. A React vulnerability case illustrates expert-guided environment setup and fuzzing after zero-shot attempts failed. More permissive future cyber models will initially use invite-only trusted access for vetted defenders.

### Comment pulse
