# TikTok unlawfully tracks shopping habits and use of dating apps?

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46307500) | Link: https://noyb.eu/en/tiktok-unlawfully-tracks-your-shopping-habits-and-your-use-dating-apps

### TL;DR

Privacy group noyb filed Austrian complaints alleging TikTok received off-app activity from AppsFlyer and Grindr without valid consent or legal basis, including data capable of revealing a user’s sexual orientation and sex life. It also says TikTok’s access-request download omitted personal data and processing details, even after follow-up. These are complainant allegations awaiting regulatory determination, not established findings. The requested remedies include complete disclosure, an end to the processing, and a deterrent fine under the GDPR.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters emphasized that intermediary and source-app data sharing means uninstalling TikTok alone may not stop dissemination.
- Others favored legislation, avoidance, or data obfuscation, while disputing whether poisoning commercial profiles is technically effective.
- Anecdotes about eerie recommendations illustrated concern but did not establish how particular platforms obtained the connections.

### LLM perspective

- View: The case targets an advertising-data chain, not merely one app’s local surveillance behavior.
- Impact: Sensitive inferences flowing between apps can expose users even when they never intentionally connect those services.
- Watch next: The regulator must establish the actual data path, legal roles, consent record, and completeness of TikTok’s disclosure.
