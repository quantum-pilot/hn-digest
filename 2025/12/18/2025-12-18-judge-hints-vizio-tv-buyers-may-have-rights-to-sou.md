# Judge hints Vizio TV buyers may have rights to source code licensed under GPL

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46308893) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/05/vizio_gpl_source_code_ruling/

### TL;DR

A California judge tentatively agreed that Software Freedom Conservancy formed a direct contract with Vizio when its administrator bought a television and requested corresponding source code for GPL- and LGPL-covered SmartCast components. SFC says Vizio’s 2019 disclosure was incomplete and sued after prolonged negotiations. The ruling could support consumers enforcing source-access promises as contract rights, an uncommon route for copyleft enforcement. It remains tentative: Vizio disputed the analysis, and the judge took the matter under submission before issuing a final opinion.
