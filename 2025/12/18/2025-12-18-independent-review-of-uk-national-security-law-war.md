# Independent review of UK national security law warns of overreach

- Score: 398 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46311355) | Link: https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-privacy-security/creating-apps-like-signal-or-whatsapp-could-be-hostile-activity-claims-uk-watchdog

### TL;DR

Independent reviewer Jonathan Hall KC warns that broad wording in two UK security laws could theoretically treat developers of end-to-end encrypted services as facilitating hostile activity when their technology frustrates intelligence collection. The same interpretation might reach journalists holding confidential or embarrassing material. This is a warning about unintended legal scope, not a newly announced policy or an actual prosecution. Against wider UK pressure on encryption, the concern is that ambiguous powers could chill lawful security engineering and reporting unless their boundaries are clarified.
