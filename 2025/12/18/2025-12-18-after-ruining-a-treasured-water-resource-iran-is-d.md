# After ruining a treasured water resource, Iran is drying up

- Score: 326 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46310976) | Link: https://e360.yale.edu/features/iran-water-drought-dams-qanats

### TL;DR

Iran’s fifth drought year has exposed decades of water-policy failure: dam reservoirs near Tehran fell to 12% capacity, agriculture consumes about 90% of withdrawals, and more than a million pumped wells have depleted aquifers by an estimated 210 cubic kilometers since 2000. Deep pumping also dries ancient gravity-fed qanats, reduces recharge, and permanently collapses underground storage, causing widespread subsidence. Hydrologists urge repairing qanats, capturing floods for recharge, changing crops, and abandoning water-intensive food self-sufficiency; relocating Tehran would address demand geographically, not reverse aquifer damage.
