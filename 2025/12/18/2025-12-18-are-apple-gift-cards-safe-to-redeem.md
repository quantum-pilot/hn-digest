# Are Apple gift cards safe to redeem?

- Score: 487 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46313061) | Link: https://daringfireball.net/linked/2025/12/17/are-apple-gift-cards-safe-to-redeem

### TL;DR

A developer’s Apple Account was disabled after he attempted to redeem an apparently tampered $500 gift card bought from a major retailer. Apple offered no initial explanation or effective escalation, cutting access to iCloud and media purchases until Executive Relations restored the account nearly a week later. The incident suggests fraud systems may treat an innocent redeemer like the thief while imposing account-wide consequences. Although resolution proved possible, Apple still had not explained why redemption triggered deactivation or established a reliable appeal path for ordinary customers without publicity.
