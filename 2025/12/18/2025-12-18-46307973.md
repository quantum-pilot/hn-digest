# Ask HN: Those making $500/month on side projects in 2025 – Show and tell

- Score: 399 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46307973) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46307973

### TL;DR

An annual Ask HN thread invites makers earning at least $500 monthly from side projects to share what works. Examples include a one-time-payment online fax service grossing over €500 per month, a public-domain audiobook subscription with restored recordings, a monthly regional-cuisine dinner club reporting 1,099 guests and $126,000 revenue in 2025, grant-funded open-source spreadsheet software, and interactive engineering books for children. The strongest common pattern is a narrow, understandable offering paired with low signup friction or an existing community need.

### Comment pulse

- Readers praised services that complete one task without accounts or subscriptions, especially faxing, and repeatedly asked how customers discover them.
- Discussion distinguished revenue from profit and highlighted grants, events, restoration work, and physical products as viable paths beyond SaaS.

### LLM perspective

- View: Small businesses often win through sharp scope and distribution, not technical novelty or recurring billing.
- Impact: Makers can validate painful, infrequent workflows before building broad platforms with persistent accounts.
- Watch next: Acquisition channels, margins, retention, support burden, and whether reported revenue remains durable after thread traffic fades.
