# Z2 – Lithographically fabricated IC in a garage fab

- Score: 331 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46178789) | Link: https://sam.zeloof.xyz/second-ic/

### TL;DR

Sam Zeloof’s Z2 demonstrates a homemade 10-micrometer polysilicon-gate process using garage equipment and no cleanroom. Each die contains a 10-by-10 NMOS test array; fifteen fabricated chips yielded one fully functional device and at least two with roughly 80% working transistors, without proper yield statistics yet. Buying wafers with gate oxide and polysilicon already deposited avoided silane and aggressive cleaning, while diffusion, photoresist dielectric, lithography, and metal deposition simplified fabrication. The resulting 1.1-volt-threshold transistors support 2.5- and 3.3-volt logic experiments.
