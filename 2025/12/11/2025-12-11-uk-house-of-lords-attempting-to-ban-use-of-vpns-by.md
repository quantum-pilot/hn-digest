# UK House of Lords attempting to ban use of VPNs by anyone under 16

- Score: 391 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46236738) | Link: https://alecmuffett.com/article/134925

### TL;DR

A blog flags a proposed UK bill amendment requiring relevant VPN providers to prohibit service to children and support monitoring and enforcement. The author argues that self-built VPNs and Tor make compliance infeasible. HN highlighted an adjacent proposal for tamper-proof device software intended to prevent recording, transmitting, or viewing child-abuse material, raising broader device-control fears. Crucially, commenters note these are amendments proposed by three House of Lords members, not an enacted ban or necessarily the position of the entire chamber.

### Comment pulse

- A VPN prohibition is technically porous → enforcement pressure could spread toward app installation, DNS, operating systems, identity checks, and Tor.
- Mandatory device scanning alarms computer-ownership advocates → counterpoint: supporters seek mechanisms against child exploitation and anonymous abuse.
- The headline overstates institutional backing → only three Lords proposed one amendment among dozens.
