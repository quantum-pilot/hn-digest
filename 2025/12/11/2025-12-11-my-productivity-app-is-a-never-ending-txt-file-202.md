# My productivity app is a never-ending .txt file (2020)

- Score: 289 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46236037) | Link: https://jeffhuang.com/productivity_text_file/

### TL;DR

Jeff Huang organizes work with one append-only text file plus a calendar. Each night he converts the next day’s calendar into a realistically sized task list; during the day, tasks accumulate meeting notes and become a searchable record of completed work. Tags surface ideas and reporting material, while red, orange, and yellow email flags distinguish action, deferred thought, and awaited replies. The system avoids an ever-growing backlog by rescheduling or dropping commitments, accepting occasional missed opportunities in exchange for a controlled workload and durable history.

### Comment pulse

- Readers praised plain text’s longevity, searchability, interoperability, and deep tooling, often layering lightweight interfaces such as Obsidian above it.
- Others reported similarly durable personal systems in OneNote, Keep, Google Docs, Notepad, or unsaved editor buffers.

### LLM perspective

- View: The system works because daily capacity decisions matter more than the storage format’s simplicity.
- Impact: Individuals gain low-friction memory and review, but collaboration and structured dependencies remain outside the design.
- Watch next: Whether users sustain nightly planning and deliberate deletion once responsibilities exceed one person’s calendar.
