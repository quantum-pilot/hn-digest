# Rivian Unveils Custom Silicon, R2 Lidar Roadmap, and Universal Hands Free

- Score: 391 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46234920) | Link: https://riviantrackr.com/news/rivian-unveils-custom-silicon-r2-lidar-roadmap-universal-hands-free-and-its-next-gen-autonomy-platform/

### TL;DR

Rivian outlined an integrated autonomy platform built around RAP1, a 5 nm processor rated at 1,600 sparse INT8 TOPS, its compiler, and the ACM3 computer scheduled for R2 in late 2026. R2 will initially ship without lidar, adding it later beside cameras and radar. Gen 2 R1 vehicles are promised hands-free driving on 3.5 million miles of marked roads, while Autonomy+ launches in 2026 for $2,500 or $49.99 monthly. Owners focused less on specifications than hardware obsolescence, upgrade eligibility, subscriptions, delivery dates, and missing CarPlay.

### Comment pulse

- Early adopters feared Gen 1 and launch R2 vehicles becoming stranded on older hardware; replies noted sensor and compute constraints.
- Subscription supporters cited continuous development and possible insurance bundling — counterpoint: many drivers prefer fixed capabilities or competitors offering autonomy as standard.
- Integrated infotainment divided owners: some value unified vehicle controls and onboard AI, while others reject any premium car lacking CarPlay.
