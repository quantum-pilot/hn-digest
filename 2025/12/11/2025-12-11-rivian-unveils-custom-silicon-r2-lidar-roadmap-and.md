# Rivian Unveils Custom Silicon, R2 Lidar Roadmap, and Universal Hands Free

- Score: 391 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46234920) | Link: https://riviantrackr.com/news/rivian-unveils-custom-silicon-r2-lidar-roadmap-universal-hands-free-and-its-next-gen-autonomy-platform/

### TL;DR

An enthusiast site summarizes Rivian’s autonomy roadmap: the 5nm RAP1 processor claims 1,600 sparse INT8 TOPS, ACM3 arrives on R2 from late 2026, and LiDAR follows later rather than at launch. Gen 2 R1 vehicles are promised hands-free driving on 3.5 million miles of marked roads, while 2026’s Autonomy+ costs $2,500 or $49.99 monthly. Rivian also announced an assistant and telemetry-driven diagnostics. Commenters worried about subscriptions, retrofit limits, rapid hardware obsolescence, delivery timing, and absent CarPlay.

### Comment pulse

- Subscription autonomy may fund continuous development and liability → counterpoint: buyers may prefer fixed capability or bundled competitors.
- Hardware generations unsettle current owners → unclear upgrade paths make early R2 and Gen 1 purchases feel prematurely obsolete.
- Rivian favors integrated software over CarPlay → some buyers reject the vehicle outright without phone-driven interfaces.
