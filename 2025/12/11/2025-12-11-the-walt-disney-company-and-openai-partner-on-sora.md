# The Walt Disney Company and OpenAI Partner on Sora

- Score: 265 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46231493) | Link: https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/

### TL;DR

Disney and OpenAI announced a proposed three-year license allowing Sora and ChatGPT Images to generate content using more than 200 Disney, Pixar, Marvel, and Star Wars characters, excluding talent likenesses and voices. Curated fan videos may reach Disney+, while Disney would adopt OpenAI tools internally, invest $1 billion, and receive additional-equity warrants. Launch is expected in early 2026, subject to definitive agreements and approvals. HN debated copyright, brand dilution, creator displacement, and the unusual investment-plus-license economics.
