# The Walt Disney Company and OpenAI Partner on Sora

- Score: 265 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46231493) | Link: https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/

### TL;DR

Under a three-year license, Sora and ChatGPT Images may generate fan-directed work from more than 200 Disney, Marvel, Pixar, and Star Wars characters, props, vehicles, and settings; talent likenesses and voices are excluded. Selected videos may reach Disney+, while Disney becomes an API customer, deploys ChatGPT internally, invests $1 billion, and receives warrants. The early-2026 plan still requires final agreements and approvals. HN treated the investment as the real lede and debated brand dilution, copyright licensing, safety controls, and whether studios will capture value while displacing creators.

### Comment pulse
