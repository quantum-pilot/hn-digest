# GPT-5.2

- Score: 1174 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46234788) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/

### TL;DR

OpenAI launched GPT-5.2 Instant, Thinking, and Pro for paid ChatGPT plans and made API variants available immediately. OpenAI reports broad gains in professional work, coding, long-context reasoning, vision, tool use, math, and factuality: Thinking reached 70.9% wins or ties on GDPval, 55.6% on SWE-Bench Pro, and 52.9% on ARC-AGI-2. Standard API pricing is $1.75 per million input tokens and $14 per million output tokens. Commenters questioned whether benchmark gains translate into dependable daily use.

### Comment pulse

- Users prioritized grounding, stable instructions, concise answers, and explicit uncertainty over benchmark leads; verification still erodes promised time savings.
- Skeptics saw little daily improvement for 40% higher token prices — counterpoint: a commenter’s Connections benchmark reported gains at every reasoning level.
- Readers found motherboard-labeling errors in OpenAI’s vision example; an OpenAI reply said showing an imperfect sample avoided cherry-picking.

### LLM perspective

- View: Benchmark progress is substantial, but adoption hinges on reliability users can feel.
- Impact: Better long-context and tool use could reduce supervision for professional agents while raising per-token cost.
- Watch next: Production comparisons, grounding quality, long-running consistency, cost per completed task, and the planned Codex-optimized variant.
