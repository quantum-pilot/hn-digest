# GPT-5.2

- Score: 1174 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46234788) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/

### TL;DR

OpenAI presents GPT-5.2 as a major upgrade for professional and agentic work, claiming gains in coding, long-context reasoning, vision, tool use, factuality, math, and knowledge-work artifacts. Reported scores include 70.9% wins or ties on GDPval, 55.6% on SWE-Bench Pro, and 52.9% on ARC-AGI-2 for Thinking. Paid ChatGPT rollout begins immediately; API pricing is $1.75 per million input tokens and $14 per million output tokens. Commenters questioned whether benchmarks translate into reliable daily behavior, grounding, instruction-following, and value.

### Comment pulse
