# What is the nicest thing a stranger has ever done for you?

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46236001) | Link: https://louplummer.lol/nice-stranger/

### TL;DR

After a severe bicycle crash, the author regained consciousness beside traffic to find an off-duty emergency physician already checking breathing, cleaning wounds, calling 911 and his wife, and arranging hospital care. He never learned the doctor’s name but still recalls the intervention 25 years later as evidence of ordinary goodness. HN replies broadened the theme through unplanned Christmas hospitality, a child’s basketball registration, silent compassion during alcoholism, an employee’s personal $50 rescue, and unsolicited snow clearing.
