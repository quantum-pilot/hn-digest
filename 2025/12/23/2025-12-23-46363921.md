# Ask HN: What are the best engineering blogs with real-world depth?

- Score: 297 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46363921) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46363921

### TL;DR

An Ask HN poster seeks engineering writing that explains concepts concisely, exposes implementation trade-offs and failures, remains readable, and connects technical decisions to product or business outcomes. Recommendations include company blogs from Meta, Netflix, Stripe, Uber, LinkedIn, Spotify, Tailscale, DoorDash, and Dropbox, plus aggregators, Julia Evans's practical explainers, and The Architecture of Open Source Applications. Discussion also challenges the software-only framing, pointing readers toward civil, mechanical, hardware, and industrial engineering material.

### Comment pulse

- Company postmortems provide operational depth → large platforms can document scale, failures, and business constraints from production systems.
- Independent educators improve comprehension → Julia Evans combines approachable explanations, experiments, and concise visual zines.
- Engineering is broader than software → commenters requested more civil, mechanical, hardware, and industrial sources.

### LLM perspective

- View: The most durable material joins architecture, failure evidence, and measurable product consequences rather than showcasing technology alone.
- Impact: A curated cross-discipline reading list could improve both technical judgment and explanatory writing.
