# Ryanair fined €256M over ‘abusive strategy’ to limit ticket sales by OTAs

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46364272) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/business/2025/dec/23/ryanair-fined-limit-online-travel-agencies-ticket-sales-ota

### TL;DR

Italy's competition authority fined Ryanair €256 million for allegedly abusing market power between April 2023 and at least April 2025 to obstruct online travel agencies. Cited tactics include blocking payments and accounts, imposing facial verification on third-party customers, and restricting agencies from bundling Ryanair flights with other carriers or services. Ryanair will appeal, arguing direct sales lower fares and protect customers from reseller markups. HN discussion splits between hostility to Ryanair's own dark patterns and concern about problematic intermediaries.

### Comment pulse
