# FCC Updates Covered List to Include Foreign UAS and UAS Critical Components [pdf]

- Score: 100 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46362275) | Link: https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-416839A1.pdf

### TL;DR

The FCC added foreign-made drones and critical components to its Covered List after an interagency national-security determination cited attack, surveillance, data-exfiltration, and industrial-base risks. New covered models cannot receive FCC authorization and therefore cannot be imported or sold, but previously authorized models and lawfully purchased drones remain usable and marketable. HN commenters expect major effects on DJI and Autel, question exemptions for existing equipment, and warn that batteries, motors, and other non-radio components may also be swept in.

### Comment pulse

- Existing fleets remain legal → the immediate rule targets new authorizations, though commenters note prior approvals might later be revoked.
- Domestic substitutes are inadequate → enterprise operators see no current equivalent to DJI and prefer incentives alongside restrictions.
- Scope may exceed radios → incorporated definitions appear to cover foreign critical components that never required FCC certification.
