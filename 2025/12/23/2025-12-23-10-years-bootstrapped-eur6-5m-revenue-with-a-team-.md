# 10 years bootstrapped: €6.5M revenue with a team of 13

- Score: 226 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46363319) | Link: https://www.datocms.com/blog/a-look-back-at-2025

### TL;DR

DatoCMS says its tenth year produced €6.5 million revenue, 10% growth, and a 65% EBIT margin with 13 employees. It also reports 3.5 billion monthly API calls and a nine-month migration from Heroku to AWS EKS that cut infrastructure costs over 25%, halved delivery latency, and caused one minute of downtime. HN bootstrappers celebrated control and resilience without dilution, while noting that polished retrospectives can hide years of difficulty and may prompt compensation questions.
