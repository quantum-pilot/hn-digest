# France targets Australia-style social media ban for children next year

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46444743) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/dec/31/france-plans-social-media-ban-for-under-15s-from-september-2026

### TL;DR

France drafted legislation to ban social media for under-15s from September 2026 and mobile phones in high schools, extending existing school restrictions. The proposal, backed by Emmanuel Macron, cites harmful content, bullying, sleep disruption, mental-health risks, and declining achievement; it still faces legal review and a divided parliament. HN debate focused less on the harms than enforcement: age verification may create surveillance, black markets, and adult friction, while VPNs and fuzzy definitions could undermine coverage.
