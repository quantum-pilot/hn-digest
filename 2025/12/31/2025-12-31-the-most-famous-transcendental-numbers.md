# The most famous transcendental numbers

- Score: 132 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46443579) | Link: https://sprott.physics.wisc.edu/pickover/trans.html

### TL;DR

Transcendental numbers are not roots of nonzero polynomials with rational coefficients; almost all real numbers qualify, yet proving specific examples is difficult. The article tours π, e, Liouville’s and Champernowne’s numbers, Chaitin’s constant, logarithms, powers such as i^i, and several famous but unproven candidates including Euler’s and Catalan’s constants. HN discussion highlighted the gulf between abundance and definability, corrected the infinite “talking ants” thought experiment, and noted that even irrationality remains unproved for some listed constants.
