# Akin's Laws of Spacecraft Design [pdf] (2011)

- Score: 251 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46442903) | Link: https://www.ece.uvic.ca/~elec399/201409/Akin%27s%20Laws%20of%20Spacecraft%20Design.pdf

### TL;DR

Akin’s 40 laws distill spacecraft engineering into practical warnings: quantify claims, design for failure, iterate continuously, respect requirements, document uncertainty, protect schedule margin, and distrust elegant analyses that violate reality. The collection stresses interfaces, accountability, effective communication, and the unforgiving consequences of technical mistakes. HN readers debated examples involving dial-up modem limits, the iPhone versus Nokia N95, and startup presentation, while emphasizing that conflicting maxims require judgment rather than mechanical obedience.
