# On privacy and control

- Score: 130 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46446938) | Link: https://toidiu.com/blog/2025-12-25-privacy-and-control/

### TL;DR

Reframing privacy as control or agency makes the stakes clearer: dependence on large platforms lets others shape access, attention, identity, and data portability. The author describes a pragmatic setup using GNU pass, Signal, GrapheneOS, a personal email domain, Firefox protections, and self-hosted contacts. HN readers liked the framing but stressed real costs: banking and government apps can break on alternative operating systems, self-hosting demands continual labor, and trusting Cloudflare merely relocates concentrated power.

### Comment pulse
