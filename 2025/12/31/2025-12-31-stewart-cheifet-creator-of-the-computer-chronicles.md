# Stewart Cheifet, creator of The Computer Chronicles, has died

- Score: 162 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46446359) | Link: https://obits.goldsteinsfuneral.com/stewart-cheifet

### TL;DR

Stewart Cheifet, creator and host of PBS programs Computer Chronicles and Net Cafe, died at 87. Across more than 400 Computer Chronicles episodes from 1984–2002, he documented personal computing’s emergence; Net Cafe similarly covered the early internet. He later taught journalism and helped the Internet Archive preserve technology media, including his programs. HN mourners emphasized that this preservation made the era accessible to younger viewers, while volunteers described ongoing work digitizing missing episodes and correcting incomplete episode metadata.
