# Stardew Valley developer made a $125k donation to the FOSS C# framework MonoGame

- Score: 502 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46445068) | Link: https://monogame.net/blog/2025-12-30-385-new-sponsor-announcement/

### TL;DR

ConcernedApe, creator of Stardew Valley, donated $125,000 to the MonoGame Foundation and committed ongoing monthly support. MonoGame says the funding will sustain its open-source C# framework and 2026 roadmap. HN readers celebrated a commercially successful user materially supporting foundational software, while disputing whether free-software beneficiaries have any moral obligation to repay maintainers. Discussion also distinguished MonoGame’s code-first libraries and game loop from full editors such as Unity, and tempered solo-development inspiration with survivorship risk.
