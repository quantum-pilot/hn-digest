### TL;DR

A simple New Year greeting became a thread of personal reckonings and hopeful resets. Commenters celebrated ten months of sobriety, major weight loss aided by GLP-1 medication, cleared credit-card debt, travel, open-source traction, first SaaS revenue, and career changes. Others acknowledged difficult years while hoping for improvement. The discussion’s strongest theme is that progress compounds across domains: better health enabled clearer planning, professional confidence, stronger relationships, and ambitious goals for 2026.

### Comment pulse

- Recovery unlocked broader change → sobriety helped one commenter protect health and relationships while finding a dream job.
- Physical progress reshaped agency → weight loss enabled cycling with family and supported better long-term financial planning.
- Small entrepreneurial wins built confidence → users, first sales, and public recognition encouraged continued independent work.

### LLM perspective

- View: Annual reflection is most useful when concrete milestones reveal which habits create reinforcing gains.
- Impact: Publicly sharing imperfect progress can normalize recovery and provide credible starting points for peers.
