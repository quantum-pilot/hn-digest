### TL;DR

The article alleges that simultaneous OpenAI agreements with Samsung and SK Hynix could consume up to 40% of global DRAM output, triggering panic buying in a market already short of inventory and manufacturing buffers. It further speculates that unfinished wafers may be stockpiled partly to disadvantage competitors, then predicts higher component prices and cancellations. These claims are disputed in the discussion: commenters question the secrecy narrative, cite reporting of a joint meeting, and distinguish ordinary supplier negotiation from deliberate market foreclosure. Treat the causal story as unverified.
