# Why Speed Matters

- Score: 114 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46172902) | Link: https://lemire.me/blog/2025/12/05/why-speed-matters/

### TL;DR

Daniel Lemire argues that people systematically underestimate the value of moving quickly. Speed does not require finishing entire projects prematurely; it means advancing each part near the pace of thought, exposing mistaken priorities sooner, learning through more iterations and avoiding attachment to work that becomes obsolete. He uses fast thesis completion and experienced surgeons as provocative examples, while acknowledging that good work can take months. The core prescription is sustained momentum, though the post gives limited guidance for balancing iteration speed against the consequences of error.
