# Why Speed Matters

- Score: 114 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46172902) | Link: https://lemire.me/blog/2025/12/05/why-speed-matters/

### TL;DR

Daniel Lemire argues that moving quickly improves work by exposing unimportant components sooner, increasing the rate of mistakes and learning, and reducing attachment to outputs that become obsolete. Speed does not require finishing whole projects rapidly or sacrificing correctness; it means maintaining momentum close to the pace of thought. Commenters sharpened this into shortening feedback loops and increasing contact with reality, while warning that an unqualified speed mandate can encourage burnout or costly errors. High-consequence systems may justify slower design and fewer iterations.
