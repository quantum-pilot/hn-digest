# PalmOS on FisherPrice Pixter Toy

- Score: 184 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46170309) | Link: https://dmitry.gr/?r=05.Projects&proj=27.%20rePalm#pixter

### TL;DR

The rePalm project now runs PalmOS 5 on Fisher-Price Pixter Color and Multimedia toys by replacing the hardware-abstraction layer and building custom cartridges with flash, RAM, SD support, and an auxiliary microcontroller for IrDA. Severe constraints required workarounds for a cacheless ARM7, missing memory protection, narrow buses, display quirks, and limited audio. The Multimedia model performs near a Palm Tungsten T; the Color reaches about 6% of that speed but still runs PIM software and games. Fifteen cartridges were produced.
