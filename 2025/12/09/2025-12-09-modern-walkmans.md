# Modern Walkmans

- Score: 162 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46201381) | Link: https://walkman.land/modern

### TL;DR

Walkman.land catalogs 11 current portable cassette players priced roughly $20–$160, ranging from basic radio-recorders to aluminum, USB-C, Bluetooth-equipped models. Features vary across recording, auto-reverse, built-in microphones, rechargeable batteries, speakers, and wireless output, while the catalog openly flags weak sound, fragile construction, plastic flywheels, or missing controls. HN readers value cassettes’ tactile, recordable, album-oriented experience, but several argue modern mechanisms are bulky and inferior to slim Japanese players from the format’s engineering peak.

### Comment pulse

- Physical media creates ritual and saleable objects → artists and listeners report appeal beyond convenience or measured fidelity.
- Cassette inconvenience is either fatal or charming → advocates cite portability, graceful degradation, visible motion, and acceptable sound on good equipment.
- Vintage repair may offer better quality → scarce parts, inflated prices, aging belts, and maintenance complicate that recommendation.
