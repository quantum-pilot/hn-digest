# 30 Year Anniversary of WarCraft II: Tides of Darkness

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46202921) | Link: https://www.jorsys.org/archive/december_2025.html#newsitem_2025-12-09T07:42:19Z

### TL;DR

Released December 9, 1995 after less than a year of development, WarCraft II refined real-time strategy with right-click commands, larger unit selection, fog of war, upgrades, and naval and aerial combat. Its mirrored factions were imperfectly balanced, notably around Orc Bloodlust, but its accessible editor and reverse-engineered formats fostered maps, utilities, and total conversions that influenced Blizzard’s later creator tools and ultimately user-made genres. HN remembered approachable modding, early internet multiplayer, emergent tactics, and continued playability despite later StarCraft dominance.
