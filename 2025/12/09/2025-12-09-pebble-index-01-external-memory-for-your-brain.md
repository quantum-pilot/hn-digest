# Pebble Index 01 – External memory for your brain

- Score: 359 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46205661) | Link: https://repebble.com/blog/meet-pebble-index-01-external-memory-for-your-brain

### TL;DR

Pebble’s $75 preorder is a button-and-microphone ring for capturing brief spoken thoughts without pulling out a phone. It stores up to five minutes of audio, syncs over Bluetooth, and uses local open-source speech recognition and an on-device model to create notes, reminders, or actions; optional cloud services improve transcription. A silver-oxide battery provides 12–15 recording hours, estimated as up to two years of typical use, but is neither rechargeable nor user-replaceable—the entire pre-production ring must be returned for recycling and replaced.

### Comment pulse

- Low-friction capture appeals to busy or distractible users → skeptics expect transcription backlogs and question advantages over phones or watches.
- Noncharging enables a smaller ring → critics call replacement a hardware subscription and potential conflict with forthcoming EU battery rules.
- Press-to-record limits passive surveillance → optional backup encryption is planned rather than already implemented.
