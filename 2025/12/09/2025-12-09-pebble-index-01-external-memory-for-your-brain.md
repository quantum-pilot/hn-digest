# Pebble Index 01 – External memory for your brain

- Score: 359 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46205661) | Link: https://repebble.com/blog/meet-pebble-index-01-external-memory-for-your-brain

### TL;DR

Pebble’s $75 preorder is a stainless-steel voice-input ring with a physical button, microphone, encrypted phone connection and five minutes of offline audio storage. Pressed recordings are transcribed and routed locally to notes, reminders or configurable actions; cloud service is optional, and the app is open source. Its silver-oxide battery supports roughly 12–15 recording hours, advertised as up to two years, but cannot be recharged or replaced by users. HN split over low-friction capture utility, smartwatch redundancy, information backlog and disposable-hardware waste.

### Comment pulse

- Disposable power dominated objections → commenters called replacement a hardware subscription and questioned future EU compliance despite recycling plans.
- Friction reduction has a constituency → hands-busy users and people managing distractibility value capture without opening a phone.
- Utility may create backlog → skeptics expect transcribed micro-thoughts to become information hoarding rather than completed work.
