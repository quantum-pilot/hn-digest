# Richard Stallman on ChatGPT

- Score: 121 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46203591) | Link: https://www.stallman.org/chatgpt.html

### TL;DR

Richard Stallman rejects calling ChatGPT “intelligence,” arguing it neither knows nor understands its outputs and generates language without concern for truth, encouraging misplaced trust. His separate, more concrete objection is software freedom: users cannot obtain or run ChatGPT themselves and must perform computing through a provider-controlled server. HN largely agrees that outputs require verification and proprietary service dependence is concerning, but disputes whether understanding is definable or necessary for usefulness, comparing language models with calculators, appliances, human intuition, and convincing behavioral simulation.
