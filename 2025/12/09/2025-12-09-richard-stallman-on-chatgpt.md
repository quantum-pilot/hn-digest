# Richard Stallman on ChatGPT

- Score: 121 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46203591) | Link: https://www.stallman.org/chatgpt.html

### TL;DR

Richard Stallman rejects the intelligence label for ChatGPT, arguing that it manipulates words without knowing their meaning and generates text without regard for truth, encouraging misplaced trust. His second objection is software freedom: users receive neither executable nor source and must perform their computing on a vendor’s server. HN agreed more strongly on skepticism and enclosure than on cognition. Critics called his definition semantic or ideological, while supporters said usefulness does not justify treating fluent output as understanding or reliable fact.

### Comment pulse

- Behavioral usefulness challenges ontology → commenters compared language models to calculators and cookers that deliver value without understanding their outputs.
- Truth remains the practical boundary → confident synthesis can assist research but cannot inherit the reliability of engineered arithmetic.
- The freedom critique travels better → even dissenters worried that capable models remain inside well-funded private enclosures.
