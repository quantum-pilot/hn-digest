# Donating the Model Context Protocol and establishing the Agentic AI Foundation

- Score: 127 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46207425) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-the-model-context-protocol-and-establishing-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation

### TL;DR

Anthropic is donating MCP to the Agentic AI Foundation, a Linux Foundation fund co-founded with Block and OpenAI and supported by major cloud and technology companies. Anthropic reports more than 10,000 active public servers, broad product adoption, a community registry, major-language SDKs, and 97 million monthly Python and TypeScript SDK downloads. Existing MCP governance remains unchanged; Block’s goose and OpenAI’s AGENTS.md join as founding projects. HN disputed whether one-year-old MCP is mature enough for foundation stewardship or merely overbuilt tool calling.

### Comment pulse

- Neutral ownership may unlock wider vendor commitment → rapid adoption makes shared governance valuable before one company controls the standard.
- Foundation timing feels premature → unresolved authentication and rapid specification changes may suffer under heavier process.
- MCP’s necessity remains contested → critics see JSON-RPC overhead; supporters value one discoverable interface across services and clients.
