# AI and the ironies of automation – Part 2

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46262816) | Link: https://www.ufried.com/blog/ironies_of_ai_2/

### TL;DR

Uwe Friedrichsen applies Lisanne Bainbridge’s 1983 analysis of industrial automation to AI agents. Automation makes people monitor systems despite humans being poor at sustained vigilance, while polished plans and confident output can conceal rare errors. As automation succeeds, workers lose the manual practice needed when unfamiliar failures occur; simulators cannot cover every surprise. AI adds another demand: people must actively direct agents, not merely supervise them. Friedrichsen argues organizations therefore need stronger review interfaces, preserved expertise, deliberate practice, and leadership skills.
