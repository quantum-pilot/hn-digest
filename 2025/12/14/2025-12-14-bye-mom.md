# Bye, Mom

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46261616) | Link: https://aella.substack.com/p/bye-mom

### TL;DR

Aella recounts flying to Boise after learning her 66-year-old mother was unresponsive in intensive care with acute liver and brain dysfunction. The family waited for an overseas sister, then withdrew life support because they believed continued treatment would conflict with their mother’s wishes about quality of life. She describes decision paralysis, tenderness at the bedside, the altered presence of a body after death, and grief’s uneven arrival. The essay also holds a complicated bond: profound religious and ideological differences, distance, past harm, and enduring maternal love.
