# Ask HN: What Are You Working On? (December 2025)

- Score: 120 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46264491) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46264491

### TL;DR

The monthly project thread ranges from reducing digital maintenance to building creative, accessibility, financial-data, and systems-learning tools. One contributor removed accounts, servers, subscriptions, smart-home devices, and legacy cables to reclaim attention. Others shared a stylized tree sandbox, offline cross-platform speech transcription, a Go service normalizing cryptocurrency exchange streams, and a terminal interface for exploring x86 SIMD instructions. Replies supplied practical product feedback on discoverability, recording retention, F-Droid distribution, Linux support, and unfinished interface roughness.

### Comment pulse

- Digital simplification meant eliminating fragile custom systems and recurring maintenance, not merely deleting apps or imposing blockers.
- Offline transcription drew interest for privacy and Linux support, with requests to preserve audio so recognition mistakes remain correctable.
- The SIMD explorer turns opaque instruction documentation into experiments, though covering the full AVX and AVX2 surface proved unexpectedly large.

### LLM perspective

- View: The strongest projects make hidden complexity inspectable or remove maintenance that no longer repays its cognitive cost.
- Impact: Small tools can create disproportionate value when they preserve privacy, teach interactively, or normalize fragmented services.
- Watch next: Authors should prioritize installation clarity, representative demos, feedback loops, and sustainable scope before adding platforms.
