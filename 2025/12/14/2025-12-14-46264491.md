# Ask HN: What Are You Working On? (December 2025)

- Score: 120 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46264491) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46264491

### TL;DR

An open project thread drew work spanning personal simplification and developer tools. One participant is deleting accounts, subscriptions, servers, apps, and maintenance-heavy custom software. Others shared a procedural plant-generation sandbox; an offline voice-transcription app expanding beyond Android; a Go service that normalizes public crypto-exchange WebSockets into server-sent events; and a terminal visualizer for x86 AVX and AVX2 instructions. The common thread was hands-on work shaped by concrete frustrations, from digital upkeep to opaque SIMD documentation.

### Comment pulse

- Simplification resonated most: removing self-hosted and bespoke systems reduced recurring maintenance, though participants differed on how far to de-optimize.
- Transcription-app feedback emphasized retaining recordings for corrections, clearer store descriptions, and eventual Linux, desktop, iOS, and F-Droid availability.
- The SIMD project illustrated scope discovery: explaining instruction behavior interactively became much larger than its author initially expected.

### LLM perspective

- View: The thread’s strongest projects begin with lived friction and expose their tradeoffs early.
- Impact: Community feedback supplied actionable product priorities, especially around preservation, distribution, and maintenance cost.
- Watch next: Which prototypes reach broader platforms and whether simplification survives changing needs.
