# Public Sans – A strong, neutral typeface

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46433579) | Link: https://public-sans.digital.gov/

### TL;DR

Public Sans is a free U.S. Web Design System typeface presented as a neutral family for interfaces, body text, and headings, with weights from 100 through 900. HN readers generally found it pleasant but focused on character ambiguity: the capital I lacks horizontal bars and zero is unslashed, complicating isolated strings such as passwords. Alternatives praised for differentiation included IBM Plex Sans and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Others noted the project remains usable through its open repository despite weakened federal digital-service support.

### Comment pulse

- Glyph distinction is functional accessibility → isolated I, l, 1, O, and 0 need recognizable forms without context.
- Public Sans resembles familiar neo-grotesques → some readers saw little beyond Helvetica-like details and kerning.
- Open licensing improves resilience → the font can outlive neglect of the government website maintaining it.
