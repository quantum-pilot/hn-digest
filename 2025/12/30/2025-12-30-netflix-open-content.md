# Netflix Open Content

- Score: 573 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46431560) | Link: https://opencontent.netflix.com/

### TL;DR

Netflix provides Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 test productions spanning animation, live action, HDR, high frame rates, Dolby Vision, and Atmos. Downloads include source-quality EXR and TIFF sequences, IMF packages, camera files, audio masters, metadata, and production artifacts from titles including Sol Levante, Nocturne, Sparks, and Meridian. HN saw rare value for codec and display benchmarking, while noting the page was last updated in 2022, newer material exists elsewhere, and Netflix generally restricts physical ownership of its commercial catalog.
