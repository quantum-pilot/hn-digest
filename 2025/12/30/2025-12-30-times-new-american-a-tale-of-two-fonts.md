# Times New American: A Tale of Two Fonts

- Score: 204 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46432862) | Link: https://hsu.cy/2025/12/times-new-american/

### TL;DR

The State Department’s return from 15-point Calibri to 14-point Times New Roman is framed as tradition and professionalism, but the essay argues both choices poorly match their stated goals. Times was optimized for compact newspaper printing and became authoritative largely through institutional inertia; Calibri was designed for screen rendering, not accessibility, and has ambiguous glyphs. HN broadly agreed the political rationale was needless, while debating legibility, licensing, and alternatives including Public Sans, Century Schoolbook, Univers, Georgia, and purpose-built accessible fonts.

### Comment pulse

- Typography should follow function → defaults signal convenience, while official documents need deliberate legibility, hierarchy, and tone.
- Calibri’s designer defends its screen performance → Times’ digital forms are thin, poorly spaced, and limited in weights.
- Government fonts should be libre → Public Sans combines federal provenance, redistribution, and stronger character distinction.
