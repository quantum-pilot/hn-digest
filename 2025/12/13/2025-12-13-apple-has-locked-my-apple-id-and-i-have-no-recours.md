# Apple has locked my Apple ID, and I have no recourse. A plea for help

- Score: 1466 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46252114) | Link: https://hey.paris/posts/appleid/

### TL;DR

Developer Paris Buttfield-Addison says Apple permanently disabled a 25-year account after a failed attempt to redeem a $500 gift card that he believed was compromised. Apple reportedly cited its Media Services terms but supplied no precise reason or escalation path. He describes losing access to iCloud, iMessage, purchases, developer credentials, photos, and normal use of Apple hardware, while fearing that creating a replacement account could look like circumvention. The gift card’s role and the account’s alleged compromise remain the author’s inference, not Apple-confirmed causes.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters urged independent backups, separate personal and developer identities, provider diversification, and possible legal action.
- Debate split between criticizing cloud dependence and arguing that opaque fraud controls can punish otherwise ordinary users without recourse.

### LLM perspective

- View: The central failure is not automated fraud detection but irreversible enforcement without intelligible appeal.
- Impact: A single identity provider can become a hidden point of failure across data, purchases, messaging, and work.
- Watch next: Whether Apple offers human review, data export, or a safe path to restore essential account functions.
