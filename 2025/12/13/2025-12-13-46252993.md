### TL;DR

The post marks Dick Van Dyke’s 100th birthday with a 2004 video and highlights his enthusiasm for Amiga computers and animation. The discussion became an affectionate centennial tribute spanning television, film, comedy, and extraordinary longevity. Commenters used his 1925 birth to measure the startling nearness of early American history, while memories from different generations showed the breadth of his appeal. They also held his exuberant public image alongside earlier alcoholism and smoking, making a full century seem less inevitable and more astonishing.
