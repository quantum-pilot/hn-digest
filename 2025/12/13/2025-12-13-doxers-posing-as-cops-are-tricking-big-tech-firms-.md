# Doxers posing as cops are tricking big tech firms into sharing people's data

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46252171) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/doxers-posing-as-cops-are-tricking-big-tech-firms-into-sharing-peoples-private-data/

### TL;DR

WIRED documents a fake emergency data request that impersonated the Jacksonville Sheriff’s Office with a lookalike domain, real officer details, and spoofed caller identity; Charter reportedly returned a target’s personal data within minutes. A hacker supplied recordings and documents and claimed a wider operation extracting data from major technology companies, but claims of hundreds of successes, revenue, compromised portals, and officer involvement were not independently confirmed. The case shows how systems optimized for urgent disclosure can be exploited when email identity substitutes for verified institutional authority.

### Comment pulse
