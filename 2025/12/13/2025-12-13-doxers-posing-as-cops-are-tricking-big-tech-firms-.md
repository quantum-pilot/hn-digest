# Doxers posing as cops are tricking big tech firms into sharing people's data

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46252171) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/doxers-posing-as-cops-are-tricking-big-tech-firms-into-sharing-peoples-private-data/

### TL;DR

WIRED reports that a doxing group impersonated police to obtain private customer data from major technology companies. Attackers used compromised or lookalike law-enforcement email accounts, authentic names and badge numbers, spoofed calls, and realistic legal documents; emergency requests often bypass slower verification because they allege imminent harm. Charter reportedly returned a target’s identity and contact details within minutes. Amazon confirmed an impersonator received basic data for fewer than 10 customers before being blocked. The investigation shows a verification system strained by 18,000 agencies and urgent public-safety demands.
