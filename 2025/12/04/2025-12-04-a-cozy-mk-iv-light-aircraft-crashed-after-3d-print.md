# A Cozy Mk IV light aircraft crashed after 3D-printed part was weakened by heat

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46152941) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c1w932vqye0o

### TL;DR

An experimental, home-built Cozy Mk IV was destroyed at Gloucestershire Airport after its engine lost power on final approach. Investigators found that a 3D-printed plastic air-induction elbow, installed during a fuel-system modification, softened from engine heat and collapsed. The pilot cleared a road and bushes but struck an instrument landing system, sustaining minor injuries. The AAIB called the material inappropriate, and the Light Aircraft Association plans an alert to inspectors about using 3D-printed parts.
