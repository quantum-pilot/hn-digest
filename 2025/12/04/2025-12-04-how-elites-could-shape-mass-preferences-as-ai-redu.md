# How elites could shape mass preferences as AI reduces persuasion costs

- Score: 466 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46145180) | Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04047

### TL;DR

An arXiv abstract models political elites choosing how strongly to reshape public preferences when persuasion has costs and outcomes follow majority rule. With one elite, the model predicts a pull toward greater polarization; cheaper persuasion accelerates it. With alternating opposing elites, strategies may instead produce cohesive “semi-lock” regions that resist reversal, so lower costs can either increase or decrease polarization. The AI connection is principally economic: commenters argue automated, personalized messaging could sharply reduce persuasion costs, though the model itself is not inherently AI-specific.
