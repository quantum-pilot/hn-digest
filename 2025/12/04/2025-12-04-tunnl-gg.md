# Tunnl.gg

- Score: 165 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46145902) | Link: https://tunnl.gg

### TL;DR

The service offers a public HTTPS URL for a local web server through one SSH reverse-tunnel command, with no client installation or account. The page pitches random subdomains, WebSocket support, and uses including webhook tests, demos, mobile testing, and CI callbacks. Discussion focuses less on convenience than exposure: anonymous tunneling attracts abuse, public endpoints invite automated probing, logs may be legally requested, and shared-domain cookies need isolation. The maintainer says source publication is planned, but service sustainability remains unproven.

### Comment pulse

- SSH ubiquity is the advantage → almost every developer already has the required client.
- Free anonymity creates operator risk → predecessors closed under abuse, while tunnel domains may be blocked for malware.
- Endpoint hygiene matters → use authentication, remove sensitive guessable paths, and isolate generated hostnames beneath a dedicated parent domain.
