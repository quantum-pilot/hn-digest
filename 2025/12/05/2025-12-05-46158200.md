# Cloudflare Down Again – and DownDetector Is Also Down

- Score: 306 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46158200) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46158200

### TL;DR

Cloudflare briefly failed again, and commenters reported that DownDetector was also unreachable, turning a routine outage check into a dependency joke. A Cloudflare statement reproduced in discussion attributed the incident to a Web Application Firewall parsing change deployed while mitigating a React Server Components vulnerability, not an attack. Discussion questioned whether global configuration changes lacked canary rollout and noted another HN thread. Reports of Edinburgh Airport disruption appeared coincidental after the airport said its air-traffic-control issue was unrelated.
