# The effect of shingles vaccination at different stages of dementia

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46164646) | Link: https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(25)01256-5

### TL;DR

A Cell paper uses Wales's exact date-of-birth eligibility cutoff for the live-attenuated shingles vaccine as a natural experiment: people born a week apart had roughly a 50-point difference in vaccination probability. Building on earlier work estimating fewer new dementia diagnoses, the study reports reductions in mild cognitive impairment diagnoses and dementia deaths among people already diagnosed. This quasi-random design reduces common observational confounding but is not a randomized trial, does not establish a mechanism, and may not generalize to other vaccines or programs.
