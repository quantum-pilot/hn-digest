# The effect of shingles vaccination at different stages of dementia

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46164646) | Link: https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(25)01256-5

### TL;DR

A Welsh vaccination cutoff creates a natural experiment: people turning 80 just after the shingles program began received one year of Zostavax eligibility, while almost identical older peers remained ineligible. Building on prior findings of fewer dementia diagnoses, analysis links eligibility for the live vaccine to fewer diagnoses of mild cognitive impairment and fewer dementia deaths among diagnosed patients. The design reduces healthy vaccinee confounding but remains observational. Results suggest benefit across the disease course without identifying one dementia subtype or proving whether viral suppression or immune effects drive it.
