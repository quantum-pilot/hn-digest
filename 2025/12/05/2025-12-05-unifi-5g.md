# UniFi 5G

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46157594) | Link: https://blog.ui.com/article/introducing-unifi-5g

### TL;DR

Ubiquiti's UniFi 5G Max lineup offers indoor and IP67 outdoor cellular WAN devices that adopt through any PoE port, plus a Dream Router model integrating 5G, Wi-Fi 7, storage, and UniFi OS. The company claims up to 2 Gbps downlink, policy-based routing, failover, load balancing, usage limits, physical SIM and eSIM support, and unlocked carrier choice. Commenters noted the outdoor unit requires a UniFi gateway, questioned the novelty, and warned that cellular backup can fail when local outages overload nearby networks.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters valued simple UniFi integration; skeptics compared the hardware with older 5G routers and modems.
- Operators saw uses for small-business failover, while noting that correlated cellular congestion can undermine resilience.
