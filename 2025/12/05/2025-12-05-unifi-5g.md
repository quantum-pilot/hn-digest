# UniFi 5G

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46157594) | Link: https://blog.ui.com/article/introducing-unifi-5g

### TL;DR

Ubiquiti’s new 5G lineup packages cellular WAN into three UniFi-managed devices. Indoor and IP67 outdoor Max units adopt as WAN interfaces through any PoE port, allowing signal-friendly placement without new cabling; the outdoor version adds stronger antennas. Both target primary, balanced or failover links, advertise up to 2 Gbps downlink, support policy routing, service-level rules and per-SIM caps. A Dream Router version integrates Wi-Fi 7, storage and UniFi OS. All models are unlocked, accept physical SIM or eSIM and provide dual-SIM flexibility.

### Comment pulse

- Fans valued simple failover and remote placement — counterpoint: critics saw familiar cellular modem capability repackaged inside a polished proprietary ecosystem.
- The outdoor unit requires a UniFi gateway; the Dream Router is the standalone all-in-one option, a distinction that confused readers.
- Cellular backup can fail during neighborhood outages because everyone shifts traffic simultaneously; others preferred dormant Starlink for independent resilience.
