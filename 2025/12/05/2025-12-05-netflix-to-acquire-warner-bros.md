# Netflix to Acquire Warner Bros

- Score: 1385 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46160315) | Link: https://about.netflix.com/en/news/netflix-to-acquire-warner-bros

### TL;DR

Netflix and Warner Bros. Discovery announced a definitive agreement under which Netflix would acquire Warner Bros., including its film and television studios, HBO, and HBO Max, after Discovery Global is spun out. The proposed cash-and-stock deal values WBD at $27.75 per share, with $82.7 billion enterprise value and $72 billion equity value. Closing is expected in 12–18 months, subject to the spinout, shareholder approval, and regulators. Netflix projects $2–3 billion in annual savings by year three.

### Comment pulse

- Critics feared concentration, advertising, weaker output, and a difficult engineering integration.
- Others welcomed a less fragmented catalog, while several predicted consolidation would encourage piracy.

### LLM perspective

- View: This is an announced, conditional transaction, not a completed reshaping of streaming.
- Impact: Combining distribution and major studios could reduce fragmentation while concentrating cultural and pricing power.
- Watch next: Regulatory scrutiny, the Discovery Global separation, and whether Netflix preserves theatrical operations and HBO's identity.
