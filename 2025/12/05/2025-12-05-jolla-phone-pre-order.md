# Jolla Phone Pre-Order

- Score: 215 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46162368) | Link: https://commerce.jolla.com/products/jolla-phone-preorder

### TL;DR

Jolla is seeking 2,000 refundable €99 reservations to produce a €499 community-defined Sailfish OS phone for Europe, the UK, Norway, and Switzerland, targeting delivery by mid-2026. Planned specifications include 5G, 12GB RAM, 256GB expandable storage, a 6.36-inch AMOLED display, replaceable 5,500mAh battery and back, Android AppSupport, five years of OS support, and a configurable physical privacy switch. Commenters welcomed a non-Android/iOS option but questioned size, North American compatibility, hardware-lockdown details, crowdfunding history, modularity, and whether Linux phones can remain daily drivers.

### Comment pulse

- Privacy hardware attracts interest → users want separate electromechanical controls for microphones, cameras, radios, and location rather than one configurable switch.
- Small-phone demand remains underserved → compact devices have loyal advocates — counterpoint: limited components and weak sales make them risky for small manufacturers.
- North American availability is uncertain → modem bands, certification, patents, and Jolla’s past market choices may outweigh stated roaming ambitions.
