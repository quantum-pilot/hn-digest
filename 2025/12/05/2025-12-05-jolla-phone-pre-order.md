# Jolla Phone Pre-Order

- Score: 215 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46162368) | Link: https://commerce.jolla.com/products/jolla-phone-preorder

### TL;DR

Jolla is seeking 2,000 refundable €99 reservations to produce a community-shaped Sailfish OS 5 phone, offering supporters a projected €499 total price and delivery by mid-2026 in selected European markets. The page snapshot shows 891 reservations. Proposed specifications include 12GB RAM, 256GB expandable storage, dual-SIM 5G, a 6.36-inch AMOLED screen, Android-app compatibility, a replaceable 5,500mAh battery, and a configurable physical privacy switch. Jolla promises five years of support, but says final specifications and manufacturing depend on funding and later confirmation.

### Comment pulse

- Privacy enthusiasts want separate hardware switches for microphone, cameras, radios, GPS, and cellular connectivity.
- Small-phone advocates dislike 6.36 inches—counterpoint: commenters doubt online enthusiasm can sustain niche production.
- U.S. buyers worry about bands and availability; one commenter cites an unfulfilled prior Jolla tablet preorder.
