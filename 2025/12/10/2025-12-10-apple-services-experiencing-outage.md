# Apple Services Experiencing Outage

- Score: 105 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46223577) | Link: https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/

### TL;DR

The captured Apple status page shows every listed consumer service as available at its last update, so it does not preserve the reported outage itself. HN users nevertheless describe intermittent failures affecting Apple Music, TV, App Store downloads, App Store Connect, TestFlight submissions, and build uploads, while others report uninterrupted streaming. The discussion suggests a transient or uneven incident whose scope cannot be reconstructed from this snapshot. Commenters also criticize Apple’s limited outage transparency compared with providers that publish detailed incident reports.
