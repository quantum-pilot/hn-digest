# GPT Image 1.5

- Score: 279 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46291941) | Link: https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-images-is-here/

### TL;DR

OpenAI’s GPT Image 1.5 release emphasizes more reliable localized editing, better instruction following and text rendering, preservation of lighting and likeness across revisions, and generation up to four times faster. It powers a redesigned ChatGPT Images workspace and is rolling out through ChatGPT and the API, where image inputs and outputs cost 20% less than GPT Image 1. OpenAI acknowledges continuing weaknesses with styles, multiple faces, multilingual text, and scientific accuracy. Early commenters reported strong edit compliance but mixed fidelity versus Google’s Nano Banana Pro.

### Comment pulse
