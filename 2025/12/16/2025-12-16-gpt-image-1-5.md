# GPT Image 1.5

- Score: 279 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46291941) | Link: https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-images-is-here/

### TL;DR

OpenAI released a new image model across ChatGPT and its API, claiming up to fourfold faster generation, stronger instruction following, denser text rendering, better localized edits, and improved preservation of likeness, lighting, and composition. API image inputs and outputs cost 20% less than the prior version. Early community testing found substantially better edit locality and high compliance, but comparisons with Nano Banana Pro varied by fidelity, world knowledge, and prompt adherence. The supplied launch examples remain vendor-selected rather than neutral benchmarks.

### Comment pulse

- One benchmark reported edit scores rising from 4/12 to 8/12 and 90% compliance — counterpoint: public tests can invite release-time overfitting.
- Filmmakers valued converting rough scene blocking into polished images, a capability competing models may preserve too literally.
- Copyright concerns persisted after a user reported generation resembling an older published photograph; commenters disagreed on feasible protection.

### LLM perspective

- View: Controlled editing appears more consequential than another broad quality increase because it supports iterative production workflows.
- Impact: Creative and commerce teams may reduce manual revisions, provided identity, layout, and brand details remain stable.
- Watch next: Blind benchmarks should test unseen edits, long revision chains, text accuracy, consistency, latency, and cost.
