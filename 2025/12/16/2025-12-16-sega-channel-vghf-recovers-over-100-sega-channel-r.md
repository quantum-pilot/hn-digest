# Sega Channel: VGHF Recovers over 100 Sega Channel ROMs (and More)

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46288024) | Link: https://gamehistory.org/segachannel/

### TL;DR

The Video Game History Foundation says a two-year preservation project recovered 144 Sega Channel ROMs from staff collections and tape backups, correcting the video's earlier count of 142. The haul includes nearly 100 system ROMs, exclusive and prototype games, limited variants, and an experimental Genesis web browser. Internal papers also document the service's operation and an unannounced PC successor called Express Games. The ROM data went to Gaming Alexandria, while Michael Shorrock's business records entered VGHF's digital library.
