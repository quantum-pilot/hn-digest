# Sega Channel: VGHF Recovers over 100 Sega Channel ROMs (and More)

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46288024) | Link: https://gamehistory.org/segachannel/

### TL;DR

The Video Game History Foundation recovered 144 Sega Channel ROMs from former executive Michael Shorrock’s collection and tape backups found by a community researcher. The set includes nearly 100 system versions, prototypes, cut-down and split releases, a cable-delivered Genesis web-browser experiment, and previously lost exclusives such as The Flintstones and Garfield’s Lost Levels. Internal documents also explain operations, marketing, an unannounced PC successor, and why subscriptions struggled. The foundation believes nearly every Sega Channel game and every unique US Genesis release is now backed up.

### Comment pulse

- Internal research showed casual players preferred ownership or rentals, while parents disliked an always-open subscription encouraging more play.
- Earlier cable-download systems predated Sega Channel — counterpoint: limited RAM, no persistent storage, and growing cartridge sizes constrained the model.
- Commenters saw the difficult recovery as a warning that subscription-only games vanish when services close and users retain no local copies.
