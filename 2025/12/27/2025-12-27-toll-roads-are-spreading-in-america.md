# Toll roads are spreading in America

- Score: 106 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46403992) | Link: https://www.economist.com/united-states/2025/12/18/toll-roads-are-spreading-in-america

### TL;DR

American tolling may expand because fuel taxes no longer fund road maintenance: cars use less petrol, Congress last raised the federal levy in 1993, and 2024 federal road spending exceeded receipts by $27 billion. Indiana authorized potential interstate tolls, while variable-price express lanes already charge for congestion relief. HN split over user-pays efficiency versus unequal access. Supporters say tolls charge drivers and externalities directly; critics call them regressive, surveillance-prone, confusing for visitors, and an incentive to neglect free lanes and transit.

### Comment pulse

- Usage pricing aligns costs → frequent drivers fund maintenance while peak tolls ration scarce road capacity.
- Ability to pay distorts access → faster infrastructure becomes routine for wealthy commuters but a substantial burden for low-wage workers.
- Transport choices shape fairness → better housing and transit could reduce forced driving instead of monetizing congestion.
