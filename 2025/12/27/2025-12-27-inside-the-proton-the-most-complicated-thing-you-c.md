# Inside the proton, the ‘most complicated thing you could possibly imagine’ (2022)

- Score: 116 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46398666) | Link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/inside-the-proton-the-most-complicated-thing-imaginable-20221019/

### TL;DR

A proton is not simply two up quarks and one down quark: experiments reveal a probe-dependent quantum object containing gluons and transient quark-antiquark seas, with constituent masses accounting for only about 1% of its total mass. High-energy scattering makes it appear increasingly gluon-dominated, while gentler views may occasionally reveal intrinsic charm quarks heavier than the proton itself. These details affect collider and cosmic-neutrino predictions. HN discussion clarified that “complexity” means nonlinear QCD equations are extraordinarily hard to solve, not necessarily biological-style organizational complexity.
