# Scientists edited genes in a living person and saved his life

- Score: 90 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46403955) | Link: https://www.popularmechanics.com/science/health/a64815804/crispr-therapy/

### TL;DR

Researchers created a bespoke in-vivo CRISPR treatment in six months for infant KJ’s severe CPS1 deficiency, which causes toxic ammonia accumulation and normally requires a liver transplant. Three infusions enabled him to tolerate protein and return home, demonstrating that a therapy can target one patient’s mutation inside the body. HN discussion focused on delivery, durability, and why translation took 13 years: editing only enough relevant cells may work, but off-target risk, dosing, organ targeting, and immune responses demand caution.
