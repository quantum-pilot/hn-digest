# Gpg.fail

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46403200) | Link: https://gpg.fail

### TL;DR

Gpg.fail catalogues 14 flaws across GnuPG, OpenPGP processing, and minisign, including signature confusion, path traversal, message malleability, memory corruption, SHA-1 downgrade, trust-packet abuse, and comment injection. Proofs, patches, and talk materials were still forthcoming, leaving severity and affected workflows unevenly documented. HN discussion split between calls to abandon GPG’s sprawling, multi-context design and warnings against panic: several attacks involve adversarial local data or ambiguous command output, while ordinary Git signing may remain unaffected. Maintainers’ “wontfix” posture drew particular distrust.
