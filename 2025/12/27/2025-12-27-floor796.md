# Floor796

- Score: 468 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46401612) | Link: https://floor796.com/

### TL;DR

Floor796 is a sprawling interactive pixel-art scene packed with animated characters, cultural references, and clickable Easter eggs. Its creator began in 2018, first spending a year building a custom editor, rendering engine, and site; each new block now reportedly takes one to 1.5 months. HN readers compared its dense, zoomable composition to Bosch, Moebius, eBoy, Flashback, and Theme Hospital, while highlighting playful interactions such as Naruto and Chuck Norris. The source capture itself mostly exposes internal tile-loading status rather than artwork.
