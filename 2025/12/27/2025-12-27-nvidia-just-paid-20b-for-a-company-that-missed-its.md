# Nvidia just paid $20B for a company that missed its revenue target by 75%

- Score: 192 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46403041) | Link: https://blog.drjoshcsimmons.com/p/nvidia-just-paid-20-billion-for-a

### TL;DR

The author calls Nvidia’s reported $20 billion Groq asset-and-talent deal a defensive purchase signaling an AI bubble, arguing Nvidia is neutralizing faster, energy-efficient LPU inference while recycling capital through customers and infrastructure partners. The piece links this concentration to data-center electricity costs, weak enterprise AI returns, and expected 2026 valuation cuts. Its headline logic is flawed, however: Groq cut projected 2025 revenue from roughly $2 billion to $500 million, not valuation; a later round valued it at $6.9 billion. HN readers repeatedly corrected that conflation.
