# Employee commits suicide after MongoDB fired her during mental health leave

- Score: 115 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46403128) | Link: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gsurman_our-beloved-irreplaceable-daughter-annie-activity-7407842359826120704-kFaq

### TL;DR

Annie Surman’s family alleges MongoDB demanded her return during mental-health disability leave, denied time to consult clinicians, terminated her health insurance, and fired her; she died by suicide soon afterward. Their wrongful-termination and wrongful-death complaint invokes federal, New York State, and city disability protections, while seeking accountability and correcting an alleged claim that she quit. HN reactions were largely condemnatory, contrasting the allegations with MongoDB’s public wellbeing messaging, though some questioned how law should distinguish protected leave from strategic leave around performance disputes.

### Comment pulse

- Accountability view → disability protections mean little if employers can withdraw insurance and employment during intensive treatment.
- Counterargument → leave can complicate performance management — counterpoint: commenters cited statutory protections and restorative workplace benefits.
- Structural response → unions, works councils, stronger safety nets, and limits on at-will employment could reduce job-loss fear.
