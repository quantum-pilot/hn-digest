# QNX Self-Hosted Developer Desktop

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46398201) | Link: https://devblog.qnx.com/qnx-self-hosted-developer-desktop-initial-release/

### TL;DR

QNX released an initial self-hosted QNX 8.0 developer desktop, eliminating mandatory cross-compilation inside its QEMU-on-Ubuntu image. It bundles XFCE on Wayland, compilers, Python, build tools, editors, a browser, file management, and samples, drawing from more than 600 unique open-source ports. Planned targets include Windows, macOS, x86, Raspberry Pi, and CI. HN welcomed renewed accessibility and recalled QNX’s famously compact demo disk, while many mourned the distinctive, lightweight Photon desktop that XFCE replaces.
