### TL;DR

Thien-Thi Nguyen’s daughter found an earlier Hacker News thread about her father’s death and created a new post because she could no longer reply there. She explains that he was intensely private and says she discovered the discussion only recently. Her purpose is simply to thank community members for the kind words they shared after his passing.
