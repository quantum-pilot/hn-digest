# India orders smartphone makers to preload state-owned cyber safety app

- Score: 397 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46104193) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/india-orders-mobile-phones-preloaded-with-government-app-ensure-cyber-safety-2025-12-01/

### TL;DR

Reuters reports that India privately ordered smartphone makers to preload the state-run Sanchar Saathi app within 90 days and prevent users from disabling it, including through updates to devices already in supply chains. Officials present the app as protection against stolen phones, spoofed identifiers, and fraudulent connections, citing millions of blocks and terminations. Critics object to compulsory installation and the lack of meaningful consent; commenters largely viewed the move as surveillance-prone, while some questioned whether education could address the stated problem instead.
