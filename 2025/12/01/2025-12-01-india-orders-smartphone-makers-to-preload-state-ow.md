# India orders smartphone makers to preload state-owned cyber safety app

- Score: 397 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46104193) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/india-orders-mobile-phones-preloaded-with-government-app-ensure-cyber-safety-2025-12-01/

### TL;DR

India’s telecom ministry privately directed major phone makers to preload Sanchar Saathi within 90 days, prevent users from disabling it, and push it to devices already in supply chains. The government says its registry counters spoofed IMEIs, fraud, and phone theft, citing millions of blocked devices and terminated connections. A source says Apple’s policies forbid government preinstalls, making negotiation likely. Discussion treated the undisclosed mandate as a consent and surveillance problem, while questioning official usage figures and downstream compliance risks for personal devices.
