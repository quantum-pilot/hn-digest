# Advent of Sysadmin 2025

- Score: 347 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46102347) | Link: https://sadservers.com/advent

### TL;DR

SadServers’ Advent of Sysadmin offers one Linux or DevOps troubleshooting scenario daily from December 1 through 12. Free accounts track progress, while an initial no-registration challenge asks participants to repair nginx routing among three Docker containers without stopping or removing them. The exercise provides explicit curl-based success tests and a local checker. Commenters value the platform for hands-on learning and technical interviews, though their joking “real-world” challenge list emphasizes that operations work also depends on communication, permissions, security practices, and organizational change.
