# We invited a man into our home at Christmas and he stayed with us for 45 years

- Score: 885 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46383552) | Link: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cdxwllqz1l0o

### TL;DR

In 1975, Cardiff newlyweds Rob and Dianne Parsons invited Ronnie Lockwood—an autistic, homeless acquaintance carrying his possessions and a frozen chicken—inside for Christmas. He remained family for 45 years. They helped him find work and stability; he later supported their children, volunteered for homeless people, and navigated a gambling addiction with them. After Ronnie died in 2020, his £40,000 bequest exactly funded a church wellbeing center’s roof. HN shared similarly lasting acts of hospitality while debating personal compassion versus systemic care.
