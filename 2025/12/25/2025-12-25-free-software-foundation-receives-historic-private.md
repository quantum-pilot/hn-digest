# Free Software Foundation receives historic private donations

- Score: 202 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46382134) | Link: https://www.fsf.org/news/free-software-foundation-receives-historic-private-donations

### TL;DR

The Free Software Foundation received two anonymous Monero donations totaling about $900,000, among its largest private gifts. The money will expand technical and infrastructure capacity plus campaigns, education, licensing, advocacy, and future opportunities. With its $400,000 winter fundraiser already three-quarters complete, the FSF planned to shift toward gaining 100 associate members by January 16. HN celebrated the privacy-preserving gifts but debated tax treatment, the absence of comparable corporate funding, and whether corporate interests fundamentally conflict with FSF principles.

### Comment pulse

- Anonymous Monero matched the mission → supporters welcomed privacy without donor recognition, though tax deductibility prompted uncertainty.
- Corporate underfunding drew criticism → counterpoint: firms often prefer permissive ecosystems and the Linux Foundation over FSF copyleft advocacy.
- Public funding preserves independence → commenters argued organizations defending users should not rely on companies whose incentives diverge.

- View: Large anonymous gifts buy capacity without visibly trading mission independence for corporate influence.
