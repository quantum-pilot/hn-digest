# Python Applied Mathematics Labs

- Score: 82 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46381839) | Link: https://labs.acme.byu.edu/Pages/intro.html

### TL;DR

BYU’s ACME Labs provide free, hands-on companions to the Foundations of Applied Mathematics textbooks. Four volumes progress from Python, linear algebra, Fourier methods, and numerical stability through optimization, uncertainty, machine learning, differential equations, and control. Self-contained labs combine theory, examples, exercises, downloadable materials, and practical projects such as tsunami routing, image compression, recommendation systems, and epidemiological models. HN praised the public GitHub-based laboratory material but argued that keeping the underlying textbooks paid limits the educational resource’s openness and reach.
