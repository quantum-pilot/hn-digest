# Microsoft denies rewriting Windows 11 in Rust using AI

- Score: 67 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46381813) | Link: https://www.windowslatest.com/2025/12/24/microsoft-denies-rewriting-windows-11-using-ai-after-an-employees-one-engineer-one-month-one-million-code-post-on-linkedin-causes-outrage/

### TL;DR

Microsoft Distinguished Engineer Galen Hunt wrote that his goal was eliminating C and C++ from Microsoft by 2030 through AI and algorithms, targeting one engineer rewriting one million lines in one month. After backlash, Hunt called it a language-migration research project—not a Windows rewrite or a declaration that Rust is the endpoint—and Microsoft communications issued the same denial. The article remains skeptical, connecting the episode to broader AI-code ambitions and resource-heavy WebView2 apps. HN split over literal versus aspirational readings.
