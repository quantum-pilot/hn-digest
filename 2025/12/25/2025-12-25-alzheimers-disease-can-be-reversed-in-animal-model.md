# Alzheimer’s disease can be reversed in animal models? Study

- Score: 410 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46384919) | Link: https://case.edu/news/new-study-shows-alzheimers-disease-can-be-reversed-achieve-full-neurological-recovery-not-just-prevented-or-slowed-animal-models

### TL;DR

Researchers restored brain NAD+ balance with P7C3-A20 in two advanced transgenic mouse models, one amyloid-based and one tau-based. Pathology and cognitive function recovered, while blood phosphorylated tau 217 normalized; preventive treatment also stopped disease development. The authors emphasize balanced NAD+ rather than high-dose precursor supplementation and call for human trials. HN combined hope with sharp skepticism: engineered mice poorly represent heterogeneous human Alzheimer’s, past mouse successes often failed clinically, and self-experimenting with research chemicals is dangerous.
