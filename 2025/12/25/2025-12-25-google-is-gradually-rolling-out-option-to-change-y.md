# Google is 'gradually rolling out' option to change your gmail.com address

- Score: 65 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46387192) | Link: https://9to5google.com/2025/12/24/google-change-gmail-addresses/

### TL;DR

Google’s Hindi support page revealed a gradual rollout allowing users to replace one `@gmail.com` address with another. The old address remains an alias for receiving mail and signing in; account data and service access stay intact, and nobody else can claim it. Users cannot change or delete the new address for 12 months, can make at most three changes, and may see the old address persist in older records. HN welcomed relief from youthful usernames while emphasizing account-recovery failures and Google dependency.
