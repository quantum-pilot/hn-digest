# I'm a laptop weirdo and that's why I like my new Framework 13

- Score: 287 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46391410) | Link: https://blog.matthewbrunelle.com/im-a-laptop-weirdo-and-thats-why-i-like-my-new-framework-13/

### TL;DR

The author traded an M1 Max MacBook Pro for a Framework 13 to recover something modern laptops had removed: the pleasure and utility of modification. Earlier machines supported RAM, storage, wireless, optical-drive, operating-system, and performance experiments that taught practical skills even when they produced little conventional work. Framework’s replaceable memory, SSD, screen, keyboard, hinges, ports, and mainboard revive that relationship. HN praised accessible official parts and customization, while debating build quality, battery efficiency, upgrade economics, shipping delays, and whether reliable sealed machines better serve professionals.

### Comment pulse

- Official parts turn flaws into upgrades → owners replaced keyboards and hinges cheaply instead of discarding otherwise functional machines.
- Modularity may smooth obsolescence → counterpoint: mainboard, RAM, shipping, and efficiency costs can erase the economic advantage.
- Customization may outlast repair marketing → users want richer keyboards, touchpads, ports, and community modules tailored to workflows.
