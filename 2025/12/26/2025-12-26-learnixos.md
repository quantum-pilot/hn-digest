# LearnixOS

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46391599) | Link: https://www.learnix-os.com

### TL;DR

LearnixOS is a work-in-progress book and codebase for building a POSIX-style operating system in Rust with almost no external libraries. It targets programmers with basic assembly and memory knowledge, then progresses through standalone binaries, bootloading, CPU modes, paging and allocation, interrupts, filesystems, processes, a shell, and eventually Doom. The author intends to document both concepts and implementation decisions, including Rust and toolchain details. HN welcomed the tutorial but debated its confusing NixOS-adjacent name, early prose errors, originality among OS courses, and language-specific emphasis.
