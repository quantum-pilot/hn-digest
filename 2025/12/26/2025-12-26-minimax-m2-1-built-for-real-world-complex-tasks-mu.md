# MiniMax M2.1: Built for Real-World Complex Tasks, Multi-Language Programming

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46388213) | Link: https://www.minimaxi.com/news/minimax-m21

### TL;DR

MiniMax positions M2.1 as an open-weight, lower-cost model for multilingual coding, native mobile and web development, office automation, and agent tool use. It claims stronger instruction following, shorter responses, and an 88.6 average on its VIBE full-stack benchmark, with deployment through APIs or local inference. Early HN users find it roughly near Sonnet 4.5 and compelling for routine work at its price, but less dependable for deep debugging or complex planning.
