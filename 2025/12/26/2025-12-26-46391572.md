### TL;DR

An Ask HN prompt began with The Changing World Order and The Gulag Archipelago, then produced an unusually broad communal syllabus. Recommendations span literary classics, contemporary fiction, science fiction, technical manuals, telecommunications history, mysteries, graphic novels, and children’s books repeatedly read aloud. Commenters paired titles with candid judgments rather than bare lists, praising the Hyperion Cantos while criticizing other works as dated, disappointing, or overlong. The thread’s value lies less in consensus than context: parenting, professional interests, nostalgia, and unfinished goals shaped each list.

### Comment pulse

- Eclectic lists beat a single canon → readers mixed classics, obscure technical works, genre fiction, and memoir according to curiosity.
- Repetition counts as reading → parents’ year-end lists centered on beloved children’s books read dozens of times.
- Science-fiction judgments emphasized longevity → commenters separated groundbreaking concepts from prose, morality, and storytelling that still hold up.
