# Fathers’ choices may be packaged and passed down in sperm RNA

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46407502) | Link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/how-dads-fitness-may-be-packaged-and-passed-down-in-sperm-rna-20251222/

### TL;DR

Mouse studies increasingly suggest sperm carries environmentally responsive RNAs alongside DNA. Diet, stress, nicotine, and exercise alter RNA cargo, much of it loaded during epididymal maturation via vesicles; injecting affected sperm RNA into zygotes can reproduce metabolic or behavioral traits. A 2025 study linked paternal exercise microRNAs to embryonic gene regulation, mitochondrial activity, and fitter offspring, with similar RNAs elevated in trained men. Researchers emphasize that the mechanism remains incomplete and human inheritance is unproven, a caution echoed throughout HN’s discussion.
