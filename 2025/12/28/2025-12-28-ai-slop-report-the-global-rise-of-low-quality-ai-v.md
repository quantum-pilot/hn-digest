# AI Slop Report: The Global Rise of Low-Quality AI Videos

- Score: 154 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46409125) | Link: https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/

### TL;DR

Kapwing manually classified trending YouTube channels worldwide and sampled 500 Shorts from a fresh account, finding 104 AI-generated videos and 165 “brainrot” videos—21% and 33% respectively. Leading slop channels have billions of views and estimated annual revenue above $4 million, giving creators and platforms strong incentives to keep producing them. The report warns that synthetic volume crowds out handmade work, exhausts attention, and erodes trust. HN users described subscriptions, RSS, and recommendation-blocking extensions as practical defenses.
