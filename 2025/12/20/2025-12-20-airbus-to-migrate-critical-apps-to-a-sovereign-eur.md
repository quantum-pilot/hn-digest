# Airbus to migrate critical apps to a sovereign Euro cloud

- Score: 430 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46334533) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/19/airbus_sovereign_cloud/

### TL;DR

Airbus plans a January request for proposals for a digitally sovereign European cloud, with a provider decision targeted before summer. The possible contract exceeds €50 million and could span ten years, covering sensitive ERP, manufacturing, CRM and product-lifecycle systems, including aircraft designs. Airbus says European control is necessary because foreign laws may expose data or interrupt access, but finding a provider with sufficient scale remains uncertain. The tender is preparation, not evidence that a migration or vendor selection has occurred.

### Comment pulse

- Readers largely supported reducing reliance on US-controlled cloud providers, citing jurisdiction, espionage history and geopolitical service risk.
- Others stressed that “cloud” spans applications and infrastructure, arguing portability and commodity components matter more than provider nationality alone.
