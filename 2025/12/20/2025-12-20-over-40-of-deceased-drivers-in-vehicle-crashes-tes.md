# Over 40% of deceased drivers in vehicle crashes test positive for THC: Study

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46337123) | Link: https://www.facs.org/media-center/press-releases/2025/over-40-of-deceased-drivers-in-motor-vehicle-crashes-test-positive-for-thc-study-shows/

### TL;DR

An ACS conference abstract reviewed 246 deceased drivers tested after fatal crashes in Montgomery County, Ohio, from 2019 through September 2024. Active THC appeared in 41.9%, with a mean concentration of 30.7 ng/mL; annual positivity varied from 25.7% to 48.9% and did not change significantly after recreational legalization. The authors urge stronger warnings against cannabis use before driving. However, the dataset lacks a driving-population control, distribution details, alcohol results, and direct impairment measurement, so it cannot establish that THC caused these crashes.

### Comment pulse

- Some found the prevalence alarming; others stressed that blood thresholds correlate poorly with impairment in habitual users.
- Readers questioned selection, mean-versus-median reporting, baseline prevalence, and missing co-intoxicants; the abstract was not yet peer reviewed.
