# The Deviancy Signal: Having "Nothing to Hide" Is a Threat to Us All

- Score: 189 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46333830) | Link: https://thompson2026.com/blog/deviancy-signal/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that routine transparency creates a behavioral baseline that future surveillance systems can classify as normal. If politics or social norms later change, adopting encryption or anonymity after years of openness may itself become a conspicuous “deviancy signal.” Transparent users also expose private contacts through metadata, weakening others’ operational security. The proposed remedy is collective, default privacy: widespread encryption and anonymity make protected behavior ordinary rather than suspicious. Comments broadly supported privacy but disputed whether this specific baseline-change mechanism is demonstrated rather than rhetorically plausible.

### Comment pulse
