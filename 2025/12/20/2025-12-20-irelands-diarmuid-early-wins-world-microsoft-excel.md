# Ireland’s Diarmuid Early wins world Microsoft Excel title

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46339031) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj4qzgvxxgvo

### TL;DR

Ireland’s Diarmuid Early won the 2025 Microsoft Excel World Championship in Las Vegas, defeating three-time champion Andrew Ngai and earning $5,000 plus the title belt. The event began with 256 competitors and culminated in an arena final where low scorers were eliminated every five minutes. Modern Excel esports emphasizes timed general problem-solving—mazes, poker hands, historical matching—rather than accounting alone. Early says clean solutions to early levels accelerate later work, and the visibility has already attracted clients to his New York financial business.
