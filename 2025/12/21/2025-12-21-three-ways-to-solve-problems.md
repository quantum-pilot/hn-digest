# Three ways to solve problems

- Score: 107 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46345125) | Link: https://andreasfragner.com/writing/three-ways-to-solve-problems

### TL;DR

Using Gerald Weinberg's definition of a problem as the gap between perceived and desired states, the essay offers three responses: change reality, reinterpret the current state, or revise the goal. The latter two can reveal that no action is needed or that a partial result delivers most value at much lower cost. For founders and product managers, the practical discipline is declining most imperfections so scarce effort reaches the few consequential ones. Commenters added waiting for conditions to change, while warning that delay easily becomes habitual avoidance.
