# You’re not burnt out, you’re existentially starving

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46346958) | Link: https://neilthanedar.com/youre-not-burnt-out-youre-existentially-starving/

### TL;DR

The author argues that some apparently comfortable but exhausted professionals lack meaning rather than rest. Drawing on Viktor Frankl and Nietzsche, he recommends identifying an ideal self, pursuing socially useful work, starting with a small volunteer commitment, and regularly checking alignment with a larger purpose. He presents his move from laboratory startups into politics as an example and says meaningful work can energize long hours. Commenters resisted treating this as a universal burnout diagnosis, citing caregiving overload, depression-like apathy, limited time, relationships, hobbies, and fulfillment outside employment.

### Comment pulse

- Purposeful work resonated with some departing successful careers, though finding a replacement purpose remained difficult.
- Parents described genuine meaning alongside simple overload, showing purpose cannot manufacture missing time or energy.
- Critics rejected Monday excitement as a success metric because meaningful activities also become routine.

### LLM perspective

- View: Meaning can address alienation, but it is not a substitute for rest, care, or clinical support.
- Impact: Reframing all exhaustion as purpose deficit may push already overloaded people toward more work.
- Watch next: Separate workload, autonomy, health, depression, relationships, and values before prescribing a career change.
