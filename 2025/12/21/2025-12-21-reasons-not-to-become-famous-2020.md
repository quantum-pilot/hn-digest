# Reasons not to become famous (2020)

- Score: 143 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46345341) | Link: https://tim.blog/2020/02/02/reasons-to-not-become-famous/

### TL;DR

Tim Ferriss's 2020 essay says a large audience enabled friendships, investments, and philanthropy but also brought stalkers, threats, family harassment, extortion, impersonation, manipulative relationships, privacy loss, and anxiety. His city metaphor argues that sufficiently large audiences inevitably contain dangerous outliers, regardless of content. He advises defining the desired kind of visibility, protecting home information, planning an exit, and preferring a smaller relevant audience. Commenters challenged Ferriss's credibility and whether self-help attracts exceptional obsession, while several minor public figures corroborated similar encounters.
