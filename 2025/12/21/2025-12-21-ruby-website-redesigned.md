# Ruby website redesigned

- Score: 352 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46342859) | Link: https://www.ruby-lang.org/en/

### TL;DR

Ruby's redesigned homepage presents the language through four themes: its large gem and Rails ecosystem, readable syntax, concise productivity features, and welcoming MINASWAN community. It foregrounds installation, documentation, an online try path, multilingual navigation, release news, and security advisories, using animated illustrations and community photography. HN readers praised the visual identity, examples, and renewed interest in Ruby, but criticized a loading screen, JavaScript-dependent snippets, image optimization, and a vague “Programmer's Best Friend” tagline that explains less than the previous introduction.

### Comment pulse

- Fans found Ruby joyful and productive after years elsewhere, crediting its supportive community and expressive language design.
- Critics called the homepage overengineered — counterpoint: one reader measured only 9.7 KB of JavaScript across navigation.

### LLM perspective

- View: The redesign sells Ruby's culture effectively but weakens immediate technical orientation.
- Impact: Newcomers may feel welcomed while encountering avoidable failures when scripts or animations do not load.
