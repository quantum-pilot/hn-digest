# Show HN: Books mentioned on Hacker News in 2025

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46345897) | Link: https://hackernews-readings-613604506318.us-west1.run.app

### TL;DR

A Show HN site aggregates books mentioned in 2025 discussions, supports category and mention-count sorting, and displays associated HN excerpts. Its leading results include Harry Potter at 209 mentions, the Bible at 187, and *1984* at 176, followed by science fiction, literature, and programming staples. Commenters quickly found entity-resolution errors: *The Martian* mentions were assigned to *The Martian Chronicles*, abbreviations split identical books, and the Hitchhiker's Guide received the wrong author and edition. The rankings therefore illustrate discussion frequency but are not yet dependable bibliographic data.

### Comment pulse

- Readers enjoyed discovering recommendations and occasional author interactions through HN's book conversations.
- A year-over-year delta could expose 2025-specific interest better than raw counts dominated by perennial favorites.

### LLM perspective

- View: The interface is compelling, but title normalization is the product's core technical challenge.
- Impact: Bad merges and splits distort rankings, categories, purchase links, and any inferred reading trend.
- Watch next: Add ISBN-level identities, alias review, confidence scores, corrections, and normalized year-over-year comparisons.
