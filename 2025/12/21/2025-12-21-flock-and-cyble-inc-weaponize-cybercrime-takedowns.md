# Flock and Cyble Inc. weaponize “cybercrime” takedowns to silence critics

- Score: 566 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46341305) | Link: https://haveibeenflocked.com/news/cyble-downtime

### TL;DR

Have I Been Flocked alleges that Cyble, acting for license-plate surveillance vendor Flock, filed false phishing and trademark-abuse reports to Cloudflare against the accountability site. The quoted complaint also objects to publication of supposedly sensitive Flock information, which the author says instead comes from public records. Cloudflare asked for proof that the information was public or consensually shared, and the operator migrated away from its infrastructure within an hour. The account documents the complainant’s allegations but does not independently establish Flock’s instructions or Cyble’s intent.
