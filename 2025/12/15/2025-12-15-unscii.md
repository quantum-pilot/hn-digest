# Unscii

- Score: 306 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46270282) | Link: http://viznut.fi/unscii/

### TL;DR

Unscii is a family of bitmap-inspired Unicode fonts designed for character-cell art, terminals, and programming. Its principal variants use 8×8 and 8×16 glyphs, with stylistic alternatives and a larger GPL-licensed build incorporating Fixedsys Excelsior and GNU Unifont coverage; most other variants are public domain. Version 2 mapped many existing legacy-computing glyphs to Unicode 13 code points, fixed shapes, and added characters. The project studies classic Amiga, Atari, Commodore, IBM, Macintosh, X11, and terminal fonts while retaining private-use mappings for compatibility.
