# Unscii

- Score: 306 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46270282) | Link: http://viznut.fi/unscii/

### TL;DR

The project is a family of bitmap-inspired Unicode fonts designed for character-cell art, terminals, and programming. Its principal faces use 8×8 or 8×16 glyphs, with stylistic variants and a fuller terminal edition incorporating Fixedsys Excelsior and GNU Unifont coverage. Version 2.0 mapped many PETSCII, Teletext, and Videotex forms newly standardized in Unicode 13, while retaining private-use mappings for compatibility. Downloads span true bitmap HEX and PCF plus vectorized TTF, OTF, and WOFF; most variants are public domain, while the Unifont-derived full face is GPL.
