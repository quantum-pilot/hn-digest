# Google is dead. Where do we go now?

- Score: 379 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46425198) | Link: https://www.circusscientist.com/2025/12/29/google-is-dead-where-do-we-go-now/

### TL;DR

A South African entertainment business owner says revenue fell 50% in three months despite escalating Google Ads spending, even exhausting promotional credit at ten times the normal budget without returns. He plans to test TikTok and Instagram, revive email outreach, advertise physically, and diversify products. HN reframed the claim as one small advertiser’s Google Ads failure, not Google’s death: attention may be shifting toward video, messaging, and chatbots, while intense auction competition and weaker consumer spending can separately erase small-business returns.
