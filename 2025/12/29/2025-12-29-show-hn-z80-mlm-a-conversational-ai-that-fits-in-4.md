# Show HN: Z80-μLM, a 'Conversational AI' That Fits in 40KB

- Score: 463 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46417815) | Link: https://github.com/HarryR/z80ai

### TL;DR

Z80-μLM compresses inference, weights, and a chat interface into a roughly 40KB CP/M binary for a 4MHz Z80 with 64KB RAM. It uses trigram hashing, 2-bit weights, 16-bit integer arithmetic, and character-by-character generation to produce terse, personality-like replies or play 20 Questions. The model neither understands grammar nor supports deep context, but HN celebrated the retrocomputing achievement and explored tiny cognitive cores, alternate histories of early neural computing, and weight-level secret extraction.
