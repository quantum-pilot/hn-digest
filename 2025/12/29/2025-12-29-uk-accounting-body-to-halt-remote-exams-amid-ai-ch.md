# UK accounting body to halt remote exams amid AI cheating

- Score: 164 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46420289) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/business/2025/dec/29/uk-accounting-remote-exams-ai-cheating-acca

### TL;DR

The Association of Chartered Certified Accountants will end remote exams from March except in exceptional cases, saying AI-assisted cheating now outpaces available safeguards. Remote testing began during Covid, but professional-accounting exams already had a history of cheating and regulatory fines. HN broadly favored returning high-stakes assessment to controlled venues, while debating whether AI created the problem or merely amplified predictable weaknesses. Educators described large gaps between online coursework and supervised exam performance and a shift toward heavier in-person testing.
