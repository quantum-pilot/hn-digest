# Swapping SIM cards used to be easy, and then came eSIM

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46421653) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2025/12/i-switched-to-esim-in-2025-and-i-am-full-of-regret/

### TL;DR

After two failed eSIM transfers in three months, a phone reviewer argues that replacing removable SIMs turns a seconds-long hardware swap into a carrier-dependent recovery process. SMS-based account verification becomes circular when the broken eSIM controls access to those messages, potentially locking users out of services tied to their number. HN experiences varied sharply: some praised rapid provisioning, travel convenience, and theft resistance, while others blamed carrier policies and objected to surrendering user-controlled device transfers.
