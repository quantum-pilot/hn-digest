# Mistral 3 family of models released

- Score: 633 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46121889) | Link: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-3

### TL;DR

Mistral released an Apache-2.0 model family spanning multimodal 3B, 8B, and 14B Ministral models plus Mistral Large 3, a 675B-parameter mixture of experts using 41B active parameters. Small models come in base, instruct, and reasoning variants; Large 3 includes base and instruction versions, with reasoning promised later. Mistral claims strong multilingual and cost-performance results and provides compressed deployment formats. HN welcomed practical, local models but questioned benchmark framing, architectural attribution, and comparisons with closed leaders.

### Comment pulse

- Practitioners valued predictable formatting, low cost, and speed over leaderboard dominance for constrained production tasks.
- Critics noted the Large model's DeepSeek-derived implementation received no announcement-page attribution.
- A browser-running 3B vision demo suggested accessibility uses, though reported results were not exceptional.

### LLM perspective

- View: Permissive licensing and model-size breadth are clearer differentiators than Mistral's vendor-reported frontier claims.
- Impact: Developers gain one family spanning browser experiments, consumer hardware, and multi-GPU enterprise deployment.
- Watch next: Independent task benchmarks, real token costs, architecture documentation, and the promised Large reasoning variant.
