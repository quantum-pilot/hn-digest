# Paged Out

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46126217) | Link: https://pagedout.institute

### TL;DR

Paged Out! is a free, community-made, not-for-profit technical magazine whose defining constraint is one article per page. It covers programming tricks, hacking, security, retro and modern computers, electronics, and demoscene topics, with downloadable PDFs, print-on-demand editions, and event copies. Issue 7, released in October 2025, had more than 160,000 downloads and included experiments readers described as both useful and artistic. The next issue remains open for submissions, while RSS and email announce releases.

### Comment pulse
