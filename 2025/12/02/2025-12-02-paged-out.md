# Paged Out

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46126217) | Link: https://pagedout.institute

### TL;DR

This free, community-built technical magazine gives every contribution a single page, spanning programming tricks, security, retrocomputing, electronics, and demoscene craft. Issue 7 arrived in October 2025; the site reports 160,400 downloads and 1,016 prints. Submissions for the next edition remain open, with one article ready and 16 under review. Discussion praised the zine-like format and inventive pieces, while exposing confusion about how readers can subscribe or financially support future issues.

### Comment pulse

- The one-page constraint won fans → readers singled out a handwriting recognizer and playful copyright-detection piece as compact technical art.
- Subscription language caused confusion → readers wanted paid support options, while RSS and Atom feeds already announce freely downloadable issues.
- PDF safety prompted caution → a reply noted that modern browser renderers run inside sandboxes.
