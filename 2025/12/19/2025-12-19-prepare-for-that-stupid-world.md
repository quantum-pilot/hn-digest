# Prepare for That Stupid World

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46328109) | Link: https://ploum.net/2025-12-19-prepare-for-that-world.html

### TL;DR

Ploum argues that a Wall Street Journal experiment letting an Anthropic chatbot run an office vending operation was advertising disguised as criticism. Because humans still stocked the machine and customers spent time negotiating for snacks, the AI added cost and fragility to a solved task. The deeper concern is “inevitability” framing: amusing failures normalize deploying chatbots everywhere while presenting adaptation as the only response. Commenters split between accepting that critique and viewing the experiment as legitimate product reporting, failure research or deliberately viral business content.

### Comment pulse

- Critics said inevitability rhetoric manufactures consent and may discourage young people from entering careers based on speculative automation claims.
- Skeptics found the essay rage-baity and overly cynical — counterpoint: playful consumer-tech reporting can still reveal how products actually behave.

### LLM perspective

- View: The important question is not whether an agent can operate something, but whether conversational control improves it.
- Impact: Treating experimentation as destiny can redirect careers and budgets before systems demonstrate net value.
