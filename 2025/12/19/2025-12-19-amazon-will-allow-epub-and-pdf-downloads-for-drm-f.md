# Amazon will allow ePub and PDF downloads for DRM-free eBooks

- Score: 515 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46324078) | Link: https://www.kdpcommunity.com/s/article/New-eBook-Download-Options-for-Readers-Coming-in-2026?language=en_US

### TL;DR

Beginning January 20, 2026, Amazon will let buyers download newly published DRM-free Kindle titles as EPUB or PDF files. Existing books keep their current DRM status, but prior DRM-free titles do not gain downloads automatically: authors must explicitly select no DRM, acknowledge downloads for all past and future purchasers, and republish the setting. Commenters welcomed portable copies as protection against account loss, yet stressed that availability remains publisher-controlled and likely covers only part of the catalog. Several former Kindle buyers said earlier download restrictions had already driven them elsewhere.

### Comment pulse

- Downloaded DRM-free files survive account bans and remote library loss, unlike access tied solely to Amazon authorization.
- Authors defended explicit retroactive consent; counterpoint: readers saw the opt-in as a narrow rollback rather than broad ownership.
- DRM-free availability varies substantially by publisher and genre according to commenters' libraries.
