# You can now play Grand Theft Auto Vice City in the browser

- Score: 253 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46329696) | Link: https://dos.zone/grand-theft-auto-vice-city/

### TL;DR

DOS.Zone runs the open-source reVC engine for Grand Theft Auto: Vice City inside a browser using WebAssembly and modern browser APIs. The hosted technology demo includes only limited assets; playing the full game requires users to supply legally obtained original resources validated by SHA-256 checksums. The site claims its executable contains no proprietary game assets and is unaffiliated with rights holders. Readers praised the smooth performance and immediate playability, but debated whether reverse-engineered code and any server-hosted demonstration assets actually satisfy copyright law.
