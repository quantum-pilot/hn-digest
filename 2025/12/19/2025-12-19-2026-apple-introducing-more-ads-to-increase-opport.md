# 2026 Apple introducing more ads to increase opportunity in search results

- Score: 245 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46322556) | Link: https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/ad-placements/0082-search-results

### TL;DR

Apple says App Store searches will gain additional ad positions in 2026, extending placements beyond the current top slot. Existing search campaigns become automatically eligible for every position; advertisers cannot select or bid for a specific location. Ads retain the same product-page format and cost-per-tap or cost-per-install billing, with auctions considering bids and relevance. Apple cites search preceding nearly 65% of downloads and claims top ads average over 60% conversion. Commenters expect worse discovery, brand-name interception, and erosion of Apple's premium positioning.

### Comment pulse

- Critics see sponsored exact-name results as a toll on developers whose users already intended to install their apps.
- Some called Apple's search poor already — counterpoint: competing app stores were described as no better.
- Readers questioned whether Apple's 65% figure distinguishes known-app lookups from genuine category discovery.
