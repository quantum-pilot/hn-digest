# 8-bit Boléro

- Score: 140 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46324702) | Link: https://linusakesson.net/music/bolero/index.php

### TL;DR

Linus Åkesson performs Ravel's *Boléro* on nine homemade 8-bit instruments after more than half a year of work, 9 hours 42 minutes of footage, and 52 mixer channels. The ensemble includes C64-based keyboards, the Commodordion, floppy-drive instruments, Chipophone, and new NES timpani. Their decaying triangle sound exploits nonlinear mixing with the NES sample channel. Most audio and video were captured together; the repeatable percussion automaton was close-miked section by section and combined with a separate visual take. Readers celebrated the craft, humor, and retro-computing ingenuity.
