# Cursor Acquires Graphite

- Score: 166 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46327325) | Link: https://graphite.com/blog/graphite-joins-cursor

### TL;DR

Graphite has signed a definitive agreement to be acquired by Cursor, subject to closing conditions. Graphite says its brand, product, and full team will continue, with added resources for stacked pull requests and its merge queue. Planned work connects local development, background agents, and pull requests, combines Graphite's AI Reviewer with Cursor's Bugbot, and uses Cursor's model expertise. The announcement frames review as the new bottleneck as AI accelerates code creation. Most HN discussion moved to the companion thread; one user questioned whether Graphite would fare better independently.

### Comment pulse
