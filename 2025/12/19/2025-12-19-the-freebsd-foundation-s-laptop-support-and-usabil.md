# The FreeBSD Foundation's Laptop Support and Usability Project

- Score: 127 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46326519) | Link: https://github.com/FreeBSDFoundation/proj-laptop

### TL;DR

The FreeBSD Foundation and Quantum Leap Research committed $750,000 to a one-to-two-year laptop usability program beginning in Q4 2024. Its goal is broad “out of the box” support through FreeBSD 14.x or later updates covering contemporary Wi-Fi, audio, suspend and resume, graphics, Bluetooth, documentation and other high-value gaps. Developers are the primary target, though desktop users should benefit. Work is managed through public roadmaps and monthly updates, but listed dates are placeholders and scope will be adjusted as progress and resources allow.

### Comment pulse

- Prospective users prioritized reliable suspend, Wi-Fi and USB-C displays; Framework documents current support, while older refurbished Dell reports were positive.
- Enthusiasm for a polished workstation experience was tempered by limited Apple Silicon support and uncertainty over which laptops work completely today.
