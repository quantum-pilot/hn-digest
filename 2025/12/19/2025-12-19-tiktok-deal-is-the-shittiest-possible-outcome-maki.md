# TikTok Deal Is the Shittiest Possible Outcome, Making Everything Worse

- Score: 282 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46327406) | Link: https://www.techdirt.com/2025/12/19/tiktok-deal-done-and-its-somehow-the-shittiest-possible-outcome-making-everything-worse/

### TL;DR

Techdirt condemns a reported TikTok restructuring in which Oracle, Silver Lake and Abu Dhabi’s MGX would collectively own 45% of US operations, ByteDance would retain 19.9%, existing-investor affiliates 30.1%, and others 5%. Oracle would oversee data protection while recommendations are retrained on US data. The author argues this neither removes Chinese influence nor addresses surveillance, propaganda or privacy, instead transferring control toward politically connected investors. The piece is openly polemical, and the supplied material does not independently verify its motives or future safeguards.
