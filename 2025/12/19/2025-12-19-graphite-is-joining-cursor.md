# Graphite is joining Cursor

- Score: 153 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46327206) | Link: https://cursor.com/blog/graphite

### TL;DR

Cursor says it has entered a definitive agreement to acquire Graphite, whose code-review platform serves hundreds of thousands of engineers. Cursor argues that AI has accelerated writing code while review, safe merging, and collaboration remain bottlenecks. Graphite will initially retain its product, team, and independent operation; planned integrations span local development, pull requests, and review intelligence shared across both systems. The announcement omits transaction terms and concrete timelines. HN debate focused on Cursor's defensibility, IDE versus CLI workflows, and whether acquisition threatens Graphite's standalone strengths.
