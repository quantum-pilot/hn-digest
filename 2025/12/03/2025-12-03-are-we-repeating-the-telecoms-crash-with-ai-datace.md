# Are we repeating the telecoms crash with AI datacenters?

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46133141) | Link: https://martinalderson.com/posts/are-we-really-repeating-the-telecoms-crash-with-ai-datacenters/

### TL;DR

The author argues AI datacenters differ fundamentally from the telecom bust: fiber demand was vastly overestimated while multiplexing multiplied existing capacity, leaving 95% dark, whereas GPU efficiency gains are slowing and agent workloads could increase token demand 10–100 times. Long construction and hardware lead times still encourage overbuilding, so adoption delays, debt stress, or efficiency breakthroughs could cause painful write-downs. Commenters challenge the utilization premise, noting subsidized pricing, uncertain willingness to pay, local models, specialized hardware, and speculative enterprise demand.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics argued current demand is distorted by free tiers, investor subsidies, and executive mandates.
- Bulls expect agentic office workflows to consume vastly more tokens than consumer chat.
- Both sides identified ASICs, model efficiency, financing, and adoption timing as decisive uncertainties.
