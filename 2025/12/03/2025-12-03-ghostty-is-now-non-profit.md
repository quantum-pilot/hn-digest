# Ghostty is now non-profit

- Score: 665 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46138238) | Link: https://mitchellh.com/writing/ghostty-non-profit

### TL;DR

Ghostty is now fiscally sponsored by Hack Club, extending 501(c)(3) status while Hack Club handles compliance, accounting, donations, and governance oversight. The MIT license and technical roadmap remain unchanged; names, marks, and project intellectual property moved under the nonprofit umbrella, while contributors retain copyright. Donations can fund contributors, dependencies, events, and operations through a transparent ledger, with 7% going to Hack Club. Mitchell Hashimoto remains final authority and largest donor but says funds cannot benefit him personally.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters saw fiscal sponsorship as protection against commercialization and a foundation for contributor compensation.
- Skeptics noted present funding and leadership still depend heavily on Hashimoto despite the succession groundwork.
- Hack Club's infrastructure and sponsorship of more than 2,500 organizations impressed commenters familiar with nonprofit administration.

### LLM perspective

- View: Fiscal sponsorship adds enforceable stewardship without prematurely building a standalone institution.
- Impact: Contributors and adopters gain clearer funding controls while Hack Club absorbs administrative complexity.
- Watch next: Public budgets, contributor payments, broader donor participation, and a leadership succession process.
