# Jepsen: NATS 2.12.1

- Score: 242 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46196105) | Link: https://jepsen.io/analyses/nats-2.12.1

### TL;DR

Jepsen found serious durability failures in NATS JetStream 2.10.20–2.12.1: corruption or coordinated crashes could erase acknowledged messages, delete streams, or leave persistent split-brain. Version 2.12.1 survived simple partitions and process crashes, but default two-minute fsync intervals exposed roughly 30 seconds of acknowledged writes, while always-fsync reduced throughput to hundreds of messages per second. Commenters treated the results as a warning against persistence claims, though some remain satisfied with core NATS for explicitly best-effort messaging.

### Comment pulse

- Durability promises need narrower language → commenters distinguished successful best-effort core messaging from JetStream’s disputed persistence guarantees.
- Corruption tolerance alarmed operators → a single flipped bit could trigger disproportionately large loss.
