# IBM to acquire Confluent

- Score: 318 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46192130) | Link: https://www.confluent.io/blog/ibm-to-acquire-confluent/

### TL;DR

Confluent says it signed a definitive agreement to be acquired by IBM for $31 per share in cash, with closing expected around mid-2026 subject to stockholder approval, regulatory review, and customary conditions. Confluent would remain a distinct brand and business after closing; until then it operates independently. CEO Jay Kreps frames IBM’s enterprise reach and open-source experience as a way to broaden Confluent’s event-streaming platform and support real-time, AI-oriented operations. The announcement is promotional and leaves integration details, long-term product autonomy, and execution outcomes unresolved.
