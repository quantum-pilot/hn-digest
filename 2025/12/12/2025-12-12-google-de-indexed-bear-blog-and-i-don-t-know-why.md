# Google de-indexed Bear Blog and I don't know why

- Score: 421 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46239752) | Link: https://journal.james-zhan.com/google-de-indexed-my-entire-bear-blog-and-i-dont-know-why/

### TL;DR

A new Bear Blog initially appeared in Google, then every post shifted to “Crawled—currently not indexed” after the author mistakenly requested validation for an RSS URL. Search Console offered no actionable explanation, repeated submissions failed, and Bear's developer found no DNS, HTML, content, or structural problem. Bing, DuckDuckGo, and Brave indexed the same site. The author eventually moved to another subdomain with redirects and avoided Search Console submission, while acknowledging that the validation timing may be coincidence rather than cause.
