# CM0 – A new Raspberry Pi you can't buy

- Score: 200 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46244922) | Link: https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2025/cm0-new-raspberry-pi-you-cant-buy

### TL;DR

Raspberry Pi's CM0 packages Pi Zero 2-class computing, Wi-Fi, and eMMC onto a postage-stamp-sized module with castellated edges for direct PCB soldering. EDAtec's CM0NANO carrier exposes Ethernet, USB, HDMI, camera, display, and GPIO connections, but the module has only 512MB RAM and eMMC measured around 19–20MB/s. Availability is officially limited to China, reportedly because its discontinued LPDDR2 supply would compete with Zero 2 W production. Commenters note AliExpress listings, though official standalone distribution remains uncertain.

### Comment pulse
