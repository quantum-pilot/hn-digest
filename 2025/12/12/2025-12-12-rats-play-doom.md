# Rats Play DOOM

- Score: 391 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46248323) | Link: https://ratsplaydoom.com/

### TL;DR

The project open-sources a rodent VR rig combining a tracked treadmill ball, panoramic display, motorized firing lever, and precisely timed sugar-water rewards, controlled by Python and a Raspberry Pi. Its second version is modular and documented with printable parts, electronics, firmware, and software. However, the source is internally inconsistent: its Results section says rats learned navigation and firing, while its Limitations section and the author’s HN reply say the newer rats only reached habituation and never played DOOM. Behavioral validation therefore remains unproven.

### Comment pulse
