# Tell HN: HN was down

- Score: 462 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46301921) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46301921

### TL;DR

Hacker News returned 502 errors for authenticated requests while some unauthenticated visitors received stale, presumably cached pages. The report inferred an outage around 1:41:58 PM GMT from the final comment cadence and found only one third-party status page detecting it. Administrator Dan Gackle’s initial theory was overload after relaxing anti-crawler protections that had blocked legitimate users. A secondary failure prolonged diagnosis: PagerDuty woke him at 5:24 AM, a cursory check looked healthy, and he mistakenly resolved the alert before returning to sleep.

### Comment pulse

- Readers treated the downtime lightly, thanking the operator and questioning whether a low-stakes discussion site needs overnight paging.
- Many discovered an automatic HN-checking habit, repeatedly opening the unavailable site to seek discussion about its own outage.

### LLM perspective

- View: The stale cache made partial availability misleading, turning a capacity event into an observability failure.
- Impact: Legitimate-user protection and crawler limits now require tighter tuning rather than simply relaxing enforcement.
- Watch next: Confirmed root cause, alert checks that bypass caches, and safer anti-crawler thresholds.
