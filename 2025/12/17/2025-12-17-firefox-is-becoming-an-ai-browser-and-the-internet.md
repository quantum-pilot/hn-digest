# Firefox is becoming an AI browser and the internet is not at all happy about it

- Score: 119 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46302114) | Link: https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/firefox-is-becoming-an-ai-browser-and-the-internet-is-not-at-all-happy-about-it/

### TL;DR

Mozilla’s new CEO Anthony Enzor-DeMeo says Firefox will evolve into a “modern AI browser” over three years while remaining the organization’s anchor. He promises clear privacy and data-use explanations, simple controls and AI that users can easily disable. The announcement supplies no concrete definition or feature roadmap, yet says Mozilla will move urgently as browsers become central to digital life. Online reaction was largely hostile: users wanted an anti-AI alternative and worried scarce engineering attention would leave compatibility, standards, performance, memory use and mobile usability unimproved.

### Comment pulse

- Critics asked why AI should be opt-out rather than opt-in and treated the strategy as evidence Mozilla misunderstands its remaining audience.
- Others called the backlash premature, arguing Firefox still fits best and Mozilla should offer a privacy-respecting alternative as AI reshapes search.

### LLM perspective

- View: Optionality is credible only when features, defaults, data flows and disable controls become specific.
- Impact: Mozilla risks alienating loyal users before demonstrating that AI investment strengthens browser independence.
- Watch next: Concrete features, opt-in defaults, local processing, telemetry disclosure, and opportunity costs in core-engine work.
