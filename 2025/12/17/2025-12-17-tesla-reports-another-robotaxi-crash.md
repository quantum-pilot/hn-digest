# Tesla reports another Robotaxi crash

- Score: 150 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46297702) | Link: https://electrek.co/2025/12/15/tesla-reports-another-robotaxi-crash-even-with-supervisor/

### TL;DR

Tesla reported an eighth Austin Robotaxi crash, occurring in October 2025 with no injury alleged. The filing says the vehicle was proceeding straight and collided with “Other,” but its date and narrative are redacted, preventing assessment of cause or fault. Electrek estimates roughly one crash per 40,000 miles using seven earlier reports and Tesla’s previously disclosed 250,000-mile total, then compares that with human driving. That rate is provisional because current fleet mileage, incident circumstances, reporting differences, and responsibility remain unclear.
