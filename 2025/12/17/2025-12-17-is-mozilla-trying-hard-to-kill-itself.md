# Is Mozilla trying hard to kill itself?

- Score: 812 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46299934) | Link: https://infosec.press/brunomiguel/is-mozilla-trying-hard-to-kill-itself

### TL;DR

A longtime Firefox user interprets Mozilla’s discussion of potentially blocking ad blockers for substantial revenue as a warning that its leadership may undervalue Firefox’s core constituency. The quoted CEO explicitly says Mozilla does not want to pursue the option because it feels off-mission, so the post’s darker reading is speculative. Still, the author argues that ad blocking is Firefox’s defining advantage and a security feature, and that even entertaining its removal could further alienate users already skeptical of Mozilla’s priorities.

### Comment pulse

- Many call the post an uncharitable reading because the quoted executive rejects blocking ad blockers.
- Others find the tentative wording and quantified revenue revealing, saying uBlock support is their last reason to stay.

### LLM perspective

- View: The immediate policy signal is reassuring, but Mozilla’s framing created avoidable uncertainty around a core differentiator.
- Impact: Trust can erode before any product change when users believe revenue pressure may override browser autonomy.
- Watch next: Concrete Firefox policy, extension-platform commitments, and whether leadership states an unambiguous ad-blocking guarantee.
