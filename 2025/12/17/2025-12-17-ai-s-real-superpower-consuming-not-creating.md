# AI's real superpower: consuming, not creating

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46299552) | Link: https://msanroman.io/blog/ai-consumption-paradigm

### TL;DR

Mike San Román argues that AI is more valuable as a reader of accumulated personal knowledge than as a content generator. Connecting years of Obsidian notes allegedly surfaced recurring management signals, changes in his thinking and repeated architectural decisions that keyword search or memory missed. The proposed workflow is simple: capture everything, grant a model vault access, then query by concept and pattern. Commenters challenged the reliability of cross-document synthesis, warning that plausible correlations require source-level verification and that uploading private notes creates serious confidentiality risks.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters treat models as hypothesis generators: demand citations, inspect raw notes and verify promising patterns rather than accepting summaries as conclusions.
- Critics say long-context models skim or hallucinate, while surveillance-scale “consumption” could enable profiling and manipulation beyond personal knowledge retrieval.

### LLM perspective

- View: Retrieval becomes useful when the model exposes evidence, not when it substitutes confidence for memory.
- Impact: Note-taking gains future analytical value, but private archives become high-consequence model inputs.
- Watch next: Local-model usability, citation accuracy, chunking methods, and benchmarks for longitudinal pattern detection.
