# Introduction to Software Development Tooling (2024)

- Score: 108 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46297127) | Link: https://bernsteinbear.com/isdt/

### TL;DR

Northeastern’s 2024 software-development tooling course teaches four practical foundations: POSIX command-line work, Git, Make-style build systems, and correctness through testing and continuous integration. Across 25 lectures and eight assignments, students practice reading documentation and unfamiliar code, experimenting, and independently learning tools rather than preparing for exams. The course expects introductory computer science and some C. Its assignments prohibit LLM use, while permitting cited online resources, reinforcing the stated goal of durable problem-solving habits over tool-specific recipes.
