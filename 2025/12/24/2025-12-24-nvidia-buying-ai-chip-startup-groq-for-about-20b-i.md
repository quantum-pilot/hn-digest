# Nvidia buying AI chip startup Groq for about $20B in cash

- Score: 281 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46379183) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/24/nvidia-buying-ai-chip-startup-groq-for-about-20-billion-biggest-deal.html

### TL;DR

CNBC reports Nvidia agreed to pay about $20 billion for Groq’s assets, talent, and inference technology, while Groq describes the arrangement as non-exclusive licensing and says it remains independent with GroqCloud excluded. Founder Jonathan Ross and other leaders will join Nvidia, whose stated aim is integrating Groq’s low-latency processors into its AI-factory platform. HN disputed whether the structure is a genuine license or acquisition-by-another-name, raising competition and employee-equity concerns while noting Groq is small and the license formally remains non-exclusive.
