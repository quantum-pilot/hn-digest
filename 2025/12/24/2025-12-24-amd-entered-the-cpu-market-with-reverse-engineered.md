# AMD entered the CPU market with reverse-engineered Intel 8080 clone 50 years ago

- Score: 147 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46375847) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/amd-first-entered-the-cpu-market-with-reverse-engineered-intel-8080-clone-50-years-ago-the-am9080-cost-50-cents-apiece-to-make-but-sold-for-usd700

### TL;DR

AMD entered microprocessors by reconstructing Intel's 8080 from a photographed pre-production chip, then adapting the design to AMD's N-channel MOS process. The article says the Am9080 could cost about fifty cents to manufacture yet sell for as much as $700 in military markets. A 1976 cross-license made AMD an authorized second source, responding to buyers' procurement requirements and clearing earlier liabilities. The chip later appeared in 28 speed and temperature variants, including military-rated versions.
