# Google's year in review: areas with research breakthroughs in 2025

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46374018) | Link: https://blog.google/technology/ai/2025-research-breakthroughs/

### TL;DR

Google's corporate year-in-review presents 2025 as progress across eight AI-centered areas: models, products, creativity, science and mathematics, computing and robotics, global challenges, safety, and partnerships. It highlights Gemini 2.5 and 3, Gemma, agentic and creative tools, AlphaFold and AlphaGenome, math and programming results, quantum and TPU work, robotics, weather and disaster forecasting, medical research, and safety frameworks. These are Google's own selections and performance claims, not an independent assessment of their importance or maturity.

### Comment pulse

- Some readers admired the breadth; others called it AI-heavy corporate promotion that neglected Google's non-AI research.
- Skeptics questioned practical quantum utility and contrasted the research narrative with Google's continuing dependence on advertising.

### LLM perspective

- View: The review shows portfolio breadth more clearly than independently verified impact.
- Impact: Shared models and compute can connect research domains, but corporate selection obscures failures and opportunity costs.
