# Lessons from the PG&E outage

- Score: 131 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46371730) | Link: https://waymo.com/blog/2025/12/autonomously-navigating-the-real-world

### TL;DR

During a PG&E outage affecting nearly a third of San Francisco, Waymo says its vehicles crossed more than 7,000 dark signals but generated an unusual concentration of remote confirmation requests. The resulting backlog caused response delays that added to existing gridlock. Waymo eventually paused service and parked vehicles in stages, then announced fleet updates supplying regional-outage context, revised emergency procedures, and expanded first-responder coordination. Its account attributes the broader congestion primarily to disabled signals while acknowledging its own operational contribution.
