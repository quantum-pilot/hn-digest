# The e-scooter isn't new – London was zooming around on Autopeds a century ago

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46373644) | Link: https://www.ianvisits.co.uk/articles/the-e-scooter-isnt-new-london-was-zooming-around-on-autopeds-a-century-ago-86263/

### TL;DR

Londoners used petrol-powered Autoped scooters more than a century before today’s shared e-scooters. Invented in America in 1915, they appeared in London by 1917, spread after wartime import restrictions ended, reached a claimed 10 mph, used 15-inch pneumatic tires, and cost £36. Newspapers showed officials and wealthy riders including suffragist Lady Florence Norman, but classified sales replaced retailer adverts by 1922, perhaps because of weather and car adoption. HN challenged the £1,600 modern-price estimate and emphasized that these were not electric vehicles.
