# A year of vibes

- Score: 163 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46352875) | Link: https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2025/12/22/a-year-of-vibes/

### TL;DR

Armin Ronacher says 2025 transformed him from hands-on programmer into a mostly hands-off lead for coding agents. He favors command-line agents, code generation, filesystem tools, and skills, while worrying about parasocial attachment and responsibility. His practical wishlist includes version control that preserves prompts and failed attempts, review workflows showing local human-agent iterations, agent-friendly observability, and norms against dumping unreviewed generated code on maintainers. HN discussion focused on retaining useful failure history without poisoning future context and setting emotional boundaries with humanlike tools.
