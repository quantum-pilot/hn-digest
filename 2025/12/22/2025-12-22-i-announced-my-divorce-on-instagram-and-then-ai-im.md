# I announced my divorce on Instagram and then AI impersonated me

- Score: 134 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46352004) | Link: https://eiratansey.com/2025/12/20/i-announced-my-divorce-on-instagram-and-then-ai-impersonated-me/

### TL;DR

After Eira Tansey posted a carefully written divorce announcement, Instagram generated first-person SEO metadata presenting her trauma as a generic journey toward happiness. A Mastodon client surfaced that hidden description beside her shared link, making the text appear appended to her words. Tansey says Meta neither asked permission nor offered review, despite requiring users to label AI content. She considers the unauthorized first-person voice impersonation that erased deliberate language about grief, humiliation, and insecurity, replacing solidarity with therapy-style inspiration and toxic positivity.

### Comment pulse

- Readers condemned nonconsensual first-person metadata and favored regulation; others argued closed platforms predictably exploit personal posts.
- A technical counterpoint: Meta generated the metadata, but a third-party Mastodon client made it appear contiguous with her post.
