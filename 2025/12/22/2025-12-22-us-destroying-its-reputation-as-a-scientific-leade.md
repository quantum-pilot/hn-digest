# US destroying its reputation as a scientific leader – European science diplomat

- Score: 143 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46357887) | Link: https://sciencebusiness.net/news/horizon-europe/us-demolishing-its-scientific-leadership-wrecking-ball-says-chief-eu-research

### TL;DR

Outgoing EU research diplomat Signe Ratso says the United States is damaging its scientific leadership through funding cuts, international biomedical-grant freezes, and political conditions on universities, though she did not substantiate the remark during her speech. She argues Europe should defend research freedom and deepen Horizon Europe links with like-minded partners. Canada, South Korea, and New Zealand have associated with collaborative grants, while Australia, Japan, and Singapore are negotiating. HN largely shared the reputational concern but criticized both the headline’s truncated attribution and Ratso’s lack of detailed argument.

### Comment pulse

- Reputation effects → researchers and conferences may avoid the United States when funding and international entry become unpredictable.
- Evidence gap → Ratso’s position lends weight, but the quoted speech did not explain mechanisms or quantify reputational loss.
- European opportunity → Horizon expansion can attract talent and partnerships, though bureaucracy and future defense priorities constrain access.
