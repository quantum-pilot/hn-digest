# US blocks all offshore wind construction, says reason is classified

- Score: 371 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46357881) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/science/2025/12/us-government-finds-new-excuse-to-stop-construction-of-offshore-wind/

### TL;DR

The Interior Department paused leases for all five US offshore wind projects under construction, including nearly complete Vineyard Wind and 80%-complete Revolution Wind, citing national-security concerns from a classified Defense Department analysis. Officials mentioned radar interference and evolving adversary technology but disclosed no project-specific findings. Previous attempts to stop permitted projects lost in court, and affected states and developers were expected to challenge the new order. HN acknowledged turbine radar interference as real but potentially mitigable, while overwhelmingly viewing classification as political cover for blocking renewable power.
