# Flock Exposed Its AI-Powered Cameras to the Internet. We Tracked Ourselves

- Score: 273 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46355548) | Link: https://www.404media.co/flock-exposed-its-ai-powered-cameras-to-the-internet-we-tracked-ourselves/

### TL;DR

404 Media reports that at least 60 Flock Condor pan-tilt-zoom cameras exposed unauthenticated livestreams and administrator panels to the internet. Anyone finding them could reportedly view live feeds, download 30 days of video, alter settings, inspect logs, and run diagnostics. Researchers found the systems through Shodan; a reporter and Benn Jordan physically visited cameras to verify real-time tracking. Unlike license-plate readers, Condor cameras followed people in streets, parking lots, paths, and a playground, sometimes closely enough to reveal phone activity.
