# The U.S. Is Funding Fewer Grants in Every Area of Science and Medicine

- Score: 190 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46355077) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/12/02/upshot/trump-science-funding-cuts.html

### TL;DR

A New York Times analysis of more than 300,000 grants reports that 2025 NIH competitive awards fell 22% and NSF new grants 25% against 2015–24 averages. After early delays, agencies spent much of their budgets through unusual multiyear upfront payments, raising average award payments but funding fewer projects and, at NIH, often less time and money per project. Reductions reached nearly every field, while graduate fellowships fell 29%. HN debated fiscal restraint versus public funding’s unique role in long-horizon, non-excludable basic research.
