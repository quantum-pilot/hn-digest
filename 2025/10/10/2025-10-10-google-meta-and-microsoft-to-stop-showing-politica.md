# Google, Meta and Microsoft to stop showing political ads in the EU

- Score: 179 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45542145) | Link: https://www.politico.eu/article/eu-political-ad-rules-google-meta-microsoft-big-tech-kick-in/

### TL;DR

As the EU's political-ad transparency rules took effect, Google, Meta, and Microsoft chose to stop carrying political advertising rather than implement the required disclosures and targeting restrictions. Their definitions may also exclude broad issue advertising involving climate, migration, social justice, or human rights. Critics argue that smaller parties, newcomers, and NGOs lose affordable reach while incumbents and opaque influencer campaigns gain an advantage. The European Commission says the law does not ban ads; the withdrawal is each platform's commercial decision.
