### TL;DR

The 2025 Nobel Peace Prize went to Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado for promoting democratic rights and pursuing a peaceful transition from dictatorship to democracy. HN supporters highlighted her coalition-building and a covert, distributed effort to collect and publish 2024 election tally records despite threats. Critics argued she had not yet achieved a political transition, while others said peace prizes often support ongoing struggles rather than reward completed outcomes. Discussion also revisited the prize's political history and controversial past recipients.
