# Boring Company cited for almost 800 environmental violations in Las Vegas

- Score: 280 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45540585) | Link: https://www.propublica.org/article/elon-musk-boring-company-violations-fines-vegas-loop

### TL;DR

Nevada regulators accused The Boring Company of nearly 800 environmental violations during Las Vegas Loop construction, including unapproved excavation, untreated water discharge, muck spills, and hundreds of missed inspections. A September cease-and-desist letter alleged roughly 100 agreement breaches plus 689 inspection failures. Although potential fines under a prior settlement reportedly exceeded $3 million, regulators proposed $242,800; the company disputed the allegations and paid after that dispute. Worker accounts and OSHA actions separately describe chemical exposure and safety incidents.

### Comment pulse

- Discussion split between infrastructure-permitting frustration and concern that contaminated water and worker injuries represent concrete harms.
- Critics called the reduced penalty a weak deterrent that effectively rewards violations at scale.
