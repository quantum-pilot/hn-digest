# Ryanair flight landed at Manchester airport with six minutes of fuel left

- Score: 483 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45539943) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/business/2025/oct/10/ryanair-flight-landed-at-manchester-airport-with-six-minutes-of-fuel-left-flight-log-suggests

### TL;DR

Ryanair flight FR3418 from Pisa reportedly landed in Manchester with 220 kg of fuel—estimated from a photographed technical log as five or six minutes' endurance—after Storm Amy prevented two Prestwick landings and another attempt at Edinburgh. The crew declared mayday and diverted; the UK Air Accidents Investigation Branch opened an inquiry. HN aviation commenters stressed that required reserves may be consumed only when circumstances leave no alternative, debated whether the decisions were proper, and cautioned against assigning fault before weather, planning, and flight data are examined.

### Comment pulse

- The remaining fuel was exceptional → experienced commenters considered it far outside routine landing margins and worthy of investigation.
- Reserve rules are subtle → crews cannot plan to consume reserves, but may use them after an alternate also becomes unavailable.
- Timing is the key unknown → discussion questioned whether an earlier diversion could have prevented the extreme final margin.
