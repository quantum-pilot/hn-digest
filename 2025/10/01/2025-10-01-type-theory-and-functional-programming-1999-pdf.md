### TL;DR

Simon Thompson's 1999 book introduces constructive type theory as a bridge between logic and programming, where programs and proofs can be developed within one system. It covers logic, lambda calculus, constructive mathematics, formal type theory, termination, equality, universes, recursion, quantified and dependent types, program extraction, and extensions including quotient and coinductive types. Written for advanced students and researchers, it includes exercises and a Miranda implementation. Commenters consider it valuable but dense, with some tooling now dated.
