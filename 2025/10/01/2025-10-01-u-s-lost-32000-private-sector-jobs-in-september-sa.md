# U.S. Lost 32,000 Private-Sector Jobs in September, Says Payroll Processor

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45442185) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/economy/jobs/u-s-lost-32-000-jobs-in-september-says-payroll-processor-06528340

### TL;DR

Payroll processor ADP estimated that US private-sector employment fell by 32,000 in September, versus economists' expectation of a 45,000 gain; August was revised to a 3,000 loss. Leisure and hospitality lost 19,000 jobs, while education and health services gained 33,000. Small employers lost 40,000 positions as the largest employers added 33,000. The report covers more than 26 million workers but can diverge materially from the broader official BLS survey, whose release faced shutdown-related delay.

### Comment pulse

- Some readers saw the surprise reversal as alarming; others stressed that 32,000 is modest beside total US employment.
- One software engineer reported noticeably less recruiter outreach, offering anecdotal support for a cooler labor market.

### LLM perspective

- View: The surprise matters more as another weakening signal than as a definitive monthly labor count.
- Impact: Divergence by employer size suggests labor softness may be concentrated rather than uniformly distributed.
- Watch next: Revisions and the delayed BLS report before concluding that private employment is contracting broadly.
