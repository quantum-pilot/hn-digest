# Solar leads EU electricity generation as renewables hit 54%

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45440387) | Link: https://electrek.co/2025/09/30/solar-leads-eu-electricity-generation-as-renewables-hit-54-percent/

### TL;DR

Eurostat figures reported by Electrek put renewables at 54% of EU net electricity generation in 2025's second quarter, up from 52.7% a year earlier. Solar produced 122,317 GWh, nearly 20% of all electricity and 36.8% of renewable output; in June it became the largest monthly source at 22%, narrowly ahead of nuclear. These are electricity-generation figures for a solar-heavy quarter, not annual shares or total energy consumption, so broader energy-transition claims require care.
