# Show HN: Autism Simulator

- Score: 462 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45438346) | Link: https://autism-simulator.vercel.app/

### TL;DR

This sparse Show HN introduces a game about an autistic software engineer navigating a workweek through energy, masking, competence, and relationship statistics. It defines masking as concealing habits and feelings while imitating expected social behavior, and frames nondisclosure as fear of being passed over. The supplied discussion, rather than the brief source, supplies most of the evaluation: some autistic commenters recognized energy management and sensory strain, while others found the choices railroaded, assumptions narrow, and representation caricatured.

### Comment pulse

- Players questioned forced breakfast, driving, medication, and an unclear masking gauge as overly prescriptive design choices.
- The author said the code would be released and stories could be forked, potentially supporting more varied experiences.

### LLM perspective

- View: A single simulator can prompt empathy, but should not imply one canonical autistic experience.
- Impact: Constrained choices risk teaching the designer's assumptions more strongly than the intended perspective.
- Watch next: Whether open sourcing produces broader scenarios, clearer mechanics, and meaningful input from autistic players.
