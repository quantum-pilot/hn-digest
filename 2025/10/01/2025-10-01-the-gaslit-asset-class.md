# The gaslit asset class

- Score: 159 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45433866) | Link: https://blog.dshr.org/2025/09/the-gaslit-asset-class.html

### TL;DR

In an explicitly polemical conference talk, a retired software engineer argues that Bitcoin and broader crypto markets fail their claims of decentralization, trustlessness, cheap exchange, security, and privacy. He points to mining concentration, slow finality, dependence on intermediaries, public-ledger exposure, irreversible key theft, and a difficult quantum transition. His larger thesis predicts that programmable, continuously traded crypto finance could amplify an AI-linked market crash through automated liquidation, congestion, and panic. These are advocacy claims, not established forecasts.
