# Category Theory Illustrated – Natural Transformations

- Score: 180 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45435422) | Link: https://abuseofnotation.github.io/category-theory-illustrated/11_natural_transformations/

### TL;DR

This illustrated chapter motivates natural transformations as morphisms between functors and as machinery for expressing when categories are equivalent rather than strictly isomorphic. It argues that category theory emphasizes relationships over isolated objects, introduces isomorphism-invariant properties, and explains why adding merely isomorphic objects can change categorical isomorphism without changing relevant structure. The exposition proceeds through preorders, equivalence classes, skeletons, and functors before formalizing equivalence in terms of morphisms. Its philosophical opening is motivational and was challenged by commenters as historically inaccurate.
