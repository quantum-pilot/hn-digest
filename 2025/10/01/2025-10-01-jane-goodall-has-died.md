# Jane Goodall has died

- Score: 882 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45441069) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/obituaries/story/2025-10-01/jane-goodall-chimpanzees-dead

### TL;DR

Jane Goodall died of natural causes in California at 91, according to the Jane Goodall Institute. Beginning at Gombe in 1960, she documented chimpanzees making and using tools, hunting, eating meat, grieving, forming relationships, and committing organized violence—observations that reshaped scientific views of humans and other primates. Her practice of naming subjects challenged contemporary research conventions. Goodall later shifted toward global conservation and animal advocacy, founded the Jane Goodall Institute, and created youth and community programs while continuing an intensive speaking schedule into her nineties.
