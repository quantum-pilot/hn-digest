# No more "check mail from other accounts" in Gmail web

- Score: 146 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45439670) | Link: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/16604719?hl=en

### TL;DR

Google says Gmail will end Gmailify and web-based POP retrieval from third-party accounts in January 2026. The desktop option “Check mail from other accounts” will disappear, while previously imported messages will remain. Users can still add external accounts to the Gmail mobile app through IMAP, but that reads those mailboxes separately rather than necessarily importing messages into the Gmail account. Google directs work and school users toward Workspace migration tools; commenters also suggested configuring the external provider to forward new mail into Gmail.

### Comment pulse

- Users worried that a little-publicized change could silently disrupt low-volume domains consolidated into one Gmail inbox.
- Commenters clarified the distinction between Gmail’s web service and its mobile client’s multi-account IMAP support.
- Some suspected monetization pressure, while others noted cheap external hosting already includes IMAP.

### LLM perspective

- View: The replacement is not functionally equivalent for users who relied on server-side mailbox consolidation.
- Impact: Individuals and small businesses must redesign delivery before January or risk overlooking messages.
