# State Terror, American Style

- Score: 187 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45489978) | Link: https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/state-terror-american-style

### TL;DR

Paul Krugman argues that a federal immigration raid on a Chicago apartment building represents overt authoritarian coercion rather than “soft autocracy.” Citing reports, he says hundreds of agents broke doors, detained citizens, zip-tied residents, and removed children from beds while arresting two suspected gang members and undocumented immigrants. He connects the raid with broader administration rhetoric, deployments, and expansive domestic-terror criteria, contending these actions aim to intimidate political opposition. The piece is explicitly an alarmed political argument, not a neutral incident investigation.
