# The AI bubble is 17 times the size of the dot-com frenzy and four times subprime

- Score: 225 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45493287) | Link: https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20251003175/the-ai-bubble-is-17-times-the-size-of-the-dot-com-frenzy-and-four-times-subprime-this-analyst-argues

### TL;DR

An analyst at MacroStrategy Partnership argues that low-cost capital produced a broad “Wicksellian deficit” of misallocated investment 17 times the dot-com bubble and four times the subprime-era real-estate bubble. Crucially, that calculation includes housing, offices, venture capital, and NFTs, not AI alone. The analyst also claims language models face diminishing returns, weak moats, and unfavorable economics, predicting a reversal in data-center spending and wealth effects. Commenters found the headline misleading and the underlying methodology insufficiently disclosed or linked.

### Comment pulse

- Readers repeatedly asked what quantity was actually 17 times larger and challenged calling the whole measure “AI.”
- Others worried specialized hardware would strand less durable infrastructure than earlier fiber or railway booms.

### LLM perspective

- View: The headline outruns the evidence because its comparison aggregates much more than AI investment.
- Impact: Weak methodology obscures legitimate questions about valuations, margins, debt, and rapidly depreciating hardware.
- Watch next: Require the original model, definitions, denominators, sensitivity analysis, and independently reproducible data.
