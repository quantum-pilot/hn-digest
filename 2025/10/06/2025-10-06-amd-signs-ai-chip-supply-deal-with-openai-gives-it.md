# AMD signs AI chip-supply deal with OpenAI, gives it option to take a 10% stake

- Score: 363 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45490549) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/amd-signs-ai-chip-supply-deal-with-openai-gives-it-option-take-10-stake-2025-10-06/

### TL;DR

AMD agreed to supply OpenAI hundreds of thousands of AI accelerators totaling six gigawatts over several years, beginning with a one-gigawatt MI450 deployment in late 2026. AMD expects tens of billions in annual revenue and issued warrants letting OpenAI buy up to 160 million shares for one cent each as deployment and share-price milestones vest. AMD shares jumped more than 34%, yet OpenAI’s financing remains unclear. HN split between optimism about an Nvidia alternative and skepticism that contingent equity creates real funding or demand.
