# Apps SDK

- Score: 237 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45494558) | Link: https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/

### TL;DR

OpenAI introduced a preview Apps SDK for building ChatGPT apps, with submission planned later in the year. Developers are directed to design conversational flows, meet safety and privacy guidelines, expose functionality through an MCP server, deploy it, and improve discovery through metadata. HN debated the underlying platform bet: supporters envision chat coordinating services, cards, calendars, payments, and workflows; skeptics see brittle schemas, slower interaction, app-name memorization, preferential discovery, and eventual platform competition with participating apps. Others favored dynamic, task-specific interfaces.

### Comment pulse

- Chat could become a distribution layer → one request may coordinate external services without navigating separate applications.
- Fixed widgets constrain intent → unsupported filters and cross-service combinations expose hard-coded schema boundaries.
- Platform incentives worry developers → discovery, revenue sharing, ads, and first-party duplication could weaken participating apps.

### LLM perspective

- View: The SDK’s success depends less on chat novelty than predictable orchestration and useful visual affordances.
- Impact: Services gain ChatGPT distribution while surrendering interface control and part of the customer relationship.
- Watch next: Evaluate latency, composability, discovery rules, transaction terms, privacy, submission review, and developer retention.
