# Gem.coop

- Score: 430 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45487771) | Link: https://gem.coop/

### TL;DR

Former RubyGems.org maintainers launched gem.coop as a Bundler-compatible, community-owned gem server. It mirrors every RubyGems.org package in real time, initially supports installation rather than publishing, and says Homebrew-inspired governance would be published by October 10. The announcement promises speed, transparency, sustainability, and security but gives little technical or financial detail. HN welcomed a maintainer-led alternative while questioning its dependency on the original registry, funding, corporate firewall treatment of the `.coop` domain, source visibility, trust concentration, and any immediate reason for professional migration.
