# Alphabet tops $100B quarterly revenue for first time, cloud grows 34%

- Score: 129 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45758874) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/29/alphabet-google-q3-earnings.html

### TL;DR

Alphabet reported quarterly revenue of $102.35 billion, exceeding the adjusted $99.89 billion estimate and crossing $100 billion for the first time. Google Cloud grew 34%, and management cited a $155 billion customer backlog. Alphabet also raised expected 2025 capital spending from $85 billion to $91–93 billion, reinforcing its ability to finance AI infrastructure from profitable existing businesses. Commenters saw that financial strength as an advantage, while questioning product execution, advertising incentives, vendor lock-in, and frequent service deprecations.

### Comment pulse

- Bulls argued Google's chips, cloud, engineering scale, and cash flow position it to outlast less disciplined AI competitors.
- Critics said strong infrastructure does not resolve weak product management or the tension between helpful answers and advertising revenue.
