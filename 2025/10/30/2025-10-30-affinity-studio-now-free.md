# Affinity Studio now free

- Score: 705 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45761445) | Link: https://www.affinity.studio/get-affinity

### TL;DR

Canva has relaunched Affinity as a free Windows and macOS application combining vector, pixel, and page-layout tools in one workspace. A free Canva account is required for download and activation; core creative and export features are unrestricted, while Canva's AI tools require a premium plan. Existing V1 and V2 files open in the new app, but new files are not backward-compatible, and V2 will receive no future updates. Offline use is supported after activation, except for connected services.

### Comment pulse
