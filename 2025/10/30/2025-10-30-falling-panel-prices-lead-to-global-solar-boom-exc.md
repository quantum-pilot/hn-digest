# Falling panel prices lead to global solar boom, except for the US

- Score: 219 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45761902) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/science/2025/10/theres-a-global-boom-in-solar-except-in-the-united-states/

### TL;DR

Chinese manufacturing has cut solar-panel prices nearly 90% over a decade and overall project capital costs about 70%, driving rapid deployment from utility-scale parks to household systems. Renewables reportedly exceeded coal generation globally in 2025's first half, yet fossil use and energy-sector emissions still rose as demand expanded. Grid connections, storage, land, and electrification remain constraints. The United States is diverging as revoked permits, reduced tax credits, tariffs, and policy uncertainty halve the IEA's 2030 growth forecast to roughly 250 GW.
