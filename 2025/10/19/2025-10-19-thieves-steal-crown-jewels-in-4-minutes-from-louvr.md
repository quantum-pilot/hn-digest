# Thieves steal crown jewels in 4 minutes from Louvre Museum

- Score: 134 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45635528) | Link: https://apnews.com/article/france-louvre-museum-robbery-a3687f330a43e0aaff68c732c4b2585b

### TL;DR

Thieves used a basket lift to reach the Louvre’s Apollon Gallery, forced a window, cut display protection, and escaped by motorbike with eight Napoleonic-era objects in a reported four-minute operation. Empress Eugénie’s diamond-set crown was recovered outside, damaged; no one was hurt. The daylight robbery exposed uneven protection amid staff complaints about crowding and understaffing. Commenters focused on the surprisingly direct method, the difficulty of fencing recognizable jewels, and arguments over whether higher prices, staffing, or access controls should follow.
