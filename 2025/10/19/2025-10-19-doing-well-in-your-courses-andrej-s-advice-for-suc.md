# Doing well in your courses: Andrej's advice for success (2013)

- Score: 337 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45635533) | Link: https://cs.stanford.edu/people/karpathy/advice.html

### TL;DR

Andrej Karpathy's 2013 guide recommends sleeping, starting early, mapping the full syllabus, inspecting prior exams, practicing recall rather than merely rereading, solving exercises, and studying alone before collaborating and teaching others. During exams, survey all questions, prioritize easier high-value work, communicate partial knowledge clearly, and use every remaining minute to check answers. Its larger message is to optimize for competent grades rather than perfection, then invest scarce time in projects, research, internships, open source, and relationships that demonstrate initiative beyond coursework.

### Comment pulse

- Active engagement beats transcription → predict a lecturer's next step, reconstruct notes afterward, and learn difficult material through problem solving.
- Consistency compounds → regular daily work and asking for directional help can outperform irregular flashes of ability.
