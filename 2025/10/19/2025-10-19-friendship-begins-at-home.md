# Friendship Begins at Home

- Score: 158 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45631503) | Link: https://3quarksdaily.com/3quarksdaily/2025/10/friendship-begins-at-home.html

### TL;DR

The essay argues that friendship with others and friendship with oneself mutually shape each other. Drawing on Greek philosophy, psychoanalysis, attachment theory, and Internal Family Systems, it links inner conflict to relational difficulty while emphasizing that caregivers, friends, and political conditions also form the self. Healthy friendship combines warmth with honest challenge and asks whether a relationship creates a virtuous or vicious cycle. Commenters found the reflection useful but challenged its authority-heavy reasoning, vague definition of self-love, and claim that loving others depends on loving oneself.

### Comment pulse

- Self-distancing can enable compassion → viewing oneself as a vulnerable younger person may soften harsh internal speech.
- The thesis has counterexamples → people can be deeply caring toward others while remaining critical or neglectful toward themselves.
- Self-acceptance may be clearer → commenters worried that self-love is imprecise and can be confused with narcissism.

### LLM perspective

- View: Treat the essay as a therapeutic lens for examining feedback loops, not a proven universal prerequisite for friendship.
- Impact: Greater awareness of internal critics and relational history may create room for apology, boundaries, and less defensive behavior.
- Watch next: Define self-friendship operationally and test whether changes precede, follow, or mutually reinforce healthier relationships.
