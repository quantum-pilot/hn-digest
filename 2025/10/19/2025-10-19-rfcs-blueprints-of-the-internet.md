# RFCs: Blueprints of the Internet

- Score: 108 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45634678) | Link: https://ackreq.github.io/posts/what-are-rfcs/

### TL;DR

The article introduces Requests for Comments as permanent, numbered documents recording Internet protocols, standards, experiments, and guidance since Steve Crocker's RFC 1 in 1969. It explains how to locate current documents, distinguish standards from informational or humorous entries, follow supersession links, and interpret normative terms such as MUST, SHOULD, and MAY. The author encourages implementers to read complete specifications rather than copy tutorials. Commenters endorsed learning by implementing an RFC but corrected the nuclear-survivability origin story and highlighted Jon Postel's central editorial role.
