# The Accountability Problem

- Score: 128 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45631678) | Link: https://www.jamesshore.com/v2/blog/2025/the-accountability-problem

### TL;DR

James Shore argues that software teams should be accountable for improving business outcomes, not for delivering fixed feature lists on exact dates. His OpenSesame case study uses Product Bets, rough financial models, small releasable increments, crowd estimates, and explicit tracking of maintenance work. The estimates are intentionally probabilistic: a roughly doubled forecast targets 75% on-time delivery. Commenters debated product-management quality, organizational politics, and whether the framework merely changes optics. Shore acknowledges the decisive test remains pending because no formal bet had finished.
