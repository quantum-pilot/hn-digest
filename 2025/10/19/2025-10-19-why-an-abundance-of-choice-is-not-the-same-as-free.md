# Why an abundance of choice is not the same as freedom

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45634641) | Link: https://aeon.co/essays/why-an-abundance-of-choice-is-not-the-same-as-freedom

### TL;DR

Sophia Rosenfeld traces how shopping, religious pluralism, romantic selection, employment, secret ballots, and twentieth-century social sciences made individual choice a modern proxy for freedom. She argues that options can enable autonomy yet remain hollow without resources, meaningful alternatives, or regard for collective consequences. The essay calls for deciding when personal selection serves shared problems and when other concepts of freedom fit better. Commenters disputed whether anyone truly equates abundance with freedom, while broadly distinguishing choice as necessary from choice as sufficient.
