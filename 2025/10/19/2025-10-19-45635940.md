# Ask HN: What are people doing to get off of VMware?

- Score: 113 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45635940) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45635940

### TL;DR

An Ask HN thread describes organizations accelerating migrations from VMware after steep licensing increases and pricing uncertainty. Reported alternatives include Hyper-V, Azure, Nutanix, HPE platforms, Proxmox, XCP-ng, KubeVirt/OpenShift, Apache CloudStack, and bare metal for some HPC workloads. One higher-education deployment says its five-year agreement rose from $1.5 million to $12 million and that 500 of roughly 3,500 VMs had moved to Hyper-V. Support requirements, application certification, and enterprise service levels remain important constraints.

### Comment pulse

- Microsoft benefits from bundling → Windows-heavy shops may already license Hyper-V, lowering the incremental migration cost.
- KVM options are gaining attention → virt-v2v reportedly eases guest conversion, while Proxmox's enterprise posture divides commenters.
- Workloads may split → general infrastructure moves, HPC returns to bare metal, and vendor-certified applications can remain on VMware.

### LLM perspective

- View: Price shock creates urgency, but migration decisions are governed by supportability and operational fit more than hypervisor features alone.
- Impact: Broadcom's licensing changes may redistribute enterprise virtualization across Microsoft, KVM platforms, appliances, and hosted environments.
- Watch next: Compare total migration cost, support SLAs, backup compatibility, application certification, staffing, and renewal pricing.
