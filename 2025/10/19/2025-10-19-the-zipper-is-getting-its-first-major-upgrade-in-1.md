# The zipper is getting its first major upgrade in 100 years

- Score: 104 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45634797) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/the-zipper-is-getting-its-first-major-upgrade-in-100-years/

### TL;DR

YKK’s AiryString removes the fabric tape beside zipper teeth, aiming for lighter, flatter, more flexible garments with less fiber, dyeing, water, and sewing work. Developed with JUKI, it requires redesigned teeth, production equipment, and dedicated garment machinery; early users include The North Face and Descente. The article presents smoother operation and lower environmental impact as benefits, but commenters found the treatment promotional and pressed unanswered questions about snags, fabric tearing, durability, repairability, and dependence on proprietary equipment.
