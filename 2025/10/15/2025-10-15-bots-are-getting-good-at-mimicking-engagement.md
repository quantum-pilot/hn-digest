# Bots are getting good at mimicking engagement

- Score: 343 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45590681) | Link: https://joindatacops.com/resources/how-73-of-your-e-commerce-visitors-could-be-fake

### TL;DR

DataCops’ CEO says behavioral tracking found 68% nonhuman traffic on one ecommerce site and a 73% average across more than 200 sites. Reported bots mimicked scrolling, hovering, internal clicks, cart abandonment, and social referrals, while legitimate scrapers also inflated visits. These are company claims rather than independently validated findings. HN readers said bot-heavy analytics are an old ad-tech problem, questioned the article’s promotional and possibly AI-assisted framing, and argued that marketers should optimize for incremental sales rather than vanity engagement.
