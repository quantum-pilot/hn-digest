# M5 MacBook Pro

- Score: 306 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45591902) | Link: https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/

### TL;DR

Apple's refreshed 14-inch MacBook Pro adds the M5 option, marketing faster AI through per-GPU-core Neural Accelerators and a 16-core Neural Engine; larger configurations retain M4 Pro and M4 Max. The product page also highlights its XDR display, ports, camera, audio, and recycled aluminum. HN discussion was less about specifications than upgrade value: many M1 owners saw little urgency, Linux-oriented users debated macOS friction, and Europeans discussed the omitted charger alongside a lower regional price.

### Comment pulse

- Upgrade appetite appears limited → commenters wanted generation-over-generation evidence rather than comparisons with M1 or older Intel systems.
- Platform experience divided users → some cited ARM and workflow friction, while others said configuration and familiarity resolve most complaints.
- Charger omission split opinion → critics noted replacement cost; supporters emphasized existing USB-C supplies and the accompanying European price reduction.
