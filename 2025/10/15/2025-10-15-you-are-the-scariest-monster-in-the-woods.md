# You are the scariest monster in the woods

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45592766) | Link: https://jamie.ideasasylum.com/2025/10/15/you-are-the-scariest-monster-in-the-woods

### TL;DR

The essay rejects AI as an autonomous monster and argues the immediate danger is humans gaining another powerful tool for control, exploitation, violence, cheating, or avoiding responsibility. Because people build, deploy, and can regulate AI, its harms are choices rather than an unstoppable external event. HN discussion strongly contested the author's dismissal of AGI and bleak account of humanity. Several commenters emphasized human cooperation, governance, and beneficial uses, while others agreed that human-directed systems are today's concrete risk.

### Comment pulse

- AGI impossibility was the main objection → commenters argued unfamiliar architectures, persistent systems, or delegated decisions could eventually produce meaningful agency.
- The human portrait was disputed → critics stressed ordinary behavior, cooperation, and constructive coordination alongside exploitation.
- Present deployment remains actionable → even AGI skeptics agreed that people wielding current models deserve scrutiny now.
