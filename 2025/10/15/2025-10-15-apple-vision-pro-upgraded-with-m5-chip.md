# Apple Vision Pro upgraded with M5 chip

- Score: 240 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45591801) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/10/apple-vision-pro-upgraded-with-the-m5-chip-and-dual-knit-band/

### TL;DR

Apple refreshed Vision Pro with M5, a redesigned Dual Knit Band, and visionOS 26 while retaining its $3,499 starting price. Apple says it renders 10% more pixels, reaches 120 Hz, runs selected AI tasks up to twice as fast, and lasts 2.5 hours generally or three hours for video. The backward-compatible $99 band adds a tungsten counterweight. HN owners praised Mac Virtual Display, travel use, and spatial photos, but questioned the weight, scarce immersive content, and continued need for a separate Mac.
