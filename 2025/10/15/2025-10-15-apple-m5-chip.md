# Apple M5 chip

- Score: 932 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45591799) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/10/apple-unleashes-m5-the-next-big-leap-in-ai-performance-for-apple-silicon/

### TL;DR

Apple’s M5 uses a third-generation 3-nanometer process, a 10-core GPU with a Neural Accelerator per core, up to 10 CPU cores, a 16-core Neural Engine, and 153 GB/s unified-memory bandwidth. Apple claims over fourfold M4 peak GPU AI compute, up to 45% higher ray-traced graphics, and 15% faster multithreading. It debuts in MacBook Pro, iPad Pro, and Vision Pro. Commenters welcomed the hardware gains but questioned selective benchmarks, software sluggishness, Linux support, and limited Neural Engine disclosure.
