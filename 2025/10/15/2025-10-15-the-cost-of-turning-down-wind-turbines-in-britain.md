# The cost of turning down wind turbines in Britain

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45590236) | Link: https://wastedwind.energy/

### TL;DR

The article says Britain's 2025 wind-curtailment and replacement-power costs exceeded £1bn because grid congestion prevents remote generation from reaching demand, forcing turbines off and often gas plants on. It links those costs to household bills and political disputes over renewable expansion. HN commenters largely reframed the issue as delayed transmission infrastructure, planning friction, and market design rather than excess wind alone. Proposed remedies included faster grid construction, location-sensitive pricing, flexible appliances, storage, and industrial demand near generation.

### Comment pulse

- Transmission is the central bottleneck → delayed north-south links coexist with new Scottish wind connections and scheduled maintenance.
- Flexible demand can absorb some surplus → dynamic tariffs already trigger batteries and water heating, but cannot replace persistent grid capacity.
- Planning reform is contentious → local opposition slows lines, while restricting challenges may accelerate construction at a governance cost.
