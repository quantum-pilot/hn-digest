# NASA chief suggests SpaceX may be booted from moon mission

- Score: 190 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45655188) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2025/10/20/science/nasa-spacex-moon-landing-contract-sean-duffy

### TL;DR

Acting NASA chief Sean Duffy said SpaceX’s Starship lunar lander is slipping and that NASA would seek competing acceleration plans to preserve a possible mid-2027 Artemis III landing before China’s stated 2030 goal. NASA asked SpaceX and Blue Origin for proposals by October 29 and planned an industry-wide request for information, an exploratory step rather than a new award. SpaceX holds a $2.9 billion Artemis III contract; Blue Origin is contracted for later missions. Commenters questioned schedule-driven safety, SLS economics, political motives, and whether any alternative could mature faster.
