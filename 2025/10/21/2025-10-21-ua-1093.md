# UA 1093

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45656044) | Link: https://windbornesystems.com/blog/ua-1093

### TL;DR

WindBorne says foreign-object debris that struck the windshield of United flight 1093 at about 36,000 feet was likely one of its balloons. The 737 MAX diverted from its Denver–Los Angeles route to Salt Lake City; the company reported no serious injuries or loss of pressurization. WindBorne notified the NTSB and FAA, noted that its 2.4-pound launch design follows weight rules, and changed operations to reduce time between 30,000 and 40,000 feet. It also plans live-flight avoidance and lower-impact hardware. The cause remained under investigation.
