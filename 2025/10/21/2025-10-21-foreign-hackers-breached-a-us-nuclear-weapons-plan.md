# Foreign hackers breached a US nuclear weapons plant via SharePoint flaws

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45657287) | Link: https://www.csoonline.com/article/4074962/foreign-hackers-breached-a-us-nuclear-weapons-plant-via-sharepoint-flaws.html

### TL;DR

A foreign actor reportedly breached the Kansas City National Security Campus through two on-premises SharePoint vulnerabilities, one enabling spoofing and another remote code execution. The site makes about 80% of non-nuclear parts for the US nuclear stockpile. Attribution remains disputed between Chinese-linked groups and a possible Russian actor, and no evidence cited shows classified data or operational systems were compromised. The incident nonetheless raises concern about lateral movement from IT into manufacturing controls and the strategic value of unclassified precision, supply-chain, or requirements data.
