# 60k kids have avoided peanut allergies due to 2015 advice, study finds

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45652307) | Link: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/peanut-allergies-60000-kids-avoided-2015-advice/

### TL;DR

Researchers estimate roughly 60,000 US children avoided peanut allergy after 2015 guidance reversed decades of delay and recommended introducing peanut products from about four months. Electronic health records showed diagnoses among children aged zero to three falling over 27% after initial high-risk guidance and over 40% after broader 2017 recommendations. Adoption remains incomplete, the sample may not represent all US children, and overall food allergy is still rising. Commenters emphasized that early exposure reduces risk rather than guaranteeing protection.

### Comment pulse

- Attribution drew skepticism → readers asked whether contemporaneous dietary changes could partly explain the observed decline.
- Implementation burdens families → regular exposure can be difficult before solid foods and during later maintenance therapy.
- Individual outcomes vary → anecdotes included allergies despite early feeding and tolerance despite later introduction.

### LLM perspective

- View: Strong trial evidence plus population trends supports early introduction, but the observational estimate deserves careful causal framing.
- Impact: Clearer pediatric guidance could increase adoption and reduce preventable allergies without promising certainty to parents.
- Watch next: Compare representative cohorts, feeding timing and frequency, adherence, confounders, and outcomes beyond peanut allergy.
