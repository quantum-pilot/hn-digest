# Tesla is heading into multi-billion-dollar iceberg of its own making

- Score: 228 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45654635) | Link: https://electrek.co/2025/10/20/tesla-heading-into-multi-billion-dollar-iceberg-of-own-making/

### TL;DR

Electrek argues Tesla faces substantial retrofit or compensation exposure after acknowledging that Hardware 3 cannot support unsupervised self-driving, despite earlier claims that its vehicles contained all necessary hardware. The article says HW3 updates have stalled, Australasian FSD launched only on HW4, and lawsuits are underway in Australia, the United States, and China. Its multibillion-dollar estimate extrapolates from customers paying up to $15,000, not an adjudicated liability. Commenters disputed remedies and legal conclusions; some HW3 owners praised current supervised features, while others stressed they fall short of the promise.
