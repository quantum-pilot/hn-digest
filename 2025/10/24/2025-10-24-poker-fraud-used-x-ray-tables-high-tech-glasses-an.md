# Poker fraud used X-ray tables, high-tech glasses and NBA players

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45693599) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cz6nd9wnzn6o

### TL;DR

US prosecutors allege an organized poker scheme used celebrity “face cards,” including NBA figures, to lure wealthy victims into rigged private games. More than 30 people were arrested. The indictment describes X-ray tables, instrumented chip trays, marked cards readable through special eyewear, hidden cameras and compromised shufflers transmitting card information to an off-site operator, then to a table “quarterback” using physical signals. Authorities say victims lost at least $7 million and proceeds were laundered through cash, cryptocurrency and shell companies. These remain allegations, which defendants may contest.
