# Alaska Airlines' statement on IT outage

- Score: 124 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45691127) | Link: https://news.alaskaair.com/on-the-record/alaska-statement-on-it-outage/

### TL;DR

Alaska Airlines said a primary data-center failure triggered a systemwide ground stop from roughly 3:30 p.m. to 11:30 p.m. Pacific on October 23. By the following afternoon, more than 400 flights had been canceled and over 49,000 passengers disrupted; Hawaiian Airlines was unaffected. Alaska said the incident was not cybersecurity-related and did not compromise flight safety, and it brought in outside experts after a similar disruption earlier that year. Commenters described long delays, poor communication, and inadequate assistance.
