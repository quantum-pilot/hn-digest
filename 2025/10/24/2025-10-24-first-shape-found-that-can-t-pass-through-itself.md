# First shape found that can't pass through itself

- Score: 178 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45694856) | Link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/first-shape-found-that-cant-pass-through-itself-20251024/

### TL;DR

Jakob Steininger and Sergey Yurkevich constructed the Noperthedron, a convex polyhedron with 90 vertices and 152 faces that cannot pass through a straight tunnel bored through an identical copy. Their proof combines local and global geometric bounds with computer checks covering roughly 18 million orientation blocks, overturning the conjecture that every convex polyhedron has the Rupert property. Commenters praised the explanation but noted the title omits the crucial restrictions: curved shapes such as spheres are already obvious counterexamples, and rotating through a curved passage is a different problem.
