# Computer science courses that don't exist, but should (2015)

- Score: 332 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45690045) | Link: https://prog21.dadgum.com/210.html

### TL;DR

A satirical 2015 syllabus proposes computer-science courses on unlearning object-oriented habits, studying classic software under severe hardware constraints, making high-level languages fast, designing humane command-line interfaces, and recognizing programmers’ recurring obsessions with formatting, taxonomy, types, files, and criticism. The list uses humor to expose gaps between academic technique and everyday engineering judgment. Commenters extended it with computing history, debugging and legacy-code labs, ethics, hype detection, and organizational friction, while debating how well lessons from older systems transfer to modern substrates.
