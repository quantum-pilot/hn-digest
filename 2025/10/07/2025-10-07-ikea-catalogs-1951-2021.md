# IKEA Catalogs 1951-2021

- Score: 325 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45504470) | Link: https://ikeamuseum.com/en/explore/ikea-catalogue/

### TL;DR

The IKEA Museum has placed decades of IKEA catalogues online, spanning the company's early 1950s publications through its final paper edition in 2021. The archive presents the catalogue as both product reference and a record of changing domestic aesthetics, lifestyles, copywriting, and distribution over more than 70 years. Commenters remembered the physical edition as unusually effective advertising and a shared family object, while using the archive to compare enduring designs, period fashions, and shifts in IKEA's materials and manufacturing quality.

### Comment pulse

- Others wanted similarly complete historical archives from watch and automobile manufacturers.
