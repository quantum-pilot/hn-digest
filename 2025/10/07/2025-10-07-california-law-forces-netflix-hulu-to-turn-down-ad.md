# California law forces Netflix, Hulu to turn down ad volumes

- Score: 320 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45499281) | Link: https://www.politico.com/news/2025/10/06/dial-it-down-california-forces-netflix-hulu-to-lower-ad-volume-00595663

### TL;DR

California enacted SB 576, requiring streaming services such as Netflix, Hulu, and Amazon Prime to keep advertisements from being louder than the programming around them. The measure extends the principle of a 2010 federal rule covering broadcast and cable television, responding to increased complaints about streaming ad volume. Industry groups initially argued that ads arrive from multiple sources and are difficult to control, then withdrew opposition after private lawsuits were barred. Enforcement belongs to the state attorney general, with compliance due July 2026.
