# Robin Williams' daughter pleads for people to stop sending AI videos of her dad

- Score: 225 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45505626) | Link: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0r0erqk18jo

### TL;DR

Zelda Williams asked people to stop sending her AI-generated videos of her father, Robin Williams, calling the recreations personally distressing and a reduction of real lives and artistic legacies into engagement-seeking imitation. She had previously opposed synthetic versions of his voice during the actors' labor campaign over AI. Her remarks address a wider trend of animating deceased people online. Commenters discussed posthumous likeness rights, deliberate harassment, and the risk that cheap synthetic media moves from celebrity novelty to abuse of ordinary families and children.

### Comment pulse

- Readers favored stronger rights over a person's voice, image, and likeness after death.
- Several warned that saturation may reduce novelty but not malicious use against grieving relatives.
