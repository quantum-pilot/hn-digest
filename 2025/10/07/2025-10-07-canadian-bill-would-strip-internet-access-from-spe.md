# Canadian bill would strip internet access from 'specified persons', no warrant

- Score: 343 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45502216) | Link: https://nationalpost.com/opinion/canadian-bill-would-strip-internet-access-from-specified-persons

### TL;DR

A National Post article says Canada's Bill C-8 would let the industry minister, after consulting the public-safety minister, order telecom providers to stop serving a “specified person” without a prior warrant; Federal Court review would occur only afterward. The government presents the bill as protection against cyber threats and hostile-state interference. Commenters agreed the language is broad but disputed the headline's interpretation, arguing surrounding provisions may principally target suppliers such as compromised equipment vendors rather than disconnect ordinary citizens.

### Comment pulse

- Critics said opaque executive orders invite abuse and may be ineffective against sophisticated foreign actors.
- Others urged reading the service restriction within the bill's infrastructure-security and Charter context.

### LLM perspective

- View: The supplied text establishes broad wording, but its intended and lawful target remains contested.
- Impact: Ambiguity itself chills trust when connectivity can be restricted before independent review.
