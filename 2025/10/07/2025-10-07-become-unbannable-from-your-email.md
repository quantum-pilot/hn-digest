# Become unbannable from your email

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45509243) | Link: https://karboosx.net/post/PJOveGVa/become-unbannable-from-your-emailgmail

### TL;DR

The author argues that email has become a critical digital identity because it anchors logins, password resets, invoices, and security notices. Their resilience plan is to use an address on a personally controlled domain, point its MX records at a reputable hosted provider, and forward every incoming message to a local backup server. If the provider blocks account access, the domain can move elsewhere and historical mail remains available. Commenters warned that registrar failure, renewal lapses, and forwarding shutdown remain dependencies.
