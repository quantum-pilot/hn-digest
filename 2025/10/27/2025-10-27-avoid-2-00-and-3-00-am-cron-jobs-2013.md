# Avoid 2:00 and 3:00 am cron jobs (2013)

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45723554) | Link: https://www.endpointdev.com/blog/2013/04/avoid-200-and-300-am-cron-jobs/

### TL;DR

This 2013 warning advises against scheduling cron jobs at 2:00 or 3:00 a.m., especially on Sundays, because daylight-saving transitions can skip or repeat local times. The author observed two Vixie-cron jobs firing roughly sixty times during one transition, creating overlapping executions and noisy mail. Safer options include scheduling outside the transition window, running servers on UTC, and adding locks or overlap protection. Later discussion notes that cron implementations have long included special DST handling, though behavior can vary.
