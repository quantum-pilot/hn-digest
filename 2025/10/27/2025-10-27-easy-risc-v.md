# Easy RISC-V

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45726192) | Link: https://dramforever.github.io/easyriscv/

### TL;DR

Easy RISC-V is an interactive introduction for readers who know basic low-level concepts but are new to the architecture. Embedded emulators let learners step through RV32I assembly while inspecting registers and memory. The tutorial presents a bare-bones 32-bit instruction set, then builds through arithmetic, bitwise operations, comparisons, shifts, branches, jumps, memory access, memory-mapped I/O, functions, stack conventions, and position-independent code. It emphasizes RISC-V’s clean, open design while noting that real chips usually include additional extensions.
