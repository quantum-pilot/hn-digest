# You are how you act

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45719788) | Link: https://boz.com/articles/you-are-how-you-act

### TL;DR

The essay contrasts a simplified Rousseau, centered on an inner good self corrupted by circumstances, with a simplified Franklin, for whom virtue is repeated action. It favors Franklin because behavior is controllable: acting consistently can form character even before feelings align, making “fake it until you make it” a theory of habit rather than deception. Commenters challenge the philosophical accuracy and binary framing, argue ethical action also depends on intention, and note that health constrains willpower. The piece is best read as practical motivation, not rigorous intellectual history.

### Comment pulse

- Critics say the Rousseau–Franklin opposition ignores more complex accounts connecting intention, habit and ethical action.
- Others accept the operational lesson that practiced behavior becomes easier, while rejecting willpower as an unlimited resource.
