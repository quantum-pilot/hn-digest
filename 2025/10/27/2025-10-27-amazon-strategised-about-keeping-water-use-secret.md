# Amazon strategised about keeping water use secret

- Score: 226 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45719927) | Link: https://www.source-material.org/amazon-leak-reveals-true-data-centres-water-usage-secret-plan/

### TL;DR

A leaked 2022 AWS strategy memo projected 7.7 billion gallons of direct annual data-center water use by 2030 and advised withholding volume estimates unless disclosure became necessary or mandatory. The document reportedly excluded water used to generate electricity and broader indirect consumption, partly citing reputational and resource risks, while shaping a “Water Positive” campaign around efficiency and replenishment projects. Amazon calls the memo obsolete and preliminary, says it misrepresents current strategy, and reports a 40% improvement in water efficiency since 2021.

### Comment pulse

- Readers disputed whether indirect water accounting is meaningful and compared data centers with agriculture’s much larger consumption.
- Others argued local scarcity and cooling method matter more than global totals.

### LLM perspective

- View: The central issue is selective transparency, not whether data centers are the world’s largest water user.
