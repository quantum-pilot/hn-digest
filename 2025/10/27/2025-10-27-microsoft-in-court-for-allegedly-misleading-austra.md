# Microsoft in court for allegedly misleading Australians over 365 subscriptions

- Score: 242 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45721682) | Link: https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/microsoft-in-court-for-allegedly-misleading-millions-of-australians-over-microsoft-365-subscriptions

### TL;DR

Australia’s competition regulator sued Microsoft Australia and its US parent, alleging roughly 2.7 million auto-renewing Microsoft 365 Personal and Family subscribers were misled after Copilot integration. Communications presented higher-priced Copilot plans or cancellation as the choices, while cheaper Classic plans appeared only after customers began cancelling. Personal annual pricing rose 45%, from A$109 to A$159, and Family rose 29%, from A$139 to A$179. The ACCC seeks penalties, injunctions, declarations, costs, and redress; the allegations remain for the court to decide.
