# Pyrex catalog from from 1938 with hand-drawn lab glassware [pdf]

- Score: 258 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45721801) | Link: https://exhibitdb.cmog.org/opacimages/Images/Pyrex/Rakow_1000132877.pdf

### TL;DR

Corning’s 1938 catalog presents 128 pages of PYREX laboratory glassware through meticulous hand-drawn product illustrations, technical data, ordering tables and period typography. It describes low-expansion borosilicate glass, chemical durability, annealing guidance, interchangeable joints and a line totaling 2,353 items, 737 newly listed. Beyond its commercial purpose, the document records how standardized scientific apparatus was marketed and specified before digital publishing. Discussion focused less on individual vessels than on the catalog’s typography, hierarchy, whitespace and enduring value as industrial design documentation.
