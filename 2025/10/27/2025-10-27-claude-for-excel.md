# Claude for Excel

- Score: 435 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45722639) | Link: https://www.claude.com/claude-for-excel

### TL;DR

Anthropic is piloting Claude for Excel with 1,000 Max, Team, and Enterprise customers. The beta can explain formulas with cell citations, navigate dependencies across tabs, update assumptions, debug spreadsheet errors, draft models, and populate templates while showing changes. Anthropic says users must verify results, especially in client-facing work. The preview lacks pivot tables, conditional formatting, data validation, data tables, macros, and VBA. It supports `.xlsx` and `.xlsm`, but the product page supplies claims rather than independent reliability evidence.

### Comment pulse
