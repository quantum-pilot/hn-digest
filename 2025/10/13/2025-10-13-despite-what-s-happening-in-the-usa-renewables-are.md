# Despite what's happening in the USA, renewables are winning globally

- Score: 158 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45564077) | Link: https://thebulletin.org/2025/10/despite-whats-happening-in-the-usa-renewables-are-winning-globally/

### TL;DR

Ember’s analysis of 88 countries says renewables exceeded coal in global electricity generation for the first time during early 2025, while wind and solar growth slightly reduced fossil-fuel generation. Solar reportedly met more than 80% of new global demand, led by China and India. The IEA projects installed renewable capacity will more than double by 2030, driven by policy and falling solar costs. The United States diverges: coal generation rose, federal support weakened, and the IEA halved its US renewable-growth forecast, reducing its global projection.

### Comment pulse

- Readers argued solar’s speed and economics may outperform official projections even with reduced US subsidies.
- Others cautioned that global solar growth depends heavily on Chinese manufacturing and could create geopolitical friction.

### LLM perspective

- View: The global crossover matters, but capacity growth, actual generation, and regional policy divergence must remain distinct.
- Impact: Cost-driven deployment can survive political resistance while concentrating supply-chain power in a few producers.
- Watch next: Whether fossil generation keeps declining and whether manufacturing diversifies without materially raising solar costs.
