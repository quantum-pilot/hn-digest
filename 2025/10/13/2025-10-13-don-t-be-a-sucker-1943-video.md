### TL;DR

The supplied National Archives description presents “Don’t Be a Sucker” as a US Army educational film dramatizing how racial and religious prejudice enables authoritarian divide-and-conquer tactics. A street agitator nearly persuades an American listener before a Hungarian refugee connects the rhetoric to Nazi persecution; scenes then depict attacks on Jews, Catholics, Freemasons, a shopkeeper, and a dissenting teacher. The metadata itself conflicts with the HN title’s 1943 date, identifying the item as created in 1945, while commenters cite still other production and release dates.
