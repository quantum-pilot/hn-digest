# The Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel 2025

- Score: 125 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45567153) | Link: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2025/summary/

### TL;DR

The 2025 economics prize recognized explanations of innovation-driven growth. Joel Mokyr received half for identifying prerequisites for sustained growth through technological progress; Philippe Aghion and Peter Howitt shared the other half for their theory of sustained growth through creative destruction. HN commenters praised Mokyr's accessible economic history and discussed whether technological progress is necessary for continuing growth, alongside population, productivity, energy, extraction, and institutional factors. A recurring side debate concerned the prize's formal name and relationship to Alfred Nobel's original awards.

### Comment pulse

- Mokyr's work is unusually accessible → economists recommended his books and the committee's technical and nontechnical summaries.
- Growth mechanisms remain contested → commenters weighed technology, population, energy efficiency, resource extraction, and measurement choices.
- Prize legitimacy arguments persisted → some reject “Nobel” shorthand, while economists regard it as their field's highest-prestige award.
