# Ofcom fines 4chan £20K and counting for violating UK's Online Safety Act

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45568767) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/10/13/4chan_ofcom_fine/

### TL;DR

Ofcom imposed the first Online Safety Act fine: £20,000 against 4chan for failing to answer information requests, with another £100 daily for up to 60 days unless it supplies illegal-content risk assessments and revenue information. Other investigations have prompted services to add hash matching or block UK users. HN discussion questioned whether Britain can enforce penalties against a small US site, while others argued ignoring formal investigations forces escalation and may lead to ISP blocking or stronger extraterritorial powers.
