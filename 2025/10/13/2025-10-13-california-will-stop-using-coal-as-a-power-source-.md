# California Will Stop Using Coal as a Power Source Next Month

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45567645) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/california/newsletter/2025-10-08/essential-california-california-dumps-coal-power

### TL;DR

California is set to end essentially all coal-sourced electricity when it stops taking power from Utah’s Intermountain Power Plant. Coal supplied only 2.2% of the state’s electricity in 2024, nearly all from that facility, after years of replacement by wind, solar, storage, and natural gas. Los Angeles’s utility is building a gas-and-hydrogen-capable plant beside Intermountain, reusing the existing transmission route. The article celebrates the emissions reduction while acknowledging transition costs for coal-dependent communities and environmental questions around replacement technologies.
