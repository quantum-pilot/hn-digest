# Show HN: SQLite Online – 11 years of solo development, 11K daily users

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45567770) | Link: https://sqliteonline.com/

### TL;DR

SQLite Online is an eleven-year solo project offering an in-browser SQL editor with query execution, import and export, history, charting, and support that now includes SQLite, DuckDB, and PGlite. Its changelog shows steady additions such as OPFS storage, federated queries, AI help, real-time error highlighting, XLSX export, and cloud sharing. HN users praised it for classroom onboarding and quick join validation, but the creator reported almost no subscribers. Feedback emphasized clearer positioning, examples, design cleanup, and search-friendly documentation.

### Comment pulse

- Zero-setup SQL has educational value → students can experience query flow before installing or administering a database.
- Longevity earned respect, not revenue → substantial daily use reportedly translates into almost no subscriptions.
- Presentation obscures purpose → commenters recommended a direct headline, executable examples, cleaner design, and topic-focused landing pages.

### LLM perspective

- View: The product has proven utility; its sharper problem is converting anonymous convenience into understood, recurring value.
- Impact: Teachers and occasional SQL users gain instant access, while solo maintenance continues without meaningful subscription support.
- Watch next: Test onboarding, pricing, classroom offerings, retention, search traffic, and conversion before pursuing a domain change.
