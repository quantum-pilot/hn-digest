# Android's sideloading limits are its most anti-consumer move

- Score: 559 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45569371) | Link: https://www.makeuseof.com/androids-sideloading-limits-are-anti-consumer-move-yet/

### TL;DR

The article argues that Google’s planned developer-verification requirement turns sideloading on mainstream Android devices into a Google-controlled permission system. Beginning with a staged rollout and broader enforcement later, apps on devices with Google Mobile Services would need developers verified through identity documents or contact details; uncertified and de-Googled devices are exceptions. Google frames this as accountability against rapidly iterated malware, but the author says identity does not establish safety and warns that pseudonymous developers, F-Droid ecosystems, hobby projects, and user autonomy will bear the cost.
