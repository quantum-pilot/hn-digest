# Quantification of fibrinaloid clots in plasma from pediatric Long COVID patients

- Score: 131 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45557267) | Link: https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-7483367/v1

### TL;DR

An unreviewed preprint describes a microfluidic assay for quantifying fibrinaloid “microclots” in pediatric Long COVID plasma. In samples from 45 patients and 14 healthy children, the authors report significantly higher clot counts among patients, especially those with persistent circulating spike protein. They estimate 94% diagnostic accuracy for the device versus 66% for traditional slide counting and propose larger studies for screening and treatment monitoring. The small, selected cohort, unsettled prevalence claims, and ambiguous use of “accuracy” mean the findings are preliminary rather than a validated clinical test.
