# The App Store was always authoritarian

- Score: 143 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45556032) | Link: https://infrequently.org/2025/10/the-app-store-was-always-authoritarian/

### TL;DR

This advocacy essay argues that Apple’s control over iOS software distribution is inherently authoritarian because one company can censor speech, favor governments, and protect its own rents. It links removal of an app archiving alleged immigration-enforcement abuses to Apple’s resistance to browser-engine choice, progressive web apps, right-to-repair, and EU competition rules. The author claims Apple trades compliance for tariff and regulatory advantages while suppressing the open web as an App Store competitor. The piece extends similar criticism to Google’s Android and Play ecosystem.
