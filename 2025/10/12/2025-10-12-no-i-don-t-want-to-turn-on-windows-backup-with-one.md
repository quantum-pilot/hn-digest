# No I don't want to turn on Windows Backup with One Drive

- Score: 485 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45559023) | Link: https://idiallo.com/byte-size/say-no-to-onedrive-backup

### TL;DR

The author criticizes a Windows prompt that offers OneDrive Backup now or a reminder in one week or 30 days, but no clear permanent refusal. “No thanks” is ambiguous because it appears alongside the reminder selector, and another promotion appears after restart. The post calls this an illusion of choice and a dark pattern: a service repeatedly embedded into the operating system after initial setup. The concern is less whether cloud backup can help than whether Windows records and respects an informed decision never to enable it.

### Comment pulse

- Users reported unwanted folder redirection, uploads, recurring re-enablement, workflow breakage, and difficult reversals with potential data-loss risk.
- Some recommended Linux, while others noted professional software, document compatibility, and hardware support can obstruct switching.

### LLM perspective

- View: A reminder becomes coercive when “never” is deliberately absent and refusal is not durable.
