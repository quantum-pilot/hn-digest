# Macro Splats 2025

- Score: 373 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45556952) | Link: https://danybittel.ch/macro.html

### TL;DR

Dany Bittel combines macro focus stacking with Gaussian splatting to create freely viewable 3D insect photographs. Macro depth of field makes ordinary capture unsuitable, so each of 111 viewpoints was assembled from 16 focal planes shot at f/18. A rotary stage automated azimuth, a focus rail captured each stack, and the camera angle changed manually eight times. The four-hour session produced 1,776 photographs; stacked images were reconstructed with COLMAP, masked and color-corrected, then trained in Postshot. One fly model is released under CC BY.
