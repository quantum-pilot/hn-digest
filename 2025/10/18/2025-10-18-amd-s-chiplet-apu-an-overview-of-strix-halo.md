# AMD's Chiplet APU: An Overview of Strix Halo

- Score: 165 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45624888) | Link: https://chipsandcheese.com/p/amds-chiplet-apu-an-overview-of-strix

### TL;DR

AMD’s Strix Halo combines two eight-core Zen 5 chiplets with a 40-CU RDNA 3.5 integrated GPU, 32 MB Infinity Cache, and 256 GB/s shared memory bandwidth. Tests put its CPU near recent desktop flagships and its GPU around a power-limited mobile RTX 5070 in some workloads, although dedicated high-end graphics remain faster. Commenters saw compelling mini-PC and large-memory inference potential today, tempered by European pricing, availability, and immature ROCm support.
