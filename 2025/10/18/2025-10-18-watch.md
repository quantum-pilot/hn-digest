# ./watch

- Score: 302 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45626130) | Link: https://dotslashwatch.com/

### TL;DR

The ./watch project deliberately exposes quartz-watch electronics and wraps its display in command-line-inspired styling. Its site organizes the build around PCB decisions, firmware, case prototypes, revisions, and an open GitHub repository. Commenters generally liked the hardware exploration but divided sharply over the face: critics said decorative prompt symbols contradict the command line’s compact precision, while others defended the retro-computing kitsch as taste. Practical suggestions included configurable faces, clearer date-style syntax, countersunk screws, and more visually distinctive PCB materials.

### Comment pulse
