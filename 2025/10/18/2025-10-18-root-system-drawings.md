# Root System Drawings

- Score: 270 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45627394) | Link: https://images.wur.nl/digital/collection/coll13/search

### TL;DR

The Root System Drawings collection exposes 1,180 indexed records spanning Central European grassland plants, crops, weeds, trees, shrubs, and plants from varied habitats. Its browsable listing provides multiple specimens for some species, inviting comparison of underground structures usually hidden from view. Commenters were struck by unexpectedly deep dandelion roots and discussed how researchers may expose, measure, and draw roots through laborious excavation and soil removal. They noted larger plants are harder to reconstruct accurately in three dimensions.
