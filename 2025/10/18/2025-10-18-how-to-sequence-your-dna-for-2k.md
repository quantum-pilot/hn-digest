# How to sequence your DNA for <$2k

- Score: 96 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45629970) | Link: https://maxlangenkamp.substack.com/p/how-to-sequence-your-dna-for-2k

### TL;DR

A small team spent about $1,100 on an Oxford Nanopore MinION, extraction supplies, a centrifuge, consumables, and improvised heating equipment to sequence one participant’s blood-derived DNA. Two runs produced roughly one gigabase, but only 13% genome coverage; bacterial contamination, a hardware failure, and just 623 of 2,048 working pores left the results unusable for reliable variant analysis. Commenters praised the experiment yet stressed that commercial short-read sequencing can deliver far deeper coverage more cheaply, with serious privacy tradeoffs.
