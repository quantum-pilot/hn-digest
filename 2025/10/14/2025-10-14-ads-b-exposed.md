# ADS-B Exposed

- Score: 277 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45578383) | Link: https://adsb.exposed/

### TL;DR

ADS-B Exposed is a ClickHouse technology demo for interactively aggregating and visualizing enormous air-traffic datasets, with presets spanning altitude, aircraft makers, military flights, and individual planes. Its repository says users can drill from roughly 50 billion records to individual observations using custom SQL. The project now also exposes places, birds, photos, and a “You” dataset, with cloud real-time and self-hosted snapshot modes. Its sparse landing page makes the data provenance and color encodings difficult to understand without reading the repository or queries.
