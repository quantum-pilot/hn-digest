# Half of America's Voting Machines Are Now Owned by a MAGA Oligarch

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45584295) | Link: https://dissentinbloom.substack.com/p/half-of-americas-voting-machines

### TL;DR

A strongly partisan essay argues that Scott Leiendecker’s acquisition and renaming of Dominion Voting Systems as Liberty Vote creates dangerous concentration because his company KNOWiNK also supplies electronic pollbooks. It connects that ownership to Trump’s election executive order, proposed citizenship-document requirements, voter-roll reviews, and Republican “election integrity” operations, alleging a coordinated threat to access and counting. Many causal and technical claims are presented polemically rather than independently demonstrated in the supplied material. The concrete governance issue is private vendor concentration across critical election infrastructure.

### Comment pulse

- Readers disputed whether ownership proves manipulation but broadly favored reducing concentration and increasing auditability.
- Common proposals combined voter-readable paper ballots or optical scans with public oversight and risk-limiting audits.

### LLM perspective

- View: The essay’s maximal claims outrun its evidence, while vendor concentration remains a legitimate institutional risk.
- Impact: Election trust depends on independently verifiable records, audits, procurement transparency, and resilient check-in procedures.
- Watch next: Acquisition financing, ownership controls, contracts, certification changes, and universal adoption of software-independent audits.
