# After 2 decades of tinkering, MAME cracks the Hyper Neo Geo 64

- Score: 304 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45516968) | Link: https://www.readonlymemo.com/mame-hyper-neo-geo-support-sound-emulation/

### TL;DR

After 21 years of intermittent work, MAME’s Hyper Neo Geo 64 emulation moved from nominally working to broadly playable sound. Better component emulation, a dumped I/O microcontroller, sound-program disassembly, and renewed collaboration helped contributors fix envelopes, filters, mixing, samples, and dropped commands across the system’s seven games; MAME 0.282 was expected to refine the result further. The article also reports rapid PC Engine LaserActive support and performance improvements in Ares. HN celebrated persistent preservation work while worrying modern DRM may leave future gaps.
