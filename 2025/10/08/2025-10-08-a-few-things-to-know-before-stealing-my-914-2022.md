### TL;DR

Norman Garrett addresses a hypothetical thief with mock-helpful instructions for operating his deeply temperamental Porsche 914. Starting requires reconnecting the battery, depressing the clutch, and precisely pumping the throttle; shifting resembles negotiating a vague linkage, while broken gauges, weak lights, oil fumes, poor brakes, and a bent wheel make escape progressively less attractive. The suggested endpoint is abandoning it near the highway for a Camry. HN readers recognized the same affectionate dysfunction in vintage vehicles, undocumented software, and developer onboarding.
