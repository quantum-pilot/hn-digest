# Suspicionless ChatControl must be taboo in a state governed by the rule of law

- Score: 259 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45517642) | Link: https://digitalcourage.social/@echo_pbreyer/115337976340299372

### TL;DR

Germany’s justice minister said suspicionless Chat Control must be unacceptable under the rule of law, that private communications cannot face general suspicion, and that Germany would reject such EU proposals. The supplied source is only a short social-media statement, so it establishes a political position rather than legislative details or a lasting outcome. Discussion distinguished mass client-side scanning from targeted surveillance authorized through warrants, while several commenters argued that any built-in backdoor creates dangerous infrastructure vulnerable to broader or arbitrary use.
