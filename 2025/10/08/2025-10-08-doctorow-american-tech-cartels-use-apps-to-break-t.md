# Doctorow: American tech cartels use apps to break the law

- Score: 277 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45518136) | Link: https://lithub.com/how-american-tech-cartels-use-apps-to-break-the-law/

### TL;DR

Cory Doctorow argues competition supports effective regulation by making firms challenge one another’s claims and limiting the profits available for regulatory capture. Concentrated industries can coordinate policy, overpower watchdogs, and use apps to recast familiar regulated activities as novel exceptions: Uber employment, Airbnb lodging, algorithmic rent recommendations, dynamic pricing, and app-based finance. Anti-circumvention law can then block tools that remove unwanted features. HN readers disputed whether fragmented markets regulate better and debated whether Uber improved a dysfunctional taxi system or merely bypassed democratically enacted rules.
