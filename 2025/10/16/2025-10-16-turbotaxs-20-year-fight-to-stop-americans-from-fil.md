# TurboTax’s 20-year fight to stop Americans from filing taxes for free (2019)

- Score: 775 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45601750) | Link: https://www.propublica.org/article/inside-turbotax-20-year-fight-to-stop-americans-from-filing-their-taxes-for-free

### TL;DR

ProPublica’s 2019 investigation argues that Intuit protected TurboTax by lobbying against government-run filing, shaping the IRS Free File bargain, hiring former officials, and limiting visibility of genuinely free options. Internal documents described government simplification as “encroachment,” proposed manufactured grassroots support, and acknowledged customer confusion around “free” advertising. Former employees described late paywalls and design patterns exploiting tax anxiety. Intuit denied wrongdoing and argued that government-prepared returns create conflicts. The reporting also shows IRS funding constraints, fragmented federal-state rules, and political choices helped sustain the system.
