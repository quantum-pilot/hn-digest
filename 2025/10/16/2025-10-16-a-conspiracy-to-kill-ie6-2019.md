# A conspiracy to kill IE6 (2019)

- Score: 145 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45608887) | Link: https://blog.chriszacharias.com/a-conspiracy-to-kill-ie6

### TL;DR

In 2009, a small YouTube web team secretly shipped an IE6-only warning banner using privileged “OldTuber” review bypasses, hoping vague deprecation pressure would push users toward modern browsers. Google Docs copied it, other teams followed, and the author says YouTube’s IE6 share halved within a month while global traffic fell. Lawyers accepted randomized browser ordering, and management later judged the outcome worthwhile. Commenters recalled IE6’s maintenance burden but disputed causality, pointing to Windows 7 replacement cycles and simultaneous IE7 decline.
