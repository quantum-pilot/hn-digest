# Steve Jobs and Cray-1 to be featured on 2026 American Innovations $1 coin

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45602124) | Link: https://www.usmint.gov/news/press-releases/united-states-mint-releases-2026-american-innovation-one-dollar-coin-program-designs

### TL;DR

The US Mint unveiled four separate 2026 American Innovation $1 coins: Norman Borlaug for Iowa, the Cray-1 supercomputer for Wisconsin, Steve Jobs for California, and mobile refrigeration for Minnesota. Their reverses depict each subject, while a shared Statue of Liberty obverse gains a gear-and-Liberty-Bell “250” privy mark for the nation’s semiquincentennial. The headline combines two distinct state coins, not one Jobs-and-Cray design. Public reaction focused less on minting details than on whether individuals, inventions, or more morally neutral symbols belong on currency.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters criticized honoring Jobs without acknowledging wage-suppression litigation and debated whether flawed people should appear on money.
- Several preferred depicting Apple products, Jobs with Wozniak, or Seymour Cray rather than elevating a single executive.
- Minnesota’s mobile-refrigeration coin prompted interest in cold-chain infrastructure for food and medicine.
