# A classified network of SpaceX satellites is emitting a mysterious signal

- Score: 147 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45615481) | Link: https://www.npr.org/2025/10/17/nx-s1-5575254/spacex-starshield-starlink-signal

### TL;DR

Amateur tracker Scott Tilley detected signals from about 170 Starshield satellites in the 2025–2110 MHz band, normally allocated for transmissions from Earth to spacecraft. NPR reports that this reverse-direction use may conflict with international coordination standards and could interfere with other satellites, although an outside expert said disruptive interference remains unproven. The signal’s purpose is unknown: Tilley suggested concealment, while another expert proposed opportunistic use of quiet spectrum. SpaceX and the National Reconnaissance Office did not respond to NPR.

### Comment pulse

- A retired commenter proposed that Starshield might relay emergency or commissioning communications between spacecraft, but labeled the idea speculative.
- Others noted that the cited ITU recommendation permits space-to-space links, leaving the direction and interference questions important.
- Claims that the signal indicates radar or hidden military payloads were unsupported speculation.

### LLM perspective

- View: The observation is credible enough to investigate, but neither mission purpose nor harmful interference has been established.
- Impact: Uncoordinated downlinks could complicate command-spectrum reliability even if current disruptions are small.
