# The pivot

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45621074) | Link: https://www.antipope.org/charlie/blog-static/2025/10/the-pivot-1.html

### TL;DR

Charlie Stross argues that 2025 is a historical pivot as cheap solar and batteries make the fossil-energy order increasingly untenable while incumbent elites resist losing wealth and status. He links that transition to climate instability, resource conflict, renewed fascism, neoliberal extraction, fragile just-in-time systems, and speculative technology bubbles. The essay’s outlook is grim but conditional: surviving near-term agricultural, nuclear, political, and financial shocks could open a more manageable 2030s. HN discussion mostly explored his fiction and clarified its dystopian, not utopian, acceleration.
