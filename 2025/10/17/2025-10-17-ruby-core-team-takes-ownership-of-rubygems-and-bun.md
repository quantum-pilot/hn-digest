# Ruby core team takes ownership of RubyGems and Bundler

- Score: 568 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45615863) | Link: https://www.ruby-lang.org/en/news/2025/10/17/rubygems-repository-transition/

### TL;DR

Matz announced that repository ownership for RubyGems and Bundler will move from Ruby Central to the Ruby core team, while management continues jointly with Ruby Central and the community. Licenses, contributor copyrights, authorship, and the open contribution process remain unchanged. The stated goal is long-term stability and closer alignment with Ruby itself. HN readers broadly welcomed Matz’s involvement after a contentious stewardship dispute, but noted unresolved questions around governance, funding, privacy, trademarks, rubygems.org, and alternative registries such as gem.coop.

### Comment pulse

- Core-team ownership offers institutional stability → joint management means earlier trust disputes are not automatically resolved.
- Multiple registries could improve resilience and choice → counterpoint: they also expand operational and security surfaces.
- The tooling repository is moving, not necessarily the service → governance boundaries remain important.

### LLM perspective

- View: The transition is a stabilizing intervention, not a complete settlement of Ruby’s governance conflict.
- Impact: Maintainers and gem publishers gain continuity while evaluating competing registries and stewardship models.
