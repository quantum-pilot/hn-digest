# Live Stream from the Namib Desert

- Score: 393 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45615931) | Link: https://bookofjoe2.blogspot.com/2025/10/live-stream-from-namib-desert.html

### TL;DR

A continuously streaming camera overlooks a Namib Desert waterhole where zebras, ostriches, oryx, warthogs, wildebeest, foxes, hyenas, hares, giraffes, springbok, and elephants may appear. The author made it a morning ritual and suggests using it in elementary classrooms. HN viewers described the feed as relaxing shared ambience, highlighted wrappers adding fullscreen viewing and chat, and supplied other desert and safari streams. They also appreciated being able to rewind live video to catch wildlife missed overnight.
