# EVs are depreciating faster than gas-powered cars

- Score: 269 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45615237) | Link: https://restofworld.org/2025/ev-depreciation-blusmart-collapse/

### TL;DR

Rest of World reports that used electric vehicles often lose value faster than combustion cars, citing US and UK studies, Tesla price cuts, Hertz’s fleet losses, and BluSmart’s distressed liquidation in India. Uncertain battery health, rapid model changes, repair costs, charging constraints, and weak local demand complicate residual-value forecasts, especially for fleets. Conditions vary substantially by brand, region, and usage. The article also notes improving battery longevity data, warranties, health reports, certified-used programs, and battery-as-a-service models that could narrow information gaps and stabilize pricing.
