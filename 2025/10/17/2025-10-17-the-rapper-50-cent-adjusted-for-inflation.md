# The Rapper 50 Cent, Adjusted for Inflation

- Score: 461 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45618790) | Link: https://50centadjustedforinflation.com/

### TL;DR

A single-purpose interactive site calculates that the rapper’s June 1994 stage name, adjusted using US Bureau of Labor Statistics inflation data, would now be 109 Cent. Its image and graph scale with the cumulative price increase. The creator says monthly updates normally arrive on the 15th, although a government shutdown delayed September 2025 data. HN readers enjoyed the joke, suggested historical and currency extensions, spotted a possible image-boundary bug, and debated how artistic valuation differs from consumer-price inflation.
