# Amazon’s Ring to partner with Flock

- Score: 463 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45614713) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/16/amazons-ring-to-partner-with-flock-a-network-of-ai-cameras-used-by-ice-feds-and-police/

### TL;DR

Ring announced that agencies using Flock’s camera network will be able to request footage directly from Ring doorbell owners for investigations. The sharing is described as a user request rather than automatic access, but the partnership potentially connects Flock customers—including police and federal agencies reported to include ICE, the Secret Service, and Navy—to millions more cameras. Flock analyzes license plates and supports natural-language video searches. TechCrunch places the deal against documented concerns about biased law-enforcement AI and Ring’s previous privacy and security failures.
