# California enacts law enabling people to universally opt out of data sharing

- Score: 418 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45523033) | Link: https://therecord.media/california-signs-law-opt-out-browsers

### TL;DR

California enacted a first-of-its-kind requirement for web browsers to offer residents an easy-to-find control that sends a universal request to opt out of personal-data sale or sharing. It builds on rights already established by the California Consumer Privacy Act, replacing repeated site-by-site choices with a browser-level signal; unlike a broader vetoed proposal, it does not cover mobile operating systems. Related laws signed the same day require simple social-account cancellation followed by data deletion and expand disclosures about data brokers’ collections and recipients. Effective enforcement remains the practical question.
