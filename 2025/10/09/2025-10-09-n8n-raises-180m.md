# N8n raises $180M

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45525336) | Link: https://blog.n8n.io/series-c/

### TL;DR

n8n says it raised a $180 million Series C led by Accel, bringing total funding to $240 million and valuation to $2.5 billion. The automation company positions itself between unpredictable fully autonomous agents and rigid coded workflows, combining model calls, deterministic logic, human oversight, integrations, and collaboration. It reports sixfold user growth and tenfold revenue growth over the year, and plans more integrations, community-built nodes, interfaces, ecosystem support, and hiring. These growth figures and claims of production value come from n8n’s own announcement rather than independently presented financial data.
