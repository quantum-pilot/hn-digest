# Tariffs Are Way Up. Interest on Debt Tops $1T. and Doge Didn't Do Much

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45530234) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/economy/federal-budget-fiscal-2025-e8d21595

### TL;DR

Congressional Budget Office year-end figures put the fiscal 2025 deficit at $1.8 trillion, essentially unchanged despite customs revenue more than doubling to $195 billion. Tariffs still supplied only 3.7% of federal revenue. Net interest reached $1.029 trillion under the article's chosen measure, while spending excluding interest rose 4%; a $131 billion noncash student-loan adjustment softened the reported increase. Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid each grew, and publicly held debt approached 100% of GDP.
