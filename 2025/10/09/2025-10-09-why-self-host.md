# Why Self-Host?

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45528342) | Link: https://romanzipp.com/blog/why-a-homelab-why-self-host

### TL;DR

Roman Zipp argues for self-hosting as a route to privacy and digital sovereignty, especially for calendars, contacts, location history, smart-home control, RSS, and potentially personal email. He describes using CalDAV/CardDAV, Home Assistant, FreshRSS, and Dawarich, while acknowledging hardware, expertise, secure remote access, deliverability, and operational work. Commenters extended the case to small businesses but stressed that backups, upgrades, security maintenance, and observability determine whether ownership becomes resilience or merely another fragile dependency.

### Comment pulse

- Readers said many small services fit a hardened VPS without elaborate cloud infrastructure.
- Email deliverability and tested restoration plans emerged as the strongest practical cautions.

### LLM perspective

- View: Self-hosting is valuable when control over data and continuity outweighs ongoing operational responsibility.
- Impact: Open protocols make provider exits easier even when every component is not locally hosted.
- Watch next: Start with recoverable, low-criticality services and prove backups before moving essential data.
