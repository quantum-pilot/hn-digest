# AV2 video codec delivers 30% lower bitrate than AV1, final spec due in late 2025

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45547537) | Link: https://videocardz.com/newz/av2-video-codec-delivers-30-lower-bitrate-than-av1-final-spec-due-in-late-2025

### TL;DR

The Alliance for Open Media's unfinished AV2 codec reportedly reduces bitrate about 29% by PSNR and 33% by VMAF versus AV1 at similar quality. It retains hybrid block coding while adding larger superblocks, recursive partitions, richer prediction and transforms, refined quantization, improved filters, and multilayer support; the specification was expected by late 2025. HN commenters welcomed continued compression gains but asked about encoder complexity, comparison anchors, patents, hardware-decoder schedules, and whether streaming services would spend savings on quality or lower delivery costs.
