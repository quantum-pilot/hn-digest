# LineageOS 23

- Score: 156 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45553835) | Link: https://lineageos.org/Changelog-30/

### TL;DR

LineageOS 23.0 ships Android 16's initial QPR0 because Google had not released QPR1 source to AOSP and now withholds many monthly security fixes until quarterly releases. The project will claim patch levels only when all fixes are public; reduced Pixel source and newer eBPF requirements also complicate support. The release adds a rewritten Aperture camera, improved Twelve music player, ad-free Catapult TV launcher, expanded virtual-machine and mainline-kernel work, and many devices. HN discussion compared LineageOS's freedom and hardware breadth with GrapheneOS's narrower security focus.

### Comment pulse

- Google's source retreat strains custom ROMs → delayed patches and private partner access weaken timely, independently verifiable releases.
- LineageOS extends hardware life → users value newer Android versions, monthly available patches, de-Googled operation, and consistent behavior.
- Security tradeoffs remain → broad device support and hackability often mean unlocked bootloaders and less hardening than GrapheneOS.
