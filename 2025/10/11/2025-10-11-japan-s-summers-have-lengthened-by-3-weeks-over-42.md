# Japan's summers have lengthened by 3 weeks over 42 years, say resaerchers

- Score: 127 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45552578) | Link: https://english.kyodonews.net/articles/-/62626

### TL;DR

A Mie University group analyzed Japan Meteorological Agency observations across roughly 200 land and ocean zones from 1982–2023, defining local summer thresholds from annual temperature ranges. It found summer began about 12.6 days earlier and ended 8.8 days later, extending roughly 21.4 days overall; winter changed little while spring and autumn shortened. Researchers attributed the shift mainly to warmer surrounding seas reducing seasonal cooling. HN commenters connected it to long cherry-blossom records and personal observations, while debating broader climate causes and impacts.
