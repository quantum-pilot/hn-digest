# California invests in battery energy storage, leaving rolling blackouts behind

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45706527) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/environment/story/2025-10-17/california-made-it-through-another-summer-without-a-flex-alert

### TL;DR

California’s grid-scale battery power grew from roughly 500 megawatts in 2020 to more than 15,700 megawatts, helping shift daytime solar into evening peaks. Officials credit batteries alongside 26,000 megawatts of broader new resources and coordination for avoiding rolling blackouts since 2020 and Flex Alerts since 2022. Most lithium-ion systems cover four to six hours, so gas still fills longer gaps. The article also notes permitting, aging-grid and fire risks. Commenters caution that mild weather, stronger hydropower and power-versus-energy capacity complicate the triumphant headline.
