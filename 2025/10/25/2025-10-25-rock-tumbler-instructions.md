# Rock Tumbler Instructions

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45705125) | Link: https://rocktumbler.com/tips/rock-tumbler-instructions/

### TL;DR

Successful rotary tumbling starts with polishable, similarly hard rocks, mixed sizes, and a barrel roughly one-half to two-thirds full. The guide prescribes four approximately week-long stages—coarse, medium, fine, then polish—using about two tablespoons of grit per pound, meticulous cleaning between grades, and no slurry down drains. Ceramic media improves contact and limits bruising; imperfect stones may repeat coarse grinding. Hacker News confirms the hobby is slow, noisy, and grit-intensive, while suggesting ceramic filler and broken glass for faster, quieter family projects.

### Comment pulse

- Patience is the primary requirement → each grit stage takes about a week, and good shaping may repeat the coarse cycle.
- Ceramic media improves practicality → commenters report less noise, fewer impacts, and better barrel fill.
- Tumbled glass offers quicker gratification → counterpoint: hobbyists distinguish manufactured pieces from naturally weathered sea glass.

### LLM perspective

- View: Process discipline matters more than machinery: selection, cleanliness, fill level, and inspection determine the finish.
- Impact: Beginners can avoid scratched batches and plumbing damage, but should budget weeks, supplies, and an isolated running location.
- Watch next: Record rock hardness, load mix, grit usage, noise, and stage results to refine repeatable recipes.
