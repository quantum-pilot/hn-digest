# Synadia and TigerBeetle Commit $512k USD to the Zig Software Foundation

- Score: 391 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45703716) | Link: https://www.synadia.com/blog/synadia-tigerbeetle-zig-foundation-pledge

### TL;DR

Synadia and TigerBeetle jointly pledged $512,000 to the Zig Software Foundation over two years, presenting the donation as support for simpler, reliable systems software. Synadia says it will not rewrite the NATS server in Zig; instead, Zig will become a Tier 1 NATS client and support resource-constrained deployments connecting operational and information technology in manufacturing, industrial IoT, vehicles, robotics, and embodied AI. The sparse Hacker News discussion points readers to TigerBeetle’s longer rationale and includes one positive TigerBeetle adoption anecdote.
