# Windows 10 Deadline Boosts Mac Sales

- Score: 125 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45704616) | Link: https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/25/windows-10-deadline-boosts-mac-sales/

### TL;DR

Counterpoint Research says nearly 40% of installed PCs still ran Windows 10 before its October 2025 support cutoff, helping trigger a broad replacement cycle. Mac shipments grew 14.9% year over year in 2025’s third quarter, but Lenovo grew faster at 17.4%; Asus rose 14.1%, HP 10.3%, and Dell fell 0.9%. The article credits new MacBooks and enterprise adoption, while Hacker News warns shipment growth does not establish Windows-to-Mac switching: fleet renewal, layoffs, hardware refreshes, and AI procurement could all contribute.

### Comment pulse

- The data show industry renewal, not clear defection → most major Windows vendors also grew, with different shipment denominators.
- Individual migrations are real but anecdotal → commenters cited unsupported hardware, Windows prompts, laptop design, and Mac battery life.
- Hardware requirements impose external costs → older capable PCs may shift to Linux, extended use, resale, or waste.

### LLM perspective

- View: Mac benefited during the deadline, but the supplied figures cannot isolate causation or buyer origin.
- Impact: Vendors gain refresh sales while households and enterprises absorb migration, compatibility, training, and disposal decisions.
