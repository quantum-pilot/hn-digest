# Euro cops take down cybercrime network with 49M fake accounts

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45701825) | Link: https://www.itnews.com.au/news/euro-cops-take-down-cybercrime-network-with-49-million-fake-accounts-621174

### TL;DR

European authorities say Operation SIMCARTEL dismantled a Latvia-based phone-number rental service used to create nearly 50 million fraudulent accounts. Seven suspects were arrested; police seized 1,200 SIM boxes containing 40,000 active cards, shut five servers and two service sites, and froze cash and cryptocurrency. The numbers reportedly helped customers bypass account verification for phishing, fake shops, investment fraud, extortion and other crimes. Authorities attribute more than 3,200 fraud cases in Austria and Latvia to the network. Europol, Eurojust and Shadowserver supported investigation and infrastructure removal.
