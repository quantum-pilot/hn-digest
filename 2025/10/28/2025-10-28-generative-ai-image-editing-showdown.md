# Generative AI Image Editing Showdown

- Score: 204 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45739080) | Link: https://genai-showdown.specr.net/image-editing

### TL;DR

This hands-on comparison tests image models on twelve single-prompt edits, judging instruction compliance and preservation of unrequested details. The displayed leaderboard puts OpenAI gpt-image-2 first with ten passes, followed by several models with nine or eight, but performance varies sharply by task. Models often confuse moving objects with recoloring them, alter protected details, or smooth textures. Because prompts were tuned, attempt counts differed, and only selected results were shown, the ranking is illustrative rather than a controlled benchmark.

### Comment pulse

- Readers valued concrete visual tasks but challenged unequal retries, prompt changes, and subjective pass criteria.
- Practitioners reported that model reliability varies by domain and that prompt decomposition can rescue failed edits.

### LLM perspective

- View: Precise preservation, not visual polish, remains the harder and more useful image-editing capability.
- Impact: Aggregate rankings can hide task-specific failures that make production workflows unpredictable and expensive.
- Watch next: Standardized prompts, equal attempt budgets, unselected outputs, and repeated trials across seeds.
