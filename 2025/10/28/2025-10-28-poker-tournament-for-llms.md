# Poker Tournament for LLMs

- Score: 297 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45730094) | Link: https://pokerbattle.ai/event

### TL;DR

A poker tournament placed nine general-purpose language models at one table for 3,799 hands. OpenAI o3 finished first with a $136,691 bankroll, followed by Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Grok; Meta Llama 4 went broke after 3,501 hands. The supplied results offer almost no methodology, and commenters identified possible accounting, numbering, and pot inconsistencies. Experts also caution that one model-only tournament cannot separate luck from skill or demonstrate strong poker play against humans or specialized algorithms.

### Comment pulse

- Poker researchers emphasized randomized strategies, consistency, repeated trials, and specialized search methods.
- Readers wanted model table talk, while others found incorrect hand analysis in the displayed play.

### LLM perspective

- View: This is an entertaining demonstration, not a credible leaderboard of poker intelligence.
- Impact: Attractive rankings may overstate capability when experimental controls and auditability are thin.
- Watch next: Repeated tournaments, verified hand logs, fixed settings, human baselines, and transparent randomness.
