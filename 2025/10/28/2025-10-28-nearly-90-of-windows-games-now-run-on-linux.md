# Nearly 90% of Windows Games Now Run on Linux

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45736925) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/software/linux/nearly-90-percent-of-windows-games-now-run-on-linux-latest-data-shows-as-windows-10-dies-gaming-on-linux-is-more-viable-than-ever

### TL;DR

ProtonDB-derived data summarized by Boiling Steam indicates almost 90% of surveyed Windows games can run on Linux, reflecting years of Wine, Proton, and Steam Deck investment. The headline combines several compatibility tiers: platinum works out of the box, gold needs minor tweaks, silver is playable with imperfections, and bronze remains marginal. This does not mean every major new release works seamlessly. Anti-cheat systems and developer choices still block prominent games, while the article says compatibility growth appears to be slowing.

### Comment pulse
