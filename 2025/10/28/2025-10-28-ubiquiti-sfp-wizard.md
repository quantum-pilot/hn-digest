# Ubiquiti SFP Wizard

- Score: 220 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45732874) | Link: https://blog.ui.com/article/welcome-to-sfp-liberation-day

### TL;DR

Ubiquiti’s $49 pocket SFP Wizard claims to test SFP and QSFP modules, display optical health metrics, copy an inserted module’s profile, and program compatible Ubiquiti optics through a mobile app. The launch pairs it with discounted 1G–100G modules and markets broad compatibility. Hacker News saw the price and portability as notable but questioned how completely it measures optical power, whether it programs only Ubiquiti modules, and how “universal” support behaves amid longstanding vendor coding and real-world transceiver incompatibilities.
