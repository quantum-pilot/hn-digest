# EuroLLM: LLM made in Europe built to support all 24 official EU languages

- Score: 556 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45733707) | Link: https://eurollm.io/

### TL;DR

EuroLLM presents an open-source European language-model family trained for all 24 official EU languages. Its flagship 9B model was trained on more than four trillion tokens spanning 35 languages, with base and instruction-tuned versions available; a 1.7B model targets edge devices. The project, backed by European research and computing programs, frames multilingual coverage as infrastructure for digital sovereignty. Claims that EuroLLM outperforms similarly sized models come from the project’s own site, and a commenter notes the 9B release dates to 2024.

### LLM perspective

- View: EuroLLM’s differentiation is deliberate language coverage and openness, not frontier-scale general capability.
- Impact: Smaller-language users and European organizations gain a model designed around their linguistic and sovereignty priorities.
- Watch next: Independent benchmarks by language, adoption beyond research, and delivery of promised vision and voice features.
