# Samsung makes ads on smart fridges official with upcoming software update

- Score: 480 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45737338) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2025/10/samsung-makes-ads-on-3499-smart-fridges-official-with-upcoming-software-update/

### TL;DR

Samsung is rolling out contextual advertisements to 2024 Family Hub refrigerators costing up to $3,499. A software update will place ads in an idle-screen widget alongside news, calendars, and weather, and reportedly in a Daily Board tile. Owners can opt out, use ad-free Art or Album screens, or skip the update, but those choices also forgo some connected features or improvements. The change exemplifies how remotely updated appliances can acquire unwanted business models after purchase, despite customers already paying premium hardware prices.
