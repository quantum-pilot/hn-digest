# The next chapter of the Microsoft–OpenAI partnership

- Score: 340 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45732350) | Link: https://openai.com/index/next-chapter-of-microsoft-openai-partnership/

### TL;DR

Microsoft and OpenAI’s definitive agreement values Microsoft’s stake at roughly $135 billion, or 27% diluted, while preserving Azure API exclusivity and Microsoft’s model-and-product IP rights through 2032. An independent panel must verify any OpenAI AGI declaration; research IP has narrower limits, and consumer hardware is excluded. OpenAI also committed another $250 billion of Azure services, though Microsoft lost first refusal on compute. Hacker News debated dilution, hardware strategy, open-weight flexibility, and Microsoft’s enduring leverage over OpenAI’s infrastructure and commercial reach.
