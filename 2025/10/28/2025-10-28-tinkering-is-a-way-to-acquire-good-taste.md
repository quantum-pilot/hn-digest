# Tinkering is a way to acquire good taste

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45739499) | Link: https://seated.ro/blog/tinkering-a-lost-art

### TL;DR

The essay argues that small, unnecessary technical experiments build “taste”: repeated exposure to tools and approaches teaches developers to distinguish mediocrity from excellence through direct experience. Its examples include GLSL, Rust macros, C++ templates, Swift, and Helix, while warning that configuration can become avoidance rather than learning. Hacker News connected this practice to judging AI-generated work and sandbox projects, but challenged taste as potentially subjective, expensive, joy-reducing, or harmful when rigid preferences become team-status weapons.

### Comment pulse
