# YouTube is taking down videos on performing nonstandard Windows 11 installs

- Score: 510 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45744503) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/DataHoarder/comments/1oiz0v0/youtube_is_taking_down_videos_on_performing/

### TL;DR

A Reddit post reports that YouTube removed videos showing how to install Windows 11 without a Microsoft account or on unsupported hardware, citing statements from a creator and urging viewers to archive them. The supplied material does not establish YouTube’s reason or policy basis, so the takedown claim remains secondhand. HN discussion supplied text instructions for bypassing setup, debated whether automated moderation might confuse legitimate guides with malicious command-running videos, and explored alternative hosting. Much of the conversation instead became a contested comparison of Windows and Linux practicality.

### Comment pulse
