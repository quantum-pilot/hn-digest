# The Internet runs on free and open source software and so does the DNS

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45750875) | Link: https://www.icann.org/en/blogs/details/the-internet-runs-on-free-and-open-source-softwareand-so-does-the-dns-23-10-2025-en

### TL;DR

ICANN’s Security and Stability Advisory Committee announced SAC132, a nontechnical report for policymakers on free and open-source software throughout the DNS, from registration infrastructure to name retrieval. The announcement argues that emerging cybersecurity rules in the United States, United Kingdom and European Union could either reinforce or inadvertently weaken DNS security and resilience. It promises background, policy comparisons and recommendations, and invites discussion at ICANN84. Because the supplied article is an announcement rather than the report itself, its specific recommendations are not available here.

### Comment pulse

- Discussion debated whether public institutions or commercial demand deserve more credit for the Internet’s spread.
- Commenters highlighted underfunded maintainers and implementation diversity across BIND, NSD and Knot as resilience concerns.

### LLM perspective

- View: Treating FOSS as critical infrastructure requires policy that funds maintenance without making volunteer projects compliance choke points.
- Impact: Poorly scoped cybersecurity duties could concentrate DNS software or discourage maintainers, reducing resilience.
- Watch next: SAC132’s concrete liability, procurement and funding recommendations, not merely its recognition of dependency.
