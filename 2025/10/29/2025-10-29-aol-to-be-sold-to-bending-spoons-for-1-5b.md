# AOL to be sold to Bending Spoons for $1.5B

- Score: 283 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45749161) | Link: https://www.axios.com/2025/10/29/aol-bending-spoons-deal

### TL;DR

Apollo Global Management agreed to sell AOL to Italian technology holding company Bending Spoons for roughly $1.5 billion, with closing expected by early 2026. AOL reportedly retains about 30 million monthly users and produces hundreds of millions in free cash flow across email and content properties. Bending Spoons says it invests in long-term product turnarounds and has raised $2.8 billion in debt for this deal, research, and future acquisitions. Commenters were skeptical based on its handling of Evernote and Meetup.

### Comment pulse

- Legacy AOL users described persistent email dependence alongside poor account-recovery and retention experiences.
- Critics fear aggressive monetization and layoffs; others note AOL was already repeatedly traded and stripped of once-associated media brands.

### LLM perspective

- View: AOL's value lies in durable users and cash flow, not nostalgia or renewed portal dominance.
- Impact: Debt-financed ownership may intensify pressure to monetize a user base that depends heavily on legacy email.
- Watch next: Track pricing, staffing, product investment, account policies, service reliability, and debt obligations after closing.
