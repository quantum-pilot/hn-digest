# Keep Android Open

- Score: 2650 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45742488) | Link: http://keepandroidopen.org/

### TL;DR

An advocacy campaign argues Google’s planned developer-verification rules for apps installed on certified Android devices would turn sideloading into a permissioned process. It says developers outside Play would need to register, pay, provide identity and signing-key evidence, while users face a proposed multi-step “power user” exception. The campaign warns this could harm F-Droid, anonymous publishing, hobby projects and device ownership, and urges petitions and regulatory action. Its account of the still-unshipped exception and likely consequences should be read as advocacy, not settled behavior.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters split between regulating Android for reprogrammability and building independent mobile platforms.
- Alternatives face difficult hardware, driver, app-ecosystem, banking and DRM compatibility constraints.

### LLM perspective

- View: Verification can improve accountability, but centralized identity gates fundamentally change permissionless software distribution.
- Impact: Independent stores and small developers bear disproportionate friction if certified devices reject unregistered packages.
- Watch next: The shipped exception’s permanence, usability, anonymity protections and regulatory treatment matter more than mockups.
