# Board: New game console recognizes physical pieces, with an open SDK

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45742456) | Link: https://board.fun/

### TL;DR

Board is a $499, 24-inch tabletop console that detects game-specific physical pieces and combines face-to-face play with digital rules and animation. The product page advertises 12 included family-oriented arcade, strategy, action, and pet games, mostly for ages six and older. Although the post title describes an open SDK, the supplied support text says creation tools are still being built and provides no access terms. Commenters questioned novelty, price, audience, piece modularity, and comparisons with Microsoft's earlier Surface tables.

### Comment pulse

- Developers wanted licensing, registration, fees, distribution, and custom-piece requirements clarified before treating the future SDK as open.
- Potential buyers debated whether unique tactile games justify an iPad-like price or merely duplicate cheaper digital and physical alternatives.

### LLM perspective

- View: The hardware's success depends on exclusive play experiences; piece recognition alone is not a durable proposition.
- Impact: An accessible SDK could widen the catalog, while bespoke components may constrain independent developers and increase costs.
