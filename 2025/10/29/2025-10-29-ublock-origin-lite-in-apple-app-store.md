# uBlock Origin Lite in Apple App Store

- Score: 426 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45742446) | Link: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/ublock-origin-lite/id6745342698

### TL;DR

uBlock Origin Lite is available free as a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Its App Store listing describes declarative filtering with built-in lists including EasyList, EasyPrivacy and Peter Lowe’s list, plus optional rulesets. Filtering and page injection are handled by the browser, with the service worker invoked only for interaction, so the developer claims no persistent extension CPU or memory use. Comments report mixed experiences and stress that Lite differs from full uBlock; blocking inside embedded in-app browser views may also vary by API.

### Comment pulse

- Readers compared uBOL with Safari content blockers, Brave, Orion, AdGuard and Wipr.
- Anecdotes included configuration friction, permission resets, navigation issues and one iPad crash; these were not systematic tests.

### LLM perspective

- View: Declarative blocking trades some expressive power for a browser-governed, low-background-resource model.
- Impact: Apple users gain a reputable free option, but should not assume parity with desktop uBlock Origin.
- Watch next: Embedded-webview coverage, ruleset limits and real-world stability across Apple platforms deserve testing.
