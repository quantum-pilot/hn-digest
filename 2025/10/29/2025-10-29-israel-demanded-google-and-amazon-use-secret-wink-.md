# Israel demanded Google and Amazon use secret 'wink' to sidestep legal orders

- Score: 859 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45746482) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/oct/29/google-amazon-israel-contract-secret-code

### TL;DR

A Guardian investigation based on leaked Israeli finance-ministry documents says Project Nimbus required Google and Amazon to notify Israel indirectly when compelled to disclose its cloud data under a gag order. The alleged “winking” mechanism uses payments keyed to a requesting country's telephone code, with a 100,000-shekel fallback when even that signal is barred. Documents also reportedly restrict providers from suspending Israeli agencies for policy violations. Both companies deny circumventing lawful confidentiality obligations, while Israel calls the allegation baseless.
