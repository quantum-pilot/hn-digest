# Dutch spy services have restricted intelligence-sharing with the United States

- Score: 240 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45646572) | Link: https://intelnews.org/2025/10/20/01-3416/

### TL;DR

Dutch intelligence chiefs told De Volkskrant that cooperation with US agencies remains “excellent,” while confirming that the Netherlands now evaluates sharing more critically and sometimes withholds particular intelligence. They did not identify withheld material or specific reasons, describing decisions as case-by-case. The services are simultaneously expanding cooperation with European partners, including Scandinavian countries, France, Germany, Britain, and Poland. Much of the surrounding discussion about Trump, Russia, leaks, and US cloud access is speculation rather than evidence supplied by the officials.
