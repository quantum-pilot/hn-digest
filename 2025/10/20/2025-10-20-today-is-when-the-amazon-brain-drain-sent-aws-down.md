# Today is when the Amazon brain drain sent AWS down the spout

- Score: 209 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45649178) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/10/20/aws_outage_amazon_brain_drain_corey_quinn/

### TL;DR

Corey Quinn argues that AWS’s US-EAST-1 outage exposed a loss of institutional knowledge after layoffs, attrition, and contentious return-to-office policies. The incident facts cited include elevated errors, a DynamoDB endpoint DNS problem, cascading failures, and roughly 75 minutes before AWS publicly narrowed the fault. The staffing explanation, however, is the columnist’s inference: no evidence directly connects departed engineers to this failure. Quinn supports concern about workforce churn with broad Amazon layoff figures, reported attrition data, and anecdotes, none establishing outage causation.

### Comment pulse

- Many commenters agreed that layoffs trade immediate savings for delayed operational risk, often using personal impressions rather than data.
- Others considered 75 minutes a respectable diagnosis time and noted that public updates may intentionally lag internal hypotheses.
- Commenters disputed whether the outage timeline actually aligned with US West Coast working hours.
