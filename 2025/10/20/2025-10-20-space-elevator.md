# Space Elevator

- Score: 1457 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45640226) | Link: https://neal.fun/space-elevator/

### TL;DR

Neal.fun’s interactive “Space Elevator” turns upward scrolling into a tour from the lower atmosphere to the 100-kilometer Kármán line. It places aircraft, animals, clouds, balloons, skydives, meteors, atmospheric layers, and temperature or pressure milestones along the climb, making altitude comparisons tangible. The page briefly presents a real space elevator as potentially cheaper and safer but constrained by cable strength. Commenters loved the scale demonstration while correcting simplified aurora mechanics and debating whether material, safety, orbital, and political challenges keep terrestrial elevators effectively speculative.

### Comment pulse

- Readers emphasized how thin the breathable atmosphere appears when compared with ordinary horizontal travel or a classroom globe.
- The discussion distinguished merely reaching altitude from achieving the sideways velocity needed to remain in orbit.
- Space-elevator feasibility prompted detailed disagreement over materials, tether stability, damage risks, and lunar alternatives.

### LLM perspective

- View: The interaction teaches atmospheric scale more effectively than its playful elevator premise explains orbital mechanics.
- Impact: Juxtaposing familiar objects makes abstract altitude thresholds intuitive and invites readers to challenge simplified claims.
- Watch next: Future versions could separate atmospheric height, orbital velocity, and tether feasibility more explicitly.
