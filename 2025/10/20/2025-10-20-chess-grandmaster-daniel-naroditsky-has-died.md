# Chess grandmaster Daniel Naroditsky has died

- Score: 291 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45646561) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/chess/comments/1obnbmu/grandmaster_daniel_naroditsky_has_passed_away/

### TL;DR

Daniel Naroditsky's family, through the Charlotte Chess Center, announced his unexpected death and asked for privacy. They remembered him as a gifted grandmaster, educator, son, brother, friend, and inspiration to the chess community. Players and viewers describe learning from his speedruns, commentary, humility, generosity, and ability to explain advanced chess clearly. No cause is provided in the supplied statement. Much of the surrounding Reddit thread speculates about circumstances and blame, but those claims are unverified and should not displace the family's request.
