# Apple takes down ICE tracking apps after pressure from DOJ

- Score: 606 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45457333) | Link: https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/apple-takes-down-ice-tracking-app-after-pressure-from-ag-bondi

### TL;DR

Apple removed ICEBlock and similar apps after the U.S. Justice Department demanded action, citing risks to immigration-enforcement officers. ICEBlock let users anonymously report nearby ICE activity; Attorney General Pam Bondi said it endangered agents, while creator Joshua Aaron denied that claim and compared it with crowdsourced speed-trap reporting. The report connects scrutiny to a deadly ICE-facility shooting whose suspect had searched for tracking apps, but does not establish that ICEBlock enabled the attack. The episode raises platform-control and government-pressure questions.
