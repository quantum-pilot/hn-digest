# Anduril and Palantir battlefield communication system has flaws, Army memo says

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45464269) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/03/anduril-palantir-ngc2-deep-flaws-army.html

### TL;DR

An internal Army memo reportedly rated an early NGC2 battlefield-communications prototype from Anduril, Palantir, Microsoft, and partners “very high risk.” It said authorized users could access applications and classified data without need-based controls, activity was not logged, and third-party applications contained numerous vulnerabilities. Army leadership characterized the assessment as part of vulnerability triage and mitigation, while the vendors did not comment. Because this concerns a prototype, the dispute is whether missing security controls are normal staging or an architectural warning.
