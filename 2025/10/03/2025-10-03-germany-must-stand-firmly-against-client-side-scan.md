# Germany must stand firmly against client-side scanning in Chat Control [pdf]

- Score: 501 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45464921) | Link: https://signal.org/blog/pdfs/germany-chat-control.pdf

### TL;DR

Signal president Meredith Whittaker urges Germany to retain its opposition to the EU proposal critics call “Chat Control.” Her letter argues that mandated scanning of messages, photos, and videos on users' devices would negate end-to-end encryption by creating privileged access attackers could exploit. Signal says it would leave the market rather than build such scanning into its service. This is an advocacy letter, not a neutral legislative analysis, and it frames Germany's position as decisive for European privacy and cybersecurity.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters strongly supported Signal and connected the proposal with Germany's historical experience of state surveillance.
- Discussion clarified that “Chat Control” is a critics' label; the proposal's official name concerns preventing child sexual abuse.
