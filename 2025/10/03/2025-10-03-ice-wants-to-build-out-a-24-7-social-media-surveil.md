# ICE Wants to Build Out a 24/7 Social Media Surveillance Team

- Score: 159 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45465964) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/ice-social-media-surveillance-24-7-contract/

### TL;DR

WIRED reports that ICE is exploring a multiyear contractor program to turn public social-media activity and commercial database records into enforcement leads. Draft documents envision nearly 30 analysts across Vermont and California, with the latter staffed continuously and urgent cases researched within 30 minutes. The effort remains at the request-for-information stage, not a finalized contract. Proposed safeguards limit impersonation and off-network storage, while the article warns that combining social posts, personal records, and algorithmic tools risks sweeping political speech and associates into enforcement dossiers.
