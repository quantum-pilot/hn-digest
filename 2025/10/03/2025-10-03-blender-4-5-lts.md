# Blender 4.5 LTS

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45458791) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1036262/

### TL;DR

Blender 4.5 LTS, supported through 2027, closes the 4.x feature series with a maturing Vulkan backend, EEVEE rendering fixes, faster multithreaded texture loading, parallel shader compilation, and usability refinements. Node systems gain greater consistency, compositor access to common procedural nodes, custom mesh-normal control, and a native point-cloud object type. Vulkan approaches OpenGL parity but remains non-default because of limitations including very large meshes. Development now shifts toward Blender 5.0 and format-breaking support for larger content.
