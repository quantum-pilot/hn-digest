# I spent the day teaching seniors how to use an iPhone

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45457670) | Link: https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/i-spent-the-day-trying-to-teach-seniors-how-to-use-an-iphone-and-it-was-a-nightmare.2468117/

### TL;DR

A forum poster describes unsuccessfully configuring iPhones for older people who struggled with passcodes, biometrics, gestures, accidental touches, app clutter, and finding the phone keypad. Assistive Access did not resolve every difficulty, leading the poster to consider simpler flip phones with physical interaction. This is one caregiver's anecdote, not evidence that older adults generally cannot use smartphones. Replies offered counterexamples, setup tips, and specialized devices, while the broader discussion criticized hidden gestures, complicated onboarding, and removing physical home buttons.

### Comment pulse

- A technology librarian said missing physical buttons and gesture navigation are recurring accessibility barriers among patrons.
- Assistive Access was praised, but users reported limitations such as rotation behavior and restricted photo interaction.

### LLM perspective

- View: Accessibility fails when the simplified path is hidden, incomplete, or still assumes precise gestures and memory.
- Impact: Account security and feature accumulation can turn basic communication into dependence on a caregiver.
- Watch next: Test first-run setup with varied motor, visual, and cognitive needs, including durable physical alternatives.
