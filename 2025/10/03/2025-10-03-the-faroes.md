# The Faroes

- Score: 150 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45462297) | Link: https://photoblog.nk412.com/Faroe2025/Faroes/n-cPCNFr

### TL;DR

A travel photo essay portrays the Faroe Islands as a dramatic North Atlantic landscape of grass-covered basalt cliffs, fast-changing weather, free-roaming sheep, turf-roofed buildings, and colorful villages. It describes hiking to Kallur lighthouse on privately owned land via exposed sheep paths, often for a fee, and notes the site’s association with a James Bond film. The account is impressionistic rather than comprehensive: a commenter corrected its claim that the islands lack sandy beaches, and the author agreed to amend it.
