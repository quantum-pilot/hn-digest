# The collapse of the econ PhD job market

- Score: 146 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45464984) | Link: https://www.chrisbrunet.com/p/the-collapse-of-the-econ-phd-job

### TL;DR

The author argues that the economics PhD market is contracting sharply, citing falling Job Openings for Economists postings, fewer interview invitations, rising applicant volume, and weak tenure-track placement. They project roughly 1,000 total openings and about 400 permanent academic positions in 2025, but acknowledge extrapolation and survey gaps. Proposed causes include government and institutional hiring freezes, lower undergraduate demand, demographic decline, shrinking tech opportunities, AI automation, and lost public trust. The piece is polemical, and several causal claims extend beyond the cited market counts.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The clearest evidence concerns fewer openings; the essay’s cultural and technological explanations remain less established.
- Impact: Candidates face longer queues, weaker bargaining power, and greater need to prepare nontraditional career paths early.
