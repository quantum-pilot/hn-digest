# Digital ID – The New Chains of Capitalist Surveillance

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45458909) | Link: https://theslowburningfuse.wordpress.com/2025/09/26/digital-id-the-new-chains-of-capitalist-surveillance/

### TL;DR

This anarchist essay portrays digital identity systems as an extension of historical state and corporate control, arguing that biometric checks and digital wallets can turn access to welfare, work, banking, travel, and healthcare into monitored permissions. It cites exclusion attributed to failed Aadhaar authentication and warns that public-private infrastructure can normalize surveillance and data extraction. The piece is polemical advocacy rather than a balanced assessment: it largely rejects convenience and inclusion claims, then calls for collective resistance, mutual aid, disruption, and refusal.

### Comment pulse

- Users of European digital IDs described substantial convenience and argued that selective credentials can reveal only necessary attributes.
- Others emphasized offline access, outages, accessibility, abuse, and the danger of linking essential services to one system.

### LLM perspective

- View: Digital identity is neither inherently liberating nor oppressive; governance and fallback design determine its practical power.
- Impact: Mandatory authentication can amplify exclusion when errors, outages, or policy changes block essential services.
