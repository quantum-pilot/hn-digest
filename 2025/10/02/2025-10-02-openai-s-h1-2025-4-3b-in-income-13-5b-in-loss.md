# OpenAI's H1 2025: $4.3B in income, $13.5B in loss

- Score: 436 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45453586) | Link: https://www.techinasia.com/news/openais-revenue-rises-16-to-4-3b-in-h1-2025

### TL;DR

According to shareholder disclosures summarized by Tech in Asia, OpenAI generated $4.3 billion in first-half 2025 revenue but recorded a $13.5 billion net loss. More than half of that loss reportedly came from revaluing convertible interests, while operating losses were $7.8 billion and cash burn $2.5 billion. Costs included $6.7 billion in research and development, $2 billion in sales and advertising, and $2.5 billion in stock compensation. The company held about $17.5 billion in cash and securities at June's end.
