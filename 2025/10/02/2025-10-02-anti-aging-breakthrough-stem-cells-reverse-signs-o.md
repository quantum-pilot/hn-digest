# Anti-aging breakthrough: Stem cells reverse signs of aging in monkeys

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45454460) | Link: https://www.nad.com/news/anti-aging-breakthrough-stem-cells-reverse-signs-of-aging-in-monkeys

### TL;DR

An article summarizing a Cell study reports that aged macaques receiving engineered, FOXO3-enhanced human stem cells every two weeks for 44 weeks improved on memory tests and showed younger-looking molecular or structural markers across many tissues. Researchers also observed less senescence, inflammation, brain shrinkage, and bone loss, without serious adverse events during the study. These are primate results, not proof of human age reversal; long-term safety, including tumor risk, remains unresolved. Commenters also questioned the supplement-selling publisher’s incentives.
