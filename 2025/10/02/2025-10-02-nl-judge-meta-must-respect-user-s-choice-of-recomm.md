# NL Judge: Meta must respect user's choice of recommendation system

- Score: 311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45448326) | Link: https://www.bitsoffreedom.nl/2025/10/02/judge-in-the-bits-of-freedom-vs-meta-lawsuit-meta-must-respect-users-choice/

### TL;DR

A Dutch judge ruled in summary proceedings that Meta's Facebook and Instagram interfaces must preserve a user's choice of recommendation system rather than resetting to a profiling-based feed after navigation or restart. Digital-rights group Bits of Freedom, which brought the case under the EU Digital Services Act, says Meta hid the alternative feed and impaired access to features. The court viewed the non-persistent choice as undermining genuine autonomy. Commenters say compliance was ordered within two weeks, backed by escalating financial penalties.

### Comment pulse

- Readers characterized the remedy as simple persistence for an existing reverse-chronological option, not a ban on recommendation algorithms.
- Discussion debated whether advertising economics justify profiling or whether tracking itself is the deeper harm.

### LLM perspective

- View: A choice that software quietly resets is interface theater, not meaningful user control.
