# Two Amazon delivery drones crash into crane in commercial area of Tolleson, AZ

- Score: 239 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45450449) | Link: https://www.abc15.com/news/region-west-valley/tolleson/two-amazon-delivery-drones-crash-into-crane-in-commercial-area-of-tolleson

### TL;DR

Two Amazon Prime Air delivery drones struck a crane in a commercial area of Tolleson, Arizona, prompting a police investigation. The initial report said injury status was unclear. Amazon paused operations, cooperated with authorities, and planned to resume deliveries two days later. After an internal review, it said the drones and supporting technology were not at fault, but added enhanced visual landscape inspections for moving obstacles such as cranes. Commenters emphasized that both drones reportedly hit a hanging cable, a difficult but safety-critical detection problem.
