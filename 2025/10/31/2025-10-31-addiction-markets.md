# Addiction Markets

- Score: 131 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45774640) | Link: https://www.thebignewsletter.com/p/addiction-markets-abolish-corporate

### TL;DR

Matt Stoller argues that corporate gambling, especially app-based sports betting, converts addiction into a business model and shifts public finance toward losses borne disproportionately by vulnerable people. He traces legalization from state lotteries through the 2018 sports-betting expansion, citing debt, bankruptcy, athlete abuse, and growing public opposition. Proposed responses range from stake and advertising limits to repealing online betting. The obstacle is institutional: states now depend on gambling revenue, while operators and prediction markets contest regulatory boundaries.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The strongest case targets business incentives that maximize harmful use, not gambling as a uniformly pathological activity.
- Impact: Reform forces states to replace revenue while limiting firms whose best customers may be the least able to stop.
- Watch next: Measure repeal proposals, stake limits, VIP restrictions, advertising rules, and replacement revenue against harm outcomes.
