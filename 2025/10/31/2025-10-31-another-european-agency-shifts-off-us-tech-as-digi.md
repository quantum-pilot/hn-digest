# Another European agency shifts off US Tech as digital sovereignty gains steam

- Score: 219 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45773974) | Link: https://www.zdnet.com/article/another-european-agency-ditches-big-tech-as-digital-sovereignty-movement-gains-steam/

### TL;DR

Austria's Economy Ministry moved 1,200 employees to a Nextcloud collaboration platform hosted on Austrian infrastructure, seeking greater control over sensitive data and reduced dependence on non-European providers. The four-month migration retained Teams for external meetings and integrated Outlook calendars, making it a hybrid transition rather than a complete Microsoft exit. The article places it alongside European public-sector sovereignty efforts, while contrasting Austria's troubled 20,000-desktop LibreOffice rollout to show that open-source migration still depends on planning, compatibility, support, and user acceptance.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters framed ownership of infrastructure and data as strategic resilience against vendor or government leverage.
- Skeptics cited incumbent quality, compliance assurances, migration costs, and entrenched cloud dependence as reasons businesses remain on US platforms.
