# ICE and the Smartphone Panopticon

- Score: 211 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45767325) | Link: https://www.newyorker.com/culture/infinite-scroll/ice-and-the-smartphone-panopticon

### TL;DR

The article describes smartphones and social media as two-way surveillance infrastructure: citizens document ICE activity and use apps such as ICEBlock and Eyes Up, while ICE reportedly monitors social feeds and combines them with records and biometric data. Apple and Google removed several tracking or evidence-archiving apps after stated safety concerns and apparent government pressure. The HN discussion calls this selective platform enforcement, compares the tools with Waze police alerts, and considers PWAs or independent distribution as partial escape routes.
