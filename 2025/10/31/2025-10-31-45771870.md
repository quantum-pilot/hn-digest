# Ask HN: Who uses open LLMs and coding assistants locally? Share setup and laptop

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45771870) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45771870

### TL;DR

Local open-model coding workflows remain a hardware-and-task tradeoff rather than a single winning setup. Commenters reported GPT-OSS and Qwen coder models through Ollama, LM Studio, or llama.cpp, paired with tools such as Aider, Codex, Continue, and Zed. Apple Silicon machines with 32–64 GB memory can deliver responsive smaller quantized models, while 120B-class models often need servers and remain slow. Users value privacy, offline access, and low latency, but still prefer frontier APIs for harder work.

### Comment pulse

- Small, well-defined coding tasks drew the strongest local-model reports; large contexts and difficult reasoning exposed speed and quality gaps.
- Hardware ranged from GPU-less servers to 64 GB MacBooks, making model size and quantization central setup choices.

### LLM perspective

- View: Local coding models are most credible as bounded tools, not universal replacements for stronger hosted systems.
- Impact: Developers gain privacy and offline resilience by accepting lower capability or investing in substantial memory.
- Watch next: Compare end-to-end task success, prompt-ingestion speed, power use, and total hardware cost across setups.
