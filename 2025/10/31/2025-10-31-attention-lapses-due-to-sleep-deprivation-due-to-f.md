# Attention lapses due to sleep deprivation due to flushing fluid from brain

- Score: 495 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45771636) | Link: https://news.mit.edu/2025/your-brain-without-sleep-1029

### TL;DR

In a study of 26 volunteers tested rested and sleep-deprived, attention failures coincided with cerebrospinal-fluid movement out of the brain, followed by flow back in as attention recovered. The lapses also aligned with slower responses, missed stimuli, pupil constriction, and reduced heart and breathing rates. Researchers hypothesize that a sleep-starved brain intrudes sleep-like cleansing activity into wakefulness, trading attention for fluid flow. The observations establish coordinated timing, while the responsible circuit and causal direction remain unresolved.
