# AMD could enter ARM market with Sound Wave APU built on TSMC 3nm process

- Score: 273 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45767916) | Link: https://www.guru3d.com/story/amd-enters-arm-market-with-sound-wave-apu-built-on-tsmc-3nm-process/

### TL;DR

An unconfirmed report says AMD's “Sound Wave” could be its first ARM-based APU, targeting thin devices and possibly 2026 Surface hardware. Customs records reportedly reveal a compact BGA package, while leakers claim TSMC 3 nm production, a 5–10 W envelope, six hybrid CPU cores, four RDNA 3.5 compute units, 16 GB onboard memory, and an AI engine. Commenters note AMD already ships ARM-containing products through Xilinx, so the novelty would be an integrated ARM CPU-and-GPU client design.

### Comment pulse

- Some saw a delayed revival of AMD's canceled K12 direction; others argued Zen was the financially safer historical choice.
- Claims that ARM inherently saves power drew pushback emphasizing process technology, implementation goals, software support, and platform standardization.
