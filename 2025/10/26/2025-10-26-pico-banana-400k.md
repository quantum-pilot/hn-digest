# Pico-Banana-400k

- Score: 392 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45708524) | Link: https://github.com/apple/pico-banana-400k

### TL;DR

Apple’s Pico-Banana-400K packages about 400,000 text-guided image-editing examples derived from Open Images. Gemini 2.5 Flash generated instructions, Nano-Banana produced edits, and Gemini 2.5 Pro automatically scored compliance, realism, preservation, and technical quality. The release includes roughly 257,000 successful single-turn examples, 56,000 failures for preference learning, and 72,000 multi-turn sequences across 35 operations. Its automated, related-model evaluation may propagate model biases, and the restrictive CC BY-NC-ND license limits commercial use and derivative redistribution.
