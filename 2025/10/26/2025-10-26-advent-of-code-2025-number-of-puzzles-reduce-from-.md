# Advent of Code 2025: Number of puzzles reduce from 25 to 12 for the first time

- Score: 472 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45710006) | Link: https://adventofcode.com/2025/about#faq_num_days

### TL;DR

After ten years, Advent of Code creator Eric Wastl is shortening the annual event from 25 daily puzzle days to 12 because puzzle production consumes substantial personal time. The event still begins December 1 and releases one puzzle daily, ending mid-month; its global leaderboard is also gone after stress, infrastructure pressure, unhealthy competition, and even DDoS attacks. Hacker News mostly welcomed a finishable schedule around work and family, though some preferred alternate-day releases or argued that participants could always stop early.

### Comment pulse

- Twelve days match many participants’ real limits → later puzzles previously collided with harder problems, year-end work, and family time.
- Reduced supply is not universally better → critics valued choice and said unfinished puzzles remain available after December.
- Tradition prompted design alternatives → commenters proposed alternating releases or splitting each puzzle’s two parts across days.
