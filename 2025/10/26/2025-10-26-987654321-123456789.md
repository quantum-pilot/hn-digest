# 987654321 / 123456789

- Score: 357 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45712620) | Link: https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2025/10/26/987654321/

### TL;DR

Reversing the nonzero digits of any base produces a striking ratio: the descending-digit number divided by the ascending-digit number equals nearly the base minus two, with remainder exactly one less than the base. In decimal, 987654321/123456789 is about 8.0000000729; in hexadecimal, floating-point precision rounds the corresponding result to exactly 14. Hacker News supplied an algebraic proof, related multiplication patterns, and calculator curiosities, while debating the author’s narrower claim that executable checks complement proofs by exposing different mistakes.
