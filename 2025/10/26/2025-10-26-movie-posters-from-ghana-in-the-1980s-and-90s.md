# Movie posters from Ghana in the 1980s and 90s

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45712807) | Link: https://www.utterlyinteresting.com/post/bizarre-movie-posters-from-africa-that-are-so-bad-they-re-good

### TL;DR

The article describes hand-painted Ghanaian movie posters created for traveling VHS screenings in the late 1980s and 1990s. With imported films often lacking promotional material, artists painted durable flour sacks from limited references, prioritizing dramatic, attention-grabbing imagery over fidelity. These commercial signs later became internationally collected art, with named artists gaining recognition. The article celebrates their exaggeration and improvisation, but its “so bad they’re good” framing is contested by a Ghanaian commenter, who says realistic work was also common and garish examples receive disproportionate attention.
