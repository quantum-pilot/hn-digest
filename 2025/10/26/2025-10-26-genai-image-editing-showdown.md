### TL;DR

Despite the HN title, the supplied page benchmarks text-to-image generation, explicitly forbidding inpainting, remixing, and corrective editing. Across 15 prompt-adherence tests, it ranks OpenAI gpt-image-2 first at 12 passes, followed by gpt-image-1 at 11 and several models at 10. Hard cases combine exact text, geometry, ordering, anatomy, or unusual relationships; no model solved every task. Commenters identified the apparent wrong-tab link, questioned missing dates and hosted-model pipelines, and debated safety refusals versus enterprise requirements.
