# Way past its prime: how did Amazon get so rubbish?

- Score: 280 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45479103) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/oct/05/way-past-its-prime-how-did-amazon-get-so-rubbish

### TL;DR

Cory Doctorow uses Amazon to illustrate “enshittification”: a platform first subsidizes users, then favors business customers, and finally extracts value from both after each side becomes dependent. He argues that Prime, DRM, weakened retail alternatives, paid search placement, mandatory-feeling logistics services, merchant fees, cloning, and inadequate fraud control transformed useful commerce into costly, low-quality discovery. The piece is an explicitly political antitrust argument; its numerical claims and causal account are presented as support for stronger competition and interoperability rules.

### Comment pulse

- Some readers emphasized convenience and logistics, complicating a simple account of universal decline.
