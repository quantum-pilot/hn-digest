# The QNX Operating System

- Score: 152 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45481892) | Link: https://www.abortretry.fail/p/the-qnx-operating-system

### TL;DR

This history traces QNX from Gordon Bell and Dan Dodge's University of Waterloo influences through QUNIX, an early IBM PC microkernel real-time operating system, and its evolution into QNX. Its small kernel used message passing for services, drivers, filesystems, and network-transparent processes, supporting reliable multitasking on constrained hardware. The account covers Ontario's networked ICON school computers, later multi-architecture Neutrino releases, the abandoned Amiga partnership, and QNX's eventual automotive direction, while mixing documented history with some author judgments.
