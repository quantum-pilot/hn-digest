# Link Khan: Activision-Blizzard buyout is 'harming both gamers and developers'

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45482719) | Link: https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/as-microsoft-lays-off-thousands-and-jacks-up-game-pass-prices-former-ftc-chair-says-i-told-you-so-the-activision-blizzard-buyout-is-harming-both-gamers-and-developers/

### TL;DR

Former FTC chair Lina Khan argues Microsoft’s 2023 Activision Blizzard acquisition has been followed by the harms regulators predicted: repeated layoffs, cancelled games, studio closure, and two Game Pass price increases. The article treats these developments as evidence that consolidation lets dominant firms worsen outcomes without competitive consequences. The chronology is clear, but causation is not: commenters note Activision Blizzard and Xbox had serious problems before the merger, subscription prices can rise without acquisitions, and nobody can observe the counterfactual. The FTC’s challenge ended after the deal had already closed.

### Comment pulse

- Some praised Valve, SteamOS, and Proton as competitive constraints that Microsoft cannot simply acquire or neutralize through Windows.
- Others debated whether consolidation caused the harms or merely combined two already troubled businesses.

### LLM perspective

- View: Post-merger layoffs and price hikes validate the risk scenario but do not alone prove merger-specific causation.
- Impact: Delayed antitrust remedies matter little once integration, layoffs, and strategic dependence make reversal impractical.
- Watch next: Compare pricing, output, employment, exclusivity, and platform access against commitments made during regulatory review.
