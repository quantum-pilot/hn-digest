# Fire destroys S. Korean government's cloud storage system, no backups available

- Score: 809 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45483386) | Link: https://koreajoongangdaily.joins.com/news/2025-10-01/national/socialAffairs/NIRS-fire-destroys-governments-cloud-storage-system-no-backups-available/2412936

### TL;DR

A fire at South Korea’s National Information Resources Service damaged 96 critical systems and destroyed the government G-Drive used by roughly 750,000 civil servants. The article says its architecture lacked external backups and individually stored work files were permanently lost, with agencies attempting reconstruction from recent local copies, email, paper, and other official systems. Formal reports stored in Onnara may return after that service recovers, so the incident is not a total loss of government records. The exact extent of any same-site redundancy remains unclear in discussion.
