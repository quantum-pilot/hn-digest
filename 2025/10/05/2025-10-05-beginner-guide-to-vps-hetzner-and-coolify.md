# Beginner Guide to VPS Hetzner and Coolify

- Score: 254 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45480506) | Link: https://bhargav.dev/blog/VPS_Setup_and_Security_Checklist_A_Complete_Self_Hosting_Guide

### TL;DR

This beginner-oriented checklist walks through preparing an Ubuntu VPS: provider selection, package updates, a sudo user, SSH keys, disabling passwords and root login, UFW rules, automatic updates, application deployment, TLS, monitoring, backups, troubleshooting, and final verification. It recommends Hetzner for price and European presence and frames self-hosting as a route to operational confidence. Despite the title and introductory references, the supplied article does not actually explain installing or operating Coolify, a mismatch repeatedly highlighted by commenters who expected that comparison or setup guidance.

### Comment pulse

- Readers compared Hetzner and OVH pricing, performance, identity requirements, and potentially shared CPU capacity.
- Experienced users questioned Coolify’s reliability and backup support, often preferring Docker Compose, Traefik, and scripts.
