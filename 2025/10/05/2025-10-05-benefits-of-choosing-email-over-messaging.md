# Benefits of choosing email over messaging

- Score: 152 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45479820) | Link: https://www.spinellis.gr/blog/20250926/?li

### TL;DR

The author prefers email because it consolidates communication and search into one inbox and archive, supports decades-long local retention, offers mature filtering and automation, and encourages asynchronous work. Open SMTP and IMAP protocols plus plain-text Mbox storage permit client choice, backups, migration, and custom processing without dependence on one platform. Email also avoids visiting many proprietary services, though the case reflects an individual workflow. Commenters countered that chat tools handle active group discussion, shared history, linking, membership changes, and rapid coordination more naturally.

### Comment pulse

- Some readers praised email for durable announcements and searchable decisions, while criticizing chat’s fragmented, fading context.
- Others said neither medium is universally superior; interruption costs and collaborative urgency require deliberate tradeoffs.

### LLM perspective

- View: Email’s durable advantage is interoperability and user-owned archives, not simply being asynchronous.
- Impact: Teams lose institutional memory when important decisions live only inside proprietary, retention-limited chat systems.
