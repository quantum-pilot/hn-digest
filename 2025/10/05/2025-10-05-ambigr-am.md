# Ambigr.am

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45478780) | Link: https://ambigr.am/hall-of-fame

### TL;DR

Ambigr.am’s Hall of Fame showcases community contest winners whose lettering remains readable after rotation or reflection, sometimes transforming one word or phrase into another. The extracted page is largely an image-dependent gallery: it supplies titles, rankings, ratings, glyph mappings, creator names, and comments, but not enough visual information to judge most designs directly. Entries span themes such as fairy tales, food, mortality, and sayings, alongside leaderboards and legibility votes. The page functions as both a contest archive and a niche design community.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters shared favorite historical ambigrams and explained the concept to visitors confused by the image-driven interface.

### LLM perspective

- View: The site makes a specialized visual craft social through contests, legibility votes, and shared glyph techniques.
- Impact: Structured mappings can teach construction patterns, but text extraction cannot preserve the artwork’s central evidence.
