# Thunderscan: A clever device transforms a printer into a scanner (2004)

- Score: 128 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45472765) | Link: https://www.folklore.org/Thunderscan.html

### TL;DR

Thunderware’s 1984 Thunderscan replaced an ImageWriter ribbon cartridge with an optical sensor, reusing the printer’s precise motors to create a sub-$200 high-resolution scanner. Andy Hertzfeld’s Macintosh software improved sampling, rendered 32 light levels through error-diffusion dithering, retained five-bit grayscale data, added image adjustments and inertial scrolling, and eventually scanned bidirectionally. Full pages could take over an hour, but quality and price drove roughly 100,000 sales before flatbeds surpassed it. HN admired the hardware reuse, durable software, and modern-feeling scrolling.
