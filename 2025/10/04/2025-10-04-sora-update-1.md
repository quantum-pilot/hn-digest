# Sora Update #1

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45469437) | Link: https://blog.samaltman.com/sora-update-number-1

### TL;DR

Sam Altman says OpenAI will give character rightsholders granular controls over Sora generations, including opting out, while exploring paid video generation and revenue sharing for owners who permit fan-created uses. He acknowledges controls may initially miss prohibited cases and expects rapid iteration across products. Usage exceeded OpenAI’s expectations, with many videos reaching small audiences, increasing monetization pressure. HN interpreted the announcement skeptically, speculating that Japanese media owners prompted the change and questioning licensing, likeness coverage, compute economics, and whether small creators would participate.
