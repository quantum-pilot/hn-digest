# Circular Financing: Does Nvidia's $110B Bet Echo the Telecom Bubble?

- Score: 211 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45473033) | Link: https://tomtunguz.com/nvidia_nortel_vendor_financing_comparison/

### TL;DR

The analysis compares Nvidia’s reported $110 billion of AI investments and expanding GPU-backed debt with telecom-era vendor financing. Risks include customer concentration, collateral whose useful life may be shorter than accounting schedules, off-balance-sheet data-center vehicles, custom silicon, and uncertain monetization. Yet Nvidia differs materially from Lucent: stronger cash flow and credit, profitable hyperscaler customers, constrained rather than idle capacity, and no cited accounting fraud. The author concludes that circular financing deserves scrutiny, but current demand and counterparties make a direct replay of the telecom collapse far from certain.
