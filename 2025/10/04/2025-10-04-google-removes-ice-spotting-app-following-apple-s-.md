# Google removes ICE-spotting app following Apple's ICEBlock crackdown

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45472799) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/news/791533/google-apple-ice-tracking-app-store-red-dot-iceblock

### TL;DR

Google removed Red Dot, an Android app for anonymously reporting nearby ICE sightings, one day after Apple removed ICEBlock and similar iOS apps under Justice Department pressure. Google said it independently bans apps posing high abuse risk and removed location-sharing tools involving a “vulnerable group” after related violence; it reportedly received no DOJ warning. ICEBlock’s developer called such reporting protected speech and Apple’s action authoritarian capitulation. Red Dot combined user submissions with purportedly verified sources. The frozen reporting establishes store removal, not whether the apps caused violence.
