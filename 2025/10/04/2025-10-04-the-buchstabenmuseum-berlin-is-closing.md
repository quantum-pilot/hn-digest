# The Buchstabenmuseum Berlin is closing

- Score: 152 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45472678) | Link: https://www.buchstabenmuseum.de/en/

### TL;DR

Berlin’s Buchstabenmuseum announced its closure after 20 years, with final public opening through October 5, 2025, and an unresolved search for long-term collection storage. The museum describes itself as the first devoted to rescuing, documenting, and displaying three-dimensional lettering and signs from public space as urban history. Its final exhibition traced disappearing department-store identities through their typography. HN readers mourned a distinctive piece of Berlin’s design culture, attributed the closure to fixed costs and scarce support, and recommended related museums and technical attractions.
