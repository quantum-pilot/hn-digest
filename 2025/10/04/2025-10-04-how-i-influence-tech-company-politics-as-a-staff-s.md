# How I influence tech company politics as a staff software engineer

- Score: 256 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45473852) | Link: https://www.seangoedecke.com/how-to-influence-politics/

### TL;DR

A staff engineer argues that engineers should influence company politics without attempting executive-style scheming. The practical method is to make high-profile priorities succeed and maintain several well-developed technical proposals until organizational attention creates the right opening. Reliability incidents, performance complaints, billing concerns, or executive embarrassment generate demand for action; a prepared engineer can supply a sound project before a weak initiative fills the vacuum. This aligns technical judgment with leadership priorities, increases political capital, and treats good timing as part of senior engineering responsibility rather than passive opportunism.
