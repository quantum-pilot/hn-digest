# VST3 audio plugin format is now MIT

- Score: 615 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45678549) | Link: https://forums.steinberg.net/t/vst-3-8-0-sdk-released/1011988

### TL;DR

Steinberg released VST 3.8 under the MIT license, replacing the format's previous licensing model. The SDK also adds MIDI 2.0 interfaces, preliminary Wayland support, VSTGUI concurrency and layout features, documentation changes, examples, and validator fixes. Commenters welcomed the long-awaited opening, especially for existing plugins that may become open source, but several attributed the move to pressure from the newer CLAP format and criticized Steinberg's past control of VST. Others hope broader openness eventually reduces cross-format compatibility work.

### Comment pulse

- Open licensing wins approval → developers can integrate and redistribute the SDK with fewer legal constraints.
- CLAP is seen as competitive pressure → advocates call its design better, while others question how widespread adoption actually is.
- Workflow fragmentation persists → creators still juggle VST, AU, AAX, wrappers, rescans, and multiple DAWs.
