# Programming with Less Than Nothing

- Score: 418 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45678511) | Link: https://joshmoody.org/blog/programming-with-less-than-nothing/

### TL;DR

Josh Moody turns a FizzBuzz interview into a comic demonstration of combinatory logic, constructing booleans, numbers, arithmetic, lists, strings, and recursion from the S and K combinators. Because JavaScript eagerly evaluates the Y combinator into a stack overflow, the example moves to Skoobert, Moody's lazy experimental language. The post eventually prints FizzBuzz and explains that variable-free combinatory logic is Turing complete but extremely hard to read. It is both an educational stunt and a showcase for Skoobert.
