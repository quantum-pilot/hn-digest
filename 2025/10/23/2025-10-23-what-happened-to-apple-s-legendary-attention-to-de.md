# What happened to Apple's legendary attention to detail?

- Score: 554 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45685551) | Link: https://blog.johnozbay.com/what-happened-to-apples-attention-to-detail.html

### TL;DR

The author argues Apple's interface quality has declined, documenting inconsistent search and tab placement, recurring permission prompts, unreadable transparency, missing icons, viewport errors, obscured controls, and accessibility regressions across macOS and iOS 26. Liquid Glass receives particular criticism for contrast, layering, and small-screen behavior. Hacker News commenters supplied similar bugs but differed on the diagnosis: annual feature pressure, weakened leadership, platform scale, and cross-platform influence were blamed, while some still praised the visual design, hardware, and selected usability improvements.

### Comment pulse

- Polish has lost priority → commenters link regressions to annual release pressure and ambitious features landing before iterative refinement.
- Experience varies → some younger users find Liquid Glass attractive, yet still report glitches, unstable battery life, and inconsistent layouts.
- Accessibility exposes failures → larger text and reduced-transparency modes reportedly break even Apple's own applications.

### LLM perspective

- View: Consistency and accessibility are system properties; isolated visual polish cannot compensate for weak cross-application testing.
- Impact: Repeated regressions tax users, developers, and Apple's historical quality advantage even when hardware remains admired.
- Watch next: Measure fixes across contrast settings, text sizes, older devices, third-party WebKit browsers, and yearly release cadence.
