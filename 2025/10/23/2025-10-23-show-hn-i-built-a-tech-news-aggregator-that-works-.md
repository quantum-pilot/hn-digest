# Show HN: I built a tech news aggregator that works the way my brain does

- Score: 129 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45684689) | Link: https://deadstack.net/recent

### TL;DR

DeadStack clusters technology coverage from multiple publications, presents a short generated synopsis for each story group, and offers recent, top, yesterday, tag, social, and newsletter views. The supplied snapshot shows broad coverage but not an explanation of its implementation. Hacker News readers liked having context before leaving the site and the fully automated editorial workflow, while several found the dense summary list difficult to scan. Others wanted read-state tracking that permanently hides stories passed without engagement.

### Comment pulse

- Presentation divides readers → summaries feel fast and useful, but spacing and link styling can create a wall of text.
- Persistent read state appeals → readers want scrolling past an item to prevent it from resurfacing.
- Automation impressed commenters → the creator says o3-mini supplies the site's editorial layer without manual intervention.
