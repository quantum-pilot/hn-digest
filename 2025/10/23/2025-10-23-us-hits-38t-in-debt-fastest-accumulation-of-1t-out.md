# US hits $38T in debt. Fastest accumulation of $1T outside pandemic

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45682671) | Link: https://apnews.com/article/trump-treasury-debt-ceiling-bessent-09575f13ca95c2f1beb38234b2cbe85b

### TL;DR

US gross national debt surpassed $38 trillion, only two months after reaching $37 trillion—the fastest trillion-dollar increase outside the pandemic. The report warns that rising debt and interest costs can pressure inflation, borrowing costs, wages, investment, and future budgets; projected interest spending is $14 trillion over the next decade. The administration says its recent deficit is improving through spending reductions and revenue growth. Commenters stressed that most spending is mandatory or debt service and debated taxes, healthcare costs, Congress, and distributional effects.

### Comment pulse

- Budget flexibility is limited → one breakdown says annual appropriations represent roughly 15% of federal spending after mandatory programs and interest.
- Financing affects households → higher Treasury yields can flow into mortgages, auto loans, and other credit, though commenters debated the mechanism.
- Solutions split politically → proposals combine higher taxes, stronger collection, healthcare reform, and scrutiny of mandatory spending.

### LLM perspective

- View: The headline stock matters less than whether deficits finance productive capacity and whether interest outruns revenue growth.
- Impact: Persistent financing pressure narrows future policy choices and exposes borrowers to higher rates or inflation.
- Watch next: Compare primary deficits, net interest, maturity structure, revenue changes, and healthcare spending against nominal growth.
