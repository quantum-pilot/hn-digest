# SpaceX disables 2,500 Starlink terminals allegedly used by Asian scam centers

- Score: 224 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45680547) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2025/10/starlink-blocks-2500-dishes-allegedly-used-by-myanmars-notorious-scam-centers/

### TL;DR

SpaceX says it proactively disabled more than 2,500 Starlink kits near suspected scam centers in Myanmar, where the service is unlicensed. Reports describe compounds using trafficked workers, while Myanmar's military says a raid detained 2,198 people and seized 30 terminals. Responsibility around particular centers remains politically contested amid civil war: the junta accused Karen National Union leaders, which deny involvement. Commenters debated that context, SpaceX's delayed response, and the power inherent in remotely controlling connectivity.
