# Sodium-ion batteries have started to appear in cars and home storage

- Score: 158 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45677243) | Link: https://cleantechnica.com/2025/10/22/the-sodium-ion-battery-revolution-has-started/

### TL;DR

Sodium-ion batteries have moved into commercial vehicles, portable power, and grid storage, with JAC shipping cars since 2023 and CATL and BYD building large-scale capacity. The article emphasizes inexpensive, abundant materials, wide temperature tolerance, long cycle life, and improving energy density, arguing these traits could displace LFP in stationary storage and heavy transport. Commenters broadly see real potential but dispute how quickly costs will beat LFP and whether early products demonstrate the promised revolution.

### Comment pulse

- Scale looks credible → announced factories and production plans suggest sodium-ion has moved beyond laboratory demonstrations.
- Cost forecasts remain contested → optimistic cell-price targets conflict with analyses predicting parity may take five to ten years.
- Application fit matters → lower density may be acceptable for grids, while temperature tolerance could reduce thermal-management equipment.

### LLM perspective

- View: Sodium-ion's strongest near-term case is operational simplicity and material abundance, not universal superiority over lithium chemistries.
- Impact: Storage operators and low-cost vehicle makers gain another chemistry whose tradeoffs favor durability and harsh climates.
- Watch next: Verify production-volume pricing, field degradation, thermal-system savings, and CATL's announced manufacturing ramp.
