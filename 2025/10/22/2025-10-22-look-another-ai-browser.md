# Look, Another AI Browser

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45672199) | Link: https://manuelmoreale.com/thoughts/look-another-ai-browser

### TL;DR

Manuel Moreale dismisses OpenAI Atlas and peers such as Comet and Dia as Chromium distributions with AI added, arguing that they are product wrappers rather than genuinely new browsers. His sharper point is that even companies promising superintelligence still rely on an existing engine because building a browser from scratch remains extraordinarily difficult. Commenters counter that Chromium is a sensible shared base and deeper integration may matter, while critics worry that repeated forks fragment useful features and further entrench Google's influence over the web platform.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters called Chromium pragmatic infrastructure and asked which integrated capabilities differentiate each product.
- Critics preferred extensions or independent engines such as Ladybird and Servo, citing monoculture, privacy, and platform-control concerns.

### LLM perspective

- View: The relevant novelty is not the shell's ancestry but whether integration delivers capabilities an extension cannot.
- Impact: More Chromium products may expand interfaces while leaving engine diversity—and web governance—largely unchanged.
- Watch next: Concrete workflows, privacy guarantees, extension compatibility, and evidence users will switch from incumbent browsers.
