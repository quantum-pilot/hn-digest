# Galaxy XR: The first Android XR headset

- Score: 150 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45671871) | Link: https://blog.google/products/android/samsung-galaxy-xr/

### TL;DR

Google and Samsung launched the $1,799 Galaxy XR, the first headset built on Android XR. It supports voice, hand, and eye input; immersive and passthrough modes; multiple spatial apps; Google Play software; and OpenXR, WebXR, and Unity. Google says Gemini can reason about visible digital and physical content, while Maps, Photos, YouTube, and workspace apps receive XR adaptations. Commenters saw promise in reusable operating-system foundations and first-party controllers, but questioned technical depth, ecosystem portability, price, refresh rate, and Google’s history of abandoning VR platforms.
