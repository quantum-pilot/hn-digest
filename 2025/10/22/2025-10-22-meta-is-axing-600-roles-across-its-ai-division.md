# Meta is axing 600 roles across its AI division

- Score: 435 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45671778) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/news/804253/meta-ai-research-layoffs-fair-superintelligence

### TL;DR

Meta confirmed reporting that it plans to cut roughly 600 jobs across FAIR and its AI product and infrastructure groups while continuing to recruit for the newer TBD Lab superintelligence team. The restructuring follows Meta's $14.3 billion Scale AI investment, its hiring of Alexandr Wang, a subsequent hiring pause, and FAIR leader Joelle Pineau's departure. Wang's memo says a smaller team should require fewer conversations and give each person more scope and impact; affected employees may apply for other Meta roles.
