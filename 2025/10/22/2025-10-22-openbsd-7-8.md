# OpenBSD 7.8

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45664147) | Link: https://cdn.openbsd.org/pub/OpenBSD/7.8/ANNOUNCEMENT

### TL;DR

OpenBSD 7.8, the project's 59th release, expands platform, security, networking, virtualization, and hardware support. Highlights include Raspberry Pi 5 enablement, parallel TCP processing across up to eight threads, AMD SEV-ES confidential guests, new Qualcomm graphics drivers, improved suspend behavior, broader Ethernet and Wi-Fi support, and new `watch`, LLDP, and BPF logging utilities. The release also updates LibreSSL and OpenSSH, strengthens numerous pledge and validation paths, and delivers extensive fixes throughout the kernel, userland, networking stack, installer, and packages.
