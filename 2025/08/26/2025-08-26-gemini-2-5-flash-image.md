# Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

- Score: 1093 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45026719) | Link: https://developers.googleblog.com/en/introducing-gemini-2-5-flash-image/

### TL;DR

Google introduced Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, or “nano-banana,” as a preview model for image generation and editing through the Gemini API, AI Studio, and Vertex AI. It targets consistent subjects across edits, natural-language local changes, visual-template adherence, world-aware generation, and fusion of multiple input images. Google prices each 1,290-token image at about $0.039 and applies invisible SynthID watermarks. The company acknowledges remaining weaknesses in long text, fine factual detail, and consistency. Commenters praised editing progress but reported rerolls, altered product details, refusals, and restoration artifacts.
