# Will Smith's concert crowds are real, but AI is blurring the lines

- Score: 364 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45022184) | Link: https://waxy.org/2025/08/will-smiths-concert-crowds-were-real-but-ai-is-blurring-the-lines/

### TL;DR

Andy Baio investigated claims that Will Smith fabricated concert crowds and found the fans, signs, and performances trace back to real European tour photos and footage. The apparent fakery arose in two transformations: Smith’s team seemingly animated still crowd photos with an image-to-video model, producing malformed faces and text, while YouTube separately applied an undisclosed Shorts enhancement experiment that sharpened and smeared the montage further. Commenters stressed that the first step caused the worst artifacts, criticized unwanted platform processing, and questioned whether photographed attendees consented to synthetic animation.
