# Fuck up my site – Turn any website into beautiful chaos

- Score: 329 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45057020) | Link: https://www.fuckupmysite.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnews.ycombinator.com&torchCursor=true&comicSans=true&fakeCursors=true&peskyFly=true

### TL;DR

Fuck Up My Site is a parody tool that proxies a chosen website and applies configurable visual sabotage: a torch cursor, Comic Sans, fake cursor trails, a buzzing fly, runaway buttons, and popup whack-a-mole. Its disclaimer says effects are temporary, personal data is neither stored nor transmitted, sensitive information must never be entered, and financial, healthcare, government, and banking sites are blocked. Despite those warnings, commenters report Google Safe Browsing, Firefox, Safari, Chrome, and OpenDNS flagging or blocking it, apparently because the simulated popups resemble deceptive behavior.
