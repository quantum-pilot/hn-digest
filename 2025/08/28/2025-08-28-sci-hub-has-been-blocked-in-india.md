# Sci-Hub has been blocked in India

- Score: 285 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45048473) | Link: https://sci-hub.se/sci-hub-blocked-india

### TL;DR

In Sci-Hub founder Alexandra Elbakyan’s advocacy account, a New Delhi court ordered Indian telecom companies to block Sci-Hub and newer project Sci-Net after complaints from Elsevier, Wiley, and the American Chemical Society. She says a related case begun in 2020 remains unresolved, Sci-Hub paused new papers after a 2021 interim order, and two-factor authentication later impeded acquisition. Sci-Net launched in 2025 as a community-upload system. The article argues the block harms access and national interests; those legal and human-rights conclusions are the author’s contested position.
