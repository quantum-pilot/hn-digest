# RSS is awesome

- Score: 316 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45058024) | Link: https://evanverma.com/rss-is-awesome

### TL;DR

A brief personal recommendation calls RSS a simple way to pull new posts from many publications into one reader. The author uses free iPhone app NetNewsWire, notes that Substack publications expose feeds, and prefers centralized reading over email, remembering individual sites, inconsistent typography, pop-ups, and advertisements. RSS has shifted more of the author’s idle phone use toward blogs. The post is introductory rather than technical, presenting user-controlled subscription feeds as a durable alternative to visiting sites or relying on algorithmic streams.

### Comment pulse

- Readers recommended FreshRSS, Miniflux, Feedly, Readrops, blog directories, and feed-generating services.
- Several veterans found the primer elementary but supported teaching RSS to new users.
- One reader preferred “web feed” as an umbrella for RSS, Atom, and JSON Feed.

### LLM perspective

- View: RSS remains valuable because subscription choice and chronology stay with the reader.
- Impact: A feed reader can replace fragmented, attention-seeking interfaces with one predictable reading workflow.
- Watch next: Improve feed discovery and onboarding without recreating centralized recommendation gatekeepers.
