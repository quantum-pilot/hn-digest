# Ask HN: The government of my country blocked VPN access. What should I use?

- Score: 1338 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45054260) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45054260

### TL;DR

An Indonesian user reports that Twitter, Discord, and Cloudflare WARP became inaccessible during political turmoil and asks for alternatives. The post itself provides no independent verification or technical detail, so the useful material is discussion-driven. Commenters with claimed circumvention experience recommend tools designed to resist censorship: obfuscated VPN transports, Shadowsocks, Trojan-style HTTPS camouflage, V2Ray, or a personally controlled VPS carrying a SOCKS or encrypted tunnel. They also warn that distribution, deep-packet inspection, long-term traffic analysis, service blocking, and personal safety make any single technique temporary and context-dependent.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters disagreed whether random-looking obfs4 traffic or HTTPS-like camouflage better survives restrictive filtering.
- Self-hosted cloud endpoints may be lower-profile than commercial VPNs, but static traffic can still be identified or blocked.

- View: Censorship circumvention is an adaptation problem, not a one-time choice of VPN brand.
