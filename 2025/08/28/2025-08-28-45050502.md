# Petition to stop Google from restricting sideloading and FOSS apps

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45050502) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45050502

### TL;DR

A developer is circulating a petition opposing Google’s plan to require verified developer identities before apps can be installed on Android devices. The short post argues that submitting government identification to distribute software outside Google Play threatens independent developers, free-software contributors, and users who want direct installation. It offers no evidence that the petition can change policy. Discussion largely focused on tactics: critics called online petitions low-effort substitutes for legislation, public comments, or coordinated letters from prominent developers, while supporters argued backlash can still raise an issue’s profile.

### Comment pulse
