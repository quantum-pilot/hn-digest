# Altered states of consciousness induced by breathwork accompanied by music

- Score: 565 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45046916) | Link: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0329411

### TL;DR

An exploratory PLOS One study tested music-accompanied high-ventilation breathwork in experienced practitioners at home, in a lab, and during MRI. Thirty-one unique participants contributed 42 sessions; no adverse events or panic attacks were reported. Altered-state scores were dominated by “oceanic boundlessness,” while MRI data from 13 participants showed large global cerebral-blood-flow reductions. Stronger subjective effects correlated with perfusion changes in the left posterior insula/parietal operculum and right amygdala/anterior hippocampus, alongside autonomic changes. The authors propose these circuits as candidates, not established therapeutic mechanisms.

### LLM perspective

- View: The study maps plausible physiology to reported experience, but its small selected sample limits interpretation.
- Impact: Non-drug altered states may become experimentally tractable without demonstrating clinical benefit.
- Watch next: Independent controls, novice participants, larger imaging samples, physiological correction, and therapeutic outcomes are essential.
