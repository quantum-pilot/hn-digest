# AI adoption linked to 13% decline in jobs for young U.S. workers: study

- Score: 443 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45052423) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/28/generative-ai-reshapes-us-job-market-stanford-study-shows-entry-level-young-workers.html

### TL;DR

A non-peer-reviewed Stanford paper analyzing ADP payroll records reports a 13% relative employment decline since 2022 for workers aged 22–25 in occupations highly exposed to generative AI, including customer service, accounting, and software development. Older workers in those fields and workers in less-exposed roles generally held steady or grew. Researchers say they tested education, remote work, outsourcing, and broader economic changes as alternative explanations. They associate declines mainly with automating uses of AI; occupations where AI complements workers showed more muted employment changes.
