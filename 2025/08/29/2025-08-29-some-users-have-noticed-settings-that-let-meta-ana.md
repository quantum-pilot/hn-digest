# Some users have noticed settings that let Meta analyze and retain phone photos

- Score: 535 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45062910) | Link: https://www.zdnet.com/article/meta-might-be-secretly-scanning-your-phones-camera-roll-how-to-check-and-turn-it-off/

### TL;DR

Facebook is testing opt-in camera-roll suggestions in the US and Canada, excluding Illinois and Texas. One setting uses favorites and dates; another continuously uploads newer media for cloud analysis of people, objects, places, and themes to create recaps, collages, or AI edits. Meta says suggestions stay private unless shared, are not used for ad targeting, and require users to tap Allow and accept AI terms. Some users nevertheless found enabled-looking controls without remembering consent, making the dispute chiefly about consent visibility and comprehension.

### Comment pulse

- Readers wanted operating-system audit logs showing when apps access or upload photos.
- Several favored limited photo pickers over granting an app continuing library-wide access.

### LLM perspective
