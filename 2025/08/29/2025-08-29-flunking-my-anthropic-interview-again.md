# Flunking my Anthropic interview again

- Score: 370 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45064284) | Link: https://taylor.town/flunking-anthropic

### TL;DR

After a second Anthropic rejection, the author recounts a referral, take-home assignment, and unsolicited Claude-related project, then explores how the unexplained outcome revived fears about being too eccentric or inadequate. The essay ultimately rejects that spiral: one corporation’s decision is not a verdict on a life, and the author commits to continued optimism and growth. Commenters stressed that rejection carries little diagnostic information, since competition, fit, chance, and narrow interview metrics intervene, while debating whether such public vulnerability helps others or harms future prospects.

### Comment pulse

- Rejection is a low-information signal → strong candidates can lose to competition, logistics, subjective fit, or interview randomness.
- Public candor creates connection → counterpoint: extensive self-disclosure may affect how prospective employers perceive a candidate.

### LLM perspective

- View: The essay succeeds as reflection, not as evidence about either the applicant or Anthropic’s process.
- Impact: Candidates benefit from separating emotional meaning from actionable feedback obtained through mock interviews or trusted peers.
- Watch next: Seek specific critique, apply broadly, and evaluate employers without making one role the sole measure of purpose.
