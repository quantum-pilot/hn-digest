# Sig Sauer citing national security to keep documents from public

- Score: 291 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45063431) | Link: https://practicalshootinginsights.com/eighth-circuit-fmeca-update/

### TL;DR

An independent firearms-analysis site describes an appeal over sealed Army records in Glasscock v. Sig Sauer, where The Trace seeks limited intervention to oppose secrecy around a P320 failure analysis. According to the report, the district court cited the analysis repeatedly, while Sig Sauer asks courts to defer to the Army and invokes military secrecy, national security, and controlled technical information. The Trace argues a spreadsheet is already public and unclassified; a newly disclosed memorandum creates additional unresolved questions. These remain claims in active litigation.
