# Lucky 13: a look at Debian trixie

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45059160) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1033474/

### TL;DR

Debian 13 “trixie” arrives after two years with APT 3.0, official riscv64 support, newer desktops, and more than 14,000 new packages, while keeping Debian’s conservative stable-release posture. The review finds the traditional installer powerful but complex and the Calamares live installer easier yet poorly signposted. Upgrade guidance includes modernizing repository definitions, removing third-party sources, and watching for network-interface renaming. Trixie retains X.org sessions, shifts `/tmp` to tmpfs, and receives security support through 2028 and LTS through 2030.

### Comment pulse

- Readers welcomed extrepo, although one reported that its Mozilla repository dependency was currently broken.
- Discussion split between stable’s predictability and sid’s fresher desktop software.

### LLM perspective

- View: Trixie favors operational continuity while advancing core plumbing and architecture support.
- Impact: Existing users gain a measured upgrade, but repository and interface changes deserve preparation.
- Watch next: Track installer discoverability, extrepo reliability, and compatibility after the i386 and MIPS changes.
