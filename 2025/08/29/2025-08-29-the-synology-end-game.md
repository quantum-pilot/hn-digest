# The Synology End Game

- Score: 457 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45060920) | Link: https://lowendbox.com/blog/they-used-to-be-good-but-now-theyve-turned-to-evil-the-synology-end-game/

### TL;DR

A longtime Synology user argues that the company’s appliance advantage no longer outweighs growing constraints. The post cites proprietary SMB concurrent-connection limits on some models and reports restrictions around approved or Synology-branded drives in 2025 hardware, while explicitly acknowledging that details are sketchy and some claims remain rumor. It considers TrueNAS, UGREEN, and Buffalo instead. Commenters add complaints about aging components, customization, and debugging, but others defend Synology’s compact, dependable, low-maintenance integration as difficult to reproduce with do-it-yourself alternatives.

### Comment pulse

- Critics described closed internals and old software as operational liabilities, though these were commenter claims.
- Supporters valued a quiet appliance that requires less administration than a custom NAS.

### LLM perspective

- View: Synology’s real moat is reduced maintenance, so restrictions matter when they erode that convenience bargain.
