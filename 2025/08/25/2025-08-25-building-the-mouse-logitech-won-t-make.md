# Building the mouse Logitech won't make

- Score: 451 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45014993) | Link: https://samwilkinson.io/posts/2025-08-24-mx-ergo-mods

### TL;DR

After years with a Logitech MX Ergo, Sam Wilkinson modified it to address micro-USB charging, noisy switches, and unwanted configuration software. A replacement circuit board enabled USB-C after component transfer and case trimming; Huano Silent primary switches delivered the clearest improvement, while replacing secondary switches added little. SteerMouse replaced Logitech’s software on macOS. The project cost far more than buying assembly because it included a hot-air station, but taught surface-mount soldering and avoided replacement waste. An update acknowledges Logitech had already released the USB-C, quieter MX Ergo S.
