# Google will allow only apps from verified developers to be installed on Android

- Score: 3050 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45017028) | Link: https://9to5google.com/2025/08/25/android-apps-developer-verification/

### TL;DR

Google plans to require apps installed on certified Android devices to be registered by verified developers, regardless of Play Store, third-party store, or direct APK distribution. Google says verification confirms identity rather than reviewing app content, aiming to deter repeat malware and impersonation while preserving distribution choice. A separate console and hobbyist workflow are planned. Access begins in October 2025, opens broadly in March 2026, becomes mandatory in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand in September 2026, and expands globally in 2027. Critical implementation and bypass details remain unclear.

### Comment pulse

- Critics saw centralized identity approval as a threat to private, independent, and adversarial app development.
- Play Integrity and dependence on certified Google devices were treated as broader ownership problems.
- Supporters argued registered identities and package keys can deter impersonation without requiring content review.
