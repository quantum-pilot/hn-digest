# Temporary suspension of acceptance of mail to the United States

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45016517) | Link: https://www.post.japanpost.jp/int/information/2025/0825_01_en.html

### TL;DR

Japan Post temporarily stopped accepting certain goods shipments to the United States from August 27, 2025, ahead of the August 29 end of de minimis treatment. It says new U.S. rules require transport operators to deposit tariffs and prepare customs declarations, but implementation procedures remained unclear. The suspension covers small packets, parcels, and goods EMS containing commercial goods or individual gifts worth more than $100. Documents and lower-value personal gifts remain accepted, while the compliant UGX courier service is offered as an alternative.
