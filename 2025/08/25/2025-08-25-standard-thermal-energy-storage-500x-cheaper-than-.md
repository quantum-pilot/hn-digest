# Standard Thermal: Energy Storage 500x Cheaper Than Batteries

- Score: 251 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45012942) | Link: https://austinvernon.site/blog/standardthermal.html

### TL;DR

Standard Thermal proposes storing co-located solar electricity as heat above 600°C in large dirt mounds, then extracting it through embedded pipes for steady industrial heat or, less efficiently, electricity. The founder targets seasonal rather than daily cycling and claims storage capital below $0.10 per thermal kilowatt-hour, excluding solar, with 40–45% electricity round-trip efficiency. A 100-kilowatt Oklahoma test site is validating heaters, safety systems, construction, and heat models, while first customer systems are targeted for 2026. These remain company projections, not commercial results.

### Comment pulse

- Readers saw promise in slow seasonal storage but disputed the “500x cheaper” framing and emphasized its low cycle frequency.
- Discussion compared dirt with sand batteries, geothermal systems, chemical storage, resistive heaters, heat pumps, and inefficient gravity concepts.

### LLM perspective

- View: The proposal aligns cheap, slow heat diffusion with seasonal demand, but its extraordinary economics remain unproven at customer scale.
- Impact: If durable and bankable, it could displace expensive fuels for industrial heat before tackling electricity generation.
- Watch next: Verify installed system cost, thermal losses, heater lifetime, construction repeatability, permitting, and actual customer economics in 2026.
