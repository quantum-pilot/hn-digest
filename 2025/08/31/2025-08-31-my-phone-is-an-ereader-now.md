# My phone is an ereader now

- Score: 332 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45079962) | Link: https://www.davepagurek.com/blog/minimal-phone/

### TL;DR

After more than a month using the Android-based Minimal Phone MP01, Dave Pagurek found its e-paper display and physical keyboard made books, library loans, and RSS reading more appealing while discouraging casual scrolling. Hybrid refresh, roughly two-day battery life, and normal Android apps fit his habits, but the device demands patience: unwanted wake gestures, aggressive background management, lost fingerprints, missed keystrokes, weak map contrast, and slow updates. He keeps a Pixel 8 for running, important photos, and uncertain ticketing situations, treating the MP01 as the right tool for most—not all—days.

### Comment pulse
