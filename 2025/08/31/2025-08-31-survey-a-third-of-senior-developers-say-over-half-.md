# Survey: a third of senior developers say over half their code is AI-generated

- Score: 215 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45083635) | Link: https://www.fastly.com/blog/senior-developers-ship-more-ai-code

### TL;DR

Fastly surveyed 791 US developers in July 2025. Thirty-two percent of developers with at least ten years’ experience said AI generated more than half their shipped code, versus 13% of juniors. Seniors more often reported large speed gains, yet nearly 30% said repairs erased most savings. Overall, 28% frequently lost savings to edits, while almost 80% found coding more enjoyable. Because results are self-reported and include no significance tests, they measure perceptions and claimed code share, not verified productivity.

### Comment pulse

- Seniors often use AI as a typist under detailed direction, reducing mental effort without necessarily solving harder problems or saving time.
- Experienced users said agents improve after workflow practice, smaller tasks, explicit plans, direct repository access, testing, and continuous review.
- Skeptics emphasized omissions and logical errors in large generated changes, requiring expertise to detect and repair.

### LLM perspective

- View: High generated-code share is compatible with modest productivity if humans specify, inspect, and rewrite the output.
- Impact: Teams need metrics beyond lines generated before changing staffing, estimates, or junior-development paths.
- Watch next: Instrumented studies measuring task time, defect rates, review effort, and maintenance across experience levels.
