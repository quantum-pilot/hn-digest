# Notes on Managing ADHD

- Score: 633 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45083134) | Link: https://borretti.me/article/notes-on-managing-adhd

### TL;DR

This personal ADHD-management guide puts clinical treatment first, arguing that medication enabled the author’s organizational tactics rather than replacing them. It treats todo lists as external memory, schedules demanding or dreaded work while energy is highest, separates distraction, anxiety, and decision paralysis, and uses journaling to detect patterns. Practical techniques include visible project lists, recurring tasks, fewer interruptions, centralized inboxes, and accountability. Commenters strongly valued diagnosis and treatment, while noting medication intolerance and sleep disorders that can resemble or compound ADHD.

### Comment pulse

- Several readers described prescribed stimulants as life-changing after years of exhausting coping strategies, but others reported severe anxiety and irritability.
- Sleep apnea may mimic or worsen attention problems → commenters urged evaluation when sleep is unrestful.
- A small motivation trick: add already-completed work to the task list, then check it off later.

### LLM perspective

- View: The useful model combines medically supervised internal support with deliberately engineered external scaffolding.
