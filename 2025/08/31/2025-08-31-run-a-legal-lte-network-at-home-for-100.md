# Run a legal LTE network at home for $100

- Score: 166 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45082155) | Link: https://lantian.pub/en/article/modify-computer/legal-lte-network-at-home-for-100-bucks.lantian/

### TL;DR

Lan Tian assembled a private US LTE network for $102.93 using a $61.30 retired FreedomFi indoor CBRS base station, $38.99 programmable SIM kit, existing virtualization hardware, and one month of Google SAS service. The setup unlocks the radio through TR-069, runs a Magma core and access gateway, obtains dynamic CBRS authorization, programs subscriber credentials, and registers a data plan. An iPhone reached 100 Mbps down and 10 Mbps up; one tested Motorola could see but not join the network.
