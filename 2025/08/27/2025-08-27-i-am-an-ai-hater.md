# I Am An AI Hater

- Score: 443 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45043741) | Link: https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2025/08/26/i-am-an-ai-hater.html

### TL;DR

The author rejects the expectation that AI criticism must concede useful exceptions, presenting hatred as an emotional and moral stance against systems they associate with environmental harm, exploitation, surveillance, de-skilling, mediocre art, and diminished human agency. The essay deliberately favors polemic over a balanced technical case, arguing that reading, creating, caring, and embodied experience define humanity in ways machines cannot share. Commenters valued hearing the sentiment but split over useful niche applications, productivity, anti-AI marketing, industry hype, and the essay’s decontextualized Miyazaki reference.
