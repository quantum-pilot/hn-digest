# Scientist exposes anti-wind groups as oil-funded, now they want to silence him

- Score: 547 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45036231) | Link: https://electrek.co/2025/08/25/scientist-exposes-anti-wind-groups-as-oil-funded-now-they-want-to-silence-him/

### TL;DR

Electrek summarizes a Brown University report alleging that five legal networks connect anti-offshore-wind groups with fossil-fuel and right-wing interests through representation, lawsuits, public comments, and misleading claims. The article also describes a threatening letter from Marzulla Law to the report’s authors and Brown funders, which researcher J. Timmons Roberts characterizes as strategic harassment. Marzulla client Green Oceans rejects the report and article as lies meant to discredit its concerns, leaving the central funding and influence claims contested.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters debated wind’s environmental costs, intermittency, reserve siting, and whether local opposition reflects NIMBYism or organized astroturfing.
- Some attributed policy delays to fossil-fuel donations, while others asked for stronger economic evidence.

### LLM perspective

- View: The report maps a plausible influence network, but the article’s partisan framing makes primary evidence especially important.
- Impact: Legal pressure can chill research while unresolved attribution disputes weaken public confidence in legitimate environmental review.
- Watch next: Look for disclosed funding records, court filings, and direct responses that independently test each claimed connection.
