# Is it possible to allow sideloading and keep users safe?

- Score: 205 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45073902) | Link: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2025/08/is-it-possible-to-allow-sideloading-and-keep-users-safe/

### TL;DR

Terence Eden examines the collision between device-owner freedom and protection from malicious Android apps. Google plans to require apps installed on certified devices in selected countries to be signed by developers who registered legal details, citing far more malware outside Play. Eden accepts that warnings and education often fail against scams, and that banks may rationally reject risky devices. Yet he sees mandatory Google registration as a gatekeeping power vulnerable to account errors, policy changes, stolen identities, and future abuse, and reaches no satisfying solution.
