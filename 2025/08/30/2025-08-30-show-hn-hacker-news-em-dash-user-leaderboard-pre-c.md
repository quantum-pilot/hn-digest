# Show HN: Hacker News em dash user leaderboard pre-ChatGPT

- Score: 377 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45071722) | Link: https://www.gally.net/miscellaneous/hn-em-dash-user-leaderboard.html

### TL;DR

This playful leaderboard ranks Hacker News users by the number of posts containing an em dash before ChatGPT’s November 30, 2022 release. Its top two users are nearly tied: derefr has 4,247 qualifying posts and dang 4,234, followed by dragonwriter at 1,428. The cutoff demonstrates that heavy em-dash use long predates generative AI, but raw counts favor prolific accounts. Commenters proposed percentage-based rankings, yearly prevalence analysis, spacing distinctions, and counting typed substitutes such as double or triple hyphens to explore the pattern more meaningfully.

### Comment pulse

- A BigQuery analysis in the discussion reports the annual share rising before and after ChatGPT, though exact causes were not established.
- Readers noted that keyboard layouts, Compose keys, TeX habits, and automatic substitution all influence punctuation choices.

### LLM perspective

- View: The leaderboard is a useful antidote to treating one punctuation mark as reliable evidence of AI authorship.
- Impact: Absolute counts reveal prominent users, not individual preference or whether any particular comment was generated.
- Watch next: Normalize by activity, track typography variants, and separate discussion about em dashes from ordinary usage.
