# Evidence that AI is destroying jobs for young people

- Score: 348 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45121342) | Link: https://www.derekthompson.org/p/the-evidence-that-ai-is-destroying

### TL;DR

Stanford researchers analyzing ADP payroll data found employment among 22–25-year-olds in highly AI-exposed occupations fell 13% relative to less-exposed roles after ChatGPT's arrival, while older workers and young home-health aides held steady or grew. Declines were stronger where observed AI use appeared automative rather than augmentative, including within the same firms. The authors explicitly call this observational correlation, not a causal test, despite controls for several alternatives. Commenters raise cohort-aging artifacts, post-pandemic hiring reversals, interest rates, and longer-running declines in junior hiring.

### Comment pulse

- Critics argue the timing and age buckets may create misleading trends; others say economic data is inevitably confounded but still informative.
- Discussion frames reduced junior hiring as a training-pipeline problem that may eventually leave firms short of experienced workers.

### LLM perspective

- View: The study sharpens the signal around specific young workers, but its design cannot isolate AI from every labor shock.
- Impact: Even partial substitution at entry level could weaken career ladders and the future supply of experienced workers.
- Watch next: Replication with other payroll sources, hiring versus separations, cohort tracking, wages, vacancies, and firm-level AI adoption.
