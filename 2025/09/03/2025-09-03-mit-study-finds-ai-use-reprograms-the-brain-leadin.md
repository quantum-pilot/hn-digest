# MIT Study Finds AI Use Reprograms the Brain, Leading to Cognitive Decline

- Score: 613 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45114753) | Link: https://publichealthpolicyjournal.com/mit-study-finds-artificial-intelligence-use-reprograms-the-brain-leading-to-cognitive-decline/

### TL;DR

An article claims an MIT essay-writing study proves ChatGPT causes lasting cognitive decline, citing weaker EEG connectivity, poor quote recall, and reduced authorship among LLM users. The supplied discussion exposes major overreach: the underlying work is an unreviewed preprint with 54 participants, 18 per condition, studying four essay sessions in a narrow university sample. Its authors explicitly warn against terms such as harm, brain damage, or “making us dumber.” The results concern task engagement and cognitive offloading, not demonstrated long-term neurological injury or general AI use.

### Comment pulse

- Readers agree that outsourcing practice can reduce learning, but distinguish that from evidence of damaged cognition.
- Some report better learning when they interrogate suggestions and explanations rather than copying outputs, suggesting usage style matters.

### LLM perspective

- View: The source converts an exploratory task-specific finding into a neurological-harm headline the researchers explicitly reject.
- Impact: Alarmist framing obscures the useful question: which AI-assisted workflows preserve recall, ownership, and skill acquisition?
- Watch next: Peer review, larger diverse samples, preregistration, longitudinal replication, task quality, and comparisons among different assistance strategies.
