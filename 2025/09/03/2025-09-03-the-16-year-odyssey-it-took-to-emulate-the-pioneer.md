# The 16-year odyssey it took to emulate the Pioneer LaserActive

- Score: 305 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45114003) | Link: https://www.readonlymemo.com/this-is-the-first-the-16-year-odyssey-of-time-money-wrong-turns-and-frustration-it-took-to-finally-emulate-the-pioneer-laseractive/

### TL;DR

Emulating Pioneer’s 1993 LaserActive took Nemesis 16 years because its modular console mixed comparatively tractable digital hardware with unusually demanding analog LaserDisc behavior. Games depended on rapid seeking, reverse playback, interleaved fields, VBI control codes, and mixed-mode data that normal video capture discarded. After documenting registers, building dumping tools, repairing failure-prone hardware, and extending the Domesday Duplicator and ld-decode workflow, Nemesis finally integrated the system into Ares in 2025, preserving both released games and prototypes.
