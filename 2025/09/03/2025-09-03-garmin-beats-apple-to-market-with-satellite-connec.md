# Garmin beats Apple to market with satellite-connected smartwatch

- Score: 245 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45118286) | Link: https://www.macrumors.com/2025/09/03/garmin-satellite-smartwatch/

### TL;DR

Garmin's Fenix 8 Pro brings inReach satellite and LTE connectivity to a smartwatch, enabling location check-ins, text messages, cellular calls, short voice messages, tracking links, weather, and SOS coordination through Garmin Response. The AMOLED version starts at $1,200 with up to 27 days in smartwatch mode; a 4,500-nit microLED model costs $2,000 and claims up to 10 days. Satellite plans start at $7.99 monthly with some usage fees. The article's comparison to an unreleased satellite Apple Watch was based on rumors at publication.

### Comment pulse

- Outdoor users value SOS without a phone, durability, physical buttons, long battery life, maps, and accessible local activity files.
- Critics question price, subscription fees, microLED battery tradeoffs, software polish, and whether Garmin can match Apple's iPhone integration.
