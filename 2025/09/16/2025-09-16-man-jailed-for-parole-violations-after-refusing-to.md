# Man jailed for parole violations after refusing to decrypt his Tor node

- Score: 985 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45261163) | Link: https://reddit.com/r/TOR/comments/1ni5drm/the_fbi_couldnt_get_my_husband_to_decrypt_his_tor/

### TL;DR

A Reddit post by Conrad Rockenhaus's wife alleges federal retaliation after the former Tor operator refused to decrypt relay traffic, claiming officials misrepresented SPICE virtualization software and later used fraudulent warrants while denying medical care. HN commenters reviewing the supplied transcript and parole warrant offered a sharply different account: supervision required monitored devices, while alleged violations included cannabis use, missed restitution cooperation, lost probation contact, new credit, and an unauthorized phone. They also said his underlying conviction involved deliberately disrupting a former employer's systems. The competing narratives remain unresolved here.

### Comment pulse
