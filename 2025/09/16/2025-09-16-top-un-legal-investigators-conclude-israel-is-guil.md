# Top UN legal investigators conclude Israel is guilty of genocide in Gaza

- Score: 1311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45259553) | Link: https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/un-concludes-israel-guilty-genocide-gaza

### TL;DR

An independent UN Human Rights Council commission concluded in a 72-page report that Israel committed genocide against Palestinians in Gaza from October 2023 through July 2025. It found four prohibited acts and said statements plus patterns of killing, deprivation, healthcare destruction, cultural damage, sexual violence, and harm to children established genocidal intent. The commission urged arms-transfer suspensions and sanctions. This is an investigative commission’s legal finding, not a final International Court of Justice judgment; the separate South Africa case remains pending and may take years.
