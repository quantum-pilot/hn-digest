# Things you can do with a Software Defined Radio (2024)

- Score: 938 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45262835) | Link: https://blinry.org/50-things-with-sdr/

### TL;DR

Using a roughly $30 RTL-SDR Blog V4 dongle, an antenna kit, wire, and mostly Linux software, the author spent a week exploring fifty receive-only radio projects. Examples include FM and digital radio, aircraft and ship tracking, weather stations and balloons, satellite images, amateur packet and Morse transmissions, utility meters, pagers, time signals, weather fax, LoRaWAN, and unexplained signals. The approachable diary also covers antenna sizing, frequencies, modulation, software, failed decodes, and German restrictions on listening to nonpublic communications.
