# Robert Redford has died

- Score: 458 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45261159) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2025/09/16/movies/robert-redford-dead.html

### TL;DR

Robert Redford died in his sleep at his Utah home at 89, according to his publicist, who gave no specific cause. His career spanned star-making performances in Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid, The Sting, and All the President’s Men; an Oscar-winning directing debut with Ordinary People; and decades of environmental advocacy. His largest cultural legacy may be the Sundance Institute and festival, which cultivated independent filmmakers and documentaries outside Hollywood, even as Redford later criticized the festival’s commercialization.
