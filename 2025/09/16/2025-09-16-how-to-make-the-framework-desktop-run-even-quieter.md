# How to make the Framework Desktop run even quieter

- Score: 334 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45266039) | Link: https://noctua.at/en/how-to-make-the-framework-desktop-run-even-quieter

### TL;DR

Noctua redesigned the Framework Desktop's side-panel grille and funnel-shaped fan duct, reporting roughly 7dB(A) less noise at 50% fan speed and up to 5dB(A) less at higher speeds at equal APU temperature. The parts are not planned for production, but CAD files are available for home printing. Adding an 80mm exhaust fan slightly reduced temperature while increasing noise; swapping to the quieter 1,800RPM G2 fan saved 1–1.5dB but sacrificed worst-case headroom versus the supplied 2,400RPM model. Commenters discussed alternatives and safety constraints.
