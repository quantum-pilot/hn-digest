# U.S. investors, Trump close in on TikTok deal with China

- Score: 400 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45267643) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/tech/details-emerge-on-u-s-china-tiktok-deal-594e009f

### TL;DR

US and Chinese negotiators are reportedly finalizing a framework that would place TikTok’s American operation in a new entity roughly 80% owned by US investors and under an American-majority board, including one government-designated member. Oracle, Silver Lake, Andreessen Horowitz, and existing ByteDance investors would participate; Chinese shareholders would retain just under 20%. US users would migrate to a new app whose recommendation system would be rebuilt by American engineers using licensed ByteDance technology, while Oracle hosts data. Final terms remain unsettled and subject to scrutiny.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters question the government-appointed director, Oracle’s privileged role, and whether ownership changes actually resolve influence concerns.
- Others worry a US-only app could fragment creators and audiences or substitute American political control for Chinese control.
