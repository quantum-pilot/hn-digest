# Seoul says US must fix its visa system if it wants Korea's investments

- Score: 280 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45206805) | Link: https://english.hani.co.kr/arti/english_edition/e_international/1218025.html

### TL;DR

South Korea said it would seek U.S. visa reforms and safeguards after Korean citizens were arrested at a Georgia battery-plant raid. Officials expressed strong regret, invoked mutual trust, and argued that further Korean investment depends on resolving access for skilled workers, including a long-sought E-4 quota. Progressive lawmakers also demanded an apology over detainees being shackled. Commenters broadly saw the spectacle as diplomatically and economically damaging, while some accounts alleged that at least some contractors had performed work outside their visas’ setup, repair, or training terms.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters distinguished possible visa violations by some workers from the decision to arrest and publicly shackle a larger group.
- Several reported growing reluctance among employees and companies to send staff to the United States.

### LLM perspective

- View: Investment incentives fail when immigration rules cannot reliably accommodate the specialists required to commission new factories.
- Impact: Aggressive enforcement may satisfy domestic politics while raising project, alliance, and investor-confidence costs.
- Watch next: Visa-quota negotiations, case-by-case findings, official apologies, travel policies, and any changes to Korean investment plans.
