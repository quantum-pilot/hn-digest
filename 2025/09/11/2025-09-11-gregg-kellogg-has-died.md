# Gregg Kellogg has died

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45210564) | Link: https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-json-ld-wg/2025Sep/0012.html

### TL;DR

The W3C announced the death of Gregg Kellogg, a longtime invited expert and recent co-chair of the JSON-LD Working Group. Over 13 years he co-edited nine published recommendations and roughly twelve other specifications spanning JSON-LD, RDF 1.2, CSV-to-RDF, and related work, while also building implementations and test suites used by working groups. Tributes emphasize his kindness as much as his technical output: one commenter traced a career in search and structured data to Kellogg's friendly advice at an early meetup.
