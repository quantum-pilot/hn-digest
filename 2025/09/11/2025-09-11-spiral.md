# Spiral

- Score: 237 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45212960) | Link: https://spiraldb.com/post/announcing-spiral

### TL;DR

Spiral argues that AI creates a “Third Age” of data systems, where machines demand both machine-scale inputs and outputs that warehouses and lakehouses handle inefficiently. Its proposed object-store-native database builds on Vortex, a Linux Foundation columnar format the company claims matches Parquet compression while greatly improving scans, writes, and random reads. Spiral promises unified governance, efficient storage across varied object sizes, and eventually direct S3-to-GPU decoding. The announcement discloses $22 million in funding but provides no usable database, product details, or independent end-to-end results.
