- Score: 146 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45210399) | Link: https://alignmentalignment.ai

### TL;DR

This satirical site invents an organization devoted to aligning the proliferating institutions that study AI alignment. Its jokes target recursive bureaucracy, uncertain AGI timelines, corporate-funded independence, policy work detached from democratic scrutiny, inflated agent readership, and executive status without subject expertise. HN readers overwhelmingly treated it as effective parody, highlighting the countdown to the next AGI prediction and its implausibly self-important institutional language. Several briefly wondered whether it was real, which became part of the joke's commentary on the field.
