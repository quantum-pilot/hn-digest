# Stripe Launches L1 Blockchain: Tempo

- Score: 804 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45129085) | Link: https://tempo.xyz

### TL;DR

Stripe and Paradigm introduced Tempo, an EVM-compatible layer-one blockchain intended specifically for stablecoin payments. Its private testnet advertises more than 100,000 transactions per second, sub-second finality, predictable near-zero fees payable in any stablecoin, a dedicated payments lane, native stablecoin exchange, batch transfers, ISO 20022-compatible memos, privacy, and compliance allowlists or blocklists. Initial validators will be independent design partners before a planned permissionless transition. The project targets remittances, payouts, embedded finance, microtransactions, agent commerce, and tokenized deposits; all performance and product claims remain project assertions.
