# Melvyn Bragg steps down from presenting In Our Time

- Score: 291 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45124143) | Link: https://www.bbc.co.uk/mediacentre/2025/melvyn-bragg-decides-to-step-down-from-presenting-in-our-time/

### TL;DR

Melvyn Bragg is leaving BBC Radio 4’s *In Our Time* after presenting every episode since its 1998 launch, totaling well over 1,000 conversations across science, history, religion, arts, and culture. The BBC says the program remains among its most-listened-to on-demand offerings worldwide and is especially popular with listeners under 35. Bragg, whose BBC career began in 1961, will continue working with Radio 4 on projects including a new 2026 series. Selected favorite episodes will mark his departure, while *In Our Time* will return with an unannounced presenter.
