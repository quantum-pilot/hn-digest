# Alibaba's new AI chip: Key specifications comparable to H20

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45273747) | Link: https://news.futunn.com/en/post/62202518/alibaba-s-new-ai-chip-unveiled-key-specifications-comparable-to

### TL;DR

A machine-translated market report, citing CCTV, says Alibaba subsidiary Pingtouge’s new PPU has 96GB HBM2e memory, up to 700GB/s inter-chip bandwidth, a PCIe 5.0 interface, and 400W power use. On those selected specifications it reportedly exceeds Nvidia’s A800 and approaches the export-limited H20, although H20 uses newer HBM3. The report also describes a China Unicom computing-center agreement including 16,384 Pingtouge cards. It provides no workload benchmarks, software-stack evaluation, price, manufacturing detail, or independent verification.
