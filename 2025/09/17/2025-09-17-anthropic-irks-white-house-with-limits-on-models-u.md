# Anthropic irks White House with limits on models’ use

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45279143) | Link: https://www.semafor.com/article/09/17/2025/anthropic-irks-white-house-with-limits-on-models-uswhite-house-with-limits-on-models-use

### TL;DR

Semafor reports, based on two anonymous senior officials, that Anthropic angered Trump administration figures by refusing contractor requests to exempt some federal law-enforcement uses from its ban on domestic surveillance. The policy reportedly limits FBI, Secret Service, and ICE use, even as Claude is available through AWS GovCloud and Anthropic serves defense and national-security customers. Officials call the terminology broad and politically selective; Anthropic declined comment. Commenters dispute the article’s framing, noting that negotiated software contracts routinely contain known usage restrictions and specialized terms.
