# DeepMind and OpenAI win gold at ICPC

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45279357) | Link: https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/146536

### TL;DR

A brief Codeforces post notes that Google DeepMind and OpenAI separately claimed gold-medal-level performance on the 2025 ICPC World Finals. Discussion supplies more detail for OpenAI: its system reportedly solved all 12 problems, with 11 first-submission successes and nine attempts on the hardest, while the best human team solved 11. However, the frozen source provides company announcements rather than independent documentation and omits comparable compute, cost, timing, scaffolding, and oversight details. Direct ranking against constrained three-person student teams is therefore difficult to interpret.
