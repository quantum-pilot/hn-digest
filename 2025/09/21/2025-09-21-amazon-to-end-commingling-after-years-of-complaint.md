# Amazon to end commingling after years of complaints from brands and sellers

- Score: 393 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45319463) | Link: https://www.modernretail.co/operations/amazon-to-end-commingling-program-after-years-of-complaints-from-brands-and-sellers/

### TL;DR

Amazon says it will end inventory commingling later in 2025, retiring a system that pooled identical products from different sellers under one barcode. The practice improved delivery and storage efficiency but made counterfeit, expired, stolen, or grey-market goods difficult to trace to their source. Amazon says its denser logistics network reduced the speed advantage, while brands spent $600 million annually relabeling products. Sellers welcomed the change, though commenters suspected economics and competition—not consumer protection—finally drove it and warned seller switching can still produce counterfeits.
