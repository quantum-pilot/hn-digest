# Why your outdoorsy friend suddenly has a gummy bear power bank

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45322135) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/tech/781387/backpacking-ultralight-haribo-power-bank

### TL;DR

The Haribo-branded 20,000mAh power bank became an ultralight-backpacking curiosity because the author measured 9.95 ounces, slightly below a 10.3-ounce Nitecore competitor, at about $23 versus $100. It supports fast and pass-through charging, but its built-in gummy-bear cable cannot recharge the bank, requiring another cable. The article had not capacity-tested it. Commenters stressed tested watt-hours under realistic loads over rated milliamp-hours; one cited 55.4Wh at 18W, roughly matching Nitecore, while durability and cell type remained unsettled.
