# Oxford loses top 3 university ranking in the UK

- Score: 224 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45323793) | Link: https://hotminute.co.uk/2025/09/19/oxford-loses-top-3-university-ranking-for-the-first-time/

### TL;DR

The Times and Sunday Times 2026 guide placed LSE first, St Andrews second, Durham third, and Oxford and Cambridge jointly fourth, attributing Durham’s rise largely to improved student evaluations of teaching. The result reflects a particular weighting of surveys, graduate outcomes, entry standards, completion, research, and other measures—not an objective hierarchy. Commenters argued Oxbridge’s intensive tutorial system, resources, selective cohort, and reputation remain distinctive, while others emphasized that rankings are gameable and priorities vary by subject, research impact, affordability, accessibility, and student experience.
