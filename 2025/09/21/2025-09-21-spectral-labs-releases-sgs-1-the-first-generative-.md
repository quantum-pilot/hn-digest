# Spectral Labs releases SGS-1: the first generative model for structured CAD

- Score: 302 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45319876) | Link: https://www.spectrallabs.ai/research/SGS-1

### TL;DR

Spectral Labs says SGS-1 turns images, meshes, drawings, or partial assemblies into watertight B-Rep STEP parts and outperformed GPT-5 with CadQuery and HoLa on its 75-image, ten-run benchmark. Demonstrations cover brackets, sketch conversion, and reverse engineering; acknowledged limits include thin structures, organic curves, resolution, and full assemblies. CAD practitioners strongly challenged the company's “parametric” and easy-editing claims, noting STEP lacks feature history, while one tester reported wrong dimensions and broken geometry. Others still saw value in cleaning scans or producing starting solids.
