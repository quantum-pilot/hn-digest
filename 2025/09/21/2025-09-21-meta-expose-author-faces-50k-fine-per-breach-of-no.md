# Meta exposé author faces $50k fine per breach of non-disparagement agreement

- Score: 416 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45322050) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/sep/21/meta-expose-author-sarah-wynn-williams-faces-bankruptcy-after-ban-on-criticising-company

### TL;DR

Former Meta executive Sarah Wynn-Williams is reportedly near bankruptcy after an arbitration order barred promotion of her memoir and exposed material breaches of her 2017 non-disparagement agreement to $50,000 damages each. A UK MP accused Meta of silencing a whistleblower, while Meta says the agreement was voluntary, no damages have yet been collected, and the book contains false claims. Wynn-Williams has alleged misconduct involving China, teenagers, and harassment; Meta disputes those allegations. Commenters debated contractual consent, enforceability, narrator credibility, and whether suppression amplified interest.

### Comment pulse
