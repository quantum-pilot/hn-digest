# Rail travel is booming in America

- Score: 109 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45326230) | Link: https://www.economist.com/united-states/2025/09/21/rail-travel-is-booming-in-america

### TL;DR

Amtrak carried 28.6 million riders in the nine months to July, up 6%, while fare revenue rose 11%; Brightline's Florida ridership also grew 11%. More service, flexible pricing, road congestion, airport friction, and station-centered population growth are helping. Federal investment is expanding routes, rolling stock, and tunnels, but delays, slow infrastructure work, corridor bottlenecks, and Brightline's costly financing constrain the boom. Commenters praised productive, scenic trips while noting that price and duration favor rail mainly on shorter intercity routes.
