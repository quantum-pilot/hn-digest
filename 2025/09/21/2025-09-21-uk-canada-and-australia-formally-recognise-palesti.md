# UK, Canada and Australia formally recognise Palestinian state

- Score: 141 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45322919) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/politics/live/2025/sep/21/keir-starmer-palestine-recognition-announcement-gaza-uk-politics-live

### TL;DR

The UK, Canada, and Australia formally recognized Palestinian statehood in a coordinated, diplomatically significant but largely symbolic break with the Trump administration; Canada became the first G7 country to do so. Starmer framed recognition as preserving a two-state solution, excluding Hamas from future governance, and paired it with promised Hamas sanctions and calls for aid access. Israel's government and UK Conservatives called it a reward for terrorism. Palestinians quoted welcomed recognition but questioned borders and enforcement; HN likewise split between symbolic-first and action-first views.
