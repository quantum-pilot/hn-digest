# YouTube says it'll bring back creators banned for Covid and election content

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45352213) | Link: https://www.businessinsider.com/youtube-reinstate-channels-banned-over-covid-content-policies-2025-9

### TL;DR

Alphabet told the House Judiciary Committee that YouTube will offer some creators terminated under repeated COVID-19 and election-integrity violations a route back. YouTube retired its election policy in 2023 and standalone COVID policies by December 2024; a pilot will cover only a subset of suspended creators, with restoration and monetization details unresolved. Alphabet also says Biden officials pressured platforms to remove some material that did not violate policy. Commenters debated whether moderation suppresses misinformation or sustains civil discourse, and whether Google is now rewriting its own responsibility.
