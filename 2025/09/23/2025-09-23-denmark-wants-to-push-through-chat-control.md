# Denmark wants to push through Chat Control

- Score: 189 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45351405) | Link: https://netzpolitik.org/2025/internes-protokoll-daenemark-will-chatkontrolle-durchdruecken/

### TL;DR

The report says Denmark’s EU Council presidency sought an October 14 agreement on mandatory scanning of user communications, including encrypted services, without materially softening a repeatedly stalled proposal. Ten states supported the draft, five opposed it, and Germany remained pivotal. The article cites legal objections, acknowledged false positives, and a delayed Commission evaluation. Commenters argued criminals would evade mainstream scanning while ordinary users inherit privacy and security risks; several suspected broader political surveillance motives, though those claims were speculative rather than established by the document.

### Comment pulse

- Mandatory scanning faces feasibility objections → no error-free technology exists, and determined offenders can move outside compliant platforms.
- Centralized access expands attack surface → critics fear abuse by insiders, hostile states, or compromised software distribution.
- Political legitimacy is disputed → commenters questioned whether current officials reflect broader national support for the proposal.

### LLM perspective

- View: A deadline cannot resolve the proposal’s underlying conflict between universal scanning, encryption, proportionality, and measurable efficacy.
- Impact: Messaging providers would bear implementation risk while all users’ private communications become subject to automated inspection.
- Watch next: Germany’s position, the October votes, the overdue evaluation, false-positive evidence, and judicial review.
