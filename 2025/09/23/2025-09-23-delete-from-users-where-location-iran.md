### TL;DR

An Iranian software engineer recounts losing a Microsoft Store app and account, encountering unexplained service blocks, and fearing similar access loss across developer platforms. The author attributes these experiences to sanctions and rejects treating ordinary Iranians as responsible for their government’s conduct. Commenters agreed the human cost is severe but disputed causes and remedies: some emphasized substantial U.S. compliance risk, others noted waivers or licenses can permit services, and several distinguished politically motivated exclusion from operational blocking driven by abuse or limited business incentives.
