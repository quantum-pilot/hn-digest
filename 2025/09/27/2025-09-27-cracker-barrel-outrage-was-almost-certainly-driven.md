# Cracker Barrel Outrage Was Almost Certainly Driven by Bots, Researchers Say

- Score: 99 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45394942) | Link: https://gizmodo.com/cracker-barrel-outrage-was-almost-certainly-driven-by-bots-researchers-say-2000664221

### TL;DR

Gizmodo reports that PeakMetrics classified 44.5% of 52,000 early X posts about Cracker Barrel’s logo change as likely-or-higher bot activity, including 49% of roughly 1,000 boycott posts. Researchers describe genuine dissatisfaction being amplified across multiple platforms, but identify no responsible actor and provide no baseline for normal bot prevalence. Commenters therefore rejected the idea that automation made the outrage unreal: nostalgia and redesign complaints existed, while coordinated amplification may have helped turn them into a larger culture-war story.
