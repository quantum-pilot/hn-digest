# The Amazon Kindle War Against Piracy

- Score: 92 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45393505) | Link: https://goodereader.com/blog/kindle/the-amazon-kindle-war-against-piracy

### TL;DR

The article says Amazon has progressively closed ways to download, back up, decrypt, or jailbreak purchased Kindle books. A recent update for newer Kindles reportedly stores a DRM account secret in an inaccessible location, while Amazon earlier removed “download and transfer” over USB and forced updated desktop software. Version 15.18.5 also produced “Invalid ASIN” errors for some sideloaded books, which Amazon says it is fixing. The anti-piracy measures may reassure publishers but further reduce legitimate customer control and portability.
