# Typst: A Possible LaTeX Replacement

- Score: 629 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45393842) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1037577/

### TL;DR

Typst is a Rust-based, Apache-licensed typesetting system offering TeX-quality line breaking and mathematics with terser markup, integrated programming, clearer errors, and fast incremental compilation. It handles floating elements and long tables well and already has over 800 packages. Its deficits include weaker widow and orphan control, missing specialized capabilities, uneven documentation, possible breaking changes, and almost no journal templates. The author already uses it for physics writing and predicts eventual LaTeX replacement, while converting submissions through Pandoc.

### Comment pulse

- Production users report smaller deployment images and dramatic compilation gains, including 2,000-page documents dropping from 18 minutes to one.
- Researchers praise rapid learning but identify typesetting polish, missing features, template acceptance, and stability as adoption barriers.
