# Ishkur's Guide to Electronic Music

- Score: 219 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45394642) | Link: http://music.ishkur.com/

### TL;DR

This preservation dataset reconstructs Ishkur’s Guide to Electronic Music from its Flash-era v2.5 and HTML v3 editions. Version 3 records 166 genres, 28 scenes, 352 directed time-bounded links, and 11,317 tracks; v2.5 contributes 187 genres, 305 links, and 1,178 representative tracks. The repository includes structured files, descriptions, raw assets, and extraction scripts, with substantial manual reconstruction. Commenters celebrated a long-lived old-web artifact while debating whether today’s multiplying microgenres make a comprehensive continuation impractical.

### Comment pulse
