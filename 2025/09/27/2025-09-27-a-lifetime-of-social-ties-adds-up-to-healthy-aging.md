# A lifetime of social ties adds up to healthy aging

- Score: 162 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45393501) | Link: https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2025/09/lifetime-social-ties-adds-healthy-aging

### TL;DR

A MIDUS analysis of more than 2,100 adults links accumulated social support—from parental warmth, community connection, faith, friends, and family—with slower epigenetic aging on two measures and lower IL-6 inflammation. It found no significant association with short-term stress markers. The observational result identifies a durable association, not proof that social ties reverse aging. Commenters emphasized relationship quality, meaning, and recovery communities, while noting that the biological mechanism remains unclear and access to supportive networks is unequal.
