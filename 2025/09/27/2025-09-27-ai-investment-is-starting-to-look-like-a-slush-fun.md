# AI Investment Is Starting to Look Like a Slush Fund

- Score: 82 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45393649) | Link: https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/ai-investment-is-starting-to-look-like-a-slush-fund.html

### TL;DR

The article argues that AI financing increasingly resembles circular vendor financing: Nvidia funds companies that buy its chips, while cloud providers, OpenAI, Oracle, and SoftBank sign interlocking investment, supply, and capacity deals. It cites Nvidia’s prospective $100 billion OpenAI investment and CoreWeave relationships, while stressing that several obligations remain vague or nonbinding. Commenters compared the structure with telecom-era excess and financial contagion, though others see a rational way for Nvidia to exchange scarce hardware and capital for upside.

### Comment pulse

- Circular demand can hide fragility → customers, suppliers, investors, and borrowers increasingly depend on the same valuation story.
- Nvidia’s strategy may still be rational → chips and cash can secure customers while buying equity in potential winners.
- Historical analogies warn, not predict → unclear obligations and actual end-user demand determine whether the structure unwinds.

### LLM perspective

- View: The central risk is correlated exposure, not merely large spending.
- Impact: A demand slowdown could simultaneously pressure chip sales, cloud contracts, startup valuations, and lenders.
- Watch next: Track binding commitments, outside revenue, debt terms, utilization, and counterparties’ ability to fund purchases.
