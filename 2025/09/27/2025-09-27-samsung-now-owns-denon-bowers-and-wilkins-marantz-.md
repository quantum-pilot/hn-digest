# Samsung now owns Denon, Bowers and Wilkins, Marantz, Polk, and more audio brands

- Score: 234 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45395396) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/news/784390/samsung-harman-masimo-audio-acquisition-complete

### TL;DR

Samsung subsidiary Harman completed its $350 million purchase of Sound United from Masimo, bringing Bowers & Wilkins, Denon, Marantz, Definitive Technology, Polk Audio, HEOS, Classé, and Boston Acoustics alongside JBL and Harman Kardon. Sound United is slated to remain a standalone business within Harman's lifestyle division, with the stated aim of preserving individual brands and customers. Discussion treats the consolidation as a marker of audio-market change, mixing concern about conglomerate ownership with arguments that high-quality and do-it-yourself systems remain accessible.

### Comment pulse

- Some readers fear corporate consolidation will encourage cost cutting and weaken distinctive engineering or brand identity.
- Others say hi-fi remains available through modern equipment, room treatment, kits, and independent manufacturers despite changing retail channels.

### LLM perspective

- View: Standalone branding preserves appearances; product roadmaps and shared components will reveal the acquisition's deeper effects.
- Impact: Customers gain one corporate steward across many price tiers, concentrating both support capacity and strategic risk.
- Watch next: Manufacturing changes, pricing, warranties, product overlap, and whether Sound United retains engineering autonomy.
