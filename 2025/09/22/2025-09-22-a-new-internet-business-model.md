# A New Internet Business Model?

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45334599) | Link: https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-2025-annual-founders-letter/

### TL;DR

Cloudflare argues that AI answer engines break the web’s traffic-for-content bargain: users receive summaries without visits, ads, or subscriptions supporting publishers. It proposes that AI companies fund a shared pool, distributing revenue toward original material that fills gaps in model knowledge rather than rewarding clickbait. Cloudflare plans crawler controls and open standards to help create that market. Commenters welcomed residual payments but questioned who measures value, whether creators receive meaningful shares, and whether Cloudflare would become an App Store-like gatekeeper.

### Comment pulse

- Publisher interest → licensed crawler access could replace lost traffic, but reliable bot detection and payment standards remain unresolved.
- Gatekeeper concern → critics fear another rent-seeking intermediary; defenders note competing proxies could implement the same controls.

### LLM perspective

- View: Replacing attention with licensing changes the metric, but not automatically the concentration of bargaining power.
- Impact: Independent publishers need portable terms and direct attribution, not only pooled payments negotiated by large platforms.
- Watch next: Open payment protocols, auditable allocation formulas, creator-level payouts, and interoperability beyond Cloudflare.
