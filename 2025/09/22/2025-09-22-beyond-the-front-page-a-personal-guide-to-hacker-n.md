# Beyond the Front Page: A Personal Guide to Hacker News

- Score: 200 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45331030) | Link: https://hsu.cy/2025/09/how-to-read-hn/

### TL;DR

The guide explains Hacker News as a curated external comment section whose durability comes from selective ranking, karma-gated moderation tools, strict norms, and unusually patient human moderation. It also warns about headline-only reactions, reflexive negativity, recurring low-yield debates, and the site’s U.S.-tech-worker bias. Instead of compulsively refreshing the front page, the author recommends filtered RSS, Algolia searches for prior discussion, strategic comment sampling, and full-thread AI summaries. Commenters credited shared culture and moderation, while suggesting privacy-preserving discussion finders and personalized content filters.
