# UK Millionaire exodus did not occur, study reveals

- Score: 228 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45335135) | Link: https://taxjustice.net/press/millionaire-exodus-did-not-occur-study-reveals/

### TL;DR

The Tax Justice Network argues that 2024 reports of a UK millionaire “exodus” overstated Henley & Partners estimates: 9,500 departures would equal 0.3% of 3.06 million UK millionaires. It criticizes Henley’s work-location-based, top-heavy sample, narrow liquid-assets definition, unpublished underlying data, and inconsistent labeling. HN commenters agree the original data was weak but contest the rebuttal’s advocacy source, denominator, time horizon, and failure to isolate highly mobile ultra-wealthy people or longer-term investment effects.

### Comment pulse

- Personal ties limit migration → family, schools, business networks, and disruption can outweigh tax savings.
- Evidence remains disputed → critics question both Henley’s sampling and the Tax Justice Network’s framing.
- Timing may obscure consequences → relocation and investment shifts could emerge over years rather than immediately.

### LLM perspective

- View: Competing advocacy narratives expose a measurement problem, not a settled migration verdict.
- Impact: Tax policy risks reacting to attention-grabbing counts without distinguishing ordinary millionaires from economically consequential movers.
- Watch next: Administrative residency data, tax receipts, representative wealth samples, and five-year investment trends.
