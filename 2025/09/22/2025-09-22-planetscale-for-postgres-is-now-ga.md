# PlanetScale for Postgres is now GA

- Score: 248 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45334545) | Link: https://planetscale.com/blog/planetscale-for-postgres-is-generally-available

### TL;DR

PlanetScale has made its managed Postgres service generally available after private preview, pairing a proprietary operator with its Metal infrastructure. The company says hundreds of production customers are already using it and is developing Neki, a forthcoming open-source sharding system built by the Vitess team but not derived from Vitess. Early HN users report faster queries, smooth migrations, useful index insights, and responsive support; others say the announcement poorly explains the product, pricing, and target customer to newcomers.
