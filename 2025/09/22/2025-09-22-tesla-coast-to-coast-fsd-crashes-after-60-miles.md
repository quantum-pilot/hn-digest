# Tesla coast-to-coast FSD crashes after 60 miles

- Score: 279 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45332120) | Link: https://electrek.co/2025/09/21/tesla-influencers-tried-elon-musk-coast-to-coast-self-driving-crashed-before-60-miles/

### TL;DR

Two Tesla shareholder-influencers attempted a San Diego-to-Jacksonville drive using FSD v13.9 but struck visible road debris within roughly 60 miles, damaging their Model Y’s suspension and sway-bar bracket. Electrek contrasts this with Elon Musk’s unfulfilled 2017 coast-to-coast autonomy promise and notes that Tesla classifies FSD as Level 2 driver assistance requiring attention. HN debate centers on delayed human intervention, whether average-human performance is the right benchmark, and the mismatch between prolonged automation and reliable supervision.
