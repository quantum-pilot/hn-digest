# South Korea's President says US investment demands would spark financial crisis

- Score: 247 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45328579) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/21/south-koreas-president-lee-trump-investment-financial-crisis.html

### TL;DR

South Korean President Lee Jae Myung told Reuters that meeting current U.S. demands for a $350 billion cash investment without a currency swap could trigger a crisis comparable to South Korea’s 1997 collapse. A July verbal trade framework would reduce U.S. tariffs, but disputes over investment mechanics have prevented a written agreement. HN commenters broadly interpret Lee’s warning as a firm negotiating boundary, while debating U.S. credibility, the value of written deals, tariff politics, and why markets appear resilient.
