# Charlie Kirk killed at event in Utah

- Score: 573 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45202200) | Link: https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/live-blog/live-updates-shooting-charlie-kirk-event-utah-rcna230437

### TL;DR

Conservative activist and Turning Point USA co-founder Charlie Kirk was fatally shot while speaking outdoors at Utah Valley University before roughly 3,000 attendees. Officials described a targeted attack from a nearby building and canceled classes while investigators sought evidence; the live report contained conflicting early statements about whether a person of interest was detained. Utah's governor called it a political assassination, and officials across parties condemned the killing. HN moderators and commenters emphasized rejecting celebration, retaliation, and normalization of political violence.

### Comment pulse

- De-escalation was the central demand → participants urged compassion and refusal to channel grief or anger into further aggression.
- Security appeared limited → witnesses reported no entrance checkpoint, while six uniformed officers and plainclothes personnel covered the event.
- Broader risk remains disputed → commenters contrasted heightened fear with claims that earlier decades experienced more political violence.
