# Google appears to have deleted its political ad archive for the EU

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45411332) | Link: https://www.thebriefing.ie/google-just-erased-7-years-of-our-political-history/

### TL;DR

The Briefing reports that Google’s EU political-ad archive no longer exposed seven years of searchable ads, spending, targeting, and creative material from 27 countries after Google’s political-ad ban began. The supplied excerpt establishes disappearance from the public interface, not permanent deletion or Google’s motive. HN commenters found a narrow rescue window through BigQuery’s seven-day time travel and called for coordinated archiving. Debate centered on whether private platforms ever owed preservation, especially when a database was explicitly presented as an advertising-transparency resource.
