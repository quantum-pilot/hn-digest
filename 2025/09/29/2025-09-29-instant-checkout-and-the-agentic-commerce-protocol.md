# Instant Checkout and the Agentic Commerce Protocol

- Score: 188 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45416080) | Link: https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/

### TL;DR

OpenAI launched Instant Checkout for US ChatGPT users buying single items from US Etsy sellers, with Shopify merchants, carts, and more regions planned. Its open Agentic Commerce Protocol, co-developed with Stripe, passes order details to merchants, who remain seller of record and handle payment, fulfillment, returns, and support. OpenAI says recommendations remain organic and unsponsored, users confirm every action, and merchants pay a completion fee. HN commenters saw a natural revenue path but feared ranking conflicts, accidental purchases, chargebacks, and eventual advertising-driven degradation.
