# Vertical Solar Panels Are Out Standing

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45414215) | Link: https://hackaday.com/2025/09/25/vertical-solar-panels-are-out-standing/

### TL;DR

One year of data from bifacial solar panels in snowy Ohio suggests that vertical north-south mounting can outperform conventional tilted panels in winter and produce power earlier and later in the day. It still lost on total warm-season energy: across six months from solstice to solstice, the vertical array generated 77% of tilted bifacial output, while tilted single-sided panels reached 90%. The best orientation therefore depends on latitude, snow, load timing, land and structure constraints, maintenance, and whether annual energy or steadier seasonal production matters most.
