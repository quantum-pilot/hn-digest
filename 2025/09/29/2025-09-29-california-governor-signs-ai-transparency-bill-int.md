# California governor signs AI transparency bill into law

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45418428) | Link: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/09/29/governor-newsom-signs-sb-53-advancing-californias-world-leading-artificial-intelligence-industry/

### TL;DR

California’s official announcement says Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 53, requiring large frontier-model developers to publish safety frameworks, provide a channel for reporting critical incidents, and protect whistleblowers. The law also creates CalCompute planning for a public computing cluster, authorizes civil penalties, and directs annual recommendations as technology changes. The administration presents this as innovation-compatible oversight. HN commenters instead debated vague definitions, low or discretionary penalties, possible compliance-industry capture, copyright omissions, and whether enforcement will make the measure consequential or merely performative.
