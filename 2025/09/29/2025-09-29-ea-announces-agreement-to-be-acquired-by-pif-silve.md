# EA Announces Agreement to be Acquired by PIF, Silver Lake, and Affinity Partners

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45413083) | Link: https://ir.ea.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/EA-Announces-Agreement-to-be-Acquired-by-PIF-Silver-Lake-and-Affinity-Partners-for-55-Billion/default.aspx

### TL;DR

Electronic Arts agreed to a $55 billion all-cash take-private by Saudi PIF, Silver Lake, and Affinity Partners. Shareholders would receive $210 per share, a stated 25% premium; financing comprises about $36 billion in equity and $20 billion in committed debt, with PIF rolling over its 9.9% stake. Andrew Wilson would remain CEO, and closing is targeted for Q1 FY27 subject to shareholder and regulatory approval. HN commenters feared debt-driven layoffs, heavier monetization, diminished creative independence, and stewardship risks for EA’s franchises.

### Comment pulse

- Going private is not freedom from growth pressure → leveraged financing adds interest obligations that may intensify cost-cutting and extraction.
- EA’s legacy divided readers → former employees recalled creative influence, while critics described decades of franchise exploitation and management decline.
- Workers carry downside → commenters expected layoffs in an already difficult games market regardless of their feelings toward EA.
