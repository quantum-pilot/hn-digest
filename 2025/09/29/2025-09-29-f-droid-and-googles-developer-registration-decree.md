# F-Droid and Google’s developer registration decree

- Score: 1371 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45409794) | Link: https://f-droid.org/2025/09/29/google-developer-registration-decree.html

### TL;DR

F-Droid argues Google’s planned developer-verification regime threatens independent Android distribution by requiring centralized registration, fees, identity documents, terms acceptance, and enumeration of application identifiers. Because F-Droid builds reviewed source itself, it says it cannot force upstream developers to register or claim their identifiers, potentially preventing installs and updates on certified devices. The project rejects security as sufficient justification, citing transparent builds and Play Protect, and urges regulatory intervention. HN discussion broadly valued marketplace diversity but disputed regulators’ intent and likely response.

### Comment pulse

- Curation provides distinct protection → F-Droid users cited its response to closed-source ownership changes and avoidance of ad-heavy subscription traps.
- Registration creates a choke point → developers fear anonymous or personal software becomes impossible without Google’s identity, payment, and policy approval.
- Regulatory causality is contested → some blame weak DMA enforcement; others see Google exploiting security exceptions or lobbying for restrictive rules.
