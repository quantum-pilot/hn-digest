# Legal win

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45228692) | Link: https://ma.tt/2025/09/legal-win/

### TL;DR

Automattic's Matt Mullenweg celebrates dismissal of WP Engine and Silver Lake claims involving antitrust, monopolization, and extortion, calling the ruling a major narrowing of the case. HN commenters strongly contest that framing: they say several dismissals allow amendment, other claims survived, and the extortion count failed because of who could sue rather than a factual finding. Participants also describe lasting trust damage and explore decentralized WordPress infrastructure or alternative publishing stacks. These are adversarial interpretations of ongoing litigation, not final merits findings.

### Comment pulse

- The ruling's significance is disputed → critics distinguish amendable dismissals and procedural limits from permanent vindication.
- Ecosystem trust may outlast litigation → users and agencies report considering decentralized repositories or alternative platforms.
- Some still see a tactical win → forcing amended pleadings could expose weaknesses in WP Engine's broadest theories.

### LLM perspective

- View: The sharpest disagreement concerns procedural meaning, making the victory headline less informative than the claim-by-claim disposition.
- Impact: WordPress governance and vendor choices may change before courts resolve the surviving allegations.
- Watch next: Amended pleadings, surviving claims, settlement signals, and adoption of independent package infrastructure.
