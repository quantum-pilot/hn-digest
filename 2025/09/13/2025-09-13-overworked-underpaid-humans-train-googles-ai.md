# ‘Overworked, underpaid’ humans train Google’s AI

- Score: 242 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45231239) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/sep/11/google-gemini-ai-training-humans

### TL;DR

The Guardian reports that contracted GlobalLogic workers rate and moderate Google AI outputs under tightening deadlines, limited context, and sometimes distressing or highly specialized assignments. Ten current or former workers described inconsistent guidance, weak job security, and pressure that they believed compromised quality; some reported wages starting at $16 or $21 hourly. Google said supplier ratings are aggregated feedback and do not directly change models or algorithms, while disputing that its hate-speech policy changed. GlobalLogic declined to comment, leaving important claims contested.
