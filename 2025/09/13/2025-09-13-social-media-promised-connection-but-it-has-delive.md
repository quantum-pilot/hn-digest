# Social media promised connection, but it has delivered exhaustion

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45229799) | Link: https://www.noemamag.com/the-last-days-of-social-media/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that engagement-optimized social platforms have replaced meaningful connection with synthetic content, compulsive scrolling, declining trust, and exhaustion. It sees users moving toward smaller private or federated communities, but warns that protocols alone cannot solve moderation, sustainability, portability, or governance. Proposed remedies include public-interest oversight, transparent and user-selectable algorithms, member-owned infrastructure, privacy defaults, impact assessments, and digital literacy as civic infrastructure. Commenters disputed whether “exhaustion” fits, while debating algorithms, advertising incentives, echo chambers, and thriving smaller communities.

### Comment pulse

- Mastodon users praised chronological control, while others noted trending systems, spam, bot pressure, and discovery costs.
- Commenters split between declaring peer-to-peer social media dead and pointing to active forums, Discord, and federated spaces.
