# An annual blast of Pacific cold water did not occur

- Score: 134 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45232100) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2025/09/12/climate/pacific-cold-water-upwelling.html

### TL;DR

The Gulf of Panama's January-to-April cold-water upwelling failed to appear in 2025, reportedly the first absence in more than four decades of monitoring. Researchers link the anomaly to trade winds reaching only about one-quarter of their usual strength, but the roles of La Niña, regional pressure patterns, warm surface water, and climate change remain unresolved. The missing nutrient pulse could affect fisheries, marine mammals, and corals. Commenters supplied physical-oceanography context and pointed readers toward the underlying PNAS paper.
