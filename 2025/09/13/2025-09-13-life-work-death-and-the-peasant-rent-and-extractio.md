# Life, work, death and the peasant: Rent and extraction

- Score: 304 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45228472) | Link: https://acoup.blog/2025/09/12/collections-life-work-death-and-the-peasant-part-ivc-rent-and-extraction/

### TL;DR

The supplied excerpt argues that typical premodern peasant holdings were far smaller than households could efficiently farm or even reliably subsist upon. Families therefore sought access to elite-controlled land, while rents, taxes, debt, conscription, and forced labor captured much of their surplus. The author's models estimate roughly 2,500–3,600 annual work hours under extraction, though assumptions vary widely by yield and society. HN discussion connected land scarcity to constrained mobility, hierarchy, bargaining power after population shocks, and modern tenure—while disputing deterministic conspiracy accounts.

### Comment pulse
