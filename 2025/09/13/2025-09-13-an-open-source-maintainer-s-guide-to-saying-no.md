# An open-source maintainer's guide to saying “no”

- Score: 131 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45234593) | Link: https://www.jlowin.dev/blog/oss-maintainers-guide-to-saying-no

### TL;DR

The maintainer argues that successful open source depends on a coherent vision, not accepting every plausible feature. Contributors should first explain problems and fit; maintainers inherit indefinite review, compatibility, and support costs even when generated code is cheap. Documented philosophy makes rejection more predictable, while contributor-owned modules or separate projects can accommodate useful but non-core ideas. HN discussion largely endorsed firm boundaries, citing untested AI pull requests, drive-by contributions, and the asymmetry between a quick patch and years of maintenance.
