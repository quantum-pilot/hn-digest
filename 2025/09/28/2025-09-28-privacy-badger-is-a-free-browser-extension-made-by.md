# Privacy Badger is a free browser extension made by EFF to stop spying

- Score: 532 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45404021) | Link: https://privacybadger.org/

### TL;DR

EFF's free Privacy Badger extension blocks third-party domains it observes tracking users across sites, rather than broadly blocking advertisements or relying only on curated URL lists. It sends Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals, restricts trackers that ignore them, handles cookies and some fingerprinting, and can preserve necessary resources through cookie blocking instead of total blocking. Discussion disputes whether it adds value beside uBlock Origin, with some calling it redundant and others citing behavioral and content-level protections the tools implement differently.

### Comment pulse

- Privacy advocates favor contextual advertising; ad-industry commenters counter that behavioral targeting has measurably higher returns.
- Users disagree whether Privacy Badger complements uBlock Origin through distinct detection and link-cleaning behavior or merely duplicates protection.
