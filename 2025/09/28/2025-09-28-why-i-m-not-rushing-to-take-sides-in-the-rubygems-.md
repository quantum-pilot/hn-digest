# Why I'm not rushing to take sides in the RubyGems fiasco

- Score: 143 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45405221) | Link: https://justin.searls.co/posts/why-im-not-rushing-to-take-sides-in-the-rubygems-fiasco/

### TL;DR

Justin Searls urges caution over the 2025 RubyGems and Bundler governance dispute, arguing that public accounts omit a decade of unresolved mistrust. He recounts personal impressions, private reports, leaked Ruby Together minutes, fundraising controversies, withdrawn post-install messaging, disputed infrastructure claims, and repository-attribution incidents involving Andre Arko. Searls acknowledges limited direct involvement, incomplete knowledge of current events, and Ruby Central’s poor execution and communication. His thesis is not that one party is vindicated, but that ecosystem custody should not be decided from a one-sided narrative.
