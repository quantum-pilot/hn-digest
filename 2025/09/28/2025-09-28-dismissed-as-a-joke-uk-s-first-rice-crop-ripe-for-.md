# Dismissed as a joke, UK's first rice crop ripe for picking after hot summer

- Score: 107 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45402496) | Link: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1wgeq702dyo

### TL;DR

A Cambridgeshire trial grew nine rice varieties in four small paddies during the UK's hottest recorded summer, exploring crops suited to a warmer, wetter agricultural future. Researchers say Britain remains at rice's climatic edge, but wider cultivation could become feasible with 2–4°C warming. Rewetting the Fens' drying peat could also retain carbon; early trial results suggest rice methane does not exceed the avoided emissions. Commenters questioned commercial economics, labor, scale, flooding methods, subsidies, and rising-sea risks.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics doubt UK rice can compete without large farms, cheap labor, tariffs, subsidies, or a high-value local niche.
- Supporters argue food resilience, peat preservation, and flood mitigation could justify public support beyond crop profits alone.
