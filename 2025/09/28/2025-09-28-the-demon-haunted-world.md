# The Demon-Haunted World

- Score: 145 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45404373) | Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Demon-Haunted_World

### TL;DR

This encyclopedia overview describes Carl Sagan’s 1995 book as an accessible case for scientific and skeptical thinking. Sagan treats science as an imaginative but disciplined, self-correcting method, illustrated by the unfalsifiable “dragon in my garage” and a “baloney detection kit” built around independent confirmation, competing hypotheses, quantification, logical rigor, parsimony, and falsifiability. The entry also notes Sagan’s criticism of pseudoscience and scientific misuse, plus reviews that praised its clear thinking while questioning omissions, length, and some of his own unsupported assertions.
