# Show HN: Toolbrew – Free little tools without signups or ads

- Score: 151 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45404667) | Link: https://toolbrew.co/

### TL;DR

Toolbrew presents a broad collection of free browser utilities without signups or advertising. Its catalog spans text and encoding tasks, date and work-hour calculations, UUID generation, media downloaders, document conversion, text comparison, password generation, and requested additions. The page is primarily a directory rather than a technical explanation. Discussion praised its design and compared it with CyberChef and other tool collections, while users requested mobile-friendly input types and PDF merging. The creator acknowledged that the YouTube downloader remains buggy.

### Comment pulse

- Users liked the polished design and suggested Germany holiday optimization, PDF merging, and mobile-specific input types.
- Commenters noted self-hosted toolsets can avoid pasting sensitive data into arbitrary websites.
