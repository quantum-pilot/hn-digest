# Why I gave the world wide web away for free

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45403501) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/sep/28/why-i-gave-the-world-wide-web-away-for-free

### TL;DR

Tim Berners-Lee says he persuaded CERN in 1993 to place the web’s intellectual property in the public domain because universal participation required free access. He argues that today’s platform economy betrayed that sharing-oriented vision by trading personal data, optimizing addictive algorithms, and trapping user activity in silos. His proposed response combines Solid, an interoperable standard intended to return data control to individuals, with urgent AI governance. He advocates legally accountable personal AI and a CERN-like international nonprofit for AI research rather than company-led rules.
