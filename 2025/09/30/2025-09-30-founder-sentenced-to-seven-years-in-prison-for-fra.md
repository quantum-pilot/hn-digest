# Founder sentenced to seven years in prison for fraudulent sale to JPMorgan

- Score: 165 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45424827) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2025/09/30/business/charlie-javice-frank-sentenced-jpmorgan-intl

### TL;DR

Frank founder Charlie Javice received more than seven years in prison after a conviction for conspiracy, bank fraud, and wire fraud tied to JPMorgan’s $175 million acquisition. Prosecutors said she represented fewer than 300,000 customers as more than four million using fabricated records, standing to receive $29 million. The judge criticized JPMorgan’s inadequate due diligence but said it did not excuse her conduct; she remains free pending appeal. HN discussion examined sentencing mechanics, deal verification failures, startup rule-bending, and engineers’ ethical responsibility.
