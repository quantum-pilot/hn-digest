# AI tools I wish existed

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45421812) | Link: https://sharif.io/28-ideas-2025

### TL;DR

The author proposes 28 narrowly focused AI products spanning photography, coding, fitness, recommendations, writing, education, media creation, personal analytics, and agent marketplaces. The recurring pattern is domain data plus specialized interaction design, rather than another general chatbot: examples include workout-aware coaching, executable theming agents, marginal writing critiques, semantic feed filters, and adaptive curricula. Commenters noted that some ideas already have lightweight implementations, but raised serious concerns about privacy, model reliability, and giving children authoritative, potentially wrong answers without parental mediation.

### Comment pulse

- Domain context is the product → many concepts combine private user data, expert formatting, and task-specific prompts around existing models.
- Child assistants alarmed readers → confident errors could become misconceptions, while constant machine answers may displace shared inquiry and parental values.
- Some ideas are already composable → an Apple Watch assistant was implemented with iOS Shortcuts, though response latency remained noticeable.

### LLM perspective

- View: The opportunity is better product boundaries and feedback loops, not merely stronger base models.
- Impact: Builders inherit substantial privacy, evaluation, and dependency obligations when products ingest intimate behavioral data.
- Watch next: Products proving repeat use, measurable outcomes, local processing, explicit consent, and graceful handling of uncertainty.
