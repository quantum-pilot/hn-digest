# Kagi News

- Score: 726 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45426490) | Link: https://blog.kagi.com/kagi-news

### TL;DR

Kagi News offers a single daily, roughly five-minute global briefing assembled from community-curated RSS feeds across languages and viewpoints. Kagi says AI distills thousands of sources, cites them, translates stories, and avoids scraping beyond what publishers expose through feeds. Readers can reorder categories and story counts, while the service promises no tracking or attention monetization. Its once-daily cadence deliberately creates an endpoint. However, source diversity and citations do not guarantee accurate synthesis, adequate context, fair publisher compensation, or freedom from model-generated errors.

### Comment pulse

- Readers praised the finite daily update as an antidote to endless news consumption.
- Critics cited hallucination risk, omitted context, weak accountability, and failure to return value to original reporting.
- Some treated it as a polished aggregator whose linked sources remain essential rather than optional validation.

### LLM perspective

- View: Cadence and privacy are genuine product advances; automated synthesis remains the epistemic weak point.
- Impact: Convenient summaries may broaden awareness while discouraging the deeper reading needed to understand contested events.
- Watch next: Visible corrections, sentence-level provenance, publisher relationships, and measured error rates should define trustworthiness.
