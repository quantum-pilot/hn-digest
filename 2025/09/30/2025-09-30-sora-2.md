# Sora 2

- Score: 482 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45427982) | Link: https://openai.com/index/sora-2/

### TL;DR

OpenAI presents Sora 2 as a more controllable video-and-audio model with improved physical consistency, multi-shot instruction following, persistent state, synchronized speech, sound effects, and styles spanning realism, cinema, and anime. A new invite-based iOS social app lets users generate, remix, browse a customizable feed, and insert verified likenesses through “cameos.” OpenAI says cameo owners control access and removal, teens receive stricter limits, and the feed favors creation over time spent. Initial rollout covers the United States and Canada; model limitations remain acknowledged.

### Comment pulse

- Some saw the feed-first, short-video interface as an AI-native TikTok rather than a serious editing system.
- Viewers found demonstrations technically impressive yet visibly uncanny, especially facial and mouth movement.
- Discussion raised copyright, consent, personalized advertising, and whether synthetic social content serves genuine human demand.
