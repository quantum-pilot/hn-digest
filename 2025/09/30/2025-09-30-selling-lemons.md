# Selling Lemons

- Score: 185 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45425746) | Link: https://frankchimero.com/blog/2025/selling-lemons/

### TL;DR

Frank Chimero applies Akerlof’s “market for lemons” to today’s internet: when buyers cannot distinguish quality, prices and visibility converge, good suppliers exit, and low-quality offerings dominate. He sees this pattern in generic e-commerce brands, dating, search, social media, hiring, and AI-generated content. Platforms, sellers, and buyers may each act rationally while collectively degrading the market. His prescription is deliberately mundane: stop optimizing proxies, algorithms, and outcome theater at the expense of the concrete products, design, code, and ethical choices customers actually experience.
