# Just let me select text

- Score: 701 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45360475) | Link: https://aartaka.me/select-text.html

### TL;DR

Artyom Bologov argues that disabling text selection turns meaningful interface content into image-like media, blocking ordinary workflows such as copying, translation, quotation, accessibility, and precise bug reporting. His example is a dating profile written in German that required screenshot OCR because Bumble would not expose the text. He rejects copyright protection as ineffective against automated scraping and says native-app aesthetics should not override user control. Interactive drag surfaces may require exceptions, but the default for readable text, including labels and links, should remain selectable and reusable.
