# Rights groups urge UK PM Starmer to abandon plans for mandatory digital ID

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45359356) | Link: https://bigbrotherwatch.org.uk/press-releases/rights-groups-urge-starmer-to-abandon-plans-for-mandatory-digital-id/

### TL;DR

Big Brother Watch and seven other rights organizations urge Keir Starmer to abandon an anticipated mandatory digital-ID scheme intended to deter illegal immigration. They argue routine identity checks could permanently alter citizens’ relationship with the state, expand into public and private services, damage civil liberties, and fail its stated goal. Commenters were more divided: many see unified authentication as overdue relief from fragmented paperwork, while others distrust mandatory phone-based implementation, vendor capture, exclusion, surveillance creep, and future governments’ use of centralized identity infrastructure.

### Comment pulse

- Digital identity can simplify services → reusable verification may reduce paperwork and make access auditing clearer.
- Mandatory implementation creates exclusion risks → old phones, homelessness, disability, visitors, and delegated care expose edge cases.
- Governance determines acceptability → commenters want public ownership, open systems, limited purpose, and offline alternatives.
