# Kazeta: An operating system that brings the console gaming experience of 90s

- Score: 312 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45098269) | Link: https://kazeta.org/

### TL;DR

Kazeta is an operating system designed to recreate cartridge-era console simplicity on modern PCs: put a DRM-free game on an SD card or other external medium, insert it, boot, and play without accounts, launchers, updates, subscriptions, or internet access. It supports Linux and Windows games plus emulation, automatically captures saves, and offers a retro BIOS-style manager when no “cart” is present. The pitch emphasizes durable ownership, distraction-free play, and accessibility for families and less technical users.

### Comment pulse

- Enthusiasts value real game data on removable media, scarcity, and kid-friendly simplicity; skeptics prefer a Steam Deck or multi-game cards.
