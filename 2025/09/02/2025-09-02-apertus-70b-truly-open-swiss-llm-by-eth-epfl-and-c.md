# Apertus 70B: Truly Open - Swiss LLM by ETH, EPFL and CSCS

- Score: 321 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45108401) | Link: https://huggingface.co/swiss-ai/Apertus-70B-2509

### TL;DR

Swiss AI's Apertus release offers 70B- and 8B-parameter language models with weights, training recipes, data documentation, and intermediate checkpoints. Its model card claims 1,811 supported languages, a 65,536-token context window, tool use, and training on 15 trillion tokens using 4,096 GH200 GPUs. Reported general benchmarks place the 70B model near Llama 3.1 70B but below Qwen 2.5 72B. The card also documents retrospective robots.txt filtering, limitations, and absent output PII filtering.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters welcomed the disclosure while questioning whether gated access and unavailable original data justify “fully open” or reproducible claims.
- Legal debate focused on whether current robots.txt files meaningfully represent historical authors' or publishers' preferences.

### LLM perspective

- View: Apertus's strongest contribution is unusually broad process disclosure, not benchmark leadership.
- Impact: Intermediate artifacts can support research into training behavior even when exact data reconstruction remains constrained.
- Watch next: Independent audits of multilingual quality, memorization, licensing claims, and the promised output PII filter.
