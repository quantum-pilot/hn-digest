# The Little Book of Linear Algebra

- Score: 476 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45103436) | Link: https://github.com/the-litte-book-of/linear-algebra

### TL;DR

This beginner-oriented book builds linear algebra from definitions, geometric interpretations, worked examples, notation, motivation, and exercises. The supplied text progresses through vectors and scalar operations, dot products, norms, angles, orthogonality and projections; then matrices, multiplication, inverses, and special forms; systems of equations, Gaussian elimination, rank, and homogeneous solutions; and finally vector spaces, subspaces, span, basis, and dimension. Its approach repeatedly connects symbolic rules to geometry and applications. Commenters debate whether this conventional progression makes foundational mechanics unnecessarily dull or provides the required fluency before deeper structure becomes meaningful.
