# Apple pulls iPhone torrent app from AltStore PAL in Europe

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45098411) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/news/767344/apple-removes-itorrent-altstore-pal-ios-marketplace

### TL;DR

Apple removed iTorrent's notarization and revoked its developer's alternative-distribution rights, taking the torrent client off AltStore PAL in the EU. Apple says the action complied with government sanctions rules, while developer Daniil Vinogradov said distribution disappeared without warning or an initial explanation. Reporting and linked discussion suggest his Russian developer-account details, despite residence in Malta, may be relevant. The supplied material does not establish which sanctions rule applied or whether Apple handled updated residency evidence correctly.
