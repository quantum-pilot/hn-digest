# How the “Kim” dump exposed North Korea's credential theft playbook

- Score: 404 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45152066) | Link: https://dti.domaintools.com/inside-the-kimsuky-leak-how-the-kim-dump-exposed-north-koreas-credential-theft-playbook/

### TL;DR

DomainTools analyzes a leaked operator dataset it attributes with high confidence to DPRK-aligned Kimsuky activity, while judging Chinese infrastructure involvement moderately likely. The dump reportedly contains phishing domains, stolen South Korean PKI material, administrative logs, OCR of authentication documents, hand-built Windows shellcode, and a Linux rootkit. It also suggests reconnaissance against Taiwanese government, academic, cloud, and exposed Git repositories. The report frames the campaign as credential-first espionage, but its China-collaboration and physical-location conclusions remain analytical inferences rather than demonstrated coordination.
