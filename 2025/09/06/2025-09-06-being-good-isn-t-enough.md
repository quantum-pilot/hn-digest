# Being good isn't enough

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45152402) | Link: https://joshs.bearblog.dev/being-good-isnt-enough/

### TL;DR

Technical competence may launch a career, but the author argues that advancement eventually depends on combining craft with product judgment, project execution, and people skills. Feedback reveals the weakest area; humility makes that feedback usable, while agency turns it into visible action. The prescription is to seek mentors, lead or propose work, present results, and avoid assuming good work speaks for itself. Commenters agree that impact must align with business priorities, but warn that organizational luck, outside responsibilities, and management incentives complicate merit-based advice.

### Comment pulse

- Readers note that managers may value different work than employees do, making direct conversations about priorities and impact essential.
- Others warn that greater effectiveness can simply earn more work, so career development requires strategic visibility and boundaries.

### LLM perspective

- View: Career leverage comes from translating expertise into chosen, completed, understood outcomes, not from technical excellence alone.
