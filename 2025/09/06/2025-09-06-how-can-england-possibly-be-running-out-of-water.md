# How can England possibly be running out of water?

- Score: 353 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45151641) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/news/ng-interactive/2025/aug/17/how-can-england-possibly-be-running-out-of-water

### TL;DR

England's water shortage reflects both unusually dry weather and structural fragility: rising demand, changing rainfall, slow reservoir construction, heavy abstraction, and pipes that lose about one trillion litres yearly—roughly 20% of treated water. The article says replacement proceeds at only 0.05% of pipes annually, while water companies face incentives to avoid unpopular restrictions. Proposed responses include nine reservoirs before 2050, metering, rainwater reuse, farm storage, leak repair, and restored wetlands. Commenters primarily blame privatization and underinvestment, though counterexamples complicate that diagnosis.
