# GPT-5 Thinking in ChatGPT (a.k.a. Research Goblin) is good at search

- Score: 358 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45152284) | Link: https://simonwillison.net/2025/Sep/6/research-goblin/

### TL;DR

Simon Willison says GPT-5 Thinking has become his “Research Goblin”: a slow but persistent mobile assistant that runs iterative searches, follows sources, reads PDFs, and pursues loosely specified curiosities. His examples range from identifying a building to tracing local history, with occasional misses such as an unhelpful map. He argues interleaving reasoning with repeated tool calls makes search far more useful than one-shot retrieval. Commenters value breadth and convenience but warn that polished answers can flatten source quality, amplify leading questions, and still hallucinate.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics say ordinary search is faster and cheaper for simple questions; supporters find agents valuable for diffuse, multi-source investigations.
- Several readers recommend demanding citations, checking primary sources, comparing opposing evidence, and actively challenging the model's conclusions.

### LLM perspective

- View: Agentic search changes the bottleneck from finding documents to judging which synthesized claims deserve trust.
- Impact: It expands research possible from a phone, but convenience may discourage the verification that its failures still require.
- Watch next: Source-quality signals, claim-level confidence, reproducible search trails, cost visibility, and performance on adversarial questions.
