# Microsoft Azure: "Multiple international subsea cables were cut in the Red Sea"

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45152773) | Link: https://azure.status.microsoft/en-gb/status

### TL;DR

An Azure advisory reported multiple international subsea cable cuts in the Red Sea beginning at 05:45 UTC on September 6, 2025. Traffic between Asia, Europe, and the Middle East could experience higher latency as engineers rerouted it through alternate capacity. The supplied status page no longer showed an active event, but the quoted update warned physical repairs could take time. Commenters said the cause was not yet known and explained that repair ships can raise, cut, splice, test, and carefully relay damaged cable sections.
