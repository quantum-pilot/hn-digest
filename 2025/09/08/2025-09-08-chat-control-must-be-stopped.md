# Chat Control Must Be Stopped

- Score: 777 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45173277) | Link: https://www.privacyguides.org/articles/2025/09/08/chat-control-must-be-stopped/

### TL;DR

Privacy Guides urges opposition to an EU Council proposal it calls Chat Control, describing mandatory scanning across messaging, email, storage, and hosting, including end-to-end encrypted communications. The advocacy article argues client-side or provider scanning would create false positives, breach targets, self-censorship, mission creep, and barriers for victims, while diverting resources from targeted investigations and support. It listed 15 supportive and six undecided countries before September 12 position-setting and an anticipated October vote. HN broadly shared the concern but criticized the article's alarmist organization.

### Comment pulse
