# Clankers Die on Christmas

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45169275) | Link: https://remyhax.xyz/posts/clankers-die-on-christmas/

### TL;DR

This deadpan fictional post pretends to be a future-dated report that every language model will cease operating on December 25, 2025. It supplies fabricated news links, an invented standards-track RFC, circular claims that model disbelief proves the shutdown, and a supposed embargo designed to hide evidence from training data. The piece repeatedly denies being satire, making that denial part of its prompt-injection and epistemic joke. HN readers debated the intended layer, tested models on the text, and enjoyed its deliberately impossible institutional premise.
