# Ex-WhatsApp cybersecurity head says Meta endangered billions of users

- Score: 344 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45174221) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/sep/08/meta-user-data-lawsuit-whatsapp

### TL;DR

Former WhatsApp security chief Attaullah Baig sued Meta, alleging retaliation after he reported deficient controls affecting three billion users. His complaint claims roughly 1,500 engineers could access or move contact information, IP addresses, and profile photos without adequate detection or audit trails, and that more than 100,000 accounts were hacked daily without sufficient remediation. Meta denies the characterization, says Baig was dismissed for poor performance, and notes a labor regulator rejected his initial retaliation complaint. HN discussion sharply disputed both access culture and risk interpretation.
