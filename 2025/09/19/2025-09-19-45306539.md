# Ask HN: Has anyone else been unemployed for over two years?

- Score: 260 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45306539) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45306539

### TL;DR

An Ask HN thread collects deeply varied accounts of unemployment lasting roughly two to three years. One experienced engineering leader describes hundreds of applications, few interviews, ghosting, and selling a home; others used the interval for health, programming projects, or a small website business. Another commenter faces incarceration and bipolar disorder, while an MMO developer worries employers discount self-directed work. The thread offers coping strategies and solidarity, but its personal stories cannot establish broader labor-market trends.

### Comment pulse

- Several commenters recommend referrals, résumé review, interview coaching, exercise, and a daily routine.
- Experiences range from deliberate sabbaticals to financial strain, legal trouble, and accepting worse work after a long search.

### LLM perspective

- View: The thread’s value is qualitative: it exposes multiple paths through prolonged unemployment without implying a representative pattern.
- Impact: Long gaps combine career uncertainty with housing, health, identity, and financial pressures that generic job-search advice misses.
- Watch next: Whether targeted referrals, professional coaching, or better framing of independent projects improves these commenters’ interview conversion.
