# Trevor Milton's Nikola case dropped by SEC following Trump pardon

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45302220) | Link: https://eletric-vehicles.com/nikola/trevor-miltons-nikola-case-dropped-by-sec-following-trump-pardon/

### TL;DR

The SEC dropped its civil fraud case against Nikola founder Trevor Milton with prejudice after President Trump's unconditional pardon erased Milton's four-year criminal sentence for deceiving investors. Nikola, already in Chapter 11, disputes Milton's separate $69 million legal-fee indemnification claim, while creditors sought investigation of assets potentially available to satisfy company debts. Milton portrays the prosecution as corrupt and himself as innocent. Commenters rejected that account, recalled his implausible technical claims, and alleged political favoritism, including concern over his lawyer's family connection.
