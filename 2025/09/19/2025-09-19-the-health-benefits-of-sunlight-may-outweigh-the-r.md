# The health benefits of sunlight may outweigh the risk of skin cancer

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45298034) | Link: https://www.economist.com/science-and-technology/2025/09/17/the-health-benefits-of-sunlight-may-outweigh-the-risk-of-skin-cancer

### TL;DR

Large observational studies in light-skinned British and Swedish populations associate greater sunlight exposure with lower all-cause mortality despite increased skin-cancer risk. Researchers propose mechanisms beyond vitamin D, including nitric-oxide-driven blood-pressure reduction and immune effects, but confounding remains substantial and several pathways lack proof. The article argues advice may eventually vary by latitude and skin color, while retaining one firm boundary: avoid sunburn. Commenters strongly questioned exposure proxies and controls, emphasizing established UV DNA damage and continued sunscreen and screening.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The evidence supports investigating an exposure optimum, not replacing established skin-cancer precautions with generalized sun-seeking advice.
- Impact: Better individualized guidance could balance cardiovascular, immune, eye-development, vitamin-D, and cancer risks across skin types and climates.
- Watch next: Prioritize causal mechanisms, stronger confounder controls, dose-response data, diverse populations, and outcomes after Australia's revised guidance.
