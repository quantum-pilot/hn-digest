# David Lynch LA House

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45296638) | Link: https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/david-lynch-house-los-angeles-for-sale

### TL;DR

David Lynch’s Los Angeles estate is listed for $15 million, combining five contiguous parcels and seven structures across 2.3 acres. The approximately 11,000-square-foot compound has ten bedrooms and eleven bathrooms. Lynch began with Lloyd Wright’s Beverly Johnson House in 1987, then added a pool house, a Brutalist residence, studio facilities, guest space, terraces, and walkways. Parts housed Asymmetrical Productions and appeared in Lost Highway, making the listing simultaneously a real-estate offering, architectural assemblage, workplace, and film location.
