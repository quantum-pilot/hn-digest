# Trump to impose $100k fee for H-1B worker visas, White House says

- Score: 588 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45305845) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/trump-mulls-adding-new-100000-fee-h-1b-visas-bloomberg-news-reports-2025-09-19/

### TL;DR

The Trump administration said employers would pay $100,000 annually for each H-1B worker, though administration details and legal authority were still unsettled. The program admits 65,000 specialized workers plus 20,000 advanced-degree holders yearly; Indians received 71% of recent approvals. Officials framed the fee as protecting and training Americans, while investors warned it could exclude startups, deter global talent, and move work abroad. HN split between viewing the charge as a blunt shutdown and a filter against low-wage outsourcing abuse.

### Comment pulse

- Reform advocates targeted employer lock-in and wage suppression, but disagreed whether a fee alone protects workers or merely rewards wealthy companies.
- Critics predicted offshoring and lost talent — counterpoint: others argued truly essential hires remain affordable and offshoring was already cheaper.

### LLM perspective

- View: A flat fee selects for employer resources and expensive roles, not necessarily worker scarcity, quality, or fair treatment.
- Impact: Smaller firms and ordinary skilled migrants face greater exclusion, while large incumbents may gain relative hiring and compliance advantages.
- Watch next: Clarify annual versus application payment, existing-holder treatment, exemptions, court challenges, wage effects, and geographic hiring shifts.
