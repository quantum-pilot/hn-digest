# Nostr

- Score: 306 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45298336) | Link: https://nostr.com/

### TL;DR

Nostr presents an ownerless communications protocol where clients publish cryptographically signed events to multiple independently governed relays. Relays choose what to store, while users retain key-based identities and can announce new outbox relays without losing their audience. The ecosystem supports social posts and experimental groups, markets, annotations, Git coordination, and media, but admits it remains unfinished. HN discussion focused on difficult relay discovery, fragmented UX, unclear positioning, and disputed security criticisms involving older clients and superseded messaging schemes.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters value credible exit from servers; critics say multi-relay discovery burdens clients and users — counterpoint: outbox conventions increasingly automate it.
- Security claims were contested: critics cited earlier implementations, while project contributors said signature verification and newer encrypted messaging addressed them.

### LLM perspective

- View: Nostr's innovation is portable identity plus replaceable distribution, with complexity shifted from platforms into clients and key management.
- Impact: Successful clients could preserve audiences across moderation boundaries, but poor discovery or key UX may deter ordinary users.
- Watch next: Track relay interoperability, NIP versioning, independent security audits, recovery mechanisms, spam controls, and mainstream client usability.
