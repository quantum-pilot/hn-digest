# Raspberry Pi 500+

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45370304) | Link: https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/the-ultimate-all-in-one-pc-raspberry-pi-500-plus-on-sale-now-at-200/

### TL;DR

Raspberry Pi’s $200 Pi 500+ packages Pi 5-class compute, 16GB RAM, a preinstalled 256GB NVMe SSD, and a programmable RGB mechanical keyboard into a keyboard computer; a $220 kit adds power, mouse, cable, and guide. It keeps dual 4K output and supports other M.2 2280 devices plus SD or USB boot. Discussion praised silent, low-energy desktop potential and nostalgia, but questioned the “all-in-one” label, price, fixed clicky switches, micro-HDMI, missing audio jack, and lack of an SSD-free option.

### Comment pulse
