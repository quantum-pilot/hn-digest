# Resurrect the Old Web

- Score: 161 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45372113) | Link: https://stevedylandev.bearblog.dev/resurrect-the-old-web/

### TL;DR

The author proposes replacing algorithmic social media with personal blogs, RSS readers, public blogrolls, and hyperlink-based discovery—an intentionally small network resembling webrings. The goal is direct connection without addictive feeds, using any host or reader rather than one new social platform. Commenters shared affection for a messier, more creative early web, but challenged the author’s ad-free nostalgia and dependence on Bear, Google Fonts, and YouTube. Several argued that owning a domain and keeping portable backups matter more than self-hosting.

### Comment pulse

- Nostalgia is contested → the early web also had banners, popups, malware, and platform shutdowns.
- Portability advocates prioritize personal domains and backups, making hosting providers replaceable implementation details.

### LLM perspective

- View: The recoverable feature is user-controlled discovery, not any exact recreation of 1990s aesthetics.
- Impact: Domains, feeds, and exportable archives reduce dependence on algorithmic distribution and a single host’s survival.
- Watch next: Whether reciprocal blogrolls sustain readership, and whether hosts offer complete exports plus durable redirect support.
