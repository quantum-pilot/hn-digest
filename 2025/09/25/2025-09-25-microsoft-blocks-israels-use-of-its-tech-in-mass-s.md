# Microsoft blocks Israel’s use of its tech in mass surveillance of Palestinians

- Score: 752 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45373564) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/sep/25/microsoft-blocks-israels-use-of-its-technology-in-mass-surveillance-of-palestinians

### TL;DR

Microsoft disabled some Azure storage and AI services used by Israel’s Unit 8200 after its inquiry found terms violations. The Guardian says the unit had stored up to 8,000TB of intercepted Palestinian calls and used Azure to analyze them; sources said the data was moved and may go to AWS. The cutoff does not end Microsoft’s wider IDF relationship. Commenters debated whether customer privacy reasonably limited Microsoft’s visibility, or whether earlier employee warnings made its claimed ignorance implausible.
