# Demand for human radiologists is at an all-time high

- Score: 234 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45372335) | Link: https://www.worksinprogress.news/p/why-ai-isnt-replacing-radiologists

### TL;DR

Despite more than 700 FDA-cleared radiology AI models, radiologist demand and pay remain high. The article attributes this to narrow task coverage, performance drops across hospitals, biased or sanitized datasets, regulatory and malpractice barriers to autonomy, and radiologists’ broader clinical work. Earlier mammography aids sometimes increased callbacks or encouraged automation bias. Cheaper, faster imaging may also increase scan volume faster than productivity. Commenters agreed liability keeps humans signing reports, but disputed how much radiologists do beyond interpretation and whether better models will eventually remove that bottleneck.
