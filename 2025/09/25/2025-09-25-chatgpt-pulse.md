# ChatGPT Pulse

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45375477) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-pulse/

### TL;DR

ChatGPT Pulse is a mobile preview for Pro users that performs nightly research from memory, chat history, feedback, and optional Gmail or Google Calendar context, then presents temporary personalized cards the next day. Users can curate topics, rate results, save cards, or continue them as chats. OpenAI acknowledges irrelevant or outdated suggestions and plans broader app connections and timely actions. Commenters split between finding proactive assistance useful and seeing recycled conversations, while raising privacy, dependency, persuasion, and ecosystem-integration concerns.

### Comment pulse

- Personalization needs sensitive context → potential utility rises with connected data, alongside privacy and manipulation risks.
- Early usefulness appears uneven → some saw stale rehashes, while others argued active steering is essential for AI value.
- Platform integration becomes strategic → proactive assistants compete through calendars, email, memory, and workflows, not models alone.

### LLM perspective
