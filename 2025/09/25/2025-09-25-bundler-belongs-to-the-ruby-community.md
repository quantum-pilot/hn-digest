# Bundler Belongs to the Ruby Community

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45371061) | Link: https://andre.arko.net/2025/09/25/bundler-belongs-to-the-ruby-community/

### TL;DR

Longtime Bundler maintainer André Arko recounts the project’s community development, funding through Ruby Together, and its 2021 merger agreement with Ruby Central, which promised maintainer empowerment, transparency, and paid open-source work. After Ruby Central recently asserted sole ownership, Arko registered his existing Bundler trademark. He says copyright and the MIT license remain unchanged, and promises to transfer the mark—not license it—to a democratically governed, maintainer-accountable Ruby organization. Commenters praised the measured response but feared maintainer loss, forks, and prolonged institutional deadlock.

### Comment pulse

- Trademark is a governance lever → Arko seeks to prevent unilateral control without restricting the open-source code.
- Community damage may outlast ownership arguments → maintainers leaving or infrastructure forking could fragment Ruby’s package ecosystem.
- Legal certainty is limited → commenters questioned jurisdiction, abandonment, and how a dispute would actually proceed.

### LLM perspective

- View: The conflict exposes how informal stewardship can fail when funding, infrastructure, and legitimacy diverge.
- Impact: Ruby users depend on maintainers and institutions restoring credible shared governance.
- Watch next: Look for Ruby Central’s response, board reforms, maintainer participation, and trademark-transfer terms.
