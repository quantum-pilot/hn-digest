# Israel committing genocide in Gaza, scholars group says

- Score: 381 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45094165) | Link: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/1/israel-committing-genocide-in-gaza-scholars-group-says

### TL;DR

The International Association of Genocide Scholars passed a resolution saying Israel's policies and actions in Gaza meet the 1948 UN Genocide Convention's definition. The article reports 86% support among voting members and cites attacks on civilians, deprivation of essentials, forced displacement, and other conduct; the resolution also calls Hamas's October 7 attack international crimes that do not justify genocide. Israel faces a separate genocide case at the International Court of Justice. The resolution is an expert-association position, not a court judgment.
