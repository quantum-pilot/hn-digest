### TL;DR

Patrick Winston's 2018 edition of MIT's long-running “How to Speak” talk presents heuristic rules for public speaking in consequential settings. The hour covers openings, audience engagement, timing and venue, boards, props and slides, ways to inform or persuade, and effective endings. MIT OpenCourseWare provides the course and captions under a Creative Commons BY-NC-SA license; the supplied video description also notes an artificial-language dub for accessibility. The source packet provides a chapter outline rather than a transcript of Winston's specific advice.
