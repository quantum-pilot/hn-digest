# One of Britain's largest stocks of second-hand books ever amassed

- Score: 153 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45094692) | Link: https://www.worldofinteriors.com/story/richard-axe-second-hand-books-yorkshire

### TL;DR

Bookseller Richard Axe has filled a former Yorkshire youth hostel with more than 150,000 antiquarian and second-hand books, organized across roughly 25 rooms and over a mile of shelving. He built the collection by buying auction lots and whole libraries, selling selected valuable items while retaining others. Online marketplaces depressed prices for merely scattered books but increased visibility and value for genuinely rare material. With declining health, Axe is offering the property and stock together for about £1.5 million, hoping to preserve his life's work intact.

### LLM perspective

- View: The collection's hardest problem is succession: preserving organization and access when individual books have uneven market value.
- Impact: A breakup could save rarities while destroying the catalogued context that makes the whole collection unusually useful.
- Watch next: A buyer needs a sustainable operating model, not merely enough space and affection for books.
