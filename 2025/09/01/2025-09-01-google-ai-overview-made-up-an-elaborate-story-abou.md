# Google AI Overview made up an elaborate story about me

- Score: 698 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45092925) | Link: https://bsky.app/profile/bennjordan.bsky.social/post/3lxojrbessk2z

### TL;DR

Benn Jordan reported receiving questions about his position on Israel after a Google AI Overview apparently attributed another creator’s video and trip to him, contradicting his stated support for Palestinian statehood. The supplied post contains only Jordan’s brief reaction; the detailed reconstruction comes from commenters, who identified a similarly titled Ryan McBeth video and speculated that search terms involving Israel and the country Jordan caused the misattribution. Discussion emphasized reputational harm from plausible, prominently displayed summaries.

- Experts warned that polished summaries are most dangerous when mostly accurate, since unfamiliar readers cannot spot the consequential error.
