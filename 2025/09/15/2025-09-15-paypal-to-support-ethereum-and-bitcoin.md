# PayPal to support Ethereum and Bitcoin

- Score: 419 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45249915) | Link: https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2025-09-15-PayPal-Ushers-in-a-New-Era-of-Peer-to-Peer-Payments,-Reimagining-How-Money-Moves-to-Anyone,-Anywhere

### TL;DR

PayPal introduced one-time payment and request links that can be shared in messages, expire after ten days if unclaimed, and can be canceled or followed with reminders in the app. It also plans to add Bitcoin, Ethereum, PYUSD, and other assets to US peer-to-peer transfers across PayPal, Venmo, and compatible wallets. Commenters debated whether crypto benefits from another intermediary, balancing familiar recovery and usability against fees, account-freeze experiences, custody risk, and protections that may differ by jurisdiction.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: PayPal is making crypto transfers familiar by reintroducing the intermediary that cryptocurrency was meant to avoid.
- Impact: Simpler access may broaden use while concentrating custody, fee, and dispute risks in one provider.
- Watch next: Watch the fee schedule, supported wallets, transfer safeguards, jurisdiction-specific protections, and domain-authentication messaging.
