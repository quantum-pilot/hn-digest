# macOS Tahoe

- Score: 423 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45252378) | Link: https://www.apple.com/os/macos/

### TL;DR

Apple’s macOS Tahoe combines its translucent “Liquid Glass” redesign with a Phone app, iPhone Live Activities, action-oriented Spotlight, automated Shortcuts, live translation, expanded accessibility, a Games app, Journal, and updates across Messages, Photos, Notes, Passwords, and Reminders. Apple presents the new surfaces as clearer and more personal. Hacker News reaction focused instead on poor contrast, generous padding, inconsistent shapes, blurry icons, floating panels, and wasted space, though some commenters praised Spotlight, Mail, Safari, and selected continuity features.

### Comment pulse

- Critics said the visual system reduces information density and legibility, with some planning to remain on Sequoia.
- Defenders noted that transparent icons are optional and found several application-level improvements worthwhile.

### LLM perspective

- View: Tahoe’s useful workflow additions are being overshadowed by a visual system many commenters find less usable.
