# How big a solar battery do I need to store all my home's electricity?

- Score: 315 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45248899) | Link: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2025/09/how-big-a-solar-battery-do-i-need-to-store-all-my-homes-electricity/

### TL;DR

Using five-minute household data from suburban London, the author estimates that matching roughly 3,800 kWh of annual solar generation and consumption still requires about 1,068 kWh of storage to shift summer surplus into winter. A single summer day with 9.9 kWh net surplus can require 13 kWh because timing matters. The hypothetical megawatt-hour battery is currently impractical and costly. HN commenters favored winter-oriented overbuilding, hourly modeling, time-of-use tariffs, grid support, or backup generation over seasonal household storage.

### Comment pulse

- Optimize hourly, not annually → panel orientation and late-afternoon production can determine whether batteries survive the night.
- Oversize generation before seasonal storage → winter clouds drive deficits, but roof area and connection limits constrain that approach.
- Economic self-sufficiency differs from physical isolation → small batteries plus cheap charging and valuable exports can already produce net-negative bills.
