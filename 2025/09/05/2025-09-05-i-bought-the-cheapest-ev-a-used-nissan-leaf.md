# I bought the cheapest EV, a used Nissan Leaf

- Score: 470 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45136103) | Link: https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2025/i-bought-cheapest-ev-used-nissan-leaf

### TL;DR

Jeff Geerling bought a 2023 Nissan Leaf SV Plus with roughly 36,000 miles and about 94% reported battery health. After trade-in adjustment and an expected used-EV credit, he calculates an effective $11,000 cost. The Leaf fits his short daily driving, home charging, and occasional-road-trip profile, delivering quiet operation, strong low-speed torque, one-pedal driving, and lower routine maintenance. Its compromises include an uncooled battery, fading CHAdeMO fast-charging support, fragmented charging systems, limited range, bulky adapters, and awkward controls. He recommends matching an EV to circumstances rather than treating it as universal.

### Comment pulse
