# OpenAI eats jobs, then offers to help you find a new one at Walmart

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45137658) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/09/05/openai_jobs_board/

### TL;DR

OpenAI plans a jobs platform where workers can present AI skills learned through its Academy to participating employers, with Walmart among the launch partners. Application chief Fidji Simo acknowledges that AI will disrupt work and says OpenAI can help people adapt, though the company gave no further detail. The article questions whether its certifications will carry labor-market value and notes potential competition with Microsoft-owned LinkedIn. Commenters challenge the premise that AI has already eliminated many jobs and reject dismissive assumptions about Walmart's technology and retail roles.

### Comment pulse

- Readers describe job effects as diffuse: fewer backfills, thinner junior pipelines, merged teams, outsourcing, and productivity-driven headcount reductions.
- Others see an uncomfortable conflict when the company promoting workplace disruption also certifies and brokers the workers adapting to it.
