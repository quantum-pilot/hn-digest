# The Universe Within 12.5 Light Years

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45144337) | Link: http://www.atlasoftheuniverse.com/12lys.html

### TL;DR

An educational map catalogs 33 stars within 12.5 light-years of the Sun and emphasizes that most nearby stars are faint red dwarfs. It profiles systems including Proxima and Alpha Centauri, Barnard's Star, Sirius, Epsilon Eridani, Procyon, Tau Ceti, and several binaries or triples, pairing distances with stellar types and orbital notes. The page gives an intuitive neighborhood view, but its age is unclear; commenters say later discoveries such as Luhman 16 are absent, so it should not be treated as a current census.
