# Britain jumps into bed with Palantir in £1.5B defense pact

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45313793) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/09/20/uk_palantir_defense_pact/

### TL;DR

The UK announced a defense partnership under which Palantir plans to make Britain its European defense headquarters, invest up to £750 million, and help develop AI systems for intelligence, planning, decision support, and targeting. Officials said the broader arrangement could unlock £1.5 billion and create up to 350 jobs, with work falling under the military’s Digital Targeting Web. Commenters questioned strategic dependence on a U.S. contractor, data jurisdiction, and whether procurement crowds out domestic capability; others noted Britain lacks an obvious comparable supplier.

### Comment pulse

- Investment claims have tradeoffs → upfront capital and jobs could be offset by future payments and weakened domestic development.
- Procurement structure shapes competition → commenters favored open-source defaults and contracts small enough for British SMEs.
