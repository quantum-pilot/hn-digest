# Trumpcard (Official US Government Website)

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45308778) | Link: https://trumpcard.gov/

### TL;DR

An official U.S. government site advertises a Trump Gold Card: after a nonrefundable processing fee, vetting, and a $1 million gift, approved applicants may receive permanent residence through available EB-1 or EB-2 visas. A corporate version requires $2 million per sponsored employee and permits sponsorship transfer; a planned $5 million Platinum Card promises up to 270 U.S. days annually without tax on foreign income. HN reactions mix disbelief with debate over legality, branding, fairness, precedent, and tax advantages.

### Comment pulse

- Wealth-based migration has international precedents → supporters accept vetted investment routes, while critics oppose letting rich applicants bypass ordinary queues.
- Authority remains unclear → commenters question whether existing visa classifications permit the advertised benefits without legislation.
- Branding undermines institutional neutrality → readers object to placing a sitting president’s name on an official government program.
