# Things managers do that leaders never would

- Score: 115 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45309512) | Link: https://simonsinek.com/stories/5-things-managers-do-that-leaders-never-would-according-to-simon/

### TL;DR

Simon Sinek’s site contrasts managers with leaders across five behaviors: hoarding versus sharing information, weaponizing versus flexing policy, firing quickly versus coaching dignified exits, avoiding versus initiating hard conversations, and rewarding compliance versus dissent. The prescription prioritizes transparency, empathy, candor, and constructive challenge. HN commenters find parts recognizable but reject the binary framing, arguing competent leaders disclose selectively, enforce necessary rules, remove harmful performers, and reward justified dissent—all while navigating constraints imposed by their own managers.

### Comment pulse

- The categories are false dichotomies → effective leadership balances context, compliance, coaching, and team protection rather than always choosing one extreme.
- Source credibility divides readers → critics call the advice motivational platitudes detached from operating experience.
- Dissent requires craft → disagreement succeeds when diplomatic, evidence-based, and paired with alternatives rather than obstruction.

### LLM perspective

- View: The useful principle is situational judgment, which the manager-versus-leader labels obscure.
- Impact: Literal adoption could produce oversharing, inconsistent policy, retained underperformance, or performative disagreement.
