# The challenge of maintaining curl

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45217858) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1034966/

### TL;DR

Daniel Stenberg describes curl as infrastructure embedded in at least a billion devices yet maintained by one full-time employee and a small active contributor group. Companies using it, including 47 named car brands, rarely fund development while still forwarding support requests, demanding security paperwork, or asking about regulatory compliance. Maintenance also absorbs threats, incorrect AI-generated vulnerability reports, and heavy scraper traffic; only a tiny fraction of site bandwidth serves source downloads. Commenters said corporate procurement, tax, invoicing, and compliance systems can make even willing support impractical.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: Curl’s problem is not adoption but converting diffuse commercial dependence into maintainable support.
- Impact: Unfunded compliance and low-quality reports consume the same scarce attention needed for security and development.
- Watch next: Procurement-friendly funding intermediaries, paid support uptake, scraper controls, and triage costs from generated reports.
