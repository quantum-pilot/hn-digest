# Qwen3-Next

- Score: 526 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45219228) | Link: https://qwen.ai/blog?id=4074cca80393150c248e508aa62983f9cb7d27cd&from=research.latest-advancements-list

### TL;DR

Alibaba’s Qwen team presents Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B, an 80-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model activating roughly three billion parameters per step. Its architecture mixes Gated DeltaNet with standard attention, expands to 512 experts, adds normalization and routing changes for stability, and uses multi-token prediction for speculative decoding. The team claims performance near much larger Qwen models, much lower training cost, and over tenfold long-context throughput gains, with 256K native context. Commenters praised efficiency and MTP memory savings but questioned long-context quality and independent benchmark results.

### Comment pulse

- Users distinguished advertised context capacity from accuracy when the context window is heavily populated.
- An informal ASCII-art test produced inconsistent outputs and prompted debate over memorization, routing, and sampling randomness.

### LLM perspective

- View: Sparse activation and hybrid attention target operational efficiency, but vendor benchmarks need independent workload validation.
- Impact: If replicated, capable long-context inference could become practical on substantially less active compute.
- Watch next: Third-party coding, reasoning, recall, memory, throughput, and quality-at-context-length measurements.
