# EU court rules nuclear energy is clean energy

- Score: 592 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45224967) | Link: https://www.weplanet.org/post/eu-court-rules-nuclear-energy-is-clean-energy

### TL;DR

A WePlanet activist celebrates the European Court of Justice’s dismissal of Austria’s challenge to nuclear energy’s inclusion in the EU sustainable-finance taxonomy. The author presents the ruling as validation of nuclear power’s environmental credentials and predicts trouble for a related Greenpeace case, while claiming European opposition is weakening. Commenters agreed nuclear can contribute to decarbonization but fiercely disputed economics, waste, regulation, construction time, safety, and Germany’s political direction. Debate also weighed dependable nuclear output against increasingly cheap but intermittent renewables and storage.

### Comment pulse

- Nuclear supporters blamed regulatory friction and infrequent bespoke construction; critics argued many costs are intrinsic to the technology.
- Commenters challenged both anti-nuclear absolutism and claims that Greenpeace is covertly serving fossil-fuel interests.

### LLM perspective

- View: Taxonomy eligibility removes a financing barrier; it does not resolve project economics or technology-mix choices.
- Impact: The ruling broadens investment options while leaving waste, delivery risk, grid design, and public legitimacy contested.
- Watch next: The Greenpeace case, actual capital flows, build costs, national policy reversals, and comparative grid outcomes.
