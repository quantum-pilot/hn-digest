# Ships are sailing with fake insurance from the Norwegian Ro Marine

- Score: 219 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45221996) | Link: https://www.nrk.no/vestland/xl/over-100-ships-have-sailed-without-legitimate-insurance-from-the-norwegian-company-ro-marine-1.17565216

### TL;DR

NRK and Dossier Center report that more than 100 ships used illegitimate insurance documents from Ro Marine, a Norwegian company lacking authorization to sell insurance. Their evidence links many policies to Russian oil transport, sanctioned entities, and other risky cargo, though customers offered differing accounts of whether they knew coverage was fake. Ro Marine’s Russian owner, two Norwegians, and a Bulgarian were charged over forgery and unauthorized insurance; the accused denied or did not answer allegations. Commenters focused on sanctions leakage, flag-state verification failures, and slow enforcement.
