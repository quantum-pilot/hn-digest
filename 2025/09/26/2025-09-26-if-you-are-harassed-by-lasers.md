# If you are harassed by lasers

- Score: 100 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45389965) | Link: https://www.laserpointersafety.com/harassment.html

### TL;DR

This safety guide distinguishes observable laser harassment from camera artifacts and unexplained sensations. If a visible beam is deliberately aimed at eyes or head, it advises protecting vision, seeking medical guidance after exposure, and contacting authorities when warranted. But apparent beams in recordings often come from lens flare, sensor blooming, dirt, insects, webs, or headlights. Light-blocking barriers provide a simple test because ordinary visible and infrared laser light cannot pass through walls. Persistent flashes or heat without an observable source warrant medical evaluation, not assumed targeting.
