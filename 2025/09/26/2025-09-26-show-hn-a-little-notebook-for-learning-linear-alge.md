# Show HN: A little notebook for learning linear algebra with Python

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45384617) | Link: https://little-book-of.github.io/linear-algebra/books/en-US/lab.html

### TL;DR

This extensive Python notebook teaches linear algebra through executable NumPy and visualization exercises. Ten chapters progress from vectors, geometry, and matrices through linear systems, vector spaces, transformations, determinants, eigenvalues, orthogonality, least squares, QR, SVD, PCA, conditioning, and applications. Worked code, plots, “Try It Yourself” prompts, and final problems connect concepts to image compression, regression, PageRank, dynamical systems, and overdetermined fitting. The material favors concrete computation and intuition, though commenters questioned whether it adequately develops abstract vector-space definitions and flagged a potentially misleading span visualization.
