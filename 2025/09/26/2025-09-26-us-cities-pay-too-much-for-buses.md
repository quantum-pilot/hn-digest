# US cities pay too much for buses

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45386578) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-26/us-cities-are-paying-too-much-for-new-transit-buses

### TL;DR

A reported study of roughly 64,000 U.S. transit buses bought from 2000 through 2024 finds high, inconsistent prices: average diesel buses cost $500,000 and electric models $1.1 million, with ostensibly similar procurements sometimes differing twofold. Researchers blame a domestic duopoly, small orders, growing customization, and Buy America restrictions that exclude cheaper foreign suppliers. They propose pooled purchasing, standardized federally favored models, reimbursement caps tied to comparable low-cost purchases, and limited foreign sales before domestic production is required. The article argues savings could improve fleet replacement and service budgets.

### Comment pulse
