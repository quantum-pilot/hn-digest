# Pop OS 24.04 LTS Beta

- Score: 378 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45384481) | Link: https://system76.com/pop/pop-beta/

### TL;DR

System76’s Pop!_OS 24.04 LTS beta introduces its largely feature-complete COSMIC desktop and shifts development toward release-candidate bug fixing. The release uses kernel 6.16.3, Mesa 25.1.5-1, and NVIDIA driver 580, replacing several GNOME applications and Pop!_Shop with COSMIC counterparts. System76 warns users to back up before upgrading, notes that dock favorites and PPAs are not preserved, and lists beta gaps involving cross-protocol drag-and-drop, gaming placement, display shortcuts, accessibility, notification indicators, printing, and Flatpak add-ons.

### Comment pulse

- Early users praised integrated tiling, settings, multi-monitor behavior, responsiveness, and an out-of-box experience requiring fewer assembled components.
- Longtime Pop users criticized the extended 22.04-to-24.04 delay and uncertainty while COSMIC remained unfinished.
