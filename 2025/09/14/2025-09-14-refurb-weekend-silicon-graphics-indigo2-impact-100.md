# Refurb Weekend: Silicon Graphics Indigo² Impact 10000

- Score: 145 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45237717) | Link: http://oldvcr.blogspot.com/2025/09/refurb-weekend-silicon-graphics-indigo.html

### TL;DR

A free purple SGI Indigo2 was restored from uncertain condition to a working IRIX 6.5.22 workstation. The 195 MHz R10000 and Solid IMPACT graphics survived, but the author replaced the failed timekeeper module, incompatible optical drive, unusable disks, and storage with a ZuluSCSI image, then raised RAM from 64 to 192 MB. Diagnostics ultimately passed despite intermittent amber startup behavior. Alongside the repair, the article traces Indigo2, MIPS, IMPACT, IRIX, and SGI’s eventual shift toward Intel-era systems.
