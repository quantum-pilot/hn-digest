# Vibe coding has turned senior devs into 'AI babysitters'

- Score: 88 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45242788) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/14/vibe-coding-has-turned-senior-devs-into-ai-babysitters-but-they-say-its-worth-it/

### TL;DR

Experienced developers interviewed by TechCrunch say AI coding accelerates prototypes, boilerplate, tests, and interface work but shifts substantial effort into requirements, review, correction, and security checks. One founder rebuilt a project after trusting automated review; another estimates spending 30–40% of his time fixing generated code. A Fastly survey reportedly found 95% of nearly 800 developers spend extra time correcting AI output. HN commenters questioned whether the workflow saves time, erodes enjoyment and learning, or simply transfers junior work onto senior reviewers.
