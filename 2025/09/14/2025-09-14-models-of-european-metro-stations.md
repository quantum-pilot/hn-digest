# Models of European metro stations

- Score: 665 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45238055) | Link: http://stations.albertguillaumes.cat/

### TL;DR

An interactive gallery maps and models thousands of metro, tram, and rail stations, pairing three-dimensional layouts with detailed city-by-city explanations. The text compares platform arrangements, mezzanines, depth, construction eras, transfer corridors, cross-platform interchanges, fare control, and how geography or inherited networks shape design. Examples range from Barcelona’s long passages and “Spanish solution” to London’s shallow and deep systems, Parisian labyrinths, and New York’s local-express tracks. For cities the author has not visited, models rely on public drawings, photographs, videos, and procurement materials.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The project reveals stations as layered circulation systems rather than dots on a transit map.
- Impact: Comparable spatial models can help riders, historians, designers, and accessibility researchers understand transfers before entering unfamiliar networks.
- Watch next: Source citations, corrections, accessibility metadata, performance improvements, and coverage beyond the project’s European emphasis.
