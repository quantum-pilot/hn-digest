# Nvidia buys $5B in Intel

- Score: 887 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45288161) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/nvidia-and-intel-announce-jointly-developed-intel-x86-rtx-socs-for-pcs-with-nvidia-graphics-also-custom-nvidia-data-center-x86-processors-nvidia-buys-usd5-billion-in-intel-stock-in-seismic-deal

### TL;DR

Nvidia agreed to buy $5 billion of Intel stock at $23.28 per share, roughly a 5% stake, alongside a multi-generation product partnership. Intel will develop custom x86 data-center CPUs for Nvidia and consumer x86 RTX SoCs combining Intel CPU and Nvidia GPU chiplets through NVLink with shared memory. Timelines, specifications, foundry use, governance, and regulatory approval remain unresolved. HN debate centers on whether the alliance rescues Intel and strengthens x86 or sidelines Arc, Gaudi, Linux-friendly drivers, and GPU competition.

### Comment pulse

- Strategic logic extends beyond consumers → Intel supplies x86 expertise and possible foundry diversification from Nvidia’s concentrated manufacturing exposure.
- Kaby Lake-G offers a warning → Intel’s earlier AMD package suffered discontinuation and fragmented driver responsibility.
