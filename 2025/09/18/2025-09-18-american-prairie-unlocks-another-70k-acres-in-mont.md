# American Prairie unlocks another 70k acres in Montana

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45291132) | Link: https://earthhope.substack.com/p/victory-for-public-access-american

### TL;DR

American Prairie acquired Montana’s 70,000-acre Anchor Ranch, listed at $35 million, expanding its holdings beyond 600,000 acres and reopening a 3.8-mile private section of Bullwhacker Road. The road had blocked western access to roughly 50,000 acres of the Upper Missouri River Breaks National Monument. The nonprofit plans habitat restoration and a larger bison range within a proposed 3.2-million-acre reserve. HN readers welcome conservation and access, while noting local ranching concerns, liability, and competing property-rights traditions.
