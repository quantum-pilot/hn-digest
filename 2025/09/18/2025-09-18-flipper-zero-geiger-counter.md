# Flipper Zero Geiger Counter

- Score: 240 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45289453) | Link: https://kasiin.top/blog/2025-08-04-flipper_zero_geiger_counter_module/

### TL;DR

A third-party Flipper Zero module displays Geiger counts per second and minute, graphs and records readings to CSV, and converts measurements into several dose units while warning those conversions are imprecise. Its J305 tube detects beta and gamma radiation, not alpha. A companion app hashes detection timestamps into balanced coin or dice rolls. HN experts praise the educational build but stress that cheap, uncompensated tubes can misstate dose, saturate in hazardous fields, poorly assess radon, and should not replace calibrated safety instruments.

### Comment pulse

- Detector choice follows purpose → cheap tubes suit novelty and education; scintillators add sensitivity, spectroscopy, or higher usable ranges at greater cost.
- DIY readings require caution → emergency decisions demand calibrated, energy-compensated equipment and operator knowledge.
- Physical construction needs improvement → exposed high-voltage parts and the tube should receive a protective enclosure.
