# You Had No Taste Before AI

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45288551) | Link: https://matthewsanabria.dev/posts/you-had-no-taste-before-ai/

### TL;DR

The author argues AI did not create mediocre work; it exposed people who already lacked critical judgment. He defines useful taste as recognizing contextual fit and quality, iterating beyond first drafts, and respecting ethical boundaries. Deep domain expertise distinguishes refined from merely functional output, while breadth helps users judge across the many domains AI crosses. Suggested practice includes comparing one's best and worst work, studying excellent examples, and iterating deliberately. Commenters disputed whether taste means aesthetics, autonomous judgment, quality standards, or shifting social consensus.
