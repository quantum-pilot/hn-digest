# KDE is now my favorite desktop

- Score: 765 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45288690) | Link: https://kokada.dev/blog/kde-is-now-my-favorite-desktop/

### TL;DR

After a week using KDE on a NixOS gaming PC, the author prefers it even to Windows and macOS for its speed, feature depth, and integrated controls. Examples include detailed Wi-Fi information, screenshot cropping and blurring, per-application window rules, Flatpak permissions, hardware health, and one-click sleep inhibition. A dual-display taskbar mistake was the notable flaw. HN users largely agree Plasma’s long emphasis on polish and built-in customization has paid off, though some still find its visual design inconsistent.
