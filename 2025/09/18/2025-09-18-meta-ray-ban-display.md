# Meta Ray-Ban Display

- Score: 614 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45283306) | Link: https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-connect-2025/

### TL;DR

Meta announced $799 Ray-Ban Display glasses bundled with an EMG Neural Band, launching September 30 in limited U.S. stores. A 69-gram frame combines camera, audio, AI, and a private monocular color display for messages, navigation, captions, translation, calls, and photo previews. Subtle muscle signals control the interface; mixed-use battery life is up to six hours, or 30 with the case. HN readers admire the wristband and miniaturization but raise recording, AI-training, bystander-privacy, proprietary charging, and software concerns.
