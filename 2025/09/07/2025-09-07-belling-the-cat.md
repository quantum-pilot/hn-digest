# Belling the Cat

- Score: 225 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45157906) | Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belling_the_Cat

### TL;DR

“Belling the Cat” tells of mice who endorse attaching a warning bell to a dangerous cat, then discover nobody will accept the risk of doing it. The medieval fable, often but inaccurately treated as classical Aesop, became an idiom for plans that sound desirable yet lack a feasible executor. Across later political, literary, artistic, and musical adaptations, its emphasis shifted, but the durable lesson remains the gap between collective agreement, enforcement, and actual implementation.
