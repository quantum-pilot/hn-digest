# I am giving up on Intel and have bought an AMD Ryzen 9950X3D

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45155986) | Link: https://michael.stapelberg.ch/posts/2025-09-07-bye-intel-hi-amd-9950x3d/

### TL;DR

After a replacement Intel Core Ultra 9 285K system also failed, the author moved his Linux desktop to an AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D and a new AM5 motherboard. He cannot isolate whether the failed part was CPU or board, nor prove a four-hour batch workload caused it. His benchmarks show AMD slightly faster in two compilation tasks but slightly slower in one test suite. Measured idle power rose from 46W to 55W, with household consumption increasing by roughly 1–2 kWh daily.

### Comment pulse

- Readers disputed the failure diagnosis, citing cooling, motherboard settings, ventilation, RAM, power delivery, and broader platform instability.
- Several recommended ECC memory, though motherboard support, visibility, and price remain inconsistent.
