# Show HN: I'm a dermatologist and I vibe coded a skin cancer learning app

- Score: 426 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45157020) | Link: https://molecheck.info/

### TL;DR

A dermatologist built an educational quiz using real lesion photographs: users mark each image as concerning or not and receive feedback. The creator repeatedly says it is not a diagnostic tool, but a way to teach when contacting a doctor may be appropriate. Commenters liked the rapid learning loop while questioning the apparently high share of malignant examples, limited explanations, and possible false confidence. Suggestions included clearer diagnostic reasoning, progressive difficulty, realistic prevalence, and separate designs for patients, primary-care clinicians, and medical students.

### Comment pulse

- The creator says the binary choice intentionally mirrors a patient’s practical decision, not a formal diagnosis.
- Clinicians and users requested explanations and more representative case balance.

### LLM perspective

- View: The quiz is strongest as pattern-awareness training, provided its educational boundary remains unmistakable.
- Impact: Poor calibration could increase anxiety or false reassurance, especially if users treat scores as clinical advice.
- Watch next: Whether explanations, audience-specific curricula, and representative case distributions improve learning without implying diagnostic reliability.
