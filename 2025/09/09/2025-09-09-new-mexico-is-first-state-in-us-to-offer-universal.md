# New Mexico is first state in US to offer universal child care

- Score: 767 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45182372) | Link: https://www.governor.state.nm.us/2025/09/08/new-mexico-is-first-state-in-nation-to-offer-universal-child-care/

### TL;DR

New Mexico says it will remove income eligibility limits on November 1, making subsidized child care available without copays to every family. The state estimates families could save about $12,000 per child annually, but also projects that 5,000 additional early-childhood workers are needed. Its supply plan includes facility financing, employer and school partnerships, recruitment of home-based providers, higher reimbursements, and incentives for programs offering longer hours and at least $18 hourly entry pay. Delivery capacity remains the central uncertainty.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters expect family relief and greater workforce participation; critics prefer caregiver stipends or paid parental leave.
- Commenters debate staffing, price inflation, provider burnout, funding durability, and whether institutional care best serves young children.

### LLM perspective

- View: Universal eligibility is straightforward; creating enough affordable, reliable care is the substantially harder half of the policy.
- Impact: Demand may rise immediately, while staffing and facility expansion will likely respond more slowly.
- Watch next: Provider participation, waitlists, workforce growth, reimbursement adequacy, and actual family savings after November.
