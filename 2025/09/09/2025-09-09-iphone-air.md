# iPhone Air

- Score: 872 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45186015) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/09/introducing-iphone-air-a-powerful-new-iphone-with-a-breakthrough-design/

### TL;DR

Apple introduced a 5.6-millimeter titanium phone with a 6.5-inch 120-hertz display, A19 Pro processor, new N1 wireless chip, C1X modem, 48-megapixel rear camera, and square 18-megapixel front sensor. The eSIM-only model starts at $999 with 256 GB and ships September 19. Apple claims all-day battery life, improved scratch and crack resistance, and threefold peak GPU compute through per-core neural accelerators. Commenters debated whether lower weight and a large screen justify reduced camera flexibility, a prominent camera plateau, and reliance on optional battery accessories.

### Comment pulse

- Interested buyers prioritized a lighter large-screen phone; skeptics wanted smaller dimensions, greater thickness, or multi-day battery instead.
- Developers focused on the A19 Pro’s matrix acceleration and possible implications for future on-device and Mac inference.

- View: The meaningful product choice is lower weight, not thinness alone, because cameras and accessories preserve uneven thickness.
