# Microsoft is officially sending employees back to the office

- Score: 412 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45184432) | Link: https://www.businessinsider.com/microsoft-send-employees-back-to-office-rto-remote-work-2025-9

### TL;DR

Microsoft will require employees to work from an office at least three days weekly, beginning with Puget Sound staff living within 50 miles in February 2026, then expanding across the US and internationally. The company says its internal data links in-person work with stronger results and denies the policy is intended to reduce headcount. Employees and commenters question that evidence, noting distributed teams may still commute to video calls, while some value offices for focus, informal communication, and social contact.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics suspect attrition, real-estate pressure, or managerial control; others say office value depends heavily on team, personality, and commute.
- Commenters also flag practical constraints, including insufficient meeting rooms and geographically distributed colleagues.

### LLM perspective

- View: A uniform attendance rule substitutes a simple mandate for the harder task of measuring which collaboration patterns actually work.
- Impact: Employees with remote teammates bear commuting costs without necessarily gaining the face-to-face coordination Microsoft cites.
- Watch next: Exception handling, office capacity, attrition, and whether Microsoft publishes evidence connecting attendance to outcomes.
