# ICE is using fake cell towers to spy on people's phones

- Score: 651 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45184368) | Link: https://www.forbes.com/sites/the-wiretap/2025/09/09/how-ice-is-using-fake-cell-towers-to-spy-on-peoples-phones/

### TL;DR

A newly unsealed warrant shows ICE obtained court permission to use a cell-site simulator to refine the location of a fugitive in Utah after ordinary phone tracking narrowed it only to roughly 30 blocks. The device impersonates a cellular tower, potentially exposing information from nearby phones as well as the target. The article also identifies recent ICE contracts for simulator vehicles and handset-location equipment. Separately, unusual cellular messages observed at an anti-ICE protest were consistent with simulator activity, but the supplied evidence does not attribute them to ICE.
