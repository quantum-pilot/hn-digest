### TL;DR

Mistral AI says it raised a €1.7 billion Series C at an €11.7 billion post-money valuation, led by semiconductor-equipment maker ASML. The companies plan to develop AI-enabled products for ASML customers and pursue joint research, while Mistral says the capital will fund scientific work and customized, decentralized frontier AI for engineering and industry. Commenters see potential in semiconductor optimization but question what Mistral's language-model expertise uniquely contributes; theories about European strategic sovereignty remain discussion speculation, not an announced rationale.
