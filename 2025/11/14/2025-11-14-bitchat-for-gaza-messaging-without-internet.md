# Bitchat for Gaza – messaging without internet

- Score: 319 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45929358) | Link: https://updates.techforpalestine.org/bitchat-for-gaza-messaging-without-internet/

### TL;DR

Tech for Palestine recommends Bitchat for communication during Gaza connectivity outages. The app claims encrypted, account-free messaging over phone-to-phone Bluetooth relays, with internet relays and location channels when connectivity exists; messages remain on devices rather than a central server. The organization says it evaluated the technology and has contributors involved, but the supplied material offers no independent security audit or field measurements. Commenters focused on cold-start distribution, short Bluetooth range, jamming, confiscation risk, delivery latency, and whether existing adoption is sufficient.

### Comment pulse

- Distribution may matter more than protocol → emergency tools fail if people lack them before networks disappear.
- Bluetooth mesh has uncertain field scale → density enables relaying, while range, movement, nighttime gaps, and jamming can interrupt delivery.
- Device seizure creates operational risk → even a privacy-oriented app may itself attract scrutiny or expose retained messages.
