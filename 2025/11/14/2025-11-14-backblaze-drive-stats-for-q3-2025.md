# Backblaze Drive Stats for Q3 2025

- Score: 158 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45926383) | Link: https://www.backblaze.com/blog/backblaze-drive-stats-for-q3-2025/

### TL;DR

Backblaze tracked 328,348 data drives in Q3 2025 and reported a 1.55% annualized quarterly failure rate, up from 1.36%, while lifetime AFR stayed near 1.31%. Three models exceeded its 5.88% outlier threshold, but the Toshiba model's 16.95% figure largely reflected drives temporarily removed for firmware work rather than mechanical failures. The report explains its operational failure definition and publishes the dataset. Commenters valued the transparency but cautioned that workload, aging inventory, availability, batches, and shipping limit consumer purchasing conclusions.
