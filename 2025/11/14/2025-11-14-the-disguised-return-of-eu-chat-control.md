# The disguised return of EU Chat Control

- Score: 465 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45929511) | Link: https://reclaimthenet.org/the-disguised-return-of-the-eus-private-message-scanning-plot

### TL;DR

The article relays former MEP Patrick Breyer's warning that a revised EU proposal could reintroduce messaging surveillance without explicit detection orders. The draft instead requires providers to take “all appropriate risk mitigation,” language Breyer argues might compel client-side or other scanning, including text and metadata, while age checks could require identification or biometrics. These are interpretations of a proposal, not enacted requirements, and a commenter challenged some statistical rhetoric. Breyer advocates explicit protections for encryption, anonymity, judicial oversight, and against mandatory scanning.
