# I think nobody wants AI in Firefox, Mozilla

- Score: 1124 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45926779) | Link: https://manualdousuario.net/en/mozilla-firefox-window-ai/

### TL;DR

Mozilla announced an opt-in AI Window as a third Firefox browsing mode beside Normal and Private, with deeper integration than its existing chatbot sidebar but few implementation details. The article reports that all 52 early responses in Mozilla's feedback thread rejected the idea, while acknowledging they may be an unrepresentative minority. Commenters opposed intrusive promotion and resource diversion, yet others argued Firefox must experiment to attract users and diversify revenue. Several distinguished useful background machine learning from conspicuous generative-AI interfaces.

### Comment pulse

- Users object more to nagging than availability → optional features become hostile when repeatedly promoted or enabled around existing workflows.
- Mozilla's strategic bind divides readers → focus protects core quality, while experimentation may be necessary against Chromium dominance and Google dependence.
- Early feedback is uniformly negative but self-selected → it demonstrates strong opposition, not the preference of all Firefox users.

- View: Opt-in design reduces direct harm but cannot answer whether AI Window advances Firefox's distinctive mission.
