# Space Truckin' – The Nostromo (2012)

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46053566) | Link: https://alienseries.wordpress.com/2012/10/23/space-truckin-the-nostromo/

### TL;DR

The retrospective traces how Alien’s Nostromo became a grimy, functional “truck driver” alternative to polished or fantastical science fiction. Dan O’Bannon drew on Dark Star’s shabby realism, while Ridley Scott combined that sensibility with the grounded machinery of 2001. Ron Cobb and Chris Foss produced months of varied concepts before effects supervisor Brian Johnson used Cobb’s “Nostromo A” sketch as the model basis; Scott then changed its yellow paint to grey. Cobb’s engineering-minded interiors reinforced claustrophobia, corporate utility, and a convincingly used future.
