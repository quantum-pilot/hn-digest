# Space Truckin' – The Nostromo (2012)

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46053566) | Link: https://alienseries.wordpress.com/2012/10/23/space-truckin-the-nostromo/

### TL;DR

The production history traces Alien’s worn industrial aesthetic from Dark Star’s shabby realism and 2001’s functional credibility through a turbulent collaborative design process. Dan O’Bannon assembled Ron Cobb, Chris Foss, H.R. Giger, and Moebius; months of competing ship concepts ended when a Cobb sketch became the model’s basis. Ridley Scott then repainted, rebuilt, and reshot it, while salvaged industrial parts, cramped sets, retro controls, Egyptian motifs, and immense refinery scale made an ordinary workers’ vessel feel believable, oppressive, and corporate.

### Comment pulse

- Form followed narrative → an oily mineral carrier made the crew feel like expendable workers rather than glamorous explorers.
- Creative waste produced coherence → months of discarded concepts let Scott discover the restrained gray mass that suited the film.
- Authorship was cumulative → O’Bannon recruited distinctive artists, Cobb supplied functional logic, and Scott imposed the final visual mood.
