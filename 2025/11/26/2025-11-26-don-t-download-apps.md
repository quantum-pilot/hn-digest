# Don't Download Apps

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46061623) | Link: https://blog.calebjay.com/posts/dont-download-apps/

### TL;DR

The author urges people to reject retailer and service apps, citing aggressive installation tactics, data collection, personalized pricing, and binding-arbitration clauses that may reach beyond app use. A Taiwan phone-store anecdote illustrates weak consent, while future legal scenarios are explicitly predictions. Commenters dispute the claim that apps know payday timing or necessarily raise prices, describing many programs as ordinary loyalty discounts. Others share concerns about fingerprinting, approximate location, telemetry, and deliberately inferior mobile websites, favoring browsers or PWAs.

### Comment pulse
