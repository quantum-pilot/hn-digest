# NATO Ended Russia's Estonian Air Incursions

- Score: 86 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45936111) | Link: https://themilitaryanalyst.com/2025/11/13/the-real-story-how-nato-ended-russias-estonian-air-incursions/

### TL;DR

The Military Analyst claims NATO secretly countered a September Russian incursion into Estonian airspace by combining AWACS data, stealthy Italian F-35s, Swedish Gripens, surveillance assets, and electronic warfare. In its account, Russian MiG-31s lost radar and communications, were warned over an emergency channel, and withdrew without seeing NATO aircraft. The article presents this as decisive deterrence, but supplies no corroborating sources. Commenters identify factual or geographic errors, dispute that incursions stopped, and characterize the narrative as plausible-sounding military fiction rather than established reporting.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics cited missing sourcing, location errors, and an alleged later incursion as reasons to doubt the account.
- Others warned that revealing realistic defensive responses may itself provide useful intelligence to an adversary.
