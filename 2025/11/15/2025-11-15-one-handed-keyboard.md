# One Handed Keyboard

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45936262) | Link: https://github.com/htx-studio/One-Handed-Keyboard

### TL;DR

HTX Studio open-sourced a one-handed mechanical keyboard after hearing from a father whose daughter permanently lost right-hand function. The wired design integrates a trackball, mouse buttons, scroll wheel, and directional controls to reduce switching between keyboard and mouse. Its repository provides QMK firmware, VIA mappings, CAD models, fabrication guidance, and eight PCBs covering three left- and right-hand variants built around an STM32G431 controller. Commenters praised the project and compared alternatives including chorded, mirrored, split, and wearable keyboards, while noting substantial learning curves.

### Comment pulse

- Users with temporary or permanent limitations shared alternatives, emphasizing that standard keyboards, mirrored layers, and specialized hardware suit different adaptations.
- Chorded designs promise very high speed, but commenters questioned whether a year-long learning investment pays off.
