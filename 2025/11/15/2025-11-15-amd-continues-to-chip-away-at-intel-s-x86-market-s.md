# AMD continues to chip away at Intel's x86 market share

- Score: 75 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45940413) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/amd-continues-to-chip-away-at-intels-x86-market-share-company-now-sells-over-25-percent-of-all-x86-chips-and-powers-33-percent-of-all-desktop-systems

### TL;DR

Mercury Research figures reported by Tom’s Hardware put AMD at 25.6% of x86 client-and-server unit shipments in 2025’s third quarter, with desktop share reaching 33.6%. Client share rose sequentially and annually, while mobile remained slightly lower year over year. Server unit share edged up to 27.8%, though newer EPYC products reportedly lifted revenue share faster than units. The article attributes desktop momentum to Ryzen demand and Intel’s recent product and supply difficulties, while noting AMD’s gains have slowed.
