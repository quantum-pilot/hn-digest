# Transgenerational Epigenetic Inheritance: the story of learned avoidance

- Score: 103 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45939550) | Link: https://elifesciences.org/articles/109427

### TL;DR

An eLife commentary reviews evidence that *C. elegans* exposed to pathogenic *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* PA14 learn avoidance and can pass it to unexposed F1 and F2 descendants. A new independent study reproduced F2 inheritance under the original assay, supporting earlier Murphy-lab results after another group failed beyond F1. Disagreement may reflect sodium-azide versus temperature immobilization, initial PA14 attraction, bacterial P11 RNA expression, or unidentified laboratory variation. Commenters stress that finicky worm assays do not justify claims about inherited human trauma or broad policy conclusions.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: Replication strengthens the worm finding while simultaneously revealing how protocol-sensitive its interpretation remains.
- Impact: Researchers gain a testable model, not evidence for specific multigenerational effects in humans.
- Watch next: Preregistered multicenter replication, P11 measurements, matched immobilization protocols, effect sizes, and mechanism tests.
