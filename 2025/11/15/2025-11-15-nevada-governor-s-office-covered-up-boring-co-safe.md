# Nevada Governor's office covered up Boring Co safety violations

- Score: 76 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45939798) | Link: https://fortune.com/2025/11/12/elon-musk-boring-company-tunnels-injuries-osha-citations-fines-rescinded-nevada-governor-office-documents-altered/

### TL;DR

Fortune reports that Nevada OSHA proposed three citations totaling $425,595 after two firefighters suffered permanent chemical-burn scarring during a Boring Company tunnel drill. Hours after company president Steve Davis contacted Governor Joe Lombardo's office, senior officials convened and the citations were withdrawn before Boring presented its case. Records later omitted or deleted meeting details. State officials call the irregularities mistakes and say the citations lacked legal support; former regulators and anonymous staff allege improper political interference and a chilling effect on enforcement.
