# Linux on the Fujitsu Lifebook U729

- Score: 159 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45937989) | Link: https://borretti.me/article/linux-on-the-fujitsu-lifebook-u729

### TL;DR

The author finds a refurbished Fujitsu Lifebook U729 an inexpensive, lightweight, and unusually trouble-free NixOS laptop: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sound, brightness controls, touchscreen, and webcam worked out of the box. The main obstacle was a greyed-out Secure Boot setting. Their workaround required installing Windows, applying Fujitsu updates, then running DeskUpdate to move the BIOS from version 2.17 to 2.31, after which Secure Boot could be disabled. The microphone and fingerprint reader remain untested, and Absolute Persistence was disabled in firmware.
