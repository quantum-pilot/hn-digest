# Things that aren't doing the thing

- Score: 80 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45939431) | Link: https://strangestloop.io/essays/things-that-arent-doing-the-thing

### TL;DR

The short essay lists preparation, scheduling, announcing, self-criticism, envy, fantasy, and reading as activities that can resemble progress without actually completing a task; its blunt conclusion is that only action counts as doing. Commenters mostly challenge the binary framing. They argue thinking can reduce wasted effort, coordination and resource allocation enable execution, queues prevent chaotic prioritization, and research may be a necessary preceding task. The useful distinction is therefore between preparation that removes a real constraint and preparation that indefinitely substitutes for contact with the work.

### Comment pulse

- Deliberate thinkers prefer extended preparation followed by fast execution, especially when early action would create avoidable rework.
- Systems-minded commenters say strategy, coordination, records, and resources are not execution but remain essential to choosing and completing worthwhile work.
