# Our investigation into the suspicious pressure on Archive.today

- Score: 1195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45936460) | Link: https://adguard-dns.io/en/blog/archive-today-adguard-dns-block-demand.html

### TL;DR

AdGuard says a newly registered French group, Web Abuse Association Defense, pressured it to block Archive.today over alleged illegal material, invoking French intermediary-liability law and threatening action. AdGuard contacted Archive.today, which quickly removed the identified pages and denied receiving earlier notices. Its investigation found sparse organizational history, concealed operators, inconsistent complaint dates, and possible impersonation in an older complaint. AdGuard stresses these clues prove neither identity nor motive, plans a French police report, and cannot confirm any connection to a separately reported FBI investigation.
