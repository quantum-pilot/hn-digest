# Ask HN: How would you set up a child’s first Linux computer?

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45864732) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45864732

### TL;DR

An HN parent asks how to recreate a successful first-computer experience for an eight-year-old using a secondhand desktop, Linux, and free software. Suggestions emphasize involving the child in hardware assembly and distro selection, keeping recovery simple, locking administrative access initially, and preloading discoverable tools such as drawing apps, games, Scratch, or a basic programming environment. The strongest caution is to follow the child's interests and school collaboration needs rather than treating Linux freedom as the goal regardless of friction.
