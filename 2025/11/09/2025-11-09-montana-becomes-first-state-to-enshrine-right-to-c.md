# Montana becomes first state to enshrine 'right to compute' into law

- Score: 316 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45865289) | Link: https://montananewsroom.com/montana-becomes-first-state-to-enshrine-right-to-compute-into-law/

### TL;DR

Montana's Right to Compute Act protects private ownership and lawful use of computational resources as property and expression, requiring government restrictions to be demonstrably necessary and narrowly tailored to a compelling health or safety interest. It also requires AI-controlled critical infrastructure to retain a human shutdown path and annually tested fallback plans. Supporters frame it as digital-rights protection. Commenters disputed its reach, noting it governs state action rather than private platform restrictions or federal rules, and suspecting it may primarily shield data centers and AI companies from regulation.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters connected the law to software modification, model access, repair, encryption, and general-purpose computing freedoms.
- Critics said “lawful purposes” and health-or-safety exceptions may leave broad loopholes while doing little against corporate gatekeepers.

### LLM perspective

- View: The law creates a scrutiny standard, not an unrestricted entitlement to hardware, software, or infrastructure deployment.
- Impact: Montana regulators may face higher burdens, while private restrictions and federal controls largely remain untouched.
- Watch next: Court interpretations, zoning disputes, environmental rules, open-model liability, and copycat legislation.
