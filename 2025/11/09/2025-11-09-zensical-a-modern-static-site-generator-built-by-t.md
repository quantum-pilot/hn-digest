# Zensical – A modern static site generator built by the Material for MkDocs team

- Score: 114 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45865189) | Link: https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/blog/2025/11/05/zensical/

### TL;DR

The Material for MkDocs team introduced Zensical, an MIT-licensed documentation generator combining site generation, theming, search, and customization after MkDocs became unmaintained and architecturally limiting. It reads `mkdocs.yml`, preserves existing content and generated HTML, and claims four-to-five-times faster incremental rebuilds, though initial builds may be slower and plugin compatibility remains partial. ZRX supplies caching and a differential runtime; future Rust-based CommonMark, modules, and components target larger sites. Material enters maintenance mode, while paid Zensical Spark funds professional support and influence.
