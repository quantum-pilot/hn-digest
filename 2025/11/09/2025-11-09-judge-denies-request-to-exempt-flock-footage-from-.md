# Judge denies request to exempt Flock footage from Public Records Act

- Score: 196 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45861829) | Link: https://www.goskagit.com/news/local_news/court-denies-request-that-it-find-flock-safety-camera-data-is-exempt-from-public-records/article_f1edd028-d242-479c-ada8-f2dfca73a1b1.html

### TL;DR

A Washington judge denied two cities' request to declare images and data from Flock Safety license-plate cameras outside the state Public Records Act or exempt from disclosure. The cities argued police do not possess usable records until querying Flock and warned disclosure could worsen public privacy; the requester argued third-party custody does not remove records serving a government purpose from transparency law. Both cities disabled their cameras during litigation. The article also notes federal agents reportedly searched some Washington agencies' Flock data without local awareness.

### Comment pulse

- Transparency supporters warned agencies could evade records laws simply by outsourcing data storage to vendors.
- Privacy critics opposed both public release and suspicionless collection, arguing sensitive movement records should not exist uncontrolled.
