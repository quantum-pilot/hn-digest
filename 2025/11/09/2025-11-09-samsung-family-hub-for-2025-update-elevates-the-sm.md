# Samsung Family Hub for 2025 Update Elevates the Smart Home Ecosystem

- Score: 284 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45866165) | Link: https://news.samsung.com/us/samsung-family-hub-2025-update-elevates-smart-home-ecosystem/

### TL;DR

Samsung's 2025 Family Hub refrigerator update brings One UI to supported 2024 models, expands AI Vision recognition to 37 fresh foods and user-labeled packaged items, adds Bixby Voice ID and accessibility syncing, and extends Knox Matrix security to more appliances. A cover-screen widget pilots news, weather, calendars, and dismissible advertisements; owners can disable ads, which Samsung says are contextual or non-personal. Commenters overwhelmingly rejected appliance advertising, account dependence, privacy risk, and costly food recognition they considered unreliable or unnecessary.

### Comment pulse

- Existing Samsung users described intrusive terms prompts and updates as reasons to keep appliances offline.
- A showroom worker said inventory recognition still needs extensive correction and excludes doors, drawers, and freezers.
