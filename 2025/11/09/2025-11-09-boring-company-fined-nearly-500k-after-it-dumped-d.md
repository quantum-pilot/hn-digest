# Boring Company fined nearly $500K after it dumped drilling fluids into manholes

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45862674) | Link: https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/elon-musk-boring-company-fined-150000426.html

### TL;DR

Clark County's water regulator fined the Boring Company $493,297.08, including $131,297.08 in cleanup costs, over alleged drilling-fluid and solid-waste discharges into Las Vegas sewer infrastructure. Regulatory documents say employees refused inspectors' stop orders, temporarily disconnected equipment, then resumed after believing inspectors had left; the company later acknowledged improper discharge and accepted operating conditions. Crews removed 12 cubic yards of waste. Boring did not answer the article's requests for comment, while commenters questioned whether the penalty meaningfully deters repeated violations.
