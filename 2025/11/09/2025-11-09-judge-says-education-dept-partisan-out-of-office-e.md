# Judge says Education Dept partisan out-of-office emails violated First Amendment

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45861897) | Link: https://www.npr.org/2025/11/08/nx-s1-5602859/education-department-out-of-office-emails-ruling

### TL;DR

A federal judge ruled that the Education Department violated employees' First Amendment rights by replacing their shutdown autoreplies with first-person partisan language blaming Democratic senators. Employees had prepared neutral notices before an official overrode them without warning. Judge Christopher Cooper ordered restoration of union members' messages or removal of the partisan language from all affected accounts, reasoning that public workers do not become political billboards. Discussion centered on the distinction between directing official job communications and compelling an employee to voice unrelated partisan views.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters emphasized that the First Amendment constrains government employers differently from private companies.
- Skeptics questioned employee control over official accounts; replies stressed federal civil service is intentionally nonpartisan.
