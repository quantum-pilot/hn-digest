# Reviving Classic Unix Games: A 20-Year Journey Through Software Archaeology

- Score: 125 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45865159) | Link: https://vejeta.com/reviving-classic-unix-games-a-20-year-journey-through-software-archaeology/

### TL;DR

A preservation effort begun in 2006 traced authors of Conquer, a multiplayer Unix strategy game distributed through USENET in 1987, to obtain relicensing permission. Edward Barlow and Adam Bryant approved GPL releases; Bryant later also approved the rewritten Version 5, and another contributor cleared PostScript utilities. Richard Caley had died, so his contributions remain under original terms rather than being relicensed. The revived projects add modern Make and C99 work, GitHub Actions, Debian and Alpine packaging, reproducible builds, and browser play through ttyd.
