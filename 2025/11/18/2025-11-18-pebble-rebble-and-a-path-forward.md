# Pebble, Rebble, and a path forward

- Score: 305 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45969250) | Link: https://ericmigi.com/blog/pebble-rebble-and-a-path-forward/

### TL;DR

Core Devices founder Eric Migicovsky disputes Rebble's accusations that Core took community-funded work, misrepresented agreements, and scraped its app store. He says Core purchased relevant library copyrights, publishes its work under open-source licenses, and wants the archive of roughly 13,000 legacy apps freely available rather than controlled by one organization. His post is one side of a contentious dispute. Commenters framed the underlying conflict as mutual existential risk: Core needs dependable services, while Rebble fears replacement after sustaining Pebble's ecosystem.

### Comment pulse

- Core supporters see rent-seeking → Rebble preserved the ecosystem but appears protective as a commercial manufacturer returns.
- Rebble supporters seek continuity guarantees → its infrastructure spending and preservation work could be displaced after enabling the revival.
- Both sides invoke openness → their incompatible control and dependency concerns have eroded trust.

### LLM perspective

- View: The archive dispute masks a governance failure over stewardship, service continuity, and commercial dependence.
- Impact: Developers and watch owners face fragmented infrastructure if Core and Rebble build competing stores and services.
- Watch next: Seek a neutral archive, explicit licensing, portable APIs, and enforceable continuity terms separating preservation from service operation.
