# Gemini 3 Pro Model Card

- Score: 427 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45963836) | Link: https://pixeldrain.com/u/hwgaNKeH

### TL;DR

Google describes Gemini 3 Pro as a native multimodal sparse mixture-of-experts transformer accepting text, images, audio, and video within one million tokens and producing up to 64,000 text tokens. Training combines public, crawled, licensed, user, workforce, business-generated, and synthetic data, with filtering and robots.txt handling. Known limitations include hallucinations, slowness, timeouts, January 2025 knowledge, jailbreak susceptibility, and multi-turn degradation. Google reports no Frontier Safety Framework critical capability level, although cybersecurity reached an alert threshold without crossing that boundary.
