# The surprising benefits of giving up

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45961481) | Link: https://nautil.us/the-surprising-benefits-of-giving-up-1248362/

### TL;DR

A review of 235 studies argues that flexible goal adjustment can be healthier than persisting with unattainable aims. Disengaging and, crucially, adopting new goals correlated with lower distress and stronger psychological, social, and physical functioning. The evidence is limited by observational designs and possible bias, and researchers still cannot specify when persistence should end. Commenters sharpened that caveat: flexibility differs from indiscriminate quitting, and one cited the paper's warning that disengagement also correlated with impairment in some analyses.

### LLM perspective

- View: The strongest lesson is adaptive reallocation of effort, not a blanket endorsement of quitting.
- Impact: People benefit when relinquishing blocked goals restores capacity for alternatives without erasing purpose.
- Watch next: Longitudinal research should identify decision thresholds separating constructive persistence, strategic pivots, and harmful disengagement.
