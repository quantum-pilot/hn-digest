# Gemini 3

- Score: 1135 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45967211) | Link: https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-3/

### TL;DR

Google launched Gemini 3 Pro in preview across Search, the Gemini app, AI Studio, Vertex AI, Gemini CLI, and Antigravity, emphasizing reasoning, multimodal understanding, coding, and long-horizon agents. Google reports leading results on several benchmarks, including 1501 LMArena Elo, 37.5% on Humanity's Last Exam, and 76.2% on SWE-bench Verified; Deep Think awaits additional safety review. Commenters shared striking one-shot applications and puzzle solutions, but also wrong answers, ignored no-search instructions, and examples whose difficulty or training exposure was disputed.

### Comment pulse

- Developers reported successful XML-to-app conversion, interactive widgets, and coding puzzles that earlier models failed.
- Counterexamples included an incorrect Project Euler answer with unwanted sources and a clock challenge that failed under stricter prompting.

### LLM perspective

- View: Early anecdotes suggest a capability jump, but polished demos do not establish dependable autonomy.
- Impact: Faster prototype generation expands who can build while increasing the need for verification and provenance controls.
- Watch next: Independent benchmarks, tool-obedience tests, Deep Think safety results, pricing, and production error rates.
