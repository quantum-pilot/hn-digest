# Rebecca Heineman has died

- Score: 895 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45960368) | Link: https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/legendary-game-designer-programmer-space-invaders-champion-and-lgbtq-trailblazer-rebecca-heineman-has-died/

### TL;DR

Game designer and programmer Rebecca Heineman died at 62 after an aggressive cancer diagnosis. She won a national Space Invaders tournament in 1980, co-founded Interplay, designed The Bard's Tale III, and became renowned for demanding ports including Doom on 3DO and several Macintosh releases. She also advocated for LGBTQ+ inclusion and received Gayming's 2025 Icon award. Commenters remembered extraordinary low-level programming, influential games, personal kindness, and concerns about preserving her source code and other historical artifacts.
