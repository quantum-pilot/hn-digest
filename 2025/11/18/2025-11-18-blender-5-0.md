# Blender 5.0

- Score: 395 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45972519) | Link: https://www.blender.org/download/releases/5-0/

### TL;DR

Blender 5.0 overhauls color management with HDR, wide-gamut, ACES, Rec.2020, and per-file working spaces. Rendering gains multiple-scattering skies, improved volume and subsurface algorithms, metallic iridescence, faster EEVEE compilation, and production-ready adaptive subdivision in Cycles. It also expands compositor assets, Grease Pencil motion blur, Sequencer workflows, Geometry Nodes-based modifiers, UV editing, VR navigation, and file interoperability, while removing Collada and introducing breaking APIs. Commenters especially welcomed HDR and higher-order node features, while wishing open CAD had comparable momentum.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime users praised Blender's sustained progress and the addition of bundles, closures, and reusable graphical-programming structures.
- CAD discussion identified FreeCAD's geometry kernel and robust filleting as harder barriers than interface polish alone.
