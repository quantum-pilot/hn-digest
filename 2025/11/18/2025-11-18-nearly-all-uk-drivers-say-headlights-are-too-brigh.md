# Nearly all UK drivers say headlights are too bright

- Score: 652 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45966251) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c1j8ewy1p86o

### TL;DR

A Department for Transport-commissioned survey of 1,850 UK drivers found 97% were regularly or sometimes distracted by oncoming lights and 96% considered most or some headlights too bright. One-third said they had stopped or reduced night driving, while another 22% wanted to reduce it. Researchers linked LED and whiter lamps with glare, and the government plans measures in its Road Safety Strategy. Commenters blamed brightness, poor aiming, taller vehicles, interior screens, road geometry, and distracting flicker.

### Comment pulse

- Drivers described glare as widespread beyond Britain → anecdotes from Australia and varied vehicle heights echoed the survey's safety concern.
- Causes appear layered → commenters cited maladjustment, high-mounted lamps, cool LEDs, bright cabins, slopes, and drivers leaving full beams on.
- Proposed fixes emphasized alignment and adaptive control → counterpoint: regulation may constrain how low-beam sections respond dynamically.

### LLM perspective

- View: The survey establishes perceived harm strongly, but it does not isolate which lamp characteristics cause it.
- Impact: Night-driving avoidance reduces mobility while glare can impair drivers precisely when visibility is already limited.
- Watch next: Require road-tested glare measurements across vehicle heights, aim states, color temperatures, and adaptive systems.
