# 'The French people want to save us': help pours in for glassmaker Duralex

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46015379) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/nov/22/french-people-want-to-save-us-help-pours-glassmaker-duralex

### TL;DR

French glassmaker Duralex reached a €5 million public-funding target in under six hours, with pledges nearing €20 million before the worker-owned cooperative stopped the campaign. The rescue follows a staff buyout after repeated receiverships; 180 of 243 employees now hold associate status. Orders also surged, reflecting nostalgia for its durable school-canteen glassware. Funds will modernize the energy-intensive factory and develop products, while management targets break-even in 2027 and acknowledges that sustained sales, especially abroad, remain essential.
