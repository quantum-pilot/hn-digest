# The realities of being a pop star

- Score: 87 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46016613) | Link: https://itscharlibb.substack.com/p/the-realities-of-being-a-pop-star

### TL;DR

Charli XCX describes pop stardom as exhilarating, absurd, transient, and isolating: parties, fashion, travel, creative community, devoted audiences, and powerful performances coexist with endless waiting rooms, transit, embarrassment, paranoia, and public projection. She argues successful women are confined to marketable personas and punished when they display complexity or change. Old friends and self-deprecation help test whether fame has altered her, though flattering yes-people remain. She rejects the expectation that artists must be truthful role models, preferring performance, danger, invention, and fantasy.
