# The realities of being a pop star

- Score: 87 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46016613) | Link: https://itscharlibb.substack.com/p/the-realities-of-being-a-pop-star

### TL;DR

Charli XCX describes pop stardom as exhilarating performance and absurd privilege wrapped around long stretches of transit, waiting and artificial spaces. Fame enables creative community, gifts, access and intense audience connection, yet also produces embarrassment, paranoia, “yes people” and public efforts to confine her to a marketable persona. She argues that misogyny helps turn female artists' growth into accusations of stupidity or hypocrisy. Her final defense of fantasy rejects the expectation that performers must always be truthful role models.
