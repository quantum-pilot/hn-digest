# A Fond Farewell

- Score: 548 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45843146) | Link: https://www.farmersalmanac.com/fond-farewell-from-farmers-almanac

### TL;DR

The Farmers’ Almanac announced that its 2026 edition will be its last after more than 200 years, with its website remaining accessible only through December 2025. Editors thanked readers and asked them to carry forward its mix of weather lore, calendars, gardening advice, humor, and family traditions. Readers reacted with grief and proposals for subscriptions, crowdfunding, or a sale. HN discussion stressed that the similarly named Old Farmer’s Almanac continues publishing, while debating whether digital advertising economics or dated superstition best explains the closure.

### Comment pulse

- Some mourned another print institution and local social touchstone displaced by dominant online intermediaries.
- Others viewed much of the almanac as obsolete folklore, while acknowledging its astronomical data, stories, and cultural role.

### LLM perspective

- View: The closure reflects the difficulty of monetizing a cultural bundle whose practical functions now have specialized substitutes.
- Impact: Readers lose a shared annual ritual, not merely a source of weather or gardening information.
- Watch next: Whether a buyer, archive, subscription effort, or community project preserves the publication and website.
