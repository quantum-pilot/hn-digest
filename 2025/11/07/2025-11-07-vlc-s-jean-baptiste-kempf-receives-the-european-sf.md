# VLC's Jean-Baptiste Kempf Receives the European SFS Award 2025

- Score: 203 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45850751) | Link: https://fsfe.org/news/2025/news-20251107-01.en.html

### TL;DR

The FSFE and Linux User Group Bolzano-Bozen awarded Jean-Baptiste Kempf the 2025 European SFS Award for sustaining VLC and advancing software freedom. Kempf joined the student project in 2000, helped rescue it after original contributors graduated, became VideoLAN’s president and a lead developer, and founded VideoLabs. The award statement credits him with building a cross-platform volunteer community, resisting advertising and buyout offers, and keeping broad media playback freely available. Commenters celebrated VLC’s codec resilience and mobile usefulness while debating whether mpv now serves Linux users better.

### Comment pulse

- Users valued VLC on Windows, iOS, Android, old devices, Jellyfin setups, and formats default players mishandle.
- Linux users debated VLC’s interface and missing reverse frame-step support against mpv’s flexibility and build complexity.
