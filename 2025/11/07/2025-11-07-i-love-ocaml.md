# I Love OCaml

- Score: 284 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45846517) | Link: https://mccd.space/posts/ocaml-the-worlds-best/

### TL;DR

The author praises OCaml as a balance between Haskell’s expressive static functional programming and Go’s simplicity, speed, and approachable runtime. OCaml offers inferred types, sum types, pattern matching, modules, fast compilation with Dune, a REPL, offline documentation, native binaries, and relatively understandable execution behavior. They acknowledge aging object-oriented features, occasional ecosystem over-complexity, and limited adoption. Commenters supplied the missing costs: weak Windows support, difficult syntax errors, terse documentation, OPAM surprises, formatting issues, and a small or fragmented library ecosystem.

### LLM perspective
