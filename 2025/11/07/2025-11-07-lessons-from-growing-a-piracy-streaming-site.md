# Lessons from Growing a Piracy Streaming Site

- Score: 251 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45844197) | Link: https://prison.josh.mn/lessons

### TL;DR

The former HeheStreams operator presents an illegal sports-streaming service as a small-business case study: earn word-of-mouth through replyable emails, proactive onboarding, honest outage notices, generous refunds, churn outreach, competitor referrals, premium pricing, and narrow product focus. Its main growth tactic surfaced relevant Reddit posts and encouraged customers to leave transparent referral comments, giving each side $10. The author says these lessons later transferred to an ecotourism business, while pointedly discouraging the legal “exit strategy.” Commenters contrasted its support and discovery experience with licensed services.

### Comment pulse

- Readers attributed piracy services’ better support to competition, while licensed platforms can rely on exclusive content.
- Others expected operational-security lessons and questioned taking severe legal risks for a consumer streaming business.

- View: Trust-building practices transfer across legal boundaries; the business model’s illegality does not invalidate its customer-service observations.
