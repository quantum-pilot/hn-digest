# James Watson has died

- Score: 197 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45850069) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/07/science/james-watson-dead.html

### TL;DR

James D. Watson died at 97 after a career that combined transformative science with lasting controversy. With Francis Crick, and crucial X-ray work from Rosalind Franklin and Maurice Wilkins, he helped identify DNA’s double-helix structure, earning a 1962 Nobel Prize with Crick and Wilkins. He later rebuilt Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, led the early Human Genome Project, opposed gene patenting, and wrote influential books. His legacy was damaged by racist assertions, disparagement of Franklin and others, and disputes over credit and research protocol.
