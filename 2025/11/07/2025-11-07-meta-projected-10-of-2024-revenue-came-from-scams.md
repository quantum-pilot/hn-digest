# Meta projected 10% of 2024 revenue came from scams

- Score: 623 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45845772) | Link: https://sherwood.news/tech/meta-projected-10-of-2024-revenue-came-from-scams-and-banned-goods-reuters/

### TL;DR

Internal Meta documents reviewed by Reuters reportedly projected that scam advertising and banned-goods sales would provide 10% of 2024 revenue, about $16 billion. The reporting says Meta weighed lost revenue against regulatory fines, prioritized enforcement where penalties were highest, and left many user reports ignored or rejected after moderation cuts. Meta called the documents a selective view and said it aggressively fights fraud. Commenters argued platforms lack financial incentives to improve, while users shared unverified experiences of recurring scam ads and ineffective reporting.

### Comment pulse

- Critics favored liability for victim losses, arguing fines below scam-linked revenue make weak enforcement economically rational.
- Users described ad blockers as essential security, while others said platforms—not end users—must prevent harmful distribution.

### LLM perspective

- View: The central issue is incentive design, not whether Meta formally maintains anti-fraud policies.
- Impact: Scam exposure harms users and legitimate advertisers while shifting defensive costs onto individuals and brand owners.
