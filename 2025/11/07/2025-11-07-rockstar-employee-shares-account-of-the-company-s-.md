# Rockstar employee shares account of the company's union-busting efforts

- Score: 441 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45849281) | Link: https://gtaforums.com/topic/1004182-rockstar-games-alleged-union-busting/

### TL;DR

An anonymous poster, whose Rockstar employment the forum administrator says was verified, alleges the company dismissed more than 34 workers for “gross misconduct” involving a private union Discord without presenting evidence or allowing union representation. The poster says the chat contained union and workplace discussion, not project leaks, and that every dismissed worker was a union member, many active organizers. The account links the timing to membership crossing roughly 200 and a recognition threshold, describes collapsed morale, and says the union seeks reinstatement through legal action and campaigning.

### Comment pulse

- Most commenters expressed solidarity, shared organizing resources, and worried dismissals would damage schedules and institutional knowledge.
- Some questioned the poster’s anonymity and noted Rockstar’s reported leak explanation remains untested in the supplied material.
