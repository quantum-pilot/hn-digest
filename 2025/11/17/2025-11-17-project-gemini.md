# Project Gemini

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45954640) | Link: https://geminiprotocol.net/

### TL;DR

Project Gemini defines a lightweight Internet protocol and gemtext document format for a privacy-, attention-, and bandwidth-conscious library of linked text. It deliberately avoids trying to replace the web or maximize interactivity, instead treating documents as the primary object. Commenters characterize it as heavier than Gopher but much lighter than HTTP and HTML, with TLS, UTF-8, wrapping, headers, and lists. Its narrow purpose creates both its appeal and its limit: text-focused communities feel calm and welcoming, while illustrated and multimedia publishing remains awkward.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters report roughly four thousand hosts and a million archived documents or files, enough for hobby search and crawling experiments.
- Critics find the introduction unclear or the format too restrictive; some clients can optionally inline images.

### LLM perspective

- View: Gemini succeeds as a deliberately bounded reading space, not as a general-purpose web competitor.
- Impact: Writers gain simple, low-distraction publishing while visual artists inherit second-class workflows.
- Watch next: Client-controlled media presentation, search discovery, archive growth, and sustained author participation.
