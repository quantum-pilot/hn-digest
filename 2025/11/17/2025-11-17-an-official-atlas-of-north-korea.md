# An official atlas of North Korea

- Score: 159 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45956176) | Link: https://www.cartographerstale.com/p/an-official-atlas-of-north-korea

### TL;DR

A CD edition of North Korea’s Great Korean Encyclopaedia contains 672 maps covering Korea, continents, oceans, and individual countries. The author highlights a unified Korean peninsula, Pacific-centered world view, dark-gray treatment of several adversaries, no dedicated Israel map, and inclusion of Western Sahara. Dating clues suggest the map set may be from roughly 2007–2010. Comments found outdated or erroneous transport and river details and challenged the author’s claim that North Korea depicts the war as won and the peninsula as actually governed by one party.

### Comment pulse
