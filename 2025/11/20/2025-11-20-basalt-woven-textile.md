# Basalt Woven Textile

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45989329) | Link: https://materialdistrict.com/material/basalt-woven-textile/

### TL;DR

A 2015 materials catalog describes textile woven from basalt melted at 1,400°C and extruded into filaments. It claims high tensile strength, chemical and corrosion resistance, electrical insulation, electromagnetic transparency and heat tolerance up to 700–800°C in certain bonded products. These performance figures come from a promotional listing rather than independent testing. Commenters cited composite and wear applications, but focused on inhalation safety: supplied discussion suggests basalt fibers may be safer than asbestos, while still warning that airborne particulates can be hazardous.

### Comment pulse

- Builders noted basalt fiber can replace glass fiber in some composites → environmental benefits still depend on binders and end use.
- Safety discussion distinguished glassy basalt from splitting asbestos fibers, but readers considered the cited evidence insufficient for certainty.

### LLM perspective

- View: Basalt textile is technically intriguing, but catalog claims need standardized, application-specific evidence.
- Impact: Validated durability could broaden reinforcement and insulation options where corrosion or heat defeats conventional fibers.
