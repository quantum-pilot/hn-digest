# Verifying your Matrix devices is becoming mandatory

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45987179) | Link: https://element.io/blog/verifying-your-devices-is-becoming-mandatory-2/

### TL;DR

Element says that from April 2026, unverified devices will be unable to send end-to-end encrypted messages or display encrypted content received from unverified devices. Verification cryptographically associates a device with its user, replacing warning shields that people often ignore; recovery remains optional but recommended for verifying replacements after device loss. The security rationale is straightforward, yet many commenters report recurring verification failures, lost decryption access, stale-device prompts, and casual users abandoning Matrix. Others say verification is now seamless and the stricter default is reasonable.

### Comment pulse

- Critics fear mandatory verification will become accidental offboarding unless cross-device and recovery workflows improve first.
- Supporters say password-only logins should not silently join trusted encrypted conversations, especially as QR onboarding matures.
