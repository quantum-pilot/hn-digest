# Kagi Assistants

- Score: 112 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45997294) | Link: https://blog.kagi.com/kagi-assistants

### TL;DR

Kagi launched Quick Assistant for all plans and Research Assistant for Ultimate users, combining task-specific models with multilingual searches, citations, code, and specialist APIs. Quick targets sub-five-second answers; Research favors deeper synthesis without defaulting to long reports. Kagi reports a 95.5% SimpleQA score from August, but says it will not optimize further because benchmark answers can conflict with current authoritative sources or reward undesirable crawling. It attributes much of the performance to Kagi Search returning less noisy context.
