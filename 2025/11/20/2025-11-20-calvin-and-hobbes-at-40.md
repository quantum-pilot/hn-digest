# 'Calvin and Hobbes' at 40

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45991787) | Link: https://www.npr.org/2025/11/18/nx-s1-5564064/calvin-and-hobbes-bill-watterson-40-years-comic-strip-lee-salem

### TL;DR

NPR marks 40 years since Calvin and Hobbes debuted on November 18, 1985, revisiting a 2005 interview with editor Lee Salem. He recalls Bill Watterson's strip as immediately fresh, funny and visually strong, with Hobbes balancing Calvin's unruly imagination and commentary. Whether Hobbes is objectively real matters less than his reality to Calvin. Readers celebrated the comic's layered humor, artistic integrity and enduring childhood resonance, while debating Calvin's influence as a contrarian model and Watterson's increasingly curmudgeonly later tone.
