# Nano Banana Pro

- Score: 770 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45993296) | Link: https://blog.google/technology/ai/nano-banana-pro/

### TL;DR

Google introduced Nano Banana Pro, or Gemini 3 Pro Image, for image generation and editing grounded in Gemini reasoning and optional Search data. It claims stronger multilingual text rendering, infographics, localized edits, lighting and camera controls, up to 4K output, fourteen input images, and consistency for five people. Rollout spans Gemini, NotebookLM, Workspace, Ads, Flow, APIs, AI Studio, and Vertex AI. Early commenter tests report better instruction adherence, while flagging pricing, occasional semantic errors, recognizable AI aesthetics, and difficult billing setup.

### Comment pulse

- One editing benchmark scored Pro 10/12 versus 7/12 for its predecessor, though commenters disputed individual judgments.
- Developers praised infographic and layout results but reported API permissions, billing friction, cost, and uncertain intermediate-image handling.
