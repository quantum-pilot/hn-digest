# 210 IQ Is Not Enough

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45993073) | Link: https://taylor.town/iq-not-enough

### TL;DR

The essay contrasts Christopher Langan, a highly intelligent public figure portrayed as embittered and unfulfilled, with Kim Ung-yong, whose parents claimed an IQ of 210 and extraordinary childhood achievements. Kim later rejected the label “failed genius,” choosing civil engineering, teaching and a life he considered happy. The author’s point is that intelligence, status and achievement cannot guarantee contentment or moral wisdom. Commenters broadened this: opportunity, social skill, motivation, maturity and resources often matter more than raw cognitive ability.
