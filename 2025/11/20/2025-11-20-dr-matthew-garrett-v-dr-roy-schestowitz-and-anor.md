# Dr Matthew Garrett v Dr Roy Schestowitz and Anor

- Score: 103 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45992403) | Link: https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewhc/kb/2025/3063

### TL;DR

The High Court found Roy and Rianne Schestowitz liable for 24 defamatory Techrights and Tuxmachines publications accusing software engineer Matthew Garrett of operating abusive IRC sockpuppets, committing crimes and using cocaine. The defendants filed no witness evidence, and the judge found their thin circumstantial case incapable of proving Garrett controlled the accounts. Truth, honest-opinion and public-interest defenses failed; their harassment counterclaim also failed. Garrett received £70,000, with an injunction and publication of a judgment summary indicated as further remedies.
