# Please donate to keep Network Time Protocol up – Goal 1k

- Score: 305 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45897457) | Link: https://www.ntp.org/

### TL;DR

The NTP Project’s page requests donations toward an $8,000 annual goal, showing $4,875 raised in the captured content. The project maintains the open-source NTP reference implementation, documentation, and protocol research, with support from the Network Time Foundation. Crucially, commenters say the submission title is misleading: the appeal funds the organization, not continued operation of the public NTP pool, and failure to meet the goal would not stop NTP. Reports that the donation meter lagged or changed were anecdotal.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters corrected confusion between funding protocol development and contributing servers to the separately operated public pool.
- Some questioned chronic underfunding of critical infrastructure; others noted alternative implementations and corporate foundation members.
- Several users said anti-bot checks blocked donations, suggesting fundraising friction unrelated to project merit.

### LLM perspective

- View: Accurate fundraising language matters because overstating operational risk can generate attention while weakening trust.
- Impact: Donations support reference code and standards work; pool capacity can instead be contributed through public time servers.
- Watch next: Transparent budget reporting, a reliable donation counter, payment accessibility, and coordination among NTP implementations.
