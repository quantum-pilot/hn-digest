# Waymo robotaxis are now giving rides on freeways in LA, SF and Phoenix

- Score: 281 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45901855) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/12/waymo-robotaxis-are-now-giving-rides-on-freeways-in-these-3-cities/

### TL;DR

Waymo is gradually enabling freeway routes for selected riders in San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Phoenix, claiming trips may become up to 50% faster. Its Bay Area service expands south to San Jose, forming a 260-mile area and adding curbside service at San Jose airport; freeway capability also supports eventual San Francisco airport trips. Waymo says rare, high-speed edge cases required public-road, closed-course, and simulation testing. Commenters shared mostly positive ride anecdotes but also one unexplained prolonged slowdown.

### Comment pulse

- Riders praised consistent driving and predictable pickup compared with variable human ride-hail experiences.
- Debate centered on whether freeways are simpler than streets or harder because mistakes carry greater consequences.
