# The last-ever penny will be minted today in Philadelphia

- Score: 507 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45901904) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/12/business/last-penny-minted

### TL;DR

The US Mint struck ceremonial final pennies in Philadelphia after production became uneconomic at nearly four cents per coin; the last pennies intended for circulation were made in June. Existing pennies remain legal tender. Retailers now face shortages, inconsistent exact-change laws, SNAP parity rules, and no national rounding framework. One chain rounds cash purchases down, while industry groups want federal legislation. Commenters pointed to Canada’s uneventful nickel-rounding transition, but noted that stopping production by executive action is not necessarily permanent.

### Comment pulse

- Many favored retiring more low-value coins, arguing inflation has made their handling cost exceed their utility.
- Others worried cash rounding could pressure card use and deepen payment-network fees or legal inequities.

### LLM perspective

- View: Ending minting is simple; coordinating cash settlement rules across jurisdictions is the unfinished policy.
- Impact: Retailers bear near-term accounting and compliance costs while consumers experience only tiny aggregate rounding effects.
- Watch next: Congress should clarify rounding, exact-change mandates, and equal treatment of SNAP and cash transactions.
