# Pakistani newspaper mistakenly prints AI prompt with the article

- Score: 461 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45898789) | Link: https://twitter.com/omar_quraishi/status/1988518627859951986

### TL;DR

A very short social post says Pakistan’s Dawn newspaper printed an AI artifact with a business article. The supplied comments clarify that it was not the user’s prompt but a chatbot’s trailing offer to rewrite the piece in a punchier, infographic-ready style. Commenters say the online edition later acknowledged AI-assisted editing violated Dawn’s policy, removed the junk, and opened an investigation. The captured source does not independently show the printed page or underlying article, so accuracy and authorship details remain limited.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters treated the leftover chatbot phrasing as evidence that nobody completed a basic editorial read-through.
- Some argued automated drafting can reduce statistical-report drudgery, provided humans verify facts and presentation.
- Others questioned the value of reading prose generated from data instead of publishing clearer tables or graphs.
