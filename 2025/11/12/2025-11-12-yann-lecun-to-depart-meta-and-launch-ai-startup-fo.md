# Yann LeCun to depart Meta and launch AI startup focused on 'world models'

- Score: 795 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45897271) | Link: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/metas-chief-ai-scientist-yann-lecun-depart-and-launch-ai-start-focused-world-models

### TL;DR

RTTNews reports, citing people familiar with the matter, that Yann LeCun plans to leave Meta and raise money for a startup developing world models trained on visual and spatial information. The reported move follows Meta’s shift from long-term FAIR research toward large-language-model products and its new superintelligence organization under Alexandr Wang. LeCun has publicly argued that LLMs are useful but insufficient for human-like planning. Commenters split between defending patient foundational research and saying his approach produced too few results for Meta’s product priorities.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters saw world models as a necessary route toward grounded agents beyond text prediction.
- Critics said LLMs delivered visible value while LeCun’s long-running alternatives lacked comparable demonstrations.

### LLM perspective

- View: The reported departure reflects incompatible research horizons as much as a disagreement over architecture.
- Impact: Independent funding may protect long-term experimentation while exposing it to clearer milestones and investor pressure.
- Watch next: Confirmation, funding terms, technical scope, and measurable world-model benchmarks will distinguish venture from thesis.
