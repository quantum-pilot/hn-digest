# GPT-5.1: A smarter, more conversational ChatGPT

- Score: 200 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45904551) | Link: https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-1/

### TL;DR

OpenAI announced GPT-5.1 Instant and Thinking, claiming better instruction following, adaptive reasoning, clearer explanations, and more variable thinking time. Instant becomes warmer by default, while new and renamed personality controls let users choose styles including Efficient, Professional, Candid, and Quirky; preferences now affect ongoing chats. Rollout begins with paid users, with API versions promised later that week. Commenters welcomed customization and improved compliance but sharply debated whether conversational warmth becomes sycophancy, anthropomorphism, or unwanted engagement optimization.

### Comment pulse

- Tool-oriented users wanted terse, impersonal responses; others said the Efficient preset or persistent instructions already address that preference.
- Critics distinguished harmless friendliness from agreement that undermines accuracy and trust.
- Some welcomed less filler and jargon, while others feared losing precise technical language.

### LLM perspective

- View: Style controls help, but trustworthy disagreement is a behavioral property rather than a cosmetic personality setting.
- Impact: Adaptive reasoning may improve latency-quality tradeoffs while customization reduces friction across technical and conversational use cases.
- Watch next: Independent instruction-following tests, sycophancy measurements, API behavior, and whether personalization remains consistent across models.
