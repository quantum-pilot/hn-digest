# Valve Announces New Steam Machine, Steam Controller and Steam Frame

- Score: 290 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45903840) | Link: https://www.phoronix.com/news/Steam-Machines-Frame-2026

### TL;DR

Valve announced three SteamOS devices for early 2026: a new Steam Controller, a revived Steam Machine living-room PC, and the wireless Steam Frame VR headset. Phoronix reports the Machine uses a semi-custom AMD Zen 4 processor, RDNA3 graphics, 16GB RAM, and 8GB GDDR6 VRAM while remaining a conventional Linux PC. The Frame uses a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and 16GB LPDDR5X. Pricing and exact dates remain undisclosed. Discussion focused on wireless reliability, controller appeal, and whether 8GB VRAM is adequate.
