# Steam Frame

- Score: 880 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45903325) | Link: https://store.steampowered.com/sale/steamframe

### TL;DR

The frozen store-page capture contains no substantive product copy, so the available account is discussion-driven. Commenters describe Valve’s Steam Frame as a wireless, standalone ARM Linux and SteamOS VR headset with 16GB RAM, per-eye 2160-square displays, foveated streaming, and a dedicated 6GHz link to a gaming PC. Enthusiasm centers on root access, installable applications, expandable storage, and an open front port. Reservations include unknown pricing, monochrome passthrough, modest display resolution, motion sickness, and uncertain native-game performance.

### Comment pulse

- VR enthusiasts split over Valve prioritizing gaming and cost instead of full-color augmented reality.
