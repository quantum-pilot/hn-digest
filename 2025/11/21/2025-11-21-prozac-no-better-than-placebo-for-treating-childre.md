# Prozac 'no better than placebo' for treating children with depression, experts

- Score: 184 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45999622) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/society/2025/nov/20/prozac-no-better-than-placebo-for-treating-children-with-depression-experts-say

### TL;DR

A meta-analysis of 12 fluoxetine trials published from 1997 through 2024 found symptom improvements in depressed children and adolescents too small to be clinically meaningful. Researchers observed that earlier studies were more positive than later ones and argued that known harms, including sleep, weight, concentration, and suicidal-ideation risks, outweigh negligible average benefit. UK guidance currently prioritizes therapy and permits antidepressants only alongside psychological treatment under specialist supervision. A psychiatry representative urged caution because guidelines also weigh safety, feasibility, and patient preferences.
