# Prozac 'no better than placebo' for treating children with depression, experts

- Score: 184 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45999622) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/society/2025/nov/20/prozac-no-better-than-placebo-for-treating-children-with-depression-experts-say

### TL;DR

A meta-analysis of 12 trials published from 1997 through 2024 concluded that fluoxetine produced statistically small but not clinically meaningful average improvements for depression in children and adolescents. The authors argue known side effects may outweigh that benefit and want guidelines changed. NICE responded that psychological therapies are first-line and medication is considered only with therapy and specialist supervision for some moderate or severe cases. Another psychiatrist urged caution because guidelines weigh more than average effect size. This report should not guide individual treatment changes.
