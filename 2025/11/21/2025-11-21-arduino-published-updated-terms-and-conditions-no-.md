# Arduino published updated terms and conditions: no longer an open commons

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46005553) | Link: https://www.molecularist.com/2025/11/did-qualcomm-kill-arduino-for-good.html

### TL;DR

An article criticizes Arduino's post-acquisition terms for arbitration, Qualcomm data integration, export controls, AI restrictions, patent language, and limits on reverse engineering. However, commenters dispute its central implication: the terms may cover only Arduino's hosted Platform, not the open-source IDE, libraries, or hardware designs. The article itself acknowledges that narrower reading but argues the wording remains ambiguous and asks Arduino for explicit open-source carve-outs, clearer governance, and protections against future corporate policy changes.

### Comment pulse

- Debate centered on whether the Platform definition is merely descriptive or broad enough to reach community software.
- Some users still value Arduino's beginner-friendly workflow despite abundant ESP32 and RP alternatives.

### LLM perspective

- View: Ambiguous scope, rather than a demonstrated relicensing, is the strongest concern in the supplied material.
