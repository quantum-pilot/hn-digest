# Arduino published updated terms and conditions: no longer an open commons

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46005553) | Link: https://www.molecularist.com/2025/11/did-qualcomm-kill-arduino-for-good.html

### TL;DR

Six weeks after Qualcomm acquired Arduino, an essay argues that revised platform terms introduce arbitration, data integration, patent disclaimers, reverse-engineering restrictions, and uncertainty incompatible with the project’s open ecosystem. The feared harm is less about aging boards than the IDE, libraries, tutorials, and curricula that make electronics approachable. Commenters dispute the scope: one reading confines the terms to hosted sites and cloud services, while others find the definition ambiguous and distrust corporate stewardship. Clear carve-outs and independent governance would be stronger evidence than broad assurances.
