# How a French judge was digitally cut off by the USA

- Score: 378 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46003778) | Link: https://www.heise.de/en/news/How-a-French-judge-was-digitally-cut-off-by-the-USA-11087561.html

### TL;DR

Heise reports that US sanctions against ICC judge Nicolas Guillou, imposed after arrest warrants for Benjamin Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant, triggered account closures or restrictions involving Amazon, Airbnb, PayPal, Expedia, card networks, banks, and dollar transactions—even while he lived in France. Guillou argues this dependence lets US policy reach deeply into European life and urges digital and banking sovereignty. He also wants the EU to activate its blocking regulation, which can prohibit compliance with conflicting foreign sanctions and enable damages claims.
