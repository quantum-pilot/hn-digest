# How Cops Are Using Flock's ALPR Network to Surveil Protesters and Activists

- Score: 187 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46006521) | Link: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/11/how-cops-are-using-flock-safetys-alpr-network-surveil-protesters-and-activists

### TL;DR

EFF says public records covering more than 12 million Flock searches by over 3,900 agencies show more than 50 agencies ran hundreds of queries connected to 2025 protests. Reasons were often only “protest,” although some searches concerned threats or alleged crimes. Because Flock's nationwide ALPR network records every passing vehicle with time and location, EFF argues these searches expose lawful demonstrators to warrantless tracking. Its review captures explicit protest terms, while vague reason fields may conceal additional uses that the dataset cannot prove.
