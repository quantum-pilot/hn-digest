# HP and Dell disable HEVC support built into their laptops' CPUs

- Score: 214 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46002989) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2025/11/hp-and-dell-disable-hevc-support-built-into-their-laptops-cpus/

### TL;DR

Some HP and Dell laptops cannot use HEVC acceleration in Windows browsers even though their Intel or AMD silicon contains the capability. HP lists affected business lines; Dell’s disclosures are harder to find, and both vendors direct users toward licensed software or qualifying premium configurations. Neither explained the decision. Rising US royalties—from $0.20 to $0.24 per unit above 100,001 devices in January—offer a plausible motive, not confirmation. Symptoms and workarounds vary because licensing, codecs, OEM drivers, applications, and hardware are separate layers.
