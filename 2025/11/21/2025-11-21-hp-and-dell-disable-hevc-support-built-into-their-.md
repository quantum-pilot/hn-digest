# HP and Dell disable HEVC support built into their laptops' CPUs

- Score: 214 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46002989) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2025/11/hp-and-dell-disable-hevc-support-built-into-their-laptops-cpus/

### TL;DR

HP and Dell have omitted HEVC hardware acceleration on some business laptops even though their Intel or AMD processors can perform it. HP discloses affected lines in data sheets; Dell's limitations are harder to identify. Both point users toward licensed software or selected premium configurations. The article infers licensing costs may motivate the choice as US royalties rise from $0.20 to $0.24 per high-volume unit, but neither vendor confirmed that reason. Users report confusing browser failures and possible performance degradation.
