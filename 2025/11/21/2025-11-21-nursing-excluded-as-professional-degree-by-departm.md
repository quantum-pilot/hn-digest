# Nursing excluded as 'professional' degree by Department of Education

- Score: 140 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46000015) | Link: https://nurse.org/news/nursing-excluded-as-professional-degree-dept-of-ed/

### TL;DR

A proposed US Education Department definition implementing new federal loan rules omits graduate nursing, physician-assistant, and physical-therapy programs from the higher-limit “professional degree” category. Nursing advocates argue that losing access to larger borrowing limits and Grad PLUS could make advanced training less accessible and worsen staffing gaps, especially in underserved areas. The classification concerns financial-aid treatment, not whether nursing is a profession. Those downstream workforce effects remain forecasts, and the listed measures are scheduled for July 2026.
