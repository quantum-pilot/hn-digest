# RuBee

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029932) | Link: https://computer.rip/2025-11-22-RuBee.html

### TL;DR

IEEE 1902.1 is an active-tag networking protocol that communicates through low-frequency magnetic coupling rather than conventional far-field radio. Its roughly 1,200-baud links are slow, but tags can be detected reliably around metal, water, people, explosives, and secure facilities while becoming extremely difficult to intercept at distance. Those properties moved a cold-chain idea into a durable defense niche: armory inventory, shot counting, classified-tool tracking, and phone detection. The standardized protocol survives through specialized deployments despite limited success against modern RFID elsewhere.
