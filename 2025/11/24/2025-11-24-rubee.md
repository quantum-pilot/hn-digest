# RuBee

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029932) | Link: https://computer.rip/2025-11-22-RuBee.html

### TL;DR

RuBee is an obscure IEEE 1902.1 asset-tracking network using battery-powered tags and 131 kHz magnetic coupling. Unlike far-field RFID, it can detect tagged objects through metal, water, containers, and human bodies at useful short ranges while becoming difficult to intercept farther away. Its slow 1,200-baud operation suits reliable presence detection more than data transfer. Originally imagined for cold-chain monitoring, RuBee found a niche tracking weapons, tools, ordnance, and phones around military and Department of Energy facilities.

### Comment pulse

- RuBee’s niche follows physics → shielding resistance and steep distance decay matter more than throughput in secure inventories.
- Simple temperature indicators can verify cold-chain breaches → continuous tags additionally warn before thresholds are exceeded.
- Weapon instrumentation remains politically fraught → tracking, maintenance, accountability, tampering, and surveillance concerns overlap.
