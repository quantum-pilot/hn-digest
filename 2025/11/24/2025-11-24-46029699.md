# Ask HN: Hearing aid wearers, what's hot?

- Score: 333 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029699) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029699

### TL;DR

An experienced hearing-aid wearer seeks replacements for five-year-old Phonak Audeo 90 RIC devices, prioritizing music, nature, meetings, and noisy environments. Responses are personal reports rather than controlled comparisons: one longtime wearer praises Oticon Intent’s spatial processing, directionality, noise reduction, and music mode, while another recommends Phonak Audéo Sphere Infinio for noise. Others emphasize that aids do not restore ordinary hearing and suggest live-caption glasses, phone transcription, directional microphones, or remote microphones as complementary tools.

### Comment pulse

- Live captions can materially improve meetings → speaker separation, subscriptions, privacy, and accuracy remain practical questions.
- Hearing-in-noise remains the hardest problem → directional processing helps, but remote microphones may isolate speech more reliably.

### LLM perspective

- View: The best solution may be a fitted aid plus situational accessibility tools, not one device.
- Impact: Buyers should evaluate speech-in-noise, music fidelity, call routing, and battery life separately.
- Watch next: Audiologist trials using real environments, personalized programs, and companion-device compatibility.
