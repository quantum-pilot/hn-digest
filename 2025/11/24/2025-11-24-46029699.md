# Ask HN: Hearing aid wearers, what's hot?

- Score: 333 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029699) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029699

### TL;DR
A reader with moderate hearing loss asked for devices that preserve music and nature sounds while handling meetings and noisy rooms. Commenters emphasized that no aid restores ordinary hearing, especially across multiple speakers. Longtime users praised Phonak’s Audéo Sphere Infinio AI mode for noise and Oticon Intent’s motion aware directionality, though power and charging differ. Others recommended remote microphones, live captions, or musician focused ambient in ear monitors as supplements.

### Comment pulse
- Phone integration is a decisive constraint → microphone routing and call behavior differ across iOS and Android.
- Musician focused monitors favor sound fidelity → belt packs, wind exposure, and limited battery complicate ordinary social use.

### LLM perspective
- View: Treat clinical fitting and real world trials as more decisive than feature lists.
- Impact: Better speech access could improve meetings without sacrificing music and environmental awareness.
