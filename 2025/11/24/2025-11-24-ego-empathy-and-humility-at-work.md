# Ego, empathy, and humility at work

- Score: 154 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029534) | Link: https://matthogg.fyi/a-unified-theory-of-ego-empathy-and-humility-at-work/

### TL;DR

The essay treats workplace ego as persistent self-justification that can surface through jargon, gatekeeping, certainty, or dismissing others’ context. It reframes empathy as gathering new information and humility as letting that information change behavior, arguing that both improve technical problem-solving, trust, and career growth without requiring submission. Commenters support curiosity but challenge the essay’s broad inferences: acronyms and firm tool recommendations may save time or express valid expertise, and some boundaries are necessary rather than exclusionary.
