# The Cloudflare outage might be a good thing

- Score: 240 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46029908) | Link: https://gist.github.com/jbreckmckye/32587f2907e473dd06d68b0362fb0048

### TL;DR

A Cloudflare configuration error triggered an outage, exposing how ordinary faults at a dominant intermediary can disable unrelated digital services, even internet-dependent tire pumps. The essay treats disruption as a useful warning: essential systems need offline fallbacks, provider diversity, and operational slack rather than assuming permanent connectivity. Commenters complicated that prescription. Large platforms are often more internally redundant than self-hosted alternatives, bot and DDoS pressure makes them attractive, and multi-provider resilience can cost more than occasional downtime. The core hazard is correlated failure, not simply centralized ownership.

### Comment pulse
