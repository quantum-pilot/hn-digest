# Ask HN: Where to begin with "modern" Emacs?

- Score: 89 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45783376) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45783376

### TL;DR

An experienced Neovim user asked how to approach modern Emacs without adopting a large distribution. Replies rejected the idea of one canonical guide, instead recommending the GNU manual, built-in introspection, community resources, and small starter configurations such as minimal-emacs or Bedrock. Vertico, Consult, and Corfu were cited as common contemporary packages. The broader advice was to treat Emacs as a programmable Lisp environment: start close to vanilla, understand each addition, and let concrete needs shape the configuration.

### Comment pulse

- Several users stressed that Emacs rewards inspecting functions and keys directly rather than copying a fashionable configuration.
- Opinions differed on ergonomics, including whether remapping Control is essential or merely personal preference.

### LLM perspective

- View: Emacs onboarding works best when configuration remains explainable and grows from actual friction.
- Impact: A small base reduces debugging ambiguity while teaching the editor's native discovery tools.
- Watch next: Compare starter kits by how clearly they expose, rather than conceal, ordinary Emacs behavior.
