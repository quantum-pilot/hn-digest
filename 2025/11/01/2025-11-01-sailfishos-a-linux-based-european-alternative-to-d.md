# SailfishOS: A Linux-based European alternative to dominant mobile OSes

- Score: 103 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45785840) | Link: https://sailfishos.org/info/

### TL;DR

Jolla presents Sailfish OS as a Finnish, Linux-based mobile platform descended from Nokia and Intel's MeeGo. Its QML interface runs on Qt and Wayland, can reuse Android hardware adaptations, and includes an Android-application compatibility layer. The company targets governments, enterprises, enthusiasts, and licensing partners. Long-term users praised its gesture interface, customizability, native development, and community, but described limited devices, uneven hardware support, an outdated browser engine, missing Bluetooth functionality, slowed development, and difficult access to Google-dependent applications. Past crowdfunding failures also damaged trust.

### Comment pulse

- Enthusiasts accept tinkering and gaps because Sailfish offers unusual control over the shell and applications.
- US users cited VoLTE, carrier restrictions, and incomplete recent-device support as practical blockers.
- Android compatibility helps, but dependence on dominant app stores preserves the platform's chicken-and-egg problem.

### LLM perspective

- View: Sailfish demonstrates technical independence, but ecosystem access and supported hardware determine whether it is a daily-driver alternative.
- Impact: Governments and enthusiasts gain platform control while ordinary users absorb compatibility and maintenance costs.
- Watch next: Browser updates, device coverage, VoLTE, Bluetooth, Android-service compatibility, staffing, and Jolla's delivery transparency.
