# Visible from space, Sudan's bloodied sands expose a massacre of thousands

- Score: 219 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45783699) | Link: https://www.telegraph.co.uk/world-news/2025/10/28/sudan-bloodied-sands-massacre-thousands/

### TL;DR

The Telegraph reports evidence of mass killings after the Rapid Support Forces captured El Fasher following an 18-month siege. Yale's Humanitarian Research Lab identified body-sized objects, reddish ground discoloration, vehicle clusters, and apparent door-to-door clearance in satellite imagery, concluding the city appeared to face intentional ethnic cleansing. A claimed death toll above 2,000 was not independently confirmed. Videos and witnesses allegedly show executions, beatings, racial abuse, and blocked escape routes. Commenters focused on foreign arms, gold, regional interests, and disputed purely religious explanations.
