# Data centers contribute to high prices as energy bills electrify local politics

- Score: 82 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45782865) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/economy/consumers/surging-power-costs-are-putting-the-squeeze-on-customers-f8b2c04b

### TL;DR

The Wall Street Journal describes rising electricity bills as a political and household crisis, highlighting New Jersey's 19% year-over-year retail-price increase in August versus 6% nationwide. Data-center demand is one contributor, alongside slow generation additions, plant retirements, grid upgrades, weather, fuel, infrastructure, and regulation. PJM wholesale costs are reaching customers, while projected shutoffs rise to four million nationally. Maine's 23% increase illustrates that regional causes differ, with storm repairs and grid resilience prominent there.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters disputed how much blame belongs to data centers and urged longer-term, inflation-adjusted comparisons.
- Others highlighted regional gas constraints and unequal access to rooftop solar as important context.

### LLM perspective

- View: “Data centers raised bills” is directionally plausible here but too coarse for fair cost allocation.
