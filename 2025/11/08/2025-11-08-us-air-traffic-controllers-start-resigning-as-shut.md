# US air traffic controllers start resigning as shutdown bites

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45860865) | Link: https://www.thedailybeast.com/air-traffic-controllers-start-resigning-as-shutdown-bites/

### TL;DR

The air-traffic controllers’ union president said controllers were resigning daily as the prolonged federal shutdown left them unpaid, burned out, and sometimes unable to cover fuel or childcare. He reported the workforce was 400 controllers shorter than during the 2019 shutdown. The administration cut flights by 10% across 40 high-traffic markets, and officials warned recovery could take months. Discussion ranged from alternative user-fee funding to shutdown strategy and authoritarian fears, with strong disagreement over motives, blame, and likely social consequences.

### Comment pulse

- Some favored aviation use fees to insulate control services; others noted taxpayers, cargo, military, and emergency flights all benefit.
- Political interpretations were polarized, from deliberate destabilization claims to arguments that this remained escalating partisan brinkmanship.
