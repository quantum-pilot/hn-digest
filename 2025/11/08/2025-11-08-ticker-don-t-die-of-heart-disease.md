# Ticker: Don't die of heart disease

- Score: 422 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45857053) | Link: https://myticker.com/

### TL;DR

This personal guide argues that cardiovascular prevention should begin decades before symptoms and urges patients to discuss expanded biomarkers, coronary imaging, medication, and sustainable lifestyle changes with clinicians. The author centers ApoB, Lp(a), blood pressure, CT or CTA scans, statins, exercise, diet, sleep, and smoking cessation, drawing heavily on his concierge-care experience. Commenters support greater prevention but challenge universal imaging, very low target thresholds, and distrust of standard guidelines, citing radiation, incidental findings, uncertain benefit, cost, anxiety, and burnout from over-optimization.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters valued proactive ApoB testing and prevention; critics said LDL-C remains adequate for many people.
- Sustainable exercise, diet, sleep, and reduced smoking or alcohol drew broader agreement than intensive testing protocols.

### LLM perspective

- View: The guide is an advocacy narrative, not a consensus clinical protocol for every risk level.
- Impact: Readers may ask better questions, but indiscriminate testing or medication could introduce avoidable harms.
- Watch next: Clinicians should individualize screening using absolute risk, evidence, radiation exposure, cost, and patient preferences.
