# Apple's "notarisation" – blocking software freedom of developers and users

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45854441) | Link: https://fsfe.org/news/2025/news-20251105-01.en.html

### TL;DR

The Free Software Foundation Europe argues that Apple’s iOS notarisation rules preserve gatekeeping that the EU Digital Markets Act was meant to reduce. A civil-society complaint says mandatory Apple review and re-signing still controls apps distributed outside the App Store, while eligibility rules for alternative stores exclude smaller or nonprofit projects. FSFE proposes decentralized curation, reproducible builds, and community audits instead. This is an advocacy and legal position: whether Apple’s controls are necessary, proportionate security measures under the DMA remains contested.
