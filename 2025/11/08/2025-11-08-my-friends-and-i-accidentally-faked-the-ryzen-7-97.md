# My friends and I accidentally faked the Ryzen 7 9700X3D leaks

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45855933) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/pcmasterrace/comments/1orc6jl/my_friends_and_i_accidentally_faked_the_ryzen_7/

### TL;DR

A group altered Linux’s reported CPU name on an overclocked Ryzen 7 9700X, submitted a PassMark result labeled “9700X3D,” and unintentionally seeded widespread leak coverage for a processor they say is not real. PassMark accepted the mismatch, and outlets repeated the single result; some added unsupported specifications, while others flagged suspicious clock speeds or later corrected stories. The episode demonstrates that software-visible hardware identity, including CPUID through virtualization or debugging mechanisms, can be spoofed, so isolated public benchmark entries cannot authenticate unreleased products.

### Comment pulse
