# 52 Year old data tape could contain Unix history

- Score: 169 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45857695) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/11/07/unix_fourth_edition_tape_rediscovered/

### TL;DR

University of Utah staff found a nine-track tape labeled as an original Bell Labs copy of UNIX Fourth Edition from 1973. If the label is accurate and the media remains readable, it could preserve the first UNIX release whose kernel and core utilities were rewritten in C; only fragments and documentation are otherwise known to survive. The tape, apparently received by Utah Teapot creator Martin Newell, is being hand-delivered to the Computer History Museum, where specialists plan an analog capture and software-assisted recovery.
