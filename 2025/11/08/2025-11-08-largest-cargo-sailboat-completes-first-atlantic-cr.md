# Largest cargo sailboat completes first Atlantic crossing

- Score: 151 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45859471) | Link: https://www.marineinsight.com/shipping-news/worlds-largest-cargo-sailboat-completes-historic-first-atlantic-crossing/

### TL;DR

The 136-metre Neoliner Origin completed its first Atlantic crossing after a storm damaged its aft semi-rigid sail, forcing partial use of its auxiliary motor and remaining sail. The French roll-on/roll-off vessel carries up to 5,300 tonnes, targets monthly Europe–North America service at about 11 knots, and is claimed to cut emissions 80–90% versus conventional cargo ships. Commenters welcomed the experiment but stressed its much smaller capacity and slower passage than major container ships, making schedule reliability and per-ton economics crucial.

### Comment pulse
