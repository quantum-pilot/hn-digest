# Thunderbird adds native Microsoft Exchange email support

- Score: 301 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45978423) | Link: https://blog.thunderbird.net/2025/11/thunderbird-adds-native-microsoft-exchange-email-support/

### TL;DR

Thunderbird 145 now provides native Exchange email through Exchange Web Services, removing the need for an add-on for supported accounts. It handles OAuth2 setup for standard Microsoft-hosted domains, folders, synchronization, common message operations, attachments, search and quick filtering. Calendar, contacts, Microsoft Graph, custom tenant applications, NTLM and some on-premise authentication remain future work. Commenters welcomed a stronger Outlook alternative but noted EWS retirement for Exchange Online limits its horizon, while on-premise EWS remains supported.

### Comment pulse

- Users praised Thunderbird's historical role as a cross-platform, open alternative to Outlook and proprietary mail ecosystems.
- Enterprise usefulness remains conditional → administrators can disable third-party access, and current authentication support excludes some organizational configurations.
