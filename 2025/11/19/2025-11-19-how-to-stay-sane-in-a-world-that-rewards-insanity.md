# How to stay sane in a world that rewards insanity

- Score: 154 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45980106) | Link: https://www.joanwestenberg.com/p/how-to-stay-sane-in-a-world-that-rewards-insanity

### TL;DR

This opinion essay argues that polarization succeeds because certainty, tribal loyalty, and provocation produce attention, community, influence, and money, while nuance offers slower rewards. Public identities then become traps: changing position risks audience and status. The author recommends deliberately reading strong opposing arguments, separating a cause’s importance from the truth of individual claims, and cultivating communities where uncertainty and changing one’s mind are safe. These are personal observations and prescriptions, not empirical findings, and comments challenge whether influencers actually lose followers when abruptly switching views.
