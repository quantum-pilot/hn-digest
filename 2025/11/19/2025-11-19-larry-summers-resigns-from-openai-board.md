# Larry Summers resigns from OpenAI board

- Score: 199 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45979190) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/19/larry-summers-epstein-openai.html

### TL;DR

Larry Summers resigned from OpenAI’s board and took leave from his Harvard roles after newly released correspondence with Jeffrey Epstein drew scrutiny. Harvard opened a review, and his spokesperson said co-teachers would finish the semester’s remaining classes; he was not scheduled to teach the next semester. Summers had earlier said he would step back from public commitments and expressed shame for continuing communication with Epstein. The disclosed documents came from a congressional subpoena of Epstein’s estate. OpenAI said it respected the resignation and acknowledged Summers’ contributions.
