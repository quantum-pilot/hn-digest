# Building more with GPT-5.1-Codex-Max

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45982649) | Link: https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-1-codex-max/

### TL;DR

OpenAI introduced GPT-5.1-Codex-Max for long-running agentic coding in Codex, with API access planned later. Its central feature is native compaction across context windows, intended to sustain coherent work over millions of tokens and multi-hour sessions. OpenAI reports higher scores on three coding benchmarks and 30% fewer thinking tokens than GPT-5.1-Codex at equal medium reasoning effort; those are vendor-reported evaluations. The model also adds Windows training and an xhigh reasoning option, while the announcement stresses sandboxing, restricted network access, logs, and human review.
