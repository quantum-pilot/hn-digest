# The patent office is about to make bad patents untouchable

- Score: 120 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45985890) | Link: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/11/patent-office-about-make-bad-patents-untouchable

### TL;DR

The EFF opposes proposed USPTO rules restricting inter partes review, the administrative process used to challenge allegedly invalid patents before the Patent Trial and Appeal Board. It says petitioners could be forced to surrender court validity defenses, barred after any earlier validity fight, or denied review when district court is projected to move faster. EFF argues these procedural gates would shield overbroad patents and leave small defendants facing multimillion-dollar litigation, citing prior podcasting, fitness-data and delivery-notification patents canceled through review.
