# This week in 1988, Robert Morris unleashed his eponymous worm

- Score: 436 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45812024) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/on-this-day-in-1988-the-morris-worm-slithered-out-and-sparked-a-new-era-in-cybersecurity-10-percent-of-the-internet-was-infected-within-24-hours

### TL;DR

The article recounts Robert Tappan Morris releasing a self-propagating Unix program in November 1988. It exploited weaknesses in sendmail and finger, spread across VAX and Sun-3 systems, and multiplied far more aggressively than intended, slowing or disabling connected institutions. Morris was later convicted under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act and received probation, a fine, and community service. The frequently repeated estimate that 6,000 machines represented 10% of the internet was strongly disputed by commenters, though eyewitnesses remembered serious operational disruption.
