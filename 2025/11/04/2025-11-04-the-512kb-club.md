# The 512KB Club

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45812499) | Link: https://512kb.club/

### TL;DR

The 512KB Club catalogs content-focused websites whose total uncompressed resources stay below 512KB, dividing members into green under 100KB, orange under 250KB, and blue under 512KB. Its premise is that strict budgets counter JavaScript, font, and image bloat. Commenters praised server-rendered HTML, progressive enhancement, and native browser features, while noting that personal sites face easier constraints than commercial ones. They also cautioned that transfer size is only a proxy: runtime memory, accessibility, responsiveness, privacy, and useful imagery still matter.

### Comment pulse

- Readers viewed a byte budget as a productive constraint, especially for simple, content-led sites.
- Others warned against turning one measurable number into a complete quality score.

### LLM perspective

- View: A hard transfer budget is useful discipline, but it cannot certify that a site serves users well.
- Impact: The list gives developers concrete examples for challenging default-heavy web stacks and unnecessary assets.
- Watch next: Audits that pair bytes with runtime cost, accessibility, privacy, caching, and realistic application complexity.
