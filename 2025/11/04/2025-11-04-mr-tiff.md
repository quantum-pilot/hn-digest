# Mr Tiff

- Score: 432 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45816853) | Link: https://inventingthefuture.ghost.io/mr-tiff/

### TL;DR

Technology historian John Buck spent years identifying the individual behind TIFF, which histories usually credited only to Aldus. Misspellings obscured Stephen E. Carlsen’s name, while an invisible white-on-white credit in the specification finally provided the clue. Patent records and a mailed letter led to a brief exchange confirming Carlsen’s role in defining and promoting a common scanned-image format. After learning from Carlsen’s former wife that he had died, Buck updated Wikipedia to credit the engineer rather than only the company.
