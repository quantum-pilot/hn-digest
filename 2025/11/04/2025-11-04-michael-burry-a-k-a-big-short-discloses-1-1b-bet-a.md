# Michael Burry a.k.a. "Big Short",discloses $1.1B bet against Nvidia&Palantir

- Score: 171 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45813734) | Link: https://sherwood.news/markets/michael-burry-big-short-discloses-1-1-billion-options-bet-against-nvidia-palantir-puts/

### TL;DR

Scion Asset Management disclosed put options covering roughly five million Palantir shares and one million Nvidia shares, reported at notional values of $912 million and $187 million. Those positions represented 80% of its disclosed U.S. equity holdings. Both companies had risen sharply amid AI enthusiasm, while Burry warned of bubbles. Commenters stressed that the $1.1 billion headline is not the premium paid or maximum loss: a 13-F shows underlying share value, leaving strike prices, expirations, premiums, and exact strategy unknown.
