# When stick figures fought

- Score: 363 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45806348) | Link: https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/when-stick-figures-fought

### TL;DR

Animation Obsessive traces Zhu Zhiqiang's Xiao Xiao, a Chinese Flash series whose simple, highly choreographed stick-figure fights spread globally through internet cafes, Newgrounds, and countless copies in the early 2000s. Self-taught Zhu turned a spare-time animation into commercial work, but struggled to convert viral reach into durable ownership and income. He sued Nike over a similar Stickman campaign, initially won, then lost on appeal when judges found the figures too different and simple to copyright. Commenters recalled Flash as an unusually accessible creative and technical gateway.
