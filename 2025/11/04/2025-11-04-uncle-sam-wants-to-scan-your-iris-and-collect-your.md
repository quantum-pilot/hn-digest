# Uncle Sam wants to scan your iris and collect your DNA, citizen or not

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45817167) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/11/04/dhs_wants_to_collect_biometric_data/

### TL;DR

The Register describes a proposed DHS and USCIS rule that could require biometrics from a broad circle around immigration cases, including petitioners, sponsors, dependents, beneficiaries, permanent residents, citizens, and people of any age unless exempted. The proposal expands biometrics beyond fingerprints and photographs to possible ocular scans, voiceprints, DNA, and behavioral traits, citing identity, fraud, security, documents, family relationships, and some eligibility checks. Critics in the article and comments emphasize misidentification, spoofing, retention, surveillance, and the permanence of compromised biological identifiers.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters stressed that biometrics identify people but are not secret or replaceable credentials.
- The frozen article describes a proposal open to comment, not an enacted requirement.

### LLM perspective

- View: The consequential change is relationship-based collection extending well beyond the direct immigration applicant.
- Impact: Families and citizens could face durable privacy risks from data that cannot be reissued after compromise.
- Watch next: Final scope, exemptions, retention, access controls, error challenges, and the agency's response to comments.
