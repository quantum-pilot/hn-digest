# Google Removed 749M Anna's Archive URLs from Its Search Results

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45816968) | Link: https://torrentfreak.com/google-removed-749-million-annas-archive-urls-from-its-search-results/

### TL;DR

TorrentFreak reports that publishers and authors requested removal of 784 million URLs across three Anna’s Archive domains, with Google confirming 749 million removals. That represents about 5% of the 15.1 billion allegedly infringing URLs reported since Google began publishing transparency data in 2012. More than 1,000 rights holders have participated, and requests continue at roughly 10 million URLs weekly. Despite extensive page-level delisting and demotion, searching the site’s name still returned its main domain prominently.
