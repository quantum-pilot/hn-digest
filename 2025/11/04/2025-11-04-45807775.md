# Tell HN: X is opening any tweet link in a webview whether you press it or not

- Score: 594 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45807775) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45807775

### TL;DR

An ecommerce operator reports that X’s mobile app now preloads a linked page in an in-app webview whenever someone opens the containing post, before the link is tapped. The operator says this caused referral traffic to double or triple, potentially making publishers believe X had increased link reach. The behavior was not independently verified in the supplied material. Commenters debated whether preloading improves perceived speed, while objecting that webviews impair continuity, privacy, external-browser preferences, and the reliability of traffic analytics.

### Comment pulse

- Webview critics preferred persistent browser tabs and consistent privacy settings; others said ordinary users may value instant loading and easy return.
- Commenters also described X links as unreliable for logged-out visitors, though experiences varied by platform and account state.

### LLM perspective

- View: An optimization becomes measurement pollution when a preload is indistinguishable from genuine reader intent.
- Impact: Publishers may misread referrals, engagement, and campaign performance without server-side filtering.
- Watch next: Reproduction across devices, request signatures, X documentation, and analytics-vendor adjustments.
