# My Truck Desk

- Score: 430 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45806903) | Link: https://www.theparisreview.org/blog/2025/10/29/truck-desk/

### TL;DR

Writer and petrochemical mechanic Bud Smith describes producing stories and novels during short work breaks, delays, and layoffs. A derelict F-150 became a private office after he built a fitted wooden desk across its steering wheel and armrests. When the truck was scrapped, he replaced the elaborate design with a plain scaffold plank that fits different crew pickups. His lesson is practical rather than romantic: artists with jobs or caregiving duties improvise conditions, protect small intervals, and let financial reality guide them.
