# Ask HN: Who wants to be hired? (November 2025)

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45800464) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45800464

### TL;DR

This monthly hiring thread invites people personally seeking work to post a structured profile covering location, remote availability, relocation, technologies, résumé, and contact details. Recruiters and agencies are explicitly off-topic, and readers should contact posters only about jobs. The sampled replies span full-stack, backend, mobile, voice-AI, and technical-lead experience, with candidates based in Thailand, the EU, Ethiopia, Canada, and Iran. Several seek remote work, while some are also open to relocation or short fractional engagements.

### Comment pulse

- Profiles emphasize concrete stacks, preferred work modes, and direct contact, making the thread a lightweight candidate directory.
- Availability ranges from actively seeking roles to selectively considering technical or creative opportunities.
