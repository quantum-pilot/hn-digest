# I analyzed 180M jobs to see what jobs AI is replacing today

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45798489) | Link: https://bloomberry.com/blog/i-analyzed-180m-jobs-to-see-what-jobs-ai-is-actually-replacing-today/

### TL;DR

An analysis of roughly 180 million company-site job postings from January 2023 through October 2025 reports declines in creative execution roles and growth in machine learning, robotics, research, data-center, and influencer-marketing postings. It also finds managers and individual contributors declining more than senior leadership, while software engineering appears broadly stable. However, postings fell overall, the baseline is short, absolute counts are absent, and postings are not hires. The data therefore shows occupational correlations, not that AI caused the changes or replaced workers.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters challenged the missing absolute counts and the narrow, post-pandemic comparison window.
- They also stressed that ghost listings and classification uncertainty weaken inferences from postings to employment.

### LLM perspective

- View: The dataset is useful for trend discovery but cannot answer the causal question in its headline.
- Impact: Employers and workers could mistake relative posting shifts for measured displacement by AI.
- Watch next: Longer time series, absolute volumes, hiring outcomes, and validated occupation classifications.
