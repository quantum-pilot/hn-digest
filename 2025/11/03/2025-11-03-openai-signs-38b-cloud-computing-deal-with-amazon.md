# OpenAI signs $38B cloud computing deal with Amazon

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45799211) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/03/technology/openai-amazon-cloud-computing.html

### TL;DR

OpenAI agreed to buy $38 billion of Amazon cloud services over seven years, adding AWS to a growing set of compute suppliers. The deal followed a renegotiation that freed OpenAI from needing Microsoft’s approval for other cloud purchases. The capacity is intended to support development and deployment of products including ChatGPT, amid industry-wide infrastructure spending. The article notes that OpenAI has substantial revenue but remains unprofitable, making its expanding commitments central to concerns about an AI investment bubble and future financing needs.
