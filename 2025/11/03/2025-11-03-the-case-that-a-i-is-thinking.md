# The Case That A.I. Is Thinking

- Score: 211 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45802029) | Link: https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2025/11/10/the-case-that-ai-is-thinking

### TL;DR

The essay argues that language models may exhibit a limited form of thinking—understanding without consciousness or an inner life. It frames understanding as compression and recognition: training distills regularities into high-dimensional representations that support analogy, planning-like behavior, and useful problem solving. Neuroscientists see models as testable systems for studying cognition, though cited interpretability work is not peer-reviewed. The author ultimately urges middle skepticism: models remain inefficient learners, freeze after training, lack embodied experience, struggle with physical and spatial reasoning, and may be reaching scaling limits.
