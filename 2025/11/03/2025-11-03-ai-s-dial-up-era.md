# AI's Dial-Up Era

- Score: 374 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45804377) | Link: https://www.wreflection.com/p/ai-dial-up-era

### TL;DR

The essay likens today’s AI moment to the mid-1990s internet: transformative potential may coexist with bubbles, failures, and unreliable forecasts. It argues that employment effects depend on whether productivity unlocks enough unmet demand before markets saturate, using radiology and historical industrial examples to illustrate that automation can initially expand jobs and later reduce them. Repetitive, low-risk digital tasks appear more exposed, but roles may transform rather than vanish. Even if many AI companies fail, the author expects infrastructure investment to outlast them.

### Comment pulse

- Critics questioned whether dial-up is the right analogy, proposing centralized mainframes or timesharing instead.
- Others argued on-device models or a single technical breakthrough could overturn infrastructure-heavy forecasts.
