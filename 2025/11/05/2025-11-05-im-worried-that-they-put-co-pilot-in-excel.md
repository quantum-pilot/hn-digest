# I’m worried that they put co-pilot in Excel

- Score: 404 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45820872) | Link: https://simonwillison.net/2025/Nov/5/brenda/

### TL;DR

A quoted monologue warns that Excel Copilot could let executives bypass experienced finance staff and alter critical spreadsheets without recognizing hallucinated errors. “Brenda” represents the mid-level expert whose tacit knowledge keeps financial operations working. Commenters refined the contrast: deterministic automation can be tested and mentally modeled, while generative output remains variable and difficult to explain. They argued that AI may assist knowledgeable spreadsheet users, but unreviewed use by people lacking domain or Excel expertise risks rapid, confident, large-scale mistakes.
