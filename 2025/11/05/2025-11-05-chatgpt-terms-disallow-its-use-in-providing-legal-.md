# ChatGPT terms disallow its use in providing legal and medical advice to others

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45825965) | Link: https://www.ctvnews.ca/sci-tech/article/openai-updates-policies-so-chatgpt-wont-provide-medical-or-legal-advice/

### TL;DR

The report’s extracted body is largely missing, but its correction and discussion narrow the claim: OpenAI said model behavior had not changed, and commenters read the policy as restricting third parties from building services that provide tailored legal or medical advice to others, not banning individuals from asking related questions. Users described ChatGPT as useful for interpreting records or terminology, yet disputed its reliability, privacy implications, and suitability for consequential decisions. The precise terms were not quoted in the supplied discussion.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters called the limitation reasonable because repackaged model output can masquerade as validated professional advice.
- Users reported useful explanations, but emphasized judgment, clinician review, and the sensitivity of uploaded medical records.
