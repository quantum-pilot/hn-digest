# DNS LOC Record (2014)

- Score: 126 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46087596) | Link: https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-weird-and-wonderful-world-of-dns-loc-records/

### TL;DR

Cloudflare's DNS server accepted queries and could encode LOC responses, yet failed to parse the text records stored in its database. A customer report exposed the missing link despite only 743 such entries. RFC 1876 represents latitude, longitude, and altitude in fixed-width fields, plus size and precision bytes whose hexadecimal nibbles encode a digit and power of ten, spanning 1 centimeter to 90,000 kilometers. Cloudflare added a parser and tests; readers debated the obscure feature's practical value and current deployment.
