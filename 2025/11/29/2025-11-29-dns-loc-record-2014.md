# DNS LOC Record (2014)

- Score: 126 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46087596) | Link: https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-weird-and-wonderful-world-of-dns-loc-records/

### TL;DR

DNS LOC records encode latitude, longitude, altitude, physical size, and precision. In 2014, Cloudflare held only 743 such records, yet a customer exposed a missing parser between textual records and the fixed binary wire format. The repair required handling coordinates plus compact base-and-exponent precision fields that span centimeters to 90,000 kilometers. Commenters imagined homelab and fleet-location uses, while noting TXT records can store richer operational metadata and that LOC accuracy may not identify devices within a room.
