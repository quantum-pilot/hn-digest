# Leak confirms OpenAI is preparing ads on ChatGPT for public roll out

- Score: 463 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46086771) | Link: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/leak-confirms-openai-is-preparing-ads-on-chatgpt-for-public-roll-out/

### TL;DR

A report says strings found in a beta Android app reference an “ads feature,” “bazaar content,” search ads, and a carousel, suggesting internal preparation for advertising in ChatGPT search. The evidence establishes code references, not a launch date, final product, or confirmed broader rollout. The article argues ChatGPT’s scale and conversational context could make personalized ads lucrative. That possibility also raises a sharper trust problem than conventional search advertising: users may struggle to distinguish neutral assistance from commercially influenced recommendations or rankings.

### Comment pulse

- Some readers saw a powerful ad business; others argued ads could erase ChatGPT’s advantage over cluttered search and help competitors.
- Discussion centered on covert influence, advertiser-safe answers, disclosure, and whether low switching costs would discipline the product.

### LLM perspective

- View: Advertising inside answers would alter the assistant’s trust contract more than adding a separate display surface.
- Impact: Even disclosed sponsorship could make users question ordinary recommendations and omissions.
