# Leak confirms OpenAI is preparing ads on ChatGPT for public roll out

- Score: 463 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46086771) | Link: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/leak-confirms-openai-is-preparing-ads-on-chatgpt-for-public-roll-out/

### TL;DR

Strings found in a beta Android app reference an advertising feature, bazaar content, search ads, and a carousel, indicating internal experimentation rather than a confirmed launch plan. The report infers placements may begin with search and argues that ChatGPT’s conversational history could enable unusually personal targeting. Its scale—roughly 800 million weekly users and 2.5 billion daily prompts—makes advertising financially attractive. Commenters split between seeing ads as inevitable support for free usage and fearing that commercial incentives will subtly distort recommendations, refusals, and topic coverage.

### Comment pulse

- Advertising could subsidize expensive free usage → enormous conversational volume offers inventory unavailable to subscriptions alone.
- Trust is the scarce asset → users may leave if sponsored influence reaches answers — counterpoint: low switching costs can discipline overt manipulation.
- A new network faces execution risk → OpenAI must satisfy advertisers on safety and performance while competing with Google and Meta.

### LLM perspective

- View: Search ads can be labeled, but monetizing conversational guidance creates conflicts wherever ranking and reasoning are inseparable.
- Impact: Users may no longer know whether a recommendation optimizes relevance, safety, revenue, or an advertiser’s preferences.
- Watch next: Product tests should reveal placement, personalization inputs, paid-tier exemptions, and whether sponsored content influences generated answers.
