# We're learning more about what Vitamin D does to our bodies

- Score: 106 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46088998) | Link: https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/11/21/1128206/vitamin-d-bodies-bone-health-immune/

### TL;DR

Vitamin D is established as important for calcium absorption and bone health, while research links deficiency with blood pressure, cardiovascular events, respiratory illness, and immune-related gene activity. Those links do not establish that supplements prevent the associated conditions: evidence is mixed, and a review of 37 trials found supplementation generally did not prevent acute respiratory infections. Research is complicated by sunlight, diet, skin pigmentation, varying responses, and disagreement over ideal blood levels. The article recommends focusing on avoiding measured deficiency rather than assuming broad benefits.
