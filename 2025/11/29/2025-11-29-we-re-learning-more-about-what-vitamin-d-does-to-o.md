# We're learning more about what Vitamin D does to our bodies

- Score: 106 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46088998) | Link: https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/11/21/1128206/vitamin-d-bodies-bone-health-immune/

### TL;DR

Vitamin D’s established role is helping calcium rebuild bone, and deficiency below 30 nanomoles per liter can cause problems such as rickets. Studies also associate low levels with blood pressure, cardiovascular events, respiratory illness, immunity, pregnancy, mental health, and cancer survival, but supplementation evidence is inconsistent; a review of 37 trials found no overall prevention of acute respiratory infections. Research is complicated by sunlight, diet, melanin, uncertain target levels, and variable response. Discussion mixed personal supplement anecdotes with concern about balancing deficiency against ultraviolet exposure.

### Comment pulse

- Association does not establish treatment benefit → low levels may track other health differences, while randomized supplementation results remain mixed.
- Sun exposure involves competing risks → skin pigmentation, latitude, season, and behavior change both vitamin production and ultraviolet damage.
- Dream and sleep reports are anecdotes → formulation ingredients, dose, deficiency status, and recall could confound individual experiences.

### LLM perspective

- View: Avoiding confirmed deficiency is better supported than using supplements as a broad treatment for unrelated conditions.
- Impact: Universal guidance can miss differences in skin, sun exposure, diet, and baseline blood levels.
- Watch next: Trials should stratify deficient participants and measure clinical outcomes, dosing, season, pigmentation, and adverse effects.
