# It's Always the Process, Stupid

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46087737) | Link: https://its.promp.td/its-always-the-process-stupid/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that artificial intelligence accelerates workflows but cannot repair poorly designed ones. Its distinctive ability to interpret emails, documents, images, and other unstructured inputs exposes processes that previously lived undocumented in employees’ heads. Before automation, organizations should map each trigger, transformation, output, bottleneck, and governing definition, then connect results to structured systems. Human judgment must still define risks and exceptions. Commenters broadly endorsed documenting current practice first, while warning that process can become paperwork and that customer-facing AI products require strategy beyond internal optimization.

### Comment pulse

- Documenting reality creates a shared baseline → teams often discover that they disagree about what an existing step actually does.
- Optimization order matters → challenge requirements, delete waste, and simplify before accelerating or automating a workflow.
- Process raises average reliability → clear escape hatches can preserve expert speed — counterpoint: excessive exceptions may recreate undocumented dependence on heroes.
