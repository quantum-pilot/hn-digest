# It's Always the Process, Stupid

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46087737) | Link: https://its.promp.td/its-always-the-process-stupid/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that AI amplifies the workflow it enters: a sound process becomes faster, while a broken one produces bad results faster. Because models can process unstructured material, they often expose tacit, undocumented decisions rather than eliminate the need for governance. Teams should first map the real trigger, transformation, output, bottlenecks, and risks, then choose what to automate. Comments sharpened the advice: document current behavior separately from the desired process, preserve escape hatches, and recognize that some unstructured edges are legitimate.
