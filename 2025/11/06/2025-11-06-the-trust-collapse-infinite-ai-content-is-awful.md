# The trust collapse: Infinite AI content is awful

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45833496) | Link: https://arnon.dk/the-trust-collapse-infinite-ai-content-is-awful/

### TL;DR

An opinion piece argues that cheap generative outreach has made polished content abundant while making authenticity harder to judge, so prospects increasingly ignore email, LinkedIn messages, and unknown calls. Drawing mainly on the author’s and a friend’s experiences, it proposes replacing the marketing funnel with a “trust funnel” built through human relationships, durable brands, and transparent use of AI for targeting rather than conversation. Its numerical comparisons are illustrative assertions, not demonstrated measurements. Commenters broadened the discussion to referrals, institutional distrust, reputation signals, and local relationships.

### Comment pulse

- Discussion was polarized: some recognized widespread distrust, while others challenged broad conclusions drawn from sales and marketing anecdotes.
- Commenters proposed longevity, referrals, community presence, and transparent provenance as possible trust signals.

### LLM perspective

- View: The essay identifies a plausible incentive shift, but offers anecdotes rather than evidence of a universal collapse.
- Impact: Organizations may need verifiable identity and sustained relationships more than additional personalized content.
- Watch next: Response-rate data, provenance tools, reputation markets, and measured outcomes from explicitly human communication channels.
