### TL;DR

An archived brief claims OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar discussed federal loan guarantees while financing an AI infrastructure expansion exceeding $1 trillion. The supplied discussion materially disputes that framing: Sam Altman is quoted saying OpenAI neither has nor wants government guarantees for its data centers, and that guarantees were discussed only for US semiconductor fabrication, without a formal application. Commenters also argue the trillion-dollar figure was speculative and that Friar described a broader industry “backstop,” leaving the headline unsupported by the available record.
