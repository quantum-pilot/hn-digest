# Cloudflare Tells U.S. Govt That Foreign Site Blocking Efforts Are Trade Barriers

- Score: 275 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45835123) | Link: https://torrentfreak.com/cloudflare-tells-u-s-govt-that-foreign-site-blocking-efforts-are-digital-trade-barriers/

### TL;DR

Cloudflare’s first submission to the U.S. Trade Representative argues that foreign site-blocking regimes create trade barriers for American internet providers. It alleges that shared-IP blocking in Spain, Italy’s rapid Piracy Shield process, automated French sports blocks, and South Korea’s expanding URL list cause broad collateral damage with weak remedies. Cloudflare cites disruptions affecting legitimate sites and services, including a Google Drive block in Italy. Rights holders favor stronger blocking, while Cloudflare asks Washington to press for narrower, reviewable procedures.
