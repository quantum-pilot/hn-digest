# FAA to cut flights by 10% at 40 major airports due to government shutdown

- Score: 145 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45830797) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/05/faa-cuts-flight-capacity-shutdown.html

### TL;DR

U.S. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy announced a 10% capacity reduction at 40 major airports, expected to remove roughly 3,500–4,000 flights per day beginning Friday. Officials described it as a proactive safety response to unpaid air-traffic controllers, thin staffing, and operational disruptions during the shutdown, then in its thirty-sixth day. The affected airports and duration were not yet specified. FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford said further measures remained possible, while the controllers’ union warned that operational recovery could take weeks.
