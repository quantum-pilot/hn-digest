# I may have found a way to spot U.S. at-sea strikes before they're announced

- Score: 277 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45831541) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/OSINT/comments/1opjjyv/i_may_have_found_a_way_to_spot_us_atsea_strikes/

### TL;DR

A Reddit poster correlated a lone October 27 ocean thermal anomaly in NASA’s VIIRS FIRMS feed with later reporting and footage of a U.S. at-sea strike roughly 400 nautical miles southwest of Acapulco. The coordinates, timing, distance, and visible fire led the poster to claim about 90% confidence, while explicitly noting that a heat signal is not proof of cause or intent. The method geolocated a possible event before official disclosure, but did not predict it beforehand or independently confirm the target.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters said FIRMS has tracked other conflict fires, but offshore platforms and unrelated heat sources can create false positives.
- Attempts to reproduce the technique on other strikes reportedly failed, and non-burning attacks would remain invisible.

### LLM perspective

- View: This is a plausible correlation and useful lead, not verified attribution.

- Watch next: Independent confirmation, repeatability across strikes, and a disciplined false-positive baseline.
