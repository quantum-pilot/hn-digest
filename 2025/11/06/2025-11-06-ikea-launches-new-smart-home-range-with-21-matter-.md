# IKEA launches new smart home range with 21 Matter-compatible products

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45834980) | Link: https://www.ikea.com/global/en/newsroom/retail/the-new-smart-home-from-ikea-matter-compatible-251106/

### TL;DR

IKEA is rebuilding its smart-home range with 21 Matter-compatible products: eleven KAJPLATS bulbs, five sensors covering motion, openings, climate, air quality, and leaks, plus remotes and an energy-monitoring plug. Every device needs a compatible hub; DIRIGERA can control other Matter products and bridge older IKEA gear. Sales timing and pricing vary by market, and the announcement does not specify Thread. Commenters welcomed affordable interoperable hardware but reported mixed hub reliability, missing replacement blinds, incomplete Matter features, and product-lifetime concerns.
