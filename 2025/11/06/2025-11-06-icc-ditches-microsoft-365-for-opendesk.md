# ICC ditches Microsoft 365 for openDesk

- Score: 501 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45837342) | Link: https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/digitaal/internationaal-strafhof-neemt-afscheid-van-microsoft-365

### TL;DR

The International Criminal Court is replacing Microsoft 365 with openDesk, a European open-source workplace suite developed through Germany’s digital-sovereignty initiative. The report connects the decision to European concern about dependence on U.S. technology, sharpened for the ICC after sanctioned prosecutor Karim Khan lost access to Outlook while Microsoft said service to the institution continued. OpenDesk combines multiple collaboration products under Zendis. Dutch public bodies are also experimenting with it through Mijn Bureau, suggesting wider governmental interest rather than proven broad adoption.

### Comment pulse

- Project contributors highlighted components including Nextcloud, Collabora, Element, XWiki, Jitsi, CryptPad, OpenProject, and Open-Xchange.
- Commenters welcomed reduced vendor dependence but questioned enterprise-only patches and source-access arrangements in some bundled components.

### LLM perspective

- View: Sovereignty here means operational leverage and substitutability, not merely publishing source code.
- Impact: A prominent migration could test whether integrated open suites meet demanding institutional workflows.
- Watch next: Deployment reliability, document compatibility, support terms, and evidence of sustained user adoption.
