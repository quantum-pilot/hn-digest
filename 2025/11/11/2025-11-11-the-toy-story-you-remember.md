# The 'Toy Story' You Remember

- Score: 1087 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45883788) | Link: https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/the-toy-story-you-remember

### TL;DR

Toy Story was rendered digitally but color-managed for a 35mm theatrical release, with Pixar reportedly compensating for film’s shifts before exposing each frame onto stock. Early home video inherited that film-mediated appearance; later direct-digital masters became sharper, cleaner, brighter, and differently colored. The essay argues that current releases therefore diverge from the original theatrical intent. Commenters complicate that conclusion: film stock, labs, projectors, scanning, grading, displays, and memory all vary, so no single comparison proves exactly what audiences originally saw.

### Comment pulse
