# SoftBank sells its entire stake in Nvidia

- Score: 296 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45884937) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/11/softbank-sells-its-entire-stake-in-nvidia-for-5point83-billion.html

### TL;DR

SoftBank sold its entire 32.1-million-share Nvidia position in October for $5.83 billion and separately reduced its T-Mobile stake for $9.17 billion. Its finance chief framed the moves as prudent asset monetization, while a source said proceeds would help fund commitments including $22.5 billion for OpenAI, ABB’s robotics business, and related financing. The source denied that Nvidia’s valuation motivated the exit. Nvidia shares dipped 2%, but the transaction is better read as capital reallocation than a demonstrated retreat from AI.
