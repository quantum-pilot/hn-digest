# Canada loses its measles-free status, with US on track to follow

- Score: 199 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45888697) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy7e2lv4r8xo

### TL;DR

PAHO withdrew Canada's measles-elimination status after one strain circulated for 12 consecutive months, ending three decades of national elimination and removing the Americas' regional designation. Canada reported more than 5,000 cases in 2025, concentrated in under-vaccinated communities in Ontario and Alberta; some local coverage was far below the 95% needed to halt transmission. The United States could similarly lose status if its outbreak continues into January. Canada can regain designation after stopping associated transmission for another 12 months.

### Comment pulse
