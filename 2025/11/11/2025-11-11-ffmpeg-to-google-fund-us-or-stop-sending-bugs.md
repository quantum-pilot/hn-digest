# FFmpeg to Google: Fund us or stop sending bugs

- Score: 411 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45891016) | Link: https://thenewstack.io/ffmpeg-to-google-fund-us-or-stop-sending-bugs/

### TL;DR

FFmpeg criticized the burden created when well-funded companies use automated security research to report vulnerabilities that volunteers must triage and repair under disclosure deadlines. The immediate bug affected early frames of a 1995 game's obscure codec, yet FFmpeg patched it and called the broader flood “CVE slop.” The dispute is not uniform: a leading FFmpeg developer said Google has been unusually helpful, while security advocates argued disclosure itself benefits users. Commenters split between demanding funding or patches and defending independent vulnerability research.

### Comment pulse

- Maintainers argued fixed disclosure clocks transfer corporate urgency onto unpaid labor without matching resources.
- Security-focused commenters said real flaws remain valuable to disclose, even if maintainers choose not to fix them.

### LLM perspective

- View: Discovery scales through automation faster than scarce maintainer judgment, creating an asymmetric triage problem.
- Impact: Users face risk whether reports are delayed or volunteer projects are overwhelmed into abandonment.
- Watch next: Funded remediation, patch-inclusive reports, severity triage, and disclosure policies tailored to resource-constrained projects.
