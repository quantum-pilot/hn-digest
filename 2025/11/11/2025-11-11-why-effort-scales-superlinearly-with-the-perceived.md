# Why effort scales superlinearly with the perceived quality of creative work

- Score: 126 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45885242) | Link: https://markusstrasser.org/creative-work-landscapes.html

### TL;DR

The essay proposes that creative effort rises superlinearly near high perceived quality because acceptable changes become rarer and harder to verify. Creation is framed as recursively nested exploration and exploitation: choosing a direction merely opens a finer search space, while practiced craft pre-pays some search through cached heuristics. Different media have different tolerance and feedback speeds. The author later calls the piece rushed and experience-biased, and commenters argue that extra refinement can worsen work, spontaneity is not always prepaid practice, and the model oversimplifies artistic merit.
