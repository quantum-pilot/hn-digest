# A modern 35mm film scanner for home

- Score: 110 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45891907) | Link: https://www.soke.engineering/

### TL;DR

Knokke is a proposed automated 35mm scanner claiming 4,064 DPI, roughly 22-megapixel output, per-frame controls, and full-roll scans under five minutes. Its cross-platform Korova software is promised as open source, with public repair documentation and replacement parts. A Kickstarter is planned for Q1 2026 at €999, ahead of a stated €1,599 retail price. Commenters welcomed modern transport and software but questioned dust, film flatness, missing sample scans, 35mm-only support, and value versus camera or existing-scanner setups.

### Comment pulse

- Buyers wanted sample scans before trusting marketing specifications or image-quality claims.
- Automated feeding appealed to frequent users, while others considered the price steep beside DSLR and Plustek alternatives.
