# iPhone Pocket

- Score: 406 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45885813) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/11/introducing-iphone-pocket-a-beautiful-way-to-wear-and-carry-iphone/

### TL;DR

Apple and ISSEY MIYAKE introduced iPhone Pocket, a special-edition 3D-knitted carrier inspired by pleated clothing and designed to stretch around any iPhone plus small items. It can be handheld, attached to a bag, or worn on the body; stretched fabric reveals part of the display. The Japan-made short strap costs $149.95 in eight colors, while the long strap costs $229.95 in three. Commenters mostly mocked the price and utility, though some defended synthetic materials and fashion-oriented design.

### Comment pulse

- Many readers expected a small iPhone model and treated the textile accessory as an expensive novelty.
- Critics questioned durability and waste; defenders noted premium synthetics and Miyake's pleating tradition can carry genuine design value.
