# X5.1 solar flare, G4 geomagnetic storm watch

- Score: 108 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45893004) | Link: https://www.spaceweatherlive.com/en/news/view/593/20251111-x5-1-solar-flare-g4-geomagnetic-storm-watch.html

### TL;DR

Sunspot region 4274 produced an X5.1, R3-class solar flare peaking at 10:04 UTC on November 11, followed by a fast, Earth-directed halo coronal mass ejection. NOAA issued a G4-or-greater geomagnetic-storm watch, with possible arrival from 16:00 UTC on November 12. Two earlier CMEs could complicate timing and impact. If solar-wind and magnetic-field orientation prove favorable, aurora might reach northern France, Germany, Ukraine, Switzerland, Austria, Nevada, and Arkansas, but the forecast explicitly offers no guarantee.
