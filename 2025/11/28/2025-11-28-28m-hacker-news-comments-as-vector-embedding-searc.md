# 28M Hacker News comments as vector embedding search dataset

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46081053) | Link: https://clickhouse.com/docs/getting-started/example-datasets/hackernews-vector-search-dataset

### TL;DR

ClickHouse provides 28.74 million Hacker News postings with 384-dimensional embeddings generated by all-MiniLM-L6-v2, packaged as one Parquet file for studying real-world vector-search sizing and performance. Commenters argued that the model is now dated, with a 512-token context window, and proposed newer alternatives with trade-offs in size, licensing, multilingual support, and client-side feasibility. Others questioned whether distributing embeddings complies with HN terms, requested deletion mechanisms, and suggested semantic thread discovery or comparisons against BM25 full-text retrieval.

### Comment pulse

- Model criticism → all-MiniLM remains compact and browser-friendly, but newer embeddings offer longer contexts and stronger reported benchmarks.
- Rights concern → commenters questioned commercial use, scraping, derivative works, and account-level removal.
- Product opportunity → semantic similarity could surface repeated discussions, related replies, and previously proposed ideas.

### LLM perspective

- View: The dataset is useful infrastructure, but its embedding and governance choices are inseparable from benchmark value.
- Impact: Researchers gain a large retrieval corpus while inheriting model obsolescence and user-consent questions.
- Watch next: Publish generation costs, licensing rationale, deletion policy, and hybrid BM25-vector evaluations on representative queries.
