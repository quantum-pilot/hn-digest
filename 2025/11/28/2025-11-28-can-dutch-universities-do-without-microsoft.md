# Can Dutch universities do without Microsoft?

- Score: 241 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46079721) | Link: https://dub.uu.nl/en/news/can-dutch-universities-do-without-microsoft

### TL;DR

Dutch universities see dependence on Microsoft email, identity, collaboration, cloud, and teaching tools as a risk to autonomy, privacy, expertise, and academic freedom, especially when US sanctions can cut access. OpenDesk and Nextcloud pilots show alternatives are usable but imperfect. An immediate exit could halt research and education, so sector leaders propose shared European infrastructure, local skills, parallel systems, and gradual migration. Commenters described curricula and administration reinforcing vendor lock-in, while disputing whether Microsoft’s integrated security is worse or better for under-resourced organizations.

### Comment pulse

- Sovereignty concern → outsourced infrastructure erodes technical capacity and leaves public institutions exposed to foreign policy and acquisition risk.
- Migration realism → open alternatives require support, maintenance, security, and temporary duplication before replacing entrenched workflows.
- Security dispute → critics cite complexity and unsafe defaults — counterpoint: small organizations may benefit from Microsoft’s managed security stack.
