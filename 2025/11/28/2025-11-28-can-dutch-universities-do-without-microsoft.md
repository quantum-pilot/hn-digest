# Can Dutch universities do without Microsoft?

- Score: 241 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46079721) | Link: https://dub.uu.nl/en/news/can-dutch-universities-do-without-microsoft

### TL;DR

Dutch universities see geopolitical sanctions, cloud concentration, and lost in-house expertise as risks to academic autonomy, yet Office 365 and related infrastructure are too embedded for an abrupt exit. Five universities are testing Nextcloud with about 75 researchers, while OpenDesk and European services offer additional building blocks. SURF favors shared infrastructure and parallel migration despite support, security, and compatibility costs. HN commenters agreed dependence is entrenched but divided over its causes and whether replacement would improve security.

### Comment pulse

- Vendor dependence begins in teaching → students described public curricula whose tools, support, and career pathways centered proprietary ecosystems.
- Sovereignty requires coordinated investment → commenters argued Europe can run core services but lacks aligned institutions, funding, and urgency.
- Security judgments conflict sharply → enterprise users described dangerous complexity — counterpoint: defenders called Microsoft 365 safest for small firms without specialists.
