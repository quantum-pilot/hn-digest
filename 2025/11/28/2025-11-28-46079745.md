### TL;DR

Hacker News relies on readers who inspect the chronological submission queue, promote worthwhile links, and flag spam before most items disappear from view. The post argues that a few minutes of quiet curation can improve the front page more than another comment. Discussion reframed this anonymous work as a way to encounter valuable material outside the site's usual taste and noted that community and moderator mechanisms can rescue submissions missed on their first pass.

### Comment pulse

- Curation rewards the curator → unfiltered pages expose worthwhile oddities and posts outside the site's usual taste.
- Moderators provide a fallback → the pool can revive overlooked submissions days later and test them on the front page.
- Alternative feeds widen discovery → list pages, chronological readers, classic rankings, and RSS let users choose their own screening workflow.
