# Tell HN: Want a better HN? Visit /newest

- Score: 248 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46079745) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46079745

### TL;DR

A brief Tell HN submission argues that good links often disappear in `/newest`, so readers can improve front-page selection by spending a few minutes voting or flagging there. Most detail comes from the discussion: commenters value the feed for unfiltered, surprising posts that never advance, note moderators’ `/pool` mechanism for giving overlooked submissions another chance, and recommend RSS or alternate HN list views. Participants described this curation as both community maintenance and personally rewarding discovery, despite substantially more noise.

### Comment pulse

- Discovery reward → browsing unfiltered links exposes useful oddities outside normal front-page taste.
- Alternative workflows → `/pool`, RSS, `/classic`, and configurable readers provide other routes through overlooked material.
