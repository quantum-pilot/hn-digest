# Norway wealth fund to vote for human rights report at Microsoft, against Nadella

- Score: 298 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46096555) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/30/norway-wealth-fund-to-vote-for-human-rights-report-at-microsoft-agm-against-management.html

### TL;DR

Norway’s $2 trillion wealth fund said it would support a nonbinding proposal asking Microsoft to report on risks from cloud data centers in countries with serious human-rights concerns. It also planned to oppose Satya Nadella’s reappointment as board chair and his pay package. The fund held 1.35% of Microsoft, worth $50 billion. Comments supplied missing context: the proposal cites Saudi Arabia, while Microsoft says existing disclosures and annual independent assessments already address rights risks and legally required data requests.
