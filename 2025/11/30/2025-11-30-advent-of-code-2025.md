# Advent of Code 2025

- Score: 688 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46096337) | Link: https://adventofcode.com/2025/about

### TL;DR

Eric Wastl's annual December programming event now runs 12 days, with two-part puzzles that accept any language and are designed to finish within 15 seconds on decade-old hardware. The global leaderboard is gone after creating stress, distorted comparisons, and even attacks; private boards remain. Wastl advises solving for personal learning rather than speed and explicitly discourages AI assistance. Participants largely welcomed the revised format, although some disputed how far beginners can realistically progress without a computer science background.

### Comment pulse
