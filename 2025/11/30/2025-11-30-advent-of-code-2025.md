# Advent of Code 2025

- Score: 688 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46096337) | Link: https://adventofcode.com/2025/about

### TL;DR

Advent of Code returns with daily programming puzzles beginning December 1, but the 2025 event lasts 12 days after ten years of longer schedules. Creator Eric Wastl removed the global leaderboard because it stressed users and infrastructure, encouraged unhealthy competition, and even attracted DDoS attacks; private leaderboards remain. Participants may use any language, should debug with examples and hand-checked tests, and are discouraged from outsourcing solutions to AI because the purpose is human practice. Commenters broadly welcome a shorter, less competitive event.
