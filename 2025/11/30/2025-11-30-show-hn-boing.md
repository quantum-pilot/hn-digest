### TL;DR

The repository offers little written explanation, but commenters describe a tiny browser toy built around pulling, catching, and releasing a springy ball while sound responds to motion. Players quickly turned its simple interaction into scales and short melodies through automated clicks. Others enjoyed imperfect wobble, damping, and squash more than strict physical accuracy, comparing the experience with door springs and other tactile memories. The warm response celebrates a focused, account-free diversion whose charm comes from immediate play rather than instructions or progression.
