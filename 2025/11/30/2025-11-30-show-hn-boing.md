### TL;DR

The frozen source exposes only repository metadata for Boing, a small TypeScript, CSS, and HTML project, without explaining its behavior. The HN discussion therefore supplies the usable description: commenters treated it as a playful interactive ball or door-spring simulation whose drag distance affects its motion and sound, then wrote scripts to automate clicks and play melodies. They praised its deliberately wobbly feel and single-purpose design, arguing that slightly imperfect, damped physics can feel more tangible than a rigid idealized spring.
