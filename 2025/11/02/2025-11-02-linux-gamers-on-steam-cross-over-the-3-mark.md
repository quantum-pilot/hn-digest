# Linux gamers on Steam cross over the 3% mark

- Score: 515 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45792503) | Link: https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/11/linux-gamers-on-steam-finally-cross-over-the-3-mark/

### TL;DR

Valve’s October 2025 Steam survey placed Linux at 3.05%, up 0.41 percentage points, while Windows held 94.84% and macOS 2.11%. The article estimates this represents millions of monthly users, though Valve has not published a recent active-user total. SteamOS accounts for 27.18% of surveyed Linux systems, showing the Steam Deck’s large contribution; Arch follows at 10.32%. Commenters described smoother recent migrations and strong Proton compatibility, but unsupported multiplayer anti-cheat remains a major barrier for some game libraries.

### Comment pulse

- Switchers reported comparable performance and fewer reasons to retain Windows, though several still encountered driver or configuration work.
- Debate centered on whether Valve could deliver a broader Linux desktop without gaining excessive ecosystem control.
