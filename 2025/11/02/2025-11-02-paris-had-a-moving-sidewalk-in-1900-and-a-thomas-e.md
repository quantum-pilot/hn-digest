# Paris Had a Moving Sidewalk in 1900, and a Thomas Edison Film Captured It (2020)

- Score: 143 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45793466) | Link: https://www.openculture.com/2020/03/paris-had-a-moving-sidewalk-in-1900.html

### TL;DR

Paris's 1900 Exposition featured an elevated, continuously moving sidewalk captured by James Henry White for Thomas Edison's company. Contemporary descriptions outline three adjacent platforms: stationary, moderate-speed, and roughly six miles per hour, letting visitors board progressively and circle the grounds. An earlier paid walkway at Chicago's 1893 exposition was reportedly unreliable, making Paris's installation the greater spectacle. Commenters connected the stepped-speed concept to later science fiction, identified the filmmaker, and focused on the children and passenger behavior preserved in the footage.
