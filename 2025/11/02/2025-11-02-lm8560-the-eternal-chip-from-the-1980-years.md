# LM8560, the eternal chip from the 1980 years

- Score: 113 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45787842) | Link: https://www.tycospages.com/other-themes/lm8560-the-eternal-chip-from-the-1980-years/

### TL;DR

The LM8560 condensed a 1980s LED alarm clock’s counter, alarm logic, backup handling, and duplex-display driver into one inexpensive fixed-function chip. Rather than using quartz, it counts 50 or 60 Hz mains cycles; a 9-volt battery and imprecise RC oscillator preserve time during outages. Its specialized two-phase display wiring reduced components but now complicates repairs and hobby projects. The article catalogs hidden button combinations, timing quirks, common faults, and decades of personal clock-radio experience, celebrating simplicity and longevity over programmability.
