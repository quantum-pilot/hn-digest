# Jakarta is now the biggest city in the world

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46042810) | Link: https://www.axios.com/2025/11/24/jakarta-tokyo-worlds-biggest-city-population

### TL;DR

UN DESA’s harmonized 2025 geospatial method ranks Jakarta as the world’s most populous city at nearly 42 million, ahead of Dhaka near 40 million and Tokyo at 33 million. Cities now hold 45% of humanity; megacities increased from eight in 1975 to 33, yet smaller cities house more people and grow faster. The dataset covers over 12,000 cities and distinguishes cities, towns, and rural areas. Commenters debated Jakarta’s transit gains, affordability, food, congestion, pollution, flooding, sinking, and limited international visibility.
