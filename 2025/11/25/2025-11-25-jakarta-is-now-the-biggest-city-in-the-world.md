# Jakarta is now the biggest city in the world

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46042810) | Link: https://www.axios.com/2025/11/24/jakarta-tokyo-worlds-biggest-city-population

### TL;DR

UN DESA’s harmonized geospatial method estimates Jakarta at nearly 42 million residents, ahead of Dhaka at almost 40 million and Tokyo at 33 million. Cities now contain 45 percent of humanity; megacities increased from eight in 1975 to 33 in 2025, with 19 in Asia. Yet smaller cities collectively house more people and grow faster. By 2050, cities are projected to absorb two-thirds of global population growth. The ranking reflects a standardized grid-based definition, not municipal boundaries, and the expanded dataset covers more than 12,000 cities.
