# FLUX.2: Frontier Visual Intelligence

- Score: 218 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46046916) | Link: https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-2

### TL;DR

Black Forest Labs presents FLUX.2 as a unified image-generation and editing family with up to ten references, 4-megapixel editing, stronger typography, structured prompting, and improved consistency. Pro and Flex are hosted; the 32-billion-parameter Dev model has open weights but a commercial license, while the smaller Apache-licensed Klein is forthcoming. The vendor claims leading quality and value. Commenters welcomed local control yet noted over 100GB of weights, middling independent editing scores, higher multi-image costs, aggressive API filtering, and uneven gains over FLUX.1.

### Comment pulse

- Open-weight appeal → local fallback reduces dependence on vendor pricing and API changes despite formidable hardware requirements.
- Benchmark skepticism → early independent tests found iterative gains, inconsistent visual quality, and no universal advantage over competitors.
- Workflow control → JSON prompts, hex colors, multiple references, and adjustable steps may matter more than headline aesthetics.
