# Ozempic does not slow Alzheimer's, study finds

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46047513) | Link: https://www.semafor.com/article/11/25/2025/ozempic-does-not-slow-alzheimers-study-finds

### TL;DR

A two-year Novo Nordisk study found semaglutide did not slow Alzheimer’s progression, disappointing hopes generated by observational signals and animal research. The report notes earlier neurological claims were already moderated after a Parkinson’s failure, while cardiovascular and kidney benefits look more robust; the announcement cut Novo’s shares 6%. Commenters stressed that dose and delivery may matter, asked about diabetic subgroups and vascular dementia, and discussed plausible GLP-1 mechanisms involving inflammation, mitochondria, neurogenesis, metabolism, and appetite signaling. The result does not settle every formulation or neurological hypothesis.
