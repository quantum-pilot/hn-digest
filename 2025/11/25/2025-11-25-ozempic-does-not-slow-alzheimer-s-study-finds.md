# Ozempic does not slow Alzheimer's, study finds

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46047513) | Link: https://www.semafor.com/article/11/25/2025/ozempic-does-not-slow-alzheimers-study-finds

### TL;DR

Novo Nordisk says a two-year study found semaglutide did not slow Alzheimer’s progression, tempering hopes that GLP-1 drugs might directly affect neurodegeneration beyond benefits associated with weight loss and metabolic health. The brief report gives little trial detail, while commenters question the tested formulation and dose, possible diabetic subgroups, vascular dementia, and plausible inflammatory or mitochondrial mechanisms. Those hypotheses remain speculative here; the supplied material does not establish benefit for another dose, route, or patient group.
