# Unison 1.0

- Score: 177 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46049722) | Link: https://www.unison-lang.org/unison-1-0/

### TL;DR

Unison 1.0 declares its language, distributed runtime, and workflow stable after years of development. Definitions are content-addressed and stored in a database rather than source files, enabling native versioning, fewer name-based conflicts, code reuse, and deployment through Unison Cloud or exported binaries. The release adds mature collaboration, runtime, and distributed-systems tooling. HN celebrates the milestone and algebraic abilities, but questions limited adoption, absent C FFI, ecosystem dependence, and whether Bring Your Own Cloud sufficiently answers lock-in concerns.
