# Orion 1.0

- Score: 342 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46047350) | Link: https://blog.kagi.com/orion

### TL;DR

Kagi says Orion 1.0 brings its six-year-old macOS browser out of beta, pairing native WebKit with zero telemetry, built-in blocking, extensions, profiles, and extensive customization. The free browser is funded through tips and Orion+ subscriptions; Linux remains alpha and Windows is targeted for late 2026. HN enthusiasm for a non-Chromium option was tempered by reports of severe 1Password lag, requests for an offline installer, doubts about closed-source privacy claims, and skepticism that raw speed drives switching.

### Comment pulse
