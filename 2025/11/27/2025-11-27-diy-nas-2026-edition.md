# DIY NAS: 2026 Edition

- Score: 380 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46065034) | Link: https://blog.briancmoses.com/2025/11/diy-nas-2026-edition.html

### TL;DR

The build combines an Intel N355 Topton board, 32GB DDR5, eight SATA ports, dual NVMe slots, 10GbE, and a compact JONSBO case under TrueNAS, costing about $1,189 before bulk hard drives. It targets storage plus homelab workloads, saturates the network from its flash pool, and averages roughly 67W idle with installed disks. Rising component prices weaken the DIY value. HN readers debated whether its component priorities, software stack, sourcing, and annual-build format provide convincing value or reliability.
