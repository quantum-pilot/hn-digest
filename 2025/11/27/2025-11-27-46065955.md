# Tell HN: Happy Thanksgiving

- Score: 458 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46065955) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46065955

### TL;DR

A fifteen-year member thanks Hacker News moderators and community for sustaining a knowledgeable, sharp-edged discussion forum despite perennial claims of decline. Longtime readers agreed that the site's decay has been slower than elsewhere and attributed its durability to consistent work, curiosity, and builders sharing experience. The celebratory thread portrayed HN less as a perfect archive than as a continuing social institution: useful across careers and generations, hospitable to both visible contributors and quiet readers, and resilient enough to turn recurring disagreement into belonging.

### Comment pulse
