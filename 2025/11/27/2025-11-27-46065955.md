# Tell HN: Happy Thanksgiving

- Score: 458 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46065955) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46065955

### TL;DR

A brief Thanksgiving note thanks Hacker News moderators and celebrates fifteen years of a community that remains useful, argumentative, and knowledgeable despite recurring claims of decline. The discussion supplies most of the substance: longtime readers describe unusually slow cultural decay, daily learning, classroom use, valued disagreement, and years of lurking without posting. Several credit consistent moderation and Algolia search for preserving the site’s utility. The thread functions less as analysis than as an annual ritual of appreciation among readers with decade-plus histories.
