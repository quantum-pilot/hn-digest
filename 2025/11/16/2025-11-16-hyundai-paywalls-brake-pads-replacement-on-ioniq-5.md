# Hyundai Paywalls Brake Pads replacement on Ioniq 5 N

- Score: 208 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45942518) | Link: https://www.thedrive.com/news/replacing-brake-pads-on-a-hyundai-ioniq-5-n-requires-a-professional-mechanics-login

### TL;DR

Hyundai's official Ioniq 5 N rear-brake procedure requires software-controlled service mode. Approved J2534 interfaces cost roughly $800–$2,000, Hyundai software starts at $60 weekly, and bidirectional functions require professional NASTF credentials; the dealer GDS tool costs about $6,000. Hyundai attributes authentication to safety and security, while the article argues routine ownership rights are being constrained despite formal independent-shop access. Commenters shared unofficial 12-volt motor workarounds, praised older serviceable cars, and stressed that this issue extends beyond EVs.
