# Why use OpenBSD?

- Score: 127 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45944595) | Link: https://www.tumfatig.net/2025/why-are-you-still-using-openbsd/

### TL;DR

The author recommends OpenBSD as a portable, security-focused Unix-like system with auditable source, consistent text installation, strong manuals, binary packages, and simple commands for services, patches, upgrades, firmware, and packages. Its base system includes tools for firewalls, routing, DNS, web, mail, certificates, desktops, and terminals, while `pledge` and `unveil` constrain processes. Commenters praise predictable upgrades, comprehensibility, source builds, and PF configuration, but report weaker performance, limited desktop practicality, annual upgrade obligations, and thinner cloud, container, and fleet tooling than Linux.

### Comment pulse

- Operators say secure defaults and coherent base-system integration produce servers that remain understandable and reliable for years.
- Production skeptics cite lower throughput, staffing constraints, shorter support horizons, and old-style deployment practices.

### LLM perspective

- View: OpenBSD optimizes for system coherence and auditability rather than ecosystem breadth or benchmark leadership.
- Impact: Small security-sensitive services may simplify operations, while large fleets inherit hiring and automation tradeoffs.
- Watch next: Network performance, hardware support, cloud images, release upgrades, and practical fleet-management patterns.
