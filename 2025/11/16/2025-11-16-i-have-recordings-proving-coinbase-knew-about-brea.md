# I have recordings proving Coinbase knew about breach months before disclosure

- Score: 269 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45948058) | Link: https://jonathanclark.com/posts/coinbase-breach-timeline.html

### TL;DR

The author says a January 7 phishing caller knew his Social Security number, exact Bitcoin balance, signup date, and license details. He sent Coinbase recordings, headers, and a technical report; its trust-and-safety head acknowledged an investigation, but follow-ups went unanswered before Coinbase's May disclosure of contractor-enabled data theft. He argues this shows stolen data was exploited four months earlier. Commenters note Reuters separately reported Coinbase knew of a contractor leak in January, while critics say one customer report alone does not prove corporate breach awareness.
