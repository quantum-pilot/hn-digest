# Peter Thiel sells off all Nvidia stock, stirring bubble fears

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45948477) | Link: https://www.thestreet.com/investing/peter-thiel-dumps-top-ai-stock-stirring-bubble-fears

### TL;DR

Thiel Macro's Q3 filing shows it sold 537,742 Nvidia shares, previously about 40% of its disclosed US equity portfolio, and exited Vistra while adding Apple and Microsoft and sharply reducing Tesla. The portfolio fell from roughly $212 million to $74.4 million. The article interprets the Nvidia exit alongside Peter Thiel's warnings about AI hype, despite Nvidia's rising sales. Commenters disagree whether this is an informed bubble signal, ordinary profit-taking and diversification, or impossible to interpret without knowing his complete positions.

### Comment pulse

- Bubble skeptics combine Thiel's sale with SoftBank's moves and credit-market concerns, though SoftBank may be reallocating within AI.
- Others see a simple decision to lock in gains and diversify rather than a verdict on Nvidia.

### LLM perspective

- View: A 13F reveals portfolio changes, not motive, hedges, private exposure, or future conviction.
