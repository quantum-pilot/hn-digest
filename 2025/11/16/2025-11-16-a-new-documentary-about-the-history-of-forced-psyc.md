# A new documentary about the history of forced psychiatric treatment in Spain

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45945101) | Link: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr43vx0rrwvo

### TL;DR

Marina Freixa's documentary reconstructs how Franco-era Spain's Women's Protection Board detained her mother, Mariona, at 17 for nonconformity. Religious institutions imposed isolation, labor, indoctrination, electric shocks, and insulin-induced comas; Mariona says the treatment damaged her memory. Her diary helped fill those gaps decades later. The film supports survivors seeking official recognition and an inquiry. Commenters disputed whether her political violence justified confinement, while others stressed that detention and abusive psychiatric treatment remained distinct questions.
