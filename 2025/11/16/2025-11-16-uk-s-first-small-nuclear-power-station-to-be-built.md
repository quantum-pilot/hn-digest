# UK's first small nuclear power station to be built in north Wales

- Score: 152 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45944056) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c051y3d7myzo

### TL;DR

The UK plans three Rolls-Royce small modular reactors at Wylfa on Anglesey, with room for eight, backed by £2.5 billion in government investment. Work is expected to start next year, target generation in the mid-2030s, create up to 3,000 jobs, and potentially power about three million homes. Final contracts, regulatory approval, factories, and workforce training remain outstanding. Debate centers on local benefits, waste, cost and timelines versus renewables; commenters also note the 470 MWe design is relatively large and has not yet been built.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters value firm low-carbon generation alongside weather-dependent renewables and seasonal demand.
- Critics expect delays and poor economics — counterpoint: advocates say storage requirements make renewable-only comparisons incomplete.
