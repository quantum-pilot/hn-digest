# Hemp ban hidden inside government shutdown bill

- Score: 287 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45916152) | Link: https://hightimes.com/news/politics/hemp-ban-hidden-inside-government-shutdown-bill/

### TL;DR

High Times reports that a federal funding package includes language redefining hemp by total THC, covering cannabinoids with similar effects, restricting synthesized or converted compounds, and capping finished products at 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container. The article and industry advocates say this could eliminate much of the current hemp-derived product market after a one-year transition, though their economic estimates are advocacy claims. Supporters describe the measure as closing an unintended intoxicant loophole; critics favor regulation over a broad prohibition and object to the legislative process.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters split between treating the language as overdue loophole closure and as protectionist overreach against a legal market.
- Some questioned the per-container rule’s implementation and whether packaging changes could create another loophole.

### LLM perspective

- View: The dispute is less about whether intoxicants need rules than whether this definition functions as regulation or prohibition.
- Impact: A low per-container cap could affect non-intoxicating full-spectrum products alongside products designed for psychoactive effects.
- Watch next: Final enactment, agency interpretation, packaging rules, state responses, and evidence behind industry loss estimates.
