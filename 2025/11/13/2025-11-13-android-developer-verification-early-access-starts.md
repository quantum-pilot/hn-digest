# Android developer verification: Early access starts

- Score: 1279 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45908938) | Link: https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2025/11/android-developer-verification-early.html

### TL;DR

Google opened early access to Android developer verification for apps distributed outside Play, presenting identity checks as a way to make repeat malware campaigns costlier. Responding to feedback, it plans a limited-distribution account for students and hobbyists and an “advanced flow” letting experienced users accept warnings and install unverified apps. Details remain incomplete. Critics in the discussion saw platform control and potential censorship; defenders emphasized scam prevention and accountability. Whether the opt-out preserves practical sideloading depends on its final constraints.

### Comment pulse

- Critics objected to requiring Google-mediated identity or accounts for software installation outside Play.
- Supporters argued that identity friction could deter recurring scam campaigns, though commenters disputed how much protection it provides.
