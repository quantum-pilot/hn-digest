# Android 16 QPR1 is being pushed to the Android Open Source Project

- Score: 237 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45910381) | Link: https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/115533432439509433

### TL;DR

GrapheneOS says Google finally pushed Android 16 QPR1 to the Android Open Source Project, more than two months after the update reached supported phones. GrapheneOS had already migrated kernel and firmware components in September, but said the missing userspace source blocked a full migration. Commenters expect the release to help custom ROMs target QPR1 and possibly newer Pixel hardware. Explanations for the delay ranged from regulatory effects to deliberate platform control, but the supplied material establishes no motive.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters welcomed the source drop for custom ROM work, while disputing whether those projects remain practical for ordinary users.
- Theories about Google’s motive were speculative and sharply contested.
