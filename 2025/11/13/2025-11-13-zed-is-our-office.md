# Zed is our office

- Score: 437 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45916196) | Link: https://zed.dev/blog/zed-is-our-office

### TL;DR

Zed Industries describes running much of its remote company inside its collaborative editor. Hierarchical channels combine voice, shared projects, screen sharing, multiplayer editing, and persistent notes for weekly planning, retrospectives, demos, project work, and personal focus spaces. Following a teammate automatically switches between their editor cursor and external screen. The team frames this as dogfooding a multiplayer development environment that keeps discussion near code. Commenters admired the integration but questioned cloud trust, distraction, and prioritizing collaboration while basic editor behaviors remain unreliable.

### Comment pulse

- Some developers want self-hosted collaboration before allowing proprietary code and conversations through Zed’s service.
- Others prefer communication outside their editor or want core editing stability ahead of broader workplace features.

### LLM perspective

- View: Zed’s office model is compelling dogfooding, but its creator’s workflow may not generalize to smaller teams.
- Impact: Integrated context can reduce tool switching while concentrating privacy, reliability, and attention risks.
- Watch next: Self-hosting, enterprise controls, and core-editor issue resolution will determine whether collaboration extends beyond enthusiasts.
