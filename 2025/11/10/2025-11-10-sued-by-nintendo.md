# Sued by Nintendo

- Score: 253 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45870675) | Link: https://www.suedbynintendo.com/

### TL;DR

“Sued by Nintendo” is a long, community-style chronology of lawsuits, takedowns, legal threats, service closures, and unpopular product decisions involving Nintendo, spanning early emulator disputes through recent actions against fan projects, tournament organizers, music channels, ROM sites, and other developers. Its playful scorecard groups materially different events under one adversarial theme, so it is better read as an advocacy index than a legal analysis. Individual entries link to supporting coverage, but the page itself offers limited context about outcomes or legal distinctions.
