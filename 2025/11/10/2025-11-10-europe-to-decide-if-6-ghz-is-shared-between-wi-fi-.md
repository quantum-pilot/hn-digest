# Europe to decide if 6 GHz is shared between Wi-Fi and cellular networks

- Score: 162 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45874659) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2025/11/09/europe_to_decide_if_6/

### TL;DR

European regulators are considering how the upper 6 GHz band, 6425–7125 MHz, should be divided or shared between unlicensed Wi-Fi and licensed mobile networks. Wi-Fi groups warn that exclusive cellular use would constrain Wi-Fi 6E and 7, while operators cite 5G and future 6G capacity. Germany reportedly favored mobile needs; the US allocated the full band to unlicensed use. The European Commission expects technical conditions from CEPT by July 2027. Commenters disputed which use delivers greater public benefit.

### Comment pulse

- Apartment dwellers said 6 GHz’s short range reduces neighbor interference and makes high-bandwidth Wi-Fi more stable.
- Mobile proponents argued licensed operators could use the band efficiently in dense venues and underserved wireless-first markets.
- Others favored Wi-Fi alongside more wired infrastructure—counterpoint: mandated Ethernet would add construction cost and cannot serve every device.
