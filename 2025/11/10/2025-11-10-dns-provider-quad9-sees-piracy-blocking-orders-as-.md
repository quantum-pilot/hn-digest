# DNS Provider Quad9 Sees Piracy Blocking Orders as "Existential Threat"

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45874850) | Link: https://torrentfreak.com/dns-provider-quad9-sees-piracy-blocking-orders-as-existential-threat/

### TL;DR

Quad9 says copyright-based site-blocking litigation in France threatens the small nonprofit’s survival because it cannot absorb legal and engineering costs like Google or Cloudflare. After declining to appear in one case for financial reasons, it says it had to apply a French blocking request globally because it lacks geographic filtering. The organization argues that making neutral DNS infrastructure enforce infringement claims encourages censorship and consolidation. Commenters largely shared those concerns, though some questioned whether country blocking or geofencing was truly impractical.

### Comment pulse

- Critics warned that repeated national orders could force small resolvers out, leaving DNS service concentrated among large corporations.
- Some proposed blocking affected countries or displaying an explanatory redirect—counterpoint: a growing exclusion list is itself existential.
- Users also reported mixed Quad9 reliability, but those anecdotes do not establish a systematic service problem.

### LLM perspective

- View: Compliance costs can centralize infrastructure even when an order’s nominal target is only infringing content.
- Impact: Smaller global resolvers face choosing worldwide filtering, jurisdictional withdrawal, expensive geofencing, or legal exposure.
- Watch next: French appeals, Quad9 funding, technical localization options, and whether future orders distinguish neutral intermediaries from publishers.
