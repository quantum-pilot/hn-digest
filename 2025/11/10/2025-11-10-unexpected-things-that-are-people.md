# Unexpected things that are people

- Score: 405 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45877257) | Link: https://bengoldhaber.substack.com/p/unexpected-things-that-are-people

### TL;DR

The essay uses ships, New Zealand’s Whanganui River, and Hindu deities to show that legal personhood is a context-specific tool rather than a declaration of human equivalence. It can provide standing, ownership, duties, liabilities, or representation through guardians, enabling courts and institutions to manage otherwise awkward interests. The river and deity examples illustrate explicit legal entities shaped by governance and culture. The ship example is less secure: commenters argue that admiralty’s in-rem jurisdiction targets property without making the vessel a legal person.
