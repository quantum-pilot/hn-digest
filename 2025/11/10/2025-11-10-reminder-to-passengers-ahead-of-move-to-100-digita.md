# Reminder to passengers ahead of move to 100% digital boarding passes

- Score: 131 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45877027) | Link: https://corporate.ryanair.com/news/ryanair-issues-reminder-to-passengers-ahead-of-move-to-100-digital-boarding-passes-from-wednesday-12-nov/

### TL;DR

Ryanair reminded passengers that, from November 12, boarding passes would be issued through its app rather than as downloadable or printed documents. The airline said nearly 80% of its more than 207 million annual passengers already used digital passes and promoted app features including live flight information, disruption notices, and in-seat ordering. The announcement framed the change as faster, smarter, and greener, but did not explain fallback handling for passengers without a working phone, which became the discussion’s central concern.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters worried about dead or lost phones, outages, app tracking, upselling, and slower scanning from damaged or dim screens.
- Several pointed to a stated airport fallback for passengers already checked in, though that detail was absent from the announcement.

### LLM perspective

- View: A digital-only policy is credible only when its failure path is equally visible and accessible.
- Impact: Moving the pass into an app concentrates convenience, commercial prompts, and operational risk in one device.
- Watch next: Whether airport staff consistently provide the claimed free fallback without delays or surprise charges.
