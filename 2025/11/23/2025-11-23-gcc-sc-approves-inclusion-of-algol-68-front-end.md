# GCC SC approves inclusion of Algol 68 Front End

- Score: 218 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46020151) | Link: https://gcc.gnu.org/pipermail/gcc/2025-November/247020.html

### TL;DR

The GCC Steering Committee approved an experimental Algol 68 front end for trunk and appointed Jose E. Marchesi as maintainer. The language will not build by default, count toward release criteria, or obligate unrelated GCC developers to fix its issues; unmaintained or bit-rotted code may be removed. The committee also invited proposals for making out-of-tree front ends easier to maintain. Commenters welcome accessible historical experimentation but debate Algol 68’s practical relevance, influence on later languages, and whether niche front ends survive without sustained users.

### Comment pulse

- Trunk inclusion lowers maintenance friction → explicit experimental status contains obligations for the broader GCC project.
- Algol 68 is historically influential → counterpoint: influence and a fixed specification do not guarantee contemporary utility.
