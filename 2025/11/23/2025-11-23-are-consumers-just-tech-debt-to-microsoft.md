# Are consumers just tech debt to Microsoft?

- Score: 208 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46025196) | Link: https://birchtree.me/blog/are-consumers-just-tech-debt-to-microsoft/

### TL;DR

The author argues Microsoft increasingly treats consumer Windows as a legacy burden while prioritizing AI, cloud services, and enterprise customers. He speculates that a cheaper MacBook and Valve’s planned Linux-based Steam Machine could finally shift consumer market share, though he expects gradual change. Commenters agree desktop applications depend less on operating-system compatibility but dispute the alternatives: Apple also frustrates desktop users, Linux still has gaps, and Windows has gained WSL2, security, accessibility, developer, and quality-of-life features despite hostile defaults.
