# After my dad died, we found the love letters

- Score: 745 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46021825) | Link: https://www.jenn.site/after-my-dad-died-we-found-the-love-letters/

### TL;DR

After her father’s death, the author discovers that his years working away concealed a three-year relationship with Edward, who believed they were building an open life together. Photos and letters reveal a joyful, affectionate man unlike the distant patriarch his family knew. The discovery deepens grief but also exposes harm: he repeatedly refused his wife’s divorce requests, used family pressure to keep the marriage intact, and had other affairs. Commenters wrestle with compassion for decades closeted and accountability for lives constrained by deception.
