# After my dad died, we found the love letters

- Score: 745 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46021825) | Link: https://www.jenn.site/after-my-dad-died-we-found-the-love-letters/

### TL;DR
After her father’s death, the author discovered letters and met Edward, his partner of three years, revealing a joyful life hidden from his family after decades in the closet. The discovery deepened grief: she could finally see the gentle person constrained by family and cultural expectations, while also confronting affairs, promises to Edward, and his use of those pressures to prevent her mother’s requested divorce. Commenters balanced compassion for AIDS era fear and traditional expectations against accountability for harms imposed on his wife, partner, and children.
