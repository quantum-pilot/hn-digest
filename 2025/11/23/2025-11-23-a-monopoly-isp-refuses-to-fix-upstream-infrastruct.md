# A monopoly ISP refuses to fix upstream infrastructure

- Score: 579 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46019685) | Link: https://sacbear.com/xfinity-wont-fix-internet/

### TL;DR

A Sacramento Xfinity customer reports six or seven daily outages lasting about 125 seconds, totaling an estimated 117 hours across 17 months. A neighbor on a different line sees the same timing, while repeated modem, router, wiring, and technician interventions have not resolved it. The author infers a scheduled upstream infrastructure failure from duration and minute clustering, but that specific cause remains unverified. Commenters share similar DOCSIS failures and recommend modem logs, outside-plant escalation, regulators, or municipal competition.
