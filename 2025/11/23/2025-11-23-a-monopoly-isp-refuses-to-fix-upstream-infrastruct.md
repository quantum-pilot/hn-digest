# A monopoly ISP refuses to fix upstream infrastructure

- Score: 579 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46019685) | Link: https://sacbear.com/xfinity-wont-fix-internet/

### TL;DR
A Sacramento Xfinity customer documented six or seven daily disconnects lasting about 125 seconds, shared by a neighbor on another line. Seventeen months of monitoring, modem synchronization errors, repeated equipment tests, and ineffective technician visits support a fault beyond one home, although the author’s scheduled timeout theory is not proven. Commenters reported similar cable failures and emphasized reliability over headline bandwidth. They recommended modem diagnostics, outside plant escalation, local cable commissions, and public or municipal competition when ordinary support cannot route technical evidence.
