# Google broke its promise to me – now ICE has my data

- Score: 994 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47782570) | Link: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/04/google-broke-its-promise-me-now-ice-has-my-data

### TL;DR

Former Cornell Ph.D. candidate Amandla Thomas-Johnson says Google gave ICE his account subscriber data—IP and physical addresses, identifiers, and session timing—under an April 2025 administrative subpoena without advance notice. He had briefly attended a pro-Palestinian protest, left the United States, and was never accused of a crime. EFF argues Google broke its longstanding notification promise and asked California and New York attorneys general to investigate deceptive practices. Commenters noted the subpoena reportedly lacked a court-enforced gag order.
