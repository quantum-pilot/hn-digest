# Google Gemma 4 Runs Natively on iPhone with Full Offline AI Inference

- Score: 265 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47774971) | Link: https://www.gizmoweek.com/gemma-4-runs-iphone/

### TL;DR

Google AI Edge Gallery can download Gemma 4 models for fully offline inference on iPhones using the GPU. The app combines text, image recognition, voice, and extensible Skills; smaller E2B and E4B variants target mobile constraints, with E2B recommended for speed and memory. The article claims low latency and privacy benefits for field or regulated work but supplies no device, throughput, memory, thermal, or battery measurements. Developers reported 2B models practical while questioning production readiness and App Store acceptance.
