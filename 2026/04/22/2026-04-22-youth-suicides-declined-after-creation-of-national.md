# Youth Suicides Declined After Creation of National Hotline

- Score: 192 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47865622) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/04/22/science/988-youth-suicides-decline.html

### TL;DR

A JAMA research letter found U.S. suicides among ages 15–34 from July 2022 through December 2024 were 11% below historical projections after the 988 hotline launched, representing 4,372 fewer deaths. The ten states with the largest call increases were 18.2% below projections versus 10.6% in the lowest-uptake states; declines were smaller among adults over 65 and absent among English youth. Authors cannot exclude post-pandemic or service changes. Hacker News shared both lifesaving conversations and traumatic police escalations, favoring sustained funding, mobile crisis teams, means restriction, and proactive human outreach.

### Comment pulse

- Callers described skilled listeners easing acute despair — counterpoint: others reported rushed calls or police responses that escalated trauma.
- Barriers between suicidal impulses and lethal means, including locked firearms, can reduce deaths while longer-term care addresses underlying distress.
- School-year seasonality suggests institutional stress matters; hotlines cannot replace checking on people or broader mental-health and economic support.
