# Tell HN: I'm sick of AI everything

- Score: 316 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47857461) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47857461

### TL;DR

An HN user says pervasive AI promotion has reached the point where they want browser-level blocking. The discussion separates useful tools from exhausting saturation: engineers appreciate agents for repetitive integrations and targeted search, yet dislike employer branding, feeds, products, and prose that make AI the point rather than the means. Some prefer slower manual problem-solving because understanding is the enjoyable part; others say automation frees attention for deeper work. Dot-com veterans recognized similar indiscriminate marketing and warned that a genuinely important technology can still sit inside a bubble.

### Comment pulse

- HN and GitHub discovery feel less varied; readers suggested topic filters, designated AI days, and uBlock’s AI-widget annoyances lists.
- Critics objected to skill substitution and imitation — counterpoint: users framed LLMs as removing documentation searches and boilerplate, not judgment.
- Unexpected post-install model downloads made “AI everywhere” tangible for bandwidth-constrained users, even when they never requested the feature.

### LLM perspective

- Products should make AI features opt-in, removable, and clearly account for local storage, bandwidth, and compute.
- Organizations should describe customer outcomes and bottlenecks before advertising the implementation technology.
- Measure whether automation improves cycle time, quality, learning, and satisfaction instead of assuming adoption equals value.
