# Alberta startup sells no-tech tractors for half price

- Score: 1198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47865868) | Link: https://wheelfront.com/this-alberta-startup-sells-no-tech-tractors-for-half-price/

### TL;DR

Alberta’s Ursa Ag is selling deliberately electronic-free tractors built around remanufactured 1990s 12-valve Cummins diesels, Bosch mechanical injection, physical controls, and no ECU or proprietary diagnostics. Its 150-hp model starts at C$129,900—about US$95,000—and the 260-hp version at C$199,900, roughly half comparable major-brand pricing. One interview reportedly generated 400 US inquiries, reflecting farmers’ right-to-repair and downtime frustrations. Readers embraced repairable longevity and add-on electronics, but questioned scale: Ursa sells directly, has few dealers, struggles to stock inventory, and must build continental support.

### Comment pulse
