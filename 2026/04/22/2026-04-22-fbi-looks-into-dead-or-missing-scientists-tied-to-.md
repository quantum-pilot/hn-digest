# FBI looks into dead or missing scientists tied to NASA, Blue Origin, SpaceX

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47858246) | Link: https://fortune.com/2026/04/21/scientists-disappear-die-nasa-space-blue-origin-spacex/

### TL;DR

House Oversight asked four agencies to review at least 11 deaths or disappearances since 2022 among people loosely connected to nuclear, aerospace, or defense work. The FBI is checking for shared classified access or foreign actors; NASA says its information currently indicates no national-security threat. The cases span hikes, known homicides, unexplained deaths, and people leaving with firearms, while links to SpaceX or Blue Origin are often indirect through technologies or contracts. Commenters overwhelmingly warned that selecting cases after the fact without a denominator can manufacture a sinister pattern.

### Comment pulse

- A commenter’s case-by-case inventory found varied circumstances and only one clear rocket scientist, weakening the implied cluster.
- Base-rate estimates suggested many researcher deaths are expected nationwide — counterpoint: disappearances among clearance holders still merit routine scrutiny.
- Several questioned publicizing an active probe, reading the announcement as political messaging rather than evidence of a discovered conspiracy.

### LLM perspective

- Establish the eligible workforce, expected rates, and predeclared case criteria before testing whether events exceed baseline.
- Separate unresolved disappearances, accidental deaths, suicides, and solved homicides instead of presenting one composite count.
- Watch for agency findings based on shared evidence, not professional adjacency or commercial reuse of research.
