# FDA approves first gene therapy for treatment of genetic hearing loss

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47919733) | Link: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-ever-gene-therapy-treatment-genetic-hearing-loss-under-national-priority-voucher

### TL;DR

FDA granted accelerated approval to Otarmeni, the first gene therapy for severe-to-profound sensorineural hearing loss caused by confirmed biallelic `OTOF` variants. The one-time dual-AAV1 treatment is surgically infused into the cochlea to restore otoferlin production; candidates need preserved outer hair cells and no prior implant in that ear. In an ongoing single-arm pediatric trial, 16 of 20 evaluable patients improved hearing. Continued approval depends on durability, speech, and quality-of-life evidence. HN responses celebrated the milestone while noting its narrow genetic eligibility leaves many forms of deafness untreated.

### Comment pulse
