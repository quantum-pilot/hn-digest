# Microsoft and OpenAI end their exclusive and revenue-sharing deal

- Score: 719 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47921248) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-27/microsoft-to-stop-sharing-revenue-with-main-ai-partner-openai

### TL;DR

Microsoft and OpenAI are revising their partnership: Microsoft will stop paying OpenAI a revenue share, and the relationship will no longer be exclusive. The companies describe the change as simplifying an increasingly complex arrangement. OpenAI has pursued multiple cloud providers, including Amazon, to meet growing computing needs and serve a wider software audience. Microsoft retains the 27% stake it received during OpenAI’s restructuring last year. The update reframes one of AI’s foundational alliances without ending it, while leaving many commercial details undisclosed.
