# Dutch central bank ditches AWS and chooses Lidl for European Cloud

- Score: 301 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47922712) | Link: https://www.techzine.eu/news/infrastructure/140634/dutch-central-bank-chooses-lidl-for-european-cloud/

### TL;DR

De Nederlandsche Bank plans a major contract with Schwarz Digits, the Schwarz Group unit behind Lidl and the Stackit cloud, to reduce reliance on American providers. Stackit grew from internal infrastructure for Lidl and Kaufland, keeps data under European law, and is expanding through an €11 billion German data-center investment. DNB previously warned that Dutch finance was overly dependent on foreign IT, while admitting European clouds remain less mature than US hyperscalers. HN discussion welcomed provider competition but stressed that sovereignty migrations are costly, uncertain, and complicated by managed-service lock-in.

### Comment pulse
