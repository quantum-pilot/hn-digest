# German men 18-45 need military permit for extended stays abroad

- Score: 338 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47639976) | Link: https://www.dw.com/en/german-men-need-military-permit-for-extended-stays-abroad/a-76662677

### TL;DR

Germany’s 2026 military-service law requires men aged 18–45 to request Bundeswehr approval before spending more than three months abroad. Because service remains voluntary, career centers must generally grant requests when no specific service is expected; the ministry says there are currently no penalties, and it is drafting exceptions. The rule supports force planning as Germany targets 260,000 active troops by 2035, up from roughly 180,000. Commenters viewed today’s paperwork as either administrative preparation for emergencies or a dormant exit-control mechanism, prompting civil-liberties and sex-equality disputes.
