# 12k AI-generated blog posts added in a single commit

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47640722) | Link: https://github.com/OneUptime/blog/commit/30cd2384794c897d95aca77d173db44af51ca849

### TL;DR

A OneUptime commit message advertises 12,000 new posts spanning ClickHouse, Redis, MongoDB, MySQL, Rook/Ceph, and Dapr, including tutorials, comparisons, runbooks, and configuration guides. GitHub’s summary shows 5,012 files changed and enormous registry and line churn, so the page itself does not independently establish the headline count or content quality. Commenters reported OneUptime pages dominating technical searches and argued that mass automated publishing destroys trust even when individual articles seem adequate, because readers cannot cheaply verify expertise or hallucinations.

### Comment pulse

- **View:** The damaging unit is not one weak article but thousands that make search prevalence look like corroboration.
