# Gold overtakes U.S. Treasuries as the largest foreign reserve asset

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47635056) | Link: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/gold-overtakes-u-s-treasuries-as-the-worlds-largest-foreign-reserve-asset-in-2026-can-gold-challenge-the-u-s-dollars-dominance-and-hold-its-ground/articleshow/126420128.cms?from=mdr

### TL;DR

Foreign official gold holdings are reportedly nearing $4 trillion, slightly above roughly $3.9 trillion in US Treasuries for the first time since 1996. The article attributes the crossover to a 70%-plus 2025 gold rally and accelerated central-bank buying amid inflation, debt, sanctions, and geopolitical concerns; it also acknowledges the dollar remains the dominant reserve currency. Commenters emphasized that valuation gains, not a sudden Treasury liquidation, explain much of the milestone, flagged the article’s January 9 date and promotional tone, and placed the shift within multi-year diversification trends.

### Comment pulse

- Gold avoids issuer default and some freeze risk → it lacks Treasuries’ liquidity and cannot replace every reserve-management function.
- Central-bank buying accelerated before 2026 → recent politics may reinforce, rather than originate, the diversification trend.
- Dollar decline narratives overread one comparison → gold can exceed foreign Treasury holdings while the dollar remains the leading reserve currency.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** This is a composition and valuation signal, not evidence that the dollar system has already been displaced.
- **Impact:** Reserve managers gain diversification; US borrowing sensitivity could rise if foreign Treasury demand weakens persistently.
- **Watch next:** Holdings by tonnes versus market value, Treasury ownership flows, reserve-currency shares, real yields, and central-bank purchase disclosures.
