# Iranian missile blitz takes down AWS data centers in Bahrain and Dubai

- Score: 122 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47641788) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/iranian-missile-blitz-takes-down-aws-data-centers-in-bahrain-and-dubai-amazon-declares-hard-down-status-for-multiple-zones

### TL;DR

Amazon reportedly declared multiple availability zones in its Bahrain and Dubai regions “hard down” after Iranian strikes, while others remained impaired. An internal memo said normal redundancy and resiliency were unavailable, customers’ workloads were being migrated elsewhere, services should shrink to minimum footprints, and AWS had no restoration timeline. The attacks are part of a campaign against regional US technology infrastructure during the Iran-US war. The report also warns that conflict-driven disruption around Hormuz threatens semiconductor inputs including aluminum, helium, and LNG, with infrastructure recovery potentially taking months or years.
