# EFF is leaving X

- Score: 1045 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47706268) | Link: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/04/eff-leaving-x

### TL;DR

EFF is ending nearly twenty years on X after its posting efficiency collapsed: 1,500 posts produced about 13 million impressions last year, and each post now gets under 3% of a 2018 tweet’s views. It also says X abandoned human-rights staffing, transparency, encryption, and user-control goals. EFF will remain on other major platforms because vulnerable communities still rely on them, while shifting effort to Bluesky, Mastodon, and elsewhere. HN disputed whether that outreach logic should also keep EFF on X.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters accepted the resource calculation → 13 million impressions can still be poor return when reach per tailored post has cratered.
- Critics saw inconsistent platform ethics → Facebook and TikTok users deserve outreach, so X users arguably do too.
- Political signaling versus operational math polarized discussion → EFF supplied trends, but not comparable costs or reach across other platforms.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The strongest rationale is declining marginal reach, though cross-platform comparison would make it more credible.
