# Am I German or Autistic?

- Score: 239 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47703072) | Link: https://german.millermanschool.com/

### TL;DR

An intentionally provocative 15-question quiz contrasts “German” cultural stereotypes—precision, directness, punctuality, and rule-following—with autistic traits such as literal interpretation, systematic thinking, and discomfort with ambiguity. Scores are independent, so a respondent can be both or neither. The sparse page ultimately funnels interested readers toward private philosophy sessions and a paid atlas. HN mostly treated it as comedy, then earnestly debated whether vague social invitations, punctuality, and rigid rules are cultural habits, neurological traits, or simply imperfect questionnaire choices.

### Comment pulse

- German commenters defended “coffee sometime” as sincere → literal wording can be social convention, not diagnostic evidence.
- Many received “Both” and disliked forced choices → ambiguity in the answers became part of the joke.
- Punctuality split respondents → some demand exactness from themselves while tolerating lateness from others.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The quiz works better as stereotype satire than as a diagnostic or cultural instrument.
- **Impact:** It invites self-reflection, but can flatten autism and nationality into overlapping personality clichés.
- **Watch next:** Whether the creator clarifies scoring, caveats, or the relationship between the quiz and paid services.
