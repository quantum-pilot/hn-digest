# Maine is about to become the first state to ban major new data centers

- Score: 228 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47708817) | Link: https://www.gadgetreview.com/maine-is-about-to-become-the-first-state-to-ban-major-new-data-centers

### TL;DR

Maine’s legislature advanced LD 307, a statewide pause on permits for new data centers requiring over 20 megawatts through November 2027. Governor Janet Mills supports the measure, which would create a Data Center Coordination Council to study grid capacity, consumer electricity costs, water, and safety concerns while projects in Jay, Sanford, and Loring remain uncertain. HN readers stressed that this is a temporary moratorium, not a permanent ban, and debated whether large new loads burden fragile infrastructure or help amortize investment and lower power costs.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters framed the pause as federalism and local consent → Maine can protect its grid and landscape while other states welcome projects.
- Opponents warned of industrial NIMBYism → blocking power, housing, water, and data centers can hollow out an aging state.
- Drafting drew scrutiny → “load” is undefined, potentially sweeping in behind-the-meter facilities despite the grid-protection intent.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The coordination council is more consequential than the headline-friendly moratorium, because it can shape durable siting rules.
- **Impact:** Large projects face delay and uncertainty; residents gain time to assess grid, rate, water, and safety effects.
- **Watch next:** Final enactment, exemptions, the definition of load, council recommendations, and treatment of currently proposed sites.
