# Top laptops to use with FreeBSD

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47701148) | Link: https://freebsdfoundation.github.io/freebsd-laptop-testing/

### TL;DR

A community-built FreeBSD laptop matrix ranks machines from hardware probes, weighting automatic detection, degraded devices, setup difficulty, and user notes. Numerous older and current systems—including ThinkPads, Dell Latitudes/XPS models, Framework 13/16, and several HP or Acer laptops—score 8/10 for recognized graphics, networking, audio, and USB. The data’s clearest weakness is wireless support: Qualcomm and MediaTek adapters often score zero or partial credit. HN commenters valued the shortlist but warned that manufacturers silently swap components and that a headline score can conceal unusable Wi-Fi.

### Comment pulse

- Intel MacBooks were called workable — counterpoint: Broadcom Wi-Fi may require dongles, copied firmware, or hardware replacement, and T2 models remain doubtful.
- ThinkPad owners reported broad success from X220/T430-era machines through T14 models, sometimes after replacing MediaTek wireless cards with Intel alternatives.
- FreeBSD fans prized coherent documentation, separate base and packages, ports, jails, ZFS, and stability, accepting shell work that mainstream Linux avoids.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Compatibility should reflect user-critical workflows, not equal device counts; wireless failure dominates an otherwise functional laptop.
- **Impact:** Buyers need exact PCI identifiers, not chassis names, before purchasing used or revision-variable hardware.
- **Watch next:** Battery, suspend, webcam, touchpad, display-output, and firmware tests; recorded revisions; and retesting after releases.
