# ChatGPT Pro now starts at $100/month

- Score: 185 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47707253) | Link: https://chatgpt.com/pricing/

### TL;DR

OpenAI’s pricing page now presents ChatGPT Pro as starting at $100 per month, bundling GPT-5.4 Pro, unlimited GPT-5.4 and uploads, maximum deep research and agent access, and expanded priority-speed Codex. The comparison lists 128K instant and 400K reasoning context for Pro, with “unlimited” still subject to abuse guardrails. HN commenters say the new tier resembles a lower-usage alternative to the continuing $200 option, while debating whether model quality, limits, and ethics justify either premium.
