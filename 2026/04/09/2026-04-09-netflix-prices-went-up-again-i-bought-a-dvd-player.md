# Netflix Prices Went Up Again – I Bought a DVD Player Instead

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47709251) | Link: https://aywren.com/2026/04/09/netflix-prices-went-up-again-i-bought-a-dvd-player-instead/

### TL;DR

Facing Netflix’s ad-supported increase from $7.99 to $8.99 plus fees—and unwilling to pay $19.99 for ad-free viewing—the author plans to cancel and redirect roughly $10 monthly toward discounted DVDs and Blu-rays. An $89 multi-format player and sale-priced box sets offer a durable library without catalog removals or plan restrictions, fitting their infrequent viewing and tighter finances. HN discussion shared the desire for durable access but treated physical media as one practical option, not a universal replacement.

### Comment pulse

- Backups drew legal nuance: owning a disc can permit archival copying, yet bypassing DRM and public exhibition remain separately restricted.
- Blu-ray generally beats streaming quality, commenters said; bargain DVDs may compress low-resolution video heavily, making price comparisons misleading.
- Libraries offer free, deliberate discovery; mail-rental services survive, but slower logistics and low volume make old Netflix economics hard to reproduce.
