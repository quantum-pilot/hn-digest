# Show HN: How LLMs Work – Interactive visual guide based on Karpathy's lecture

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47886517) | Link: https://ynarwal.github.io/how-llms-work/

### TL;DR

An interactive guide visualizes an LLM pipeline: web filtering, BPE tokenization, next-token transformer training, stochastic inference, supervised tuning, RLHF, tool use, and retrieval-augmented generation. Using representative figures—44 TB, 15 trillion tokens, and 405 billion parameters—it frames assistants as post-trained internet simulators with parameter memory and context-window working memory. Hacker News largely rejected the page as AI-generated repackaging of Andrej Karpathy’s lecture, citing generic design and weak proofreading. Debate centered on an earlier single-hard-drive analogy, though readers found it closely paraphrased the lecture and technically possible with enterprise hardware.

### Comment pulse

- Readers saw little value in machine-generated restatement when anyone could prompt similar material and the author’s expertise remained unclear.
- Generic dark-mode styling and familiar generated prose became credibility signals before commenters evaluated the educational substance.
- Comparing 44 TB with one drive seemed careless — counterpoint: 44 TB enterprise drives exist, and Karpathy made a similar approximation.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Interactive presentation can aid learning, but source attribution does not substitute for expert verification of every simplification.
- **Impact:** Small factual or framing errors undermine trust across the entire guide, especially for beginners unable to detect them.
- **Watch next:** Public corrections, explicit generation disclosures, technical review, learner testing, and comparisons with the original lecture’s precision.
