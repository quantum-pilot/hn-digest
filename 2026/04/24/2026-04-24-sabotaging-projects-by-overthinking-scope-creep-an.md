# Sabotaging projects by overthinking, scope creep, and structural diffing

- Score: 345 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47890799) | Link: https://kevinlynagh.com/newsletter/2026_04_overthinking/

### TL;DR

Kevin Lynagh argues vague success criteria turn prior-art research into sabotage: nearby tools expand scope, perfectionism suppresses experimentation, and faster LLM coding funds unnecessary features. A weekend shelf succeeded because the goal was social woodworking, while an Emacs fuzzy-search project wasted hours on anchors he never needed. He applies the lesson to a small structural-diff prototype for reviewing agent code: Rust entities, greedy matching, and command-line output first. Hacker News connected the pattern to PhDs and side projects, favoring small releases and practical learning before deeper literature review.
