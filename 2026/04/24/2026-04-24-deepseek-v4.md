# DeepSeek v4

- Score: 1782 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47884971) | Link: https://api-docs.deepseek.com/

### TL;DR

DeepSeek’s API now exposes `deepseek-v4-flash` and `deepseek-v4-pro` through both OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible formats. Calls can enable thinking and set reasoning effort; the older `deepseek-chat` and `deepseek-reasoner` aliases currently map to Flash’s non-thinking and thinking modes but are scheduled for deprecation on July 24, 2026. HN praised the concise developer documentation, low pricing, and reported mathematical usefulness. Discussion remained mixed on comparative quality: Flash drew enthusiasm for speed and agentic iteration, while Pro was described as stronger in some proofs but slower and less reliable operationally.

### Comment pulse

- Research anecdotes lacked exact model versions and harness details — counterpoint: several users still found Pro’s proof refinement unusually useful.
- Deterministic, batch-invariant kernels attracted attention because repeatability can simplify debugging and evaluation.
