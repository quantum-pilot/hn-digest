# Why I Write (1946)

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47884768) | Link: https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/why-i-write/

### TL;DR

George Orwell traces his vocation from childhood isolation, imaginary stories, involuntary description, and delight in language to political commitments imposed by imperialism, poverty, fascism, and the Spanish Civil War. He names four motives—egoism, aesthetic enthusiasm, historical record, and political purpose—and argues every writer mixes them. His mature aim was to expose lies while making political writing art, reconciling truth, partisanship, and style without decorative vagueness. Hacker News admired the prose and creative compulsion, discussed differences in verbal inner experience, and noted that the next supposedly doomed novel became Nineteen Eighty-Four.
