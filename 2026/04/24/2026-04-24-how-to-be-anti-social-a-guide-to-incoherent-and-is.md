# How to be anti-social – a guide to incoherent and isolating social experiences

- Score: 290 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47888372) | Link: https://nate.leaflet.pub/3mk4xkaxobc2p

### TL;DR

The post uses an inverted self-help list to describe how conversations become hostile and isolating: assume malice, treat feelings as proof, dodge challenges, ask loaded questions, harden under dissent, recruit allies with selectively framed stories, and withhold grace or curiosity. Its central warning is that unexamined assumptions turn ambiguity into conflict and make understanding impossible. The author later clarified that the brief rant targeted uncharitable family disputes and reflexive blame for Bluesky outages, not a diagnosis or portrait of a particular person.
