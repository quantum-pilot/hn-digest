# Ubuntu 26.04

- Score: 299 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47885596) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1069399/

### TL;DR

Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, “Resolute Raccoon,” shipped on schedule with TPM-backed full-disk encryption, more memory-safe components, tighter application permissions, and Arm Livepatch. Canonical promises five years of maintenance for Desktop, Server, Cloud, WSL, and Core; official flavors receive three years, though Ubuntu MATE skipped this cycle. Hacker News discussion centered on desktop friction—disabled middle-click paste, blocking password prompts, and opinionated tiling—and Canonical’s Snap strategy. Users weighing alternatives recommended Debian, Mint, or Fedora, while noting Ubuntu’s strong hardware and driver support.

### Comment pulse

- Rust rewrites of coreutils and sudo drew scrutiny because new CVEs showed memory safety addresses only one class of security bugs.
- Disabling selection paste reduced accidental actions — counterpoint: longtime Linux users saw the change as needless GNOME breakage.
- Debian, Mint, and Fedora appealed to users dissatisfied with Snap and Canonical’s community direction; Ubuntu retained pragmatic hardware advantages.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** An LTS release succeeds through predictable maintenance and migration safety more than headline desktop changes.
- **Impact:** Ubuntu MATE users face an unusually long support and upgrade decision after their flavor skipped this LTS.
