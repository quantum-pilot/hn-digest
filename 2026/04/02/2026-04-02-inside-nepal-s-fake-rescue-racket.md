# Inside Nepal's Fake Rescue Racket

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47613078) | Link: https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/03/27/inside-nepal-s-fake-rescue-racket

### TL;DR

A Nepal police investigation describes a commission-based network of trekking firms, helicopter operators, hospitals, and intermediaries allegedly manufacturing or exaggerating emergencies, combining passengers while billing separate charters, fabricating manifests and records, and ordering unnecessary care. Investigators confirmed 171 fake rescues among 4,782 foreign patients from 2022–2025; 32 people were charged in March 2026 and nine arrested. Earlier reforms after a 2018 inquiry went unenforced. HN focused on the article’s treatment of altitude sickness, stressing that rapid descent can be lifesaving and Diamox with hydration is standard.

### Comment pulse

- Experienced trekkers say AMS can turn fatal unpredictably even near 11,000–14,000 feet, so recommending evacuation is not itself evidence of fraud.
- Documented baking-powder adulteration, reused X-rays, fake admissions, and duplicate billing are harder to reconcile with legitimate caution.
- Insurers may tolerate small distributed losses when verification costs and risk of denying genuine rescues are also high.
