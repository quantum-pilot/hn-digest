# The Weather Channel – RetroCast

- Score: 303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47609090) | Link: https://weather.com/retro/

### TL;DR

RetroCast turns current local weather into a browser recreation of The Weather Channel’s 1980s–90s television presentation: blocky graphics, iconography, CRT-like blur, smooth jazz, nearby-city conditions, astronomy, narrative outlooks, and a five-day forecast. The supplied page localized itself to Falkenstein and exposed unit settings, but its short presentation stops rather than looping. HN praised the convincing filter stack and nostalgia, repeatedly requesting continuous playback and more music. Comparisons with fan-made WeatherStar emulators split by age and geography; several users said this version better matched their childhood broadcasts and supports non-US locations.

### Comment pulse
