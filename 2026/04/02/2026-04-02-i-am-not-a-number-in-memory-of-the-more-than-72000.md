# I Am Not A Number. In memory of the more than 72,000 Palestinians killed

- Score: 394 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47612053) | Link: https://bkhmsi.github.io/i-am-not-a-number/

### TL;DR

The site turns a reported death toll into an interactive memorial: 60,199 Palestinian lives are represented as individual lights, filterable by age, beneath a dedication to more than 72,000 deaths in Gaza. Its sparse design emphasizes that each statistic was a person rather than merely a number. HN readers described mouse movement across names as harrowing, argued the total is an undercount, and suggested connecting entries to biographical stories or reporting.
