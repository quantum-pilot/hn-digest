# Artemis II will use laser beams to live-stream 4K moon footage at 260 Mbps

- Score: 315 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47615449) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/artemis-ii-will-use-laser-beams-to-live-stream-4k-moon-footage-one-giant-step-beyond-the-s-band-radio-comms-of-the-apollo-era

### TL;DR

Tom’s Hardware says Orion’s O2O optical communications terminal can send data at up to 260 Mbps, potentially supporting 4K video, imagery, procedures, and flight plans during Artemis II. Ground stations in New Mexico and California require clear skies, while the Deep Space Network remains a radio backup; both links disappear during an estimated 41-minute far-side blackout. Commenters challenged the headline: Artemis II does not land, prior missions have imaged the far side, and one cited mission material describing a pre-recorded 4K transmission near the Moon rather than a livestream.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **View:** O2O’s meaningful milestone is testing high-bandwidth deep-space optical networking, not merely prettier video.
- **Impact:** Future crews could move richer science, operational data, and media without relying solely on constrained radio links.
