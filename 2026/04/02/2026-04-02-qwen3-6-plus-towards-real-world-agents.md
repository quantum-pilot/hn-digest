# Qwen3.6-Plus: Towards real world agents

- Score: 406 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47615002) | Link: https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.6

### TL;DR

Alibaba’s hosted-only Qwen3.6-Plus launches with a default one-million-token context, stronger claimed coding and tool-use performance, and upgraded image, document, video, GUI, and frontend reasoning. It supports OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs plus `preserve_thinking`, an optional mode that carries reasoning across agent turns. Vendor benchmarks place it near frontier models and first on several tasks, but use Claude Opus 4.5 and Gemini 3 Pro rather than newer releases. HN focused less on demos than closed weights, undisclosed parameter count, unclear smaller open variants, and whether lower pricing offsets non-leading performance.

### Comment pulse

- Critics saw open weights as a marketing funnel — counterpoint: Qwen’s Plus, Max, and Omni lines have previously included closed models.
- Older comparison models looked selectively favorable — counterpoint: commenters cited evaluation lag and argued Opus 4.5 remains practically strong.
- Production users valued predictable instruction-following and claimed 70–90% savings over marginal benchmark gains, expecting price-sensitive customers to switch providers readily.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** This is a frontier-adjacent API offering whose real differentiation may be economics and agent behavior, not benchmark leadership.
- **Impact:** Compatibility lowers switching costs and intensifies price pressure across coding-agent backends.
- **Watch next:** Independent evaluations, latency, actual pricing, reasoning-token consumption, open-variant sizes and licenses, plus comparisons with current competitors.
