# Delve allegedly forked an open-source tool and sold it as its own

- Score: 265 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47615434) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/01/the-reputation-of-troubled-yc-startup-delve-has-gotten-even-worse/

### TL;DR

TechCrunch reports an anonymous whistleblower’s allegation that Delve presented its Pathways no-code agent builder as original work despite allegedly forking Sim.ai’s Apache-licensed SimStudio without required attribution. Sim.ai’s CEO said Delve had no separate license agreement and a proposed commercial deal did not materialize, while Delve did not answer TechCrunch and related pages appeared removed. Apache licensing permits commercial reuse when its conditions are followed, so the core issue is alleged noncompliance and misrepresentation, not merely selling open-source code. The claims remain allegations, alongside separate disputed compliance accusations.

### Comment pulse

- Many viewed missing notices as readily fixable, but allegedly denying the fork turned it into a durable credibility failure.
- Debate separated permissive reuse from compliance: Apache permits commercialization but requires license, copyright, change, and NOTICE obligations.
- Critics saw special irony in a compliance vendor allegedly mishandling basic licensing—counterpoint: this may be less serious than other accusations.
