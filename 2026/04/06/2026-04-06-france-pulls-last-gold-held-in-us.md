# France pulls last gold held in US

- Score: 554 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47658146) | Link: https://www.mining.com/france-pulls-last-gold-held-in-us-for-15b-gain/

### TL;DR

Banque de France sold 129 tonnes of older, non-standard gold held at the New York Fed and bought an equivalent quantity of modern-standard bars in Europe, now stored in Paris. The operation ran from July 2025 through January 2026, leaving France’s total reserve unchanged at about 2,437 tonnes. Selling long-held assets realized roughly €13 billion in gains and helped produce €8.1 billion net profit for 2025 after a 2024 loss. HN readers stressed this was accounting realization, not wealth created by repatriation.

### Comment pulse

- The transaction modernized bars and consolidated custody without physically transporting the New York stock.
- Realized gains reflect old book values meeting current prices, then repurchasing equivalent exposure.
- Readers debated timing and terminology — counterpoint: a capital gain is real in accounting even when reserve weight stays unchanged.

- **View:** “Repatriation” and “gain” describe separate effects that the headline misleadingly fuses.
- **Impact:** France gains direct custody and standardized reserves; its economic gold exposure barely changes.
