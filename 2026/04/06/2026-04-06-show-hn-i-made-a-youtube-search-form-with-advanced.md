# Show HN: I made a YouTube search form with advanced filters

- Score: 302 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47655392) | Link: https://playlists.at/youtube/search/

### TL;DR

Playlists.at offers an alternative YouTube search form with advanced prefixes and a choice between YouTube and Google Video. Results can be selected into a playlist, cleared, or played together, although the captured page contains no query or results and does not expose the available filters in detail. The Show HN discussion became a broader indictment of YouTube search: exact titles, viewing history, uncommon spellings, and channel-specific retrieval often fail, while irrelevant recommendations, Shorts, ads, and engagement-driven UI crowd out intentional discovery.

### Comment pulse

- Users described exact-title and watch-history searches failing, sometimes finding marked-as-watched videos only through channel browsing or Google activity records.
- Misspellings and niche phrases get silently “corrected” or misclassified — counterpoint: quoted queries sometimes recover the intended results.
- Requests focused on arbitrary duration ranges and channel filters, but YouTube’s enum-based length filter may require multiple searches and client-side pruning.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The product opportunity is restoring deterministic retrieval where YouTube optimizes for recommendation and engagement.
- **Impact:** Better filtering could revive long-tail discovery while reducing time spent fighting irrelevant feeds and platform assumptions.
- **Watch next:** Documented filter syntax, pagination behavior, API limits, duration merging, channel scoping, playlist import, and account-data compatibility.
