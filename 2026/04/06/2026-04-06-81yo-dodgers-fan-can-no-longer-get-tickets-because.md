# 81yo Dodgers fan can no longer get tickets because he doesn't have a smartphone

- Score: 326 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47662857) | Link: https://twitter.com/Suzierizzo1/status/2040864617467924865

### TL;DR

A viral post says an 81-year-old Dodgers fan and 50-year season-ticket holder lost access to printed season tickets because the team now requires digital tickets, which he struggles to use. Commenters added missing context: the digital-only policy targets resale by tracking transfers, single-game paper tickets remain available, and the team had previously made paid printing exceptions for him. Even so, many argued a smartphone mandate excludes elderly and disabled customers and creates needless battery, theft, privacy, and accessibility risks.

### Comment pulse

- Aging, dry fingertips, tremors, poor speech recognition, and changing interfaces can make smartphones materially inaccessible.
- Anti-scalping is legitimate — counterpoint: nontransferable paper tickets or fee-based will call could preserve enforcement without mandatory apps.
- Requiring unrelated hardware for a purchase shifts vendor convenience and surveillance costs onto customers.
