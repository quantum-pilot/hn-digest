# I won't download your app. The web version is a-ok

- Score: 809 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47661439) | Link: https://www.0xsid.com/blog/wont-download-your-app

### TL;DR

A developer argues that most service apps merely render text, media, or forms yet demand large downloads, permissions, background access, and app-store mediation. Websites preserve user control through extensions, blockers, scripts, links, and browser sandboxing; companies often degrade them to gain telemetry, notifications, retention, and ad exposure. HN readers agreed app-only tickets and essential services are exclusionary, but supplied the economic counterexample: one site’s simple app wrapper generated thousands of downloads and increased paid upgrades tenfold because mainstream users actively prefer app-store distribution.
