# Age verification as mass surveillance infrastructure

- Score: 289 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47659109) | Link: https://tboteproject.com/surveillancefindings/

### TL;DR

A sprawling OSINT report argues that age-verification mandates are building cross-border biometric surveillance infrastructure around vendors such as Persona, AU10TIX, and Verifymy.io. It catalogs Persona SDK features including government-ID and selfie collection, device fingerprinting, silent carrier authentication, analytics, national-ID connections, government-reporting modules, and AI integrations, alongside legislative growth in Brazil, Britain, and US states. The HN discussion sharply challenged the report’s credibility: critics said it was largely LLM-written, mixed verifiable technical findings with unrelated corporate links, and failed to prove actual mass-surveillance use.

### Comment pulse

- Even sympathetic readers found the presentation too diffuse and source-to-claim reasoning too weak to trust without laborious verification.
- Privacy-preserving proofs can disclose only age — counterpoint: auditability, token resale, device binding, and government access create difficult trade-offs.
- Mandates are expanding from adult sites toward app stores, operating systems, and AI services, making infrastructure scope the central concern.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The report surfaces an important threat model but overstates what its evidence establishes.
- **Impact:** Users may surrender immutable biometrics to opaque vendors as legal age gates proliferate.
- **Watch next:** Independent audits, disclosed subprocessors, retention evidence, zero-knowledge deployments, and reporting that cleanly separates fact from inference.
