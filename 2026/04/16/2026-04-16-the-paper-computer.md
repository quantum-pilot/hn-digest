# The paper computer

- Score: 267 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47747770) | Link: https://jsomers.net/blog/the-paper-computer

### TL;DR

James Somers imagines “paper computing”: AI quietly bridges physical work and digital systems so people can process email with pen strokes, mark printed drafts, rearrange note cards, or organize projects spatially while retaining synchronization, portability, and automation. He argues paper and rooms offer human-scale flexibility and fewer distractions than bundled screens. A complementary software version would make devices enter strict, task-specific modes. The broader hope is that advanced transcription, vision, and robotics restore tactile, natural interfaces without surrendering digital convenience.
