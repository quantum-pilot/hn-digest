# IPv6 traffic crosses the 50% mark

- Score: 767 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47777894) | Link: https://www.google.com/intl/en/ipv6/statistics.html?yzh=28197

### TL;DR

Google’s adoption metric—the share of its users reaching Google over IPv6—has touched 50%, but discussion noted a weekly cycle: Saturday peaks near 50% and weekday troughs around 45%. Commenters attributed the pattern to IPv6-heavy mobile and residential networks replacing lagging corporate and educational connections on weekends. The milestone does not mean half of Internet traffic is IPv6. Hacker News split over whether adoption is plateauing or merely noisy, while IPv4-only GitHub and Twilio services still block IPv6-only systems. Legacy IP allowlists make enabling dual-stack service risky for providers.

### Comment pulse

- Weekend crests suggest consumer mobile and residential networks lead deployment, while weekday enterprise traffic pulls the aggregate downward.
- Adoption may still be growing through noisy bursts — counterpoint: dual-stack coexistence weakens urgency and enterprises lack a compelling migration payoff.
- Enabling IPv6 can silently bypass or break customer IP allowlists, exposing how brittle address-based authorization has become.
