# Claude Opus 4.7

- Score: 1386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47793411) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

### TL;DR

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.7 across its products and APIs at unchanged $5/$25-per-million input/output pricing. It claims stronger difficult coding, long-running autonomy, instruction following, self-verification, file memory, professional output, and vision up to 2,576 pixels, plus xhigh effort, task budgets, and Claude Code ultrareview. Migration may require prompt retuning and 1.0–1.35× more input tokens under a new tokenizer. Hacker News focused less on launch benchmarks than operational trust: adaptive-thinking changes hide summaries, Claude variability drove some users to Codex, and aggressive cyber filters reportedly block legitimate research and benign work.
