# Qwen3.6-35B-A3B: Agentic coding power, now open to all

- Score: 867 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47792764) | Link: https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.6-35b-a3b

### TL;DR

Qwen released Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 35 billion total but 3 billion active parameters, as downloadable weights and through Qwen Studio and APIs. The team reports large gains over Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, including 73.4% on SWE-bench Verified, 51.5% on Terminal-Bench 2.0, and strong vision-language results. It supports thinking and non-thinking modes, preserves prior reasoning for agentic work, and connects to OpenClaw, Qwen Code, and Claude Code through OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible APIs. Local users reported consumer-hardware quantizations while debating serious-work readiness.
