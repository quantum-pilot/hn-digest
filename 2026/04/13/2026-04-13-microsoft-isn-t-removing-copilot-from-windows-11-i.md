# Microsoft isn't removing Copilot from Windows 11, it's just renaming it

- Score: 319 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47751936) | Link: https://www.neowin.net/opinions/microsoft-isnt-removing-copilot-from-windows-11-its-just-renaming-it/

### TL;DR

Microsoft’s Windows 11 pullback from Copilot is primarily branding and placement, not removal. In an Insider Notepad build, the Copilot button becomes a generic writing icon offering rewrite, summary, tone, and formatting tools, while “AI features” becomes “Advanced features” with a per-app disable toggle. Microsoft had promised more intentional, useful integrations and fewer unnecessary entry points, so the change technically fits its wording. Commenters nonetheless see concealment and misplaced priorities, preferring a global opt-out and basic product fixes over scattered assistants.
