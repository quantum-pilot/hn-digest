# The Problem That Built an Industry

- Score: 96 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47730712) | Link: https://ajitem.com/blog/iron-core-part-1-the-problem-that-built-an-industry/

### TL;DR

A modern flight booking still traverses infrastructure shaped by SABRE, the American Airlines–IBM system conceived in the 1950s and launched in 1964. Its descendant, IBM z/TPF, is a transaction-focused mainframe runtime designed for short, isolated work units and capable of roughly 10,000 normal or 50,000 peak transactions per second. The author follows one booking through MakeMyTrip, Amadeus, airline passenger systems, payment settlement, and e-ticketing, arguing that specialized architecture and durable protocols can outlast fashion. Low-cost carriers choose simpler Navitaire systems, sacrificing interoperability that enables automatic re-accommodation.
