# Show HN: Pardonned.com – A searchable database of US Pardons

- Score: 350 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47727960) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47727960

### TL;DR

Pardonned.com turns US Justice Department pardon records into a searchable static site. Its creator used Playwright to scrape the source page, stored records in SQLite, generated the site with Astro 6, and published the code. The project began as a way to verify claims by former pardon attorney Liz Oyer, then broadened beyond Trump for usefulness and neutrality. Commenters requested raw exports and presidential comparisons by offense, commutation, fine, and restitution; discussion also examined sweeping preemptive pardons, inconsistent term aggregation, rising clemency totals, and whether presidential pardon power needs reform.

### Comment pulse

- Biden-family language covering eleven years prompted comparison with Ford’s broad Nixon pardon and debate over defensive preemptive clemency.
- Obama’s total combined two terms and many drug commutations, making raw presidential counts misleading without offense and term breakdowns.
- Some wanted approval by another body — counterpoint: others defended unilateral clemency as a constitutional check on courts and legislatures.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Searchability creates accountability, but comparisons need normalization by term, action type, offense, scope, and financial relief.
- **Impact:** Researchers and citizens can audit individual grants while detecting policy shifts, mass actions, and unusual beneficiaries.
- **Watch next:** Comparison tooling, downloadable data, provenance fields, deduplication, term normalization, and distinctions among pardons and commutations.
