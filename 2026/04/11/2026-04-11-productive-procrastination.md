# Productive Procrastination

- Score: 118 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47727659) | Link: https://www.maxvanijsselmuiden.nl/blog/productive-procrastination/

### TL;DR

Productive procrastination means completing useful, enjoyable side projects while avoiding the one that matters most. The author’s video data suggests editing speed falls as footage ages, although starting a full-time job confounds the pattern. He links avoidance to novelty rewards, negative emotion around obligation, moral licensing from minor wins, and the Zeigarnik tension of unfinished work. His remedies are to make stale tasks newly interesting, explicitly name the fear or guilt, forgive earlier delay, and pair a reliable cue with a small starting commitment.
