### TL;DR

On Artemis II’s third day, Orion was more than 180,000 miles from Earth as its four-person crew viewed the Moon, including the Orientale basin from a geometry NASA says exposed the entire feature to human eyes for the first time. That is narrower than humanity’s first sight of the lunar far side; Apollo crews already saw it.
