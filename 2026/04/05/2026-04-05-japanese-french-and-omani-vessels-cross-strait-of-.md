# Japanese, French and Omani vessels cross Strait of Hormuz

- Score: 141 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47649811) | Link: https://japantoday.com/category/politics/japanese-french-and-omani-vessels-cross-the-strait-of-hormuz

### TL;DR

Several Oman-operated tankers, a French-owned CMA CGM container ship, and Japanese-linked gas carriers crossed the Strait of Hormuz after Iran began permitting vessels without US or Israeli ties. One ship reportedly changed its AIS destination to Owner France and went dark during transit. The crossings matter because the strait normally carries about one-fifth of global oil and LNG, but they do not establish a general reopening: traffic remained sporadic and roughly 45 Japanese-linked vessels were still stranded. Comments treated access as geopolitical signaling, often beyond the report’s evidence.
