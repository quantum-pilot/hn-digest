# Maryland becomes first state to ban surveillance pricing in grocery stores

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47951007) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/apr/29/maryland-grocery-stores-ban-surveillance-pricing

### TL;DR

Maryland became the first U.S. state to prohibit grocery stores and delivery services from using personal data to charge an individual more, targeting location-, search-, and demographic-based surveillance pricing. Governor Wes Moore signed the law while other states consider similar rules. Advocates warn exemptions for loyalty programs, promotional discounts, and attorney-general-only enforcement let sellers recreate discrimination by raising baseline prices and personalizing discounts. Hacker News shared that concern, debated whether shelf-level personalization is practical, and warned of an arms race between seller algorithms and shopping agents.

### Comment pulse

- Physical shelves constrain personalization — counterpoint: electronic labels, facial recognition, RF tracking, QR scans, and individualized apps make it technically plausible.
- Buyers may need shopping agents against seller algorithms; others preferred simpler consumer protections and buying less over another opaque intermediary.
- Critics called the law easy to game through discounts, while banning every personalized discount could erase legitimate benefits.
