# Zed 1.0

- Score: 1454 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47949027) | Link: https://zed.dev/blog/zed-1-0

### TL;DR

Zed has declared version 1.0 after five years and more than a million lines of Rust, positioning its custom GPU-driven GPUI stack as a faster alternative to Electron editors. The cross-platform release combines language support, Git, SSH remoting, debugging, parallel AI agents, edit prediction, and Agent Client Protocol integrations; Zed for Business and CRDT-based DeltaDB collaboration are next. Hacker News users praised its speed, Vim mode, extensibility, and remote workflow, while others questioned its differentiation and scrutinized service terms covering customer-data processing, telemetry, arbitration, and background AI providers.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime users compared Zed favorably with Sublime and JetBrains, citing responsiveness, resilience on large files, and accessible extension work.
- The data license limits processing to service, telemetry, and law — counterpoint: broad lawful-business-purpose telemetry wording remains opaque.
- SSH users valued a unified editor-terminal-agent pane, though missing dynamic port forwarding and past Git integration gaps still matter.
