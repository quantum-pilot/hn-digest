# Kyoto cherry blossoms now bloom earlier than at any point in 1,200 years

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47953275) | Link: https://jivx.com/kyoto-bloom

### TL;DR

Kyoto’s cherry-blossom record combines 838 full-bloom observations spanning 812–2026, drawn from imperial diaries, monastery records, and modern weather data. For most of the millennium, peak bloom fell in early or mid-April; dates drifted later during the Little Ice Age, then moved sharply earlier after about 1900. The 30-year average is now earlier than anywhere else in the series. Peak bloom arrived March 29 in 2026, over two weeks before the pre-modern average, although the earliest single observation remains March 25, 2023.
