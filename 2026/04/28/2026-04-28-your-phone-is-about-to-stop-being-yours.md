# Your phone is about to stop being yours

- Score: 895 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47935853) | Link: https://keepandroidopen.org/en/

### TL;DR

An advocacy campaign says Google’s 2027 Android Developer Verification program will make registration the default prerequisite for distributing apps outside Play, requiring a fee, government ID, signing-key evidence, and app identifiers. It argues that the proposed power-user route—Developer Mode, repeated warnings, restart, and a 24-hour wait—is burdensome and controlled through Play Services. Seventy-one organizations warn of harm to F-Droid, pseudonymous developers, education, and digital sovereignty. Hacker News shared the ownership concern but challenged the page’s absolutes: ADB remains available, while an “advanced flow” preserves unverified installs.

### Comment pulse

- Several commenters switched or considered switching to iOS, reasoning Android’s tolerable rough edges depended on relative software freedom.
- Critics compared phones with PCs — counterpoint: others called PCs historical outliers and phones security-sensitive appliances closer to consoles.
- Commenters corrected the headline’s absolutism: personal ADB installation remains, and Google proposes registered, hobbyist, and advanced-user distribution paths.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The dispute is less about personal compilation than whether ordinary distribution requires Google-mediated identity and friction.
- **Impact:** F-Droid maintainers, students, privacy developers, and users of niche or life-critical apps could bear disproportionate costs.
- **Watch next:** Implementation details, ADB access, advanced-flow durability, regulator responses, F-Droid compatibility, and measurable changes in malware.
