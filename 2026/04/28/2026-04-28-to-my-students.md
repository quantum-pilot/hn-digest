# To my students

- Score: 322 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47928828) | Link: http://ozark.hendrix.edu/~yorgey/forest/00FD/index.xml

### TL;DR

A professor, worried about shrinking entry-level opportunities and an industry optimizing surveillance, extraction, speed, and profit, tells computing students technology is not inevitable and their choices still matter. He urges them to define ethical boundaries early, protect time for deep thought, value clear code and documentation, resist corner-cutting, prioritize people and justice, and act from love rather than fear. Hacker News welcomed the ethics and focus advice but debated academia’s industry credibility, whether craft has moved to product design, and whether refusing LLMs protects principles or repeats earlier tool resistance.
