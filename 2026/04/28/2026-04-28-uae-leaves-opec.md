# UAE Leaves OPEC

- Score: 314 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47934120) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/uae-says-it-quits-opec-opec-statement-2026-04-28/

### TL;DR

The United Arab Emirates quit OPEC and OPEC+, weakening Saudi-led producer coordination during an Iran-war energy shock. Gulf exporters were already struggling to use the Strait of Hormuz, normally carrying about one-fifth of global crude and LNG, amid Iranian threats and attacks. Reuters links Abu Dhabi’s move to frustration that Arab and Gulf partners offered inadequate political and military support after repeated Iranian attacks, while calling it a victory for President Trump’s campaign against OPEC-backed high prices.
