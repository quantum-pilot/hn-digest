# NASA Force

- Score: 209 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47807209) | Link: https://nasaforce.gov/

### TL;DR

NASA Force is a four-day hiring initiative created with the U.S. Office of Personnel Management for early- to mid-career engineers, technologists, and innovators. It offers mission-focused federal term appointments typically lasting one to two years, with possible extensions. Recruits may work on flight systems, lunar infrastructure, VIPER rover operations, deep-space logistics, Orion software, sample curation, lunar resource plants, air-traffic AI, or propulsion. The pitch emphasizes cross-disciplinary systems work, rapid technical development, and contributions moving from concept into real NASA missions.

- Term appointments can import scarce expertise quickly while weakening retention if permanent pathways remain unclear.
