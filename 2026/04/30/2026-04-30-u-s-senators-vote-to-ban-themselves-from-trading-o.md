# U.S. Senators Vote to Ban Themselves from Trading on Prediction Markets

- Score: 264 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47967289) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/senators-vote-to-ban-themselves-from-trading-on-prediction-markets-ae4535dd

### TL;DR

The Senate unanimously adopted an immediately effective rule barring senators, officers, and staff from trading on prediction markets, responding to insider-trading concerns around platforms such as Kalshi and Polymarket. Because it is a Senate rule rather than federal law, enforcement and interpretation rest with the Ethics Committee. Hacker News welcomed the conflict-of-interest boundary but questioned broad wording that might sweep in insurance or event-contingent contracts, noted some platforms already exclude politicians, debated extending it to other influential insiders, and suspected the narrow reform distracts from unresolved congressional stock trading.

### Comment pulse
