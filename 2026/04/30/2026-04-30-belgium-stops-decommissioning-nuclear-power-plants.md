# Belgium stops decommissioning nuclear power plants

- Score: 723 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47961319) | Link: https://dpa-international.com/general-news/urn:newsml:dpa.com:20090101:260430-930-14717/

### TL;DR

Belgium will halt its nuclear decommissioning program and enter exclusive negotiations to acquire ENGIE’s domestic nuclear operations. The potential nationalization covers seven reactors, staff, subsidiaries, assets, liabilities, and dismantling obligations, with a basic agreement targeted for October. Three reactors are already off-grid. The move follows parliament’s reversal of the 2003 phaseout mandate and accompanies plans for new plants, as Belgium seeks less fossil-import dependence amid limited renewable expansion. Discussion broadly favored preserving safe capacity but split over aging reactors, new-build economics, and waste.

### Comment pulse

- Climate-focused supporters called past anti-nuclear policy a major emissions mistake — counterpoint: others blamed fossil interests and rejected nuclear-versus-climate framing.
- Keeping safe operating plants drew more agreement than new construction, whose long schedules and high capital costs compete with solar, batteries, and wind.
- Waste storage debate contrasted Germany’s decades-long search with Finland’s completed repository, exposing political as well as technical barriers.
