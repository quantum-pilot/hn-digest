# Meta in row after workers who saw smart glasses users having sex lose jobs

- Score: 479 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47961838) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y7yvgy0w6o

### TL;DR

Meta ended its AI-training contract with Kenyan outsourcer Sama weeks after annotators said they reviewed intimate footage captured by Meta smart glasses; Sama says 1,108 jobs will go. A workers’ group alleges retaliation, while Meta says Sama failed standards, Sama denies this, and causation remains unresolved. Meta says users consent when sharing content with Meta AI and human review improves performance; UK and Kenyan regulators are investigating. Hacker News condemned opaque human access, questioned upload triggers and meaningful consent, and also faulted users for wearing networked cameras in intimate settings.

### Comment pulse
