# How Mark Klein told the EFF about Room 641A [book excerpt]

- Score: 394 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47965060) | Link: https://thereader.mitpress.mit.edu/the-whistleblower-who-uncovered-the-nsas-big-brother-machine/

### TL;DR

Cindy Cohn recounts retired AT&T technician Mark Klein arriving unannounced at EFF in January 2006 with schematics and firsthand knowledge of NSA surveillance. At AT&T’s Folsom Street facility, a fiber splitter copied internet-backbone traffic: one path continued normally while another entered access-restricted Room 641A. Earlier insiders had only offered unverifiable whispers; Klein supplied court-usable evidence for EFF’s Hepting lawsuit. EFF secured separate counsel for him, filed his declaration under seal, and then faced Justice Department warnings that even unmarked documents might be classified.
