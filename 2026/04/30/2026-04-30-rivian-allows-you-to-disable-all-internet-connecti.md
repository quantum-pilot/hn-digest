# Rivian allows you to disable all internet connectivity

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47967786) | Link: https://rivian.com/support/article/can-i-disable-all-data-collection-from-my-vehicle

### TL;DR

Rivian says owners can stop all vehicle data from leaving by disabling cellular connectivity. Canadian vehicles expose a Data and Privacy toggle; elsewhere owners must schedule service to disable the eSIM. The tradeoff is loss or limitation of navigation, lane keeping, and over-the-air feature, safety, performance, and bug-fix updates, while paid subscriptions continue until separately canceled. Hacker News praised a supported privacy off-switch but questioned whether dealers can install safety recalls offline, whether OTA-only remedies are adequate, and whether disabling driver assistance is technically necessary or punitive.

### Comment pulse

- Owners previously removed telematics hardware for privacy; commenters welcomed official support but rejected trading emergency features for control.
- Recall handling is unclear: traditional dealers can flash modules locally, while commenters were unsure Rivian guarantees a non-OTA remedy.
- Lane keeping may require current geofenced maps — counterpoint: readers questioned whether total disablement reflects design priorities rather than necessity.
