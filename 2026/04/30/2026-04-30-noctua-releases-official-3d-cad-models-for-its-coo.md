# Noctua releases official 3D CAD models for its cooling fans

- Score: 478 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47927627) | Link: https://www.noctua.at/en/3d-cad-models

### TL;DR

Noctua now provides 3D CAD models for many products, aimed at mechanical integration, renderings, animations, and visualization. External and mounting dimensions are intended to be accurate, but impeller geometry and some internal structures are deliberately altered to protect intellectual property. The company therefore warns against performance simulation, 3D printing, reproduction, or commercialization and supplies models without warranties. Hacker News builders welcomed avoiding hand measurement for brackets and cooling assemblies, while commenters debated whether scanning or sectioning could recover true geometry and whether patents or trade-secret rules meaningfully deter cloning.

### Comment pulse

- Official geometry could save hours of iterative measurement even where nominal fan sizes are standardized.
- Altered internals preserve integration utility while withholding an easy manufacturing reference; some saw this as a reasonable compromise.
- Commenters disputed IP barriers and noted existing clones — counterpoint: faithful CAD still lowers copying effort and may reveal design intent.
