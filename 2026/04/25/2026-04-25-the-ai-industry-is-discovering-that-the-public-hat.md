# The AI Industry Is Discovering That the Public Hates It

- Score: 188 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47904568) | Link: https://newrepublic.com/article/209163/ai-industry-discovering-public-backlash

### TL;DR

The New Republic argues public hostility toward AI is accelerating, citing condemned violence, polling gaps, job anxiety, data-center costs, and unproven productivity claims. Stanford found 73% of experts positive about AI’s long-term job effects versus 23% of the public; Gen Z excitement also fell. The author says leaders amplified fear while seeking subsidies and resisting accountability, so white papers cannot restore trust without verifiable action. Hacker News blamed displacement-first messaging, concentrated gains, forced workplace adoption, and weak evidence, while debating AI taxes, UBI feasibility, environmental costs, and creative work.

### Comment pulse

- Leaders marketed replacement and danger to governments and corporations, making hostility predictable when ordinary users saw few benefits.
- Critics separated job loss, environmental load, creative displacement, and scalable fraud because each demands a different policy response.
- Sharing gains through an AI-funded UBI sounded appealing — counterpoint: commenters argued current revenues cannot finance meaningful payments or replace work’s status.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The trust deficit concerns who bears costs and controls deployment more than whether the technology sometimes works.
- **Impact:** Workers and data-center communities may demand enforceable compensation, consent, and accountability before accepting further expansion.
- **Watch next:** Independent productivity audits, utility-rate agreements, local approvals, labor outcomes, regulation campaigns, and representative attitude surveys.
