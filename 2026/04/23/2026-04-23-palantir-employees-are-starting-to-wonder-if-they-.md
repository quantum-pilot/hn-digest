# Palantir employees are starting to wonder if they're the bad guys

- Score: 649 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47878633) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/palantir-employees-are-starting-to-wonder-if-theyre-the-bad-guys/

### TL;DR

Current and former Palantir employees told WIRED that expanding ICE work, possible Maven involvement in strikes that killed Iranian schoolchildren, Alex Karp’s political remarks, and a company manifesto have triggered an internal ethical crisis. Workers pressed leaders about audit-log deletion and malicious customer workflows; one privacy employee said prevention is near-impossible, leaving audits and contract enforcement afterward. Hacker News argued staff knowingly joined a U.S. defense contractor, but distinguished foreign defense work from domestic surveillance, questioned whether sales-focused Slack objections conceal moral concern, and examined how normalization enables self-justification.
