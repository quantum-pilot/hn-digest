# Meta to cut 10% of jobs

- Score: 371 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47879986) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/23/meta-job-cuts-10-percent-8000-employees/

### TL;DR

Meta reportedly plans to eliminate 10% of its workforce—about 8,000 jobs—starting May 20 and cancel hiring for 6,000 open roles. An internal memo framed the cuts as an efficiency drive to offset other investments, after tens of billions spent on metaverse efforts and amid heavy AI spending. Hacker News debated whether overhiring, narrow engineering scopes, management empire-building, or weak interview signals created bloat; others cautioned that outsiders cannot know affected work and noted Meta still employs fewer people than several peers. Some instead read the move as macroeconomic caution.

### Comment pulse

- One camp saw too little meaningful work for too many capable engineers, tracing headcount growth to managers building empires.
- Layoffs will remove people doing real work — counterpoint: critics saw narrow scopes and meeting-heavy roles as evidence of organizational bloat.
- Meta’s standardized interview loop optimizes fairness and comparability, but commenters questioned whether rigid signals predict team success.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Canceling 6,000 requisitions signals a broader capacity reset, not merely a performance-driven reduction among current employees.
- **Impact:** Remaining staff absorb reorganized workloads while candidates and recruiters lose a substantial planned hiring pipeline.
- **Watch next:** May 20’s affected functions and regions, severance terms, future headcount, AI spending, and product delivery.
