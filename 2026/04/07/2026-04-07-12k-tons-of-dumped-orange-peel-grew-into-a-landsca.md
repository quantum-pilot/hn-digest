# 12k Tons of Dumped Orange Peel Grew into a Landscape Nobody Expected (2017)

- Score: 259 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47677142) | Link: https://www.sciencealert.com/how-12-000-tonnes-of-dumped-orange-peel-produced-something-nobody-imagined

### TL;DR

In a 1997 Costa Rican experiment, orange-juice producer Del Oro spread 12,000 tonnes of discarded peel across three hectares of degraded pasture beside a national park. A rival sued, stopping the project in its second year, and the site was forgotten. Fifteen years later, researchers found dense forest: richer soil, more species, and 176% more above-ground biomass than a nearby control. The exact mechanism remains uncertain, but suppressing invasive grass and restoring depleted soil likely helped.
