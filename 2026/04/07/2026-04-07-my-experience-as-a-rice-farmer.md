# My Experience as a Rice Farmer

- Score: 350 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47614528) | Link: https://xd009642.github.io/2026/04/01/My-Experience-as-a-Rice-Farmer.html

### TL;DR

A software engineer recounts helping family cultivate rice in Japan: clearing winter stubble and ditches, ploughing and leveling, diverting river water, repairing a sinkhole, machine-transplanting seedlings, hand-filling gaps, maintaining electric fencing, then draining fields before harvest. Standing water suppresses weeds and pests rather than feeding rice; frogs and salamanders help, while boar breached the fence. The deeper concern is economic: aging farmers, tiny part-time holdings, fixed sales structures, weak mechanization economics, and rural migration threaten continuity. HN welcomed the sensory and communal rewards but warned against romanticizing punishing labor.
