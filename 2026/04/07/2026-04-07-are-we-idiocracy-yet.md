# Are We Idiocracy Yet?

- Score: 590 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47672818) | Link: https://idiocracy.wtf/

### TL;DR

A satirical site assigns reality a 58% “Idiocracy proximity” score by pairing scenes from Mike Judge’s film with modern examples across politics, entertainment, corporations, education, health care, justice, and the environment. Its comparisons range from reality-TV politics and algorithmic outrage to corporate education and declining test scores, but several are loose, selective, or historically backward. The HN discussion treated the film as potent cultural satire while questioning its eugenic premise and the site’s tendency to mistake superficial resemblance for prediction.

### Comment pulse

- Viewers recalled audiences missing the satire, echoing similar misreadings of Starship Troopers.
- Critics favor cultural incentives and undereducation over genetic decline because those causes are plausible and actionable.
- Several matches fail basic chronology or dominance tests — counterpoint: mindless reality entertainment still fits the film’s broader warning.
