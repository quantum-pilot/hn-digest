# Lunar Flyby

- Score: 241 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47676509) | Link: https://www.nasa.gov/gallery/lunar-flyby/

### TL;DR

NASA’s Artemis II flyby gallery documents Orion’s April 6 crewed pass with Earthrise and Earthset, close lunar terrain, crew observations, spacecraft views, and a solar eclipse seen from deep space. One frame shows a nearly 54-minute totality, Earthshine revealing faint lunar features, with Venus visible; others identify Saturn, Mars, Ohm and Vavilov craters. HN readers found the modern imagery unexpectedly stirring after decades of Apollo-era visuals. The posted 1920-pixel images are scaled previews: live GoPro bandwidth is constrained, and full Nikon files are expected after splashdown.
