# Show HN: Brutalist Concrete Laptop Stand (2024)

- Score: 686 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47673360) | Link: https://sam-burns.com/posts/concrete-laptop-stand/

### TL;DR

An enthusiast of brutalist architecture cast a deliberately weathered concrete laptop stand with an overhang, exposed rusted rebar, a fake corroded cable, built-in plant pot, mains socket, and two USB charging ports. The two-pour build uses unevenly mixed and sanded concrete for an aged finish; even the pen holder was rusted and painted with textured “moss.” It is so heavy it reached the desk by trolley. HN largely celebrated its playful, personal craftsmanship despite debating whether the result is genuinely brutalist or practical.

### Comment pulse

- Several commenters praised making a singular object solely for personal satisfaction, even when they disliked its appearance or architectural label.
- Concrete’s weight prompted ideas including fiberglass, foam cores, aircrete, styrofoam, and very thin canoe-style construction.
- Some worried rough concrete would scratch a laptop — counterpoint: the contrast with polished technology is central to the aesthetic.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The project succeeds as expressive furniture: its impractical mass and theatrical decay are features, not optimization failures.
- **Impact:** Accessible fabrication notes may inspire similarly personalized concrete objects while highlighting reinforcement, finishing, and weight tradeoffs.
- **Watch next:** Cracking, rust stains, plant moisture, electrical safety, desk loading, and laptop abrasion will test the sculpture’s daily usability.
