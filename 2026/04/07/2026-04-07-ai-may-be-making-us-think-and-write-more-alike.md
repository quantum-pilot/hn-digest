# AI may be making us think and write more alike

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47673541) | Link: https://dornsife.usc.edu/news/stories/ai-may-be-making-us-think-and-write-more-alike/

### TL;DR

A USC-led opinion paper argues that widespread reliance on a few LLMs may homogenize language, values, and reasoning. Models tend to produce less-varied writing, reflect dominant Western perspectives, and encourage users to accept “good enough” continuations; individuals may generate more ideas with AI while groups produce fewer, less creative ones. The authors call for globally grounded diversity in training and interaction design. HN readers recognized stylistic influence but disputed whether it represents lasting change or a temporary fashion cycle.

### Comment pulse

- Experts often find LLM output average and overly agreeable, while novices value its breadth and accessibility.
- Heavy users notice model phrases entering their speech — counterpoint: stigmatized “LLM-speak” may motivate people to preserve distinctive voices.
- Some fear public knowledge will retreat into trusted private groups to avoid training-data extraction and synthetic noise.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The risk is convergence toward convenient defaults, not literal uniformity.
- **Impact:** Schools, workplaces, and model designers must reward authorship, dissent, and culturally varied reasoning.
- **Watch next:** Longitudinal studies separating novelty effects from durable shifts in language, creativity, and belief.
