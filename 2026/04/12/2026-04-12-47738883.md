# Tell HN: docker pull fails in spain due to football cloudflare block

- Score: 631 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47738883) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47738883

### TL;DR

During a La Liga match, a Spanish developer found Docker pulls failing because access to Docker Hub’s Cloudflare R2 storage endpoint was intercepted under a football-piracy court order, producing a certificate mismatch. The block broke a self-hosted GitLab runner and reportedly affects unrelated Cloudflare-backed services, including safety and smart-home systems. Commenters described this as indiscriminate infrastructure damage, debated whether centralization or internet-dependent devices share blame, and proposed complaints, public documentation, legal pressure, VPNs, or an external pull-through registry as mitigations.

### Comment pulse

- Spaniards reported silent traffic drops and failures involving alarms, doors, and location trackers, broadening concern beyond inconvenienced developers.

- Suggested responses included documenting blackout schedules, filing consumer complaints, pursuing political action, and caching Docker images through a foreign VPS.
