# Apple has removed most of the towns and villages in Lebanon from Apple maps?

- Score: 292 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47742680) | Link: https://maps.apple.com/frame?center=33.723388%2C35.614698&span=1.983925%2C4.004193

### TL;DR

An Apple Maps view of Lebanon appeared to lack labels for many towns and villages, prompting speculation that Apple had recently removed places amid the war. The supplied page itself does not establish when or why labels disappeared. Commenters found reports and a 2020 screenshot suggesting Apple Maps may always have shown little outside major Lebanese cities, even while using OpenStreetMap roads. Others highlighted Maroun al-Ras and politicized naming precedents, but the thread’s strongest conclusion was uncertainty: missing labels appear widespread, including areas not bombed, and may predate the war.
