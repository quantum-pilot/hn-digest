# US appeals court declares 158-year-old home distilling ban unconstitutional

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47736298) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/law/2026/apr/11/appeals-court-ruling-home-distilling-ban-unconstitutional

### TL;DR

The Fifth Circuit upheld a 2024 ruling that the federal ban on distilling at home exceeds Congress’s taxing power. The Reconstruction-era statute, enacted in 1868 to combat liquor-tax evasion, threatened up to five years in prison and a $10,000 fine. Jones reasoned that banning production eliminates the taxable event and therefore reduces rather than raises revenue, unlike manufacturing and labeling regulations. She warned the government’s theory lacked a limiting principle and could justify criminalizing any hard-to-monitor home activity. The decision favors the Hobby Distillers Association and four members.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **View:** This is a structural federalism ruling, not a general declaration that homemade liquor is safe or nationally legal.
- **Impact:** The holding narrows taxation power where a prohibition destroys the very transaction Congress claims it is taxing.
- **Watch next:** Supreme Court review, Sixth Circuit commerce analysis, nationwide enforcement policy, state distilling laws, and post-ruling tax regulation.
