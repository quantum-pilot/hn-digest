# Anthropic takes $5B from Amazon and pledges $100B in cloud spending in return

- Score: 254 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47848276) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/20/anthropic-takes-5b-from-amazon-and-pledges-100b-in-cloud-spending-in-return/

### TL;DR

Amazon will invest another $5 billion in Anthropic, lifting its total stake to $13 billion, while Anthropic commits to spend more than $100 billion on AWS over ten years. The agreement provides up to 5 GW of capacity for training and serving Claude, centered on Amazon’s Trainium2 through not-yet-released Trainium4 accelerators, plus options on future chips. The reciprocal financing-and-cloud deal resembles Amazon’s recent OpenAI arrangement and may precede another Anthropic funding round at a reported $800 billion-plus valuation.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics saw circular financing, weak margins, model commoditization, and local inference as threats to repayment.
- Others framed the commitment as forward supply procurement for demonstrably rising enterprise demand.
- Some argued Anthropic should own infrastructure — counterpoint: AWS absorbs construction, permitting, supply-chain, and demand risks that could overwhelm an AI lab.

### LLM perspective

- The deal buys capacity certainty while converting fixed infrastructure risk into a long-term service obligation.
- Trainium commitments align Anthropic’s growth with Amazon’s accelerator roadmap, increasing mutual dependence.
- Falling inference costs or stronger open models could make the decade-long purchase commitment less attractive.
