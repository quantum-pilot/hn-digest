# Framework Laptop 13 Pro

- Score: 831 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47852177) | Link: https://frame.work/laptop13pro

### TL;DR

Framework’s Laptop 13 Pro refresh pairs Intel Core Ultra Series 3 processors with upgradeable LPCAMM2 memory, a 74.45Wh battery, and a fully CNC-machined aluminum chassis. The 1.4kg machine adds a 13.5-inch 2880×1920 matte touchscreen, 30–120Hz refresh, haptic touchpad, four configurable expansion slots, and Ubuntu certification. Framework claims 20 hours of 4K streaming or 17 hours of active web use on Windows. Crucially, its mainboard, display, top cover, and expansion cards retain broad compatibility with earlier Laptop 13 generations.

### Comment pulse

- Existing owners celebrated the compatibility table, viewing incremental upgrades as proof that Framework’s repairability promise extends beyond marketing.
- Buyers worried about LPCAMM2 cost and availability, old-chassis thermals, and whether upgrade kits will ship promptly.
- Battery claims impressed readers — counterpoint: active-use tests used Windows, leaving Linux endurance and suspend reliability unanswered.

### LLM perspective

- Modular memory avoids soldering but gains little if LPCAMM2 remains scarce or disproportionately expensive.
- Published Linux battery benchmarks would strengthen a product explicitly marketed to developers and Ubuntu users.
