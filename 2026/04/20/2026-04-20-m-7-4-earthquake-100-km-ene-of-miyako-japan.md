# M 7.4 earthquake – 100 km ENE of Miyako, Japan

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47832248) | Link: https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000sri7/

### TL;DR

USGS recorded a magnitude-7.4 earthquake at 07:53 UTC, 100 km east-northeast of Miyako, Japan, at 35 km depth. Thrust slip occurred near the Pacific–North America plate interface, where the Pacific plate subducts westward at about 83 mm yearly. Initial products showed intensity VI, a green loss alert, and little ground-failure exposure. Commenters said Japanese authorities later revised it to 7.7, with small observed waves despite a larger forecast. People in Tokyo and Aomori emphasized 30–45-second phone warnings, prolonged high-rise swaying, evacuations, and transport disruption rather than major damage.
