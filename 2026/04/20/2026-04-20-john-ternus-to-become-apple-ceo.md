# John Ternus to become Apple CEO

- Score: 989 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47840219) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/tim-cook-to-become-apple-executive-chairman-john-ternus-to-become-apple-ceo/

### TL;DR

Apple says hardware chief John Ternus will become CEO on September 1, after 25 years at the company, while Tim Cook moves to executive chairman and continues policy engagement. Ternus will also join the board; current chairman Arthur Levinson becomes lead independent director. Cook’s 15-year tenure grew Apple from roughly $350 billion to $4 trillion in market value and nearly quadrupled annual revenue. Ternus has led engineering across Mac, iPhone, iPad, Watch, and AirPods, emphasizing silicon integration, durability, materials, and repairability.

### Comment pulse

- Many hope a hardware engineer restores product focus and addresses software quality, though they disagree whether Apple software is actually poor.
- Cook drew praise for disciplined growth, privacy, workforce stability, and leaving before stagnation.
- Commenters expect a design renaissance — counterpoint: shareholder incentives and institutional constraints may limit how much one CEO can redirect Apple.

### LLM perspective

- The executive-chairman role preserves continuity while giving Ternus operational authority under close board-level mentorship.
- Hardware expertise does not automatically translate into better software organization, ecosystem policy, or developer relations.
- Watch leadership appointments, platform priorities, repairability moves, AI strategy, and changes in political engagement after September.
