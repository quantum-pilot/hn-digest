# NSA is using Anthropic's Mythos despite blacklist

- Score: 433 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47832222) | Link: https://www.axios.com/2026/04/19/nsa-anthropic-mythos-pentagon

### TL;DR

Axios reports, citing two sources, that the NSA uses Anthropic’s restricted Mythos Preview while the Defense Department calls Anthropic a supply-chain risk and litigates its cutoff. One source said use extends elsewhere in Defense; the NSA’s purpose is unknown, though other organizations scan systems for vulnerabilities. Anthropic limits access to 40 organizations because of offensive cyber capability. The contradiction follows talks where Defense demanded all lawful uses while Anthropic excluded mass domestic surveillance and autonomous weapons. Hacker News read this as political theater, strategic scarcity, or demand for the tool.
