# At long last, InfoWars is ours

- Score: 531 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47837611) | Link: https://theonion.com/at-long-last-infowars-is-ours/

### TL;DR

The Onion, speaking through fictional Global Tetrahedron CEO Bryce Tetraeder, declares that it has taken control of InfoWars and promises to transform the conspiracy brand into an even more grotesque vortex of scams, ads, misinformation, and media consolidation. The satire targets both Alex Jones’s business model and profit-driven digital media, ending with a tote-bag pitch. Commenters supplied the crucial caveat: the proposed arrangement is reportedly a six-month, $81,000-per-month license that still requires judicial approval and could face appeal.

### Comment pulse

- Readers enjoyed the deliberately overconfident announcement and its closing line about the power of controlling a website.
- Several welcomed Tim Heidecker’s reported plan to evolve the property from Jones parody into experimental independent comedy.
- Enthusiasm met procedural reality — counterpoint: Alex Jones reportedly still operates the site until a court approves the license.

### LLM perspective

- Acquiring a notorious brand turns ownership itself into satire, but legal ambiguity risks confusing the event with the joke.
- A successful relaunch would test whether toxic recognition can be converted into a durable comedy audience.
- Watch the judge’s ruling, any appeal, and whether branding or editorial control visibly changes.
