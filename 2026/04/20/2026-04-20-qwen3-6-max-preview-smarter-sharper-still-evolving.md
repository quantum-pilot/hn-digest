# Qwen3.6-Max-Preview: Smarter, Sharper, Still Evolving

- Score: 509 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47834565) | Link: https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.6-max-preview

### TL;DR

Alibaba’s Qwen team unveiled Qwen3.6-Max-Preview, a proprietary hosted model positioned above Qwen3.6-Plus in agentic coding, knowledge, and instruction following. Reported gains include SkillsBench +9.9, SciCode +6.3, NL2Repo +5.0, and Terminal-Bench 2.0 +3.8; the team claims first place on six coding benchmarks. Alibaba Cloud will expose OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs, including preserved reasoning across turns for agents. Hacker News emphasized that harness quality and workload fit can outweigh small benchmark gaps, compared Kimi K2.6’s lower stated pricing, and worried that the flagship is cloud-only while Qwen’s open-weight models drive local adoption.

### Comment pulse

- A 4090 owner found local agents slower and rougher than Claude Code, showing model weights alone do not reproduce a polished coding system.
- Users reported Chinese models winning particular Rust or documentation tasks — counterpoint: tool calling, documents, spreadsheets, and IDE integrations still drive vendor lock-in.
- Commenters framed open weights as Qwen’s strategic value; a proprietary Max preview risks becoming marketing for cloud inference rather than user-owned compute.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Self-reported benchmarks establish direction, not product superiority; independent agent evaluations and full cost-latency data matter more.
- **Impact:** Hosted users gain a frontier option, while local developers must choose open models and accept capability or tooling gaps.
- **Watch next:** API availability, independent benchmarks, pricing, context limits, open-weight follow-ups, harness compatibility, and performance on non-Python repositories.
