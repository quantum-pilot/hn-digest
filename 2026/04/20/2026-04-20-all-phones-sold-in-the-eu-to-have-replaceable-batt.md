# All phones sold in the EU to have replaceable batteries from 2027

- Score: 902 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47834195) | Link: https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2026/04/20/eu-to-force-replaceable-batteries-in-phones-and-tablets-from-2027/

### TL;DR

From February 18, 2027, portable-device batteries in phones and tablets sold in the EU must be user-removable with commercially available tools; any specialized tool must ship free. Replacement batteries must remain available for five years after the model’s final sale. The regulation joins existing USB-C, durability, and five-year software-update requirements, aiming to extend device life, reduce replacement costs, and curb electronic waste. Officials estimate potential consumer savings of €20 billion by 2030 amid low recycling rates for the bloc’s roughly five million tonnes of annual e-waste.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters welcomed quick swaps, cheaper repairs, longer device life, and freedom from carrying wired power banks.

- Some said current repair shops suffice — counterpoint: paid skilled disassembly is not equivalent to owner replacement, especially for a routine wear item.
