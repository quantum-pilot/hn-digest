# I bought Friendster for $30k – Here's what I'm doing with it

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47914165) | Link: https://ca98am79.medium.com/i-bought-friendster-for-30k-heres-what-i-m-doing-with-it-d5e8ddb3991d

### TL;DR

Mike Carson acquired friendster.com after its previous owner paid $7,456 at auction; Carson paid $20,000 in Bitcoin plus an ad-generating domain, then secured the expiring trademarks. A social-network reboot drew little enthusiasm, so the iOS app now lets anyone join but requires tapping phones to connect. Planned features expose friends-of-friends and soften links after a year without another tap. Hacker News liked proximity-based verification and lightweight onboarding but called forced reconnection a chore, questioned erasing sentimental or rolodex-like ties, and noted the traded domain may make the $30k price misleading.
