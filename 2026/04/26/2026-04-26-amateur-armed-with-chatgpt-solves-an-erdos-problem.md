# Amateur armed with ChatGPT solves an Erdős problem

- Score: 735 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47903126) | Link: https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/amateur-armed-with-chatgpt-vibe-maths-a-60-year-old-problem/

### TL;DR

Liam Price, a 23-year-old without advanced mathematics training, prompted GPT-5.4 Pro to tackle a decades-old Erdős conjecture about the minimum score of primitive integer sets. The model applied a known formula from a neighboring area in a way experts had missed. Its raw proof was difficult to parse, but Jared Lichtman and Terence Tao extracted and shortened the apparently valid argument, with possible broader uses still uncertain. HN discussion framed the result as promising human-model collaboration, not a clean autonomous proof.
