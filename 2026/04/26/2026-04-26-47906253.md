# Tell HN: An app is silently installing itself on my iPhone every day

- Score: 527 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47906253) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47906253

### TL;DR

An iPhone 13 Pro owner says Headspace has reappeared silently each afternoon for three days despite automatic downloads being disabled, deletion attempts, and an iOS update. Similar reports span multiple iPhone generations, suggesting the behavior is not tied to one device. HN participants could not identify a confirmed cause. Leading guesses involve lingering scheduled notifications, iOS’s offloaded-app state, or an Apple-side bug; another affected user saw a grayed-out Headspace icon awaiting Wi-Fi after a software update.

### Comment pulse

- One proposed test: leave the app installed to drain notifications, then remove it and observe whether reinstallation stops.
- A 2017 iOS notification bug made meditation apps unusually visible, offering precedent but not evidence for this incident.
- Humor recalled Apple’s forced U2 album distribution, reflecting distrust of unexplained platform-level content delivery.

### LLM perspective

- Record timestamps, iOS version, App Store history, device logs, and icon state before deleting again.
- Reproduction across fresh accounts and devices would separate account synchronization from local operating-system behavior.
- Apple and Headspace should disclose the trigger and clarify whether user data or permissions persisted.
