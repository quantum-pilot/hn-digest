# Sometimes powerful people just do dumb shit

- Score: 257 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47760750) | Link: https://www.joanwestenberg.com/sometimes-powerful-people-just-do-dumb-shit/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that observers routinely mistake powerful people’s mistakes for hidden strategy, an unfalsifiable “4D chess” story that flatters believers and shields leaders from accountability. It cites Napoleon’s Russia campaign, Musk’s Twitter purchase, Trump’s behavior, and OpenAI’s reported TBPN acquisition as examples of arrogance, ego, weak feedback, and luck being recast as genius. Commenters broadly accepted that elites remain fallible, but challenged the essay’s simplified Napoleon history and urged case-by-case analysis: apparently foolish actions may reflect unfamiliar incentives, optionality, or incomplete public information.

### Comment pulse

- Critics say Napoleon had documented goals and logistics; disease and Russian retreat, not merely winter arrogance, shaped the catastrophe.
- Powerful actors may exploit weak enforcement, making conduct irrational for ordinary people rational under elite incentives.
