# Spain to expand internet blocks to tennis, golf, movies broadcasting times

- Score: 397 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47768195) | Link: https://bandaancha.eu/articulos/telefonica-consigue-bloqueos-ips-11731

### TL;DR

A Barcelona commercial court has authorized Telefónica Audiovisual Digital to send dynamic blocklists of domains, URLs, and IP addresses associated with unauthorized broadcasts to Spain’s major and smaller ISPs. The measure expands beyond LaLiga matches to Champions League, tennis, golf, and entertainment programming. Because one shared IP can host thousands of unrelated sites, prior football blocks have disrupted legitimate services, an effect Spain’s government has acknowledged. Commenters fear broader censorship, VPN restrictions, and recurring outages, while debating whether expensive, bundled legal sports packages drive piracy.
