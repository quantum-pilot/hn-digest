# WiiFin – Jellyfin Client for Nintendo Wii

- Score: 239 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47759341) | Link: https://github.com/fabienmillet/WiiFin

### TL;DR

WiiFin is an experimental GPLv3 Jellyfin client for Nintendo Wii and vWii, built with C++, GRRLIB, MPlayer CE, and mbedTLS. It supports password or QuickConnect authentication, multiple profiles, media browsing, music, TV navigation, resume tracking, HTTPS, Wiimote input, and server-transcoded video with subtitles and playback controls. Real hardware remains rough: every video requires server-side transcoding, audio is stereo-only, and subtitles must be burned in. Commenters treated it as evidence of Jellyfin’s expanding client ecosystem, while noting broader reliability and security questions.
