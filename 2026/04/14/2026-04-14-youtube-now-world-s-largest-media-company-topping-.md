# YouTube now world's largest media company, topping Disney

- Score: 270 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47705278) | Link: https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/digital/youtube-worlds-largest-media-company-2025-tops-disney-1236525130/

### TL;DR

MoffettNathanson estimates YouTube generated $62 billion in 2025, surpassing Disney’s $60.9 billion media business when Disney Experiences is excluded, and values the platform at $500–560 billion. More than $40 billion came from ads; Premium, Music, NFL Sunday Ticket, and roughly 10 million YouTube TV subscribers add a large subscription engine. YouTube has paid creators and media partners over $100 billion, while AI tools may accelerate production. Commenters praised its unmatched archive and learning value but worried about monopoly power, rising prices, weak search, recommendation quality, privacy, and disappearing content.
