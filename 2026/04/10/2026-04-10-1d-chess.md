# 1D Chess

- Score: 619 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47719740) | Link: https://rowan441.github.io/1dchess/chess.html

### TL;DR

Rowan Monk implements Martin Gardner’s 1980 one-dimensional chess on an eight-square line. Each side has a king, knight, and rook: kings move one square, knights jump exactly two, and rooks slide along the line. Checkmate wins; stalemate, threefold repetition, and bare kings draw. White plays an AI, and the page reveals a forced mating line under optimal play. Commenters explored variants with longer boards or castling, compared other dimension-reduced games such as one-dimensional Go and Pac-Man, and found the small ruleset surprisingly tricky until learning the published sequence.
