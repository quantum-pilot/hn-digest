# A new trick brings stability to quantum operations

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47713495) | Link: https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2026/04/a-new-trick-brings-stability-to-quantum-operations.html

### TL;DR

ETH Zurich researchers built a neutral-atom swap gate whose operation depends on a geometric phase rather than timing-sensitive dynamical phases. Ultracold potassium fermions in an optical lattice overlap wavefunctions; exchange symmetry then swaps paired spin states at 99.91% fidelity. They applied the same gate concurrently across 17,000 atom pairs and also demonstrated entangling half-swaps. Commenters emphasized this is robust parallel gate physics, not a programmable 17,000-qubit computer: pairs do not interact and individual addressing still requires integration with a quantum gas microscope.
