# Microsoft suspends dev accounts for high-profile open source projects

- Score: 359 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47716412) | Link: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-suspends-dev-accounts-for-high-profile-open-source-projects/

### TL;DR

Microsoft automatically suspended Windows Hardware Program accounts used by WireGuard, VeraCrypt, Windscribe, and MemTest86, preventing signed Windows releases and potentially urgent security patches. Maintainers reported no clear warning, explanation, human support, or fast appeal; WireGuard faced a nominal 60-day process. Microsoft said projects missed mandatory identity verification announced by emails and portal notices since October 2025, then began helping after press coverage and promised communication review. The incident exposes a supply-chain bottleneck: a platform’s centralized signing gate can silently disable widely used open-source software, with public visibility functioning as escalation.
