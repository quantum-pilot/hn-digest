# Industrial design files for Keychron keyboards and mice

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47720419) | Link: https://github.com/Keychron/Keychron-Keyboards-Hardware-Design

### TL;DR

Keychron has published 686-plus production design files covering 88 keyboard and mouse models, including cases, plates, stabilizers, encoders, keycaps, shells, and full assemblies in STEP, DXF, DWG, and PDF formats. The source-available repository includes CAD guidance, printing instructions, generated inventory, and contribution rules. Personal study, modification, and commercial compatible accessories are permitted; manufacturing substantially similar Keychron keyboards or mice and using its trademarks are not. The release gives hobbyists and engineers real tolerances and packaging decisions to study, while enabling custom plates, cases, materials, and add-ons without reverse engineering.
