# France to ditch Windows for Linux to reduce reliance on US tech

- Score: 434 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47719486) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/10/france-to-ditch-windows-for-linux-to-reduce-reliance-on-us-tech/

### TL;DR

France plans to move some government computers from Windows to Linux, beginning with DINUM, its digital agency, as part of a wider effort to reduce dependence on US technology and regain control of state data and infrastructure. No distribution, scale, or migration timetable was announced. The government has separately begun replacing Teams with Jitsi-based Visio and plans to move its health-data platform to a trusted provider by year-end. Commenters called the headline overstated, but mandatory ministry plans by fall create real, politically reversible pressure.

### Comment pulse

- Linux desktop usability has improved, but government adoption still needs standardized management, hardware integration, support, and choices among competing distributions and interfaces.
- National migrations often stall after small pilots — counterpoint: sanctions and sovereignty concerns provide stronger motivation than ordinary licensing savings.
- Browser-based work reduces native Windows dependencies, yet entrenched Microsoft CRM, identity, document management, and cloud contracts make institutional change generational.
