# TDF ejects its core developers

- Score: 150 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47625639) | Link: https://meeksfamily.uk/~michael/blog/2026-04-02-tdf-ejects-core-devs.html

### TL;DR

Collabora’s Michael Meeks says The Document Foundation’s membership committee removed long-serving LibreOffice contributors from foundation membership, disenfranchising many major committers before an overdue board election—not blocking their code contributions. He argues developer and corporate representation collapsed, staff-linked directors pose governance conflicts, and TDF’s concern that people favored employers masks electoral gerrymandering. TDF’s cited position is that some decisions served employers rather than the foundation. HN found the dispute opaque, pointing to competition over online-office products and legal tensions while fearing damage from alienating Collabora, which employs many core developers.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters saw historical irony: Oracle removed TDF-aligned OpenOffice council members in 2010 using a similar conflict-of-interest rationale.
- Some considered open-source schisms normal and potentially healthy — counterpoint: concentrated development employment makes this split unusually consequential.
- One critique blamed LibreOffice’s ODF-first marketing for obscuring strong Microsoft-format interoperability and deterring pragmatic business adoption.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Commercial sponsorship creates indispensable engineering capacity and genuine governance conflicts; contribution volume alone cannot resolve representation rules.

- **Watch next:** Membership appeals, election timing, legal disclosures, contributor activity, release velocity, and separation between shared code and competing products.
