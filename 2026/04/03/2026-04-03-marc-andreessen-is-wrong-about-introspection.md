# Marc Andreessen is wrong about introspection

- Score: 366 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47627056) | Link: https://www.joanwestenberg.com/marc-andreessen-is-wrong-about-introspection/

### TL;DR

The essay rebuts Marc Andreessen’s reported claim that introspection arose around Freud’s era, pointing to Socrates, Stoicism, Augustine, Mencius, Shakespeare, diaries, and letters as much older evidence of self-examination. Its larger argument is that a philosophy of relentless action and measurable abundance cannot define human flourishing without asking what people value internally. Otherwise, GDP, clicks, purchases, or engagement become inadequate substitutes for meaning, inviting Goodhart’s Law. HN mostly broadened the critique to society’s habit of treating business success as authority outside its domain.
