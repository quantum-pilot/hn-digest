# F-15E jet shot down over Iran

- Score: 339 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47628326) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/apr/03/us-fighter-jet-confirmed-shot-down-over-iran

### TL;DR

The Guardian reports, citing unnamed U.S. officials and wreckage analysis, that an F-15E was shot down over Iran after nearly five weeks of war. One of its two crew members had been rescued late Friday, while the other remained missing during a risky search near southwest Iran. Iranian media first called the wreckage an F-35 and later claimed a pilot was captured, neither claim verified. A separate A-10 crashed near the Strait of Hormuz; its pilot was rescued, but the cause remained unclear.
