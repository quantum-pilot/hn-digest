# Oracle Files H-1B Visa Petitions Amid Mass Layoffs

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47631732) | Link: https://nationaltoday.com/us/tx/austin/news/2026/04/03/oracle-files-thousands-of-h-1b-visa-petitions-amid-mass-layoffs/

### TL;DR

Oracle America filed 2,690 H-1B petitions in FY2025 and 436 so far in FY2026, while the company reportedly began broad layoffs and offered no public explanation. The article frames the overlap as evidence that visa hiring may displace cheaper U.S. labor, but supplies no role-by-role or timing comparison. HN strongly challenged that inference: commenters said major cuts were in India, petitions predated the latest layoffs, and specialized roles are not interchangeable. Others described token job postings and argued layoffs should temporarily restrict visa use, as PERM filings can.

### Comment pulse

- Several alleged employers satisfy recruitment rules through obscure postings — counterpoint: others demanded evidence rather than anecdotes.
- Commenters questioned a purported $100,000 fee; one reply said it covered only new petitions using consular processing and might face judicial review.
- Oracle’s poor working conditions may deter local applicants, some argued, creating circular dependence on workers whose visas bind them more closely.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Aggregate petition and layoff totals cannot establish worker replacement without matching dates, locations, roles, wages, and outcomes.
- **Impact:** Missing workforce detail converts a legitimate oversight question into a proxy battle over immigration.
- **Watch next:** Oracle disclosures, approved-versus-filed counts, layoff geography, occupational overlap, compensation, and the fee order’s actual applicability.
