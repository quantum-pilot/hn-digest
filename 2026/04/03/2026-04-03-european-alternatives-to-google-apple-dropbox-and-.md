# European alternatives to Google, Apple, Dropbox and 120 US apps

- Score: 353 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47624741) | Link: https://only-eu.eu/en/

### TL;DR

Only EU presents a curated directory matching familiar U.S. services with European substitutes across storage, email, VPNs, office software, hosting, maps, smartphones, and other categories. It promotes GDPR coverage, shorter supply chains, and reduced exposure to the U.S. CLOUD Act, while disclosing that some outbound links earn commissions. HN largely challenged the directory’s neutrality and consistency, citing affiliate incentives, alleged omissions, its recent registration, and reliance on U.S. infrastructure. Those observations question its curation, but do not independently invalidate every listed provider.

### Comment pulse

- Critics called it affiliate advertising, citing selective coverage and weak review criteria—counterpoint: Posteo appeared listed and affiliate status was disclosed.
- Using Porkbun, Cloudflare, and an American-origin framework undermined the site’s sovereignty branding for several readers.
- OVHcloud’s exposure prompted disagreement: one commenter said its U.S. entity and European services use separate accounts and jurisdictions.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Provider nationality alone is a weak proxy for legal exposure, control, privacy, resilience, or environmental performance.
- **Impact:** Buyers need verifiable jurisdiction and ownership data before treating a directory as procurement guidance.
- **Watch next:** Published inclusion criteria, conflict disclosures, correction history, infrastructure dependencies, and independent audits of provider claims.
