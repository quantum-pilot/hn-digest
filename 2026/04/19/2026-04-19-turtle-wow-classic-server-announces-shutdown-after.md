# Turtle WoW classic server announces shutdown after Blizzard wins injunction

- Score: 106 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47825160) | Link: https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/turtle-wow-classic-server-announces-shutdown-after-blizzard-wins-injunction/

### TL;DR

World of Warcraft private server Turtle WoW will shut down May 14 after Blizzard obtained an injunction and the parties reached a settlement. Its forums and social channels will close October 16, while servers jump to the final patch so players can see remaining raids. Across eight years, the project extended vanilla WoW with new zones, races, dungeons, and raids—an unofficial “Classic Plus” model. Commenters largely accept Blizzard’s copyright claim while mourning technically ambitious, community-led work that many found more inventive than Blizzard’s own recent offerings.

### Comment pulse

- Former operators stressed that private servers require protocol reverse-engineering, content systems, scaling, hosting, and ongoing development.
- Some distinguish donations covering infrastructure from profit, while acknowledging courts may view recurring payments as commercial revenue.
- Blizzard can protect its IP — counterpoint: licensing, acquisition, or official recognition could preserve innovation and community goodwill.
