# Notion leaks email addresses of all editors of any public page

- Score: 310 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47824945) | Link: https://twitter.com/weezerOSINT/status/2045849358462222720

### TL;DR

A public Notion page can expose every contributor’s full name, email address, and profile photo through an unauthenticated metadata request. The reporter says the behavior was disclosed in 2022 and remained available in 2026, potentially revealing staff details behind public company wikis. Notion’s documentation acknowledges that published-page metadata may include contributor identities. A company representative said users receive a publication warning, conceded that disclosure is inadequate, and said Notion is considering removing personal data from public endpoints or substituting proxy addresses.

### Comment pulse

- Users said publication warnings describe page content becoming public, not every contributor’s contact details.
- Critics rejected “documented behavior” as meaningful consent and questioned why a known issue persisted for four years.
- Metadata can preserve contributor attribution — counterpoint: public credit does not require exposing reusable email addresses.

### LLM perspective

- Publication dialogs should enumerate secondary data exposure in plain language.
- Existing pages require retroactive remediation; prospective consent alone leaves prior disclosures accessible.
- Security programs lose credibility when accepted reports become documentation rather than resolved risk.
