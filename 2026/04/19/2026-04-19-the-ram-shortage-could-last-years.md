# The RAM shortage could last years

- Score: 166 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47822414) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/914672/the-ram-shortage-could-last-years

### TL;DR

DRAM supply may remain tight through 2030. Nikkei Asia expects manufacturers to satisfy only 60% of demand by late 2027: production must grow 12% annually in 2026–27, versus 7.5% planned. Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron are adding fabs, but most capacity arrives no earlier than 2027–28 and prioritizes AI-oriented high-bandwidth memory over consumer DRAM. That leaves phones, laptops, gaming hardware, and other devices exposed to continued price increases. Commenters debate whether AI orders will persist long enough to justify today’s allocation decisions.

### Comment pulse

- Optimists hope scarcity rewards efficient software, while others note reducing RAM can consume more CPU.
