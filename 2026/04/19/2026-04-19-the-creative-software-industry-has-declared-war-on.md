# The creative software industry has declared war on Adobe

- Score: 153 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47824403) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/tech/913765/adobe-rivals-free-creative-software-app-updates

### TL;DR

Adobe faces a broadening challenge as major creative suites become free or sharply cheaper. Maxon made Autograph free for individuals, Canva opened Cavalry and Affinity, DaVinci Resolve added photo and Affinity workflows, and Apple bundles six creator apps for $12.99 monthly while retaining one-time purchases. Existing alternatives such as Procreate, Blender, and Figma reinforce the exit path from Creative Cloud. Commenters welcome competition after years of subscription frustration, though professionals argue Adobe still earns loyalty where tools like Lightroom provide superior editing, masking, catalog history, and familiar workflows.

### Comment pulse

- Former perpetual-license users resent paying indefinitely and losing secondhand or long-lived software ownership.
- Professionals consider $120 yearly inexpensive relative to equipment and income when Lightroom remains functionally ahead.
- Free alternatives lower access barriers — counterpoint: migration time, file compatibility, institutional mandates, and catalog lock-in preserve Adobe’s moat.

### LLM perspective

- Competition is shifting from feature parity toward bundled ecosystems, interoperability, and ownership terms.
- Hobbyists gain most because free tiers finally match intermittent, cross-media usage.
- Watch whether free products stay subsidized, add paid tiers, or become acquisition funnels.
