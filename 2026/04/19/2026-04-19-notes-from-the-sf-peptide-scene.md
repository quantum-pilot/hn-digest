# Notes from the SF peptide scene

- Score: 114 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47824681) | Link: https://12gramsofcarbon.com/p/notes-from-the-sf-peptide-scene

### TL;DR

After two weeks in San Francisco, the author uses a peptide-themed party as the entry point to a comic cultural survey. Gray-market injectable drugs, elaborate theme parties, AI-saturated founders, inscrutable billboards, shifting politics, autonomous driving, group houses, and multi-agent overbuilding all illustrate a city where extreme sincerity rapidly turns niche ideas into identities and businesses. The author contrasts that trend-driven bubble with New York’s irony and results orientation, while arguing the same earnestness that produces excess also enables ambitious startups and social movements.
