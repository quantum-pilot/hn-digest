# Airline worker arrested after sharing photos of bomb damage in WhatsApp group

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47824068) | Link: https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/dubai-police-spied-private-whatsapp-5HjdXwr_2/

### TL;DR

Dubai police arrested an airline employee after he shared an image of strike damage only in a closed WhatsApp group with colleagues. Authorities said electronic monitoring revealed the material, identified his account, summoned him to a meeting, and detained him on charges of publishing information harmful to state interests, carrying up to two years. An advocacy group says the case indicates surveillance of private exchanges; however, the report does not establish whether access came from network monitoring, device spyware such as Pegasus, or someone inside the chat.
