# Archive of BYTE magazine, starting with issue #1 in 1975

- Score: 519 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47806096) | Link: https://archive.org/details/byte-magazine-1975-09

### TL;DR

The Internet Archive hosts BYTE magazine’s inaugural September 1975 issue, “The World’s Greatest Toy,” as a 300-dpi scan plus PDF, EPUB, full text, OCR, DAISY, JP2, and torrent downloads. Its contents capture early personal computing at component level: choosing microprocessors, recycling integrated circuits, decoding keyboards, building serial interfaces, writing an assembler, reviewing a microcomputer kit, and programming Life. The wider BYTE collection preserves a magazine that readers remember as a monthly technical education, industry catalog, and serendipitous gateway into computing.
