# Swiss authorities want to reduce dependency on Microsoft

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47827383) | Link: https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/swiss-authorities-want-to-reduce-dependency-on-microsoft/91280532

### TL;DR

Switzerland’s federal administration says it will reduce dependence on Microsoft gradually and over the long term, even after deploying Microsoft 365 to roughly 54,000 workstations. A feasibility study found an open-source replacement possible, and officials are examining Germany’s public-sector efforts, including Schleswig-Holstein’s migration. The shift is driven by digital-sovereignty, cost, and data-security concerns: Swiss federal and cantonal bodies reportedly spent more than CHF1.1 billion on Microsoft licenses over a decade, while the U.S. Cloud Act may expose data held by American providers regardless of server location.
