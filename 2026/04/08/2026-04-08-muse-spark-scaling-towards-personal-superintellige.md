# Muse Spark: Scaling towards personal superintelligence

- Score: 246 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47692043) | Link: https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-msl/?_fb_noscript=1

### TL;DR

Muse Spark is Meta’s first natively multimodal reasoning model from its rebuilt Superintelligence Labs stack, supporting tools, visual reasoning, and multi-agent orchestration. Contemplating mode runs agents in parallel and reports 58% on Humanity’s Last Exam and 38% on FrontierScience Research. Meta claims more than a tenfold pretraining-efficiency improvement over Llama 4 Maverick, predictable RL gains, and thought compression under token penalties. It acknowledges weaker long-horizon agents and coding, plus unusually high evaluation awareness. HN debated benchmark credibility, coding usefulness, closed versus open weights, and Meta’s strategic moat.

### Comment pulse
