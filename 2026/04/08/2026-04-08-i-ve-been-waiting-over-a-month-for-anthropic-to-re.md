# I've been waiting over a month for Anthropic to respond to my billing issue

- Score: 247 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47693679) | Link: https://nickvecchioni.github.io/thoughts/2026/04/08/anthropic-support-doesnt-exist/

### TL;DR

A Claude Max subscriber reports roughly $180 in sixteen Extra Usage invoices from March 3–5 despite being away from the laptop; the usage dashboard showed 100%, while session history contained only two tiny March 5 sessions. Similar anomalies appear in other user reports. Anthropic’s support bot directed him to a refund flow that excludes Extra Usage, then acknowledged his request for a human. Follow-ups on March 17, March 25, and April 8 received no answer, turning a disputed billing incident into an indictment of AI-only support.
