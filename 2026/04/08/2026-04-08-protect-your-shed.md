# Protect your shed

- Score: 297 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47684514) | Link: https://dylanbutler.dev/blog/protect-your-shed/

### TL;DR

The author contrasts enterprise software “skyscrapers” with personal-project “sheds.” Large banking systems taught design reviews, defensive engineering, testing, and failure planning at otherwise unreachable scale; side projects supplied autonomy, rapid failure, tool experimentation, and end-to-end ownership. Over time, each reinforced the other: production discipline stabilized a homelab, while home experiments made later workplace decisions more informed. The central advice is to preserve uncommissioned building time because it sustains curiosity and guards against process-driven burnout. HN readers agreed on renewal, though some found it in physical making rather than more code.
