# Who is Satoshi Nakamoto? My quest to unmask Bitcoin's creator

- Score: 267 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47685320) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/04/08/business/bitcoin-satoshi-nakamoto-identity-adam-back.html

### TL;DR

John Carreyrou argues Adam Back is Bitcoin creator Satoshi Nakamoto, assembling circumstantial evidence from Back’s British Cypherpunk background, Hashcash, 1997–99 proposals anticipating Bitcoin’s architecture, rare phrases and hyphenation errors, and an activity gap overlapping Satoshi’s public years. A broad mailing-list analysis ranked Back highest on shared technical vocabulary and found 67 of 325 exact hyphenation matches, but conventional stylometry was inconclusive. Back denied the claim and withheld requested metadata; no early private-key signature exists. HN split over meaningful convergence versus confirmation bias, p-hacking, and subjective body-language interpretation.
