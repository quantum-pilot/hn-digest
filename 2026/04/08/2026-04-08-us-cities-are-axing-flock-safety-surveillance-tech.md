# US cities are axing Flock Safety surveillance technology

- Score: 610 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47689237) | Link: https://www.cnet.com/home/security/when-flock-comes-to-town-why-cities-are-axing-the-controversial-surveillance-technology/

### TL;DR

More than 45 cities have canceled Flock Safety contracts amid concerns over automated license-plate readers, data sharing, and misuse. Cameras record every passing vehicle, search descriptions beyond plates, and can reconstruct movement; newer tools add searches for clothing and 60-mph drones launched for 911 calls or gunfire. Flock encrypts and deletes its cloud copy after 30 days, but customers control access, enabling local agencies to share with ICE. HN distinguished event-specific drone response from persistent tracking, while questioning whether proven crime reduction justifies unavoidable surveillance.
