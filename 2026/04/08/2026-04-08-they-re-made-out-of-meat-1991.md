# They're made out of meat (1991)

- Score: 381 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47688678) | Link: http://www.terrybisson.com/theyre-made-out-of-meat-2/

### TL;DR

Terry Bisson’s 1991 dialogue has two nonhuman surveyors confront an absurd discovery: Earth’s radio-building, singing, loving intelligence is entirely biological, including its brain. Unable to accept “thinking meat,” they erase contact records, alter abductees’ memories, and mark the sector unoccupied because humans cannot exceed light speed. Reconnecting with a friendly hydrogen intelligence to avoid cosmic loneliness turns their disgust into hypocrisy. HN’s many reposts attest to the story’s durability; readers discussed adaptations, especially whether visualizing the speakers as human undermines the premise or loses the final joke.
