# Škoda DuoBell: A bicycle bell that penetrates noise-cancelling headphones

- Score: 501 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47687248) | Link: https://www.skoda-storyboard.com/en/skoda-world/skoda-duobell-a-bicycle-bell-that-outsmarts-even-smart-headphones/

### TL;DR

Škoda and University of Salford researchers built DuoBell, a mechanical bicycle bell intended to remain audible through active noise cancellation. One resonator targets a claimed 750–780 Hz “safety gap”; another adds a higher tone, while rapid irregular hammer strikes resist algorithmic suppression. Škoda reports up to 22 metres more pedestrian reaction distance in London trials with Deliveroo couriers and says it will publish the research. HN was skeptical: listeners could not reproduce the frequency notch, ANC designs vary, the bell is not purchasable, and the presentation resembled attention-first concept marketing.

### Comment pulse

- Some cyclists want car-grade horns because insulated vehicles cannot hear bells — counterpoint: excessive volume risks hearing damage and may not change driver behavior.
- Pulsed broadband “shh” reversing alarms were proposed as harder for ANC to cancel and less irritating than tonal beeps.
- Critics noted an unstandardized filter gap could move through software updates, creating an avoidable bell-versus-headphone arms race.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Irregular broadband transients seem more durable than exploiting one vendor-dependent frequency response, but comparative data must decide.
- **Impact:** Pedestrians gain warning only if deployment, audibility, localization, and acceptable loudness all hold outside curated trials.
