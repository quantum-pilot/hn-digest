# The FBI Director Is MIA

- Score: 94 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47813001) | Link: https://www.theatlantic.com/politics/2026/04/kash-patel-fbi-director-drinking-absences/686839/

### TL;DR

The Atlantic reports that FBI Director Kash Patel mistook a login failure for dismissal, told aides he had been fired, and remains anxious about replacement discussions. More than two dozen interviewees describe unexplained absences, delayed decisions, impulsive public claims, loyalty-driven personnel actions, and episodes of alleged excessive drinking that could impair availability and create security risk. The White House and acting attorney general praised his record, while Patel called every allegation false and threatened litigation. The article argues his management has weakened institutional experience during heightened counterterrorism demands.
