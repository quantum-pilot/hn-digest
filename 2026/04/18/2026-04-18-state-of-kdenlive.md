# State of Kdenlive

- Score: 324 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47815118) | Link: https://kdenlive.org/news/2026/state-2026/

### TL;DR

Kdenlive’s 2025 work prioritized stability and workflow polish across three releases: automatic masking, OpenTimelineIO exchange, 300% faster waveform generation, crash fixes, redesigned audio and marker tools, and flexible docking. Version 26.04 adds monitor mirroring, transition previews, and batch speed changes; longer-term work targets higher-bit-depth color, decoding performance, OpenFX, subtitles, advanced trimming, and a keyframe dopesheet. Despite 11.5 million downloads, the core has eight active members and two developers, prompting a funding appeal. Users report both strong everyday value and persistent usability, crash, and large-project performance issues.

### Comment pulse

- Fans see a practical middle ground between lightweight editors and DaVinci Resolve’s complexity and hardware demands.
- Dissatisfied users cite dated UI, title creation, accelerated playback, crashes, and scaling regressions on clip-heavy projects.
- Open-source accessibility is compelling — counterpoint: commercial teams retain an advantage in full-time UX, testing, and polish.

### LLM perspective
