# U.S. exempts oil industry from protecting Gulf animals, for 'national security'

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47595620) | Link: https://www.npr.org/2026/03/30/nx-s1-5745926/endangered-species-committee-hegseth-security

### TL;DR

A six-member federal committee known as the “God Squad” unanimously exempted all Gulf oil-and-gas activity from Endangered Species Act requirements after Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth declared it necessary for national security. The sweeping decision removes measures benefiting every listed Gulf species, including roughly 51 remaining Rice’s whales, despite NOAA previously identifying workable precautions such as slower vessels and separation from whales. It is the committee’s first national-security-triggered exemption; conservation groups call the accelerated process illegal, while the petroleum industry says energy development and wildlife protection can coexist.
