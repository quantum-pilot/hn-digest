# I quit. The clankers won

- Score: 365 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47598511) | Link: https://dbushell.com/2026/04/01/i-quit-the-clankers-won/

### TL;DR

Despite its title, the essay urges developers not to quit blogging. Its case is that AI saturation makes recognizable human voices more valuable, while writing publicly improves memory, tests assumptions, sharpens professional judgment, and may unblock future readers. The author rejects generative AI on ethical, economic, and quality grounds, advocating the old, open, and independent web instead of Big Tech platforms. HN largely endorsed deliberate mental exercise through writing, reading, conversation, and mentoring, but split over whether coding agents cause deskilling or simply demand a new professional skill.

### Comment pulse

- Writing, long-form reading, mentorship, navigation, and daily coding were proposed as practical ways to preserve attention and sharpen reasoning.
