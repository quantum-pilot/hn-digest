### TL;DR

The supplied Forbes scrape contains navigation but not the article itself, so the available substance is the headline and HN reaction. The headline frames abandoned products and incomplete deals as an OpenAI “graveyard.” Commenters split sharply: defenders call cancellation normal startup experimentation and argue successful launches like ChatGPT and Codex are omitted; critics say OpenAI’s scale, rhetoric, burn, and user lock-in make repeated dead ends costly and trust-eroding. GPT-4o and Sora became focal examples, alongside speculation that executive turnover contributes to strategic thrash.

### Comment pulse

- Experimentation supporters said most launches should fail and selective coverage of failures discourages future attempts.
- Critics compared repeated shutdowns to Google’s product graveyard: early-stage iteration becomes a costly “roach motel” once many users commit.
- Smart staff cannot overcome bad incentives, commenters argued; others suspected leadership departures were cause, consequence, or both.
