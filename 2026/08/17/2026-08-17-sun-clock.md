# Sun Clock

- Score: 285 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49333824) | Link: https://sunclock.net/

### TL;DR

Sun Clock is a free, ad-free 24-hour web clock that maps sunrise, solar noon, sunset, golden hour, twilight, and the moon’s phase, position, rise, and set times for a chosen location. It reverses rotation in the Southern Hemisphere to follow the Sun’s apparent path, offers manual coordinates and display controls, includes an annual calendar, and works offline as a PWA. Location and settings remain in-browser without cookies. Commenters praised its SunCalc foundation, requested multi-location comparison, and questioned defining golden hour as a fixed pre-sunset hour at extreme latitudes.
