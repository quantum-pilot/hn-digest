# An update on leaving Gmail for Fastmail

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49334409) | Link: https://moddedbear.com/an-update-on-leaving-gmail-for-fastmail/

### TL;DR

Months after leaving Gmail, the author reports Fastmail has been smooth and worth its price. Rather than forwarding everything, they kept Gmail as a junk inbox and updated roughly ten important accounts, using category subdomains to route messages automatically. Fastmail supports custom domains and disposable masked addresses; a new domain was greylisted by Gmail, while an older blog domain delivered immediately. Commenters praised support and provider portability through owned domains, but reported mixed spam filtering and noted that Google Photos, Drive, OAuth, and family usage complicate departure.
