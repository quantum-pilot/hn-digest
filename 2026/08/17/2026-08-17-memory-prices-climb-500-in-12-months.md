# Memory prices climb 500% in 12 months

- Score: 658 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49334960) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/ram/memory-prices-climb-500-percent-in-12-months-up-to-10x-the-lowest-ever-tracked-prices-128gb-of-ddr5-now-usd3-399

### TL;DR

PCPartPicker-based estimates show DDR5 kit prices rising roughly 355–485% year over year, with some 128GB kits at $3,399—over ten times a tracked low. DDR4 rose 120–177%, and European memory prices reportedly climbed 345%, alongside storage increases. The article attributes the squeeze to AI datacenters, HBM consuming more DRAM layers, and hyperscalers reserving 2027 capacity; vendors expect shortages beyond 2027. Commenters considered delaying upgrades, keeping spares, writing leaner software, or moving workloads cloudward, while debating whether cautious fab expansion reflects another memory boom-bust cycle.
