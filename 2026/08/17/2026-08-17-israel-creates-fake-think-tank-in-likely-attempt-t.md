# Israel creates fake think tank in likely attempt to dupe AI chatbots

- Score: 1045 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49337392) | Link: https://responsiblestatecraft.org/israel-influence-chatgpt/

### TL;DR

A purported U.S.-style think tank called the Hanover Institute published more than 100 Israel–Palestine reports in 11 days, without bylines, while a footer disclosed creation for Israel’s Government Advertising Agency by Piro. The article argues its neutral formatting, statistics, and citations are designed to influence chatbot answers; Piro advertises “AI Story Optimization” and received $900,000. Piro says it is adding verifiable facts and countering misinformation. Commenters saw this as an old propaganda tactic adapted to LLM retrieval, threatening online trust and elevating provenance.
