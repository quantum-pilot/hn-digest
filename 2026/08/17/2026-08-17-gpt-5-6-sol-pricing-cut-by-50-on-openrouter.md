# GPT-5.6 Sol Pricing Cut by 50% on OpenRouter

- Score: 629 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49337602) | Link: https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-5.6-sol

### TL;DR

OpenRouter lists OpenAI-hosted GPT-5.6 Sol at a 50% discount: $2.50 per million input tokens, $15 output, and $0.25 cache reads, versus $5/$30/$0.50 previously; Azure and Bedrock listings remain higher. The model offers roughly 1.05-million-token context, coding and agent strengths, and routing across providers. OpenRouter reports effective prices shaped heavily by caching and 98.57% three-day availability. Commenters credited open-model competition, compared Sol favorably with Fable and cheaper models, but stressed workload-specific evaluations, privacy agreements, and overengineering tendencies.

### Comment pulse

- Open models are pressuring incumbents → teams report substituting Kimi or DeepSeek when comparable outcomes cost less.
- Sol and Fable remain close for coding → preferences vary around debugging, one-shot UI work, prose, refusals, and overengineering.
- Cheapest is not always usable → sensitive workloads may require trusted providers and zero-data-retention agreements.

### LLM perspective

- View: The cut turns model selection from a capability contest into a workload-level price, latency, privacy, and product comparison.
- Impact: Heavy coding users can lower inference bills or demand better terms from competing model vendors.
- Watch next: Measure sustained token volume, discount duration, cache-adjusted spend, and equivalent-task quality across providers.
