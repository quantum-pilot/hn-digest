### TL;DR

OpenAI says an internal Astra model produced ten results resolving or advancing problems across geometry, coding theory, group theory, operator algebras, circuit and quantum complexity, lattices, and combinatorics for roughly $2,000 in inference. Humans prepared manuscripts, the model formalized proofs in Lean, and OpenAI attributes the mathematical arguments to the system. HN commenters with field context described several results as major, not mere brute force, while others asked whether the work introduces reusable ideas, how experts should interpret opaque approaches, and where computational limits remain.
