# Andy Pavlo joins ClickHouse to establish ClickHouse Labs

- Score: 335 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49156011) | Link: https://clickhouse.com/blog/andy-pavlo-joins-clickhouse

### TL;DR

CMU database professor Andy Pavlo is joining ClickHouse to found ClickHouse Labs, an industry research group intended to work directly with engineers, customers, and partners rather than publish in isolation. The lab will validate ClickHouse’s backlog of ideas, improve its PostgreSQL managed service, study transactional and analytical systems, and explore both databases for agents and agents for database development. HN welcomed a non-AI corporate lab, requested academic funding, and discussed convergence among ClickHouse, StarRocks, Trino, object storage, and open table formats.
