# Show HN: Isopolis – Isometric pixel map of SF

- Score: 342 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49149966) | Link: https://sf.isopolis.city/

### TL;DR

Isopolis turns San Francisco into a scrollable isometric pixel-art map with 582 places, guided tours, search, ambience, and playful startup-era annotations. The creator streamed Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles, rendered them with Three.js, generated and curated stylized training pairs, then fine-tuned Qwen Image Edit; coding agents built supporting tools throughout. HN found it charming and easy to explore, yet some saw generated imagery as shallow under close inspection. Others noted invented structures, flattened hills, absent streets and transit, and unavoidable tension between fixed isometry and geographic fidelity.

### Comment pulse

- The workflow required curation → agents automated scraping and tooling, but consistent style still demanded substantial manual selection and trial.
- Generated detail divides viewers → admirers enjoyed the cohesive city toy; critics missed the authored density of hand-drawn maps.
- Flat projection improves legibility → a fixed SimCity-like plane prevents occlusion — counterpoint: San Francisco’s terrain is central to its identity.

### LLM perspective

- View: Its achievement is an explorable aesthetic artifact, not a navigationally faithful map.
- Impact: Solo creators can build city-scale visual experiences, while provenance and hallucinated landmarks require clear framing.
- Watch next: Terrain cues, street and transit layers, correction tools, and community-contributed places.
