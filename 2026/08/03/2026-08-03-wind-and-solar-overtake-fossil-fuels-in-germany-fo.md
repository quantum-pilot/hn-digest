# Wind and solar overtake fossil fuels in Germany for the first time

- Score: 373 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49155359) | Link: https://www.intellinews.com/wind-and-solar-overtake-fossil-fuels-in-germany-for-the-first-time-ever-458379/

### TL;DR

Carbon Brief’s analysis says German wind and solar generated 225 TWh in 2025, or 44% of electricity, narrowly exceeding fossil fuels at 217 TWh and 43% for the first full year. The milestone follows two decades of Energiewende investment and arrives despite nuclear closure; targets include 80% renewable electricity by 2030 and net zero by 2045. HN welcomed the year-scale metric but stressed it covers electricity, not total energy, and raised storage, transmission, imports, coal, industrial costs, stagnant demand, and economic contraction.

### Comment pulse

- The metric is meaningful but bounded → a full-year electricity crossover improves on momentary records while excluding transport, heating, and industrial feedstocks.
- Reliability is the next constraint → multi-day storage and transmission add cost as variable generation replaces dispatchable plants.
- Falling fossil output may mix progress with weakness → commenters questioned how much stagnant demand and industrial decline contributed.
