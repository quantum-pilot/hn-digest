# ICE Collected Nearly 1M People's DNA Last Year–Including Young Children

- Score: 253 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49154237) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/ice-dna-collection-fbi-codis/

### TL;DR

Georgetown researchers estimate ICE may have added roughly 920,000 DNA profiles to the FBI’s CODIS detainee index in 2025, amid nearly one million new detainee entries overall. Most immigration detention is civil, yet profiles can be searched indefinitely against unrelated crimes and physical samples are retained. Records also show CBP submitted DNA from 492 children under 14, despite a stated age floor of 14. DHS defended collection as legally required identification and border security; critics cite unclear explanations, permanent familial privacy consequences, and constitutional challenges.
