# Taylor Farms has rewritten its cyclospora statement four times in sixteen days

- Score: 303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49157128) | Link: https://www.marlerblog.com/case-news/taylor-farms-has-rewritten-its-cyclospora-statement-four-times-in-sixteen-days-it-still-has-not-said-what-changed-at-that-plant-after-2013-or-why-two-thousand-negative-tests-should-mean-an/

### TL;DR

Attorney Bill Marler catalogs five versions of Taylor Farms’ cyclospora messaging after a shredded-iceberg recall. He says the company removed an early traceback to a specific farm, asserted then dropped an FDA apology, and foregrounded roughly 2,000 negative tests without explaining detection limits. He connects the current outbreak to the same Mexican facility’s 2013 episode and asks for its hazard analysis, water-testing records, adjacent-land reviews, and corrective changes. HN debated whether revisions reflect normal evolving evidence or strategic narrative control, while noting difficult traceability and the author’s litigation interest.
