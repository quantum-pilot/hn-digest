# More German than many Germans

- Score: 613 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49151734) | Link: https://mertbulan.com/more-german-than-many-germans/

### TL;DR

A Turkish computer-science graduate recounts arriving in Hamburg for a 2017 internship, returning for full-time work, and becoming a German citizen eight years later. Trusting colleagues, flat hierarchies, worker protections, social equality, predictable rules, historical accountability, and everyday inclusion overturned inherited stereotypes and matched his own values. He acknowledges an unusually soft landing through an English-speaking technology job, income, and supportive network. HN echoed Germany’s stability and rule consistency while debating bureaucracy, cultural integration, economic stagnation, xenophobia, migration politics, and whether residents underappreciate existing advantages.
