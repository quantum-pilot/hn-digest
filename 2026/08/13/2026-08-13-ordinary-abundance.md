# Ordinary abundance

- Score: 202 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49285770) | Link: https://ordinaryabundance.com/

### TL;DR

A short historical vignette recalls the 1837 arrival of New England pond ice in Calcutta, celebrated because imported cold transformed what people could preserve and eat in tropical heat. The discussion used refrigeration as a lens on ordinary abundance: hot water, air conditioning, clean water, electricity, and instant communication quickly become invisible through hedonic adaptation. Some welcomed perpetual desire as fuel for progress; others noted material improvements do not guarantee greater happiness. Several proposed chosen hardship to renew gratitude, while acknowledging it differs from unavoidable deprivation.
