# Donkey.bas is 45 Years Old – 131 line of Glory

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49289465) | Link: https://donkeybas.com/

### TL;DR

A browser recreation marks 45 years since DONKEY.BAS, the tiny lane-switching game Bill Gates and Neil Konzen wrote to demonstrate color graphics and sound in early IBM PC DOS and BASICA. The JavaScript port preserves the original CGA-style premise—switch lanes to avoid a donkey—while taking some liberties and adding sound, CRT, cheat, and fullscreen options. Commenters traded memories of QBasic, GORILLA.BAS, and Nibbles, praised preservation through playable ports, and noted that the recreated audio sounds more capable than early PC speakers.
