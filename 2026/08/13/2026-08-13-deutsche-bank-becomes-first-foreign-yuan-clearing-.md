# Deutsche Bank becomes first foreign yuan clearing bank in Europe

- Score: 378 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49284774) | Link: https://tradersunion.com/news/central-banks/show/2973571-deutsche-bank-becomes/

### TL;DR

China authorized Deutsche Bank to clear renminbi transactions from Frankfurt, making it the first foreign institution in Europe with that designation after a memorandum with the People’s Bank of China. European clearing had relied on Chinese banks; adding a major euro and dollar clearer gives companies another route for direct yuan settlement and extends Beijing’s overseas payment infrastructure. The article calls the immediate change practical, not a challenge to dollar or euro dominance. Commenters split between viewing it as gradual de-dollarization and emphasizing China’s capital controls and dollar-based asset depth.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters expect fewer intermediaries and fees for Europe-China trade, particularly as energy and industrial relationships shift.
- Reserve-currency skeptics cite yuan controls and entrenched dollar assets—counterpoint: wider clearing is necessary infrastructure even without reserve dominance.

- View: Clearing access lowers transactional friction, but currency internationalization also requires liquidity, investable assets, and confidence in convertibility.
- Impact: European companies and Deutsche Bank gain settlement options; Chinese authorities gain a non-Chinese node in their payments network.
