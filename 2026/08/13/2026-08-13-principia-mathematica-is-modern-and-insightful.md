# Principia Mathematica is modern and insightful

- Score: 269 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49279928) | Link: https://okmij.org/ftp/Computation/Impressions/PrincipiaMathematica.html

### TL;DR

A close reading of 1910’s Principia Mathematica argues that its opening chapter anticipates programming-language concepts including referential transparency, alpha-equivalence, variable scope, substitution, lambda terms, types, and relation-defined functions. It also treats definitions as theoretically dispensable yet essential expressions of intent, and hints at constructivist existence proofs. Commenters balanced admiration with the work’s formidable notation, repetition, and historical incompleteness: Gödel later established limits on its foundational ambition, while modern Lean and Coq projects have formalized portions and checked original proof sketches.

### Comment pulse

- Mark Dominus likened its bulk to repeated code written before stronger abstraction techniques allowed common sections to be combined.
- Russell’s shorter Introduction to Mathematical Philosophy and the Logicomix comic were recommended as gentler entry points.
- Its dot hierarchy can encode grouping without parentheses, though one commenter questioned whether that improves programming notation.
