# Gemini 3.7 Flash

- Score: 588 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49289112) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/

### TL;DR

Google introduces Gemini 3.7 Flash three weeks after 3.6, targeting coding and agents with improved planning, tool use, instruction following, web generation, and document-heavy work. Reported gains include DeepSWE 65.3% versus 49.0%, GDP.pdf 34.0% versus 22.0%, and AutomationBench 30.4% versus 17.0%. Introductory pricing through 2026 is $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens, then doubles. Commenters praised speed and visual ability but questioned its niche against cheaper models; one SVG test exposed invalid cross-browser output despite attractive rendering in Safari.

### Comment pulse

- Visual-task users found Flash competitive with pricier models, though rivals appear to be closing Gemini’s historical vision advantage.
- Price skeptics dislike a scheduled doubling—counterpoint: production teams may retain proven older models because revalidation costs exceed token savings.
