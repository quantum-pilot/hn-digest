# Choose Boring Technology (2015)

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49289512) | Link: https://mcfunley.com/choose-boring-technology

### TL;DR

This 2015 essay treats organizational capacity for novelty as roughly three “innovation tokens.” Mature, familiar tools preserve attention for the product because their capabilities and failure modes are better understood; every additional language, database, or service adds operational and cognitive costs across the company. New technology is justified when the existing stack makes a problem prohibitively hard, provided adoption is discussed openly and overlapping systems are migrated away. Commenters still value the heuristic, especially for AI agents, but critics prefer explicit requirements, evidence, and risk analysis over labels like “boring.”
