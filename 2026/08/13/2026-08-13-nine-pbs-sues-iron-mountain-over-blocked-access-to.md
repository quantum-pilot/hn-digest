# Nine PBS sues Iron Mountain over blocked access to archival data

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49285418) | Link: https://current.org/2026/08/nine-pbs-sues-iron-mountain-over-blocked-access-to-archival-data/

### TL;DR

Nine PBS sued to recover more than 50 terabytes of programming and archival material spanning 70 years after its storage vendor, Open Source Storage, cut access on March 6 and became unreachable. A St. Louis default judgment affirmed the broadcaster’s ownership and immediate right to possession, yet Iron Mountain, which holds the infrastructure in Denver, refused transfer because OSS controls the physical services housing it. A Denver judge ordered preservation pending a hearing. Discussion focused on custody-chain liability, uncertain colocation details, and missing backup redundancy.
