# Mistral OCR 4.1

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49288889) | Link: https://docs.mistral.ai/models/ocr-4-1

### TL;DR

Mistral’s public-preview OCR service adds paragraph-level bounding boxes, structural block labels, and block confidence scores, priced at €3.50 per thousand pages or €4.38 with annotations. Commenters reported sharply document-dependent results: intricate historical typography remained difficult, one workflow caught fabricated sentences by proofreading Mistral output with Claude, while another found Mistral superior on handwritten email addresses. Some valued its speed, low-friction structured output, and permissive transcription; others called it expensive relative to conventional OCR and competing document services.

### Comment pulse

- Generalist vision models may transcribe better but can alter text or refuse sensitive material; OCR-specific models can silently hallucinate.
- Cross-provider reconciliation and source-text checks improve confidence, though no generic complex-document workflow was considered reliably turnkey.
- Users want browsable input-output examples, especially for figures, tables, handwriting, and bounding-box accuracy.

### LLM perspective

- View: OCR quality is a portfolio of document-specific tradeoffs, not one leaderboard score.
- Impact: Archives and clinical or legal pipelines need provenance-preserving review when omissions, censorship, or fabricated text carry high costs.
- Watch next: Confidence calibration, layout benchmarks, real examples, and systems that flag disagreements instead of silently choosing one transcription.
