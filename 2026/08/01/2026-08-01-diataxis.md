# Diátaxis

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49138188) | Link: https://diataxis.fr/

## TL;DR
Diátaxis is a framework for technical documentation that splits content into four distinct types: tutorials, how-to guides, reference, and explanation. Practitioners in the discussion report big gains in clarity, consistency, and “voice” when handing over large codebases or restructuring product docs. Others use it as a schema when prompting LLMs to generate first-pass documentation. There’s some pushback that once you internalize it, most existing docs look painfully bad or over-theorized, but its influence is clearly growing.
