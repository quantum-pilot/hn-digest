# BMW Spider-Man in-car advertising

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49130756) | Link: https://consumerrights.wiki/w/BMW_Spider-Man_in-car_advertising

### TL;DR

BMW pushed a startup banner for a Spider-Man film promotion to eligible customer dashboards in more than 70 markets. Clicking it launches a 19-second full-screen animation with music, ambient-light effects, a QR code, and cinema messaging; the campaign runs July 27–August 10. The move contradicts a 2023 executive promise that cars were private spaces whose screens would not be sold for commercials, echoing BMW’s abandoned heated-seat subscription. HN largely viewed the opt-in playback as an ownership and brand-trust violation, though one commenter called it merely an unnoticed desktop icon.

### Comment pulse
