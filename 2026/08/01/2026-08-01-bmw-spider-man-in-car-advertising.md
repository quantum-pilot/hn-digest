# BMW Spider-Man in-car advertising

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49130756) | Link: https://consumerrights.wiki/w/BMW_Spider-Man_in-car_advertising

## TL;DR
BMW pushed a Spider-Man movie promotion into its in-car screens, framing it in a press release as a “special surprise” for drivers. Hacker News commenters reacted very negatively, arguing this turns a historically “driver-focused” premium brand into an ad-delivery platform, alongside earlier grievances like subscription features and proprietary parts. Others note that many automakers and electronics brands (Mercedes, Stellantis, Samsung TVs/fridges) already inject ads post-purchase, suggesting a broader industry race to monetize attention inside products people thought they’d already paid for.
