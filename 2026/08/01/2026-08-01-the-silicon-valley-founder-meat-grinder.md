# The Silicon Valley Founder Meat Grinder

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49138045) | Link: https://zaksa.zip/blog/silicon-valley-founder-meat-grinder/

### TL;DR
A cautionary story of “Jim,” a Silicon Valley founder who rides early success into the stereotypical SF party/drug/sex scene, loses relationships and focus, and burns out financially and emotionally. The piece argues that the “founder lifestyle” meat-grinder rewards image, risk, and networking just long enough for some people to self-destruct, while boring, execution-focused companies quietly do the real work. HN commenters add nuance: persistence beats raw intelligence, “founder” as identity is dangerous, and financial “recklessness” is relative.
