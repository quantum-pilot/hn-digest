### TL;DR

This typography manual examines the smallest marks that shape reading: periods, commas, ellipses, bullets, interpuncts, colons, semicolons, question marks, exclamation marks, and interrobangs. It connects glyph design, sidebearings, kerning, Unicode distinctions, keyboard entry, and language-specific conventions for decimals, time, initials, lists, mathematics, and punctuation spacing. HN readers enjoyed the craft while debating prescriptivism: exact code points and historical forms delight specialists, yet most readers care mainly that intent remains clear. Discussion highlighted Russian typographic rigor, linguistic variation, compose-key shortcuts, and better angled interrobang designs.
