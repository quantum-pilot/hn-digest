# A Surveillance Treaty in Disguise: Canada Signs UN Cybercrime Convention

- Score: 257 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49134694) | Link: https://www.michaelgeist.ca/2026/07/a-surveillance-treaty-in-disguise-the-trouble-with-canadas-quiet-decision-to-sign-the-un-cybercrime-convention/

- TL;DR  
Michael Geist argues Canada’s signature on the new UN Cybercrime Convention effectively endorses a global surveillance framework, since vague “cybercrime” provisions and cross‑border cooperation tools could be exploited by authoritarian states to target activists and journalists. Commenters worry that opaque treaty-making and a signatory list including China, Russia, Saudi Arabia and North Korea signal weak human‑rights safeguards. Others note signatures are mostly symbolic until ratified, and that Canada routinely signs UN instruments with little public scrutiny.
