# On the non-use of AI in my writing process

- Score: 124 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49134038) | Link: https://www.antipope.org/charlie/blog-static/2026/08/on-the-non-use-of-ai-in-my-wri.html

### TL;DR

Charles Stross declares his fiction entirely LLM-free: he enjoys writing, already proved his craft before modern models, rejects AI detectors that mistake authorial habits for generation, and objects to pirated training data, scraping, cloud prompt reuse, and anthropomorphism. He distinguishes generative substitution from analysis, welcoming a local manuscript tool that maps scenes, timelines, characters, and name frequency while leaving decisions to the author. HN debated whether embodiment meaningfully separates models from people, whether agents can manage long-range narrative structure, and whether the deeper objection is quality or human authorship itself.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The clearest boundary is agency: decision-support can expose structure without outsourcing the expressive choices readers attribute to an author.
- **Impact:** Authors face provenance accusations, data-control risks, and market competition even when they never use generative systems.
- **Watch next:** Develop local-only analyzers with auditable storage, timeline accuracy, no training reuse, disclosure, and tests against false AI attribution.
