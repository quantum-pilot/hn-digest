# On the non-use of AI in my writing process

- Score: 124 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49134038) | Link: https://www.antipope.org/charlie/blog-static/2026/08/on-the-non-use-of-ai-in-my-wri.html

- TL;DR  
Science fiction author Charles Stross explains why he avoids AI in his fiction-writing: large language models are ungrounded word‑associators, optimized for pastiche, not meaning, and embedded in exploitative, enclosure‑driven tech capitalism. He concedes narrow, tooling-style uses but rejects AI as coauthor or plotter, especially for long, interdependent narratives. HN commenters debate embodiment and “stochastic parrot” critiques, imagine non-generative AI tools for authors, and argue his objections mix real labor concerns with skepticism about machine-made literature’s lack of human “soul.”
