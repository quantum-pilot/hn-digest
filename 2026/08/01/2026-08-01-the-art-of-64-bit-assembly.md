### TL;DR
A new ~800-page volume on 64-bit x86 assembly extends a long-running series known for deep, practical low-level coverage, especially under Windows and MASM. Commenters with experience of earlier editions and the author’s “Write Great Code” regard him as competent, if sometimes overly anti-compiler and optimization-obsessed. Discussion veers into meta-complaints about HN reactions and skepticism toward an AI-themed marketing intro. Others highlight MASM’s strong macro system versus GNU as, and wish for an equivalent Linux-centric, GAS/FASM-based treatment.
