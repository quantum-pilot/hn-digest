# How Google helped destroy adoption of RSS feeds (2023)

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49136821) | Link: https://openrss.org/blog/how-google-helped-destroy-adoption-of-rss-feeds

### TL;DR

The article argues Google weakened RSS adoption through repeated embrace-and-withdraw cycles: removing Chrome discovery, restricting FeedBurner after acquisition, closing Reader in 2013, briefly dropping Alerts feeds, killing its browser extension, and ending Google News RSS support in 2017. Each change stranded users or reduced confidence in open feeds, while proprietary links remained. HN connected this history to ad-funded walled gardens and remembered Reader’s closure as a turning point, but stressed RSS remains active, inexpensive to publish, and embedded in podcasts and modern open-web projects; Mozilla also removed native feed features.
