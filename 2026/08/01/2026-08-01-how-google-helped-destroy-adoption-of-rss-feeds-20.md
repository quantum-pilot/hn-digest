# How Google helped destroy adoption of RSS feeds (2023)

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49136821) | Link: https://openrss.org/blog/how-google-helped-destroy-adoption-of-rss-feeds

- TL;DR  
  Google popularized RSS through Google Reader, then abruptly killed it while pivoting to Google+ and engagement-driven feeds, undermining a simple, open syndication standard. HN commenters recall Reader’s shutdown as a turning point toward today’s ad-optimized, walled-garden web, where algorithmic timelines replace user-controlled subscriptions. Some argue RSS never died—podcasts, blogs, and indie sites still rely on it—and note that social networks and podcasts quietly rebranded feed consumption, leaving explicit RSS tools niche and poorly marketed.
