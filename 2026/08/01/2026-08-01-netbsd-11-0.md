# NetBSD 11.0

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49136736) | Link: https://blog.netbsd.org/tnf/entry/netbsd_11_0_released

### TL;DR

NetBSD 11.0 finally ships across its many architectures, with separate sub-700MB CD images, full DVD images, and decompressed flash images. The project openly lists three unresolved security pullups: a local hdaudio privilege check, an ipfilter null dereference, and a PF use-after-free; the latter firewalls are absent from default released kernels, and fixes are planned for 11.1 within two months. HN praised the unusually candid release posture and highlighted portability, pkgsrc, new NPF filtering, and a roughly 10ms x86 MICROVM boot, while questioning ecosystem health and older toolchain versions.
