# NetBSD 11.0

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49136736) | Link: https://blog.netbsd.org/tnf/entry/netbsd_11_0_released

### TL;DR
NetBSD 11.0 lands as a solid, conservative BSD release focused on portability and correctness rather than headline features. The announcement highlights npf firewall enhancements, a new x86 MICROVM kernel that can boot in roughly 10 ms, and various hardware updates, while candidly listing unresolved issues. Hacker News discussion zooms out to the broader BSD ecosystem, describing NetBSD’s niche (runs everywhere, pkgsrc), contrasting it with FreeBSD and OpenBSD, questioning its older GCC choice, and wondering about Wine and desktop viability.
