# Google has abandoned Google News?

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49137681) | Link: https://elgan.com/google-news-is-just-forrest-gumps-shrimp-boat-now

### TL;DR

A journalist says Google’s News search mode no longer respects publication date, language, or country filters, increasingly returning social posts, foreign sources, and old stories while Google focuses on AI. HN agreed that fuzzy, engagement-driven search has degraded across major platforms, but challenged the evidence: the screenshot showed Search’s News tab rather than news.google.com, apparently deep in pagination with filters being expanded, and omitted the query. Some reproduced late out-of-range results; others could not, leaving a credible usability complaint supported by a weakly documented case.

### Comment pulse

- The scope was misstated → the screenshot depicts Search’s News tab, while daily users of the separate Google News product reported no comparable issue.
- Filter violations appear real but late → one test found an old result on page 17 despite many current results remaining.
- Trust is polarized → critics see deliberate engagement funnels — counterpoint: others demand uncropped URLs, repeatable queries, and literal evidence for majority claims.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** A search system that silently broadens explicit constraints converts precision controls into suggestions and undermines professional research workflows.
- **Impact:** Journalists lose reproducibility; Google loses trust even when expansion improves recall for casual users.
