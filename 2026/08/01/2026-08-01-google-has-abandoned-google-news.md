# Google has abandoned Google News?

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49137681) | Link: https://elgan.com/google-news-is-just-forrest-gumps-shrimp-boat-now

- TL;DR  
HN discusses a column claiming Google has effectively abandoned Google News: search filters allegedly fail, foreign-language and social-media links dominate, and results feel random and outdated. Many commenters generalize this to a broader decline in consumer tech, blaming ad-driven optimization and “enshittification” for making search fuzzy and untrustworthy. Others push back, saying the author mixed up Google News with Search’s News tab, cherry-picked screenshots, and that date filters still work in their testing.
