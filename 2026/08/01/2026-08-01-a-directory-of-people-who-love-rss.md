# A directory of people who love RSS

- Score: 159 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49136063) | Link: https://andrewshell.org/2026/07/i-%e2%99%a5-rss/

### TL;DR

Andrew Shell launched a directory for websites with syndication feeds, pairing a submission badge with a FeedLand-powered live blogroll. The modest project aims to make independent sites easier to discover and encourage more personal publishing. HN welcomed another attempt at an RSS revival, while asking for freshness signals, posts-per-day counts, batch OPML submissions, and clearer contribution feedback. A spirited terminology dispute followed: Atom advocates called RSS technically flawed, while pragmatists argued the familiar RSS label matters less than keeping open, structured feeds easy to find.

### Comment pulse
