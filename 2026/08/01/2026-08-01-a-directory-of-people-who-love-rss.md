### TL;DR
A community-built “directory of people who love RSS” sparks enthusiasm for revitalizing RSS-style feeds and personal blogs. Commenters share parallel efforts to catalog feeds and request practical metadata like post frequency. A technical subthread erupts over RSS vs. Atom: some argue RSS is a messy, legacy format that should be replaced by Atom; others counter that RSS overwhelmingly dominates in practice and naming pedantry only confuses users. Several note that what really matters is more people blogging, not which feed spec wins.
