# New EU-wide product repair rules come into force

- Score: 256 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49415621) | Link: https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/0824/1588931-repair-rules/

### TL;DR

EU rules require manufacturers of covered products—including washing machines, vacuums, phones, and tablets—to offer repair after guarantees expire when EU law deems it technically repairable. Work must be free or reasonably priced and timely; manufacturers must publish service information and supply reasonably priced parts. Ireland will expand RepairMyStuff.ie as its national repair directory. The policy targets 35 million tonnes of premature-disposal waste and projects €4.8 billion over 15 years. Commenters stressed that access, software support, and affordable parts determine whether formal repair rights change replacement behavior.
