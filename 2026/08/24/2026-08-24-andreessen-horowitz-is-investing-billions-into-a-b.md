# Andreessen Horowitz is investing billions into a bleak future

- Score: 726 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49416055) | Link: https://www.modelrepublic.org/articles/a16z-portfolio

### TL;DR

An advocacy report examines 18 Andreessen Horowitz investments across deceptive advertising, gambling, AI companions, fintech, and legally troubled startups. It argues the portfolio shows a repeated willingness to exploit regulatory gaps and externalize consumer harm, while the firm spends heavily to weaken AI oversight through lobbying, a $100 million super PAC, and allies in government. Examples include undisclosed bot advertising, underregulated wagering, unsafe companion platforms, and missing customer deposits. HN discussion focused on ethical defection, investor incentives, and omitted surveillance companies.
