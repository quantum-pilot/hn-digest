# The entire city of San Francisco as a video game

- Score: 583 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49422784) | Link: https://sf.thijs.gg/

### TL;DR

A browser-based 3D open world turns Apple-derived San Francisco geometry and imagery into an explorable map. Players can walk in first or third person, sprint, jump, glide, drive cars, teleport through the minimap or address search, share location links, choose avatars, and encounter nearby players with chat. An arcade mode adds drifting, tokens, and items; streamed detail levels and separate controls support desktop and mobile. Commenters wanted a downloadable higher-resolution edition and richer gameplay, while former residents said revisiting exact neighborhoods produced unusually strong nostalgia.
