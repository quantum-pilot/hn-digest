# Oceans hit highest temperature on record

- Score: 682 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49424606) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c62m4gpnp78o

### TL;DR

Copernicus measured 21.1°C across extra-polar seas on August 22, edging above March 2024’s record and every earlier August. The timing is unusual because seas normally peak in March or April. A strengthening El Niño adds heat atop decades of human-driven warming; marine-heatwave days have more than tripled since the early 1990s. Warmer water expands sea level, harms reefs and fisheries, can intensify rain and storms, and absorbs carbon dioxide less efficiently. Commenters disputed whether markets and rapid renewable growth are displacing fossil fuels fast enough.
