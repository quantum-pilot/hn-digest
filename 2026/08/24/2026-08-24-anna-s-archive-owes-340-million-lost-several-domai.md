# Anna's Archive Owes $340 Million, Lost Several Domains, but It's Still Online

- Score: 290 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49420902) | Link: https://torrentfreak.com/annas-archive-owes-340-million-lost-several-domains-but-its-still-online/

### TL;DR

Anna’s Archive returned after extended August downtime that its operator suspected was a coordinated network attack, not a confirmed legal action. Earlier in 2026, music companies obtained injunctions that removed four domains and a $322 million default judgment; publishers later won another $19.5 million. The site shifted to domains beyond apparent U.S. court reach. Commenters celebrated broad access to books, while debating paid downloads, centralized infrastructure, copyright hypocrisy, and whether commercial AI training differs morally from a public shadow library.
