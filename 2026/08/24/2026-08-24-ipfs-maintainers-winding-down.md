# IPFS Maintainers Winding Down

- Score: 359 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421489) | Link: https://ipshipyard.com/blog/2026-the-end-of-ipfs-at-shipyard/

### TL;DR

Protocol Labs will stop funding Shipyard, ending that team’s IPFS engineering, maintenance, and infrastructure work on September 30, 2026. Dedicated stewardship will cease for Kubo, Helia, Boxo, desktop tools, gateways, bootstrap nodes, and other widely used components; Protocol Labs will decide the public infrastructure’s future. Shipyard reports tripling gateway capacity while cutting operating costs about 80 percent. Commenters stressed that IPFS itself is not shutting down, yet questioned whether proposed individual grants identify anyone responsible for releases, vulnerabilities, and continuity after October 1.

### Comment pulse

- Decentralized architecture does not decentralize maintenance → core implementations, security response, domains, and public gateways still depend on funded people and institutions.
- Browser adoption repeatedly receded → unreliable JavaScript support plus Cloudflare and Brave withdrawals left HTTP gateways carrying the user experience.
- Alternatives address narrower layers → commenters praised Iroh and DASL — counterpoint: neither directly replaces every maintained IPFS component.
