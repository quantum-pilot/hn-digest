# Xiaomi: New CPU matches Apple cores single threaded, much faster multithreaded

- Score: 973 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49420873) | Link: https://twitter.com/lemire/status/2091894299289874926

### TL;DR

Xiaomi’s Xring O3 reportedly approaches Apple cores in single-thread performance and exceeds them in multicore tests, although Apple’s next chip and limited handset availability could change the comparison. Its ARM C1-Ultra cores combine 44 MB of cache, SME2 matrix acceleration, SVE2 vector support, and an unusually wide 21-port design with six 128-bit SIMD ports. The broader trend is clear: transistor budgets are buying more cache and parallel execution resources. Commenters cautioned that phone cooling, sustained power efficiency, benchmark design, core counts, and reliance on ARM-designed blocks complicate headline results.
