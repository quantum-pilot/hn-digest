# Where did all the public bathrooms go?

- Score: 334 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49422800) | Link: https://daily.jstor.org/where-did-all-the-public-bathrooms-go/

### TL;DR

Paris’s ornate nineteenth-century pissoirs emerged after cholera reframed waste as a health threat and an 1850 urination ban created demand. Built only for men, they also became covert meeting places for same-sex encounters, drawing police and blackmailers. In US cities, saloons supplied toilets until temperance advocates promoted public “comfort stations.” Private facilities then restricted access, public construction ebbed during Prohibition, and stations decayed. Today pedestrians often depend on businesses, making bodily privacy conditional on payment and social judgment. Commenters debated maintenance, vandalism, fees, disability access, and sharply differing international norms.
