# Jabber/XMPP: 25 Years of Digital Independence

- Score: 259 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421536) | Link: https://gultsch.de/posts/25-years-of-digital-independence/

### TL;DR

The essay treats digital communication as infrastructure requiring open standards, federation, self-hosting, and replaceable vendors; open-source clients alone cannot prevent service lock-in. It presents XMPP’s IETF and XMPP Standards Foundation processes as durable collective governance, contrasting them with Matrix’s dependence on Element’s implementation and specification influence. XMPP adapted slowly to mobile delivery and encryption, but now supports modern features through independently implemented extensions. Commenters valued its longevity and new agent or telephony uses while criticizing configuration complexity, inconsistent extension support, and weaker room history.

### Comment pulse

- Standards governance separates infrastructure from vendors → competitors can implement shared protocols and replace one another without migrating the whole network.
- Extensibility preserved longevity but fragmented expectations → users cannot know whether a client-server pair delivers basic Jabber or modern messaging.
- Matrix better models persistent rooms and history → families report mature deployments — counterpoint: XMPP supporters reject its predominant-vendor dependence.
