# OpenAI: GPT 5.6 Sol price reduction (until at least Nov 21)

- Score: 332 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421074) | Link: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing

### TL;DR

Promotional pricing for GPT-5.6 Sol runs through at least November 21, 2026. For standard short contexts, one million tokens cost $4 input, $0.40 cached input, $5 cache writes, and $20 output; long-context rates are $8, $0.80, $10, and $30. Eligible regional processing adds 10 percent. Terra and Luna remain cheaper at $2/$12 and $0.20/$1.20 for standard input/output. Commenters calculated the change as 20 percent off input and 33 percent off output, welcoming competition while questioning whether discounts signal weaker demand.
