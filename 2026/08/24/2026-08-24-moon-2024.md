# Moon (2024)

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49426466) | Link: https://ciechanow.ski/moon/

### TL;DR

An interactive essay builds lunar intuition from first principles: gravity and barycenters produce orbital motion; tidal forces synchronized the Moon’s spin, drive ocean cycles, lengthen Earth’s day, and move the Moon outward. Simulations then connect its inclined, precessing orbit to libration, phases, earthshine, and rare solar or lunar eclipses. Surface sections explain impact craters, maria, regolith, and the full moon’s opposition surge. Commenters praised the active-essay format, while arguing that AI can lower implementation cost but not replace the judgment required to choose instructive visualizations.
