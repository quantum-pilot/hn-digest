# Iceberg Collapses and Flips over in Ilulissat, Greenland (July 25, 2026) [video]

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49211438) | Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UufMqwyO7pY

### TL;DR

A continuous 4K recording captures roughly an hour of a huge iceberg fracturing near Ilulissat, Greenland, on July 25. Falling sections progressively changed its balance until the remaining mass rolled over, exposing darker, rougher ice that had been submerged. The description explains that melting, erosion, and breakage can shift a floating iceberg’s center of gravity until it rotates into a more stable orientation, displacing water and producing local waves. The event occurred near Ilulissat Icefjord and its active Sermeq Kujalleq glacier; no injuries were reported.
