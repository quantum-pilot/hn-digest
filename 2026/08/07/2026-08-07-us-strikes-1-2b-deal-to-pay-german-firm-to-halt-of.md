# US strikes $1.2B deal to pay German firm to halt offshore wind projects

- Score: 847 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49208314) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c1e1vg0gjl5o

### TL;DR

The US Interior Department will pay Germany’s RWE $1.2 billion to surrender offshore-wind leases near California, Louisiana, and the New York Bight. RWE says permitting offers no foreseeable path and will redirect $900 million into a Louisiana LNG export terminal while planning $19.6 billion in US generation investment over six years. The administration has struck similar wind-exit deals with TotalEnergies and Duke Energy as it favors fossil fuels. HN called the payout itself a subsidy, argued over climate and energy-security costs, and disputed comparisons with other countries’ renewable transitions.
