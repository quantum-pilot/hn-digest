# US strikes $1.2B deal to pay German firm to halt offshore wind projects

- Score: 847 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49208314) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c1e1vg0gjl5o

### TL;DR
The Trump administration’s Interior Department will pay German utility RWE $1.2B to surrender US offshore wind leases, after declaring there is “no path forward” for permitting. RWE will redirect the money into US gas, including a Louisiana LNG export terminal, as part of a wider pivot from US offshore wind under Trump, who openly derides turbines and champions fossil fuels. Hacker News discussion focuses on subsidy hypocrisy, climate risk versus short‑term profit, and how this fits patterns of political decay and geostrategic myopia.
