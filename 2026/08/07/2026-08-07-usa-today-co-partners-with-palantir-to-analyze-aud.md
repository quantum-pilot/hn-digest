# USA Today Co., partners with Palantir to analyze audience data

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49210589) | Link: https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/08/americas-largest-newspaper-chain-usa-today-co-partners-with-palantir-to-analyze-audience-data/

### TL;DR
USA Today Co., which owns 200+ U.S. papers, is partnering with Palantir to mine first-party audience data and optimize ads and subscriptions as search referrals fall and Google leans into AI summaries. Executives say Palantir’s AI will turn anonymous traffic into “known relationships” while keeping data ownership and editorial independence in-house. The move spotlights a wider industry shift away from search, and HN commenters question privacy, Palantir’s ethics and pricing, and whether analytics can fix shallow, weakened local journalism.
