# USA Today Co., partners with Palantir to analyze audience data

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49210589) | Link: https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/08/americas-largest-newspaper-chain-usa-today-co-partners-with-palantir-to-analyze-audience-data/

### TL;DR

USA Today Co., owner of more than 200 local newspapers, will use Palantir to connect audience behavior, convert anonymous visits into identified first-party relationships, and improve advertising, subscriptions, recommendations, and offers. Quarterly unique visitors fell from 180 million to 158 million as search referrals declined, though World Cup coverage still drew 65% of its 97 million pageviews from search. The publisher says its data remains under its control and editorial decisions stay independent. HN questioned why ordinary analytics require an expensive, controversial vendor and lamented weakened local reporting.

### Comment pulse

- Critics mocked the flagship’s lowbrow reputation — counterpoint: ownership of hundreds of local papers makes the partnership consequential beyond one national brand.
- Historical newspapers had little article-level feedback; some argued that weak signals protected editorial judgment from engagement optimization.
- Readers suspected Palantir offers buzzwords at high cost and asked whether cheaper open analytics could deliver the same audience segmentation.
