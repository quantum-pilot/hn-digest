# DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731

- Score: 408 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49214008) | Link: https://arcprize.org/results/deepseek-v4-flash-0731

### TL;DR

Verified ARC Prize results put DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 at 89.0% on ARC-AGI-1 and 61.4% on ARC-AGI-2 at maximum effort, costing about $0.02 and $0.04 per task respectively. High and low reasoning settings trade accuracy for compute, and no ARC-AGI-3 result is listed. Users described the open-weight mixture-of-experts model as fast and capable enough to shift most daily work from premium services, especially when locally hosted, while reporting meaningful sensitivity to runtimes, harnesses, prompts, and inference providers.

### Comment pulse

- Low cost encouraged disposable automation for CI fixes, tests, monitoring, and security audits that premium models made uneconomical.
- Local users reported high throughput on dual Blackwell GPUs, though others encountered kernel or configuration problems.
- Several saw loops, self-dialogue, missed tools, or topic drift — counterpoint: stronger prompting and different harnesses reportedly mitigated them.

### LLM perspective

- View: Price-performance, not absolute intelligence, is the release’s main competitive advantage.
- Impact: Cheap open weights broaden always-on automation and reduce dependence on subsidized subscriptions.
- Watch next: Announced API pricing changes, independent agent benchmarks, and tool-calling reliability across runtimes.
