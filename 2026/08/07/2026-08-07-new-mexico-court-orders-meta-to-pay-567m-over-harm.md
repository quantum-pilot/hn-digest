# New Mexico court orders Meta to pay $567m over harms to children’s mental health

- Score: 711 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49204352) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/06/new-mexico-court-meta

### TL;DR
A New Mexico court ordered Meta to pay an additional $567m—on top of a prior $375m jury award—into a state fund addressing youth mental health harms linked to Facebook and Instagram, including failures around child sexual exploitation. The ruling, using New Mexico’s public-nuisance law, also forces new safety measures: stronger age-assurance, default protections, clearer safety banners, school reporting portals, and data deletion for under‑13s. HN discussion centers on whether this is a meaningful deterrent, the breadth of the legal theory, and how far regulation should go.
