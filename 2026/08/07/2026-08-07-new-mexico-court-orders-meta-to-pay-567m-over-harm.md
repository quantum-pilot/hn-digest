# New Mexico court orders Meta to pay $567m over harms to children’s mental health

- Score: 711 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49204352) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/06/new-mexico-court-meta

### TL;DR

A New Mexico judge ordered Meta to fund $567 million in remediation after a jury found it knowingly harmed children’s mental health and concealed child sexual exploitation, bringing total penalties to $942 million. Of the fund, $420 million supports youth treatment; the rest covers prevention, screening, and costs over five years. Meta must improve age assurance, default protections, school reporting, disclosures, data deletion, and compliance reporting, though the court declined universal age verification. Meta will appeal. HN debated whether the amount deters Meta and whether public-nuisance law can survive review.

### Comment pulse

- Relative to New Mexico’s population and estimated local revenue, commenters argued $942 million is unusually large, not a routine global-company fine.
- Critics questioned using a sweeping public-nuisance statute — counterpoint: supporters said platform design burdened schools, hospitals, law enforcement, and public health.
- Discussion split between addictive feeds and trafficking failures, with some noting the case centered substantially on child exploitation and moderation.
