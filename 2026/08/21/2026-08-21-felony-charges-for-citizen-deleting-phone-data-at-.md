# Felony charges for citizen deleting phone data at US Border

- Score: 987 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49386895) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/21/us/politics/samuel-tunick-deleted-phone-felony.html

### TL;DR

Federal prosecutors charged Samuel Tunick with obstruction after a passcode he gave Customs and Border Protection erased his GrapheneOS phone during a January 2025 Atlanta airport search. Authorities say border officers may inspect devices and that destroying data to prevent lawful search warrants prosecution. Tunick, a left-wing activist secretly placed on a watch list, argues the case targets privacy tools and political activity; he faces up to five years. Comments debated search legality, evidentiary intent, encryption versus deletion, and whether technical workarounds reduce or increase risk.
