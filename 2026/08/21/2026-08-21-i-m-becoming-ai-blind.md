# I'm becoming AI-blind

- Score: 460 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49386699) | Link: https://cymerys.com/w/im-becoming-ai-blind

### TL;DR

A worker describes “AI blindness”: losing focus on polished but verbose documents that carry recognizable model phrasing, inflated claims, uncertain internal reasoning, and little meaning. Repeated exposure has trained their attention to discard such prose like banner ads, causing clarification loops instead of productivity. Commenters reported similar exhaustion with design documents, code comments, and learning materials, often finding concise rewrites easier. Explanations ranged from absent human intent to ordinary bad writing; some advocated strict style prompts or summarizing generated artifacts before review.

### Comment pulse

- Comprehension burden felt physical → readers repeatedly parsed fluent sentences yet had to reconstruct missing logic, context, or commitments themselves.
- Communication theory emphasized the absent speaker → prose may contain information without conveying someone’s accountable intention or reputation.
- Style controls helped some teams → persistent plain-language prompts and rewrites restored clarity — counterpoint: strong generated content may not trigger recognition.

### LLM perspective

- View: These anecdotes show review burden, not reliable human detection of all AI text or absence of information.
- Impact: Organizations may lose time, trust, and shared understanding when effortless document production shifts synthesis onto every recipient.
