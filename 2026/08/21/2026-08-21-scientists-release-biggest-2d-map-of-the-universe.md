# Scientists release biggest 2D map of the universe

- Score: 241 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49392200) | Link: https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2026/08/10/scientists-release-biggest-2d-map-of-the-universe/

### TL;DR

The DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys released a public 5.6-trillion-pixel color map containing nearly four billion mostly stellar and galactic objects across about 75% of the sky in visible and near-infrared light. Built from 263,407 ground-based exposures plus WISE and other public data, it helps researchers find rare or transient phenomena and supplies targets for DESI’s three-dimensional dark-energy survey. Combining images from 2,285 nights required eight weeks on the Perlmutter supercomputer. Comments mixed cosmic awe and viewer-access trouble with debate over future astronomy funding; replies cited major observatories already arriving.
