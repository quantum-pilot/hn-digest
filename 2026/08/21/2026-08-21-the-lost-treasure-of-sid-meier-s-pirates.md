# The Lost Treasure of Sid Meier's Pirates

- Score: 265 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49384896) | Link: https://remapradio.com/articles/the-lost-treasure-of-sid-meiers-pirates/

### TL;DR

The complete freely accessible preview explains how 1987’s Pirates! broke from MicroProse’s simulations and wargames, joining unconventional duels, trade, aging, exploration, and family quests into a thematic Caribbean world. Its unstable genre reflected an era before design conventions hardened: instead of merely stacking minigames, it translated pirate fiction into interacting systems and reshaped MicroProse’s work. The preview says this 1980s computer-game lineage faded because its market was tiny beside consoles. The member-only remainder was unavailable and is not covered.
