# Japan tried to build an operating system for the world, the US intervened

- Score: 370 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49384180) | Link: https://www.xda-developers.com/japan-tried-build-operating-system-entire-world-us-government-intervened/

### TL;DR

Japan’s government-backed TRON project began in 1984 as an open, royalty-free stack spanning custom processors, desktop, embedded, mainframe, and coordination systems. BTRON’s desktop replaced files with typed, linked document parts and supported unusually broad character encoding. A 1989 U.S. trade report criticized Japanese procurement advantages for schools and telecom; no sanctions followed, but reputational damage accelerated withdrawals from an already troubled project. Comments challenged the intervention framing and emphasized standards, execution, and market moats. Meanwhile royalty-free ITRON became widespread embedded infrastructure, though deployment estimates remain imprecise.
