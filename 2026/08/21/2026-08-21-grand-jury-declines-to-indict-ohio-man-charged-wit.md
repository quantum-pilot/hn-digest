# Grand jury declines to indict Ohio man charged with destroying Flock camera

- Score: 682 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49387497) | Link: https://san.com/cc/grand-jury-declines-to-indict-ohio-man-charged-with-destroying-flock-camera/

### TL;DR

An Ohio grand jury declined to indict Cody Morelock on felony vandalism allegations that he disassembled a Flock license-plate camera, pole, and solar panel, causing more than $1,000 damage; charges were dismissed. The article situates the case within expanding resistance to Flock’s shared police database and reported officer misuse. Commenters stressed that grand-jury rejection is unusual but secret and unexplained, so it cannot establish privacy-driven nullification rather than insufficient evidence. They also noted “disassembled” is narrower than the headline’s “destroying.”
