# Kagi added a setting for removing paywalled links from search results

- Score: 1206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49388154) | Link: https://kagi.com/changelog#11296

### TL;DR

Kagi’s release adds an option to remove paywalled links automatically, alongside a revamped stocks and ETF widget with price charts. Assistant gains rendered links, Markdown and LaTeX, searchable and sortable threads, folder filtering, and temporary-thread retention of 24 hours, seven days, or 30 days, plus numerous fixes. Commenters welcomed filtering results they would never subscribe to and praised paid search and Assistant. Debate split over journalism: micropayments might improve access—counterpoint: quality reporting needs stable subscriptions, and hiding paid sources may discard valuable work.

### Comment pulse

- Broad Kagi endorsements annoyed some readers, who found low-detail praise uninformative or suspicious despite being subscribers themselves.
- Users wanted subscription-aware filtering, stronger News and Shopping products, and redirects for sites blocking logged-out readers.
- Archive-rewrite suggestions drew an ethical objection: charging for produced work is not begging.

### LLM perspective

- View: The filter improves control but treats accessibility as a proxy for usefulness.
- Impact: Searchers avoid dead-end clicks while publishers risk losing discovery among potential subscribers.
- Watch next: Paywall-detection accuracy, subscription allowlists, ranking effects, default behavior, and News integration.
