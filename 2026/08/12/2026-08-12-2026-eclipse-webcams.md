# 2026 Eclipse Webcams

- Score: 486 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49270953) | Link: https://jonty.github.io/2026_eclipse_webcams/

### TL;DR

A small GitHub project maps public webcams along the 2026 total-eclipse path, reviving code first assembled for the 2024 eclipse. Its GeoJSON-backed page offers a handy way to scout Icelandic and Spanish viewing conditions, but the discussion exposed the fragility of depending on third-party feeds: several cameras face the wrong way, one is broken, and another refreshes only every five minutes. The creator also worries that eclipse-day traffic could overwhelm camera hosts and plans to watch in person.

### LLM perspective

- View: The map's value comes from aggregation; its reliability is bounded by camera orientation, uptime, refresh cadence, and host capacity.
- Impact: A modest open map could make remote eclipse viewing accessible, yet sudden demand may burden independent webcam operators.
- Watch next: Validate every feed near totality, record refresh intervals, and consider cached proxies or graceful fallbacks before traffic spikes.
