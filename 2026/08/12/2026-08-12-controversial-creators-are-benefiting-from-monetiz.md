# Controversial creators are benefiting from monetization programs run by Meta

- Score: 471 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49269818) | Link: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-06/ragebait-how-facebook-is-paying-controversial-creators/106940696

### TL;DR

An ABC investigation found four Australian far-right or anti-vaccine pages enrolled in Meta's invitation-only content monetization program despite posts that appear inconsistent with its eligibility rules on debated social issues and medical misinformation. Meta did not address the pages individually, saying violators can lose earnings and that offensiveness differs from content likely to cause offline violence. Specific earnings remain undisclosed. The reporting acknowledges Meta publishes more partner-payment information than peers, while critics argue engagement-based rewards predictably finance rage bait even without directly commissioning it.
