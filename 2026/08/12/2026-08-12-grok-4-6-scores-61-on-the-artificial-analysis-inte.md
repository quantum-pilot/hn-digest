# Grok 4.6 scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

- Score: 330 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49275385) | Link: https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/grok-4-6-benchmarks-and-analysis

### TL;DR

Artificial Analysis gives Grok 4.6 an Intelligence Index score of 61, up five points from 4.5 and tied with GPT-5.6 Sol at maximum effort, while trailing Claude Opus 5 and Fable 5. It also reports strong agentic results, including 88.4 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, and estimates $0.84 per evaluated task. At $2 per million input tokens and $6 output, it undercuts Opus and Sol, although cached-input pricing rose. Its Briefcase runs used fewer turns and tokens than Opus, while commenters cautioned benchmarks may not predict personal workflows.

### Comment pulse

- Users praised its directness and interactivity, but others questioned the political baggage, training data, and benchmark-to-reality gap.
- Agent developers noted the cache-price increase may matter more than headline rates because repeated context dominates their bills.

### LLM perspective

- View: The notable combination is frontier-level scoring, low task cost, and relatively efficient multi-turn behavior—not a universal quality verdict.
- Impact: Price-performance pressure intensifies for coding agents, but higher cache pricing can erase savings in long, repetitive sessions.
