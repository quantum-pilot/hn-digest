# CFTC declares market emergency, orders Kalshi to continue to operate in New York

- Score: 216 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49266277) | Link: https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9281-26

### TL;DR

The CFTC invoked emergency authority after Kalshi notified it of a New York attorney general complaint, ordering the exchange to keep operating under federal derivatives-law core principles. The agency says New York seeks a temporary restraining order that could halt all event contracts nationwide and demands more than $36 billion, framing the case as a threat to a uniform federal market. Commenters sharply disputed that characterization, arguing the requested relief concerns New York activity, while others noted Kalshi's New York headquarters could still create nationwide operational effects.

### Comment pulse

- Several readers said the CFTC release misstates the complaint's requested scope; others compared federal preemption to nationally regulated securities markets.
- Critics rejected the derivatives framing altogether, describing event contracts as gambling and raising concerns about youth access and regulatory capture.
