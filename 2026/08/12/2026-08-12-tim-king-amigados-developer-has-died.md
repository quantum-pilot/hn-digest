# Tim King, AmigaDOS developer, has died

- Score: 273 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49272655) | Link: https://amiga-news.de/en/news/AN-2026-08-00070-EN.html

### TL;DR

Dr. Tim King died at the end of July, according to his family. While studying computer science at Cambridge, where he earned a Ph.D. in 1979, he developed the BCPL-based Tripos preemptive multitasking operating system. He brought Tripos to MetaComCo in 1984, where it evolved into AmigaDOS. King later founded Perihelion, working on operating systems, parallel processing, and transputers, and then founded UK Online. Commenters remembered both his technical influence and a kind, generous manner toward less-experienced colleagues.
