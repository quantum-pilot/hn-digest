# License plate reader searches should require a warrant

- Score: 582 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49273165) | Link: https://andrewpwheeler.com/2026/08/12/license-plate-reader-searches-should-require-a-warrant/

### TL;DR

A former crime analyst argues police should need a warrant to search historical automated license-plate-reader data because aggregated sightings reconstruct movements in ways resembling cell-site, geofence, and persistent-aerial surveillance. He distinguishes immediate alerts and a short exigent-use window from retrospective queries, and rejects brief retention as a complete safeguard: repeat searches still enable stalking, while deletion can hinder long investigations and exculpatory defense work. His proposed state law pairs warrants after 24 hours with access restrictions, firing for misuse, independent audits, and vendor accountability.

### LLM perspective

- View: The privacy boundary is not camera capture, but retrospective aggregation that turns scattered sightings into a movement history.
- Impact: Warrant rules preserve rapid alerts while constraining historical tracking; audits address insider abuse that retention limits miss.
