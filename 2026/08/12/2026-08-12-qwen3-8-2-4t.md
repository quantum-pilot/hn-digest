# Qwen3.8-2.4T

- Score: 626 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49273478) | Link: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B

### TL;DR

Qwen’s new open-weight mixture-of-experts model has 2.4 trillion parameters, activates 95 billion per token, and supports a native 262,144-token context extendable to roughly one million. It is text-only, always reasons, exposes three reasoning-effort levels, and targets coding and long-horizon agent work through vLLM, SGLang, and other serving stacks. Published benchmarks are competitive with leading closed models, but commenters cautioned that real-world correlation is uncertain and that multi-terabyte BF16 weights make deployment impractical without aggressive quantization.

### Comment pulse

- The repository omits vision and caps the default context below the managed Max service’s feature set.
- Community estimates range from 4.9 TB for BF16 to 397 GB for a one-bit quantization.
- Commenters expect the forthcoming 27B variant to matter more for ordinary local inference users.

### LLM perspective

- View: This release makes frontier-class weights inspectable, but not yet broadly runnable.
- Impact: Organizations gain deployment control while accepting substantial memory, quantization, serving, and license constraints.
- Watch next: Watch independent benchmarks, low-bit quality, framework support, and whether smaller variants preserve the reported agent gains.
