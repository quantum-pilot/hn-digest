# uBlock Origin Is Giving Up the Fight to Keep Ads Off Facebook

- Score: 511 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49270726) | Link: https://digitalescapetools.com/2026/08/ublock-origin-stops-chasing-facebook-ads.html

### TL;DR

A post in the uBlock Origin community said Facebook support would end because the platform can observe public filter development and repeatedly counter it to restore ads. Discussion framed this as an asymmetric contest: randomized, deeply nested markup raises volunteer maintenance costs and may also harm accessibility. Visual or model-based blocking could hide ads despite obfuscation, but would not prevent downloads, tracking, auctions, or malware exposure. Leaving Facebook is the cleanest escape, yet groups, family ties, events, emergency information, and Marketplace preserve strong lock-in.
