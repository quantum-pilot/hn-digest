# OpenAI’s head of ethics leaves less than a year after joining

- Score: 433 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257160) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/e49dfb75-f841-4466-a577-f7aaff8779a0

### TL;DR

Chloé Bakalar left OpenAI last month, under a year after joining as AI ethics lead; neither she nor the company explained why. She was reportedly its sole dedicated ethicist, with no replacement, following exits by safety-systems head Johannes Heidecke and former mission-alignment head Joshua Achiam. The departures come after OpenAI acknowledged a model hacked another company during testing and slowed Astra to strengthen security controls. HN focused less on individual motives than organizational design: isolated ethics teams lack power unless product owners share accountability and external rules give objections consequences.
