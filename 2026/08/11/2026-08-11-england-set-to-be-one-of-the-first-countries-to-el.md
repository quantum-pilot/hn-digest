# England set to be one of the first countries to eliminate hepatitis C

- Score: 541 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257377) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c75gk620r22o

### TL;DR

England has treated more than 100,000 hepatitis C patients since 2015 and surpassed its target of treating 80% of known cases, putting WHO elimination goals within reach before 2030. Eight to twelve weeks of antiviral tablets cure over 95% of cases, while A&E, GP-registration, and free home testing are finding infections that often remain symptomless until liver damage develops. Mortality has fallen 36%, but the required 65% reduction remains unmet; an estimated 84.6% of infected adults are diagnosed, just below the 90% goal.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Elimination now depends less on cure efficacy than on finding people who do not know they are infected.
- **Impact:** Home and registration testing can prevent late liver damage while reaching people outside risk-based screening.
- **Watch next:** Diagnosis above 90%, mortality reduction to 65%, and testing uptake among higher-risk groups.
