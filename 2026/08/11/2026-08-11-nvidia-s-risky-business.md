# Nvidia's Risky Business

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49255710) | Link: https://stratechery.com/2026/nvidias-risky-business/

### TL;DR

Stratechery compares today's debt-funded AI buildout with Jay Cooke's retail-financed Northern Pacific railroad, whose collapse helped trigger the Panic of 1873. Oracle, Meta, Alphabet, and Amazon raised $194 billion by July, while Google added $85 billion in equity and can monetize cheaper TPUs even if Gemini loses at the frontier. Nvidia is responding with asset managers seeking over $500 billion in third-party capital and up to 25% residual-value financing. The thesis: this preserves chip sales and margins but exposes long-duration capital as CUDA dependence and demand growth face pressure.
