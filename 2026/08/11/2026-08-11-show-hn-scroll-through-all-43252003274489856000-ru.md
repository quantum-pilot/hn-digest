# Show HN: Scroll through all 43252003274489856000 Rubik's Cube states

- Score: 288 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49251179) | Link: https://everycube.alen.is/

### TL;DR

Every Cube maps the 43,252,003,274,489,856,000 reachable Rubik's Cube configurations to an integer and renders the selected state live in 3D as the user scrolls or drags a ruler. There is no stored catalog: rank/unrank math decomposes the index into corner and edge permutations and orientations, while tested move logic locates superflip and checkerboard markers. The static demo begins with gentle orientation changes. HN admired the visualization, estimated exhaustive scrolling would take 9.5 years at lightspeed wheel motion, and proposed a Hamiltonian ordering where adjacent states differ by one move.
