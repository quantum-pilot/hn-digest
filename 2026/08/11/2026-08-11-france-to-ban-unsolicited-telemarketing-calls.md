# France to ban unsolicited telemarketing calls

- Score: 1027 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49254880) | Link: https://www.lemonde.fr/en/france/article/2026/08/06/france-to-ban-unsolicited-telemarketing-calls-from-august-11_6756208_7.html

### TL;DR

France now requires prior consent for telemarketing calls, replacing an opt-out registry that call centers often ignored. Consent can be withdrawn, while existing contractual relationships allow some commercial offers. Consumers can report violations online; penalties reach €75,000 per call for individuals and €375,000 for companies. The law responds to weekly unwanted calls affecting roughly three-quarters of residents, but could threaten 40,000–50,000 Moroccan call-center jobs because France supplies over 80% of that sector’s revenue. Commenters broadly favored shifting control to recipients.

### Comment pulse

- Enforcement remains the challenge: readers proposed authenticated caller IDs, carrier liability, whitelists, or direct compensation for documented violations.
- Some said opt-out lists already work when backed by law — counterpoint: spoofing and offshore intermediaries can evade national regimes.
- Data leakage and resale were seen as upstream causes that a calling ban alone cannot fix.
