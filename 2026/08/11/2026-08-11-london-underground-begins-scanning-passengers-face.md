# London Underground begins scanning passengers' faces

- Score: 337 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49255496) | Link: https://www.btp.police.uk/news/btp/news/england/btp-expands-live-facial-recognition-lfr-trial-into-london-underground-stations/

### TL;DR

British Transport Police expanded a live facial-recognition trial into London Underground stations, beginning at Victoria and rotating deployments through November. Temporary cameras compare passersby against a watchlist of people wanted for high-harm offenses using NEC NeoFace M40; an officer reviews matches before engagement. BTP says nonmatches are deleted immediately, alerts within 24 hours, and ordinary CCTV after 31 days, with signage and alternative routes available. A visitor’s account challenged the notice safeguards, describing poorly positioned signs and police hostility toward photographing the setup.
