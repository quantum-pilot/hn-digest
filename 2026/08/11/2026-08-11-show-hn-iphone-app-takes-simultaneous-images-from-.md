# Show HN: iPhone app takes simultaneous images from 2 lenses, fuses into 1 photo

- Score: 285 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49226623) | Link: https://photosynthesis.camera

### TL;DR

Photosynthesis is an upcoming iPhone camera app that fires two rear lenses together, then stitches the wider frame with the more detailed center captured by the second lens, without generative AI. It aligns color and geometry automatically, provides manual seam and ghosting repair, supports 1×+0.5× on two-lens phones and zoom pairings on Pro models, and exports layered or spatial formats. The creator says a quality mode substitutes sequential Deep Fusion shots when better. HN liked the optical idea but questioned comparison integrity, native-camera overlap, seam quality, and export-limited pricing.
