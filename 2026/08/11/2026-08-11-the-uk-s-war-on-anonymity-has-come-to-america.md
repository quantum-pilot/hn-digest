# The UK's war on anonymity has come to America

- Score: 651 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49251411) | Link: https://www.effort.news/uk-lobby

### TL;DR

Effort argues that five foreign NGOs and US affiliates are importing Britain's age-assurance playbook into 21 statehouses and Congress, using child-safety policy to normalize digital identity and weaken anonymous internet use. It traces overlapping leadership, contracts, and lobbying: 5Rights engaged on 42 bills in 18 states, 11 now law; Reset Tech Action reported $1.35 million in lobbying; ISD received government funding. HN broadly opposed universal identification but challenged the article's UK-centric causality, noting US porn-ID laws developed earlier or in parallel and debating parent-controlled device modes versus society-wide safeguards.

### Comment pulse
