# Mojo 1.0

- Score: 386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49261128) | Link: https://www.modular.com/blog/modular-26-5-mojo-1-0-is-here

### TL;DR

Mojo 1.0 marks Modular’s commitment to a steadier, production-ready language after years of breaking changes. The release standardizes declarations, closures, and pointers; adds Python-style lambdas, a more reliable LSP, AI skills, and diagnostics for invalidated references. During 1.x, development should be mainly additive, while future work targets async programming, pattern matching, unions, and broader systems use across CPUs, GPUs, and accelerators. Nearly 200 contributors shaped the open standard library, but the compiler and toolchain remain closed pending a promised 2026 release.
