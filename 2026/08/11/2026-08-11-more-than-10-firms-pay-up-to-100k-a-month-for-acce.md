# More than 10 firms pay up to $100k a month for access to Truth Social posts

- Score: 258 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49255672) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ce3q5nxpgk1o

### TL;DR

Trump Media says more than 10 customers, mainly high-frequency trading firms, pay $60,000–$100,000 monthly for Truth API, launched in early August to deliver influential Truth Social posts ahead of the public feed. It expects durable revenue from traders reacting to President Trump's market-moving statements, while his family remains majority owner. The offering arrives amid a $238 million quarterly loss on $1.7 million revenue and a wider push into crypto and investments. HN overwhelmingly viewed monetized priority access to presidential communications as self-dealing that erodes trust in US markets.
