# Woman pulled over twice after Flock-linked software connected her to homicide

- Score: 254 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49261218) | Link: https://guessingheadlights.com/yall-failed-me-woman-pulled-over-at-gunpoint-twice-after-flock-camera-glitch/

### TL;DR

Amber Newell was stopped at gunpoint twice in one week after a Flock automated license-plate camera matched her car to a homicide alert that Milwaukee police no longer needed but failed to remove. Brookfield's chief says officers correctly treated the live alert as dangerous; Newell and her young daughter were left fearful of another stop. HN rejected framing this as a simple forgotten-delete mistake: real-time interception amplifies stale hotlist data, rewards speed over verification, and can turn ordinary records errors into repeated life-threatening encounters.
