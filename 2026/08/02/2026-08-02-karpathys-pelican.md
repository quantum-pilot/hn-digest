# Karpathy’s Pelican

- Score: 404 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49140998) | Link: https://twitter.com/karpathy/status/2083749667410727319

### TL;DR

Karpathy gave Opus 5 the opening of The Lord of the Rings, a one-million-token budget costing about $10, and two hours to generate 5,500 lines of Three.js that procedurally animate the passage. The janky result suggests custom interactive worlds can become cheap enough to create on demand, but also exposes weak visual auditing: the model relied on slow screenshots and could not efficiently watch or play its output. HN treated such scenes as unsaturated spatial-reasoning benchmarks, while disputing whether Three.js fluency measures general understanding or trained specialization.

### Comment pulse
