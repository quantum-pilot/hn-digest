# Show HN: I'm a 15 Year Old Wannabe Engineer, This Is a Cycloidal Gearbox I Built

- Score: 315 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49140396) | Link: https://github.com/tom-ilan/cycloidal_gearbox

### TL;DR

A 15-year-old maker built a parametric Fusion 360 generator and iterated through three 3D-printed cycloidal gearboxes. The hand-cranked prototype validated a 1:9 profile; a NEMA 17-sized version failed because printing could not meet tight tolerances; enlarging the third version produced a working 90 mm PLA drive. Measured output was 1.3 N·m from a 0.21 N·m motor at 66% efficiency, using 0.15 mm clearance. HN praised the documented failure-to-success iteration and explained cycloidal drives’ low backlash, multiple contacts, and robotics-friendly torque.
