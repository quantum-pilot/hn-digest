# You Know GDPR Is Good Based on Who Hates It

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49488282) | Link: https://matduggan.com/you-know-gdpr-is-good-based-on-who-hates-it/

### TL;DR

The author defends GDPR as a necessary counterweight to surveillance capitalism, arguing that hostile industry reaction demonstrates the regulation constrains profitable data extraction. Cookie banners are framed as deliberately annoying compliance choices rather than legal requirements, while Europe’s market size and enforcement institutions spread privacy standards abroad through the “Brussels Effect.” Commenters sharply divide over results: practitioners report better data-handling decisions, while critics cite banner fatigue, persistent tracking, compliance costs, weak consequences after breaches, and tension between privacy regulation and opposition to anonymity tools.

### Comment pulse

- Blame manipulative implementation → GDPR requires meaningful, rejectable consent, not obstructive banners — counterpoint: critics see little privacy improvement despite constant friction.
- Compliance changes behavior → practitioners say legal exposure forces deliberate handling of personal data rather than default collection.
- Regulation exports standards → supporters value EU consumer protections, while small businesses report market-entry and design burdens.
