# Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49490870) | Link: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/roman-space-telescope/

### TL;DR

NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope was scheduled to launch August 30, 2026, aboard a Falcon Heavy. With a field of view at least 100 times larger than Hubble's, Roman is designed to survey dark energy, dark matter, exoplanets, and infrared astrophysics, potentially measuring light from a billion galaxies. It will also block starlight to image exoplanets and disks directly. Commenters emphasized wide-field survey speed, anticipated public data, and complementary observations with Rubin, Hubble, and Webb.
