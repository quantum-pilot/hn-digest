# Hy4 preview

- Score: 260 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49492632) | Link: https://www.tencent.com/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/

### TL;DR

Tencent released Hy4 preview as an open-source mixture-of-experts model with 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active parameters, and a context window exceeding one million tokens. Tencent claims gains across coding, office work, games, science, and a 31.8% inference-throughput improvement from model-assisted optimization. Its internal expert evaluation narrowly beat two named rivals, while API pricing emphasizes low cache cost. Commenters liked observed output quality but questioned vendor-controlled benchmarks, usage statistics, chart presentation, and grand claims of recursive self-improvement.
