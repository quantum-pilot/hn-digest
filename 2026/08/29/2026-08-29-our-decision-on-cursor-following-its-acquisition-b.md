# Our decision on Cursor following its acquisition by SpaceX

- Score: 810 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49486172) | Link: https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/

### TL;DR

OpenAI says it plans to end model access through Cursor on November 12, using a change-of-control cancellation window after SpaceX acquired the editor. It cites prior contract and terms-of-service violations by Elon Musk's companies and says it cannot ensure future compliance, especially for its upcoming Astra model. OpenAI is giving the maximum contractual notice and promises transition support. Commenters debated distillation, competitive retaliation, inconsistent copyright positions, and whether users should migrate, bring their own keys, or choose alternative editors and agents.
