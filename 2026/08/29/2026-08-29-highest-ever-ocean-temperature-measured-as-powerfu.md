# Highest-ever ocean temperature measured as powerful El Niño forms

- Score: 147 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49494231) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/environment/story/2026-08-26/highest-ever-ocean-temperature-measured-as-powerful-el-nino-forms

### TL;DR

Copernicus reported a global average sea-surface temperature of 70°F on August 22, the highest daily value in its record beginning in 1979 and above the previous March 2024 peak. Scientists attribute the record to a powerful El Niño layered onto decades of human-driven warming. The unusual August timing is concerning because global ocean temperatures normally peak in March or April. Warmer seas raise sea levels, intensify atmospheric heat and moisture, threaten marine and coastal systems, and absorb less carbon dioxide.
