# Lawmakers added $1 to car insurance policies. That money paid for Flock cameras

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49494182) | Link: https://www.texastribune.org/2026/08/28/texas-flock-cameras-auto-insurance-fee-mvcpa-grants/

### TL;DR

A Texas Tribune analysis found at least $30 million from a $1 annual auto-insurance fee increase helped fund roughly 3,200 Flock license-plate cameras. The 2023 law targeted catalytic-converter theft but never mentioned readers; some sponsoring lawmakers said surveillance was not intended. Police credit searches with cleared cases and recovered vehicles, while critics cite warrantless interstate tracking and documented misuse. After the Tribune's inquiries, Governor Greg Abbott paused state funding for local Flock grants. Commenters disputed whether the network actually reduced theft.
