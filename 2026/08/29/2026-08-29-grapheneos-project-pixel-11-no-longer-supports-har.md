# GrapheneOS project: pixel 11 no longer supports hardware memory tagging (MTE)

- Score: 284 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49490702) | Link: https://bsky.app/profile/grapheneos.org/post/3mua32q4ds22e

### TL;DR

GrapheneOS says its partial Pixel 11 port cannot be completed because ARM Memory Tagging Extension support appears absent across software, firmware, and probably hardware. The project alleges Google removed the security feature to reduce cost, although the brief post provides no independent hardware confirmation. Commenters describe MTE as central to GrapheneOS memory-safety hardening and view supporting the phone as a rollback to pre-Pixel 8 security. Discussion also criticizes reduced RAM, higher pricing, modest performance gains, and other recent Pixel compromises.

### Comment pulse

- Existing Pixel 9 owners felt vindicated, while Pixel 10 and 11 users reported region-dependent SIM differences and disputed broader model quality.
- GrapheneOS supporters said forced allocator-level MTE can protect apps that do not opt in, making hardware removal especially consequential.
- Some expect future Motorola support to offer an alternative once current Pixels reach end of life.
