# StemDeck, a free, open-source and local AI stem separator

- Score: 218 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49486081) | Link: https://github.com/stemdeckapp/stemdeck

### TL;DR

StemDeck is a free, open-source interface around the existing Demucs six-stem model, processing audio locally without accounts, quotas, or uploads. It accepts common audio formats and optional YouTube URLs, then offers waveform playback, looping, per-stem mixing, analysis, and exports through desktop or self-hosted builds. The project candidly positions itself below commercial tools in model quality, polish, batching, and mobile support. Commenters welcome the accessible packaging while noting it introduces no new separation model and suggesting stronger alternatives for DJ workflows.

### Comment pulse

- Packaging is the contribution → the project combines Demucs, FFmpeg, analysis, installers, and a multitrack interface rather than advancing model quality.
- Local processing appeals → privacy, zero subscription cost, and open-source deployment trade against slower CPUs and fewer professional features.
- Existing alternatives vary → commenters mention paid DJ-integrated models and Audacity’s OpenVINO plugins for different workflows.
