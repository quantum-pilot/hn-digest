# Calibrate Before You Accelerate: Bias Toward Action in a New Role

- Score: 145 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49491714) | Link: https://tucker.wales/writing/bias-towards-action/

### TL;DR

New hires often rush to prove value, but action without organizational context can damage systems they do not yet understand. The author proposes three phases: collect information by mapping stakeholders, reading history, and investigating existing constraints; synthesize recurring problems and separate quick wins from systemic work; then accelerate through a visible low-risk improvement and a written hypothesis reviewed by colleagues. Commenters contrasted chaotic leaders who changed everything immediately with methodical ones, while noting that urgent failures may still require careful experimentation.
