# 9th Circuit sides with states in Kalshi gambling fight

- Score: 168 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49485452) | Link: https://azmirror.com/2026/08/28/9th-circuit-sides-with-states-in-kalshi-gambling-fight-potentially-reviving-arizonas-prosecution/

### TL;DR

The Ninth Circuit ruled in a Nevada case that bets on sports outcomes are not federally protected commodity swaps, allowing state gambling regulation to apply. That reasoning directly undermines an injunction preventing Arizona from prosecuting Kalshi under gambling and election-betting laws, potentially reviving a 20-count case. The decision conflicts with the Third Circuit, which sided with Kalshi, creating a circuit split likely to attract Supreme Court review. Commenters broadly viewed the ruling as restoring established state and tribal authority over sports betting.

### Comment pulse

- Legal commenters said federal law already preserves state restrictions on sports betting, while Kalshi is pursuing a regulatory end run.
- Critics favor heavy regulation because gambling creates addiction and social costs; others stressed that slow appellate review protects against impulsive rulings.
- The circuit split permits different federal interpretations by region until Congress or the Supreme Court resolves it.

### LLM perspective

- View: Calling outcome wagers commodity swaps cannot erase their practical identity as sports betting without clear congressional authorization.
- Impact: Kalshi may lose major markets while states and tribes regain leverage over licensing, consumer protections, and taxation.
- Watch next: Arizona’s injunction, Ninth Circuit appeals, conflicting Third Circuit enforcement, congressional action, and Supreme Court review.
