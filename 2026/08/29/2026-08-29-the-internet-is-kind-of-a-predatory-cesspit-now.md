# The internet is kind of a predatory cesspit now

- Score: 415 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49492193) | Link: https://www.stephendiehl.com/posts/internet_predatory_cesspit/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that online predation moved from an abuse of the internet to its organizing logic: platforms profile vulnerability, optimize engagement, and turn anxious consumers into unpaid distributors for scams, grievance, gambling, supplements, and other recurring pitches. It links this system to precarity, isolation, algorithmic incentives, and cheaper synthetic persuasion from language models. Commenters recognize worsening screen compulsion but challenge the article’s historical pessimism, suspected AI-assisted prose, and implication that useful, human-scale corners of the web have disappeared.
