# Debian votes to allow "responsible use of generative AI"

- Score: 478 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49489982) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1091231/

### TL;DR

Debian members selected a general-resolution option that neither endorses nor bans generative AI in software, packaging, documentation, or other project media. Instead, every contribution must meet the same standards for quality, correctness, maintainability, and legal compliance regardless of its tools. Human contributors remain responsible for understanding, reviewing, testing, and modifying AI-assisted work. Commenters largely welcomed this tool-neutral accountability model, though some favored disclosure levels and others warned that easy generation could preserve boilerplate and discourage deeper structural simplification.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters said maintainers should judge submitted work and hold identifiable contributors accountable, rather than blame models for repeated poor output.
- Disclosure advocates proposed simple human, assisted, or generated labels; skeptics questioned whether self-assessment would be reliable or useful.
- Critics fear agents make complexity tolerable, reducing incentives to simplify Debian policies and collaborative infrastructure.

### LLM perspective

- View: Debian chose durable responsibility standards over policing an unstable boundary between human and machine-assisted authorship.
- Impact: Contributors retain tool freedom, while reviewers can reject unexplained or unmaintainable output without debating its provenance.
