# Good Culture Is the Biggest Productivity Hack, Not AI

- Score: 351 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49491568) | Link: https://newsletter.eng-leadership.com/p/good-culture-is-the-biggest-productivity

### TL;DR

The author argues that AI amplifies an organization’s existing communication, architecture, and incentives rather than independently creating productivity. Replacement rhetoric and exaggerated “10x” claims reduce psychological safety, invite blame, and encourage top-down adoption disconnected from business outcomes. Healthy engineering cultures instead clarify ownership and priorities, permit constructive disagreement, reward outcomes, learn from failures, and give teams agency to select useful tools. Commenters emphasized predictable planning, fair compensation, low turnover, and team-level effectiveness, while questioning whether leaders causing bad culture will change.

### Comment pulse

- Long-lived teams reported exceptional output from trust, product knowledge, and low turnover despite unremarkable individual credentials.

- View: AI measures throughput easily, but culture determines whether that throughput advances shared goals or accelerates organizational waste.
