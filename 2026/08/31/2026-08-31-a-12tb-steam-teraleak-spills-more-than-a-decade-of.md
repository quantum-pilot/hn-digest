# A 12TB Steam “teraleak” spills more than a decade of lost PC gaming history

- Score: 375 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49506182) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2026/08/a-12tb-steam-teraleak-spills-more-than-a-decade-of-lost-pc-gaming-history/

### TL;DR

A 12TB archive circulating by BitTorrent reportedly contains thousands of depots from Steam’s pre-SteamPipe era, spanning 2003–2013. It includes public builds plus prototypes and playtests from Valve and third-party publishers, exposing cut Portal 2 mechanics, early Left 4 Dead 2 and CS:GO material, and files associated with Half-Life 2: Episode 3. Its provenance remains uncertain: observers say files came from publicly accessible infrastructure, but they may have been privately collected years ago. Preservation value competes with piracy, ownership, and legal risks.

### Comment pulse

- Preservationists highlighted otherwise-lost live-service versions and dedicated servers that could restore period-accurate play.
- Portal 2 prototypes drew interest for cut dialogue and mechanics, though commenters disputed whether slow-motion improved accessibility.
- Some users encountered encrypted depots, showing that possession of the archive does not guarantee usable assets.
