# Google Has Removed MV2 Extensions from the Chrome Web Store, Including UBO

- Score: 678 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49514878) | Link: https://webiterate.dev/google-removed-extensions-ublock-origin-108/

### TL;DR

The article reports that Google removed all remaining Manifest V2 extensions from the Chrome Web Store, including uBlock Origin, completing a long-planned transition to Manifest V3. Existing MV2 installs on Chrome 138 or earlier reportedly remain but cannot receive updates or be reinstalled from the store. Because other Chromium browsers also depend on that marketplace, removal extends beyond Chrome even where MV2 still runs. Brave is separately hosting AdGuard, uBlock Origin, uMatrix, and NoScript. Google presents MV3 as improving extension security, privacy, performance, and control.
