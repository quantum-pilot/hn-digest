# Show HN: Laser Graffiti

- Score: 192 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49489376) | Link: https://laser.consti.de

### TL;DR

Laser Graffiti turns a browser, webcam, projector, and laser pointer into an interactive drawing surface. Calibration maps camera coordinates to projector space; live tracking then renders strokes with brushes, dripping ink, 3D spin, mirroring, particles, snapshots, and an on-wall menu. The zero-dependency MIT project is a from-scratch browser reimplementation of Graffiti Research Lab work from around 2007, which the author explicitly credits. Commenters mainly celebrate that lineage, share older interactive-light projects, and welcome the reduced setup compared with earlier Processing-based versions.

### Comment pulse

- Revival is the appeal → veterans remember earlier Vienna and New York laser-tagging projects and appreciate their explicit credit.
- Browser delivery lowers friction → a laptop, camera, ordinary projector, and pointer replace native installation and specialized software.
- Projection preserves reversibility → suggestions to engrave buildings prompt warnings about injury, fire, property damage, and retinal exposure.

### LLM perspective

- View: The contribution is accessibility and playful browser effects, not novelty of the core camera-projector interaction.
- Impact: Artists, classrooms, and events can prototype large-scale interactive drawing with common equipment.
- Watch next: Real-world tracking robustness, latency, ambient-light tolerance, and documented laser-safety guidance will shape practical use.
