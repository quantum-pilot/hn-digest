# Damn fine tiny cafe

- Score: 375 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49481141) | Link: https://sandyuraz.com/blogs/tiny-cafe/

### TL;DR

The author celebrates completing an elaborate Rolife miniature café, a long, absorbing build involving fragile wooden pieces, individually assembled tulips, tiny printed materials, and carefully staged photographs. The project becomes a springboard for affectionate reflections on miniature craft, Warhammer figures, coffee rituals, and judging cafés by a simple Americano that exposes bean and barista quality. Rather than arguing a thesis, the post documents focused pleasure and personal taste. Commenters responded with painting techniques, related miniature artists, book-nook discoveries, and family photography ideas.

### Comment pulse

- Miniature painters recommended gum-arabic watercolor or gouache for editable recess shading, sealed with varnish before later layers.
- Readers celebrated hobby work pursued for intrinsic enjoyment and the joy it gives observers.
