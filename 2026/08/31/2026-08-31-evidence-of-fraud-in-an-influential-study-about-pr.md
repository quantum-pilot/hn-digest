# Evidence of Fraud in an Influential Study About Procrastination

- Score: 237 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49516199) | Link: https://datacolada.org/138

### TL;DR

Data Colada argues that Study 2 of a highly cited 2002 deadlines-and-procrastination paper used severely tampered or fabricated data after a new replication failed. Its analysis reproduces the published statistics from files emailed in 2006, then identifies four anomalies: an implausibly large effect, duplicated proofreading records for 18 of 20 participants in one condition, missing correlations among related measures, and almost no rounding in self-reported minutes. Both original authors requested retraction; the journal said it was considering next steps under COPE guidelines.
