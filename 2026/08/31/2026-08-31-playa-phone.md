# Playa Phone

- Score: 631 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49510514) | Link: https://playaphone.com/

### TL;DR

Playa Phone is a repurposed phone booth at Burning Man that makes free five-minute internet calls worldwide. Visitors can dial friends or strangers, while anyone can call its Nevada number and hope a passerby answers; busy and unanswered calls behave like an ordinary phone. A live page reports connection and call status. Commenters shared memories of the booth enabling spontaneous contact, a surprise unofficial wedding, and earlier playa VoIP projects. Discussion framed interactive, participant-built experiences as the event's enduring appeal despite rising cost and scale.
