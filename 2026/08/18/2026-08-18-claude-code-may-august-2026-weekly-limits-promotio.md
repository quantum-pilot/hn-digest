# Claude Code May–August 2026 weekly limits promotion

- Score: 292 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49348751) | Link: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15910845-claude-code-may-august-2026-weekly-limits-promotion

### TL;DR

Anthropic extended a promotion giving Claude Code users 50% higher weekly limits through August 31, 2026. It applies automatically to Pro, Max, Team, and legacy seat-based Enterprise accounts across CLI, IDE, desktop, and web; Free and consumption-based Enterprise seats are excluded. Five-hour limits, other Claude products, plans, and billing are unchanged, and the page says standard weekly limits return afterward. Commenters debated whether this is a temporary allowance before a painful reduction, citing outages and verbose models, while others reported ample Claude capacity or better experiences with competing systems.

### Comment pulse

- Efficiency versus peak capability dominated: latency, verbosity, consistency, and cost increasingly matter more than marginal benchmark gains.
- Users reported opposite quota experiences across Claude and Codex, implying workload, codebase, and harness affect practical limits.
- A commenter claimed permanence was planned—counterpoint: the supplied official page commits only through August 31 and says limits revert.
