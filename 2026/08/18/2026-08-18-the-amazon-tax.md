# The Amazon tax

- Score: 1363 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49345263) | Link: https://seths.blog/2026/08/the-amazon-tax/

### TL;DR

The essay calls Amazon search ads a compulsory merchant toll rather than demand creation. Ads outrank exact-match products, forcing sellers to bid defensively for shoppers already seeking them; the author’s publisher paid $1 per click on a query naming his book. He argues more than $50 billion in annual spending raises prices, weakens product investment, rewards cheap high-margin goods, and incentivizes worse organic results. Commenters recognized similar behavior across app stores and search, but debated fraud or trademark theories versus lawful comparative advertising and whether the remedy is competition enforcement.
