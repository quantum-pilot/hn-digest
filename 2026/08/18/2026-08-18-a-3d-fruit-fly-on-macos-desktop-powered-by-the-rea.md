# A 3D fruit fly on macOS desktop powered by the real FlyWire connectome

- Score: 373 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49353221) | Link: https://github.com/DenisSergeevitch/desktop-fly

### TL;DR

DesktopFly is an open-source macOS overlay showing a procedural fruit fly that walks on windows, grooms, sleeps, and flees the cursor. A 1kHz leaky-integrate-and-fire simulation uses 668 FlyWire neurons and roughly 19,000 measured synaptic connections; a brain view renders 23,210 soma locations and supports stimulation. Cursor motion becomes modeled looming or wind input, while selected neuron rates trigger hand-built gait, flight, steering, grooming, and sleep behaviors. The author separates measured wiring from assumed dynamics and body geometry. Commenters disputed whether “connectome-powered” overstates scripted behavior gated by a partial neural simulation.
