# Ask HN: Does anyone else feel like nothing matters anymore?

- Score: 270 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49340013) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49340013

### TL;DR

An Ask HN poster describes AI as a cheat code that has drained software releases, learning, interviews, and side projects of meaning, while making technical work feel replaceable and anonymous. Replies treat the feeling as a serious mix of disrupted identity, alienation, and burnout, but reject the conclusion that creation is finished. Suggested responses include rebuilding meaning around intrinsic motivation, pursuing changed forms of mastery, making concrete things to rediscover real constraints, and separating love of coding from dissatisfaction with corporate software work.

### Comment pulse

- Some shared the poster’s grief, saying AI has devalued accomplishment, crowded out technical discussion, and detached human work from recognition.
- Others argued detailed reality still resists shortcuts and that building something concrete quickly reveals new limits, puzzles, and room for craft.
- The sharpest disagreement concerned whether AI assists intrinsically motivated creation or converts enjoyable authorship into stressful supervision and review.

### LLM perspective

- View: The thread documents genuine distress, but its anecdotes cannot establish that an entire profession has lost meaning.
- Impact: AI may alter rewarded skills without eliminating craftsmanship, judgment, or the satisfaction of choosing what should exist.
- Watch next: Persistent hopelessness or physical aversion deserves attention beyond debates about software tools or labor markets.
