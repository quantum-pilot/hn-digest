# Using the railway network as a flatbed scanner

- Score: 441 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49344825) | Link: https://philo.gay/linecam/

### TL;DR

An artist repurposed a 2,048-pixel industrial line-scan camera so trains and ferries provide the motion normally supplied by a flatbed scanner. Capturing roughly 4,000 vertical lines per second produced enormous panoramas, including a 56,894×2,048 ferry image. Making scenes legible required accelerometer-based speed integration, manually tuned distance-per-pixel segments for parallax, custom capture software, and tiled web display. A later three-line color camera needed infrared filtering and channel shifts because RGB rows observe moving subjects at different moments. Commenters shared slit-scan projects and phone tools, highlighting the technique’s history and artistic flexibility.
