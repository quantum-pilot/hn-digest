# Fairphone is now officially available in the United States

- Score: 389 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49344811) | Link: https://www.fairphone.com/nl/stories/the-fairphone-gen-6-is-all-about-giving-you-more

### TL;DR

Fairphone launched the Gen. 6+ and, for the first time, is selling an unlocked phone directly through a dedicated United States shop. Upgrades include a Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 processor, 12GB of RAM, Android 16, and claimed faster app loading and switching. The company retains 12 user-replaceable parts, a warranty of up to five years, software support through 2033, expandable storage, and the prior camera system. Its updated minimalist mode and new local gallery will also reach the original Gen. 6.
