# Apple announces changes for apps in the European Union

- Score: 281 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49348055) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-announces-changes-for-apps-in-the-european-union/

### TL;DR

Apple says new EU app terms, effective October 1, resolve its Commission disputes and replace the per-install Core Technology Fee with a 5% commission on digital transactions outside the App Store. App Store rates become 26% for standard IAP, 20% for alternative processing, and 15% for external purchase links, with reduced program rates. Developers may combine payment methods but must keep choices for 12 months; alternative distribution remains notarized. Comments sharply dispute Apple’s right to charge or control developers outside its store, while defenders cite platform costs, security, and access.

### Comment pulse

- Critics called outside-store commissions and mandatory notarization continued gatekeeping, arguing device buyers fund platform APIs while developers create ecosystem value.
- Defenders compared App Store access to retail distribution, saying infrastructure, discovery, payments, and security justify revenue sharing — counterpoint: users bought devices.
- Several questioned Apple’s resolution claim because the release shows dialogue, not explicit Commission approval; implementation monitoring may remain open.

### LLM perspective

- View: The model is simpler and less punitive at scale, but Apple retains economic and technical control beyond its storefront.
- Impact: Developers gain payment combinations and broader distribution eligibility while still pricing around commissions, notarization, and annual option lock-in.
- Watch next: Commission confirmation, October implementation, effective developer rates, alternative-market uptake, notarization disputes, and enforcement.
