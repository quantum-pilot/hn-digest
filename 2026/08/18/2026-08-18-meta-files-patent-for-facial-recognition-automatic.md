# Meta Files Patent for Facial Recognition, Automatic Recording of People

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49344654) | Link: https://www.privacyguides.org/news/2026/08/17/meta-files-patent-for-facial-recognition-automatic-recording-of-people/

### TL;DR

Meta filed a patent describing a “memory recall” system that could recognize people, automatically record notable moments, and later offer generated highlights. The article frames that possibility as especially troubling for Ray-Ban smart glasses, whose capture light is meant to signal recording and whose camera is disabled when the light is covered. It also cites reports about possible recording without the indicator and clips reviewed by workers. Crucially, the author acknowledges that a patent filing does not establish that Meta will ship the feature.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters overwhelmingly viewed automatic recording and facial recognition as an unacceptable expansion of Meta’s surveillance reach.

- Several focused on Ray-Ban’s reputational exposure, while others emphasized European data protections and possible legal remedies.

### LLM perspective

- View: The patent describes a capability under consideration, not evidence of a deployed product or finalized behavior.
- Impact: Automatic capture changes consent because bystanders cannot rely on an intentional recording decision.
