# How does IKEA come up with names for its products?

- Score: 450 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49349984) | Link: https://www.ikea.com/se/en/customer-service/knowledge/articles/6f564c4d-2ccc-46de-b643-545a3948dc79.html

### TL;DR

IKEA’s naming system began because founder Ingvar Kamprad struggled to remember numeric product codes. Products receive real Swedish words, usually 4–12 letters, preferably containing Å, Ä, or Ö, pleasant to pronounce, untrademarked, and not surnames. Categories constrain sources—sofas use Swedish places, bookshelves men’s names, and children’s products animals or nature—while services use locally descriptive terms. IKEA says candidates are screened for unwanted meanings, affiliations, and global fit, supporting 2,000–3,000 new products yearly. Commenters praised memorable service design but supplied embarrassing cross-language counterexamples and playful, intentional-seeming names.

### Comment pulse

- Swedish cognates and puns such as Fejka and Trådfri delight customers while reinforcing brand identity.
