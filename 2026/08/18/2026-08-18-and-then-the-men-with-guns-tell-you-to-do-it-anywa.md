# And then the men with guns tell you to do it anyway

- Score: 445 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49348912) | Link: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/08/and-then-the-men-with-guns-tell-you-to-do-it-anyway/

### TL;DR

Using Egypt’s 2011 uprising, when authorities compelled carriers to shut networks and send pro-regime SMS while armed men confronted staff, the essay argues technical safeguards ultimately meet physical coercion. It applies that lesson to UK cell-broadcast alerts: governments need authenticated, geographically targeted warnings, yet operator discretion can both resist propaganda and suppress legitimate emergencies. No design can simultaneously compel and permit refusal or guarantee only good use. Commenters emphasized public rules, trust, checks, transparency, and auditability, while examples showed overuse creates alarm fatigue that causes people to disable life-saving channels.
