# Beware Management Consultants

- Score: 609 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49351324) | Link: https://about.iceland.co.uk/our-story/the-dark-ages/beware-management-consultants/

### TL;DR

A satirical rowing parable contrasts a winning team of seven rowers and one captain with a losing team of seven captains and one rower. Auditors identify the imbalance, but consultants answer with a reorganization that preserves managerial excess, adds reporting layers, and offers the rower only conditional non-monetary recognition. After an even worse defeat, leadership fires the rower, rewards itself, blames the boat, commissions a replacement, and outsources rowing. The joke targets bureaucracy’s ability to protect itself while misdiagnosing obvious operational problems.
