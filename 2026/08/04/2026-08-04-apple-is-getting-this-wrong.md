# Apple is getting this wrong

- Score: 277 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49164649) | Link: https://openai.com/index/apple-is-getting-this-wrong/

### TL;DR

OpenAI publicly rejects Apple’s trade-secret allegations, saying Apple’s lawyers confused two former employees, misrepresented a counsel conversation, withheld specifics for months, and are seeking an unnecessary injunction. It publishes emails and iMessages it says show Apple personnel using a departed employee’s lingering iCloud access and requesting help locating information. HN found the evidence difficult and ambiguous rather than exculpatory, criticizing an official corporate post for emotional, one-sided litigation. Readers also saw a broader offboarding failure: personal cloud accounts and work data remained entangled after employment ended.
