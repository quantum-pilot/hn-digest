# AI-Generated Images Discourage Me from Reading Your Blog

- Score: 759 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49167113) | Link: https://nelson.cloud/ai-generated-images-discourage-me-from-reading-your-blog/

### TL;DR

An independent blogger argues that synthetic illustrations now function as a distrust signal: if an image looks machine-made, readers may assume the prose is too and leave. The preference is not for polished human art, but for evidence of authorship—even a crude Paint sketch. HN largely recognized the reaction, describing generic AI visuals as decorative, uncanny, and low-effort. Others distinguished useless “vibe” imagery from diagrams that genuinely explain something, though generated technical graphics raised separate accuracy concerns.

### Comment pulse

- Decorative AI art signals absent taste and authorship → familiar visual artifacts make unrelated posts feel interchangeable.
- Platform thumbnail requirements encourage filler imagery → publishers need something shareable even when prose needs no illustration.
- Explanatory graphics can earn their place → counterpoint: generated technical diagrams may look authoritative while encoding errors.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Visual provenance has become part of readers’ credibility assessment, independent of the underlying prose.
- **Impact:** Bloggers using generated decoration may lose careful readers before their argument receives consideration.
- **Watch next:** Clear disclosure, restrained illustration, and evidence that diagrams were checked against the text.
