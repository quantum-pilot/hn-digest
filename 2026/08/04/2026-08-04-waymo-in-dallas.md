# Waymo in Dallas

- Score: 292 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49172836) | Link: https://waymo.com/blog/shorts/dallas-open-to-all/

### TL;DR

Waymo has opened its driverless ride service to anyone in Dallas after welcoming nearly 150,000 interest-list riders since February. Airport-terminal and freeway testing remain underway before those routes reach passengers. The company presents the service as safe, reliable, and mobility-expanding, including for people whose medical conditions limit driving. HN riders praised predictable behavior and a calm user experience, but Dallas locals said the initial zone omits much of the dispersed metroplex. A larger argument split robotaxi advocates from supporters of buses, walkability, local jobs, and public transit.
