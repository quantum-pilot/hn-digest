# In Memory of My Wife, Elise Cawley, with Thanks for 36 Wonderful Years

### TL;DR

Stephen Wolfram memorializes Elise Cawley, his wife of 36 years, after a sudden cardiovascular event during recovery from heart surgery. He portrays a brilliant pure mathematician whose work ranged across dynamical systems, Teichmüller spaces, and Gibbs theory, and whose larger commitments were truth, beauty, family, and humanistic mathematics. The account follows their meeting, four children, homes she designed, intellectual arguments, community service, and plans left unfinished. HN readers found the extraordinary detail intimate rather than showy, emphasizing how vividly his admiration, grief, and their shared life come through.
