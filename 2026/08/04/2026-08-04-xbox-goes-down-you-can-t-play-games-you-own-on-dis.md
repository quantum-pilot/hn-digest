# Xbox goes down. You can't play games you own on disc

- Score: 653 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49167448) | Link: https://birchtree.me/blog/xbox-goes-down-you-cant-play-games-you-own-on-disc/

### TL;DR

An extended Xbox outage reportedly blocked digital titles and games owned on disc, illustrating how modern physical media no longer guarantees offline access. Discs often serve as licenses and installers for hard-drive copies that require updates or authentication, unlike cartridges that remain independently playable decades later. The author favors PC because preservation routes exist there. HN broadened the complaint to Microsoft account friction, DRM, server shutdowns, and vanished licenses, arguing ownership should include offline use, backups, transfer, resale, and inheritance; others noted emulation and cracking often preserve abandoned games.

### Comment pulse

- Ownership matters more than physical versus digital format → buyers want durable offline use, backups, device movement, resale, and inheritance.
- DRM-free stores offer the clearest present option → GOG installers remain usable without depending on a vendor’s authentication service.
- Older consoles were not universally preservation-safe → online modes closed, peer hosting exposed players, and community reverse engineering restored some services.
