# libexpat now funded by the City of Munich for up to 6 months

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49176606) | Link: https://blog.hartwork.org/posts/libexpat-city-of-munich-open-source-sabbatical/

### TL;DR

Munich’s Open Source Sabbatical will employ libexpat maintainer Sebastian Pipping for up to six months, turning work that competed with his day job and personal life into paid full-time maintenance. Priorities are fixing five known vulnerabilities, adding XML 1.0 Fifth Edition support, and improving robustness and maintainability; work has already begun on a Mozilla-reported flaw. HN welcomed a city funding critical infrastructure it does not own and noted that outside developers can apply, while discussion connected the program to Munich’s contested history with LiMux and public-code policy.
