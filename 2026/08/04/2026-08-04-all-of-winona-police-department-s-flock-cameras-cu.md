# All of Winona Police Department's Flock cameras cut down and stolen

- Score: 373 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49171656) | Link: https://www.valleynewslive.com/2026/08/04/every-flock-camera-winona-minnesota-cut-down-stolen-coordinated-theft/

### TL;DR

Thieves cut down and removed all eight Flock license-plate cameras operated by Winona police, plus two nearby county units, causing about $24,000 in city losses. Police said the cameras cover major entrances, record vehicle descriptors, and delete locally owned data after 30 days; no suspects were identified. HN treated the theft as both an anti-surveillance statement and an operational embarrassment. Much of the discussion challenged what “local ownership” and deletion mean when vendors, police networks, derived records, exceptions, or federal access may exist.

### Comment pulse

- “Owned locally” does not settle access or retention → contracts, shared networks, derived data, and investigative exceptions may broaden use.
- The system failed its most visible test → cameras covering city entrances apparently did not identify their own thieves.
- Replacement is not inevitable → residents could treat the loss as an opportunity to reconsider pervasive plate surveillance.
