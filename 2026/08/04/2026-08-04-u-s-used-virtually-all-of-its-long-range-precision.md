# U.S. used 'virtually all' of its long-range precision missiles during Iran war

- Score: 368 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49166860) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/us-has-used-virtually-all-of-its-long-range-precision-missiles-report.html

### TL;DR

Reuters sources say five months of war with Iran have consumed virtually all U.S. Army ATACMS and PrSM long-range land-attack missiles, while roughly 65% of Patriot interceptors, at least 38% of THAAD inventory, and nearly half the Tomahawk stock may also be gone. Officials dispute readiness concerns and say production is expanding; PrSM orders rise in 2027. HN cautioned that the headline overstates two relatively small, transitional inventories, but debated whether depletion still reduces deterrence, forces riskier crewed strikes, or weakens responses to Russia or China.

### Comment pulse
