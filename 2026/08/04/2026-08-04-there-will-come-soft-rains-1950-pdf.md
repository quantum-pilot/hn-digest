# There Will Come Soft Rains (1950) [pdf]

- Score: 386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49162653) | Link: https://users.wpi.edu/~zrbutzke/Docs/BradburyStories(1).pdf

### TL;DR

Bradbury’s postwar parable depicts a fully automated California home carrying on for a family vaporized by nuclear war. Its cheerful clocks, meals, cleaning machines, nursery, and poetry become grotesque through their emptiness; after a tree ignites the kitchen, depleted defenses fail and the house destroys itself. HN used the story’s now-arrived 2026 date to revisit Cold War anxiety, arguing nuclear fear is not quaint, and contrasted Bradbury’s resilient local automation with today’s cloud-dependent IoT. Readers also shared musical and Soviet animated adaptations.
