# Apple says more ex-employees may have taken confidential data to OpenAI

- Score: 367 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49170479) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/04/apple-says-more-ex-employees-may-have-taken-confidential-data-to-openai/

### TL;DR

Apple is seeking a preliminary injunction and expedited discovery in its trade-secret case against OpenAI, io, and former employees Chang Liu and Tang Yew Tan. It says an internal investigation identified 11 more former employees who may be witnesses or involved, and alleges proprietary discussions, screenshots, retained devices, and access to 37 documents. OpenAI denies possessing or wanting Apple secrets and attributes residual access to Apple’s controls. HN stressed that security failures would not authorize access, while questioning whether Apple can justify blocking an entire product program.

### Comment pulse
