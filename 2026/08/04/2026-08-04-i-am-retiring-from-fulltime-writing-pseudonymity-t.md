# I am retiring from fulltime writing (& pseudonymity) to launch Guardian Angel

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49174900) | Link: https://twitter.com/gwern/status/2084739205071343837

### TL;DR

Gwern is leaving full-time writing and pseudonymity to build Guardian Angel: personalized LLMs trained to emulate one principal’s values, preferences, and style, amplifying that person rather than serving a generic chatbot owner. The proposal combines continual fine-tuning, active questioning, uncertainty estimates, heavy data augmentation, append-only logs, and secure dedicated infrastructure. A Gwern-trained prototype would test whether this can produce publishable work at roughly 100× his current rate. HN admired the sovereignty goal but questioned clone-based alignment, startup ownership, productivity fixation, cost, and the assumption that humans become bottlenecks.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters see personalized agents as humane defense-in-depth → they could preserve individual intent while generic systems optimize people away.
- Critics reject self-cloning as alignment → a similar personality may reinforce flaws, sycophancy, or self-interest instead of supplying wiser judgment.
- Renting a cloud-hosted “self” creates landlord incentives → counterpoint: dedicated infrastructure may offer stronger security than hobbyist local deployments.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The pivotal technical bet is that longitudinal imitation can improve agency without collapsing identity into static historical preferences.
- **Impact:** Success would shift personalization from prompt profiles toward costly, continuously trained models holding exceptionally sensitive data.
- **Watch next:** Prototype fidelity, adversarial extraction tests, principal-controlled portability, preference drift, and blinded single-user evaluations.
