# AI by Hand

- Score: 279 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49300568) | Link: https://www.byhand.ai/

### TL;DR

AI by Hand is Prof. Tom Yeh's research publication about model interpretability and explainability at the mathematical and algorithmic level. Its library spans multilayer perceptrons, attention, fine-tuning, activations, matrix multiplication, worked calculations, seminar videos, architecture drawings, workbooks, and token problems. Free subscribers receive new articles and live seminars; a $29 monthly membership unlocks the full library and is pitched as employer-funded professional development. Commenters found the subscription-first page confusing, criticized locked posts and scarce visualizations, and recommended alternative from-scratch or visually oriented resources.
