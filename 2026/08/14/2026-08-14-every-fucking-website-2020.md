# Every Fucking Website (2020)

- Score: 783 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49299222) | Link: https://lxe.github.io/everywebsite/

### TL;DR

A compact parody reproduces the modern web’s obstacle course: topical banner, surprise discount modal, provocative subscription prompt, cookie consent, and a sprawling generic footer surrounding almost no content. Commenters updated its 2020 checklist with autoplay video, paywalls, app-install interstitials, blocked scrolling, hijacked back buttons, layout shifts, Cloudflare challenges, oversized assets, and dozens of third-party domains. The uncomfortable counterpoint came from a Shopify owner whose disliked purchase-notification popup materially increased conversions: intrusive patterns persist because they capture attention and make money, not because designers overlook the irritation.

### Comment pulse

- The parody loads quickly, remains responsive, and exposes content without many scripts, making its technical restraint another layer of the joke.
- Mobile visitors especially resent undismissable app prompts that consume the page and obstruct browser navigation.
- NoScript users expect 12–18 external domains by default, with major news sites sometimes reaching 20–50 after dependencies unfold.
