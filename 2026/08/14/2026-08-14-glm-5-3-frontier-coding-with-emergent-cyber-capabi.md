# GLM-5.3: Frontier coding with emergent cyber capabilities

- Score: 1082 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49294997) | Link: https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3

### TL;DR

Z.ai says GLM-5.3 keeps GLM-5.2’s base model and derives its gains entirely from scaled post-training on more realistic, verifiable long-horizon environments. Reported scores jump from 4.6 to 28.3 on Terminal-Bench 3.0 and more than double on exploitation benchmarks. Real-world scanning with security teams yielded 2,436 findings across 269 projects, 53 publicly disclosed and 2,383 embargoed. Weights are promised after two weeks of safety hardening. Commenters welcomed accessible security capability but noted closed models still lead deeper exploitation and local deployment remains extremely demanding.

### Comment pulse

- Subscribers found it capable and willing on defensive research that closed models refused — counterpoint: release hardening may reduce cyber capability.

- A 288-GiB GLM-5.2 quant ran around 0.7 tokens per second on mixed SSD, CPU, and GPU hardware.
