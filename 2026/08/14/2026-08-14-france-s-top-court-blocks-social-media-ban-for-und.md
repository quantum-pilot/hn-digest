# France's top court blocks social media ban for under-15s

- Score: 218 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49300671) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/world/frances-top-court-rules-social-media-ban-curtails-freedom-expression-2026-08-14/

### TL;DR

France’s Constitutional Council struck down legislation that would have barred under-15s from social-media accounts starting September 1 and closed existing accounts within four months. The court found the blanket restriction disproportionately limited expression and communication, while mandatory proof of age lacked defined conditions, limits, and privacy safeguards. President Macron ordered a rewrite intended before spring 2027. Commenters largely supported the ruling because age checks can become identity checks for everyone, but split among device-based age assurance, parental controls, platform-design regulation, and accepting that teenagers will evade any gate.

### Comment pulse

- A child-device signal could avoid identifying adults — counterpoint: shared or adult devices prevent reliable assurance about the actual user.
- Others argued addictive or radicalizing platform design should be regulated for everyone rather than placing verification burdens on users.
- One French commenter called the bill wasted parliamentary time; replies suggested doomed legislation can prepare constitutional changes or political blame.
