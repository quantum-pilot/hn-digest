# I turned my RSS feeds into an e-ink newspaper to stop reading on my phone

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49299081) | Link: https://heyjonny.dev/posts/rss-to-eink-newspaper/

### TL;DR

To reduce eye strain and stop defaulting to his phone, the author turned unread Feedbin items into a personal EPUB newspaper for the 4.3-inch Xteink X4. Its open-source Crosspoint firmware installed easily despite the device lacking touch, backlight, or a bookstore. His feedpaper tool fetches unread posts, excludes unsuitable video, JavaScript-heavy, and link-only feeds, marks items read, and builds the EPUB; Homebrew installation currently targets macOS. Commenters shared similar Kindle and e-ink pipelines, newspaper nostalgia, and recurring problems with partial feeds and missing images.
