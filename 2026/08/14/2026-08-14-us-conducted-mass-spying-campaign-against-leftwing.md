# US conducted mass spying campaign against leftwing and anti-ICE protesters

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49294199) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/13/us-government-spied-anti-ice-protesters

### TL;DR

Nearly 30 DHS reports disclosed in the prosecution of 15 Minneapolis protesters show Operation Puppet Master used undercover agents at meetings, infiltrated Signal chats, recorded events, collected license plates, and obtained financial records from unions and nonprofits through administrative subpoenas. The inquiry tied mainstream progressive groups to an alleged anti-ICE conspiracy, although none were charged and one report called Sunrise’s protests peaceful. A defense attorney alleges agents encouraged criminal discussion; DHS and DOJ declined specifics. Commenters focused on unchecked executive power, guilt by association, and resisting infiltration.
