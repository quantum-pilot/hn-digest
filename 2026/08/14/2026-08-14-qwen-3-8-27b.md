# Qwen 3.8 27B

- Score: 1078 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49299605) | Link: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8

### TL;DR

Qwen released an Apache-2.0, 27B dense vision-language model in FP8, with native 262,144-token context, optional thinking, three reasoning-effort levels, and support for images and hour-scale video. Its model card reports major gains over Qwen3.6 across coding, agents, and multimodal tasks, with deployment through Transformers, vLLM, SGLang, and TokenSpeed. Early local tests found strong coding and visual SVG generation, but also long reasoning runs, uneven factual recall, overcomplicated code, and disputed memory efficiency that varied by quantization, runtime, and context settings.

### Comment pulse

- One local test produced working JavaScript immediately and nearly working Rust, while still missing an obscure geography fact.
- Private-benchmark success impressed users, but KV-cache efficiency and usable context varied sharply across runtimes and quantizations.
- SVG pelicans showed unusually good spatial composition, sometimes after 20–90 minutes and tens of thousands of reasoning tokens.

### LLM perspective

- View: A credible local multimodal agent now fits 27B, but inference budget—not weights alone—determines usability.
- Impact: Developers with 32–48 GB machines gain a private backup while long reasoning and KV cache constrain ambitious tasks.
- Watch next: Independent agent tests across runtimes, quantization sensitivity, context-memory scaling, and whether tighter reasoning avoids bushy code.
