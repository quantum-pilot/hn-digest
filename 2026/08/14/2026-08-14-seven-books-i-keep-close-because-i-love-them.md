# Seven books I keep close because I love them

- Score: 344 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49299675) | Link: https://blog.plover.com/2026/08/02/

### TL;DR

Mark Dominus describes the seven books within arm's reach not as reference workhorses, but as intellectual company he hopes will shape his writing. They span Roget's concept-organized thesaurus, Thomas Browne's humane curiosity, the Decameron's worldly resilience, foundational mathematical-logic papers, Comenius's illustrated bilingual teaching, an NIV Bible for cultural literacy, and an illuminated medieval book of hours. Personal stories explain each bond. Commenters praised the essay's learned warmth while debating Bible translation choices, Samson's motives, and whether Christianity or Aristotle constrained medieval thought.
