# Count Binface receives over a quarter of votes in Clacton by-election

- Score: 439 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49301260) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ce97mm3vvemo

### TL;DR

Satirist Jon Harvey’s Count Binface finished second in Clacton’s by-election with 9,455 votes, or 26.9%, behind Nigel Farage’s 22,239 and 63.3%. The contest followed Farage resigning and running again while major parties boycotted, making the novelty candidate his principal rival. The result dwarfed Binface’s previous high of 308 votes and cleared the 5% threshold for recovering his £500 deposit. The article frames such candidacies as a British tradition of mocking power; commenters debated whether a serious independent could have mounted a stronger challenge.
