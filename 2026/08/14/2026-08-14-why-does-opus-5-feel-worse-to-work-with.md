# Why does Opus 5 feel worse to work with?

- Score: 845 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49296740) | Link: https://mun-logadan.github.io/why-does-opus-5-feel-worse/

### TL;DR

The author argues Opus 5 is more capable than 4.7 or 4.8 yet a worse collaborator because it guesses through ambiguity, revises plans, and proceeds without clarification. Their hypothesis: benchmark and RLVR training reward self-contained task completion, penalizing questions that real projects need because intent, business constraints, and consequences cannot be exhaustively specified. Many commenters likewise reported elliptical prose, excessive commentary, unrequested subagents, and overbuilt changes, prompting switches to older models or Sol. Others found previous models less capable and framed the tradeoff as autonomy versus human oversight.

### LLM perspective

- View: Benchmark completion and collaborative calibration differ; improving the first can visibly degrade the second without lowering raw capability.
- Impact: Teams pay review, compute, and cognitive costs when a model treats ambiguity as permission rather than a boundary.
