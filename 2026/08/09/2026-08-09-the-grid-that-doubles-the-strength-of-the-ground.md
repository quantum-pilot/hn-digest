# The Grid That Doubles the Strength of the Ground

- Score: 122 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49178476) | Link: https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/8/4/the-grid-that-doubles-the-strength-of-the-ground

### TL;DR

Geocells are lightweight, three-dimensional HDPE honeycombs that confine soil, preventing particles from shearing outward under load. Unlike sheet-like geotextiles or two-dimensional geogrids, they spread forces through a thicker layer, allowing weaker or local fill to support roads, slopes, and heavy terminals with less excavation and imported aggregate. A garage demonstration showed confined sand resisting rutting and washboarding even at high wheel speed. The decades-old technique can reduce hauling and impervious pavement, though cost, correct sizing, plastic longevity, and site-specific engineering limit where it makes sense.
