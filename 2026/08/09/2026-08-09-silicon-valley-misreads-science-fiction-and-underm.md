# Silicon Valley misreads science fiction and undermines democracy

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49232221) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/historian-jill-lepore-says-the-tech-industry-is-led-by-bad-readers-who-are-undermining-democracy/

## TL;DR
Jill Lepore argues that major tech firms are morphing into an “artificial state,” informally taking over core government functions—speech rules, adjudication, even constitutional design—without democratic consent. She traces this to a 19th‑century fusion of “technology = progress” and to science fiction warnings about machine rule that Silicon Valley often misreads as instruction manuals. Examples span Apple’s 1984 ad, Twitter’s “town hall” myth, Facebook’s Oversight Board, and AI “constitutions.” Lepore predicts this model will fail, especially as it consumes the natural world it depends on, and calls for re‑democratizing tech decisions.
