# Silicon Valley misreads science fiction and undermines democracy

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49232221) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/historian-jill-lepore-says-the-tech-industry-is-led-by-bad-readers-who-are-undermining-democracy/

### TL;DR

Jill Lepore argues that large technology companies have become “artificial states”: private institutions assuming governmental functions without democratic consent. She traces the shift from early personal-computing ideals to platforms claiming constitutions, oversight bodies, and public-square status, while remaining commercially governed and socially unrepresentative. Science fiction, she says, usually presented such systems as warnings, not product roadmaps. Her broader test is whether technological change improves human freedom and welfare; speed, scale, and efficiency alone do not constitute progress, especially when communities bear data centers’ environmental costs.
