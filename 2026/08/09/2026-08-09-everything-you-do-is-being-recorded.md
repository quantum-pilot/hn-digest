# Everything you do is being recorded

- Score: 184 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49230477) | Link: https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/05/ai-wearable-surveillance-countermeasures/687203/

### TL;DR

As AI-enabled pins, glasses, and pendants approach mainstream use, nearby conversations may be captured without meaningful consent. Conventional ultrasonic jammers exploit microphone hardware, but modern speech-recovery models can denoise recordings and infer missing syllables. Newer defenses instead inject speech-shaped decoys: MicFrozen generates voice-tuned ultrasonic anti-speech, while the proposed Spectre I claims jamming and future microphone detection. The article expects an escalating surveillance contest extending to lip-reading or vibration analysis. Commenters favored stronger data liability and real alternatives, rejecting the idea that widespread device use proves consumers welcome surveillance.
