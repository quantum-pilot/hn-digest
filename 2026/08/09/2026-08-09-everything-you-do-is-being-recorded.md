# Everything you do is being recorded

- Score: 184 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49230477) | Link: https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/05/ai-wearable-surveillance-countermeasures/687203/

## TL;DR
AI-powered wearables are turning everyone into a potential wiretapper, making casual conversations recordable and recoverable even through noise and jamming. Traditional countermeasures (white noise, ultrasonic jammers) are being defeated by powerful speech-recovery models originally built for hearing aids and conferencing. New devices like Spectre I and research tools like MicFrozen try “audio obfuscation” instead of mere noise, but the article argues the economic and technical asymmetry means surveillance will likely stay ahead—echoing the Mafia’s losing arms race against law-enforcement wiretaps.
