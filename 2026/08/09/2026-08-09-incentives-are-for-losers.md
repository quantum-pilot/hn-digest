# Incentives are for losers

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49227652) | Link: https://www.experimental-history.com/p/incentives-are-for-losers

### TL;DR

The essay argues that blaming bad incentives can become moral surrender: people should cultivate an independent purpose and choose rightly even when rewards point elsewhere. Because value systems do not mature automatically, the author says moral reckonings must expose shallow beliefs and replace status-seeking with conviction. Goodhart’s Law still justifies auditing and revising reward systems, but not treating courage as impossible. Commenters challenged the individualist framing: defiance carries unequal costs, jobs tie compliance to survival, and collective-action systems cannot assume everyone will ignore rewards.
