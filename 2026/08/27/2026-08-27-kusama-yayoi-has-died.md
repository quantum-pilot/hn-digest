# Kusama Yayoi has died

- Score: 263 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49458709) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/26/arts/yayoi-kusama-dead.html

### TL;DR

Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama died in Tokyo at 97, leaving a career spanning Infinity Net paintings, soft sculptures, antiwar happenings, novels, and mirrored installations. Her repeated marks and forms helped shape Pop and Minimalism, while later Infinity Rooms became museum blockbusters. The obituary also notes psychiatric framing often overshadowed her formal skill, and records her 2023 apology for racist writing. Commenters remembered her work as joyful and personally formative, while debating selfie culture, damaged exhibits, and restrictive photography policies.
