- Score: 1884 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49458161) | Link: https://www.businessinsider.com/nvidia-in-talks-to-buy-hugging-face-13-billion-dollars-2026-8

### TL;DR

Despite the headline, the article says Nvidia and Hugging Face are only discussing a possible acquisition valued above $13 billion; no deal is agreed, talks could fail, and neither company commented. Nvidia already invested in Hugging Face, whose model and dataset hub sits at the center of open AI development. Commenters saw strategic value in pairing that ecosystem with Nvidia hardware, but worried ownership could weaken support for AMD, Intel, Apple tooling, and open-source projects while giving Nvidia privileged usage data.
