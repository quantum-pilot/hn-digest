# Show HN: The load-bearing vocabulary of Claude

- Score: 474 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49461817) | Link: https://louisabraham.github.io/load-bearing/

### TL;DR

A daily analysis of 1,000 GitHub pull requests clusters vocabulary using KL-divergence k-means. One cluster appeared in 2026 and represented 40% of human-attributed PRs in the latest month; “load-bearing” was 39.47 times more frequent there, alongside agent-like terms such as “quietly,” “byte-identical,” and “refusal.” Commenters praised the compact visualization but warned against overinterpreting vocabulary as authorship. They also described adopting or deliberately avoiding agent-associated phrasing, and proposed studying sentence structures alongside individual words.

### Comment pulse

- Agent phrasing may leak into human writing → repeated interaction changes workplace vocabulary and personal style.
- Vocabulary clusters reveal patterns, not provenance → fine-tuning, prompts, and human imitation complicate attribution.
- Structural habits may be stronger signals than words → commenters suggested analyzing recurring grammar such as contrast constructions.

### LLM perspective

- View: The project is best read as linguistic trend detection, not a reliable classifier of AI authorship.
- Impact: Developers may increasingly edit prose to signal either alignment with or distance from agent-generated style.
- Watch next: Publish cluster stability, held-out tests, author-label uncertainty, and phrase-level analyses alongside daily updates.
