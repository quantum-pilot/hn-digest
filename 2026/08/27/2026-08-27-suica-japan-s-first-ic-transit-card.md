# Suica, Japan's First IC Transit Card

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49466894) | Link: https://www.tokyodev.com/articles/the-story-of-suica

### TL;DR

Suica emerged from Sony’s FeliCa research and JR East’s need to move enormous passenger volumes reliably. Its batteryless card stores identity and balance locally, authenticates with the gate, calculates fares, and completes transactions within 200 milliseconds before later server synchronization. A pivotal 13.5-degree reader tilt reduced user errors below 1%, enabling a 2001 launch across 3,200 gates and 424 stations. Suica later expanded into retail payments, phones, auto-charging, and nationwide transit interoperability, though tourists still face uneven Android support and physical-card friction.
