# CEO fired developers to make room for AI. Developers create open source AI CEO

- Score: 967 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49458418) | Link: https://github.com/SenteLabsAI/OpenExecutive

### TL;DR

Open Executive is an Apache-licensed virtual leadership team built around one orchestrator and eight specialist agents covering strategy, finance, people, legal, operations, marketing, product, and boards. It combines built-in business material with uploaded company documents, ChromaDB retrieval, SQLite decision memory, scheduled follow-ups, and several chat integrations. The system requires a single API instance because its scheduler can double-fire when horizontally scaled. Discussion welcomed AI advice for founders but disputed whether relationship-building, accountability, and long-horizon judgment can be automated or evaluated reliably.

### Comment pulse
