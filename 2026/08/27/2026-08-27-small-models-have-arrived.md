# Small Models Have Arrived

- Score: 610 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49466917) | Link: https://calv.info/small-models-have-arrived

### TL;DR

The author argues fast, inexpensive models have crossed a practical threshold: a personalized-news experiment fell from roughly $1 with earlier models to about $0.10, making consumer AI economics more plausible. Small models also suit the large share of business work that rewards responsiveness and steady coordination rather than rare breakthroughs. Frontier models remain essential for difficult discovery. Commenters agreed that focused harnesses can unlock older or local models, but debated whether startups can differentiate from frontier labs and whether broad knowledge still consistently favors larger models.
