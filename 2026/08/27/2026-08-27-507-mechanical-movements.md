# 507 Mechanical Movements

- Score: 563 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49465169) | Link: https://507movements.com/

### TL;DR

Henry T. Brown’s illustrated reference compiles 507 mechanisms spanning dynamics, hydraulics, pneumatics, steam engines, gearing, presses, clocks, and miscellaneous machinery. First copyrighted in 1868 and presented here in a 1908 edition, it pairs numbered engravings with facing descriptions and a detailed index. Brown says he selected practically useful movements from American and foreign sources, with more than one quarter previously absent from published collections. Readers value modern animated adaptations but want clearer names when mechanisms appear outside the book’s context.
