# Decompiling a Nintendo 64 game in 84 days

- Score: 229 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49466006) | Link: https://blog.chrislewis.au/decompiling-a-nintendo-64-game-in-84-days/

### TL;DR

Snowboard Kids reached 100% matching decompilation in 84 days: every function now has C that compiles to the original machine code. That compares with 596 days for its sequel, though the new game was smaller and the lead developer brought two years of experience. Agents handled library code, recurring IDO compiler quirks, parallel worktrees, and permutation-assisted matching, but experts remained essential for roughly 4.8% of matching commits. Next steps include documentation, cleanup, static recompilation, modding, and possible content ports.
