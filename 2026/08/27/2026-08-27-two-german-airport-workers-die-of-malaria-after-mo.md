# Two German airport workers die of malaria after 'mosquito arrives on plane'

- Score: 179 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49468315) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cz6zwgg9y8go

### TL;DR

Six male Frankfurt Airport employees working in different roles and locations contracted malaria, and two died. German officials believe infected mosquitoes arrived by aircraft, but laboratory work is still examining mosquito species, origin, patient blood samples, and whether one or several insects caused the cluster. Traps and employee alerts are in place. Authorities describe public risk as very low because malaria is mosquito-borne rather than directly person-to-person. Airport transmission is rare, and absent travel history can delay diagnosis; Frankfurt recorded another airport-malaria case in 2023.
