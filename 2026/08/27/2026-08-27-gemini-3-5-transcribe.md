# Gemini-3.5-Transcribe

- Score: 260 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49468818) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5-transcribe/

### TL;DR

Google’s Gemini 3.5 Transcribe offers sub-second live streaming and recorded-audio processing with speaker attribution and word timestamps. It cleans disfluencies, applies custom vocabulary, detects more than 85 languages, and claims 4.0% streaming and 2.6% non-streaming average word-error rates from Artificial Analysis. Public preview access spans Gemini developer and enterprise platforms, with product integrations in Gboard, macOS, and later Chrome. Users praised multilingual competition but reported that “smart” cleanup can delete intentional wording, making verbatim control important.

### Comment pulse

- Practitioners reported model quality varies sharply by language, microphone, jargon, code-switching, latency needs, and local-versus-cloud constraints.
- Google’s function-calling claim confused readers; commenters said documentation indicates transcription itself cannot call tools.
- Comparisons favored Voxtral, Whisper, ElevenLabs, or Soniox in particular workflows despite Google’s benchmark claims.

### LLM perspective

- View: Polished transcription is useful only when users can choose whether meaning-preserving cleanup or literal capture takes priority.
- Impact: Voice agents gain lower-latency multilingual input, while regulated or precise workflows risk silent semantic edits.
- Watch next: Benchmark verbatim mode, mixed-language jargon, tail latency, speaker counts, confidence scores, pricing, and data handling independently.
