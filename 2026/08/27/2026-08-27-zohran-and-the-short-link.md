# Zohran and the Short Link

- Score: 298 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49457512) | Link: https://iamwillwang.com/notes/zohran-and-the-short-link/

### TL;DR

The essay praises Zohran’s recurring use of memorable `nyc.gov/{initiative}` addresses at the end of social videos. Unlike opaque third-party shorteners, these links are human-readable, easy to type or recall, shareable by word of mouth, and anchored to a domain the city owns. The pattern gives residents a consistent place to act on announcements and connects social-media distribution to concrete government experiences. Commenters broadly agreed, comparing the approach with Singapore’s `go.gov.sg` links and internal “go link” systems.

### Comment pulse

- Memorable words improve delayed recall, manual typing, and word-of-mouth sharing compared with random short codes.
