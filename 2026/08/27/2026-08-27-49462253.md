# Tell HN: PayPal blocks GrapheneOS

- Score: 478 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49462253) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49462253

### TL;DR

A GrapheneOS user reports that PayPal crashes at launch with a root-detection security exception, initially wondering whether contactless-card activation caused it. Several commenters reproduce the failure, while others say PayPal still works under different profiles or exploit-protection settings. The original poster later restored access by disabling secure app spawning. A detailed reply attributes the problem to incorrect anti-tampering code rather than an intentional GrapheneOS ban, though configurations and outcomes vary enough that the exact affected conditions remain unsettled.

### Comment pulse

- Critics called the block perverse because GrapheneOS adds protections — counterpoint: PayPal must distinguish hardened users from motivated attackers.
- Workarounds included compatibility mode or disabling secure app spawning; reports differed by PayPal version, profile, and contactless configuration.

### LLM perspective

- View: The evidence supports a compatibility regression more strongly than a deliberate platform-wide ban.
- Impact: Security-tool conflicts force users to weaken device protections or abandon financial apps, undermining both systems’ goals.
