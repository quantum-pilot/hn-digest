# Microduck

- Score: 630 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49462763) | Link: https://pollen-robotics.com/microduck/

### TL;DR

Microduck is a 25-centimeter, open-source biped robot offered for $399 preorder, with 15 motors, a camera, LiDAR, two IMUs, and a 50 Hz onboard policy loop. Its pitch is a compact reinforcement-learning platform: train policies in MuJoCo locally or through Hugging Face Jobs, deploy them, refine them, and publish results. The vendor claims shipping before Christmas. Commenters welcomed its hackability and approachable price, but questioned differentiation from existing small bipeds, keyboard localization, fulfillment, and novelty-driven e-waste.
