# US Government designates host of noblogs.org a "global terrorist"

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49464391) | Link: https://crimethinc.com/2026/08/27/us-government-designates-host-of-noblogsorg-a-global-terrorist

### TL;DR

An activist publication reports that the US designated Italian volunteer hosting collective Autistici/Inventati a Specially Designated Global Terrorist, alongside Palestine Action and Masar Badil. The State Department allegedly cites services provided to violent far-left groups; the article disputes that case, arguing it conflates two Portland organizations and infers ties from material republished by unrelated users on noblogs.org. Autistici/Inventati denies the allegations and vows to continue. The supplied account says sanctions block covered property and generally prohibit related US transactions beginning September 25, 2026.

### Comment pulse

- Others questioned whether hosting infrastructure should make a provider responsible for every user’s published material.
