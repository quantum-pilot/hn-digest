# Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash

- Score: 238 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49467922) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-with-gemini-omni-1-1-flash/

### TL;DR

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash adds controls for production-oriented generative video: scenes can reference ten seconds of prior context and extend in ten-second increments to forty seconds; first and last frames can define transitions; three-second video references can guide motion and consistency. Developers can draft at 360p, claimed up to 60% faster and one-third the 720p cost, then upscale to 1080p or 4K. Commenters saw advertising potential but highlighted labor disruption, browser performance, cost, restrictions, and missing audio-driven lip synchronization.
