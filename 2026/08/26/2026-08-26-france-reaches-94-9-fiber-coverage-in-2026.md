# France reaches 94.9% fiber coverage in 2026

- Score: 296 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49448872) | Link: https://cartefibre.arcep.fr

### TL;DR

France’s Arcep map reports near-universal fiber availability, with address-level deployment data current through early 2026 and more than 95% coverage across many areas. HN commenters described subsidized fiber reaching isolated mountain homes, often cheaply, and framed broadband as durable public infrastructure comparable to electricity or water. They contrasted strong French and Spanish coverage with slower markets elsewhere, while distinguishing premises passed from actual subscriptions and noting that installation barriers, mobile alternatives, or adequate legacy service can suppress adoption.

### Comment pulse

- Subsidies deliver social returns → rural links support remote work and regional resilience beyond direct ISP profit.
- Fiber is a long-lived asset → passive infrastructure can serve for decades while endpoint technology upgrades cheaply.
- Coverage is not adoption → building access, installation costs, pricing, and sufficient alternatives still shape subscriptions.
