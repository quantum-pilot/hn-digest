### TL;DR

Forty years after a 75-cent Unix accounting discrepancy exposed German hackers working for East Germany and the Soviet KGB, Cliff Stoll revisits the improvised investigation behind The Cuckoo’s Egg. With no cybersecurity playbook, the chase used modem traces, shell scripts, soldering, logs, and persistence across international networks. HN celebrated Stoll’s enduring energy and the story’s formative influence on security careers, while reflecting on the early internet’s trust-through-ignorance and today’s commercialization, surveillance, and eroding user freedom.
