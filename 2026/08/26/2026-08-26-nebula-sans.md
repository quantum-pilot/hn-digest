# Nebula Sans

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49450448) | Link: https://www.nebulasans.com

### TL;DR

Streaming service Nebula released Nebula Sans, an SIL Open Font License derivative of Source Sans 3 adjusted to replace its costly Whitney SSm branding. The humanist sans offers upright and italic styles across six weights, Whitney-compatible metrics, Source Sans alternates, customized punctuation and asterisk, and tabular numerals useful for stable video timestamps. HN debated whether such subtle customization was corporate bikeshedding, but defenders highlighted multi-platform licensing costs, creator reuse, and permanent legal clarity; discussion also praised Nebula’s calmer, subscription-funded alternative to recommendation-driven YouTube.

### Comment pulse

- Nebula itself attracts algorithm-weary viewers → ad-free creator content feels calmer, though catalog staleness and YouTube’s scale limit habitual use.
