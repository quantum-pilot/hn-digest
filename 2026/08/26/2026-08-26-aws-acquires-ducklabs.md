# AWS Acquires DuckLabs

- Score: 958 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49448321) | Link: https://ducklabs.com/news/2026/08/26/ducklabs-to-join-aws

### TL;DR

AWS is acquiring DuckLabs, the 30-person Amsterdam company behind DuckDB, while the nonprofit DuckDB Foundation retains the open-source project’s intellectual property and MIT licensing. The team will stay together, continue DuckDB, DuckLake, and Quack, add a technical advisory board, and open extension signing beyond itself. DuckLabs says AWS supplies scale and customer reach without forcing it to build a large sales organization. HN welcomed the structural safeguards but worried corporate reorganizations could erode the focused technical culture that produced DuckDB.
