# Meta reaches $17B settlement over social media harms to children

- Score: 474 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49448819) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/world/us/meta-settles-with-us-states-over-social-media-harms-2026-08-26/

### TL;DR

Meta agreed to pay up to $18 billion over ten years to settle most U.S. state claims that Facebook and Instagram were designed to addict children and misrepresented safety. Without admitting wrongdoing, it will generally limit teens to two hours daily, block midnight-to-6 a.m. use, and suppress school-hour notifications; recommendations and targeted advertising remain. HN split between seeing the payment as trivial relative to Meta’s revenue, welcoming enforceable usage limits, doubting causal evidence about broad social-media harms, and wanting user-controlled feeds without infinite scroll or shorts.

### Comment pulse

- The penalty may be merely operational cost → guaranteed payments span a decade and preserve core advertising — counterpoint: mandatory limits materially reduce engagement.

- Causal evidence remains contested → extreme use correlates with harm, but intervention effects appear mixed and restrictions may reduce youth autonomy.
