# Tim Curry has died

- Score: 575 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451448) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/aug/26/tim-curry-dies-rocky-horror-show-stephen-king-it-legend-film

### TL;DR

Tim Curry died peacefully at 80, leaving a five-decade career defined by extraordinary theatrical versatility. He originated Dr Frank-N-Furter in The Rocky Horror Show, transformed into Darkness in Legend and Pennywise in It, and ranged across Broadway, film comedy, television, and animation. After a 2012 stroke affected his mobility and memory, he continued working and meeting fans. HN’s remembrances showed how different generations discovered him through Rocky Horror, Clue, Muppet Treasure Island, Home Alone 2, games, fantasy, and voice roles.
