# U.S. State Department pauses immigrant visa applications

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49452709) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/u-s-state-department-pauses-immigrant-visa-applications-25b31b23

### TL;DR

The State Department paused immigrant-visa processing and postponed embassy interviews while training officers on stricter “public charge” guidance; no restart date was given. The move accompanies broader Trump-administration restrictions, including reviews of B-1/B-2 visas and plans targeting up to 200,000 temporary-visa holders who later sought asylum. HN emphasized uncertainty for families and applicants stranded after travel, while disputing whether work visas are covered and debating effects on American workers, global talent, constitutional policy, and the administration’s motives.

### Comment pulse

- Open-ended postponements create acute risk → applicants may be separated from work, homes, or family without a replacement date.
- Visa categories matter → commenters noted immigrant visas differ from H-1B and other nonimmigrant work statuses.
- Economic interpretations split → some foresee talent moving abroad; others argue guest-worker programs suppress wages.
