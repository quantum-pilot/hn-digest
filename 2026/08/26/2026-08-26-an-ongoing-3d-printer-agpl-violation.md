# An ongoing 3D-printer AGPL violation

- Score: 298 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49452980) | Link: https://lwn.net/SubscriberLink/1089390/46116614cc74b814/

### TL;DR

Software Freedom Conservancy alleges Bambu Lab violates AGPLv3 by shipping Bambu Studio, derived from PrusaSlicer, while withholding dynamically loaded components and server-side functionality; it also cites missing GPLv2 firmware sources. A reverse-engineered client triggered a DMCA takedown, so SFC’s baltobu project mirrors replacements while considering litigation and trade remedies. HN users balanced license enforcement against Bambu printers’ exceptional usability, sharing LAN-only alternatives and debating whether lawsuits, import restrictions, or consumer pressure can compel compliance.
