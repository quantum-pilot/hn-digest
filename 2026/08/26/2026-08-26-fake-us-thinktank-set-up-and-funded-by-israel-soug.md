# Fake US thinktank set up and funded by Israel sought to game AI for propaganda

- Score: 239 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49447600) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/26/fake-thinktank-israel-ai-propaganda

### TL;DR

The Guardian reports that an apparently nonexistent Hanover Institute published 124 quasi-academic reports totaling 560,000 words in nine days, funded through contractors for Israel’s government. The content presents Israeli positions as neutral research and is optimized for chatbot citation and eventual training-data ingestion. Foreign-agent filings reveal a broader, multimillion-dollar influence network. HN debated whether “fake” meaningfully distinguishes this operation from ordinary thinktanks or SEO, while recognizing undisclosed machine-targeted propaganda as a new model-poisoning problem requiring measurement and scrutiny.
