# Kinney Drugs pulls back AI phone assistant after hundreds of customer complaints

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49244569) | Link: https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/07/kinney-drugs-pulls-back-ai-phone-assistant-after-hundreds-customer-complaints/

### TL;DR

Kinney Drugs is scaling back Burt, its AI pharmacy phone assistant, after hundreds of complaints about incoherent calls, incorrect dosages, and missed prescription notifications. Incoming calls will return to the touch-tone system; Burt will remain only for opt-in outbound refill texts. The company says the system is HIPAA-compliant but acknowledged that privacy and security compliance did not deliver a usable experience. HN readers argued pharmacy calls combine older users, difficult drug names, insurance exceptions, and high consequences, making them a poor place for weak general-purpose voice models without human handoff.
