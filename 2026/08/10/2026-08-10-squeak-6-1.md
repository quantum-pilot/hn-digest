# Squeak 6.1

- Score: 212 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49242653) | Link: https://squeak.org/release_notes/6.1/

### TL;DR

After four years, the “Vanessa” release arrives with more than 1,700 patches and 9,000 method changes as the Smalltalk environment approaches 30. Headliners include a hierarchical tree browser, restored Objectland demonstrations, extensive Morphic and high-DPI work, stronger inspectors and debuggers, process and unwinding fixes, ephemeron-based finalization, richer regex and IPv6 support, and ARM64 FFI. The project retains deprecated APIs where possible and documents several platform-specific defects. HN’s response centered on Smalltalk’s live image, object introspection, and enduring educational value.

### Comment pulse
