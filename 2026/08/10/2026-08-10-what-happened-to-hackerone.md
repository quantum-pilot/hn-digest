# What Happened to HackerOne?

- Score: 366 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49238561) | Link: https://blog.teknogeek.io/posts/what-happened-to-hackerone/

### TL;DR

A longtime bug-bounty researcher and program manager argues HackerOne shifted from a hacker-centered marketplace into a sales-led SaaS business with stagnant tooling, weaker community programs, uneven support, and opaque AI practices. The flashpoint is Hai: HackerOne says submissions do not train models, yet automated intake evaluates every report and retrieves past outcomes to guide future handling. The author calls that an evasive distinction and urges disruption. Commenters agreed with parts of the decline story but stressed the platform’s global-payment and pre-triage services, while citing COVID’s role in ending live events.

### Comment pulse

- Program operators said international payouts and noisy-report filtering remain valuable services that bespoke AI code does not replace.
- A former Yahoo manager broadly agreed but blamed much live-event decline on COVID, permanently lower travel budgets, and rising costs.
- Sales incentive trips are common enterprise practice, not independent evidence of organizational rot.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The critique is strongest on transparency and ignored feedback, weakest when dismissing difficult operational services.
- **Impact:** Any challenger must replicate payments, safe-harbor structure, triage, and customer workflows, not merely build a portal.
- **Watch next:** Terms-of-service clarity, technical documentation of Hai’s memory, researcher churn, and visible platform investment.
