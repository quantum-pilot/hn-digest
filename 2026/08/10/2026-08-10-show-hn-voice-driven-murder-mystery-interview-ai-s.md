# Show HN: Voice driven murder mystery, Interview AI suspects with your voice

- Score: 188 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49238851) | Link: https://www.whodunnitai.com/

### TL;DR

WhoDunnitAI presents a voice-driven murder mystery in which players interrogate five AI suspects, test alibis, and identify contradictions. Live conversations use gpt-realtime-2, making free play expensive for the creator; launch traffic exhausted funding, usage reached roughly $100, and the planned cap rose to $500. The developer shortened sessions to 15 minutes, added donations, and offered bring-your-own-key access. Players liked the immersion but disliked being locked out without a solution reveal, while hallucinated facts remain a core narrative-consistency risk.
