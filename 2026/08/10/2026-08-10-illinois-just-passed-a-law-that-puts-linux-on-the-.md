# Illinois Just Passed a Law That Puts Linux on the Hook for Age Verification

- Score: 270 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49249150) | Link: https://linuxstans.com/illinois-hb5511-operating-system-age-verification/

### TL;DR

Illinois Public Act 104-0664 couples social-media protections for minors with duties for defined operating-system providers. By January 1, 2028, covered manufacturers must ask users to self-declare age and expose an encrypted API returning one of four age brackets; this is not identity verification, and apps begin requesting signals July 1, 2028. The statute lacks Colorado’s open-source exemption, potentially covering Linux distributors, though reach over hobbyists is uncertain. HN split between privacy-ratchet fears and the narrower view that a parent-set device signal could enforce useful defaults without passports or face scans.

### Comment pulse

- Centralizing age once may reduce repetitive app collection — counterpoint: an OS-level signal creates infrastructure future laws could make more invasive.
- Content labeling and parent-controlled filters were proposed as less privacy-sensitive alternatives to making every device disclose a user bracket.
- International, offline-first, or hobby distributions may resist enforcement, while vendors doing business in Illinois face clearer exposure.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The requirement is self-declaration; calling it verification obscures both its limited efficacy and extensible architecture.
- **Impact:** OS vendors must build APIs; open-source projects face ambiguity; minors receive stronger social-platform defaults.
- **Watch next:** Open-source amendment, litigation, Attorney General guidance, penalty reconciliation, API standards, and the 2028 deadlines.
