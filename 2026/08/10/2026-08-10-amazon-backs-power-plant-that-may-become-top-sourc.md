# Amazon backs power plant that may become top source of US climate pollution

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49249971) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/amazon-funds-biggest-gas-power-plant-in-us-despite-climate-pledge/

### TL;DR

Amazon confirmed it is investing in on-site gas generation for a Pecos County, Texas data center, arguing the project will pay its own energy costs, avoid raising local rates, and connect to the grid when capacity becomes available. The permit allows up to 33 million tons of CO₂ annually, though facilities rarely reach permitted maxima; critics say even the possibility conflicts with Amazon’s 2040 net-zero pledge as its emissions rise. HN debate split between rejecting new fossil infrastructure and emphasizing reliability, affordability, nuclear power, and precise interpretation of permit ceilings.

### Comment pulse

- New gas plants lock in emissions — counterpoint: commenters argued grids still need dispatchable power and abrupt bans impose affordability risks.
- Calling it the “top source” overstates a permit ceiling; actual output and pollutant-specific climate effects must be measured.
- Nuclear attracted support as firm low-carbon supply, but the article’s economic driver is avoiding years-long grid interconnection delays.
