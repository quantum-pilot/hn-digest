# Mark Zuckerberg attacks 'closed' AI rivals as Meta returns to open models

- Score: 337 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243880) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/4e3957f8-ea7c-4c46-a3de-cdce8e526878

### TL;DR

Meta released weights for Muse Glimmer and promised the more powerful Muse Spark within weeks, reversing its earlier decision to withhold Spark on safety grounds. Mark Zuckerberg framed open models as a check on institutional power, defended learning through distillation, and argued distributed scrutiny improves security. The shift accompanies heavy infrastructure spending, including a $1 billion host-community fund, even as infrastructure costs cut free cash flow 91%. HN welcomed more competition but disputed Meta’s “open source” language, historical credit, trustworthiness, and motive: empowering individuals versus commoditizing rivals while monetizing compute.

### Comment pulse
