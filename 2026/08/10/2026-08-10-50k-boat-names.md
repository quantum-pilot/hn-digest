# 50k Boat Names

- Score: 149 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243029) | Link: https://www.beautifulpublicdata.com/boat-names/

### TL;DR

Jon Keegan mined several years of NOAA Marine Cadastre AIS broadcasts to build a searchable collection of more than 50,000 vessel names, highlighting puns, pop-culture references, occupations, and wealth signals. It is a sample, not a registry: AIS is mandatory mainly for larger commercial vessels, many recreational craft opt in, and only about one-third of 150,000 sampled boats supplied names. HN readers enjoyed the wordplay but found confusing deduplication, inconsistent categories, and an ownership-income chart easily mistaken for the share of each income group owning boats.

### Comment pulse

- Search results seem deduplicated by name, not vessel; unclear counts and overbroad categories undermine confidence in the interface.
- Boat demographics invite denominator mistakes: 7% of owners earning under $25,000 is not 7% ownership within that income bracket.
- Nerdy names prompted new puns; sailing diaries prompted debate over author-centered logs versus stories written for readers.
