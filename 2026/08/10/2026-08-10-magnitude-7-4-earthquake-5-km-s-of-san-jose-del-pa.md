# Magnitude 7.4 Earthquake – 5 km S of San José del Palmar, Colombia

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49245251) | Link: https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tjl2/executive

### TL;DR

A reviewed magnitude-7.4 earthquake struck five kilometers south of San José del Palmar, Colombia, at 12:34 UTC, about 110 kilometers deep inside the subducting Nazca plate. USGS estimated 10.5 million people experienced strong to very strong shaking and assigned an orange alert for potentially widespread damage and casualties. Its one-week forecast gives a 69% chance of at least one magnitude-5-plus aftershock, 14% for magnitude 6-plus, and 2% for magnitude 7-plus. HN eyewitnesses in Medellín and Bogotá described prolonged shaking, evacuations, panic, congested communications, and only seconds of phone warning.

### Comment pulse

- Alerts arrived roughly five seconds before shaking for some users, enough to notice but often too little to decide or act.

- Congested terrestrial communications separated families; one rural household near Pereira remained reachable through Starlink.
