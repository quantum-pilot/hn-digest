# 'Pervert glasses': Backlash against Meta's smart glasses grows

- Score: 161 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49244783) | Link: https://www.seattletimes.com/business/technology/pervert-glasses-backlash-against-metas-smart-glasses-grows/

### TL;DR

Meta’s camera-equipped glasses are drawing backlash because ordinary-looking frames enable people to record strangers without meaningful consent, then publish pickup and prank videos. Users have hidden or drilled out the white recording light, prompting Meta to disable cameras when tampering is detected; venues and a courtroom have restricted them. The problem extends beyond one vendor as major competitors prepare eyewear. HN discussion focused less on whether public recording is legal than whether mass distribution and monetization should require consent, while preserving recording for evidence of misconduct.

### Comment pulse

- Distrust targets both Meta and arbitrary wearers; even privacy-respecting software cannot control hostile users.
- Conspicuous indicators gained support, including designs where covering the signal also blocks or disables the camera.
- Some preferred regulating distribution and commercial use over recording itself — counterpoint: boundaries between memory, gossip, and media remain difficult.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** A recording light helps only when tamper resistance, social norms, and enforcement make it credible.
- **Impact:** Venues, employers, schools, and courts must establish wearable-camera policies before covert capture becomes routine.
- **Watch next:** Indicator standards, facial-recognition decisions, platform moderation, and consent rules for monetized footage.
