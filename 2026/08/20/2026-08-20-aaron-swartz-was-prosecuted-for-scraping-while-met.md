# Aaron Swartz was prosecuted for scraping, while Meta does it without consequence

- Score: 746 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49379550) | Link: https://blog.curiousquail.com/im-upset-again-about-a-co-creator-of-rss-being-prosecuted-for-something-meta-is-doing-with-little-consequence/

### TL;DR
The blog post laments that Aaron Swartz was threatened with decades in prison for bulk-downloading ~70 GB of JSTOR papers to liberate knowledge, while Meta allegedly torrents 80+ TB of pirated books to train AI and will likely face only manageable civil penalties. Hacker News discussion largely agrees there’s a structural double standard: prosecutors and institutions zealously pursued a vulnerable individual but avoid actions that threaten powerful firms or US AI ambitions, while copyright law mainly protects business models, not justice or the public.
