# Aaron Swartz was prosecuted for scraping, while Meta does it without consequence

- Score: 746 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49379550) | Link: https://blog.curiousquail.com/im-upset-again-about-a-co-creator-of-rss-being-prosecuted-for-something-meta-is-doing-with-little-consequence/

### TL;DR

A polemic condemns the disparity between Aaron Swartz’s federal prosecution after downloading roughly 70GB from JSTOR and Meta’s alleged use of about 80TB of pirated books for AI training. It portrays Swartz’s goal as public knowledge and Meta’s as private profit. Commenters broadly agree the prosecution was excessive, but contest the analogy’s details: Swartz used physical access and repeatedly evaded blocks, while 35 years was a statutory maximum, not a likely sentence. Others stress unequal legal power and his personal vulnerability.

### Comment pulse

- Selective-enforcement criticism dominated → government pursued Swartz after JSTOR settled, while large companies can absorb litigation and align with national AI priorities.
- Factual objections narrowed the analogy → Swartz used physical access and evaded blocks — counterpoint: neither justified disproportionate prosecution.
- First-hand testimony rejected symbolic mythmaking → commenters urged attention to Swartz’s mental-health vulnerability and institutions that intensified pressure.
