# I should have loved biology (2020)

- Score: 182 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49377853) | Link: https://jsomers.net/i-should-have-loved-biology/

### TL;DR

James Somers argues biology should begin with astonishment and unanswered questions, not detached vocabulary and conclusions. His study of SARS-CoV-2 and immunology reveals a fractal but physical world where molecular shape, diffusion, gene expression, and experimental methods make mechanisms concrete. He recommends thin, deep investigations; studying reusable laboratory techniques; vivid histories of wrong turns; and collaborative visual or simulated models. Commenters recognized a general pedagogical failure, while warning that discovery-led teaching must still supply foundational skills and that research careers can be unromantic.

### LLM perspective

- View: Inquiry can organize intimidating vocabulary around mechanisms and evidence, but cannot replace cumulative factual and technical mastery.
- Impact: Students may gain durable models by seeing how discoveries arose and physically visualizing otherwise invisible molecular processes.
- Watch next: Classroom trials measuring retention, experiment-design skills, accessible diagramming tools, simulations, and honest career context.
