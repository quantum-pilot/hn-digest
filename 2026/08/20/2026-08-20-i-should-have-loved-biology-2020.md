# I should have loved biology (2020)

- Score: 182 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49377853) | Link: https://jsomers.net/i-should-have-loved-biology/

### TL;DR
Somers argues he “should have loved biology” but school reduced it to dead vocabulary instead of a detective story about how life works. He reintroduces biology as physical, computational machinery—cells as self-modifying programs, proteins as interacting shapes—grounded in historical experiments, modern methods like RNA‑seq, and vivid illustrations. He then dreams of new tools: collaborative diagrams, simulations, and dynamic models that let people “see the unseeable.” HN readers extend this to pedagogy, contrasting hands‑on discovery with rote courses and noting harsh life‑science career realities.
