# Windows brings out the Rorschach test in everyone (2003)

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49371006) | Link: https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20030825-00/?p=42803

### TL;DR

Raymond Chen recounts visual ambiguity repeatedly forcing Windows assets to change. A Windows 95 anti-piracy hologram showed its photographer’s infant son pointing at a logo; one government inferred nudity below the cropped waist, so a rushed replacement added a shirt and overalls but lost the arm animation. He also recalls alleged hidden images in cloud art and Windows XP complaints that a wallpaper resembled buttocks, a user icon resembled Hitler, and a switch-user cartoon resembled anatomy. Commenters celebrated Chen’s lore, though one cited an archive disputing the wallpaper account.
