# Windows brings out the Rorschach test in everyone (2003)

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49371006) | Link: https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20030825-00/?p=42803

### TL;DR
Raymond Chen recounts how seemingly innocuous Windows visuals kept provoking wildly subjective reactions: a Windows 95 anti-piracy hologram with a shirtless baby was condemned by one government as “nudity,” forcing a rushed redesign; later, Windows XP wallpapers and UI icons were accused of resembling buttocks, Hitler, or obscene body parts. He likens this to a Rorschach test. Hacker News readers mostly revel in Chen’s storytelling, swap similar “offended by pixels” tales from Linux/BSD, and nitpick the exact history of XP’s wallpapers.
