# Linux 7.2

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49376265) | Link: https://www.igalia.com/2026/08/19/Linux-72-Released.html

### TL;DR

Linux 7.2 continues the trend of heavy kernel releases, with notable work on GPU/CPU scheduling and graphics. Igalia’s contributions center on an experimental fair DRM GPU scheduler (opt‑in due to a late regression), better sched_ext debugging, runtime power management and stability fixes for Raspberry Pi GPUs, a 14‑year‑old futex robust‑list bug fix, and HDMI 2.1 FRL groundwork in amdgpu. HN discussion focuses on how HDMI 2.1 legal barriers eased, who actually reads kernel changelogs, and HDMI vs DisplayPort tradeoffs.
