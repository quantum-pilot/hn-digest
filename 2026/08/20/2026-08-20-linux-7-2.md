# Linux 7.2

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49376265) | Link: https://www.igalia.com/2026/08/19/Linux-72-Released.html

### TL;DR

Linux 7.2 arrived on schedule after the second-busiest cycle, adding cache-aware CPU scheduling, MGLRU improvements, sched_ext sub-schedulers, and automatic multi-size transparent hugepages. Igalia’s contribution summary highlights better sched_ext crash diagnostics, runtime GPU power management for Raspberry Pi 4/5, long-standing Pi 3 GPU crash fixes, a 14-year-old futex corruption fix, and initial AMD HDMI 2.1 FRL support. A fairer DRM scheduler remains opt-in after a late regression. Discussion centered on HDMI enablement, Raspberry Pi benefits, and who reads kernel release summaries.
