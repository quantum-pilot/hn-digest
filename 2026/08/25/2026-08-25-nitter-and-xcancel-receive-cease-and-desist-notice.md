# Nitter and XCancel receive cease and desist notices

- Score: 1149 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437283) | Link: https://github.com/zedeus/nitter/issues/1442

### TL;DR

Public Nitter instances stopped working after their operator received cease-and-desist letters; XCancel and other public mirrors reportedly received them too. Self-hosted instances initially still worked, while maintainers sought legal advice and support. HN framed the shutdown as the predictable cost of letting one private platform control access to socially important user content. The sharper concern was public agencies relying on X for emergency and civic updates while anonymous readers increasingly face login, identity, or biometric gates.

### Comment pulse

- Essential updates should not depend on X → public agencies need accessible websites or RSS — counterpoint: centralization provides discoverability and moderation.
- Anonymous readers are abandoning gated platforms → Nitter and XCancel were their remaining doorway, while similar restrictions spread across Reddit and Imgur.
- Legal shelter could sustain interoperability projects → some advocated protection abroad, while critics questioned geopolitical framing and urged rehosting instead.
