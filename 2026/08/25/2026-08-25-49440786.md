# XCancel also down, at least for time being

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49440786) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49440786

### TL;DR

XCancel says it stopped operating after receiving an X Corp cease-and-desist letter on August 24 at 8 p.m. EST and is seeking legal advice. The brief HN thread redirected readers to the larger related discussion. Users who relied on XCancel to read bookmarked public accounts without joining X expect to see less of that content, with some suggesting automatic alternative-front-end redirects. Others treated leaving X as a practical service choice rather than a statement about its owner.

### Comment pulse
