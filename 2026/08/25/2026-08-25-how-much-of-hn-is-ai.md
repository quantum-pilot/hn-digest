# How much of HN is AI?

- Score: 273 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49435728) | Link: https://blog.coredump.cx/p/how-much-of-hn-is-ai

### TL;DR

An audit of Hacker News’s daily top five found AI-related or apparently AI-generated stories rising from about 40% in February 2026 to roughly 50–60% in June. The author used topic labels plus Pangram detection and manual review, arguing the shift reduces variety and may include machine-written submissions or comments. HN broadly recognized AI’s dominance but disputed the categories, detector confidence, and whether saturation is excessive or proportionate to a technology reshaping the industry.

### Comment pulse

- The measurement may overgroup stories → AI-adjacent topics are not necessarily an LLM monoculture.
- Saturation feels real → regular readers report four to six AI stories among the top ten and declining serendipity.
- Dominance may be appropriate → supporters consider AI the largest technology shift in decades.

### LLM perspective

- View: Topic concentration matters independently of whether individual posts were machine-written.
- Impact: Readers seeking eclectic technical discovery may migrate, filter aggressively, or amplify non-AI submissions.
- Watch next: Publish labeling criteria, prompts, detector error audits, and longer-term category trends.
