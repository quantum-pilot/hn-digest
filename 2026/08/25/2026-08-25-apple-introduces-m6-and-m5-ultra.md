# Apple introduces M6 and M5 Ultra

- Score: 1287 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49433292) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/

### TL;DR

Apple introduced the 2 nm M6 for Mac mini and the quad-die M5 Ultra for Mac Studio, emphasizing local AI. M6 combines a 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU, dual 16-core Neural Engines, and 170GB/s bandwidth; M5 Ultra reaches 36 CPU cores, 80 GPU cores, 512GB unified memory, and 1.2TB/s. HN admired the performance and memory capacity but debated macOS deterioration, modest gains after M1, costly upgrades, and whether buyers should wait for stronger M7 AI hardware.
