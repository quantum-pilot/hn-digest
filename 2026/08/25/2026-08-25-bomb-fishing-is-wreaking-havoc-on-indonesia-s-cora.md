# Bomb fishing is wreaking havoc on Indonesia's coral reefs

- Score: 351 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434820) | Link: https://e360.yale.edu/digest/bomb-fishing-coral-reefs

### TL;DR

Hydrophones recorded more than 3,500 explosions around Indonesia’s Spermonde Archipelago, supporting an estimate above 8,500 blasts yearly across 350 square miles. Each improvised bomb can kill within a 90-foot radius and pulverize roughly 200 square feet of reef, leaving unstable rubble that resists coral recovery. Researchers propose open-source, GPS-synchronized acoustic detection for enforcement. HN commenters cited Thailand’s externally pressured reforms as evidence that enforcement works, while debating poverty, tourism incentives, restoration, and the article’s damage arithmetic.
