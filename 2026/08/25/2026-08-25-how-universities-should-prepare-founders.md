# How Universities Should Prepare Founders

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49428121) | Link: https://paulgraham.com/prepare.html

### TL;DR

Paul Graham argues universities should prepare founders by teaching powerful subjects, cultivating habitual builders, normalizing startups, and giving students unscheduled time for independent projects—not by adding entrepreneurship classes or pitch competitions. Projects deepen knowledge, reveal compatible cofounders, normalize self-direction, and generate unexpected ideas; prototypes should impress users rather than simulated investors. HN valued learning-by-doing but challenged YC’s founder archetype, noting opportunity costs, family wealth, non-venture businesses, basic research, and society’s need for capable people outside startups.
