# Don't Wordle

- Score: 381 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49432319) | Link: https://dontwordle.com/

### TL;DR

Don’t Wordle reverses the familiar puzzle: players must avoid guessing the hidden five-letter word while every guess still obeys accumulated green, yellow, and gray clues. The shrinking valid-word pool makes losing deliberately surprisingly difficult; five undos offer escape routes, while “purist” play forbids them. HN players traded strategies built around rare letters, repeated letters, and delayed vowel discovery, but also objected to confusing duplicate-letter constraints and the site’s sprawling cookie-consent interface.
