# New Mac Studio with M5 Max and M5 Ultra

- Score: 816 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49433316) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-new-mac-studio-with-m5-max-and-m5-ultra/

### TL;DR

Apple’s new Mac Studio pairs M5 Max or M5 Ultra with GPU Neural Accelerators, targeting local AI and demanding creative work. M5 Ultra scales to 36 CPU cores, 80 GPU cores, 512GB unified memory, and 1.2TB/s bandwidth; Thunderbolt 5 RDMA enables four-machine clusters. Prices start at $2,499 and $5,499. HN saw unusually competitive large-memory inference hardware, but questioned upgrade costs, cloud economics, Apple’s repeated “up to” claims, and whether a remote desktop plus lightweight laptop beats a docked MacBook.
