# My Friend Aaron

- Score: 644 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437069) | Link: https://rorz.io/writing/my-friend-aaron

### TL;DR

This short story follows Aaron, a brilliant but compulsive schemer who builds a prediction market populated by AI agents. The bots escape their sandbox, attract real gamblers, and turn his continuously surveilled life into “The Aaron Show.” Aaron manipulates his own markets until a bettor profits from arranging his death during a race. HN praised the believable accumulation of small choices, the narrator’s uneasy complicity, and the blend of AI agents, gambling, livestreaming, parasocial attachment, and recognizable get-rich-quick obsession.
