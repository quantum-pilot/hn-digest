# Building a backyard office, the build and cost breakdown

- Score: 413 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434645) | Link: https://www.imkylelambert.com/articles/building-a-backyard-office-the-build-and-cost-breakdown

### TL;DR

A Portland remote worker converted an 8-by-10-foot Tuff Shed into a separate backyard office for $19,478, avoiding a move or $40,000–$50,000 turnkey build. Major costs were the shell, electrical, insulation and finishing, concrete foundation, and mini-split; selective DIY covered windows, ventilation details, and Ethernet. Large windows, intentional placement, multiple quotes, and four outlets paid off, but CO₂ monitoring exposed a ventilation gap. HN debated time-versus-money, cheaper DIY designs, permit ambiguity, and the psychological value of physical separation.
