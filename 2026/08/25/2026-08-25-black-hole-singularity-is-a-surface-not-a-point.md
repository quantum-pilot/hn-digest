# Black hole singularity is a surface not a point

- Score: 293 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437210) | Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21590

### TL;DR

Hamilton and McMaken argue that a black hole’s singularity is a spacelike surface, not a central point: infalling observers on different angular paths lose causal contact before reaching it rather than meeting there. For rotating holes, they place the likely singular surface near the inner horizon, where perturbations drive mass inflation. They further speculate that quantum states reside on this effectively two-dimensional surface in equilibrium with trapped Hawking radiation. HN distinguished the textbook geometric claim from the paper’s less-standard quantum implications.
