- Score: 1555 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49438052) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/aug/25/dolly-parton-country-singer-dead

### TL;DR

Dolly Parton died at 80 after a brief cancer battle, leaving an exceptional legacy across songwriting, performance, business, and philanthropy. She wrote country standards including “Jolene” and “I Will Always Love You,” earned 25 country No. 1 singles, and built enterprises around Dollywood. HN’s tributes emphasized how her Imagination Library and other giving made her influence tangible far beyond music, including for commenters who received her books as children or now read them with their own families.
