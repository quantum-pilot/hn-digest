# Starbase, LA

- Score: 277 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49436822) | Link: https://www.spacex.com/sites/starbase-la

### TL;DR

SpaceX proposes a self-sufficient Louisiana Gulf Coast spaceport designed for thousands of annual Starship flights, combining propellant production, power, deep-water shipping, vehicle processing, and an airport. It pledges at least $100 billion and more than 3,000 jobs, alongside shoreline protection, marsh creation, and wildlife monitoring. HN saw major construction opportunities and strategic advantages from southbound launch corridors and nearby methane infrastructure, while questioning payment risk, environmental oversight, corporate messaging, and whether promised benefits will reach local communities.
