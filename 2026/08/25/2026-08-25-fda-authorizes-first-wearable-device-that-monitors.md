# FDA authorizes first wearable device that monitors ketone and blood sugar levels

- Score: 480 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49439017) | Link: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-authorizes-first-wearable-device-continuously-monitors-both-ketone-levels-and-blood-sugar

### TL;DR

The FDA authorized Abbott’s Libre Duo 10 Day for diabetic patients aged two and older, making it the first U.S. wearable to continuously track ketones and the first device globally to pair continuous ketone and glucose readings. It samples every minute, sends trends and alerts to a smartphone, and may expose rising diabetic ketoacidosis risk earlier than single-use tests. HN welcomed the safety advance but stressed affordability, reimbursement, and uncertainty about how much ketone data improves existing closed-loop glucose-and-insulin systems.
