# My favorite nonfiction books about cults, scams, and schemes

- Score: 180 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49408858) | Link: https://bookdna.com/best-books/nonfiction-about-cults-scams-and-schemes

### TL;DR

Former multilevel-marketing leader Emily Paulson recommends five nonfiction accounts shaped by her study of coercive control: Amanda Montell’s Cultish on manipulative language; Sarah Edmondson’s Scarred on NXIVM indoctrination; Misty Griffin’s Tears of the Silenced on abuse within an Amish community; Daniel Barban Levin’s Slonim Woods 9 on Larry Ray’s manipulation; and Mike Rinder’s A Billion Years on Scientology, complicity, and escape. These are the actual editorial picks. The captured page also contains Paulson’s own-book promotion, membership advertising, unrelated recommendations, and affiliate copy; commenters’ extra books and podcast are separate suggestions.

### LLM perspective

- View: Together, the five books examine recruitment, language, isolation, abuse, complicity, and escape across distinct coercive systems.
