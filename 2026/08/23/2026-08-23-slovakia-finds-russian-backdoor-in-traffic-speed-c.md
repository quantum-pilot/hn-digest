# Slovakia finds Russian backdoor in traffic speed cameras

- Score: 345 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49409200) | Link: https://risky.biz/risky-bulletin-slovakia-finds-russian-backdoor-in-traffic-speed-cameras/

### TL;DR

Slovakia’s NBU says NERO R-ONE traffic cameras contain an SMS-triggered backdoor granting shell and network access from hardcoded Russian phone numbers. Investigators identified them as rebranded Semicon CORDON PRO.M units after media tied procurement to a Cyprus shell company and reports of fake certifications. The €30 million, EU-funded program planned 279 cameras; deployment is now paused pending an independent audit. NBU also reported disabled Secure Boot, vulnerable web management, and unauthenticated live streams, undermining the ministry’s closed-network defense. Comments debated procurement, auditable firmware, supply-chain risk, and Slovak politics.
