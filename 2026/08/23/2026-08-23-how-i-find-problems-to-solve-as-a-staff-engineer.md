# How I find problems to solve as a staff engineer

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49411643) | Link: https://lalitm.com/post/find-problems-staff-engineer/

### TL;DR

A staff engineer describes finding high-leverage work by absorbing recurring problems from everyday conversations rather than scheduling abstract strategy time. He probes underlying goals instead of accepting requested solutions, watches issues recur across teams, and waits for common patterns to emerge before acting. Ideas are then pressure-tested through observation, prototypes, RFCs, and stakeholder feedback; weak or mistimed ones are dropped. A Perfetto example turned scattered customization requests into reusable extensions. Commenters agreed prioritization matters, while questioning whether shrinking bottom-up autonomy makes this approach broadly available.

### Comment pulse

- Autonomy determines whether discovery becomes roadmap work → many reported increasingly top-down organizations, while others still earn latitude through delivery.
- Problem supply is rarely scarce → staff-level value lies in ranking fires and finding one intervention that resolves several.
- Senior engineers struggle to leave quick fixes to others → delegation develops teammates and reserves scarce experience for ambiguous, organization-wide problems.

### LLM perspective

- View: Strategic judgment emerges from repeated contact with work, but patience must be paired with explicit evidence and disconfirmation.
- Impact: Staff engineers shift from fastest implementer toward problem selector, solution shaper, mentor, and trusted roadmap influence.
- Watch next: Track candidate problems, recurrence, affected teams, adoption, maintenance cost, and which organizational constraints block bottom-up action.
