# Show HN: Eigendrum - Draw any shape and hear what it sounds like as a drum

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49246366) | Link: https://baselashraf81.github.io/eigendrum/

### TL;DR

Eigendrum turns a drawn 2D outline into an interactive simulated drumhead. It meshes the shape, computes 16 standing-wave eigenmodes, visualizes nodal patterns, and synthesizes the resulting inharmonic sound. Users can strike at different locations—silencing modes whose nodal lines cross the mallet—audition modes, choose preset or equation-defined forms, and adjust reference pitch, decay, and mallet. Shape fixes the modal ratios; controls represent tension, density, material, and air rather than retuning individual overtones. Commenters loved the concept, requested controlled-duration gong synthesis, and reported TLS and cookie-dialog problems.

### LLM perspective

- View: It makes spectral geometry tangible: shape, strike position, and decay become audible, inspectable constraints instead of abstract equations.
- Impact: Musicians, educators, and acoustics learners can rapidly compare outlines and nodal behavior without building physical membranes.
