# At-home test for infected ticks could improve Lyme Disease diagnosis

- Score: 208 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49310682) | Link: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/the-first-at-home-test-for-infected-ticks-could-improve-lyme-disease-diagnosis-180989235/

### TL;DR

LymeAlert, planned for August at about $50, is a 15-minute lateral-flow test that crushes a removed tick and detects Borrelia burgdorferi; an app can interpret strips, connect telehealth, and contribute hotspot data. Its aim is to return information within the 72-hour prophylaxis window, unlike mail-in testing. Crucially, it tests the tick, not human infection: only 1%–5% of bites cause Lyme disease, and the CDC discourages tick testing because false results can mislead care. Commenters questioned unpublished accuracy, detection limits, and antibiotic consequences, while recognizing access and education needs.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics fear positive results will prompt unnecessary treatment and false negatives will wrongly override existing prophylaxis criteria.
- People in emerging risk areas welcome an accessible precaution—counterpoint: infection awareness cannot substitute for clinician-guided assessment of attachment, geography, and symptoms.
