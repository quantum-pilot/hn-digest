### TL;DR

A reviewed magnitude-7.7 reverse-faulting earthquake struck at 10-kilometre depth, 68 kilometres north-northwest of Ende, Indonesia, on August 14 at 21:58 UTC. USGS estimated roughly two million people experienced strong-to-violent shaking, with potentially widespread casualties and damage; PAGER was yellow. Its one-week forecast gave a 55% chance of at least one magnitude-6-plus aftershock and 9% for magnitude 7-plus. Discussion focused on small localized tsunami observations, aftershock risk, and whether 2026 feels unusually active, while commenters cautioned against inferring broad trends from impressions.
