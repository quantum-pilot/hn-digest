# Engineers will do anything to avoid learning from history

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49314744) | Link: https://horn.gg/blog/engineers-will-do-anything-to-avoid-learning-from-history/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that “agentic orchestration” largely rediscovers program management: define requirements, choose priorities, divide work, inspect artifacts, and demand regular check-ins. It compares this pattern with data science revisiting statistics and crypto replaying financial history, blaming novelty incentives, disciplinary distrust, and overconfidence. Suggested reading spans PMBOK, The Mythical Man-Month, Royce, High Output Management, and The Goal. Commenters agreed managerial experience helps, but countered that tokens, prompt variation, drift, evaluation, concurrency, and agents’ peculiar failure modes make the analogy incomplete.
