# The Ploopy A+ Trackball Is Here

- Score: 134 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49306443) | Link: https://blog.ploopy.co/the-aplus-is-finally-here-499

### TL;DR

Ploopy’s A+ updates its ambidextrous, 3D-printed Adept trackball with eight buttons, two programmable rotary knobs, a detachable wrist rest, LEDs, gesture shortcuts, layers, and on-device settings. It retains the PMW-3360 sensor, 1,000 Hz polling, Omron switches, QMK/VIA firmware, and fully published hardware and assembly files under reciprocal open licenses. The $99 CAD kit opens for preorder August 19, 2026. Commenters welcomed hardware remapping and repairable openness, while debating the name, case finish, ergonomics, and the absence of ball-twist input.

### Comment pulse

- Owners praised assembly, hardware-stored remapping, and ball-based scrolling; one reported hand pain, a broken button, and imperfect replacement-case fit.
- Several wanted twist sensing for CAD or zooming, though commenters cited prior products, dual-sensor complexity, and a Kensington patent.

### LLM perspective

- View: A coherent enthusiast upgrade: programmability and repairability are its differentiation, not an unprecedented pointing mechanism.
