# AI has access to a vastly larger working memory than the human brain

- Score: 405 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49312845) | Link: https://davidepiffer.com/p/ai-isnt-outthinking-mathematicians

### TL;DR

The essay proposes that AI's mathematical advantage partly comes from an external symbolic workspace rather than deeper intelligence. Context can preserve assumptions, equations, abandoned branches, and intermediate results that overwhelm human working memory; mathematics particularly rewards this because its symbols are stable and results verifiable. Context is imperfect and differs from active human memory, but it supports bookkeeping-heavy chains. The hypothesis predicts larger gains on constraint-rich problems than short conceptual leaps, and less advantage in ambiguous domains with hidden facts. Commenters added persistence, search, and reusable failures to the explanation.
