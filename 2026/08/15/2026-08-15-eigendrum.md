# eigendrum

- Score: 213 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49305250) | Link: https://eigendrum.com/#p=circle

### TL;DR

Eigendrum is an interactive browser instrument that turns selectable or hand-drawn 2D outlines into vibrating drumheads. It computes 16 standing-wave modes, lets users strike movable locations, and silences modes whose nodal lines cross the strike point, changing timbre with position. The displayed example has a 130 Hz fundamental and inharmonic second mode at 1.594×, creating a thud. Its 2,061-unknown mesh solves in 3.7 seconds with 0.537% error. Users can inspect meshes, isolate modes, and adjust reference pitch and decay. Comments praised the concept but reported site-access and cookie-dialog friction.
