# The other Sean Byrne doesn't exist

- Score: 371 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49307592) | Link: https://conic.al/writing/the-other-sean-byrne-doesnt-exist/

### TL;DR

Apple denied Sean Joseph Byrne access to App Store Connect after matching him to a U.S. Entity List entry, despite receiving identity documents and his explanation. Court records indicate the listed “Sean Byrne” was an alias used by an Irish aircraft-parts company, not a separate person, yet the sparse 2009 record persists. Nasdaq and shippers previously cleared identical false positives; Apple stopped responding. Byrne fears early, networked applicant screening could silently propagate such matches and is asking the government to review an entry no real listed person can contest.
