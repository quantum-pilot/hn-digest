# A spectre is haunting Unicode

- Score: 164 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49310926) | Link: https://www.dampfkraft.com/ghost-characters.html

### TL;DR

Japan’s 1978 JIS X 0208 included several “ghost characters” with unclear meanings or provenance. A 1997 investigation traced most to obscure place-name records and cataloging errors: one glyph gained an accidental stroke from the seam between pasted paper pieces. Only 彁 remained without a confirmed source or historical precedent, though a misreading of 彊 is considered likely. Because JIS fed later encodings, the characters entered Unicode permanently. Commenters debated whether later newspaper scans explain 彁, how exceptional these ghosts are, and Han unification’s role in CJK encoding.
