# Jeff Dean leaving Alphabet

- Score: 297 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184746) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/05/technology/google-researchers-ai-startup.html

### TL;DR

Jeff Dean is leaving Google after 27 years to lead Discovery Loop, a public-benefit AI company he is forming with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals. The team wants to automate experimental loops so AI can improve its own systems and accelerate work in hardware, drugs, materials, and other sciences. Alphabet is investing, providing computing capacity for at least a year, and planning collaboration, suggesting an amicable split. The departure follows Google’s internal AI reorganization, prominent talent losses, and continued competition with smaller US and Chinese labs.
