# Civilian plane crash in New Mexico tied to military GPS blocking

- Score: 489 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49181099) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/a-civilian-plane-crashed-in-new-mexico-was-the-militarys-tech-to-blame/

### TL;DR

A May medevac flight from Roswell to Ruidoso crashed into New Mexico’s Capitan Mountains, killing two pilots and two nurses. The article argues a White Sands GPS-jamming exercise contributed: the inexperienced crew lost satellite navigation on a moonless night, controllers were assisting several affected aircraft, and the pilots abandoned an instrument plan for a visual descent across unseen terrain. GPS later returned briefly, but jamming resumed. The episode illustrates how expanding counter-drone electronic warfare can remove navigation and terrain-warning safeguards far beyond test or conflict zones.
