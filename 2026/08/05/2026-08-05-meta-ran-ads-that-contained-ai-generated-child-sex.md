# Meta Ran Ads That Contained AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Imagery

- Score: 319 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49187977) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/meta-ran-ads-that-contained-ai-generated-child-sexual-abuse-imagery/

### TL;DR

Tech Transparency Project researchers found more than 50 paid Meta ads containing explicit AI-generated child sexual abuse imagery or sexualized depictions of minors, sometimes linking to nudify services. Meta’s library showed distribution across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and Threads from November through early August, including ads approved after Meta introduced new detection technology and some posted after WIRED began asking questions. Meta removed them after contact, cited automated-review improvements and prior enforcement, but repeated or identical violations and newly live examples raise serious questions about its ad-screening system.
