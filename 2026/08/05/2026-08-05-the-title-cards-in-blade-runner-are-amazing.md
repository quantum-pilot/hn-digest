# The title cards in Blade Runner are amazing

- Score: 424 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49189287) | Link: https://randsinrepose.com/archives/blade-runner-title-cards/

### TL;DR

The essay uses Blade Runner’s opening cards to argue that typography conveys feeling as well as information. A Goudy Oldstyle family, varied through capitals, small caps, italics, red, spacing, and book-like composition, establishes a literary yet dystopian mood before the story begins. Comparing it with a work print’s blunt Impact title illustrates how countless small design decisions create emotional quality. The broader lesson extends to AI-built products: generating functional software is easy, but the final refinement—and the accumulated human judgment behind it—separates acceptable work from memorable craft.
