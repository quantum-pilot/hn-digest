# Discovery Loop

- Score: 917 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184960) | Link: https://www.discoveryloop.com/

### TL;DR

Discovery Loop, founded by Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals, plans to automate experimental cycles: propose trials, run them at scale, evaluate results, and iterate. It will begin with machine-learning research, use its own systems to improve its technology stack, then pursue measurable problems across science and engineering. The public-facing vision is thousands of parallel experiments and much smaller teams achieving faster, higher-quality discovery. The founders emphasize experience spanning distributed systems, chips, infrastructure, models, and products, and are recruiting a lean, in-person team.
