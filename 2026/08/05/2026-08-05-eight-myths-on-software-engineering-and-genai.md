# Eight Myths on Software Engineering and GenAI

- Score: 290 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49176830) | Link: https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3807963

### TL;DR

An ACM Queue article challenges eight common claims about generative AI in software engineering. Research cited by the authors says developers spend about 14% of their time writing code, so faster generation may simply move bottlenecks into design, review, testing, and integration. Lines of code is a poor productivity metric; gains vary by task, experience, familiarity, and prompting. Individual licenses do not produce organizational transformation, adoption depends on trust and support, and enterprises cannot match startup speed because legacy systems, compliance, reliability, and coordination remain.

### Comment pulse
