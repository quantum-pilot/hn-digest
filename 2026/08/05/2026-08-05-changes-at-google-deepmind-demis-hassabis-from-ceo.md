# Changes at Google DeepMind: Demis Hassabis from CEO to Chair, Jeff Dean departs

- Score: 831 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184755) | Link: https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/

### TL;DR

Google is restructuring its AI leadership. Demis Hassabis will leave day-to-day management to become Google DeepMind chair and Alphabet chief scientist, while continuing to lead Isomorphic Labs and advise research. CTO and chief AI architect Koray Kavukcuoglu becomes senior vice president overseeing Gemini models, frontier research, and product teams, reporting to Sundar Pichai. After 27 years, Jeff Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat are departing to create an independent public-benefit corporation focused on machine learning, science, and engineering; Alphabet will invest, provide cloud support, and collaborate.

### Comment pulse

- Many viewed Dean and Ghemawat’s departure as larger than Hassabis’s title change, given their foundational infrastructure and AI work.
- Commenters disagreed whether the reshuffle signals strategic failure or normal talent economics in a startup-rich market.
- Some saw Hassabis’s role as a science-focused promotion; others questioned whether it is a face-saving reduction in authority.
