# Cops Used Flock to Track a Man Across State Lines for a Pretextual Weed Search

- Score: 326 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184190) | Link: https://www.404media.co/cops-used-flock-to-track-a-man-across-state-lines-to-create-pretext-to-search-his-car-for-weed/

### TL;DR

According to court records reviewed by 404 Media, Wisconsin police used Flock license-plate data to map Edward Abrams-Phillips’s repeated travel into Michigan, where marijuana is legal, and intercept him after a return trip. Officers cited that pattern, Michigan’s status as a marijuana source, and alleged odor when searching his vehicle. He was initially wanted in connection with bail jumping and domestic-violence allegations, but the bail-jumping charge was dismissed; the case ultimately produced a marijuana-possession conviction. The report presents the added drug investigation as surveillance-enabled pretext.
