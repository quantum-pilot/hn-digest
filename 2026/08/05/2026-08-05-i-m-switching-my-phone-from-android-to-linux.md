# I'm switching my phone from Android to Linux

- Score: 444 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49188022) | Link: https://runarcn.no/android-to-linux/

### TL;DR

Dissatisfied with Google Play Services, tracking, AI integration, custom-ROM obstacles, and tighter app-installation controls, the author is moving a Fairphone 4 from Android to SailfishOS. Sailfish offers appealing gestures, a Linux-like environment, and easy SSH access, but the unofficial port has outdated libraries, broken GPS and Waydroid, and uneven community apps. Ubuntu Touch has working Waydroid but weaker native apps and integration. Banking, government authentication, and ride-hailing still require Android, so the author will carry a backup Galaxy connected through the Fairphone’s hotspot.

### Comment pulse

- Many blamed proprietary Play Services rather than Android itself, while others criticized Android’s architecture and fragmented tooling.
- Banking, attestation, cameras, keyboards, maps, and transport apps were seen as the largest barriers to daily-driving mobile Linux.
