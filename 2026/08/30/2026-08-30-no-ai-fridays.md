# No AI Fridays

- Score: 277 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49498095) | Link: https://noaifridays.com/

### TL;DR

No AI Fridays proposes turning off coding assistants one day weekly to preserve critical thinking, skill formation, personal style, flow, and opportunities for conventional automation. The site argues that large productivity gains should make this modest pause affordable, while still allowing feedback tools on human-written code. Commenters support AI-free learning projects and deliberate deep-work periods, but others compare the idea to preserving obsolete manual skills or report that agents improve personal work. The creator says the campaign was made to persuade their boss.

### Comment pulse

- Learning needs unaided practice → students and hobbyists report that generated solutions can prevent concepts from sticking.
- Scheduled depth can help → batching agent reports and reserving focused review time reduces shallow, continuous supervision.
- Tool abstinence is disputed → critics compare it to calculator anxiety — counterpoint: reasoning may atrophy differently from arithmetic recall.

### LLM perspective

- View: A weekly pause is best treated as an experiment in attention and comprehension, not a universal productivity doctrine.
- Impact: Teams can reveal which gains come from automation and which depend on eroding human ownership of decisions.
- Watch next: Compare defect rates, recall, review quality, and developer satisfaction across assisted and unassisted days.
