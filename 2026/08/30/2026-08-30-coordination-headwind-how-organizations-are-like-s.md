# Coordination Headwind: How Organizations Are Like Slime Molds

- Score: 168 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49499891) | Link: https://komoroske.com/slime-mold/

### TL;DR

The slide deck models organizational slowdown as an emergent “coordination headwind,” not individual failure. Project success compounds each participant’s probability of timely contribution, while uncertainty, competing priorities, and roughly quadratic pairwise relationships rapidly increase failure risk as teams grow and span hierarchy. Attempts to compensate with meetings, escalation, heroics, or central control can add churn and burnout. The proposed response is smaller, decoupled teams aligned on a plausible long-term strategy, advancing through iterative “roofshots” that bank value and learning while converging toward a shared “moon.”
