# FreeCORE TrueNAS Core – Continued

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49494856) | Link: https://freecore.org/

### TL;DR

FreeCORE is an independent fork carrying TrueNAS CORE 13.3 forward on FreeBSD 15 and OpenZFS after upstream CORE development ended. The project says version 15.0-U1 is stable and supports an in-place upgrade from TrueNAS CORE, with source hosted on Codeberg and GitHub. The sparse launch page provides little evidence about maintainership depth or longevity. Commenters welcomed a FreeBSD continuation but debated whether appliance distributions justify dependency risk versus plain FreeBSD or Linux, and raised concerns about TrueNAS build-script availability and GPL expectations.

### Comment pulse

- A tested appliance stack saves operational time → interfaces and community validation can reduce dangerous storage mistakes.
- Vanilla systems avoid project abandonment risk → counterpoint: administrators may prefer supported configuration over mastering every subsystem.
- Commenters alleged upstream build scripts became unavailable → they disputed whether that violates GPL requirements or primarily its spirit.
