# “I just chose words carefully”

- Score: 960 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49503601) | Link: https://unsung.aresluna.org/i-just-chose-words-carefully/

### TL;DR

A late-1990s Super Metroid guide achieved fully justified monospace text without expanded spaces or hyphenation: its author manually chose words so every line ended exactly at the right margin across more than 17,000 words. The post presents this as an extreme version of familiar constraint-driven editing, such as fitting interface labels. Commenters recalled composing justified email and shaped text, cited deliberately aligned documentation and scripts, and debated whether layout constraints improve thought and creative cadence or merely reward exact length over clarity.

### Comment pulse

- Tight visual constraints force lexical reconsideration → writers substitute words, restructure sentences, and sometimes develop a distinctive rhythm.
- Alignment can make contrasts easier to scan → equal-length programming terms and carefully shaped comments create visual structure.
- Exact length does not guarantee readability → formatting discipline may optimize appearance while leaving explanation unimproved.
