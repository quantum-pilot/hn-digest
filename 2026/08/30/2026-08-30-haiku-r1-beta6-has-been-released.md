# Haiku R1/beta6 has been released

- Score: 343 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49499867) | Link: https://www.haiku-os.org/news/2026-08-26_haiku_r1_beta6

### TL;DR

Haiku R1/beta6 arrived roughly two years after beta5 and one week after the desktop operating system's 25th anniversary, with downloads and upgrades available. The supplied announcement contains no substantive release notes. Commenters celebrated Haiku's focused desktop design, low memory use, visual identity, and potential audio niche, but reported hardware-specific boot and USB-audio regressions. Discussion also centered on Haiku's rule against AI-generated contributions: some favored strict quality gates instead, while others proposed separate repositories for otherwise unusable ports and drivers.
