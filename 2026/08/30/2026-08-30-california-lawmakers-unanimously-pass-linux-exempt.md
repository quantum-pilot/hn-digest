# California lawmakers unanimously pass Linux exemption from age-verification law

- Score: 757 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49495372) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/software/linux/california-lawmakers-unanimously-pass-linux-exemption-from-age-verification-law-software-distributed-under-the-gpl-mit-bsd-and-apache-licenses-are-exempt

### TL;DR

California’s legislature passed AB 1856, which would exempt openly licensed operating systems from the Digital Age Assurance Act before its January 2027 implementation; the bill has been sent to Governor Gavin Newsom. The amendment excludes software whose licenses permit copying, redistribution, and modification, covering common GPL, MIT, BSD, and Apache distributions. It also carves out package-manager components and host-only add-ons, restricts unnecessary age-signal requests, and adds a good-faith safe harbor. Proprietary platforms remain covered, while SteamOS’s mixed distribution leaves its status uncertain.

### Comment pulse
