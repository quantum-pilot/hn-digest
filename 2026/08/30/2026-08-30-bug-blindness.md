# Bug Blindness

- Score: 392 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49494520) | Link: https://danluu.com/bug-blind/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that users and developers often stop noticing software defects because they unconsciously learn workarounds, defend favored products, or share the system’s own mental model. Dogfooding alone may therefore preserve blind spots: skilled users normalize friction that blocks newcomers. The author cites search quality, disliked enterprise software, and personal habits formed around broken interfaces, while acknowledging that some examples concern broader “quality blindness” rather than strict bugs. Receptive teams can improve by repeatedly identifying unnoticed failures and treating user workarounds as product evidence.
