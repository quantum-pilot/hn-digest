# European Commission Revives Push for Encryption Backdoors in ProtectEU Strategy

- Score: 436 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49499394) | Link: https://reclaimthenet.org/eu-protecteu-strategy-encryption-backdoor-law-enforcement

### TL;DR

A privacy-focused publication interprets the European Commission's ProtectEU internal-security strategy as groundwork for future encryption backdoors. The strategy itself is described as a multi-year vision rather than concrete legislation, calling for a roadmap toward lawful, effective law-enforcement access and technological ways to access encrypted data. The article argues those phrases mask an effort to weaken end-to-end encryption. HN commenters broadly opposed the direction, citing security risks, abuse by future governments, criminal migration to unrestricted tools, and concerns about EU institutional accountability.

### Comment pulse

- Exceptional access creates reusable weakness → criminals, hostile states, and automated attackers would not reserve it for lawful purposes.
- Commission persistence worries critics → counterpoint: commenters noted Parliament overwhelmingly rejected the only cited legislative vote.
- Surveillance powers outlive their original sponsors → future governments could redirect tools built for today's security threats.
