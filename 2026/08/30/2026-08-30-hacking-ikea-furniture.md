# Hacking IKEA Furniture

- Score: 339 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49497810) | Link: https://greenlightning.eu/diy/hacking-ikea-furniture/

### TL;DR

Unable to find affordable, living-room-friendly workbenches, the author converted two IKEA Kallax units and a discarded desktop into 80-by-60-centimeter equipment benches costing about €130 each. MDF reinforcement, rubber vibration layers, pre-drilling, templates, and securing the top produced a much sturdier result, though some lateral wobble remains. Mistakes included mismatched screws, incorrectly cut boards, and bad shelf-hole measurements. Commenters praised IKEA's accessible modularity but debated durability, sameness, used furniture, and whether building from solid wood offers better value.

### Comment pulse

- Cheap standardized parts lower experimentation risk → abundant dimensions, inserts, and drawings make customization approachable.
- Particle-board furniture can survive years when assembled carefully → counterpoint: repeated disassembly often weakens joints.
- Solid wood offers longevity and individuality → prefab drawers and cabinets still save tools, workspace, time, and finishing effort.
