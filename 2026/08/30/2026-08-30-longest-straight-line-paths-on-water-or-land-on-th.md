# Longest Straight Line Paths on Water or Land on the Earth (2018)

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49496782) | Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07389

### TL;DR

A 2018 paper formulates the longest uninterrupted straight-line routes over Earth's water and land as chaotic optimization problems shaped by islands, lakes, and fractal coastlines, then solves them with branch-and-bound. Commenters said the computed sea route confirmed an earlier Reddit globe-and-string result, while the land result runs roughly from Portugal toward Macao. They also proposed a longer Senegal-to-China land route allegedly missed because below-sea-level terrain was classified as water, though replies questioned whether it crosses the Suez Canal.
