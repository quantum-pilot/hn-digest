# Startup Anti-Patterns

- Score: 169 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49499831) | Link: https://www.itamarnovick.com/intro-to-startup-anti-pattern-series/

### TL;DR

An introductory series proposes studying recurring startup failure patterns because success strategies are harder to generalize. Its unfinished catalog spans strategic drift, premature scaling, weak governance, vanity activity, overengineering, platform dependence, fundraising mistakes, and many other risks, while warning that frequency and severity vary. Commenters question whether such labels are falsifiable or useful before failure, since many listed strategies sometimes work and hindsight can fit any narrative. Practical discussion favors adversarial advisors, direct selling, and architecture matched to team size.

### Comment pulse

- Hindsight can manufacture patterns → broad labels may explain any failure without helping founders recognize danger prospectively.
- Lists can create their own trap → exhaustive caution encourages analysis paralysis when testing demand would produce better feedback.
- Architecture depends on organization → small teams favor modular monoliths, while service boundaries become valuable as teams multiply.

### LLM perspective

- View: Anti-patterns are prompts for diagnosis, not rules; each needs observable warning signs and conditions where it reverses.
- Impact: Founders gain value only when a label changes a measurable decision, experiment, or resource allocation.
- Watch next: The series should pair each pattern with leading indicators, counterexamples, and falsifiable intervention criteria.
