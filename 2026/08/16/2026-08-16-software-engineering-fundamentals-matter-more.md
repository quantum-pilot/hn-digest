# Software Engineering fundamentals matter more

- Score: 318 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49314902) | Link: https://rhonabwy.com/2026/08/15/software-engineering-fundamentals-matter-more-than-ever/

### TL;DR

The author treats coding agents as implementation tools that have crossed the “can it be done” threshold, especially with concise context, deterministic feedback, type checking, and red/green TDD. But passing tests is not the profession’s hardest part: engineers still choose interfaces, seams, state, abstractions, failure behavior, maintainability, debuggability, and long-term tradeoffs. The author says models assume requirements and remain prompt-injection risks. Commenters split between extensive unsupervised success and reports of architectural mess, while agreeing that measurable specifications and reference patterns improve output. They disputed whether universal “fundamentals” exist across domains.

### Comment pulse

- An IKEA analogy framed AI as consistently good-enough production—counterpoint: bespoke software hides decisions and failure modes unlike standardized furniture.
- Maintainability is goal-dependent and subjective, yet neglect raises cognitive load and can stall teams after fast initial delivery.
- Learning advice combined domain-specific practice, repeatedly asking why until physical constraints emerge, and studying algorithms, data structures, and systems.
