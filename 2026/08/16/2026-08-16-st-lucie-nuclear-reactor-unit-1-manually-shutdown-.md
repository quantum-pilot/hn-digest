# St Lucie Nuclear Reactor Unit 1 manually shutdown, 3 control rods drop into core

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49320856) | Link: https://www.wptv.com/news/treasure-coast/region-st-lucie-county/saint-lucie-nuclear-power-plant-unit-1-manually-shut-down-after-3-control-rods-drop-into-reactor-core

### TL;DR

St. Lucie Unit 1 in Florida was manually tripped from full power on August 13 after three control rods dropped into the core. The NRC classified it as a non-emergency; all systems responded normally, operators held the reactor in hot standby and removed decay heat through the condenser, and Unit 2 was unaffected. NextEra later said Unit 1 returned to 100%. Commenters explained that inserted control rods suppress fission rather than add fuel, so conservative shutdown is expected, but the report did not identify why the rods dropped.
