# Research papers using "kidney disappointment" instead of "kidney failure"

- Score: 437 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49319389) | Link: https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22kidney+disappointment%22

### TL;DR

Google Scholar results show medical papers repeatedly replacing “kidney failure” with the nonsensical “kidney disappointment,” alongside prose full of similarly mechanical synonym swaps. The examples span fields, publishers, and years, including pre-generative-AI work. Commenters identified the pattern as a “tortured phrase”: authors or paper mills may run copied text through paraphrasers to evade plagiarism detectors. Others favored poor machine translation or non-native English, but the word-by-word substitutions made naive de-plagiarization the leading explanation rather than evidence of modern LLM authorship.

### Comment pulse
