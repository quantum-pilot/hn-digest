# Nvidia dramatically reduces amount of OpenAI infra financing it may guarantee

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49323686) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/nvidia-scales-back-250-billion-openai-data-center-guarantee-wsj-reports-2026-08-14/

### TL;DR

Reuters, citing a Wall Street Journal report, says Nvidia may backstop under $120 billion of the first phase of a proposed OpenAI data center in Ohio, down from $250 billion previously discussed. No agreement had been signed; one could arrive soon. Investor concern reportedly drove the narrower exposure. OpenAI is still discussing a binding lease for the full 10-gigawatt SB Energy campus, while Nvidia separately announced financing platforms targeting over $500 billion from third parties. Commenters stressed the proposal’s unprecedented scale and the difference between discussions and an actual guarantee.

### Comment pulse

- A 10-gigawatt campus could transform Ohio’s power demand and local workforce, making “data center” sound too modest for the proposal.
- Skeptics called Nvidia a quasi-bank—counterpoint: high hardware margins could cushion partial backstop losses while outside financiers bear greater exposure.
- Treating rapidly depreciating, difficult-to-repair GPUs as a financed asset class raised doubts about collateral value.
