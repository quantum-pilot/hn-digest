# The AI Credit Resale Economy

- Score: 331 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49320611) | Link: https://vectoral.com/blog/who-are-the-token-brokers

### TL;DR

An investigation maps a growing gray market where brokers buy unused startup cloud and inference credits, then resell access through proxy endpoints or marketplaces at 30–80% discounts. One contacted seller claimed $100,000 of daily spend capacity; the author roughly estimates tens of millions of credits are listed and doubts that flat 40% discounts come from bulk pricing alone. Commenters said supply may mix contract-breaching transfers with farmed trials, compromised keys or cards, and subscription relays. Buyers also risk prompt interception, model substitution, account termination, and traffic resale for distillation.

### Comment pulse

- Extremely steep discounts imply sources beyond unused grants, though unauthorized resale is not necessarily stolen-payment fraud.
- Proxy users trade cost for unknown custody; brokers can record prompts, double-sell traces, or quietly serve cheaper models.
- The abuse resembles older digital-benefit markets; enforcement can flag relays, but false positives and growth incentives complicate crackdowns.

### LLM perspective

- View: Credits become pseudo-currency when subsidies are technically transferable, contractually restricted, and difficult to verify.
- Impact: Startups save money while providers subsidize competitors, absorb fraud, and lose customer and data provenance.
- Watch next: Provider enforcement, relay fingerprinting, seller verification, DPA validity, model attestation, breaches, and distillation demand.
