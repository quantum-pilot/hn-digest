# The weekend is 100 years old

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49320984) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/money/2026/aug/16/the-weekend-is-100-years-old-skiveday-fridays-and-hybrid-working-ruined-it

### TL;DR

The two-day weekend reached a milestone because Henry Ford moved factory workers to five eight-hour days without a pay cut in 1926, though unions, religious groups, and employers had developed the idea earlier. Its deeper value is synchronized leisure: the Soviet experiment with staggered individual rest days damaged family and community life. Today hybrid work makes Friday quieter while messages invade Saturday and Sunday, widening the divide between flexible “time owners” and clock-bound workers. Commenters emphasized that even the seven-day week and industrial clock are social technologies, not timeless defaults.
