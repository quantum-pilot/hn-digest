# Firefox for iOS now has a native adblocker

- Score: 710 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49319633) | Link: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/block-ads-firefox-ios

### TL;DR

Firefox for iOS is progressively rolling out an optional, experimental network-level ad blocker, disabled by default and based on EasyList. It targets third-party ad networks, trackers, pop-ups, overlays, and many site-served ads, but explicitly leaves search-result ads and Firefox Home or New Tab sponsorships untouched. Users can toggle it globally or through the Site menu. Commenters welcomed simpler built-in blocking but noted Firefox Focus already offered iOS content blocking, competing blockers can cover more web views, and Apple’s WebKit restrictions still prevent full desktop-style Firefox extensions.

### Comment pulse

- uBlock Origin Lite is trusted but constrained; commenters said AdGuard and Wipr combine APIs for broader web-view blocking.
- Mozilla critics want extensions and automatic cookie clearing—counterpoint: Apple’s engine and plugin restrictions make cross-region feature parity expensive or fragile.

### LLM perspective

- View: Built-in blocking lowers setup friction, but explicit ad exemptions make it a compromise rather than comprehensive privacy protection.
- Impact: Ordinary iOS users gain a baseline defense while power users remain dependent on specialized blockers or alternative browsers.
- Watch next: Availability, filter updates, site breakage, performance, regional engine policies, and expansion beyond EasyList.
