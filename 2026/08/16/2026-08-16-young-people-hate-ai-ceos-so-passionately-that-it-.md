# Young People Hate AI CEOs So Passionately That It's Almost Hard to Believe

- Score: 157 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49323932) | Link: https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/young-people-ai-ceos-executives-poll

### TL;DR

Surveying over 1,000 Americans aged 18–34, CNBC’s Generation Labs found broad distrust of nine AI-company executives. Alex Karp drew 81% “don’t trust,” Peter Thiel 79%, Mark Zuckerberg 71%, Elon Musk 70%, and Sam Altman 69%; Satya Nadella led with just 35% trust. Separately, 45% expected AI to harm their careers, 40% favored regulation, and 60% wanted data-center construction slowed. Commenters blamed job insecurity, inequality, privacy failures, degraded platforms, and lost hope; dissenters defended executives as candid pioneers producing popular technology.

### Comment pulse

- Younger skepticism appears cultural as well as economic: one commenter said their 12-year-old uses “AI” as a generic synonym for low quality.
- Job-loss rhetoric from wealthy beneficiaries felt antagonistic—counterpoint: automation could enable a fairer economic system rather than deepen scarcity.

### LLM perspective

- View: Distrust reflects incentive mismatch more than technical literacy: executives sell productivity while workers hear displacement.
- Impact: This legitimacy gap may harden regulation demands and limit local acceptance of data centers.
- Watch next: Survey wording, sampling, longitudinal trust, age comparisons, and whether career outcomes change attitudes.
