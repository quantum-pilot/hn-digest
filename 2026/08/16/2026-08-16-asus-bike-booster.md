# Asus Bike Booster

- Score: 624 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49268580) | Link: https://www.asus.com/accessories/bike-booster/asus-oxiis/oxiis-intelligent-bike-booster/

### TL;DR

ASUS’s Oxiis E250G1 is a removable friction-drive unit marketed as converting conventional bicycles into e-bikes. It presses a 500W-peak motor against the tire, uses a wireless cadence sensor and incline-detecting adaptive boost, and includes a brake-sensing taillight. The sparse product page gives no price, range, battery, weight, or availability details. Commenters said friction drives are simple and useful for occasional assistance, but warned of tire wear, lower efficiency, poor wet or dirty traction, theft risk, and incompatibility with common fenders or rear racks.
