# Chicken products recalled in five states due to “false marks of inspection”

- Score: 194 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49483182) | Link: https://www.thehealthy.com/news/chicken-recall-fsis-august-2026/

### TL;DR

Shanghai Ravioli Corporation recalled 24,900 pounds of frozen, not-ready-to-eat Buffalo chicken rangoons and mozzarella sticks distributed to foodservice businesses in five New England states. The article says USDA inspectors found false inspection marks labeled “EST. 18004” and classified the recall as Class I. No illness or contaminant is identified in the supplied report; the stated risk is that uninspected food could contain undeclared allergens, bacteria, or other hazards. Businesses are advised to discard the products or return them.
