# Luanti removed from Google Play due to baseless AI copyright notice

- Score: 484 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49475079) | Link: https://blog.luanti.org/2026/08/27/luanti-dmca-tracer-ai/

### TL;DR

Luanti says Google Play removed its open-source voxel-game platform after Tracer.AI filed a vague DMCA notice for Microsoft alleging use of Minecraft assets. Luanti says its app ships no games or proprietary Minecraft content, submitted a counter-notice, and experienced a similar 46-day removal in 2023. It asks Microsoft and Tracer to require human verification, and Google to improve notice review and restoration. Commenters focused on weak accountability, platform-specific pseudo-DMCA processes, penalties for false claims, and risks to small creators.

### Comment pulse

- Automated enforcement can remove lawful projects without evidence → Luanti says the notice identifies no specific copied asset.
- Google controls restoration through its platform process → commenters argued counter-notices should end disabling unless litigation follows.
- Bonds could deter high-volume false notices → counterpoint: meaningful amounts might also prevent small creators defending genuine rights.
