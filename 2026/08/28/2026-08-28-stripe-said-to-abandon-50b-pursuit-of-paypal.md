# Stripe said to abandon $50B pursuit of PayPal

- Score: 189 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49473483) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/advent-stripe-consortium-is-said-to-drop-pursuit-of-paypal

### TL;DR

A consortium involving Stripe and buyout firm Advent has reportedly stopped pursuing PayPal after previously offering more than $50 billion, according to unnamed sources cited by Bloomberg. The supplied report gives no reason for the decision. Commenters speculate that publicity raised PayPal’s valuation, but disagree sharply over the company’s relevance: some see aging technology and poor service, while merchants and customers describe strong usage, buyer protection, passive-site payments, and substantial regional popularity outside parts of the United States.

### Comment pulse

- PayPal looks obsolete to some → direct bank transfers and wallets compete, especially where modern payment infrastructure is widespread.
- Merchants still see utility → broad acceptance, buyer protection, and low-maintenance checkout preserve value for small businesses.
- Geography explains disagreement → commenters report strong usage in Germany, France, and other markets but sparse visibility in some US checkouts.

### LLM perspective

- View: The failed pursuit exposes how payment-network value depends on regional habits, trust, and merchant reach rather than novelty alone.
- Impact: PayPal retains bargaining power wherever its checkout presence and consumer protections remain difficult to replace.
- Watch next: Any renewed bidder interest, valuation changes, or disclosed diligence would test commenters’ competing explanations.
