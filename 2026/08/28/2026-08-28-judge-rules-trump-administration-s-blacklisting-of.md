# Judge rules Trump administration’s blacklisting of Anthropic was illegal

- Score: 570 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49473522) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/27/technology/anthropic-government-blacklisting-ruling.html

### TL;DR

A federal judge ruled that the Trump administration unlawfully designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk and barred federal contractors from using it. The court found the action retaliated against protected speech after Anthropic opposed mass surveillance and autonomous lethal-weapons uses, while the government retreated from claims that Anthropic could sabotage deployed models. A separate appeal remains pending, and the administration could appeal this ruling. Commenters emphasized that weak evidence alone was not decisive; public retaliation made the First Amendment violation unusually clear.
