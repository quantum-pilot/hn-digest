# Get your Windows license refund

- Score: 679 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49478340) | Link: https://en.refund4freedom.org/

### TL;DR

Refund4Freedom argues that buyers should be able to decline preinstalled Windows, see its price separately, and obtain a simple refund without returning the computer. Its Italy-focused guide recommends documenting the license terms before accepting them, contacting the manufacturer in writing, filing a formal refund request after denial, and reporting unresolved cases to the Italian competition authority. Vendor practices differ: the campaign describes procedures for Lenovo, Asus, and Acer, but not HP or Dell, and lists individual refunds obtained through persistence or legal action.

### Comment pulse

- Some users reported substantial refunds; others noted the campaign has persisted since Windows Refund Day in 1999.
- Critics said buyers should choose license-free vendors — counterpoint: comparable laptops without Windows remain limited and sometimes cost more.
- Discussion broadened to smartphones, where hardware and operating-system choice is perceived as even more tightly coupled.
