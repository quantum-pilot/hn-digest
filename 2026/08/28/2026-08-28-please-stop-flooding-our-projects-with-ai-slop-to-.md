# Please stop flooding our projects with AI slop to furnish your CV

- Score: 211 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49474143) | Link: https://neilalexander.dev/2026/06/30/flooding-contributions

### TL;DR

An open-source maintainer argues that AI has made it cheap to manufacture GitHub activity through trivial pull requests and formulaic vulnerability reports, shifting review costs onto volunteers while furnishing contributors’ résumés. Correct changes can still be unwelcome when they add little value or establish incentives for more noise. Commenters debate automated filtering, differentiated contribution signals, and whether rejecting harmless fixes unfairly polices motives. The broader complaint is that hiring metrics have turned contribution into a game that erodes trust.

### Comment pulse

- Automate triage → bots could reject templated low-value submissions, though contributors warn conversational review bots create a hostile experience.
- Treat attention as scarce → even correct patches impose review and maintenance costs that vanity metrics conceal.
- Fix hiring incentives → visible contribution counts invite gaming, while personal connections become more valuable and less equitable.

### LLM perspective

- View: Provenance matters less than whether a contributor understands, justifies, and remains accountable for the change.
