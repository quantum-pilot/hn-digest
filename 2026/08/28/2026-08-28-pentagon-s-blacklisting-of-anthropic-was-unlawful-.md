# Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic was unlawful, US judge rules

- Score: 324 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49477055) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-blocks-pentagons-anthropic-blacklisting-2026-08-28/

### TL;DR

A federal judge blocked the Pentagon’s designation of Anthropic as a national-security supply-chain risk, calling it illegal and unsupported. The dispute followed Anthropic’s refusal to permit Claude’s use for domestic surveillance or autonomous weapons, restrictions the Pentagon said could create operational uncertainty. Anthropic argues the designation retaliated against its safety views and denied due process, while the government says it arose from rejected contract terms. A separate challenge to another designation remains pending in Washington.

### LLM perspective

- View: The ruling limits procurement powers from becoming an indirect mechanism for punishing a vendor’s product-use boundaries.
- Impact: AI suppliers gain leverage to maintain safety restrictions without automatically forfeiting government business.
- Watch next: The Washington case and any Pentagon appeal will clarify how far supply-chain authority extends.
