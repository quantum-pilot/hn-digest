# Inception-style curved map for turn-by-turn directions

- Score: 488 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49477564) | Link: https://www.orbify.eu/demo/

### TL;DR

Orbify's interactive demo presents a patent-pending, curved 3D navigation view, rendered with PlayCanvas and a preloaded scene, but the supplied page gives little technical explanation. Commenters found the proof of concept visually compelling and potentially useful for route context. Their main usability concern is that sharp or consecutive turns can hide the road ahead, reducing planning time and possibly increasing distraction. Others proposed predictive camera orientation, elevation, simplified detail, or a minimap, while citing older curved-world visuals as possible prior art.

### Comment pulse

- Curved perspective makes nearby streets visually memorable → users saw promise for turn-by-turn context and landmarks.
- Sharp turns push future route segments off-screen → predictive camera rotation or elevation could preserve look-ahead time.
- Earlier posters, games, and shaders used similar effects → commenters questioned how broad any patent claim could be.
