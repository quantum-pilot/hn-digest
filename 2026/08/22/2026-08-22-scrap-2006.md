# Scrap (2006)

- Score: 364 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49402189) | Link: https://twitter.com/moxie/status/2091218652133732491

### TL;DR

An old circa-2006 journal entry follows Moxie Marlinspike renovating an unheated Pittsburgh wreck with salvaged materials. After learning that discarded cast-iron tubs vanished overnight into the city’s scrap economy, he lets scrappers Ron and Wade remove a broken basement furnace. The 17-step extraction becomes comic and dangerous: a stuck pipe blocks the only exit, claustrophobic Wade panics, climbs over the furnace, and Ron frees it with an axe—all for steel worth four cents per pound. Discussion connected the tale to present-day Pittsburgh, renovation safety, poverty, and nostalgia for personal-blog discovery.
