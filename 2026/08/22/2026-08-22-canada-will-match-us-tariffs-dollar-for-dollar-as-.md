# Canada will match US tariffs 'dollar for dollar' as trade talks break down

- Score: 604 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49397074) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgvyy4x2mvo

### TL;DR

US tariffs of 50% took effect on roughly 5% of Canadian exports after eleventh-hour trade talks collapsed, adding to existing levies on steel, aluminum, autos, and lumber. Prime Minister Mark Carney said changed US terms were unacceptable and promised dollar-for-dollar retaliation; the US blamed Canadian walk-backs. Canada’s exposure is substantial: about 70% of its exports go south, while cited forecasts put losses at 90,000 jobs and 0.3–0.6% of GDP. Commenters split over retaliation, negotiation, and longer-term diversification.

### Comment pulse
