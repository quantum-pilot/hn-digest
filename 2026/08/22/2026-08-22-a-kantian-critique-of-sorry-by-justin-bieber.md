# A Kantian Critique of "Sorry" by Justin Bieber

- Score: 211 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49399524) | Link: https://decodingvibes.com/blog/a-kantian-critique-of-sorry-by-justin-bieber/

### TL;DR

The essay playfully applies Kantian ethics to the narrator of Justin Bieber’s “Sorry.” Because the apology asks whether it can still win forgiveness, redemption, or renewed intimacy, the author classifies it as a self-interested hypothetical imperative rather than duty owed regardless of outcome. Conditional blame-taking and shared-guilt language further weaken it. The piece limits judgment to the song’s persona and admits a knowingly insincere reading; commenters similarly warned against conflating singer, songwriter, and narrator while debating how sincere apologies handle mutual grievances.

### Comment pulse

- A song’s persona is underdetermined → listeners cannot confidently infer Bieber’s motives, especially when performers may not write their material.
