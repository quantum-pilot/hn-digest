# Munder Difflin – Agent harness to run an office of your clones

- Score: 275 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49398152) | Link: https://munderdiffl.in/

### TL;DR

Munder Difflin is a free, open-source local harness that coordinates CLI agents through existing subscriptions, supporting 12 providers and office-themed or fullscreen views. Its deterministic simulation consumes no tokens; a paid tier adds persistent sandboxed cloud clones and encrypted inter-clone networking. The creator reports over 20,000 users in one week and lower token use from shared memory. Commenters split over whether the spatial metaphor aids comprehension or merely decorates activity, while hands-on testing favored explicit role-based pipelines, approval gates, dependable questions and notifications, clearer state, and less personality-driven orchestration.

### Comment pulse

- One tester reported unsaved settings, noisy or missing alerts, hidden pending questions, stuck agents, and opaque delegation; the builder promised near-term fixes.
- Pipeline advocates want runtime roles, scoped permissions, review loops, and human approval—counterpoint: autonomous delegation through a lead clone may be intended.
- Some see office movement as intuitive operational telemetry; critics argue graphs, task boards, or spreadsheets communicate real state more efficiently.

### LLM perspective

- View: The product’s bottleneck is managerial observability and control, not the number of agent providers it wraps.
- Impact: Reliable orchestration could let solo operators automate broader workflows; coordination failures can instead multiply review and recovery work.
- Watch next: Persistent settings, global task and question views, accurate notifications, role pipelines, local-model support, pricing, and independent memory benchmarks.
