### TL;DR

Starting with ElevenLabs, TwelveLabs, and ThirteenLabs, the author searched every number from 0 through 99 and built an annotated directory of companies using number-plus-Labs names, marking AI-related entries. The result is less an explanation than a playful census: clustering among higher numbers raises questions about independent naming, trend-following, and domain speculation. A deliberately retro SeventyOneLab site breaks the otherwise uniform startup aesthetic. Commenters supplied more examples, number-specific origin stories, a 23Labs collaboration joke, and competing claims about who popularized the convention.
