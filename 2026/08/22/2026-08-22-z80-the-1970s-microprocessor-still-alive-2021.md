# Z80 – The 1970s Microprocessor Still Alive (2021)

- Score: 134 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49398158) | Link: https://www.computer.org/csdl/magazine/mi/2021/06/09623402/1yJTvlRLmhi

### TL;DR

Federico Faggin, Ralph Ungermann, and Masatoshi Shima left Intel to create Zilog, combining 8080 compatibility with added capabilities in the 5-volt Z80. An 11-person team delivered a working prototype on March 9, 1976 for under $400,000; its 8-bit data bus and 16-bit addressing supported 64K bytes and all 78 8080 instructions. It powered home computers and CP/M systems before 16-bit PCs displaced it, yet enhanced cores persisted in calculators, ASICs, consumer devices, and embedded markets. Discussion mixed technical debate with nostalgia for learning and contemporary hobby use.
