# Belgian car salesman becomes prince after DNA test proves royal parentage

- Score: 104 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49401621) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/22/europe/prince-belgium-secret-son-scli-intl

### TL;DR

Clément Vandenkerckhove, a 26-year-old Belgian car salesman, was legally recognized as Prince Laurent’s son after a DNA test and a civil-registry process completed about six months earlier. Recognition gives him equal inheritance rights to Laurent’s private estate alongside three half-siblings, but no royal allowance, official duties, or claim to Belgium’s throne. He may retain the surname honoring the mother who raised him. Commenters debated whether the outcome reflects ordinary parental responsibility or unearned hereditary privilege, amid mostly royal-comedy jokes.
