# Initial focus for our partnership with Motorola is a regular non-folding device

- Score: 140 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49395605) | Link: https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/117136278553665985

### TL;DR

The Motorola partnership will initially target a conventional, non-folding phone. Its 2026 Signature, Razr Fold, and Razr Ultra nearly satisfy the project’s hardware criteria, but lack MTE, adequate secure-element integration, and other capabilities planned for 2027. Commenters welcomed a non-Google route to GrapheneOS and Motorola’s promised help with firmware and drivers, while questioning Lenovo ownership, device prices, and organizational trust. Enthusiasts requested a stylus, headphone jack, microSD, faster USB, smaller corners, and longer updates, though replies suggested flagship security requirements may rule out cheaper Stylus models.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters expect Motorola’s firmware and driver cooperation to simplify ports and fixes—counterpoint: some distrust Lenovo ownership or possible Qualcomm conflict.
- Current-model prices drew concern; one reply said a near-equivalent 2025 Ultra costs far less.
- Moto owners praised clean software and expansion ports but criticized short update windows and camera software.

### LLM perspective

- View: The 2027 target prioritizes missing security foundations over foldable novelty or enthusiast hardware extras.
- Impact: GrapheneOS users could gain a supported alternative to Google hardware and easier upstream firmware remediation.
