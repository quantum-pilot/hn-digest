# NetBSD and my life (2005)

- Score: 114 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49402781) | Link: https://mail-index.netbsd.org/netbsd-advocacy/2005/09/10/0000.html

### TL;DR

In a 2005 advocacy email, UK administrator Gary Rolland recounts replacing unstable Windows servers with NetBSD 2.0.2 after repeated emergency callouts disrupted family life. A two-server trial survived failures elsewhere, winning approval for a gradual migration; eventually 29 servers supported more than 4,800 users, moved 870GB daily, and ran MySQL, Apache, Postfix, and Samba. Greater stability freed time to train colleagues, enabled weekend administration by SSH, and ended routine weekend callouts. Comments turned the story into broader reflection on operating systems as career catalysts, historical platform choices, and present-day usability.

### Comment pulse

- Readers credited Gentoo and BSD experimentation with forming systems mental models, launching careers, and reviving obsolete hardware.
- NetBSD admirers emphasized portability and documentation—counterpoint: FreeBSD historically offered broader drivers, while modern BSD package availability can constrain desktops.
- Several wanted to try NetBSD on low-risk or embedded systems; the original email never explained its selection.
