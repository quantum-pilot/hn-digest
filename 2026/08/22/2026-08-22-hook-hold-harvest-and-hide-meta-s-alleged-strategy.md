# Hook, hold, harvest and hide: Meta's alleged strategy laid out in first week

- Score: 209 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49398904) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/22/meta-trial-children-privacy

### TL;DR

In the first week of a six-to-eight-week trial, California and 28 other states alleged Meta designed addictive products, collected under-13s’ data without parental permission, and obscured child harms. Their hook-hold-harvest-hide phrase is a prosecutor’s courtroom mnemonic, not revealed Meta terminology. Meta denies liability, citing safety tools and more than one million disabled underage accounts. A former safety engineer testified about teen harm surveys and an unanswered 2021 email to Mark Zuckerberg; Meta disputes his credibility. An advisory jury will recommend outcomes to the judge.
