# Moderna reports first positive Phase 3 for mRNA neoantigen therapy in melanoma

- Score: 553 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49361395) | Link: https://twitter.com/NoubarAfeyan/status/2090050162441752787

### TL;DR

Moderna and Merck announced positive topline Phase 3 results for investigational intismeran autogene, an individualized mRNA neoantigen therapy combined with KEYTRUDA after complete resection of stage IIB–IV melanoma. INTerpath-001 reportedly met its primary recurrence-free-survival endpoint and a key secondary distant-metastasis-free-survival endpoint. The announcement calls this the first positive Phase 3 result for both individualized neoantigen and mRNA-based cancer therapy. However, it supplies no effect size, confidence interval, event counts, survival curves, or safety details, leaving clinical magnitude unassessable.
