# Meta's blockbuster trial draws parallels to big tobacco

- Score: 311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49355825) | Link: https://www.economist.com/business/2026/08/18/metas-blockbuster-trial-draws-parallels-to-big-tobacco

### TL;DR

Four states leading a 29-state coalition opened a federal consumer-protection trial alleging Meta designed Facebook and Instagram to hook children, misled the public about risks, and violated children’s privacy. Despite tobacco rhetoric, the Oakland case centers deception rather than proving addiction. Meta denies harm, says its products benefit youngsters, and calls the analogy inappropriate. The plaintiffs’ penalty estimate reportedly fell from $1.4tn to nearly $200bn. Investors see a high proof burden and reportedly fear engagement-feature remedies more than payouts; Meta retains appeal options.
