# Air Theremin – A browser theremin you play by waving at your webcam

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49359425) | Link: https://theremin.bizibah.com/

### TL;DR

This browser instrument produces a continuous tone from webcam hand gestures, phone motion sensors, or a mouse fallback. In hand mode, spreading both hands controls volume, raising them changes pitch, leaning back softens the tone, and joining palms silences it. Phone users tilt left-right for volume and forward-back for pitch, with startup grip calibration and out-of-frame muting. Controls offer sine, triangle, warm, and reed waves plus reverb, note snapping, vibrato, tremolo, echo, recording, and pause. It was created by Pavel Gurov and based on theremin.site.
