# Children's stunted lungs show recovery in ultra low emission zone

- Score: 395 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49355105) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c1l1r1zne1ro

### TL;DR

A five-year study followed more than 3,400 six-to-nine-year-olds in London’s Ulez and comparable children in Luton, testing lungs before the 2019 launch and four years afterward. London children’s initially lower capacity nearly caught up; clinically impaired capacity fell from 14% to 9%, versus 9% to 7% in Luton, while nitrogen-dioxide exposure declined faster in London. Researchers link recovery to cleaner air. The comparison helps address broad trends and Covid, but changed travel and unmeasured city differences prevent definitive attribution; both cities remain above WHO guidelines.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The comparison strengthens the association, but this was not a randomized policy experiment.
- Impact: Results suggest children’s impaired lung growth may recover relatively quickly as urban air improves.
- Watch next: Replication across cities, individual exposure data, travel changes, and lifelong health outcomes.
