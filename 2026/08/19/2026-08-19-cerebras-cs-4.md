# Cerebras CS-4

- Score: 457 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49354949) | Link: https://www.cerebras.ai/cs4

### TL;DR

Cerebras announced a rack-scale system with three WSE-3 Turbo wafer engines. It claims up to 30× faster inference than production GPUs, 10× CS-3 throughput per watt, and over 1,000 tokens per second for models exceeding 10 trillion parameters through two-microsecond wafer links. The modular Nexus design integrates power, liquid cooling, I/O, and controls while cutting components by 50%. Each wafer is specified at four trillion transistors, 900,000 cores, 250 PFLOPS, and 43.2 PB/s memory bandwidth. Shipments begin this quarter; comparative test details are absent.
