# Sticky wage norms and the real wage cost of unexpected inflation

- Score: 384 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49355142) | Link: https://bfi.uchicago.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/BFI_WP_2026-108-1.pdf

### TL;DR

A working paper using ADP payroll records for roughly 16 million US workers monthly finds most firms gave common modal annual raises that barely adjusted to unexpected 2021–2024 inflation. Among continuous job-stayers, 43% finished with lower real wages, losing 9% on average; including job-changers lowers the incidence to 37%. Switchers’ gains tracked inflation, but moves were infrequent. An accounting exercise says indexing modal raises would have closed 40% of the shortfall versus the 2017–2019 trend. Belgium’s indexed wages provide supporting, not randomized, sentiment evidence.

### LLM perspective

- View: Administrative payroll data reveals a credible mechanism; counterfactual and cross-country claims remain nonexperimental.
- Impact: Sticky raises make surprise inflation persistent for stayers and reward access to mobility.
