# OpenRouter is joining Stripe

- Score: 819 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49364559) | Link: https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/openrouter-is-joining-stripe/

### TL;DR

OpenRouter says it will join Stripe in a transaction expected to close within weeks, subject to customary conditions. The 90-person company says its name, product, roadmap, integrations, and model-neutral routing will remain unchanged. It reports processing more than 10 trillion tokens daily across 400-plus models for over 10 million users. OpenRouter argues Stripe’s customer network, global infrastructure, growth expertise, and fraud defenses will accelerate its marketplace, observability, cost-management, and routing services without compromising provider neutrality.
