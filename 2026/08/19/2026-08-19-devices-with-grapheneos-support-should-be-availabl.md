# Devices with GrapheneOS support should be available in 2027

- Score: 602 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49360242) | Link: https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/117078064184215730

### TL;DR

GrapheneOS says its first newly supported devices should arrive in 2027 and will be flagships, with higher-end hardware—and higher prices—than Pixels. Lower-cost models will take longer because their update commitments and hardware security fall short, largely due to Qualcomm’s product segmentation; Motorola would need to fund longer support below flagship level. A commenter points to Motorola’s 2027 Signature and Razr fold/flip as candidates, while the supplied statement itself names no models.

### Comment pulse

- Users wanted lower-cost, compact, foldable, and SD-card options; others noted current Motorola models fail GrapheneOS hardware requirements.
- Linux-phone advocates met a consistent counterpoint: Android offers crucial app compatibility, sandboxing, permissions, and power management.
- Fairphone interest remained, but commenters cited insufficient update commitments and hardware-backed security.
