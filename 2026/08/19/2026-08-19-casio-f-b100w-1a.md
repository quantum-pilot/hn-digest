# Casio F-B100W-1A

- Score: 358 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49362887) | Link: https://www.casio.com/uk/watches/casio/product.F-B100W-1A/

### TL;DR

Casio’s £55 retro-styled digital watch adds Bluetooth phone pairing and accelerometer-based step tracking to an 8.8 mm, 26 g resin body. It promises roughly two years on a CR2016 battery, automatic time correction, dual time, about 300 app-configurable cities, life logs, phone finding, five alarms, a timer, and a 200-record stopwatch. Daily goals range from 1,000 to 50,000 steps, with reminders and a seven-hour graph. It uses bio-based resin, offers unspecified water resistance, and had no customer reviews listed.
