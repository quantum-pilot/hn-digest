# OpenLogi

- Score: 1547 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49355606) | Link: https://openlogi.org/en

### TL;DR

OpenLogi is a Rust, local-first replacement for Logitech Options+ on macOS and Linux, with an early untested Windows preview. It communicates directly over HID++ through Bolt, Unifying, Bluetooth, or USB, providing 41 button actions, DPI presets, SmartShift, per-app profiles, battery status, and plain TOML configuration. The dual-licensed app requires no account or telemetry; device renders are fetched, while update checks are opt-in. Commenters welcomed escaping vendor cloud dependencies and shared related reverse-engineering projects, but debated AI-generated presentation, code trust, maintenance longevity, and existing Linux alternative Solaar.
