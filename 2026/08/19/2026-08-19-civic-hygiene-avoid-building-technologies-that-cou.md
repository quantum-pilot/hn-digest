# Civic Hygiene – avoid building technologies that could be used by a police state (2013)

- Score: 429 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49363433) | Link: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2013/11/civic-hygiene/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that technologists should evaluate databases and surveillance against a hostile future government, not merely today’s trusted one. A hypothetical sexuality registry shows how data collected for legitimate planning could enable persecution after an election; contemporary examples include web filtering, ISP click records, and health-data sharing. “Civic hygiene” means avoiding systems easily repurposed into police-state infrastructure while recognizing that some records serve necessary civil functions. The central test is whether the next administration could readily weaponize what is built now.
