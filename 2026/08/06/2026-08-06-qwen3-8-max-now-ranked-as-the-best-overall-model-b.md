# Qwen3.8 Max now ranked as the best overall model by agentic index

- Score: 478 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49200652) | Link: https://artificialanalysis.ai/?intelligence=agentic-index

### TL;DR

Qwen3.8 Max briefly topped Artificial Analysis’s Agentic Index at 55.4 versus Claude Opus 5’s 55.3, then fell to second—58.4 versus 59.2—when the site updated its grader and τ³-Banking benchmark during the discussion. The agentic score averages GDPval-AA v2 and τ³-Banking, whereas the broader Intelligence Index spans nine evaluations and still placed several models ahead. HN viewed Qwen’s jump as evidence that Chinese labs have reached the frontier and anticipated smaller local versions, but the live ranking change reinforced doubts about unstable methodology, benchmark relevance, and omitted communication quality.
