# Software development with AI is starting to feel like cooking steak

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49198069) | Link: https://blog.sydorets.com/en/posts/almost-no-skill-required-to-cook-a-steak/

### TL;DR

The essay compares AI-assisted programming to cooking steak: producing something edible is easy, but consistent quality requires understanding inputs, technique, constraints, and intent. Models execute recipes rapidly, yet cannot infer the builder’s mental picture or decide which tradeoffs matter; requirements, examples, tests, and feedback remain human work. Premium tools may still serve mediocre output if organizations optimize for acceptability. HN accepted the judgment thesis but attacked the analogy—excellent steak can be mechanically repeatable—and suggested a film director coordinating specialists as a better model for iterative oversight.

### Comment pulse
