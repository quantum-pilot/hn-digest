# Nashville uses eminent domain to block data center near zoo

- Score: 318 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49191624) | Link: https://www.costar.com/article/970809918/nashville-council-approves-eminent-domain-action-to-halt-data-center-project

### TL;DR

Nashville’s Metro Council voted 27–5 to authorize acquiring a 23-acre site beside the zoo through eminent domain, blocking DC Blox’s planned $700-million, 69,220-square-foot data center. A zoo petition drew 500,000-plus signatures and cited noise, light, environmental, and power impacts; DC Blox says the office property previously hosted data-center functions without community burden. The city also imposed zoning restrictions and a permit moratorium through December 1. HN debated the project’s intensity and animal risk, but focused on whether eminent domain dangerously substitutes for zoning and negotiated mitigation.

### Comment pulse

- Even sympathetic locals called seizure a dangerous precedent; others valued decisive action after permitting and litigation proved too slow.
