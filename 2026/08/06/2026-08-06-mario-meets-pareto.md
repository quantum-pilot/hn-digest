# Mario Meets Pareto

- Score: 988 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49195231) | Link: https://www.mayerowitz.io/blog/mario-meets-pareto

### TL;DR

A Mario Kart 8 analysis teaches Pareto efficiency: a build is dominated when another improves one statistic without worsening another. Among 585 unique speed-and-acceleration combinations, the Pareto frontier leaves only 14 efficient choices; adding mini turbo introduces a third dimension and expands the frontier. The method cannot choose a winner without personal weights, but it removes inferior options when the player’s utility function is unknown. HN applied the idea to software security, usability, cost, and game-item optimization, emphasizing that claimed tradeoffs are suspect until a system lies on the frontier.
