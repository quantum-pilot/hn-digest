# Herdr is joining Y Combinator. The runtime stays open

- Score: 185 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201003) | Link: https://herdr.dev/blog/herdr-is-joining-y-combinator/

### TL;DR

Herdr’s solo founder is joining Y Combinator’s F26 batch after the terminal agent runtime reached 25,000 stars, 340,000 downloads, and 500-plus plugins. Its persistent hierarchy—agent panes within tabs and projects—manages long-running coding agents locally or over SSH, while the bundled TUI remains first-class. The runtime moved from AGPL to Apache 2.0 and will stay free. The company plans connected multi-machine clients atop the open core while preserving a lean, extensible runtime. HN welcomed the milestone but scrutinized licensing, commercialization, and a crowded agent-multiplexer market.

### Comment pulse

- Open source enables personal forks, Linux support, self-hosting, and trust — counterpoint: investors push differentiating cloud and enterprise features toward closed products.
- The AGPL-to-Apache switch divided readers between permissive adoption and concern that companies can capture improvements without contributing back.
- Users praised Herdr’s orthogonality: terminal output remains useful without deep integrations, while remote sessions mirror across WSL, macOS, and servers.
