# Federal Communications Commission scraps limit on broadcast TV ownership

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49200390) | Link: https://www.nbcnews.com/business/media/federal-communications-commission-scraps-limit-broadcast-tv-ownership-rcna587641

### TL;DR

By a 2–1 vote, the FCC repealed the 39% cap on the share of US households one broadcaster may reach, replacing it with case-by-case review. Chairman Brendan Carr says the 2004-era restriction prevents local stations from matching unregulated streaming and social platforms. Commissioner Anna Gomez and Free Press argue Congress codified the limit, leaving the agency no authority to erase it, and warn of layoffs and reduced viewpoint diversity. The change helps Nexstar’s $6.2 billion Tegna acquisition, whose combined stations would reach at least 60% of households; litigation is expected.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters broadly expected courts to reject an agency override of an explicit statute, especially after judicial deference to agencies narrowed.
- Some called broadcast television obsolete — counterpoint: antennas still deliver free news, sports, and PBS, while local stations receive cable carriage.
- Others preferred within-market ownership limits, arguing national reach matters less than local concentration and editorial control.
