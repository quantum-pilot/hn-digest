# Improving GPT‑5.6 Sol in ChatGPT, expanding GPT‑5.6 Luna access for free users

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49199357) | Link: https://openai.com/index/improving-gpt-5-6-sol-in-chatgpt/

### TL;DR

OpenAI updated ChatGPT’s paid default to GPT-5.6 Sol across quick and deeper responses, adding an effort slider and claiming tighter answers with better factual reliability. In an internal financial, medical, and legal evaluation, error-containing responses were 68% less common than with GPT-5.5 Instant; Luna’s reduction was 62%. Free and Go users get Luna by default, unlimited text chats next week, and a Think button, while tool limits remain. Work and Codex models are unchanged. HN welcomed reasoning access but questioned whether it was new and found the model-effort matrix confusing.
