# Crime Pays but Botany Doesn't

- Score: 664 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49192566) | Link: https://www.crimepaysbutbotanydoesnt.com/reading-list

### TL;DR

The guide makes self-taught botany approachable by prioritizing three foundations: scientific naming, evolutionary taxonomy, and plant systematics. Latin binomials avoid ambiguous common names, while family-level traits and synapomorphies let observers classify unfamiliar plants by relationship rather than memorization. It recommends Michael Simpson’s Plant Systematics, Raven’s Biology of Plants, and a broad reading list covering evolution, ecology, fungi, biogeography, and regional natural history. HN readers extended the lesson into field practice: identification apps, birding, restoration, native gardening, and repeated observation turn natural scenery into legible patterns.
