# Quake – 30th Anniversary Update

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201930) | Link: https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-US/news/quake-30th-anniversary-update

### TL;DR

For Quake’s 30th anniversary, id Software and MachineGames released Dawn of the Machine free to owners. Its 19-map campaign adds a soundtrack, deathmatch map, enemy and weapon variants, dimension-shifting puzzles, and a loop where runes open altered paths while health and ammunition upgrades persist. The update also adds prototype maps in an id Vault, achievements, cheats, technical fixes, and mod-localization changes. GOG and Epic versions lack the episode, while Switch hosts face level-transition disconnects. HN celebrated three decades of LANs and mods but contrasted single-player support with neglected arena multiplayer.
