# xAI, SpaceX, and the Race for AI Buildout

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201342) | Link: https://illegal.solutions/posts/xai_pollution

### TL;DR

The author argues xAI accelerated its Memphis Colossus build by operating unpermitted gas turbines, shifting pollution and health risks onto nearby, predominantly Black communities. An NAACP-backed Clean Air Act suit faces a federal dismissal request on national-security grounds. xAI says 69 Mississippi turbines will be removed by July 2027 and replaced by a gas plant, while backup turbines, substations, grid connections, and delayed wastewater work remain confusingly described. HN disputed the polemical framing but argued weak penalties reward unlawful acceleration; effective sanctions must remove operational value or impose personal liability.
