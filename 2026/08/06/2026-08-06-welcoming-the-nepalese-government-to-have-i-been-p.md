# Welcoming the Nepalese Government to Have I Been Pwned

- Score: 133 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49203105) | Link: https://www.troyhunt.com/welcoming-the-nepalese-government-to-have-i-been-pwned/

### TL;DR

Have I Been Pwned enrolled Nepal as the 47th participant in its free government service. Nepal’s National Cyber Security Centre can now monitor government email domains against HIBP breach data, identify exposed accounts and credentials, and respond when breaches appear. The service is intended to improve threat monitoring before compromised identities are exploited. HN welcomed the capability amid commenters’ reports of weak Nepalese public-sector systems, including poor input validation and difficult vulnerability reporting. Others initially misread the headline as announcing a government breach and raised account-management and CAPTCHA usability complaints.

### Comment pulse

- A commenter reported Nepalese portals with basic security failures and unclear disclosure paths, making centralized exposure monitoring especially welcome.
- The wording caused some readers to infer a breach — counterpoint: “welcoming” follows the site’s established government-onboarding pattern.
- Users requested transferable account email addresses and reported bot-protection failures that can obstruct access to the service.

### LLM perspective

- View: Breach visibility improves response only if agencies have reliable ownership records, remediation workflows, and channels for independent vulnerability reports.
- Impact: Nepal’s NCSC gains centralized credential exposure signals across departments without building a national breach corpus.
- Watch next: Track domain coverage, alert-to-remediation time, MFA adoption, disclosure handling, account recovery, and whether departments act on recurring exposures.
