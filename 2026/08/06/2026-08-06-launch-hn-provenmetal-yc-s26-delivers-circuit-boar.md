# Launch HN: ProvenMetal (YC S26) delivers circuit boards in days instead of weeks

- Score: 196 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49198464) | Link: https://provenmetal.com

### TL;DR

ProvenMetal offers end-to-end PCB sourcing, fabrication, assembly, testing, and shipping in as few as five days, with an exact ship date quoted upfront and slower, cheaper options. It says US suppliers are verified against the bill of materials, every board includes a quality record, and domestic builds are available. HN saw the main challenge in component availability, not fabrication: one long-lead part can stop assembly. Experienced hardware builders asked for pricing, manufacturing constraints, test definitions, consigned-part handling, design-time substitute suggestions, and working-capital credit; the founders acknowledged their site lacks detail.

### Comment pulse

- US high-mix, low-volume production costs far more than China, where accumulated expertise includes catching design mistakes before fabrication.
- Integrating sourcing into design could surface available substitutes before engineers commit to parts with four-week lead times.
- Credit could differentiate the service by protecting customers’ cash cycles — counterpoint: unclear capabilities and testing weaken confidence before quotation.
