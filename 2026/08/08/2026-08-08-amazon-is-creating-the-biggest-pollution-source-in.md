# Amazon Is Creating the Biggest Pollution Source in the Country

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49223845) | Link: https://newrepublic.com/post/214111/amazon-data-center-biggest-pollution-source-entire-country

### TL;DR
Amazon is developing GW Ranch in rural West Texas: an AI data center campus powered by its own 7.65 GW natural-gas plant, permitted to emit up to 33 million tons of CO₂ annually—more than any current U.S. coal plant. The facility will initially sit off the Texas grid, using brackish water, while Amazon touts separate “carbon‑free” projects and a 2040 net‑zero pledge. Critics note the enormous climate impact, meager local benefits, and a growing bipartisan backlash against data centers.

### Comment pulse
- Off‑grid gas is seen as a shortcut around U.S. grid bottlenecks and permitting, prioritizing AI speed over decarbonization—counterpoint: punitive carbon pricing could realign incentives quickly.  
- SpaceX’s gas‑powered Terafab and alleged use of unpermitted turbines illustrate lax enforcement; commenters argue AI workloads could be scheduled around daytime solar instead.  
- Locating plants in sparsely populated West Texas doesn’t solve global CO₂ loading; U.S. historical emissions heighten calls to stop new fossil infrastructure.
