# Amazon Is Creating the Biggest Pollution Source in the Country

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49223845) | Link: https://newrepublic.com/post/214111/amazon-data-center-biggest-pollution-source-entire-country

### TL;DR

According to permits and reporting cited by the article, Amazon acquired a Pecos County, Texas, site for an AI data-center campus powered initially off-grid by a proposed 7.65-gigawatt gas plant. A state permit allows 33 million tons of carbon-dioxide emissions, which Distilled says would make GW Ranch the country’s largest single pollution source. Amazon has filed permits for three data-center buildings and begun clearing land. It points to 10 gigawatts of carbon-free Texas projects and planned brackish-water use, but the proposal strains its 2040 net-zero pledge.

### Comment pulse

- Off-grid power avoids local grid load — counterpoint: it accelerates deployment by substituting fossil generation for delayed transmission and renewables.
- Remote siting limits nearby disruption, not climate damage; commenters reject empty-land arguments because emissions disperse globally.
- Carbon pricing could internalize the cost and push operators toward cleaner power once grid capacity becomes available.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Permitted emissions describe a proposed maximum, not measured pollution; that distinction does not reduce the scale.
- **Impact:** Pecos County gains construction and pollution exposure; Amazon’s climate pledge faces a credibility test.
- **Watch next:** Final permits, turbine buildout, actual generation, grid connection, water use, opposition, and emissions accounting.
