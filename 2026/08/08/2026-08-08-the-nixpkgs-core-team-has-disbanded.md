# The Nixpkgs core team has disbanded

- Score: 383 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49217993) | Link: https://discourse.nixos.org/t/the-nixpkgs-core-team-has-disbanded/79413

### TL;DR

After ten months, the Nixpkgs core team is dissolving because its governance workload proved incompatible with technical contribution and damaged members’ health. Recruiting produced only one active applicant. The team credits itself with adding 19 committers, expanding merge automation, improving GitHub coordination and security handling, and establishing an initial AI policy. It faults the Steering Committee’s weak delegation, micromanagement, unclear communication, and slow decisions; responsibilities now have no direct owner beyond that committee. HN cautioned that Nixpkgs survived two decades without this structure, while recurring resignations still suggest governance trouble.

### Comment pulse
