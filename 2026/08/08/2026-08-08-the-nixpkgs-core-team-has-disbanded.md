# The Nixpkgs core team has disbanded

- Score: 383 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49217993) | Link: https://discourse.nixos.org/t/the-nixpkgs-core-team-has-disbanded/79413

### TL;DR
The recently created Nixpkgs core team (effectively down to two people) has dissolved, citing burnout and persistent friction with the NixOS Steering Committee (SC). They describe an SC that neither delegates effectively nor makes timely top‑level decisions, leading to micromanagement, slow progress on moderation, grants, AI policy, and GitHub governance, plus a distrustful, combative community climate. HN discussion frames this as another symptom of long‑running governance problems rather than a technical crisis, though some fear it signals Nix’s broader decline.
