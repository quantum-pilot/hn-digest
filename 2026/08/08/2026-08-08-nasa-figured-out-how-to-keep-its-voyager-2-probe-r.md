# NASA figured out how to keep its Voyager 2 probe running for another year

- Score: 353 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49218179) | Link: https://www.space.com/space-exploration/voyager/nasa-figured-out-how-to-keep-its-48-year-old-voyager-2-probe-running-for-yet-another-year

### TL;DR

NASA has extended Voyager 2’s three remaining science instruments for another year by switching off non-science hardware and substituting lower-power ways to keep the spacecraft warm. Its radioisotope generator loses about four watts of output annually; without the change, another instrument would have closed later in 2026. Voyager 1 is due to receive the same adjustment. HN readers celebrated nearly five decades of remote maintenance, while noting the program relies on scarce institutional knowledge and part-time specialists and now returns focused interstellar data rather than its original ten-instrument planetary mission.
