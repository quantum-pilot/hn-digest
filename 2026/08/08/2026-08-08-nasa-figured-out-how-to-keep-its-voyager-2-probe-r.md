# NASA figured out how to keep its Voyager 2 probe running for another year

- Score: 353 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49218179) | Link: https://www.space.com/space-exploration/voyager/nasa-figured-out-how-to-keep-its-48-year-old-voyager-2-probe-running-for-yet-another-year

### TL;DR
NASA has squeezed another year of science out of 48-year-old Voyager 2 by implementing a “Big Bang” power reconfiguration: shutting down non-essential hardware and substituting lower-power components to keep its last three instruments alive despite steadily weakening plutonium RTG output. Voyager 1 will get the same treatment soon. Hacker News commenters marvel at the tiny, overstretched team sustaining these probes, the hand-crafted fixes sent across light-day distances, and the mission’s status as a pinnacle of long-term engineering.
