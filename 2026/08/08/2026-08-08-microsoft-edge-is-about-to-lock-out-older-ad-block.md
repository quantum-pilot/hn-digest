# Microsoft Edge is about to lock out older ad blockers, just like Chrome did

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49220392) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/tech/976880/microsoft-edge-extensions-ad-blockers-mv2-mv3

### TL;DR

Microsoft will gradually disable Manifest V2 extensions in Edge beginning with preview channels this month, reaching stable consumer users by the end of 2026 and managed enterprise devices in early 2027. The change removes full uBlock Origin and other MV2-only tools; Microsoft points users toward MV3 replacements such as uBlock Origin Lite and says only 58 meaningfully used store extensions remain on MV2. HN debate treated the transition as a Chromium-governance issue: downstream browsers inherit Google’s platform choices, though contributors dispute that maintaining the old request-blocking hook is prohibitively difficult.

### Comment pulse

- MV3’s predeclared filtering model reduces flexibility; some users find uBlock Origin Lite adequate, while others reject the trade.
- Chromium forks depend on upstream scale — counterpoint: contributors argue preserving the request-blocking hook is modest if vendors prioritize it.
- Firefox offers engine diversity, but distrust of Mozilla leaves users debating forks and newer independent engines.
