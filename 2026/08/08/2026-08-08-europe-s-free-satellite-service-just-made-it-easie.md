# Europe's free satellite service just made it easier to track wildfires

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49220313) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/08/europes-free-satellite-service-just-made-it-easier-to-track-wildfires/

- TL;DR  
  - The EU’s Copernicus Browser now ships a built‑in “wildfires” layer for Sentinel‑2 imagery, based on Pierre Markuse’s decade‑old visualization script. It fuses visible, near‑IR, and short‑wave IR bands to render active flames, burning vegetation, and burn scars in intuitive colors. Compared with NASA’s faster but coarser FIRMS data, Sentinel‑2 offers 10 m detail every few days, making it a powerful free tool for monitoring wildfires and even war damage amid increasingly severe fire seasons.
