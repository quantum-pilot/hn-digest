# Europe's free satellite service just made it easier to track wildfires

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49220313) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/08/europes-free-satellite-service-just-made-it-easier-to-track-wildfires/

### TL;DR

The free Copernicus Browser added a default wildfire layer for Sentinel-2 imagery on August 4, integrating a JavaScript visualization Pierre Markuse created nearly a decade ago. It combines visible red, near-infrared, and short-wave infrared bands so active fires appear white or yellow, vegetation red, and burned areas dark. Sentinel-2 offers detail down to 10 meters but revisits locations every few days; NASA FIRMS updates within hours at roughly 250-meter resolution. HN welcomed easier access while noting confusing controls, industrial false positives, and the imagery’s parallel use in war-zone OSINT.
