### TL;DR
Movile Cave in southeastern Romania, accidentally breached in 1986, is a rare, long-isolated ecosystem running entirely without sunlight. Sealed beneath clay and limestone, it contains toxic, low‑oxygen air and warm, chemically rich pools where chemosynthetic microbes convert sulfides, methane, and ammonia into biomass. Around 50 invertebrate species, most blind, colorless, and endemic, form a full food web based on these microbial mats.
