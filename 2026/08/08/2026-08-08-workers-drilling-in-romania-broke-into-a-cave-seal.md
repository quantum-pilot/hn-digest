# Workers Drilling in Romania Broke into a Cave Sealed for 5.5M Years

- Score: 130 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49171223) | Link: https://travelandtannins.com/workers-drilling-in-romania-broke-into-a-cave-sealed-for-5-5-million-years-and-found-an-entire-living-world-that-has-never-seen-the-sun/

### TL;DR

Movile Cave, discovered during Romanian drilling in 1986, hosts a chemosynthetic ecosystem 18 meters underground. Microbes use sulfide, methane, and ammonium rather than sunlight, supporting about 50 invertebrate species, many endemic and adapted to darkness. The article says geology, chemistry, and absent Chernobyl fallout support isolation for roughly 5.5 million years. HN readers challenged that presentation: fallout only proves no recent surface water entered, and species arrived at different times, so ancient isolation depends on broader geological and genetic evidence. Much discussion also criticized the article’s repetitive, possibly AI-assisted prose.
