# Ardour 9.0

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46903001) | Link: https://ardour.org/whatsnew.html

### TL;DR

Ardour 9.0 is a major release for the open-source digital audio workstation, adding dedicated MIDI pianorolls, direct MIDI and audio cue editing, recording into cue slots, offline region-specific effects, a multi-source perceptual analyzer, note brushing, keyboard automation editing, and reusable mixer strips. It also reorganizes the interface, accelerates dense macOS views, expands plugin, controller, Lua, import, and tempo support, and fixes numerous crashes. Commenters praised its affordability and recording workflow, welcomed stronger composition tools, cautioned that full DAWs remain difficult to learn, and reported early Fedora crashes.

### Comment pulse

- Community sustains development → longtime donors praised affordable binaries and open source; early adopters supplied crash traces and feature questions.
