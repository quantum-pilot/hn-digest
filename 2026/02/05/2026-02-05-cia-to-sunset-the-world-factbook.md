# CIA to Sunset the World Factbook

- Score: 380 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46899100) | Link: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-02-05/cia-closes-world-factbook-online-resource/106307724

### TL;DR

The CIA will stop publishing The World Factbook, a free, searchable, public-domain country reference used by millions, journalists, academics, and schools. Its lineage began with the classified World War II JANIS program, moved to the CIA in 1947, became an annual Factbook in 1971, opened publicly in 1975, and went online in the 1990s. The CIA gave no reason; workforce buyouts and reported cuts provide context, not a confirmed cause. Commenters debated Wikipedia as a replacement, warning that mirrors preserve history but cannot maintain current data.
