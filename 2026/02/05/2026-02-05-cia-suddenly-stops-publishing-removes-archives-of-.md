# CIA suddenly stops publishing, removes archives of The World Factbook

- Score: 422 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46899808) | Link: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/5/the-world-factbook/

### TL;DR

Simon Willison reports that the CIA ended The World Factbook without explaining why, removed the live site and its historical versions, and redirected every page to a farewell notice. The public-domain reference had run since 1971 and online since 1997. Annual downloadable archives exist through 2020 on the Internet Archive; Willison extracted the 384 MB 2020 package and republished it for browsing. Commenters mourned a basic country reference used in education, travel, and immigration litigation, while political and soft-power explanations remained speculation unsupported by the supplied announcement.
