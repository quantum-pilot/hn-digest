# The time I didn't meet Jeffrey Epstein

- Score: 383 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46903929) | Link: https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=9534

### TL;DR

Scott Aaronson says his name appears in 26 released Epstein documents despite never meeting or contacting Jeffrey Epstein. In 2010, intermediary Charles Harper proposed an Epstein-funded Cryptography in Nature project. Aaronson met Harper once, then forwarded the funding email to family. His brother surfaced Epstein’s conviction, and his mother warned him away. Later meeting attempts and a proposed conference went nowhere, and he forgot the episode. Commenters praised trusted outside counsel but debated hindsight, moral vetting, elite networks, and whether joking comparisons minimized culpability.
