# European Commission Trials Matrix to Replace Teams

- Score: 351 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46901452) | Link: https://www.euractiv.com/news/commission-trials-european-open-source-communications-software/

### TL;DR

The European Commission is preparing a communications trial based on Matrix, an open, community-developed protocol, as a sovereign complement and backup—not a replacement—for Microsoft Teams. Signal currently fills that role but was considered insufficiently flexible for the Commission’s scale. Matrix could also connect EU bodies securely; a working Parliament link already exists. Commenters supported reducing US-software dependency but split sharply over Matrix and Element’s speed, stability, standardization, and onboarding. The Matrix project lead cited open-governance delays, past product-focus mistakes, and an upstream-funding model now centered on AGPL and enterprise features.

### Comment pulse

- Resilience comes before replacement → the Commission wants an independent fallback and inter-institution bridge while retaining Teams.

- Open standards complicate product polish → protocol governance and unfunded commercial reuse slowed Element; enterprise revenue now supports focused app and server development.
