# Discord Rival Gets Overwhelmed by Exodus of Players Fleeing Age-Verification

- Score: 182 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47050376) | Link: https://kotaku.com/discord-alternative-teamspeak-age-verification-check-rivals-2000669693

### TL;DR

TeamSpeak says a surge of users fleeing Discord’s planned worldwide age-verification rollout exhausted hosting capacity in several regions, especially the United States, and prompted expansion into new locations. The migration follows privacy concerns about submitting identity or biometric data, sharpened by an earlier breach involving third-party verification documents. Discord’s network effects and richer, free community features remain formidable, however. Commenters expect TeamSpeak and other rivals to benefit without assuming Discord will disappear, and distinguish resistance to age checks from resistance to surveillance-oriented data collection.
