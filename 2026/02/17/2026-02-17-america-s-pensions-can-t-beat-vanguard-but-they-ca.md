# America's pensions can't beat Vanguard but they can close a hospital

- Score: 179 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47048248) | Link: https://www.governance.fyi/p/americas-pensions-cant-beat-a-vanguard

### TL;DR

The essay argues America’s roughly $6 trillion in public pensions pays high fees for alternative assets yet earns about what a low-cost 60/40 index portfolio delivers. It says this underperformance also finances extraction: private-equity-backed firms cut jobs, close hospitals, and weaken local institutions. Drawing on older municipal-bond investing and foreign models, it proposes preparing bailout conditions—fee caps, fair benchmarks, and qualifying domestic infrastructure allocations—because captured pension institutions are unlikely to reform before another public rescue creates political leverage.
