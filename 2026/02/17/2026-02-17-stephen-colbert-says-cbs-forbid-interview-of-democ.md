# Stephen Colbert says CBS forbid interview of Democrat because of FCC threat

- Score: 180 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47051793) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/02/stephen-colbert-says-cbs-forbid-interview-of-democrat-because-of-fcc-threat/

### TL;DR

Stephen Colbert said CBS blocked an on-air interview with Texas Democratic Senate candidate James Talarico after FCC chair Brendan Carr threatened to reconsider talk shows' bona fide-news exemption from equal-time rules. CBS denied prohibiting it, saying lawyers presented compliance options and the show chose YouTube. Critics noted no rule change had occurred, only Talarico's Democratic primary opponents could request comparable time, and right-wing radio was excluded from Carr's warning. HN discussion focused on regulatory leverage chilling speech and the risk of selective enforcement.

### Comment pulse

- Broad discretionary regulation can induce private censorship → firms with licenses, mergers, or penalties pending may preemptively suppress disfavored speech.
- Candidate-airtime rules can promote fairness → Carr's radio carve-out fueled partisan-enforcement fears — counterpoint: cable networks fall outside broadcast rules.
- CBS's legal caution exceeded the rule's strictest reading → merely naming Talarico or mentioning an online interview would not trigger equal time.
