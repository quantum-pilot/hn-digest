# Thank HN: You helped save 33k lives

- Score: 197 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47049824) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47049824

### TL;DR

Thirteen years after Watsi debuted on Hacker News, founder Chase Adam reports that donors have funded 33,241 surgeries with more than $20 million. Hacker News traffic and Paul Graham’s early check helped Watsi enter Y Combinator as its first nonprofit; 619 monthly donors still giving today began at least a decade ago. Adam also describes burnout from treating exponential growth as the only valid success, eventually accepting that nonprofit demand and donor supply favor slower, sustainable progress.
