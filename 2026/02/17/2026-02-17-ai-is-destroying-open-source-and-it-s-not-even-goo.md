# AI is destroying open source, and it's not even good yet

- Score: 398 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47042136) | Link: https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2026/ai-is-destroying-open-source/

### TL;DR

Jeff Geerling argues that cheap AI generation is overwhelming open-source maintainers with low-quality vulnerability reports and pull requests, while leaving humans to pay the verification cost. Curl’s useful reports reportedly fell from 15% to 5%, contributing to its decision to end bug bounties, and GitHub now lets projects disable pull requests. Geerling concedes local models helped his own migration only because he understood, tested, and reviewed every change; contributors should bear that same responsibility before submitting work.
