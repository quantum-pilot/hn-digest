### TL;DR

Homeland Security investigator Greg Squire’s team identified a 12-year-old abuse victim after ordinary room details defeated an offender’s cropping. A regionally sold sofa narrowed the search, then brick expert John Harp recognized a Flaming Alamo wall brick whose heavy shipping radius reduced 40,000 customers to dozens. Investigators matched social-media and address records, finding the girl lived with her mother’s boyfriend, a convicted sex offender who received more than 70 years.
