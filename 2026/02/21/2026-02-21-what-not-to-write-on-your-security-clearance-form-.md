# What not to write on your security clearance form (1988)

- Score: 357 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47102576) | Link: https://milk.com/wall-o-shame/security_clearance.html

### TL;DR

Les Earnest recounts losing glasses at age twelve in 1943 with a homemade cipher key hidden in their case. A finder suspected Japanese espionage, prompting the FBI to spend six weeks tracing the prescription before discovering a schoolboy. Years later, Earnest truthfully wrote on a naval security-clearance form that he had been investigated as a Japanese spy. The security officer tore up the form, ordered him to omit the incident, and the clearance was granted. Commenters debated bureaucratic categories, historical pragmatism, and the modern danger of concealing records.
