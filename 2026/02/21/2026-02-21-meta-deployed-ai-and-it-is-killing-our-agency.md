# Meta Deployed AI and It Is Killing Our Agency

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47097502) | Link: https://mojodojo.io/blog/meta-is-systematically-killing-our-agency/

### TL;DR

An advertising agency says Meta repeatedly bans newly created staff work accounts minutes or hours after extensive identity verification, before any advertising or posting. Because appeals sit behind the blocked login and support offers only new-account or appeal loops, specialists also risk linked personal-account bans. The agency’s update says support representatives confirmed account creation and monitoring are handled almost entirely by AI, without effective human override. Commenters disputed whether separate work accounts violate Meta policy, but shared similar automated-ban experiences and criticized designing business access around personal identities.

### Comment pulse

- Critics called the AI framing clickbait and blamed duplicate-account rules — counterpoint: the update attributes automation directly to repeated Meta support confirmations.
- Agency operators said Meta deliberately expects one legal-name personal account to manage unrelated clients, creating security and employment risks.
- Comparable Google and Marketplace stories suggest automated enforcement loops extend beyond this agency, especially before users can reach paid human support.
