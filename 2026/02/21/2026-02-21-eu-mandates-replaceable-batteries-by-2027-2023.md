# EU mandates replaceable batteries by 2027 (2023)

- Score: 212 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47098687) | Link: https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/new-law-more-sustainable-circular-and-safe-batteries-enters-force-2023-08-17_en

### TL;DR

The EU’s 2023 Batteries Regulation takes a full-lifecycle approach covering sourcing, carbon footprints, harmful substances, collection, recycled content, material recovery, labels, digital passports, and supply-chain due diligence. From 2027, the Commission says consumers must be able to remove and replace portable batteries throughout a product’s life. HN strongly favored repairability and waste reduction, but disputed the headline’s implication for phones: commenters cited compliance with commercially available tools and exemptions for smartphones or tablets meeting separate durability and repairability standards, potentially allowing designs unlike old snap-in batteries.

### Comment pulse

- Replaceability can extend useful life → failed batteries need not condemn otherwise functional devices or force risky glued-glass repairs.
- Product engineering involves tradeoffs → sealed packs enable thinness and water resistance — counterpoint: serviceable designs need not require tool-free swapping.
- Battery access is only one longevity constraint → software support, parts availability, DRM, processors, and memory can still obsolete hardware.
