# Andrej Karpathy talks about "Claws"

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47099160) | Link: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/21/claws/

### TL;DR

Simon Willison relays Andrej Karpathy’s proposal that Claw is becoming a category name for persistent personal-agent systems layered above LLM agents. Such software runs locally, communicates through messaging, schedules work, maintains context, and invokes tools; Karpathy prefers smaller, containerized implementations such as NanoClaw over OpenClaw’s much larger and riskier codebase. The sparse HN discussion focused less on the category itself than on sourcing and terminology, arguing that the submission should link Karpathy’s original post and questioning whether another label clarifies or merely markets familiar orchestration.
