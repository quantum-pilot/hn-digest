# Attention Media ≠ Social Networks

- Score: 518 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47110515) | Link: https://susam.net/attention-media-vs-social-networks.html

### TL;DR

The essay distinguishes social networks—feeds chosen from friends and followed accounts—from “attention media” optimized for engagement through infinite scroll, manufactured notifications, and algorithmic strangers. The author left mainstream services and favors Mastodon’s finite, chronological feed. HN agreed that “social media” marked a friends-to-publishers shift, but disputed Mastodon as a sufficient remedy: platform design, learned behavior, moderation, data control, and online social norms can remain unhealthy even after recommendation algorithms are removed.
