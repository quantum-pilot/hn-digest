# Loops is a federated, open-source TikTok

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47113618) | Link: https://joinloops.org/

### TL;DR

Loops is an open-beta, open-source short-video platform using ActivityPub to federate videos, comments, discovery, and engagement across Loops instances and compatible services such as Mastodon and Pixelfed. It offers chronological Following and algorithmic For You feeds, creator tools, browser and mobile access, no ads, and community-governed servers. Commenters split over whether decentralization meaningfully fixes short-form video: critics see the swipe feed and recommendation algorithm as inherently addictive, while defenders argue the format can efficiently carry educational and creative material without centralized advertising incentives.

### Comment pulse

- Federation removes platform lock-in and corporate control — counterpoint: recommendation incentives can still reward engagement bait regardless of ownership.
- Some doubted the fediverse could attract mainstream interests; others said users can create missing channels or instances themselves.
- Desktop users liked browser access but criticized scroll-only navigation, animation delays, and the absence of keyboard controls.
