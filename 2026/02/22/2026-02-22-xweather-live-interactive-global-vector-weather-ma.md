# Xweather Live – Interactive global vector weather map

- Score: 124 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47111626) | Link: https://live.xweather.com/

### TL;DR

Xweather Live is an ad-free interactive global vector map combining real-time radar, forecasts, air quality, wind, severe-weather alerts, and AI-generated impact summaries. It targets general, transportation, and maritime exploration, with dedicated tropical-storm and winter-weather views. The creator says its advantage over established alternatives is breadth: additional layers cover lightning, hail, renewable energy, wildfires, and cyclone data. Commenters praised smooth timeline scrubbing, particularly outside the United States, while suggesting global hot-and-cold extremes, aviation-specific differentiation, and climate-zone views.
