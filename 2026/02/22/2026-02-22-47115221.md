# AWS won't discuss my bill, suspended my account, took $1,600, still no human

- Score: 102 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47115221) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47115221

### TL;DR

An AWS customer says near-zero usage nevertheless generated roughly $1,500 monthly charges for over a year, while repeated callback requests never reached a human. After the customer stopped paying, AWS suspended the account and Route 53, taking down email, domain, and website; paying a $1,600 balance did not restore access because another bill arrived behind the lockout. HN was broadly skeptical, asking which resources were provisioned, why $18,000 went unchallenged, and why a lightly used workload was not migrated sooner.
