# I spent two days gigging at RentAHuman and didn't make a single cent

- Score: 113 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47004319) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/i-tried-rentahuman-ai-agents-hired-me-to-hype-their-ai-startups/

### TL;DR

A WIRED reporter spent two days testing RentAHuman, a new marketplace where AI agents supposedly pay people for real-world work, and earned nothing. Stripe setup errored, unsolicited offers never arrived even after the rate dropped from $20 to $5, and available bounties largely promoted AI products. The one accepted flower-delivery job concealed startup advertising and triggered relentless bot follow-ups; a flyer job collapsed through human logistical confusion. Commenters split between calling the platform empty hype and defending an early network still trapped by sparse supply and demand.
