# GPT-5.2 derives a new result in theoretical physics

- Score: 323 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47006594) | Link: https://openai.com/index/new-result-theoretical-physics/

### TL;DR

An OpenAI preprint argues that a single-minus gluon tree amplitude, usually treated as zero, becomes nonzero on a special half-collinear slice of momentum space. Human researchers calculated cases through n=6; GPT-5.2 Pro simplified those expressions and conjectured an all-n formula. A scaffolded model independently reasoned for roughly 12 hours and produced a proof, which the authors checked against Berends-Giele recursion and the soft theorem. Commenters saw a substantial AI-assisted research result but disputed the headline’s autonomy implication, prior-art status, and access to the underlying process.
