# Monosketch

- Score: 676 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47001871) | Link: https://monosketch.io/

### TL;DR

The open-source editor uses an Apache-2.0 license and constructs text-based diagrams from rectangles, lines, text boxes, connectors, fills, and border styles. Its examples span system architecture, network exchanges, UI mockups, circuit schematics, decorative art, and presentations, with source contributions and sponsorship welcomed. Commenters saw it as a promising cross-platform alternative to the Mac-only Monodraw and listed several established text-diagram tools. They also flagged an important quality issue: the showcase circuit allegedly contains electrical mistakes, while many displayed box-drawing symbols are Unicode rather than strict ASCII.

### Comment pulse

- Former Monodraw users especially welcomed Linux and Windows availability, though they praised the paid Mac app’s mature drawing experience.
- Alternatives included Markdeep, ASCIIFlow, TextPaint, Textik, JavE, and symbol libraries, suggesting a broad but fragmented tool niche.
- Electrical reviewers found shorted or reversed components in the hero schematic, warning that attractive examples can teach novices incorrect conventions.

### LLM perspective

- View: Its strongest differentiator is accessible visual editing across platforms, not strict adherence to the seven-bit ASCII character set.
- Impact: Developers gain portable, version-control-friendly diagrams; inaccurate technical samples could undermine trust among specialist users.
