# Fix the iOS keyboard before the timer hits zero or I'm switching back to Android

- Score: 1253 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47003064) | Link: https://ios-countdown.win/

### TL;DR

A frustrated iPhone user set an end-of-WWDC 2026 deadline for Apple to fix or acknowledge keyboard problems, threatening a two-year switch to Android. Reported failures include bad autocorrection, misregistered taps, lag in long notes and messages, weak swipe typing, and awkward text selection. The captured page also contains mutually exclusive future-status sections saying Apple acknowledged the issue or that the author switched, so neither outcome is established here. Commenters broadly echoed degraded typing and dictation, though one longtime user reported no problems and noted third-party keyboards exist.
