# OpenAI – How to delete your account

- Score: 1801 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47193478) | Link: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6378407-how-to-delete-your-account

### TL;DR

OpenAI says account deletion is permanent, removes ChatGPT and API access, and deletes data within 30 days except limited legal or security retention. Users can use the Privacy Portal or delete in ChatGPT after a recent sign-in and typed confirmation. Web Plus cancels automatically, but Apple App Store or Google Play subscriptions require separate cancellation. Accounts cannot be reactivated, though most users may reuse the email after 30 days. HN commenters treated deletion as a boycott and reported failed codes, disabled buttons, portal errors, and delayed exports.
