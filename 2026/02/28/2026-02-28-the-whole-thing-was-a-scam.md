# The whole thing was a scam

- Score: 516 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47197505) | Link: https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/the-whole-thing-was-scam

### TL;DR

Gary Marcus alleges that Anthropic's Pentagon dispute was predetermined: while Sam Altman publicly supported Dario Amodei, OpenAI had reportedly been negotiating since Wednesday to replace Anthropic and ultimately accepted broadly similar restrictions. Marcus links the timing to Greg Brockman's $25 million contribution to a Trump-aligned PAC and calls the supply-chain-risk label punitive. Despite criticizing Anthropic's hype, copyright settlement and safety retreat, he argues it deserves identical terms. Hacker News largely suspected corruption, but asked whether the two contracts are actually equivalent and whether Anthropic would accept OpenAI's version.

### Comment pulse

- The chronology invites a pay-to-play inference → public solidarity coincided with private negotiations to take the disputed business.
- Similar is not identical → commenters want clause-level comparison and confirmation that Anthropic would sign the alternative terms.
- The designation appears punitive — counterpoint: suspicious timing and donations do not alone prove that a contribution caused the award.
