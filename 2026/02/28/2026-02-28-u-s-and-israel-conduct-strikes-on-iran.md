# U.S. and Israel Conduct Strikes on Iran

- Score: 281 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47191414) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/02/28/world/iran-strikes-trump

### TL;DR

Live reporting described attacks on at least nine Iranian cities, with Trump announcing Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei's death before Iranian confirmation and Israel reporting several senior commanders killed. Both governments sought regime change, but American intelligence warned the Revolutionary Guards might instead consolidate control. Iran fired missiles at Israel and American-hosting Gulf states; Hormuz shipping, regional airports and thousands of flights were disrupted. Civilian tolls, including a reported girls' school strike, remained unverified. The supplied Hacker News comments were largely redirects and slogans rather than substantive discussion.
