# Statement on the comments from Secretary of War Pete Hegseth

- Score: 1118 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47188697) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/statement-comments-secretary-war

### TL;DR

Anthropic says Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth plans to designate it a supply-chain risk after negotiations stalled over excluding two uses of Claude: mass domestic surveillance of Americans and fully autonomous weapons. It argues current models are too unreliable for autonomous combat, surveillance violates rights, and the designation is legally unprecedented. Commercial customers would remain unaffected; military contractors would be restricted only on Department of War work. Hacker News largely praised the costly stand, while questioning its narrow American framing and durability under future leadership.

### Comment pulse

- Forgoing military revenue signals sincerity → principles become more credible when they carry immediate commercial risk.
- The red lines remain narrow → critics objected that foreign surveillance appears permissible and autonomous weapons are rejected mainly for current unreliability.
- Corporate ethics depend on leadership — counterpoint: threatened litigation and lost contracts create concrete commitments that successors must actively reverse.
