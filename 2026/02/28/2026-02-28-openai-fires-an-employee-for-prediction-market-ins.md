# OpenAI fires an employee for prediction market insider trading

- Score: 264 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47195317) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/openai-fires-employee-insider-trading-polymarket-kalshi/

### TL;DR

OpenAI fired an unnamed employee after concluding they used confidential company information for personal gain on prediction markets including Polymarket; it disclosed neither the person nor the trades. Separately, Unusual Whales flagged 77 positions across 60 wallets around OpenAI events, including 13 new wallets that wagered $309,486 shortly before the ChatGPT Browser launch. The article calls this the first confirmed firing of a major-tech employee over such trading. HN discussion framed insider knowledge as a structural test: it improves market information while violating employment duties and undermining fair participation.
