# OpenAI agrees with Dept. of War to deploy models in their classified network

- Score: 1327 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47189650) | Link: https://twitter.com/sama/status/2027578652477821175

### TL;DR

Sam Altman says OpenAI agreed to deploy its models on the Department of War’s classified cloud networks, with forward-deployed engineers and technical safeguards. He says the contract prohibits domestic mass surveillance and requires human responsibility for force, including autonomous weapons, and asks that other AI companies receive the same terms. HN debate centered on whether these match Anthropic’s disputed red lines: supporters cited constraints grounded in law and policy, while skeptics argued “all lawful use” leaves enforcement to government interpretations that can shift or be stretched.
