# Croatia declared free of landmines after 31 years

- Score: 475 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47189535) | Link: https://glashrvatske.hrt.hr/en/domestic/croatia-declared-free-of-landmines-after-31-years-12593533

### TL;DR

Croatia declared itself free of all known minefields after a 31-year clearance effort under the Ottawa Convention. Teams removed nearly 107,000 mines and 407,000 unexploded ordnance items at a cost of about €1.2 billion; 208 people died, including 41 deminers. The milestone improves safety and unlocks farmland, rural development, and tourism. HN commenters celebrated while stressing that “mine-free” cannot prove every stray device is gone, especially where wartime maps were poor or older explosives remain outside catalogued areas.
