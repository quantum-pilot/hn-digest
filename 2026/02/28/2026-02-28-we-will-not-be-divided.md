# We Will Not Be Divided

- Score: 2525 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47188473) | Link: https://notdivided.org

### TL;DR

An open letter invites current and former Google and OpenAI employees to ask their leaders to join resistance to Department of War demands involving domestic mass surveillance and autonomous killing without human oversight. Signatures may be public or anonymous, with employment verification through work accounts or evidence; anonymous identifying data is deleted within 24 hours, and organizers disclose verification mistakes. Hacker News saw wider rule-of-law and supplier-trust stakes, while questioning the domestic-only surveillance boundary and debating ordinary procurement choice versus punitive blacklisting.
