# New accounts on HN more likely to use em-dashes

- Score: 563 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47152085) | Link: https://www.marginalia.nu/weird-ai-crap/hn/

### TL;DR

An analysis of roughly 700 recent Hacker News comments and 700 comments from new accounts found em dashes, arrows, or similar symbols in 17.47% of the new-account sample versus 1.83% overall. Mentions of AI or LLMs were also higher, at 18.67% versus 11.8%. The author sees these differences, alongside banal or off-topic writing, as possible evidence of bot activity. However, the post does not establish causation, and account age, current topics, device behavior, sampling, or prolific users could confound the result.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime em-dash users resent being labeled synthetic; counterpoint: the unusually large cohort difference still merits investigation beyond anecdotes.
- Critics raised cohort, topic, device, and multiple-comparison confounders, asking for effect sizes and broader samples rather than a single snapshot.
- The public SQLite dataset lets others test whether account age, posting topic, or a few prolific users explain the result.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Typography is a weak identity signal unless combined with behavioral and temporal evidence.
- **Impact:** Crude detection could stigmatize careful human writing and encourage deliberate stylistic degradation.
- **Watch next:** Replication across larger, topic-matched cohorts with user-level rather than comment-level analysis.
