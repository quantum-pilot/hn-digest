# Following 35% growth, solar has passed hydro on US grid

- Score: 370 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47154009) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/02/final-2025-data-is-in-us-energy-use-is-up-as-solar-passes-hydro/

### TL;DR

Full-year U.S. data for 2025 show solar generation rising 35% and surpassing hydropower for the first time. Yet electricity demand climbed 2.8%, or 121 TWh, so solar’s additional 85 TWh did not fully cover growth; coal generation rose 13% amid constrained and costlier gas expansion. Planned 2026 additions include 43 GW of solar, 12 GW of wind, and 24 GW of batteries, positioning renewables near one-quarter of generation if demand does not surge. HN debated policy motives, market economics, and the need to distinguish nameplate capacity from delivered energy.
