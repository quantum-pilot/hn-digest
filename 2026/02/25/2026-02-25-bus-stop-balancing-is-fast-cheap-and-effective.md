# Bus stop balancing is fast, cheap, and effective

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47153798) | Link: https://worksinprogress.co/issue/the-united-states-needs-fewer-bus-stops/

### TL;DR

Bus-stop balancing removes closely spaced stops so buses spend less time braking, boarding, rejoining traffic, and missing signals. The article proposes moving older U.S. routes from roughly 700–800-foot spacing toward 1,300 feet, citing speed gains of 4.4–14% in San Francisco and a Vancouver pilot that removed one-quarter of stops, saved riders five minutes on average, and cut annual operating costs by C$700,000. HN agreed speed and reliability matter but warned that hostile walking conditions, disability access, route geometry, and chronic underfunding can make a modest extra walk consequential.

### Comment pulse

- Fewer stops can improve frequency → shorter round trips need fewer vehicles, freeing labor budgets for more service or avoiding cuts.
- Coverage losses can be small on dense grids → overlapping walksheds mean many riders already have multiple nearby stops.
- Walking is not equally cheap → counterpoint: unsafe crossings, missing sidewalks, age, and disability can turn consolidation into exclusion.
