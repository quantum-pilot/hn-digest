# Amazon accused of widespread scheme to inflate prices across the economy

- Score: 645 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47145907) | Link: https://www.thebignewsletter.com/p/amazon-busted-for-widespread-price

### TL;DR

California Attorney General Rob Bonta is seeking a preliminary injunction before Amazon’s 2027 trials. He alleges Amazon used its Buy Box power and Vendor Central orders to pressure suppliers into raising rivals’ prices, stopping discounts, or withdrawing cheaper products—an alleged hub-and-spoke price-fixing scheme that could inflate prices beyond Amazon. The requested order would restrict off-platform pricing coordination and add a monitor. HN sellers agreed the incentives are real but disputed the framing: Amazon offers attractive volume and lowest-price positioning, while critics say those benefits depend on coercing market-wide parity.

### Comment pulse

- Lowest-price enforcement can raise prices everywhere → sellers may preserve Amazon access by lifting cheaper direct or rival-channel prices.
- Vendor Central makes pressure consequential → large brands risk lucrative purchase orders — counterpoint: selling to Amazon remains voluntary and terms can be attractive.
- Litigation delay favors incumbents → an injunction could stop alleged harm before trial, but redacted evidence prevents outsiders from judging its strength.
