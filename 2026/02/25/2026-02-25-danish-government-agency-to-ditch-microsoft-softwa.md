# Danish government agency to ditch Microsoft software (2025)

- Score: 719 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47149701) | Link: https://therecord.media/denmark-digital-agency-microsoft-digital-independence

### TL;DR

In June 2025, Denmark’s Digitalisation Ministry planned to move more than half its staff from Microsoft Office to LibreOffice the following month and complete the shift that autumn, while retaining the option to reverse course if complexity proved excessive. The goals were lower dependence on U.S. vendors, reduced licensing and Windows 10 migration costs, and greater digital sovereignty; Copenhagen, Aarhus, and Germany’s Schleswig-Holstein were pursuing similar moves. HN argued that office documents are the easy layer: identity, Entra, device management, Teams, compliance, and embedded workflows determine whether dependency actually falls.
