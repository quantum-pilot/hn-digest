# Breaking Free

- Score: 177 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47178678) | Link: https://www.forbrukerradet.no/breakingfree/

### TL;DR

The Norwegian Consumer Council’s Breaking Free report frames enshittification as a consumer and societal problem that policy can reverse. Backed by more than 70 groups across Europe and the US, its letters ask governments to rebalance provider power, make switching and modification practical, promote openness, interoperability, portability, and ethical public procurement, enforce existing competition, privacy, and consumer laws, and close loopholes through a Digital Fairness Act. HN welcomed enforceable remedies but debated a cited estimate that Meta derived 10% of revenue from fraudulent or banned-goods advertising.
