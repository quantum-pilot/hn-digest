# NASA announces overhaul of Artemis program amid safety concerns, delays

- Score: 191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47182483) | Link: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nasa-artemis-moon-program-overhaul/

### TL;DR

NASA administrator Jared Isaacman recast Artemis around incremental testing after calling a 2028 landing unrealistic without more preparation. Artemis II is delayed to at least April 1 by hydrogen and helium-system problems. A revised Artemis III would test commercial landers, docking, navigation, life support, communications, propulsion, and spacesuits in low Earth orbit in 2027; Artemis IV and V would target one or possibly two 2028 landings. NASA also plans annual missions, faster SLS production, and a standardized upper stage instead of the Exploration Upper Stage.
