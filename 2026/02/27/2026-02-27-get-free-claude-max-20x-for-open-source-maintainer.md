# Get free Claude max 20x for open-source maintainers

- Score: 399 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47178371) | Link: https://claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss

### TL;DR

Anthropic is offering six months of Claude Max 20x to as many as 10,000 open-source contributors. Primary maintainers or core members qualify through a public repository with at least 5,000 GitHub stars or one million monthly npm downloads plus recent commits, releases, or reviews; maintainers of quietly critical projects may still apply. HN split between gratitude for a direct benefit worth roughly $1,200 and criticism that a temporary, popularity-based offer is customer acquisition rather than durable ecosystem support, especially after models trained on public code.

### Comment pulse

- Maintainer economics make the offer meaningful → an Express and Lodash contributor reported earning only $10 from OSS in 2025.
- Eligibility metrics favor GitHub-style celebrity → major projects can miss star and npm thresholds — counterpoint: the application explicitly invites exceptions.
- Six months feels promotional → critics prefer renewable access; others call temporary professional-tier compute unusually costly and generous.
