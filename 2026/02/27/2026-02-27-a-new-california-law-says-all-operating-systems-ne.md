# A new California law says all operating systems need to have age verification

- Score: 279 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47181208) | Link: https://www.pcgamer.com/software/operating-systems/a-new-california-law-says-all-operating-systems-including-linux-need-to-have-some-form-of-age-verification-at-account-setup/

### TL;DR

California’s AB 1043, effective January 1, 2027, requires operating-system providers to collect a user’s birth date or age during account setup and expose an API signal placing that user in one of four age brackets. The article calls this age verification, although the described mechanism can be self-reported and does not require face scans. Windows already collects birth dates, but compliance and enforcement look unclear for Linux distributions, downloadable legacy versions, and embedded systems. HN reaction centered on technical feasibility, privacy creep, and regulatory overbreadth.

### Comment pulse

- Lawmakers appear detached from implementation → commenters questioned enforcement against downloadable distributions, old versions, and systems without commercial vendors.
- Embedded devices expose scope ambiguity → many appliances run an operating system but lack any account-setup interface.
- The timing puzzled readers → the bill was signed in October 2025 but resurfaced months before its effective date.
