# Gemini 3 Deep Think

- Score: 578 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46991240) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-deep-think/

### TL;DR

Google announced an upgraded specialized reasoning mode for science, mathematics, coding, and engineering, available to AI Ultra subscribers and selected API testers. It reports 48.4% on Humanity’s Last Exam without tools, 84.6% on ARC-AGI-2, a 3455 Codeforces Elo, and gold-level Olympiad results. Early users reportedly found a paper’s logical flaw, improved crystal-growth fabrication, and designed physical components. Commenters welcomed the benchmark jump but questioned cost, overfitting, fair model-class comparisons, confusing release terminology, and the gap between laboratory scores and uneven Gemini product experiences.
