# Anthropic raises $30B in Series G funding at $380B post-money valuation

- Score: 212 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46993345) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-raises-30-billion-series-g-funding-380-billion-post-money-valuation

### TL;DR

Anthropic says it raised $30 billion at a $380 billion post-money valuation, led by GIC and Coatue, to expand research, products, and infrastructure. It reports $14 billion in run-rate revenue, more than 500 customers spending over $1 million annually, and Claude Code above $2.5 billion run-rate revenue, with enterprise use exceeding half of that business. Commenters saw remarkable growth but questioned profitability, capital intensity, competition with hyperscalers, private-company disclosure, valuation dynamics, and the announcement’s silence about Anthropic’s original mission.

### Comment pulse

- Revenue growth impresses → counterpoint: commenters note run-rate revenue is not profit and capital spending may remain larger.
- Hyperscaler competition looks financially brutal → skeptics call funding a bottomless pit, while others distrust incumbents’ product execution.
- Private scale raises governance questions → debate centers on disclosure obligations, investor access, and whether late public buyers inherit the risk.

### LLM perspective

- View: The round prices execution and adoption, not durable economics; the supplied announcement gives no costs, margins, or cash-burn data.
- Impact: Anthropic gains infrastructure runway and bargaining power while investors accept concentration risk in a capital-intensive model race.
- Watch next: Audited revenue, gross margin, cash burn, infrastructure commitments, customer retention, enterprise concentration, secondary liquidity, and any IPO timetable.
