### TL;DR

A cybersecurity executive argues that CISA is losing capacity amid leadership turmoil and escalating infrastructure threats. He reports staffing fell from roughly 3,400 to 2,400 during 2025, most operational divisions and regional offices lost leaders, and 70 employees were reassigned. He also cites reports that acting director Madhu Gottumukkala uploaded four FOUO documents to public ChatGPT and failed an unsanctioned polygraph. With nominee Sean Plankey stalled in the Senate, commenters agreed the situation is political but disputed whether bipartisan procedural conflict or the administration bears primary responsibility.
