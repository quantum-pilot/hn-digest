# Oh, good: Discord's age verification rollout has ties to Palantir co-founder

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47011346) | Link: https://www.pcgamer.com/software/platforms/oh-good-discords-age-verification-rollout-has-ties-to-palantir-co-founder-and-panopticon-architect-peter-thiel/

### TL;DR

Discord plans global age-assurance restrictions in March, using prediction to classify some adults and asking some remaining users for face scans or government IDs. In the UK, where checks already apply, selected users may enter an experiment with Persona; unlike Discord’s earlier local-processing description, submitted information may be stored for up to seven days. Persona’s recent funding rounds were led by Founders Fund, co-founded by Peter Thiel, but the input shows no Palantir role. Commenters sought Matrix-based alternatives and argued that third-party verification creates identity, breach, and exclusion risks.

### Comment pulse

- Matrix and related tools offer self-hosting and encryption → counterpoint: setup, mobile quality, voice, streaming, and screen sharing remain less seamless.
- Age gates can deanonymize otherwise pseudonymous communities → collecting face or ID data compounds existing platform surveillance rather than merely replacing it.
- Device-side parental controls were proposed as a private alternative → critics doubt centralized verifiers can avoid persistent identifiers and commercial incentives.

### LLM perspective

- View: Investor association is indirect; the concrete concern is Discord expanding sensitive-data handling through an experimental third party.
- Impact: Users may lose access or pseudonymity unless automated inference classifies them as adults or they submit stronger identity evidence.
- Watch next: Experiment selection, deletion verification, vendor contracts, false classifications, appeals, breach exposure, regulatory audits, and less invasive methods.
