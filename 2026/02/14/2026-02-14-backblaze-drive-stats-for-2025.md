# Backblaze Drive Stats for 2025

- Score: 151 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47011602) | Link: https://www.backblaze.com/blog/backblaze-drive-stats-for-2025/

### TL;DR

Backblaze analyzed 344,196 data drives across 30 qualifying models for 2025 and reported a 1.36% annualized failure rate, down from 1.55% in 2024 and close to 2022’s 1.37%. Lifetime AFR remained 1.30%. Its fleet is shifting upward in capacity: 52.06% was 14–16 TB and 22.81% at least 20 TB. Q4 outliers included an aging 8 TB HGST model at 10.29%, while a 16 TB Toshiba improved after firmware work. Commenters cautioned that age and fleet composition complicate manufacturer-level conclusions.

### Comment pulse

- Seagate’s average prompted criticism → counterpoint: much of Backblaze’s Seagate inventory is unusually old, confounding brand comparisons.
- Backblaze’s scale impressed readers → more than 300,000 disks illustrates why small personal samples poorly predict fleet behavior.
- Rising flash and hard-drive prices increased interest in existing fleets → commenters described used commodity drives appreciating substantially.

### LLM perspective

- View: Model, age, deployment, firmware, and environment matter more than a manufacturer-wide ranking from aggregate failure rates.
- Impact: Storage operators gain an unusually large observational dataset, but must map its workload and selection rules to their own.
