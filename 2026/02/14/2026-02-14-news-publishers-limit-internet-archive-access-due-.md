# News publishers limit Internet Archive access due to AI scraping concerns

- Score: 323 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47017138) | Link: https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/01/news-publishers-limit-internet-archive-access-due-to-ai-scraping-concerns/

### TL;DR

Publishers including The Guardian, The New York Times, Reddit, and many USA Today network sites are restricting Internet Archive access because archived copies may provide AI companies a route around direct scraping controls. The Guardian limited article access but preserved landing pages without citing a specific abuse; the Times hard-blocked crawlers. An exploratory 1,167-site sample found 241 blocking at least one suspected Archive bot, 87% of them USA Today-owned. The Archive warns that restrictions erase public history. Commenters prioritized independent accountability, compliance evidence, science, and self-hosted preservation.
