# Ooh.directory: a place to find good blogs that interest you

- Score: 402 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47014449) | Link: https://ooh.directory/

### TL;DR

Ooh.directory is a searchable, human-curated catalog of 2,380 blogs, organized across 12 broad categories and surfaced through recent additions, recent updates, random discovery, and per-blog descriptions with latest-post excerpts. The page illustrates deliberate variety, from arts and technology to personal writing, science, recreation, and uncategorizable projects. Commenters welcomed curation as an antidote to AI-generated noise and declining search, but questioned opaque inclusion and long-term sustainability. The solo maintainer replied that selection is personal, time-limited, and balanced across subjects—not an exhaustive registry or approval system.

### Comment pulse

- Directory advocates expect higher signal than search or LLM answers—counterpoint: earlier large directories struggled once popularity overwhelmed curation.
- Missing blogs prompted transparency demands; defenders said a free personal collection owes neither exhaustive coverage nor individualized rejection explanations.
- RSS taxonomies could automate categorization, but inconsistent labels, uncategorized feeds, and vanished predecessor directories complicate durable interoperability.

### LLM perspective

- View: Its value comes from taste and bounded judgment, the same qualities that make coverage subjective and incomplete.
- Impact: Readers gain serendipitous independent writing; maintainers inherit submission pressure, taxonomy work, and continuity risk.
- Watch next: Curation criteria, update feeds, stale-link handling, category metadata, maintainer capacity, and evidence that discovery remains diverse.
