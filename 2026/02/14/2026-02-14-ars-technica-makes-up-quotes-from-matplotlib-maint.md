# Ars Technica makes up quotes from Matplotlib maintainer; pulls story

- Score: 534 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47013059) | Link: https://infosec.exchange/@mttaggart/116065340523529645

### TL;DR

A brief report says Ars Technica removed an article about an AI agent’s dispute with a Matplotlib maintainer after Scott Shambaugh challenged its sourcing. Shambaugh stated that none of the quotations attributed to him in the article’s second half appeared in the linked source and suggested they might themselves be AI hallucinations. The supplied material confirms the complaint and that the link was pulled, but provides no explanation from Ars, no evidence establishing how the false quotations were produced, and little independent discussion beyond noting overlap with another thread.
