# Homeland Security Wants Social Media Sites to Expose Anti-ICE Accounts

- Score: 332 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47009582) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/13/technology/dhs-anti-ice-social-media.html

### TL;DR

According to the report, DHS sent hundreds of administrative subpoenas to Google, Meta, Reddit, and Discord seeking identities behind accounts criticizing ICE or reporting agent locations. The demands require no prior judicial approval; companies may refuse, yet Google, Meta, and Reddit reportedly complied with some. Certain users received 10–14 days to challenge. DHS cited officer safety and broad authority, while the ACLU called the requests speech-targeting. In two described challenges, DHS withdrew before rulings. Commenters stressed that resisting shifts legal costs onto individuals.
