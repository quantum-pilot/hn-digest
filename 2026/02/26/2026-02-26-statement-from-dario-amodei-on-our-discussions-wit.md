# Statement from Dario Amodei on Our Discussions with the Department of War

- Score: 400 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47173121) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/statement-department-of-war

### TL;DR

Dario Amodei says Anthropic strongly supports US defense and already supplies Claude for classified intelligence analysis, planning, cyber operations, and other national-security work. It nevertheless refuses two requested uses absent current safeguards: mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons, which it considers democratically dangerous or technically unreliable. Amodei says the Pentagon threatened removal, a supply-chain-risk designation, and Defense Production Act compulsion; Anthropic will not yield but would support an orderly migration. Commenters praised the stand while criticizing its narrow domestic scope and openness to future autonomous weapons.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters saw rare corporate backbone — counterpoint: skeptics called it public relations from a company already embedded in military systems.
- Domestic surveillance has stronger constitutional constraints, but commenters asked why foreign populations deserve fewer protections.
- Anthropic’s reliability objection appears temporary, leaving fully autonomous weapons possible once models and guardrails improve.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** This is a bounded contract dispute, not a rejection of military AI or intelligence work.
- **Impact:** A supplier exit would force mission-critical migration while testing whether model vendors can enforce use restrictions.
- **Watch next:** Formal government action, litigation, transition plans, and negotiated language for the two safeguards.
