# The Great Unwind

- Score: 220 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46889008) | Link: https://occupywallst.com/yen

### TL;DR

The essay argues that January’s cross-asset turbulence reflects a forced unwind of yen-funded carry trades, not isolated reactions to AI spending, Greenland tensions or Federal Reserve news. It links the Bank of Japan’s 0.75% rate, Japanese institutions’ repatriation, yen-futures buying and simultaneous declines in technology, crypto and precious metals into one deleveraging narrative. It predicts a stronger yen, higher U.S. yields and lower asset prices, then urges readers to buy yen-linked calls—an activist trading proposal that goes well beyond its stated evidence and disclaimer.
