# In Tehran

- Score: 89 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46888498) | Link: https://www.lrb.co.uk/blog/2026/january/in-tehran

### TL;DR

Writing from Tehran, Raha Nik-Andish recounts joining January 8 protests sparked by economic collapse and witnessing tear gas, gunfire, wounded demonstrators and a plainclothes agent stabbing a boy. The ensuing blackout, arrests and government warnings isolated residents; the author’s cousin remained detained. Nik-Andish describes mourning, vanishing livelihoods and private fury at the clerical government. Reported death tolls vary radically—from official and media estimates in the thousands to an ambulance driver’s 100,000 claim—underscoring how censorship and rumor obscure the violence’s scale.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters disputed whether five-figure death estimates are credible, citing censorship, official statements and the absence of independently verifiable evidence.
- Debate also split over Western intervention: some demanded action, while others rejected military escalation or a world-police role.
