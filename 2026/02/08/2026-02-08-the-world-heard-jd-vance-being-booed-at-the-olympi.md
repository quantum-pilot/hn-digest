# The world heard JD Vance being booed at the Olympics. Except for viewers in USA

- Score: 245 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46931948) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/feb/07/jd-vance-boos-winter-olympics

### TL;DR

At Milan’s Winter Olympics opening ceremony, Team USA drew cheers, then large sections audibly booed Vice-President JD Vance when cameras showed him. The writer and foreign broadcasts captured the reaction, while NBC’s U.S. presentation appeared not to; NBC denied editing crowd audio. The column argues globally circulating feeds and phone recordings make narrative curation self-defeating and could damage broadcaster trust before the 2026 World Cup and 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Commenters complicated the geographic framing: some Americans heard the boos, and several proposed routine commentary-focused audio mixing rather than censorship.

### Comment pulse
