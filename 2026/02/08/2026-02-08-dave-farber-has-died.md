# Dave Farber has died

- Score: 199 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46933401) | Link: https://lists.nanog.org/archives/list/nanog@lists.nanog.org/thread/TSNPJVFH4DKLINIKSMRIIVNHDG5XKJCM/

### TL;DR

A message from David J. Farber’s Keio colleagues says the networking pioneer died suddenly at his Tokyo home February 7, aged 91. Farber worked at Bell Labs and RAND, served as FCC chief technologist and an EFF board member, taught at four U.S. universities, then joined Keio in 2018, teaching through January 2026 and co-hosting IP-Asia discussions. The tribute credits his students with foundational Internet contributions and remembers him as mentor and conscience. Commenters recalled his presence throughout early network research, kindness to newcomers, lifelong curiosity, mailing-list archives, and humor.
