# Hetzner Prices increase 30-40%

- Score: 167 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47120145) | Link: https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/

### TL;DR

Hetzner’s official tables set new prices for new and existing services from April 1, 2026, excluding VAT. German and Finnish cloud instances commonly rise by about 30–36%, with comparable increases across US and Singapore plans, object storage, volumes, and snapshots. Dedicated-server adjustments vary much more by model, while auction servers rise 3%. Commenters connect the changes to memory, storage, energy, maintenance, and replacement costs, yet generally conclude that Hetzner remains cheaper than major hyperscale providers.

### Comment pulse

- Object storage drew particular criticism because its new price weakens Hetzner’s advantage over competing European providers.
- Some questioned increases on existing hardware; operators countered that power, labor, maintenance, and eventual replacement remain continuing costs.
- Memory add-ons may face steeper changes than the base-server tables suggest.
