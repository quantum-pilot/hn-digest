# Elsevier shuts down its finance journal citation cartel

- Score: 514 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47119530) | Link: https://www.chrisbrunet.com/p/elsevier-shuts-down-its-finance-journal

### TL;DR

Elsevier retracted 12 finance papers co-authored by Brian Lucey because he oversaw editorial decisions on manuscripts he co-authored, breaching conflict-of-interest rules; Lucey and collaborator Samuel Vigne were also removed from several editorial roles. The author interprets publication volume, co-authorship changes, overlapping journal editors, and elevated cross-citations as evidence of a citation cartel, then advances unverified financial-misconduct theories. HN treated the episode as a symptom of incentives that reward publication counts, journal placement, and citations rather than research quality.
