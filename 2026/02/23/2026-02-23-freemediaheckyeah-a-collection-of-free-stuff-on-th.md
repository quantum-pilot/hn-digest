# Freemediaheckyeah – A collection of free stuff on the internet

- Score: 256 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47117780) | Link: https://fmhy.net/

### TL;DR

Freemediaheckyeah is a community-maintained directory of free online resources spanning privacy tools, AI, streaming, music, games, books, software, education, mobile platforms, and self-hosting. It adds search, safety tooling, backups, and contribution channels, while stating that it hosts no files; many categories include piracy-oriented sources rather than only libre software. HN users praised its upkeep and usefulness as a search-engine alternative, but debated whether piracy constitutes preservation, ordinary infringement, or both depending on availability and intent.
