# Hacker News.love – 22 projects Hacker News didn't love

- Score: 196 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47120188) | Link: https://hackernews.love/

### TL;DR

Hacker News.love pairs dismissive launch-thread quotes about 22 projects—from Dropbox and GitHub to ChatGPT and Claude Code—with later valuations, acquisitions, adoption figures, or fundraising. Its lesson is that builders should persist despite early skepticism. The new HN discussion rejected the binary framing: selected negative comments often sat beside praise, raised valid product or ethical concerns, and were not disproved by commercial success. Commenters also challenged several outcome claims and criticized the site’s scroll hijacking and apparent AI-generated tone.

### Comment pulse

- Popularity does not invalidate criticism → Tailwind maintainability, Warp telemetry, and Uber regulation can remain real concerns after growth.
- Selection bias drives the narrative → non-top negative quotes and omitted praise cannot represent “HN” — counterpoint: communities often underweight usability.
- Retrospectives need consistent outcomes → funding, acquisition, users, and revenue are not interchangeable measures of a project’s merit.

- **View:** Launch criticism is best treated as risk discovery, not a verdict.
