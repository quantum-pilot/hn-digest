# A simple web we own

- Score: 164 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47124149) | Link: https://rsdoiel.github.io/blog/2026/02/21/a_simple_web_we_own.html

### TL;DR

The essay argues that people can reclaim agency online by owning modest computers, controlling their software and local networks, and publishing simple hypertext and RSS instead of rebuilding centralized platforms with elaborate federation. Its example uses Raspberry Pis, Markdown, a small open-source generator, and GitHub Pages, while proposing neighborhood networks and cooperatives. HN liked the ownership goal but stressed that last-mile providers, DNS, discovery, usability, and hardware supply chains still leave individuals dependent on institutions.
