# Hetzner (European hosting provider) to increase prices by up to 38%

- Score: 426 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47121029) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/BuyFromEU/comments/1rce0lf/hetzner_european_hosting_provider_to_increase/

### TL;DR

Hetzner customers report cloud VPS price increases of roughly the mid-30% range from April 1, with some comparisons reaching 38%, while dedicated-server changes appear smaller and more variable. The company attributes increases to unusually expensive memory, storage, energy, and other infrastructure inputs amid AI-driven demand. Commenters note Hetzner often remains substantially cheaper than hyperscalers, but object to sharp increases for existing plans and especially costly memory add-ons. The discussion also renews pressure to reduce software’s memory footprint.

### Comment pulse

- Many view the increase as an AI tax; others describe it as an ordinary demand shock across constrained hardware markets.
- Dedicated-server examples rose far less than cloud VPS prices, complicating claims of one uniform increase.
- Even after the change, several comparisons still put Hetzner well below AWS, Google Cloud, and DigitalOcean.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Compute buyers should model hardware-input volatility explicitly; ultra-low VPS pricing was not a durable baseline.
- **Impact:** Small projects with memory-heavy stacks lose runway first because fixed hosting costs rise before revenue or usage.
- **Watch next:** Actual April invoices, capacity availability, competitor repricing, and whether memory optimization meaningfully offsets higher plans.
