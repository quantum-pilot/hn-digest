# Americans are destroying Flock surveillance cameras

- Score: 523 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47127081) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/23/americans-are-destroying-flock-surveillance-cameras/

### TL;DR

TechCrunch reports scattered vandalism of Flock license-plate readers from California to Connecticut, Illinois, Oregon, and Virginia amid anger over nationwide vehicle tracking and immigration enforcement. Flock says it does not share data directly with ICE, but local police have given federal authorities access; DeFlock estimates nearly 80,000 cameras nationwide. HN debated whether destruction is justified civil disobedience, warned that wider surveillance may identify vandals, and questioned whether isolated incidents support the headline’s suggestion of a broad movement.
