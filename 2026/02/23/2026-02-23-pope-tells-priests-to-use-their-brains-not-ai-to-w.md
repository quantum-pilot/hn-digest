# Pope tells priests to use their brains, not AI, to write homilies

- Score: 520 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47119210) | Link: https://www.ewtnnews.com/vatican/pope-leo-xiv-tells-priests-to-use-their-brains-not-ai-to-write-homilies

### TL;DR

In a private question-and-answer session with Rome’s clergy, Pope Leo XIV urged priests to rely more on their own minds than AI when preparing homilies. His broader advice emphasized prayer, continuing study, attentive knowledge of local communities, priestly friendship, humility, and care for isolated older people. Commenters largely saw homilies as personal and community-specific work that should preserve the speaker’s voice, though some distinguished such reflection from technical writing where AI assistance can help communicate ideas.

### Comment pulse
