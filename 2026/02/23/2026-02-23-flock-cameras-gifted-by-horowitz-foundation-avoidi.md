# Flock cameras gifted by Horowitz Foundation, avoiding public oversight

- Score: 226 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47128960) | Link: https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/vegas-police-are-big-users-of-license-plate-readers-public-has-little-input-because-its-a-gift

### TL;DR

Las Vegas police operate roughly 200 Flock license-plate readers and have run more than 23,000 vehicle searches since late 2023, while sharing data with hundreds of agencies. Most equipment and software came through Horowitz Family Foundation donations to a private police foundation, bypassing purchasing-triggered public hearings and Nevada open-meeting rules. Reporting also found Metro’s policy is not posted publicly and its 2023 contract grants Flock broad data rights. HN argued that surveillance acquisitions need approval regardless of whether their price is $0.
