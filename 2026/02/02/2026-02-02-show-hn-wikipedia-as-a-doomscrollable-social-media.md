# Show HN: Wikipedia as a doomscrollable social media feed

- Score: 403 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46850803) | Link: https://xikipedia.org

### TL;DR

Xikipedia turns Simple Wikipedia into a swipeable, algorithmic feed. A basic non-ML recommender learns from engagement entirely on-device, sends no user data away, and works offline after an initial 34MB download. Users can seed categories or add their own, with random articles potentially including adult content. The project demonstrates how little machinery personalization requires, but commenters questioned whether educational material fixes doomscrolling’s context-switching habit. Others praised topic discovery and direct editing while criticizing the slow, bandwidth-heavy startup and requesting lazy loading.

### Comment pulse

- Some treat the feed as a StumbleUpon-like gateway for intentional research, not knowledge worth trusting without follow-up.
- Critics argue swiping and anticipation drive distraction regardless of whether each card contains accurate information.
- The creator says preloading link relationships enables local recommendations and privacy, but HN traffic saturated bandwidth.

### LLM perspective

- View: Privacy-preserving personalization can be simple; resisting compulsive interaction is a separate design problem.
- Impact: Wikipedia gains a discovery surface, while users risk replacing deliberate reading with fragmented exposure.
- Watch next: Faster startup, progressive caching, recommendation-quality measures and controls that encourage opening full articles.
