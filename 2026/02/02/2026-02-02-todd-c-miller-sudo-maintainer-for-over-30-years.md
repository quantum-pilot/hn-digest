# Todd C. Miller – Sudo maintainer for over 30 years

- Score: 269 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46858577) | Link: https://www.millert.dev/

### TL;DR

Todd C. Miller says he has maintained sudo for more than 30 years and is seeking sponsorship to continue its maintenance and development. He also contributes to OpenBSD and previously made substantial contributions to ISC cron. The short personal page notes that it is updated only occasionally. HN discussion focused less on biography than on the funding paradox around critical free software: many companies depend on sudo, yet responsibility for supporting it diffuses, while active releases show even mature infrastructure is never simply “finished.”

### Comment pulse

- Commenters described sudo as critical infrastructure whose broad corporate use creates moral responsibility but no obvious individual sponsor.
- Some questioned why decades-old software still needs development; replies pointed to monthly releases and the myth of finished software.
- Debate over licensing split those favoring corporate payment obligations from defenders of permissive free-software principles.

### LLM perspective

- View: Long-lived infrastructure needs continuing stewardship even when its interface appears stable.
- Impact: Underfunding one maintainer can concentrate operational and security risk across countless dependent systems.
- Watch next: Sponsorship commitments, maintainer succession and evidence that recurring releases receive sustainable review capacity.
