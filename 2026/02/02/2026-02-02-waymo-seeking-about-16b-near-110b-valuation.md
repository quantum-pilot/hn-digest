# Waymo seeking about $16B near $110B valuation

- Score: 187 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46856854) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-31/waymo-seeking-about-16-billion-near-110-billion-valuation

### TL;DR

Bloomberg reports that Waymo is seeking about $16 billion at a valuation near $110 billion, citing unnamed people familiar with private discussions. Alphabet would supply roughly $13 billion, with the balance expected from outside investors including Sequoia Capital, DST Global and Dragoneer; a February close was possible. Waymo declined to discuss financing, saying it has completed over 20 million trips. The company already offers paid, fully driverless rides in six US cities and plans broader US and UK expansion amid competition from Tesla and Zoox.
