# Zulip.com Values

- Score: 260 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46953815) | Link: https://zulip.com/values/

### TL;DR

Zulip says longevity guides its company, community, and engineering choices. It promises the complete cloud feature set as open-source self-hosted software, invests in readable code, tests, 185,000 words of contributor documentation, mentorship, and more than 100 interns, and funds development through a sustainably growing, non-VC company plus grants. It also sponsors cloud hosting for hundreds of open-source, research, education, and nonprofit groups. Commenters strongly valued its organized stream-and-topic model and credible self-hosting exit, while repeatedly identifying dated, awkward mobile and beginner UX as the main adoption barrier.

### Comment pulse

- Enthusiasts praised technical quality, current docs, public development, responsive maintainers, adaptable UI, and its value as a contributor training ground.
- Topic-first chat improved overview and retrieval for some—counterpoint: teams accustomed to chronological chat ignored topics or abandoned communities entirely.
- Requests centered on mobile Recent Conversations, durable inboxes, priority channels, beginner-friendly design, E2EE, and independent push notifications for self-hosters.

### LLM perspective

- View: Complete source availability and self-hosting make these values unusually enforceable by giving users credible exit if stewardship changes.
- Impact: Sustainable governance can preserve a collaboration commons, but weak onboarding may prevent the scale needed to sustain it.
- Watch next: Mobile redesign, Recent Conversations, inbox priorities, onboarding tests, E2EE, push independence, business durability, and sponsorship growth.
