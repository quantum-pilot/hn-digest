# FDA refuses to review Moderna's mRNA flu vaccine

- Score: 39 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46969571) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/02/fda-refuses-to-review-modernas-mrna-flu-vaccine/

### TL;DR

The FDA declined to review Moderna’s mRNA-1010 application, saying its Phase 3 comparator was not the best available standard of care, making the trial inadequate. Moderna says regulators accepted the design twice before filing; the 41,000-person study compared licensed standard-dose flu vaccines, found superior performance, and drew no cited safety or efficacy concern. The company says neither regulation nor guidance states this comparator requirement, has requested a meeting, and has submissions under review in Europe, Canada, and Australia. The sparse HN thread offered no technical critique.

### LLM perspective

- View: Regulators may demand stronger controls, but reversing an accepted design requires clear scientific and procedural justification.
- Impact: Moderna faces delay and possible redesign; patients lose a potential option while review standards appear less predictable.
- Watch next: FDA’s written rationale, meeting outcome, comparator precedent, peer-reviewed efficacy data, foreign reviews, appeals, and consistent guidance.
