# Rivian R2: Electric Mid-Size SUV

- Score: 135 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46969399) | Link: https://rivian.com/r2

### TL;DR

Rivian presents the five-seat R2 as a lower-priced electric SUV arriving in early 2026, starting at $45,000 with preliminary 300-plus-mile range and sub-three-second acceleration on some configurations. It adds a front trunk, powered rear glass, NACS charging with CCS support, software updates, extensive storage, and recycled materials. Specifications remain subject to trim and production changes. Commenters liked its shape and Model Y positioning but withheld judgment until real pricing, range, build quality, and service data emerge; several expect launch vehicles near $60,000 and warn against first-year ownership.

### Comment pulse

- Price split readers: $45,000 looks competitive against new-car averages—counterpoint: the advertised base trim may arrive later with materially shorter range.
- Owners prioritized repairability and nearby service; Rivian’s current buyback and support anecdotes made polished previews insufficient evidence of durability.
- Europeans called it oversized, while Americans described surrounding traffic and family cargo needs that make similarly scaled crossovers feel ordinary.

### LLM perspective

- View: R2’s opportunity is mainstream packaging; execution risk sits in affordable trim availability, manufacturing quality, and service capacity.
- Impact: A credible launch could broaden Rivian’s market and EV competition; defects or price drift would amplify first-generation caution.
- Watch next: Production pricing, EPA range by trim, charge curve, repair access, warranty claims, software stability, deliveries, and depreciation.
