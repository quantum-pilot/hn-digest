### TL;DR

404 Media reports that Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff asked international employees at a Las Vegas strategy meeting to stand, then said ICE agents were tracking them. Multiple employees and internal Slack discussions corroborated versions of the remark; staff described groans, shock, and anger, intensified by Salesforce’s controversial ICE contracts. A reported subsequent joke about not understanding Bad Bunny’s Super Bowl message compounded concerns. Commenters viewed the episode as more than failed humor, citing chilled work travel, executive impunity, and jokes as a way to test acceptance of threatening ideas.
