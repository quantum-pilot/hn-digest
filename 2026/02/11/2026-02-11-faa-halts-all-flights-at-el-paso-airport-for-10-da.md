# FAA Halts All Flights at El Paso Airport for 10 Days

- Score: 124 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46972610) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/11/us/faa-el-paso-flight-restrictions.html

### TL;DR

The FAA abruptly closed El Paso airspace to 18,000 feet for a planned 10 days, then lifted the order within eight hours. Federal officials blamed a Mexican cartel drone the military neutralized. Four briefed sources instead said the Pentagon deployed new laser counter-drone technology before the FAA completed a safety review; an earlier target was reportedly a party balloon. Local leaders received no warning, and medical flights diverted. Commenters initially inferred military escalation from the restriction’s shape, but later reporting shifted the discussion toward interagency dysfunction and unanswered coordination failures.

### Comment pulse

- Some mapped the trapezoidal restriction to strikes or MANPAD threats—counterpoint: 18,000 feet is also the ordinary boundary of Class A airspace.
- The ten-day duration and absent medical exemptions fueled alarm, while unaffected nearby airports complicated theories about a broad regional threat.
- As closure vanished, mundane coordination failure displaced conspiracies: Pentagon testing proceeded before aviation reviewers had enough timing, location, and effects data.
