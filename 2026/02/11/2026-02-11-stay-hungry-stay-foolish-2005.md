# Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish (2005)

- Score: 32 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46971248) | Link: https://stevejobsarchive.com/stories/stay-hungry-stay-foolish

### TL;DR

Two decades after Steve Jobs’s Stanford commencement address, his archive released an enhanced video and preparation story for a 15-minute talk viewed over 120 million times. The college dropout spent six months collecting notes, ignored Apple PR suggestions, and wrote with Laurene Powell Jobs before reading the text verbatim. Its three personal stories foreground love, death, fear, authenticity, and hope rather than technology. Commenters debated whether its closing injunction encourages endless craving or sustaining curiosity, and whether Jobs’s unorthodox path offers a model or simply illustrates survivor bias.
