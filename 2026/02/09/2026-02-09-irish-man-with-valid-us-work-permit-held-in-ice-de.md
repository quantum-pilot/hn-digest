# Irish man with valid US work permit held in ICE detention for five months

- Score: 205 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46948923) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/feb/09/irish-man-seamus-culleton-ice-detention

### TL;DR

Seamus Culleton, an Irish national who overstayed a 2009 visa-waiver entry, has spent five months in ICE custody despite a current work authorization, pending marriage-based green-card application, US-citizen wife, business, and no criminal record. Detention made him miss his final residency interview; a judge approved bond, yet ICE kept him after producing disputed deportation signatures Culleton says were forged. He reports squalor, insufficient food, and untreated illness. Commenters condemned detention conditions and due-process failures, while others stressed that work authorization did not erase his underlying removability.
