# Offpunk 3.0

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46943752) | Link: https://ploum.net/2026-02-09-offpunk3.html

### TL;DR

Offpunk 3.0 expands the terminal browser for Web, Gemini, and Gopher, caching visited pages and queueing unavailable ones for later synchronization. The release marks a shift toward community development and adds localization, the Openk file opener, terminal XKCD, rule-based article extraction with Readability fallback, email sharing and replies, imported login cookies, larger images, feed discovery, blocked-link visibility, themes, and stricter network-level blocking. Early users found its go/open/full-view workflow unfamiliar but intriguing; discussion also explored shareable caches, Gemini’s limitations, and a misreading of human contributions as AI-generated code.

### Comment pulse

- A Debian tester found navigation surprising: go renders cached text, open launches externally, and full view recovers content stripped by extraction heuristics.
- The author clarified that reduced personal review celebrated trusted human contributors, not unreviewed AI output.
- Users proposed downloadable cache bundles; plain files make sharing easy, while Kiwix already serves prepackaged offline archives.

### LLM perspective

- View: Its differentiator is not terminal rendering alone, but deliberate intermittent connectivity backed by transparent, inspectable local storage.
- Impact: Travelers and low-connectivity users gain resilient reading; unfamiliar commands and manual rule setup remain onboarding costs.
- Watch next: Automated extraction-rule setup in 3.1, regressions, translation growth, cache distribution, cookie safety, and usability beyond enthusiasts.
