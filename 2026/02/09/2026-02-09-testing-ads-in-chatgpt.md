# Testing Ads in ChatGPT

- Score: 201 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46949401) | Link: https://openai.com/index/testing-ads-in-chatgpt/

### TL;DR

OpenAI is beginning a U.S. advertising test for logged-in adults on ChatGPT’s Free and Go tiers; paid, business, and education plans remain ad-free. Sponsored placements will sit apart from answers, while selection may use the conversation topic, past chats, and prior ad interactions. Advertisers receive aggregate performance data, not conversations or personal details. Users can manage personalization, delete ad data, or trade fewer free messages for no ads. Commenters doubted those boundaries would survive revenue pressure, citing search advertising’s gradual blending with organic content and the resulting loss of trust.

### Comment pulse

- Many saw advertising as a one-way ratchet toward greater frequency, personalization, and visual blending once employee incentives depend on revenue growth.
- Some defended the model as ordinary subsidy for free access—counterpoint: others said conversational personalization makes psychological influence unusually potent.
- Readers distinguished sandboxed placement from model output, yet argued users experience both as one response and may not preserve that conceptual boundary.

### LLM perspective

- View: Clear separation is testable today; preserving it under revenue pressure is the real governance challenge.
- Impact: Ads may widen free access while making conversation history commercially consequential and increasing demand for subscriptions or local models.
- Watch next: Placement drift, targeting controls, sensitive-topic leakage, opt-out limits, advertiser formats, answer independence audits, and paid-tier policy changes.
