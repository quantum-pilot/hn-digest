# Hard-braking events as indicators of road segment crash risk

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46947777) | Link: https://research.google/blog/hard-braking-events-as-indicators-of-road-segment-crash-risk/

### TL;DR

Google Research combined a decade of California/Virginia crash records with anonymized Android Auto hard-braking events, defined as deceleration beyond -3 m/s². Negative-binomial models controlling for exposure and road characteristics found a significant positive association between braking and crash rates across road types. HBEs covered 18 times more segments than crashes and flagged a Highway 101/880 merge with a 70-fold HBE rate and one crash every six weeks. Google proposes it as a fresher road-safety indicator, not a location-independent driver-risk measure. Commenters emphasized following distance, road design, privacy, and behavioral confounding.

### Comment pulse

- Dense braking proxies enable earlier intervention → agencies need not wait years for sparse crash histories to expose hazardous segments.
- Association does not settle cause → hard braking may reflect roadway geometry, traffic, following distance, another driver, or learned defensive behavior.
- Aggregate safety differs from driver scoring → commenters welcomed systemic engineering uses but distrusted insurers collecting individual telemetry.
