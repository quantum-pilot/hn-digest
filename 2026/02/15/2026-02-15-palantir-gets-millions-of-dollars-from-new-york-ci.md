# Palantir Gets Millions of Dollars from New York City's Public Hospitals

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47025624) | Link: https://theintercept.com/2026/02/15/palantir-contract-new-york-city-health-hospitals/

### TL;DR

New York City Health and Hospitals has paid Palantir nearly $4 million since 2023 to improve Medicaid and public-benefit billing, including automated scans of clinical notes for missed charges. The contract permits work with protected health information and, with hospital-system approval, reuse of de-identified data outside research; Palantir says it neither uses nor shares data beyond the contract. Activists fear re-identification and conflict with Palantir's ICE work, while HN commenters asked whether the software delivers measurable returns and what comparable alternatives exist.
