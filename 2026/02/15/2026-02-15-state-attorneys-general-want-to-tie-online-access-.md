# State Attorneys General Want to Tie Online Access to ID

- Score: 101 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47026848) | Link: https://reclaimthenet.org/40-attorneys-general-back-ids-online-safety-act

### TL;DR

Forty state and territorial attorneys general urged Congress to adopt Senate bill S. 1748 rather than House bill H.R. 6484. The Senate approach creates an FTC-enforced duty of care for harms to minors, preserves more state authority, and orders Commerce, FTC, and FCC research into device- or operating-system-level age verification. The article argues such infrastructure could tie lawful browsing to persistent identity credentials and chill anonymous speech. HN commenters favored voluntary child-safe devices or content labels, while others viewed identity surveillance as the measure's purpose.

### Comment pulse

- Opt-in child profiles could meet much of the safety goal → counterpoint: a child flag itself could become a targeting signal.
- Content-side age labels preserve family control → granular ratings avoid requiring every user to identify themselves before access.
- Critics fear remote attestation and closed computing → open operating systems could struggle if services demand approved age tokens.
