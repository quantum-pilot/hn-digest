# Palantir vs. the "Republik": US analytics firm takes magazine to court

- Score: 190 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47025188) | Link: https://www.heise.de/en/news/Palantir-vs-the-Republik-US-analytics-firm-takes-magazine-to-court-11176508.html

### TL;DR

Palantir sued Swiss online magazine Republik after it refused to publish a requested counterstatement concerning reports, based largely on government files, about the company's unsuccessful outreach to Swiss military, police, and health authorities. Palantir alleges significant inaccuracies but did not tell Heise which corrections it seeks; Republik stands by its research. Under Swiss law, the court considers whether an alternative factual account could be published, not which account is true. The case amplified the original reporting and brought the small, subscriber-owned outlet donations and solidarity.
