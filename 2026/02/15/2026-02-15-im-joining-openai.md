# I’m joining OpenAI

- Score: 347 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47028013) | Link: https://steipete.me/posts/2026/openclaw

### TL;DR

Peter Steinberger is joining OpenAI to make consumer agents broadly usable, while planning to move OpenClaw into an independent open-source foundation. He says building another large company is less appealing than gaining access to frontier research and distribution; OpenAI will sponsor the project, support continued work, and preserve multi-model ambitions and user data ownership. HN discussion split between excitement over the agent harness and concern that rapid adoption rewarded weak security practices, shifted risk to self-hosting users, and may obscure foundational contributions from smaller dependencies.
