# Discord distances from age verification firm after ties to Peter Thiel surface

- Score: 204 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47021421) | Link: https://kotaku.com/discord-palantir-peter-thiel-persona-age-verification-2000668951

### TL;DR

Discord says its use of Persona for age verification was a limited test that has ended, after scrutiny of the vendor’s backing by Peter Thiel’s Founders Fund and links to identity and anti-fraud services. The clarification does not alter Discord’s plan for a broader global age-assurance rollout tied to the UK Online Safety Act. Although Discord says Persona held data for seven days, users remain wary after a previous third-party incident exposed roughly 70,000 identity documents.

### Comment pulse

- Many saw the statement as damage control because Discord still intends to verify ages, regardless of which contractor handles it.
- Commenters argued identity source material should never be retained and questioned Discord’s ability to vet vendors securely.
- Migration remains difficult when a social platform’s network effects keep communities and friends in place.
