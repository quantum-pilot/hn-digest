# Hideki Sato, designer of all Sega's consoles, has died

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47024907) | Link: https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/hideki-sato-designer-of-segas-consoles-dies-age-75/

### TL;DR

Hideki Sato, the Sega hardware leader associated with every company console from the Master System through the Dreamcast, has died at 77. Joining Sega in 1971, he rose through research and development and later served as acting president. His teams translated arcade expertise into home machines, from the Mega Drive’s cost-conscious 16-bit design to the Dreamcast’s modem, VMU, and communication ambitions. Commenters celebrated that technical legacy while correcting the headline’s implication: Sato oversaw later console programs rather than personally designing every machine.

### Comment pulse

- Dreamcast owners remembered its modem and VMU as ideas that arrived before broadband and online console services became commonplace.
- Technical discussion praised Sega’s unconventional architectures and the emulation work needed to preserve them.
- Some blamed Dreamcast piracy for Sega’s exit; others argued hardware economics and competition were more consequential.
