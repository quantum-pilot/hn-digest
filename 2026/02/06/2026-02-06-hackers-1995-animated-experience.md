# Hackers (1995) Animated Experience

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46912800) | Link: https://hackers-1995.vercel.app/

### TL;DR

David Vidovic’s browser tribute to Hackers (1995) opens with an optional soundtrack and a navigable “mainframe.” Desktop visitors move, rotate, fly, look, and interact by keyboard and mouse; mobile users get movement and look sticks plus two-finger vertical flight. A music player, leaderboard, interactive “Garbage” target, timed copying sequence, and completion alias add game-like structure. Commenters responded with intense nostalgia: the film and soundtrack inspired software careers, friendships, and community identity, while some learned to love its artistic unreality and others used the tribute to remember lost friends.
