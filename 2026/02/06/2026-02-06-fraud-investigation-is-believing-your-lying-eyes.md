# Fraud investigation is believing your lying eyes

- Score: 122 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46915587) | Link: https://www.bitsaboutmoney.com/archive/fraud-investigation/

### TL;DR

Patrick McKenzie argues fraud detection should treat schemes as repeatable businesses, not isolated applicants. Using Minnesota childcare cases, he recommends checking observable operations against claims, mapping repeat actors and shared providers, scrutinizing implausible growth, identity recruitment, weak financial intermediaries, and peripheral paperwork, then using adaptive models and proportionate prepayment review. He says easy real-world proof should outweigh low-quality forms and conviction-only thresholds. Commenters disputed his reading of a 2019 auditor report and warned that politically charged investigations can stigmatize communities; McKenzie maintained he accurately reported investigators’ beliefs.
