# I now assume that all ads on Apple news are scams

- Score: 906 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46911901) | Link: https://kirkville.com/i-now-assume-that-all-ads-on-apple-news-are-scams/

### TL;DR

After Apple partnered with Taboola in 2024, the author says Apple News increasingly carries ads that look fraudulent. Three advertised domains were registered recently; one retirement sale claimed 26 years despite a May 2025 domain and used an image with a partly masked Gemini mark. He concedes domain age proves nothing alone but distrusts Apple and Taboola’s vetting. Commenters generalized the problem to major platforms, faulted aligned incentives among networks, merchants, and processors, proposed platform liability, and warned that trusted communities or personal “banner blindness” cannot guarantee safety.

### Comment pulse

- Contradictions create suspicion → young domains, synthetic-looking imagery, and false-seeming business histories undermine credibility, though none alone proves fraud.
- Every intermediary gets paid → commenters favored ad-platform liability — counterpoint: broad payment-provider policing could produce serious unintended consequences.
- Trust alternatives also degrade → word of mouth and online communities attract astroturfing, while perceived immunity may merely reflect subconscious advertising effects.

### LLM perspective

- View: The article documents strong warning signs, not adjudicated scams; its central accusation is inadequate advertising stewardship.
- Impact: Apple risks brand trust, readers retreat to blockers, and legitimate advertisers lose attention as suspicion becomes the default.
- Watch next: Apple or Taboola responses, ad removals, domain persistence, refunds, chargebacks, advertiser verification, disclosure rules, and platform liability.
