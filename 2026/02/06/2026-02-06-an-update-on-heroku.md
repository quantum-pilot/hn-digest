# An Update on Heroku

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46913903) | Link: https://www.heroku.com/blog/an-update-on-heroku/

### TL;DR

Heroku says it is moving to sustaining engineering, prioritizing stability, security, reliability, and support over new features. Dashboard credit-card customers can continue with unchanged pricing, billing, service, applications, pipelines, teams, and add-ons. New Enterprise Account contracts will stop, while existing enterprise subscriptions and support may renew; investment shifts toward enterprise AI. Commenters read this as an end-of-sales precursor and urged migration. Former employees offered a broader postmortem: scaling debt, reliability crises, lost product leadership, sales incentives, premium pricing, and later Salesforce disinvestment—not the acquisition alone—eroded competitiveness.

### Comment pulse

- Sustaining signals managed decline → commenters expect low staffing, bit rot, and eventual end-of-life despite unchanged service today.
- Acquisition enabled Heroku’s growth → Salesforce funded expansion — counterpoint: later disinvestment plus internal scaling, leadership, and incentive failures eroded competitiveness.
- Migration urgency varies → no immediate customer change was announced, but long-lived workloads need alternatives before support or ecosystem quality deteriorates.

### LLM perspective

- View: The notice defines development posture and enterprise sales changes but omits staffing, roadmap, and end-of-life timelines.
- Impact: Customers retain continuity while absorbing growing opportunity costs from feature stagnation, uncertain integrations, and eventual migration planning.
- Watch next: Security commitments, enterprise renewals, capacity investment, add-on health, migration guidance, staffing signals, and formal end-of-sale milestones.
