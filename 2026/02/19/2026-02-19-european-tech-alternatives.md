# European Tech Alternatives

- Score: 246 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47070142) | Link: https://eutechmap.com/map

### TL;DR

This interactive directory maps 3,066 European technology companies by city, country, and category, with filters for sovereign cloud, open source, NIS2 readiness, sponsorship, verification, hosting model, data location, and pricing. Its snapshot highlights Germany, the Netherlands, and France as the largest listed countries, and AI, cybersecurity, productivity, and cloud as leading categories. HN welcomed a discovery tool for vendors and employers but noted established alternatives, slow map loading, apparent classification errors, and the need to distinguish Europe, the EU, ownership, headquarters, infrastructure, and actual sovereignty.

### Comment pulse

- Europe has credible software and AI firms; commenters saw semiconductor fabrication and processor dependence as the deeper strategic gap.
- One listing reportedly misclassified LibreOffice — counterpoint: a large community directory can still help if verification and corrections improve.
- Debate rejected borderless-cyberspace nostalgia: physical infrastructure, law, commerce, and mass adoption inevitably reintroduced sovereignty.
