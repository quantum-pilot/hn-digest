# How AI is affecting productivity and jobs in Europe

- Score: 162 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47068320) | Link: https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/how-ai-affecting-productivity-and-jobs-europe

### TL;DR

A study of more than 12,000 European firms estimates that AI adoption raises labour-productivity levels by 4% on average, with no detectable short-run employment loss and higher wages at adopting firms. The authors use matched US adoption patterns as an instrument to reduce selection bias and stress that this is a one-time level effect, not annual growth. Gains concentrate in medium and large firms and rise with software, data, and training investment, implying that finance and integration capacity matter more than simply acquiring AI.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** AI appears complementary where firms can reorganize work; adoption statistics alone obscure the capability needed to capture gains.
- **Impact:** Training-intensive returns may widen productivity and wage gaps between large adopters and resource-constrained small firms.
- **Watch next:** Long-run employment by skill, wage distribution, small-firm adoption, and whether estimated multipliers survive alternative AI measures.
