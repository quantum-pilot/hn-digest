# IRS lost 40% of IT staff, 80% of tech leaders in 'efficiency' shakeup

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47077849) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/2026/02/19/irs_job_cuts/

### TL;DR

IRS CIO Kaschit Pandya said the agency lost about 40% of its IT staff and nearly 80% of technology executives during the 2025 federal shakeup, prompting a reorganization around cross-functional delivery teams. However, inspector-general counts fell from 8,504 IT workers in October 2024 to 7,135 a year later, about 16%, while roughly 1,000 technologists were temporarily assigned to frontline tax-season work. The inspector general warned staffing losses put 2026 filing-season changes at risk; Pandya acknowledged the restructuring had not yet produced better outcomes and expects AI to help.

### Comment pulse
