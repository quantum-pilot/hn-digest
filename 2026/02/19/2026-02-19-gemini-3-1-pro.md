# Gemini 3.1 Pro

- Score: 596 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47075318) | Link: https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-3-1-pro/

### TL;DR

Google describes Gemini 3.1 Pro as its most advanced complex-task model, accepting text, images, audio, and video across a one-million-token context and producing up to 64K tokens. Company benchmarks show large gains over Gemini 3 Pro on ARC-AGI-2, terminal work, agent workflows, search, and competitive coding, while SWE-Bench is near peers, MMMU-Pro slips slightly, and one-million-token retrieval remains 26.3%. Automated safety changes are small; frontier tests stay below critical-capability levels despite a cyber alert threshold and stronger situational awareness.

### Comment pulse

- A developer reported materially better recall across a 200K-token codebase, but still preferred Claude for tightly constrained code generation.
- SVG results varied sharply between users — counterpoint: isolated demos cannot establish general capability.
- Availability through subscription-based Gemini CLI remained unclear to commenters.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Benchmark gains are broad, but harnesses, tools, and self-reported settings complicate vendor comparisons.
- **Impact:** Large-context agent workflows may improve most, while developers still need task-specific model selection.
- **Watch next:** Independent evaluations, pricing, latency, CLI rollout, and long-context reliability beyond retrieval tests.
