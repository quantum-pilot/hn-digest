# California's new bill requires DOJ-approved 3D printers that report themselves

- Score: 224 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47077844) | Link: https://blog.adafruit.com/2026/02/19/californias-new-bill-requires-doj-approved-3d-printers-that-report-on-themselves/

### TL;DR

California Assembly Bill 2047, introduced February 17, would prohibit selling or transferring unapproved 3D printers after March 1, 2029. Approval would require manufacturer attestations and Justice Department certification of “firearm blocking technology”; knowingly bypassing it would be a misdemeanor, and violations could bring civil penalties up to $25,000. The article argues geometry cannot reliably reveal intended use, ordinary printers lack suitable processing, and open firmware defeats blocking. Commenters likewise framed the proposal as technically unenforceable surveillance of general-purpose tools.

### Comment pulse

- Ambiguous parts, printer kits, user networking, and printers capable of reproducing printers expose an endless enforcement problem.
- Gun-law context polarized discussion — counterpoint: even participants favoring regulation questioned controls that burden every lawful maker.
