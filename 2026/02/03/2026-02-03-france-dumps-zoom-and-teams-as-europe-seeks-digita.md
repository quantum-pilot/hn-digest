# France dumps Zoom and Teams as Europe seeks digital autonomy from the US

- Score: 717 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46873294) | Link: https://apnews.com/article/europe-digital-sovereignty-big-tech-9f5388b68a0648514cebc8d92f682060

### TL;DR

France plans to move 2.5 million civil servants from Zoom, Teams, Webex, and GoTo Meeting to its Visio service by 2027, citing confidentiality and dependence on non-European providers. The shift joins Austria’s adoption of LibreOffice and Schleswig-Holstein’s migration of 44,000 inboxes toward open alternatives. Momentum comes from privacy, competitiveness, U.S. political pressure, and fear that sanctions could disable critical services. Microsoft says its European cloud options keep data under European law. Commenters welcomed funded open software but noted sovereignty also requires deployable infrastructure, maintenance, support, and avoiding new single-vendor dependence.
