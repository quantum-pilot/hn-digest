# I guess I kinda get why people hate AI

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47037628) | Link: https://anthony.noided.media/blog/ai/programming/2026/02/14/i-guess-i-kinda-get-why-people-hate-ai.html

### TL;DR

A programmer who benefits from AI now understands the backlash: labs market job destruction to executives and investors while offering little concrete preparation for displaced workers. Meanwhile ordinary users encounter effortless cheating, fabricated videos, algorithmic slop, hallucinated bug reports, higher hardware costs, and invasive proposed fixes such as identity checks. The author remains optimistic about AI’s usefulness but urges automatic labor-policy triggers, generation watermarking, stronger misinformation controls, and protections for open-source maintainers before public hostility grows around a technology whose current harms often arrive before its benefits.
