# Show HN: Jemini – Gemini for the Epstein Files

- Score: 227 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47031334) | Link: https://jmail.world/jemini

### TL;DR

Jemini adds a conversational search interface to the Jmail archive, offering prompts for flight records, emails, court documents, and Amazon purchases within a wider suite of file-browsing apps. The interface warns that answers may be wrong and should be checked. HN reaction was enthusiastic about AI-assisted investigation and the surrounding Jmail, Jamazon, and source-linking work, but readers raised a central trust issue: some displayed emails lacked links to disclosed source files, leaving authenticity and verification labels difficult to assess.

### Comment pulse

- Search can surface connections across heterogeneous records → readers saw investigative value beyond manually navigating separate archives.
- Provenance must accompany every result → missing source links and unexplained verification labels make injected or reconstructed material indistinguishable.
- Context invites speculative interpretations → ordinary purchases and ambiguous terms prompted disturbing inferences unsupported by the interface alone.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Retrieval is useful only when every claim resolves to inspectable evidence and transformations are clearly labeled.
- **Impact:** Journalists gain speed, while maintainers inherit high-stakes provenance, correction, and abuse-review obligations.
- **Watch next:** Citation coverage, false-answer reports, provenance labels, and reproducible paths from generated claims to source documents.
