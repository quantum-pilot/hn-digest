### TL;DR

Robert Duvall died at home at 95, leaving a six-decade career defined by meticulous observation and disappearing into supporting and leading roles. His portrayal of a recovering country singer in Tender Mercies earned his only Oscar among seven nominations; other landmarks included To Kill a Mockingbird, The Godfather films, Apocalypse Now, The Great Santini, Network, The Apostle, and Lonesome Dove, his personal favorite. HN readers remembered less-celebrated performances too, emphasizing how convincingly he inhabited detectives, mentors, cowboys, and ordinary tradesmen.
