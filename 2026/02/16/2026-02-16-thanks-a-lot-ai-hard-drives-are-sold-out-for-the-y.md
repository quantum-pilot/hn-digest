# Thanks a lot, AI: Hard drives are sold out for the year, says WD

- Score: 332 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47034192) | Link: https://mashable.com/article/ai-hard-drive-hdd-shortages-western-digital-sold-out

### TL;DR

Western Digital says nearly all its 2026 hard-drive capacity is committed, chiefly to seven large customers, with three already holding agreements into 2027 or 2028. Enterprise demand, attributed to AI infrastructure, now dwarfs a consumer segment worth only 5% of company revenue, adding storage pressure to existing RAM and processor shortages. HN commenters expected higher prices but split on duration: subsidized demand could sustain scarcity, while a buildout slowdown might create a used-hardware glut; manufacturers hesitate to add costly factories for a possible temporary spike.
