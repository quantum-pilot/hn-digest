# 14-year-old Miles Wu folded origami pattern that holds 10k times its own weight

- Score: 337 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47038546) | Link: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/this-14-year-old-is-using-origami-to-design-emergency-shelters-that-are-sturdy-cost-efficient-and-easy-to-deploy-180988179/

### TL;DR

Fourteen-year-old Miles Wu spent more than 250 hours testing 54 Miura-ori variants across three paper stocks, using 108 trials to measure compression strength. His best 64-square-inch folded sheet supported over 200 pounds—more than 10,000 times its own weight—and won a $25,000 junior science prize. Wu hopes the geometry could inform compact emergency shelters, but an engineer and HN commenters stress that full-scale structures introduce nonlinear scaling, joints, buckling, multidirectional loads, weatherproofing, and durability that paper compression tests do not resolve.
