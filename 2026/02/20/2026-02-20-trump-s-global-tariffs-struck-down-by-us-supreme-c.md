# Trump's global tariffs struck down by US Supreme Court

- Score: 1172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47089213) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c0l9r67drg7t

### TL;DR

The US Supreme Court ruled 6–3 that the International Emergency Economic Powers Act did not authorize President Trump’s emergency import taxes, invalidating the Liberation Day tariffs and certain Canada, Mexico, and fentanyl-related duties. The decision did not eliminate tariffs imposed under other statutes. Trump immediately announced a 10 percent global tariff under Section 122, which permits up to 15 percent for 150 days without congressional action, and signaled further Section 232 and 301 measures. Refund eligibility and distribution remain unresolved.

### Comment pulse

- Many welcome a constraint requiring Congress or established procedures; counterpoint: alternate statutory authorities let the administration preserve much of its tariff strategy.
- Importers paid roughly $130 billion and may pursue refunds, but commenters expect consumers who absorbed higher prices will recover little directly.
