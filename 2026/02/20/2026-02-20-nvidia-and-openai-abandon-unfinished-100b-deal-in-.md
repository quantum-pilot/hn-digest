# Nvidia and OpenAI abandon unfinished $100B deal in favour of $30B investment

- Score: 282 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47086980) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/dea24046-0a73-40b2-8246-5ac7b7a54323

### TL;DR

The Financial Times reports Nvidia is nearing a $30 billion equity investment in OpenAI, replacing an unfinished September 2025 framework that contemplated up to $100 billion across staged investments tied to 10 gigawatts of deployment. That earlier arrangement never advanced beyond a memorandum of understanding. The new check would join a fundraising round exceeding $100 billion at a reported $730 billion pre-money valuation, alongside possible investments from SoftBank, Amazon, Microsoft, and MGX, as OpenAI projects enormous computing expenditure through 2030.
