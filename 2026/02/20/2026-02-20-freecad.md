# FreeCAD

- Score: 385 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47047010) | Link: https://www.freecad.org/index.php

### TL;DR

FreeCAD presents itself as a free, open-source, cross-platform parametric 3D modeler for mechanical design, architecture, manufacturing drawings, simulation, CAM, and hobby projects. Its history-based workflow, broad format support, Python and C++ extensibility, documentation, add-ons, and lack of licensing or vendor lock-in are the core pitch. Commenters say recent 1.0 and 1.1-era improvements, including the topological-naming fix, have made switching from commercial CAD realistic, though crashes, dense icons, solver quirks, and a steep learning curve remain.

### Comment pulse
