# Facebook is cooked

- Score: 593 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47091748) | Link: https://pilk.website/3/facebook-is-absolutely-cooked

### TL;DR

After returning to Facebook after roughly eight years, the author found one followed-page post followed by ten unsolicited posts dominated by sexualized, often AI-generated women, synthetic sentimentality, and crude engagement bait. The author cannot know whether this reflects inactive-account defaults, inferred gender, or the platform broadly, but stopped after seeing apparently underage-looking synthetic images. Commenters reported sharply different feeds: some corroborated persistent thirst traps and rage bait, while active users described useful, friend-centered communities shaped by careful posting and pruning.

### Comment pulse

- Several male-identified users said liking other topics barely displaced sexualized recommendations.
- Others still get schools, hobbies, friends, and travel — counterpoint: Facebook remains indispensable infrastructure in parts of Africa and the Philippines.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Generative media lets engagement systems manufacture infinite personalized bait instead of merely ranking human posts.
- **Impact:** Default recommendations can shape vulnerable or inactive users before meaningful preference signals exist.
- **Watch next:** Better provenance, age-safety enforcement, and independent audits of demographic recommendation differences.
