# Wikipedia deprecates Archive.today, starts removing archive links

- Score: 238 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47092006) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/02/wikipedia-bans-archive-today-after-site-executed-ddos-and-altered-web-captures/

### TL;DR

English Wikipedia editors reached consensus to deprecate and blacklist Archive.today after evidence that its CAPTCHA code directed users’ computers into a DDoS attack and that archived snapshots had been altered during a dispute with a blogger. Editors plan to remove or replace more than 695,000 links across roughly 400,000 pages, using live originals or other archives where possible. The decision separates Archive.today from the unrelated Internet Archive and rests chiefly on reader security and the loss of confidence that preserved pages faithfully match their sources.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters broadly accept that DDoS code and retroactive snapshot changes destroy archival trust; counterpoint: some suspect a coordinated campaign against a useful service.
- Alternatives include other archives, self-hosting, or a Wikimedia service, though cost, scale, paywalls, and institutional trust complicate each option.
