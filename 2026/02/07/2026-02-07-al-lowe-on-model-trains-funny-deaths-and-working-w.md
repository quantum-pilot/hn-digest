# Al Lowe on model trains, funny deaths and working with Disney

- Score: 76 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46924790) | Link: https://spillhistorie.no/2026/02/06/interview-with-sierra-veteran-al-lowe/

### TL;DR

Veteran designer Al Lowe ranges beyond Leisure Suit Larry: childhood model railroading taught him hands-on learning, while digitally addressed locomotives unite that hobby with computing; he plays saxophone in big bands and quartets. He recalls making 26 Sierra products in 16 years, turning a layoff into a royalty-based contracting career, building Donald Duck’s Playground without Disney input, and using comic deaths and second-person narration to engage players. He also laments Sierra’s burned archives. Commenters shared game nostalgia, surprise at modern train control, and stories of Lowe’s generous teaching advice.
