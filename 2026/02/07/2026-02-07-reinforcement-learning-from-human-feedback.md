# Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

- Score: 104 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46923463) | Link: https://rlhfbook.com/

### TL;DR

Nathan Lambert’s book offers a gentle introduction to language-model post-training, tracing RLHF’s origins across machine learning, economics, philosophy, and optimal control. It builds from definitions, problem formulation, data collection, and instruction tuning through reward-model training, rejection sampling, policy-gradient reinforcement learning, and direct alignment methods, then covers synthetic data, evaluation, tool use, reasoning, overoptimization, and unresolved research questions. A January 2026 reorganization aligns the online text with its Manning structure, while the author is reportedly preparing a second version and seeking feedback. Discussion surfaced earlier threads and formats.

### LLM perspective

- View: It usefully frames RLHF as a pipeline spanning data, objectives, optimization, and evaluation rather than one algorithm.
- Impact: Learners gain a shared vocabulary and method map; practitioners gain context for choosing among rapidly changing post-training techniques.
