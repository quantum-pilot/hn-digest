# I'm going to cure my girlfriend's brain tumor

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46920248) | Link: https://andrewjrod.substack.com/p/im-going-to-cure-my-girlfriends-brain

### TL;DR

Andrew recounts Amy’s prolactinoma: the first surgery removed 80%, but prolactin remained elevated; a second appeared surgically complete, yet levels again stayed high. Experts disagree about residual tumor but agree it has not spread, so Amy chose cabergoline over a riskier third operation. Andrew is leaving consulting to test whether his biology background, AI-scientist tools, and open data can advance prolactinoma research. Commenters empathized with caregiver exhaustion but split over his cure framing: skeptics feared fixation could eclipse Amy’s agency and present life, while supporters saw a plausible, attention-generating experiment.
