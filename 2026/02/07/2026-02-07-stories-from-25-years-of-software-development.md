# Stories from 25 Years of Software Development

- Score: 120 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46924264) | Link: https://susam.net/twenty-five-years-of-computing.html

### TL;DR

Susam Pal recounts episodes spanning 25 years of programming: learning HTML from View Source, testing an 8086 reset vector, studying PKI after admitting ignorance in an interview, being humbled by a mentor who found a pointer bug in minutes, and discovering that smooth emulator animation failed on real set-top hardware. Later stories show career-changing encouragement and a CTF victory reinterpreted as experience rather than brilliance. The collection disclaims lessons, yet repeatedly shows experimentation, mentorship, humility, and kindness outlasting technologies. Commenters especially recognized fragile Python environments and the emulator-to-device reversal.

### Comment pulse

- The animation episode amused readers: the manufacturer defended an “impossible” claim badly—counterpoint: limited hardware ultimately made acceptable animation impractical.
- Fragile Python installers felt timeless; commenters described version-pinned dependencies, multi-day environment setup, breakable custom tooling, and containers or VM images as containment.
- Readers connected with aging expertise: accomplishments once labeled clever become expected experience, shifting desired recognition toward professionalism and generosity.
