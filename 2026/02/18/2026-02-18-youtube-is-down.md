# YouTube Is Down

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47055723) | Link: https://downdetector.com/status/youtube/

### TL;DR

Users reported a temporary YouTube disruption with inconsistent symptoms: some videos froze after seconds, the website and app failed to load for others, and home-feed or sidebar recommendations disappeared even when search, direct playback, or streams already in progress still worked. Several people initially blamed local browser updates, operating systems, or ad blockers before recognizing a service-side problem. One commenter later said recommendations had returned. The supplied material provides no official incident scope, cause, duration, or confirmation that a public postmortem will follow.

### Comment pulse

- Engineers want a technical postmortem because major YouTube failures are rare, but commenters doubt Google will publish one.
- Losing recommendations left some users unsure what to watch — counterpoint: others recommend the Subscriptions feed for deliberate creator discovery.
