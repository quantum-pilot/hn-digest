# Sizing chaos

- Score: 204 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47066552) | Link: https://pudding.cool/2026/02/womens-sizing/

### TL;DR

Using US waist measurements and charts from 15 apparel brands, the analysis shows women’s sizing stops tracking bodies after adolescence. The median 15-year-old fits roughly ASTM size 10, while the median adult woman fits size 18; most regular ranges stop at 16. Labels vary widely: Large spans 29–34-inch waists, plus sizing can begin anywhere from 12 to 18, and some ranges leave a mid-size gap. With no binding standard or disclosure requirement, the same body receives different labels across brands and garments.

### Comment pulse

- Many blame rising obesity for the size shift — counterpoint: inconsistent labels, diverse body shapes, height, and regional grading remain separate fit problems.
- Commenters question why market demand has not standardized fit; brand exclusivity, vanity sizing, fashion, and poor retailer measurement data may reward inconsistency.
- Detailed measurements outperform labels, but photo-based recommendation systems struggle because users often choose statistical-average bodies instead of supplying their own data.
