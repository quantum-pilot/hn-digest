# Asahi Linux Progress Report: Linux 6.19

- Score: 392 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47059275) | Link: https://asahilinux.org/2026/02/progress-report-6-19/

### TL;DR

Asahi Linux’s 6.19-era update delivers long-awaited USB-C DisplayPort output through the experimental `fairydust` kernel branch, currently limited to one designated port and developer support. New contributors have booted Fedora Plasma on M3 MacBook Air hardware with keyboard, touchpad, Wi-Fi, NVMe, and USB3, but software rendering, display control, speaker safety, and other integrations block release. Meanwhile M1/M2 systems gain 120 Hz internal displays, color and webcam fixes, major GPU-copy speedups, early HDR work, and continued upstreaming of the 858-patch downstream kernel delta.
