# Halt and Catch Fire: TV’s best drama you’ve probably never heard of (2021)

- Score: 680 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47056314) | Link: https://www.sceneandheardnu.com/content/halt-and-catch-fire

### TL;DR

The review argues that *Halt and Catch Fire* became exceptional by abandoning its first-season attempt at a charismatic male antihero. Across four seasons and a decade of fictional computing history, it shifts toward Donna and Cameron’s fraught partnership and an empathetic ensemble repeatedly building, failing, separating, and reconnecting. Technology supplies momentum, but process and relationships outlast every product. HN praised Lee Pace’s charisma, the later character arcs, and the ending, while viewers from the era disagreed over whether small technical inaccuracies spoiled an otherwise evocative portrayal.

### Comment pulse
