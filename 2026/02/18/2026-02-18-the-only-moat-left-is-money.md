# The only moat left is money?

- Score: 226 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47062521) | Link: https://elliotbonneville.com/the-only-moat-left-is-money/

### TL;DR

The author argues AI has made digital creation abundant while human attention remains fixed, shifting the startup bottleneck from building to distribution. New products now compete with a flood of cheap clones across worsening search, social, newsletter, and community channels. Existing momentum compounds, but newcomers need years of audience-building or capital to buy reach, potentially locking them below a self-reinforcing visibility threshold. The essay presents this as an uncertain strategic wager: spending aggressively may be wasteful, yet assuming quality alone will surface could leave entrants permanently invisible.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics say discovery was always entrepreneurship’s hardest part — counterpoint: AI magnifies the familiar risk by multiplying competent-looking supply and clone speed.
- Others defend good ideas, craftsmanship, and creativity as moats because generated competitors often fail to solve real needs or sustain quality.
- Non-scalable local work limits global competition, though automation and excess entrants can eventually disrupt even supposedly durable trades.
