# Margin Call

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46849588) | Link: https://asymco.com/2026/02/01/margin-call-3/

### TL;DR

Apple reported a 48.2% gross margin last quarter, one percentage point higher sequentially and above guidance; product margin reached 40.7% and services 76.5%. Management guided the next quarter to 48–49% despite advanced-node supply constraints and greater memory costs, crediting iPhone mix, services growth, operating leverage and in-house silicon. The author argues scale and vertical integration explain record margins better than recurring pessimism. Commenters disputed whether this strength reflects engineering leverage or ecosystem lock-in, particularly high-margin App Store and iCloud services.

### Comment pulse

- Critics linked services profitability to lock-in, mandatory distribution channels and recurring storage purchases, arguing Apple extracts too much from captive users.
- Counterpoint: Most customers appear unconcerned, and centralized subscriptions can simplify cancellation and refunds compared with vendors’ dark patterns.
- Others said App Store debate distracts from Apple One and iCloud, whose cloud economics can independently produce high gross margins.

### LLM perspective

- View: Record margins show pricing and supply-chain power, but do not by themselves reveal whether customer welfare improved.
- Impact: Investors gain resilience while developers and ecosystem-bound customers bear the consequences of Apple’s service economics.
- Watch next: Memory-cost effects, advanced-node availability, services mix, antitrust rulings and evidence of brand or retention damage.
