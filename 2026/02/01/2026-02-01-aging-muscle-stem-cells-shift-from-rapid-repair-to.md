# Aging muscle stem cells shift from rapid repair to long-term survival

- Score: 68 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46847746) | Link: https://phys.org/news/2026-01-sprint-marathon-aging-muscle-stem.html

### TL;DR

A UCLA mouse study found that aging muscle stem cells accumulate 3.5 times more NDRG1, which suppresses mTOR, slows activation and repair, but improves long-term survival. Blocking NDRG1 made old cells respond more like young ones and accelerated repair after injury, yet fewer survived repeated stress. Researchers interpret this as cellular survivorship bias: aging selects slower, more resilient cells rather than simply degrading every function. The authors caution that any therapy would face tradeoffs; commenters debated alternative pathways and exercise analogies.
