# Amiga Unix (Amix)

- Score: 107 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46845244) | Link: https://www.amigaunix.com/doku.php/home

### TL;DR

Amiga Unix, or AMIX, was Commodore’s 1990 port of AT&T System V Release 4 for the Amiga 2500UX and 3000UX, though compatible machines and WinUAE can also run it. A preservation wiki documents installation, hardware, networking, patches, software, and surviving community resources while bluntly warning that AMIX is obsolete, difficult, insecure, partly closed source, and often broken. Commenters regard it less as a practical system than a curious historical experiment whose generic Unix environment discarded much of the Amiga’s distinctive hardware and software.

### Comment pulse

- Product identity was awkward → replacing AmigaOS and poorly exploiting native hardware made AMIX resemble generic Unix more than an Amiga.
- Contemporary value was still plausible → comparatively inexpensive multiuser Unix could appeal to businesses unable to buy workstation-class systems.
- Preservation remains constrained → closed kernel and libraries ended development after Commodore, leaving enthusiasts hoping source eventually surfaces.
