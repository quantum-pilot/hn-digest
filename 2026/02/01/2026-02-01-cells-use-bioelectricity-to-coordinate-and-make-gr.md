# Cells use 'bioelectricity' to coordinate and make group decisions

- Score: 166 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46842178) | Link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/cells-use-bioelectricity-to-coordinate-and-make-group-decisions-20260112/

### TL;DR

A Nature study found that crowded epithelial tissue begins extrusion with an electrical change. Compression opens pressure-sensitive sodium channels; healthy cells spend energy restoring membrane voltage, while weak or energy-starved cells depolarize, lose water, shrink by at least 17%, and are expelled. The work adds epithelial quality control to bioelectric signaling already observed in neurons, muscles, embryos, plants, and bacterial biofilms. Commenters were intrigued by the energy cost and broader field, but some rejected the article’s “group decision” framing as anthropomorphic sensationalism.
