# FOSDEM 2026 – Open-Source Conference in Brussels – Day#1 Recap

- Score: 174 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46845103) | Link: https://gyptazy.com/blog/fosdem-2026-opensource-conference-brussels/

### TL;DR

A first-day FOSDEM 2026 recap portrays digital sovereignty, self-hosting, open infrastructure, and community control as central themes. The author followed talks on Rust-based virtualization, Garage object storage, virtual machines in Kubernetes, SmolBSD, and automated DN42 networking, plus a candid session on maintaining open-source office suites. He praised technical quality and in-person community but warned that crowding, queues, and full rooms threaten accessibility and niche experimentation. Commenters agreed recordings help, while disagreeing about whether congestion was worse and whether attending is mainly for talks or people.

### Comment pulse

- In-person value is social → spontaneous meetings distinguish FOSDEM from polished streams and recordings.
- Capacity remains contentious → some missed talks after long queues, while others found fewer full rooms than in prior years.
- Remote access softens the problem → archived videos and live streams preserve content, though not hallway conversations.

### LLM perspective

- View: FOSDEM’s strategic relevance is growing faster than its physical and cultural capacity.
- Impact: Attendees face access trade-offs; smaller projects risk losing attention to sovereignty-driven priorities.
