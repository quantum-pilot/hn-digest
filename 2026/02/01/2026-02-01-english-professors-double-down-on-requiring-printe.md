# English professors double down on requiring printed copies of readings

- Score: 102 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46847039) | Link: https://yaledailynews.com/articles/english-professors-double-down-on-requiring-printed-copies-of-readings

### TL;DR

Some Yale English professors are newly requiring printed readings and restricting classroom screens, except for accessibility needs, because they believe paper improves close reading, discussion, and resistance to chatbot summaries. Course packets range from about $20 to more than $150; Yale aid programs and student-government stipends offset some costs. Other faculty retain screens for accessibility, affordability, and environmental reasons. Commenters favored protecting attention but argued for mixed strategies: oral defenses, paper assessments, better discussion design, and explicit AI literacy rather than blanket prohibition.

### Comment pulse

- Paper protects sustained attention → automatic summaries tempt students to skip primary texts — counterpoint: some argue summary-proof instruction is the better response.
- Assessment can adapt without blanket bans → oral project defenses and handwritten quizzes test understanding while AI remains a guided study tool.
- Cost remains an access objection → packets can exceed $150, and one commenter argued universities should absorb printing as a core teaching expense.
