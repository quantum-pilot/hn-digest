### TL;DR

A June 2025 polemic argues that Palantir’s government data platforms connect three risks: AI-assisted warfare in Gaza, expanding US surveillance, and opaque political financing. It cites Palantir’s Israeli defense partnership, Project Maven, ICE and policing work, CIA seed funding, and an alleged link between Jeffrey Epstein’s $40 million investment with Peter Thiel and the company’s origins. The author attributes Lavender and Habsora targeting harms to this ecosystem and calls for restrictions on military AI and data consolidation. Commenters mainly challenged the article’s age and the significance of the Epstein connection.
