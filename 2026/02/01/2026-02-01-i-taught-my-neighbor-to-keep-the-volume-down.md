# I taught my neighbor to keep the volume down

- Score: 458 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46848415) | Link: https://idiallo.com/blog/teaching-my-neighbor-to-keep-the-volume-down

### TL;DR

In 2007, Ibrahim Diallo discovered that his Dish Network RF remote also controlled a loud downstairs neighbor’s set-top box because both used the same setting. He initially planned to explain and demonstrate the collision, but the neighbor dismissed him at the door. Diallo then kept the remote by his bed and switched off the neighbor’s television whenever its volume exceeded his tolerance. After weeks, the volume stayed low: an amusing account of accidental access turned into covert behavioral conditioning, echoed by commenters’ own retaliatory noise-control stories.
