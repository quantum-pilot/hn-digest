# One Piece of Flock Camera Data Put This Innocent Woman in Jail for 13 Days

- Score: 257 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49852065) | Link: https://www.jezebel.com/flock-cameras-data-innocent-woman-arrested-lindsey-isaacs-palm-beach-florida-lawsuit-vehicular-homicide

### TL;DR

According to the article and Lindsey Isaacs’s Senate testimony, Florida troopers linked her black Dodge Durango to a fatal crash after a Flock camera recorded it nearby, despite witnesses describing a maroon vehicle and her impounded car showing no collision damage. Arrested seven months later, she spent 13 days jailed, including 86 hours in solitary; charges were dropped after counsel showed vehicle photos, and a civil lawsuit is pending. HN disputes whether Flock caused the harm or amplified police and prosecutorial failure.
