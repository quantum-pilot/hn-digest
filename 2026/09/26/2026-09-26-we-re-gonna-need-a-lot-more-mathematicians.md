# We're gonna need a lot more mathematicians

- Score: 402 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49852717) | Link: https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/24/were-gonna-need-a-lot-more-mathematicians/

### TL;DR

In a guest post hosted by Terence Tao, Amit Sahai argues that increasingly capable AI could produce consequential ideas faster than humans can understand them. Rather than abandoning mathematics, society should expand sustained research communities—a “deployable intellectual reserve”—that can examine unfamiliar models, evidence, uncertainties, and safety claims independently. His hypothetical terawatt fusion plant illustrates why opaque correctness is insufficient for high-stakes decisions. HN debates whether human understanding is intrinsically valuable, economically sustainable, or doomed to yield to faster systems, while emphasizing domain judgment and verification fatigue.
