### TL;DR

Erika, the mother shown carrying her baby and food at a Giants game, says viral narration badly misrepresented her marriage. She recounts that Ramses repeatedly offered help, ate only after she encouraged him, and was grieving a childhood friend whose memory prompted the outing. What began as broadcast banter escalated into strangers urging divorce, locating the family’s contact details, and telling Ramses to die. Her appeal is to withhold judgment when a short, silent clip cannot reveal context or a family’s history.
