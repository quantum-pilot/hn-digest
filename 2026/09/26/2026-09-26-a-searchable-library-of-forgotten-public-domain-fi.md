# A searchable library of forgotten public-domain film clips from 1915 onward

- Score: 217 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49832768) | Link: https://www.movingimagearchive.com/

### TL;DR

The Moving Image Archive describes itself as a growing, searchable library of public-domain archival film that anyone may reuse, framing verifiable historical footage as increasingly important amid synthetic media. Its brief About page provides no details on collection size, provenance workflow, indexing, licensing checks, or sustainability. Hacker News users nevertheless found valuable historical clips and praised curation layered over large archives. Others reported slow animations, poor performance, hard-to-find search, and questioned whether the hosted project or its processing pipeline can survive without a business model.

### Comment pulse
