# Jury finds Facebook liable for deceiving users in Cambridge Analytica case

- Score: 423 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49852302) | Link: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/facebook-liable-deceiving-users-cambridge-analytica/

### TL;DR

A New Mexico jury found Facebook liable for deceiving users about privacy protections connected to Cambridge Analytica’s harvesting of data from roughly 87 million profiles. Jurors concluded the conduct affected the state’s population and found more than two million violations, including misleading statements about data-broker investigations. Damages remain undecided: the judge will set them, while state attorneys seek up to $5,000 per violation. Meta disputes the verdict and says it will continue defending its record and platform-management rights.
