# PipePipe: NewPipe hard fork implementing SponsorBlock

- Score: 504 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49842764) | Link: https://github.com/InfinityLoop1308/PipePipe

### TL;DR

PipePipe is an independent 2022 hard fork of NewPipe for Android, prioritizing frequent fixes and additional features rather than tracking upstream. Its additions include SponsorBlock, restored dislike counts, selective cookie-based access to restricted streams, AV1 and VP9 playback, filters, gestures, downloads, playlist tools, and local usage options. HN users praise its update cadence and account-free subscriptions, while comparing browsers and self-hosted frontends. A broader thread explores federated or peer-to-peer video, whose delivery gains do not solve discovery, moderation, monetization, legal risk, or network effects.

### Comment pulse

- Frequent maintenance differentiates PipePipe → users report faster fixes and fewer longstanding navigation problems than NewPipe.
- Dedicated apps still offer meaningful control → downloads, local subscriptions, filtered recommendations, and background playback reduce dependence on platform accounts.
- Peer-to-peer delivery is insufficient → cheap CDNs, weak upload capacity, moderation, discovery, creator economics, and legal exposure remain harder platform problems.
