# Breaking Up with Google Play: Why Conversations Is Now Free

- Score: 704 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49855315) | Link: https://gultsch.de/posts/breaking-up-with-google-play/

### TL;DR

Conversations, an open-source Android messaging client, is making F-Droid its primary distribution route after twelve years of paid Google Play sales. Developer Daniel Gultsch says Play revenue once provided crucial predictable income, but grants now fund work through 2029. He accepts Google’s 15% fee for distribution and tax handling; his breaking point is opaque rejections, removals, worsening review delays, and no useful human support, including security updates waiting weeks. Commenters largely echoed that monopoly gatekeeping makes poor support economically tolerable.
