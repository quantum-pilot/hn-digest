# Gemini 4 Argon

- Score: 1616 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49913571) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/

### TL;DR

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon, initially limited to trusted cyber defenders while broader guardrails and voluntary government pre-release access continue. Google claims a one-million-token output limit, leading results across software, professional-work and security benchmarks, and internal deployments including code migrations and memory optimization; these performance figures remain vendor-reported. Planned introductory API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Commenters mixed striking anecdotes about earlier Gemini models with frustration over delayed access and debate about whether any AI provider possesses a durable moat.
