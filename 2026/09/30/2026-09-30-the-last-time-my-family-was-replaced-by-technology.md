# The last time my family was replaced by technology

- Score: 311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49908394) | Link: https://manuel.darcemont.fr/posts/the-last-time-my-family-was-replaced-by-technology/

### TL;DR

In an explicitly personal family story, the author recounts how his great-great-grandfather moved from farriery to automobile repair in rural France, preserving a purpose—helping people travel—while changing tools. He offers this not as proof or instruction, acknowledging the farriers whose transitions failed, but as a way to ask whether developers value coding itself or making useful things. HN appreciates the empathy yet stresses that AI adoption may be faster, less tangible, and more competitive, with severe individual losses hidden beneath long-run societal gains.
