# Surprisingly complex waves reveal the brain's inner workings

- Score: 243 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49912955) | Link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/surprisingly-complex-waves-reveal-the-brains-inner-workings-20260930/

### TL;DR

Intracranial recordings from epilepsy patients performing memory tasks reveal cortical activity moving not only as planar waves but as sources, sinks, and rotating spirals. Different tasks correlate with different patterns, suggesting a possible fast coordination layer atop slower-changing neural connections; related mouse work found mirrored rotating waves and spiral cellular wiring. Researchers disagree whether waves actively shape computation or merely reflect synaptic activity. HN discussion likewise emphasized small, constrained cohorts, unresolved causality, and possible roles for astrocytes.

### Comment pulse

- Causality remains unsettled → synapses generate the observed fields, while evidence that extracellular waves materially drive neurons is limited.
- Astrocytes offer an overlooked mechanism → their contacts across many synapses and coupled networks could potentially respond to extracellular patterns.
- The headline overreaches → critics describe evidence from small epilepsy cohorts doing constrained tasks — counterpoint: others find the patterns genuinely revealing.
