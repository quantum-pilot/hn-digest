# 56k.rip – the 1996 dial-up internet experience

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49915126) | Link: https://56k.rip/

### TL;DR

56k.rip recreates a 1996-style desktop with a simulated BIOS boot, teal interface, period-inspired icons and onboarding instructions for dialing in. The captured source documents only the completed boot, desktop and opening instructions, not every application or network behavior. Commenters enjoyed the nostalgia and compared it with richer virtual-network projects, but several found the illusion historically inconsistent: pages and I/O felt too fast, modem sounds seemed wrong, colors exceeded the claimed palette, and some visuals looked more like later Windows eras.
