# Most data centers refusing to say how much water, electricity they use

- Score: 214 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49907057) | Link: https://nltimes.nl/2026/09/30/data-centers-refusing-say-much-water-electricity-use

### TL;DR

DutchNews, covering a Trouw and Lighthouse Reports investigation, says public environmental data exists for only about a quarter of large Dutch data centers. The national agency lists 104 centers, versus 186 commercial facilities counted by an industry group, excluding many private in-house sites. Data centers used 5.1 billion kWh in 2024, 4.6% of national electricity; water was estimated at 0.1%. HN debate focused on enforcement, EU directive implementation, local resource constraints, and whether environmental concerns lack proportion.

### Comment pulse

- Disclosure obligations need enforcement → commenters favor substantial penalties when required electricity and water reports are missing.
- EU directives require national implementation → readers distinguished them from directly applicable regulations and noted Dutch law was recently clarified.
- Aggregate water figures can mislead → national usage may be small while concentrated demand strains local infrastructure — counterpoint: critics see concern as disproportionate.
