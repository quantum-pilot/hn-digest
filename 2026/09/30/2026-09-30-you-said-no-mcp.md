# You said no MCP

- Score: 658 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49906637) | Link: https://earendil.com/posts/you-said-no-mcp/

### TL;DR

Pi has reversed its public opposition to MCP and added it to core after protocol improvements and broader architectural work made the integration useful beyond MCP. Its Codemode runs JavaScript in a harness-side sandbox to discover, sequence, parallelize, and combine structured tool calls while preserving session state. The team still criticizes weak composition, text-heavy servers, and inconsistent harness patterns, preferring MCP as discoverable structured APIs. HN applauds the transparent reversal and interoperability, though commenters debate whether ordinary CLIs, REST, or OpenAPI already solve most needs more simply.
