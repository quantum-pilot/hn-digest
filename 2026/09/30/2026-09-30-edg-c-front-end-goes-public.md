# EDG C++ front-end goes public

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49913192) | Link: https://edgcpp.org/#transition

### TL;DR

EDG’s long-running C++ front end is now open source under The C++ Alliance, which becomes its nonprofit home while retaining experienced maintainers and John Spicer’s leadership. Work will share one public codebase across community contributions, openly published maintenance and collectively funded features; a sponsorship committee will oversee reviews and priorities. The announcement emphasizes continuity, but commenters supplied sobering context: EDG the company is winding down, and some customers may migrate toward Clang despite the front end’s reputation for correctness, configurability and standards influence.
