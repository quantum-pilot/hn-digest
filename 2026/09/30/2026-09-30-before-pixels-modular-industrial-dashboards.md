# Before pixels: Modular industrial dashboards

- Score: 269 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49912792) | Link: https://unsung.aresluna.org/before-pixels-modular-industrial-dashboards/

### TL;DR

A photographic tour of German and Polish museums collects modular pre-digital control panels from air traffic, rail, tram and power systems. The author treats many controls tentatively, presenting the panels as reusable design and interaction systems rather than asserting how each worked. Commenters identified the railway examples as modular track-plan signaling: operators selected endpoints, while relay circuits configured and locked nonconflicting routes before opening signals. Others explained that standardized physical modules were individually wired to separate cabinets containing the underlying logic.
