# Singapore govt dating app uses Gale-Shapley stable marriage algorithm

- Score: 436 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49906432) | Link: https://twitter.com/tuakdotsol/status/2105105417760391258

### TL;DR

A social post says Singapore’s FirstDate service matches public servants aged 21–35 using a Gale–Shapley stable-matching process built from stated preferences and dealbreakers. It describes one match per cycle, a 72-hour decision window, mutual contact disclosure, and Singpass verification, contrasting this with swipe-based commercial incentives. HN commenters questioned whether profile rankings capture attraction or long-term compatibility, whether a practical system can fully rank thousands of candidates, and which side receives the algorithm’s proposer-optimal advantage.

### Comment pulse

- Stated preferences poorly predict relationships → people may misunderstand their needs, change over time, or react differently after meeting.
- Commercial and state incentives may differ → commenters expect government to value durable relationships while subscription apps benefit from continued use.
- Implementation details matter → full rankings are impractical, inferred rankings may distort intent, and proposer direction affects who gets the best stable match.
