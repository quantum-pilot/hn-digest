### TL;DR

A Polish writer connects September 2026’s energy, food, climate, debt, security, employment, and demographic pressures through one thesis: societies replaced buffers with cheaper dependencies, leaving shocks able to cascade. The essay ranges from Hormuz and European fuel supplies to Polish heating, borrowing, drought, defense, and household resilience, while acknowledging uncertainty and recommending modest 72-hour preparations. HN reactions split between praise for the synthesis and criticism that it underweights Poland’s prosperity, full gas storage, renewables, and solvable problems.
