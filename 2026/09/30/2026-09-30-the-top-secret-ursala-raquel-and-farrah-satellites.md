# The top secret URSALA, RAQUEL, and FARRAH satellites (2025)

- Score: 293 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49915082) | Link: https://www.thespacereview.com/article/4951/1

### TL;DR

Recently declassified records illuminate a four-decade U.S. program of suitcase-sized, spin-stabilized signals-intelligence satellites launched as secondary payloads. URSALA, RAQUEL, FARRAH, GLORIA, and CARRIE intercepted and geolocated radar or communications emissions, evolving from bespoke strategic collectors into longer-lived systems that sent near-real-time data to deployed forces through mobile terminals. Confirmed documents describe missions, frequencies, longevity, and tactical integration; some operational effects and end dates remain classified or uncertain. HN marvels at historical capability while noting how difficult unsorted declassified archives are to navigate.
