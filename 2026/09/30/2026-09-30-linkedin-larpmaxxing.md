# LinkedIn Larpmaxxing

- Score: 291 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49904314) | Link: https://hereticpleb.vercel.app/blog/linkedin-larpmaxxing/

### TL;DR

The author argues LinkedIn rewards polished self-promotion over technical growth, using repetitive computer-vision demos as an example. To test their difficulty, they collected and labeled 200 feed screenshots, trained a YOLOv8 detector, and produced a working demo in 90 minutes. HN readers agreed the feed can be performative but stressed LinkedIn’s practical value: it functions as a professional directory, recruiter channel, and sometimes mandatory job credential, making disengagement harder than simply ignoring an unpleasant social network.
