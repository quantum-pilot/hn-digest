# Why the Bronze Age Collapsed

- Score: 382 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49890732) | Link: https://www.worksinprogress.news/p/why-really-caused-the-bronze-age

### TL;DR

Patrick Fitzsimmons advances a hypothesis about why eastern Mediterranean empires failed to re-form after the upheavals around 1200 BC. Bronze depended on scarce, long-distance tin and thus favored states controlling trade and stockpiles; locally available iron eventually let peripheral communities field comparable weapons, dispersing coercive power and obstructing reconsolidation. He cites increased violence and shorter reigns after iron adoption but acknowledges incomplete datasets and disputed causality. HN emphasizes multi-causal collapse, challenges some metallurgy and conquest examples, and notes iron proliferated after rather than before the collapse.
