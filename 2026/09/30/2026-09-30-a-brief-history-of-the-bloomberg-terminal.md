# A brief history of the Bloomberg terminal

- Score: 369 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49909583) | Link: https://spectrum.ieee.org/bloomberg-terminal

### TL;DR

Bloomberg’s terminal grew from Innovative Market Systems’ 1981 bet that traders would pay for integrated bond data and quantitative tools. Early Market Master hardware combined a monochrome display, specialized keyboard and private telephone network; later versions added navigation, communications, news and biometric authentication. Dedicated terminals eventually gave way to PC and mobile software, while the name came to mean an entire data, analytics, messaging and trading environment. Commenters admired its information-dense expert interface, distinguishing difficult learning from inefficient daily use.
