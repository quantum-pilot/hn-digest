# The AI Race Just Got Awkward

- Score: 403 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49910553) | Link: https://insufferable.dev/posts/the-ai-race-just-got-awkward/

### TL;DR

The author argues Chinese labs have shifted from distilling Western models to publishing inference techniques that Western competitors quietly adopt. They attribute steep reductions in long-context KV-cache requirements and cache-read prices to DeepSeek’s MLA, compressed-attention, cross-layer reuse, and low-precision caching work, then infer improved Western inference margins. This is an interpretation, not proof of adoption or market outcomes. HN discussion debated the economics and ethics of distillation, open publication, frontier costs, and possible Chinese strategic motives.
