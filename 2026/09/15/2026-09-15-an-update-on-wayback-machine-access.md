# An update on Wayback Machine access

- Score: 670 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49716176) | Link: https://blog.archive.org/2026/09/15/an-update-on-wayback-machine-access/

### TL;DR

The Internet Archive says waves of high-volume automated traffic forced new Wayback Machine protections, including clearer messages when requests receive HTTP 429 “too many requests” responses. The safeguards sometimes block legitimate visitors; the Archive says it is improving bot detection and asks affected users to email their operating system, browser, and IP address for investigation. HN participants blamed scraper services using archived copies as fallbacks, described corporate, residential, and IPv6 false positives, and emphasized donations and open access amid rising infrastructure pressure.
