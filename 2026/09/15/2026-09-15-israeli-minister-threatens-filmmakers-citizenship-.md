The unreleased film uses anonymous testimony from 24 Israeli military and intelligence officers to allege systemic civilian targeting; the IDF rejects promotional claims, specifically denies approval to kill 500 civilians for one target, and requested the full film. Industry supporters argue its questions warrant examination. HN discussion condemned Zohar while disputing anonymity and introducing unverified wider claims.
