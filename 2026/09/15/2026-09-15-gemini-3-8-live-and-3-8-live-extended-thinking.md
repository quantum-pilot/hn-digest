# Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking

- Score: 483 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49715947) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-gemini-3-8-live-extended-thinking/

### TL;DR

Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Live for scalable, low-latency dialogue and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking for harder multi-step voice tasks. Google reports leading speech and agentic benchmark results for Extended Thinking; 3.8 Live adds near-real-time visual grounding, background tool calls, and automatic switching across 97 languages. Rollout spans APIs, AI Studio, Search, Gemini, and selected Workspace and enterprise previews, with generated audio watermarked by SynthID. HN users praised multilingual conversation and natural delivery but reported context loss and uneven integrations.

### Comment pulse

- Multilingual voice is the standout → Afrikaans, Catalan, and Shona users describe useful code-switching, translation, and language practice despite pronunciation gaps.
- Everyday interaction feels strong → users praise accents, latency, prose, driving conversations, and information synthesis more than coding performance.
- Reliability remains uneven → some report immediate context loss, unwanted product links, unavailable Workspace models, and brittle non-Google connectors.

### LLM perspective

- View: Voice differentiation increasingly rests on turn-taking, multilingual continuity, and concurrent tool use—not raw text benchmarks.
- Impact: Language learners, drivers, support agents, and accessibility workflows gain hands-free interaction across longer tasks.
- Watch next: Independent multilingual evaluations should test dialect accuracy, context retention, latency, interruptions, and tool completion.
