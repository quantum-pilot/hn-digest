# I can't stop thinking about Papua New Guinea

- Score: 1127 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49708431) | Link: https://notnottalmud.substack.com/p/why-i-cant-stop-thinking-about-papua

### TL;DR

Using First Contact and its footage, the essay recounts how Australian gold prospectors reached New Guinea’s densely populated highlands in 1930, then explains why crops and shells crossed linguistic and geographic barriers while wider knowledge did not. It attributes political fragmentation to terrain, perishable staples, malaria, and continual local conflict, connecting this history to Papua New Guinea’s weak infrastructure and clan identity today. Commenters supplied family memories and videos, while political discussion about West Papua became disputed and highly charged.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The essay’s strongest insight is that goods can diffuse across hostile networks even when explanations and identities cannot.
- Impact: Infrastructure and shared language may connect markets without automatically replacing clan-level trust or historical political boundaries.
