# 25 years of mass surveillance is enough

- Score: 928 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49710883) | Link: https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/09/25-years-of-mass-surveillance-is-enough.html

### TL;DR

Bruce Schneier and Cindy Cohn argue that post-9/11 surveillance expanded from targeted investigations into routine population-scale collection, often supplied by private data brokers and platforms. They say officials have never demonstrated benefits commensurate with the financial, constitutional, and social costs, while documented misuse and overcollection undermine First and Fourth Amendment protections. Their proposed reset requires warrants before collecting, accessing, or using Americans’ data, stronger enforcement and privacy law, protected encryption, and letting Section 702 remain expired. HN commenters largely favored structural limits.
