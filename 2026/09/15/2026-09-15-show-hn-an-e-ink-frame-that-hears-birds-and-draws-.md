# Show HN: An e-ink frame that hears birds and draws them as 1800s illustrations

- Score: 2184 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49711544) | Link: https://github.com/arnegiacomo/fugleramme

### TL;DR

Fugleramme turns locally detected bird calls into a changing e-ink tableau: BirdNET-Go identifies species, while a Raspberry Pi arranges hand-curated, public-domain natural-history cutouts on a textured page. Its library exceeds 800 cutouts for 400-plus species, with strongest coverage in northern and central Europe; a web-only kiosk works without the display. HN readers loved the deliberately limited, artful result, while noting the roughly €500 hardware cost, regional coverage gaps, and opportunities for lower-power wireless e-ink installations.
