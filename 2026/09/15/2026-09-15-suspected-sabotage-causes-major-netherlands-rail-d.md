# Suspected sabotage causes major Netherlands rail disruption

- Score: 523 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49710253) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c8ly49w9g1edo

### TL;DR

More than 35 apparent sabotage incidents disrupted Dutch railways: pipes, cables, and other objects placed across tracks made safety systems register occupied sections, forcing cancellations around Amsterdam, Schiphol, Eindhoven, and Utrecht. One train struck metal without serious damage; police, prosecutors, and AIVD are investigating, with no suspect, motive, or confirmed link to concurrent farmer protests. HN commenters explained how fail-safe signalling can be abused cheaply at scale and disputed comparisons with a recent French derailment involving a much heavier rail fragment.
