# IKEA made a mod for Skyrim [video]

- Score: 574 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49639647) | Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iZODN0QUgjI

### TL;DR

IKEA released KALLAX STORAGEBORN, a free Skyrim creation built around inventory storage and clutter. It includes new quests, dungeons, items, voiced companion KALLAX, and weapons such as the Allenblade, distributed through Bethesda’s Creations menu. Commenters were surprised by the production effort and generally liked the unlikely brand pairing, but debate returned to IKEA’s earlier legal pressure on The Store is Closed, an indie game whose IKEA-like setting raised trademark and parody questions.
