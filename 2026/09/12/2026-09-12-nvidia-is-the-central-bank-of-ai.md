# Nvidia is the central bank of AI

- Score: 490 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49673098) | Link: https://www.economist.com/interactive/briefing/2026/09/03/nvidia-is-the-central-bank-of-ai

### TL;DR

The analysis argues Nvidia increasingly finances the demand that buys its chips through startup stakes, revenue backstops, equipment-value guarantees, and infrastructure commitments. It estimates roughly $300 billion of potential customer-related exposure, while emphasizing that obligations are staggered and Nvidia’s cash generation remains formidable. The risk is correlated: slower compute demand could reduce chip sales and asset values while triggering guarantees. Custom hyperscaler chips, expanding supply, and falling inference costs could pressure margins without requiring an outright AI collapse.
