# Great Lakes sturgeon may be 400 years old:Scientists rethinking how to save them

- Score: 154 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49624456) | Link: https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ontario-great-lakes-sturgeon-lifespan-study-9.7329250

### TL;DR

A 44-year capture-and-recapture dataset suggests some Great Lakes lake sturgeon could live beyond 400 years, far above the traditional 150-year estimate. Researchers inferred age from observed growth rates because fin-ray rings become difficult to distinguish as growth slows; one male added only seven centimetres over 34 years. Scientists say the model could force century-scale restoration plans to extend further. Human activity erased an estimated 99% of historic populations, which still depend on habitat work and hatchery releases. HN expressed wonder alongside methodological skepticism.

### Comment pulse

- Longevity estimates are model-based → slow measured growth extrapolates to centuries, but commenters wanted closer scrutiny of the methodology.
- Restoration becomes intergenerational stewardship → recovery plans may need to outlast their designers and distribute fish-raising expertise across communities.
- Sturgeon carry ecological and cultural importance → they consume invasive species and are regarded as elder beings in Anishinaabe tradition.
