# Apple iPod Engraver (2019)

- Score: 227 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49619848) | Link: https://dunstanorchard.com/apple-ipod-engraver/

### TL;DR

A former Apple online-store engineer recalls enhancing the 2005 iPod personalization page with a rotatable product view, live engraving preview, and animated shipping-time changes. JavaScript cycled JPEG frames, ImageMagick rendered submitted text server-side, and CSS supplied the highlight effect—simple techniques that felt unusually polished under contemporary browser constraints. HN admired a role explicitly devoted to adding small moments of delight, debated whether modern client rendering would actually improve the solution, and shared nostalgia for Apple's colorful, physical-looking interfaces.
