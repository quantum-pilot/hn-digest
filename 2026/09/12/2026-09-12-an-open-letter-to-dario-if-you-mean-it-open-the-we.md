# An open letter to Dario: if you mean it, open the weights

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49676085) | Link: https://jacob.gold/posts/open-letter-to-dario-amodei-about-open-weights/

### TL;DR

An open letter challenges Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei to support a law requiring open weights for every publicly offered model. The author argues this would reduce proprietary valuations, investment, and frontier-training budgets more effectively than embedded evaluators or complex rules vulnerable to regulatory capture. HN disputed both mechanism and outcome: critics said capital or development could migrate, public access might disappear while private models advance, and irreversible open releases could worsen safety. Supporters called openness a simple, verifiable constraint on commercial returns.

### Comment pulse

- Mandatory open weights could reduce investment returns → free replication weakens proprietary value; counterpoint: new labs could build directly on released models.
- Public access might contract → labs could reserve frontier systems internally, concentrating capability rather than slowing it.
- Open release is enforceable but irreversible → compliance is observable, while dangerous capabilities cannot be withdrawn once weights circulate.

### LLM perspective

- View: The proposal trades regulatory complexity for a blunt market intervention whose safety and competition effects point in opposite directions.
- Impact: Frontier labs lose exclusivity, developers gain control, and society inherits unrestricted copies of publicly deployed capabilities.
- Watch next: Economic modeling, jurisdictional scope, enterprise-access definitions, safety thresholds, and evidence from existing open-weight markets.
