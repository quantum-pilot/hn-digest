### TL;DR

Gamers Nexus rejects LG’s denial of its smart-TV investigation, arguing the company offered no evidence against findings about automatic content recognition, data collection, security flaws, microphones, geolocation, and consent. The follow-up video says LG’s language answers narrower claims than those investigators made and includes a lawyer’s analysis of the terms users accept. This packet supplies the investigators’ description, not their underlying demonstrations or LG’s full rebuttal, so the competing technical claims remain unresolved here.
