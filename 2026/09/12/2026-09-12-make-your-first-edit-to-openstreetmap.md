# Make your first edit to OpenStreetMap

- Score: 474 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49674050) | Link: https://high5apps.github.io/josm-plugin-website-wizard/

### TL;DR

The tutorial walks newcomers through adding an official website to a nearby OpenStreetMap shop or amenity using JOSM and its Website Wizard plugin. It covers downloading a small familiar area, filtering places without website tags, locating an official site, saving the tag, and uploading a documented changeset. Commenters support local incremental mapping but question JOSM as a first editor, recommending the simpler web editor, StreetComplete, Every Door, or other task-focused mobile tools before advanced desktop workflows.

### Comment pulse

- Start local and small → Correcting familiar businesses, paths, crossings, and hours produces visible improvements without deep mapping expertise.
- JOSM may overwhelm beginners → Its installation, filters, panels, and plugin setup add friction before the first contribution.
- Task-focused apps lower barriers → StreetComplete and Every Door guide on-the-ground edits through narrower interfaces.
