# We must pace the frontier

- Score: 656 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49672510) | Link: https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier

### TL;DR

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei argues frontier AI capabilities should advance more slowly so alignment, interpretability, security, and oversight can catch up. He cites accelerating AI-assisted AI development and recent agent-security incidents as reasons for urgency, forecasting potentially catastrophic cyber capability within 6–12 months if safeguards lag. His three-part proposal combines embedded third-party evaluators, coordination among democratic countries, and eventual global agreements. Commenters dispute his forecasts, motives, feasibility, antitrust implications, and China strategy, while some favor operator liability or restrictions on labor displacement instead.

### Comment pulse

- Embedded evaluators could make safety commitments verifiable → ongoing access would cover training processes and incidents, not merely finished models.
- Pacing may create regulatory advantage → critics see safety framing as protection for established closed-model companies.
- International coordination appears contradictory → commenters doubt rivals will slow while democratic governments also seek a durable lead.
