# Fuck it, make it anyway

- Score: 569 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49671329) | Link: https://www.joelotter.com/posts/2026/09/make-it-anyway/

### TL;DR

A game developer describes losing motivation as generative AI devalues skills, recognition, and the pleasure of hand-building software. Because coding with assistants feels joyless and alien, he rejects competing on speed and chooses to keep making games and tools the difficult way, as he already chose C++ and a custom engine for learning and enjoyment. HN debated whether creators value craft, outcomes, recognition, or professional scarcity, with some embracing AI-enabled building and others defending human process and audience connection as legitimate ends.
