# Starlink Signal Leakage Threatens Radio Astronomy's Most Critical Frequencies

- Score: 143 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49667375) | Link: https://www.gadgetreview.com/starlinks-signal-leakage-is-threatening-radio-astronomys-most-critical-frequencies

### TL;DR

Gadget Review reports that a Curtin University team analyzed roughly 76 million images over 29 days and detected 112,534 unintended emissions from 1,806 Starlink satellites across 73–235MHz. The reported leakage entered two protected radio-astronomy bands, appeared in up to 30% of images at some frequencies, and could overwhelm faint signals sought by SKA-Low. Current ITU protections reportedly address intentional transmissions rather than hardware leakage. Commenters favor spacecraft engineering fixes over relocating telescopes, but criticize the article’s presentation and lack of concrete mitigation tradeoffs.

### Comment pulse
