# LG denies TV spying claims, says tracking and snooping concerns 'not true'

- Score: 540 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49645480) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/big-tech/lg-strongly-denies-tv-security-claims-says-tracking-and-snooping-concerns-not-true-online-investigation-claims-216-000-000-spy-tvs-record-audio

### TL;DR

LG denied Gamers Nexus’s allegations that its smart TVs continuously upload logs or record ambient conversations, while confirming local-network device discovery. LG says voice data reaches servers only after an enabled wake word or remote-button activation; other wake-word audio is processed locally and deleted. It describes automatic content recognition and advertising as separately opt-in. Tom’s Hardware had not independently verified either side and noted LG did not address every issue, including alleged plain-text transcript storage.
