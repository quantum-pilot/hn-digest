# US sanctions force The Netherlands off Microsoft and toward alternative NixOS

- Score: 380 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49891550) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/software/the-netherlands-is-rolling-alternative-nixos-based-software-ecosystem-after-u-s-sanctions-on-icc-took-microsoft-off-the-table-trial-programs-running-now-first-release-expected-at-end-of-2027

### TL;DR

A report says U.S. sanctions that disrupted Microsoft access for the International Criminal Court prompted Dutch planning for DAWO, a government work environment intended to reduce dependence on foreign software and services. Built around NixOS and overseen by the Interior Ministry, it emphasizes reproducible, signed configurations across agencies. This is not a completed national switch: eight municipalities are running pilots, with a first stable release planned for late 2027. HN discusses digital sovereignty, reciprocal trade versus dependency, support costs, and whether the sanctions merely exposed an existing concentration risk.
