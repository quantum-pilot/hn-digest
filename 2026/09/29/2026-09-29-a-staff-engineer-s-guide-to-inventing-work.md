# A Staff Engineer's Guide to Inventing Work

- Score: 302 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49878857) | Link: https://sujithjay.com/inventing-work

### TL;DR

Platform staff engineers often must discover and justify their own roadmap. The author organizes signals from systems, users, the organization and industry: incidents, costs, toil, interviews, overloaded use cases, prototypes, OKRs, repeated management concerns, migration laggards, descriptive writing and delayed adoption of proven trends. Each signal differs in urgency, evidence and whether it leads or lags. The central warning is that work is abundant; the harder discipline is choosing among competing signals instead of letting the latest outage or loudest executive dominate.
