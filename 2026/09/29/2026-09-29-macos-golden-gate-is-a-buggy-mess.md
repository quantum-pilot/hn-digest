# macOS Golden Gate Is a Buggy Mess

- Score: 461 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49894005) | Link: https://www.squareorbits.com/blog/2026/09/macos-golden-gate-is-a-buggy-mess/

### TL;DR

After several weeks with macOS 27 Golden Gate, the author documents alignment mistakes, stale assets, menu-bar crashes, broken settings search, Mission Control failures, disappearing controls, and a color-picker hang requiring restart. They argue this clashes with Apple’s refinement-focused promise and suggest annual releases may impede polish. HN responses show markedly different experiences: some report serious Mission Control and audio problems, while others call Golden Gate an overall improvement and dispute whether every Firefox-related symptom or visual nit is Apple’s fault.

### Comment pulse

- Small visual defects erode confidence → readers see neglected alignment as evidence that deeper quality controls may also be weak.
- Mission Control regressions disrupt heavy workflows → users report broken shortcuts, flickering spaces, rearranged windows, and multi-monitor failures.
- Golden Gate is not universally unstable → several users report improvement or only rough edges — counterpoint: others describe severe regressions.
