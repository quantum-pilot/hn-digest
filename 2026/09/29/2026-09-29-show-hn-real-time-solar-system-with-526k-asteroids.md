# Show HN: Real-time Solar System with 526k asteroids and all tracked satellites

- Score: 287 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49898778) | Link: https://space.bl2.net/

### TL;DR

Space Now is a browser-based, real-scale solar-system viewer showing more than 526,000 asteroids and comets alongside tracked satellites, planets, moons, spacecraft, orbits, and time controls. It uses JPL data plus CelesTrak and SatNOGS catalogs, WebGL2 rendering, and worker-based propagation; the asteroid dataset loads in the background. The captured live catalog is dated September 30, so its exact counts should not be treated as September 29 figures. HN praises its performance and perspective while questioning visibility scale, historical propagation, and approximate satellite positions.
