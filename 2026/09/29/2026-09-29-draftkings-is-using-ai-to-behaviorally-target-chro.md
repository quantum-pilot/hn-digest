# DraftKings is using AI to behaviorally target chronic gamblers

- Score: 545 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49896050) | Link: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/09/draftkings-using-ai-supercharge-harms-online-behavioral-advertising

### TL;DR

EFF cites New York Times reporting that DraftKings trains a model on customers’ betting records to identify likely losing gamblers, then targets them with return promotions. EFF argues this first-party-data use can exploit problem gamblers and exemplifies why restrictions limited to third-party data sales are insufficient; its advocated remedy is banning behavioral advertising. HN commenters largely condemned gambling firms’ incentives and pervasive promotion, though one lottery worker described stronger responsible-gambling practices, suggesting operators and jurisdictions vary.

### Comment pulse

- Regulation must realign gambling-company incentives → commenters doubt voluntary ethics can outweigh profits from vulnerable customers.

- Industry conduct varies → one lottery worker recalled compulsive-player targeting, while another described training and prominent assistance resources.
