# 1 in 8 cancer cases worldwide are caused by infections, study finds

- Score: 270 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49892120) | Link: https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/9.7361622

### TL;DR

A Lancet Oncology study led by the WHO’s cancer agency estimates infections caused 2.3 million new cancer cases in 2024, about 12% worldwide. H. pylori and HPV each accounted for roughly three-quarters of a million cases, with hepatitis viruses and Epstein-Barr virus also major contributors. The analysis applies estimated infection-attributable fractions to global incidence data, while regional disparities reflect unequal access to vaccination, screening and treatment. The finding frames a substantial share of cancer burden as potentially preventable through population-level measures.
