# U.S. postal inspectors shut down website selling counterfeit postage labels

- Score: 242 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49899090) | Link: https://postalemployeenetwork.com/news/2026/09/26/u-s-postal-inspectors-shut-down-website-selling-millions-of-counterfeit-postage-labels/

### TL;DR

U.S. postal inspectors and partner agencies seized LabelsBank.com under a court order and charged Faheem Akram, a Pakistani national, with conspiracy, counterfeit-postage, and wire-fraud counts. The indictment alleges the site sold more than 5.1 million unauthorized USPS labels to over 5,000 customers, often at a fixed $2 price, causing more than $126 million in losses. These are charges, not convictions; Akram is presumed innocent. HN users describe suspected counterfeit labels in marketplace purchases, replacement-shipping patterns, and weak platform incentives, but those anecdotes remain unverified.
