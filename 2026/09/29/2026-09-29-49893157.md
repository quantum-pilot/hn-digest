# Ask HN: What are you reading?

- Score: 330 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49893157) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49893157

### TL;DR

An Ask HN thread seeking substantial reading recommendations became a broad, eclectic bookshelf. Suggestions ranged from Donella Meadows’s systems thinking and Ted Chiang’s short fiction to the Silo novels, Lonesome Dove, East of Eden, Russian classics and Vernor Vinge’s science fiction. Others offered a neuroscience textbook, analog-computing material and the IRC-styled novel *null: $cat /dev/null_*. Rather than converge on one “meaty” title, the thread showed that readers interpret depth through very different combinations of ideas, scale, style and commitment.

### Comment pulse
