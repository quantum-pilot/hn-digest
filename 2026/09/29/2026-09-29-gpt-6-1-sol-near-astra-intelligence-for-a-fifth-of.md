# GPT 6.1 Sol: Near-Astra intelligence for a fifth of the price

- Score: 987 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49896586) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol/

### TL;DR

OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol approaches GPT-6 Astra across coding, professional, computer-use, and factuality evaluations at sharply lower cost. API pricing is $2 per million input tokens, $0.10 cached, and $10 output; OpenAI reports DeepSWE performance matching Astra at roughly one-fifth the cost. It is currently available through the API, Codex, and ChatGPT Work, but not Chat. HN welcomed cheaper caching while questioning whether vendor benchmarks reflect inconsistent real-world coding experiences.
