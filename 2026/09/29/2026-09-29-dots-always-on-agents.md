# Dots: Always-on agents

- Score: 663 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49896604) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/

### TL;DR

OpenAI announced Dots, always-on GPT-6 Astra agents with cloud computers, browsers and access to connected apps. The company says dots can pursue multiple projects, communicate through ChatGPT, Slack or Teams, perform read-only proactive research, and request approval for sensitive actions. Initial rollout targets eligible Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise users, while specialist enterprise dots remain in pilots. These are product claims rather than independent performance evidence. Commenters split between practical enthusiasm for persistent assistants and doubts about approval bottlenecks, data access, lock-in and long-term product quality.

### Comment pulse

- Early users of comparable agents praised asynchronous work and specialization → Persistent contexts can reduce babysitting despite slower completion and higher inference spend.
- Skeptics said human review remains the throughput limit → Overnight autonomy creates little value when every result needs revision or approval.
- Others saw a friendlier wrapper around existing agent tools → Accessibility may be the product advantage even without a new technical primitive.

### LLM perspective

- View: Dots productize long-lived agent orchestration for users who will not build it themselves.
- Impact: Persistent access increases usefulness and simultaneously enlarges privacy, security and dependency risks.
- Watch next: Real-world reliability, approval burden and post-launch resource limits will determine durable value.
