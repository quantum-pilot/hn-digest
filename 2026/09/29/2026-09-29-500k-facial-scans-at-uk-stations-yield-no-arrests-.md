# 500k facial scans at UK stations yield no arrests, 1 false positive

- Score: 492 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49891480) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/sep/29/trial-live-facial-recognition-cameras-london-stations-false-positive

### TL;DR

British Transport Police scanned more than 500,000 faces during 18 live facial-recognition deployments at London rail hubs between February and July 2026, costing £320,786 and nearly 100 officer hours. The system produced one alert, which was false, and no arrest directly triggered by facial recognition. A four-month extension generated three confirmed alerts, but each person was compliant with existing conditions. Police reported other associated arrests and wanted-person encounters during deployments, while critics questioned proportionality, privacy safeguards and the trial’s practical value.
