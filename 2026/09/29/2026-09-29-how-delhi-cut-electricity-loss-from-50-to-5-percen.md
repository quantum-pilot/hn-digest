# How Delhi cut electricity loss from 50 to 5 percent

- Score: 539 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49892245) | Link: https://spectrum.ieee.org/delhi-electricity-loss

### TL;DR

Delhi reduced combined technical and commercial electricity losses from above 50% in 2002 to roughly 5–6% in 2026 while raising reported reliability beyond 99.9%. The overhaul joined regulatory unbundling and private distribution with SCADA monitoring, upgraded transformers, capacitor banks, insulated lines, digital metering, easier payments, theft enforcement, staff training, and community engagement. This was not simply half the electricity vanishing physically: theft, inaccurate billing, and weak collection meant utilities were paid for under half their supply.
