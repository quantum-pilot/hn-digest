# Vermont replacing power plants with home batteries

- Score: 248 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49897993) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20260928-a-virtual-power-plant-hidden-in-vermont-homes-is-keeping-the-lights-on-during-storms

### TL;DR

Green Mountain Power aggregates batteries in more than 5,500 Vermont homes with EVs, solar, demand response, and utility storage into a 110MW virtual power plant, described as the state’s largest power source. Customers can lease two batteries for $55 monthly over ten years, gaining outage backup while allowing grid dispatch with a reserve retained. The utility says the system saved customers $11 million last year and helped retire aging peakers, though four remain rarely used. HN debates customer contracts and costs but reports positive experiences with resilience and peak shifting.

### Comment pulse
