# Flock used >100 times to track veteran who recorded traffic stop

- Score: 359 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49578310) | Link: https://reason.com/2026/09/02/wisconsin-cops-used-flock-over-100-times-to-track-a-navy-veteran-after-he-lawfully-recorded-a-traffic-stop/

### TL;DR

A federal lawsuit alleges Waukesha County officers used Flock license-plate cameras more than 100 times to search for Navy veteran Napoleon Jones’s white BMW after he recorded a traffic stop and filed a complaint. Jones says a deputy unlawfully stopped, arrested, and held him for five hours without charges; reporting cites an internal memorandum saying the stop was illegal. His amended complaint characterizes later searches as retaliation without legitimate law-enforcement purpose. These remain allegations being litigated, not adjudicated findings.
