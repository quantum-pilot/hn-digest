# How the Disaster of "Forever Chemicals" Was Kept Secret

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49576986) | Link: https://www.propublica.org/podcast/forever-chemicals-pfas-pfos-3m-secret-kris-hansen

### TL;DR

ProPublica’s podcast recounts former 3M scientist Kris Hansen’s discovery in the late 1990s that PFOS appeared in every general-population blood sample she tested. Reporter Sharon Lerner says internal records showed Hansen’s bosses already knew of widespread contamination while assuring her the chemicals were harmless, illustrating how loyalty, compartmentalization, and denial sustained secrecy. The report attributes developmental, immune, hormonal, liver, thyroid, and cancer harms to PFAS exposure. 3M says its science and practices evolved and cites major treatment and water-system investments.

- View: Organizational secrecy can persist through distributed obedience and motivated disbelief without every participant holding the full picture.
- Impact: Workers and communities bear long-lived exposure while scientific uncertainty delays corporate and regulatory action.
