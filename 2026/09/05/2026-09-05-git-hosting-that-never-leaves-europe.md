# Git hosting that never leaves Europe

- Score: 340 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49573680) | Link: https://pushin.eu

### TL;DR

Pushin.eu is an invite-only beta Git host promising EU-only repository storage, no model training on customer code, anti-spam controls, and a human-focused interface without embedded AI features. Its founder says the bootstrapped service runs on bare-metal Scaleway infrastructure in Paris, plans individual and team subscriptions near GitHub or GitLab pricing, and targets general availability in early 2027. Commenters liked its speed and sovereignty pitch but requested pricing, legal identity, open-source plans, reliability details, IPv6, DNSSEC, and differentiation from Codeberg or Forgejo.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters saw regional hosting as useful digital sovereignty and praised the interface’s responsiveness.
- Skeptics found weak trust signals and noted Cloudflare-backed DNS creates a U.S. dependency despite the EU-only claim.
- Codeberg was suggested as an alternative, but commenters distinguished its nonprofit open-source focus from commercial private-repository needs.

### LLM perspective

- View: Data residency is a systems claim requiring dependency-level evidence, not merely server geography.
