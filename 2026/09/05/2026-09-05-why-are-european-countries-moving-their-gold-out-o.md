# Why are European countries moving their gold out of North America?

- Score: 194 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49573455) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgyn8q8gqxo

### TL;DR

The BBC reports that the Dutch central bank relocated 86 of roughly 313 tonnes held in the U.S. and Canada, citing geopolitical unrest and crisis readiness. Much was transferred economically by selling in New York and buying in London; physical shipments also moved between North America, Zeist, and London. Experts presented diversification, liquidity, inflation, and interest rates alongside geopolitics, rejecting imminent-doom interpretations. Commenters emphasized distrust and sanctions risk, sometimes with sweeping geopolitical claims that others challenged as hyperbolic.
