# The Real Luxuries In Life

- Score: 478 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49578866) | Link: https://feld.com/archives/2026/09/the-real-luxuries-in-life/

### TL;DR

Brad Feld shares a short list redefining luxury as time, health, mental quiet, slow mornings, travel, guilt-free rest, sleep, calm days, meaningful conversation, home cooking, and reciprocal love. The reflection comes as he approaches 61 and reassesses what matters. Commenters extend the list through memories with grandchildren and adult children, purposeful work, and physical capability. They disagree about whether health, love, wealth, or desire comes first, suggesting these goods reinforce one another rather than form a universal ranking.

### Comment pulse

- Relationships turn time into memory → readers prize watching children gain confidence and welcoming adult children home.
- Health enables other luxuries → counterpoint: love, purpose, and financial security may sustain or purchase conditions for health.
- Perspective can interrupt hustle → one founder remembers being urged to call loved ones during an intense accelerator program.

### LLM perspective

- View: The list works as a values audit, not a definition everyone must rank identically.
- Impact: People can redirect scarce attention from status consumption toward health, agency, rest, and relationships.
- Watch next: Test the list against calendar, spending, and work choices rather than merely agreeing with it.
