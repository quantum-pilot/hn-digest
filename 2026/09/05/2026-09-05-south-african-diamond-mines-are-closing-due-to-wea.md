# South African diamond mines are closing due to weak sales and lab-grown stones

- Score: 152 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49580778) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/business/diamond-mines-are-closing-and-south-africans-have-few-alternatives-fa3b8cbf

### TL;DR

The Wall Street Journal reports De Beers will pause South Africa’s Venetia mine for two years as natural-diamond prices and sales weaken amid cheaper lab-grown stones. A local union says more than 1,200 jobs are at risk in a region with few comparable employers. Analyst Paul Zimnisky estimates roughly 10 major mines closed or suspended in two years, output is 25% below five years ago, and one-carat natural-diamond prices fell 47% in a decade. De Beers says US independent-retailer sales recently improved after renewed marketing.
