# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.2

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49571632) | Link: https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v4-2

### TL;DR

Artificial Analysis updated its composite intelligence index with harder, more realistic tasks and doubled private held-out weighting to 40%. Version 4.2 adds AA-Briefcase for multi-step knowledge work and GDP.pdf for reasoning across 4,592 PDF pages, removes saturated GPQA Diamond, and revises grading infrastructure. Its results place Claude Fable 5.1 first and GPT-6 Astra second, with Astra leading GDP.pdf at a 33.2% all-pass rate. Commenters welcomed harder private tests but questioned methodology changes, comparability, and real-world predictive value.
