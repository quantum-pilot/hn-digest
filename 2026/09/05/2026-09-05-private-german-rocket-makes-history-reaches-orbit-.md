# Private German rocket makes history, reaches orbit from European soil

- Score: 551 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49580369) | Link: https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/isar-aerospace-second-launch-norway-andoya-spaceport-spectrum-rocket

### TL;DR

Isar Aerospace’s second Spectrum rocket reportedly reached orbit from Norway’s Andøya Space Center, becoming the first orbital launch from Western European soil. After entering an elliptical orbit, the upper stage circularized and deployed five cubesats plus a non-deployable experiment, according to the company. The first flight failed after a vent-valve opening and loss of attitude control; later attempts faced valve, weather, boat, leak, and fluid-system delays. Isar claims Spectrum can carry 1,000 kilograms to low Earth orbit and its Munich facility could build over 30 annually.
