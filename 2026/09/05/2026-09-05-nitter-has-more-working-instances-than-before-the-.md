# Nitter has more working instances than before the takedowns

- Score: 677 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49571634) | Link: https://codeberg.org/mv12star/shitter/wiki/Instances

### TL;DR

A repository wiki lists multiple publicly working Nitter-style front ends, two Tor services, redirectors, rate-limited instances, and guidance for operators seeking session tokens or resilience against complaints. The captured page displays “976 Instances,” but the supplied material does not explain that counter or independently verify the headline’s before-and-after comparison. Discussion centered less on availability than ethics: some argued that reading X through Nitter still sustains its relevance, while others valued access to posts without logging in, advertising, tracking, or direct platform engagement.

### Comment pulse

- Boycott advocates said even indirect readership rewards people who keep posting on X and weakens collective exit pressure.
- Nitter users valued privacy and access to embedded links—counterpoint: others argued the content still preserves X’s cultural influence.
- Commenters distinguished decentralized resistance from mere rule evasion and debated whether individual abstention has meaningful effects.
