# The "$60 Gaming PC" – AMD BC-250 (2025)

- Score: 337 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49576386) | Link: https://devquasar.com/hardware/the-60-gaming-pc-amd-bc-250/

### TL;DR

The author presents AMD’s BC-250, a repurposed crypto-mining board built around a cut-down PS5 APU, as a highly capable budget Linux gaming project. The 2025 article quotes $70–$100 typical board prices and reports playable demanding games, but requires custom BIOS, cooling modifications, storage, power, and a case. Current commenters say boards now cost $150–$300-plus, idle near 80 watts, and vary under unlocked cores, making the build entertaining and powerful but neither reliably cheap nor turnkey.
