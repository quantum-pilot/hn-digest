# AI handles incidents, engineers lose touch with their systems

- Score: 379 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49574167) | Link: https://www.sylvainkalache.com/blog/ai-handles-incidents-engineers-lose-touch-with-their-systems

### TL;DR

AI incident-response systems can inspect alerts, query telemetry, form hypotheses, and sometimes implement fixes, reducing routine toil and likely average recovery time. The author warns that this also removes the low-risk incidents through which engineers build operational intuition, leaving underpracticed humans to handle rare, ambiguous failures automation cannot solve. Borrowing from aviation, he proposes recurring simulations, tabletop exercises, and hands-on control to counter “comprehension debt.” Commenters agreed on skill erosion but doubted most companies would fund disciplined practice without stronger incentives.

### Comment pulse

- Engineers described AI-generated systems they could review but no longer understood intuitively, making later troubleshooting increasingly dependent on AI.
- Some teams already rehearse recent outages weekly — counterpoint: critics argued prevention work may outperform generic incident drills.
- Practitioners said careful context, planning, and supervision can make AI strengthen diligence rather than replace troubleshooting judgment.
