# Netherlands pulls gold out of the US

- Score: 355 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49575034) | Link: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-04/why-the-netherlands-moved-its-gold-from-us-and-canada/107111990

### TL;DR

De Nederlandsche Bank moved 86 tonnes of gold formerly held in the US and Canada: 59 tonnes were sold and repurchased in London, while 27 tonnes were physically relocated through the Netherlands. DNB said London holdings meet trading standards and are more immediately usable during crises, improving resilience amid geopolitical unrest. The bank did not identify a specific threat. Experts connected the move to declining European trust in US policy and Federal Reserve independence, but those motives are interpretations. Commenters debated its geopolitical symbolism versus modest scale.

### Comment pulse

- Some viewed relocation as evidence that US leverage is weakening trust among allies and could raise financing costs.
- Others noted the amount was roughly 1.5% of reported foreign gold in US custody, arguing against exaggerating the systemic shift.
- Commenters explained London’s appeal as liquidity and tradability, not simply geographic safety or EU membership.
