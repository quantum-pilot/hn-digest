# Making Startups Powerful

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49684196) | Link: https://paulgraham.com/powerful.html

### TL;DR

Paul Graham proposes evaluating startups by how much power they can build, not merely near-term revenue. He recommends owning customer relationships and payment flows, creating platforms and network effects, going full stack, noticing unintended uses, helping customers earn money, acquiring customers and data early, exposing APIs, and playing long games competitors undervalue. The constraint is customer benefit: durable power must arise because a weak startup improves users’ lives. Discussion especially endorsed treating unexpected users as product signals rather than branding problems.

### Comment pulse

- Misuse reveals unmet demand → Users forcing a product into unintended jobs may expose a larger adjacent market.
- Generosity can compound → Creating more value than captured builds trust and distribution, though bootstrapped firms face tighter limits.
- Customer concentration cuts both ways → One large buyer simplifies delivery but can distort roadmap and architecture through leverage.
