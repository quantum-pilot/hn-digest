# Don't be the out of touch Kung Fu master

- Score: 255 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49677577) | Link: https://twitter.com/ID_AA_Carmack/status/2098443262214230095

### TL;DR

The post compares hand-written programming skills to martial arts that evolved from battlefield necessities into sports and self-development. As assembly moved above manual opcode encoding, AI may move careful manual coding from practical technique toward chosen craft. The author warns experts not to dismiss more effective tools simply because tradition built their identity. HN divided over the analogy: some welcomed escaping syntax and API drudgery to focus on systems, while others said wrestling with implementation creates understanding and that productivity is not programming's only purpose.
