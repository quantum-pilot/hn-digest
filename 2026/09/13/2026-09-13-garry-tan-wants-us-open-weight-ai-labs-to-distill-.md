# Garry Tan wants US open-weight AI labs to 'distill' frontier models, too

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49685253) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/y-combinators-garry-tan-wants-u-s-open-weight-ai-labs-to-distill-frontier-models-too/

### TL;DR

Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan opposes regulatory restrictions on model distillation and proposes allowing American open-weight labs to learn from domestic frontier models through legitimate customer access. He does not endorse fraud or stolen credentials. Tan argues proprietary labs trained on broad public and copyrighted material, making strict control over API outputs hypocritical, while open weights reduce dependence on one dominant provider. Discussion largely agreed on incumbent inconsistency but noted providers can still refuse customers who systematically harvest outputs.

### Comment pulse

- Frontier labs lack moral exclusivity → Their models depended on vast quantities of human work gathered without individual permission.
- Service refusal remains legitimate → Lawful distillation does not obligate a provider to keep serving detected extraction workloads.
- Tan has aligned incentives → Cheaper open models would reduce costs and dependency across YC’s startup portfolio.
