# Fable 5.1 Solves the Cyphral Distich, a 370-year-old cipher

- Score: 930 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49688695) | Link: https://www.vals.ai/blogs/fable-solves-cyphral-distich

### TL;DR

An experimenter reports Fable 5.1 selected and solved Sir Thomas Urquhart's 64-number Cyphral Distich in 44 minutes using 176,000 tokens. The proposed book cipher indexes the corresponding word in each of 32 nearby Proquiritations, yielding a Royalist prayer for Charles II. It also produced a mostly decoded 285-number Octastich, with nine unresolved letters and pagination caveats requiring physical verification. HN praised problem selection and persistence but found a 2014 comment already suggesting a book cipher, weakening novelty claims.
