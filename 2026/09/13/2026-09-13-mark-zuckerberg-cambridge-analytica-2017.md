# Mark Zuckerberg: "Cambridge Analytica" (2017)

- Score: 296 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49688157) | Link: https://twitter.com/TechEmails/status/2099214399840059428

### TL;DR

A transcribed internal Facebook email from January 30, 2017, released through 2026 litigation materials, shows Mark Zuckerberg asking how novel Cambridge Analytica’s Trump-campaign work actually was. Andrew Bosworth replied that the campaign’s advantage likely came from fully using Facebook’s tools: rapid creative testing, audience segmentation, video, and base-focused fundraising. He was skeptical Cambridge Analytica possessed uniquely advanced data or expertise. The exchange concerns advertising strategy, not a retrospective account of the later-publicized collection of Facebook friends’ profile data.
