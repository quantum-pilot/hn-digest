# Data collected by cars and sold to third parties

- Score: 427 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49683953) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/column/994172/your-car-is-selling-your-data

### TL;DR

The article describes an FTC settlement barring General Motors for five years from selling customer data to reporting agencies and brokers. GM had collected behaviors such as speeding and nighttime driving through its OnStar Smart Driver feature, then shared profiles with LexisNexis and Verisk for insurance use; some drivers reportedly saw premiums rise without understanding their enrollment. GM must improve location-tracking controls and data access and deletion. HN users described suspected continuing telemetry, opaque broker chains, and narrow socially useful applications amid broader privacy concerns.
