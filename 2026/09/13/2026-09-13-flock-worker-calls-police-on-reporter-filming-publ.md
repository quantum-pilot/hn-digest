# Flock worker calls police on reporter filming public camera installation

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49683853) | Link: https://www.investigatetv.com/2026/09/08/flock-worker-calls-police-investigatetv-reporter-filming-public-camera-installation/

### TL;DR

InvestigateTV reports that a Flock installer called 911 after reporter Brendan Keefe filmed a public camera installation and followed his vehicle at a distance. Three officers stopped Keefe, acknowledged his conduct did not appear unlawful, and released him after roughly 17 minutes. Flock says workers may call police over perceived threats or harassment but does not oppose public photography. A separate employee called police on creators filming near a distribution center. HN debated worker safety, lawful reporting, and hypocrisy from a surveillance company.
