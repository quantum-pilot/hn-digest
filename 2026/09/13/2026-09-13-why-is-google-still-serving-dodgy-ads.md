# Why is Google still serving dodgy ads?

- Score: 813 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49686445) | Link: https://www.atomic14.com/2026/09/13/why-is-google-still-serving-dodgy-ads

### TL;DR

The author reports repeatedly seeing and reporting a YouTube ad that imitates an iPhone storage warning, only for Google to say it violated no policy. When shown the creative, Google’s Gemini classified it as deceptive because it mimicked system UI, displayed nonfunctional controls, and used fear-based claims. The author asks why comparable automated review is not deployed. Commenters describe recurring scam ads, ineffective blocking and reporting, and publisher costs, while debating whether weak enforcement reflects scale, incentives, negligence, or broader ad-market economics.

### Comment pulse

- Review outcomes contradict stated policy → the reported ad mimics a system alert, yet repeated complaints allegedly produced no enforcement.
- Publishers cannot reliably block recurring scammers → new accounts and rotating hosted subdomains bypass coarse domain and category controls.
- Liability could realign incentives → commenters argue platforms and embedding publishers profit from distribution while users absorb fraud risk.
