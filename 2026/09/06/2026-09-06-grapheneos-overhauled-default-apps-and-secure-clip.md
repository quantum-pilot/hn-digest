# GrapheneOS Overhauled Default Apps and Secure Clipboard

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49590512) | Link: https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/117225539756835649

### TL;DR

GrapheneOS says its next release will modernize the bundled Messaging app with an Android Compose interface plus numerous fixes and improvements. The supplied post itself does not describe a secure clipboard; commenters clarified that clipboard work appeared in a separate GrapheneOS thread, making the HN title broader than this captured source. Discussion welcomed the longer-term plan for independent RCS with MLS-based end-to-end encryption, which could eventually remove reliance on Google Messages, and debated future replacements for aging AOSP gallery and keyboard apps.

### Comment pulse

- Users welcomed a non-Google RCS path, but the post describes it as a longer-term plan rather than this release.
- Commenters sought better keyboards and galleries—counterpoint: FUTO’s non-open-source license was said to prevent GrapheneOS bundling.
- GrapheneOS reportedly plans a substantially modified ReFra fork, while its exact upstream relationship remains undecided.
