# Following legal advice, the Nitter project will continue

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49589003) | Link: https://github.com/zedeus/nitter

### TL;DR

Nitter says it will continue operating after obtaining legal advice, despite August 24 cease-and-desist letters from X demanding permanent removal of its repository and public instances. The project promises more details soon, so the supplied update does not disclose counsel’s reasoning, jurisdiction, negotiated terms, or resolution of X’s demands. Nitter remains an AGPLv3 alternative frontend that proxies requests, removes ads and client-side JavaScript, and aims to reduce tracking while offering lightweight pages and RSS. Commenters welcomed continuation but recognized ongoing legal and operational uncertainty.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters called alternative access important because substantial public information remains exclusive to X and increasingly requires login.
- Commenters noted that legal pressure can exhaust small projects regardless of merits; jurisdiction and funding remain consequential unknowns.
- Broader discussion favored adversarial interoperability and portable social graphs, while acknowledging incumbent network effects.

- View: Continuing after advice signals legal confidence, not a court victory or permanent protection.
