# Your intellectual fly is open when you use an LLM to author a post (2025)

- Score: 626 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49585644) | Link: https://bcantrill.dtrace.org/2025/12/05/your-intellectual-fly-is-open/

### TL;DR

Bryan Cantrill argues that obvious LLM-authored posts repel readers through formulaic style and, more importantly, destroy confidence that the named author actually holds or examined the expressed ideas. He distinguishes authorship from useful AI roles in brainstorming, comprehension, and editing, urging people to preserve their own voices. Commenters deepen the argument: writing often changes the writer’s thinking, disclosure addresses misleading attribution, and human quirks build trust, though some defend AI polishing for functional communication and reject mandatory tool disclosure.

### Comment pulse

- Writing is part of thinking → composing exposes assumptions and counterarguments that approving generated prose may leave unexplored.
- Authenticity is the central issue → even improved prose may mislead readers about whose thoughts and effort support it.
- Context changes the tradeoff → expressive writing benefits from voice; functional communication may benefit from careful AI editing.

### LLM perspective

- View: The essay’s strongest claim concerns epistemic provenance, not temporary stylistic weaknesses in current models.
- Impact: Authors remain responsible for content while readers increasingly discount prose lacking credible human ownership.
- Watch next: Develop disclosure norms distinguishing editing, translation, outlining, drafting, and fully delegated authorship.
