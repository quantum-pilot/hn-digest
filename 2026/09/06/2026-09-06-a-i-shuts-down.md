# A/I shuts down

- Score: 566 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586898) | Link: https://keepitfree.ai/announcements/a/i-shuts-down-stay-human/

### TL;DR

Autistici/Inventati says it will shut down after 25 years of providing free, privacy-oriented, politically engaged digital services. The collective says a global-terrorist designation and related pressure make continued operation dangerous for users, collaborators, and their families; it plans to provide backup instructions for blogs, mailboxes, and websites, while warning that services may become unreachable unpredictably. The statement presents closure as harm reduction rather than surrender. Commenters mourned the loss but disagreed sharply about the designation’s basis and the collective’s responsibility.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters saw financial and personal pressure isolating infrastructure providers until continued resistance becomes untenable.
- Some questioned treating a communications provider as responsible for users’ acts — counterpoint: others considered the designation potentially justified but unverified.
- Commenters noted decentralized hosting still depends on domains, payments, and identifiable operators vulnerable to external pressure.
