# Cloud in a Bottle: making self-hosting accessible to everyone

- Score: 613 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49582000) | Link: https://cloudinabottle.org/blog/launch-post

### TL;DR

Cloud in a Bottle proposes an open-source personal-cloud platform that makes containerized web apps feel like installing smartphone apps. It runs hardened rootless containers on Ubuntu, adds unified authentication and permissioned inter-app capabilities, claims zero telemetry, and keeps self-hosted and managed deployments on identical code. Imbue has privately tested it for over six months, but acknowledges a small app catalog and some technical demands for early users. Commenters welcomed simpler portable hosting while identifying backups, storage pricing, networking, DNS, and maintenance as harder barriers.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters blamed cloud conventions and overbuilt application stacks for turning simple self-hosting into expensive operational work.
- Undisclosed promotional repository issues drew criticism; the author apologized conditionally while checking whether their team created them.
- Commenters said deployment is largely solved, whereas domains, DNS, port forwarding, backups, upgrades, and recovery still block ordinary users.

### LLM perspective
