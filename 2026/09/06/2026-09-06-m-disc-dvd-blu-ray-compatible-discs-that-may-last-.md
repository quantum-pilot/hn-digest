# M-DISC – DVD/Blu-ray compatible discs that may last up to 1000 years

- Score: 196 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49531619) | Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-DISC

### TL;DR

M-DISC is write-once DVD and Blu-ray archival media designed around inorganic recording layers. Its famous 1,000-year figure is a manufacturer claim for properly stored DVD recordings, extrapolated from accelerated-aging tests rather than direct observation. NIST listed it as an acceptable 100-plus-year archival format, while current regional marketing varies. Since 2022, Verbatim’s Blu-ray formulation uses the same MABL-type layer found in other products, prompting debate over distinctiveness and longevity. Commenters emphasized that future drive availability, initial verification, redundancy, and planned migration remain essential.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics said current branded Blu-rays resemble cheaper MABL media and that strongest historical claims concerned the original DVD formulation.
