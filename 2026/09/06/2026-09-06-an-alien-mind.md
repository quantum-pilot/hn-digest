# An Alien Mind

- Score: 381 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49588080) | Link: https://openai.com/index/an-alien-mind/

### TL;DR

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki argues that scaling could produce increasingly alien, self-improving machine intelligence whose alignment and behavior remain poorly understood. He distinguishes goal-following from broader value alignment, says chain-of-thought monitoring is weakening as models gain capability, and expects safety confidence to constrain further scaling. Although defensive AI and automated alignment research motivate continued development, he says no lab can responsibly maintain maximum speed much longer without shared safety bars, third-party oversight, voluntary pauses, and international coordination.

### Comment pulse

- The defensive-development case was read as arms-race logic — counterpoint: others said recognizing competition does not make it avoidable.
- Commenters questioned whether corporate incentives and concentrated control are compatible with the essay’s caution.
