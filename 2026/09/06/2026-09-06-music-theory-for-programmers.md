# Music Theory for Programmers

- Score: 368 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49541888) | Link: https://runjs.app/blog/music-theory-for-programmers

### TL;DR

The article teaches musical foundations through JavaScript and Web Audio, beginning with sampled waveforms, envelopes, harmonics, frequency ratios, octaves, consonance, and equal temperament before constructing scales, chords, and a progression. Its programmer-oriented thesis is that physics and arithmetic explain many conventions. Commenters sharply dispute that reduction: musicians say the piece omits harmony, rhythm, cultural variation, and important theory, while supporters value it as an approachable explanation of acoustic underpinnings that can motivate deeper study rather than replace it.

### Comment pulse

- The coding approach offers an accessible bridge → interactive sound examples let programmers test frequency, timbre, intervals, and tuning directly.
- Scope is contested → critics call it acoustics or musical foundations, not sufficient music theory for performance or composition.
- Physics is not destiny → harmonic relationships matter, but culture, instrument spectra, rhythm, and convention also shape musical systems.
