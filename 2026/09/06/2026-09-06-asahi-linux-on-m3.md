# Asahi Linux on M3

- Score: 410 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698) | Link: https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/

### TL;DR

Asahi Linux has added official installer support for Macs using M3, M3 Pro, and M3 Max chips, gated behind Expert mode while the work matures. The project says webcams, microphones, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, USB up to 10 Gb/s, and hardware video decoding including AV1 largely work. Major gaps remain: GPU acceleration and full display-controller support are unfinished, sleep and MacBook HDMI are unavailable, and M3 Ultra Mac Studio is unsupported. The team hopes to remove Expert mode around Fedora Linux 45 beta.
