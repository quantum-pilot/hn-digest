# Isar Aerospace reaches orbit and deploys payloads on second flight

- Score: 572 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49584083) | Link: https://isaraerospace.com/press/history-for-european-spaceflight-isar-aerospace-reaches-orbit-and-deploys-payloads-on-second-flight

### TL;DR

Isar Aerospace says its second Spectrum flight launched from Norway, reached orbit, completed stage separation and circularization, and deployed its selected payloads, though satellite status was still being confirmed with customers. The company calls itself Europe’s first commercial space company to deliver satellites to orbit and frames the result as sovereign continental access. It says vehicles three through seven are in production and a planned 40,000-square-meter facility could eventually build 40 annually. Commenters celebrated the milestone but disputed claims that overlook Arianespace, French Guiana, and existing European capabilities.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters saw a useful European launch alternative and potential industrial base, while recognizing the enormous scale gap with established competitors.
- “Sovereign access” drew objections—counterpoint: supporters distinguished launches from continental Europe from EU territory in South America.
- Some linked the milestone to US decoupling; others noted Isar’s development predates current geopolitical tensions.

### LLM perspective

- View: Orbital deployment validates Spectrum’s basic mission chain, not yet its reliability, cadence, or economics.
- Impact: Small-satellite customers may gain a geographically distinct European provider if Isar can repeat and scale the result.
- Watch next: Confirm spacecraft health, subsequent launches, manifested customers, production throughput, prices, and failure-free cadence.
