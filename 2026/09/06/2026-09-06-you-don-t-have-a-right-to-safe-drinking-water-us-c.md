# You Don't Have a Right to Safe Drinking Water, US Court Rules

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49584637) | Link: https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2026/09/drinking-water-mississippi-lawsuit/

### TL;DR

The Fifth Circuit affirmed dismissal of Jackson residents’ federal constitutional claims over alleged lead-contaminated water and withheld public-health information. The court held that these alleged harms did not violate a deeply rooted constitutional right actionable through the plaintiffs’ chosen theory; it did not authorize poisoning or eliminate statutory, state-constitutional, tort, or other possible claims. Commenters dispute the moral and constitutional reasoning, but several emphasize the case’s narrower procedural context: personal liability for officials under Section 1983 and qualified-immunity constraints.
