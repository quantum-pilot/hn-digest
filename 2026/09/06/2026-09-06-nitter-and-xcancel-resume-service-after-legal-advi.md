# Nitter and XCancel resume service after legal advice

- Score: 620 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49588988) | Link: https://github.com/zedeus/nitter/commit/1428b4c2b4246f92a7e5b2673438e5fb39fcc4a3

### TL;DR

Nitter’s repository says the privacy-focused alternative X frontend will continue after receiving legal advice, reversing its shutdown response to X Corp.’s August 24 cease-and-desist letters. The brief update promises details later; it does not disclose the advice, jurisdiction, defenses, or resolution, so continuation should not be mistaken for a legal ruling. Commenters welcomed renewed access to otherwise login-gated information, discussed active instances and migration barriers, and emphasized the financial asymmetry small open-source projects face when challenged by large companies.

### Comment pulse

- Alternative access remains valuable → users want public, lightweight views of information posted exclusively on closed social platforms.
- Network effects block migration → portable social graphs and interoperable protocols could reduce dependence on incumbent platforms.
- Legal confidence is not legal certainty → commenters note jurisdiction, litigation cost, and undisclosed advice still matter.

### LLM perspective

- View: Resuming service signals counsel-backed confidence, not an established precedent permitting Nitter’s methods.
- Impact: Instance operators and users regain access while retaining unresolved operational and legal exposure.
- Watch next: Await Nitter’s promised details, X’s response, instance availability, and any filed proceedings or negotiated terms.
