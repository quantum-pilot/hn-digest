# Growing proof that autonomous cars save lives

- Score: 386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49629886) | Link: https://spectrum.ieee.org/are-self-driving-cars-safe

### TL;DR

The article says evidence increasingly favors automated driving safety: cited studies associate pedestrian-aware braking with 27% fewer pedestrian crashes and Waymo vehicles with substantially fewer police-reportable and injury crashes than human drivers in matched cities. It also emphasizes major caveats, including Waymo’s limited cities and fair-weather operations, underreported human crashes, uneven operator disclosure, and absent national performance standards. The proposed remedy is better federal reporting as deployments expand, rather than treating current comparisons as definitive.
