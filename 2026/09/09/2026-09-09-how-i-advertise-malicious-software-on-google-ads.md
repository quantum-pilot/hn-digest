# How I advertise malicious software on Google Ads

- Score: 419 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49624856) | Link: https://xlii.space/eng/malicious-software-on-google-ads/

### TL;DR

The developer of RACE, a signed and notarized macOS terminal multiplexer, says Google Ads suspended his account for “Malicious software” and a “Compromised Site” after he spent $500. Safe Browsing, Search Console, VirusTotal, signature checks, code review, and infrastructure inspection reportedly found no issue, yet repeated appeals supplied no actionable explanation. After the post gained Hacker News attention, Google reinstated the account without identifying the trigger, leaving the developer unable to know whether anything required fixing.
