# Copyright does more harm than good and should be abolished

- Score: 304 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49622042) | Link: https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/117231186011306184

### TL;DR

A short post argues copyright should be abolished because it allegedly benefits corporate monopolies more than individual creators, enables wrongful takedowns, and restricts repair, modification, and backups. HN discussion largely favored reform but disagreed on remedy: proposals ranged from two-year terms to 20–30 years, narrower reproduction-and-distribution rights, stronger penalties for false DMCA claims, and abolition. Defenders warned that weakening copyright could let technology companies exploit creators without payment, while creators in the thread described both piracy losses and current-law harms.

### Comment pulse

- Duration is the core problem for many → most revenue arrives early, while protection can extend decades beyond an author's death.
- Scope and enforcement also matter → shorter terms would not necessarily prevent access restrictions or unilateral, mistaken takedowns.
- Abolition could expand remixing and public access → counterpoint: creators may lose leverage against companies copying their work without licenses.
