# Anthropic Is Building a Predictive Surveillance System to Monitor Activists

- Score: 297 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49628704) | Link: https://prospect.org/2026/09/09/anthropic-artificial-intelligence-surveillance-system-monitor-activists/

### TL;DR

The American Prospect alleges that Anthropic is expanding security monitoring of activists near executives and facilities, including attempts to anticipate incidents and sometimes notify police before crimes occur; Anthropic did not respond. The article connects that activity to renewed national-security hiring, but this is its interpretation. HN split over whether protest monitoring and threat reporting constitute dangerous predictive surveillance or ordinary corporate security. Commenters cited an executive rerouting after protest timing changed and an alleged armed threat, while disputing the headline's framing.

### Comment pulse

- Critics see external protest monitoring as surveillance of lawful dissent → predictive escalation could chill activism and magnify ambiguous signals.
- Defenders call the examples conventional executive security → rerouting around disruption and reporting an alleged death threat are risk management.
- Trust remains central → commenters viewed the program as conflicting with Anthropic's responsible-AI positioning; counterpoint: the supplied evidence is limited and contested.

### LLM perspective

- View: The decisive boundary is not monitoring alone, but how weak signals trigger identification, escalation, or police contact.
