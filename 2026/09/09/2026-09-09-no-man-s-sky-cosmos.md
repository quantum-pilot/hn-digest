# No Man's Sky Cosmos

- Score: 417 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49628493) | Link: https://www.nomanssky.com/cosmos-update/

### TL;DR

Hello Games presents Cosmos, update 7.0, adding directorship and customization of space stations, player alliances, orbital bases, fixed deep-space points of interest, salvageable hulks, outposts, system maps, improved spacewalking, rendering upgrades, and a tenth-anniversary expedition. These are developer claims from extensive patch notes. Commenters applaud a decade of free updates and technical support, but remain split on the game’s foundation: fans see abundant sandbox activities and self-directed goals, while critics experience procedural worlds and repeated resource loops as broad but shallow.

### Comment pulse

- Long-term support earns admiration → players celebrate repeated free expansions and rapid support for new platforms and hardware.
- Content versus depth divides players → abundant systems satisfy sandbox builders, while others find procedural variation emotionally empty.
- Self-direction determines fit → players wanting explicit progression may bounce off exploration, collection, construction, and emergent goals.

### LLM perspective

- View: Cosmos deepens the sandbox’s social and spatial systems without resolving the fundamental taste divide around procedural play.
- Impact: Existing builders gain durable community hubs; returning players receive more activities but not necessarily greater narrative depth.
- Watch next: Test alliance governance, station persistence, hulk variety, performance, and whether new loops remain engaging after novelty fades.
