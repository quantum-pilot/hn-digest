# DeepSeek launching v4.1 flash cheaper and more capable than v4 pro

- Score: 412 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49624603) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49624603

### TL;DR

DSeek says V4.1 Flash will launch around September 10 Beijing time, outperform V4 Pro across its tested metrics, and temporarily receive all Pro endpoint traffic at Flash pricing. Off-peak prices are listed as $0.003 for cached input, $0.15 for uncached input, and $0.60 for output, with peak rates doubled, though the source omits the billing unit. HN welcomed cheaper, faster models but strongly questioned replacing a validated production model without version pinning or a deprecation window.
