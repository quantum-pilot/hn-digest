# Apple Watch Series 12

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49630566) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/introducing-apple-watch-series-12-with-the-all-new-health-sensing-system/

### TL;DR

Apple introduced Series 12 from $399, adding five-second heart-rate sampling, more frequent HRV readings, a readiness score, faster charging, and up to 10 hours of outdoor-workout battery life while retaining a 24-hour everyday estimate. Later software will add Health Age and Audio Intelligence, including sound alerts, 15-second Live Rewind text, and conversation summaries. Apple says isolated hardware immediately deletes raw audio. HN focused less on fitness gains than consent, legality, upgrade value, and continued frustration with daily charging.
