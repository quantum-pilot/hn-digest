# iPhone Duo

- Score: 1274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49630931) | Link: https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/

### TL;DR

Apple’s iPhone Duo is its first foldable phone, pairing a 7.6-inch inner display with a 5.4-inch outer display and software modes for side-by-side apps, hands-free viewing, and multiple orientations. Apple lists an A20 Pro chip, titanium frame and hinge cover, IP68 resistance, dual batteries, two 48MP rear cameras, Touch ID, and worldwide eSIM-only service. Preorders begin October 16 and availability October 23; Apple claims up to 31 hours of inner-display video playback.
