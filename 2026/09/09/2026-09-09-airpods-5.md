# AirPods 5

- Score: 481 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49630253) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-introduces-airpods-5-with-best-in-class-open-ear-active-noise-cancellation/

### TL;DR

Apple announced $129 AirPods 5 and a $149 wireless-charging version, available September 18. Apple claims its open-ear ANC removes 50% more noise than AirPods 4 with ANC, while new acoustics, Adaptive EQ, Siri AI, and Live Translation broaden the feature set. The wireless-case model adds stem volume control, five hours of ANC playback, and 22 hours with its case. HN discussion weighed wireless convenience and ANC against wired fidelity, ecosystem dependencies, battery limits, and Apple's marketing of inherited Pro features.
