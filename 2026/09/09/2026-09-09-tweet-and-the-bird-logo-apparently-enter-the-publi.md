# “Tweet” and the bird logo apparently enter the public domain

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49621751) | Link: https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2026/09/tweet-and-the-bird-logo-apparently-enter-the-public-domain-but-x-maintains-its-grip-on-the-twitter-mark-for-now-x-v-project-bluebird.htm

### TL;DR

Legal scholar Eric Goldman analyzes a preliminary-injunction ruling in X Corp. v. Project Bluebird. The court found X likely had not abandoned TWITTER because its App Store listing uses “formerly known as Twitter,” but likely abandoned TWEET and the bird logo, which disappeared from current branding and were repudiated during rebranding. Goldman stresses this is not a final merits decision. Commenters further caution that loss of trademark rights does not necessarily place a logo in the public domain because copyright may independently remain.
