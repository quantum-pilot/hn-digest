# Shopify acquires Tailwind

- Score: 1078 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49626190) | Link: https://tailwindcss.com/blog/tailwind-is-joining-shopify

### TL;DR

Tailwind creator Adam Wathan announces that Tailwind is joining Shopify, which he describes as a stable long-term home where the framework can evolve against a large real product. He says Tailwind CSS and other open-source projects will remain MIT-licensed and team-maintained. Existing commercial customers retain access, but new Tailwind Plus and ui.sh signups will close as commercial growth stops. Commenters connect the deal to declining documentation traffic and earlier layoffs, while debating whether AI, product stagnation, or the template business model caused the pressure.
