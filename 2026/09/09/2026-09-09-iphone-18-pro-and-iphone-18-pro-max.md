# iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49630151) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/

### TL;DR

Apple announced iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max with a variable-aperture 48MP main camera, A20 Pro chip, redesigned vapor chamber, smaller Dynamic Island, and Siri AI beta through iOS 27. Apple claims substantial sustained-performance and battery gains, including up to 36 and 45 hours of video playback on eSIM-only Pro and Pro Max models. Prices start at $1,199 and $1,299; preorders open September 12, with availability September 18. Several AI features carry regional, language, or usage limitations.
