# How Fairphone built the Fairphone Gen 6+

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49563784) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/nearly-impossible-how-fairphone-built-the-ethical-repairable-fairphone-gen-6/

### TL;DR

Fairphone’s $650 Gen 6+ prioritizes longevity: users can replace 12 parts with one included T5 screwdriver, and the company promises software updates and replacement parts through 2033. The US-bound phone has 12GB RAM, microSD and physical SIM support, but trades sleekness for a thicker plastic body, IP55 splash resistance, and slower, weaker cameras than premium rivals. Fairphone says ethical sourcing, recycled materials, living wages, and five-year use guide its design. Commenters praised repairability yet questioned update timeliness, security hardware, and whether parts truly outlast support windows.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime owners reported multi-year use and battery replacements, supporting repairability’s value while exposing finite security-support dates.
- Security-focused commenters cited missing protections and delayed patches—counterpoint: others viewed those standards as unusually stringent for typical threat models.
- Some users wanted alternative operating systems; others noted repairable competitors or older conventional phones can also last many years.
