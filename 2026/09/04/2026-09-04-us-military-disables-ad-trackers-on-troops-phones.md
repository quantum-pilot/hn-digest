# US Military disables ad trackers on troops' phones

- Score: 188 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49564555) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/04/military-disables-phone-ad-trackers

### TL;DR

Reuters reports that U.S. military branches disabled advertising identifiers on various government phones and computers after concerns that commercially sold location data could help track deployed personnel. Timelines and device coverage differ by branch, and lawmakers Ron Wyden and Pat Harrigan requested a Pentagon investigation. A privacy technologist called the change positive but warned that apps can combine other device, network, and location signals. Commenters likewise saw advertising-ID blocking as incomplete, while debating dedicated phones, personal-device bans, and operational practicality.

### Comment pulse

- Advertising IDs are one leak among many → fingerprinting and app-derived signals may still expose personnel after identifiers disappear.
- Personal-phone bans are impractical → bases are living environments, while sensitive facilities already impose localized device restrictions.
