# Google AI Mode shows same products 21.6% more expensive than traditional search

- Score: 383 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49563386) | Link: https://productrise.app/blog/google-ai-mode-prefers-more-expensive-products

### TL;DR

Productrise says its 23-day study compared over two million listings across more than 100,000 paired Google shopping queries. For identical products appearing in both interfaces, AI Mode’s lead price averaged 21.6% higher; prices differed in 38.1% of matches, with AI Mode higher 68.4% of those times. Overall medians were $149 versus $100, but product overlap was only 1.28%. Commenters cautioned that traditional shopping emphasizes low-priced offers, while AI Mode may favor manufacturer or informational results, and displayed prices can omit shipping or conditions.

### Comment pulse

- Critics said the comparison partly captures different product-selection goals, not simply inferior price ranking within identical inventories.
- Some examples suggested AI Mode might surface manufacturer pricing while shopping results favor cheaper third parties with shipping or checkout caveats.
- Users still considered hidden higher-priced defaults harmful for buyers who assume AI recommendations already performed comparison shopping.
