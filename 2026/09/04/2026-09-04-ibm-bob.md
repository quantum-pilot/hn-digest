# IBM Bob

- Score: 272 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49563851) | Link: https://bob.ibm.com/

### TL;DR

IBM markets Bob as an AI development partner spanning IDE and shell use, parallel agents, natural-language implementation, CI/CD automation, analytics, and enterprise modernization. It emphasizes Java, RPG, COBOL, IBM i, mainframes, Red Hat integrations, and approval-oriented guardrails. Customer testimonials claim faster migrations and rapid prototypes, but the page provides no independent evaluation or technical comparison. HN discussion largely mocked the historically loaded “Bob” name and corporate tone; commenters also questioned a testimonial carousel dominated by managers, executives, advocates, and IBM-associated voices.
