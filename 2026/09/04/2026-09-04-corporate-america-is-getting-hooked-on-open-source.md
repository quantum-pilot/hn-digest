# Corporate America is getting hooked on open-source AI

- Score: 297 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49566137) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/04/technology/open-source-ai-anthropic-openai.html

### TL;DR

Companies are increasing their use of open and open-weight AI models to reduce cost, customize systems, and gain deployment control. AT&T says such models rose from 20% to 40% of its AI usage and can save up to 80%, while OpenRouter reports open models reached 58% of U.S. usage last month. The article says enterprises still mix approaches: closed models handle demanding work, while open models serve simpler specialized tasks. Privacy, licensing, infrastructure, model quality, and Chinese-origin systems remain adoption constraints.

### Comment pulse

- Some commenters saw models becoming commodities that pressure closed-provider pricing — counterpoint: hardware, scaling, and frontier capability remain defensible advantages.
