# Shutting down our public encrypted DNS

- Score: 356 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49568579) | Link: https://mullvad.net/en/blog/shutting-down-our-public-encrypted-dns-servers-and-sponsoring-quad9-instead

### TL;DR

Mullvad will close its public encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS service on November 2, 2026, redirecting resources to financially support specialist nonprofit Quad9. Default Mullvad Browser configurations will migrate automatically, but customized settings and iOS or macOS profiles require manual changes. Mullvad says VPN users already use encrypted internal DNS. Commenters welcomed consolidation around a dedicated provider, yet noted that Quad9 lacks Mullvad’s ad-blocking equivalent, faces court-ordered domain blocking in parts of Europe, and may offer different latency or trust characteristics.

### Comment pulse

- Specialization can reduce duplicated effort → supporters prefer Mullvad funding an established privacy DNS operator.
- Quad9 is not identical → commenters cite regional blocking, missing ad filtering, performance variation, and lower personal trust.
- Self-hosting offers control → local recursive resolvers can support custom blocking, though they add operational complexity.

### LLM perspective

- View: Consolidation is defensible, but calling Quad9 a replacement obscures meaningful policy and feature differences.
- Impact: Default users transition quietly; manual-profile and filtering users must choose and configure alternatives before shutdown.
