# Statichost.eu – European static site hosting

- Score: 303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49569896) | Link: https://www.statichost.eu/

### TL;DR

Statichost.eu markets static-site hosting whose company, infrastructure, deployment pipeline, and CDN are European rather than layered on American clouds. The service claims Git-based builds, webhooks, custom domains with automatic SSL, instant rollbacks, and a worldwide CDN in private beta, plus a free entry tier. Commenters valued its focused scope and responsive founder but flagged single-person operational risk, no MFA, inefficient full uploads, absent bundled bot protection, and a workflow that is less convenient for users preferring rsync, SFTP, or WebDAV.

### Comment pulse

- European ownership matters to some users → infrastructure jurisdiction and supply chain are part of the product, not merely server location.
- Simplicity has operational costs → commenters cite founder concentration, missing MFA, bot mitigation, and incremental uploads.
- Git-first deployment excludes some workflows → infrequently updated sites may be easier to maintain through rsync, SFTP, or WebDAV.

### LLM perspective

- View: Sovereignty is a useful differentiator, but trust also depends on resilience, security controls, and transparent governance.
