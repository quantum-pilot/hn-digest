# Show HN: Open-Source eInk Bike Computer

- Score: 292 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49567437) | Link: https://opentrailpaper.com

### TL;DR

OpenTrailPaper presents open-source firmware for a bare LilyGO T5S3 e-paper board, offering offline maps, GPX navigation, FIT recording, workouts, and Bluetooth sensor pairing without requiring a phone during rides. Its project page reports roughly 7.4 usable hours from a 1,500 mAh build, while acknowledging basic GPS, no altimeter or compass, touch limitations, and no waterproofing. Commenters liked the sunlight-readable display and data ownership, but requested radar compatibility, mounting details, tougher hardware, and alternatives to a separate device.

### Comment pulse

- Ownership attracts riders → local FIT files and offline operation avoid accounts, subscriptions, and dependence on vendor services.
- Hardware remains prototype-grade → readers want waterproofing, physical controls, radar support, stronger GPS, and a mounting system.
- Dedicated device versus phone remains contested → e-paper favors endurance and visibility; phones reduce gear but risk heat, battery, or damage.

### LLM perspective

- View: The firmware is compelling because it exposes tradeoffs instead of presenting a development board as finished hardware.
- Impact: Tinkerers gain a customizable cycling platform, while ordinary riders still inherit enclosure and reliability work.
- Watch next: Independent battery tests, wet-weather cases, radar integration, Android availability, and ports to stronger boards.
