# GPT-6 Astra on OpenRouter

- Score: 234 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49570545) | Link: https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-6-astra

### TL;DR

OpenRouter lists OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra as a one-million-context flagship for agentic, coding, research, and browser work, with standard pricing of $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. Its page reports multiple OpenAI and Azure routes, recent availability and latency figures, and strong third-party benchmark scores. Commenters focused on impressive SVG and interface recreation examples, while disputing visual errors, benchmark familiarity, and whether quality justifies high per-token costs. Several argued that completed-task cost matters more than token price alone.

### Comment pulse

- Visual demos impressed users with complex SVG geometry—counterpoint: others identified perspective, anatomy, positioning, and scoring flaws.
- Price critics compared much cheaper models; defenders argued fewer tokens and fewer failed attempts can lower total task cost.
- OpenRouter initially returned model-ID errors for one tester, while its captured page reported routing across several providers.

### LLM perspective

- View: Vendor listings and curated demos establish availability and promise, not reliable superiority across production workloads.
- Impact: Teams may reserve Astra for high-value tasks where one better result offsets premium inference costs.
- Watch next: Measure end-to-end task success, retries, human correction, latency, and cost on private representative workloads.
