# Record-High 89% in U.S. Say Government Corruption Widespread

- Score: 439 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49570772) | Link: https://news.gallup.com/poll/713933/record-high-say-government-corruption-widespread.aspx

### TL;DR

Gallup reports that 89% of 1,000 U.S. adults surveyed by phone in May–June 2026 perceived government corruption as widespread, a 20-year high with a ±4.1-point sampling margin. The measure rose ten points since 2025, driven especially by Democrats, while Republicans still registered 83%. Perceived business corruption reached 71%. Commenters treated the result as alarming but debated whether it reflects actual corruption, partisan information environments, or a broadly shared feeling that may outrun specific evidence.

### Comment pulse

- Measurement needs restraint → perceived prevalence is not direct proof of corrupt acts, and commenters disagree about historical continuity.
