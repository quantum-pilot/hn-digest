# Nobody is saying why OpenAI and Anthropic had outages

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49567594) | Link: https://www.wired.com/story/nobody-is-saying-why-openai-and-anthropic-had-outages-today/

### TL;DR

OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI experienced overlapping disruptions Thursday morning, but the supplied reporting establishes no common cause. OpenAI attributed its partial ChatGPT and Codex outage to a routing error from 7:43 to about 8:17 am PT. Anthropic said it identified and fixed the cause of elevated errors but declined further comment. xAI cited an outage at its Memphis compute center and apologized to compute partners. Major cloud and network providers reported no matching incident, while scattered Gemini reports remained unconfirmed.
