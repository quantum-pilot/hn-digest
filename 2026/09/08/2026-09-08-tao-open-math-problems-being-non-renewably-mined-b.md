# Tao: Open math problems being non-renewably mined by AI

- Score: 378 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49616968) | Link: https://mathstodon.xyz/@tao/117237320796901560

### TL;DR

Terence Tao compares worthwhile open mathematics problems to scarce drinking water amid an infinite ocean: possible questions are unlimited, but most are unproductive, trivial, impossible with known methods, or disconnected from further insight. He warns that AI may non-renewably mine the curated stock of fruitful problems. Commenters interpret this as concern that indiscriminate solution extraction can erode understanding and researcher development, while others argue solutions create new questions, formal proofs still advance knowledge, and humans will redirect attention to unexplored frontiers.

### Comment pulse
