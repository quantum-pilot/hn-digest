# LibreOffice breaks download records after declaring it has no AI features

- Score: 685 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49610538) | Link: https://manualdousuario.net/en/libreoffice-download-record-no-ai/

### TL;DR

LibreOffice 26.8 reportedly exceeded one million direct installer downloads in its first week, a release-window record excluding Linux repository updates. The Document Foundation separately described default absence of generative AI as a feature, requiring any future integration to remain optional, user-controlled, vendor-independent, telemetry-free, format-preserving, and unable to transmit content without authorization. The article suggests that position helped demand, but provides no causal evidence. Commenters disputed whether the record reflects ordinary growth, metric selection, or AI avoidance, while agreeing that unwanted integrations can repel users.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics called the headline post hoc causality and asked which channels, updates, bundles, and release patterns the download metric covers.
- Supporters saw AI-free software as differentiation—counterpoint: useful, optional features with genuine off switches could satisfy both camps.
- Longtime users praised LibreOffice for real office work, document conversion, and independence from subscription vendors.
