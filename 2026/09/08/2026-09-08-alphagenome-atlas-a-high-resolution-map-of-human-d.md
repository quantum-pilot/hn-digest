# AlphaGenome Atlas: a high-resolution map of human DNA

- Score: 569 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49611251) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/alphagenome-atlas/

### TL;DR

Google DeepMind introduces AlphaGenome Atlas, a one-petabyte database of model-predicted regulatory effects for all nine billion possible single-nucleotide changes in the human genome. Its AlphaGenome Variant Impact score combines coding and non-coding predictions to help researchers prioritize variants. DeepMind cites a rare-disease case and an analysis of over 54,000 UK Biobank participants that found 22% more non-coding associations. These are predictive research aids, not experimentally verified effects or clinical diagnoses. Commenters requested independent validation and questioned access restrictions.
