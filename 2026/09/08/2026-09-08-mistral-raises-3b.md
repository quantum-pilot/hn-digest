# Mistral raises €3B

- Score: 825 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49605767) | Link: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-makes-sovereign-open-weight-ai-to-frontier/

### TL;DR

Mistral says it raised €3 billion in a Samsung-led Series D at a post-money valuation above €21 billion, with EQT-managed Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity co-leading alongside numerous investors. The company plans to expand research, compute, infrastructure, commercial reach, and an open-weight “sovereign AI” stack spanning models, private deployment, and products. Claims that this is Europe’s largest technology equity round and that Mistral uniquely supplies the full stack are company assertions. Commenters valued European control and enterprise fit but questioned capability, price competitiveness, documentation, and reliance on non-European capital.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters argued sovereignty, deployment control, and regulation-sensitive customers can matter more than leading public benchmarks.
- Critics placed Mistral awkwardly between stronger US and cheaper Chinese models—counterpoint: narrower RAG and OCR workloads may still fit.
- Commenters disputed whether multinational investors weaken the “European” story, while noting a European fund co-led the round.
