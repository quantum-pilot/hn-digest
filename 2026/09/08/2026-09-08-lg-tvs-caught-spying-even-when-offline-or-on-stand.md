# LG TVs caught spying even when offline or on standby

- Score: 537 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49612329) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/tech/991190/lg-tv-spying-standby-recording-wi-fi-scanning-gamers-nexus

### TL;DR

The article, relying on a Gamers Nexus investigation, alleges LG OLED televisions scanned local networks and nearby Wi-Fi devices, sent data to LG Ad Solutions, used automatic content recognition across apps and inputs, and stored microphone recordings while offline or apparently in standby before later upload. These are reported findings, not an LG-confirmed account in the supplied packet. Commenters sought regulation requiring functional “dumb” modes and clear labeling, discussed firmware analysis and blocking, and corrected an unsupported claim that TVs connect to Xfinity networks without consent.

### Comment pulse

- Users wanted televisions to work without accounts, network access, advertising, or data collection, with privacy requirements visible before purchase.
- Commenters argued ACR is industry-wide—counterpoint: broad prevalence does not validate every LG-specific allegation.
- Firmware investigators described possible blocking or modification paths, but some shared analyses were explicitly unverified or incomplete.
