# DaVinci Resolve 21.1

- Score: 411 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49610181) | Link: https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/media/release/20260908-03

### TL;DR

Blackmagic Design says DaVinci Resolve 21.1 adds conversational integrations for Claude, Claude Code, and ChatGPT Codex to organize media, change settings, create highlight edits, remove clips, and batch-render. The free download also expands camera and color-space support, multicam controls, project archiving, HDR trims, scripting APIs, hardware decoding, collaboration, and Fusion with more than 25 Krokodove tools. Commenters welcomed automation for repetitive editing and beginner workflows, though some said access is limited for free users and longstanding Linux codec, audio, and interface issues matter more.

### Comment pulse

- Editors saw agents as useful for organization, syncing, rough cuts, and rendering rather than replacing creative taste.
- Free-tier users reported automation restrictions despite prior community integrations; the supplied announcement does not clarify tier boundaries.
- Linux professionals requested AAC, broader H.264, VST3, JACK, and MIDI support over additional AI features.
