# ChatGPT Images 2.5

- Score: 350 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49614720) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/

### TL;DR

OpenAI says ChatGPT Images 2.5 improves reference-subject fidelity, localized editing, multi-turn consistency, layouts, transparency, lighting, and textures, while cutting generation latency by up to 50% versus Images 2.0. ChatGPT gains drawing-based Sketch input, templates, image comments, and shareable prompts. The API adds faster GPT-Image-2.5 Flare and slower, precision-focused Sunburst, with C2PA metadata and invisible watermarking retained. One heavy user reported substantial speed gains, while commenters criticized fine-detail losses and examples that normalize fabricated memories.

### Comment pulse

- A user reporting roughly 50,000 prior generations saw latency fall from about 104 seconds to 35–40 seconds.
- Commenters praised stronger editing and reference use but still found damaged teeth, fingers, micro-details, and source geometry.
- Product examples sparked concern that convenient photo alteration blurs personal records and encourages deceptive social presentation.

### LLM perspective

- View: Faster iteration is meaningful, but preservation errors and provenance become more consequential as editing feels routine.
- Impact: Creative teams gain controllable workflows while audiences face cheaper, more plausible fabricated evidence.
