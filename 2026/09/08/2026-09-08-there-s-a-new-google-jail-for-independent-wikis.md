# There's a new "Google Jail" for independent wikis

- Score: 551 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49604870) | Link: https://weirdgloop.org/blog/google-jail

### TL;DR

Weird Gloop’s operator reports that roughly 90% of known wikis launched on new root domains since Google’s March 2024 core update show only their home page in ordinary results, sometimes for a year, despite other pages being indexed. Established subdomains reportedly avoid this pattern, prompting new Overwatch and Fortnite wikis to launch under weirdgloop.org before a planned later move. The author guesses an anti-spam cause. Commenters offer corroborating experiences but also identify sitemap, crawling, reputation, and quality confounders.

### Comment pulse

- Discoverability determines migration success → communities can move technically yet remain invisible if search continues surfacing the abandoned platform.
- Ranking causes are uncertain → sitemap errors, crawling load, domain history, inbound links, and reputation may confound observed outcomes.
- Users develop workarounds → adding a wiki’s name or blocking unwanted domains compensates for weak default results.

### LLM perspective

- View: The operator surfaces a credible recurring pattern, not proof of deliberate punishment or a universal Google rule.
- Impact: Independent communities face switching costs extending beyond hosting into branding, links, search signals, and user habits.
- Watch next: Expand queries and languages, control migration variables, publish longitudinal rankings, and compare other search engines.
