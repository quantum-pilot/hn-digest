### TL;DR

Tristan Buckmaster states that he and Levent Alpöge released Lean-verified finite-time blowup results with smooth forcing for incompressible porous media, Boussinesq, and three-dimensional incompressible Euler, extending work by Diego Córdoba and Luis Martínez-Zoroa with LLM assistance. He says a hypo-dissipative Navier–Stokes result remains unfinished; none is the unforced Millennium Prize solution. Buckmaster then recounts disputed conversations with OpenAI about a separate alleged forced Navier–Stokes proof, credit, authorship, timing, career remarks, and possible data influence, while explicitly saying he has not seen that proof and is not alleging data use.
