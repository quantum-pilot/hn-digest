# Among European Companies That Use a CDN, Nearly 9 in 10 Use Cloudflare

- Score: 380 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49607443) | Link: https://ciphercue.com/blog/european-cdn-concentration-cloudflare-nine-in-ten

### TL;DR

CipherCue detected Cloudflare on 39,547 of 44,143 CDN-using companies in its tracked set across eight European countries, an 89.6% share of identified CDN users rather than all companies. The cohort skews toward small and midsize firms, detection relies on DNS and HTTP fingerprints, hidden configurations may be missed, and vendors can overlap. The author argues this concentration creates correlated outage risk at the internet’s front door, while explicitly separating CDN location from origin hosting or data residency. Commenters emphasized Cloudflare’s price and simplicity.

### Comment pulse

- Small-site operators praised Cloudflare’s generous free tier and simpler global deployment compared with hyperscaler load balancers.
- Critics stressed sovereignty and surveillance concerns — counterpoint: the study measures availability concentration, not data location or privacy compliance.
- Commenters debated multi-CDN resilience, noting redundancy can add substantial cost and operational complexity.
