# We built our house for LAN parties (2024)

- Score: 537 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49579443) | Link: https://lanparty.house/

### TL;DR

Kenton Varda and Jade Wang describe designing their Austin home around hosting LAN parties, including 18 built-in gaming stations, remotely located computers and servers, extensive cabling, dedicated cooling, fold-away equipment, and permanent Dance Dance Revolution pads. The project also integrates family and cat-oriented features, while openly acknowledging unusual cost and energy use. Commenters celebrate the combination of friendship, engineering, and hospitality, though some question wealth, long-term social use, family practicality, and cooling. Varda says fixed stations remove LAN setup’s largest friction.

### Comment pulse

- Infrastructure enables community → preconfigured stations eliminate hours of updates, cabling, and compatibility work at traditional LAN parties.
- The house reflects fortunate priorities → commenters admire spending resources on shared experiences while noting extraordinary privilege.
- Operational details matter → cooling, power, cable runs, food, children, maintenance, and long-term participation determine whether the concept endures.
