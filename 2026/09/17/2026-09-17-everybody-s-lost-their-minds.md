# Everybody's Lost Their Minds

- Score: 355 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49745570) | Link: https://www.netmeister.org/blog/everybodys-lost-their-minds.html

### TL;DR

The author delivers a personal anti-AI polemic, arguing that constant agent supervision has drained enjoyment and understanding from engineering while concentrating power, environmental costs, and ethical harms. In cybersecurity, the essay contends that finding more vulnerabilities misses the enduring bottleneck: deploying patches and maintaining basic asset, update, and reboot infrastructure. Commenters sharply divided between similar burnout and concrete stories of agents rescuing urgent projects, with several concluding that usefulness and dissatisfaction can coexist and that confident forecasts remain premature.

### Comment pulse

- Agents can unlock urgent delivery → one commenter described completing a contingency integration overnight — counterpoint: generated speed still required human testing and corrections.
- The verdict remains unsettled → critics called the essay emotive and one-sided, while supporters valued precisely that personal account.
