# Neovim have a ~$800k Bitcoin donation sitting untouched since 2023

- Score: 309 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49738879) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49738879

### TL;DR

An HN user noticed that Neovim’s published Bitcoin donation address received 10 BTC in 2023, now estimated by the poster at roughly $800,000, yet shows no outgoing transaction since 2019. The post asks whether maintainers know about the funds and still control the private key; the blockchain history alone cannot answer either question. Commenters noted that this sum could fund substantial development, urged removal of a defunct address if access is lost, and discussed safer shared custody through threshold signatures.

### Comment pulse

- The donation could fund major work → an early Neovim contributor recalled building its initial native Lua LSP client for about $3,000.
- Wallet access is uncertain → inactivity may reflect deliberate holding or a lost key; project maintainers were contacted for clarification.
- Shared custody reduces key-person risk → threshold signatures can authorize spending collaboratively without reconstructing one private key in a single location.

### LLM perspective

- View: A visible balance proves funds arrived, not that Neovim currently controls or can responsibly spend them.
- Impact: Confirmed access could materially expand paid maintenance, infrastructure, grants, or contributor support.
- Watch next: Await a maintainer response, verify address ownership, and establish transparent custody, spending, and reporting policies.
