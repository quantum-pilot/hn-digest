# Fujitsu launches made-in-Japan next-generation CPU FUJITSU-MONAKA

- Score: 625 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49715813) | Link: https://global.fujitsu/en-global/pr/news/2026/09/14-02

### TL;DR

Fujitsu announced November sales for its Arm-based FUJITSU-MONAKA CPU and Japan-manufactured MONAKA servers targeting sovereign AI and HPC. The company specifies 2nm compute cores, 5nm cache and I/O, 3.8GHz operation, 8,800MT/s memory, matrix acceleration, confidential computing, and air-cooled deployments. Fujitsu claims twice the CPU inference throughput, half the servers and power, and up to 80% less cooling energy, but supplies no independent benchmarks. Commenters welcomed competition while questioning fabrication origin, software maturity, core count, and real memory bandwidth.
