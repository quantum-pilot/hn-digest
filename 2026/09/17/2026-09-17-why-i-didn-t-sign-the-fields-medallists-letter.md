# Why I didn’t sign the Fields medallists’ letter

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49738091) | Link: https://gowers.wordpress.com/2026/09/17/why-i-didnt-sign-the-fields-medallists-letter/

### TL;DR

Timothy Gowers agrees AI threatens mathematical culture but rejects the letter's hierarchy placing conceptual understanding above problem-solving. He argues mathematicians occupy a valuable spectrum, and an AI flood could increase both undigested and successfully absorbed results. His deeper concern is institutional: if famous open problems no longer motivate students and policymakers withdraw funding, future experts may not preserve or teach the field. Rather than asking labs to slow inevitable releases, he urges new roles, incentives, curation, and training structures. HN debate focused on funding, problem selection, dependency, and human purpose.
