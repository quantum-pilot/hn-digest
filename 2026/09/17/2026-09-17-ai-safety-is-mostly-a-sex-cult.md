# AI safety is mostly a sex cult

- Score: 312 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49737985) | Link: https://skywriter.blue/@segyges.bsky.social/3mvom4b4dn22q

### TL;DR

The author argues that influential Berkeley rationalist and effective-altruist networks shaped AI-safety language, institutions, and leaders while intertwining doomsday beliefs, communal life, sexual subcultures, and nonprofit funding. From those asserted connections, the piece alleges conflicts of interest: people whose status depends on catastrophic-risk narratives may favor slowing research or antitrust exemptions regardless of evidence. These are polemical claims rather than demonstrated causal findings. HN readers split between finding the network history revealing and dismissing most of the essay as ad hominem surrounding narrower, valid concerns about incentives and market concentration.
