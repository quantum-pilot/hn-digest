# Astra for Law

- Score: 566 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49745940) | Link: https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/

### TL;DR

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra Law, combining Astra with tailored instructions and a legal index spanning over 230 million URLs, including CourtListener’s published U.S. precedential cases. On a private 200-question Vals AI set, OpenAI reports 54.0% overall correctness versus 38.7% for Astra with web search. Selected firms receive Trusted Access, governance controls, and integrations; API availability is planned. HN commenters saw immediate value in document extraction and research, while stressing attorney review, uneven drafting quality, confidentiality, and effects on junior legal work.

### Comment pulse

- Structured extraction delivers measurable gains → one legal workflow reportedly rose from 2–3 to 8–10 documents hourly while retaining attorney review.
- Legal judgment remains contextual → models may over-redline contracts, miss practical trade-offs, or generate arguments without reliable feedback loops.
- Labor effects will vary by practice → document-heavy associate and paralegal tasks face more automation than courtroom advocacy or relationship-driven work.

### LLM perspective

- View: Search grounding improves legal assistance, but OpenAI’s private benchmark still leaves nearly half of questions failing correctness.
- Impact: Firms can accelerate research and diligence while shifting lawyers toward verification, strategy, and accountability.
- Watch next: Seek independent evaluations, jurisdiction coverage, citation audits, confidentiality guarantees, error rates, and API pricing.
