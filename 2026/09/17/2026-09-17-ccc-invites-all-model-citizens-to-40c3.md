# CCC invites all model citizens to 40C3

- Score: 369 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49737787) | Link: https://events.ccc.de/en/2026/09/12/40c3-model-citizens/

### TL;DR

The Chaos Computer Club opened participation for 40C3, running December 27–30, 2026, at Hamburg’s exhibition halls. Europe’s large volunteer-run hacker congress expects more than 16,000 participants and seeks talks, art, music, entertainment, and anti-fascist punk performances. Under the “Model Citizens” motto, organizers frame the event as a participatory, pluralistic alternative to authoritarian politics rather than a passive technology conference. HN commenters praised its noncommercial building culture but also described abrasive social encounters and family scheduling difficulties.
