# Canada welcomes EU proposal to become 'associate member'

- Score: 278 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49740507) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwly7vkke4jxo

### TL;DR

Canadian prime minister Mark Carney welcomed the EU’s proposed, currently undefined “associate member” relationship, while rejecting full membership or a new rival power bloc. He proposed deeper cooperation on defence, critical minerals, energy, technology, mobility, and Erasmus+, framing it as strategic autonomy amid a Canada-U.S. trade dispute. Any arrangement could take years and requires all 27 EU members plus Canadian parliamentary debate. Trump threatened tariffs, while an October summit in Montreal is expected to begin defining details. HN discussion focused on diplomatic choreography and the label’s ambiguity.
