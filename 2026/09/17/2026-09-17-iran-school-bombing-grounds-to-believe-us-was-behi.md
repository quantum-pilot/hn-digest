# Iran school bombing: grounds to believe US was behind atrocity, UN finds

- Score: 348 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49739018) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/17/iran-school-bombing-un-mission-us-military-behind-attack

### TL;DR

An independent UN mission found reasonable grounds to believe US strikes on an identifiable Iranian primary school and a sports complex on 28 February were indiscriminate war crimes. The school strike killed 156 civilians, including 120 children; investigators said outdated intelligence was insufficiently verified and found no military use of the school. US Central Command denied striking Lamerd, and the Pentagon has not published its school investigation. The same mission accused Iranian authorities of crimes against humanity during protest repression. Commenters disputed intent versus reckless negligence and questioned accountability.
