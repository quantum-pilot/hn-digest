# Mullenweg has returned as CEO after attempted board ouster

- Score: 269 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49688259) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/automattic-confirms-mullenweg-has-returned-as-ceo-after-attempted-ouster-by-board/

### TL;DR

Automattic says Matt Mullenweg is again chairman and CEO with board support after a board vote placed him on paid leave for 33 hours and 20 minutes. TechCrunch reports that Mullenweg resisted departure, removed other Slack administrators, declared himself back in control, and posted erratic pirate-themed messages. The company confirmed his return but declined details about individual directors or reported board changes. Commenters questioned whether official governance truly stabilized or operational control simply overtook the attempted removal.
