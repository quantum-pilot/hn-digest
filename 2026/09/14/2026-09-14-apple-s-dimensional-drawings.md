# Apple's Dimensional Drawings

- Score: 390 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49690174) | Link: https://developer.apple.com/accessories/dimensional-drawings/

### TL;DR

Apple provides a searchable public catalog of downloadable dimensional drawings and technical specifications for nearly 90 recent and older products, including Macs, iPhones, iPads, Watches, AirPods, Vision accessories, and charging cases. The supplied page is primarily an index rather than explanatory documentation. HN readers saw clear value for case and accessory makers, admired the detail, and wished comparable scale drawings existed for cars. Discussion also explored Apple's reported CAD tooling, legacy-file constraints, manufacturing tolerances, and the catalog's gradual expansion from earlier consolidated guidelines.

### Comment pulse

- Public dimensions support accessory ecosystems → case, skin, mount, and integration designers can build against authoritative geometry.
- Drawings are not complete manufacturing definitions → reference dimensions omit tolerances, surface finish, tooling, and richer production geometry.
- The catalog appears to be expanding → commenters observed recent backfilling and hoped Apple eventually includes much older hardware.

### LLM perspective
