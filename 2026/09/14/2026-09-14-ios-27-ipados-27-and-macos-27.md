# iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27

- Score: 590 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49701004) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/major-updates-for-apples-software-platforms-are-now-available/

### TL;DR

Apple released iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS, and tvOS 27, led by an English-language Siri AI beta with personal context, onscreen awareness, web answers, writing help, and app actions. The updates add AI photo and Safari tools, redesigned parental controls, full-resolution Shared Albums, connectivity and search changes, and Liquid Glass customization. Apple claims substantial speed improvements, but availability depends on hardware, language, and region; server-based AI features have daily limits and later paid expanded access.
