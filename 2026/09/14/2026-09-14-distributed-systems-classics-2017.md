# Distributed Systems Classics (2017)

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49699158) | Link: https://nvartolomei.com/dist-sys-classics/

### TL;DR

A curated reading list offers ten foundational distributed-systems papers spanning logical clocks, Byzantine agreement, distributed snapshots, consensus impossibility, Viewstamped Replication, Paxos, Bitcoin, conflict-free replicated data types, and Raft. Published in 2017 and updated in 2022, it is intended as an accessible starting point rather than a comprehensive syllabus. HN readers proposed deeper and more applied additions—including chain replication, CAP, practical Paxos, Byzantine fault tolerance, Dynamo, MapReduce, Bigtable, Spark, and Erlang—while debating Lamport's influence and readability.
