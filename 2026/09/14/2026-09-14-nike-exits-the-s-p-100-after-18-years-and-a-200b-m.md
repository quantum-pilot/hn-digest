# Nike exits the S&P 100 after 18 years and a $200B market-cap wipeout

- Score: 301 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49691343) | Link: https://fortune.com/2026/09/08/nike-stock-plummets-sp500-market-cap-index/

### TL;DR

Nike will leave the S&P 100 on September 21 after nearly 18 years, while remaining in the S&P 500. Fortune reports its market capitalization fell from $264 billion in November 2021 to roughly $57 billion, alongside weaker revenue, eight consecutive quarters of declining China sales, and a struggling direct-to-consumer strategy. CEO Elliott Hill’s turnaround emphasizes wholesale relationships, inventory reduction, and performance products. Commenters linked lost retail shelf space, high prices, quality concerns, and stronger specialist competitors to the decline.
