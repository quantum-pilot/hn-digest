# A beginning for mathematics

- Score: 216 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49698699) | Link: https://www.daniellitt.com/blog/2026/9/13/a-beginning-for-mathematics/

### TL;DR

The essay assumes AI will soon become broadly superhuman at mathematics and argues the profession should separate producing results from producing human understanding and capable mathematicians. Papers can no longer reliably signal expertise, so the author proposes PhDs centered on rigorous oral defense, unfamiliar examples, sustained discussion, and mastery regardless of AI provenance. Rather than preserve journals or reserve open problems, institutions should reward exposition, question selection, research programs, seminars, and community learning. HN discussion contests the premise but engages the proposed adaptation.
