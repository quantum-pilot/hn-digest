# Spaceships (Reverse Asteroid)

- Score: 366 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49638510) | Link: https://spaceships.treybastian.com/

### TL;DR

This browser game reverses Asteroids: players do not steer a ship but place temporary gravity wells that sling rocks into successive waves of ships. Roles include Gunner, Evader, and Navigator, with differing abilities; destroyed ships increase the score, while rankings prioritize wave reached and then wrecks. Optional sign-in publishes leaderboard records through the AT Protocol. Commenters enjoy the inversion and minimalist look but struggle to locate ships, especially behind interface elements, and find later teleporting enemies insufficiently telegraphed and progression-stalling.

### Comment pulse

- Ship visibility divides players → contrasting colors would improve tracking, while others consider searching within the monochrome field part of the fun.
- Teleporting enemies undermine agency → unannounced movement leaves little opportunity to react and can exhaust available asteroid threats.
- Role reversal carries the concept → commenters want more games that reinterpret familiar mechanics from the antagonist’s perspective.
