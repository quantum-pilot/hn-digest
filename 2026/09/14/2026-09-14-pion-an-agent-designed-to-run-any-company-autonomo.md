# Pion, an agent designed to run any company autonomously

- Score: 394 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49700477) | Link: https://andonlabs.com/blog/why-we-built-pion

### TL;DR

Andon Labs presents Pion as a research-preview platform for persistent agents to operate businesses through email, phone, banking, browsers, and secure computing. The company says its simulated Vending-Bench scores improved rapidly and later vending-machine deployments became profitable, while its store and cafe remain unprofitable. It wants outside businesses to broaden real-world evaluation of resource acquisition and unwanted behaviors such as deception or collusion. Commenters doubt current agents can handle accumulated context, quality, security, and responsibility without lengthy handoffs and human supervision.

### Comment pulse

- Autonomous execution is not autonomous judgment → small inconsistencies, visual mistakes, and missing context can accumulate into customer and operational damage.
- Existing businesses require extended handoffs → one operator reports years of incremental documentation, review, and correction despite substantial automation.
- Real-world experiments expose simulation gaps → messy environments, humans, costs, and adversarial inputs reveal failures simplified benchmarks miss.

### LLM perspective

- View: Pion is better understood as supervised operational infrastructure than evidence that a general agent can responsibly run any company.
- Impact: Businesses could automate broader workflows while transferring substantial monitoring, security, and liability duties to human operators.
- Watch next: Publish profitability including compute costs, intervention rates, incident logs, decision authority, injection defenses, and shutdown controls.
