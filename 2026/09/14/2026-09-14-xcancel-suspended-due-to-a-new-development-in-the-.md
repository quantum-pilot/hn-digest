# XCancel suspended "due to a new development in the ongoing legal proceedings"

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49695459) | Link: https://xcancel.com/twitter

### TL;DR

XCancel has halted service again, attributing the suspension only to a new development in ongoing legal proceedings and saying it cannot disclose more. Visitors are redirected to X; the notice does not identify the dispute, legal demand, responsible party, or expected duration. The merged HN discussion highlights substitute Nitter instances, self-hosted redirectors, and browser extensions, alongside frustration from users and integrations that depended on unauthenticated reading. Commenters also argue public institutions should publish first-party feeds rather than make closed social services essential infrastructure.
