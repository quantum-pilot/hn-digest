# An atlas of periodic solutions to the three-body problem

- Score: 356 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49670852) | Link: https://www.threebodyorbits.com/

### TL;DR

An interactive atlas maps thousands of numerically computed periodic three-body orbits, placing visually similar trajectories near one another and grouping them into named families. Users can zoom from a constellation-scale overview into live orbit animations, browse by shape, period, energy, closeness, speed, or stability, and launch searches or comparisons. HN found the presentation beautiful and intrinsically fascinating, while discussing the distinction between lacking a general closed-form solution and having special periodic solutions, plus the practical meaning of linear stability under perturbation.
