### TL;DR

A.J. Jacobs remembers his mother, Ellen, through the many roles she inhabited: encouraging parent, inventive science teacher, accomplished jewelry artist, devoted grandmother and friend, loving spouse, library volunteer, and determined optimist. Their daily gratitude emails and shared musicals helped them confront worry and her dementia with humor and attention to small joys. Commenters received the essay as a moving invitation to express gratitude while loved ones can still hear it, and celebrated its intimate family details.
