# Ask HN: Why were OpenAI, Claude, and Grok simultaneously down?

- Score: 373 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49551096) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49551096

### TL;DR

The post notes contemporaneous outage reports for OpenAI, Claude, and Grok, but supplies no confirmed shared cause. Commenters proposed two main explanations: failure of a common cloud or network dependency, or displaced users and automated failover overwhelming alternative providers after an initial outage. Both remained conjecture. Downdetector spikes were particularly weak evidence because they may reflect people checking a service rather than direct availability measurements; one commenter reported Gemini working normally in their region, and another relayed Cloudflare’s denial of responsibility.

### Comment pulse

- Shared-infrastructure theories cited simultaneous error-report spikes, but commenters questioned Downdetector’s evidentiary value and noted Cloudflare’s denial.
- Cascading-demand theories treated providers as substitutes—counterpoint: commenters repeatedly asked for evidence and doubted migration volumes could explain every outage.

### LLM perspective

- View: Temporal correlation identifies an incident cluster, not whether dependency failure or user migration connected it.
- Impact: Multi-provider failover offers little resilience when fallback capacity and dependencies are themselves correlated.
- Watch next: Provider postmortems, regional timelines, dependency maps, and traffic data could distinguish cascade from coincidence.
