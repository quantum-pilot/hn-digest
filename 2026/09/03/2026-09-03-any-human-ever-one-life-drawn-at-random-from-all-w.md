# Any Human Ever – One life, drawn at random from all who have ever lived

- Score: 591 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550698) | Link: https://anyhumanever.com/

### TL;DR

The supplied landing page offers randomly generated lives by year and place, claiming they are drawn from real data and from more than 100 billion people who have lived. It provides no methodology in the supplied text. Commenters found implausible biographies, questionable citations, and an explicitly model-supplied gap-fill, while others challenged the birth-year distribution and modern monetary comparisons for subsistence societies. Some still saw entertainment or role-playing value, but the discussion strongly disputed its presentation as historical evidence.
