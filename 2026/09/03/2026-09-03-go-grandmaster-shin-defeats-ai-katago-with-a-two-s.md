# Go grandmaster Shin defeats AI KataGo with a two-stone handicap

- Score: 315 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49544762) | Link: https://www.kedglobal.com/artificial-intelligence/newsView/ked202607210007

### TL;DR

World-ranked Go player Shin Jin-seo reportedly defeated KataGo 2–1 in a three-game series while receiving a two-stone handicap. After losing the opener, he adopted a restrained, territory-focused style, winning the finale by 11.5 points after 221 moves. The article frames this as human adaptation rather than parity with modern Go engines. Commenters stressed that the handicap means Shin began advantaged, and some also cited KataGo’s move-time limit. They nevertheless praised his disciplined avoidance of tactical complexity and ability to preserve the initial buffer.
