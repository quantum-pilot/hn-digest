# Mom gets 6-month suspended sentence for letting 5-year-old walk to the pond

- Score: 259 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550174) | Link: https://reason.com/2026/09/02/virginia-mom-gets-6-month-suspended-jail-sentence-for-letting-5-year-old-walk-to-the-pond/

### TL;DR

Reason reports that Virginia mother Karyann Parkinson was convicted of contributing to a minor’s delinquency after allowing her five-year-old to walk roughly half a mile to a neighborhood pond. The judge suspended a six-month sentence, while a CPS finding placed her on the abuse-and-neglect registry for seven years; appeals and professional consequences remain concerns. Commenters overwhelmingly viewed the response as disproportionate, contrasting it with childhood independence elsewhere, although some cautioned that reporting may omit relevant case details.
