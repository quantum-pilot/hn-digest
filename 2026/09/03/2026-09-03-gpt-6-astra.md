# GPT-6 Astra

- Score: 1823 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49554643) | Link: https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/

### TL;DR

OpenAI presents GPT-6 Astra as a major advance in computer use, coding, scientific work, cybersecurity, and instruction alignment, citing strong internal and third-party benchmark results. It is rolling out across paid ChatGPT plans, the API, Azure, and Bedrock; standard API pricing is listed at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. OpenAI also reports stronger task-boundary adherence, while acknowledging reduced monitorability of written reasoning and heightened cyber capability requiring additional safeguards.
