# Whistleblower warns Postal Service mail ballot system has catastrophic problems

- Score: 299 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49524428) | Link: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/whistleblower-postal-service-new-mail-ballot-system/

### TL;DR

An anonymous federal official alleges USPS’s rushed Federal Ballot Mail Portal could disrupt mail voting and continued despite court constraints. The described process samples roughly 400 ballots from batches of 10,000 or more and would return an entire batch if one sampled barcode or record fails verification, potentially causing repeated delays. Senator Richard Blumenthal amplified the warning; USPS says development complies with court orders and state participation will be voluntary. A judge allowed portal development and state communications if participation remains optional, while legal challenges continue.
