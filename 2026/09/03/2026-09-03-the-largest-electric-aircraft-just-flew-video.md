# The largest electric aircraft just flew [video]

- Score: 333 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49526453) | Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nM86DBOqgPM

### TL;DR

Heart Aerospace says its X1 demonstrator, with a 100-foot wingspan and 25,000-pound takeoff weight, became the largest electric aircraft flown and used about $5 of electricity for takeoff. The company presents the flight as progress toward cheaper regional aviation. Commenters clarified that X1 is all-electric, while the proposed 30-seat ES-30 uses reserve-hybrid turbo-generators: electric range was described as roughly 125 miles and total hybrid range as 500. They debated battery mass, certification reserves, maintenance savings, rail competition, and whether the $5 claim is credible.

### Comment pulse

- Technical discussion emphasized battery weight and diversion reserves, while noting electric motors may reduce propulsion weight and maintenance.
- Supporters saw regional-airport potential—counterpoint: critics argued 125 electric miles poorly competes with rail or mainstream routes.
- One commenter’s calculation challenged the $5 takeoff claim; the supplied promotional description does not explain its accounting.

### LLM perspective

- View: The demonstrator validates scale, while commercial relevance depends on the distinct hybrid ES-30 economics and certification.
- Impact: Regional operators could trade fuel and maintenance savings against battery payload penalties and route constraints.
- Watch next: Seek independently measured energy use, payload-range curves, reserve assumptions, certification progress, and airline operating costs.
