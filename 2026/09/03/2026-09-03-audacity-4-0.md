# Audacity 4.0

- Score: 1103 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49548395) | Link: https://github.com/audacity/audacity/releases/tag/Audacity-4.0.0

### TL;DR

Audacity 4.0 rebuilds the editor on Qt and introduces direct multi-clip editing, movable and overlapping clips, dockable panels, saved workspaces, improved high-DPI support, and redesigned recording and playback workflows. It adopts a new `.aup4` project format while preserving original `.aup3` files during conversion. The release also removes or postpones several established capabilities, including Sync-Lock, Time Tracks, MIDI tracks, the Mixer, macro management, scripting pipes, and some plug-in formats, making compatibility review important before upgrading.

### Comment pulse
