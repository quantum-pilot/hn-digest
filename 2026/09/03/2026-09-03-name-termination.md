# .name Termination

- Score: 1830 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550772) | Link: https://neil.fraser.name/news/2026/09/03/

### TL;DR

Neil Fraser says Verisign will terminate third-level `.name` registrations such as `neil.fraser.name` in February, following an April proposal and July ICANN approval. He says roughly 22,000 registrations are affected, including names paid years ahead, email addresses, APIs, and embedded-device endpoints. His larger warning is takeover risk: if the corresponding second-level domain later becomes available, a new owner could recreate former subdomains and potentially impersonate identities or control systems that still trust them.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters favored grandfathering existing names or reserving corresponding second-level domains for current registrants.
- Several treated domain-based identity as inherently leased infrastructure, while others argued abrupt termination still violates reasonable permanence expectations.
- Discussion also raised Public Suffix List and cookie-isolation consequences beyond simple website availability.
