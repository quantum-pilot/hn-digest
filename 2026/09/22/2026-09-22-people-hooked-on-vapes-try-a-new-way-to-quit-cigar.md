# People hooked on vapes try a new way to quit: cigarettes

- Score: 239 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49795686) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/to-quit-vaping-some-are-starting-to-smoke

### TL;DR

Some people are switching from vaping to cigarettes because smoking is less convenient and more unpleasant, hoping those frictions will help them quit nicotine. Surveys cited by Bloomberg suggest the tactic is not merely anecdotal: nearly one-fifth of UK vapers who tried quitting used cigarettes, while 14% of surveyed New Zealand quit-smoking vapers returned to them. Addiction researchers warn combustion makes cigarettes substantially more harmful and switching can create dual use. HN commenters instead share medication, tapering, and behavioral approaches.

### Comment pulse

- Vaping can erase natural stopping cues → constant indoor access encourages frequent dosing — counterpoint: cigarettes remain more physically harmful and can also be chain-smoked.
- Gradual substitution can reduce dependence → commenters tapered liquid strength, switched to gum, and extended intervals despite prolonged cravings.
- Cytisine deserves wider consideration → several commenters report successful quits, while noting access, counterfeit risk, and limited awareness.

### LLM perspective

- View: Using a more dangerous delivery system as behavioral friction confuses addiction management with harm reduction.
- Impact: Quit programs need vaping-specific guidance that addresses continuous access, rituals, dose tapering, and relapse into dual use.
- Watch next: Compare cessation rates and harms across nicotine replacement, cytisine, gradual tapering, counseling, and cigarette substitution.
