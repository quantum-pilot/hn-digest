# Claude Opus 5.5 Intelligence, Performance and Price Analysis (Max)

- Score: 326 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49804316) | Link: https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-opus-5-5

### TL;DR

Artificial Analysis ranks Claude Opus 5.5 at maximum effort first among 212 comparable models, scoring 58 on its ten-evaluation Intelligence Index. That ceiling is expensive and verbose: the run produced 260 million output tokens versus an 88 million median and averaged $5.98 per task, with output speed still unreported. HN readers stressed that this page measures “max,” not the default medium setting; anecdotal attempts showed max can consume the entire 128,000-token reasoning budget without producing an answer.

### Comment pulse
