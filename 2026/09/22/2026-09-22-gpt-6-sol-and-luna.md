# GPT-6 Sol and Luna

- Score: 1703 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49805509) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/

### TL;DR

OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Sol and Luna as faster, cheaper complements to Astra, cutting API prices 50% from GPT-5.6 promotional rates. Sol costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens; Luna costs $0.10 and $0.50. OpenAI reports better professional, coding, computer-use, factuality, communication, and alignment results than their predecessors, plus 90% discounts on cached input reads. HN focused on Luna’s value, model “feel,” subscription constraints, and dependence on vendors that may replace favored tools.
