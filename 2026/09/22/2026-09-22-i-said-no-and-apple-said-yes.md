# I said no and Apple said yes

- Score: 848 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49797982) | Link: https://dbushell.com/2026/09/22/apple-intelligence/

### TL;DR

After upgrading to macOS 27, the author says previously disabled Apple Intelligence and Siri-related features reappeared, their former master switch was gone, and 22.28GB of model data occupied disk space. They frame this as Apple overriding consent and hiding partial restrictions under Screen Time. HN discussion strongly shared frustration with recurring nudges, but challenged a linked 2025 claim: the 15-minute setting reportedly controls generation of a transparency report, not how often personal data is sent to Apple.

### Comment pulse

- Major upgrades should preserve explicit refusals → re-enabled features and repeated prompts make opt-outs feel temporary rather than authoritative.
- The earlier telemetry claim appears mistaken → commenters said Report Duration controls user-visible transparency exports, not additional transmission frequency.
- Linux offers greater control but carries costs → supporters cited ownership, while others described hardware, driver, networking, and upgrade failures.

- View: Consent depends on durable state and clear explanations; ambiguous settings undermine trust even when privacy claims are overstated.
