# Apple has added persistent 'ads' to iOS, and it's driving users crazy

- Score: 778 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49801939) | Link: https://www.techradar.com/phones/iphone/i-wish-apple-would-just-stop-that-crap-apple-has-added-persistent-ads-to-ios-and-its-driving-users-crazy

### TL;DR

TechRadar reports persistent banners near the top of iOS Settings promoting iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple TV, or AppleCare+. Some reportedly lack a working dismissal option, can leave a Settings badge for weeks, and have appeared even for existing subscribers, which may indicate a bug. The article links the prompts to Apple’s growing services emphasis, but does not establish their prevalence. HN users treated them as part of a broader pattern of App Store advertising, update nags, and declining control over premium devices.
