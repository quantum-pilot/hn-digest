# What California is learning from solar panels built over irrigation canals

- Score: 338 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49796379) | Link: https://www.kqed.org/science/2002033/heres-what-california-is-learning-from-solar-panels-built-over-irrigation-canals

### TL;DR

California’s $20 million Project Nexus is testing three solar-canopy designs over Turlock irrigation canals. The 1.7-megawatt pilot generates electricity, reduces algae and weeds, and measures evaporation; researchers estimate 100 miles could save water for 2,700–11,000 households and produce 330–1,400 megawatts, depending on canal width. Scaling remains difficult because canal ownership, grid connections, maintenance access, and local geometry vary. HN commenters debate whether combined land and water benefits justify structures that appear far costlier than conventional solar farms.
