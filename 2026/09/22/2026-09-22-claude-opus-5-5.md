# Claude Opus 5.5

- Score: 1743 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49803892) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5

### TL;DR

Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5 as a more efficient successor to Opus 5, claiming Fable 5.1-level performance on most work, over 30% faster output, and 40% lower typical workload cost. API prices are $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, with cache reads at $0.20. Anthropic reports gains in long-horizon coding, knowledge work, communication, and alignment, while acknowledging evaluation limits. HN welcomed lower prices but questioned frontier-pacing rhetoric, verbosity, and excessive reasoning at maximum effort.

### Comment pulse

- Efficiency is the clearest improvement → lower token prices and fewer reported tokens per task may reduce practical agent costs.
- Communication quality remains subjective → some early users found clearer writing while others still encountered familiar verbosity and stock phrasing.
- Maximum effort can overthink simple tasks → one SVG attempt exhausted 128,000 reasoning tokens without answering.
