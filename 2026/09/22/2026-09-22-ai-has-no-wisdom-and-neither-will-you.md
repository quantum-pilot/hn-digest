# AI Has No Wisdom and Neither Will You

- Score: 383 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49799965) | Link: https://alexn.org/blog/2026/09/22/ai-has-no-wisdom-and-neither-will-you/

### TL;DR

The author warns that delegating both code creation and review to AI erodes the experience through which engineers learn maintainability, architecture, and contextual judgment. Those qualities reveal themselves over months, resist simple reward functions, and cannot be reduced reliably to lint rules. AI remains useful for tedious work, but unexamined output may create brittle systems and deskilled teams. HN debated this strongly: some saw institutional knowledge at risk, while others described disciplined agent workflows that accelerate learning and production without requiring line-by-line reading.

### Comment pulse

- Outsourcing implementation can drain institutional knowledge → engineers who stop making and correcting decisions lose the experience underlying judgment.
- Maintainability metrics are weak proxies → complexity scores catch local problems but miss architecture, duplication, overengineering, and context-dependent tradeoffs.
- Agents can support disciplined development → specifications and tests enable ambitious projects — counterpoint: working output does not establish long-term maintainability or understanding.
