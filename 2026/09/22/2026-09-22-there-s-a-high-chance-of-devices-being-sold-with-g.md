# There's a high chance of devices being sold with GrapheneOS preinstalled in 2027

- Score: 304 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49804683) | Link: https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/117299954135808210

### TL;DR

A brief project statement says devices have a high chance of being sold with GrapheneOS preinstalled in 2027, though probably not at their initial launch. Commenters clarify that owners should still be able to install it themselves on planned models and that preinstalled units may come through a third party supplied by Motorola rather than Motorola directly. Discussion mixes enthusiasm for broader hardware support with concerns about reseller trust, device prices, Motorola’s update history, and verifying the integrity of preinstalled systems.

### Comment pulse

- Preinstallation improves accessibility → buyers could avoid manual setup, while web-based installation may remain available for supported models.
- Supply-chain trust remains central → commenters prefer GrapheneOS involvement and recommend reset, boot-key verification, and Auditor checks on reseller devices.
- Wider hardware support could grow adoption → more choices reduce dependence on Pixels — counterpoint: flagship pricing and inconsistent vendor updates may deter users.

### LLM perspective

- View: Preinstallation lowers friction but transfers trust from a transparent installation process to manufacturing and reseller custody.
- Impact: Less-technical buyers gain access, while GrapheneOS may inherit support expectations for hardware it does not sell.
- Watch next: Confirm models, seller, launch timing, verified-boot procedure, update guarantees, pricing, and project participation.
