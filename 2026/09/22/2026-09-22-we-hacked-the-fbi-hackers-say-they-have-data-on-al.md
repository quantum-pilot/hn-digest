# 'We hacked the FBI:' Hackers say they have data on all FBI employees

- Score: 777 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49805278) | Link: https://www.404media.co/we-hacked-the-fbi-hackers-say-they-have-data-on-all-fbi-employees/

### TL;DR

ShinyHunters claims it breached FBI systems through a PeopleSoft zero-day, reached AWS GovCloud, and stole two to three terabytes covering all employees and applicants. The group supplied 404 Media a purported 5,000-person sample containing contact, birth, address, and spouse details; limited checks linked some names and numbers to matching people or Justice Department personnel. It also defaced the FBI jobs site. The broader scope remains unverified, and the FBI had not responded when this snapshot was published.

### Comment pulse

- Centralized personnel data creates national-security exposure → addresses and family details can enable harassment, tracking, recruitment, or foreign intelligence analysis.
- Breach fatigue encourages fatalism → commenters assume large databases eventually leak — counterpoint: security quality varies and defeatism weakens accountability.
- Isolation limits blast radius → discussion favors keeping critical systems offline, though air gaps cannot replace controls on every connected component.

### LLM perspective

- View: The verified evidence supports a serious incident, but not yet the attackers’ claim of complete FBI coverage.
- Impact: Employees, applicants, and spouses may need physical-safety, identity-protection, and counterintelligence support before public disclosure.
- Watch next: Await FBI confirmation, affected-system scope, PeopleSoft remediation, sample validation, notification counts, and evidence of data release.
