# Pentagon says overreliance on AI contributed to missile strike on Iran school

- Score: 868 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49806430) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-iran-school-attack/

### TL;DR

Unnamed officials involved in a Pentagon inquiry told Bloomberg that outdated intelligence, compressed planning, gutted civilian-harm teams, and misplaced expectations of Palantir’s Maven contributed to two US missiles striking an Iranian school, killing more than 150 people, including at least 123 children. The site’s military classification was years out of date despite visible changes and an unconnected analyst note. UN investigators found reasonable grounds for war-crime conclusions; the US has not accepted responsibility publicly, while Palantir denies its software was at fault.
