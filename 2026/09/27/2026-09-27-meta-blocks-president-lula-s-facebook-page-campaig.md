# Meta Blocks President Lula's Facebook Page, Campaign Ads 2 Weeks from Election

- Score: 469 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49864642) | Link: https://www.reddit.com/r/worldnews/comments/1wr3id3/meta_blocks_president_lulas_facebook_page_and/

### TL;DR

Meta temporarily disabled Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva’s campaign Facebook page and restricted its advertising account less than 11 days before the election’s first round. The page later returned, but the supplied report gives no duration, reason, or Meta explanation. Lula’s coalition demanded human review, restored advertising access, preserved logs, and threatened electoral litigation, alleging the disruption harmed campaign equality. Commenters treated platform control as a sovereignty risk, though they disputed whether this reflected political interference, ordinary error, or justification for national blocking.
