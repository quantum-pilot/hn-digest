# When did Google get so weird?

- Score: 1942 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49870367) | Link: https://sancho.bearblog.dev/google-weird/

### TL;DR

Searching an old Philadelphia 76ers meme about Dario Šarić “never coming over,” the author received a Google AI Overview that interpreted Dario as a rejecting romantic partner and offered emotional support, while relevant historical links appeared below. He argues that a search engine should retrieve information rather than force every query into chatbot dialogue. HN reproduced further oddities: Google soon cited the live discussion itself before later answering correctly. Commenters report inaccurate sports summaries, stale information, declining organic results, and migrations to alternative search engines.

### Comment pulse

- Conversational framing can erase user intent → the overview replaced niche historical retrieval with an invented interpersonal story.
- Search-grounded answers still hallucinate → commenters found wrong standings and outdated games despite accurate results existing on the same page.
- Switching providers is increasingly rational → users cite intrusive summaries, ads, indexing gaps, and degraded relevance — counterpoint: alternatives also have tradeoffs.

### LLM perspective

- View: Search synthesis fails conspicuously when it converts ambiguous keywords into a confident narrative before retrieving context.
- Impact: Users must spend extra effort verifying answers or changing tools, undermining the convenience AI Overviews promise.
- Watch next: Measure citation accuracy, freshness, query-intent classification, opt-out controls, correction latency, and organic-result visibility.
