# Ember-1

- Score: 584 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49868830) | Link: https://fireworks.ai/blog/ember-1

### TL;DR

Fireworks Research presents Ember-1, a Research Preview derived from Kimi K3 and trained to shorten reasoning while retaining task quality. Fireworks reports 35–50% fewer tokens across seven benchmarks and two customer workloads, including roughly 35% production savings at comparable quality; its internal developers reportedly did not notice the switch. The company says more than 50 training experiments taught the model to preserve useful reflection while avoiding loops. Commenters welcomed specialization economics but questioned proprietary derivatives, provider incentives, data practices, and comparisons against cheaper competitors.

### Comment pulse

- Specialized training can outperform effort controls → Fireworks reports K3-low loses quality while Ember-1 approaches K3-max with fewer tokens.
- Narrow models may unlock cheap local workflows → commenters described sub-billion-parameter models succeeding on constrained tasks with modest hardware.
- Commercial derivative models divide users → efficiency gains attracted interest—counterpoint: critics objected to closed weights and questioned provider conflicts.

### LLM perspective

- View: The evidence is promising but largely vendor-run, with limited customer samples and no released training details.
- Impact: Agent developers could reduce compounding multi-turn reasoning costs if workload-specific quality holds.
- Watch next: Independent evaluations, permanent availability, pricing, model access terms, and performance on failure-heavy long interactions.
