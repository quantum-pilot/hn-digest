# Show HN: Lofi Cities – Pixel-art city nights with browser-generated lofi

- Score: 312 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49869574) | Link: https://loficities.com/

### TL;DR

The captured page presents Lofi Cities as a Paris pixel-art night scene with local time and endless music generated live in the browser; little additional product documentation was available. HN commenters liked the visual premise and requested more cities and indoor views, but many said a prominent promotional billboard broke immersion. Others reported inaccurate East Asian lettering, clipped mobile search, pulsing interface badges, and an AI-generated feel. The creator acknowledged lettering and badge feedback and added cafés and apartments to the roadmap.

### Comment pulse

- The atmosphere has clear appeal → commenters praised the execution and wanted more cities, apartments, cafés, and alternate views.
- Monetization disrupted immersion → the billboard’s scale, styling, and promotional copy felt detached from the pixel-art scene.
- Reported polish gaps weakened authenticity → commenters cited incorrect scripts, mobile clipping, and generic pulsing badges.
