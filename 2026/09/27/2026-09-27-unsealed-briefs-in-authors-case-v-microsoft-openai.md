# Unsealed Briefs in Authors’ Case v. Microsoft/OpenAI

- Score: 626 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49863864) | Link: https://authorsguild.org/news/ag-v-openai-top-execs-knew-mass-book-piracy-was-illegal/

### TL;DR

An Authors Guild press release summarizes unsealed plaintiff filings in its copyright case against OpenAI and Microsoft. The plaintiffs allege internal messages show executives knew LibGen was legally and reputationally risky, anticipated models replacing writers, discussed completing George R.R. Martin’s series, and later removed LibGen material from systems. These are advocacy claims supporting partial summary judgment, not court findings. HN focuses on possible willfulness, whether synthetic fiction could economically displace authors without matching their artistry, and technologists’ apparent misunderstanding of why readers value authorship.
