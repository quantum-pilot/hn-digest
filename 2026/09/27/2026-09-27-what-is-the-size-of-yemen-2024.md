# What is the size of Yemen? (2024)

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49862809) | Link: https://theborys.substack.com/p/what-is-the-size-of-yemen

### TL;DR

The author’s map tracing estimated Yemen at roughly 456,000 square kilometers, far below widely repeated figures of 528,000 or 555,000. He traces the discrepancy to two inherited errors: North Yemen’s area apparently reused a 1904 Ottoman vilayet boundary, while a 1976 table seemingly double-counted South Yemen’s Thamud area. Later sources combined those figures after unification. HN notes that a 2005 Yemeni government document contained a plausible corrected number and enabled Wikipedia’s update, while debating political borders, measurement methods, and other persistent clerical errors.
