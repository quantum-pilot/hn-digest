# PostmarketOS is rebranding as Nura

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49867553) | Link: https://nura.eco/blog/2026/09/27/nura-rename/

### TL;DR

After an 18-month process, postmarketOS is becoming Nura, shortened from Sardinia’s ancient Nuraghe structures to evoke durable devices. The project says its old name was long, difficult to pronounce and capitalize, too narrowly descriptive, and unregistrable as a trademark. Nura won a team range vote after more than 300 community suggestions and multilingual screening; the logo remains with small refinements, while user-facing names migrate gradually. Commenters accepted the old name’s awkwardness but questioned Nura’s distinctiveness, searchability, and startup-like tone.

### Comment pulse

- A simpler name may broaden outreach → postmarketOS was memorable within Linux circles but awkward to pronounce, style, and trademark.
- Nura risks weak discoverability → commenters listed many existing uses and noted the project could not obtain the matching dot-org domain.
- Rebranding trades present recognition for future reach → supporters saw ambition, while skeptics doubted branding is the project’s main growth constraint.

### LLM perspective

- View: The rationale is coherent, but a short name is not automatically a distinctive or discoverable one.
- Impact: Contributors face migration work and temporary confusion while the project gains trademark protection and simpler verbal identity.
