# In an $80 motel room, a discovery to shed light on the origins of life

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49866951) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/26/science/motel-science-discovery.html

### TL;DR

A makeshift motel-room expedition identified two new photosynthetic Paulinella species, raising the known total to six. Fresh observations of reversed scale-overlap patterns led the team to electron microscopy and DNA sequencing. Paulinella matters because its bacterium-to-organelle transition occurred about 100 million years ago, far more recently than the roughly 1.5-billion-year-old event underlying plants, leaving more intermediate biology to study. The collaboration grew from scientists supporting one another during a temporary federal-funding freeze. Commenters praised sketching and citizen science but criticized the headline’s “origins of life” framing.
