# M5 Ultra Mac Studio Review

- Score: 260 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49787313) | Link: https://www.macstories.net/stories/m5-ultra-mac-studio-review-the-dream-mac-for-local-ai-agents/

### TL;DR

Federico Viticci finds the 256GB M5 Ultra Mac Studio practical for local AI agents, citing roughly 2.5× faster prompt processing and about 70% faster generation than his M3 Ultra in tested workflows. Its 1.2TB/s memory bandwidth and unified memory support models and long contexts beyond a 32GB RTX 5090, although the 5090 remains faster on fitting workloads. HN readers welcomed the gains but questioned benchmark optimization, the $18,000 test configuration, and whether local models match cloud coding productivity.
