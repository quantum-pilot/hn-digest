# Turn off and restrict access to Apple Intelligence features on Mac

- Score: 339 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49790409) | Link: https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-mchlb2e44f94/mac

### TL;DR

Apple documents how macOS users can restrict Writing Tools, image creation, and third-party intelligence extensions through Screen Time’s Content & Privacy settings. Each control sits under Intelligence & Siri after restrictions are enabled; availability varies by language, region, and OS version. HN discussion focuses less on the procedure than its discoverability: users find the parental-control location unintuitive, want downloaded model storage reclaimed, and criticize ambiguous toggles, fragmented settings, limited utility, and differing behavior across Apple platforms.

### Comment pulse

- The controls are poorly discoverable → non-parents would reasonably search Siri or Privacy settings rather than Screen Time restrictions.
- Disabling features should reclaim storage → commenters report large local models remaining despite having no intention to use them.
- Platform controls lack coherence → macOS offers grouped restrictions, while commenters report more fragmented per-app steps on iOS.
