# MiMo v2.6

- Score: 1076 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49792730) | Link: https://mimo.xiaomi.com/mimo-v2-6

### TL;DR

Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.6-Pro and Flash as omnimodal models, plus a Pro UltraSpeed service advertised at up to 20× output speed. Xiaomi says 30 reinforcement-learning steps over roughly 750,000 trajectories cost $2.62 million for Pro and $0.85 million for Flash, improving held-out software-engineering scores while extending visual, cyber, tool-use, and research capabilities. It published training environments, RL code, and a technical report. HN readers praised the live training dashboard, while questioning benchmark reliability and broader geopolitical claims.
