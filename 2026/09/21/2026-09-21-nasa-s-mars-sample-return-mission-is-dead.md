# NASA’s Mars Sample Return mission is dead

- Score: 443 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49791939) | Link: https://www.science.org/content/article/nasa-s-mars-sample-return-mission-dead

### TL;DR

A congressional compromise spending bill backed ending NASA’s existing Mars Sample Return program, though the bill still required passage and signature when reported. It redirected $110 million toward future Mars technologies while leaving Perseverance’s collected cores without a return plan. MSR’s projected cost had risen to $11 billion before NASA proposed a $7 billion redesign. Scientists emphasized the samples’ potential biosignatures and US leadership; HN commenters debated poor program management, private launch alternatives, crewed missions, and China’s planned sample return.

### Comment pulse

- Cost growth made cancellation politically plausible → critics saw excessive complexity and legacy planning, while defenders doubted unproven commercial substitutes.
- Waiting for astronauts risks indefinite delay → human Mars timelines remain uncertain while scientifically selected samples already exist.
- International consequences extend beyond NASA → ESA had developed an orbiter, and China planned a separate 2028 launch.
