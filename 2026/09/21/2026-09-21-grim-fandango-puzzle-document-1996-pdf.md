# Grim Fandango Puzzle Document (1996) [pdf]

- Score: 372 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49783495) | Link: http://gameshelf.jmac.org/2008/11/13/GrimPuzzleDoc_small.pdf

### TL;DR

This 72-page 1996 internal Grim Fandango document lays out the adventure across four years, combining character lists, location maps, puzzle-flow diagrams, cut-scenes, and detailed solution logic. Its charts deliberately move from linear tutorials toward increasingly nonlinear groups, while jokes, drawings, and asides give a production artifact unusual personality. HN readers celebrated how that voice carried into the finished game and highlighted a later-described deadline trick: the still-undesigned final puzzle was concealed beneath overlapping nonsense text.
