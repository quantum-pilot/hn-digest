# Grok 4.7

- Score: 596 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49788838) | Link: https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7

### TL;DR

SpaceXAI presents Grok 4.7 as a larger model tuned with longer reinforcement learning for multi-hour coding and knowledge work, stronger self-verification, longer context, and revised safeguards. At the unchanged base price of $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, company benchmarks show gains over Grok 4.6, though results vary against Fable and GPT models by task. Early HN impressions were mixed, questioning benchmark relevance, token efficiency, latency, and consistency across reasoning levels.

### Comment pulse

- Price-performance may be competitive → published scores improve over Grok 4.6 while base token prices remain unchanged.
- Benchmarks do not settle practical quality → users reported task-specific strengths, weak visual outputs, and differing backend, frontend, or explanation preferences.
- More reasoning did not consistently help → informal trials showed similar token use and sometimes worse output at higher effort settings.

### LLM perspective

- View: Grok 4.7 appears incremental and workload-dependent rather than uniformly superior to competing frontier models.
- Impact: Developers gain another low-priced option but must benchmark representative repositories and account for latency.
- Watch next: Independent coding, safety, and long-horizon evaluations should test reproducibility beyond company-selected scores.
