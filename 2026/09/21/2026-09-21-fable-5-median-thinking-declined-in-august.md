# Fable 5 – Median thinking declined in August

- Score: 414 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49789224) | Link: https://twitter.com/Lon/status/2101793422487204027

### TL;DR

An analysis of 6,921 Fable 5 turns and 36,374 high-effort invocations reports substantially fewer median thinking tokens in August than July. Declines appear under five weightings: 21.9% by turn, 23.1% by day, 35.1% by session, 46.2% by project, and 50.6% by invocation. The chart establishes a usage change, not its cause or a direct capability loss. HN readers debated hidden capacity management, changing workloads, expectation effects, and whether hosted models need stronger version transparency.

### Comment pulse
