# Disney+: New user agreement allows ads before movies in all subscriptions

- Score: 504 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49784336) | Link: https://consumerrights.wiki/w/Disney%2B_ad_policy_change

### TL;DR

An incomplete Consumer Rights Wiki entry says Disney+ terms updated in January 2025 permit limited advertising-related material even in “ad-free” tiers, including rights-required ads, live-event breaks, bundle promotion, branded integrations, and sponsorships. It also cites a September 2026 email to German subscribers saying ads may appear before or after content. The page flags its own sourcing and verifiability as needing work. HN disputed whether these are narrow operational exceptions or another step toward paid television with commercials.

### Comment pulse

- The change is being overstated → some readers interpret it mainly as embedded live-content ads and Disney service promotions.
- Ad-free should mean no promotion → paying a premium while receiving upsells or sponsorships violates the product’s ordinary promise.
- Subscribers can rotate or cancel services → monthly purchasing limits exposure — counterpoint: exclusive catalogs weaken meaningful competition.
