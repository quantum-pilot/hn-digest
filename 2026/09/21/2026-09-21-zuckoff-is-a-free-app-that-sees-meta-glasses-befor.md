# ZuckOff is a free app that sees Meta glasses before they see you

- Score: 397 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49785397) | Link: https://www.wired.me/story/meta-smart-glasses-detector-app-zuckoff

### TL;DR

ZuckOff scans Bluetooth advertisements for manufacturer signatures associated with Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta, Snap Spectacles, and similar camera glasses, offering rough proximity but neither recording status nor wearer identity. Its developer built fingerprints from purchased hardware; the basic iPhone scanner is free, with paid monitoring and history features. The app responds to covert-recording concerns as indicator lights can be obstructed. HN commenters support detection in principle but strongly prefer an existing open-source alternative, questioning ZuckOff’s data collection, proprietary design, marketing, and monetization.

### Comment pulse

- Ambient camera detection addresses a real consent gap → glasses may look ordinary and recording indicators can be defeated.
- Open-source alternatives inspire greater trust → commenters favor inspectable code and claimed lower data collection over a proprietary scanner.
- Monetization raises suspicion → paid features and merch feel opportunistic — counterpoint: supporters consider purchases reasonable funding if detection works.
