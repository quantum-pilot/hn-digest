# Meta VR Glasses

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49824268) | Link: https://www.meta.com/vr-glasses/

### TL;DR

Meta markets VR Glasses for spring 2027 as a 100-gram headset powered by a pocket-sized compute puck. The product page emphasizes personal cinema, streaming, infinite virtual workspaces, prescription inserts, more than 75 controller-free VR games at launch, Xbox Cloud Gaming, and connected consoles or handhelds. It provides no price or detailed display and compute specifications. HN readers liked the lighter form factor and latency pressure VR creates, but many said distrust of Meta, account policies, and socially isolating use cases outweighed the hardware’s appeal.
