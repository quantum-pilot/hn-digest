# Fixing the Portobello Police Station Clock

- Score: 414 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49817469) | Link: https://pointinthecloud.com/2026-04-11-211700.html

### TL;DR

Volunteers entered the nineteenth-century Portobello police-station tower to reset its three-faced clock after the building entered community ownership. They disengaged a gear pawl, rotated the shared shaft by hand, and inferred unseen hand positions from counterweights. The chiming system proved newer: a PIC-based controller counts hourly triggers and striker cycles, with an “advance” button setting its hour counter. The clock kept time and struck four correctly, though they disconnected the chime afterward. HN readers enjoyed the hands-on local restoration.
