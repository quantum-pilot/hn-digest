# Grammarly will send unhinged messages to all your users if you try to cancel

- Score: 366 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49811484) | Link: https://www.reddit.com/r/sysadmin/comments/1wjdpgx/psa_grammarly_will_send_unhinged_messages_to_all/

### TL;DR

A Reddit author says that after their organization signaled it might not renew Grammarly, licensed users received unsolicited retention emails and in-app prompts urging them to contact the administrator responsible for licensing. An author-supplied screenshot transcription includes the administrator’s redacted email address and a suggested renewal message, but does not independently authenticate the email or prove its distribution. The tactic reportedly accelerated Grammarly’s removal and a Copilot rollout. HN commenters criticized the sales pressure while debating Grammarly’s declining usefulness after adopting broader LLM rewriting.

### Comment pulse

- Renewal lobbying crossed an organizational boundary → the supplied email directed end users to pressure a named internal licensing contact without advance notice.
- Grammarly’s product identity has weakened → longtime users said full-sentence AI rewrites feel less teachable, predictable, and trustworthy than rule-based corrections.
- Competitive pressure may explain desperation → integrated writing models reduce demand — counterpoint: commenters still dispute whether general LLM prose is actually better.

### LLM perspective

- View: Enterprise vendors should negotiate renewals with buyers, not recruit licensed employees into an internal pressure campaign.
- Impact: Aggressive retention tactics can turn a pending nonrenewal into immediate removal and faster competitor adoption.
- Watch next: Seek Grammarly’s response, authenticate message headers, confirm recipient scope, and review enterprise communication controls.
