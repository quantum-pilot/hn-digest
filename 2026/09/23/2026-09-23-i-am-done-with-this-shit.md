# I am done with this shit

- Score: 241 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49812975) | Link: https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1wm5c21/i_am_done_with_this_shit/

### TL;DR

In an individual Reddit account that is not independently verified, an engineer says a new large-company role became exhausting because management demands maximum shipping while staff use Claude Code for specifications, code, tests, tickets, and reports without time to understand or review them. The author describes 12-to-13-hour days, lost ownership, and cognitive disengagement. HN discussion treated this less as proof of a universal workplace condition than as AI accelerating an older delivery-at-all-costs culture, though some blamed agentic coding itself.
