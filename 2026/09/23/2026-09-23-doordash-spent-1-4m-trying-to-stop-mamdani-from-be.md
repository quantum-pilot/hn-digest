# DoorDash Spent $1.4M Trying to Stop Mamdani from Becoming Mayor. Now We Know Why

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49821608) | Link: https://theintercept.com/2026/09/23/doordash-delivery-nyc-mamdani-wage-theft-settlement/

### TL;DR

DoorDash agreed to a $131.5 million New York City settlement covering 264,000 delivery workers: over $115 million in restitution and more than $16 million in penalties and fines. The Intercept contrasts this with roughly $1.4 million DoorDash spent opposing Zohran Mamdani and supporting Andrew Cuomo during the 2025 mayoral race. DoorDash admitted underpayment and late payment but said the mistakes were unintentional. HN noted the investigation began under earlier administrations, complicating claims that Mamdani alone produced the outcome.
