# Why is Hacker News like that?

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49817280) | Link: https://drewdevault.com/blog/Why-is-HN-like-that/

### TL;DR

The author argues Hacker News’s claimed apoliticism masks a rightward, pro-capital bias produced by user flagging, automated downranking, moderator choices, and Y Combinator’s institutional culture. Flagged criticism of Grok and Elon Musk serves as the central example; the essay then traces YC leaders, investments, and political associations to explain the pattern. HN respondents sharply dispute the diagnosis: some consider those comments repetitive political derailments, others see selective suppression, and a moderator preserved the thread while retaining user flags as authentic community reaction.

### Comment pulse

- Apolitical moderation can preserve prevailing power → labeling structural criticism off-topic disproportionately suppresses challenges to a technology’s owners and financing.
- Product threads benefit from topical focus → repetitive political declarations add little technical information — counterpoint: ownership and social consequences can be materially relevant.
- Community intent is contested → critics see systematic ideology, while others see varied users and overgeneralization from a small comment sample.
