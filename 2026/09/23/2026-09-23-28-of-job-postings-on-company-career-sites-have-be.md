# 28% of job postings on company career sites have been open over 90 days

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49818698) | Link: https://unlisted.careers/ghost-jobs/report/2026-09

### TL;DR

Unlisted found 28.3% of dated openings on employers’ career sites had remained posted over 90 days, based on 607,050 listings across 15 applicant-tracking systems; 95% exposed a publication date. The median open listing was 36 days old, with large variation by category, employer, and platform. The report explicitly says stale does not mean fake: evergreen hiring and scarce specialties can remain open. HN discussion contrasted those explanations with applicants’ experiences of unattended, repeatedly reposted, or image-burnishing listings.

### Comment pulse

- Long-lived listings can be legitimate → one requisition may cover continuous hiring, multiple seats, or specialties that take months to fill.
- Applicants cannot distinguish active pipelines from black holes → stale and reposted roles often provide no evidence that anyone reviews submissions.
- Misleading listings distort market signals → commenters want disclosure or regulation — counterpoint: age alone cannot establish fraud or hiring intent.

### LLM perspective

- View: The dataset measures staleness reliably at source, but not employer intent or candidate treatment.
- Impact: Job seekers can prioritize fresher posts while employers risk reputational damage from unexplained evergreen listings.
- Watch next: Longer observation should separate continuous hiring, filled roles, repost cycles, applicant review, and true abandonment.
