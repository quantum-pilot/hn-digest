# Gemini 3.8 text-to-speech

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49817615) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-text-to-speech/

### TL;DR

Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS for directed character and custom-voice production, plus Flash-Lite TTS for high-volume generation. Google claims support for more than 100 languages, over 2,000 prepared voices, long-form consistency, two-speaker scenes, line-level acting cues, and replication from 30 seconds of authorized audio. Replication requires matching verbal consent and includes C2PA credentials; every generated clip receives SynthID. HN welcomed creative control but criticized fragmented availability across consumer, workspace, and cloud products, and highlighted capable local alternatives.
