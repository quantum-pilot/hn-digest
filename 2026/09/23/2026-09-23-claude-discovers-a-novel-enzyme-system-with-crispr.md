# Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats

- Score: 594 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49820134) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system

### TL;DR

Anthropic reports that roughly 950 Claude agents searched more than 200,000 reverse transcriptases using 210 million tokens, narrowing 3,500 candidate systems to 20. They identified a previously undescribed arrangement around a known phage enzyme: a reverse transcriptase, partner gene, and regularly spaced DNA-repeat array expressed as short RNAs. Human scientists confirmed initial laboratory observations, but ART’s function remains unknown and results are a preprint. HN welcomed scalable genome mining while criticizing “discovery” and CRISPR comparisons as premature.
