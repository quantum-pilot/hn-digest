# Samsung accidentally freezes its smart fridges with a software update

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49815381) | Link: https://www.androidauthority.com/samsung-accidentally-freezes-its-smart-fridges-with-a-software-update-3714472/

### TL;DR

Samsung halted a SmartThings refrigerator update in Korea after some owners reported complete cooling failures, dark interior lights, broken automatic doors, and spoiled food. Samsung said the error occurred during update testing and that service centers were taking emergency measures; technicians reportedly replaced motherboards in some units. The unexplained deployment of test software raises a deeper reliability issue: network features can disable an appliance’s essential function. HN commenters favor disconnectable or “dumb” appliances and emphasize risks to food and refrigerated medicine.
