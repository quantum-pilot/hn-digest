# Italian parliament votes for return to nuclear energy

- Score: 703 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49819221) | Link: https://apnews.com/article/italy-nuclear-chernobyl-4891b6b7c7791ae84db6b0bf0f7cf567

### TL;DR

Italy’s Senate voted 81–51, with seven abstentions, to establish a legal framework for potentially restoring nuclear power after nearly four decades. The law authorizes twelve months of decrees on licensing, safety, waste, and site criteria, but approves no reactor construction. The government favors small modular and advanced designs for climate and energy security. Obstacles include unresolved legacy waste, local opposition, past referendums, and estimated SMR costs of €3–6 billion per 300MW unit before grid infrastructure.
