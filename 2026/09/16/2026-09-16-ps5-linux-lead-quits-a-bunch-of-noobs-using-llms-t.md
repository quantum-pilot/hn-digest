# PS5 Linux lead quits: "a bunch of noobs using LLMs" that "they don't understand"

- Score: 318 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49727627) | Link: https://frvr.com/blog/news/ps5-linux-lead-quits-as-open-source-projects-have-become-a-bunch-of-noobs-using-llms-that-they-dont-even-understand/

### TL;DR

An article says PS5 Linux lead Andy Nguyen is leaving the scene after AI-assisted researchers found and reported a hypervisor flaw he relied upon, jeopardizing installation on newer firmware and planned PS5 Pro support. Nguyen blamed participants who use LLM-discovered hacks without understanding them and said another finder broke an agreement to delay disclosure. HN commenters disputed the simplified anti-AI framing: some emphasized maintainer exhaustion and lost community purpose, while others argued competing discovery, bounty rules, and personal circumstances made prompt reporting understandable.

### Comment pulse

- Maintainer burden is becoming asymmetric → cheap generated contributions and discoveries impose costly review, explanation, and project disruption.
- The dispute concerns disclosure incentives → commenters said independent discovery created pressure to claim Sony’s bounty before another reporter.
- Community purpose exceeds output → hobbyists value shared expertise, while users may care mainly about receiving a working exploit.
