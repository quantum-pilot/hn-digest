# Original Sony PlayStation 2 security chip 'broken wide open' after 26 years

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49725356) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/playstation/26-year-old-sony-ps2-security-chip-broken-wide-open-after-four-years-of-effort-reverse-engineering-enthusiast-successfully-unlocks-cxp102064-mechacon-chip

### TL;DR

After four years of chemical decapping, optical analysis, and reverse engineering, researchers dumped the original PlayStation 2’s CXP102064 MechaCon security-chip ROM, aided by a software extraction exploit found from imperfect optical dumps. The work should support low-level emulation, vulnerability research, and possible replacement modchips. A participating researcher cautioned that copied discs already run through existing exploits, game data is generally unencrypted, and the dump alone cannot create a hardware optical-drive emulator. Commenters highlighted preservation benefits while questioning what currently improves.
