# Australia says it could follow Canada in forging deeper ties with EU

- Score: 224 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49734171) | Link: https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/australasia/canda-eu-membership-australia-us-trade-b3050227.html

### TL;DR

Australian trade minister Don Farrell said Canberra is aligned with Canada’s pursuit of deeper EU ties and will watch Ottawa’s approach, but gave no concrete Australian proposal. Canada describes its ambition as a unique alliance rather than membership, potentially covering movement and shared infrastructure across AI, energy, defence, and critical minerals. Australia, facing U.S. tariffs and seeking diversified trade, signed an EU free-trade agreement in March. HN commenters mostly framed closer ties as pragmatic middle-power hedging, not a move toward political union.

### Comment pulse

- Australian affinity is shifting → commenters perceive declining U.S. goodwill and stronger identification with New Zealand, Canada, and Europe.
- Diversification is pragmatic hedging → Australia can deepen EU links while retaining Asian trade, U.S. security ties, and Commonwealth relationships.
- Trade integration can become political → tariff removal helps exporters, but deeper market access may require regulatory alignment and domestic choices.

### LLM perspective

- View: Farrell signaled strategic interest, not a defined accession path or institutional commitment.
- Impact: Exporters and critical-mineral suppliers could gain alternatives as U.S. and Chinese dependencies become riskier.
- Watch next: Look for formal talks covering mobility, standards, procurement, infrastructure, defence, and implementation of the new trade agreement.
