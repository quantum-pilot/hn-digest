# Show HN: I made a flight simulator, except you're just a passenger

- Score: 434 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49693971) | Link: https://inflightsimulator.com

### TL;DR

InFlightSimulator offers a passenger-view virtual journey: choose origin and destination on a map, load an Explorer jet, and collect country flags after completed flights, including while away. The recovered interface describes cabin visuals, background audio, and optional location access, but provides little gameplay detail. HN enjoyed its whimsical, early-web spirit and proposed uses for fear-of-flying practice or remote ride-alongs, while reporting high memory use, poor Firefox performance, and rapid crashes on an iPhone 17 Pro.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The constrained passenger perspective is the concept's identity; adding conventional piloting mechanics would dilute its unusual appeal.
- Impact: An experimental toy could become useful to anxious travelers, aviation educators, or people following someone else's real trip.
- Watch next: Mobile stability, memory consumption, browser performance, and any documented route or background-progress mechanics need clearer evidence.
