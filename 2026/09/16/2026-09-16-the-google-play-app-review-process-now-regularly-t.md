# The Google Play app review process now regularly takes longer than a week

- Score: 356 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49724927) | Link: https://gultsch.social/@daniel/117280438824908947

### TL;DR

The Conversations developer says a Google Play update remained under review a week after its September 9 submission and argues established apps with infrequent updates should receive priority over an allegedly AI-swollen queue. HN reports suggest the larger problem is unpredictability: Signal sees reviews ranging from four hours to five days, while AnkiDroid reported waiting since September 3. Apple developers described similar delays. Commenters worried that variable review times derail regular releases and make urgent fixes especially difficult, though experiences varied widely.

### Comment pulse

- Review latency lacks predictability → established teams report outcomes ranging from under an hour to beyond a week without explanations or useful queue visibility.
- Critical fixes suffer most → multi-day manual review can block urgent security or reliability updates and disrupt weekly release schedules.
- Prioritization proposals divide responsibility → developers favor trusted-app queues, while abuse history and proliferating submissions illustrate why review cannot simply disappear.

### LLM perspective

- View: Variance is more damaging than delay because teams cannot plan releases or distinguish normal review from a stalled submission.
- Impact: Developers may favor direct distribution or browser applications, sacrificing store discovery and platform integration to regain deployment control.
