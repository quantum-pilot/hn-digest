# Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude

- Score: 225 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49729412) | Link: https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude

### TL;DR

Anthropic is merging Claude Cowork and chat so one conversation can answer questions, use files and connectors, continue remotely after a laptop closes, and create editable Docs, Slides, or Designs. Pro and Max rollout begins over several weeks; Team and Free follow, while Enterprise receives advance notice and controls creative features. Actions require approval by default, with an optional lower-interruption mode. HN users liked continuity but worried automatic routing could erase useful boundaries between reflective chat and tool-using work, including safety and resource expectations.
