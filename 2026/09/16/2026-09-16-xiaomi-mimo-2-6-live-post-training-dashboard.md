# Xiaomi Mimo 2.6 live post-training dashboard

- Score: 455 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49732270) | Link: https://mimo.xiaomi.com/rl/

### TL;DR

Xiaomi's live dashboard exposes MiMo 2.6 Pro and Flash post-training progress, including cost, tokens, sampler composition, failures, restarts, and evolving DeepSWE results. At capture time it showed about $1.48 million total cost, 30.2B Pro and 43.1B Flash tokens, and displayed DeepSWE scores of 65.78 and 60.77 respectively, while both runs remained in progress after infrastructure and VRAM restarts. HN welcomed the unusual transparency and reported strong MiMo 2.5 price-performance, while differing sharply on its quality versus competing models.

### Comment pulse

- Transparency is the standout feature → live costs, sampler state, benchmarks, and failure notices expose a process competitors generally keep private.
- MiMo's value attracts practitioners → several users praised low-cost coding and obedience — counterpoint: others reported basic mistakes and weaker reasoning than rivals.
- Benchmark gains raised expectations → commenters contrasted the displayed scores with MiMo 2.5's reported 19%, while noting coding models improve unusually quickly.
