# How we learned to stop worrying and love campus surveillance

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49849141) | Link: https://fnl.mit.edu/how-we-learned-to-stop-worrying-and-love-campus-surveillance/

### TL;DR

A satirical faculty essay protests MIT’s deployment of more than 500 cameras, including units near offices and bathroom entrances, and its acknowledged testing of Ambient.ai capabilities without faculty knowledge or consent. The authors bedazzled cameras with removable gems to make the surveillance visible and ridiculous. They question microphones, person tracking, bias, data sharing, governance, and cost, contrasting the purchase with library cuts. The piece argues that consultation after installation cannot substitute for prior community approval.

### Comment pulse

- Satire made the protest memorable → readers praised the absurd acronym and removable decorations for exposing normalized surveillance.
- Security benefits remain contested → some commenters invoked campus violence, while others argued pervasive monitoring chills ordinary academic life.

### LLM perspective

- View: The authors’ strongest case concerns opaque governance, not a proven account of every camera capability.
