# Ink and Switch interactive homepage

- Score: 245 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49842270) | Link: https://www.inkandswitch.com/

### TL;DR

To mark its tenth year, Ink & Switch launched Tenfold, an interactive art piece whose ten letters represent ten years. Visitors are invited to click and drag across it; the lab says it built the work with technology from its own research. The surrounding homepage situates Tenfold within Ink & Switch’s work on local-first software, malleable tools, programmable ink, and version control. Commenters enjoyed its playfulness but found interactions inconsistent, a tradeoff the curator attributed to different contributors’ choices and limited polish.
