# The Mafia may be keeping fentanyl out of Italy

- Score: 197 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49842487) | Link: https://economist.com/europe/2026/09/24/the-mafia-may-be-keeping-fentanyl-out-of-italy

### TL;DR

Italy recorded only two fatal fentanyl intoxications in 2024, versus 95 in Germany, despite comparable cocaine use and higher estimated high-risk opioid use. Prosecutors, police, and organized-crime writer Roberto Saviano hypothesize that Mafia groups avoid fentanyl because its lethality attracts crackdowns and damages established heroin and cocaine markets. No direct evidence shows coordinated prohibition: investigators have no wiretap or agreement. Stolen hospital vials and emerging demand suggest the situation may change. HN commenters discuss long-term profit incentives and measurement uncertainty.
