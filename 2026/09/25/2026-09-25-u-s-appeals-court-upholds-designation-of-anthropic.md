# U.S. appeals court upholds designation of Anthropic as supply chain risk

- Score: 443 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49845977) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/pentagon-anthropic-ai-risk-appeals-court.html

### TL;DR

A divided D.C. Circuit panel upheld one Pentagon designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, finding sufficient support for concerns that constrained Claude models could disrupt national-security functions. A separate federal court had already ruled a parallel designation unlawful; the government relied on two distinct legal designations requiring separate cases. The appellate ruling will not take immediate effect, allowing rehearing or Supreme Court review. HN commenters split over whether military control justifies the designation or whether it punishes Anthropic for retaining autonomous-weapons and surveillance restrictions.

### Comment pulse

- Military systems cannot depend on supplier vetoes → supporters say model constraints may unpredictably block contractually authorized national-security operations.
- The designation exceeds ordinary procurement choice → critics argue contractors could exclude Claude without broadly stigmatizing Anthropic’s supply-chain role.
- Safety terms create precedent questions → commenters debate whether commercial vendors can restrict military use without losing government-adjacent markets.
