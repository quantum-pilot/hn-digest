# Factorio that you can touch

- Score: 331 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49845133) | Link: https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-447

### TL;DR

Wube has released downloadable, printable Factorio miniatures developed with help from Prusa after a 2024 playtest inspired early prototypes. The collection focuses on early-game machinery and enemies: 15 model sets, 65 individual models, and 247 STL files. Designers rebuilt game assets for physical reality, splitting machines to avoid FDM supports, correcting isometric visual tricks, and reworking textured or fragile organic forms. Commenters celebrated the studio’s craft and openness, while discussing Factorio’s famously absorbing gameplay and the limitations of translating rendered assets.
