# Dutch governments builds alternative for Microsoft based on NixOS

- Score: 958 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49841563) | Link: https://www.dawo.community/en/

### TL;DR

DAWO is an open Dutch community joining government, industry, and society to build a digitally autonomous government workplace. Rather than one replacement product, it proposes inspectable, replaceable building blocks spanning DAWO-NixOS, cloud infrastructure, collaboration tools, and AI. Its stated goals include autonomy, security, data protection, innovation, and verifiability. Commenters welcomed reduced dependence on US technology, compared French and German public-sector efforts, and praised NixOS reproducibility while noting that immutable systems can complicate fast-moving software experimentation.
