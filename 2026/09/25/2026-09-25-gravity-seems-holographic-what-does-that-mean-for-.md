# Gravity seems holographic. What does that mean for reality?

- Score: 194 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49845998) | Link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/gravity-seems-holographic-what-does-that-mean-for-reality-20260925/

### TL;DR

Quanta surveys why physicists suspect gravitational space may admit a lower-dimensional description. Black-hole entropy scales with horizon area, while AdS/CFT gives a precise duality between gravity in anti-de Sitter space and a boundary quantum theory. Neither result proves our apparently de Sitter universe is holographic: researchers disagree whether boundary data, bulk geometry, or a deeper framework is fundamental. The article treats holography as a powerful, well-supported idea in special settings, not a settled account of reality.
