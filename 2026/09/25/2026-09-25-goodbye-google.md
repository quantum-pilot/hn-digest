# Goodbye Google

- Score: 250 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49840314) | Link: https://robert.ocallahan.org/2026/09/goodbye-google.html

### TL;DR

Robert O’Callahan resigned from Google because his chip-design tooling work would ultimately make AI cheaper and faster, conflicting with his belief that AI progress is dangerously rapid. He describes existential risk as uncertain but serious, alongside concerns about dependency, loneliness, concentrated power, disruption, cybersecurity, and accountability. He plans to maintain Pernosco and rr, study agent debugging, and cautiously use AI for human-benefiting projects. Commenters debated whether leaving sacrifices internal leverage, whether his continued AI use is inconsistent, and how rapid disruption undermines long-term education and career planning.

### Comment pulse
