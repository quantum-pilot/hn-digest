# Pentium II at 600Mhz with Voodoo 3 Emulated on 86Box with M6 Mac Mini

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49841285) | Link: https://nyaa.sh/reviews/mac-mini-m6-emulation

### TL;DR

Using a modified 86Box 6.0 build on an M6 Mac Mini, the author sustained a configured 600 MHz Pentium II Deschutes target with Voodoo 3 graphics and Windows 98 SE; 650 MHz produced audio underruns. An M4 comparison passed 500 MHz but failed 550 MHz, implying a 20% higher stable target under the same setup. The author cautions that 86Box’s P6 timing and cache model is imperfect, so benchmark scores do not represent equivalent physical Pentium performance.
