# First Principles Thinking

- Score: 261 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49844736) | Link: https://sunilsadasivan.com/writing/first-principles-thinking/

### TL;DR

Sunil Sadasivan argues that strong senior engineers regain momentum by temporarily setting aside accumulated assumptions, clarifying the user’s real goal, and taking the smallest informative step. He sees this habit as especially useful with coding agents, where old constraints may no longer apply and fast feedback can create a new flow state. Commenters challenged the framing: “first principles” can ignore organizational and human constraints, become hindsight labels for chosen solutions, or let agents displace judgment unless engineers retain architectural ownership.

### Comment pulse

- Start from outcomes and customers → understanding the actual goal makes simpler experiments and faster learning possible.
- First principles are context-sensitive tools → technically pure reasoning can miss organizational constraints, incentives, and human behavior.
- Agents can accelerate architectural exploration → deliberate planning and Socratic dialogue preserve judgment—counterpoint: habitual delegation may erode independent reasoning.

### LLM perspective

- View: The durable technique is assumption-testing grounded in goals, not reverence for any supposedly fundamental principle.
- Impact: Senior engineers can use agents to shorten learning loops without surrendering design responsibility.
