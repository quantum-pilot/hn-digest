# Meta takes down a critical video about meta AI Glasses after filming at Meta

- Score: 626 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49827794) | Link: https://www.reddit.com/r/facebook/comments/1wotwrk/meta_takes_down_a_critical_video_about_meta_ai/

### TL;DR

A Reddit post reproducing a translated Dutch news report says Meta removed Roel Maalderink’s satirical video from Facebook and Instagram for possible bullying and harassment. Made with privacy group Bits of Freedom, the video showed Maalderink wearing Meta camera glasses outside Meta’s Amsterdam office and asking employees how they felt about being filmed. The creators called removal self-serving censorship; HN viewers disagreed over whether the interactions constituted harassment, while broadly debating covert recording, public-space rights, platform power, and proportionate regulation.
