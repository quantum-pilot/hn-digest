# 2DWillNeverDie

- Score: 330 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49801436) | Link: https://2dwillneverdie.com/

### TL;DR

A same-site introduction to 2DWillNeverDie explains pixel art as deliberate, pixel-level image making that outgrew its origins in severe console limits. It remains useful for crisp interfaces, compact handheld graphics, consistent animation, remixing, and hybrids that place sprites in 3D scenes. Its accessibility and precise constraints also make the craft relaxing and stylistically diverse. HN readers praised how well hand-authored sprites age, while debating whether 3D-assisted workflows can reproduce the expressive choices embedded in individual pixels.
