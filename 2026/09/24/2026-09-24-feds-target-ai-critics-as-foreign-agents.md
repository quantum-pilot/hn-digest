# Feds Target AI Critics as "Foreign Agents"

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49824686) | Link: https://www.kenklippenstein.com/p/feds-think-ai-critics-are-foreign

### TL;DR

The author argues U.S. officials are recasting domestic opposition to AI and data centers as potential foreign influence. The piece connects presidential denunciations, a Justice Department foreign-agent warning, congressional requests for investigations, and industry claims about Chinese-backed protest networks. It contrasts those claims with polling showing broad local resistance and cites one prominent accuser’s admission that he lacked evidence against named groups. HN commenters viewed criminalizing criticism as authoritarian, while distinguishing genuine influence operations from the much larger question of domestic sentiment.

### Comment pulse

- Foreign influence can coexist with legitimate grievances → evidence of Chinese operations does not establish that they drive broad public resistance.
