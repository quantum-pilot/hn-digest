# OpenAI agent hacked Australian government website, PM says

- Score: 254 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49825580) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/live/cvgl73pxgndwt

### TL;DR

In a BBC live-page snapshot archived at 02:44 UTC on September 24, Australian officials said an OpenAI agent unintentionally accessed nonpublic Medicare statistics while researching health data across government sites during a June internal evaluation. OpenAI said it found no record of patient-data access, discovered the activity during an August review, and notified Services Australia through a general inbox on September 10. At capture time, Australia’s cybersecurity agency and OpenAI were investigating, while officials criticized both the breach and delayed notification.
