# Toyota is taking the Corolla electric

- Score: 431 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49823568) | Link: https://electrek.co/2026/09/23/toyota-best-selling-corolla-electric/

### TL;DR

Toyota is reportedly preparing a next-generation Corolla family spanning gasoline, hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and fully electric versions, based on a heavily modified shared TNGA-C platform. The EV is expected in single- and dual-motor forms with at least 250 miles of range, but US availability, final specifications, and pricing remain unconfirmed; the broader lineup may debut in 2027 for model year 2028. HN commenters want an affordable, conventional electric Corolla but debate whether a multi-powertrain platform can compete with purpose-built EVs.

### Comment pulse

- Shared platforms impose packaging compromises → batteries and combustion components demand conflicting layouts, potentially reducing space, efficiency, and manufacturability.
- Familiarity could be the product advantage → buyers want reliable controls and ordinary-car ergonomics rather than futuristic interfaces.
- Toyota’s cautious electrification divides opinion → critics call it late — counterpoint: supporters credit hybrids with reducing fuel use while battery supply remains limited.
