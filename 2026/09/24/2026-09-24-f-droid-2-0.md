# F-Droid 2.0

- Score: 1443 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49831968) | Link: https://f-droid.org/2026/09/24/f-droid-2.0-a-new-chapter-for-android-freedom.html

### TL;DR

F-Droid 2.0 is the official client’s largest redesign in a decade, rebuilding its interface with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose after 14 test releases. It adds Discover, broader categories, description and translated-text search, combinable filters, automatic updates, and smoother installs through newer Android APIs. Privacy features remain, but Tor configuration changed; privileged-extension support, panic-triggered app wiping, and the new Nearby implementation are absent initially. HN welcomed modernization while splitting over Material Design, update ergonomics, and alternative clients.

### Comment pulse

- Modern internals improve sustainability → current Android tooling lowers contributor barriers and reduces long-standing maintenance debt.
- The redesign divides users → some find its hierarchy clear, while others dislike borderless Material styling and additional update taps.
- Alternative clients expand choice → Droid-ify offers different ergonomics — counterpoint: clients may lag F-Droid’s index-signing and privacy protections.
