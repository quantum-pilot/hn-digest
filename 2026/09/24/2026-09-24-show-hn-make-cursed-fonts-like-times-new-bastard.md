# Show HN: Make cursed fonts like Times New Bastard

- Score: 848 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49823738) | Link: https://bastardica.mitpit.com

### TL;DR

Bastardica is a browser-based font foundry for deliberately unsettling typefaces. Users combine glyphs from multiple fonts at configurable strides, adjust scale and offsets, preview results, and export standard TTF, OTF, or WOFF2 files. Processing stays local through Pyodide and fontTools, while OpenType substitutions make the mixtures work wherever ligatures are supported. The creator warns that outputs are derivative works requiring compatible source licenses. HN commenters enthusiastically propose pranks, share related experiments, and emphasize that an immediate visual demo drives engagement.
