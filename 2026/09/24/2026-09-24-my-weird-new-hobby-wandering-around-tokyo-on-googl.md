# My weird new hobby: Wandering around Tokyo on Google Maps

- Score: 418 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49805344) | Link: https://ahmedhossamdev.com/writing/my-weird-new-hobby-wandering-around-tokyo/

### TL;DR

The author describes wandering Tokyo through Street View, revisiting older captures to notice small changes and imagine the lives behind frozen scenes. A white Nissan 350Z appeared in the same place across captures from 2009 onward, suggesting repeated home parking rather than abandonment. After publication, a Tokyo resident checked the address and found both car and house gone, making the archive more poignant. HN readers shared similar discoveries involving former cars, changed neighborhoods, deceased relatives, and virtual or physical city walks.
