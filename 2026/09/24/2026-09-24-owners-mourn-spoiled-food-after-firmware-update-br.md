# Owners mourn spoiled food after firmware update bricks Samsung smart fridges

- Score: 317 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49829960) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/owners-mourn-spoiled-food-after-firmware-update-bricks-samsung-smart-fridges/

### TL;DR

Samsung says an internal test related to a refrigerator software update caused some Korean Bespoke AI fridges to lose power or freeze on an update screen on September 22. Reports centered on four-door models from 2024 onward; customers described spoiled food, hundreds of service cases, and scarce holiday repair capacity. Samsung suspended testing and activated emergency service measures but did not disclose affected-unit counts or prevention plans. HN discussion focused on why smart features can disable core refrigeration instead of failing safely.
