# Two-tier encryption in the UK

- Score: 502 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49828731) | Link: https://macanorak.com/two-tier-encryption-in-the-uk/

### TL;DR

UK iCloud users now face unequal protection: those who enabled Apple’s Advanced Data Protection before its February 2025 withdrawal retain broader end-to-end encryption, while newer or late adopters cannot enable it. The author links this to a reported secret Technical Capability Notice demanding lawful-access capability, initially worldwide and later narrowed to UK citizens. Apple withdrew the feature rather than build access, while challenging secrecy around the notice. HN commenters condemn government pressure but also dispute Apple’s privacy reputation and the strength of default iCloud protections.

### Comment pulse

- Exceptional access creates systemic risk → any mechanism built for authorized recovery may also become exploitable by criminals or hostile governments.
- Secret notices undermine democratic scrutiny → gag rules prevent users and lawmakers from evaluating consequential security mandates.
- Apple’s resistance has practical limits → supporters praise refusing a backdoor — counterpoint: critics cite weaker defaults and compliance in larger markets.

- View: Withdrawing the feature avoided a new access mechanism but shifted the security cost onto uninformed and future UK customers.
