# GrapheneOS' rewritten Messages app is released

- Score: 268 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49663373) | Link: https://github.com/GrapheneOS/Messaging/releases/tag/13

### TL;DR

GrapheneOS released version 13 of its SMS/MMS app, rebuilding every screen with Jetpack Compose and Material 3. The release adds large-screen layouts, pinning, archiving, snoozing, unread controls, richer message details, redesigned media and sharing flows, accessibility work, and extensive crash and notification fixes. Security changes include stricter shared-content validation, restricted widget intents, parser allocation limits, and opt-in YouTube previews. The app is currently available through GrapheneOS’s Alpha channel rather than broadly outside the operating system.
