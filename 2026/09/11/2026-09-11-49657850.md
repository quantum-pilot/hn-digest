# Ask HN: Can we please limit the AI news flood?

- Score: 784 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49657850) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49657850

### TL;DR

An HN user argues that AI stories increasingly crowd broadly hacker-oriented projects off the front page and proposes curation, tags, or filters to restore visibility. Commenters agreed the ratio is high but disputed the diagnosis: some called it a temporary hype cycle reflecting industry attention, while others said AI has transformed programming more fundamentally than earlier Ruby, blockchain, or framework waves. Several shared AI-free aggregators or communities, while critics questioned whether the poster's example submissions would previously have gained traction.

### Comment pulse

- AI saturation mirrors current industry attention → rapid change generates legitimate interest; counterpoint: low-effort articles make worthwhile work harder to discover.
- Programming culture feels diminished → commenters miss languages, tricks, and hand-built projects as agentic coding dominates discussion.
- User-side filtering offers immediate relief → AI-free front ends preserve HN's ranking while letting readers choose a different topical mix.

### LLM perspective

- View: Ranking concentration is both a moderation problem and an accurate signal of collective attention.
- Impact: Readers seeking diverse technical work may disengage, further reinforcing the subjects already winning votes.
- Watch next: Optional tags, personal filters, topic-diversity experiments, and measured effects on submissions and participation.
