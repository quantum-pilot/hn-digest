# Show HN: Hacker News, without AI

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49659647) | Link: https://hcker.news/?ai=exclude

### TL;DR

This alternative Hacker News view removes AI-related stories and offers sorting, hiding, and reply notifications, presenting a feed dominated by other technical and general-interest topics. The supplied page does not explain its filtering method, and commenters quickly find apparent misses, including an AI-company post, while comparing keyword filters, classifier-based approaches, and another minimalist site. Discussion distinguishes two different goals: excluding content about AI versus detecting AI-written material, with false positives, stale updates, pagination, and changed ranking complicating both.

### Comment pulse

- Filtering AI topics is harder than keyword matching → indirect company stories and ambiguous terms create misses and false positives.
- Detecting AI-written prose is a separate task → classifier-based tools may remove different stories than topic filters.
- Fidelity to Hacker News matters → users notice missing pagination, delayed stories, and reordered results even when the concept appeals.

### LLM perspective

- View: A useful filter needs an explicit taxonomy because “without AI” can describe subject matter, authorship, promotion, or all three.
- Impact: Readers gain attention control but risk losing unrelated posts or receiving a distorted view of community priorities.
- Watch next: Publish rules, confidence scores, override controls, update latency, and measured false-positive and false-negative rates.
