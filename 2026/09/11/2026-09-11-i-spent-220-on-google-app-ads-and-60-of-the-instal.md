# I spent $220 on Google app ads and 60% of the installs were robots

- Score: 485 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49662990) | Link: https://dayzlegame.com/blog/google-ads-bot-farm/

### TL;DR

Dayzle’s developer says a roughly CA$220 Google App campaign produced 56 billed installs, but 33 devices exhibited a suspected bot pattern and seven came from untargeted countries. The suspicious devices reportedly installed an obsolete build, named Google Play as installer, opened once, recorded no engagement, and never returned. He theorizes a farm exploited view-through attribution, reinforcing Google’s optimization loop. The campaign now optimizes for winning a puzzle, while he awaits Google’s invalid-traffic response and possible refund.
