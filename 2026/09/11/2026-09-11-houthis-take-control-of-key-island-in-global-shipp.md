# Houthis 'take control' of key island in global shipping route

- Score: 374 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49658299) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/live/cmd683p01eljt

### TL;DR

Contemporaneous reports said Houthi fighters had reached Perim Island, which divides the Bab al-Mandab Strait at a critical Red Sea shipping chokepoint, after Yemeni government forces reportedly withdrew. The BBC live page noted no immediate Houthi confirmation. The movement said international shipping remained safe except Saudi vessels, while Saudi oil exports had become more dependent on the Red Sea amid disruption at Hormuz. Separately, satellite imagery showed pipeline fires whose cause and damage could not yet be determined.
