# Mexican student creates an acoustic fire extinguisher to put out fire in seconds

- Score: 379 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49652237) | Link: https://www.upsocl.com/en/16-year-old-mexican-student-creates-an-acoustic-fire-extinguisher-that-uses-sound-waves-to-put-out-fires-in-seconds/

### TL;DR

An article says 16-year-old Ángela Karime Venegas Hernández built “Vortex Tech,” a battery-powered speaker system emitting 30 pulses per second to suppress flames by displacing oxygen. It claims more than 100 tests extinguished candles, wood, liquids, grease, and electronic-equipment fires within five to eight seconds, without residue or harm. Commenters applaud her experimentation but note acoustic suppression is established technology, question novelty and product readiness, and suggest fixed-distance settings such as grills or grain silos may be more plausible applications.
