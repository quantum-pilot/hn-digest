# Logo Programming

- Score: 269 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49622406) | Link: https://el.media.mit.edu/logo-foundation/what_is_logo/logo_programming.html

### TL;DR

Logo is presented as a Lisp dialect designed for learning through immediate feedback, small composable procedures, extensible vocabulary, and flexible words and lists. Turtle graphics make commands and recursion visually observable, while procedures become indistinguishable in use from built-in primitives. Some implementations extend the model with objects or concurrent processes. Hacker News commenters recalled Logo as a gentle, memorable introduction to abstraction, debugging, recursion, geometry, and agent-based simulation, including for young children and nontechnical graduate students.
