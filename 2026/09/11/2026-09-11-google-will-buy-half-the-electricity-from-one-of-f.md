# Google will buy half the electricity from one of Finland's nuclear power plants

- Score: 344 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49652105) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c8r6y4me2g6o

### TL;DR

Google announced a €13 billion Finnish AI-infrastructure program covering three new data centers, an expanded Hamina site, and energy and community projects. A 22-year agreement lets it buy up to half of Fortum's Loviisa nuclear plant output, which currently supplies about 10% of Finland's electricity. Google projects 37,000 construction jobs and a €3.6 billion annual GDP boost. HN welcomed low-carbon power but debated electricity prices, limited permanent employment, tax benefits, grid capacity, and whether the contract enables new generation.
