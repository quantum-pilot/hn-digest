# A misalignment of AI in mathematics

- Score: 860 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49662371) | Link: https://mathandai.org/

### TL;DR

A declaration signed by prominent mathematicians argues that AI companies' race to solve open problems mistakes a proxy—finished proofs—for mathematics' deeper goals: conceptual understanding, attribution, education, and human transmission of ideas. Its authors claim rushed, mass-produced solutions could exhaust valuable problems without integrating methods into the field, though AI could still accelerate genuine study if governed differently. HN debated whether machine proofs would instead stimulate exposition and new work, and whether changed credit systems, secrecy, or compute-rich laboratories are now unavoidable.

### Comment pulse
