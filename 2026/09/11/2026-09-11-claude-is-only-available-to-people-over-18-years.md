# Claude is only available to people over 18 years

- Score: 633 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49656225) | Link: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15171100-age-assurance-on-claude

### TL;DR

Anthropic says consumer Claude accounts are restricted to adults and may be disabled when its safety systems detect indicators of minor use. Affected users can appeal through Yoti using facial age estimation, identity documents, or a verified digital credential. Anthropic states Yoti deletes submitted personal data after checking and returns only pass or fail. HN debated whether the policy protects minors or reduces regulatory liability, and whether outsourcing verification meaningfully addresses privacy and breach risks. Commenters also noted the restriction predates this discussion.

### Comment pulse

- Third-party processing still creates risk → commenters distrust uploading faces or documents even when Anthropic receives only a result.
- Protecting minors conflicts with parental autonomy → regulation may help children lacking support; counterpoint: blanket exclusion removes legitimate educational access.
