# Spain Orders Blocks on Archive.today and Its Mirrors

- Score: 350 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49772961) | Link: https://reclaimthenet.org/spain-blocks-archive-today-and-mirrors

### TL;DR

Reclaim The Net reports that Spain’s Intellectual Property Commission administratively ordered Archive.today and three mirrors blocked after an unnamed rights holder complained, without a court ruling. Blocking reportedly began across operators in mid-August under a 2021 rapid-mirror protocol, yet the domains were absent from the ministry’s September 11 public blocklist. Affected users see an illegality warning. HN reports suggest uneven enforcement: some Spanish users retained access while others saw errors, prompting discussion of circumvention and collateral damage from broader sports-related blocking.
