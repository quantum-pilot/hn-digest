# RSA-896

- Score: 215 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49771966) | Link: https://saweis.net/posts/rsa-896.html

### TL;DR

Stephen Weis reports factoring RSA-896 after using Claude to port CADO-NFS to GPUs and orchestrate up to 2,048 GPUs on idle Anthropic capacity. The ten-day run consumed about 30 GPU-years. Weis explicitly says it produced no meaningful General Number Field Sieve improvement and does not threaten deployed RSA-2048 keys; he argues it demonstrates that data-center-scale actors can attack RSA-1024. Discussion focused on compute cost, what “idle” capacity means, and the limits of extrapolating to larger keys.
