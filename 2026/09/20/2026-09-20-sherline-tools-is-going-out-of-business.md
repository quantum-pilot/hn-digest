# Sherline Tools Is Going Out of Business

- Score: 205 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49776627) | Link: https://toolguyd.com/sherline-tools-shutting-down-usa-production/

### TL;DR

Sherline says it is winding down US manufacturing after pandemic effects, rising operating costs, workforce constraints, and changing purchasing habits made continued production unsustainable. It expects to build machines, tooling, accessories, and replacement parts only as remaining resources allow through October 2026, with some operations ending sooner; an owner reportedly said the business would cease by year-end at latest. HN discussion split between lamenting diminished hands-on machine building and arguing that better, cheaper printers, routers, conversions, and imported components transformed the market.

### Comment pulse

- Sherline’s closure reflects competitive mismatch → commenters said its small work envelope and slowly changing products lost value against newer machines and conversions.
- Home making is evolving, not disappearing → affordable reliable printers and CNC tools let users make parts without first building machinery.
- Existing owners face support uncertainty → remaining inventory may cover parts and warranties temporarily, but specialized accessories could become scarce.
