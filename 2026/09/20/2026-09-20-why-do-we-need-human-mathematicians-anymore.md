# Why do we need human mathematicians anymore?

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49774521) | Link: https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/19/why-do-we-need-human-mathematicians-anymore/

### TL;DR

In a guest post hosted by Terence Tao, Po-Shen Loh argues that mathematics should explicitly serve human flourishing. Even if AI produces most proofs, humans must choose research directions and retain frontier expertise to supervise proliferating, potentially untrusted “control points.” He predicts oversight demand could exceed the available skilled workforce and force AI development to slow. Consequences include accepting AI-assisted discovery, preserving expert pipelines, rewarding teaching, and pursuing socially useful work. Commenters question corporate incentives, limited species comparisons, and whether usefulness requires human understanding.
