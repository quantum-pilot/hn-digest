# Samsung is expected to more than double output of its HBM4 and HBM4E DRAM

- Score: 432 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49778029) | Link: https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/20/samsung-to-double-hbm4-output-next-year-sources-say

### TL;DR

Seoul Economic Daily reports that Samsung is expected to at least double HBM4 and HBM4E output next year, inferred partly from outsourced glass-carrier cleaning rising from 20,000 to 50,000 sheets monthly. Industry sources project total HBM wafer input increasing about 40%, from 180,000 to 250,000 per month, while the HBM4 family’s shipment share rises from roughly 40% to 80%. Commenters highlighted wafer thinning’s manufacturing difficulty and HBM’s bandwidth, capacity, packaging, latency, and cost tradeoffs.

### Comment pulse

- HBM packaging is a critical constraint → thinning, drilling, plating, stacking, and alignment add capacity and yield challenges beyond processor fabrication.
- HBM is not a universal DRAM replacement → extreme bandwidth requires wide buses, while package integration, capacity cost, and latency limit consumer appeal.
- More capacity may not settle the market → commenters disagreed whether AI demand will absorb expansion or reverse before new fabs mature.
