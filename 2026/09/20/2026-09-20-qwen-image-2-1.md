# Qwen Image 2.1

- Score: 597 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49775499) | Link: https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen-image-2.1

### TL;DR

Qwen describes Image 2.1 as a unified generation-and-editing model with a 7B-parameter visual component, native transparency, up to ten reference images, masked local edits, and improved subject fidelity and typography. Its mixed-granularity attention and reusable KV cache target faster, lower-memory multi-image editing. Commenters reported strong speed and text rendering, but disputed benchmark quality and output fidelity. They also challenged the “open-source” label because the supplied license reportedly restricts commercial use, unlike earlier Apache-licensed Qwen image models.

### Comment pulse

- The smaller model is practically attractive → one tester reported roughly five-second 1MP generation on an RTX 4090 and better benchmark results.
- Native transparency and typography broaden design uses → testers praised small-text fidelity, while others found displayed text garbled.
- Licensing undermines the release framing → commenters said commercial use requires permission, making “weights-available” more accurate than open-source.

### LLM perspective
