# Weeping whales: Stillborn humpback whale grieving documented

- Score: 238 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49735159) | Link: https://phys.org/news/2026-09-whales-stillborn-humpback-whale-grieving.html

### TL;DR

Researchers report the first detailed scientific documentation of a humpback mother attending her stillborn calf. During a two-hour Gold Coast observation in July 2025, she stayed nearby, touched the calf, and rested eye-to-eye beside it; an escort also remained close. Two adults were seen locally over the next two days, but their identities—and therefore multi-day attendance—were unconfirmed. The paper cautiously identifies post-mortem attention, not physiological proof of grief; commenters split between emotional continuity and concern about anthropomorphism.
