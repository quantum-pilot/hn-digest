# Korea raises data breach fines to 10% of revenue

- Score: 263 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49759466) | Link: https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/korea-raises-data-breach-fines-to-10-of-revenue/12869899

### TL;DR

Korea’s revised Personal Information Protection Act allows fines up to 10% of total annual revenue for leaks affecting at least 10 million people through intent or gross negligence. The enforcement decree targets repeated serious violations within three years or ignored corrective orders followed by a breach; the prior cap was 3% of sales. Companies must notify users within 72 hours when exposure is highly likely, even before confirmation. Regulators can reduce penalties for preventive investment or prompt detection, disclosure, and containment. HN welcomed deterrence but questioned the liability threshold.
