# Warren Buffett Steps Down as Berkshire Chairman, Names Son to Replace Him

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49752614) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/18/business/warren-buffett-berkshire-chairman.html

### TL;DR

Warren Buffett, 96, stepped down immediately as Berkshire Hathaway chairman while remaining on its board as chairman emeritus. His older son, Howard G. Buffett, assumes the chair under a succession plan discussed publicly since at least 2011. The change continues Berkshire's transition after Greg Abel became chief executive at the end of 2025. HN debate focused on hereditary optics versus role separation: commenters stressed that Howard's nonexecutive chairmanship safeguards ownership and culture and can oversee the CEO, rather than running daily operations as Abel does.

### Comment pulse

- Succession separates governance from operations → Howard chairs the board and represents family ownership, while Greg Abel remains responsible for running Berkshire.
- The family appointment invites scrutiny → critics contrasted it with Buffett's past attacks on inherited advantage — counterpoint: supporters emphasized longstanding planning and stewardship.
- Investment forecasts remain contested → shareholders disagreed whether Berkshire's cash holdings and mature portfolio will outperform an index after Buffett's departure.

- View: The transition preserves continuity by dividing Buffett's former authority between a professional chief executive and a family-linked chair.
