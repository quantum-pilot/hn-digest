# Microsoft exec called AI scraping 'the largest theft of labor in human history'

- Score: 859 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49752056) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/microsoft-exec-called-ai-scraping-the-largest-theft-of-labor-in-human-history-new-unredacted-filings-reveal/

### TL;DR

Newly unredacted allegations in publishers’ copyright litigation say Microsoft and OpenAI internally recognized that mass training-data collection threatened journalism. The Times’ brief quotes a Microsoft researcher calling it unprecedented theft, reports paywall-circumvention discussions and stripped copyright notices, and says Copilot reduced Times click-through by up to 93%. Nadella testified that paywalled material should be licensed. However, much comes from the plaintiffs’ characterization; underlying exhibits remain sealed, quotes lack full context, fair-use legality remains unresolved, and neither company commented. HN debate split over licensing and information freedom.
