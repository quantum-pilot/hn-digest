# Android 17 is the first since 3.x to add new APIs without releasing to the AOSP

- Score: 468 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49758736) | Link: https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/117282080803799576

### TL;DR

Android 17 QPR1 introduces developer APIs without a simultaneous Android Open Source Project release, the first such case since Android 3.x. Those APIs are currently limited to Pixel OS because QPR1 and QPR3 releases became Pixel-only starting with Android 16. Commenters clarified that the APIs are expected to reach AOSP and other manufacturers with Android 17 QPR2 in December 2026, making this a temporary lead rather than permanent exclusivity. Debate centered on Google’s platform control and separate claims of delayed security content.
