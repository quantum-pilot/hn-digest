# Qwen 3.8 Omni Flash

- Score: 327 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49747925) | Link: https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.8-omni-flash

### TL;DR

Qwen launched Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, accepting text, images, audio, and video across a one-million-token context and extending perception into planning, tool use, editing, translation, meeting follow-up, and real-time interaction. Qwen reports an average gain above 25% over Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus across 29 evaluations, sharply lower media-input pricing, and audio performance exceeding Gemini 3.8 Flash under its methodology. HN readers welcomed the claimed cost-performance but stressed that bespoke testing matters more than aggregate benchmarks and noted model-selection overload, unusual reasoning traces, and a 441 MiB webpage download.

### Comment pulse
