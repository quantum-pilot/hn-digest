# I vibed a proof of Conway's conjecture

- Score: 205 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49755024) | Link: https://overreacted.io/how-i-vibed-a-proof-of-conways-conjecture/

### TL;DR

An amateur reports producing a Lean-checked proof of Conway’s 50-year-old refinement conjecture for omnific integers after a month of coordinating multiple AI models. Early agents generated grandiose terminology, circular arguments, and thousands of misleading lines; progress came after repeatedly discarding work, keeping mathematical ideas only slightly ahead of formalization, and using adversarial review and dependency maps. The certificate passed mechanical checks but has not been independently verified by mathematicians. The author estimates roughly 40 billion tokens and $40,000 at API prices.
