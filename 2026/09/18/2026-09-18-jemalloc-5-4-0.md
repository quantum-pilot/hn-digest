# Jemalloc 5.4.0

- Score: 323 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49750152) | Link: https://github.com/jemalloc/jemalloc/releases/tag/5.4.0

### TL;DR

Jemalloc 5.4.0 lands more than 160 commits centered on maintenance, correctness, portability, and internal simplification. New support lets custom extent hooks mark pinned mappings and exposes pinned-memory statistics; per-CPU arena selection can resume, and C++ infallible allocation becomes compile-time configured. The incompatible adaptive tcache policy removes seven legacy controls. Fixes cover errno preservation, overflow, C23 null frees, thread-state lifecycles, deadlock, and profiling guards. Major refactors modularize allocator subsystems, statistics, page allocation, background threads, and operating-system integration.
