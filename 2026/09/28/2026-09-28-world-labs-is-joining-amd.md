# World Labs is Joining AMD

- Score: 298 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49883760) | Link: https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/amd-announcement

### TL;DR

World Labs says it has signed a definitive agreement to join AMD after collaborating on training and inference optimization for AMD GPUs. Fei-Fei Li will become AMD executive vice president and chief scientist, while Justin Johnson and Ben Mildenhall continue leading the team inside a proposed research organization spanning hardware, software, foundation models, applications, and open models. Closing is expected by year-end, subject to approvals. HN debates whether AMD is buying differentiated spatial-intelligence research, unified model potential, talent, marketing strength, or an immature product with limited practical output.
