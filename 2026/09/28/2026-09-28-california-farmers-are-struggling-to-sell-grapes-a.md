# California farmers are struggling to sell grapes as demand for wine drops

- Score: 357 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49883539) | Link: https://www.kqed.org/news/12101534/california-farmers-are-struggling-to-sell-grapes-as-demand-for-wine-drops

### TL;DR

California wine-grape growers face persistent oversupply as drinking declines. US case sales fell 23% from 2020 to 2025, while growers have removed or idled roughly a quarter of nearly 600,000 pandemic-era vineyard acres. About half of this year’s crop entered harvest without contracts, forcing farmers to sell below cost, abandon grapes, or replace vines with nuts and olives. The article attributes weaker demand to aging boomers, health- and cost-conscious younger consumers, competing drinks and cannabis, global contraction, production costs, and export tariffs.
