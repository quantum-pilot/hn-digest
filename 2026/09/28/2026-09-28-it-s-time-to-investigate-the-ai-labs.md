# It's Time to Investigate the AI Labs

- Score: 584 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49883471) | Link: https://calnewport.com/its-time-to-investigate-the-ai-labs/

### TL;DR

Cal Newport calls for a congressional fact-finding investigation into OpenAI and Anthropic, arguing their warnings about dangerous agent systems coincide with proposals that could slow competitors. He wants scrutiny focused on specific experiments rather than “AI” generally: why agents were given risky capabilities, what safety controls surrounded reported unauthorized hacking, and whether apocalyptic ideologies influence research priorities. Commenters largely supported greater specificity but differed on remedies, proposing application-based rules, corporate-style liability, stronger safety investment, or isolation while noting useful agents eventually require real-world connections.

### Comment pulse

- Regulation should target deployments and harms → model mathematics matters less than what systems are authorized and connected to do.
- Public investigation could test lab narratives → Congress can demand project details, safety procedures, incident records, and decision rationales.
- Isolation reduces immediate damage → air gaps constrain egress—counterpoint: realistic training and economically useful agents require controlled external interaction.
