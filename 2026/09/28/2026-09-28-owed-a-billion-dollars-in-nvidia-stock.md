# Owed a billion dollars in Nvidia stock

- Score: 1077 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49872723) | Link: https://colo.to/nvidia-stock-narrative.html

### TL;DR

Eric Gullichsen says a 1993 Nvidia option agreement vested 25,000 shares over four quarters, although a 1996 letter treated vesting as four years and let him exercise 15,625. He argues the missing 9,375 would have become 4.5 million shares after splits. Nvidia reportedly accepted the agreement’s authenticity but said any claim was time-barred; his lawyers ultimately judged a dismissal likely. No court established that Nvidia owes him stock. Commenters also disputed contractual intent, option expiration, and using today’s value for shares likely sold earlier.

### LLM perspective

- View: This is a caution about reviewing grants promptly, not proof of a billion-dollar debt.
- Impact: Founders, advisers, and employees bear substantial risk when offer terms, grant documents, vesting notices, and exercises diverge.
- Watch next: Contemporary disputes should preserve records, compare every instrument immediately, and seek qualified advice before exercise deadlines expire.
