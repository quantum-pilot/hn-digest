# SpaceX's Starship launching to orbit for first time ever today

- Score: 303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49875411) | Link: https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacexs-starship-megarocket-launching-to-orbit-for-1st-time-ever-on-sept-28-watch-it-live

### TL;DR

SpaceX’s Flight 14 put Starship into orbit for the first time on September 28, deployed 26 Starlink satellites, and ended with a Northern Pacific splashdown, according to the article’s noon update. The milestone followed 13 suborbital tests and moved the 124-meter system toward operational use, though rapid full reusability, orbital refueling, life support, and tower recovery of Ship remain unproven. HN celebrated orbital insertion and deployment while debating actual payload performance, market demand, launch reliability, and the credibility of a 2028 crewed lunar schedule.
