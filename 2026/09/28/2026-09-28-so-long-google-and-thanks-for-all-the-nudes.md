# So long Google, and thanks for all the nudes

- Score: 259 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49881951) | Link: https://lecaro.me/20260921-google-less.html

### TL;DR

Working with ten Android 6 phones led the author toward lightweight, source-built, de-Googled software and alternative distribution. The breaking point was Google Play rejecting the author’s Tabby text editor with an allegedly unrelated NSFW screenshot, then denying an appeal without explanation. Unable to inspect submitted translated listings or safely test possible causes without risking account deletion, the author plans to use F-Droid and itch.io instead. Commenters framed the episode as a due-process failure, while noting alternative stores offer far less access to paying users.

### Comment pulse

- Store rejection lacks accountable process → commenters wanted specific violations, empowered human review, and appeal or arbitration proportional to livelihood risk.
- Hobby developers face rising barriers → testing mandates and opaque enforcement favor organizations able to fund compliance and support work.
- Alternative distribution cannot match platform reach → developers may retain control outside Google Play but lose discovery and paying customers.
