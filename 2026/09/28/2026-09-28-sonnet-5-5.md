# Sonnet 5.5

- Score: 859 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49881850) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5

### TL;DR

Anthropic presents Claude Sonnet 5.5 as a faster, more efficient model for well-scoped coding and everyday knowledge work, priced like Sonnet 5 at $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens. It claims 30% faster generation, up to 30% lower task cost, major benchmark gains, and cyber safeguards resembling higher-tier models. HN weighs its practical niche against Opus subscriptions, competing low-cost models, review capacity, and benchmark distortions such as different fallback rates, emphasizing that vendor scores do not automatically determine workflow value.

### Comment pulse
