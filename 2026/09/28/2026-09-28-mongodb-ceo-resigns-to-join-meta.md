# MongoDB CEO resigns to join Meta

- Score: 357 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49879000) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/technology/mongodb-ceo-desai-steps-down-lead-metas-enterprise-platform-2026-09-28/

### TL;DR

MongoDB CEO Chirantan “CJ” Desai is leaving after less than a year to lead Meta’s new enterprise AI business, reporting directly to Mark Zuckerberg as chief enterprise platform officer. Meta says the unit will package its models, agents, Muse assistant, business agent, and coding tools for corporate deployment. MongoDB reinstated former CEO Dev Ittycheria on an interim basis while searching for a permanent replacement. MongoDB shares fell 18% in Monday morning trading, though Piper Sandler called the reaction excessive and maintained a positive strategic view.
