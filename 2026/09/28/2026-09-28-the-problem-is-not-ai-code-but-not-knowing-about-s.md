# The problem is not AI code, but not knowing about system architecture or intent

- Score: 377 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49880312) | Link: https://www.ssp.sh/brain/the-problem-is-not-the-ai-code-but-nobody-knows-anything-anymore/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that average AI-generated code is less dangerous than organizations losing architectural knowledge, design intent, and ownership. Fast-moving teams can generate specifications, tickets, code, tests, and reports without anyone internalizing why decisions were made; easier creation then multiplies maintenance obligations. The author says fundamentals, taste, and system thinking remain essential. HN describes “commitment laundering,” where AI artifacts acquire authority without provenance, but notes that shallow corporate decision-making predates AI and that disciplined human review can preserve understanding.
