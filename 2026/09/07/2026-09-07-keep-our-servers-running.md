# Keep Our Servers Running

- Score: 985 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49593563) | Link: https://blog.archive.org/2026/09/01/keep-our-servers-running-your-recurring-donation-goes-3x-this-september/

### TL;DR

The Internet Archive says it independently operates infrastructure preserving 210 petabytes without ads, user-data sales, or access fees. During September, the initial payment on a new recurring donation of at least $25 receives a 2:1 match, so $25 unlocks $50 more; later monthly payments are not described as tripled. The organization says recurring gifts, averaging about $25, support servers, storage, power, cooling, and staff. Commenters highlighted volunteer opportunities, questioned legal-risk spending and cancellation friction, and explained how small donations can help charities accept large pledges.
