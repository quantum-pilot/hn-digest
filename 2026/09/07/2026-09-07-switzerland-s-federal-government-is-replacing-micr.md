# Switzerland's Federal Government Is Replacing Microsoft on 3k Computers

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49594251) | Link: https://itsfoss.com/news/switzerland-replace-microssoft-pilot/

### TL;DR

Switzerland’s federal administration is investing CHF 9 million in a pilot placing openDesk alongside Microsoft 365 on 3,000 of more than 54,000 workstations, targeting completion by the end of 2027. The move follows a 172-person proof of concept that rated core document and email work positively but found large video conferences limited. The article attributes the project to sovereignty concerns about foreign access, vendor dependence, and licensing costs. A full federal migration remains speculation; separately, Cyber Command reportedly targets an openDesk replacement by October 2026.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters viewed the pilot as a chance to reduce Microsoft lock-in and redirect spending toward open-source tools.
- Skeptics predicted retraining, compatibility, fleet-management, and productivity costs—counterpoint: parallel operation exists precisely to test those risks.
- Commenters worried users may ignore openDesk without a removal deadline, while others said a pilot should not prejudge full migration.
