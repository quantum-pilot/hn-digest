# Bill Gates tries to install MovieMaker (2003)

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49599481) | Link: https://www.techemails.com/p/bill-gates-tries-to-install-movie-maker

### TL;DR

In a 2003 email later disclosed through litigation, Bill Gates recounts spending over an hour unsuccessfully trying to download Movie Maker and buy Digital Plus. He encountered timeouts, poor search, confusing Windows Update navigation, large patches, repeated installations and reboots, opaque dialogs, lost form data, and cluttered program listings. The ensuing Microsoft thread agrees the experience is broken but struggles to assign end-to-end ownership across web, update, marketing, and product teams. Commenters recognized the pattern as organizational fragmentation, not a single interface defect.
