# Splash-free urinals (2025)

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49597895) | Link: https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/4/4/pgaf087/8098745?login=false

### TL;DR

A PNAS Nexus study derived urinal shapes from differential equations so a urine stream meets the surface at or below an approximately 30-degree critical angle. Under experimental conditions intended to represent human urination, the resulting designs produced 1.4% as much splashback as a common contemporary commercial urinal. The authors argue that adoption could improve hygiene and accessibility while reducing cleaning labor, chemicals, water, and cost. Commenters compared cheaper splash-reducing mats, reported mixed real-world results, and questioned why the researched fixtures remain difficult to buy.

### Comment pulse
