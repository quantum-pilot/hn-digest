# Smartphone makers don't bother to comply with EU repairability requirements

- Score: 288 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49597189) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/personal-tech/2026/09/07/smartphone-makers-dont-bother-to-comply-with-eu-repairability-requirements/5294532

### TL;DR

Right to Repair Europe reports that one year after EU smartphone and tablet repairability rules began, only about 18% of 2,334 newly registered smartphone records linked to genuine spare-parts prices or repair instructions. Roughly half left the required URL blank, while 19% pointed to irrelevant pages; some manufacturers still self-awarded top repairability classes. The campaign group also found broad price ranges and indirect links even among technically compliant entries. Commenters blamed weak early enforcement and self-certification, while noting model counts may overrepresent obscure, low-selling brands.

### Comment pulse

- Enforcement advocates argued unverified self-reporting predictably produces empty fields, irrelevant links, and inflated scores.
- Others said EU enforcement typically escalates slowly—counterpoint: one year without visible action risks normalizing noncompliance.
- Commenters questioned model-based statistics because a few compliant Apple or Samsung models could represent most actual sales.
