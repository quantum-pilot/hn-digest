# Caltech Mathathon – first hackathon ever devoted to research level mathematics

- Score: 257 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49596055) | Link: https://mathathonchallenge.com/index.html

### TL;DR

A student-led Caltech initiative plans a 40-hour event for 100 teams to use frontier models on open mathematical problems and new theories, backed by more than $2 million in AI credits. Participants will defend results before mathematicians; prizes will reward promising explanations during the event and verified work later. Organizers say they are unpaid and do not represent Caltech, its departments, or sponsors. The page cites recent AI-assisted mathematics as motivation, including one explicitly unverified result, but does not guarantee solvable problems or publish outcomes yet.

### Comment pulse
