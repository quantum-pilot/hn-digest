### TL;DR

Variety critic Owen Gleiberman reviews Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s 174-minute Elizabeth Holmes documentary as a fascinating, ethically uneasy reality-TV psychodrama. Filmed during Holmes’s final 34 days before prison, it examines her continued denial rather than relitigating guilt. After access ends, Amanda Seyfried reads transcripts and reflects Holmes back through performance, layering reality and representation. The critic argues the film avoids becoming an apology, while commenters marvel at its uncanny trailer and meta structure and debate how identity and charisma enabled Theranos.
