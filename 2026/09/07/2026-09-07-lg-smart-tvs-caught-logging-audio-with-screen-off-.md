# LG smart TVs caught logging audio with screen off and snooping on local devices

- Score: 1130 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49594878) | Link: https://www.notebookcheck.net/LG-smart-TVs-caught-logging-audio-with-screen-off-and-snooping-on-local-devices.1391214.0.html

### TL;DR

An article summarizing Gamers Nexus testing alleges that retail LG OLED televisions scanned local networks for devices and nearby Wi-Fi details, fed information into LG’s advertising operation, and stored microphone audio while the screen appeared off before uploading it after reconnection. It also reports undisclosed webOS remote-code-execution vulnerabilities and recommends disconnecting TVs from the internet. LG had not commented when published.
