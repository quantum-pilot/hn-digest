# 216M Spy TVs – The LG Smart TV Problem [video]

- Score: 712 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49592375) | Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IFVTcM28KA

### TL;DR

GamersNexus says LG televisions it tested use automatic content recognition to collect viewing and household-related data, citing LG advertising executives’ statements about controlling the screen and extending insights across devices. Its video also alleges exploitable security flaws could turn affected sets into listening devices, including capture of ambient conversation and playback audio. The supplied description does not document the 216 million estimate’s derivation, affected models, exploit prerequisites, or testing methods. Commenters focused on consent terms, disconnection, and possible wiretap implications.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters objected to LG terms placing responsibility on owners to notify people whose voices might be captured.
- Several keep televisions offline and use external computers, treating smart features as an avoidable surveillance surface.
- Legal discussion questioned consent and wiretap compliance, but commenters repeatedly noted they were not lawyers.
