# Tiny $70 Xteink X3 e-reader

- Score: 202 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49596629) | Link: https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/09/xteink-e-reader-best-technology-years/688539/

### TL;DR

An Atlantic reviewer calls the $69 Xteink X3 his best recent gadget because its credit-card size made books available during moments otherwise spent scrolling; he reports reading three books in a month and cutting daily phone use by 25 minutes. The device is cheap and limited: no touch, color, or light, with weak stock software. Community Crosspoint firmware adds features. Commenters confirm portability’s appeal but flag lower display quality, fragile construction, awkward input, sunlight quirks, and uncertainty around firmware flashing on some stock.

### Comment pulse

- Pocketability changes behavior → owners report replacing brief phone checks with reading and completing more books.
