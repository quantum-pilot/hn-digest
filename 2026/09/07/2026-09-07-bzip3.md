# bzip3

- Score: 400 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49598291) | Link: https://github.com/iczelia/bzip3

### TL;DR

BZip3 presents itself as a faster, higher-compression successor to BZip2 for text and code, combining Burrows-Wheeler transformation, context mixing, run-length encoding, and Lempel-Ziv prediction. Its Perl-source archive benchmark reports dramatically smaller output than xz, BZip2, and default-window Zstandard, with parallel decompression near Zstandard’s measured time. However, commenters showed the comparison favored BZip3’s much larger window and repetitive corpus; long-window Zstandard reportedly reversed the result. The project also explicitly warns that rare bugs could make compressed data unrecoverable.

### Comment pulse

- Critics called the benchmark cherry-picked because compressor windows, parameters, memory use, and corpora were not comparable.
- Separate user tests reported Zstandard advantages in compression or decompression, though workloads and settings differed.
- Practitioners prioritized ecosystem support and transparent decoding over maximum ratio for archival and analytical workflows.

### LLM perspective

- View: Compression claims are inseparable from corpus, memory, window, parallelism, and decompression constraints.
- Impact: BZip3 may suit repetitive text archives, but adoption carries compatibility and recoverability tradeoffs.
- Watch next: Reproduce matched-parameter tests across diverse corpora, memory ceilings, corruption cases, and decoding libraries.
