# WeatherNext 3

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49552299) | Link: https://deepmind.google/science/weathernext/

### TL;DR

Google DeepMind presents WeatherNext 3 as its first global AI weather model generating hourly forecasts, using raw satellite imagery and an ensemble approach. Google claims targeted temperature and humidity at five-kilometer resolution, other surface variables around ten kilometers, better accuracy, and applications spanning consumer products, renewable energy, and enterprise tools. Weather Lab exposes experimental layers and comparisons but directs users to official agencies for warnings. Commenters welcome accessible visualization while contrasting the announcement with poor local Google forecasts and reduced observational inputs.
