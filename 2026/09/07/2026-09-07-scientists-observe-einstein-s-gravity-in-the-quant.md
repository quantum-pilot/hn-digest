# Scientists observe Einstein's gravity in the quantum world

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49569838) | Link: https://www.ox.ac.uk/news/2026-08-28-scientists-observe-einsteins-gravity-in-the-quantum-world

### TL;DR

Researchers used a Quantum Galileo Interferometer to split ultracold rubidium atoms into two wave paths, magnetically hold one stationary, let the other fall, then recombine them. The measured phase difference matched the prediction obtained by applying Einstein’s equivalence principle to a quantum wave. The team calls this the first direct measurement of that free-fall quantum phase. Crucially, the result neither quantizes gravity nor unifies gravity with quantum mechanics; it only shows consistency in the tested regime and does not reach the masses or durations needed to test Penrose’s proposed breakdown.
