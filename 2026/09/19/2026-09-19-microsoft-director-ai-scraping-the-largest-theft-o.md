### TL;DR

A New York Times summary-judgment brief alleges internal Microsoft and OpenAI materials acknowledged that training-data collection and chatbot substitution could damage publishers. It quotes Microsoft researcher Brent Hecht calling mass scraping unprecedented labor theft, reports Copilot reduced Times click-through by up to 93%, and cites OpenAI discussions of publisher displacement and a paywall workaround. Nadella testified that paywalled training or grounding material should be licensed. Crucially, the underlying documents remain sealed or redacted, so the brief’s quotations lack full context and the copyright case remains unresolved.
