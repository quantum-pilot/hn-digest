# San Francisco Onion Futures Company

- Score: 385 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49763296) | Link: https://onionfutures.com/

### TL;DR

The San Francisco Onion Futures Company sells private, transferable contracts for physical delivery of yellow onions in specified months, with daily algorithmic pricing and pickup or partner delivery in four cities. Buyers receive transferable keys but no refunds or cash settlement, even after failed delivery. The operator says this is legal because federal law prohibits onion futures on a board of trade, while the company claims it runs neither an exchange nor secondary market. Commenters treated it as performance art or free-market activism while questioning that legal distinction.

### Comment pulse

- The structure tests statutory boundaries → private bilateral sales and physical settlement are designed to avoid the prohibited organized-exchange category.
