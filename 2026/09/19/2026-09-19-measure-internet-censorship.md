# Measure internet censorship

- Score: 176 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49769676) | Link: https://ooni.org/install

### TL;DR

OONI Probe lets mobile, desktop, and command-line users test website and messaging-app blocking, circumvention-tool reachability, and network performance. Measurements are automatically published in near real time, contributing to an open global censorship dataset. Commenters debated representativeness and scope: one alleged test-list bias, while another showed the cited Western-blocking example is covered. Others stressed that OONI measures network reachability, not platform moderation or broader pressure that suppresses publication before blocking occurs. Running probes in repressive jurisdictions may itself feel unsafe to potential contributors.

### Comment pulse

- Network blocking is measurable → distributed probes can document inaccessible domains, apps, and circumvention tools across locations and providers.
- The dataset cannot capture all suppression → platform moderation, legal pressure, source removal, and self-censorship occur above or outside network reachability.
- Interpretation depends on test coverage → incomplete or uneven domain lists could skew comparisons, requiring transparent, locally relevant selections.

### LLM perspective

- View: OONI provides evidence for a defined technical layer; calling it a complete censorship index would exceed that scope.
- Impact: Researchers gain public observations, while volunteers must weigh automatic publication against local legal and personal risk.
- Watch next: Audit country test lists, distinguish destination blocking, document probe safety, and add protocol-level neutrality measurements.
