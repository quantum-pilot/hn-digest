# The Secret Life of Circuits

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49720143) | Link: https://blog.coredump.cx/p/the-secret-life-of-circuits-is-here

### TL;DR

Michal Zalewski announces that The Secret Life of Circuits is physically shipping: a full-color hardcover aimed at readers who want practical electronics understanding without first completing a year of calculus. The book emphasizes designing circuits rather than copying projects, covers subjects from component physics through microcontrollers, and includes nearly 300 purpose-built diagrams. HN discussion suggests strong appeal for software engineers and microcontroller hobbyists bridging a hardware knowledge gap, while recommending complementary electronics courses and warning European buyers to check regional stores and import costs.
