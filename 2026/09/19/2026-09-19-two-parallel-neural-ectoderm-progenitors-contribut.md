# Two parallel neural ectoderm progenitors contribute to the developing brain

- Score: 632 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49763697) | Link: https://med.stanford.edu/news/all-news/2026/09/two-separate-brains.html

### TL;DR

Mouse lineage tracing supports two mutually exclusive neural ectoderm progenitors emerging during gastrulation: Otx2-expressing cells form forebrain and midbrain, while Gbx2-expressing cells form hindbrain. Their chromatin states and developmental fates remain distinct. Applying this model, researchers differentiated human pluripotent stem cells into functional hindbrain motor neurons previously difficult to produce, enabling laboratory study of brain-stem disorders including SMA and ALS. The team proposes this dual origin is conserved across roughly 550 million years. HN readers emphasized the cell-culture technique while criticizing the press release's “two organs” framing.
