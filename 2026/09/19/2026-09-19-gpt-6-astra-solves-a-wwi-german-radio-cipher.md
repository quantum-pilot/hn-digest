# GPT-6 Astra Solves a WWI German Radio Cipher

- Score: 383 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49763987) | Link: https://www.prinzai.com/p/gpt-6-astra-solves-a-wwi-german-radio

### TL;DR

The author reports that GPT-6 Astra decoded a previously unsolved 170-character German ADFGVX radio message sent on November 27, 1918. It applied the documented key “TRUPPENVERSCHIEBUNG,” despite records indicating that key began use on December 9, and produced a message reporting an English cruiser at Sevastopol and an Allied squadron arriving on the 26th. The author checked those events against ship records. HN debate centered on whether trying a known but chronologically unexpected key constitutes cryptanalysis, useful search automation, or an overstated headline.
