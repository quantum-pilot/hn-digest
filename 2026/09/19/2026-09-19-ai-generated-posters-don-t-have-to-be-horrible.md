# AI-generated posters don’t have to be horrible

- Score: 1656 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49764791) | Link: https://john.hartnup.uk/2026/06/07/ai-event-posters.html

### TL;DR

AI poster generation need not converge on the familiar glossy “craft fair” look: naming a visual tradition such as Bauhaus, risograph, punk fanzine, or Memphis can produce markedly different designs. The author still finds recurring AI artifacts, clichés, and occasional text contamination from chat context. HN discussion shifted from aesthetics to purpose: cheap event posters may benefit from faster options, but readability and cultural fit remain essential, and a polished image does not necessarily signal a worthwhile event.
