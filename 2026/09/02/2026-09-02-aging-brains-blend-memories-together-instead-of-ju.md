# Aging brains blend memories together instead of just forgetting them

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49535548) | Link: https://studyfinds.com/aging-brains-blend-memories-together-instead-of-forgetting-them-study-finds/

### TL;DR

A brain-imaging study of 61 adults found memory-pairing accuracy fell after young adulthood, with middle-aged and older groups performing similarly. In younger participants, hippocampal activity similarity between learning and recall correlated with accurate retrieval; among older participants, stronger similarity instead tracked more cross-category mix-ups. The researchers interpret this as broader, less selective reinstatement rather than simple forgetting. Brain volume, baseline organization, and measured attention did not fully explain the pattern. The authors caution that sparse ages 30–50, overlapping performance, and ambiguous imaging measures limit lifespan-wide conclusions.
