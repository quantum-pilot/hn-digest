# Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.8 Flash Cyber

- Score: 996 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49537553) | Link: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/

### TL;DR

Google introduces two variants sharing the same foundation: Gemini 3.8 Flash, broadly available at $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens, and Flash Cyber, restricted to trusted defenders through Fairwind. Google claims improvements in long-horizon engineering, reasoning, tool use, vulnerability discovery, and patching, while warning that greater diligence can consume more tokens. Its Cyber benchmark and internal deployment results are vendor-reported. Commenters praised Flash’s speed and price but disputed comparisons with higher-effort frontier models and real-world code-review quality.
