# Google avoids a breakup of its ad tech business

- Score: 366 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49537131) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/02/technology/google-ad-tech-remedies.html

### TL;DR

Judge Leonie Brinkema declined the Justice Department’s request to break up Google’s advertising-technology business after previously finding unlawful monopolization in publisher tools and the connecting exchange. A sealed remedies ruling instead grants most proposed business-practice changes, as modified by the court, while rejecting forced source-code disclosure; specifics await redaction and public release. Google plans to appeal. The report says this network business generated $30 billion last year but under 1% of Alphabet profit, prompting debate over whether limited profitability understates its strategic control over digital advertising infrastructure.
