# Commodore 64 released September 1, 1982

- Score: 340 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49533497) | Link: https://dfarq.homeip.net/commodore-64-released-september-1-1982/

### TL;DR

The article marks September 1, 1982 as the Commodore 64’s release, while acknowledging evidence of limited August shipments and distinguishing those from later mass production. It argues that an unusually capable, affordable machine—despite slow storage and other compromises—became a durable gateway into computing. The author estimates roughly 10.6 million C64 units sold, describes learning programming and repair on one, and credits that access with shaping an IT career. Commenters supplied similarly personal accounts, though the precise launch date remains historically uncertain.
