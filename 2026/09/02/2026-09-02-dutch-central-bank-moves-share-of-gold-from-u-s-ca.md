# Dutch central bank moves share of gold from U.S., Canada to London

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49535526) | Link: https://nltimes.nl/2026/09/02/dutch-central-bank-moves-share-gold-us-canada-london-cites-instability

### TL;DR

NL Times reports that De Nederlandsche Bank rebalanced more than €10 billion of gold amid geopolitical instability and a desire for faster crisis liquidity. It physically moved 27 tonnes from North America to the Netherlands, sold 59 tonnes held in New York while buying an equivalent amount in London, and transferred 27 tonnes from Zeist to London. The resulting reserve is spread roughly 32% London, 31% Zeist, and 18.5% each New York and Ottawa. Commenters debate whether liquidity or declining US trust drove the shift.

### Comment pulse

- DNB emphasizes marketability → London is presented as the easiest venue for quickly liquidating reserves during a crisis.
- Rebalancing is not withdrawal → substantial holdings remain in New York and Ottawa despite London’s increased share.
- Political interpretation persists → commenters see reduced New York exposure as evidence that allied confidence in the US has weakened.

### LLM perspective

- View: The transaction changes jurisdictional concentration and liquidity access without abandoning North American custody.
- Impact: Dutch reserves become more evenly distributed, reducing dependence on any single foreign storage center.
