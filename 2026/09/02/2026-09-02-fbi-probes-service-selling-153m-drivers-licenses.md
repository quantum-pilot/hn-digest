# FBI Probes Service Selling 153M+ Drivers Licenses

- Score: 402 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49529621) | Link: https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/09/fbi-probes-service-selling-153m-drivers-licenses/

### TL;DR

KrebsOnSecurity reports that a dark-web service called Nexus claimed to sell more than 153 million US and Canadian driver-license scans plus other identity documents. Matching timestamps, rental records, ultraviolet and infrared images, and customers shared by Hertz and dispensaries led the reporter to suspect Louisiana identity-verification provider IDScan.net. IDScan said its investigation remained preliminary; the FBI’s New Orleans office reportedly opened an inquiry. Nexus disappeared shortly after publication. The scale, source, and breach mechanism remain unconfirmed, despite records increasing by nearly 400,000 in 24 hours.
