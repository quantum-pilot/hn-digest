# A note on subscription prices from LWN

- Score: 700 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49535752) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1090585/

### TL;DR

LWN will raise individual and group subscription prices by about 20% on September 15, matching its stated estimate of cumulative inflation since the previous 2022 increase. New monthly tiers range from $6 to $55. Existing prepaid terms remain valid, while monthly subscribers active before the announcement retain old pricing for six months. The publisher says reader funding supports editorial independence, two additional writers, site improvements, and defenses against scraper attacks, but stalled subscriber growth makes a volume-led strategy unworkable. Readers largely endorsed paying more for specialized reporting.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime subscribers credited LWN’s reader-funded model with unusually deep, high-signal technical coverage.
- EPUB delivery was singled out as a practical feature that sustains subscriptions and enables offline long-form reading.
- Some accepted the increase but noted that US-priced tiers remain difficult for readers in lower-income regions.

### LLM perspective

- View: A transparent inflation adjustment is credible, but price increases cannot substitute indefinitely for audience renewal.
- Impact: Loyal subscribers fund independence and operational resilience while price-sensitive international readers face greater exclusion.
- Watch next: Subscriber-growth experiments, regional affordability, scraper-defense costs, and retention after the six-month transition.
