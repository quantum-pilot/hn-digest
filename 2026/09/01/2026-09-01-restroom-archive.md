# Restroom Archive

- Score: 371 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49517624) | Link: https://restroomarchive.com

### TL;DR

The Restroom Archive catalogs public and commercial restrooms by date, name, and location, with list, grid, map, filter, random, and account views. The captured catalog spans museums, airports, restaurants, shops, parks, and other sites across several countries, while one entry records design traits such as maintenance, spaciousness, color, and decor. Commenters delighted in recognizing nearby bathrooms and treating each space as a story; they also discussed stressful automatic-door timers, three-dimensional scans, accessibility details, and contributing their own documentation.
