# The creator of Jujutsu has joined ERSC

- Score: 228 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49525297) | Link: https://ersc.io/blog/martin-joins-ersc

### TL;DR

East River Source Control announced Jujutsu creator Martin von Zweigbergk as CTO, leading engineering for its next-generation version-control platforms while remaining a core maintainer of the Apache-licensed project. ERSC says its forthcoming Storage product targets enterprise and AI-driven scale beyond Git’s remote storage model, with private beta planned later in September. Commenters praise Jujutsu’s undo, commit manipulation, delayed conflict resolution, and safer workflows, but question adoption costs and ERSC’s differentiation until staff clarify that it is enterprise infrastructure rather than another social-code forge.
