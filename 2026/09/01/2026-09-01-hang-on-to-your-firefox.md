# Hang on to Your Firefox

- Score: 801 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49527748) | Link: https://www.newsonaut.com/articles/hang-on-to-your-firefox

### TL;DR

The essay urges users to retain Firefox despite disagreements with Mozilla, arguing that abandoning it over issues such as maintaining an X account sacrifices the web’s remaining major independent engine. Chromium derivatives do not provide engine-level competition, leaving Safari as the other holdout. The author frames Firefox’s declining share as a reason to support outreach rather than demand ideological purity. Commenters broadly valued browser diversity and strong ad blocking, but argued that criticism of Mozilla’s advertising, telemetry, customization, and product decisions reflects concern rather than sabotage.

### Comment pulse

- Power users said persistent criticism signals investment in Firefox’s survival, not preference for Chrome.
- Coalition-minded readers accepted disagreements with Mozilla because independent engine diversity outweighs alignment on every issue.
- Web developers debated whether compatibility pressure, rapid standards evolution, and weak incentives for old-browser support reinforce Chromium dominance.
