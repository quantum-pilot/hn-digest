# Movie Scene Map – 13,312 films, series, games, anime and manga

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49524320) | Link: https://moviescenemap.com/

### TL;DR

Movie Scene Map plots filming locations across movies, series, games, anime, and manga, with browsing, surprise discovery, layers, and a related guessing game. The HN title advertises 13,312 works, while the later captured page reports 13,338 works, 15,565 locations, and 166 countries, indicating a growing dataset rather than one fixed count. Commenters enjoyed discovering nearby and travel-related scenes, but identified incorrect location data, overlapping markers, and missing context about titles; they requested genre, earnings, and review filters.
