# Introducing Ad Blocker for Firefox on iOS

- Score: 431 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49521973) | Link: https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/ad-blocker-on-ios/

### TL;DR

Firefox for iOS is adding an optional, built-in ad blocker using Apple's WebKit Content Blocker and EasyList. Disabled by default, it blocks many third-party ads and related trackers before loading, alongside Enhanced Tracking Protection. It does not block first-party ads, search-result advertising, or Firefox's sponsored new-tab content. Mozilla says direct integration is necessary because iOS lacks Firefox's desktop and Android extension flexibility. Commenters criticized those exclusions, delayed or experimental rollout, alleged telemetry coupling, and continued financial dependence on Google.

### Comment pulse

- Search ads can enable convincing scams → commenters saw their exclusion as a serious safety gap, not merely residual clutter.
- Built-in blocking improves iOS choice → counterpoint: existing Safari blockers offer broader capabilities and have existed for years.
- Gradual rollout confused interested users → public launch language did not match feature availability or persistence.
