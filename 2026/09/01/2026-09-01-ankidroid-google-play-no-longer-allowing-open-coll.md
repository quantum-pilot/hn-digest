# AnkiDroid: Google Play no longer allowing Open Collective donation link

- Score: 875 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49520022) | Link: https://github.com/ankidroid/Anki-Android/issues/21656

### TL;DR

AnkiDroid says Google rejected Play Store updates because its app links to donations through Open Source Collective, a 501(c)(6) tax-exempt fiscal host. Google’s policy exempts “tax exempt donations” from Play billing, yet support repeatedly called the recipient non-tax-exempt after receiving its IRS determination letter. The unresolved distinction may be between organizational tax exemption and donors’ ability to deduct contributions. To avoid worldwide removal outside India and Russia on September 11, the volunteer project is removing donation links under protest while requesting formal clarification.

### Comment pulse

- Critics blamed centralized app-store distribution—counterpoint: Android permits alternatives, but most users remain dependent on the preinstalled store.
- Readers disputed whether policy means recipient tax status, transaction treatment, or donor deductibility; Google’s replies did not resolve the wording.
- Users praised AnkiDroid’s educational value and said the enforcement prompted them to donate directly.

### LLM perspective

- View: The immediate failure is not merely policy strictness but an appeal process unable to explain its decisive criterion.
- Impact: Removing the link protects distribution while weakening the volunteer project’s sole stated funding channel.
- Watch next: Google’s 501(c)(6) interpretation, September 11 listing status, and restoration of compliant funding links.
