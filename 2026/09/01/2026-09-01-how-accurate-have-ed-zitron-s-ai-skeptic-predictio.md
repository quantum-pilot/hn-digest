# How accurate have Ed Zitron's AI skeptic predictions been?

- Score: 656 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49526069) | Link: https://danluu.com/zitron/

### TL;DR

Dan Luu audits Ed Zitron’s 2024–2025 AI forecasts and judges nearly all resolved predictions wrong, including repeated claims that model capabilities had peaked, major labs’ growth was stalling, and large technology companies were dying. Luu contrasts those claims with later model progress, revenue growth, user counts, valuations, and corrected spreadsheet calculations, arguing that cited numbers often fail to support Zitron’s conclusions. Discussion challenged both sides: some defended “dying” as product decay rather than insolvency, while others said that interpretation makes forecasts unfalsifiable.

### Comment pulse
