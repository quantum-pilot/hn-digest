# List of references on Sony websites to players "owning" their digital games

- Score: 393 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49642531) | Link: https://consumerrights.wiki/w/Sony_PlayStation_digital_game_ownership_lawsuit

### TL;DR

A Consumer Rights Wiki page documents a California lawsuit alleging Sony misleads PlayStation Store customers with “Buy” and “Purchase” language while granting only revocable, non-transferable licenses. Sony seeks individual arbitration or dismissal, arguing reasonable consumers would not believe they own digital games. The page contrasts that position with Sony support and marketing pages repeatedly describing players as owning games, licenses, or add-ons. The case remains unresolved, with an October 1, 2026 hearing listed in the supplied material.
