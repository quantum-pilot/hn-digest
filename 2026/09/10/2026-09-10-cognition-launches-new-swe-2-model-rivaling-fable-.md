# Cognition launches new SWE-2 model, Rivaling Fable 5.1 and GPT-Astra

- Score: 412 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49645443) | Link: https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2

### TL;DR

Cognition launched SWE-2 in Devin, claiming its Kimi K3-based coding model scores 50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main, within one point of Fable 5.1 at 64% lower cost. Its single-run reinforcement-learning method trains multiple effort levels using cost penalties matched to the base model's performance-cost frontier. Cognition also reports fewer exploratory steps, stronger testing, and better verification than SWE-1.7. HN questioned benchmark generalization, closed deployment, Cognition's past demos, and whether vendor scores predict real workloads.
