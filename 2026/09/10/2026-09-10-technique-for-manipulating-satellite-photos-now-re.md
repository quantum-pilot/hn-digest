# Technique for Manipulating Satellite Photos Now Reveals Ancient Images (2025)

- Score: 333 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49645437) | Link: https://spinoff.nasa.gov/Manipulating_Satellite_Photos_Now_Reveals_Ancient_Images

### TL;DR

NASA describes how decorrelation stretch, developed for enhancing satellite imagery, became a tool for revealing faint ancient markings. The technique expands subtle color differences through matrix transformations rather than merely increasing ordinary contrast. Jon Harman adapted a NASA paper into the Dstretch ImageJ plug-in after recognizing its archaeological potential; researchers subsequently reported previously undocumented paintings and details at Angkor Wat, Norwegian rock-art sites, Beni Hassan, and elsewhere. Commenters connect it to remote sensing and medical imaging, share simpler LAB workflows, and caution that results vary in practice.
