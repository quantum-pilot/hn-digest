# Stockfish 19

- Score: 275 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49599992) | Link: https://stockfishchess.org/blog/2026/stockfish-19/

### TL;DR

The Stockfish team says version 19 gains up to 44 Elo over Stockfish 18, introduces universal CPU-detecting binaries, and expands support for RISC-V, LoongArch, huge pages, and WebAssembly. Its SFNNv16 network removes redundant threat features, adds pawn-pair features, and uses quantization-aware training; stricter input validation now terminates on malformed positions or commands. Commenters welcome simpler packaging and smaller networks but debate analysis usability after one famous tactical position required deeper search, while others note improved fortress evaluations and match strength.
