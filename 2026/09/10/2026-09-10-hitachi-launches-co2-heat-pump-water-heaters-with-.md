# Hitachi launches CO2 heat pump water heaters with solar-friendly tariff controls

- Score: 310 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49627634) | Link: https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/09/07/hitachi-launches-co2-heat-pump-water-heaters-with-solar-friendly-tariff-controls/

### TL;DR

Hitachi plans to launch 370- and 460-liter Y-series EcoCute water heaters in Japan from November 2026. The CO2-refrigerant heat pumps add broader support for electricity plans that reward daytime consumption, including solar-oriented tariffs, plus a five-year warranty. Existing predecessors already supported connected energy management, so the principal change appears to be tariff configuration rather than a new heating architecture. Hitachi has not disclosed complete efficiency figures or whether compressors, refrigerant circuits, or heat exchangers changed.
