# More questions about whether researchers can trust OpenAI with unpublished math

- Score: 806 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49639408) | Link: https://mathstodon.xyz/@andreasthom/117240535270608201

### TL;DR

Mathematician Andreas Thom says he previously told OpenAI that he and colleagues had discussed unpublished work on the expander matching problem with ChatGPT. He asked whether those conversations entered training data or were accessible during solving; Mark Sellke replied categorically that this “did not happen.” Thom now argues the response addressed only direct access, because OpenAI says it cannot exclude de-identified usage data improving its models. He calls that gap dishonest and warns it could undermine mathematical collaboration.
