# All grown-ups were once children, but only few of them remember it

- Score: 263 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49638280) | Link: https://mathstodon.xyz/@tao/117244102901892965

### TL;DR

The essay argues adults often erase the value of children's curiosity-driven play by supplying an efficient answer too soon. Its example is children competing to name the largest number, eventually discovering that adding one defeats every proposal and reveals that natural numbers are unbounded. HN extended the idea to piñatas, literature, work, mathematics, and AI: many valued anticipation and exploration over nominal goals, while others stressed that adult obligations constrain playful living and disputed whether nonhuman tools necessarily diminish meaningful experience.
