# Tell HN: OpenAI keeps re-enabling the 'allow training' setting

- Score: 453 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49643556) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49643556

### TL;DR

An HN user alleges OpenAI repeatedly re-enabled an account setting that permits model training, despite the user deliberately disabling it and recording when. Replies were mixed: several people reported similar experiences with OpenAI or Claude, while others said their preference remained disabled for months or years across Europe and the United States. Commenters also disputed whether OpenAI's settings page and privacy-request form are separate requirements. The thread provides anecdotes, not evidence establishing a systematic reset or its cause.

### Comment pulse

- Unexpected opt-in changes deserve an audit → screenshots cannot establish system behavior, but repeated user reports raise consent concerns.
- Experiences vary across accounts and regions → several users found settings unchanged, while others recalled disabling similarly named controls.
- One opt-out path should suffice → commenters said the in-product toggle and privacy portal reach the same function, despite confusing presentation.
