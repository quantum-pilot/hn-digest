# OpenAI might have stolen another major proof

- Score: 294 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49638353) | Link: https://twitter.com/ValerioCapraro/status/2097791836269977996

### TL;DR

A strongly worded post alleges OpenAI may have trained GPT-6 Astra on private conversations in which mathematicians Andreas Thom and Gábor Kun explored Gromov’s soficity conjecture, later presented among Astra’s solved problems. The author says substantiation alongside separate allegations from Levent Alpöge and Tristan Buckmaster would indicate systemic unattributed use of unpublished research. The supplied item does not establish that training occurred. Discussion instead exposes an unresolved provenance problem: outsiders cannot audit whether model outputs derive from private research sessions.
