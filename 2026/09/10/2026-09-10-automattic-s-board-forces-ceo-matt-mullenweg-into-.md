# Automattic's board forces CEO Matt Mullenweg into leave of absence

- Score: 440 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49636283) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/09/automattics-board-forces-ceo-matt-mullenweg-into-leave-of-absence/

### TL;DR

Automattic confirmed founder and CEO Matt Mullenweg is on paid leave, with CFO Mark Davies serving as interim CEO. Mullenweg alleged in company Slack that board members conspired behind his back and denied him time for independent legal review; the board gave no public reason. WordPress.org says its leadership and work are unchanged. The report situates the rupture amid litigation with WP Engine, staff departures, account deactivations, and layoffs. HN largely welcomed intervention but recognized Mullenweg's earlier open-source contributions.

### Comment pulse

- Paid leave signals a severe board rupture → public accusations make reconciliation seem unlikely, though Mullenweg remains a director rather than being terminated.

- Automattic faces instability either way → employees reportedly felt relief or mixed emotions, while the board's timing and reasons remain undisclosed.
