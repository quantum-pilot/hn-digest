# Show HN: What if the speed of light was 5 km/h?

- Score: 584 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49637385) | Link: https://rivendell.dmitrybrant.com/relativity/

### TL;DR

An interactive simulation lowers the displayed speed of light to walking pace so users can experience light-travel delay, aberration, Lorentz contraction, Doppler color shifts, and relativistic beaming in a familiar environment. Its creator says the effects emerge from implementing the underlying mathematics consistently. Commenters praise its apparent fidelity, compare it with an older MIT project, and use it to discuss reference frames and cosmic distances. Others stress that changing light speed alone is not physically independent, framing the project as intuition-building rather than an alternate universe model.
