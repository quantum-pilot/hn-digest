# Microsoft Office renamed to “Microsoft 365 Copilot app”

- Score: 320 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46496465) | Link: https://www.office.com

### TL;DR

Microsoft now presents its unified Office hub as the Microsoft 365 Copilot app, combining document creation, file access, collaboration, cloud storage, security, and an AI assistant for organizations, homes, and education. Its own page alternates among several variants of the name and still invokes former Office branding. HN commenters overwhelmingly saw this as needless dilution of a trusted, widely recognized brand, comparing it with Microsoft’s long history of confusing renames. Some argued enterprise lock-in makes naming largely irrelevant.

### Comment pulse

- Inconsistent names on one page defeat the attempted clarification → users cannot tell whether Office, Microsoft 365, or only its hub changed.
- Cynics suspect Copilot branding inflates adoption and supports higher prices → counterpoint: the hub now includes substantive AI features.
- Strong application names may survive the package rebrand → Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook remain customers’ everyday vocabulary.
