# RevisionDojo, a YC startup, is running astroturfing campaigns targeting kids

- Score: 431 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46499976) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46499976

### TL;DR

A post alleges RevisionDojo, a YC-backed IB test-prep startup that raised $3.4 million, systematically manufactures student enthusiasm on Reddit. Documented tactics include sockpuppet posts advertising cheatsheets or supposed exam leaks, coordinated voting and replies, paying students for promotions, pressuring critics, soliciting official exam papers, and repeating the pattern after acquiring SAT tool OnePrep. r/IBO moderators were investigating. Commenters treated the case as part of a mature astroturfing industry, urged regulatory reporting, and expressed frustration that platform manipulation has become routine.
