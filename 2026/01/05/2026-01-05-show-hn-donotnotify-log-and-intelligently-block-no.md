# Show HN: DoNotNotify – Log and intelligently block notifications on Android

- Score: 316 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46499646) | Link: https://donotnotify.com/

### TL;DR

DoNotNotify is an Android app that intercepts notifications and applies app-name, text, or regular-expression rules to block promotions while preserving urgent alerts. Its developer says all processing stays offline, with no tracking or collected personal information. HN welcomed relief from apps mixing transactional notices with ads, but many hesitated because the closed-source app can read OTPs and private messages. Replies noted it lacks Internet permission, suggested open-sourcing it, and compared Android alternatives with iOS’s coarser controls.
