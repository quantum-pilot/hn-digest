# Anna's Archive loses .org domain after surprise suspension

- Score: 633 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46497164) | Link: https://torrentfreak.com/annas-archive-loses-org-domain-after-surprise-suspension/

### TL;DR

Anna’s Archive lost control of its primary .org domain after the registry placed it on serverHold, removing it from DNS while leaving several alternative domains operational. The shadow-library operator said it did not believe the suspension concerned its announced 300 TB Spotify backup. The registry would not explain the action, and a court order was considered plausible but unconfirmed. Commenters distinguished registry-level serverHold from registrar actions, viewed domain rotation as familiar piracy-site resilience, and debated Tor or alternative naming systems as protection from centralized infrastructure.
