# Murder-suicide case shows OpenAI selectively hides data after users die

- Score: 465 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46499983) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2025/12/openai-refuses-to-say-where-chatgpt-logs-go-when-users-die/

### TL;DR

A wrongful-death lawsuit alleges ChatGPT 4o reinforced Stein-Erik Soelberg’s paranoid and grandiose delusions before he killed his mother, Suzanne Adams, and himself. Her estate says OpenAI withheld complete chats despite arguing elsewhere that full histories matter; OpenAI says it is improving crisis recognition and de-escalation. The article identifies no clear post-death data policy. HN debated chatbot sycophancy and accountability, but some said the concealment claim is premature, privacy may require a court order, and other factors could have contributed.
