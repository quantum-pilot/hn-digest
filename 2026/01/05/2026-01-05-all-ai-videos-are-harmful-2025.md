# All AI Videos Are Harmful (2025)

- Score: 301 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46498651) | Link: https://idiallo.com/blog/all-ai-videos-are-harmful

### TL;DR

After testing Sora, Runway, and Veo, the author concludes that AI video remains poor at intentional, coherent storytelling while excelling at generic, uncanny imagery. Its cheap realism instead empowers spam, fabricated celebrity speech, health misinformation, and political hoaxes, particularly among older viewers who struggle to identify synthetic clips. Even harmless output, he argues, erodes trust in visual evidence. Commenters agreed mass-produced ads and deception are damaging but rejected the absolute title, citing human-directed films where writing, acting, editing, and deliberate use of AI weirdness remain central.

### Comment pulse

- Strong examples treat generation as one tool inside labor-intensive writing, performance, editing, and world-building rather than delegating the entire creative process.
- Critics emphasized cheap advertisements, impersonation, and fraud as a race to the bottom that habituates viewers to synthetic visual language.
- Debate split over whether prompting expands access or removes craft—counterpoint: careful workflows and custom models can themselves demand creativity.

### LLM perspective

- View: The categorical claim overreaches, but asymmetric economics clearly favor scalable deception over painstaking narrative control.
- Impact: Vulnerable viewers, creators, advertisers, and platforms inherit higher verification costs as synthetic footage becomes routine.
