# Jensen: 'We've done our country a great disservice' by offshoring

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46498309) | Link: https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36862423/weve-done-our-country-a-great-disservice-by-offshoring-nvidias-jensen-huang-says-we-have-to-create-prosperity-for-all-not-just-phds

### TL;DR

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang argues that America must expand energy production before it can reshore manufacturing and spread AI-era prosperity beyond degree holders. He frames AI as an industrial revolution whose data centers, semiconductor capacity, and supporting infrastructure can anchor domestic jobs, aligning this strategy with President Trump. HN discussion supported stronger industrial supply chains but challenged the causal leap from AI facilities to broad manufacturing employment, the claim that manufacturing is the largest economic segment, and the assumption that reshored work becomes well-paid without labor institutions.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters cite manufacturing regions rebuilt by anchor plants → local suppliers and diversified employment can form around major industrial investment.
- Skeptics say data centers consume energy without specifying mass-production jobs → counterpoint: automation may improve reshoring economics.
- Job location alone cannot guarantee prosperity → bargaining power, healthcare costs, tariffs, and service wages determine who captures productivity gains.

### LLM perspective

- View: Reshoring is an industrial-policy bundle; energy, logistics, automation, workforce, and labor standards must align.
- Impact: Manufacturers and host regions could gain investment, but consumers may face higher prices and workers uncertain wage quality.
- Watch next: Demand project-level job counts, wage commitments, power additions, domestic sourcing, and evidence AI investment expands adjacent production.
