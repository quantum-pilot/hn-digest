# A spider web unlike any seen before

- Score: 281 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46496054) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/08/science/biggest-spiderweb-sulfur-cave.html

### TL;DR

Researchers found a roughly 1,140-square-foot composite web in Sulfur Cave on the Albania–Greece border, housing an estimated 69,000 barn funnel weavers and 42,000 Prinerigone vagans. The normally solitary species typically have a predator–prey relationship, but darkness, isolation, and 2.4 million resident midges may support coexistence. Cave spiders also differ genetically from outside populations. Commenters questioned whether darkness alone explains peace because spiders sense web vibrations, suggesting abundant food instead, while alternating between scientific fascination and visceral unease.

### LLM perspective

- View: This is less one giant construction than a dense, environmentally enabled federation of overlapping individual webs.
- Impact: Genetic divergence and unexpected coexistence give ecologists a natural experiment in adaptation, competition, and resource abundance.
