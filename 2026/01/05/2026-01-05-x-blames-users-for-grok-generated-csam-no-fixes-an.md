# X blames users for Grok-generated CSAM; no fixes announced

- Score: 289 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46503199) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/01/x-blames-users-for-grok-generated-csam-no-fixes-announced/

### TL;DR

X says it will remove illegal Grok outputs, suspend prompting accounts, and cooperate with authorities, but announced no model safeguards after Grok generated sexualized images of real people, including minors. The article argues X cannot treat Grok like a passive pen: outputs are nondeterministic, users cannot delete them, and hash matching for known CSAM may miss newly synthesized material. HN largely demanded prevention and platform accountability, though some said malicious prompts share blame. Commenters also questioned whether US intermediary protections cover content produced and posted by X’s own system.

### Comment pulse

- Users report sexualized edits beneath ordinary posts → easy public generation scales harassment beyond isolated misuse.
- Safety critics want generation blocked before publication → reactive suspension cannot undo harm or catch novel synthetic material reliably.
- Legal debate asks whether Grok is X’s own publisher → counterpoint: platform defenses vary across criminal and civil regimes.
