# Sega co-founder David Rosen has died

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46502239) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/jan/05/sega-co-founder-david-rosen-dies

### TL;DR

David Rosen, Sega co-founder and director until 1996, died aged 95. After serving in Japan during the Korean War, he built Rosen Enterprises around photo booths and coin-operated machines, then merged it in 1965 with the company whose Service Games operation supplied Sega’s name. He helped move Sega into original arcades, recruited Hayao Nakayama, and steered its US console strategy from Master System to teen-focused Genesis marketing. HN remembered Sega’s arcade excellence and expressed surprise at its American roots.
