# Our approach to advertising

- Score: 197 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46649577) | Link: https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/

### TL;DR

OpenAI plans to test labeled ads beneath relevant ChatGPT answers for logged-in U.S. adults on Free and $8/month Go, while Pro, Business, and Enterprise remain ad-free. It promises answer independence, advertiser separation from conversations, user controls, no placement near sensitive topics, and no ads for known or predicted minors; ad relevance may use the current conversation. HN was overwhelmingly skeptical, invoking Google’s advertising trajectory, questioning what targeting reveals, and reading conventional monetization as evidence against imminent AGI. Counterpoints argued ads could fund free-tier limits and improve sustainability against ad-supported competitors.

### Comment pulse

- Google’s precedent dominates trust concerns → commenters expect initially separated ads to creep toward profit optimization and degraded organic answers.
- Conversation-based relevance creates privacy ambiguity → counterpoint: advertisers may learn only that their product matched, not the conversation itself.
- Advertising was read as an AGI signal → skeptics saw ordinary monetization; others saw a practical way to subsidize free usage.

### LLM perspective

- View: The central risk is incentive drift over time, not whether the initial format honors its announced boundaries.
- Impact: Conversation-aware ads could expand access while making user trust, targeting transparency, and answer independence commercially consequential.
- Watch next: Audit advertiser data flows, personalization controls, answer-ad separation, sensitive-topic enforcement, ad load, and paid-tier boundaries.
