# On Being a Human Being in the Time of Collapse (2022) [pdf]

- Score: 158 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46644962) | Link: https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~rogaway/papers/crisis/crisis.pdf

### TL;DR

In his final discrete-mathematics lecture, Phillip Rogaway argues that climate and civilizational collapse make computer science’s business-as-usual culture indefensible. He links the field to surveillance, behavioral manipulation, warfare, ecological damage, and political disengagement, then challenges claims that technology is neutral or innovation inherently beneficial. His seven suggestions emphasize honesty, social responsibility, examining who funds work, asking whether systems should exist, and choosing rebellion, withdrawal, or both. HN split between seeing this as necessary moral clarity and excessive pessimism, while debating self-efficacy, ethical employment, propaganda, material scarcity, and technological problem-solving.
