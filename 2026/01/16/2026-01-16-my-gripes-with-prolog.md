# My Gripes with Prolog

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46641348) | Link: https://buttondown.com/hillelwayne/archive/my-gripes-with-prolog/

### TL;DR

The author admires Prolog’s bidirectionality and logic-programming power but finds everyday use needlessly awkward. ISO lacks portable strings and collections; predicates replace composable functions; booleans are control flow; cuts, negation, free variables, symbolic terms, sorting, punctuation, and noninteractive querying create surprises. Picat often supplies friendlier alternatives. HN broadly agreed the ergonomics can hurt, while practitioners argued that Prolog demands a different mental model, good teaching resources, and understanding its interpreter. They highlighted metainterpreters and practical SWI-Prolog projects as evidence of its unusual expressive beauty.
