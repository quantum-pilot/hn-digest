# STFU

- Score: 578 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46649142) | Link: https://github.com/Pankajtanwarbanna/stfu

### TL;DR

STFU is a tiny Web Audio app that captures nearby sound and replays it roughly two seconds later, intended to make people stop blasting phone audio in public. Its creator says a one-prompt Claude request produced a working version, while offering only a joking explanation of the underlying science. HN enjoyed the revenge fantasy and shared similarly awkward interventions, but questioned whether the app was ever deployed. Commenters noted delayed-auditory-feedback speech jamming uses much shorter delays, debated polite requests versus escalation risk, and proposed an empathetic alternative: offer inexpensive earbuds.

### Comment pulse

- Public audio breaches shared-space norms → listeners compared it to pollution and resented being forced into someone else’s entertainment.
- Direct requests sometimes work → counterpoint: others have seen refusal, offense, or violence, making confrontation feel unsafe.
- A two-second echo is probably social annoyance, not speech jamming → commenters said cognitive disruption typically uses delays of hundreds of milliseconds.
