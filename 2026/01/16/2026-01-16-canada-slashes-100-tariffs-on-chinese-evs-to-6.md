# Canada slashes 100% tariffs on Chinese EVs to 6%

- Score: 380 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46648778) | Link: https://electrek.co/2026/01/16/canada-breaks-with-us-slashes-100-tariffs-chinese-evs/

### TL;DR

Canada will admit up to 49,000 Chinese electric vehicles annually at a 6.1% tariff, replacing the 100% barrier for that quota. The broader agreement cuts Chinese tariffs on Canadian canola and lifts seafood restrictions, while Ottawa expects affordable imports and Chinese-backed domestic supply-chain investment. The article frames the move as a break from US protectionism. HN commenters debated whether 49,000 vehicles is modest or market-shaping, welcomed cheaper competition, warned about Canadian auto jobs and surveillance, and linked the shift to declining confidence in US trade policy.

### Comment pulse

- The quota equals roughly one-quarter of recent annual EV sales → commenters expect rapid uptake despite its small share of all cars.

- Canada gains a more predictable partner → critics flag Chinese surveillance risks and note the original 100% tariff predated current US policy.
