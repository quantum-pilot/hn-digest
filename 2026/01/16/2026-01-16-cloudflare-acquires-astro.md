# Cloudflare acquires Astro

- Score: 668 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46646645) | Link: https://astro.build/blog/joining-cloudflare/

### TL;DR

Astro’s company is joining Cloudflare after its hosted database, commerce, and broader platform experiments failed to attract enough users. The framework will remain MIT-licensed, openly governed, platform-agnostic, and maintained by its existing full-time team, now Cloudflare employees. Astro says the deal restores focus on content-driven sites and Astro 6 while pairing its framework with Cloudflare’s infrastructure. HN welcomed sustainable funding and strong performance, but questioned Cloudflare’s strategic payoff and whether deeper integration could eventually make non-Cloudflare deployments second-class.

### Comment pulse
