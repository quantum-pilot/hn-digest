# Dell UltraSharp 52 Thunderbolt Hub Monitor

- Score: 113 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46648885) | Link: https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/dell-ultrasharp-52-thunderbolt-hub-monitor-u5226kw/apd/210-bthw/monitors-monitor-accessories

### TL;DR

Dell’s $2,899.99 UltraSharp U5226KW is a 51.5-inch, 4200R curved IPS Black display running 6144×2560 at 120Hz. It offers 129ppi, 2,000:1 contrast, 400-nit brightness, broad color coverage, a KVM, 2.5GbE, extensive HDMI, DisplayPort, and USB connectivity, plus Thunderbolt 4 with 140W power delivery. HN reactions split over whether one huge screen improves multitasking or creates neck and shoulder strain. Owners praised FancyZones workflows and immersion, while critics preferred denser 32-inch 6K panels or cheaper multi-monitor arrays.

### Comment pulse
