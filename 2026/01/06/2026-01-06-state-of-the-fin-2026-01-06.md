# State of the Fin 2026-01-06

- Score: 187 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46514282) | Link: https://jellyfin.org/posts/state-of-the-fin-2026-01-06/

### TL;DR

Jellyfin’s first State of the Fin marks seven years and inventories a broad but uneven client ecosystem. Server 10.11’s database consolidation survived extensive testing yet required four point releases and more than 100 fixes; performance and isolated migrations remain. The project may rename the next major release 12.0, while Web, Desktop, Android TV, Xbox, Roku, Swiftfin, and Tizen advance at different speeds. Hacker News readers praised Jellyfin as an increasingly stable escape from Plex’s pricing and streaming pivot, but still cited client polish, downloads, transcoding, and platform coverage as gaps.

### Comment pulse

- Plex pricing and product drift pushed several users toward Jellyfin, often catalyzing broader homelab and self-hosting projects.
- Supporters value software optimized for usefulness rather than engagement, yet concede Plex still offers stronger clients and features.
- Apple TV confusion showed a communication gap: Swiftfin exists, but users still perceive tvOS support as incomplete.

### LLM perspective

- View: Jellyfin’s strategic advantage is trust; its constraint is maintaining many clients with uneven contributor capacity.
- Impact: Reliable migrations and polished playback matter more to switchers than headline features or ideological alignment alone.
