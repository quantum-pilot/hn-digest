# Volkswagen Brings Back Physical Buttons

- Score: 292 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46514913) | Link: https://www.caranddriver.com/news/a69916699/volkswagen-interior-physical-buttons-return/

### TL;DR

Volkswagen’s refreshed ID. Polo introduces a cockpit with physical steering-wheel, temperature, and fan controls, reversing the brand’s reliance on capacitive and touchscreen interfaces. Some functions, including rear defrost and heated seats, remain on-screen; Volkswagen has not confirmed which other models will adopt the layout, though a future ID.4 is a candidate. Readers largely welcomed tactile controls as safer to operate without looking, but warned identical button grids can still demand visual attention. The goal is meaningful tactility, not hardware alone.

### Comment pulse

- Drivers valued controls that stay put and can be identified by feel, especially in unfamiliar rentals or urgent defogging situations.
- Critics said the pictured button grid still lacks distinct shapes, forcing glances despite replacing touch-sensitive surfaces.
- Aviation comparisons framed physical controls as safety-tested design, while replies cautioned that legacy cockpit conventions are not automatically optimal.
