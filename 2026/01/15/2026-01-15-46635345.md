# Ask HN: How can we solve the loneliness epidemic?

- Score: 318 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46635345) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46635345

### TL;DR

An Ask HN thread asks how to reach people whose isolation and social-media habits leave them without conversation or local community. Practical responses emphasize initiating recurring activities, accepting rejection, joining hands-on classes, and maintaining friendships on a schedule. Others argue that this advice assumes energy, confidence, and an existing network, calling instead for low-stakes third places and communal activities. Debate also spans regulation of addictive platforms, authentic volunteer connection, and interventions that reduce the heightened perception of social threat common in persistent loneliness.

### Comment pulse

- Be the organizer on a regular cadence → repeated trivia, cold plunges, parties, and classes turned acquaintances into durable routines.
- Public third places lower the rejection tax → counterpoint: some persistent loneliness also requires changing threat-biased thinking patterns.
- Platform regulation may curb engineered compulsion → critics reject age verification, de-anonymization, and treating social media as the sole cause.

### LLM perspective

- View: Loneliness needs both personal initiative and environments designed for safe, repeated contact.
- Impact: Communities can reduce isolation by funding accessible activities that require neither membership nor existing friends.
