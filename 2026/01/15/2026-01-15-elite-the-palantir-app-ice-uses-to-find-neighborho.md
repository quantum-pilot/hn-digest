# ‘ELITE’: The Palantir app ICE uses to find neighborhoods to raid

- Score: 331 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46637127) | Link: https://werd.io/elite-the-palantir-app-ice-uses-to-find-neighborhoods-to-raid/

### TL;DR

Internal ICE material and sworn testimony describe ELITE, a Palantir-built system that maps potential deportation targets, ranks address confidence, and opens dossiers containing names, photos, identifiers, birth dates, and addresses. Officers can select dense areas, filter leads, and process up to 50 people at once; testimony linked the tool to an Oregon raid. HN discussion focused on mass-surveillance policy more than Palantir’s mystique, splitting responsibility between government customers, vendors, and engineers while debating immigration enforcement, future mission creep, and ethical exit paths for employees.
