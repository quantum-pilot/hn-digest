# Google Books removed all search functions for any books with previews

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46769201) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/google/comments/1qn1hk1/google_has_seemingly_entirely_removed_search/

### TL;DR

A user reports that Google Books discovery sharply worsened between January 20 and 23, making searches across previewable books effectively unusable. The title’s broader claim appears overstated: commenters found that in-book search, reference navigation, and previews still work after locating a volume, and public-domain books remain searchable. The apparent regression concerns cross-book ranking and discovery, forcing users to know the target beforehand. Speculation blamed publisher contracts or defenses against model extraction, but the supplied material offers no confirmed explanation from Google.
