# DHS keeps trying and failing to unmask anonymous ICE critics online

- Score: 156 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46768081) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/01/instagram-ice-critic-wins-fight-to-stay-anonymous-as-dhs-backs-down/

### TL;DR

An anonymous Pennsylvania community-watch account challenged DHS summonses seeking Meta registration and access records, including emails, phone numbers, and IP logs. DHS invoked a customs statute and alleged that posts exposing agents impeded enforcement, while the ACLU characterized the material as lawful rights information, fundraising, and vigils. DHS withdrew this demand, as it had after challenges involving six other groups, without explaining why. The outcome protects these accounts for now but leaves uncertain how often platforms disclose identities when users cannot obtain counsel.
