# Over 36,500 killed in Iran's deadliest massacre, documents reveal

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46760329) | Link: https://www.iranintl.com/en/202601255198

### TL;DR

Iran International says documents and anonymous government sources place deaths from Iran’s January 8–9 protest crackdown above 36,500, rising from earlier internal figures of 27,500 to 33,000. It also reports hospital evidence and witness accounts alleging executions of wounded patients, removals from treatment, coerced burial practices, and killings across more than 400 cities. The outlet explicitly says communication restrictions prevent independent verification and even state agencies may lack a final count. Commenters treated the violence as credible but disputed source bias, scale, and logistical plausibility.

### Comment pulse

- The reported toll demands corroboration → it rests on unpublished classified records, anonymous insiders, medical accounts, and images reviewed by one outlet.
- Scale is logistically conceivable → thousands of clash sites spread casualties nationally — counterpoint: commenters found 36,500 deaths in two days extraordinary.
- Uncertainty itself compounds harm → blackout conditions, pressured families, and disordered body registration obstruct both identification and accountability.
