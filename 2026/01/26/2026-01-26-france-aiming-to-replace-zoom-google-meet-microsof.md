# France Aiming to Replace Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, etc.

- Score: 466 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46767668) | Link: https://twitter.com/lellouchenico/status/2015775970330882319

### TL;DR

France’s economy ministry reportedly plans to replace Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and similar services with a sovereign videoconferencing system by 2027. The software already exists but is not broadly available, leaving procurement, migration, interoperability, and user adoption unresolved. The author sees geopolitical logic but questions whether entrenched habits can be displaced on that timeline. Commenters viewed digital sovereignty and local hosting as achievable with political commitment, while noting Europe’s fragmented market, migration costs, and the benefits US vendors receive from serving European institutions.

### Comment pulse

- Sovereignty is feasible technically → open and locally hosted conferencing alternatives exist, but organizational migration requires mandates, support, and tolerated disruption.
- Geopolitical pressure strengthens adoption → dependence on US platforms now carries strategic risk — counterpoint: fragmentation can weaken Europe’s substitute.
- Habit may outrank feature parity → employees already embedded in familiar workflows can defeat a nominally complete replacement.
