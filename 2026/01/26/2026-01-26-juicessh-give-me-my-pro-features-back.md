# JuiceSSH – Give me my pro features back

- Score: 185 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46768909) | Link: https://nproject.io/blog/juicessh-give-me-back-my-pro-features/

### TL;DR

An Android user says a JuiceSSH Pro purchase stopped being recognized in December 2025, the replacement price rose by $20, repurchases sometimes failed to activate, and support did not respond. The post reverse-engineers and modifies version 3.2.2 to bypass purchase and authentication checks, then rebuilds and self-signs the APK; cloud sync and plugins still fail. Commenters corroborated lost old licenses and failed new purchases, while recommending refund requests, Termux, or Android’s newer Debian-based Terminal where supported.

### Comment pulse

- Paid access appears abandoned → multiple users report prior licenses disappearing, second payments failing, and unanswered support requests.
- The patch restores local gates, not the service → cloud synchronization and separately distributed plugin APKs remain unavailable.
- Open alternatives reduce lock-in → Termux supports keys, aliases, and multihop; Android’s Terminal offers Debian but has narrower device support.
