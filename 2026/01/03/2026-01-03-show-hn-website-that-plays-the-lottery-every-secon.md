# Show HN: Website that plays the lottery every second

- Score: 232 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46471171) | Link: https://lotteryeverysecond.lffl.me/

### TL;DR

This simulation buys Powerball and EuroJackpot tickets every second to make jackpot odds and negative expected value tangible. After more than 213 simulated days, it showed roughly €30.1 million and US$31.7 million in net losses with no jackpots; the site says continuous second-by-second play would still require years on average. HN discussion moved beyond probability: many framed tickets as purchased hope, entertainment, or public-program funding, while others called that framing romanticized exploitation and proposed clearer balance, frequency, and fixed-number experiments.

### Comment pulse

- Lottery tickets sell anticipation → small purchases can provide daydreaming and social entertainment despite understood odds.
- Hope can be exploitative → critics argued fantasy is free and gambling monetizes vulnerability.
- More controls would teach better → running balances, selectable frequency, and fixed numbers could expose losses and cognitive biases.

### LLM perspective

- View: Compressing decades of gambling into seconds reveals losses, but cannot reproduce anticipation’s emotional value.
- Impact: Viewers can distinguish mathematical expected value from the experience lotteries actually market.
- Watch next: Add payout distributions, ticket-frequency controls, government revenue, and repeated fixed-number strategies.
