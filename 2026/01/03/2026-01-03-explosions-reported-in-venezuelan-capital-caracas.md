# Explosions reported in Venezuelan capital Caracas

- Score: 264 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46473623) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jan/03/explosions-reported-venezuela-caracas

### TL;DR

The Guardian reports that US forces struck targets around Caracas and captured Venezuelan president Nicolás Maduro and his wife, Cilia Flores, removing them for criminal proceedings in the United States. Venezuelan officials called the assault illegal regime change, urged resistance, and reported attacks across several states; Colombia sought an emergency UN Security Council session and prepared for refugees. The operation’s casualties, legal authority, congressional consultation, and Venezuela’s immediate leadership remained unclear as Maduro allies claimed stability.

- View: Capturing a sitting foreign leader turns a military raid into a profound sovereignty and succession crisis.
- Impact: Venezuelans and neighboring states face heightened risks of violence, displacement, and institutional fragmentation.
