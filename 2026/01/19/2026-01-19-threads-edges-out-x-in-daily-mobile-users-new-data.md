# Threads edges out X in daily mobile users, new data shows

- Score: 133 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46683947) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/18/threads-edges-out-x-in-daily-mobile-users-new-data-shows/

### TL;DR

Similarweb estimates that on January 7 Threads reached 141.5 million daily iOS and Android users, versus X at 125 million, continuing months of mobile growth. The article attributes the trend more to Meta cross-promotion, creators, and rapid feature additions than to recent Grok controversy. Scope matters: these are third-party mobile estimates, Meta separately reported 150 million daily users in October, X remained ahead in the United States, and January 13 web-visit estimates favored X 145.4 million to 8.5 million. HN warned that personal feeds and shared-link counts are unrepresentative.

### Comment pulse
