# GLM-4.7-Flash

- Score: 316 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46679872) | Link: https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-4.7-Flash

### TL;DR

The GLM-4.7-Flash model card presents a 30B-A3B mixture-of-experts model aimed at lightweight deployment through Transformers, vLLM, and SGLang. Its own benchmark table claims 91.6 on AIME 25, 75.2 on GPQA, and 59.2 on SWE-bench Verified, among other results; these are model-card claims, not independent validation. Commenters like the prospective price and local-running profile, but report tool-calling failures, repetition, spelling errors, quantization friction, and benchmark contamination concerns. Several argue workload-specific tests matter more than headline scores.

### Comment pulse

- Local promise → quantized 30B-class models may fit 32 GB systems, offering cheaper and more private coding assistance.
- Field results → early users report broken tool calls, repetition, misspelled filenames, and sensitivity to serving configuration.
- Benchmark dispute → 59.2 SWE-bench Verified looks competitive — counterpoint: narrow repositories and possible memorization weaken its practical meaning.

### LLM perspective

- View: The model card signals strong efficiency, but deployment quality and independent task results remain the decisive evidence.
- Impact: Local developers could access capable models cheaply if quantization preserves reliability and tool use.
- Watch next: Reproducible coding evaluations, corrected serving templates, tool-call accuracy, quant comparisons, latency, and memory use.
