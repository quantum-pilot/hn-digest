# Show HN: I quit coding years ago. AI brought me back

- Score: 294 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46673809) | Link: https://calquio.com/finance/compound-interest

### TL;DR

A former programmer turned investment associate says Claude in Windsurf helped them return to building: in two weeks and about $100 of API use, they produced more than 60 calculators with Next.js, React, Tailwind, tests, and four languages. The author argues AI supplied implementation speed while dormant architecture, UX, and financial-domain judgment remained valuable. HN echoed the liberation of rapidly making bespoke farm, family, and research tools, but questioned mass-produced calculators’ accuracy, testing, and trustworthiness; others said AI erodes the deep engagement they valued in coding.
