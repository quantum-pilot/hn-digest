# Apple testing new App Store design that blurs the line between ads and results

- Score: 265 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46680974) | Link: https://9to5mac.com/2026/01/16/iphone-apple-app-store-search-results-ads-new-design/

### TL;DR

Apple is reportedly A/B testing App Store search results on iOS 26.3 that remove the blue background from sponsored listings, leaving a small Ad label as the main distinction from organic results. The article connects this to plans for multiple sponsored results and warns that visual integration may mislead users while raising ad clicks and revenue; Apple had not clarified broader rollout. Commenters frame it as an industry-wide native-ad pattern, linking poorer search and discovery to subscriptions, declining trust, and reduced enthusiasm for browsing apps.
