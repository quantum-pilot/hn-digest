# Nonviolence

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46683410) | Link: https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/nonviolence

### TL;DR

Martin Luther King Jr. developed nonviolence from Thoreau, Christian agape, Gandhi, and the Montgomery boycott. His six principles reject both physical violence and hatred, target evil rather than people, accept suffering, seek reconciliation, and trust justice’s eventual advance. He later extended the method to nuclear-age survival and defended it amid Black Power criticism. HN debated whether nonviolence succeeds independently or gains leverage from violent alternatives, while citing higher success rates for less violent movements and questioning those studies’ causal claims.
