# Google AI Studio is now sponsoring Tailwind CSS

- Score: 378 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46545077) | Link: https://twitter.com/OfficialLoganK/status/2009339263251566902

### TL;DR

Google AI Studio says it will sponsor Tailwind CSS but discloses neither the tier nor amount. HN welcomed the support while warning it may barely change Tailwind’s finances: commenters cited substantial existing sponsorship and conflicting evidence about Google’s monthly level. Debate widened to why a mature CSS library with recurring revenue struggled—some blamed expansion or competing free UI offerings, while others stressed browser churn, build-tool complexity, and eight years of continuous development. Several readers saw strategic logic: maintaining Tailwind helps AI systems generate fashionable interfaces.

### Comment pulse
