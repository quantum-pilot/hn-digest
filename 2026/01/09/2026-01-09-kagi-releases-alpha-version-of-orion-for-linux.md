# Kagi releases alpha version of Orion for Linux

- Score: 327 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46553343) | Link: https://help.kagi.com/orion/misc/linux-status.html

### TL;DR

Kagi’s Orion for Linux entered an unstable testing alpha with menus, dialogs, navigation, parallel tabs, session restore, sidebar tabs, bookmarks, history, and a password-management foundation. WebKit extensions and synchronization are not yet implemented, and commenters said access appears limited to Orion+ supporters. HN welcomed a commercially backed WebKit browser as a counterweight to Chromium and Gecko, while raising two adoption barriers: closed source limits distribution integration and trust, and missing DRM or extension support could relegate Orion to secondary-browser use.
