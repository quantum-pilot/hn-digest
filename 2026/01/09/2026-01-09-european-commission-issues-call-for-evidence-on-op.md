# European Commission issues call for evidence on open source

- Score: 409 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46550912) | Link: https://lwn.net/Articles/1053107/

### TL;DR

The European Commission is gathering evidence for an Open Digital Ecosystem Strategy aimed at reducing dependence on non-EU software and improving choice, competitiveness, supply-chain security, and infrastructure resilience. Submissions were open to developers, companies, foundations, governments, industry, and researchers through February 3, 2026. HN welcomed European technology sovereignty but rejected treating open source as cost-free. Commenters favored public funding, open publication of taxpayer-funded code, and procurement coordination, while debating whether Europe needs shared open infrastructure, stronger local vendors, or both.

### Comment pulse

- Sustainable open source requires payment → small foundational projects need maintenance funding, not expectations of free labor.
- Decentralized procurement recreates lock-in → individual agencies rationally choose Azure while Europe collectively loses strategic autonomy.
- Open code is not the whole market → domestic proprietary vendors can also reduce foreign dependence.
