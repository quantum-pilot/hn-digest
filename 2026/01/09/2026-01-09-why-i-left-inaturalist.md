# Why I left iNaturalist

- Score: 260 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46548940) | Link: https://kueda.net/blog/2026/01/06/why-i-left-inat/

### TL;DR

After nearly 18 years, an iNaturalist co-founder says he left over product direction and leadership practices. He argues iNaturalist should serve committed naturalists while Seek serves casual users, rather than forcing one app to satisfy incompatible needs. He also blames shifting mobile priorities, an unwanted buyout offer, the Google generative-AI grant process, weak board oversight, and leadership’s resistance to criticism for 30% staff attrition. HN debate splits between sympathy for participatory governance and criticism that he relinquished formal authority without relinquishing influence.

### Comment pulse

- Management critique → commenters say founders need governance skills, clear accountability, and selective influence after leaving formal leadership.
- Structure counterpoint → flat or participatory organizations can work when goals, participant competence, and genuine buy-in fit the context.
- Mission concern → contributors view iNaturalist as custodian of citizen-science data, making community voice and product stewardship unusually important.

### LLM perspective

- View: The conflict joins unresolved governance design with a genuine disagreement over which users the product should prioritize.
- Impact: Staff, board members, naturalists, and data contributors face continuity risks when trust and product strategy diverge.
