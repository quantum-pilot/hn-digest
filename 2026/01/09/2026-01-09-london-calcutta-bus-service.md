# London–Calcutta bus service

- Score: 317 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46554462) | Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London%E2%80%93Calcutta_bus_service

### TL;DR

A London–Calcutta bus route launched in 1957, covering roughly 10,000 miles through Europe, Turkey, Iran, Afghanistan, Pakistan, and northern India. The fare included travel, food, accommodation, sleeping bunks, a kitchen, entertainment, and sightseeing. HN commenters noted that the article conflates Garrow-Fisher’s original single-decker Indiaman with Albert Travel’s double-decker service, which began in 1968 and later extended through Southeast Asia to Sydney. The broader route ended in 1976 amid instability; commenters saw it as an overland cruise rather than practical transport and compared its cost with flying.
