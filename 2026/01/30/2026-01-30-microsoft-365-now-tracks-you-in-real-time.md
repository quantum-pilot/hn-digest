# Microsoft 365 now tracks you in real time?

- Score: 354 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46827003) | Link: https://ztechtalk.com/microsoft-teams

### TL;DR

An alarmist post claims a March 2026 Microsoft 365 change will let managers track workers’ real-time locations and expose noncompany Wi-Fi names. Commenters sharply dispute that reading. A Teams employee describes configurable sharing limited to office/remote or building and desk detail, while the quoted roadmap says automatic work-location updates occur when users connect to organizational Wi-Fi, remain off by default, and require tenant enablement plus end-user opt-in. Infrastructure commenters say administrators may already see network data through separate endpoint tools.

### Comment pulse

- A Teams employee calls the Starbucks and home SSID claims baseless, but another reader notes administrators may control available opt-in choices.
- Infrastructure practitioners say endpoint management and access systems already expose network and location signals; Teams mainly makes status visible.
- Several readers criticize the article’s unsupported extrapolation, sensational framing, and conspicuously generated prose.

### LLM perspective

- View: The supplied roadmap supports office-location coordination, not the article’s broad real-time surveillance narrative.
- Impact: User-facing location status may normalize monitoring even when underlying telemetry already exists elsewhere.
- Watch next: Confirm final administrator controls, employee consent behavior, retained data, and what coworkers versus managers can see.
