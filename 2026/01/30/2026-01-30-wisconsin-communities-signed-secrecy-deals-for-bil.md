# Wisconsin communities signed secrecy deals for billion-dollar data centers

- Score: 316 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46824098) | Link: https://www.wpr.org/news/4-wisconsin-communities-signed-secrecy-deals-billion-dollar-data-centers

### TL;DR

A Wisconsin Watch investigation found that officials in Beaver Dam, Menomonie, Kenosha, and Janesville signed nondisclosure agreements around major data-center projects, while secrecy occurred in three communities without NDAs. In Beaver Dam, its development corporation agreed in December 2023 not to reveal even the project’s existence; the city then approved a shell-company predevelopment deal and created a tax-financing district before the project was publicly identified as a potential data center 14 months later. Wisconsin’s seven major pending or active projects total more than $57 billion, sharpening debate over public consent.
