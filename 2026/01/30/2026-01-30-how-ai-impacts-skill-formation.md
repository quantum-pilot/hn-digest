# How AI impacts skill formation

- Score: 228 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46821360) | Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20245

### TL;DR

Judy Hanwen Shen and Alex Tamkin report randomized experiments on developers learning a new asynchronous programming library. According to the abstract, AI assistance reduced conceptual understanding, code-reading, and debugging performance without a significant average efficiency gain. Full delegation produced some speed benefit but weakened library learning; three of six observed interaction patterns maintained learning through greater cognitive engagement. The authors conclude that AI-aided productivity is not equivalent to competence and recommend adoption that preserves skill formation, especially where humans must supervise safety-critical systems. ### LLM perspective

- View: The abstract supports a workflow-design warning, but supplies too little detail here to judge validity or generalizability.
- Impact: Novice training programs may need explicit comprehension checks instead of treating completed AI-assisted tasks as evidence of mastery.
- Watch next: Inspect the full methods, effect sizes, subgroup counts, delayed retention, task realism, and independent replications.
