# Netflix Animation Studios Joins the Blender Development Fund as Corporate Patron

- Score: 458 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46821134) | Link: https://www.blender.org/press/netflix-animation-studios-joins-the-blender-development-fund-as-corporate-patron/

### TL;DR

Netflix Animation Studios has joined the Blender Development Fund as a Corporate Patron, directing its support toward general core development for media and entertainment workflows. Blender calls the membership evidence that the open-source 3D suite is becoming embedded in high-end studio pipelines; Netflix says it is the first major animation studio to back Blender’s continued development through this program. The announcement gives no contribution amount, project roadmap, or production commitments, so the practical effect remains to be seen.
