# OpenClaw – Moltbot Renamed Again

- Score: 593 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46820783) | Link: https://openclaw.ai/blog/introducing-openclaw

### TL;DR

Peter Steinberger has renamed the fast-growing local agent platform from Moltbot to OpenClaw after Anthropic objected to its original Clawd name and the interim branding proved awkward. He says trademark searches, domains, and migration code are now in place. The release adds Twitch and Google Chat channels, two model integrations, image support in web chat, and 34 security-related commits. The post still acknowledges prompt injection as unsolved while promising more maintainers, gateway reliability, security work, and sustainable contributor funding.

### Comment pulse

- Users like local ownership and messaging integration, but several cannot yet distinguish the platform’s value from terminal agents beyond heartbeat and memory.
- Security dominates: commenters recommend turning on sandboxing, isolated accounts, external containment, and treating an unsandboxed setup as model-controlled remote execution.
- Token bills surprised testers, including reports of $5 in half an hour and $560 over a weekend; local models trade cost for hardware.

### LLM perspective

- View: The durable story is not another rebrand but rapid demand for persistent, channel-spanning local agents.
- Impact: Broad account access magnifies convenience, token expense, supply-chain exposure, and prompt-injection consequences.
- Watch next: Secure defaults, audited integrations, maintainership capacity, predictable costs, and evidence the 34 hardening commits materially reduce risk.
