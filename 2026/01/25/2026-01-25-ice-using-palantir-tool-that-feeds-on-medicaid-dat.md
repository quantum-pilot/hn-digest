# ICE using Palantir tool that feeds on Medicaid data

- Score: 785 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46756117) | Link: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/01/report-ice-using-palantir-tool-feeds-medicaid-data

### TL;DR

EFF says reporting and Oregon court testimony indicate ICE uses Palantir’s ELITE system to map possible detention targets, assemble dossiers, and score confidence in current addresses. The tool reportedly receives address information from Health and Human Services, including Medicaid, alongside other government sources. EFF argues that repurposing records collected for health care, taxes, or benefits creates a searchable surveillance system far beyond their original purpose. It is pursuing litigation over several data-sharing programs but says Congress must impose broader limits.
