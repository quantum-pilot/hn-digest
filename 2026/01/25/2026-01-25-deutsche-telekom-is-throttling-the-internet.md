# Deutsche Telekom is throttling the internet

- Score: 567 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46751899) | Link: https://netzbremse.de/en/

### TL;DR

A coalition of consumer and digital-rights groups alleges Deutsche Telekom deliberately leaves interconnection points congested, making nonpaying services slow while well-funded providers can purchase reliable access. Its complaint asks Germany’s Federal Network Agency to treat the practice as a net-neutrality violation. The campaign presents years of customer reports involving Cloudflare, universities, GitHub, video, games, and workplace services, often worsening at peak hours and improving through VPNs or other carriers. Telekom disputes the throttling accusation; the submitted page primarily documents the complainants’ case and user experiences.
