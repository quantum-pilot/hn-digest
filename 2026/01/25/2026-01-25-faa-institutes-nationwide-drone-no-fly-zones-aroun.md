# FAA institutes nationwide drone no-fly zones around ICE operations

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46755999) | Link: https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/faa-drone-no-fly-zone-ice-dhs

### TL;DR

FAA notice FDC 6/4375 creates continuously moving restricted airspace around DHS facilities, vehicles, convoys, and escorts nationwide. Civilian drones must remain 3,000 feet away laterally and 1,000 feet vertically; violators risk penalties, lost privileges, prosecution, or interception. Unlike ordinary temporary restrictions, the notice supplies no coordinates, activation times, or public location feed, so a lawful pilot may unknowingly enter the zone when a covered vehicle approaches. Exceptions require advance coordination for defense, emergency, law-enforcement, or disaster missions.
