# White House alters arrest photo of ICE protester, says "the memes will continue"

- Score: 250 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46755734) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/01/white-house-posts-altered-arrest-photo-to-make-it-appear-ice-critic-was-sobbing/

### TL;DR

The official White House account posted an altered arrest photo that made Minnesota civil-rights attorney Nekima Levy Armstrong appear to be crying, about 30 minutes after Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem shared the apparent original. DHS later used the unaltered image. Armstrong had been arrested after protesting at a church whose pastor was alleged to lead a local ICE office. A White House spokesman defended the manipulation as a meme; Armstrong’s lawyer called it fabricated defamation that could prejudice her prosecution.
