# Himalayas bare and rocky after reduced winter snowfall, scientists warn

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46583464) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/clyndv7zd20o

### TL;DR

Himalayan winters are delivering less snow, while warming makes remaining snowfall melt faster. Multiple datasets show declining precipitation across western and central areas; northwestern snowfall fell 25% over the past five years versus a 1980–2020 average, and 2024–2025 snow persistence reached a 23-year low. The combined loss of glaciers and seasonal snow threatens water, irrigation, hydropower, ecosystems, and slope stability across major river basins. HN discussion emphasized food security, climate-driven migration and conflict, and the bleak symbolism of Himalaya meaning house of snow.
