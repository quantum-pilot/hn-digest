# Apple picks Google's Gemini to power Siri

- Score: 601 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46589675) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/12/apple-google-ai-siri-gemini.html

### TL;DR

Apple and Google announced a multiyear partnership using Gemini and Google Cloud technology as the foundation for future Apple models, including a delayed Siri upgrade expected in 2026. Apple says processing will remain on its devices and Private Cloud Compute, while its ChatGPT agreement stays unchanged; financial terms were undisclosed. HN readers mostly saw pragmatic outsourcing: Google absorbs frontier-training costs while Apple controls product integration and can potentially switch suppliers. Debate centered on privacy, contractual wording, dependency risk, and whether better models can repair Siri’s poor reliability.
