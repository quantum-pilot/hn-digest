# Floppy disks turn out to be the greatest TV remote for kids

- Score: 470 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46587934) | Link: https://blog.smartere.dk/2026/01/floppy-disks-the-best-tv-remote-for-kids/

### TL;DR

A parent built a tactile Chromecast remote that lets a three-year-old choose one video by inserting a labeled floppy disk, avoiding complex menus, autoplay, and phones. Each disk stores a small command on track zero; a modified drive detects insertion, an ATmega reads it, and an ESP8266 relays play or pause actions over Wi-Fi. The battery-powered prototype required solving spin-up current, grounding, sleep, and disk-damage problems. HN praised the playful engineering, compared RFID-based products, criticized modern TV interfaces, and debated whether young children should independently access screens.
