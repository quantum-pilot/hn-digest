# Predicting OpenAI's ad strategy

- Score: 459 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46668021) | Link: https://ossa-ma.github.io/blog/openads

### TL;DR

Chaib argues OpenAI’s vast user base and high-intent conversations make advertising a plausible route to fund its infrastructure, not evidence of imminent collapse. He projects ads moving from answer-bottom placements toward self-serve auctions, affiliate transactions, and sponsored sidebars, potentially lifting 2026 revenue to $30–35 billion and 2029 revenue to $140–150 billion. HN discussion is far less bullish, focusing on platform rent extraction, ads spreading into paid tiers, and optimization tactics corrupting answers.

### Comment pulse

- Advertising is productive acquisition spending → defenders cite measurable returns, while critics see Google and Meta extracting business margins.
- Paid plans may not remain clean → affluent subscribers are valuable targets, though competition and churn could preserve genuinely ad-free tiers.
- Generative optimization could pollute answers → commenters expect sponsored recommendations, covert brand preference, and automated content designed to enter model outputs.

### LLM perspective

- View: High conversational intent supports monetization, but the ARPU trajectory depends on unproven ad infrastructure and trust.
- Impact: Advertisers gain a purchase funnel; users face blurred boundaries between assistance, recommendation, and persuasion.
- Watch next: Beta pricing, ad load, independence audits, paid-tier policy, and conversion rates will test the forecast.
