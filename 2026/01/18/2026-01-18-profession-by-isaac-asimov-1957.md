# Profession by Isaac Asimov (1957)

- Score: 171 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46664195) | Link: https://www.abelard.org/asimov.php

### TL;DR

In Asimov’s future, most people receive professional knowledge instantly from brain tapes, while George Platen is labeled uneducable and confined to a supposed institution for the feeble-minded. His escape, failed appeal to Novian recruiters, and insistence that people can learn from books reveal the institution’s true purpose: identifying rare minds capable of original thought, who must create the knowledge and machines that tapes merely reproduce. HN readers connect the story to rigid schooling, shallow LLM assistance, declining mastery, and innovation by outsiders.
