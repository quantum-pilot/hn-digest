# jQuery 4

- Score: 696 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46664755) | Link: https://blog.jquery.com/2026/01/17/jquery-4-0-0/

### TL;DR

jQuery’s January 17 release post announces 4.0.0, its first major version in nearly a decade and a 20th-anniversary modernization. The release drops IE 10 and older plus several legacy browsers, removes deprecated and internal APIs, migrates source from AMD to ES modules, adds Trusted Types/CSP support, and adopts native focus-event order. Those cuts save over 3KB gzipped; the slim build also removes Deferreds and Callbacks. HN celebrated jQuery’s longevity, simplicity, and unusually strong migration tooling while acknowledging that most current use centers on established codebases.
