# Erdos 281 solved with ChatGPT 5.2 Pro

- Score: 288 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46664631) | Link: https://twitter.com/neelsomani/status/2012695714187325745

### TL;DR

Neel Somani reported that GPT-5.2 Pro produced a proof for Erdős problem 281 without using a known solution, and Terence Tao initially highlighted its unusually careful handling of limits and quantifiers. The accompanying correction matters: the underlying result was already implied by Davenport–Erdős (1936) and Rogers, so this was not the first solution, and Tao moved it from his strongest AI-contribution category. The generated proof nevertheless appears distinct from the literature proof. HN debated novelty, verification, overlooked long-tail problems, and hype.
