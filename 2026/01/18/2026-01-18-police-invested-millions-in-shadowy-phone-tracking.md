# Police Invested Millions in Shadowy Phone-Tracking Software Won't Say How Used

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46672150) | Link: https://www.texasobserver.org/texas-police-invest-tangles-sheriff-surveillance/

### TL;DR

The Texas Observer reports that police agencies use Tangles, a PenLink platform combining scraped information and commercial mobile-location data, to develop leads without warrants. Goliad County described geofencing a roughly 300-mile corridor from a receipt, yet its records did not mention Tangles or connect it to an arrest. Texas DPS holds a five-year $5.3 million contract, while other agencies also bought access but supplied few concrete outcomes. Civil-liberties advocates call this an end-run around judicial oversight; HN focused on parallel construction, constitutional rights, and local investigative journalism.
