# Open Chaos: A self-evolving open-source project

- Score: 287 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46566812) | Link: https://www.openchaos.dev/

### TL;DR

OpenChaos makes its deployed website subject to weekly community control: anyone submits a pull request, GitHub reactions determine its net score, and an eligible winner merges Saturdays before Vercel redeploys. The recovered, intentionally mutable README currently lists CI, conflict, malware, vote-threshold, and rhyming-title rules, but these may not reflect the January 10 launch state; even the rules can change. HN readers compared the concept to Twitch Plays, Nomic, Screeps Quorum, and GitConsensus, while proposing LLM committees that review adversarial contributions.
