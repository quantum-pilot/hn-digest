# NASA announces unprecedented return of sick ISS astronaut and crew

- Score: 86 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46565695) | Link: https://www.livescience.com/space/space-exploration/nasa-cancels-spacewalk-and-considers-early-crew-return-from-iss-due-to-medical-issues

### TL;DR

NASA postponed a 6.5-hour ISS spacewalk and will end Crew-11’s mission early after an unnamed astronaut developed a medical problem. The astronaut is stable, but the agency withheld diagnostic details and had not announced a return date; Crew-12 was scheduled for mid-February. HN commenters emphasized that this is a planned early return, not an emergency evacuation, backed by extensive training and round-the-clock ground control. Discussion also explored isolation, immune-system tradeoffs, and historical transmission of respiratory or gastrointestinal illness during spaceflight.
