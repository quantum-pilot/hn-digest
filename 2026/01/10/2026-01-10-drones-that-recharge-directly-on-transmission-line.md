# Drones that recharge directly on transmission lines

- Score: 151 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46567218) | Link: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/voltair

### TL;DR

Voltair proposes a distributed fleet of weatherized drones that recharge from pads mounted on utility poles, giving each pad roughly 1,000 square miles of coverage. Power-grid inspection is the initial market across seven million miles of infrastructure, with road, rail, telecom, weather, forestry, rescue, and insurance applications envisioned at claimed tenfold lower prices. HN questions technical constraints from prior line-induction prototypes, legality and liability, military usefulness under jamming, and surveillance; practitioners say automated anomaly detection can digest expanded inspection imagery efficiently.
