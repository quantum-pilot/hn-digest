# Microsoft May Have Created the Slowest Windows in 25 Years with Windows 11

- Score: 272 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46567138) | Link: https://www.eteknix.com/microsoft-may-have-created-the-slowest-windows-in-25-years-with-windows-11/

### TL;DR

A YouTube comparison ran Windows XP, Vista, 7, 8.1, 10, and 11 on a ThinkPad X220, testing boot time, battery, application launches, memory, and video editing. Windows 11 ranked worst overall: it booted slowest, launched Paint and File Explorer slowest, used the most idle RAM, and performed poorly in OpenShot, but led file transfers and system-app storage. The unsupported laptop limits the benchmark’s force. HN nevertheless reports similar latency and blames web apps, services, ads, AI, and preloading; Linux attracts switchers despite battery and compatibility tradeoffs.
