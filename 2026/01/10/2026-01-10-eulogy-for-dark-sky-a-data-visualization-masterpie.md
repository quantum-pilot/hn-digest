# Eulogy for Dark Sky, a data visualization masterpiece (2023)

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46565132) | Link: https://nightingaledvs.com/dark-sky-weather-data-viz/

### TL;DR

Dark Sky’s 2023 shutdown erased more than a forecast provider, the essay argues: it removed a model of context-sensitive information design. Its rolling 12-hour view emphasized what mattered now, weekly temperature bars preserved relative magnitudes, rough precipitation categories avoided false precision, and maps used color and arrows for immediate decisions. HN users praise minute-scale rain alerts and historical weather, debate whether Apple Weather matches its accuracy, and recommend independent successors, while lamenting acquisitions that preserve data or features but discard coherent product design.
