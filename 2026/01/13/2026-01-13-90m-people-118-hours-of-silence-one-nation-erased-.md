# 90M people. 118 hours of silence. One nation erased from the internet

- Score: 254 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46603910) | Link: https://state-of-iranblackout.whisper.security/

### TL;DR

An archived forensic report argues Iran’s January shutdown was coordinated: ten networks destabilized together, TCI routing updates jumped 368%, IPv6 visibility vanished, and middleboxes targeted messaging and circumvention tools. It connects the blackout to mass arrests and disputed casualty estimates, framing lost connectivity as cover for repression. HN readers largely treated shutdown capability as authoritarian infrastructure, debated how Starlink could be jammed or located, and questioned the report’s propagandistic tone, sourcing, casualty certainty, and startup-like presentation.
