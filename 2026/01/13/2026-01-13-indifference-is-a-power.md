# Indifference is a power

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46601121) | Link: https://aeon.co/essays/why-stoicism-is-one-of-the-best-mind-hacks-ever-devised

### TL;DR

Lary Wallace argues Stoicism is not grim endurance or emotional numbness, but selective indifference toward what lies outside one’s control. Through Epictetus, Seneca, Marcus Aurelius, and prisoner James Stockdale, the essay presents adversity as training, negative visualization as gratitude practice, and Stoicism as a precursor to cognitive behavioral therapy. HN readers distinguished classical practice from social-media versions that glorify toughness. They debated whether reframing loss builds acceptance or suppresses emotion, broadly agreeing that disciplined response must include later emotional processing rather than permanent dissociation.

### Comment pulse

- Classical Stoicism regulates responses → practitioners examine emotions and choices instead of pretending feelings do not exist.
- Pop Stoicism distorts the method → toughness branding can legitimize avoidance, bottled anger, or manosphere posturing.
- Reframing can build acceptance → CBT-like perspective reduces catastrophizing — counterpoint: skipped emotional processing accumulates psychological debt.
