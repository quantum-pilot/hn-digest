# Network of Scottish X accounts go dark amid Iran blackout

- Score: 298 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46599574) | Link: https://www.heraldscotland.com/news/25759181.network-scottish-x-accounts-go-dark-amid-iran-blackout/

### TL;DR

Several X accounts posing as Scottish independence supporters stopped posting when Iran imposed an internet blackout, reinforcing researchers’ attribution of the network to Iranian influence operations. The personas had thousands of followers and recently fabricated dramatic unrest across Scotland; earlier blackouts produced similar disappearances. A Clemson study estimated one Iranian-backed network generated 4% of independence-related content. HN discussion stressed that such campaigns may seek less to persuade than to flood discourse with noise, erode confidence in truth, and exploit authentic political divisions without invalidating genuine independence support.
