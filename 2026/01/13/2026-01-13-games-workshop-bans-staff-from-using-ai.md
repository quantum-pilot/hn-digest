# Games Workshop bans staff from using AI

- Score: 192 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46607681) | Link: https://www.ign.com/articles/warhammer-maker-games-workshop-bans-its-staff-from-using-ai-in-its-content-or-designs-says-none-of-its-senior-managers-are-currently-excited-about-the-tech

### TL;DR

Games Workshop prohibits AI-generated content and AI in design, competitions, and unauthorized external work, while letting a few senior managers evaluate the technology. CEO Kevin Rountree emphasized data governance, intellectual-property protection, and continued hiring across art, writing, sculpting, and concept development. HN saw the caution as commercially rational for a company built on distinctive, copyrightable assets and an anti-AI hobby community. Debate covered generic output, legal uncertainty, inconsistent attitudes toward replacing artists versus programmers, and fans using AI-powered 3D tools against Games Workshop’s pricing and enforcement.
