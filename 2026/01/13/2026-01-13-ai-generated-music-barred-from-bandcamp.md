# AI Generated Music Barred from Bandcamp

- Score: 506 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46605490) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/BandCamp/comments/1qbw8ba/ai_generated_music_on_bandcamp/

### TL;DR

Bandcamp now bars music generated wholly or substantially by AI, bans AI impersonation, asks users to flag suspected works, and reserves removal rights. The policy frames the marketplace as a human connection between artists and fans. HN welcomed a defense against synthetic spam and described migrating from Spotify to purchases, but debated an ambiguous boundary: generative tracks versus restoration, stem separation, mastering, or artist-trained experimentation. Skeptics fear provenance deception and marketplace flooding; others expect AI eventually to become a legitimate instrument like sampling.

### Comment pulse

- Provenance matters → listeners felt deceived when apparently human music proved synthetic, making authorship part of the product.
- The boundary is functional → cleanup and stem separation preserve human work, while one-prompt generation can flood catalogs.
- AI may become an instrument → bespoke models can enable new forms — counterpoint: today’s output remains mostly pastiche.
