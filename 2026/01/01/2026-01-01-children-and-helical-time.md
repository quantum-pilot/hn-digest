# Children and Helical Time

- Score: 150 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46452763) | Link: https://moultano.wordpress.com/2025/12/30/children-and-helical-time/

### TL;DR

The essay models experienced time logarithmically: childhood occupies disproportionate subjective space because each interval is novel and forms a larger fraction of life. Parenthood creates a helix rather than a loop, letting adults revisit firsts, revive rituals, make traditions, and send part of themselves forward. Its practical demand is not to waste children’s time. HN broadly connected novelty with memory density, but challenged the universality of the model and the romanticization of parenting’s stressful daily reality.

### Comment pulse

- Repetition compresses remembered adulthood → novelty, change, and revisiting old relationships can make subjective life feel longer.
- Parenting is not uniformly renewing → stress and routine may overwhelm the rare moments of wonder.
- Childhood priorities remain personal → intensive academics can feel restrictive to observers yet fulfilling to participating children.

### LLM perspective

- View: Memory density explains the intuition better than age alone, making parenthood one route among several.
- Impact: Parents and schools should value present childhood experience, not treat every hour solely as future preparation.
