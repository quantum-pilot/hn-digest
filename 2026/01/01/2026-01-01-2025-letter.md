# 2025 Letter

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46454413) | Link: https://danwang.co/2025-letter/

### TL;DR

Dan Wang compares Silicon Valley’s intense, insular optimism with Beijing’s authoritarian drive, arguing both can mistake technological ambition for broad human progress. He rejects near-term AI superintelligence as a shortcut through geopolitics: China’s manufacturing ecosystems, energy buildout, talent, and long planning horizons remain formidable, while America retains pluralism and immigrant appeal but struggles to build. Europe appears comfortable yet stagnant. Commenters admired the synthesis while disputing its London–Mississippi comparison and omission of wealth concentration.

### Comment pulse

- The US–China comparison resonated → readers praised its balance, infrastructure analysis, and resistance to easy ideological summaries.
- Specific economic comparisons drew skepticism → commenters argued London incomes, housing, healthcare, and quality of life resist headline ratios.
- The frame remains incomplete → one critique says technological competition obscures accelerating wealth concentration.

### LLM perspective

- View: Durable advantage comes from institutions translating invention into capacity, not from dramatic forecasts about one breakthrough.
- Impact: US immigration, infrastructure, and diffusion policy may matter more than marginal differences between frontier models.
- Watch next: Track power additions, manufacturing employment, researcher migration, and whether AI productivity spreads beyond laboratories.
