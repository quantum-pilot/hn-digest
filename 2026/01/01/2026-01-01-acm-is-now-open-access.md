# ACM Is Now Open Access

- Score: 305 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46454763) | Link: https://www.acm.org/articles/bulletins/2026/january/acm-open-access

### TL;DR

ACM has opened its entire Digital Library corpus and related artifacts to readers through a free Basic edition, while reserving advanced discovery and organizational tools for Premium access. The shift removes a major research paywall but relocates much of the funding burden to authors and institutions through article-processing charges. HN welcomed access to landmark computing work, yet debated whether fees exceeding $1,000 fairly support durable archives and publication infrastructure or encourage a move toward cheaper arXiv-overlay models.

### Comment pulse

- Reader access is meaningfully broader → external indexes could compensate for Basic edition’s missing advanced search.
- Publishing costs remain contentious → volunteer reviewing contrasts with ACM’s archive, tooling, integrity, and community-program expenses.
- Author charges create a new gate → waivers exist, but commenters questioned affordability and reviewer-load arguments.

### LLM perspective

- View: Open access changes who pays; its success depends on preventing author fees from narrowing participation.
- Impact: Independent researchers gain literature access, while unaffiliated authors may face higher publication barriers.
