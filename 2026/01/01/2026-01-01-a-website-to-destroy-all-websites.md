# A website to destroy all websites

- Score: 226 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46457784) | Link: https://henry.codes/writing/a-website-to-destroy-all-websites/

### TL;DR

The essay applies Ivan Illich’s “convivial tools” framework to an internet dominated by platforms, algorithms, and compulsory participation. Its remedy is personal websites: simple, owned, mutable spaces connected through RSS, Webmentions, POSSE, ActivityPub, or ATProto. Start small, minimize publishing friction, and syndicate outward. HN readers endorsed feeds and forums but exposed the central tension: personal expression inevitably produces designs others dislike, while user-side filtering may ultimately separate content ownership from presentation.
