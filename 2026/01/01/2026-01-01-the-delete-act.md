# The Delete Act

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46449694) | Link: https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/about-drop-and-the-delete-act/

### TL;DR

California’s Delete Request and Opt-out Platform, created by the 2023 Delete Act, lets residents send one free request to delete personal information held by more than 500 registered data brokers and control future sales. Brokers must register annually, disclose collection and sharing practices, process DROP requests beginning August 1, 2026, and undergo compliance audits or face penalties. HN welcomed relief from hundreds of individual opt-outs but raised concerns about identity verification, missing records harming tenant or credit checks, political lists, and launch readiness.

### Comment pulse
