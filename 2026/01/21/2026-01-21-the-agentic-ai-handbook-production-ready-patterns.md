# The Agentic AI Handbook: Production-Ready Patterns

- Score: 199 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46701969) | Link: https://www.nibzard.com/agentic-handbook

### TL;DR

The handbook organizes 113 production-agent patterns around a core claim: reliability comes from loop design, not model magic. It recommends bounded tasks, reviewable diffs, deterministic tests, controlled tool permissions, observable action traces, replan gates, persistent project rules, and human approval for risky work. Agents fit repetitive jobs with objective completion signals, not ambiguous or weakly tested domains. HN reaction was polarized: practitioners described substantial workflow gains, while critics called the article AI-written and difficult to access; one quoted Plan-Then-Execute passage conflicts with the supplied handbook text.
