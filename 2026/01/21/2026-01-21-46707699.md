- Score: 345 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46707699) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46707699

### TL;DR

A terse Tell HN post says Bending Spoons laid off almost all of Vimeo, leaving fewer than 15 engineers. Commenters found supporting employee accounts but no definitive company-wide figure, so the exact scope remains uncertain. Debate centered on Bending Spoons’ strategy of buying mature products, cutting teams, and operating them as profitable assets: defenders called that rational stewardship of stable software, while critics argued products still need maintenance and customers deserve clearer communication about staffing and long-term support.
