# Scientists find a way to regrow cartilage in mice and human tissue samples

- Score: 237 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46709179) | Link: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/01/260120000333.htm

### TL;DR

Stanford researchers inhibited 15-PGDH, an age-linked enzyme, and regenerated functional hyaline cartilage in old and injured mice without activating stem cells. Treated human knee-replacement tissue also shifted toward cartilage formation, while a related oral inhibitor has passed Phase 1 safety testing for muscle weakness. The approach could eventually address osteoarthritis rather than merely manage pain, but human joint trials remain unproven. HN readers balanced excitement and personal need against the familiar risk that promising mouse results never translate.
