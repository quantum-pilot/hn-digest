# I'm addicted to being useful

- Score: 469 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46690402) | Link: https://www.seangoedecke.com/addicted-to-being-useful/

### TL;DR

A staff software engineer describes an internal compulsion to solve problems and be useful, likening himself to Gogol’s devoted copyist Akaky and a working dog rewarded by the work itself. Software turns that dysfunction into a satisfying career, but the author’s earlier advice aims to direct it toward recognized impact and away from corporate predators. Commenters broadened the warning: compulsive fixing can damage relationships, postpone self-care, reward organizational incompetence, and confuse usefulness with recognition, while purposeful contribution can still anchor retirement and belonging.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: Usefulness is sustainable when chosen deliberately, bounded clearly, and separated from personal worth.
- Impact: Unchecked compulsion creates burnout, exploitable employees, strained relationships, and systems that never address root causes.
- Watch next: Whether the author can preserve intrinsic motivation while refusing low-leverage work and protecting self-care.
