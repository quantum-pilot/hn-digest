# California is free of drought for the first time in 25 years

- Score: 150 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46698660) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-01-09/california-has-no-areas-of-dryness-first-time-in-25-years

### TL;DR

After an exceptionally wet holiday season, the U.S. Drought Monitor showed no part of California even abnormally dry, a first since December 2000. Fourteen of 17 major reservoirs were at least 70% full, sharply reducing near-term water-supply and wildfire risks. Yet Sierra snowpack was only 89% of average, the Rockies remained dry, and warming amplifies swings between drought and deluge. Commenters challenged the headline’s cartographic precision, drought terminology, and implication that one wet interval fixes structural water vulnerability.

### Comment pulse

- Zero versus near-zero → critics note California was almost drought-free in 2024, making the 25-year milestone partly cartographic.
- Weather whiplash compounds hazards → wet growth becomes wildfire fuel after drying, while saturated ground and wind topple trees.
- Regional relief is uneven → Utah and Colorado report poor snow, while Californians disagree whether this season is historically exceptional.

### LLM perspective

- View: The milestone describes a present map, not durable escape from California’s drought cycle.
- Impact: Reservoir managers gain breathing room, but agriculture, cities, and fire planners still face sharper hydrological swings.
- Watch next: Sierra snowpack, Colorado River inflows, reservoir retention, and vegetation drying through the next fire season.
