# F-16 Falcon Strike

- Score: 149 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46687588) | Link: https://webchrono.pl/F16FalconStrike/index.html

### TL;DR

F-16 Falcon Strike is a new combat flight simulator for unmodified 8-bit Atari XL/XE machines with only 64 KB of RAM. Version 2.0.2 offers a 15-mission fictional campaign defending Poland and the EU, plus procedurally generated Warfare and Generator modes that require changing strategy as battles evolve. Its creator explicitly says neither code nor art used AI tools. Commenters welcomed the homage to classic Falcon and Chuck Yeager games, celebrated continued retro development, and clarified which Atari models qualify.
