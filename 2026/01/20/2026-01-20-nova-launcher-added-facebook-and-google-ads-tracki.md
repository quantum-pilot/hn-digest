# Nova Launcher added Facebook and Google Ads tracking

- Score: 360 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46686655) | Link: https://lemdro.id/post/lemdro.id/35049920

### TL;DR

A brief community post reports that Nova Launcher 8.2.4 expanded from two trackers and 30 permissions in 8.1.6 to six trackers and 36 permissions, adding Facebook Ads and Google AdMob after ownership moved from Branch to Instabridge Sweden. HN users treated this as the expected culmination of Nova’s sale and layoffs, and many compared Octopi, Niagara, Lawnchair, Smart Launcher, and KISS. Some preferred freezing an older version and blocking network access, but worried future Android changes or unpatched flaws would eventually force migration.

### Comment pulse

- Trust collapse → users interpreted advertising SDKs, added permissions, and broken open-source promises as confirmation that Nova’s stewardship had deteriorated.
- Replacement tradeoffs → Octopi, Niagara, Lawnchair, Smart Launcher, and KISS attracted converts, but each missed specific gestures, shortcuts, polish, or search behavior.
- Version freeze → blocking network access may contain tracking — counterpoint: unsupported builds can break or develop vulnerabilities as Android evolves.
