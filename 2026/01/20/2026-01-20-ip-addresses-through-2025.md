# IP Addresses Through 2025

- Score: 146 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46691835) | Link: https://www.potaroo.net/ispcol/2026-01/addr2025.html

### TL;DR

Geoff Huston’s 2025 address census finds IPv4 exhausted but stubbornly viable: NAT lets more than 30 billion devices share roughly 3 billion advertised addresses, while 33 million addresses changed hands and sale prices fell about 50% during 2025. IPv6 allocation activity declined, although measured deployment grew 3.7%, led partly by China. Huston warns that prolonged dual-protocol dependence reinforces client-server constraints, fragmentation, and dominant incumbents. Commenters read falling IPv4 prices as hyperscaler demand saturation, debated CGNAT’s efficiency and security, and predicted niche value for reputable email addresses.

### Comment pulse

- Price collapse drives interpretation → AWS-scale stockpiling faded, public-address fees shifted costs, and CGNAT proved huge user populations need little IPv4 inventory.

- IPv4 may become artisanal → clean addresses still matter for email, while future IPv6-only clients could make legacy-only services unreachable.
