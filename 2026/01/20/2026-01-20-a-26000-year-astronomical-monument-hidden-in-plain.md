# A 26,000-year astronomical monument hidden in plain sight (2019)

- Score: 296 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46695628) | Link: https://longnow.org/ideas/the-26000-year-astronomical-monument-hidden-in-plain-sight/

### TL;DR

Hoover Dam’s Monument Plaza hides a celestial timestamp in its terrazzo floor. Oskar J. W. Hansen aligned a circle around the flagpole with Earth’s 25,772-year axial precession, then marked planets and stars visible at the dam’s 1936 completion, narrowing the date to a day. Recovering plans required archival detective work because Hansen’s explanation was opaque. Commenters admired the long-horizon craft, compared personal astronomical keepsakes, and debated whether the damaged star map is open, dismantled, or being reconstructed.
