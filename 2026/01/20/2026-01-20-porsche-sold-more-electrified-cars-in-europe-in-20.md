# Porsche sold more electrified cars in Europe in 2025 than pure gas-powered cars

- Score: 440 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46686640) | Link: https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2026/company/porsche-deliveries-2025-41516.html

### TL;DR

Porsche delivered 279,449 cars in 2025, down 10% worldwide, while electrified vehicles reached 57.9% of European deliveries; one-third were fully electric. Globally, battery-electric cars represented 22.2% and plug-in hybrids 12.1%. Macan led at 84,328 units, the 911 set a record, and China fell 26% amid luxury weakness and intense EV competition. HN focused less on the milestone than Porsche’s overall decline, debating Chinese automakers’ rise, whether Porsche EVs preserve driving appeal, and how forced combustion-model withdrawals shaped Europe’s mix.
