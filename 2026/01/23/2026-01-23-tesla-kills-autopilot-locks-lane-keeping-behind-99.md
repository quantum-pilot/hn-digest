# Tesla kills Autopilot, locks lane-keeping behind $99/month fee

- Score: 261 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46736683) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/01/tesla-wants-recurring-revenue-discontinues-autopilot-in-favor-of-fsd/

### TL;DR

Beginning February 14, new Tesla buyers who want automatic steering must subscribe to supervised Full Self-Driving for $99 monthly. The older Autopilot bundle combined adaptive cruise control with Autosteer; the remaining $8,000 lifetime FSD purchase also ends that day, and Elon Musk says subscription prices will rise as capabilities improve. Both remain Level 2 assistance requiring driver attention. The change arrives amid California action over deceptive self-driving marketing, wrongful-death litigation, and declining sales, margins, and emissions-credit income that make recurring revenue attractive.
