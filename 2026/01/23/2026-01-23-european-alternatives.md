# European Alternatives

- Score: 601 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46731976) | Link: https://european-alternatives.eu

### TL;DR

The directory catalogs Europe-based replacements for cloud, analytics, email, hosting, search, and other digital services. It argues that buying regionally supports local jobs and taxes, simplifies VAT and familiar payment methods, strengthens GDPR compliance, and makes legal enforcement easier under shared rules. Listings span dozens of categories with counts and user suggestions. Commenters welcomed the catalog’s growth but questioned its maintenance: submissions can go unanswered, its official-looking design masks a one-person operation, and important gaps remain in payment rails, software mirrors, and AI tooling.
