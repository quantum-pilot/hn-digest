# Proof of Corn

- Score: 293 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46735511) | Link: https://proofofcorn.com/

### TL;DR

The project assigns Claude Code the role of farm manager to test whether an AI can produce a physical crop. The agent is meant to combine sensor, weather, and satellite data; choose planting, irrigation, and harvest actions; and coordinate landowners, suppliers, and equipment operators. The project remained preliminary: it had spent $12.99, sent 14 outreach emails, and was seeking land in Iowa and Texas, with spring planting and October harvest planned. Every decision, expense, and code change is promised as public documentation.
