# What has Docker become?

- Score: 225 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46731748) | Link: https://tuananh.net/2026/01/20/what-has-docker-become/

### TL;DR

The article portrays Docker Inc. as searching for a business around a container standard that became ubiquitous infrastructure. After retreating from Swarm, it expanded through Scout and Testcontainers into supply-chain analysis and testing, then added Model Runner, cloud GPU offload, agent tooling, and MCP Defender. In December 2025 it released over 1,000 Apache-licensed hardened images free, challenging secure-image vendors without clarifying revenue. A 2025 CEO change and repeated pivots prompt acquisition speculation, while the author argues open container technology will persist regardless of the company’s fate.

### Comment pulse

- Early enterprise needs were missed → commenters cite rootless operation, internal registries, and systemd integration that Red Hat built elsewhere.
