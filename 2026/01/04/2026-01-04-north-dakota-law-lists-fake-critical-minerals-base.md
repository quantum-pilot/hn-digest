# North Dakota law lists fake critical minerals based on coal lawyers' names

- Score: 125 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46492161) | Link: https://bismarcktribune.com/news/local/government-politics/article_515812a0-d29a-4161-91f1-3e53003e2911.html

### TL;DR

North Dakota’s new critical-minerals law includes fictional friezium and stralium, names resembling coal-company attorneys Christopher Friez and David Straley. They entered during last-day amendments; participants deny or cannot explain authorship, and an earlier docterium joke had already been removed. Officials worry the error undermines first-in-the-nation rules, while the bill’s sponsor admits he should have checked. HN focused on missing edit provenance, rushed lawmaking, and inadequate expert review; some suspect satire aimed at lobbyist influence rather than accidental drafting.
