# Neural Networks: Zero to Hero

- Score: 697 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46485090) | Link: https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html

### TL;DR

Andrej Karpathy’s code-first course builds neural-network intuition from scalar backpropagation through character-level language models, multilayer perceptrons, activation and gradient diagnostics, BatchNorm, manual tensor backpropagation, a WaveNet-like architecture, GPT, and tokenization. It assumes solid Python and introductory calculus, emphasizing implementation over library surveys. HN learners praised its unusually clear, high-signal explanations and ability to expose underlying mechanics. Some found the pacing elementary for adjacent specialists or wanted clearer expectations about what completing it enables beyond educational models.
