# Web development is fun again

- Score: 245 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46488576) | Link: https://ma.ttias.be/web-development-is-fun-again/

### TL;DR

A veteran backend developer says Claude and Codex restore the solo-builder feeling lost as frontend, infrastructure, testing, observability, and tooling became separate specialties. Drawing on two decades of experience, he claims roughly tenfold productivity, enough judgment to reject weak output, and renewed space for UI experimentation rather than boilerplate. HN users with stale skills, management roles, or limited parenting time echoed the faster ramp-up. Critics argued much web complexity is optional and AI can replace enjoyable coding with exhausting review of fragile, unmaintainable code.
