# Lessons from 14 Years at Google

- Score: 726 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46488819) | Link: https://addyosmani.com/blog/21-lessons/

### TL;DR

A Google engineer distills 14 years into 21 lessons centered on users, collaboration, clarity, and compounding judgment rather than programming technique. He advocates shipping imperfect prototypes, spending novelty sparingly, deleting unnecessary work, treating compatibility as product, aligning teams before accelerating them, making glue work visible, pairing speed metrics with quality, admitting uncertainty, teaching through writing, and valuing time deliberately. Commenters reinforced the real-world dependency created by bugs and latency, debated whether abstractions move or manage complexity, and argued that organizational influence can resemble politics.

### Comment pulse
