# Standard Ebooks: Public Domain Day 2026 in Literature

- Score: 334 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46462702) | Link: https://standardebooks.org/blog/public-domain-day-2026

### TL;DR

Books published in 1930 entered the U.S. public domain on January 1, 2026, after the country’s 95-year copyright term. Standard Ebooks released 20 carefully produced free editions, including The Maltese Falcon, As I Lay Dying, Kafka’s The Castle translation, and works by Langston Hughes, Agatha Christie, Dorothy L. Sayers, and Evelyn Waugh. HN highlighted the substantial volunteer labor behind polished EPUBs and warned that public-domain dates vary internationally, sometimes producing geo-blocking and confusing cross-border availability.
