# A confession from a mainstream food delivery app engineer

- Score: 157 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46461578) | Link: https://www.reddit.com/r/confession/s/gbrh2zxeou

### TL;DR

An anonymous Reddit post claims a food-delivery platform charges for ignored priority flags, delays standard orders, scores driver desperation to suppress pay, routes benefit fees toward anti-union lobbying, and offsets predicted tips by lowering base compensation. None of these allegations is substantiated in the supplied material. HN commenters found the incentives plausible but identified credibility problems: no evidence, theatrical anonymity claims, broad cross-posting, apparent moderation removals, and operational details that may not fit how a large company separates product, engineering, and finance.
