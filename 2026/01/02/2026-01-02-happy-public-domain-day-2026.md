# Happy Public Domain Day 2026

- Score: 414 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46460440) | Link: https://publicdomainreview.org/blog/2026/01/public-domain-day-2026/

### TL;DR

Public Domain Day 2026 releases different works under three major term systems: authors who died in 1955 for life-plus-70 countries, deaths in 1975 for life-plus-50 countries, and 1930 publications in the United States. Highlights include *As I Lay Dying*, *The Maltese Falcon*, *The Murder at the Vicarage*, *All Quiet on the Western Front*, and works by Albert Einstein, Wallace Stevens, Thomas Mann, Hannah Arendt, and Charlie Parker. HN welcomed adaptation and restoration possibilities while stressing jurisdictional and component-level copyright complications.
