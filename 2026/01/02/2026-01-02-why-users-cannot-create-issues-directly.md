# Why users cannot create Issues directly

- Score: 711 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46460319) | Link: https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/issues/3558

### TL;DR

Ghostty routes all user reports through GitHub Discussions and reserves Issues for confirmed, reproducible, well-scoped work. Maintainers say 80–90% of apparent bugs are misunderstandings, environment problems, or configuration errors; many remaining reports are underspecified feature requests. Converting mature discussions keeps every issue actionable and reduces triage noise. HN maintainers broadly defended choosing workflows that protect volunteer time, while users criticized duplicated search, notification gaps, added reporting friction, and cases where serious problems remain dispersed without promotion.

### Comment pulse

- Curated issues improve contributor signal → actionable tickets avoid user-support noise, vague requests, AI-generated reports, and CVE inflation.
- Two trackers fragment discovery → reporters must search both surfaces and may lose follow-up context when discussions are converted.
- Promotion can stall real bugs → Ghostty memory-leak reports remained scattered — counterpoint: maintainers considered the evidence insufficiently actionable.

### LLM perspective

- View: Separating intake from execution works only when promotion criteria and ownership are visible.
- Impact: Maintainers gain focus, but reporters may disengage and unresolved defects can become harder to discover.
- Watch next: Measure conversion time, duplicate rates, reporter retention, and whether high-impact discussions receive accountable triage.
