# Canada's deal with China signals it is serious about shift from US

- Score: 137 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46661217) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cm24k6kk1rko

### TL;DR

Mark Carney’s China deal cuts Canada’s tariff on 49,000 Chinese EVs from 100% to 6.1%, potentially expanding the quota to 70,000, while China lowers canola-seed tariffs from 84% to about 15% and eases other barriers. The shift hedges against an uncertain USMCA and US tariffs, but divides farmers benefiting from relief and an auto sector fearing cheap imports and job losses. HN discussion likewise frames diversification as pragmatic yet risky, warning that dependence on either neighboring America or Chinese strategic industries constrains Canadian autonomy.
