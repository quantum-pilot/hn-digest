# An Elizabethan mansion's secrets for staying warm

- Score: 100 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46659550) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20260116-an-elizabethan-mansions-secrets-for-staying-warm

### TL;DR

Hardwick Hall, built during the Little Ice Age, appears to combine passive-heating strategies: north–south orientation, rooms arranged around the Sun, fake north windows, central fireplaces, 1.37m masonry walls, tapestries, and curtains. Modern measurements suggest it can be roughly 10°C warmer inside on cold days, inspiring climate-aware layouts and small behavioral changes. HN commenters broaden the lesson to masonry heaters, airtight insulation, zoning, and cross-ventilation, while criticizing the article’s estimates and warning that open fireplaces and open plans can waste more heat than they provide.
