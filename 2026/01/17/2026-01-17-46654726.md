# Ask HN: Is it still worth pursuing a software startup?

- Score: 151 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46654726) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46654726

### TL;DR

Software code may be easier to reproduce, but HN respondents argue startups still win through speed, focus, distribution, brand, customer trust, integrations, data, and domain expertise. Large companies often move slowly because coordination and incentives dominate coding time; faster copycat mills may be the nearer threat. The viable play is not another easily generated app, but a painful, specific workflow whose users will pay. Skeptics question whether expected returns beat salaried work and criticize VC-backed founders whose real objective is an exit rather than customers.

### Comment pulse

- Big-company imitation is overrated → bureaucracy slows shipping; small founders can instead copy neglected corporate products.
- Moats moved beyond features → niche knowledge, distribution, trust, integrations, and retained data compound after launch.
