# The 'untouchable hacker god' behind Finland's biggest crime

- Score: 141 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46656045) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jan/17/vastaamo-hack-finland-therapy-notes

### TL;DR

Finland’s Vastaamo breach exposed therapy records for 33,000 patients after an internet-accessible database had no firewall and a blank password. The attacker first extorted the clinic, then patients, while the full database was already public; lawyers linked at least two suicides to the breach. Investigators tied Aleksanteri Kivimäki to the crime through an accidentally uploaded home directory, bitcoin tracing, servers, searches, and an IP address. Convicted in 2024 on thousands of privacy and extortion charges, he denies responsibility and is appealing. HN debated victim privacy, attribution folklore, and commenter-identity speculation.
