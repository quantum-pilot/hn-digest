# ClickHouse acquires Langfuse

- Score: 193 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46656552) | Link: https://langfuse.com/blog/joining-clickhouse

### TL;DR

ClickHouse acquired Langfuse, whose team says its open-source license, self-hosting, cloud endpoints, contracts, support, and roadmap will remain unchanged. Langfuse already moved its high-volume tracing and analytics backend from Postgres to ClickHouse in v3; the deal adds engineering, compliance, reliability, and customer-support capacity while funding deeper production monitoring, evaluation, and experimentation workflows. HN sees a logical fit with ClickHouse’s observability expansion, but questions whether its broader acquisition spree and newly emphasized agent narrative reflect product strategy, investor fashion, or both.
