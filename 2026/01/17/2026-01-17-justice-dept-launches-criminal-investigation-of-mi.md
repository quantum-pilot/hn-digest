# Justice Dept. launches criminal investigation of Minnesota governor

- Score: 107 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46654721) | Link: https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/01/16/trump-minnesota-walz-frey-criminal-investigation/

### TL;DR

The Justice Department subpoenaed Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey, investigating whether their public criticism of a federally ordered immigration-enforcement surge constituted conspiracy to impede federal officers. The unprecedented escalation follows an immigration officer’s fatal shooting of a woman, state claims of exclusion from that inquiry, and Minnesota’s lawsuit calling the deployment unconstitutional. HN commenters largely viewed the case as political intimidation and a free-speech threat, while describing fear among Twin Cities residents and concern that national division is suppressing accountability.
