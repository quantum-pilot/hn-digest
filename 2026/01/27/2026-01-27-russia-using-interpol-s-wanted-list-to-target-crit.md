# Russia using Interpol's wanted list to target critics abroad, leak reveals

- Score: 210 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46776454) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c20gg729y1yo

### TL;DR

Leaked Interpol files reviewed by the BBC and Disclose indicate Russia repeatedly used red notices, diffusions, and informal messages to pursue critics, journalists, businesspeople, and foreign officials. Over a decade, at least 700 Russia-targeted people complained and at least 400 requests were overturned; Russia drew three times as many complaints as Turkey. Extra post-invasion screening still passed about 90% of Russian requests initially in 2024, and some controls were reportedly relaxed in 2025. Interpol disputes factual claims and says strengthened safeguards enforce its ban on political policing.
