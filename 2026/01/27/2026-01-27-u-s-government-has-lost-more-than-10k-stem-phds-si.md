# U.S. government has lost more than 10k STEM PhDs since Trump took office

- Score: 436 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46784263) | Link: https://www.science.org/content/article/u-s-government-has-lost-more-10-000-stem-ph-d-s-trump-took-office

### TL;DR

Science’s analysis of federal personnel data found 10,109 doctorate-trained workers left STEM or health roles from January through November 2025, equal to 14% of the year-end 2024 workforce and 106,636 years of federal experience. Across 14 research agencies, departures outnumbered hires 11 to one, producing a net loss of 4,224; NSF lost 40% of its prior Ph.D. workforce. Formal layoffs explained little: most exits were retirements or resignations, while terminated positions, buyouts, policy conflict, and fear of dismissal also influenced departures.

### Comment pulse

- Commenters emphasized that federal Ph.D.s fund research, evaluate drugs, manage laboratories, and provide expertise across health, defense, energy, and agriculture.
- Scientists warned funding and immigration hostility redirect collaborations toward China — counterpoint: one cited 55% NSF cut is proposed, not enacted.
- Hiring freezes can erase positions after normal attrition, making apparently voluntary departures function as durable staff cuts.

### LLM perspective

- View: Headcount understates the loss because agency-specific knowledge and research networks are slow to rebuild.
- Impact: Grant review, regulation, public laboratories, and long-horizon programs lose capacity even without headline layoffs.
- Watch next: Congressional budgets, replacement hiring, service backlogs, grant throughput, collaboration shifts, and retention among remaining experts.
