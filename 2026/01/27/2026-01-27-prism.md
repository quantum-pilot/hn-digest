# Prism

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46783752) | Link: https://openai.com/index/introducing-prism

### TL;DR

OpenAI launched a free cloud workspace for scientific writing, built from acquired LaTeX platform Crixet and powered by GPT‑5.2. It combines compilation, real-time collaboration, literature search, citations, equations, figures, voice edits, and in-place AI revisions with full-document context. Personal ChatGPT accounts receive unlimited projects and collaborators, with organizational access planned and stronger AI features eventually tied to paid plans. HN discussion compared it with Overleaf and focused less on drafting speed than on the verification burden from cheap, plausible-looking submissions.

### Comment pulse

- Overleaf already offers AI assistance, so commenters expect competition to hinge on brand, pricing, limits, and self-hostability.
- Former Crixet users valued generous compilation and sharing, but worried AI cannot be disabled and free hosting may not last.
- Editors warned easier paper generation shifts work onto unpaid reviewers — counterpoint: submission deposits might deter low-effort or hallucinated manuscripts.
