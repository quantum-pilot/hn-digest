# 430k-year-old well-preserved wooden tools are the oldest ever found

- Score: 325 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46781530) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/26/science/archaeology-neanderthals-tools.html

### TL;DR

Researchers report two unusually well-preserved early hominin tools: worked alder and poplar or willow pieces from Greece dated about 430,000 years ago, and a 500,000-year-old elephant or mammoth bone hammer from England used to knap flint. Likely makers were early Neanderthals or Homo heidelbergensis, showing varied, skilled material use long before Homo sapiens settled Europe. Commenters stressed that tools themselves are millions of years older; the breakthrough is organic preservation, while submerged landscapes and museum collections may hold further evidence.
