# Amazon to shut down Go and Fresh stores

- Score: 229 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46781707) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2026/01/27/food/amazon-fresh-go-closures

### TL;DR

Amazon will shut about 70 Amazon Fresh and Go stores after concluding that the formats lacked both a distinctive customer experience and economics suitable for large-scale expansion. Some locations will convert to Whole Foods, whose footprint is planned to grow by 100 stores, including smaller Daily Shops. Amazon is also testing grocery hybrids and a 229,000-square-foot big-box format while retaining delivery, private-label, and partner-store efforts. The supplied HN comments contain only duplicate-post notices, not substantive reactions.

### LLM perspective

- View: This is brand consolidation around Whole Foods, not an exit from Amazon’s broader grocery business.
- Impact: Store employees and local shoppers absorb closures while capital moves toward conversions, delivery, and tested formats.
- Watch next: Monitor conversion counts, planned Whole Foods openings, workforce placement, and performance of Daily Shop and hybrid stores.
