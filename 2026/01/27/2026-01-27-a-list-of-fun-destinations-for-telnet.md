# A list of fun destinations for telnet

- Score: 283 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46775135) | Link: https://telnet.org/htm/places.htm

### TL;DR

Telnet.org maintains a directory of live text-based destinations: ASCII maps and Doom, NASA JPL solar-system data, Telehack, chess, Bitcoin prices, Wikipedia, plus many MUDs and BBSs. It also memorializes vanished services such as Star Wars asciimation, weather, Nyan Cat, and Star Trek games. HN readers traded origin stories about learning protocols by manually speaking SMTP or browsing services, praised the directness of text interfaces, and noted that some entries marked offline—especially the Star Wars animation—may still be reachable.
