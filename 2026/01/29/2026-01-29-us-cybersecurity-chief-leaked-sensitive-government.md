# US cybersecurity chief leaked sensitive government files to ChatGPT: Report

- Score: 376 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46812173) | Link: https://www.dexerto.com/entertainment/us-cybersecurity-chief-leaked-sensitive-government-files-to-chatgpt-report-3311462/

### TL;DR

A secondary report says acting CISA director Madhu Gottumukkala uploaded contracting documents marked For Official Use Only to a public ChatGPT instance last summer. Other Homeland Security employees were blocked from the service, but he had obtained a special exemption. Monitoring reportedly flagged the uploads in early August, prompting a DHS damage assessment of possible exposure beyond government networks. CISA says his access was authorized, short-term, limited, and protected by DHS controls; the supplied account does not establish what was exposed or the review’s outcome.
