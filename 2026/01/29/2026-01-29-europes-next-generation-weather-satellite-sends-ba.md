# Europe’s next-generation weather satellite sends back first images

- Score: 646 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46806773) | Link: https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/Meteorological_missions/meteosat_third_generation/Europe_s_next-generation_weather_satellite_sends_back_first_images

### TL;DR

ESA released the first images from MTG-Sounder’s Infrared Sounder, captured November 15, 2025, from geostationary orbit. Its 1,700 infrared channels are designed to measure temperature, humidity, winds, and trace gases, eventually enabling three-dimensional atmospheric maps over Europe and northern Africa. The satellite covers the region every 15 minutes and supplies temperature and humidity data every 30 minutes, complementing MTG-Imager cloud and lightning data. HN readers welcome better nowcasting but ask how much forecasts improve and whether timely data will be openly accessible.
