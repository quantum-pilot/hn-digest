# TÜV Report 2026: Tesla Model Y has the worst reliability of all 2022–2023 cars (2025)

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46809105) | Link: https://www.autoevolution.com/news/tuev-report-2026-tesla-model-y-has-the-worst-reliability-among-all-20222023-cars-261596.html

### TL;DR

A summary of Germany’s 2026 TÜV inspection data says 17.3% of two-to-three-year-old Model Ys had significant defects, ranking last among 110 models and marking that age group’s highest rate in a decade; the Model 3 reached 13.1%. The dataset covers 9.5 million inspections, but failure is only a rough reliability proxy because mileage, maintenance, and safety items vary. Most electric vehicles matched combustion cars, with the Mini Cooper SE leading EVs in this age group at 3.5%. The summary omits the Model Y’s defect breakdown.

### Comment pulse

- Danish and Irish figures cited by commenters also show high Tesla failures, especially in brakes, wheels, steering, and suspension.
- Critics caution that inspection failures include maintenance and safety items, while pre-inspection practices can make cross-model rates misleading.
- Regenerative braking and battery weight may stress discs and suspension, but commenters want model-specific defect data before assigning causes.
