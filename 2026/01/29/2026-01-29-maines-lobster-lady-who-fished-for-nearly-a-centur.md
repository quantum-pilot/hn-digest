# Maine’s ‘Lobster Lady’ who fished for nearly a century dies aged 105

- Score: 228 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46804854) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jan/28/maine-lobster-lady-dies-aged-105

### TL;DR

Virginia Ginny Oliver, Maine’s Lobster Lady, died January 21 at 105 after a 97-year lobstering career begun at age eight. She worked before dawn, later served as sternman on her son Max’s boat, and continued until a fall at 103. Documentaries, children’s books, public honors, and her everyday lipstick and earrings made her a maritime icon, though family described her as humble and spirited. HN readers focused on purpose, longevity, grief, and whether late-life work reflects choice or economic necessity.
