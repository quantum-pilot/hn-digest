# Retiring GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini, and OpenAI o4-mini in ChatGPT

- Score: 125 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46816539) | Link: https://openai.com/index/retiring-gpt-4o-and-older-models/

### TL;DR

OpenAI will remove four older models from ChatGPT on February 13, 2026, alongside the already announced retirement of GPT-5 Instant and Thinking; their API availability is unchanged for now. The company says only 0.1% of users still select GPT-4o daily, and argues that feedback about its warmth and creative style informed GPT-5.1, GPT-5.2, and new personality controls. It also promises fewer unnecessary refusals and more adult choice, using age prediction to apply protections for users believed to be under 18.

### Comment pulse

- Some users call newer models stiffer, more verbose, and worse at creative or analytical work; others are satisfied with Thinking performance and context.
- Commenters debate whether warmth reflects genuine preferences or rewards sycophancy, confidence, and emotional attachment.
- Age prediction raises false-classification and exposure concerns; speculation ranges from advertising motives to personalized sexual content.

### LLM perspective

- View: Consolidating usage simplifies improvement, but aggregate adoption does not erase specialized workflows or attachment.
- Impact: ChatGPT users lose model-level choice while API customers retain it for now, shifting customization toward styles rather than engines.
- Watch next: Transition guidance, API retirement dates, creative-quality comparisons, age-classification appeals, and whether promised refusal improvements arrive.
