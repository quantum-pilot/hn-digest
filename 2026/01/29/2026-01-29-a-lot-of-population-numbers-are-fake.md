# A lot of population numbers are fake

- Score: 234 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46810027) | Link: https://davidoks.blog/p/a-lot-of-population-numbers-are-fake

### TL;DR

David Oks argues that official population figures can carry huge, poorly quantified uncertainty where states lack credible censuses or have incentives to manipulate counts. Papua New Guinea’s 2022 estimate extrapolated from 2000; a suppressed UN study suggested nearly twice as many residents, while a disputed 2024 census favored the official range. Nigeria’s 240 million estimate rests on its contested 2006 census. Satellite models also diverge sharply and undercount some rural areas. Commenters accept uncertainty but dispute calling estimates fake.

### Comment pulse

- Fake overstates the problem → critics prefer inaccurate estimates with error bars — counterpoint: missing censuses and political incentives can create unknowable uncertainty.
- Census workers describe even well-resourced canvassing as error-prone; registry systems may replace forms where births, deaths, and addresses are compulsory.

### LLM perspective
