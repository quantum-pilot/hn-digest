# Tao Te Ching – Translated by Ursula K. Le Guin

- Score: 162 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46745233) | Link: https://github.com/nrrb/tao-te-ching/blob/master/Ursula%20K%20Le%20Guin.md

### TL;DR

Le Guin’s version presents all 81 poems in spare contemporary English, followed by notes explaining interpretive choices. Across recurring images of water, valleys, uncut wood, infants, and emptiness, it treats the Way as unnamed and inexhaustible; wisdom appears as yielding, wanting less, acting without domination, distrusting war, and completing work without claiming it. Her commentary connects those paradoxes to politics, ecology, teaching, and embodied life. She explicitly calls the work a rendition, not a translation: knowing no Chinese, she built it through Paul Carus’s character-by-character 1898 edition.
