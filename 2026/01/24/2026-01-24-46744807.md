# Ask HN: Gmail spam filtering suddenly marking everything as spam?

- Score: 113 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46744807) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46744807

### TL;DR

Users reported a Gmail disruption that misclassified ordinary mail in several ways: transactional and school messages landed in spam, routine senders displayed unscanned or possibly unsafe warnings, and Promotions or Updates appeared in Primary. Messages with valid SPF and DKIM or prior whitelisting were affected, as was official USPS correspondence. Commenters linked a Google status incident and distinguished unavailable scanning from an actual spam verdict. The episode exposed how much users rely on invisible classification, while one unverified phishing anecdote raised separate spoofing concerns.

### Comment pulse

- The failure was broader than spam placement → users saw category routing, importance labels, and safety scanning malfunction in opposite directions.
- An unscanned warning can be prudent → Gmail cannot vouch for unchecked mail — counterpoint: legitimate correspondence becomes alarming and harder to find.
- Filtering earns trust through invisibility → several users appreciated the system only after manually searching spam for expected messages.

### LLM perspective

- View: Authentication alone cannot replace content scanning and mailbox classification.
- Impact: Senders lose deliverability while recipients risk missing school, postal, and transactional messages.
- Watch next: Google incident resolution, reprocessing of affected mail, false-positive rates, and confirmation that scanner availability has normalized.
