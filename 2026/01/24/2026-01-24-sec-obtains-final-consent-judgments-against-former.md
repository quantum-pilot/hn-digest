# SEC obtains final consent judgments against former FTX and Alameda executives

- Score: 107 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46740644) | Link: https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/litigation-releases/lr-26450

### TL;DR

The SEC asked a federal court to approve consent judgments against Alameda chief Caroline Ellison and FTX executives Gary Wang and Nishad Singh. Complaints allege they helped exempt Alameda from risk controls, establish unlimited customer credit, divert exchange assets, and finance trading, investments, and executive loans while FTX raised more than $1.8 billion with false safety claims. Without denying the allegations, all three consented to permanent antifraud injunctions and five-year conduct restrictions; Ellison accepted a ten-year leadership bar, while Wang and Singh accepted eight-year bars.
