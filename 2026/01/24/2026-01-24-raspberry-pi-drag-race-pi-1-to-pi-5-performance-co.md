# Raspberry Pi Drag Race: Pi 1 to Pi 5 – Performance Comparison

- Score: 110 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46745922) | Link: https://the-diy-life.com/raspberry-pi-drag-race-pi-1-to-pi-5-performance-comparison/

### TL;DR

Tests across five generations using Raspberry Pi OS, identical microSD storage, and active cooling show gains. The original could not open a browser and needed roughly eight minutes to reach its desktop; the fifth plays 1080p smoothly, approaches gigabit networking, and scores over 600 times the first model when comparing multicore against single-core Sysbench. It draws nearly triple the load power, yet delivers almost 200 times more performance per watt. Those gains accompany a $50 base price, specialized 5V/5A supply, PCIe expansion, and a dedicated fan socket.
