# Comma openpilot – Open source driver-assistance

- Score: 332 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46740029) | Link: https://comma.ai

### TL;DR

Comma markets its fourth-generation retrofit as active driver assistance offering lane centering, adaptive cruise, lane changes, dashcam recording, over-the-air updates, and 360-degree vision. It supports more than 325 models from 27 brands; the company reports 300 million driven miles and 20,000 users. Owners praised smoother lane tracking, driver monitoring, and upgrading older cars without subscriptions or replacement. Skeptics asked why capable factory systems need replacement and raised installation, insurance, attention, and quality-assurance concerns. The driver remains responsible; this is assistance, not transferred control.
