# BirdyChat becomes first European chat app that is interoperable with WhatsApp

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46746476) | Link: https://www.birdy.chat/blog/first-to-interoperate-with-whatsapp

### TL;DR

BirdyChat says its official Digital Markets Act interface will let EEA users exchange encrypted one-to-one messages, photos, and files with WhatsApp contacts while identifying through work email. Access is invite-only and rolling out gradually; both parties must be in the EEA, WhatsApp users must opt into third-party chats, and groups are not yet supported. Commenters said those constraints sharply limit reach, although persuading someone to toggle a setting may be easier than installing another app. Others questioned mixing work with personal messaging.

### Comment pulse

- Interoperability lowers one adoption barrier → BirdyChat users can contact existing WhatsApp users without making them install a second application.
- Meta’s opt-in weakens universality → contacts remain unreachable by default — counterpoint: enabling a setting is still easier than migrating platforms.
- Regional and group limits constrain real networks → cross-continent families and existing WhatsApp groups cannot benefit from the initial release.
