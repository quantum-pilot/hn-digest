# I Like GitLab

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46742432) | Link: https://www.whileforloop.com/en/blog/2026/01/21/i-like-gitlab/

### TL;DR

GitLab became the author’s private-project home when it offered free private repositories before GitHub, and inertia remains useful: about a dozen projects share integrated CI, container storage, authentication, and deployment tooling at no cost. Versioned pipeline files, manual production triggers, shared runners, and easy self-hosted runners cover the workflow; the per-project registry avoids another account and Docker Hub limits. The tradeoff is a persistently sluggish, crowded interface whose project-management, package, security, and infrastructure features mostly go unused. GitHub remains the public showcase.
