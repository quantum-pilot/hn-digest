# Man shot and killed by federal agents in south Minneapolis this morning

- Score: 414 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46745047) | Link: https://www.startribune.com/ice-raids-minnesota/601546426

### TL;DR

Federal agents killed Alex Jeffrey Pretti, 37, during a January 24 immigration operation in south Minneapolis, prompting confrontations and an evening vigil. It was the city’s second fatal shooting involving federal agents that month. Homeland Security said Pretti carried a gun and two ammunition magazines, and that a Border Patrol agent fired in self-defense. The Star Tribune said video shows several agents wrestling Pretti to the ground before shooting him repeatedly. Minnesota’s Bureau of Criminal Apprehension said it was denied access to the scene, hindering an independent state investigation.
