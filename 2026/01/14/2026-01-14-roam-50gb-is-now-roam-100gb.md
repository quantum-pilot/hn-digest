# Roam 50GB is now Roam 100GB

- Score: 234 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46617668) | Link: https://starlink.com/support/article/58c9c8b7-474e-246f-7e3c-06db3221d34d

### TL;DR

Starlink doubled Roam’s monthly high-speed allowance from 50GB to 100GB at no extra cost in most markets. After the cap, service continues with unlimited reduced-speed data for basic email, calls, and texts; restoring fast access requires a recurring Roam Unlimited upgrade. Extra per-GB purchases disappear outside Ocean Mode, whose maritime limits remain. HN strongly preferred throttling over disconnection, but disputed whether roughly 500Kbps serves today’s web or invites abuse. Several commenters saw looming satellite and terrestrial competition behind the upgrade.

### Comment pulse

- Soft caps preserve essential connectivity → many considered 500Kbps useful where the alternative is zero — counterpoint: modern sites can become practically unusable.
- Uniform throttling can reward extreme consumption → rolling limits across seconds, minutes, and days might preserve bursts while restraining sustained transfers.
- Starlink still faces market pressure → mobile and fixed broadband already compete locally, while new satellite constellations threaten its portable-internet lead.
