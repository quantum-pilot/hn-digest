# Ask HN: Share your personal website

- Score: 355 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46618714) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46618714

### TL;DR

A community project is assembling a maintained directory of personal websites, broadly defined to include blogs, digital gardens, wikis, portfolios, and other owner-controlled spaces. Submissions may be added when creators control the design and content and the site has received positive HN attention; contributors are invited to help review the large queue through pull requests. The thread became a showcase of browser-based desktops, garden tools, playful business cards, blog indexes, and lifelong project timelines, with readers offering detailed design feedback and proposing automation for scalable maintenance.
