# ASCII Clouds

- Score: 329 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46611507) | Link: https://caidan.dev/portfolio/ascii_clouds/

### TL;DR

An interactive cloud visualizer lets users randomize or save presets, tune noise, wave, vignette, color, timing, and glyph thresholds, then export images or video. HN readers admired its organic look and connected the effect to Perlin or Simplex noise and GPU post-processing techniques useful for terrain, water, and other procedural visuals. Debate centered on the ASCII label: the author uses GPU-drawn, signed-distance glyphs and variable color for speed and aesthetics, while purists wanted authentic character geometry, fixed intensity mapping, and selectable text.
