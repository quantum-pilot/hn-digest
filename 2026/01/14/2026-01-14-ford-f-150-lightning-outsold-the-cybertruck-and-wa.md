# Ford F-150 Lightning outsold the Cybertruck and was then canceled for poor sales

- Score: 377 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46618901) | Link: https://electrek.co/2026/01/13/ford-f150-lightning-outsold-tesla-cybertruck-canceled-not-selling-enough/

### TL;DR

Electrek estimates Tesla sold about 21,500 Cybertrucks globally in 2025, versus roughly 27,300 US deliveries for Ford’s F-150 Lightning, even though Ford ended Lightning production after an 18% decline. Tesla’s bundled reporting makes the Cybertruck figure indirect; the article says output is near 10% of planned capacity and notes SpaceX bought over 1,000 units last quarter. HN debated whether politics damaged a uniquely Musk-linked vehicle or whether price, range, recalls, utility, dealer incentives, and a narrow luxury-EV-truck market better explain both products’ struggles.
