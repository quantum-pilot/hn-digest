# I stopped following the news

- Score: 123 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46792580) | Link: https://mertbulan.com/2026/01/28/why-i-stopped-following-the-news/

### TL;DR

Mert Bulan stopped consuming daily world and technology news after concluding it increased stress without materially helping his goals. He retained a short weekday Hamburg newsletter and may add a slower, paid magazine covering consequential events. Replacing the news habit with books coincided with finishing seven in January after 20 during all of the previous year, and he reports feeling better. HN readers favor weekly or long-form filters, but sharply debate whether disengagement weakens democratic participation or protects attention from sensationalism.

### Comment pulse

- Low-frequency, long-form news lets stories mature → less speculation, fewer overlooked corrections, and less sensational presentation.
- Mental-health gains are tangible → some readers felt more present and productive after stopping war coverage.
- Civic withdrawal is contested → democracy needs informed citizens — counterpoint: daily feeds can produce shallow, manipulated awareness rather than understanding.
