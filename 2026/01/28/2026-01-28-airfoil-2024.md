# Airfoil (2024)

- Score: 333 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46795908) | Link: https://ciechanow.ski/airfoil/

### TL;DR

An interactive essay builds airfoil physics from molecular motion upward. Bulk airflow is the average velocity of chaotically moving particles; pressure gradients redirect that flow and exert surface forces whose perpendicular component is lift. Angle of attack or asymmetric shaping creates an uneven pressure field, while excessive angle produces boundary-layer separation and stall. Viscosity diffuses momentum, creates skin-friction drag, and helps determine laminar, oscillatory, or turbulent behavior through Reynolds number. Commenters overwhelmingly praise the visual pedagogy and recommend AeroSandbox for experimentation.
