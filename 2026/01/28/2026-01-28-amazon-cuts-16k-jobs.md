# Amazon cuts 16k jobs

- Score: 464 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46796745) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/amazon-cuts-16000-jobs-globally-broader-restructuring-2026-01-28/

### TL;DR

Amazon confirmed 16,000 corporate job cuts, bringing reductions since October to roughly 30,000—nearly 10% of its corporate workforce and its largest layoff program. CEO Andy Jassy has sought fewer layers and the closure of underperforming businesses; Amazon also cited pandemic overhiring and previously connected AI adoption with reduced corporate staffing. A mistaken Project Dawn email alerted some AWS staff early. Affected areas reportedly include AWS, Alexa, Prime Video, devices, advertising, Kindle, delivery, and supply-chain optimization. HN debate centers on automation, outsourcing, and restructuring.
