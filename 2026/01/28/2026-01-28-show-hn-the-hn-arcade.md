# Show HN: The HN Arcade

- Score: 294 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46793693) | Link: https://andrewgy8.github.io/hnarcade/

### TL;DR

The HN Arcade is a discovery site for indie games surfaced on Hacker News. Its landing page offers a browsable games section, a submission path, newsletter signup, and a “How It Works” section, with the stated aim of collecting the week’s best HN finds. Rather than presenting a single game, it packages community projects into an ongoing catalogue. The available page is intentionally sparse: it names AGY as the builder but provides no visible selection criteria, game details, or technical explanation in the captured content.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- View: The useful product is curation, preserving small games beyond one fleeting front-page cycle.
- Impact: A focused archive could compound discovery for makers while giving players a low-friction route through community-built games.
