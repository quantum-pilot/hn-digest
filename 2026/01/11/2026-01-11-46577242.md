### TL;DR

This open work thread showcases projects spanning foundational infrastructure, playful hardware, developer tooling, biotechnology, and regional community building. Contributors described modernizing Linux printing and scanning ahead of CUPS 3, identifying backyard birds with a camera, using a Lua FaaS to eliminate side-project boilerplate, engineering flavored yeasts, and building a Europe-focused technology forum with jobs.
