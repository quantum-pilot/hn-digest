# A battle over Canada’s mystery brain disease

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46572769) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c623r47d67lo

### TL;DR

New Brunswick neurologist Alier Marrero grouped about 500 patients with varied symptoms under a proposed mystery syndrome. A 2025 JAMA study of 25 cases found known conditions including functional neurological disorder, dementia, and cancer, and blamed serial misdiagnosis. Patients and advocates dispute its methods and suspect environmental toxins or government obstruction, while some remain untreated or consider assisted dying. HN debate mirrored the split: local commenters emphasized Irving influence and lost trust; skeptics centered Marrero as the common factor, and others stressed uncertainty around FND and prion disease.
