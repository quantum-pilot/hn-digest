# Firefox extension to redirect x.com to xcancel.com

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46524873) | Link: https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/toxcancel/

### TL;DR

ToXCancel is a small Firefox extension that intercepts visits to x.com or twitter.com and redirects them to xcancel.com, allowing users to read posts, threads, and replies without an X account. Version 1.3 is also available on Firefox for Android, is licensed under GPLv3, and requests optional access only to the two relevant domains. Its narrow purpose favors minimal permissions and setup, though it addresses just one service and depends on the continued availability and behavior of the chosen mirror.
