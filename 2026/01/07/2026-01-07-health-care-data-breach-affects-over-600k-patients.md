# Health care data breach affects over 600k patients, Illinois agency says

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46528353) | Link: https://www.nprillinois.org/illinois/2026-01-06/health-care-data-breach-affects-600-000-patients-illinois-agency-says

### TL;DR

Illinois officials said maps used for planning exposed sensitive customer records because their privacy settings were wrong. More than 32,000 rehabilitation-services customers had names, addresses, case details, referral information, and recipient status publicly viewable from April 2021 to September 2025. Roughly 670,000 Medicaid and Medicare Savings Program recipients had addresses, case numbers, demographics, and plan names exposed from January 2022 through September 2025. IDHS discovered the problem September 22, restricted access, prohibited customer data on public mapping sites, and said it knew of no misuse.
