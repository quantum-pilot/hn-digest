# GPTZero finds 100 new hallucinations in NeurIPS 2025 accepted papers

- Score: 652 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46720395) | Link: https://gptzero.me/news/neurips/

### TL;DR

GPTZero says its scan of 4,841 accepted NeurIPS 2025 papers found 100 human-confirmed citation hallucinations, including invented authors, titles, venues, identifiers, and hybrids of real references. It links the problem to submission growth that has outpaced reviewer capacity, while stressing that an unmatched source is only a flag until a person verifies it. Commenters agreed citation checking is weak, but disputed whether minor background errors justify broad doubts, strict penalties, or the article’s product-driven framing.
