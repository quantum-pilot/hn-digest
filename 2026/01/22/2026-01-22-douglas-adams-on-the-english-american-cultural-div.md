# Douglas Adams on the English–American cultural divide over "heroes"

- Score: 303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46719222) | Link: https://shreevatsa.net/post/douglas-adams-cultural-divide/

### TL;DR

Douglas Adams framed Arthur Dent’s passivity as culturally legible heroism: English stories often embrace people overwhelmed by events, whereas American storytelling expects protagonists to pursue goals and shape outcomes. Complaining articulately, enduring disaster, and returning to tea can therefore create kinship rather than signal failure. The discussion tested this contrast against Broadchurch, Charlie Brown, Discworld, and television adaptations, with some treating it as a genuine humor divide and others as an overbroad or historically recent pattern.

### Comment pulse
