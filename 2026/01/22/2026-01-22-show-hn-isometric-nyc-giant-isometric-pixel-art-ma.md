# Show HN: isometric.nyc – giant isometric pixel art map of NYC

- Score: 566 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46721802) | Link: https://cannoneyed.com/isometric-nyc/

### TL;DR

The creator combined Google Maps 3D tiles, agent-written GIS tooling, Nano Banana examples, and a roughly 40-pair Qwen/Image-Edit fine-tune to build an isometric New York map. Masked infill and 512×512 quadrants helped avoid seams; rented H100s produced more than 200 generations hourly below $3, yet water, trees, planning, QA, and the viewer still demanded expert intervention and manual correction. HN admired the map's clarity and ambition but disputed the “pixel art” label, visible AI artifacts, and whether automating craft preserves creative decision-making.

### Comment pulse

- Visual result → fans found the simplified city unusually legible; critics saw inconsistent continuity and a generated filter rather than pixel art.
- Workflow curiosity → readers focused on how adjacent generated tiles provide boundary conditions for masked infill and stylistic continuity.
- Creative debate → some celebrated removed drudgery; others argued tedious execution is creative and generation replaces artists' decisions with model output.
