# Doctors in Brazil using tilapia fish skin to treat burn victims (2017)

- Score: 259 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46715600) | Link: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/brazilian-city-uses-tilapia-fish-skin-treat-burn-victims

### TL;DR

Brazilian trials used sterilized tilapia skin as a biological dressing where three skin banks met only 1 percent of national demand. Rich in collagen, moisture, and tensile strength, the material can remain on burns longer than gauze with silver cream, reducing painful dressing changes, medication use, and healing time. Researchers developed a sterilization and irradiation process with two-year storage while evaluating costs and tissue composition. Commenters noted related commercial, veterinary, and alternative wound-care techniques.

### Comment pulse

- Tilapia offers practical supply advantages → it is cheap, farmed at scale, hardy, and normally discarded after processing.
- Clinicians cited polypropylene, honey, and placental materials → wound coverage has several context-dependent pathways beyond fish skin.
