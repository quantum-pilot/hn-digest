# From stealth blackout to whitelisting: Inside the Iranian shutdown

- Score: 153 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46713444) | Link: https://www.kentik.com/blog/from-stealth-blackout-to-whitelisting-inside-the-iranian-shutdown/

### TL;DR

Traffic data depicts a nearly fourteen-day Iranian communications shutdown affecting roughly 90 million people. On January 8, IPv6 routes disappeared, then traffic collapsed while 98.14 percent of IPv4 space remained globally routed, indicating filtering at the network edge rather than simple disconnection. Small restorations and daily traffic patterns suggest selective access through an evolving whitelist backed by Iran’s domestic network. Commenters focused on circumvention, scarce on-the-ground reporting, and the possibility that other governments will copy selective shutdown tactics.
