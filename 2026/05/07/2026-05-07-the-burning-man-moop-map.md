# The Burning Man MOOP Map

- Score: 510 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48049653) | Link: https://www.not-ship.com/burning-man-moop/

### TL;DR

After Burning Man’s 70,000 participants leave, roughly 150 restoration workers sweep nearly 3,900 acres for “matter out of place,” logging debris and mapping cleanup difficulty. The event must pass 120 federal spot checks, with no more than 12 exceeding one square foot of debris per acre; 2023 came close at 11. In 2025, easily buried lag bolts dominated finds, yet long-term debris per attendee continued improving. HN contributors described photo-and-pixel verification and brutal storm cleanup, crediting measurable accountability while noting trash displaced into nearby Reno remains outside the map.
