# Chrome removes claim of On-device Al not sending data to Google Servers

- Score: 429 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48050964) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/chrome/comments/1t5qayz/chrome_removes_claim_of_ondevice_al_not_sending/

### TL;DR

The supplied page was blocked, leaving only the headline and discussion: Chrome apparently removed wording that its on-device AI does not send data to Google servers. Many commenters treated the edit as evidence of expanding collection through browser-integrated AI, with particular concern that ordinary users would never realize their data left the device. Others said a wording change proves nothing and demanded the linked documentation or direct evidence. The practical worry was corporate compliance: undisclosed transmission of customer data could force organizations to restrict Chrome.
