# Grand Theft Oil Futures: Insider traders keep making a killing at our expense

- Score: 476 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48047981) | Link: https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/grand-theft-oil-futures

### TL;DR

Paul Krugman argues that repeated, unusually timed oil-futures trades before Trump administration announcements about Iran suggest insiders are profiting from advance information. One cited $920 million short position appeared 70 minutes before ceasefire reporting and gained roughly $125 million as oil fell 12%. Beyond transferring wealth, suspected rigging can deter producers, airlines, and other hedgers, weakening markets’ risk-reduction function. HN discussion emphasized war’s human cost and weak enforcement, while noting a criminal probe is underway and that futures serve legitimate commodity users.
