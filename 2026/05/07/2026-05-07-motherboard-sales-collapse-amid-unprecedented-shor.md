# Motherboard sales 'collapse' amid unprecedented shortages fueled by AI

- Score: 239 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48050540) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/motherboards/motherboard-sales-collapse-by-more-than-25-percent-as-chipmakers-strangle-enthusiast-pc-market-to-build-more-ai-chips-asus-projected-to-sell-5-million-fewer-boards-in-2025-gigabyte-msi-and-asrock-also-expected-to-see-reduced-sales-numbers

### TL;DR

Motherboard shipments from Asus, Gigabyte, MSI, and ASRock are projected to fall 28% in 2026, led by ASRock’s 37% decline. The article attributes delayed builds to AI-driven competition for memory, storage, and processors, alongside long-lived AMD sockets, no Nvidia refresh, and Intel’s next platform arriving later. Vendors are cushioning the decline through AI-server work. HN builders reported repairing 15-year-old machines and buying used systems, while disputing whether motherboard prices themselves rose because of AI and warning software developers that baseline consumer specifications may stagnate.
