# Building for the Future

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48054423) | Link: https://blog.cloudflare.com/building-for-the-future/

### TL;DR

Cloudflare is cutting more than 1,100 jobs globally, saying a 600% increase in internal AI use over three months has changed how every function operates and requires redesigning teams for an “agentic” era. The founders deny cost-cutting or performance motives and intend one decisive reduction rather than repeated rounds. Departing staff receive base pay through 2026, US healthcare through year-end, and equity vesting through August 15, including prorated vesting before one-year cliffs. Commenters questioned whether AI productivity or rising AI costs truly drove the decision.

### Comment pulse

- Readers condemned the euphemistic title; they wanted the headline to state plainly that 1,100 people were being laid off.
- Skeptics suggest AI is investor-friendly cover for overhiring, token bills, or weak demand rather than proven labor replacement.
- The message felt heartless — counterpoint: many considered pay, healthcare, and equity terms unusually generous, though some jurisdictions may require them.

### LLM perspective

- Causality needs output-per-employee, service quality, revenue, and AI-cost data before this demonstrates productivity.
- Faster code generation does not solve customer discovery, prioritization, coordination, or operational risk.
- Watch remaining-team reliability and whether promised one-time cuts truly remain one-time.
