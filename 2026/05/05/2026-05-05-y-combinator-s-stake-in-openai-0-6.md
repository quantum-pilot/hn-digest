# Y Combinator's Stake in OpenAI (0.6%?)

- Score: 370 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48016534) | Link: https://daringfireball.net/2026/05/y_combinators_stake_in_openai

### TL;DR

John Gruber argues that Y Combinator’s financial interest in OpenAI should accompany Paul Graham’s public comments about Sam Altman’s trustworthiness. An unnamed source estimates YC owns roughly 0.6%; at OpenAI’s stated $852 billion valuation, that would exceed $5 billion. Because Altman owns part of YC, claims that he has no OpenAI equity may omit an indirect interest. HN commenters debated why the stake is so small—repeated funding rounds imply dilution—and whether undisclosed incentives color leadership claims, AGI hype, or HN’s heavy AI coverage.
