# iOS 27 is adding a 'Create a Pass' button to Apple Wallet

- Score: 372 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48021561) | Link: https://walletwallet.alen.ro/blog/ios-27-wallet-create-pass/

### TL;DR

Reportedly coming in iOS 27, Apple Wallet’s new Create a Pass flow would let users scan a QR code or build a custom card without a developer account, Pass Type ID, or signing certificate. Three templates—standard, membership, and event—offer editable colors, images, and text, addressing the long tail of gyms, libraries, cafes, and local venues that never adopted PassKit. HN welcomed replacing photo-based barcode workarounds but blamed Apple’s developer-heavy model for 14 years of weak adoption and noted third-party iPhone apps and Google Wallet already provide similar functionality.

### Comment pulse
