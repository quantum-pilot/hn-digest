# When everyone has AI and the company still learns nothing

- Score: 301 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48020063) | Link: https://www.robert-glaser.de/when-everyone-has-ai-and-the-company-still-learns-nothing/

### TL;DR

Buying Copilot seats and counting prompts does not make an organization smarter. Robert Glaser argues that enterprise AI gains emerge only when discoveries inside real work loops travel from individuals to teams and reusable capabilities. He proposes joining agent operations, loop intelligence, and capability distribution: govern access, observe where intent-to-verification cycles succeed or sprawl, then feed useful skills, evaluations, and runbooks back into work. HN commenters said large-company bottlenecks remain testing, approvals, infrastructure, and release trains, while workers may hide productivity techniques if sharing merely raises workloads or layoff risk.
