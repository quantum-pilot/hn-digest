# Three Inverse Laws of AI

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48023861) | Link: https://susam.net/inverse-laws-of-robotics.html

### TL;DR

Susam Pal proposes three human-facing safeguards for AI: do not mistake conversational systems for emotional or moral agents, do not accept generated answers without verification proportional to their stakes, and do not transfer accountability to the tool. He urges warnings and less humanlike chatbot presentation, while preserving human responsibility even where automated systems act too quickly for intervention. HN agreed on scrutiny and ownership but challenged the first rule as psychologically unrealistic: people anthropomorphize everything, so vendors should design around that tendency rather than expecting users to suppress it.

### Comment pulse

- Friendly language encourages inferred intent and emotional dependence → vendors benefit from engagement, making self-restraint alone an inadequate safeguard.
- Casual metaphors are not necessarily belief → killing a process differs from treating generated conversation as conscious, trustworthy, or romantically reciprocal.
- Humans remain accountable for deployed decisions → reviewers reject “Claude suggested it” — counterpoint: real-time autonomy complicates meaningful human control.
