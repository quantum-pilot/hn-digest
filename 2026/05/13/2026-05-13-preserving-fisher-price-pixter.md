# Preserving Fisher-Price Pixter

- Score: 202 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48091812) | Link: https://dmitry.gr/?r=05.Projects&proj=37.%20Pixter

### TL;DR

A reverse-engineering project claims complete documentation and emulation of Fisher-Price’s Pixter family, preserving nearly every game across Classic, Plus, 2.0, Color, Multimedia, and Pocket variants. Work included tracing undocumented buses, dumping epoxy-covered ROMs and cartridges with a Pi Pico 2, decoding separate stack-based virtual machines, extending ARM emulation for Sharp SoCs, capturing otherwise unreadable melody-chip audio as analog samples, and documenting pinouts and formats. Multimedia cartridges proved NAND-compatible, native-code Micrium RTOS images. Commenters praised the persistence and wished discontinued hardware vendors had to release enough documentation for emulation.
