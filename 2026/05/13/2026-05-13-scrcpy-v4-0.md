# Scrcpy v4.0

- Score: 350 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48114356) | Link: https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy/releases/tag/v4.0

### TL;DR

Scrcpy 4.0 upgrades the low-latency Android mirroring and control tool from SDL2 to SDL3, enabling aspect-ratio-locked windows and dynamically resizable flex virtual displays. It adds camera torch and zoom controls, keep-active signaling without changing device settings, configurable backgrounds, clearer disconnect handling, standard fullscreen and quit shortcuts, and mDNS TCP-device discovery. Fixes cover Meta Quest flicker, rooted copy-paste, colorspace, rotation, and a silence-decoding path that consumed roughly 40 times more CPU. HN praised its reliability and highlighted tethering, desktop-style displays, screenshot-restricted apps, typing, and rescuing phones with failed screens.

### Comment pulse
