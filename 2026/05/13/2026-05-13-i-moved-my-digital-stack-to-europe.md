# I moved my digital stack to Europe

- Score: 869 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48120629) | Link: https://monokai.com/articles/how-i-moved-my-digital-stack-to-europe/

### TL;DR

A developer moved most infrastructure from US providers to European or self-hosted alternatives: Google Analytics to Matomo, Workspace and 1Password to Proton, DigitalOcean/AWS to Scaleway, Backblaze to OVHcloud, SendGrid to Lettermint, Sentry to Bugsink, and OpenAI APIs to Mistral. S3 and Sentry compatibility made several swaps easy; migration took planning but ran incident-free for two months. Cloudflare, Stripe, Claude Code, GitLab, and public GitHub remain for capability, cost, or network effects. Commenters reported rapidly growing EU-local procurement demand, while warning jurisdiction alone cannot guarantee privacy or continuity.

### Comment pulse

- Consultants said EU or domestic hosting became a standard procurement question within months, with migration demand exceeding prior years.
- Others replaced Cloudflare, AWS, GitHub, and analytics with Bunny, Hetzner/OVH, Forgejo, and Umami, often at lower cost.
- Sovereignty reduces sudden cutoff risk — counterpoint: European governments pursue surveillance too, and transatlantic legal or corporate dependencies remain.
