# Starship V3

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48116781) | Link: https://www.spacex.com/updates#starship-v3

### TL;DR

SpaceX unveiled Starship V3, a broad redesign of ship, Super Heavy, Raptor engines, avionics, and Starbase Pad 2 aimed at higher flight rates, reuse, long-duration missions, and orbital refueling. Super Heavy gets three stronger grid fins, an integrated hot stage, redesigned fuel distribution, and simultaneous startup for 33 engines. Starship adds docking and propellant-transfer hardware, improved cryogenic management, and simplified aft systems. Raptor 3 raises sea-level thrust from 230 to 250 metric tons while reducing engine mass. HN praised the integration but debated settlement feasibility and orbital-data-center economics.
