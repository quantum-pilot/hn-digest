### TL;DR

The linked Dav2d article was unavailable after exceeding its host’s daily request limit, so the discussion supplies only partial context. Commenters describe an AV2 decoder effort confronting a codec reportedly about five times costlier to decode than AV1 while offering roughly 25% smaller streams. HN debated whether that tradeoff justifies new hardware, but noted large video platforms can keep AV1 and H.264 renditions for older clients. Others stressed that a codec standard needs both a reference decoder and an independent field implementation to expose ambiguities and establish practical behavior.

### Comment pulse

- Decode efficiency is the adoption risk → fivefold AV1 complexity could defeat real-time software playback before inexpensive AV2 hardware becomes common.
- Compression compounds at platform scale → 25% fewer bits matters across billions of views — counterpoint: client hardware churn may outweigh savings elsewhere.
- Multicodec delivery prevents forced obsolescence → services can add AV2 while retaining AV1 and H.264 variants for incompatible devices.
