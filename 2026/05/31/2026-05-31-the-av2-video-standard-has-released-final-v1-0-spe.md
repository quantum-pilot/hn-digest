# The AV2 Video Standard Has Released (Final v1.0 Specification)

- Score: 337 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48340910) | Link: https://av2.aomedia.org

### TL;DR

AOMedia has finalized AV2 v1.0, defining bitstream syntax, semantics, and decoding alongside the AVM reference implementation. The AV1 successor targets better compression at lower bitrates, with expanded AR/VR, screen-content, multi-program split-screen, and broader quality-range support. Hacker News treats publication as the start of implementation, not deployment: the reference encoder is reportedly about 1 fps, production software should improve, and hardware may take years. Readers emphasize multistream and alpha-channel possibilities while debating patent exposure, image-format proliferation, and eventual neural-codec competition.

### Comment pulse
