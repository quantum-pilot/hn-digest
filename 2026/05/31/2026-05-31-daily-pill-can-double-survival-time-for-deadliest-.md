# Daily pill can double survival time for deadliest cancer, trial shows

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48346629) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/may/31/daily-pill-daraxonrasib-double-survival-time-pancreatic-pancreas-cancer-clinical-trial

### TL;DR

In a 500-patient trial of metastatic pancreatic cancer, daraxonrasib produced average survival of 13.2 months versus 6.6–6.7 months with chemotherapy, while the report says side effects were fewer. The RAS(On) multi-selective inhibitor suppresses KRAS, a driver mutated in more than 90% of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinomas. Results were presented at ASCO; access remains a next step, and related drugs are being tested in lung and colon cancer. HN welcomed the gain but stressed that oncology tolerability can still mean severe toxicity and asked whether combining daraxonrasib with chemotherapy improves outcomes.
