# Air France and Airbus found guilty of manslaughter over 2009 plane crash

- Score: 128 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48250980) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/czd2qmdvmq6o

### TL;DR

A Paris appeals court convicted Air France and Airbus of manslaughter over AF447, the 2009 Rio–Paris crash that killed all 228 aboard, reversing their 2023 acquittal. Investigators attributed the stall to iced airspeed sensors plus pilots’ failure to respond correctly; training has since improved and sensors were replaced. Each company faces a maximum €225,000 fine and plans another appeal. HN debated individual pilot error versus systemic responsibility, emphasizing known prior sensor failures, inadequate training, automation and cockpit-interface design, while some worried about overturning an acquittal through prosecution appeal.
