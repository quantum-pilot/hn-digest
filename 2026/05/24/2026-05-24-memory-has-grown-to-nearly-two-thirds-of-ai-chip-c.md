# Memory has grown to nearly two-thirds of AI chip component costs

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48258684) | Link: https://epoch.ai/data-insights/ai-chip-component-cost-shares

### TL;DR

Epoch AI estimates high-bandwidth memory’s share of AI-chip component spending rose from 52% in Q1 2024 to 63% in Q4 2025 across Nvidia, AMD, Google, and Amazon designs, weighted by production. HBM spending jumped from about $12 billion to $32 billion as 2025 total component spending reached $52 billion. Tight supply could raise 2026 costs further. HN commenters saw eventual memory expansion as a route to cheaper AI, but debated whether cautious suppliers will add enough capacity without causing another glut; consumer RAM price spikes made the shortage tangible.

### Comment pulse

- Cheap AI is not guaranteed → supply growth could cut costs dramatically — counterpoint: manufacturers may preserve scarcity to avoid a destabilizing glut.
- Consumer upgrades are collateral damage → commenters reported 96GB kits rising from roughly $250–$279 to over $1,000, freezing platform purchases.
- Memory pressure may reward efficiency → commenters proposed lower-precision formats and better training methods instead of waiting solely for fabrication capacity.
