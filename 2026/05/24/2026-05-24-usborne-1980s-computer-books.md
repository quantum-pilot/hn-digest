# Usborne 1980s Computer Books

- Score: 152 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48258194) | Link: https://usborne.com/us/books/computer-and-coding-books

### TL;DR

Usborne’s illustrated 1980s computer books taught children programming from first principles, often beyond what their parents knew. The author highlights Write Your Own Adventure Programs for data-driven design, text parsing, and subroutines, and Machine Code for Beginners for making assembly and pointers memorable to pre-teens; Usborne now publishes Scratch and Python successors and offers the originals free. HN readers credited library copies with launching software and robotics careers, while recalling how scarce computers, incompatible interpreters, and mail-order components forced creative porting, persistence, and broad hands-on learning.
