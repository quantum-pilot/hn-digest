# Alexander Grothendieck Revolutionized 20th-Century Mathematics

- Score: 128 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48254020) | Link: https://www.quantamagazine.org/how-alexander-grothendieck-revolutionized-20th-century-mathematics-20260520/

### TL;DR

Alexander Grothendieck transformed algebraic geometry by replacing fragmented definitions with a relational, highly abstract language centered on schemes. Built from prime ideals, topologies, and sheaves, schemes capture a polynomial equation’s structure independently of the number system used for its solutions. This framework connected geometry to algebra, number theory, topology, representation theory, and logic, enabling progress on the Weil conjectures and spawning further tools. HN discussion emphasized his example-averse style, community translation efforts, and modern challenges to his treatment of equality from formalized mathematics and Lean.
