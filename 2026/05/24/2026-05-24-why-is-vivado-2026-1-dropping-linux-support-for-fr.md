# Why is Vivado 2026.1 dropping Linux support for free tier?

- Score: 294 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48254309) | Link: https://adaptivesupport.amd.com/s/question/0D5Pd00001YQLdMKAX/why-is-vivado-20261-dropping-linux-support-for-free-tier-?language=en_US

### TL;DR

AMD’s Vivado 2026.1 Basic tier will support Windows but not Linux; Linux users must buy at least a Core license. AMD says Basic targets entry-level work, cites roughly 70% Windows usage, and offers students 60-day Enterprise access. Vivado 2025.2 remains license-free and usable indefinitely, but bug support ends after 2026.3. Neither explanation answers why the operating-system split exists. HN commenters called it an ecosystem own goal that burdens education, CI, hiring, and hardware customers, while discussing Lattice and open-source Yosys as imperfect alternatives.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime customers said license procurement, machine setup, and teammate onboarding cost more operationally than the fee, despite substantial FPGA spending.
- Students and hobbyists create future enterprise mindshare; educators threatened vendor changes, arguing Windows lacks parity for cross-compilation workflows.
- Lattice earned recommendations for free lower-end tooling — counterpoint: users reported Linux friction, MAC-bound licenses, closed forums, and mid-development price increases.
