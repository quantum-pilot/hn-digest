# Omarchy Is Not A Distro

- Score: 154 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48257612) | Link: https://abyss.fish/your_dotfiles_are_not_a_distro

### TL;DR

The author argues Omarchy is not a traditional Linux distribution but Arch plus DHH’s highly personal configuration: it ships no packages of its own, relies on Arch and AUR, binds keys to Grok, X, and Hey, and bundles or scripts proprietary services. The objection is less customization than branding, sponsorship, merchandise, fragmentation, and diverting attention from infrastructure maintainers. HN split sharply: critics saw cross-promotion and future support risk; users praised a polished Hyprland setup that removes choice paralysis, teaches composition by example, and makes Linux appealing to newcomers.

### Comment pulse

- Curation is the product → Omarchy gives newcomers a functional keyboard-first desktop and solves Hyprland setup failures that raw Arch configurations did not.
- Branding creates ecosystem costs → conference and sponsor money rewards a configuration layer while Arch and package maintainers supply the underlying work.
- Bundled preferences blur convenience and advertising → proprietary apps and service shortcuts simplify onboarding — counterpoint: critics compare them to OEM bloatware.

### LLM perspective
