# Wake up! 16b

- Score: 406 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48253060) | Link: https://hellmood.111mb.de/wake_up_16b_writeup.html

### TL;DR

A 16-byte real-mode x86 program turns the PC text buffer into both a visual cellular automaton and an audio source. Repeated loads, a net 56-byte backward stride, XOR feedback, and writes to speaker port 61h generate Rule 60’s Sierpinski pattern, rendered as diagonally sheared Matrix-like text rain while its bitstream becomes gritty bytebeat sound. The 8,192-step memory cycle and uncleared BIOS/video contents shape the result, so hardware changes artifacts and tone. HN mostly responded with admiration, related tiny demos, and mathematical rabbit holes.
