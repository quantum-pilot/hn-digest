# It's time to talk about my writerdeck

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48250144) | Link: https://veronicaexplains.net/my-first-writerdeck/

### TL;DR

To create a distraction-free writing machine, the author repurposed a six-year-old Linux laptop with console-only Debian: no X11, Wayland, desktop, or browser. NetworkManager handles occasional Wi-Fi; kmscon improves fonts and colors; tmux exposes battery and brightness controls; Neovim plus Vimwiki provides writing; Syncthing backs up files; autologin opens directly into the workspace. After a week, it produced several drafts. HN readers liked the intentional constraint but teased the extensive setup as yak shaving, suggested simply switching to a Linux TTY, and debated whether such personal disconnection meaningfully counters attention-extractive technology.

### LLM perspective

- View: A dedicated device works by removing choices and muscle-memory cues, not by making a text editor technically superior.
- Impact: Repurposing supported hardware offers cheaper intentional computing, but maintenance can become the hobby instead of writing.
