# Is AI Profitable Yet?

- Score: 246 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48243863) | Link: https://isaiprofitable.com/

### TL;DR

A dashboard estimates AI companies have spent $1.4 trillion cumulatively against $718 billion in AI revenue, declaring the sector unprofitable; Nvidia alone shows a $253 billion surplus while Amazon, Google, Meta, and Microsoft carry the largest gaps. Its figures mix estimated capex, lab spending, revenue, different time windows, and private-company leaks, so “spend minus revenue” is not conventional profit. HN split between seeing unprecedented systemic risk and normal infrastructure investment whose assets amortize, inference margins improve, and enterprise demand grows.
