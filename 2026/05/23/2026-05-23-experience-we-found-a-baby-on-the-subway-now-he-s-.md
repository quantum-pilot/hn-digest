# Experience: We found a baby on the subway – now he's our 26-year-old son

- Score: 238 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48245571) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2026/may/22/experience-found-baby-subway-now-26-year-old-son

### TL;DR

In 2000, New York social-care worker Danny Stewart found an abandoned newborn at Union Square station and called 911. Twelve weeks later, while testifying, a judge asked whether he and partner Pete might adopt. Pete initially resisted the unplanned parenthood, then bonded during a foster-home visit; they received custody that December and named him Kevin. At Kevin’s suggestion, the same judge later married his fathers. Now 26 and a software developer, Kevin remains close to them. HN readers were moved but questioned the unusually swift adoption process.
