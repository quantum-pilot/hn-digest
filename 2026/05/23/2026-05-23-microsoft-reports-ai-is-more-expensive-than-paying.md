# Microsoft reports AI is more expensive than paying human employees

- Score: 218 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48244434) | Link: https://fortune.com/2026/05/22/microsoft-ai-cost-problem-tokens-agents/

### TL;DR

Fortune argues that enterprise AI bills may rise even as token prices fall, because agentic systems consume far more tokens and vendors may not pass through savings. It cites Microsoft reportedly canceling most direct Claude Code licenses while steering staff to GitHub Copilot CLI, Uber exhausting its annual coding-tool budget in four months, and an Nvidia executive saying his team’s compute exceeds payroll. HN readers rejected the headline: Microsoft made no employee-cost comparison, its vendor switch may favor an internal product, and token-usage leaderboards predictably reward waste rather than productivity.

### Comment pulse

- Critics said the article conflates three unrelated facts and turns a Nvidia deep-learning-team quote into a Microsoft labor-cost claim.
- Microsoft may be consolidating on its competing Copilot product, not retreating from AI — counterpoint: Claude pricing could still influence that choice.
- Tokenmaxxing illustrates Goodhart’s law: once usage becomes an objective, spending rises without demonstrating better software or business outcomes.
