# Italy moves to Airbus A330 tankers

- Score: 226 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48248775) | Link: https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/05/21/italy-moves-to-airbus-a330-tankers-in-major-nato-aligned-shift

### TL;DR

Italy has signed a €1.39 billion contract for six Airbus A330 MRTT tanker-transports plus long-term support, abandoning the Boeing KC-46 path after cancellations and a failed tender. Both aircraft are NATO-compatible, so the shift preserves US interoperability while moving maintenance, training, and supply-chain weight toward Europe. The fleet will refuel F-35s and Eurofighters and provide strategic transport; the exact MRTT variant and Italian industrial role remain undecided. HN attributed the outcome to KC-46 delays, Boeing’s long engineering decline, delivery risk, and growing distrust of US contract and support reliability.

- Widebody competition has steep barriers → workforce scale, mature engines, certification power, and decades of capital leave Airbus and Boeing dominant.
