# GitLab Announces Workforce Reduction and End of Their CREDIT Values

- Score: 243 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48100500) | Link: https://about.gitlab.com/blog/gitlab-act-2/

### TL;DR

GitLab announced an AI-centered restructuring whose final workforce and financial scope will arrive June 2. Plans include shrinking its 60-country footprint by up to 30%, removing as many as three of eight management layers, reorganizing R&D into 60 smaller teams, automating internal approvals and handoffs, and inviting voluntary separations before May 18. It will retire the CREDIT values for Speed with Quality, Ownership Mindset, and Customer Outcomes while investing savings in agent-scale Git infrastructure, orchestration, context, and governance. Commenters saw bureaucracy reduction but distrusted the layoff framing and AI-heavy strategy.

### Comment pulse

- Critics read the new values as harder work and diminished collaboration or DEI — counterpoint: interpersonal excellence still explicitly includes diversity, inclusion, and belonging.
- Potential GitHub defectors reconsidered migration, saying GitLab squandered an opening to position itself as the stable, human-governed alternative.
- Even skeptics conceded eight management layers appear excessive; the unresolved question is whether removing three improves decisions or eliminates necessary oversight.
