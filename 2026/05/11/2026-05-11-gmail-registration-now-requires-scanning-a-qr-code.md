# Gmail registration now requires scanning a QR code and sending a text message

- Score: 542 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48092028) | Link: https://discuss.privacyguides.net/t/google-account-registration-now-requires-sending-an-sms-via-phone-instead-of-receiving-an-sms/36082

### TL;DR

A Privacy Guides report says some new Google accounts now receive a QR-code challenge that opens a prefilled SMS on a smartphone, reversing the usual flow: the user sends Google a message instead of receiving a code. Later observations suggest the challenge is risk-based rather than universal, varying with proxies and other signals. The change may frustrate disposable-number services and automated signups, but also leaves flip-phone users without an obvious path. HN commenters corrected claims that scanning alone sends anything; it is an SMS URI requiring user confirmation.

### Comment pulse

- Anti-abuse defenders said outbound SMS raises costs for phone farms — counterpoint: operators will likely adapt, leaving ordinary privacy seekers with most friction.
- Critics rejected calling Gmail free, arguing Google intentionally subsidizes it for ecosystem control, data, and business value.
- Business users cited signup walls, weak support, storage migration failures, and unwanted AI prompts as reasons to consider paid alternatives.
