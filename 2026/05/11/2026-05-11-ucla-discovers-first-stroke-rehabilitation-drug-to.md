# UCLA discovers first stroke rehabilitation drug to repair brain damage (2025)

- Score: 176 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48098261) | Link: https://stemcell.ucla.edu/news/ucla-discovers-first-stroke-rehabilitation-drug-repair-brain-damage

### TL;DR

UCLA reports that DDL-920 restored movement in mouse stroke models by reproducing a key effect of physical rehabilitation. Stroke disconnects surviving networks far from the damaged core, including parvalbumin neurons that coordinate movement through gamma oscillations. Rehabilitation restores those rhythms; the drug excited the same neurons and produced recovery. This does not regenerate cells killed at the infarct, and human safety and efficacy remain untested. HN asked whether 40 Hz electrical stimulation could target the same mechanism and whether other diseases might benefit, while cautioning against interpreting reconnection as intelligence.

### Comment pulse

- The therapy targets disconnected surviving circuitry, not dead tissue at the infarct center; headlines implying full brain-damage repair overstate the result.
- Gamma restoration may also be achievable through 40 Hz transcranial stimulation paired with robotic rehabilitation, suggesting a possible non-drug route.
- Broader neurodegenerative applications remain speculative; organized experience-dependent reconnection differs fundamentally from indiscriminate neuron growth or intelligence enhancement.
