# Where does next-token prediction leave us?

- Score: 202 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48288191) | Link: https://pop.rdi.sh/where-does-next-token-prediction-leave-us/

### TL;DR

This polemic argues that calling industries “cooked” reveals a class-blind celebration of labor displacement. AI may widen individual output while centralizing compute, data, and productive power; workers lose bargaining leverage, craft becomes unreviewable throughput, and everyone rents capabilities trained on collective human output. It also attacks default data extraction, privacy loss, datacenter externalities, and national-security framing. HN engaged mainly with the political economy, sharing fears of fungibility and second-order demand collapse, but challenged the title, the essay’s pessimism, and claims that effort is required for meaning.

### Comment pulse

- Cognitive offloading can hollow craft → developers described apathy when speed outranks code quality — counterpoint: free gifts and reduced effort can still transform people.
- Democratization may mean fungibility → easier technical output weakens specialized bargaining power, though juniors could similarly automate middle-management work.
- No class is insulated from second-order effects → managers, doctors, and tradespeople still depend on customers, mortgages, social stability, and aggregate demand.
