# I think Anthropic and OpenAI have found product-market fit

- Score: 592 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48296794) | Link: https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/27/product-market-fit/

### TL;DR

Simon Willison argues coding agents gave Anthropic and OpenAI product-market fit after November 2025, with April 2026 enterprise pricing shifting customers from subsidized seats to API-level token charges. Heavy professional use, expanded enterprise hiring, Anthropic’s rumored profitability, and its $1.25 billion monthly SpaceX compute deal suggest real willingness to pay; Uber’s exhausted budget is framed as demand, not failure. HN largely separated usefulness from viable economics: disputed ROI, trillion-dollar infrastructure needs, and cheaper open models leave long-term profitability unproven.
