# Tech CEOs are apparently suffering from AI psychosis

- Score: 526 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48295679) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/tech-ceos-are-apparently-suffering-from-ai-psychosis/

### TL;DR

The article develops Box CEO Aaron Levie’s claim that executives overestimate AI because they see impressive happy-path demos but not the debugging, verification, training, and domain work needed for production. It contrasts 115,430 tech layoffs through May 2026 and ClickUp’s 22% cut with research finding perceived productivity gains exceed measured gains and agents often remain below human quality. HN largely recognized the last-mile gap, but debated the inflammatory psychosis label, whether executive detachment is AI-specific, and whether rapid 65%-complete prototypes still justify enthusiasm.

### Comment pulse

- Executive distance predates AI → models can intensify it through flattering, plausible output, though top-down leaders sometimes correctly discard obsolete processes.
