# SimCity 3k in 4k (2025)

- Score: 256 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48297645) | Link: https://www.thran.uk/writ/hdid/2025/12/simcity-3k-in-4k.html

### TL;DR

A preservation guide makes a CD-installed SimCity 3000 Unlimited playable at 3840×2160 on Windows 10. The recipe combines GOG’s widescreen/no-CD executable, a scroll-speed tweak, a Direct3D wrapper configured for true fullscreen, an optional 4 GB patch, a disabled dead-server updater, and restored music files; Windows 11 remains untested. HN mostly celebrated the game rather than troubleshooting it, arguing that stylized abstraction and coherent presentation invite more imagination than photorealistic successors, while adding a higher-quality soundtrack tip.

### Comment pulse

- Abstraction can improve simulation → isometric art leaves room for imagination and makes traffic, zoning, economics, crime, and pollution easier to model mentally.
- Presentation remains unusually cohesive → commenters praised the adviser system, pixel art, and soundtrack, contrasting SC4’s 3D-rendered advisers.
- Use original audio where possible → Unlimited’s included tracks are reportedly lower-bitrate mono; matching files from the 1.0 CD restore stereo.
