# A few interesting modern pixel fonts

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48271448) | Link: https://unsung.aresluna.org/a-few-interesting-modern-pixel-fonts/

### TL;DR

Marcin Wichary surveys four vector fonts that deliberately mimic pixels. Analog Mono repairs VCR OSD Mono’s low baseline and raised descenders; Coral Pixels turns old subpixel color fringing into a nostalgic feature; Two Slice remains somewhat legible at only two pixels tall; and Geist Pixel emphasizes production-ready scaling, metrics, glyph coverage, and integration over novelty. HN readers added Departure Mono, PragmataPro, and Gohu, praised pixel-font creation in FontForge for tiny embedded displays and icon glyphs, and sharply disagreed over whether Geist’s system rigor compensates for its visual character.

### Comment pulse
