# Chemistry behind the Garden Grove chemical tank

- Score: 192 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48284712) | Link: https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/methyl-methacrylate-tank

### TL;DR

A 6,000–7,000-gallon methyl methacrylate tank at GKN Aerospace in Garden Grove overheated and pressurized after valves failed or clogged, prompting evacuation orders for about 40,000 people across six cities. Internal temperature reached 90°F, above the chemical’s 50°F flashpoint; crews sprayed water, monitored it every 10 minutes, and prepared spill containment while rupture or explosion threatened toxic exposure, fire, and shrapnel. HN focused on polymerization-runaway safeguards, alleging missing neutralizer and deluge capacity, while a firefighter later reported that a crack relieved pressure and crews prevented thermal runaway.
