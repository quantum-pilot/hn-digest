# Accelerando (2005)

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48159241) | Link: https://www.antipope.org/charlie/blog-static/fiction/accelerando/accelerando.html

### TL;DR

Charles Stross’s 2005 novel begins with Manfred Macx, a hyperconnected venture altruist who gives inventions away while envisioning uploads, self-replicating robotics, and matter converted into computation. In Amsterdam, an apparent Russian AI seeking asylum proves to be a collective of uploaded lobsters fleeing the coming singularity; meanwhile, Manfred’s IRS-linked former fiancée challenges his rejection of taxes, family, and scarcity economics. HN readers found the accelerating, agent-mediated future newly plausible, yet increasingly recognized the story as a warning about technological adaptation washing away humanity.
