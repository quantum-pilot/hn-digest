# Ploopy Bean: a trackpoint for every computer

- Score: 163 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48114208) | Link: https://ploopy.co/shop/bean-pointing-stick/

### TL;DR

Ploopy’s Bean is a CAD$69.99, fully assembled, open-source USB pointing-stick mouse in a 3D-printed case. It runs QMK with VIA customization and provides four Omron buttons; preorders ship immediately or within 8–20 weeks by tier. Unlike a classic TrackPoint’s nearly stationary strain gauges, commenters say Bean uses a moving Hall-effect sensor and spring, potentially feeling more like a controller thumbstick. HN split over its premise: moving the device off-keyboard sacrifices home-row convenience, while supporters valued its tiny fixed footprint, portability, configurability, and accessibility for people unable to use keyboards.

### Comment pulse

- TrackPoint loyalists prioritized 6 mm soft-rim caps, arguing modern 3 mm caps require more pressure and cause discomfort; Bean’s casing might permit modification.
- Critics considered a detached stick redundant beside mice and trackballs — counterpoint: its constant desk footprint and compact portability suit constrained setups.
- QMK can map a held button to scrolling, leaving three buttons for left, middle, and right clicks despite concerns about missing scroll control.
