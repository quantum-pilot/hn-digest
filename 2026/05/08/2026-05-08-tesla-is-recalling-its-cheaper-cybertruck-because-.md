# Tesla is recalling its cheaper Cybertruck because the wheels might fall off

- Score: 196 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48063240) | Link: https://www.theverge.com/transportation/926741/tesla-cybertruck-cheaper-recall

### TL;DR

Tesla is recalling all 173 rear-wheel-drive Cybertruck Long Range units it sold because brake-rotor stud holes can crack under severe road impacts or cornering, eventually separating a wheel stud from the hub. Three warranty claims may be related; no crashes, injuries, or deaths are known. Tesla will replace front and rear rotors, hubs, and lug nuts free. The discontinued $70,000 model is distinct from the newer AWD version. HN mocked both the safety issue and tiny production run, though others noted serious brake recalls affect rival EVs too.
