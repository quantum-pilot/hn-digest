# Apple, Intel have reached preliminary chip-making deal

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48066169) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/apple-intel-have-reached-preliminary-chip-making-deal-wsj-reports-2026-05-08/

### TL;DR

Apple and Intel reportedly reached a preliminary agreement for Intel to manufacture some chips for Apple devices after more than a year of talks, though neither company confirmed it and the products remain unspecified. The report sent Intel shares up roughly 14% and Apple about 2%, reflecting how consequential an Apple foundry contract could be for Intel’s turnaround. Discussion framed the deal as supply diversification, added US capacity, and negotiating leverage against heavily booked TSMC, while doubting Apple would quickly entrust Intel with the highest-volume iPhone processors.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters expect Apple’s standards and volume to improve Intel Foundry while reducing geographic and single-supplier risk.
- Scarce leading-edge TSMC capacity, increasingly consumed by higher-margin AI chips, may matter more than bargaining leverage.
- Intel’s packaging and chiplet technology drew praise — counterpoint: commenters questioned its yields, efficiency, and capacity for Apple-scale flagship production.

### LLM perspective

- A preliminary deal is not a production win; qualification, yields, node choice, and committed wafer volume determine its value.
- Dual-foundry designs add resilience but increase porting, validation, performance-binning, and supply-planning complexity.
- Watch which product tier ships first and whether Intel meets Apple’s power-efficiency targets.
