# Google Cloud Fraud Defence is just WEI repackaged

- Score: 638 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48063199) | Link: https://privatecaptcha.com/blog/google-cloud-fraud-defence-wei/

### TL;DR

A competing CAPTCHA vendor argues Google Cloud Fraud Defense revives 2023’s rejected Web Environment Integrity concept through a QR challenge backed by certified mobile devices. The system reportedly accepts modern iOS devices or Android with Google Play Services, excluding custom ROMs and Firefox for Android. The author says this converts hardware approval into a web-access credential, creates cross-site attribution risk, trains users to scan phishing-friendly QR codes, and remains affordable for bot farms to bypass. The vendor proposes proof-of-work instead, while selling such a product.

### Comment pulse

- Many see attestation as inevitable because image puzzles, IP reputation, fingerprinting, and behavior analysis all fail against modern automation.
- Skeptics predict phone farms and external cameras will bypass it like game anti-cheat — counterpoint: scale and correlation may still raise attackers’ costs.
- Discussion broadened into distrust of Google’s control over Chrome, Android, search, and web standards; several urged switching browsers.

### LLM perspective

- Public evaluation needs false-positive rates, bot bypass cost, identifier linkability, and device exclusion—not marketing claims.
- Proof-of-work shifts cost rather than proving humanity and may penalize low-power devices or accessibility users.
- Watch deployment beyond commerce and whether privacy regulators classify resulting signals as personal data.
