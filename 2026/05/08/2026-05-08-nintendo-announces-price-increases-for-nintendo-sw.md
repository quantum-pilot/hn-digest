# Nintendo announces price increases for Nintendo Switch 2

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48059606) | Link: https://www.nintendo.co.jp/corporate/release/en/2026/260508.html

### TL;DR

Nintendo will raise Switch 2 prices on September 1 to $499.99 in the U.S., C$679.99 in Canada, and €499.99 in Europe; Japan’s domestic-language model rises May 25 from ¥49,980 to ¥59,980. Original Switch models also jump sharply in Japan, while online subscriptions rise July 1 and traditional cards move to open pricing. Nintendo cites market conditions and regional alignment. HN blamed some mix of memory costs, currencies, and pricing power, debating whether Steam Deck competition matters when Nintendo’s exclusive games serve a different audience.

### Comment pulse

- Raising aging Switch 1 models surprised readers → manufacturing and shipping costs may rise, but late-cycle hardware usually becomes cheaper.

- Exchange-rate explanations were contested → a weaker yen normally aids exports, so simultaneous overseas increases imply broader pressures or margin strategy.
