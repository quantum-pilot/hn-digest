# Steve Wozniak cheered after telling students they have AI – actual intelligence

- Score: 583 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48233563) | Link: https://www.businessinsider.com/steve-wozniak-apple-ai-graduation-speech-2026-5

### TL;DR

At Grand Valley State University’s commencement, Apple cofounder Steve Wozniak reassured graduates facing an AI-altered job market by contrasting machine AI with their actual intelligence, earning laughter and applause. He described AI as an attempt to reproduce brain-like processes and urged students to think differently rather than follow established paths. The reception contrasted with two recent commencement speakers booed for pro-AI remarks. HN discussion moved beyond the joke to human worth, career anxiety, learning fundamentals, and the risks of indiscriminate corporate AI mandates.
