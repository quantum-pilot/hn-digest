# Cleve Moler has died

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48231319) | Link: https://www.mathworks.com/company/aboutus/founders/clevemoler.html

### TL;DR

Cleve Moler, MathWorks cofounder, chairman, and chief mathematician, has died. After nearly 20 years teaching mathematics and computer science, he authored MATLAB’s first version and co-created the LINPACK and EISPACK numerical libraries. His career also included Intel Hypercube and Ardent Computer, textbooks, online books, and major honors from IEEE, the Computer History Museum, and ICIAM. HN commenters explained MATLAB’s teaching-tool origins, shared memories of Moler’s generosity and technical foresight, and traced his influence into later Python numerical work.

### Comment pulse

- MATLAB began as roughly 2,000 lines of FORTRAN, exposing matrix routines interactively so students could explore them without compiling library calls.
- Jack Little anticipated PCs’ growth and built a C version with Steve Bangert; MATLAB and GAUSS both launched commercially in 1984.
- Alumni remembered Moler as approachable and supportive; technical descendants cited his clean interfaces, while several argued his stature warranted an HN black bar.
