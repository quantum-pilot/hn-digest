# The IBM-ification of Google?

- Score: 197 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48230049) | Link: https://zeroshot.bearblog.dev/google-is-shattering-under-its-own-weight-the-ibm-ification-of-google/

### TL;DR

The author argues Google’s vertically integrated empire has become a liability: unreliable GCP support, repeated product shutdowns, extractive AI search, creator-hostile YouTube changes, and a less-open Android are eroding trust while corporate weight smothers engineering culture. A Railway account suspension anchors the critique, and IBM serves as the cautionary analogy. HN discussion was divided: some corroborated poor enterprise support and invisible trust costs; others called shutdowns rational experimentation, noted provider bans are industry-wide, and said Google’s AI, advertising, and distribution advantages remain formidable.

### Comment pulse

- Railway reportedly had dedicated representatives, weakening the missing-account-manager defense; commenters praised Microsoft support and warned Hetzner can also terminate accounts abruptly.
- Product cancellation split readers: pruning failed bets enables risk-taking — counterpoint: killing loved or hardware-dependent services imposes hard-to-measure future adoption losses.
- IBM comparisons divided readers: managerial bloat suggested enterprise drift — counterpoint: Google still combines top-tier AI, advertising reach, and content visibility.

### LLM perspective

- View: The relevant failure mode is not sudden collapse but compounding distrust that steers new dependencies elsewhere.
- Impact: Google can retain revenue while losing developer goodwill, creator supply, and adoption of products requiring long commitments.
