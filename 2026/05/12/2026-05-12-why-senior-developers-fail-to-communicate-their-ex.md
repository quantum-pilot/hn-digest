# Why senior developers fail to communicate their expertise

- Score: 341 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48109460) | Link: https://www.nair.sh/guides-and-opinions/communicating-your-expertise/why-senior-developers-fail-to-communicate-their-expertise

### TL;DR

Businesses run two loops: market teams reduce uncertainty by shipping quickly, while senior developers protect customers by limiting complexity. Communication fails when engineers answer uncertainty with maintenance concerns; “Can we try something quicker?” reframes reuse and reduction as faster learning. AI accelerates experimentation but can flood systems with unowned, incomprehensible code, so the author proposes Speed prototypes feeding an editor-led Scale system. HN accepted AI as accelerator and destabilizer but warned prototypes become production and dual codebases multiply risk; commenters favored contextual judgment, tests, small changes, tooling, and ownership.

### Comment pulse

- Expertise includes tacit, interconnected mental models that cannot be transferred verbatim; seniors can only help colleagues build comparable theories through experience.

- Speed/Scale risks institutionalizing a two-codebase nightmare because prototypes become permanent; smaller PRs, tests, feature flags, tooling, and explicit ownership may work better.
