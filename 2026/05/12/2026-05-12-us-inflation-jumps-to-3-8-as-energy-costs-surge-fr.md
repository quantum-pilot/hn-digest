# US inflation jumps to 3.8% as energy costs surge from Iran war

- Score: 228 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48108313) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c202pgxx89lo

### TL;DR

US inflation rose from 3.3% in March to 3.8% in April, its fastest pace since May 2023. The BLS said energy drove nearly half the increase after the Iran war and Strait of Hormuz closure lifted oil; gasoline averaged $4.50 and airfares jumped 20.7%. Food and housing contributed, while 3.6% wage growth trailed prices for the first time in three years. Rate cuts now look less likely as Kevin Warsh approaches the Fed chair. HN questioned energy-centric framing, citing 2.8% inflation excluding food and fuel, and described steeper household-specific increases.

### Comment pulse

- Critics saw the war as a strategic and economic loss; some predicted higher prices could accelerate movement away from fossil fuels.
- Core inflation at 2.8% complicates the energy narrative — counterpoint: sudden fuel shocks still raise headline inflation, while real wages typically lag.
- Personal baskets diverge from averages: milk and whey rose 15%–57% for commenters, while store-level pricing variation makes comparison harder.
