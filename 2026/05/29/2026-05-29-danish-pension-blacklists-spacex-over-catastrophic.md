# Danish Pension Blacklists SpaceX over 'Catastrophic Governance'

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48324097) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-29/danish-pension-fund-blacklists-spacex-citing-governance-issues

### TL;DR

Denmark’s $25 billion AkademikerPension will exclude SpaceX from its IPO, secondary-market, and indexed portfolios. The fund says a targeted valuation of at least $1.8 trillion exceeds its $1 trillion ceiling and offers too little risk premium, while Elon Musk’s expected 80% voting control alongside CEO, CTO, and chair roles creates unacceptable governance risk. Several U.S. public pension leaders have raised similar concerns. HN mostly agreed, focusing on passive-index exposure after listing; countercomments noted free-float weighting should keep the initial allocation small.
