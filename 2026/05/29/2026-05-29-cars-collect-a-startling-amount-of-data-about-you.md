# Cars collect a startling amount of data about you

- Score: 478 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48318481) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20260513-your-car-is-spying-on-you-its-about-to-get-worse

### TL;DR

Connected cars can transmit location, trip history, passengers, driving behavior, infotainment use, and cabin-sensor inferences to automakers, apps, brokers, insurers, or authorities. Mozilla found all 25 brands it reviewed failed its privacy standards; 19 policies allowed possible data sales. One driver reportedly received a 130-page six-month trip record and a 21% insurance increase. Upcoming US impaired-driving systems may add biometric monitoring without dedicated data safeguards. Hacker News argues opt-outs are inadequate because roadside cameras complete the picture, favoring limits on collection, sharing, retention, and secondary use.

### Comment pulse

- Cheap roadside surveillance means privacy cannot be restored solely by disconnecting vehicles; regulation must cover both onboard telemetry and external camera networks.
- Weak penalties preserve profitable surveillance incentives — counterpoint: commenters dispute comparing California-only fines with nationwide sales revenue and request stronger sourcing.
- Data has economic value and may subsidize products for poorer buyers; critics argue privacy should not become a premium feature.
