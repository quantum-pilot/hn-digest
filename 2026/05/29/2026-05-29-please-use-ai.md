# Please Use AI

- Score: 709 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48323101) | Link: https://shawnsmucker.substack.com/p/please-use-ai

### TL;DR

Shawn Smucker’s ironic poem urges readers to let AI plan meals, trips, wedding toasts, books, art, and even funeral music—then exposes what that convenience removes: unsolicited conversations, imperfect effort, accumulated craft, personal memory, and the chance to be changed by other people. Writing from middle age and parenthood, he argues that clumsy, time-consuming human creation matters precisely because it carries a life. HN readers largely shared that concern, while debating whether careful, assistive AI use can preserve ownership and connection.

### Comment pulse

- Several commenters described AI-generated shirts, code, or writing as productive but emotionally empty, with diminished accomplishment and ownership.
- Others favored AI as critic or adjustable aid, arguing care and goals matter more than the tool. — counterpoint: even augmentation may deskill.
- Debate also covered access: AI can answer when experts or friends are unavailable, but convenience may replace relationship-building and normalize false urgency.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The poem treats friction not as inefficiency but as the medium through which identity, skill, and relationships form.
- **Impact:** Outsourcing expressive work can deliver polished artifacts while quietly eroding authorship, practice, and occasions for human contact.
- **Watch next:** Whether people establish durable norms that keep AI subordinate to lived experience rather than replacing it.
