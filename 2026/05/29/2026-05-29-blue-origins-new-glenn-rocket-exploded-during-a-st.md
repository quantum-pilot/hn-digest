# Blue Origin’s New Glenn rocket exploded during a static fire test

- Score: 209 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48318678) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/05/blue-origins-new-glenn-rocket-just-exploded-during-a-static-fire-test/

### TL;DR

Blue Origin’s seven-engine New Glenn first stage exploded shortly after ignition during a static-fire test at LC-36A. No one was injured, the Amazon Leo payload was elsewhere, and the root cause remained unknown, though sources placed the initial problem near the engine section. Early reports described severe pad damage, potentially including an unsalvageable lightning tower and transporter-erector. Ars projects no 2026 return and a difficult first-half 2027 recovery, threatening planned Blue Moon cargo and NASA rover missions. HN discussion added little beyond duplicate-post redirects, jokes, and hostility toward Bezos.
