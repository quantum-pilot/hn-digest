# Microsoft 0-day feud escalates as researcher threatens another exploit dump

- Score: 147 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48328175) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/05/28/microsoft-0-day-feud-escalates-as-researcher-threatens-another-windows-exploit-dump/5248085

### TL;DR

An escalating disclosure dispute put six Windows zero-days in public, with three exploited soon after proof-of-concept code appeared and three still unpatched. Researcher Nightmare Eclipse threatens another release July 14, alleging Microsoft deleted their reporting account, withheld payment and credit, then publicly blamed them. Microsoft says the flaws bypassed official channels and invoked law enforcement. Experts condemn weaponized disclosure but also fault Microsoft’s threatening response, opacity, and weak customer guidance. HN was divided: innocent users bear the risk, yet coordinated disclosure fails when vendors close or mishandle legitimate channels.

### Comment pulse

- Publishing working exploits transfers leverage onto users → three flaws were weaponized within hours — counterpoint: vendors also need consequences when coordination channels fail.

- Bug-bounty triage is operationally difficult → AI spam, incomplete reports, ownership searches, patching, and testing can turn one mishandled case into systemic distrust.
