# It's hard to justify buying a Framework 12

- Score: 201 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48323869) | Link: https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2026/its-hard-to-justify-framework-12/

### TL;DR

Jeff Geerling finds the $499 student MacBook Neo faster, quieter, nearly twice as efficient, better built, and equipped with a much better display than the Framework 12, which starts near $749 DIY or $799 assembled. Framework wins on sustained heavy loads, touchscreen flexibility, modular ports, privacy switches, repairability, upgrades, and Linux support, but its fan, speakers, display, and tablet experience disappoint; his nephew chose the Neo. HN agreed Apple dominates immediate value, yet many argued Framework’s ownership model, longevity, Linux freedom, and mission justify paying more for weaker hardware.
