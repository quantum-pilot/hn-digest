# Blue Origin's New Glenn blows up during static fire test

- Score: 468 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48317774) | Link: https://twitter.com/nasaspaceflight/status/2060164928472854821

### TL;DR

Blue Origin’s New Glenn exploded at Launch Complex 36 during a static-fire attempt ahead of mission NG-4. The supplied post offers only a brief notice and video, so cause and official damage remain unknown. Commenters studying other footage thought failure began near the base, followed by structural collapse and a larger explosion; they feared launch-stand damage could delay operations for at least a year. HN framed the event as both a severe cadence setback and an expected risk of ambitious rocket development, arguing that rapid investigation and iteration now matter.

### Comment pulse

- Root cause remained speculative → footage suggested a base failure and collapse, while commenters warned that sparse telemetry can make investigations unusually difficult.
- Development philosophy split the thread → critics saw catastrophic process failure — counterpoint: others considered visible breakage necessary when pursuing difficult, iterative R&D.
- Infrastructure dominated schedule concerns → a destroyed stand or pad is harder to replace than a vehicle and can become a single point of failure.
