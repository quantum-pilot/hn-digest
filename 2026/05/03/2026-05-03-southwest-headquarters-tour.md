# Southwest Headquarters Tour

- Score: 169 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47998946) | Link: https://katherinemichel.github.io/blog/travel/southwest-headquarters-tour-2026.html

### TL;DR

Katherine Michel’s Southwest headquarters tour traces hidden systems behind roughly 4,000 daily flights. Flight attendants rehearse fires, self-defense, and evacuations; pilots practice emergency scenarios in 23 fixed and 26 full-motion simulators, each of the latter costing $14.2 million. A tornado-fortified Network Operations Center coordinates dispatch, schedules, crews, weather, maintenance, medicine, and disruptions, while TechOps maintains more than 800 Boeing 737s with highly specialized crafts. HN celebrated workplace tours for revealing enormous human effort behind reliable everyday services, especially real-time routing of aircraft, crews, passengers, and safety constraints.
