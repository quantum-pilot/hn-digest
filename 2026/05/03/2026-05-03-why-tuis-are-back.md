# Why TUIs Are Back

- Score: 239 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48000028) | Link: https://wiki.alcidesfonseca.com/blog/why-tuis-are-back/

### TL;DR

Alcides Fonseca argues that TUIs are resurging because native desktop development has become fragmented and inconsistent across Windows, Linux, and macOS, while Electron often sacrifices platform conventions and keyboard workflows. Terminal interfaces offer fast feedback, automation, cross-platform behavior, and effortless remote use through SSH, fitting coding agents and cloud machines. He sees them as a fallback, not an ideal endpoint, and calls for durable, accessible native toolkits plus UI education. HN split between practical SSH-centered adoption and cyberpunk role-play, with critics preferring web interfaces’ standard editing, fonts, and password-manager integration.
