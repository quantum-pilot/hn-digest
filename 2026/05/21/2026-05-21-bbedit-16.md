# BBEdit 16

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48226944) | Link: https://www.barebones.com/products/bbedit/bbedit16.html

### TL;DR

BBEdit 16 brings more than 100 additions and refinements to the long-running macOS text editor, including App Intents-powered Shortcuts, grep-capable text search inside images, project-specific color schemes, streaming AI worksheets, basic vi emulation, improved Git and HTML5 checking, faster SFTP, and broader internal performance work. Upgrades cost $29.99 from older BBEdit 15 licenses or $39.99 from 14.6.9 and earlier, with some recent buyers upgraded free. HN celebrated its longevity, scriptable extensibility, usable free mode, and continued avoidance of subscriptions while debating sustainable software pricing.
