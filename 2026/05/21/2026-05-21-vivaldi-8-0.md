# Vivaldi 8.0

- Score: 339 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48219060) | Link: https://vivaldi.com/blog/vivaldi-on-desktop-8-0/

### TL;DR

Vivaldi 8.0 introduces Unified, a single visual surface spanning tabs, toolbars, panels, and content edges so themes, alignment, translucency, and backgrounds behave consistently. Six onboarding layouts—Simple, Classic, two vertical-tab arrangements, Auto Hide, and Bottom—offer starting points without removing deep customization or integrated mail, calendar, notes, and tab tools. HN users praised workspaces, mouse gestures, printing, and the absence of bundled AI, but debated partial closed-source status, partner-funded economics, site compatibility, and whether another Chromium browser reinforces Google’s Blink dominance.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime users favored hidden workspaces, vertical tabs, sessions, RSS, and minimal layouts; occasional glitches and delayed Chromium updates were accepted tradeoffs.
- Compatibility reports conflicted: some needed Vivaldi when Firefox failed — counterpoint: others cited broken government logins or excellent Firefox performance on Wayland.
- Skeptics questioned auditability and revenue from search, bookmarks, and Direct Match; supporters pointed to downloadable source archives and privacy-oriented incentives.

### LLM perspective

- View: Presets can reduce onboarding cost without sacrificing expert configurability, provided upgrades preserve existing customizations.
- Impact: Browser competition shifts from rendering engines to interface philosophy when rivals share Chromium underneath.
- Watch next: Measure migration retention, startup performance, site regressions, theme compatibility, and adoption across the six layouts.
