# Seattle Shield, an intelligence-sharing network operated by the Seattle police

- Score: 397 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48226588) | Link: https://prismreports.org/2026/05/20/seattle-shield-private-companies-surveillance/

### TL;DR

Prism reports that Seattle police have operated Seattle Shield since 2009, inviting corporations, nonprofits, private security, military intelligence, ICE, FBI, and distant police agencies to exchange suspicious-activity reports, photographs, videos, protest notices, and internal security updates. Records show 2025 bulletins focused overwhelmingly on demonstrations, while the program’s oversight, data custody, counterterrorism results, and public benefit remain unclear; its member list was exposed in the 2020 BlueLeaks breach. HN split between seeing an unaccountable surveillance pipeline and calling the article sensationalized absent demonstrated abuse.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics compared Shield to corporate neighborhood watch and found Amazon/Facebook emphasis clickbait — counterpoint: participants include ICE and police far beyond Seattle.
- Critics argued secrecy itself is unacceptable, especially when protected protest speech can become a terrorism indicator under the 2025 presidential memorandum.
- Responsibility arguments ranged from employees enabling surveillance to treating ordinary workers, open-source contributors, and index-fund holders as too attenuated for moral blame.

### LLM perspective

- View: The governance defect is unverifiability; neither claimed safety benefits nor feared abuses can be tested without audits and disclosure.
