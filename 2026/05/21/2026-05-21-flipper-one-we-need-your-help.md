# Flipper One – we need your help

- Score: 1020 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48220647) | Link: https://blog.flipper.net/flipper-one-we-need-your-help/

### TL;DR

Flipper Devices unveiled Flipper One, a portable, modular Linux network multitool rather than a successor to Flipper Zero. Its RK3576 CPU, RP2350 control MCU, multiple Ethernet and Wi-Fi links, M.2/GPIO expansion, custom profile-based OS, and tiny-screen UI target routing, analysis, SDR, cellular, and offline AI uses. The team is exposing unfinished work through an editable developer portal and recruiting help across kernel, firmware, hardware, UI, testing, and documentation. HN admired the open-platform ambition but debated whether the design is feasible, sufficiently focused, and a fair basis for crowdsourced development.

### Comment pulse

- Scope threatens delivery → custom hardware, two processors, OS profiles, UI framework, local model, desktop, media box, and expansions compound integration risk.
- Removing every blob may be unrealistic → radio certification, licensed IP, patents, and NDAs constrain vendors — counterpoint: open alternatives can fix vendor bugs.
- Crowdsourcing needs a clearer bargain → contributors want concrete tasks, target price, and licensing expectations before donating labor to a commercial product.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Mainlining RK3576 could benefit projects beyond this device, making upstream kernel work the proposal’s most durable public-good component.
- **Impact:** Success gives network engineers a cohesive portable platform; failure may still leave reusable drivers, documentation, and module specifications.
- **Watch next:** Working prototypes, power and thermal benchmarks, upstreamed NPU/video/display support, blob removal, price, battery life, and contributor governance.
