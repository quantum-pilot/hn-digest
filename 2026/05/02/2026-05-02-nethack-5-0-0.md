# NetHack 5.0.0

- Score: 348 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47988776) | Link: https://nethack.org/v500/release.html

### TL;DR

NetHack 5.0.0 modernizes the venerable dungeon crawler with more than 3,100 fixes and changes, C99-compliant source, improved cross-compilation, and Lua replacements for its build-time lex/yacc level and dungeon compilers. The developers warn that a major `.0` release may contain bugs and that old saves and bones files are incompatible. Hacker News greeted the surprise release with nostalgia and enthusiasm, debating the end of the lex/yacc era, Lua’s modding potential, balance changes, graphical clients, and cherished unfinished runs that must remain on older versions.

### Comment pulse

- Save incompatibility stung players preserving decades-old runs, though easy access to historical NetHack versions keeps those adventures playable.
- Replacing lex/yacc tooling with runtime Lua felt historically bittersweet, but commenters welcomed its portability and possibilities for mods and tooling.
- Graphical clients may attract newcomers, while veterans still value ASCII’s compact map and distinctive atmosphere.
