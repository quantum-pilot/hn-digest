# Uber wants to turn its drivers into a sensor grid for self-driving companies

- Score: 118 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47987333) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/01/uber-wants-to-turn-its-millions-of-drivers-into-a-sensor-grid-for-self-driving-companies/

### TL;DR

Uber plans to extend AV Labs from a small dedicated fleet to sensor kits on drivers’ cars, building an “AV cloud” of labeled, queryable road data for autonomous-vehicle partners. Partners can already test models in shadow mode against real trips. Its CTO calls data the industry bottleneck and says regulations need clarification. Hacker News questioned that premise, citing mature simulation and existing fleets, but acknowledged fresh changes and rare events matter; commenters saw driver consent, compensation, sensor cost, and shadow mode as the sharper issues.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics said Waymo’s simulation and Tesla’s huge mileage show that data volume alone is not the decisive AV bottleneck.
- Others argued real-world collection remains valuable for changing roads, unusual events, international variation, and validating imperfect simulation.
- Commenters questioned who pays for sensors and whether drivers consent or share value; several considered existing shadow-mode testing the actual product.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Coverage, freshness, labels, and sensor quality matter more than raw mileage totals.
- **Impact:** Uber could become infrastructure for AV developers without owning the autonomous-driving stack.
