# Six Years Perfecting Maps on WatchOS

- Score: 117 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47990606) | Link: https://www.david-smith.org/blog/2026/04/29/maps-on-watchos/

### TL;DR

After six years, Pedometer++ 8 ships the Apple Watch map its developer had been pursuing: an offline-capable custom SwiftUI tile renderer, bespoke light and dark cartography, overlaid workout metrics, and tap-to-enter browse mode within a vertical page stack. The path ran from server-generated images through a separate modal map, many failed layouts, Liquid Glass constraints, a hired cartographer and designer, and hundreds of field-test miles. Hacker News praised the craftsmanship, lamented Apple Watch’s weak first-party hiking and GPX support, and debated tile-rendering tradeoffs and whether Apple’s platform APIs constrain alternatives.

### Comment pulse

- Readers admired six years of iteration and hiring specialists rather than treating the interface as a one-person engineering problem.
- Some called Apple’s limited hiking and GPX tools a failure; others prefer room for third-party apps over default incumbents.
- Static tiles suit constrained hardware, commenters said, though tile format, styling, rotation, updates, and graphics APIs are distinct issues.
