# Job Postings for Software Engineers Are Rapidly Rising

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47982512) | Link: https://www.citadelsecurities.com/news-and-insights/2026-global-intelligence-crisis/

### TL;DR

Citadel Securities argues that fears of imminent AI-driven labor collapse outrun current evidence: software-engineering postings rose 11% year over year, intensive workplace AI use looks stable, and adoption faces compute, energy, regulatory, and organizational limits. It frames AI as a productivity shock more likely to complement workers, expand output, and redirect demand than erase labor, unless automation becomes near-total without investment or policy response. Hacker News readers challenged the charts and extrapolations, while others saw growing demand for engineers who can architect, review, secure, and maintain AI-generated systems.

### Comment pulse

- Skeptics called chart axes, short windows, and a single adoption datapoint too weak for sweeping claims; others said the live series keeps rising.
- Practitioners described agents as strong at bounded functions but unreliable at architecture; human decomposition, context, and review remain decisive.
- Recruiter attention may signal recovery, but commenters warned AI outreach can inflate activity without corresponding genuine openings.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Near-term evidence supports augmentation more strongly than wholesale displacement.
- **Impact:** Better tools may expand software demand while shifting value toward architecture, security, testing, and review.
- **Watch next:** Posting quality, hiring conversion, daily AI usage, and compensation—not headline recruiter volume alone.
