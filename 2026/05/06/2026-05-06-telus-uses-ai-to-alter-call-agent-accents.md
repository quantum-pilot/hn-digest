# Telus Uses AI to Alter Call-Agent Accents

- Score: 226 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48031109) | Link: https://letsdatascience.com/news/telus-uses-ai-to-alter-call-agent-accents-a3868f63

### TL;DR

Telus Digital is reportedly applying Tomato.ai speech-to-speech conversion to offshore call-centre agents in real time, reducing what the company calls accent-related friction. Labour groups want mandatory disclosure, arguing altered voices can deceive customers; Canadian rivals Rogers and Bell said they have no similar plans. The pipeline must balance latency, naturalness, and resilience to noisy audio. HN sharply split: some non-native speakers welcomed clearer communication, while others called it dehumanizing, said it disguises cost-driven offshoring, and argued poor microphones, background noise, limited context, and scripted sales behavior matter more than accent.

### Comment pulse
