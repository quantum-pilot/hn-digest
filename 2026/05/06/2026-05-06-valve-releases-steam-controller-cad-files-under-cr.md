# Valve releases Steam Controller CAD files under Creative Commons license

- Score: 968 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48037555) | Link: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/news/2026/05/valve-releases-steam-controller-cad-files-under-creative-commons-license

### TL;DR

Valve published external-shell CAD for the new Steam Controller and wireless Puck, including STEP and STL models plus engineering drawings marking signal and functional keep-out zones. The CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms allow attributed, share-alike noncommercial designs; accessory businesses must negotiate separately. Valve envisions stands, grips, skins, and phone mounts, continuing similar releases for Steam Deck, Index, and the original controller. HN praised owner modification, repair, and low-cost custom accessibility hardware, while debating whether Steam-dependent advanced configuration creates subtle platform lock-in despite basic Linux operation outside Steam.

### Comment pulse

- Exact geometry lowers accessory-development barriers → makers can design printable replacement parts and disability-specific controls without measuring the shell from scratch.
- Keep-out drawings expose functional constraints → modifications can preserve wireless performance instead of treating the enclosure as purely mechanical.
- Full features rely on Steam Input → critics see lock-in — counterpoint: Linux detects basic gamepad, keyboard, and mouse functions without Steam.
