# Meta deletes popular 1M follower account after Kuwaiti request

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48170810) | Link: https://twitter.com/ryangrim/status/2055992439031185782

### TL;DR

The submission alleges Meta permanently removed a journalist’s million-follower Instagram account following a Kuwaiti request, but the supplied content is only an amplification post demanding restoration and provides no documentation of Meta’s reason or government involvement. Commenters supplied fragmentary context about journalist Ahmed Shihab-Eldin’s reported Kuwaiti detention and disputed claims about political affiliations, while repeatedly noting the evidence gap. The substantive debate therefore centered less on this case’s merits than on whether platforms should provide specific suspension reasons, appeals, and legal recourse when moderation may serve foreign governments.
