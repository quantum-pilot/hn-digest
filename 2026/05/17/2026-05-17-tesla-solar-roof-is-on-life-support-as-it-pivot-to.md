# Tesla Solar Roof is on life support as it pivot to panels

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48165980) | Link: https://electrek.co/2026/05/14/tesla-solar-roof-promise-vs-reality-pivot-panels/

### TL;DR

Tesla’s integrated solar tiles never approached the promised 1,000 weekly installations: the article estimates roughly 3,000 systems total through early 2023, with a peak near 23 per week. Tesla stopped reporting solar deployments in 2024, shifted installation to third parties, and now promotes conventional TSP-420 panels with 18-zone optimization. Existing owners report service delays and shading-related underperformance. Economics are harsher: about $106,000 before incentives versus $60,000 for a roof plus panels, with 15–25-year payback. HN admired the appearance but viewed cost and opportunity cost as decisive.
