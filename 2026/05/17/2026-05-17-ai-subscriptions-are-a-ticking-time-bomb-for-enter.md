# AI subscriptions are a ticking time bomb for enterprise

- Score: 368 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48168056) | Link: https://www.thestateofbrand.com/news/ai-subscription-time-bomb

### TL;DR

The essay warns that flat-rate enterprise AI seats conceal much higher usage costs: a $20 subscription may represent hundreds of dollars in API-equivalent consumption, with autonomous agents multiplying token burn. It predicts IPO pressure and infrastructure bills will force caps, price increases, or usage billing, leaving embedded workflows exposed; enterprises should meter tokens, stress-test budgets, and preserve vendor choice. HN disputed the premise, separating profitable inference from total R&D losses and arguing competition or open models may lower costs, while agreeing extreme unlimited plans are unlikely to persist.
