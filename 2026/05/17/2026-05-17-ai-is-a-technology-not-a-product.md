# AI is a technology not a product

- Score: 303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48168626) | Link: https://daringfireball.net/2026/05/ai_is_technology_not_a_product

### TL;DR

Gruber argues Apple should treat AI like wireless networking: a pervasive enabling technology woven into products, not a standalone device the company must launch to define an era. He expects agents to change phone interactions but rejects claims that autonomous services or smaller gadgets will soon make phones obsolete; screens, cameras, microphones, and speakers still need a coherent product. HN largely favored invisible, user-centered improvements such as reliable Siri and natural-language shortcuts, while dissenters warned that this framing could excuse incrementalism when an integrated AI product might reshape computing.
