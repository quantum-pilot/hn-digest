# Hindenburg’s Smoking Room

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48140556) | Link: https://www.airships.net/hindenburg-smoking-room/

### TL;DR

The hydrogen-filled Hindenburg carried a popular smoking room and bar. Engineers isolated it behind a double-door airlock, maintained higher pressure to exclude leaking gas, allowed only one supervised electric lighter, and banned other flames throughout the airship. Because hydrogen rises and the room sat low on B Deck, its pressurization may have served public relations as much as safety; an ordinary fire spreading upward was the greater hazard. HN connected the design to multi-day airship travel, historical smoking culture, addiction, and pressure-based engineering.
