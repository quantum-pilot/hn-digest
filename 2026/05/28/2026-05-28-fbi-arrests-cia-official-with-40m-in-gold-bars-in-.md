# FBI Arrests CIA Official with $40M in Gold Bars in His Home

- Score: 451 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48302151) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/27/us/politics/fbi-arrest-cia-official-gold-bars.html

### TL;DR

FBI agents found 303 gold bars worth over $40 million, $2 million cash, and nearly three dozen luxury watches at the Virginia home of David Rush, recently a senior CIA official. Court papers say the CIA supplied him gold and foreign currency for work expenses, then could not locate them. Yet his only current charge concerns allegedly false Navy Reserve and academic claims used to collect tens of thousands in leave pay. HN focused on the unexplained gap, ranging from operational-cash theories to conspiracy and disbelief at CIA controls.
