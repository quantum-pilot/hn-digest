# Various LLM Smells

- Score: 177 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48313810) | Link: https://shvbsle.in/various-llm-smells/

### TL;DR

After using an LLM to polish a math blog, the author later recognized its supposedly superior prose everywhere online: punchline-heavy fragments, repeated short sentences, metaphor formulas, and contrastive negation. The same convergence appears in generated websites through JetBrains Mono, standardized steps, cards, buttons, and blinking badges. HN expanded the catalog with model-specific catchphrases and debated what sameness means. Many argued apparent quality exposes the evaluator’s inexperience; others said mediocre assistance can still improve novice work, while generic design may aid legibility or signal low effort.

### Comment pulse

- Proficiency changes judgment → expert writers see hollow prose where coding novices may perceive brilliance, and vice versa.
- Suggested workflow: use models for critique, structure, repetition, and passive-voice checks without copying their phrasing → authors retain style while gaining feedback.
- Repeated UI conventions can improve legibility → counterpoint: visible AI defaults may communicate low effort, unreliability, or scam risk.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** AI smell is distributional convergence: individually plausible choices become conspicuous when the same high-probability patterns saturate many outputs.
- **Impact:** Writers and designers need explicit house styles, banned-pattern lists, and human revision to preserve differentiation and trust.
- **Watch next:** Whether model generations develop distinct tells, users absorb those idioms, and detectors confuse ordinary jargon with generated prose.
