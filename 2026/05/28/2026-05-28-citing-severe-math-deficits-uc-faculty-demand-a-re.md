# Citing 'severe' math deficits, UC faculty demand a return to SAT tests for STEM

- Score: 386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48309233) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2026-05-27/uc-math-professors-demand-return-of-sat-for-stem-admissions

### TL;DR

More than 600 UC faculty, led by Berkeley mathematicians, want SAT or ACT scores required for STEM applicants beginning fall 2027, arguing six test-free years have obscured readiness. They cite at least 20% of Berkeley first-semester calculus students showing diagnostic deficits in 2021–23 and a UC San Diego report of a roughly thirty-fold rise in entrants below high-school math. Opponents say GPA better predicts success after demographic controls. HN discussion largely favored a readiness screen while disputing what tests measure and where remediation belongs.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Admissions tests can identify readiness gaps, but the reported trends do not isolate test-free policy as their cause.
- **Impact:** Requiring scores could reshape STEM selection, strengthen placement data, and alter access for applicants across unevenly resourced schools.
- **Watch next:** June 30 admissions roadmap, faculty-readiness oversight, Regents action, and comparative outcomes by income, race, and prior preparation.
