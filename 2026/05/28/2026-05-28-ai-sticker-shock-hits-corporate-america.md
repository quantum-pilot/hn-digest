# AI sticker shock hits corporate America

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48307098) | Link: https://www.axios.com/2026/05/28/ai-spending-roi-enterprise-costs

### TL;DR

Companies are reassessing enterprise AI after ballooning bills and unclear productivity gains. Axios cites Microsoft cutting most Claude Code licenses, Uber questioning costs, and one consultant’s client allegedly spending $500 million in a month without usage caps. Leaders blame low-value use cases, indiscriminate license rollouts, costly token consumption, weak employee adoption, and restricted access to proprietary data; some argue AI currently pays mainly in coding. HN saw tokenmaxxing and management incentives as bigger problems than inference itself, noting that more code and merge requests often fail to accelerate milestones.

### Comment pulse
