# Show HN: Hallucinate – Massively Multiplayer Online Rave

- Score: 406 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48304260) | Link: https://hallucinate.site

### TL;DR

Hallucinate is a browser-based multiplayer rave where visitors customize avatars, walk with WASD, dance, bounce, and speak while a video supplies the music. The page itself offers only a compact keyboard guide, but the creator says the TypeScript repository is MIT-licensed and welcomes contributions. HN enjoyed its deliberately chaotic feel and compared it with earlier VR clubs and pandemic-era online festivals. Discussion quickly shifted toward sustaining open creative projects: publish code, add a README, explain animation scaling, improve remote-DJ support, and add a crosshair to reduce camera-induced motion sickness.

### Comment pulse
