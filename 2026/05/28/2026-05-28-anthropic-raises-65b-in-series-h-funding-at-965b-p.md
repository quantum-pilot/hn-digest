# Anthropic raises $65B in Series H funding at $965B post-money valuation

- Score: 234 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48313048) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h

### TL;DR

Anthropic says it raised $65 billion at a $965 billion post-money valuation after annualized run-rate revenue crossed $47 billion, up from $14 billion in February. The round includes $15 billion previously committed by hyperscalers and will fund safety research, products, and compute expansion through up to five gigawatts from Amazon, another five from Google/Broadcom, plus SpaceX capacity. HN reacted with awe and skepticism: commenters stressed run rate is extrapolation, questioned whether private investors are capturing most upside before an IPO, and debated Anthropic’s durability versus OpenAI.

### Comment pulse

- The growth claim needs context → run-rate annualizes a recent period rather than reporting booked yearly revenue — counterpoint: rapid growth can make it conservative.
- A near-trillion private valuation shifts IPO risk → private holders capture early upside, while retail may buy a fully priced story.
- Market leadership remains unsettled → revenue momentum favors Anthropic, but billing changes, cheaper substitutes, and strong OpenAI products could quickly alter retention.
