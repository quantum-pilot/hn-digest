# AMD pulls a bait-and-switch on Linux users with Vivado licensing changes

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48307231) | Link: https://itsfoss.com/news/amd-vivado-bait-and-switch-on-linux-users/

### TL;DR

AMD’s Vivado 2026.1 licensing makes the free Basic FPGA-design tier Windows-only; Linux now requires the Core tier, reported at $1,200–$1,800 annually. Linux users may remain on 2025.2, but official support ends after 2026.3 arrives. The article calls this a betrayal of students, researchers, and hobbyists who built workflows around free Linux access. HN discussion broadly criticized the reputational damage, while clarifying that Vivado was already mainly paid and debating the economics of bundling software with FPGA hardware.

### Comment pulse

- Some interpret the paywall as monetizing sophisticated commercial Linux users rather than cutting support → a financially rational choice with poor marketing.
- Critics argue free tooling sells more FPGA hardware and attracts future engineers → counterpoint: enterprise buyers absorb development costs and license fees.
- Open bitstreams and FOSS tools offer insulation from vendor policy changes → nascent alternatives still lack silicon and AMD’s investment scale.
