# Sam Altman and Dario Amodei are both walking back AI jobs apocalypse predictions

- Score: 165 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48314363) | Link: https://fortune.com/2026/05/26/sam-altman-dario-amodei-walking-back-ai-jobs-apocalypse-prophecies-ipo/

### TL;DR

Sam Altman now says entry-level white-collar losses have arrived more slowly than he expected, while Dario Amodei has shifted from warning of 50% job elimination to arguing that automating 90% of a role can expand its remaining work. Fortune contrasts 115,000 tech layoffs through May 2026 with Yale findings of no broad labor-market shift and notes both companies’ reported IPO plans. HN readers largely suspected investor-friendly repositioning, though workplace accounts ranged from hallucination-prone assistance to autonomous problem-solving and warned that executives still confuse augmentation with replacement.
