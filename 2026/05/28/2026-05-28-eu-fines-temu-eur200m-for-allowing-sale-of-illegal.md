# EU fines Temu €200M for allowing sale of illegal products

- Score: 298 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48309302) | Link: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1k2ydn1rz8o

### TL;DR

The European Commission fined Temu €200 million under the Digital Services Act, finding it failed to assess systemic risks from illegal goods after mystery shoppers found unsafe chargers and baby toys. Temu must submit a corrective plan by August 28; regulators then have two months to judge compliance. Temu disputes the penalty as disproportionate and says it reflects its 2024 systems, not current controls. HN commenters supported marketplace liability for safety while debating cheap direct imports’ value, enforcement scalability, possible improvements, and whether Amazon and eBay receive comparable scrutiny.

### Comment pulse

- Critics call case-by-case fines whack-a-mole → supporters say distributor liability creates the missing incentive to screen otherwise unreachable suppliers.
- Some users report newer geoblocking of noncompliant goods → that supports Temu’s improvement claim, without resolving whether 2024 failures merit punishment.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The ruling treats marketplace scale as control over distribution and therefore responsibility, not neutral intermediation.
- **Impact:** Marketplaces may face higher compliance costs and reduced inventory; suppliers gain stronger incentives to meet EU safety requirements.
- **Watch next:** Any appeal, remediation results, Amazon’s DSA investigation, and UK use of new marketplace-liability powers.
