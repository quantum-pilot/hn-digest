# Google employee charged with $1M Polymarket insider trading bet on search term

- Score: 300 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48302822) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/27/google-employee-polymarket-insider-trading.html

### TL;DR

Federal prosecutors allege Google security engineer Michele Spagnuolo used confidential Year in Search data to bet that singer d4vd would be 2025’s most searched person, earning about $1.2 million through Polymarket account AlphaRaccoon. He faces commodities fraud, wire fraud, and money-laundering charges, plus a CFTC civil case; Google placed him on leave, and he was released on $2.25 million bond without entering a plea. HN debated whether prediction markets should reward informed insiders, warned ordinary bettors face severe information asymmetry, and questioned the novel fraud theory beyond traditional securities law.
