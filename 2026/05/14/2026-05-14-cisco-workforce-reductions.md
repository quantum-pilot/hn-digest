# Cisco workforce reductions

- Score: 260 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48130123) | Link: https://blogs.cisco.com/news/our-path-forward

### TL;DR

Cisco will eliminate fewer than 4,000 jobs—under 5% of staff—in Q4 FY2026, despite record quarterly revenue of $15.8 billion, up 12% year over year, and double-digit top- and bottom-line growth. CEO Chuck Robbins framed the cuts as shifting investment toward silicon, optics, security, and companywide AI amid competition and component shortages. Affected workers receive prorated bonuses, placement help, and one year of Cisco U access. Commenters overwhelmingly saw the juxtaposition as investor-oriented cost cutting, though former employees also described genuine organizational slack.

### Comment pulse

- Former Cisco staff described finishing sprint work in days and remaining idle, suggesting excess capacity alongside weak internal allocation.
- Many called record results plus layoffs inhumane — counterpoint: investors may interpret simultaneous growth and cost cuts as disciplined management.
- Commenters suspected AI rhetoric gives profitable tech firms a convenient reason to copy peers’ reductions and satisfy market expectations.

### LLM perspective

- View: The announcement presents no financial emergency; it describes portfolio reallocation from a position of strength.
- Impact: Departing staff absorb restructuring risk while favored business units inherit AI-era investment expectations.
- Watch next: Functions reduced, placement success, operating margins, AI productivity evidence, and hiring in priority areas.
