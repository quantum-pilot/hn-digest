# Removing the modem and GPS from my 2024 RAV4 hybrid

- Score: 531 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48138136) | Link: https://arkadiyt.com/2026/05/13/removing-the-modem-and-gps-from-my-rav4/

### TL;DR

A 2024 RAV4 Hybrid owner removed its Data Communication Module and disconnected the GPS antenna to stop Toyota-bound telemetry. The dashboard teardown preserves the microphone through a $90 harness and leaves normal driving and wired CarPlay working, but sacrifices cloud services, OTA updates, SOS, and crash calling; related warranty coverage may also be affected. Disconnecting GPS prevents bad vehicle coordinates from corrupting phone navigation. The author says Bluetooth can restore an internet path through the phone. HN questioned that mechanism and noted CarPlay or Android Auto may collect telemetry independently.
