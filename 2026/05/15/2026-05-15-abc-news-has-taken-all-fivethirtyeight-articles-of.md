# ABC News has taken all FiveThirtyEight articles offline

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48152553) | Link: https://twitter.com/baseballot/status/2055309076209492208

### TL;DR

FiveThirtyEight’s published archive is no longer available from ABC News, and Nathaniel Rakich highlighted a community-built index of material preserved by the Internet Archive, including articles, datasets, podcasts, graphics, and illustrations. Commenters mourned distinctive interactive work and questioned why a corporation would discard a recognizable politics-and-sports brand. They also noted its difficult economics: broad interest peaked during presidential-election years, leaving three lean years between surges. Debate split blame between ABC’s acquisition management and founders who sold, while former staff have continued similar work elsewhere.

### Comment pulse

- Nate Silver said ABC refused any buyback because he criticized its management — counterpoint: some readers blamed his original sale for enabling the outcome.
- Fans singled out interactive visualizations on gun deaths, p-hacking, microbiomes, and burritos, urging backups of surviving repositories before more artifacts disappear.
- Seasonal economics undermined the newsroom: presidential years attracted general audiences, while off-years relied on polling enthusiasts despite corporate ownership’s theoretically deeper financial buffer.
