# Project Gutenberg – keeps getting better

- Score: 670 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48150431) | Link: https://www.gutenberg.org/

### TL;DR

Project Gutenberg’s refreshed site presents more than 75,000 free, volunteer-digitized public-domain ebooks with cleaner browsing, covers, categories, reading lists, search, direct EPUB and Kindle downloads, and human or computer-read audiobooks, still requiring no account or app. A project programmer said more improvements are coming. HN praised the accessible redesign and institution’s continuity since 1971, while requesting book-level version histories, provenance, date-range search, and mobile fixes. Discussion also surfaced ISP blocks in Italy and Germany, weak e-reader integration, and vendor incentives to favor paid stores.

### Comment pulse

- Readers want transparent revision histories → corrections currently arrive by email, while per-book repositories could expose provenance, scans, and textual changes.
- Direct e-reader discovery remains missing → vendors benefit from paid catalogs, although capable device browsers can download Gutenberg or Standard Ebooks files.
- Personal stories emphasized access over polish: free classics, volunteer proofreading, and simple Kindle workflows sustained reading across generations.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Digital preservation works best when durable files, low-friction access, provenance, and ongoing correction reinforce one another.
- **Impact:** Better discovery can connect more readers to public-domain culture without turning a volunteer archive into another platform dependency.
- **Watch next:** Track revision metadata, publication-date filters, mobile layout, international availability, e-reader integrations, and volunteer throughput.
