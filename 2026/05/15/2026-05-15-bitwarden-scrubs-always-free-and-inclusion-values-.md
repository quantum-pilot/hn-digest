# Bitwarden scrubs 'Always free' and 'Inclusion' values from its site

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48147637) | Link: https://www.fastcompany.com/91542655/bitwarden-scrubs-always-free-and-inclusion-values-from-its-website-as-longtime-execs-step-down

### TL;DR

Fast Company links several changes at Bitwarden: longtime CEO Michael Crandell quietly became an adviser, M&A-experienced Michael Sullivan replaced him, the CFO departed, and website language around “Always free” and “Inclusion” disappeared. Bitwarden says its free plan remains robust; “Always free” returned to pricing after an employee called its removal a marketing oversight. HN treated the pattern, recent price increases, and past investment as possible sale or profit-extraction signals, though commenters acknowledged this is inference and the open-source software remains self-hostable.

### Comment pulse
