# Microsoft pulls plug on plans for 244-acre data center in Caledonia (2025)

- Score: 166 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48266422) | Link: https://www.tmj4.com/news/racine-county/microsoft-pulls-plug-on-plans-for-244-acre-data-center-in-caledonia-after-community-pushback

### TL;DR

Microsoft abandoned a proposed 244-acre data-center site in Caledonia, Wisconsin after hundreds of residents objected and more than 2,000 signed a rezoning petition. The farmland-adjacent parcel sat near homes and a power plant. Microsoft still wants to invest in southeast Wisconsin. HN debated whether 244 acres was excessive, noted nearby skepticism after the Foxconn development failure, and highlighted how buying land before uncertain, multi-year permitting favors large companies able to carry idle property.

### Comment pulse

- Permitting imposes financing barriers → businesses may hold unproductive land for years before approval, advantaging deep-pocketed developers with established planning-board relationships.
- Site scale needs context → 244 acres includes power, cooling, security, and redundancy infrastructure, yet commenters still placed it at the extreme upper end.
- Local resistance was not categorical → residents celebrated stopping this parcel — counterpoint: they invited Microsoft to find a compatible regional location through earlier consultation.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** This reflects a siting-process failure more than rejection of infrastructure: trust collapsed before Microsoft established shared local benefits.
- **Impact:** Municipalities need staged site options and pre-application consultation; developers need plans for power, cooling, security, land use, and reuse.
- **Watch next:** Microsoft’s next Wisconsin site, revised community outreach, zoning application timing, and measurable commitments offered to host communities.
