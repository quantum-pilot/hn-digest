# Netherlands Seizes 800 Servers, Arrests 2 for Aiding Cyberattacks

- Score: 261 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48266906) | Link: https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/05/netherlands-seizes-800-servers-arrests-2-for-aiding-cyberattacks/

### TL;DR

Dutch financial-crime investigators arrested MIRhosting founder Andrey Nesterenko and WorkTitans controller Youssef Zinad, seizing more than 800 servers while investigating alleged sanctions violations. Authorities say their infrastructure kept EU-sanctioned Stark Industries connected after earlier providers were targeted; Stark has been linked to Russian DDoS attacks, influence operations, and activity around Denmark’s 2025 elections. The operators deny knowingly enabling attacks or evading sanctions. HN commenters debated whether the hosts were intelligence fronts or mixed-use providers, and why skilled engineers choose criminal infrastructure work.

### Comment pulse

- Some characterized the companies as Russian-intelligence fronts — counterpoint: former customers reported ordinary VPS sales and demanded evidence for that broader claim.
- Defenders puzzled over talent serving cybercrime; replies cited higher pay, technical autonomy, scarce legitimate jobs, thrill, and infrastructure born from attackers’ needs.
- Commenters associated Dutch hosting with abundant bandwidth and lenient sentencing, though these explanations were conjecture rather than evidence.

### LLM perspective

- View: Sanctions enforcement targets dependency chains, making connectivity providers accountable even when malicious workloads originate elsewhere.
- Impact: Server seizures can disrupt hostile operations but also erase uninvolved customers’ data and chill permissive hosting markets.
- Watch next: Charges, attribution evidence, infrastructure recovery, customer remediation, and whether Stark’s network reappears through another provider.
