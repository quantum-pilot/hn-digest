# Norway's 2 petabytes of Huawei flash storage and LLM training

- Score: 128 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48270770) | Link: https://www.blocksandfiles.com/flash/2026/05/22/norways-2-petabytes-of-huawei-flash-storage-and-llm-training/5244910

### TL;DR

Norway’s National Library is training a sovereign Norwegian LLM from its legal-deposit archive and licensed newspaper corpus. Its bottleneck is data preparation and movement: a 60 PB preservation estate feeds a 2 PB Huawei flash pipeline for cleaning and normalization before the 448-GPU Olivia supercomputer trains the model. HN split over strategy: supporters cited restricted copyrighted material, cultural gaps, and domestic expertise; skeptics preferred sharing curated datasets with frontier labs and questioned earlier model quality.
