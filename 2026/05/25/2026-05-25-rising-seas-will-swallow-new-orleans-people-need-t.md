# Rising seas will swallow New Orleans. People need to start relocating now

- Score: 124 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48265049) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/25/climate/new-orleans-sea-level-rise-relocation

### TL;DR

A Nature Sustainability analysis argues coastal Louisiana is committed to 10–23 feet of relative sea-level rise, potentially eliminating 75% of wetlands and isolating New Orleans. Rather than let departures occur in crisis-driven bursts, its authors urge managed relocation before falling property values, insurance withdrawal, service erosion, and adaptation costs trap poorer residents. The article also stresses cultural loss and Black residents’ distrust after Katrina. HN disputed the timeline and framing, emphasizing land subsidence, uncertain century-scale projections, compensation-driven moral hazard, and comparisons between buyouts and stronger flood defenses.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Relocation policy fails if it assumes hazard awareness alone overrides rational incentives, cultural attachment, and distrust of institutions.
- **Impact:** Early buyouts, building limits, relocation assistance, and community-led destination planning could shift retreat from emergency response to public investment.
- **Watch next:** Separate subsidence and ocean-rise scenarios, publish timelines and uncertainty, compare relocation, wetland restoration, and levee costs by neighborhood.
