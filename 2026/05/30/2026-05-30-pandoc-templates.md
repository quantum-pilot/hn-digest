# Pandoc Templates

- Score: 354 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48334515) | Link: https://pandoc-templates.org/

### TL;DR

Pandoc Templates is a browsable catalog for turning Markdown into polished PDFs, LaTeX, HTML, DOCX, EPUB, slides, and other formats. It filters by output and document type—from theses, papers, and résumés to letters, books, invoices, recipes, and presentations—and shows previews, repository links, stars, and update dates. HN users welcomed discovering reusable designs and described automated multi-format publishing workflows. The main disagreement was audience and complexity: enthusiasts praised Pandoc’s ecosystem and Quarto or Typst integrations, while others found PDF typography brittle and preferred Word’s WYSIWYG control.

### Comment pulse

- Pandoc and Quarto occupy different layers → Pandoc excels at conversion, while Quarto adds a friendlier authoring and documentation workflow.
- PDF output inherits typesetter complexity → LaTeX can mishandle tables, fonts, and page breaks — counterpoint: Typst reportedly offers a smoother backend.
- Plain-text publishing improves automation → novels and papers can generate multiple versioned formats in CI, but nontechnical users may prefer WYSIWYG.
