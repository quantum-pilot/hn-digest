# Proposed new US funding rules: We can cancel any grant at any time

- Score: 311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48335135) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/05/the-office-of-management-and-budget-tries-again-to-cripple-us-science/

### TL;DR

An OMB proposal would replace agency-specific grant practices with government-wide rules that subordinate peer review to political discretion. Agencies could reject or cancel awards whenever they deem them misaligned with administration priorities or the undefined “national interest.” The rules would prohibit funding on DEI and gender topics, scrutinize applicants’ affiliations, restrict international collaboration—especially with China—and require case-by-case approval for publication and conference costs. Ars argues this would politicize research, isolate US scientists, and make funding permanently uncertain. HN offered no substantive debate, redirecting readers to a duplicate thread.
