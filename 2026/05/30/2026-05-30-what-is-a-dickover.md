# What Is a Dickover?

- Score: 492 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48330882) | Link: https://daringfireball.net/2026/05/what_is_a_dickover

### TL;DR

John Gruber proposes “dickover” for an unnecessary modal panel or full-screen curtain that blocks a site’s own content until users dismiss cookie, newsletter, app-install, terms, or similar demands. He distinguishes these from partial, nonmodal “dickbars” and from necessary blockers such as paywall authentication. Substack’s deceptive full-screen subscription prompt is a central example; delayed interruptions are likened to snatching reading material away mid-page. Hacker News embraces the memorable label, praises the article’s self-demonstrating popup, and blames repeat prompts on poor testing, ignored consent state, and conversion incentives.
