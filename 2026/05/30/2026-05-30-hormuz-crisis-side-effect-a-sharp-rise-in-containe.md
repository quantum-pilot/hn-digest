# Hormuz crisis side effect: a sharp rise in container shipping rates

- Score: 163 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48339180) | Link: https://www.lloydslist.com/LL1157327/Hormuz-crisis-side-effect-a-sharp-rise-in-container-shipping-rates

### TL;DR

The effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz has pushed average low-sulphur bunker fuel up 68% since mid-February, and carriers are passing costs to importers. The SCFI global container index doubled from late February to 2,572, its highest since September 2024; Drewry puts Shanghai–Los Angeles and Shanghai–New York up 59% and 66%. Red Sea avoidance, slower sailing, and congestion remove an estimated 19% of effective capacity despite new ships. Hacker News views this as a foreseeable systems consequence, then debates wider food and economic spillovers.
