# Corporate America Is Starting to Ration AI as Cost Skyrockets

- Score: 140 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48335388) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/corporate-america-is-starting-to-ration-ai-as-cost-skyrockets-1eb99d7a

### TL;DR

Enterprises that encouraged unrestricted AI experimentation are now rationing access as token bills double or triple. Uber reportedly exhausted its annual agentic-AI budget by March; Microsoft cited standardization in narrowing Claude Code access, Meta flagged overlapping tools, and Salesforce began tying consumption to outcomes. Google still processes 3.2 quadrillion tokens monthly, seven times last year, so this looks like optimization rather than retreat. EntelligenceAI says only 18% of coding-token spending reaches shipped products. HN questioned whether executives can distinguish ROI from usage and whether metered models belong in recurring workflows.

### Comment pulse

- Usage metrics masquerade as ROI → lines committed, pull requests, and token volume reward visible activity rather than customer or revenue outcomes.
- Recurring deterministic work should become software → repeatedly prompting models for diffs, reports, or log analysis creates slower, less accurate metered dependencies.
- Public product impact understates AI’s value → many gains are private tools — counterpoint: review debt can erase apparent productivity improvements.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Rationing marks the transition from subsidized experimentation to portfolio management, where model choice must match task economics.
- **Impact:** Teams will route simple queries to cheaper models and convert repeated successful prompts into deterministic internal tools.
- **Watch next:** Measure shipped value, review cost, error rates, vendor outages, reusable-tool creation, and spend by task and model.
