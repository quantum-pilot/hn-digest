# Leo's first encyclical attacks technological messianism

- Score: 163 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48334710) | Link: https://www.economist.com/europe/2026/05/28/leos-first-encyclical-attacks-technological-messianism

### TL;DR

Pope Leo’s first encyclical, Magnifica Humanitas, presents unregulated AI as a philosophical threat. It rejects transhumanist and posthumanist ambitions to optimize or surpass humanity, warning that treating people as improvable components can make some lives seem expendable; human limits, failure, and “woundedness” are instead sources of dignity. Leo credits technology’s benefits while urging AI’s “disarming” and multilateralism. The Economist adds an irony: stylistic clues and a Pangram scan suggested parts may be Claude-generated. HN debated who can control technology amid corporate power, democratic regulation, and arms-race pressures.

### Comment pulse

- Control, not capability, was the central concern → commenters questioned whether executives, technologists, elected governments, religious authorities, or international bodies should decide.
- Corporate accountability drew anger → commenters argued firms externalize labor disruption while controlling deployment and avoiding the social costs they create.
- Regulation split the thread → advocates wanted democratic or supranational oversight — counterpoint: critics said global competition makes uniform restraint impractical.
