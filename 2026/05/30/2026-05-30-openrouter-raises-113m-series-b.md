# OpenRouter raises $113M Series B

- Score: 337 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48338660) | Link: https://openrouter.ai/announcements/series-b

### TL;DR

OpenRouter raised a $113 million Series B led by CapitalG, with strategic participation from Nvidia, ServiceNow, MongoDB, Snowflake, Databricks, and existing investors. The model-routing gateway says weekly traffic rose from 5 trillion to 25 trillion tokens in six months, serving more than 8 million developers across 400-plus models. It plans to scale infrastructure, enterprise controls, multimodal inference, and routing for cost, latency, quality, and failover. HN users valued consolidated billing, caps, and easy experimentation, but questioned privacy, a 5% surcharge, API abstraction problems, and long-term differentiation.
