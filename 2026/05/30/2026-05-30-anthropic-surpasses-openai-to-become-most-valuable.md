# Anthropic surpasses OpenAI to become most valuable AI startup

- Score: 386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48336233) | Link: https://qazinform.com/news/anthropic-surpasses-openai-to-become-worlds-most-valuable-ai-startup

### TL;DR

Kazinform reports that Anthropic raised $65 billion in a Series H, lifting its private valuation from roughly $380 billion in February to nearly $1 trillion, above OpenAI’s reported $852 billion. The article attributes momentum to Claude, Claude Code, enterprise demand, and reported annual revenue growth from $10 billion to $47 billion. Hacker News disputes what the valuation signifies: some credit superior agentic workflow and corporate sales, others see marketing, purchasing lag, and contentious pricing. Leadership reputations also shape preferences, though commenters reject portraying either company as ethically clean.
