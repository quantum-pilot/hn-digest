# EY Canada published a cybersecurity report and most citations were hallucinated

- Score: 264 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48339580) | Link: https://gptzero.me/investigations/ey

### TL;DR

GPTZero investigated EY Canada’s 2025 report on loyalty-program cybercrime and says most of its 27 references were fabricated, broken, generic, or misattributed. It found nonexistent Gartner, McKinsey, Forbes, Wired, and Cisco sources; contradictory $200 billion market claims; and statistics whose scope, date, or origin changed across pages. The investigation argues such authoritative-looking errors spread through newspapers and AI research tools, contaminating later answers. HN focused on the organizational failure: expert review is overloaded or bypassed, while verifying polished AI output can cost more than producing sound work directly.
