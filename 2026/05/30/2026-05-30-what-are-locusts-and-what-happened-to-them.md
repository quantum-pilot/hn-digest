# What are locusts and what happened to them?

- Score: 213 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48281772) | Link: https://explosion-scratch.github.io/locusts/

### TL;DR

Locusts are not a separate insect but certain grasshoppers transformed by crowding: touch, smell, or sight triggers serotonin-driven behavioral and physical changes, producing voracious swarms. The Rocky Mountain locust’s 1874 Albert’s Swarm may have contained 12.5 trillion insects and devastated Great Plains crops, yet the species disappeared by 1902. The leading explanation is habitat concentration: its enormous migratory range depended on a few Rocky Mountain river valleys for breeding, and settlers inadvertently destroyed egg beds through plowing, irrigation, and livestock. HN highlighted the extinction paradox and cannibalistic marching.
