# Accenture to acquire Ookla

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48337987) | Link: https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/accenture-to-acquire-ookla-to-strengthen-network-intelligence-and-experience-with-data-and-ai-for-enterprises

### TL;DR

Accenture agreed to acquire Ookla from Ziff Davis, adding Speedtest, Downdetector, Ekahau, and RootMetrics to its network-intelligence business. Ookla says its platform draws on more than 250 million consumer-initiated tests monthly and over 1,000 attributes per test, supporting telecom benchmarking, cloud and edge resilience, and enterprise Wi-Fi and private 5G diagnostics. The roughly 430-person company would broaden Accenture’s data and AI offerings; price was undisclosed, and closing remains subject to regulatory approval. HN viewed the deal primarily as buying data, distribution, contracts, and trust—not difficult code.
