# Voxel Space (2017)

- Score: 242 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48336564) | Link: https://s-macke.github.io/VoxelSpace/

### TL;DR

This 2017 explainer reconstructs Comanche’s striking 1992 terrain renderer, a CPU-era 2.5D technique built from paired 1024×1024 height and color maps. For each distance slice, it samples a map line, projects heights, and paints vertical screen columns; front-to-back traversal, a per-column visibility buffer, and coarser distant steps improve speed. Prebaked color supplies texture, shading, and shadows, while one height per map coordinate excludes overhangs and complex objects. Hacker News debates whether “voxel” is technically accurate, shares ports, and recalls attempting similar renderers on early PCs.

### Comment pulse

- Critics call it a heightfield of fixed-base prisms, not volumetric pixels — counterpoint: each sample can be interpreted as a vertical voxel column.
- Historical terminology favored “voxel” because it communicated novelty; developers routinely accepted restricted rotation and geometry to fit contemporary hardware.
- Readers recreated the method in C++, Visual Basic, and AGS, demonstrating how a tiny core algorithm invites experimentation across generations.
