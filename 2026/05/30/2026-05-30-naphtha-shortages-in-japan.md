# Naphtha shortages in Japan

- Score: 153 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48331786) | Link: https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-data/h02783/

### TL;DR

The article attributes Japan’s naphtha disruption to the Iran war, with effects reaching consumer materials. Calbee will switch 14 snack products to monochrome packs because printing inks and solvents are scarce; Mizkan suspended four nattō products over container risks, while syringes, rubber gloves, insulation, and food films face shortages, price rises, or sales limits. Teikoku Databank maps 46,741 manufacturers—about 30% of its sample—into the naphtha network, despite government assurances of adequate supply. HN debated packaging and marketing, but stressed that medical and industrial constraints are the more consequential signal.
