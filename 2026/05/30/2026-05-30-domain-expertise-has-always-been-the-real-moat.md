# Domain expertise has always been the real moat

- Score: 199 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48340411) | Link: https://www.brethorsting.com/blog/2026/05/domain-expertise-has-always-been-the-real-moat/

### TL;DR

The essay argues coding agents make software production cheaper without making correctness easier to judge. A domain specialist may recognize impossible outputs that a generalist engineer accepts, while the engineer can assess architecture, reliability, and code quality that the specialist cannot. The scarce combination is expertise at both layers, with agents handling transcription and humans supplying ground truth and verification. Hacker News broadly values domain knowledge but challenges the forecast’s certainty, stressing that software engineering remains its own domain and AI expands project complexity rather than eliminating engineers.
