# Where Are the Vibecoded Photoshops?

- Score: 257 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48177228) | Link: https://indiepixel.de/blog/posts/where-are-the-vibecoded-photoshops/

### TL;DR

The author argues that AI has cheapened code production but not verification or architectural judgment, explaining why no prompt-only equivalents of Photoshop, Excel, or operating systems have appeared. They reject vibecoded as an evidence-free label that erases the testing and decisions behind AI-assisted work and discourages creators from sharing. HN broadly agreed that coding is not product development, but challenged the Photoshop test: AI’s real disruption may be private, disposable microtools that replace narrow workflows rather than mature general-purpose applications, with quality and maintainability still contested.

### Comment pulse
