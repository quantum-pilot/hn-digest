# I’ve built a virtual museum with nearly every operating system you can think of

- Score: 602 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48195009) | Link: https://virtualosmuseum.org/

### TL;DR

A one-person preservation project packages more than 1,700 preconfigured installations spanning 570 operating systems, 250 platforms, and computing history from 1948 onward into a Linux VM for QEMU, VirtualBox, or UTM. Its launcher hides emulator quirks, includes snapshots, and offers either a complete offline archive or a lite, download-on-demand edition. After 20 years of collecting and patching, the curator aims to make historical software immediately runnable. HN praised the scale while debating representative versions, rare omissions, and lost platform-specific behaviors.
