# Tesla's lithium refinery discharges 231,000 gallons of polluted wastewater a day

- Score: 437 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48198551) | Link: https://www.autonocion.com/us/tesla-lithium-refinery-texas/

### TL;DR

Tesla’s Robstown lithium refinery has a Texas permit to release 231,000 gallons of treated wastewater daily, but the drainage district says nobody notified it that its ditch would convey the flow. State outfall tests found no violation while omitting lithium and heavy metals; a later downstream sample found trace hexavalent chromium, arsenic, strontium, elevated lithium, and other industrial markers. Tesla says downstream contamination can have other sources. HN debated regulatory blind spots, infrastructure rights, and whether low concentrations support the article’s alarming framing.

### Comment pulse

- Legal authorization is fragmented → TCEQ permitted water quality, but commenters argued the drainage district separately controls use of its conveyance infrastructure.
- The independent sample is suggestive, not attribution → downstream collection can capture other sources — counterpoint: its industrial chemical pattern may still implicate Tesla.
- Measured toxicity may be overstated → arsenic was below federal drinking-water limits and chromium barely exceeded California’s limit; commenters questioned natural background levels.
