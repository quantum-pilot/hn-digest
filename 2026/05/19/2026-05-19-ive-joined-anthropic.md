# I’ve joined Anthropic

- Score: 1174 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48194352) | Link: https://twitter.com/karpathy/status/2056753169888334312

### TL;DR

Andrej Karpathy says he has joined Anthropic, returning to hands-on R&D because he expects the next few years of frontier LLM work to be formative; he also intends eventually to resume education work. Commenters said he will join pre-training and build a team using Claude to accelerate pre-training research, framing the hire as both technically promising and a strong branding move. Discussion welcomed his teaching influence but wondered whether confidentiality will constrain it, while a skeptical thread cast Anthropic’s recruiting as industry consolidation without naming clear harms.

### Comment pulse

- Recursive research automation is the technical bet → commenters linked his role to using Claude to accelerate pre-training experimentation.

- The hire strengthens Anthropic’s reputation → Karpathy is widely respected for both ML research and unusually accessible education.

- Talent concentration prompted unease → one commenter likened Anthropic to an industry tornado — counterpoint: challengers could not identify concrete destruction.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Researcher-supervised self-improvement is a narrower, testable path than claims of fully autonomous AI research.

- **Impact:** Faster experiment cycles could let Anthropic test more hypotheses per compute budget, improving efficiency beyond merely scaling runs.

- **Watch next:** Track team formation, experiment throughput, training-efficiency gains, disclosed methods, and whether Karpathy’s public teaching continues.
