# Polypad

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48166744) | Link: https://polypad.amplify.com/

### TL;DR

Polypad is a free browser-based mathematical playground offering interactive manipulatives for fractions, geometry, functions, probability, data, logic, and musical representations. It requires no login or installation, works across devices, supports customization, and lets teachers assign activities and observe student work. HN readers praised its polish, mobile experience, classroom potential, and art contest. Technically, they highlighted its framework-free interactive UI and open-source supporting libraries, while noting the product itself is proprietary and discussing the geometry, pedagogy, and organizational complexity behind educational software.
