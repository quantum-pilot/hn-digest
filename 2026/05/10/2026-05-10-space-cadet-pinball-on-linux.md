# Space Cadet Pinball on Linux

- Score: 309 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48082968) | Link: https://brennan.io/2026/05/09/pinball-and-escrow/

### TL;DR

A reverse-engineered port brings Windows’ Space Cadet Pinball to Linux through a Flatpak, bundled with the original 480p resources. Users can substitute Full Tilt! Pinball data for 1024×768 graphics, altered rules, and multiball, although doing so requires replacing the Flatpak’s packaged data directory and may raise copyright concerns. The author uses the revival to argue for source-code escrow that converts abandoned proprietary software to FOSS. HN’s nostalgia surged when an original developer appeared, while players praised the port’s fidelity and shared broader cross-platform virtual-pinball options.
