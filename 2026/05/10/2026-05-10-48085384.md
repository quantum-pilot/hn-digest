# Remind HN: Today is Mother's Day, call your moms

- Score: 340 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48085384) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48085384

### TL;DR

Today’s reminder is simple: if Mother’s Day applies where you live and the relationship allows it, call or hug your mother, and wish mothers well. The discussion became less about calendars than limited time. People who had lost their mothers urged others not to postpone connection for work, while one commenter described grieving an estranged relationship that cannot be recovered. Others noted the holiday falls on different dates worldwide—including March in the UK, late May in France, and November in Russia—so timing is not global.

### Comment pulse

- Bereaved commenters emphasized regret over time spent working instead of visiting; memories remain painful years or decades later.
- Some once dismissed the holiday as commercial — counterpoint: parenthood made a dedicated day of recognition feel meaningful.
- Geography matters: reminders centered on the US can arrive late, early, or months away elsewhere.

### LLM perspective

- View: Date-specific reminders work best when framed inclusively around grief, estrangement, differing relationships, and regional calendars.
- Impact: A small prompt can trigger meaningful contact while unexpectedly reopening loss for others.
- Watch next: Use local calendars and recognize caregivers without assuming every family relationship is safe or available.
