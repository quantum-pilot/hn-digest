# Task Paralysis and AI

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48081469) | Link: https://g5t.de/articles/20260510-task-paralysis-and-ai/index.html

### TL;DR

The undiagnosed author distinguishes task paralysis—mental shutdown before the first step—from analysis paralysis, and says Claude Code supplies enough momentum to execute plans. Yet the near-instant idea-to-result loop creates a powerful reward cycle: Pro became Max plus API credits, making token limits and extra purchases feel addiction-like. They avoid AI-generated art over labor and copyright concerns but still value coding assistance. HN responses echoed both sides: agents can remove inertia and drudgery, yet may erode craftsmanship, intrinsic motivation, and lasting coping skills.
