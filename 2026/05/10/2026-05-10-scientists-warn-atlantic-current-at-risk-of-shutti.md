# Scientists warn Atlantic current at risk of shutting down

- Score: 154 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48084836) | Link: https://e360.yale.edu/features/amoc-climate-change

### TL;DR

Concern over the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation is rising as two decades of direct observations hint at slowing and newer, observation-constrained models project roughly 50% weakening by 2100. Warming and Greenland melt reduce North Atlantic water density, reinforcing weaker circulation; crossing a tipping point could eventually disrupt European temperatures, monsoons, storms, and carbon storage for centuries. Evidence remains uncertain: records are short and models diverge sharply. HN split over catastrophic framing, with skeptics fearing alarm fatigue and others arguing high-consequence uncertainty warrants urgent emissions cuts.
