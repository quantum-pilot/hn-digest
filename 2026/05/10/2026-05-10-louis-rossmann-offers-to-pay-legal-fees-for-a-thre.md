# Louis Rossmann offers to pay legal fees for a threatened OrcaSlicer developer

- Score: 444 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48084432) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/3d-printing/louis-rossmann-tells-3d-printer-maker-bambu-lab-to-go-bleep-yourself-over-its-lawsuit-against-enthusiast-right-to-repair-advocate-offers-to-pay-the-legal-fees-for-a-threatened-orcaslicer-developer

### TL;DR

Right-to-repair advocate Louis Rossmann pledged the first $10,000 of legal costs for Pawel Jarczak, who shuttered an OrcaSlicer fork after Bambu Lab sent a cease-and-desist. The project sought to restore direct software control that Bambu restricted after blaming third-party integrations for roughly 30 million unauthorized daily cloud requests. Rossmann urged Jarczak to republish and is gauging crowdfunding support. HN largely condemned Bambu’s lock-in, though some users value its reliable, appliance-like printers and questioned whether the fork accessed private cloud APIs.

### Comment pulse
