# Casio S100X Japanese Lacquer Edition (JP Page Only)

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48048407) | Link: https://www.casio.com/jp/basic-calculators/premium/en-s100x-jc1-u/

### TL;DR

Casio’s S100X-JC1-U turns a desktop calculator into a Japanese craft object. Yamakyu Japanware artisans in Sabae hand-apply natural urushi to a machined aluminum body using translucent tame-nuri, producing black luster with red edges; Yamagata Casio builds, inspects, and serializes each unit in Japan. Functional details include a navy high-contrast FSTN display with dual anti-reflective coating, pantograph isolation keys, three-key rollover, durable two-color legends, and ergonomic key shapes. Commenters admired the craft-tech fusion, while skeptics saw luxury price anchoring around ordinary calculator functionality and collectors recognized another expensive rabbit hole.
