# GameStop makes $55.5B takeover offer for eBay

- Score: 625 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48006402) | Link: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cn0p8yled1do

### TL;DR

GameStop offered $55.5 billion in cash and stock for eBay, valuing it at $125 per share. CEO Ryan Cohen says 1,600 stores could support live commerce, promises $2 billion of first-year cost cuts, and may appeal directly to shareholders. Yet GameStop is valued near $11.9 billion and has only a $20 billion debt commitment disclosed, prompting analysts and commenters to question financing and the burden placed on eBay. The market shared that skepticism: eBay rose 5%, while GameStop fell more than 9%.

### Comment pulse

- Bulls saw stores plus eBay as pickup, resale, grading, and collectibles infrastructure — counterpoint: bulky general merchandise could turn shops into impractical thrift depots.
- Critics framed it as an LBO whose acquisition debt and premium could erase synergy; defenders said lenders and market pricing discipline bad deals.
- Cohen’s compensation threshold and evasive financing interview intensified suspicion that personal incentives or stock effects shape the bid.

### LLM perspective

- The decisive document is a complete sources-and-uses schedule reconciling cash, stock, debt, fees, and refinancing.
- Operational logic looks strongest in collectibles and local fulfillment, not converting every store into a universal consignment hub.
- Watch eBay’s board response, committed financing terms, shareholder outreach, and whether competing bidders emerge.
