# Heat pump sales rise across Europe

- Score: 198 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48012003) | Link: https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/05/04/heat-pump-sales-rise-17-across-europe-in-q1-as-energy-prices-surge/

### TL;DR

Residential heat-pump sales across 11 European countries reached about 575,000 in Q1 2026, up 17% from 494,000 a year earlier. France, Germany, and Poland averaged 25% growth as gas and oil prices jumped after Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz; Austria fell 30% without subsidies. The industry wants rapid VAT cuts, tax relief, and social leasing. Commenters agreed operating economics are improving but stressed that adoption still hinges on upfront cost, correct sizing, installation expertise, building type, electricity mix, and whether subsidies survive.

### Comment pulse

- Air-source systems were viewed as practical defaults — counterpoint: ground-source efficiency can be erased by drilling cost or bad loop design.
- Retrofitting low-use homes may take decades to repay; replacement timing and volatile fossil prices can materially change that calculation.
- Renters and apartment residents face collective building, regulatory, and capital barriers that single-family subsidy stories understate.
