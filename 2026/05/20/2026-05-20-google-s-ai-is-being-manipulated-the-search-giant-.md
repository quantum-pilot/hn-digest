# Google's AI is being manipulated. The search giant is quietly fighting back

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48205782) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20260519-google-tackles-attempts-to-hack-its-ai-results

### TL;DR

A BBC reporter manipulated ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews by publishing one fabricated blog post; within a day, systems repeated his claim of being a hot-dog-eating champion. The investigation says similar tactics affect supplement-health and retirement-finance answers, though its supporting link was broken. Google clarified that spam rules cover attempts to influence generative responses and may be testing self-promotion filters, confidence labels, and review caveats. HN debated whether the demo was contrived, but reports of scam phone numbers and opaque single-source synthesis made the broader attack credible.
