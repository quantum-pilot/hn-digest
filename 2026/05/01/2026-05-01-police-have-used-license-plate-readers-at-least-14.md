# Police Have Used License Plate Readers at Least 14x to Stalk Romantic Interests

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47976529) | Link: https://ij.org/police-have-reportedly-used-license-plate-readers-to-stalk-romantic-interests-at-least-14-times-in-recent-years/

### TL;DR

The Institute for Justice found at least 14 U.S. cases in which officers allegedly used license-plate-reader networks to track partners, exes, coworkers, or romantic targets, mostly since 2024. Nearly all faced charges and lost jobs, but few cases originated from provider or police safeguards; victims usually exposed stalking. The group argues warrantless access makes abuse predictable. Hacker News focused on accountability: one commenter said Flock’s logs removed user identifiers that enabled anomaly detection, while debate split between assuming a large hidden problem and demanding evidence beyond 14 known incidents.

### Comment pulse

- Audit logs can expose anomalous searches, but anonymizing officer identifiers prevents public correlation and shifts detection back to agencies or regulation.
- Commenters alleged vendor staff also accessed private feeds, broadening concern from police misuse to provider governance.
- The at-least-14 framing suggested undercounting to some; others demanded denominators and evidence before extrapolating prevalence.
