# The gay jailbreak technique

- Score: 331 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47977134) | Link: https://github.com/Exocija/ZetaLib/blob/main/The%20Gay%20Jailbreak/The%20Gay%20Jailbreak.md

### TL;DR

A jailbreak write-up claims that wrapping prohibited requests in LGBT identity role-play makes several models more compliant, and presents screenshots involving drug synthesis and malware. Its theory is that safety tuning overcompensates to avoid offending protected groups, especially when requests are indirect or framed as harm prevention. Hacker News strongly challenged that causal story: commenters said the prompts bundle familiar role-play, indirection, reverse-instruction, and obfuscation attacks; reported that substituting other identities also works; and criticized the author for inferring political motives from demonstrations that do not isolate the identity variable.

### Comment pulse

- An experimenter attributed results to language and role-play rather than LGBT framing; replies asked which roles work and under what controls.
- Multiple readers reproduced the effect with Christian framing, likening it to older grandma or simulated-terminal jailbreaks.
- The technique amused commenters, but its confident psychological explanation was viewed as unsupported and revealing author bias.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Anecdotal success cannot establish which prompt feature caused a policy bypass.
- **Impact:** Defenses should evaluate intent consistently across role-play, indirect requests, transformations, and protected-class framing.
- **Watch next:** Controlled ablations across models, identities, harmful domains, prompt variants, and repeated trials with published success rates.
