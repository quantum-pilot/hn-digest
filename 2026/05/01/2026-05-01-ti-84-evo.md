# Ti-84 Evo

- Score: 212 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47979583) | Link: https://education.ti.com/en/products/calculators/graphing-calculators/ti-84-evo

### TL;DR

Texas Instruments’ TI-84 Evo refreshes its exam-approved graphing line with a 156 MHz processor, 319×209 color display, 3.5 MB user memory, USB-C, rechargeable battery, icon home screen, simplified menus and keypad, contextual help, Python and TI-Basic, and four-year online-calculator access. Compared with the TI-84 Plus CE, TI claims triple processor speed and 50% more graphing area. Hacker News cited reporting that it finally replaces the Z80 lineage with ARM, prompting nostalgia for calculator programming, while debating price, classroom value, cheaper alternatives, and feature limits driven partly by exam rules.

### Comment pulse

- Z80 machines introduced many commenters to BASIC and assembly; constraints like implementing multiplication made them tangible learning platforms.
- Critics called mandatory $100-plus hardware obsolete; defenders valued graphs, statistics, durability, distraction-free exams, and used-market availability.
- Missing CAS looks commercially artificial — counterpoint: many exams and courses prohibit symbolic algebra to preserve assessed skills.
