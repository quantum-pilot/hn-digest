# Spotify adds 'Verified' badges to distinguish human artists from AI

- Score: 187 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47976856) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5yerr4m1yno

### TL;DR

Spotify is rolling out verification badges for profiles meeting authenticity signals such as linked social accounts, listening activity, merchandise, or concerts. It expects to verify more than 99% of artists users search for while deprioritizing content farms. The badge establishes a person behind the profile, not AI-free music; critics warn the criteria may disadvantage new or non-touring acts and prefer explicit AI labels. Hacker News debated whether Spotify benefits from synthetic filler, whether human context remains central to art, and whether AI-native listeners will normalize or reject generated music.
