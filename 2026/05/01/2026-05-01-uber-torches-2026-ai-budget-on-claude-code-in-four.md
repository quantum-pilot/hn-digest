# Uber torches 2026 AI budget on Claude Code in four months

- Score: 364 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47976415) | Link: https://www.briefs.co/news/uber-torches-entire-2026-ai-budget-on-claude-code-in-four-months/

### TL;DR

Briefs claims Uber exhausted its 2026 coding-AI budget by April after rolling out Claude Code and Cursor, with monthly costs of $500–$2,000 per engineer, 95% monthly adoption, and 70% of committed code AI-originated. Hacker News questioned both value and accuracy: commenters demanded feature, revenue, quality, and cycle-time outcomes rather than usage metrics; explained how long contexts, subagents, and parallel work inflate tokens; and warned AI-use performance targets invite gaming. Crucially, one reader said the underlying report disclosed no exact budget and put AI-written live backend updates at 11%, not 70%.

### Comment pulse

- Heavy spending can come from un-compacted contexts, repeated large-file reads, multi-agent review, custom-framework documentation, or many parallel worktrees.
- Skeptics asked why spend controls failed and whether delivery accelerated — counterpoint: competitive parity may require the same investment.
- One estimate put four-month spend near 0.3% of annual R&D, making ROI more material than headline budget exhaustion.
