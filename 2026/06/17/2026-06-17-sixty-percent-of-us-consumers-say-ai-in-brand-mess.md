# Sixty percent of US consumers say 'AI' in brand messaging is a turnoff

- Score: 987 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48569278) | Link: https://wpvip.com/future-of-the-web-2026/

### TL;DR

WordPress VIP’s survey of 1,200 U.S. consumers found 60% consider AI in brand messaging a turnoff, 74% say the web feels less human than a decade ago, and average bot fatigue arrives after 40 minutes. Sixty-one percent cannot name a brand using AI well, while 16% think none do. The report recommends structured sites that AI engines can cite while humans receive distinctive experiences. HN’s sharper conclusion: market useful outcomes, not implementation technology, and preserve choice, transparency, specifics, and rapid human escalation.
