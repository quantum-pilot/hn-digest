# U.S. science is in chaos

- Score: 618 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48568058) | Link: https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/americas-compact-between-science-and-politics-is-broken/

### TL;DR

Scientific American argues the postwar U.S. compact—public funding for uncertain basic research that later yields broad prosperity—is being dismantled through abrupt cuts, political filters, and commercialization. NASA’s AXIS telescope died after buyouts removed 20 team members, reassignment and shutdown caused delays, and managers denied budget leeway. Roughly 2,600 grants worth $1.4B remain in limbo; NSF and NIH awards are at three-quarters of normal. HN researchers described canceled hiring, reduced hours, and emigration plans, while dissenters urged fiscal restraint and private-sector transitions.
