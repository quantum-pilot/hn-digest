# Stop Killing Games fails to secure EU law despite 1.3M signatures

- Score: 346 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48564696) | Link: https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/stop-killing-games-fails-to-secure-eu-law-despite-1-3m-signatures-3376431/

### TL;DR

After 1,294,188 verified signatures forced formal review, the European Commission declined to propose an EU obligation requiring publishers to keep discontinued games playable. It judged a mandate disproportionate, citing intellectual property, confidential information, costs, and security, and instead plans end-of-2026 talks on a voluntary code covering clearer storefront warnings and preservation partnerships. Stop Killing Games now aims to amend the Digital Fairness Act through Parliament. HN disputed the defeat framing: an ECI guarantees consideration, not legislation, though commenters split over lobbying and whether campaigners needed broader industry compromise.

### Comment pulse

- The headline overstates finality → commenters say Parliament remains another route, and SKG claims majority support after securing 45 MEP signatures.
- Advocacy strategy is polarized → critics condemn industry-lobby influence — counterpoint: experienced campaigners say negotiated compromise and sympathetic publishers are prerequisites.
- Preservation is a consumer-rights question → supporters distinguish honestly time-limited subscriptions from products sold without clear shutdown expectations.
