# Volkswagen started blocking GrapheneOS users

- Score: 460 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48571526) | Link: https://discuss.grapheneos.org/d/35949-volkswagen-app?page=3

### TL;DR

Volkswagen’s app now appears to require Google Play Integrity certification, locking GrapheneOS and other custom-ROM users out even when Play Services are installed. Volkswagen support says alternative operating systems are unsupported because its digital services depend on certified Android security components; users counter that GrapheneOS is hardened while an old Android 10 device still works. The restriction also reportedly disables paid community API and Home Assistant workflows for preheating and charging. HN users reconsidered vehicle purchases, framing the change as Google gatekeeping, removed functionality, and loss of customer data control.
