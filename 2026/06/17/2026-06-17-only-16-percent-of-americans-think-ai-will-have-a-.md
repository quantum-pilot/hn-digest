# Only 16 Percent of Americans Think AI Will Have a Positive Impact on Society

- Score: 364 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48573332) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/17/only-16-percent-of-americans-think-ai-will-have-a-positive-impact-on-society-a-new-study-shows/

### TL;DR

Pew found 16% of U.S. adults expect AI to benefit society over 20 years, versus 40% expecting harm; 31% foresee equal positive and negative effects, and 13% are unsure. Nearly two-thirds say development is too fast, 59% distrust companies to build safely, and 67% doubt meaningful government regulation. Yet 25% use chatbots daily and 44% use ChatGPT. HN distinguished adoption from approval and voluntary assistance from deployments imposed by employers and platforms.

### Comment pulse

- Sentiment is deteriorating slowly → positive expectations slipped 17% to 16%, while negative expectations rose 35% to 40% over 14 months.
- Forced use explains adoption without approval → employers and platforms bundle AI into work and search — counterpoint: service bots can resolve routine queries faster.
- AI inherits Big Tech’s trust deficit → users connect forced adoption to social media, surveillance, engagement incentives, and harms that remain impossible to opt out.
