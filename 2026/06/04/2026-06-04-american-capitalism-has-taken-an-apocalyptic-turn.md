# American capitalism has taken an apocalyptic turn

- Score: 168 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48393043) | Link: https://economist.com/business/2026/06/03/american-capitalism-has-taken-an-apocalyptic-turn

### TL;DR

The Economist argues American capitalism increasingly sells apocalypse: AI labs, defense firms, crypto promoters, and market strategists frame products and investments as protection against civilizational collapse. That fear raises capital rather than depressing valuations; investors crowd into tech because both AI triumph and AI failure appear hostile to bonds and existing livelihoods. The piece warns that elite millenarianism plus public distrust could become politically explosive. HN shifted from market psychology to whether inequality and automation could produce unrest, and what reforms might reduce pressure.
