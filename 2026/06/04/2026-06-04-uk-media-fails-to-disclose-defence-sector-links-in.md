# UK media fails to disclose defence sector links in nearly 60% of cases

- Score: 359 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48395938) | Link: https://aoav.org.uk/2026/military-experts-or-arms-industry-insiders-uk-media-fails-to-disclose-defence-sector-links-in-nearly-60-of-cases/

### TL;DR

AOAV reviewed UK defence coverage from 2015 through May 2026 and identified 33 retired senior officers who both appeared as commentators and held commercial roles in defence, security, intelligence, technology, or related sectors. Nineteen—58%—were presented at least once only through former rank or service, without relevant business interests disclosed; several advocated spending or engagement that could benefit connected industries. The report attributes omissions to editorial failures, not deliberate concealment by veterans, and urges clearer disclosures and broader voices. HN broadly agreed relevance matters but questioned outlet coverage and disclosure scope.
