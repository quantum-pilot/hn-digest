# U.S. to dismantle system tracking Atlantic currents that are at risk of collapse

- Score: 609 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48392232) | Link: https://e360.yale.edu/digest/trump-ooi-amoc

### TL;DR

NSF plans to recover 900-plus Ocean Observatories Initiative instruments from Pacific and Atlantic sites over 15 months, ending in-water infrastructure after roughly a decade despite a 25-year minimum. The network supplies long-running ocean and ecosystem measurements, including observations used to study the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation, whose future is increasingly uncertain under warming. HN commenters stressed that discontinuity weakens modeling and early warning, contrasted basic-science costs with defense spending, and inferred climate-politics motives from choosing active retrieval rather than stopping operations; the article does not provide a budget rationale.
