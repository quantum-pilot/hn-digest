# Google employees internally share memes about how its AI sucks

- Score: 145 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48400311) | Link: https://www.404media.co/google-employees-internally-share-memes-about-how-its-ai-sucks/

### TL;DR

404 Media reports that Google employees are sharing internal memes mocking AI coding tools as overhyped and burdensome, contrasting that sentiment with CEO Sundar Pichai’s claim that AI generates 75% of new Google code. The supplied article is paywalled beyond its opening, so its sample size, examples, and methodology are unavailable. HN’s ex-Googlers argued that Memegen routinely satirizes every executive, product, and process and amplifies extremes, making memes weak evidence of broad employee opinion; others said internal ridicule still usefully tests leadership’s public narrative.
