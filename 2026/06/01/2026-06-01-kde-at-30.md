# KDE at 30

- Score: 225 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48357355) | Link: https://kde.org/anniversaries/30/

### TL;DR

At 30, KDE traces its evolution from Matthias Ettrich’s 1996 Qt/C++ desktop proposal through Konqueror, Plasma, the Steam Deck, Wayland, and KDE Linux. Its community highlights user control, privacy, international collaboration, and an unusually direct funding base: private users provide 70% of donations. The retrospective also notes KHTML’s foundational influence on WebKit and Blink. Hacker News readers celebrate KDE’s speed, customization, applications, and durable integration, while lamenting lost experiments, KDE 4’s disruption, Akonadi problems, and uncertainty around X11.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime users miss Konqueror/KParts-era ambition and applications lost after KDE 4 — counterpoint: KIO, Kate, Dolphin, and KDE Connect remain strong.
- Converts praise Plasma’s fast, Windows-familiar defaults and deep customization, even on older hardware; critics find its visuals and settings excessive or uneven.
- KDE’s app ecosystem wins affection beyond Plasma, especially Okular, Kate, Krita, Spectacle, and KolourPaint; Akonadi reliability and X11 concerns persist.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** KDE’s enduring advantage is coherent power-user choice without sacrificing approachable defaults.
- **Impact:** Its applications and integration extend KDE’s influence far beyond Plasma installations.
- **Watch next:** KDE Linux and the Wayland transition will test whether renewed ambition preserves compatibility and trust.
