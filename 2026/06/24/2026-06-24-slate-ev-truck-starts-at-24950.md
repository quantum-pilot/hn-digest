# Slate EV truck starts at $24,950

- Score: 237 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48659451) | Link: https://www.slate.auto/en

### TL;DR

Slate pitches a $24,950 bare-bones electric two-seat pickup that owners can convert into a five-seat SUV or fastback using swappable panels and more than 200 accessories, over 80% priced below $500. It emphasizes DIY wraps, accessible parts, free manuals, fleet maintenance, and charging from 120V, 240V, or 29,000 Tesla Superchargers. HN liked the modularity, physical controls, and low entry price, but realistic builds approached $35,000. Commenters questioned DIY wrap quality, dealer-installed-option reliability, rattles, pedestrian-unfriendly styling, and a reported 200-mile range.

### Comment pulse

- Modularity trades repairability for integration risk → enthusiasts welcome owner changes, while dealer accessory history suggests leaks, electrical faults, squeaks, and rattles.
