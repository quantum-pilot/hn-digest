# A deadly fungus that can infect cats and people is spreading

- Score: 182 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48658186) | Link: https://www.sciencenews.org/article/deadly-fungus-cats-people-spreading

### TL;DR

Sporothrix brasiliensis, emerging in Brazil in the 1990s, has infected more than 11,000 people, at least 200 dogs, and thousands of cats across five South American countries. Infected cats can transmit contagious yeast through scratches, bites, grooming, sneezing, and contaminated surfaces; untreated feline disease is reported as fatal, while severe human illness chiefly threatens immunocompromised people. A CDC adviser expects eventual U.S. arrival, citing absent commercial tests and minimal import screening. HN debated the human-risk framing, treatment gaps, and safe medication practices for infected cats.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The immediate evidence supports a serious veterinary and occupational-health problem; broader public danger remains insufficiently quantified in the article.
- **Impact:** Veterinarians, cat rescuers, owners, and laboratories need exposure precautions, rapid reporting, and cleaning protocols centered on bleach or ethanol.
- **Watch next:** Develop commercial diagnostics; publish human treatment outcomes, resistance surveillance, incubation ranges, and confirmed transmission rates beyond South America.
