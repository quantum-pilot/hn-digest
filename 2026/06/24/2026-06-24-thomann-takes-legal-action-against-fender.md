# Thomann takes legal action against Fender

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48664384) | Link: https://www.thomann.de/blog/en/inside/thomann-takes-legal-action-against-fender/

### TL;DR

Thomann is challenging Fender’s cease-and-desist campaign over alleged copyright in the Stratocaster body shape. Fender’s Düsseldorf default judgment against a Chinese S-style manufacturer followed missed deadlines rather than a full merits review; Thomann wants an adversarial proceeding with discovery and is directly exposed through its Harley Benton brand. It argues the 1954 design’s horns, cutaways, and contours are functional, while decades of reinterpretation underpin guitar-industry competition. HN debated whether the shape has become generic or remains protectable applied art, while questioning private-equity and commercial motives on both sides.
