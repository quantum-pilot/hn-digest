# Job: Head of Stonehenge

- Score: 224 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48455928) | Link: https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/about/our-people/careers-with-us/job-search/default-job-page/?jobRef=16449

### TL;DR

English Heritage is hiring a permanent Head of Stonehenge in Wiltshire for 36 hours weekly, starting at £64,189. The role is primarily senior site management, not archaeology: lead staff and volunteers, set long-term strategy, safeguard the World Heritage Site, improve visitor experience and sustainable income, oversee operations, retail, food, budgets, compliance, safety, partnerships, and complex solstice gatherings. HN enjoyed the grand title and ritual jokes, but questioned the pay relative to comparable technical roles; one substantive thread praised the surrounding restored landscape and public access.
