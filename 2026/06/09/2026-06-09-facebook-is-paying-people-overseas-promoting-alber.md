# Facebook is paying people overseas promoting Alberta separatism

- Score: 296 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48457181) | Link: https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/facebook-overseas-alberta-separtism-9.7223966

### TL;DR

CBC identified 14 overseas-run Facebook accounts in four Alberta separatist groups, some impersonating Canadians, copying real residents’ posts, or using AI-generated material. The accounts drew tens of thousands of reactions; two displayed Meta monetization earnings, including roughly US$14 for one active month. Researchers said inauthentic activity had tripled recently but remained a minority of group content, and real-world influence is unclear. Meta disabled several accounts after CBC’s inquiry. HN debated whether profit-seeking better explains such manipulation than state interference, while largely blaming engagement payments and unaccountable amplification.
