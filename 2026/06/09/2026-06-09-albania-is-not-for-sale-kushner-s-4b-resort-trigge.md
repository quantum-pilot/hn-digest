# Albania Is Not for Sale: Kushner's $4B Resort Triggers'Flamingo Revolution'

- Score: 434 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48461012) | Link: https://www.yacnews.com/albania-is-not-for-sale-kushners-4-billion-resort-triggers-flamingo-revolution-asset-freeze-and-an-eu-warning/

### TL;DR

Albanian prosecutors froze a land developer’s accounts while investigating disputed titles, protected-area reclassification, tender bypasses, and funding for a roughly $4 billion Kushner-backed resort spanning Sazan Island and Vjosa-Narta. Protests expanded after clearing began before completed environmental review, private guards assaulted a demonstrator, and public beach access was fenced. Prime Minister Edi Rama defends investors while acknowledging plans remain unfinished. The EU linked compliance to accession standards. HN framed the case as coastal wealth extraction, while finding hope in public mobilization, independent prosecutors, and EU leverage.
