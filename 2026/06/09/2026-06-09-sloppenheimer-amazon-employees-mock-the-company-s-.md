# 'Sloppenheimer:' Amazon employees mock the company's AI on Slack

- Score: 179 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48462823) | Link: https://www.404media.co/sloppenheimer-amazon-employees-mock-the-companys-ai-on-slack/

### TL;DR

404 Media reports that Amazon employees use an internal Slack meme channel to mock faulty AI coding output as slop and ridicule an unsuccessful attempt to motivate effective adoption. The visible article contrasts that skepticism with Jeff Bezos’s predictions of sweeping AI-driven productivity gains. HN commenters claiming Amazon experience described a fragmented ecosystem spanning Kiro, Claude Code, Codex, Bedrock models, Quick, and Agent Spaces, but disagreed about which tool dominates, whether token budgets exist, and whether adoption is pressured. Several argued that decentralized tool competition is deliberate, not organizational failure.
