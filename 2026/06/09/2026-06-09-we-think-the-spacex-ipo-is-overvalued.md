# We Think the SpaceX IPO Is Overvalued

- Score: 254 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48455233) | Link: https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/why-we-think-spacex-ipo-is-overvalued?content_id=20768396545

### TL;DR

Morningstar values SpaceX at $63 per share, 53% below the $135 IPO price, after probability-weighting three orbital-AI outcomes: 7% moonshot, 50% limited success, and 43% failure. Even its $154 moonshot assumes rapidly reusable Starship and competitive orbital data centers; matching $135 requires assigning zero probability to failure and 77% to the moonshot. HN focused less on conventional valuation than on orbital-computing feasibility, Elon Musk’s 85% voting control, and demand driven by personality, index flows, or space optimism rather than cash-flow fundamentals.

### Comment pulse

- Governance risk → Critics said super-voting shares and arbitration leave public holders powerless — counterpoint: fiduciary duties remain, and controlled firms can reward investors.
- Orbital economics → Skeptics cited heat rejection, launch mass, repairs, radiation, and hardware obsolescence; bullish estimates argued large revenue could outweigh deployment costs.
- Price formation → Some expect Musk enthusiasm and forced fund buying to override valuation — counterpoint: speculative demand remains economically real and financially risky.
