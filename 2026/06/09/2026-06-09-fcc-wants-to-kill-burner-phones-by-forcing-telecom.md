# FCC wants to kill burner phones by forcing telecoms to get all customers' IDs

- Score: 416 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48462308) | Link: https://www.404media.co/fcc-wants-to-kill-burner-phones-by-forcing-telecoms-to-get-all-customers-ids/

### TL;DR

The FCC is considering a rule requiring telecom providers to collect government-issued identification numbers and physical addresses from every new or renewing customer, effectively ending anonymous prepaid phone service. The agency frames broader customer verification as an anti-scam measure, including extra disclosures for bulk, business, and foreign accounts. Privacy advocates warn it would expose domestic-abuse survivors, journalists, low-income users, and others while creating another sensitive database. HN largely agreed, citing carrier breaches and surveillance risks, though commenters noted SIM-registration laws are already common—and unevenly enforced—across Europe and Russia.

### Comment pulse

- Mandatory identification centralizes risk → carriers with repeated breaches would hold more durable identity data, potentially long after accounts close.
- Fraud prevention has collateral costs → people needing pseudonymity for safety, reporting, or affordability would lose access alongside scammers.
- Registration is not unprecedented → counterpoint: international requirements vary widely, so foreign adoption does not establish necessity or proportionality.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Identity mandates convert phone access into a durable surveillance credential; necessity should be proven against less invasive fraud controls.
- **Impact:** Carriers inherit verification, retention, breach, correction, and deletion obligations affecting nearly every customer lifecycle.
- **Watch next:** Examine the FCC docket for scope, exemptions, retention limits, security standards, enforcement evidence, and survivor protections.
