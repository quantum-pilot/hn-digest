# Apple decided not to roll out Siri in EU after denied request for exemption

- Score: 343 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463024) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/apple-failed-make-its-ai-tool-comply-eu-regulations-eu-commission-says-2026-06-09/

### TL;DR

Apple will not initially offer upgraded Siri AI on EU iPhones and iPads after regulators denied an 18-month exemption from Digital Markets Act interoperability duties. Apple proposed an intermediary for safe third-party assistant access and says talks failed on privacy and security; the Commission says the DMA allows launch and Apple lacked a compliant solution. DMA breaches can draw fines up to 10% of global annual turnover. HN debate centered on corporate leverage, compliance scale, and whether interoperability expands user choice or device-data exposure.

### Comment pulse

- No exemption is a legitimate boundary → Apple can comply or withdraw — counterpoint: withholding a popular feature may manufacture pressure against regulators.
- Compliance is not a staffing problem → jurisdiction-specific privacy, deletion, audit, and reporting changes can span core systems and years.
- Interoperability changes the threat model → critics fear third-party assistants accessing device context; supporters cite granular permissions and Android’s decade of assistant choice.
