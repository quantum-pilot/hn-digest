# Morningstar values SpaceX at $780B, half its IPO target

- Score: 187 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48373909) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/morningstar-values-spacex-780-billion-half-its-ipo-target-2026-06-02/

### TL;DR

Morningstar estimates SpaceX at $780 billion, versus the reported $1.75 trillion IPO target and a $1.53 trillion secondary-market mark. Its discount reflects uncertain xAI/X economics, stronger AI rivals, speculative orbital data centers, and Starlink risks outside the company’s control. Analysts still expect low float and major-bank underwriting to support the stock initially, but argue long-term buyers may get a safer entry later. HN was even more skeptical, emphasizing key-person risk, thin-float price distortion, and forecasts whose uncertainty may overwhelm present fundamentals.
