# Gmail thinks I'm stupid, so I left

- Score: 547 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48375016) | Link: https://moddedbear.com/gmail-thinks-im-stupid-so-i-left

### TL;DR

After 16 years, the author is leaving Gmail because its web UI inserts unsolicited summaries and draft replies, promotes “Help me write,” and repeatedly nudges AI revision. Some prompts cannot be disabled independently from useful features, which they read as disrespectful metric-padding rather than optional assistance. They moved their own domain to Fastmail and are deciding whether to import old mail. HN largely echoed the frustration and praised Fastmail’s speed, while noting writing assistance can help non-native speakers or uncertain writers when genuinely optional.

### Comment pulse
