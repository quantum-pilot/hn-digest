# Can the stockmarket swallow Anthropic, SpaceX and OpenAI?

- Score: 677 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48364055) | Link: https://www.economist.com/finance-and-economics/2026/06/01/can-the-stockmarket-swallow-anthropic-spacex-and-openai

### TL;DR

SpaceX reportedly plans to raise $75 billion in a June Nasdaq debut, while Anthropic has filed draft IPO paperwork and OpenAI may follow; the AI firms are rumored to seek up to $60 billion each. Together, the listings could add as much as $4 trillion in market capitalization. HN discussion focused less on whether markets have enough cash than on who must absorb the shares, while debating whether transformative businesses justify unprecedented valuations or merely transfer risk to passive investors.

### Comment pulse

- Index mechanics → Nasdaq and FTSE Russell allegedly shortened inclusion windows, compelling passive buying — counterpoint: S&P changes remained unfinalized and total-market rules unchanged.
- Valuation skepticism → Critics asked about profits and defensibility against equivalent or open-weight models; supporters cited Anthropic’s reported $47 billion revenue and rapid growth.
- Economic effects → Bulls emphasized jobs, infrastructure, cheaper launches, Starlink, and supplier revenue; bears feared overvaluation, unequal gains, and an IPO-before-crash race.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** IPO capacity is allocation, not storage: new listings reprice portfolios by redirecting demand from existing securities.
- **Impact:** Passive savers may receive exposure without choosing it; incumbent shareholders, issuers, and active traders face predictable rebalance flows.
- **Watch next:** Final offer sizes, float percentages, index eligibility, lockups, profitability disclosures, allocation books, first-week liquidity, and post-listing drawdowns.
