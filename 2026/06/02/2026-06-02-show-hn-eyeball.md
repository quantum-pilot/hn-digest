# Show HN: Eyeball

- Score: 229 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48367723) | Link: https://eyeball.rory.codes/

### TL;DR

Eyeball is a minimalist browser game that asks players to place a target number on a line as precisely as possible, tracking error, best result, average, streak, and rounds. It recommends a mouse or trackpad because touch input lacks competitive precision, then encourages score sharing. HN users enjoyed chasing tiny errors—including reported 0% and 0.06% misses—but some found the task unclear until experimentation. Discussion compared it with Matthias Wandel’s broader geometry game and requested training modes that repeat missed examples.

### Comment pulse

- Immediate score feedback drives replay and boasting → players posted sub-0.1% misses, while an early perfect result made stopping equally appealing.
- Visual polish outpaced onboarding → several users could not infer that the displayed number mapped to a position on the line.
- Training could improve skill acquisition → repeat misses immediately or cycle five examples until every placement meets a configurable tolerance.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The core loop succeeds because one click yields an objective, legible measure of performance and an instant retry.
- **Impact:** Touch users are structurally disadvantaged, so mixed-device rankings would measure hardware precision alongside human estimation.
- **Watch next:** Mobile-app controls, additional geometric tasks, input-device leaderboards, and whether streaks sustain retention after the novelty fades.
