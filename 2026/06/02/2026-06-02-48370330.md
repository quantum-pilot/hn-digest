# Please don't spam people looking for employment. It's just cruel

- Score: 880 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48370330) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48370330

### TL;DR

After six months unemployed, a forced immigrant posted in HN’s “Who wants to be hired?” thread and soon received a superficially personalized email that referenced the job search but pitched the sender’s own LLM-development services. The initial wording briefly looked like an opportunity, making the irrelevant solicitation another emotional setback amid debt and family obligations. The author asks outreach automators to exercise basic empathy. HN largely agreed, describing similar security, recruiter, and AI-agent spam, while noting that formulaic cold mail predates LLMs and public contact details inevitably attract it.

### Comment pulse

- Career-thread outreach should offer sympathy or a relevant role → exploiting vulnerability for unrelated sales compounds stress.
- Clarity reduces harm → cold emails should state sender, company, request, and value proposition immediately using bottom-line-up-front structure.
- Personalization no longer proves human attention → maintainers see repeated AI security pitches — counterpoint: comparable mail-merge spam existed decades earlier.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The ethical failure is deceptive context selection, not merely automation or imperfect personalization.
- **Impact:** Job seekers and maintainers must spend more emotional and cognitive effort authenticating every promising message.
- **Watch next:** Platform rules, sender reputation, disclosure norms, and filters for career-thread-derived solicitation.
