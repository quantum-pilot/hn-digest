### TL;DR

Bitsy presents itself as a small engine for making compact games, worlds, and stories. Its sparse homepage points newcomers directly to a browser-based creation tool, an itch.io collection of finished works, documentation, a community forum, the classic version, and related projects. HN commenters praised the engine’s charming pixel art and blend of retro aesthetics with personal storytelling, while debating the balance between interactive poetry and conventional game mechanics and sharing adjacent tools.
