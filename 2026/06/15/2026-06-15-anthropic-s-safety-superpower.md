# Anthropic's Safety Superpower

- Score: 200 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48539078) | Link: https://stratechery.com/2026/anthropics-safety-superpower/

### TL;DR

Ben Thompson argues Anthropic’s safety mission is sincere, powerful, and dangerous. Frontier models commoditize quickly, so Anthropic must own user workflows and the behavioral data that improves future systems. Its 30-day retention policy, attempted silent degradation of competing-AI work, anticipated API restrictions, and fights with government all advance control while being justified as safety. Mission, talent, and business therefore reinforce one another, unlike OpenAI’s internal conflict. HN split over whether this creates private gatekeeping power or overstates Anthropic’s leverage amid export controls, open models, distillation, and compute bottlenecks.
