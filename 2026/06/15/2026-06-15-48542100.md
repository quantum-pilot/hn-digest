# Ask HN: Has anyone replaced Claude/GPT with a local model for daily coding?

- Score: 604 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48542100) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48542100

### TL;DR

HN users reported that local models can replace most or all cloud-assisted coding, especially for privacy, offline use, hobbies, and routine work, but rarely match frontier agents on architecture or difficult tasks. Common setups paired Pi or OpenCode with llama.cpp and Qwen 3.6 27B or 35B-A3B on 36–128GB unified-memory Macs or RTX 3090-class GPUs; one dual-3090 system reached about 150 tokens per second. Users valued zero marginal cost and data control, yet cited loops, edit failures, context degradation, weaker UI work, micromanagement, and hardware economics favoring subscriptions.

### Comment pulse

- Local is viable with guidance → Qwen behaves like a broad junior, requiring explicit constraints; frontier models contribute stronger planning and architectural judgment.

- Throughput alone misleads → better quantization or dense 27B models may reduce churn, while long contexts can degrade speed and quality.

- Economics depend on sunk hardware and workload → subscriptions usually win on acquisition cost — counterpoint: privacy, quotas, and offline availability change the calculus.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Local coding is already practical as a supervised accelerator, but full cloud replacement remains workload- and expertise-dependent.

- **Impact:** Privacy-sensitive developers and GPU owners gain autonomy; professionals may prefer hybrid routing to protect velocity on harder work.

- **Watch next:** Benchmark task success, correction time, energy cost, long-context reliability, edit accuracy, and frontier-model fallback frequency.
