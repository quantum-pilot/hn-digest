# Hetzner increased dedicated server prices 3-4x

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48542064) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48542064

### TL;DR

Hetzner raised new-order dedicated-server prices for the second time in months: AX102 moved from €124 to €454 and a 256GB AX162 from €244 to €844, roughly 3–4×. Existing orders retain prior prices. CEO Martin Hetzner said the company had subsidized rising hardware costs for nine months and expects competitors to reach a similar market level. HN largely blamed AI-driven RAM and storage scarcity, while debating whether hyperscalers can absorb costs longer, Hetzner is capturing underpriced value, or customers should choose auction servers and rivals.

### Comment pulse

- Commodity-hardware economics cut both ways → Hetzner’s low prices depended on off-the-shelf components, leaving it exposed when RAM and NAND costs multiplied.
- Hyperscalers may delay the shock → long-term supply contracts and high margins cushion AWS, GCP, and Azure — counterpoint: egress and other prices are rising.
- Cheaper paths remain → auction servers reportedly held steady, while users compared Vultr, OVH, and Scaleway for less demanding workloads.
