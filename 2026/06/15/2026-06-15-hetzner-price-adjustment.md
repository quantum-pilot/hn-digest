# Hetzner Price Adjustment

- Score: 305 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48540844) | Link: https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/#cloud-servers

### TL;DR

Hetzner’s new prices apply to orders and cloud rescales from June 15 at 08:00 CEST; earlier orders keep previous rates. In Europe, monthly cloud increases range from roughly 30% for CAX and CX instances to more than 2× for CCX and CPX tiers, while US CPX11 rises from $6.99 to $20.49. Dedicated servers also become much costlier, though limited offerings and auctions remain alternatives. HN attributed the shock to AI-driven RAM and storage scarcity, debated hyperscaler purchasing advantages, and requested smaller low-cost plans.

### Comment pulse
