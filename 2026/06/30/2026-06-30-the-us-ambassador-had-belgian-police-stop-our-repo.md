# The US ambassador had Belgian police stop our reporting

- Score: 659 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48730608) | Link: https://europeancorrespondent.com/en/r/the-us-ambassador-had-belgian-police-stop-our-reporting

### TL;DR

Two accredited journalists say Belgian plainclothes police detained, identified, questioned, and expelled them from a U.S. Embassy celebration in a Brussels public park after they asked Ambassador Bill White about an alleged threat. Officers reportedly had been told one reporter posed an active threat, later accepted they were journalists, but enforced the embassy’s removal order. White offered no explanation and called them losers. HN treated this as a press-freedom and local-accountability issue: some blamed embassy intimidation, others emphasized Belgian police compliance and a private event organizer’s right to exclude attendees.
