# We Are the Last People Who Know How It Works

- Score: 285 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48735633) | Link: https://unix.foo/posts/last-people-who-know-how-it-works/

### TL;DR

The essay distinguishes technical competence from acquaintance: 1990s computers forced users through boot disks, config files, jumpers, and modem failures, creating intimate knowledge through resistance. AI assistants remove that friction, preserving manuals and capability while making users more dependent on systems they understand less personally. HN agreed that experiential problem-solving matters but challenged the nostalgia: every generation inherits higher abstractions, and no one understands the full stack. AI’s nondeterminism may make this jump different, yet it can also teach when users ask how, inspect outputs, and retain verification skills.
