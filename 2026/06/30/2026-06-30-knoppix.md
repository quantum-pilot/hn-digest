# Knoppix

- Score: 240 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48732056) | Link: https://www.knopper.net/knoppix/index-en.html

### TL;DR

KNOPPIX is a bootable GNU/Linux environment for CD, DVD, or USB that automatically detects hardware and runs without touching the host disk. On-the-fly decompression packs a broad desktop and utility collection into portable media, making it useful for everyday work, education, recovery, and demonstrations. HN readers remembered it as their first safe route into Linux on shared family PCs and locked school labs, avoiding risky partitioning while gaining access to GCC and better tools. Several credited that accessibility with later Linux, DevOps, and SRE careers.

### Comment pulse
