# US Supreme Court Just Blew Up EU-US Data Transfers

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48728740) | Link: https://noyb.eu/en/us-supreme-court-just-blew-eu-us-data-transfers

### TL;DR

Privacy group noyb argues the Supreme Court’s Trump v. Slaughter ruling, which removes FTC independence, undermines a core premise of the 2023 EU–US Data Privacy Framework: independent American oversight equivalent to EU requirements. The framework remains effective until the European Commission withdraws it or the CJEU annuls it, so transfers do not stop immediately; non-personal and strictly necessary transfers also remain possible. HN focused on Europe’s dependence on U.S. clouds and subprocessors, arguing that legal sovereignty is impractical without competitive domestic infrastructure and careful distinctions between hosting and personal-data processing.
