# County with 37 Data Centers Asks Schools to 'Conserve Electricity'

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48734699) | Link: https://www.404media.co/henrico-virginia-datacenter-energy-cost-email/

### TL;DR

Henrico County, Virginia—home to 37 data centers with 17 more planned—expects its government and school electricity rate to rise 24.9%, adding about $5 million next year. Officials asked staff to switch off lights and computers, manage blinds, unplug devices, and avoid space heaters. The article links local cost pressure to data-center demand and delayed grid expansion. HN challenged that attribution: the increase applies to more than 170 entities in a negotiated Dominion contract, while renewable-transition investment also affects rates. Most agreed large new loads should finance generation and transmission upfront.

### Comment pulse

- Small-office savings are symbolic → even perfect light discipline barely offsets gigawatt-scale facilities; automated timers would outperform voluntary reminders.
- Causality is contested → clean-energy investment and long-flat generation also raise costs — counterpoint: concentrated two-gigawatt demand plainly stresses distribution.
- Cost allocation should follow load growth → commenters favored deposits, long-term minimums, or dedicated rate classes before utilities build capacity.
