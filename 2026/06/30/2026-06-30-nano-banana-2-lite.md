# Nano Banana 2 Lite

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48735444) | Link: https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-image/flash-lite/

### TL;DR

Google’s Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image targets rapid, high-volume generation and editing, promising lower latency and cost while retaining prompt control, character consistency, text rendering, and real-world knowledge near the full Nano Banana 2. Google acknowledges weak small faces, spelling, factual diagrams, localization, complex edits, blending, and occasional character drift. Early HN users reported sub-five-second images versus roughly 30 seconds for the base model, but lower nuance and consistency; they saw it as an iteration or onboarding tool, reserving the premium model for final output.
