# The labor share of income in the US is at its lowest post-war level

- Score: 455 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48734234) | Link: https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/06/the-post-covid-decline-in-the-labor-share/

### TL;DR

U.S. labor income—wages and salaries as a share of output—has fallen 1.6 percentage points below its pre-pandemic level, reaching a postwar low after a broader decline that began around 2000. The COVID pattern itself is not unprecedented: labor share rose during recession, fell through recovery, and then flattened, resembling pre-2000 cycles. A shift-share analysis finds the drop occurred within industries, not because output moved toward less labor-intensive sectors. HN stressed this cyclical finding neither explains nor normalizes the exceptional long-run decline, nor predicts whether labor’s share will rebound.

### Comment pulse
