# Gov.uk has replaced Stripe with Dutch provider Adyen

- Score: 311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48415217) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/public-sector/2026/06/04/govuk-goes-dutch-on-payments-as-it-dumps-stripe/5250763

### TL;DR

UK Government Digital Service is moving many GOV.UK Pay transactions from Stripe to Dutch processor Adyen under a three-year contract worth up to £25.3 million. The deal covers roughly 17% of payments but more than 70% of participating organizations, migrating about 1,000 local-authority, police, and armed-forces services; Worldpay remains for central government and NHS bodies. Adyen will add open-banking pay by bank without visible user disruption. HN focused less on nationality than on low payment costs, Adyen’s enterprise-only posture, and Stripe’s superior small-business onboarding.

### Comment pulse

- The contract looks modest → low UK interchange and infrequent government card payments limit volume, unlike US cloud or card-processing bills.
- Public rails can undercut card economics → Pix and UPI suggest instant transfers need not skim several percent from every transaction.
- Adyen and Stripe serve different markets → Adyen favors high-volume merchants, while Stripe’s self-service onboarding and developer focus win small businesses.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** This is procurement segmentation, not wholesale replacement: 83% of GOV.UK Pay volume and central services remain elsewhere.
- **Impact:** Local public bodies gain bank payments; cost savings remain unquantified.
- **Watch next:** Migration completion, transaction fees, pay-by-bank adoption, and whether service reliability matches Stripe.
