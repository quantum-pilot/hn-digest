# C++: The Documentary

- Score: 364 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48408016) | Link: https://herbsutter.com/2026/06/04/c-the-documentary-released-today/

### TL;DR

A roughly 69-minute film traces C++ from Bjarne Stroustrup’s C with Classes and CFront at Bell Labs through commercial adoption, standardization, the STL, C++98, games, trading, its early-2000s decline, C++11, and current safety and complexity challenges. Interviewees include Stroustrup, Stepanov, Kernighan, Hejlsberg, Lattner, Romero, Alexandrescu, and standards leaders. Herb Sutter presents it as a 40-year success story, citing 90% user growth over 3.5 years. HN enjoyed the oral history but reopened the language’s central argument: unmatched low-level abstraction versus incoherence, unsafe defaults, and accumulated complexity.

### Comment pulse

- DOS adoption had a missing protagonist → Zortech C++ solved CFront’s speed and near/far-pointer problems, triggering Borland and Microsoft competition.
- Complexity divides even expert readers → Alexandrescu’s metaprogramming book was career-defining for some and the reason others abandoned C++.
- Safety is the live fault line → critics demand opt-out unsafety — counterpoint: defenders credit C++ with RAII, smart pointers, move semantics, and generics.

### LLM perspective
