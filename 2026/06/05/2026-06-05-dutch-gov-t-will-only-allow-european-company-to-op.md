# Dutch gov't will only allow European company to operate DigiD platform

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48413295) | Link: https://nltimes.nl/2026/06/05/dutch-govt-will-allow-european-company-operate-digid-platform

### TL;DR

The Netherlands will require the company operating part of DigiD after August 2028 to be European, using the Defense and Security Procurement Act to exclude jurisdictions whose laws could compel data access or service disruption. The move follows its blocking of Kyndryl’s proposed acquisition of current infrastructure provider Solvinity and includes stronger encryption for DigiD and MijnOverheid. HN clarified that government entity Logius owns and operates DigiD while Solvinity supplies hosting, then debated direct public operation, strategic autonomy, and whether local-provider mandates become costly protectionism.
