# South Korean forums will need to scan every images with AI censorship tools

- Score: 205 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48406198) | Link: https://discuss.privacyguides.net/t/south-korean-online-communities-will-need-to-scan-every-images-with-ai-censorship-tools/38341

### TL;DR

A Privacy Guides post says South Korean forums must begin AI-scanning every user-uploaded image and video on July 1, with operators funding prescribed Nvidia hardware. The expansion targets illegal intimate imagery and CSAM, but the post alleges existing filters also block benign material. HN treated the underlying abuse as serious while criticizing the mandate as rushed and poorly scoped: small domestic communities would absorb infrastructure and compliance costs, yet major foreign services may remain exempt. Commenters anticipated false positives, vendor lock-in, and a retreat toward private or federated communities.
