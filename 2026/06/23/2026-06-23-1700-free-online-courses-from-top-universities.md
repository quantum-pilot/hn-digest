# 1,700 free online courses from top universities

- Score: 245 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48639184) | Link: https://www.openculture.com/freeonlinecourses

### TL;DR

The directory gathers 1,700 university-level courses spanning humanities, social sciences, languages, business, computing, mathematics, engineering, natural sciences, and more, with offerings from institutions including MIT, Yale, Harvard, Oxford, Stanford, and IIT campuses. Materials mix videos, audio, websites, course notes, and MOOCs; Coursera and edX learners must choose audit or no-certificate modes to avoid fees, while paid credentials generate affiliate commissions. HN welcomed the breadth but found its practical value constrained by link maintenance, heavy page resource use, and the finite attention required for real learning.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The catalog solves discovery, not verification; its scale makes automated link checks and freshness metadata essential infrastructure.
- **Impact:** Self-directed learners gain extraordinary breadth but must supply sequencing, prerequisites, assessment, and persistence that a curriculum normally provides.
- **Watch next:** Track availability, last-checked dates, replacement mirrors, memory usage, audit-mode instructions, and completion-oriented learning paths.
