# Fired by Google for creating the Google workspace CLI

- Score: 225 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48649011) | Link: https://twitter.com/JPoehnelt/status/2069482265953087602

### TL;DR

A former Google employee says he was dismissed after creating a Google Workspace CLI that topped Hacker News and rapidly gained thousands of stars and users. He describes internal praise followed by legal questioning over Google logos and colors, and suspects Workspace leaders feared disruption from agent tooling; Google announced an official CLI two days before his termination. The post does not explain what approvals were obtained. HN split sharply: supporters saw bureaucracy punishing initiative, while current and former Googlers emphasized branding, outside-work disclosure, open-source, privacy, and launch-review requirements.
