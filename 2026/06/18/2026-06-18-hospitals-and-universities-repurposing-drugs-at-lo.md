# Hospitals and universities repurposing drugs at lower cost

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48583386) | Link: https://www.kcl.ac.uk/news/hospitals-and-universities-repurposing-drugs-at-90-lower-cost

### TL;DR

King’s College London describes a parallel innovation system in which hospitals and universities test new uses for off-patent generic drugs. Their late-stage trials reportedly cost under 10% of pharmaceutical companies’ reported figures because known compounds reduce expertise, capital, and business-risk barriers, while clinicians are motivated by patient outcomes and publication. Examples span blindness, cancer prevention, and COVID-19. HN welcomed cheaper repurposing but stressed that trial cost is only one obstacle: sterile reformulation, regulatory authorization, liability, manufacturing, reimbursement, and safety can determine whether evidence reaches patients.

- **Impact:** Patients may gain affordable options sooner, while clinicians and payers inherit responsibility for evidence review, sourcing, and informed consent.
