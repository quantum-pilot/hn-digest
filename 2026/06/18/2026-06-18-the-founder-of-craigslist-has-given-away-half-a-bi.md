# The founder of Craigslist has given away half a billion dollars

- Score: 296 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48588216) | Link: https://www.independent.co.uk/us/money/craigslist-multimillionaire-craig-newmark-b2980681.html

### TL;DR

Craigslist founder Craig Newmark says he has donated $500 million and intends to give away most of his fortune, funding cybersecurity, journalism, military families, veterans, and pigeon rescue through Craig Newmark Philanthropies. He joined the Giving Pledge in 2025 and frames his wealth as luck exceeding any reasonable need, consistent with Craigslist’s rejection of aggressive venture-backed monetization and his unostentatious lifestyle. HN admired that restraint but challenged the celebratory framing, arguing Craigslist’s underinvestment in scam, spam, exploitation, and marketplace governance may have imposed social costs philanthropy does not erase.

### Comment pulse

- Simplicity built goodwill → longtime observers recalled modest offices, minimal consumption, and a product aesthetic aligned with Newmark’s stated disinterest in prestige.

- Scam responsibility remains contested → critics cite fraudulent rentals and keyword spam — counterpoint: free listings yield no scam revenue and volunteer moderation responds.

- Platform conduct belongs in the evaluation → commenters linked Craigslist to local-news decline and weak marketplace safety, while others called those problems intractable.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Newmark’s giving is substantial, but founder philanthropy and platform stewardship should be evaluated independently rather than netted together.

- **Impact:** Recipient organizations gain durable funding; Craigslist users still depend on product-level investment to reduce fraud and improve discovery.

- **Watch next:** Seek audited grant totals, recipient outcomes, Giving Pledge progress, governance details, and measurable Craigslist safety improvements.
