# Swiss parliament lifts ban on new nuclear power plants

- Score: 662 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48585746) | Link: https://www.bluewin.ch/en/news/switzerland/parliament-lifts-ban-on-new-nuclear-power-plants-3257535.html

### TL;DR

Switzerland’s National Council joined the Federal Council and Council of States in backing legislation that would again permit framework approvals for new nuclear plants, reversing the existing construction ban. The move remains provisional: voters will make the final decision, and parliament rejected a financing-clarification delay by 100–98. Supporters want an option for winter supply as hydro potential and glacier-fed inflows tighten; opponents cite financial risk and disrupted renewable planning. HN mirrored that split, arguing over nuclear’s consistent output versus construction cost, delays, shrinking reactor fleets, and uncertain SMR economics.

### Comment pulse

- Seasonality drives the case → abundant snowmelt and sun favor summer supply; commenters expect electrification and less predictable glaciers to tighten winters.
- Cost dominates opposition → critics cited multibillion-dollar overruns and rising SMR estimates — counterpoint: supporters rejected cost expectations as grounds for a technology ban.
- Alternatives carry constraints → imports from France, pumped hydro, solar, and batteries could contribute, but commenters disputed sovereignty, capacity, and ecological tradeoffs.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Lifting a ban preserves optionality; it does not establish that a project can clear permitting, construction, and voter hurdles.
- **Impact:** Utilities and vendors may study sites and designs, while renewable investors face a broader but still uncertain capacity pathway.
