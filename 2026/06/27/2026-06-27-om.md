# Om

- Score: 492 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48693391) | Link: https://daringfireball.net/2026/06/om

### TL;DR

John Gruber remembers Om Malik, who died after a long struggle with heart disease, as a rare technology critic who combined generosity, candor, curiosity, and resistance to hype. Malik moved from prolific reporting and building GigaOm to investing in 2014, deliberately trading the 24-hour news cycle for slower, deeper essays after a 2008 heart attack. He continued publishing acclaimed work from intensive care. HN readers recalled his pioneering online video presence and recent writing, while many said the tribute made them wish they had known him personally.
