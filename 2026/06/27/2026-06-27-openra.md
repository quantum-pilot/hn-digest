# OpenRA

- Score: 551 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48697560) | Link: https://www.openra.net/

### TL;DR

OpenRA’s February 2026 playtest adds random map generators for Red Alert, Tiberian Dawn, and Dune 2000, configurable by biome, players, symmetry, and resources for skirmish or multiplayer. Dune 2000 gets visual effects, Starport bulk purchasing, campaign tuning, and community-led multiplayer balance. The standalone Tiberian Dawn HD mod is feature-complete, can mix classic and remastered assets, and is moving toward core integration. Editor tools, autosaves, expansion-building bots, missions, and localization groundwork round out the release. HN praised the modernization while debating whether rebalancing improves or erases beloved asymmetry.
