# 'Careless People' author claims Meta surveilled her for 12mos to enforce silence

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48701822) | Link: https://fortune.com/2026/06/26/meta-wynn-williams-surveillance-gag-order-lawsuit-2026/

### TL;DR

Former Meta policy executive Sarah Wynn-Williams sued in federal court to void an arbitration gag order and a severance non-disparagement clause she says was signed under duress. Her complaint alleges Meta spent more than a year sending representatives to photograph public appearances, sought $50,000 per violation, and objected even when she silently joined a panel with company critics. Meta says she accepted substantial severance, breached that agreement, and published false claims. HN focused on the underlying complaint, coercion allegations, and whether suppression efforts amplified interest in her bestselling memoir.
