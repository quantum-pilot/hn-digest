# Streaming services' obnoxiously loud ads become illegal on July 1 in California

- Score: 242 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48697768) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/06/streaming-services-obnoxiously-loud-ads-become-illegal-on-july-1-in-california/

### TL;DR

Starting July 1, California’s SB 576 bars video-streaming services from playing commercials louder than their accompanying programs, extending a CALM Act-style rule beyond broadcast, cable, and satellite TV. Illinois will impose similar requirements in 2027, potentially encouraging nationwide normalization. Industry groups opposed California’s measure, citing inconsistent server-side ad pipelines and varied playback devices; compliance may require file-based or real-time loudness processing. HN overwhelmingly welcomed closing the loophole, though audio specialists noted that codecs, surround-to-stereo downmixing, device processing, and psychoacoustic tricks make perceived loudness harder than matching simple volume levels.
