# OpenTTD 16.0-Beta1

- Score: 220 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48695149) | Link: https://www.openttd.org/news/2026/06/25/openttd-16-0-beta1

### TL;DR

OpenTTD 16.0-beta1 adds reversible trains, invite-free multiplayer companies, improved map generation, CargoDist subsidies, saved NewGRF collections, cargo-payment aging controls, searchable dropdowns, and consolidated vehicle previews. The project is asking players to test the beta, report bugs, and enter its title-screen game competition. HN discussion welcomed collections as a possible answer to intimidating mod setup while celebrating OpenTTD’s decades-long appeal to hackers; some users instead highlighted adjacent transport sims, custom forks, and the codebase’s growing complexity.
