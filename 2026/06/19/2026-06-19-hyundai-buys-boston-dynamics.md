# Hyundai buys Boston Dynamics

- Score: 601 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48600312) | Link: https://startupfortune.com/hyundai-takes-full-control-of-boston-dynamics-as-softbank-exits-for-325-million/

### TL;DR

Hyundai Motor Group is expected to pay $325 million for SoftBank’s remaining 9.65% of Boston Dynamics, exercising a put option from Hyundai’s 2021 purchase of an 80% stake and making the robotics company wholly owned. The strategic case is factory integration: electric Atlas is slated to begin parts-sequencing work at Hyundai’s Georgia EV plant by 2028, backed by Hyundai’s manufacturing and component supply chain. HN corrected the headline’s implication of a new takeover and debated whether flexible humanoids justify their complexity versus purpose-built automation.
