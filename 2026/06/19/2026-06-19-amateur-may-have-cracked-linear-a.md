# Amateur may have cracked Linear A

- Score: 396 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48600107) | Link: https://aiclambake.com/clamtakes/linear-a/

### TL;DR

Amateur linguist and AI engineer Tom Di Mino says Linear A encodes an extinct Semitic language ancestral to Hebrew. Starting from recurring Minoan prayer formulas, he assigned a disputed sign the value na, linked a verb to the Semitic N-W-Y root, and produced 40 sign readings plus a 408-term lexicon; specialists at Rutgers and Cambridge reportedly are reviewing the work. HN remained cautious: the corpus is only about 7,500 characters, the Semitic premise and inherited Linear B values are contestable, and no full manuscript or peer validation was available.
