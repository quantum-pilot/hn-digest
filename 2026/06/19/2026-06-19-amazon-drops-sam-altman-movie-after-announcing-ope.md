# Amazon drops Sam Altman movie after announcing OpenAI partnership

- Score: 161 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48602639) | Link: https://www.the-independent.com/arts-entertainment/films/news/sam-altman-biopic-amazon-openai-deal-b2999321.html

### TL;DR

Amazon MGM removed Luca Guadagnino’s nearly completed *Artificial* from its slate, leaving its planned early-2027 release uncertain while the filmmakers seek another distributor. The Andrew Garfield-led drama covers Sam Altman’s 2023 ouster and return at OpenAI; test screenings were reportedly positive. The decision followed Amazon’s expanded OpenAI cloud and investment partnership, but no causal explanation was given. HN split between seeing a prudent conflict-of-interest exit and criticizing the headline’s implied causation, while broader debate focused on tech platforms owning studios.
