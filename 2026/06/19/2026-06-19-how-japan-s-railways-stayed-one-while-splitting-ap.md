# How Japan's railways stayed one while splitting apart

- Score: 180 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48570730) | Link: https://arun.is/blog/jr-logo/

### TL;DR

Japan’s 1987 breakup of indebted JNR created seven independent JR companies, yet a shared identity preserved the impression of one national railway. Over 124 days, Nippon Design Center chose JR over NR, assigned regional colors from a 15-color production constraint, redesigned an ill-omened railway kanji, and supplied manuals enabling workers to brand 10,000 vehicles overnight. Yōji Yamamoto’s bold, direction-neutral mark has remained unchanged for nearly 40 years. HN discussion tested the branding story against practical travel costs and less romantic interpretations of railway work culture.
