# SpaceX Is Buying Cursor

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48554215) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgd5g7d7gyo

### TL;DR

SpaceX agreed to acquire Anysphere, maker of the Cursor AI coding agent, for $60 billion in SpaceX shares, with closing targeted by September’s end. The deal follows an April partnership that gave SpaceX the choice to buy Cursor or pay $10 billion for joint work, and pairs Cursor’s developer distribution with SpaceX’s Colossus training infrastructure. The announcement accompanied a post-IPO surge valuing SpaceX at $2.78 trillion, above Amazon despite far smaller revenue and a quarterly loss.
