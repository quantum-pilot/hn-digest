# AWS Bedrock to require sharing data with Anthropic for Mythos and future models

- Score: 423 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48473166) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48473166

### TL;DR

AWS Bedrock users choosing Anthropic’s Fable 5, Mythos 5, or future similarly capable models must opt into 30-day retention of all traffic, with data leaving AWS’s security boundary. Anthropic says retention enables cross-exchange misuse detection and promises automatic deletion, except for safety investigations or legal obligations. HN’s main concern was enterprise compliance: adding Anthropic as a data processor complicates contracts, residency, certifications, and zero-retention guarantees. Others noted Anthropic says retained data will not train models and consumers already face similar retention, while skeptics predicted regulated customers will switch providers.
