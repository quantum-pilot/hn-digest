# Raspberry Pi 5 – 16GB RAM

- Score: 307 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48481857) | Link: https://www.adafruit.com/product/6125?src=raspberrypi

### TL;DR

Adafruit lists the 16GB Raspberry Pi 5 at $350. It pairs a 2.4GHz quad-core Arm Cortex-A76 with LPDDR4X memory, VideoCore VII graphics, dual 4K60 output, USB 3, GPIO/MIPI connectivity, and PCIe 2.0 x1; a power supply, storage, and case are separate. HN questioned the value because mini PCs bundle faster CPUs and storage for less, while commenters attributed the price to scarce single-chip high-density RAM. Supporters saw it as a niche configuration rather than the new baseline.
