# Mercedes‑Benz starts large‑scale production of electric axial flux motor

- Score: 549 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48472877) | Link: https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/bebac2af-acdc-465a-9538-adb0bf3d8ccf

### TL;DR

Mercedes-Benz has begun large-scale production of YASA-derived axial-flux motors at Berlin-Marienfelde for the electric AMG GT four-door. Their disc layout sends magnetic flux parallel to the shaft, enabling compact dimensions and high torque and power density; the three motors are only about 8–9 cm wide. Industrialization required 98 process steps, including 35 described as globally new, plus precision wire forming, laser joining, and AI-assisted inspection. HN welcomed the packaging gains but expected conventional radial motors to dominate lower-cost vehicles until axial designs prove reliability and manufacturing economics.
