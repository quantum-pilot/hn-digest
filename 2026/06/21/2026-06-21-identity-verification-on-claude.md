# Identity verification on Claude

- Score: 518 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48618455) | Link: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14328960-identity-verification-on-claude

### TL;DR

Anthropic says it is rolling out identity checks for selected Claude capabilities and platform-integrity, safety, or compliance cases. Verification through Persona requires a physical government photo ID and may require a live selfie. Persona holds the images; Anthropic controls retention and can access records for appeals, while saying it neither stores copies nor trains models on them. Failed users get multiple attempts and support. HN commenters objected to biometric gatekeeping and Persona’s fraud-improvement allowance, but noted the help page existed since April and accepts IDs from most countries.
