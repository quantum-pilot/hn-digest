# Beyond All Reason (Free Total Annihilation Inspired RTS)

- Score: 418 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48617990) | Link: https://www.beyondallreason.info

### TL;DR

Beyond All Reason is a free, alpha-stage real-time strategy game inspired by Total Annihilation, built around armies of thousands, simulated projectiles and explosions, deformable terrain, varied unit roles, and large tactical maps. After almost seven years, the team is partnering with Hooded Horse toward a full Steam release. HN praised its technical scale, deep controls, active development, and faithful revival of classic RTS spectacle. The dominant warning was social: standard 8v8 lobbies can enforce rigid monthly metas, expose beginners to blame and kicks, and create a steep onboarding wall.
