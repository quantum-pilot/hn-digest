# Tell HN: Happy Fathers Day

- Score: 246 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48620502) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48620502

### TL;DR

An HN Father’s Day thread became a collection of reflections on how parenting reshapes work, ambition, memory, and identity. Fathers described the joy of a first 80 days, decades of ordinary family moments that later became precious, and the guilt and quiet sacrifice involved in balancing jobs with children. Others honored their own fathers for technical curiosity, persistence, artistry, and providing for a family. A recurring lesson was temporal: childhood offers surprisingly few repetitions of any calendar day, so presence matters more than perfect circumstances.

### Comment pulse

- Work gains meaning through family → one founder connected years of building a boot company to seeing both sons proudly wear its products.
- Parents transmit methods, not job descriptions → children remembered curiosity, persistence, integrity, technical confidence, and willingness to tackle unfamiliar problems.
- Ordinary days become the archive → commutes, daycare runs, repairs, spilled purses, swim classes, and college moves outlast professional milestones in memory.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Fatherhood reframes success from output toward stewardship: building capability, safety, and trust in another person over time.
- **Impact:** Employers and founders shape family life through schedules, commute demands, leave, and whether workers can be present during crises.
- **Watch next:** Protect rituals, document moments, ask for help, and revisit whether career choices support the example children observe.
