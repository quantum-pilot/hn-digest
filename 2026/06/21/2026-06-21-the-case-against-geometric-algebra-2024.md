# The case against geometric algebra (2024)

- Score: 116 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48617782) | Link: https://alexkritchevsky.com/2024/02/28/geometric-algebra.html

### TL;DR

The author supports geometric algebra’s goal of making geometry more intuitive and wants exterior algebra’s wedge product, multivectors, and coordinate-free ideas taught earlier. The objection is to elevating Hestenes’ geometric product into geometry’s universal primitive: multiplying vectors creates mixed-grade scalar-plus-bivector objects without a general geometric meaning, blurs geometric entities with transformation operators, and imports a tool valuable for spinors into unrelated domains. HN split sharply: critics said the article’s attacks on GA culture weakened its technical case, while others cited obscured gauge structure, units, degenerate metrics, and practical dead ends.
