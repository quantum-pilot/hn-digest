# The back cover of C++: The Language raises questions not answered by front cover

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48421079) | Link: https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20260605-01/?p=112391

### TL;DR

Raymond Chen examines a C++ textbook whose front cover shows JavaScript and whose back cover offers vague praise reusable for almost any subject. He finds near-identical language on four other Larson and Keller books about casting, food processing, nutrition, and materials science, indicating a publisher-wide blurb template rather than subject-specific editing. Hacker News readers treated both covers as signals of weak publishing quality, while noting that authors often lack control over packaging and that poor marketing does not prove the book itself is bad.
