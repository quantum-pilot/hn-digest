# S&P 500 rejects SpaceX, also blocking entry for OpenAI and Anthropic

- Score: 1343 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48421442) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/06/sp-500-blocks-fast-spacex-entry-wont-waive-rule-for-unprofitable-ai-firms/

### TL;DR

S&P Dow Jones Indices kept its S&P 500 eligibility rules after SpaceX sought accelerated admission around its IPO: a 12-month seasoning period, at least 10% public float, and demonstrated profitability remain required. That disqualifies SpaceX’s proposed 3% float and could delay unprofitable OpenAI and Anthropic; estimated automatic buying would have been $14 billion, more than $8 billion, and $4.6 billion respectively. Nasdaq and Russell adopted faster routes. HN favored consistent gatekeeping and a year of filings, while noting free-float weighting should keep exposure modest.

### Comment pulse

- Rules protect index trust → passive investors chose a methodology, not discretionary early exposure to companies that have not met its screens.
- Delay provides evidence → 12 months of SEC filings, GAAP results, and lockup expiration reveal more than an exceptional listing-day waiver.
- Portfolio impact may be overstated → one commenter estimated 0.3% because weighting uses free float — counterpoint: $7.5 trillion tracks the index.
