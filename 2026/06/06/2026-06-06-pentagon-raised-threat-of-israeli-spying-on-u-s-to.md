# Pentagon raised threat of Israeli spying on U.S. to highest level, sources say

- Score: 370 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48427523) | Link: https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/pentagon-raised-threat-israeli-spying-us-highest-level-sources-say-rcna348565

### TL;DR

NBC reports that the Defense Intelligence Agency raised Israel’s counterintelligence threat level to critical, citing unnamed U.S. officials who described unusually aggressive efforts to monitor senior American decision-making on Iran and Lebanon. Israel and the White House denied the report; the Pentagon declined comment. Officials reportedly expect greater travel and meeting precautions but no immediate reduction in daily intelligence sharing. HN commenters largely treated allied espionage as longstanding and focused on why the assessment surfaced now, speculating that widening U.S.–Israeli policy disagreements made an existing risk newly consequential.
