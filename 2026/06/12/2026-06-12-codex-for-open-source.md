# Codex for open source

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48497195) | Link: https://openai.com/form/codex-for-oss/

### TL;DR

OpenAI is accepting applications from primary or core maintainers of active, widely used, or ecosystem-critical open-source projects. Selected applicants receive six months of ChatGPT Pro with Codex, possible API credits for review, automation, and releases, plus conditional Codex Security access. The form requires GitHub profiles and repositories, while projects outside neat criteria may explain their importance. HN commenters welcomed assistance but criticized the six-month horizon as customer acquisition that could create unsustainable workflows, questioned GitHub exclusivity, and reported silence after applications from projects with substantial stars and downloads.

### Comment pulse

- Six months risks lock-in → maintainers may adopt subsidized workflows their projects cannot sustain — counterpoint: a half-year of Pro still provides meaningful support.

- GitHub-only intake narrows eligibility → important projects and contributors hosted elsewhere cannot satisfy public-profile and repository fields, regardless of ecosystem value.

- Selection lacks feedback → maintainers cited unanswered applications despite millions of downloads and thousands of stars, making qualification criteria difficult to interpret.
