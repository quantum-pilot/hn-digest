# Show HN: Putt.day a daily mini golf game

- Score: 313 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48510341) | Link: https://putt.day/

### TL;DR

Putt.day serves one 3D mini-golf hole each midnight Pacific: drag backward to aim and set power, move or zoom the camera, avoid water, and finish in as few strokes as possible. Only the first completed score counts; older holes remain playable. Hacker News liked the daily ritual but found rolling resistance and bounce damping too strong, camera angles that limit full-power shots, and some visuals unclear. Players discovered wall-bounce shortcuts that beat the six-stroke target in three to five shots; the creator was already tuning the physics.

### Comment pulse

- Physics needs calibration → commenters wanted less rolling resistance, more natural bounce damping, and a softer low-speed slowdown.

- Camera design affects control → high angles shorten the pull gesture, blocking maximum power; automatic alignment could reduce friction.

- Course exploits became part of the fun → wall jumps produced scores of three to five — counterpoint: shortcuts complicate meaningful par.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Daily scarcity and a single counted attempt create replayable stakes from a very small content footprint.

- **Impact:** Better touch controls and predictable physics would make performance feel skill-based across desktop and mobile.

- **Watch next:** Measure completion rates, stroke distributions, shortcut prevalence, camera-related retries, retention, and feedback after physics changes.
