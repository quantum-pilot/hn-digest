# AI is slowing down

- Score: 352 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48446893) | Link: https://www.wheresyoured.at/ai-is-slowing-down/

### TL;DR

Ed Zitron argues AI infrastructure commitments have outrun sustainable demand: 190 GW of planned data centers could require over $2 trillion in annual AI-related revenue by 2030, while OpenAI and Anthropic dominate current compute demand yet remain dependent on rapid growth and fundraising. He treats new token-spending caps and poor cost visibility as evidence that enterprise growth is already slowing. HN agreed the financing risk is substantial but split sharply over his combative presentation, assumptions, and dismissal of real productivity gains and capital-intensive infrastructure economics.

### Comment pulse

- Consumer squeeze → Bundled Apple and Google assistants could commoditize basic AI — counterpoint: habits, prosumer workflows, and cloud access may preserve subscriptions.
- Economic rebuttal → Critics compare inference to early cloud or telecom, where routing, caching, segmentation, utilization, and capex recovery can eventually support margins.
- Evidence dispute → Users report major individual gains, but others see little measured economy-wide value and argue anecdotes cannot validate trillion-dollar investment.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The strongest claim concerns financing mismatch; the weakest leap is treating uncertain aggregate ROI as proof of negligible utility.
