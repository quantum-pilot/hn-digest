# Apple WWDC 2026

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48448106) | Link: https://www.apple.com/apple-events/event-stream/

### TL;DR

Apple’s WWDC 2026 overview promises a redesigned Siri AI with richer answers, natural conversation, and a dedicated app; broader Apple Intelligence features for photo editing, Image Playground, Photos, Messages, and Safari; expanded child-safety tools; and systemwide performance improvements later this year. Siri AI starts in English and will initially exclude EU iPhones and iPads. HN discussion centered on Apple tempering Liquid Glass after criticism, enthusiasm for conversational Shortcut creation and user-defined Safari extensions, skepticism about the staged keynote, and disagreement over AI-altered photography and EU privacy explanations.

### Comment pulse
