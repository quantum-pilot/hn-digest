# APC–2 – A professional record cutter for producing original playback discs

- Score: 261 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48440383) | Link: https://teenage.engineering/products/apc-2

### TL;DR

Teenage Engineering’s APC–2 is a 140kg, limited-production lathe that cuts stereo playback discs in real time through partner SUPERSENSE; pricing is by inquiry. It combines direct drive with under 0.01% wow/flutter, a feedback cutting head, heated cutting, vacuum hold-down and swarf removal, RIAA encoding/monitoring, and Ethernet/Wi-Fi control. DAW-controlled variable pitch enables locked grooves and specialty cuts. HN admired the precision and analog eccentricity but questioned the democratization pitch: professional pressing can cost about $10 per record at 100 units, while this machine appears aimed at specialists, not individuals.
