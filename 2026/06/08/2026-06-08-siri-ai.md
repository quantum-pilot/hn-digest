# Siri AI

- Score: 354 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48449084) | Link: https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/

### TL;DR

Apple says Siri AI will enter English beta later in 2026 with natural conversation, personal-context search across photos, mail, and notes, app actions, web-grounded answers, and a dedicated cross-device conversation app. Visual Intelligence expands camera and screen understanding to iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision Pro; Apple emphasizes on-device processing plus Private Cloud Compute. Support starts with iPhone 15 Pro and M1 Macs and iPads, while EU iPhone/iPad availability is delayed. HN liked the assistant-as-interface direction but distrusted Apple’s execution history and split over third-party access: user choice versus privacy exposure.

### Comment pulse

- Siri as an operating-system interface is compelling → personal context and App Intents could turn natural language into cross-app actions, not just chat.
- Apple has a credibility deficit → years of weak search, unreliable timers, and previously delayed promises make polished demos insufficient.
- Third-party assistants divide the thread → granular permissions could expand choice — counterpoint: broad device context may expose nearly every personal document.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The moat is trusted action over private device context, not chatbot quality alone.
- **Impact:** Developers can expose actions to Siri and use offline on-device foundation models without per-request fees.
- **Watch next:** Track basic-task reliability, permission granularity, action reversibility, privacy verification, beta stability, and EU interoperability.
