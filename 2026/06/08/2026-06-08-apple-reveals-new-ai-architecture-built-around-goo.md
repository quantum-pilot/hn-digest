# Apple reveals new AI architecture built around Google Gemini models

- Score: 308 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48450142) | Link: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/apple-reveals-new-ai-architecture/

### TL;DR

Apple says its revamped Apple Intelligence uses foundation models co-developed with Google from Gemini technology, adapted for on-device execution and Private Cloud Compute. A system orchestrator supplies app and task context, while new multimodal features cover image generation, editing, visual questions, speech, dictation, and language understanding. Apple maintains that neither it nor third parties can access request data. HN viewed privacy-preserving OS integration as the differentiator, but debated whether Google is merely a model supplier, whether Apple’s guarantees are verifiable, and whether shared Gemini roots weaken product differentiation.
