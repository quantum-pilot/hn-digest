# How much of Thermo Fisher's antibody data has been manipulated?

- Score: 396 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48442075) | Link: https://reeserichardson.blog/2026/05/28/how-much-of-thermo-fishers-antibody-data-has-been-manipulated/

### TL;DR

Reese Richardson and Sholto David report more than 450 vendor antibody-verification images showing apparent manipulation, mostly in Thermo Fisher’s catalog. Repeated or transformed bands, painted-over regions, duplicated noise, and reused backgrounds undermine evidence meant to establish antibody specificity, though they do not prove the reagents themselves fail. Thermo Fisher denies fabrication and describes some changes as presentation adjustments. HN commenters largely saw systematic misconduct with costly reproducibility consequences, while emphasizing the unresolved distinction between altered website images and compromised underlying experiments—and the need for an unbiased prevalence estimate.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Image forensics establishes unreliable evidence, not reagent failure; raw-data provenance is the decisive next layer.
- **Impact:** Buyers, journals, and regulators need traceable validation records rather than vendor screenshots and informal laboratory memory.
