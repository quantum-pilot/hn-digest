# Anti-social: It's fads, not friends, which now dominate social media feeds

- Score: 526 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48444228) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/worklife/article/20260520-how-social-media-ceased-to-be-social

### TL;DR

Social platforms are shifting from friend updates to algorithmically selected entertainment, while personal sharing migrates to private messaging and groups. In the UK, active posting fell from 61% to 49%; among US Gen Z, only 18% post actively versus 74% consuming passively. The model rewards engagement and targeted advertising—global social ad revenue is forecast at $317 billion in 2026—while forcing small businesses to become content producers. HN compared feeds to personalized cable, debated whether HN shares the same addictive dynamics, and reported that quitting improves productivity but increases isolation.

- **View:** Feed controls cannot realign incentives: users may select friends, but platforms profit when default recommendations maximize consumption.
- **Impact:** Creators increasingly depend on opaque recommendation systems rather than accumulated followers, turning distribution into a continuous production requirement.
