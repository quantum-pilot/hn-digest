# Google limits Meta's use of its Gemini AI models

- Score: 140 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48707103) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/28/google-limits-metas-use-of-its-gemini-ai-models-ft-reports.html

### TL;DR

Google reportedly could not supply all the Gemini capacity Meta requested around March, delaying some internal AI work and prompting Meta to conserve tokens. Other customers were affected less, while Google said compute constraints also capped Cloud growth despite first-quarter revenue reaching $20 billion and backlog nearly doubling. The report was not independently verified, and neither company commented. HN called the headline misleading because this appears to be a quota shortage, not a policy restriction, then debated demand sources, TPU allocation, pricing, and why Meta uses Gemini heavily.

### Comment pulse

- Cloud capacity is finite beneath elastic pricing → large customers routinely hit quotas when providers lack hardware or reserved headroom.
- Capacity provenance remains opaque → commenters could not separate paid Gemini demand, Search summaries, TPU rentals, and Google’s own products.
- Shortages reach ordinary users → several reported traffic errors and poor uptime, especially on free or lower-tier Gemini APIs.
