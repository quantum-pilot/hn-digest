# Professor denounces mass AI fraud on an exam at Brown

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48708991) | Link: https://english.elpais.com/education/2026-06-28/ai-fraud-at-brown-university-academic-integrity-is-at-risk.html

### TL;DR

Brown economist Roberto Serrano says at least 50 students used AI on a take-home mathematical-economics midterm adopted after a campus shooting: 89 submissions averaged 96/100 and 40 were perfect, with passages matching ChatGPT outputs. After he made the 50%-weighted final in-person, attendance fell to 59 and the average to 48; 22 absent students had perfect midterms. Serrano is ending graded take-home work and criticized Brown’s muted response. HN favored proctored or isolated-computer exams plus oral defenses, while arguing instructors should align grading incentives with learning rather than rely on honor.

### Comment pulse

- Assessment must verify understanding → in-person exams, offline computers, or oral defenses make students explain work instead of merely submitting generated answers.
- Design against metric gaming → students optimize grades per unit effort; every high-scoring path should require learning or make cheating harder than studying.
- Honor culture can discourage cheating → trust builds integrity — counterpoint: prestigious credentials and weak enforcement make prohibited AI use strategically attractive.
