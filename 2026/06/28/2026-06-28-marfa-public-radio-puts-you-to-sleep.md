# Marfa Public Radio Puts You to Sleep

- Score: 386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48703759) | Link: https://www.marfapublicradio.org/podcast/marfa-public-radio-puts-you-to-sleep

### TL;DR

Marfa Public Radio turned its operational paperwork into a membership-drive sleep podcast, pairing its need to run continuously with deliberately soporific readings of FCC rules, NPR guidance, postal regulations, licensing terms, and other essential documents. Episodes range from roughly 10 minutes to over an hour and double as an offbeat donation pitch. HN readers traded favorite audio sleep aids—from monotonous fictional baseball and outdated sports talk to foreign-language radio and the BBC Shipping Forecast—while noting the genre’s paradox: supposedly boring technical material can become unexpectedly absorbing.

### Comment pulse

- Monotony must avoid engagement → meandering delivery, stale predictions, and low-stakes narration work because listeners can stop following without missing anything.
- Boredom is subjective → history, physics, and obsolete manuals often become captivating precisely because they are technical or unfamiliar.
- Partial comprehension tires attention → one listener uses French radio until decoding effort fades into sleep after 20–30 minutes.
