# Feds Killed Polestar and Spared Volvo

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48703613) | Link: https://www.thedrive.com/news/feds-killed-polestar-and-spared-volvo-that-should-terrify-you

### TL;DR

The U.S. Commerce Department denied Geely-owned Polestar authorization under the Connected Vehicle Rule for model year 2027 onward, effectively blocking new U.S. sales, while approving sister brand Volvo. The article calls the distinction unexplained and protectionist, noting the Polestar 3 moved to South Carolina production beside Volvo’s EX90. HN argued the piece conflates manufacturing and tariffs with connected-system security: commenters suggested Polestar’s China-developed software may be decisive, while others said regulators should ban specific telemetry or remote-control risks regardless of corporate nationality and questioned inconsistent treatment of U.S. data collection.
