# MacBook Neo is so popular that Apple doubled production

- Score: 310 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48386238) | Link: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/03/macbook-neo-production-doubled-says-kuo/

### TL;DR

Supply-chain analyst Ming-Chi Kuo estimates Apple raised 2026 MacBook Neo shipments from 5 million to 10 million after its March launch. Apple says demand exceeded forecasts and drove record first-time Mac buyers; IDC figures reportedly support strong sales. At $599, or $499 for students, the A18 Pro laptop is Apple’s cheapest MacBook, prompting Dell to answer with a $699 XPS 13. HN discussion focused on how aggressive pricing reshapes entry-level laptop competition, while questioning which omissions users will accept.

### Comment pulse

- Value proposition → Commenters said equivalent Windows laptops often cost more while compromising chassis, display, keyboard, trackpad, battery, or reliability.
- Support economics → Family and enterprise IT anecdotes reported far fewer interventions after Mac adoption, making ownership cost broader than purchase price.
- Configuration tradeoff → One owner found 8GB sufficient for 20 Chrome tabs and light tools — counterpoint: heavier users were steered toward used 16GB Airs.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The Neo’s advantage is systems integration: custom silicon, product simplification, procurement scale, software efficiency, and distribution reinforce one another.
- **Impact:** PC makers face pressure to deliver premium-feeling basics below $700 without destroying margins or cannibalizing higher-tier models.
- **Watch next:** Actual sell-through versus supplier shipments, first-time-buyer retention, Dell XPS demand, return rates, RAM-related support, and 2027 model pricing.
