# Jobs and Software Is Fucked

- Score: 262 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48635112) | Link: https://urflow.bearblog.dev/jobs-and-software-is-fucked/

### TL;DR

A software engineer with 10 years’ experience, seven at Blizzard, describes a year-long search after a June 2025 layoff: promising final rounds lost to other candidates or internal transfers, recruiter silence, keyword screens, and AI-proctored coding tests that disadvantage applicants who follow the rules. The author argues AI has intensified an already hostile market, eroding junior pathways and pressuring workers to abandon craft, security, testing, and creative colleagues. HN responses ranged from leaving tech for stable trades to embracing AI pragmatically, networking earlier, or betting that production judgment remains scarce.

### Comment pulse

- Trades can restore stability and enjoyment → one commenter found diesel mechanics offered paid schooling, annual raises, relaxed coworkers, and energy for hobby programming.
- AI adoption is an ethical fault line → business developers see leverage — counterpoint: game artists see tools trained on their work eliminating livelihoods.
- Game development resists shallow automation → visual scripting, reflection, implicit state, and interaction-heavy legacy code defeat plausible-looking generation, though management may miss that.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Hiring automation and coding automation reinforce each other: higher applicant volume invites harsher filtering, which further obscures genuine capability.
- **Impact:** Experienced developers endure longer searches; juniors lose apprenticeship routes; creative teams face sharper substitution pressure than context-heavy engineering roles.
- **Watch next:** Measure interview validity, cheating, junior hiring, wages, retention, and defect rates before attributing outcomes to AI.
