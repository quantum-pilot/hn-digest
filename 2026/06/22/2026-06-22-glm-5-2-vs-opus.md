# GLM 5.2 vs. Opus

- Score: 478 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48626866) | Link: https://techstackups.com/comparisons/glm-5.2-vs-opus/

### TL;DR

A one-shot raw-WebGL platformer test found both GLM-5.2 and Claude Opus 4.8 completed a complex browser game, but with different tradeoffs. Opus finished in 33m30s, produced the cleaner and more correct result, and visually inspected screenshots; GLM took 1h10m40s, shipped missing textures and broken game logic, but cost $5.39 versus roughly $21.92. Broader benchmarks place MIT-licensed, open-weight GLM near frontier models, especially in reasoning, though it is text-only and token-hungry. HN questioned whether one-shot demos predict disciplined work on real projects.
