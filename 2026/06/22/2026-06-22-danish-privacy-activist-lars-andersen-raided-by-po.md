# Danish privacy activist Lars Andersen raided by police

- Score: 409 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48625823) | Link: https://twitter.com/LarsAnders1620/status/2068208864747540516#m

### TL;DR

Danish privacy activist and former officer Lars Andersen says masked police broke down his door after he obliquely published Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen’s social-security and phone numbers while challenging her encryption and surveillance policies. He alleges officers immediately cut power, disconnected his router, seized locally recording Nest cameras, and initially refused to state charges, preventing a record of the arrest. HN questioned the raid’s legality and evidence handling but described Andersen as a polarizing figure whose alleged tracking, doxxing, and harassment of politicians’ families may undermine his privacy campaign.
