# Scientists ejected from diabetes conference for distributing journal reprints

- Score: 302 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48433410) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/06/scientists-ejected-from-diabetes-conference-for-distributing-journal-reprints/

### TL;DR

Five registered scientists—including Diabetes Care editor-in-chief Steven Kahn and former ADA president Desmond Schatz—were removed from the American Diabetes Association meeting after distributing reprints of an editorial in Diabetes Care criticizing Trump administration actions toward biomedical research. They stood outside a room where NIH director Jay Bhattacharya was scheduled to speak; he canceled. ADA said they violated a conduct code banning protest and refused requests to stop, while posted video reportedly showed no disorder. HN debated censorship versus legitimate rule enforcement and suspected fear over federal funding shaped the response.
