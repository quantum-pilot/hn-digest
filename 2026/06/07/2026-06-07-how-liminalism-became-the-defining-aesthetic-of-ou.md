# How Liminalism Became the Defining Aesthetic of Our Time

- Score: 165 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48431085) | Link: https://hyperallergic.com/how-liminalism-became-the-defining-aesthetic-of-our-time/

### TL;DR

The essay names liminalism as a crowd-curated internet art movement built from real photographs of empty, familiar transit and commercial spaces. Tracing its lineage from de Chirico, Magritte, and Hopper through the Backrooms, it argues that uncanny placelessness expresses postindustrial decline, COVID isolation, smartphone atomization, and late-capitalist homogenization; major communities prohibit AI imagery. HN disputed its status as the era’s defining aesthetic, calling it one nostalgia-driven microniche among many, while sharpening the visual recipe: expected human activity removed from dated or intrinsically unpleasant architecture.
