# The OnlyFans Economy of American AI

- Score: 131 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48435371) | Link: https://leoveanu.com/2026-06-06-qwen3.7max/

### TL;DR

The essay argues US frontier AI no longer earns its price premium because Chinese models—especially Qwen 3.7 Max—are good enough for engineering work. It frames customer loyalty as parasocial, enabling wasteful token spending, inflated valuations, and tolerance of rate limits despite cheaper DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax access. The case rests largely on the author’s extended testing plus benchmark and usage rankings. HN partly agreed on everyday coding value, but challenged the reductive comparison: premium models may be more reliable and bundle integrations, while regulation and data-security concerns constrain Chinese-model adoption.
