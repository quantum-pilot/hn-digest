# Public Domain Image Archive

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48430539) | Link: https://pdimagearchive.org/

### TL;DR

The Public Domain Review offers an archive of 11,082 hand-picked, purportedly out-of-copyright images, updated weekly and searchable by artist, century, style, theme, tag, or gallery; users can browse, download, and reuse works. HN welcomed a curated gateway into open cultural collections but focused on provenance. The archive distinguishes underlying-work rights from digital-copy rights and links source institutions, yet labels its assessments as guidance rather than legal assurance. Commenters advised verifying source records for consequential uses and noted that publication platforms may demand stronger proof; desktop infinite scrolling also drew criticism.

### Comment pulse

- Provenance → A public-domain label is insufficient for high-stakes reuse; verify authorship, publication dates, underlying rights, digital-copy claims, and source-institution records.
