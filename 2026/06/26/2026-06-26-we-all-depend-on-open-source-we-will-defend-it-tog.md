# We all depend on open source. We will defend it together

- Score: 434 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48682737) | Link: https://akrites.org/letter/

### TL;DR

Akrites is a multi-company initiative to coordinate AI-assisted discovery, confidential remediation, upstream fixes, disclosure, and downstream patch deployment for critical open-source vulnerabilities. It proposes a shared incident-response team so maintainers receive one validated report and tested fix instead of duplicate, conflicting submissions; participants promise engineers, expertise, funding, and last-resort maintenance for abandoned packages. HN was sharply skeptical that AWS, Microsoft, OpenAI, banks, and other corporations should centralize stewardship of the commons, demanding direct maintainer support and existing contribution channels; others noted companies already produce and fund much essential open source.

### Comment pulse

- Corporate coordination risks capture → critics fear forks, centralized control, and extracting free labor — counterpoint: companies already build much production open source.
- Tangible maintainer support is the test → developers asked for hardware, CI, security reviews, mentorship, direct funding, and predictable upstream engagement.
- Bug bounties may add noise → foundation experience suggests paid maintainership and infrastructure can outperform high-volume external reports.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Akrites succeeds only if coordination reduces maintainer burden without converting community governance into corporate gatekeeping.
- **Impact:** A trusted upstream response channel could shorten remediation while giving critical-infrastructure operators time to deploy before public exploitation.
- **Watch next:** Publish governance, conflict rules, maintainer consent, funding allocations, disclosure timelines, patch uptake metrics, and criteria for last-resort stewardship.
