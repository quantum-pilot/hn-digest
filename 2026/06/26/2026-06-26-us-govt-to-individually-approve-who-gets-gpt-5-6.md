# US Govt to individually approve who gets GPT 5.6

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48683021) | Link: https://old.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1ufo0un/us_govt_to_individually_approve_who_gets_gpt_56/

### TL;DR

The Trump administration will initially approve which companies can access OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 Sol and Anthropic’s Mythos 5, extending customer vetting beyond Anthropic after frontier models demonstrated stronger vulnerability discovery. Anthropic reportedly received a changeable list of roughly 100 U.S.-based companies; Sol has no individual-access path, and Britain’s AI Security Institute is reportedly its only non-U.S. recipient. OpenAI calls the arrangement temporary and opposes government customer selection. HN feared opaque winner-picking, startup exclusion, and weakened U.S. competitiveness, while acknowledging that cyber capability creates a real distribution problem requiring predictable rules.

### Comment pulse

- Opaque approval invites capture → no published criteria, appeal path, or stable list lets the executive branch advantage incumbents and punish rivals.
- Restrictions may erode the U.S. moat → foreign developers can adopt unrestricted alternatives while American startups pay more or wait for permission.
- The exclusion may be temporary → OpenAI expects broader access within weeks — counterpoint: temporary emergency procedures can harden into durable, discretionary controls.
