# Mag 7 starting to underperform [pdf]

- Score: 177 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48719532) | Link: https://www.apollo.com/content/dam/apolloaem/pdf/daily-spark/2026/jun/28/062826-Mag7.pdf

### TL;DR

Apollo’s June 2026 deck argues the Magnificent Seven are beginning to lag the other 493 S&P 500 companies as earnings growth converges, hyperscaler free cash flow falls, and capital expenditure consumes more operating cash. Their forward-P/E premium is at a decade-plus low, yet cyclically adjusted valuations remain near records; the top ten stocks still comprise 42% of the index. HN cautioned that the underperformance window is short and historical winner-reversion claims need factor and international validation. Debate centered on whether AI spending creates durable revenue or merely raises existing-service costs.

### Comment pulse

- Recent winners often mean-revert → concentrated holdings face long-horizon risk — counterpoint: Mag 7 firms have defied similar warnings for decades.
- AI monetization remains disputed → inference raises costs for existing products, while cloud growth, enterprise adoption, and premium agents may create revenue.
- Capex discipline has two faces → lower spending may restore buybacks and cash flow, but data-center construction creates productive assets and skilled jobs.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The decisive variable is not AI demand but conversion of infrastructure spending into incremental margins and defensible cash flows.
- **Impact:** Index investors carry concentrated exposure; suppliers benefit from construction, while hyperscaler shareholders bear utilization and return-on-capital risk.
- **Watch next:** Compare Mag 7 relative returns, capex guidance, free-cash-flow margins, cloud earnings, agent revenue, data-center utilization, and buyback changes.
