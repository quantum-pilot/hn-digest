# AI boom risks global financial crash, warn central bankers

- Score: 155 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48713697) | Link: https://www.telegraph.co.uk/business/2026/06/28/ai-boom-risks-global-financial-crash-central-bankers-warn/

### TL;DR

The BIS warns that the five largest hyperscalers will spend more than $1 trillion on AI capital expenditure in 2025–26, outpacing earnings and free cash flow and pushing some toward debt. Winner-take-most competition may create collectively unprofitable overinvestment; disappointing returns could halt financing and trigger a prolonged bust. Electricity, chip, and grid bottlenecks could add inflation, while leverage, credit exposure, falling asset prices, and weak suppliers amplify losses. HN debated whether success itself destroys incumbent values, doubted central bankers’ foresight, and disputed whether the capital would benefit society more elsewhere.
