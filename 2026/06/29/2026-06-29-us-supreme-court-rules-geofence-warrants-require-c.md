# US Supreme Court rules geofence warrants require constitutional protections

- Score: 397 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48720924) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jun/29/supreme-court-geofence-warrants-case-decision

### TL;DR

In a 6–3 ruling, the Supreme Court held that police demands for Google location-history data through geofence warrants are Fourth Amendment searches, even when movement occurred publicly or covered a short period. The case arose after a three-step search narrowed 19 anonymous accounts to bank-robbery suspect Okello Chatrie. An appeals court must still decide whether each step had probable cause and sufficient particularity. HN welcomed the privacy boundary but questioned whether procedural exceptions leave violations without remedies and whether the reasoning reaches other mass-surveillance systems.
