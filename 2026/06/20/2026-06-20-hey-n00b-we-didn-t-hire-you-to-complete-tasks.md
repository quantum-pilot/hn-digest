# Hey, n00b, we didn't hire you to complete tasks

- Score: 212 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48604851) | Link: https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/hey-n00b-we-didnt-hire-you-to-complete

### TL;DR

Kent Beck argues juniors are hired as investments in future engineering capacity, not merely to clear tickets. Baseline success means working code, communication, reasonable delivery, honesty, and learning from mistakes; exceptional growth appears in learning per task, questioning unnecessary work, finding high-leverage scope, shipping small diffs, building reusable tools, testing, reviewing, and sharing lessons. HN readers found the roadmap useful but disputed whether firms still invest long term, warning that rankings can reward side quests or toxic blame.
