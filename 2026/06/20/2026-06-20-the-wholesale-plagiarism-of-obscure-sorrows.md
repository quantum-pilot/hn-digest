# The Wholesale Plagiarism of Obscure Sorrows

- Score: 302 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48611411) | Link: https://waxy.org/2026/06/the-wholesale-plagiarism-of-obscure-sorrows/

### TL;DR

An impostor site copied John Koenig’s Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows—its foreword and 311 entries—without permission, replaced the art with DALL-E 2 images, added a GPT-4 word generator, and monetized Qontour affiliate links. It now outranks official sources, while ChatGPT and Gemini misidentify it as Koenig’s work; two publisher DMCA notices reportedly achieved nothing. HN saw an enforcement asymmetry: AI lowers repackaging costs, platforms rarely arbitrate notices, and creators face expensive litigation. Commenters emphasized that humans copied the book verbatim; AI made the unauthorized package cheaper and more convincing.

### Comment pulse

- AI accelerates an older imbalance → anonymous publishers already controlled distribution, but cheap generation now multiplies polished copies — counterpoint: humans performed the verbatim theft.
