# Show HN: StartupWiki – A Free Alternative to Crunchbase

- Score: 142 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48610224) | Link: https://startupwiki.tech/

### TL;DR

StartupWiki presents a searchable, community-oriented directory of ventures, listing company descriptions, categories, funding stages, and locations, plus submissions and planned microbusiness coverage. The visible sample emphasizes established technology companies, while the page currently shows no micro companies. HN testing exposed the main launch gaps: several commenters found none of ten known startups, duplicated listings appeared, and verified badges lacked working provenance links. The creator plans to batch roughly 5,000 YC companies, fix source retrieval, and expose an agent activity ledger; users also proposed on-demand research through an API.

### Comment pulse

- Coverage determines usefulness → initial users found zero of ten startups, prompting a plan to ingest roughly 5,000 YC companies over several days.

- Verification needs inspectable provenance → badges without sources or timestamps inspire little trust; a broken database fetch currently hides the intended evidence.

- Generate records on demand → an API-triggered agent pipeline could prioritize requested companies instead of precomputing an incomplete global catalog.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** A directory’s product is trust, not volume; automation only helps when every field carries source, timestamp, and confidence metadata.

- **Impact:** Researchers gain a free discovery surface, but founders and analysts bear verification costs until coverage and provenance mature.

- **Watch next:** Track deduplication, citation-link fixes, update cadence, field-level accuracy, microbusiness coverage, API availability, and whether the ledger remains complete.
