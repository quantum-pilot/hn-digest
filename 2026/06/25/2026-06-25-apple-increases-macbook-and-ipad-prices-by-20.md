# Apple increases MacBook and iPad prices by 20%

- Score: 257 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48674096) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/0f067265-2baf-4b6e-8fb2-ed56daef6f3c

### TL;DR

Apple raised prices across Macs, iPads, HomePods, and Apple TV as AI-datacenter demand diverted memory supply and drove DRAM prices up as much as 98% in early 2026. MacBook Neo rose from $599 to $699; a 512GB Air to $1,299; a 1TB Pro to $1,999; and 128GB iPad Air to $749. Analysts expect pressure on device sales and possibly iPhones. Sparse HN discussion treated the hikes as a long-lived reset and renewed calls for user-replaceable notebook RAM.

### Comment pulse

- Higher prices may persist → commenters inferred Apple exhausted favorable supply contracts and rarely reverses increases without severe demand loss.
- Repairability could soften future shocks → one commenter urged replaceable notebook memory, specifically LPCAMM2.
