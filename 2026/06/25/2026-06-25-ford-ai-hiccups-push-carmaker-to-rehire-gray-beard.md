# Ford AI hiccups push carmaker to rehire ‘gray beard’ inspectors

- Score: 566 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48674446) | Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-25/ford-has-been-rehiring-quality-inspectors-after-ai-fell-short

### TL;DR

Ford ranked first among mainstream brands and third overall in J.D. Power’s latest initial-quality study, with 152 problems per 100 vehicles, after years of costly recalls. It credits cross-functional plant reviews, stricter supplier controls, 100,000-plus software tests, and hiring 315 veteran technical specialists since 2023; AI and cameras still detect anomalies but are no longer treated as sufficient. HN treated the result as a warning against replacing expertise with automation, despite disagreement over whether layoffs were involved; Ford still led with 51 recalls near mid-2026.
