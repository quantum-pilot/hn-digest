# Windows 10 quietly gets one more year of support and updates

- Score: 220 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48675369) | Link: https://www.neowin.net/news/windows-10-quietly-gets-one-more-year-of-support-and-updates/

### TL;DR

Microsoft has extended Windows 10’s Extended Security Updates through October 12, 2027, one year beyond the expected end; enrolled users continue automatically, and others may join before then. ESU supplies security fixes after normal support ended in 2025, giving people more time to replace incompatible hardware or move operating systems. The article frames the change as avoiding security risk across many PCs amid rising hardware prices. HN welcomed the reprieve but criticized Windows 11’s hardware and account requirements, debated LTSC as an alternative, and described migrations to Fedora or Ubuntu.
