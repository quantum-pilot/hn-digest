# IBM debuts sub-1 nanometer chip technology

- Score: 248 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48674967) | Link: https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-06-25-ibm-debuts-worlds-first-sub-1-nanometer-chip-technology

### TL;DR

IBM unveiled a research prototype for its 0.7-nanometer node, using a nanostack architecture that vertically staggers nanosheet transistors and permits different materials per layer. IBM reports nearly 100 billion transistors on a fingernail-sized chip, roughly twice its 2-nanometer density, with projected gains of 50% performance or 70% efficiency and possible production within five years. HN agreed the engineering was notable but disputed the branding: 0.7 nanometers is a process-generation label or density analogy, not a literal transistor dimension, making comparisons and marketing claims difficult to audit.
