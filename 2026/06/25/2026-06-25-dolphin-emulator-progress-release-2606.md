# Dolphin Emulator Progress Release 2606

- Score: 211 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48671392) | Link: https://dolphin-emu.org/blog/2026/06/25/dolphin-progress-report-release-2606/

### TL;DR

Dolphin 2606 delivers 443 commits led by long-awaited Game Boy Player emulation through integrated mGBA, plus working support for The Key of Avalon, the final previously unplayable Triforce title, using four client instances and a server. A resolution-aware blurred-bloom graphics mod fixes broken high-resolution lighting across more than 50 games. The release also adds per-Wiimote audio routing, custom cropping, Wii RetroAchievements, and Android 7 as the minimum. Dolphin moved behind Bunny’s CDN and firewall after AI scrapers caused outages. HN celebrated the project’s polish, portability, and unusually educational release notes.
