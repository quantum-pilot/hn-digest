# Om Malik has died

- Score: 290 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48678852) | Link: https://om.co/2026/06/24/1966-2026/

### TL;DR

Om Malik’s family announced that he died June 24, 2026, at Stanford Hospital after a long struggle with heart health, surrounded by family and friends. They invited readers to share memories and pointed to his writing, biography, and photography. HN’s remembrances described a direct, jargon-free technology journalist who challenged telecom excesses, built GigaOM into a defining publication, supported founders and colleagues generously, and later centered writing, photography, and travel. Former readers, employees, and acquaintances emphasized his attentive kindness as strongly as his influence.
