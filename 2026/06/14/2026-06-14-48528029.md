# I indexed 669 GB of my GoPro videos using my M1 Max computer and local ML models

- Score: 382 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48528029) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48528029

### TL;DR

To search years of cycling footage without uploading it, the author built a local pipeline that transcribes audio with Whisper, samples scenes at 1 fps, detects faces, objects, text, and shot types, generates descriptions, and stores text, image, and audio embeddings in three vector collections. On an M1 Max, indexing 628 videos—668.68GB and 15h13m—took 67h41m, about 4.4× playback time. Chat queries can send matching scenes to DaVinci Resolve. HN praised private memory discovery but questioned speed, noting lower-resolution sampling, rented GPUs, and built-in editor search.
