### TL;DR

Linux 7.1 arrived on schedule despite Linus Torvalds traveling across time zones and preparing to handle the next merge window partly offline. The final week brought no alarming changes: mostly small driver updates, networking fixes, runtime-verification and tracing-tool repairs, plus assorted bug fixes. Torvalds asks users to keep testing. Hacker News readers focused less on the version number—which advances mechanically—and more on practical gains: a Wi-Fi fix, anticipation for a newer NTFS driver, distro rollout timing, and debate over removing obsolete drivers amid AI-generated bug reports.
