# Why I'm Forced to Say Farewell: Google Management Has Lost Its Moral Compass

- Score: 340 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48496396) | Link: https://www.mayrhofer.eu.org/post/leaving-google/

### TL;DR

René Mayrhofer, formerly Google’s Android Platform Security director and most recently a part-time Principal Engineer in Austria, is resigning after nine years. He praises Android’s security and privacy teams but says Google’s retreat from carbon-neutral goals, broad Pentagon AI deal, and weakened internal debate violate his pacifist and European privacy commitments. He will leave August 31 and continue open security work. HN split sharply: critics called his ethical awakening late and financially convenient; defenders said drawing a line at offensive military use remains coherent and valuable.

### Comment pulse

- The ethical break came late → critics cited Google’s surveillance business and earlier compromises, questioning why military AI alone triggered resignation.
- Financial privilege enabled exit → few employees can afford public resignation, yet commenters argued that using privilege ethically still has value.
- Military work is not uniformly immoral → defense can deter invasion — counterpoint: critics distinguish allied protection from unchecked US offensive power.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Ethical commitments become credible when organizations define contract exclusions, escalation paths, and employee veto channels before controversial deals.
- **Impact:** Google loses senior Android security expertise and signals retention risk for staff whose boundaries conflict with defense work.
- **Watch next:** Look for internal debate reforms, narrower military-use clauses, additional departures, and Mayrhofer’s continued AOSP security contributions.
