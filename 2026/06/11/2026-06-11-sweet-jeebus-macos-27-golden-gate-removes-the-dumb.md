# Sweet Jeebus, macOS 27 Golden Gate Removes the Dumb Icons from Menu Items

- Score: 321 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48487435) | Link: https://daringfireball.net/2026/06/macos_27_golden_gate_removes_the_dumb_icons_from_menu_items

### TL;DR

macOS 27 Golden Gate removes the pervasive menu-item icons introduced in macOS 26 Tahoe, reversing a design criticized as distracting, inconsistent, and often inscrutable. Apple’s updated Human Interface Guidelines now recommend icons only when they clearly identify common actions, locations, devices, visual transformations, or user content. John Gruber views the rollback as evidence of healthier software-design leadership. HN mostly welcomed the restraint and reported cleaner borders and responsiveness, while debating Liquid Glass’s aesthetics and performance and whether Gruber’s own site meets comparable usability standards.

### Comment pulse

- Selective icons aid scanning → macOS 27 retains them for app launches, folders, and actions with established visual counterparts.
- Broader polish matters → beta users reported fewer inconsistent border radii and less sluggishness; some Tahoe users had reverted to Sequoia.
- Liquid Glass remains polarized → some iPhone 13 owners report severe lag — counterpoint: others describe smoother animations or no slowdown.

### LLM perspective
