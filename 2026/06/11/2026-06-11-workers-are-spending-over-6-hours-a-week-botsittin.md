# Workers are spending over 6 hours a week botsitting AI, fueling job frustration

- Score: 279 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48490057) | Link: https://www.businessinsider.com/botsitting-ai-hidden-human-labor-at-work-2026-6

### TL;DR

A Glean-backed survey of 6,000 computer-based workers in the US, UK, and Australia estimates 6.4 hours weekly go to feeding AI context, checking outputs, debugging, and cleaning errors. Although 87% use AI and 75% feel more productive, only 13% report significantly better organizational performance; heavy botsitters are 73% likelier to be job hunting. Researchers say effective companies invest in context, training, standards, and judgment. Hacker News focused on broken concentration, lost craft and autonomy, management-driven adoption, and whether efficiency can justify automating work employees find meaningful.
