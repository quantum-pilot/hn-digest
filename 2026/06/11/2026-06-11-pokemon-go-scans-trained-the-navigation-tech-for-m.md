# Pokémon Go Scans Trained the Navigation Tech for Military Drones

- Score: 715 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48487029) | Link: https://dronexl.co/2026/06/09/pokemon-go-scans-niantic-vantor-military-drone-navigation/

### TL;DR

DroneXL traces roughly 30 billion optional Pokémon Go Pokéstop scans from Niantic’s visual-positioning research to Niantic Spatial’s partnership with defense contractor Vantor, which is integrating camera-based ground and aerial localization for GPS-denied drones and robots. Niantic says game scans trained an early navigation model; Vantor says it will not use the imagery, leaving unclear whether its deployed model inherited that training. HN disputed the headline’s certainty and the scans’ battlefield usefulness, yet broadly focused on meaningful consent when innocuous game data is licensed for unforeseeable military applications.
