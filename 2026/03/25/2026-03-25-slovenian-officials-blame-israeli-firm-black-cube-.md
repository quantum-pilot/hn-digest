# Slovenian officials blame Israeli firm Black Cube for trying to manipulate vote

- Score: 520 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47519519) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/spies-lies-and-fake-investors-in-disguise-how-plotters-tried-to-flip-a-european-election-1f42b39a

### TL;DR

Slovenian officials allege that two operatives from Israeli intelligence firm Black Cube flew from Tel Aviv in December and visited the headquarters of the country’s far-right opposition. Neighbors reported the unfamiliar visitors, prompting Slovenian intelligence to seek help from European counterparts. Authorities say the operation aimed to discredit the governing Freedom Movement, which supports Palestinians and Ukraine, before Sunday’s election; the party ultimately held off its challenger. The supplied excerpt does not identify who commissioned the alleged operation or establish Black Cube’s response.
