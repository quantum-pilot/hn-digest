# Supreme Court Sides with Cox in Copyright Fight over Pirated Music

- Score: 247 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47518281) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/25/us/politics/supreme-court-cox-music-copyright.html

### TL;DR

The Supreme Court unanimously held that Cox cannot be contributorily liable merely for supplying internet service while knowing some subscribers pirate music; liability requires intent that the service facilitate infringement, such as active encouragement. The ruling reverses the Fourth Circuit’s remaining liability finding after a jury had awarded Sony $1 billion over 10,017 songs. Free-speech advocates and HN readers welcomed protection against account termination based on accusations, while Justice Sotomayor separately warned the standard may leave providers effectively immune even when they knowingly ignore repeat offenders.

### Comment pulse

- The decision’s reliance on the Betamax precedent reinforced that general-purpose tools with substantial lawful uses do not create secondary liability.
- Copyright duration prompted debate between ten-year limits, longer artist protection, and escalating renewal fees that return abandoned works to society.
- Leaving statutory safe harbor by neglecting a termination policy does not itself establish intent-based infringement liability.
