# Jury says Meta knowingly harmed children for profit, awarding landmark verdict

- Score: 275 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47519625) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-03-25/jury-says-meta-knowingly-harmed-children-for-profit-awarding-landmark-verdict

### TL;DR

After a seven-week trial, a New Mexico jury found Meta knowingly harmed children, concealed sexual-exploitation risks, and made misleading or unconscionable safety claims under state consumer law. Evidence covered undercover child accounts, internal correspondence, algorithms favoring harmful engagement, weak under-13 enforcement, sextortion, and teen-suicide content. Jurors compromised on the number of affected teenagers but applied the $5,000 maximum per violation, producing a $375 million penalty—less than one-fifth prosecutors sought. Meta will appeal; a judge will consider public-nuisance remedies and program funding in May.
