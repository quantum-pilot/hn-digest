# Data centers are transitioning from AC to DC

- Score: 299 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47511703) | Link: https://spectrum.ieee.org/data-center-dc

### TL;DR

AI racks approaching 1 megawatt are pushing hyperscale facilities toward 800-volt DC distribution. Instead of repeatedly transforming grid AC through low-voltage AC, UPS batteries, AC again, and server-level DC, the proposed architecture converts once at the perimeter and steps down near processors. Vendors claim 85% more power through equivalent conductors, 45% less copper, 5% higher efficiency, and 30% lower ownership cost at gigawatt scale. Vertiv targets late 2026 availability, but standards, protection, connectors, manufacturing, and safe servicing remain immature. HN focused especially on hot-plug hazards.

### Comment pulse

- 800-volt hot-swapping alarmed engineers; staged pins, fast MOSFET isolation, shutters, and protected rack backs must prevent arcs and human contact.
- DC racks are not new: telecom used 48 volts for decades — counterpoint: facility-wide 800-volt buses are a materially different system.
- Skeptics recalled years of promised DC adoption, while hyperscaler commitments may finally solve equipment vendors’ chicken-and-egg problem.
