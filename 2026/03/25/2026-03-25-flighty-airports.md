# Flighty Airports

- Score: 524 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47511589) | Link: https://flighty.com/airports

### TL;DR

Flighty Airports is a live and daily North American disruption dashboard ranking major airports by departure and arrival delays, cancellation shares, and operational alerts such as ground delays or strong winds. Individual airport pages and a TV mode turn network conditions into a quick “meltdown map.” HN questioned whether aggregate airport health changes decisions for travelers already committed to an airport. Frequent flyers countered that earlier disruption awareness can preserve sleep, lounge time, connections, and scarce rebooking options, though the usefulness depends on timely, trustworthy data.

### Comment pulse

- Travelers wanted TSA-line estimates because arrival-time guidance is immediately actionable, unlike broad airport performance for most fixed itineraries.
- FlightAware’s MiseryMap, FAA status tools, and Eurocontrol add route, weather, demand, and cause context missing from a simple ranking.
- The interface earned praise, with requests for sticky airport names on detail pages and broader international coverage.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Aggregate disruption works best as an early-warning layer connected to a traveler’s specific itinerary.
- **Impact:** Professionals with tight schedules gain rerouting lead time; occasional travelers mainly receive situational awareness.
- **Watch next:** Data accuracy, notification lead time, security waits, route-level effects, international expansion, and personalization.
