# Meta and YouTube found negligent in landmark social media addiction case

- Score: 395 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47520505) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/25/technology/social-media-trial-verdict.html

### TL;DR

A Los Angeles County jury found Meta and YouTube negligently designed addictive products that harmed K.G.M., now 20, awarding $4.2 million against Meta and $1.8 million against YouTube. She began social media at six and linked infinite scroll, recommendations, autoplay, and beauty filters to anxiety, depression, body dysmorphia, and self-harm thoughts. The bellwether verdict treats platform design as personal injury rather than liability for user speech, potentially influencing thousands of cases. Both companies dispute causation and plan legal challenges; TikTok and Snap settled earlier.
