# AirPods Max 2

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47398681) | Link: https://www.apple.com/airpods-max/

### TL;DR

Apple’s H2-powered over-ear refresh promises retuned high-fidelity sound, a wider-stage amplifier, up to 1.5× stronger noise cancellation, Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, Voice Isolation, and beta Live Translation. USB‑C now carries lossless, ultra-low-latency wired audio; battery life remains 20 hours, with the familiar aluminum, mesh-canopy, five-color design and Smart Case. HN’s excitement cooled because the 13.6-ounce weight, absence of a true off control, case-dependent low-power behavior, and $549 price appear unchanged, though owners defended its deep cups, reliability, ecosystem integration, and competitive premium pricing.

### Comment pulse
