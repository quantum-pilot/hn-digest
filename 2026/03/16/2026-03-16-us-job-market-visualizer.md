# US Job Market Visualizer

- Score: 386 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47400060) | Link: https://karpathy.ai/jobs/

### TL;DR

A research treemap visualizes 342 BLS occupations covering 143 million U.S. jobs: tile area represents employment, while color can show projected growth, median pay, education, or an LLM-estimated “digital AI exposure” score. The source includes a prompt pipeline for applying other qualitative lenses, but explicitly warns that exposure means reshaping, not disappearance, and omits demand elasticity, regulation, and human preference. HN found the exploration useful yet cautioned that 2024 aggregates obscure current applicant competition, geography, labor supply, and misleadingly broad occupational labels.
