# MoD sources warn Palantir role at heart of government is threat to UK security

- Score: 575 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47397797) | Link: https://www.thenerve.news/p/palantir-technologies-uk-mod-sources-government-data-insights-security-state-secrets

### TL;DR

Two anonymous senior Ministry of Defence engineers allege that Palantir’s £670m-plus UK contracts create a national-security risk even if source data remains government-owned. Their concern is the mosaic capability: combining metadata across defence, health, transport, energy, and industry can infer secrets never explicitly stored, while derived insights may sit outside sovereignty assurances. They cite a hypothetical supply shipment revealing a submarine’s location; Palantir calls the claims entirely false, and the MoD points to contractual controls. HN focused on data fusion, vendor dependence, foreign leverage, and whether self-hosting meaningfully limits access.
