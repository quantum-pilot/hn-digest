# Why I love FreeBSD

- Score: 330 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47397574) | Link: https://it-notes.dragas.net/2026/03/16/why-i-love-freebsd/

### TL;DR

The captured page contains only the article header, so the substance comes from HN users explaining FreeBSD’s appeal: decade-long server uptime, low maintenance, coherent base-system design, strong documentation, first-class ZFS and boot environments, plus built-in jails and bhyve virtualization. Several described painless upgrades and storage recovery, including importing an old array directly from live media. The counterweight is hardware breadth: Linux supports more devices and maintains a larger workaround database, while commenters reported specific Intel NIC stalls and a USB-triggered crash despite otherwise excellent reliability.

### Comment pulse

- Tape drives and some SCSI peripherals retain better FreeBSD utilities, showing its hardware disadvantage is not universal.
- Administrators valued set-and-forget services over container stacks whose promised simplicity introduced Docker, Podman, or Kubernetes maintenance.
- Linux familiarity makes occasional FreeBSD work harder because equivalent tools and paths must be relearned after long unattended periods.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** FreeBSD’s advantage is operational coherence, not an ability Debian categorically lacks.
- **Impact:** Stable servers may consume less attention; desktops and unusual hardware demand more validation.
- **Watch next:** FreeBSD 15 driver reports, Intel NIC fixes, laptop input behavior, ZFS recovery tests, and jail migrations.
