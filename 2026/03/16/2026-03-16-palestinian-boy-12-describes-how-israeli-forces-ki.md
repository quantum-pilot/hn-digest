# Palestinian boy, 12, describes how Israeli forces killed his family in car

- Score: 873 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47402950) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c70n2x7p22do

### TL;DR

In Tammun in the occupied West Bank, 12-year-old Khaled Bani Odeh says Israeli forces fired into his family’s car, killing his parents and two younger brothers, including a blind, disabled seven-year-old; he and eight-year-old Mustafa survived. Israel’s army says the car accelerated toward troops during an arrest operation, prompting fire. A nearby witness says it had stopped and received no warning, while the army says authorities are investigating.
