### TL;DR

A French educator reports that Tony Hoare died on March 5, citing a direct message from Jonathan Bowen, and remembers him chiefly through Structured Programming and Communicating Sequential Processes. The brief post points readers to an oral history and professional retrospective rather than supplying a full obituary. HN commenters initially sought independent confirmation, then mourned a foundational computer scientist whose long campaign for mathematically tractable software exceeded his best-known algorithm and cautionary anecdote. They hope generated code will finally make rigorous verification a mainstream necessity.
