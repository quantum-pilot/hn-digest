# Workers report watching Ray-Ban Meta-shot footage of people using the bathroom

- Score: 190 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47313613) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/03/workers-report-watching-ray-ban-meta-shot-footage-of-people-using-the-bathroom/

### TL;DR

A Swedish investigation using 30-plus worker interviews reports Meta contractors saw intimate, bathroom, financial-card, and sexual footage from Ray-Ban smart glasses, sometimes recorded accidentally; reporters did not inspect the material directly. Meta acknowledges contractor review of some content shared with Meta AI after privacy filtering, while its policies permit human and third-party processing when cloud features are used. The claims prompted UK scrutiny and a proposed class action challenging Meta’s privacy marketing. HN readers are unsurprised, treating wearables as sensors that turn private spaces into corporate data.
