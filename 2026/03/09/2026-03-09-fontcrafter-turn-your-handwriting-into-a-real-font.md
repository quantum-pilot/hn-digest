# FontCrafter: Turn your handwriting into a real font

- Score: 413 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47306655) | Link: https://arcade.pirillo.com/fontcrafter.html

### TL;DR

FontCrafter converts a printed handwriting template into installable fonts entirely in the browser, with no account, upload, fee, or server dependency. Users provide three variants per character, verify detections, configure uppercase and lowercase rows, descenders, normalization, kerning, ligatures, contextual alternates, accents, and more than 100 derived symbols, then export OTF, TTF, WOFF2, or Base64. HN welcomed a free competitor to subscription-based Calligraphr and the archivability of client-side software. Early testing exposed fragile crosshair registration, scan degradation down the page, and inflexible row assignments.

### Comment pulse

- Client-only processing protects handwriting privacy and lets archived copies survive vendor shutdowns or acquisitions.

- A high-quality scan still misregistered characters; manual corner placement and more distinctive markers could make recovery practical.
