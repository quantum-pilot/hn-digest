# Jolla on track to ship new phone with Sailfish OS, user-replaceable battery

- Score: 180 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47311456) | Link: https://liliputing.com/the-new-jolla-phone-with-sailfish-os-is-on-track-to-start-shipping-in-the-first-half-of-2026/

### TL;DR

After more than 10,000 preorders, Jolla says its first new Sailfish OS phones will enter production in the second quarter of 2026; a second, 1,000-unit European batch costs €649 and is due in September. The midrange handset combines a replaceable 5,450-mAh battery, microSD, modular backs, Android-app compatibility, and a configurable privacy switch, though the switch is software-defined rather than a physical disconnect. HN users welcome a Google-free alternative but debate Sailfish’s sluggish, polarizing interface, large form factor, proprietary components, update model, and whether banking or government apps remain viable.

### Comment pulse

- Longtime users praise Linux-level control and Android fallback → critics report dated aesthetics, awkward navigation, and poor responsiveness even on capable hardware.
- Alternative-phone viability hinges on institutional apps → banking often works, but payment systems, government services, and device-integrity requirements create regional uncertainty.
- Size limits otherwise willing returners → enthusiasts want compact hardware, while this 6.36-inch model repeats the market’s large-phone bias.
