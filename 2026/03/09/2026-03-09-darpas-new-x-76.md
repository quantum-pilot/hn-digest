# DARPA’s new X-76

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47311669) | Link: https://www.darpa.mil/news/2026/darpa-new-x-76-speed-of-jet-freedom-of-helicopter

### TL;DR

DARPA and US Special Operations Command have moved Bell Textron’s X-76 SPRINT demonstrator into manufacturing after a Critical Design Review. The experimental aircraft aims to combine vertical takeoff, hovering over austere terrain, and operation from unprepared surfaces with cruise speeds above 400 knots. Ground integration and testing come next, with flight tests planned for early 2028; it is a technology demonstrator, not a production aircraft. HN noted decades of Bell folding-rotor research, while debating maintenance complexity, Osprey comparisons, alternative VTOL architectures, safety, expense, and the ethics of its military mission.
