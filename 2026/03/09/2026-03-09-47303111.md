### TL;DR

March’s project thread showcases builders pursuing a hand-drawn city simulator, a cooperative-multitasking language for bare-metal devices, an AI-media literacy game, a goal-gated productivity app, and several family, email, and emergency-call services. The strongest exchanges moved beyond promotion into design questions: realistic transport and construction mechanics, coroutine semantics, verified labels for synthetic media, delayed UI feedback, telephony economics, spam protection, and tax risk. The discussion’s shared pattern is rapid independent building paired with users pressure-testing assumptions that polished demos and personal enthusiasm can obscure.
