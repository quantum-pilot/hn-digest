# New York City hospitals drop Palantir as controversial AI firm expands in UK

- Score: 243 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47535371) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/mar/26/new-york-hospitals-palantir-ai

### TL;DR

NYC Health + Hospitals will let its Palantir contract expire in October and replace it with in-house systems. The nearly $4 million arrangement, begun in November 2023, used patient information to improve insurance claims and revenue capture; hospital leadership says an absolute firewall prevented sharing with ICE and reported no incidents. Activists nevertheless objected to provisions allowing approved de-identification and non-research use. Their campaign now offers ammunition to UK opponents of Palantir’s £330 million NHS platform and expanding government access, where re-identification and state-abuse risks remain contested.
