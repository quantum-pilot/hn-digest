# Personal Encyclopedias

- Score: 795 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47522173) | Link: https://whoami.wiki/blog/personal-encyclopedias

### TL;DR

After organizing 1,351 family photos, the author interviewed his grandmother and built a local MediaWiki page reconstructing her wedding. The experiment expanded into whoami.wiki, an open-source, locally run personal encyclopedia. Agents draft interconnected histories from photos, EXIF metadata, location traces, rides, transactions, music recognition, and message archives, while the owner reviews what to keep. Cross-source reconstruction recovered forgotten places, relationships, and anecdotes, but also demonstrates how readily scattered digital trails can expose an intimate life narrative.
