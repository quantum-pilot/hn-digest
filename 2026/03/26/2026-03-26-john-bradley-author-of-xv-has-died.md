# John Bradley, author of xv, has died

- Score: 194 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47534086) | Link: https://voxday.net/2026/03/25/rip-john-bradley/

### TL;DR

The supplied page carries notice of John Bradley’s death but provides no biographical details. HN remembers him as the creator of xv, an X11 image viewer and basic editor that remains useful on Linux, OpenBSD, and remote systems. Users recall its unusually capable color and grayscale remapping tools. One developer describes Bradley readily licensing a scanner-enabled xv derivative for a 50/50 revenue split; that side project generated five-figure royalties and helped launch a Linux consultancy. The thread mourns both Bradley and the careful, personal shareware culture his work represented.
