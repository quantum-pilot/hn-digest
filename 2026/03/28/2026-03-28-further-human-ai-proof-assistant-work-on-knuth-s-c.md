# Further human + AI + proof assistant work on Knuth's "Claude Cycles" problem

- Score: 131 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47557166) | Link: https://twitter.com/BoWang87/status/2037648937453232504

### TL;DR

Bo Wang reports that Knuth’s “Claude’s Cycles” problem has expanded from Claude’s guided odd-m construction into a collaborative resolution. For m=3, Knuth enumerates 11,502 Hamiltonian cycles, 996 extending to all odd m, with 760 valid Claude-like decompositions. Ho Boon Suan then used GPT-5.4 Pro to derive a 14-page proof for even m≥8, checked computationally through 2,000; Keston Aquino-Michaels found simpler odd/even constructions with GPT and Claude, while Kim Morrison formalized the odd proof in Lean. Commenters credit AI’s breadth but still see expert representation and guidance as decisive.
