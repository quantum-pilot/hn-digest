# Airbus is preparing two uncrewed combat aircraft

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47382277) | Link: https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-airbus-is-preparing-two-uncrewed-combat-aircraft-from-kratos-for-first-flight-with-a-european

### TL;DR

Airbus is fitting two flight-proven Kratos Valkyries with its European MARS mission system and AI-supported MindShare software, targeting variant flights in 2026 and an operational German capability by 2029. The aircraft can operate autonomously, in teams, or under Eurofighter command, with connectivity added through upgraded Litening 5 pods. Each has a 5,000-plus-kilometre range, 45,000-foot ceiling, and roughly three-ton takeoff weight. Discussion centers on whether this sovereign platform can provide affordable mass or repeats Europe’s slow, costly procurement model.
