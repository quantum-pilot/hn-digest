# $96 3D-printed rocket that recalculates its mid-air trajectory using a $5 sensor

- Score: 374 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385935) | Link: https://github.com/novatic14/MANPADS-System-Launcher-and-Rocket

### TL;DR

A public prototype combines 3D-printed parts, folding fins, canards, an ESP32 flight computer, and MPU6050 inertial sensor in a launcher-guided rocket system costing about $96. Its launcher adds GPS, compass, barometric sensing, and telemetry; CAD, firmware, and OpenRocket simulations are published. Commenters admire the low-cost engineering and compare it with historically simple guidance systems, while warning that consumer components lower proliferation barriers. Crucially, critics say the available firing clips show erratic flights, no target impacts, and no accuracy data, so demonstrated guidance capability remains unproven.
