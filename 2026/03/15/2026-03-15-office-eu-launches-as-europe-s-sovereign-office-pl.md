# Office.eu launches as Europe's sovereign office platform

- Score: 250 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47390233) | Link: https://office.eu/media/pressrelease-20260304

### TL;DR

Office.eu launched in The Hague as a European-owned alternative to Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, combining documents, collaboration, storage, and email on European data centers under EU law. Built partly on open-source Nextcloud, it is inviting selected customers before a broader Q2 2026 rollout and promises migration support and comparable pricing. HN liked the sovereignty goal but questioned differentiation, source availability, certifications, and the credibility of a very young provider, noting that established European Nextcloud hosts already offer similar services.
