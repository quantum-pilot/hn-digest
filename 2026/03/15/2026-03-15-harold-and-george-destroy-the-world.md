# Harold and George Destroy the World

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47386908) | Link: https://tomclancy.info/harold-and-george.html

### TL;DR

Using Captain Underpants pranksters Harold and George as a metaphor, the essay argues that modern entertainment and politics increasingly reward adolescent spectacle: franchise films, performative war branding, commemorative coins, and corrupt sporting bodies. HN readers challenged several examples: the anniversary coin is a dime whose design predates the current administration, and its missing olive branch references the Revolutionary War rather than aggression. They also split over *Predator: Badlands*, seeing either infantilized filmmaking or a deliberate critique of macho posturing.

### Comment pulse

- Factual corrections → commenters distinguished the historical Department of War and coin symbolism from today’s deliberately aggressive political branding.
