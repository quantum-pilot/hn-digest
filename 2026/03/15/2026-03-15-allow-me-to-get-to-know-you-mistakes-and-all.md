# Allow me to get to know you, mistakes and all

- Score: 300 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47381736) | Link: https://sebi.io/posts/2026-03-14-allow-me-to-get-to-know-you-mistakes-and-all/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that LLM-polished messages erase more than quirks: they obscure intent and prevent recipients from building an accumulated model of someone’s tone, emphasis, and omissions. Imperfect wording is part of the social handshake through which people learn one another. Commenters largely recognize the generic voice and added fluff, yet defend selective use for translation, dyslexia, ADHD-related blank-page friction, structure, or vocabulary. The central practical distinction is between assistance that preserves authorial control and generated prose that substitutes a statistically smooth voice for an accountable human communicator.
