# Effort to prevent government officials from engaging in prediction markets

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47291406) | Link: https://www.merkley.senate.gov/merkley-klobuchar-launch-new-effort-to-ban-federal-elected-officials-profiting-from-prediction-markets/

### TL;DR

Senators Jeff Merkley and Amy Klobuchar introduced the End Prediction Market Corruption Act to bar the president, vice president, members of Congress, and other covered officials from trading event contracts using privileged policy information. The proposal follows reports of unusually timed payouts around Iran and Venezuela events and would strengthen CFTC enforcement. Supporters frame both actual insider profit and its appearance as threats to public trust. HN broadly favored restrictions but questioned coverage of appointees, military personnel, relatives, and tippees, and cautioned that suspicious wins alone do not prove misconduct.

### Comment pulse
