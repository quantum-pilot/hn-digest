# US economy sheds 92,000 jobs in February in sharp slide

- Score: 194 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47286933) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/6542bd0c-59ca-493b-ab5d-2d69e4e00cae

### TL;DR

February payrolls fell by 92,000, missing forecasts for a 55,000 gain and reversing much of January’s 126,000 increase; prior months were also revised down by 69,000. Unemployment rose to 4.4%, while healthcare strikes, severe weather, technology losses, and continued federal cuts complicated the signal. Markets pushed the expected first rate cut from July to September as the Federal Reserve balances labor weakness against inflation and war-driven oil risks. Commenters stressed one-off distortions and debated whether participation, prime-age employment, or broader underemployment best captures household strain.

### Comment pulse

- A Kaiser strike reportedly sidelined 28,000 workers and ended February 23; snowstorms also disrupted business surveys.
- A 4.4% headline rate looks low internationally — counterpoint: weak hiring, underemployment, healthcare insecurity, and worsening direction amplify its impact.
