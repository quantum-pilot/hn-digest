# The yoghurt delivery women combatting loneliness in Japan

- Score: 184 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47287344) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/travel/article/20260302-the-yoghurt-delivery-women-combatting-loneliness-in-japan

### TL;DR

Japan’s 31,000 Yakult Ladies do more than sell probiotic drinks: their recurring neighborhood routes provide conversation, routine, and informal welfare checks for older people living alone. The women, mostly self-employed, may notice changes, alert relatives, or seek help when a customer does not answer. This accidental social infrastructure grew from a door-to-door sales network formalized in 1963 and now extends overseas. Readers liked the human contact but wanted clearer economics, debating whether gig-style distribution genuinely supports communities or simply monetizes isolation while selling inexpensive products.
