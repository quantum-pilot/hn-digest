# Tinnitus Is Connected to Sleep

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47287763) | Link: https://www.sciencealert.com/tinnitus-is-somehow-connected-to-a-crucial-bodily-function

### TL;DR

Oxford researchers propose that deep non-REM sleep suppresses the hyperactive brain activity associated with subjective tinnitus. Noise-exposed ferrets developed sleep disruption alongside more severe symptoms, yet their abnormal auditory responses diminished during deep sleep; later human research reported a similar suppression pattern. The evidence suggests a feedback loop in which ringing worsens rest, poor rest increases stress, and stress intensifies ringing, but animal findings remain preliminary rather than a treatment. Commenters broadly recognized sleep and stress effects, while emphasizing variable causes, gradual habituation, and the lack of a universal remedy.

### Comment pulse
