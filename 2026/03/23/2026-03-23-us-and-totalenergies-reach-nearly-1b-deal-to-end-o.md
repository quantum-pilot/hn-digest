# US and TotalEnergies reach 'nearly $1B' deal to end offshore wind projects

- Score: 300 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47492599) | Link: https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/03/23/us-and-totalenergies-reach-nearly-1-billion-deal-to-end-offshore-wind-projects_6751739_4.html

### TL;DR

The US and TotalEnergies agreed to terminate 4 GW of undeveloped offshore-wind leases near New York and North Carolina. TotalEnergies will invest $928 million in US oil, gas, and LNG projects, including Rio Grande LNG; after that spending, the government will reimburse it dollar-for-dollar up to what it paid for the wind leases. Officials frame this as redirecting blocked capital toward cheaper, reliable energy. HN commenters dispute whether “payment” or “refund” best describes the deal, but broadly criticize conditioning public reimbursement on expanded fossil-fuel production.

### Comment pulse

- These sites reportedly had no construction, distinguishing this deal from December stop-work orders against projects later revived by courts.
- Lease-cancellation liability, timing, and eligible fossil spending determine the actual taxpayer cost.
- Commenters call the arrangement ideological anti-wind policy — counterpoint: TotalEnergies says US gas generation is more affordable.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** “Nearly $1 billion” is a conditional reimbursement ceiling, not an unconditional grant upon signing.
- **Impact:** Developers may price greater federal-policy risk into future US renewable leases.
- **Watch next:** Contract text, funding source, verified investment milestones, termination accounting, and emissions impact.
