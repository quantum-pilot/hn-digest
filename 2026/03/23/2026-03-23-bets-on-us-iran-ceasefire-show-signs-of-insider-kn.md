# Bets on US-Iran ceasefire show signs of insider knowledge, say experts

- Score: 156 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47494629) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/mar/23/bets-us-iran-ceasefire-show-signs-of-insider-knowledge-say-experts-polymarket

### TL;DR

Eight Polymarket accounts created around March 21 wagered nearly $70,000 on a US-Iran ceasefire by March 31 and could receive about $820,000. One quoted analyst found the wallet splitting and market-price buying suspicious; another single-purpose account had correctly bet on February’s US strikes before making the same ceasefire wager. Yet ownership is unknown and insider knowledge is unproven. Market odds rose from 6% to 24% amid more than $21 million wagered. HN debates whether such trading reveals useful information or creates corruption and manipulation incentives.

### Comment pulse
