# POSSE – Publish on your Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47486726) | Link: https://indieweb.org/POSSE

### TL;DR

POSSE means publishing first on a site you control, then syndicating copies or excerpts to social platforms with links back to the canonical original. The IndieWeb approach preserves ownership, URLs, searchability, and platform independence while still reaching friends where they already gather; optional backfeed can collect reactions. Implementations range from manual sharing to feeds, webhooks, Bridgy, and custom clients, with update and deletion complications. HN practitioners value durable ownership but report fragmented conversations, weak traffic returns, brittle automation, and cross-posts that can feel impersonal or spammy.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** POSSE works best as archival ownership plus selective participation, not maximal simultaneous broadcasting.
- **Impact:** Authors gain canonical permanence but inherit integration upkeep and moderation across multiple venues.
