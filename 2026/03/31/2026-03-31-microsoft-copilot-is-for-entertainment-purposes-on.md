# Microsoft: Copilot is for entertainment purposes only

- Score: 441 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47587866) | Link: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/for-individuals/termsofuse

### TL;DR

Microsoft’s consumer Copilot terms classify the service as entertainment-only, warn it may make mistakes, advise against relying on important guidance, and place use at the customer’s risk. Microsoft disclaims warranties, assigns users responsibility for actions and published outputs, may review submitted data, and can revoke access without notice. The scope matters: these terms cover personal Copilot experiences, not Microsoft 365 Copilot unless incorporated there; GitHub Copilot is separately governed. HN split between calling this ordinary software boilerplate and criticizing the gap between expansive marketing and minimal accountability.

### Comment pulse

- Anthropic’s UK consumer terms reportedly bar commercial use on Pro plans, broadening concern beyond Microsoft’s branding.
- Some argue disclaimers cannot cure contradictory promotion — counterpoint: warranty and fitness limitations have long been standard software terms.
