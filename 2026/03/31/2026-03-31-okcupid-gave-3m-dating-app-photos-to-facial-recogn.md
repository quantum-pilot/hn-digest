# OkCupid gave 3M dating-app photos to facial recognition firm, FTC says

- Score: 311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47591104) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/03/okcupid-match-pay-no-fine-for-sharing-user-photos-with-facial-recognition-firm/

### TL;DR

The FTC alleges OkCupid gave Clarifai nearly 3 million user photos plus demographic and location data in 2014 without notice, consent, payment, a contract, or usage restrictions, despite a privacy policy promising disclosure and opt-out opportunities. Clarifai used the images to develop facial analysis for age, sex, and race; OkCupid later denied involvement and allegedly obstructed the investigation. Match Group and OkCupid admit no wrongdoing and pay no fine under a proposed court settlement that permanently bars privacy misrepresentations. HN viewed that remedy as merely requiring future legal compliance.
