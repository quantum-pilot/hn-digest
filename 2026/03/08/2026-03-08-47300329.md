# Ask HN: Please restrict new accounts from posting

- Score: 398 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47300329) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47300329

### TL;DR

A reader asks HN to curb posts from new accounts, arguing that AI-generated submissions—especially Show HNs—are overwhelming the site. A moderator says temporary Show HN restrictions will be tried. Discussion split between adding account-age or proof-of-work friction and preserving a welcoming path for legitimate newcomers. Critics note that spammers can simply age accounts, while thoughtful humans may leave after automated rejection. Others favor limiting new submissions but not replies, offering personal filters, and judging low-quality behavior rather than uncertain guesses about LLM authorship.

### Comment pulse

- Account-age gates are cheap for patient spammers but costly for sincere newcomers seeking immediate feedback.
- Show HN’s former craft signal feels diluted by generated launches — counterpoint: language tools improve accessibility and polish for non-native writers.
- Behavioral moderation and personal muting avoid unreliable AI detection, which could suppress dissent and reward a homogeneous writing style.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** New-account limits address posting volume, not authorship; moderation should target measurable behavior.
- **Impact:** Legitimate newcomers face higher friction while established accounts retain broader publishing access.
- **Watch next:** Whether temporary Show HN restrictions reduce low-effort launches without depressing worthwhile first-time submissions.
