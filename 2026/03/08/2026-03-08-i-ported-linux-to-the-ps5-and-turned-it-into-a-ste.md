# I ported Linux to the PS5 and turned it into a Steam Machine

- Score: 304 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47296849) | Link: https://xcancel.com/theflow0/status/2030011206040256841

### TL;DR

Andy Nguyen demonstrated Linux running on a PlayStation 5, using Steam to play Grand Theft Auto V Enhanced with ray tracing. The supplied post provides no installation guide, repository, frame-rate measurements, thermal results, or dual-boot details. Discussion says the setup requires older firmware and a full exploit chain. Commenters linked the GPU support to earlier work on the closely related BC-250 APU, claiming recognition of the console’s slightly different GPU identifier required only a one-line driver change.
