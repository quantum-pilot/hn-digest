# Rijksmuseum researchers discover new painting by Rembrandt van Rijn

- Score: 135 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47249139) | Link: https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/press/press-releases/rijksmuseum-researchers-discover-new-painting-by-rembrandt-van-rijn

### TL;DR

After 65 years outside public study, the Rijksmuseum has reattributed *Vision of Zacharias in the Temple* (1633) to the 27-year-old Rembrandt. A private owner enabled a two-year examination using techniques from Operation Night Watch. Matching pigments and layer construction, macro-XRF evidence of revisions, an original signature, dendrochronology consistent with 1633, and stylistic comparisons collectively support authorship. Rejected from Rembrandt's oeuvre in 1960, the painting is now on long-term loan and display. HN commenters call this a re-analysis, not a discovery, and debate why attribution changes attention and value.
