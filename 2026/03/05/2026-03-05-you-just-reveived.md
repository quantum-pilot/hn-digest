# You Just Reveived

- Score: 218 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47257637) | Link: https://dylan.gr/1772520728

### TL;DR

A Vodafone prepaid customer unexpectedly received five days of unlimited data and 999,999 calling minutes, announced in an all-caps text containing the typo “REVEIVED.” The minutes were real but impossible to exhaust: only 7,200 minutes existed in the five-day window, consumed one at a time. The author wonders whether a human sent the gift, a template leaked a placeholder, or automation misfired. Telco commenters favored a mundane explanation—a developer testing an unfinished promotion with a real subscriber number—while others noted that “unlimited” data often conceals speed or usage limits.

### Comment pulse

- The offer may still be deliberate retention marketing → five free days can rebuild usage habits before paid service resumes.
