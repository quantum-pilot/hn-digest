# Jensen Huang says Nvidia is pulling back from OpenAI and Anthropic

- Score: 214 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47256781) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/jensen-huang-says-nvidia-is-pulling-back-from-openai-and-anthropic-but-his-explanation-raises-more-questions-than-it-answers/

### TL;DR

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang said the company’s latest investments in OpenAI and Anthropic are probably its last because both could go public in 2026. TechCrunch questioned that explanation, pointing to Nvidia’s reduced OpenAI commitment, circular chip-financing concerns, and strains around Anthropic’s China-chip criticism and Pentagon dispute. HN largely rejected the “pullback” framing: Nvidia has already helped stabilize its largest customers, gains more from selling them GPUs than repeatedly buying equity, and can now deploy capital elsewhere before their anticipated IPOs.

### Comment pulse

- Calling this a pullback overstates routine pre-IPO timing → Nvidia already secured ecosystem growth and remains positioned to profit from GPU demand.
- Consumer GPU scarcity frustrates gamers — counterpoint: Nvidia’s roughly $62 billion data-center quarter dwarfed gaming’s sub-$4 billion revenue.
- Moving downstream into frontier models would alienate customers and enter loss-making competition; supplying scarce compute preserves Nvidia’s strongest moat.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Investments were strategic ecosystem insurance; diminishing need, not necessarily deteriorating conviction, explains stopping before IPOs.
- **Impact:** OpenAI and Anthropic lose a marginal capital source while remaining crucial Nvidia customers.
- **Watch next:** IPO filings, chip commitments, OpenAI’s financing close, and Nvidia’s investments across alternative model providers.
