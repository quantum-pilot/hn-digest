# Noem Can't Explain Why She Hired 8-Day-Old Company for Ad Campaign

- Score: 192 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47259303) | Link: https://newrepublic.com/post/207381/kristi-noem-explain-company-ad-campaign

### TL;DR

At a House Oversight hearing, DHS Secretary Kristi Noem could not identify the headquarters of Safe America Media, a firm created eight days before receiving two no-bid department advertising contracts worth $143 million. The company reportedly lacks a website and federal experience, uses an address tied to a Republican operative, and relied on undisclosed subcontractors, including a consultancy with links to Noem’s circle. Representative Joe Neguse questioned whether taxpayer money received adequate scrutiny. HN treated the arrangement as apparent self-dealing and focused on bypassed procurement safeguards and unlikely accountability.
