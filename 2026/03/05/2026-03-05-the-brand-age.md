# The Brand Age

- Score: 189 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47264756) | Link: https://paulgraham.com/brandage.html

### TL;DR

Paul Graham uses the Swiss watch industry to argue that technology commoditizes substantive product differences, leaving brand as the profitable differentiator. Japanese competition, the Swiss franc’s rise, and quartz accuracy crushed mechanical-watch sales; survivors such as Patek Philippe shifted from thinness and precision toward recognizable designs, advertising, artificial scarcity, and status. Revenue recovered even as functional superiority vanished. Graham concludes that branding often conflicts with convergent good design and advises following interesting problems instead. Commenters agreed exclusivity signals access, but disputed that luxury watches are inherently ugly or poorly designed.
