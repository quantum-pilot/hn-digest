# A laser-based process that enables adhesive-free paper packaging

- Score: 125 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47558782) | Link: https://www.fraunhofer.de/en/press/research-news/2026/march-2026/sealing-paper-packaging-without-adhesives.html

### TL;DR

Fraunhofer’s PAPURE project uses a carbon-monoxide laser to break paper’s cellulose, hemicellulose, and lignin into fusible, sugar-like surface compounds; heat and pressure then bond two sheets without adding glue or plastic. Researchers are screening roughly three dozen papers, finding thicker stock works better while mineral fillers weaken seams. A 2cm-by-3mm test seal reportedly lifts 20kg, and a roll-to-roll demonstrator targets ten packages per minute by September 2026. HN liked the chemistry but noted that “adhesive-free” really means the paper makes its own adhesive.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Circularity comes from eliminating foreign material, not from whether the paper’s reaction products count as glue.
- **Impact:** Packaging makers could preserve paper recyclability while retaining familiar heat-sealing workflows.
- **Watch next:** Pilot throughput, seam aging, humidity tolerance, barrier coatings, laser energy, food-contact approval, recycling yield, and licensing terms.
