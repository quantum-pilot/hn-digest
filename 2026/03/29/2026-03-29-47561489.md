# LinkedIn uses 2.4 GB RAM across two tabs

- Score: 578 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47561489) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47561489

### TL;DR

Two Chrome screenshots show LinkedIn consuming 1.3GB in one tab and 1.1GB in its messaging tab—2.4GB for the pair. HN treated the numbers as a symbol of modern web bloat, comparing them with Voyager 1’s 69KB computer and reporting similarly heavy AWS, Slack, Reddit, and retail experiences. Commenters blamed layered web apps, indiscriminate frameworks, tracking, and weak performance testing, though the screenshots alone identify no cause. Despite widespread contempt for its feed, many still depend on LinkedIn for jobs, recruiters, and contacts.

### Comment pulse

- Voyager is vastly simpler, so the comparison is rhetorical — counterpoint: basic chat interfaces can still stay below 100MB.
- Some users see gigabyte tabs, heat, and choppy scrolling elsewhere, suggesting a broader performance-discipline problem.
- LinkedIn’s feed feels write-only, yet network effects and application requirements make deleting an account costly.
