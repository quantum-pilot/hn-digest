# Voyager 1 runs on 69 KB of memory and an 8-track tape recorder

- Score: 362 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47564421) | Link: https://techfixated.com/a-1977-time-capsule-voyager-1-runs-on-69-kb-of-memory-and-an-8-track-tape-recorder-4/

### TL;DR

Voyager 1, launched in 1977, still returns unique interstellar measurements from more than 15 billion miles away using roughly 69 KB of memory, 81,000 instructions per second, a 22.4-watt transmitter, and a 160-bit/s downlink. Its “8-track” recorder was a specialized half-inch, eight-track reel—not a consumer cartridge—and operated until a 2007 power-saving shutdown. The craft discovered Io’s active volcanism, examined Jupiter and Saturn, and crossed the heliopause in 2012. In 2025, JPL revived roll thrusters dormant since 2004 before its sole powerful command antenna entered a long maintenance outage.
