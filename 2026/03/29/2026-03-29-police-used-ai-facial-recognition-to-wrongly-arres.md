# Police used AI facial recognition to wrongly arrest TN woman for crimes in ND

- Score: 333 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47563384) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/29/us/angela-lipps-ai-facial-recognition

### TL;DR

Angela Lipps, a 50-year-old Tennessee grandmother, spent more than five months jailed and was extradited over North Dakota bank-fraud charges after West Fargo’s Clearview AI search returned her as a similar-looking potential suspect. Fargo police say they also used independent investigative steps, but have not disclosed them; readily available bank records placed her in Tennessee. Charges were dismissed without prejudice before Christmas. Police acknowledged process errors, prohibited West Fargo’s system, and added oversight. HN argued a face similarity may justify a lead, never probable cause without location or corroborating evidence.
