# The Ugliest Airplane: An Appreciation

- Score: 112 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47428132) | Link: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/air-space-magazine/ugliest-airplane-appreciation-180978708/

### TL;DR

The TransAvia PL-12 AirTruk looked bizarre because Luigi Pellarini designed outward from agricultural work rather than aerodynamic convention. A large fertilizer hopper, high cockpit, twin tail booms, stub lower wing, rugged gear, and open rear loading path enabled quick turnarounds from rough strips, strong visibility, and payload exceeding its empty weight. Derived from earlier Australasian experiments, it proved forgiving but slow and commercially modest: 120 were built before production ended in 1988. Commenters admired its functional honesty and enthusiastically nominated even uglier aircraft.

### Comment pulse

- Pilots recalled very low takeoff speeds and sluggish loaded climbs, yet praised predictable controls and easy landings.
- Debate over ugliness centered on stubby bodies and abruptly truncated fuselages rather than poor engineering.
- Its 1985 Mad Max appearance gave the obscure workhorse brief fame before surviving examples became airshow attractions.
