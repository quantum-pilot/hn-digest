# Western carmakers' retreat from electric risks dooming them to irrelevance

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47467034) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/mar/21/west-carmakers-retreat-electric-vehicle-risks-irrelevance-iran-war-evs-china

### TL;DR

The Guardian argues that legacy Western automakers are repeating Detroit’s 1980s mistake by retreating from EV investment while Chinese rivals scale batteries, software, chips, and low-cost models. Stellantis, Volkswagen, and Ford have written down tens of billions as EV margins lag combustion vehicles; policy reversals in the US and Europe encourage short-term hedging. Meanwhile BYD has overtaken Tesla and Chinese brands are gaining in emerging markets. Commenters challenged the blanket framing, citing competitive European EV launches, uneven charging infrastructure, winter range losses, and persistent demand for combustion cars.

### Comment pulse

- Owners report strong daily EV experiences with home charging, but public-charger reliability, opaque pricing, and cold-weather losses remain decisive.
- European brands offer credible models — counterpoint: Chinese competitors can undercut them by 25–50%, testing how much buyers value familiar badges.
- Supporting combustion, hybrid, and electric platforms preserves choice but dilutes engineering focus and economies of scale.

- **View:** Demand uncertainty is real, but product gaps compound when investment pauses during a technology transition.
