# Mayor of Paris removed parking spaces, reduced the number of cars

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47466697) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/21/travel/paris-transformation-anne-hidalgo-mayor

### TL;DR

Over 12 years, Paris mayor Anne Hidalgo removed parking, pedestrianized roads and plazas, built extensive cycle lanes, and closed 100 school streets to traffic. Car numbers fell, air quality improved, cycling rose, and public space became more pleasant. The tradeoffs remain politically sharp: congestion increased 4% since 2015, bus use fell 31% from 2018 to 2024, and some pedestrians fear cyclists. Commenters largely rejected CNN’s tourist-first framing, arguing residents receive the greatest health and livability benefits while acknowledging family and mobility constraints.
