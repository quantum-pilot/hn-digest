# Some things just take time

- Score: 470 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47467537) | Link: https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/3/20/some-things-just-take-time/

### TL;DR

Armin Ronacher argues that code generation can accelerate output but cannot compress the years needed to build judgment, trust, community, and durable institutions. He defends useful friction—reviews, compliance, cooling-off periods, careful shutdowns—and warns that AI-driven competition captures any saved time as more work. Weekend startups and briefly active open-source repositories may ship quickly yet lack maintenance or succession. Commenters broadly agreed that speed only helps when direction and feedback are sound, while noting that rapid prototyping can cheaply expose wrong ideas before deeper investment.

### Comment pulse
