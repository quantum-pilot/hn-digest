# Passengers who refuse to use headphones can now be kicked off United flights

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47469417) | Link: https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/21/travel/travel-news-happiest-countries

### TL;DR

United Airlines revised its contract of carriage so travelers who play audio or video without headphones may be refused transport or removed from an aircraft. CNN frames the rule as a modest restoration of courtesy amid persistently elevated unruly-passenger reports: the FAA logged more than 1,600 incidents last year, down from 5,973 in 2021 but above pre-pandemic levels. Commenters overwhelmingly welcomed enforcement, describing speakerphone and short-video audio as increasingly common across shared spaces, though some wondered whether removal is disproportionate for a noise nuisance.

### Comment pulse

- Fear of confrontation often leaves disruptive riders unchallenged, especially where bystanders may not intervene if a dispute becomes violent.
- Cabin noise already makes personal speakers ineffective; noise-canceling headphones or in-ear monitors improve both listening and comfort.
- Some blamed disappearing headphone jacks, but wireless options or inexpensive adapters still leave passengers responsible for containing their sound.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** A clear rule gives crews authority and removes ambiguity before minor incivility escalates into conflict.
- **Impact:** Consistent enforcement could reset norms beyond aviation by making private audio an explicit condition of shared transport.
- **Watch next:** Crew guidance, warning thresholds, accessibility exceptions, incident data, and whether other carriers adopt comparable language.
