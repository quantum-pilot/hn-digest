# Blocking Internet Archive Won't Stop AI, but Will Erase Web's Historical Record

- Score: 480 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47464818) | Link: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/03/blocking-internet-archive-wont-stop-ai-it-will-erase-webs-historical-record

### TL;DR

EFF argues that publishers blocking the Internet Archive to deter AI scraping will not stop commercial crawlers but will remove an independent record of edited or deleted news. The New York Times has reportedly adopted measures beyond robots.txt, with The Guardian appearing to follow. EFF distinguishes nonprofit preservation and search—uses it says courts have long protected as fair use—from unresolved litigation over model training. HN broadly valued archiving, but site operators described aggressive bot traffic and noted that major archives do not always honor robots.txt.

### Comment pulse

- Operators struggle to distinguish good crawlers from abusive ones → distributed IPs and fingerprint evasion make broad blocking an understandable collateral response.
- Preservation advocates called publisher blocks self-defeating → history disappears while AI agents imitate browsers — counterpoint: publishers still seek control over valuable human text.
- Archive governance also drew scrutiny → removal requests, robots.txt policies, and archive.is’s alleged denial-of-service campaign complicate simple good-versus-bad framing.
