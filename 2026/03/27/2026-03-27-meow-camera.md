# Meow.camera

- Score: 175 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47543204) | Link: https://meow.camera/#4258783365322591678

### TL;DR

Meow.camera is an unofficial browser viewer for live cameras attached to community-cat feeders across China’s Hello Street Cat/JieMao network. It lists featured and popular stations, shows local status, offers multiple camera angles, favorites, screenshots, clips, translated names, and links into compatible mobile apps. Feeder cameras activate only while someone is watching through the original service; dispensing food remains an app function rather than a website button. HN delighted in the low-stakes spectacle—including a pug visitor—compared it with cat aquariums, and recalled privacy risks around exposed cameras.

- **Impact:** An alternative viewer broadens access and visibility, potentially funneling attention and donations back to the feeder network.
