# If you don't opt out by Apr 24 GitHub will train on your private repos

- Score: 607 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47548243) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47548243

### TL;DR

GitHub’s April 24 change does not ingest entire private repositories at rest. For individual Copilot Free, Pro, and Pro+ users who leave training enabled, GitHub may use Copilot interaction data—including prompts, accepted or edited outputs, nearby code, comments, filenames, repository structure, and navigation—to improve models. Non-Copilot users are unaffected, and a GitHub representative says business subscriptions are excluded. HN still objects that private-code context becomes training material by default and that discovering and changing the setting imposes an unfair privacy burden.
