# Iran-linked hackers claim breach of FBI director's personal email

- Score: 189 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47543167) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/world/us/iran-linked-hackers-claim-breach-of-fbi-directors-personal-email-doj-official-2026-03-27/

### TL;DR

Iran-linked Handala Hack Team says it breached FBI Director Kash Patel’s personal Gmail and posted photographs, documents, and excerpts. A Justice Department official confirmed the compromise and said published material appeared authentic; Reuters could not independently authenticate the emails, though the address matches one exposed in earlier breaches. Reviewed correspondence mixed personal and work messages dated 2010–2019, well before Patel became director. Western researchers associate Handala with Iranian cyberintelligence personas. HN criticized account protection, while noting the breach may be old and personal data enables profiling, coercion, or human-intelligence targeting.
