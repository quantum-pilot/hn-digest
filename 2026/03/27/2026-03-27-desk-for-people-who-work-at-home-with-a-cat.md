# Desk for people who work at home with a cat

- Score: 317 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47543943) | Link: https://soranews24.com/2026/03/27/japan-now-has-a-special-desk-for-people-who-work-at-home-with-a-pet-catphotos/

### TL;DR

Bibilab’s 24,800-yen Neko House Desk integrates a two-level side enclosure, an under-desk lounging shelf connected to it, and a Surprise Cat Hole through the desktop. The layout aims to keep a pet close without surrendering the keyboard, while retaining cable slots and room for a PC tower; using that tower space removes one cat compartment. The upper section supports 20 kilograms, and a matching Cat Tower Rack is sold separately. HN likes the concept but predicts cats will ignore designated spaces and occupy whatever most effectively wins attention.

### Comment pulse

- Cats target valued objects to request attention → a purpose-built hideaway may not compete with the keyboard.
- Simple boxes, blankets, chairs, or heated pads often work better → novelty, proximity, warmth, and reinforced attention matter more than furniture.
- Attached monitor-arm beds work for some owners → elevation and visibility may satisfy feline supervision without blocking work.
