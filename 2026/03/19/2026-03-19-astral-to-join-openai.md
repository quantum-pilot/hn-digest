# Astral to Join OpenAI

- Score: 1180 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47438723) | Link: https://astral.sh/blog/openai

### TL;DR

Astral has agreed to join OpenAI’s Codex team, bringing the company behind Ruff, uv, and ty into the AI developer-tooling organization. Astral says its tools now receive hundreds of millions of downloads monthly and will remain open source after the deal closes; the team plans to keep building publicly while integrating more closely with Codex. HN’s dominant concern was concentration of essential Python infrastructure inside a capital-intensive model provider, although others viewed the deal as a predictable acquihire and practical exit for difficult-to-monetize developer tools.

### Comment pulse
