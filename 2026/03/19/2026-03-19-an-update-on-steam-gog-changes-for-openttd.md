# An update on Steam / GOG changes for OpenTTD

- Score: 253 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47442834) | Link: https://www.openttd.org/news/2026/03/19/steam-changes-update

### TL;DR

OpenTTD says Atari did not pressure the project over its Transport Tycoon Deluxe rerelease. Instead, both sides agreed that new Steam and GOG users must first buy Atari’s original game before accessing OpenTTD there; the open-source game remains free from its own website, and existing platform users avoid disruption. Atari will also contribute toward server costs, while OpenTTD retains independence. HN largely welcomed the preservation-minded compromise but emphasized storefronts’ discovery power and questioned undisclosed financial terms, future rights holders, and whether Atari’s contribution fairly reflects community labor.
