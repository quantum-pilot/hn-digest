# Juggalo makeup blocks facial recognition technology (2019)

- Score: 220 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47438675) | Link: https://consequence.net/2019/07/juggalo-makeup-facial-recognition/

### TL;DR

A 2019 report observed that black-and-white Juggalo face paint could defeat facial-recognition systems that locate high-contrast landmarks around eyes, mouth, chin, and jaw. By redrawing those features, the makeup caused some software to place landmarks incorrectly; depth-sensing systems such as Apple Face ID were not fooled. HN readers recognized the technique as dazzle camouflage but stressed its limits: models can adapt, visible makeup attracts attention, and technical countermeasures become an arms race. Many argued durable protection requires rules governing surveillance itself.

### Comment pulse

- One proposal was a constitutional restriction covering public and private use; others doubted political coalitions could overcome ubiquitous consumer cameras.
- Readers said low-confidence, adversarial inputs—not passport-photo accuracy—are the meaningful test of whether recognition systems fail safely.
- Masks and light-projecting wearables offer alternatives — counterpoint: widespread adoption would quickly motivate filters and retraining.
