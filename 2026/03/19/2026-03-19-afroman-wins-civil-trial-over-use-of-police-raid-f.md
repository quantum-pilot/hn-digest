### TL;DR

After a three-day civil trial, an Ohio jury rejected claims by seven Adams County sheriff’s officers against rapper Joseph E. Foreman. The officers alleged that his use of images from their 2022 raid in two music videos and brand promotion caused humiliation, ridicule, mental distress, embarrassment, and reputational harm. The verdict favored Foreman, who framed it as a victory for free speech and artistic freedom; the supplied excerpt does not describe damages sought or the jury’s reasoning.
