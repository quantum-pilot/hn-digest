# Afroman found not liable in defamation case

- Score: 1081 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47436950) | Link: https://nypost.com/2026/03/18/us-news/afroman-found-not-liable-in-bizarre-ohio-defamation-case/

### TL;DR

An Ohio jury found rapper Joseph Foreman, known as Afroman, not liable for defamation or false-light invasion of privacy after he turned home-surveillance footage of a fruitless 2022 police raid into the satirical video Lemon Pound Cake. The Adams County search produced no criminal charges; Foreman said the song helped raise money for property damage. HN celebrated the verdict as protection for recording public officials and called the officers’ lawsuit a Streisand effect, while debating whether allegations in the original warrant justified the raid’s heavily armed tactics.
