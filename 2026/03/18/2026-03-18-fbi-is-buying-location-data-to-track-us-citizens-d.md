# FBI is buying location data to track US citizens, director confirms

- Score: 342 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47430797) | Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/18/fbi-is-buying-location-data-to-track-us-citizens-kash-patel-wyden/

### TL;DR

FBI Director Kash Patel confirmed that the agency again buys Americans’ commercially available data, including location histories sourced through consumer apps, games, brokers, and advertising systems, for federal investigations. He said the purchases comply with the Constitution and Electronic Communications Privacy Act and produce useful intelligence, but the FBI disclosed neither frequency nor vendors. Senator Ron Wyden called the practice a warrantless Fourth Amendment workaround; the FBI’s legal theory remains untested in court. HN focused on the opaque resale chain and demanded restrictions on both collection and government purchasing.
