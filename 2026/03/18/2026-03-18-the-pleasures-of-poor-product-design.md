# The pleasures of poor product design

- Score: 246 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47420432) | Link: https://www.inconspicuous.info/p/the-pleasures-of-poor-product-design

### TL;DR

Greek architect Katerina Kamprani’s The Uncomfortable turns familiar objects into intentionally inconvenient designs, such as a chain-handled fork and waterfall-shaped teapot. Since 2011 she has created 50–60 concepts, about half as physical prototypes, by inverting design-school rules and rejecting obvious first ideas. She values exhibitions over manufacturing, partly to avoid waste and running a small business. HN discussion centered on process: AI might accelerate stalled work, but outsourcing the difficult craft can erase the pleasure and motivation that make it worthwhile.
