# Show HN: Will my flight have Starlink?

- Score: 147 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47428650) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47428650

### TL;DR

Stardrift predicts onboard Starlink by checking an airline’s rollout, the assigned aircraft type, and—when available—the specific tail number. Complete fleet-type upgrades yield confident answers; partial rollouts become probabilities until airlines assign tails, usually only days before departure. The creators normalized enthusiast-maintained spreadsheets and forum data, linking it with schedules and route search. HN users called fast, often free inflight internet transformative, while discussion broadened into satellite handoffs, United’s rollout strategy, and whether rural connectivity should favor publicly subsidized wiring or cheaper satellite coverage.

### Comment pulse

- Tail assignment limits advance certainty → fleet-level probabilities improve only when a specific aircraft is scheduled several days before departure.
- Reliable inflight broadband changes travel choices → users reported gaming over the Pacific and prioritizing airlines offering free high-speed access.
- Rural infrastructure split readers → wired service offers permanent public utility — counterpoint: serving dispersed homes may cost far more than Starlink.
