# Meta will shut down VR Horizon Worlds access June 15

- Score: 139 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47427214) | Link: https://www.engadget.com/ar-vr/meta-will-shut-down-vr-horizon-worlds-access-in-june-222028919.html

### TL;DR

Meta will delist individual Horizon Worlds and Events from the Quest Store by March 31, then remove the app and end all headset access after June 15, 2026. The social platform continues on iOS and Android as Meta shifts toward mobile, AI, and smart glasses. Hyperscape users may still capture and view 3D scans, but sharing, invitations, and co-experiences will disappear. HN treated the retreat as a major metaverse failure, while VR enthusiasts distinguished a weak social product from useful Quest hardware and sustainable niche communities such as VRChat.
