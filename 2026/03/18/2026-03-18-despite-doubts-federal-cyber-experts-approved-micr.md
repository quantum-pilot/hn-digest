# Despite Doubts, Federal Cyber Experts Approved Microsoft Cloud Service

- Score: 427 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47426057) | Link: https://www.propublica.org/article/microsoft-cloud-fedramp-cybersecurity-government

### TL;DR

ProPublica reports that FedRAMP authorized Microsoft’s GCC High in December 2024 despite five years of unresolved questions about encryption data flows, sparse documentation, vulnerability management, and much of the suite never being examined. Reviewers had spent 480 hours and ended talks in 2023, but deployment across agencies and defense contractors made rejection costly; authorization arrived with conditions and warnings about unknown risk. Microsoft says it supplied comprehensive materials and met required metrics. HN viewed incumbent entrenchment as decisive, while also criticizing FedRAMP’s slow, expensive process and Azure’s accumulated complexity.

### Comment pulse

- Deployment before approval reversed the security test → reviewers eventually weighed migration disruption against unknown technical risk.
- FedRAMP itself drew criticism → years of paperwork and multimillion-dollar compliance costs exclude smaller vendors without guaranteeing meaningful review.
- Azure users recognized architectural sprawl → overlapping products, authentication paths, and legacy layers make configuration and assurance unusually difficult.
