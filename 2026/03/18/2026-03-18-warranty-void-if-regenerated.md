### TL;DR

This AI-generated speculative fiction imagines software maintenance after code is continuously regenerated from natural-language intent. Three farm failures show why cheap generation does not eliminate operations: an updated weather model shifts cabbage timing, a changed output breaks a milk-price pipeline, and an irrigation optimizer ignores embodied knowledge until a physical override restores farmer agency. HN readers agreed maintenance remains costly, but many felt misled by the synthetic authorship and flagged agricultural, geographic, and causal errors—including a price calculation whose direction contradicts the plot.
