# Meta Platforms: Lobbying, dark money, and the App Store Accountability Act

- Score: 1136 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47362528) | Link: https://github.com/upper-up/meta-lobbying-and-other-findings

### TL;DR

An OSINT repository maps Meta's lobbying around App Store Accountability Act proposals, which shift age-verification duties toward Apple and Google rather than social platforms. It labels 47 findings proven and nine structurally possible but unproven, citing $26.3 million in 2025 federal lobbying, activity across 45 states, Digital Childhood Alliance support, and Louisiana bill drafting. It also rules out one Arabella grant pathway. HN readers challenged the two-day, AI-heavy investigation, inaccessible source documents, and weak links, while sharing broader fears about OS-level identity infrastructure.
