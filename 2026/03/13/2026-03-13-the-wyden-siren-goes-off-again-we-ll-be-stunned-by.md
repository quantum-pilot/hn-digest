# The Wyden Siren Goes Off Again: We'll Be "Stunned" by NSA Under Section 702

- Score: 335 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47366374) | Link: https://www.techdirt.com/2026/03/12/the-wyden-siren-goes-off-again-well-be-stunned-by-what-the-nsa-is-doing-under-section-702/

### TL;DR

Senator Ron Wyden is again warning that a still-secret interpretation of Section 702 materially affects Americans’ privacy while Congress debates reauthorization without sufficient information. Techdirt compares the episode to his pre-Snowden warnings and argues that internal safeguards are weak: sensitive FBI searches received political approval, query tracking was inadequate, and a recent provider-definition expansion could reach many infrastructure workers. Commenters broadly oppose secret law, but stress that Wyden’s full statement says Americans will be stunned by the delay and Congress’s ignorance—not necessarily by a specific disclosed surveillance practice.

### Comment pulse

- The framing overreaches → counterpoint: Wyden still says the hidden interpretation directly affects Americans’ privacy and merits declassification.
