# E2E encrypted messaging on Instagram will no longer be supported after 8 May

- Score: 352 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47363922) | Link: https://help.instagram.com/491565145294150

### TL;DR

Meta says Instagram will stop supporting end-to-end encrypted chats and calls after May 8, 2026. Affected users will receive download instructions and need an updated app; unlike WhatsApp, Instagram's E2EE had remained opt-in. The change removes a design in which only participants hold message keys. HN debated how much protection closed platforms truly provide, but distinguished selective interception from routinely retaining readable history. Suspected motives—including AI features, training, or child-safety pressure—were speculative, and some readers planned to leave.
