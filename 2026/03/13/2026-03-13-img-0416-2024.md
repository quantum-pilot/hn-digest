# IMG_0416 (2024)

- Score: 190 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47308547) | Link: https://ben-mini.com/2024/img-0416

### TL;DR

Between 2009 and 2012, iPhones could upload video to YouTube in two taps, leaving the camera filename as the public title. Searching arbitrary IMG numbers now reveals millions of barely viewed clips: unboxings, stunts, family announcements, and other candid fragments. The author treats this accidental catalogue as an unusually authentic feed from before optimized creators and recommendation systems. HN shared the nostalgia but disagreed over whether public upload implied meaningful consent, especially for forgotten family videos intended mainly for embedding or sharing.
