### TL;DR

A report says xAI has removed more founders and leaders while its coding product trails Claude Code and Codex. Zihang Dai and Guodong Zhang have left, leaving two of eleven original cofounders; Macrohard chief Toby Pohlen departed after 16 days, and Tesla AI head Ashok Elluswamy was reassigned. The upheaval follows xAI's combination with SpaceX and precedes a possible June IPO. HN focused less on benchmark gaps than on leadership churn, burnout, recruiting reversals, and conflicts for SpaceX investors.
