# Microgpt

- Score: 1662 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47202708) | Link: http://karpathy.github.io/2026/02/12/microgpt/

### TL;DR

Karpathy compresses the algorithmic core of GPT training and generation into 200 dependency-free Python lines. On 32,000 names, a character tokenizer, scalar autograd engine, 4,192-parameter transformer, cross-entropy loss and Adam optimizer learn next-character prediction before sampling new names. The claim is pedagogical, not that production engineering is trivial: scale adds vast data, tensor hardware, batching, modern architecture, post-training and serving systems. Hacker News readers ported it to C++ and Rust, while debating whether model probabilities can meaningfully express factual confidence.

### Comment pulse

- Ports validate the compact design → C++ roughly doubled line count but ran ten times faster; Rust exposed ownership challenges in autograd graphs.
- Confidence is easy to output but hard to interpret → token probabilities estimate likely continuations, not factual truth or calibrated correctness.
- Small here means comprehensible, not deployable — counterpoint: focused small models can still deliver excellent latency and cost elsewhere.
