# 10-202: Introduction to Modern AI (CMU)

- Score: 218 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47204559) | Link: https://modernaicourse.org

### TL;DR

CMU’s 10-202 teaches how contemporary chatbots work by having students build a minimal model through supervised learning, transformers, tokenization, efficient inference, fine-tuning, alignment, reinforcement learning, reasoning, and safety. Python and basic calculus are prerequisites; the free online version releases videos and autograded assignments two weeks after campus delivery, but excludes quizzes and exams. AI assistants are allowed for homework, yet students are urged to produce final solutions independently because closed-book assessments test whether tool-assisted work became durable understanding. HN praised the hands-on sequence while questioning the LLM-centric “modern AI” label.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters saw AI as a tutor that can diagnose homework errors and free time for deeper implementation — counterpoint: others predicted cheating or weak exams.
- Online learners praised local tests and coding assignments for exposing the gap between following lectures and implementing concepts unaided.
- Critics said “modern AI” should include current vision, robotics, and other fields rather than equating the term with LLMs.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The course couples permission to use AI with independent assessment, making mastery—not abstinence—the objective.
- **Impact:** Learners worldwide gain a structured implementation path, while campus students retain feedback and evaluation unavailable online.
- **Watch next:** Online completion, exam distributions, homework dependency handling, and syllabus changes after the first offering.
