# How to talk to anyone, and why you should

- Score: 333 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47142183) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2026/feb/24/stranger-secret-how-to-talk-to-anyone-why-you-should

### TL;DR

The article argues that conversation with strangers is disappearing under phones, headphones, remote work, automated service, vanishing third spaces, and pandemic habits. That erosion matters because small talk exercises reading cues, tolerating rejection, and recognizing shared humanity. Research suggests people underestimate how enjoyable and connecting such exchanges will be. The prescription is modest: lower the stakes, make an observation, listen, accept nonresponse, and leave when uncomfortable; filmed social-media challenges instead turn strangers into props. HN shared gains in confidence and community, but stressed anxiety, cultural norms, perceived threat, and safety.
