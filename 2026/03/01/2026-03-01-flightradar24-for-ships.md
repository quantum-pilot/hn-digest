# Flightradar24 for Ships

- Score: 172 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47205637) | Link: https://atlas.flexport.com/

### TL;DR

Flexport Atlas is a free globe-based view of the ocean container network, combining vessel tracking with port congestion, sailing schedules, routes, services, and carrier strings. Its interface includes ocean and layer controls plus a dedicated Iran analysis view, using OpenStreetMap data. HN’s main correction was scope: this is not a universal ship radar, and commenters saw sparse or regionally uneven coverage compared with broader vessel trackers. They still liked the uncluttered globe and noted that established alternatives often trade wider AIS coverage for advertising, paywalls, or enterprise-only pricing.

### Comment pulse

- Broader trackers include commercial, fishing, cruise, and pleasure vessels — counterpoint: users criticized leading services for ads, degraded free tiers, and enterprise pricing.

- The true globe projection drew praise for avoiding the distortions familiar from flat world maps.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Atlas is a logistics-network explainer first and comprehensive vessel radar second.
