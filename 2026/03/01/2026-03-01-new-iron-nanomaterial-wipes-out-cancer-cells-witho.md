# New iron nanomaterial wipes out cancer cells without harming healthy tissue

- Score: 215 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47207404) | Link: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/02/260228093456.htm

### TL;DR

Oregon State researchers engineered a ferrous metal-organic framework that exploits tumor acidity and elevated hydrogen peroxide to generate both hydroxyl radicals and singlet oxygen. The resulting oxidative stress harmed several cancer cell lines while largely sparing noncancerous cells; systemic treatment completely regressed human breast-cancer xenografts in mice without observed recurrence or toxicity. This remains early preclinical evidence, with broader tumor testing planned before human trials. Hacker News mixed grief and hope with reminders that translation commonly takes years and cancer progress is usually incremental.
