# Claude becomes number one app on the U.S. App Store

- Score: 209 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47202032) | Link: https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/charts

### TL;DR

Apple’s captured U.S. iPhone free-app chart placed Claude first, ahead of ChatGPT and Google Gemini, with three AI assistants occupying the top three positions. The page establishes a momentary download ranking, not usage, retention, revenue, or model quality. HN treated the move as a signal of shifting consumer attention. Commenters variously credited Claude’s recent model performance, Anthropic’s public positioning, and advertising; head-to-head anecdotes split over whether Opus or Codex was stronger for complex software work. The discussion also illustrated chart volatility by puzzling over unrelated retail apps boosted through rewards.

### Comment pulse

- Many developers reported Opus 4.6 outperforming GPT-5.x on planning and tool use — counterpoint: others preferred Codex 5.3 at high reasoning.
- Some attributed the surge to Anthropic’s public stance rather than capability, arguing ChatGPT still retained broader brand recognition.
- Recent advertisements emphasizing continued human thinking resonated with users who viewed AI marketing as otherwise difficult to differentiate.
