# A proposal to classify happiness as a psychiatric disorder (1992)

- Score: 169 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47412097) | Link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1376114/

### TL;DR

A 1992 satirical paper proposes major affective disorder, pleasant type, arguing that happiness satisfies psychiatric-style criteria because it is statistically unusual, clusters into symptoms, involves cognitive abnormalities, and may reflect atypical central-nervous-system function. Its absurd final move dismisses the fact that happiness is not negatively valued as scientifically irrelevant, exposing how diagnostic frameworks can mistake deviation for disease. HN treated it as a joke with bite, debating whether culture pathologizes neutral moods and sells permanent happiness, or whether well-being—security, relationships, and more positive than negative emotion—is worth pursuing.
