# US SEC preparing to scrap quarterly reporting requirement

- Score: 723 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47406779) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/us-sec-preparing-eliminate-quarterly-reporting-requirement-wsj-says-2026-03-16/

### TL;DR

The SEC is reportedly preparing a proposal that would let listed companies replace mandatory quarterly earnings reports with semiannual disclosure, while preserving quarterly reporting as an option. Publication could come next month, followed by at least 30 days of public comment and an agency vote; Reuters could not independently verify the report. Supporters say fewer filings may reduce cost and quarterly short-termism. HN discussion leaned skeptical, warning that less frequent information would favor insiders and amplify volatility as exchanges simultaneously extend trading hours and short-dated options.

### Comment pulse

- Deadline gaming already distorts operations, with orders or shipments pushed across quarter boundaries — counterpoint: slower reporting may hide performance longer.
- Lower burdens could encourage public listings; others preferred raising transparency requirements for enormous private companies.
- Readers saw round-the-clock markets plus slower disclosure as mismatched incentives favoring exchanges and professional traders.
