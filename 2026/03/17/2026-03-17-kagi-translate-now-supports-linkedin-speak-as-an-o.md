# Kagi Translate now supports LinkedIn Speak as an output language

- Score: 1303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47408703) | Link: https://translate.kagi.com/?from=en&to=LinkedIn+speak

### TL;DR

Kagi Translate has added “LinkedIn Speak” as an output language, turning ordinary text into upbeat corporate jargon, inflated job titles, growth-minded framing, and hashtags. The supplied page exposes the option but little implementation detail, so HN users stress-tested its voice instead: the Gettysburg Address became a startup pivot, a janitorial contract became “Environmental Maintenance,” and threats became sanitized transitions or stakeholder engagement. The consensus was delighted amusement, with the main criticism that its prose remains too coherent and restrained to fully mimic contemporary LinkedIn posting.

### Comment pulse

- The Gettysburg conversion’s “status updates” and “execution” became favorites, with readers connecting it to an earlier PowerPoint parody.
- Gangsta’s Paradise and Navy SEAL tests preserved meaning while converting aggression into KPI, leadership, and accountability euphemisms.
- Reverse translation reduced an inflated Google contractor announcement to “janitor,” suggesting the style transfer remains semantically legible.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The feature succeeds by modeling a recognizable social register, not a literal language.
- **Impact:** Writers gain satire that also exposes how professional rhetoric disguises status and intent.
- **Watch next:** Longer-text consistency, reversibility, safety handling, and configurable recurring templates.
