# Monkey Island for Commodore 64 Ground Up

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47408441) | Link: https://pixeldust.se/monkey-island-project

### TL;DR

Joachim Ljunggren is redrawing every background, character, and animation for a ground-up Commodore 64 conversion of The Secret of Monkey Island, with Andreas Larsson writing the code. The project is unfinished and has no release date, but published scenes already show extensive platform-specific artwork. HN readers celebrated bringing a classic to another machine while debating whether EGA, VGA, or Amiga art best captures it. Technical curiosity centered on fitting bitmap-rich scenes into C64 memory, likely disk streaming, floppy count, scrolling, and arranging the score for SID’s three voices.

### Comment pulse

- EGA proponents valued the original mood; Amiga fans favored flexible colors and richer music — counterpoint: some thought VGA improves later scenes.
- Scene-by-scene loading may avoid a RAM expansion, though 170 KB floppies and wide scrolling remain open constraints.
- Existing C64 arrangements suggest the iconic theme could fit SID, which readers considered capable of a three-voice adaptation.
