# FFmpeg 8.1

- Score: 349 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47413525) | Link: https://ffmpeg.org/index.html#pr8.1

### TL;DR

Version 8.1 “Hoare” is a recommended minor upgrade centered on broader codec, metadata, and GPU support. It adds experimental xHE-AAC Mps212 and external MPEG-H decoding, EXIF parsing, LCEVC metadata handling, Vulkan-compute ProRes encode/decode and DPX decode, D3D12 H.264/AV1 encoding plus filters, and Rockchip H.264/HEVC encoding. IAMF projection-mode Ambisonics, an HXVS demuxer, and drawvg/vpp_amf filters also land. Internal fixes advance the swscale rewrite, while eliminating runtime GLSL compilation speeds Vulkan initialization. HN praised the project’s ubiquity and discussed builds, codec licensing, and missing hardware benchmarks.
