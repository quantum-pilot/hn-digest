# Silicon Valley's "Pronatalists" Killed WFH. The Strait of Hormuz Brought It Back

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47412023) | Link: https://www.governance.fyi/p/silicon-valleys-pronatalists-killed

### TL;DR

The essay argues that hybrid work raises fertility among employed, partnered adults by easing childcare logistics: a 38-country study associates both partners working remotely at least weekly with 0.32 additional lifetime children per woman, rising to 0.45 in U.S. data and an estimated 291,000 annual births. It contrasts those findings with tech pronatalists’ return-to-office campaigns and Asian fuel-saving mandates after the Strait of Hormuz closure. HN commenters found the policy implication plausible but questioned causality, income selection, and the essay’s accusatory framing.

### Comment pulse

- **View:** The intensive-margin claim is narrower than “remote work creates parents” and should be communicated accordingly.
