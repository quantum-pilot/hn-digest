# Give Django your time and money, not your tokens

- Score: 393 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47400089) | Link: https://www.better-simple.com/django/2026/03/16/give-django-your-time-and-money/

### TL;DR

Django’s longevity and quality depend on contributors understanding the ticket, solution, and review feedback—not merely generating plausible code and prose. The author argues that end-to-end LLM mediation creates a facade of comprehension, conceals gaps from reviewers, and strips a communal process of the growth that motivates maintainers. Models may support learning or polish a contributor’s own explanation, with candid disclosure when used heavily; otherwise token money is better donated to the foundation. HN generalized the warning to all reviewed repositories, emphasizing eroded trust, asymmetric review cost, and contribution-gaming incentives.
