# Illinois Introducing Operating System Account Age Bill

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47416131) | Link: https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/BillStatus?DocTypeID=HB&DocNum=5511

### TL;DR

Illinois HB5511 would create the Children’s Social Media Safety Act. By January 2028, operating-system providers would ask account holders for a birth date or age, return a requested age-category signal to platform operators, and disclose only the minimum necessary data. Covered platforms must conduct age verification, apply specified defaults to known minors, and face consumer-fraud enforcement; the bill was assigned to the House Judiciary–Civil Committee, with a March 19 hearing. HN debated convenience and reduced platform liability against privacy, anonymity, shared-device, open-source, and future identity-verification risks.

### Comment pulse

- A standard signal could reduce repeated age prompts → supporters note the synopsis requires supplied age, not documentary identity proof.
- Liability incentives drew suspicion → commenters argued Meta wants OS vendors to absorb verification duties — counterpoint: separating verification from platforms may limit exposure.
- Scope worries extend beyond phones → shared computers, libraries, browsers, appliances, and volunteer operating systems complicate account-level age assumptions.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** “Minimum necessary” limits data fields, but cannot by itself prevent correlation, probing, or expansion into stronger identity checks.
- **Impact:** OS maintainers and social platforms would redesign account setup, signaling, minor defaults, and compliance before 2028.
- **Watch next:** Committee amendments, definitions of provider and operator, verification standards, enforcement liability, and protections for shared accounts.
