# Lego's 0.002mm specification and its implications for manufacturing (2025)

- Score: 340 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47335237) | Link: https://www.thewave.engineer/articles.html/productivity/legos-0002mm-specification-and-its-implications-for-manufacturing-r120/

### TL;DR

The often-repeated 0.002 mm LEGO figure is misleading: the article says critical brick features are controlled around ±0.01 mm, with tolerances assigned by function. Uniform ABS shrinkage, precisely machined multi-cavity molds, monitored filling and cooling, cavity-level traceability, and rejection of out-of-spec parts preserve clutch force and decades-long compatibility. It argues process stability matters more than machining alone and that tolerance should follow customer needs, because LEGO’s approach raises mold, scrap, and design costs. Commenters admire interchangeability but dispute its uniqueness and criticize modern sets’ price and model-first direction.
