# The dead Internet is not a theory anymore

- Score: 311 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47340935) | Link: https://www.adriankrebs.ch/blog/dead-internet/

### TL;DR

The author argues bot-generated activity has overtaken major online platforms, citing an automated-looking job-applicant reply, HN restrictions on new Show HN accounts and generated comments, Reddit product astroturfing, LinkedIn slop, and AI-opened or AI-reviewed GitHub pull requests. The evidence is qualitative rather than a measurement of the whole internet. Commenters reframe the problem as centralized platforms being economically optimized into spam, not the internet itself dying, and debate responses ranging from small independent communities to identity verification, fees, cryptographic proofs, and profit-free spaces.

### Comment pulse

- “Small internet” is the hopeful exit → blogs and community servers reduce mass incentives, though bots can still discover and flood them.
- Identity and payment add friction, not certainty → determined marketers can buy verified accounts while excluding anonymous or low-income humans.
- Automated marketing is self-consuming → agents that impersonate participation destroy the trust and attention their customers are paying to capture.
