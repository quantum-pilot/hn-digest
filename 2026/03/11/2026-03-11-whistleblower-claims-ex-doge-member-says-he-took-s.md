# Whistleblower claims ex-DOGE member says he took Social Security data to new job

- Score: 561 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47335572) | Link: https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/03/10/social-security-data-breach-doge-2/

### TL;DR

SSA’s inspector general is investigating a complaint alleging a former DOGE engineer claimed to retain Numident and Master Death File data—records covering more than 500 million living and deceased Americans—and wanted to sanitize and transfer it from a thumb drive to his new employer. The complaint does not allege a successful upload. The engineer denies wrongdoing; SSA and the company say investigations found no confirming evidence, and the Post withheld identities. Congress and GAO were notified amid separately acknowledged DOGE data-sharing violations and warnings about overly broad access.
