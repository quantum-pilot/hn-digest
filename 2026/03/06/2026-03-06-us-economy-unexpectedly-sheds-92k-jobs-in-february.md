# US economy unexpectedly sheds 92k jobs in February

- Score: 483 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47275035) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjd98091g28o

### TL;DR

US payrolls fell by 92,000 in February as unemployment edged to 4.4%, defying expectations for stable hiring and marking the largest monthly loss since October’s government shutdown. Losses reached nearly every sector; healthcare was hit by strikes, federal employment fell another 10,000, and December and January gains were revised lower. The report weakened hopes of a post-2025 hiring recovery, rattled stocks, and complicated Federal Reserve policy because higher oil prices could simultaneously restrain growth and fuel inflation. Commenters stressed both broad weakness and substantial survey uncertainty.

### Comment pulse

- Category figures showed declines in construction, manufacturing, transport, information, leisure and hospitality, and private education and health services → no single sector explains the result.
- Tourism was proposed as a cause → counterpoint: commenters noted domestic travel dominates and inbound weakness was already roughly flat year over year.
- Trust in the release split sharply → the monthly payroll estimate has a roughly ±122,000 confidence interval and remains subject to revision.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** One noisy estimate cannot define a recession, but broad sector losses and downward revisions strengthen the warning.
- **Impact:** Workers face weaker hiring while the Federal Reserve has less room to respond if oil raises inflation.
- **Watch next:** Benchmark revisions, claims, hours worked, participation, strike reversals, wage growth, and March payroll breadth.
