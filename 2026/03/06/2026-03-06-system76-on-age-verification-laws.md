# System76 on Age Verification Laws

- Score: 798 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47270784) | Link: https://blog.system76.com/post/system76-on-age-verification/

### TL;DR

System76 argues that age-signaling mandates threaten privacy and open computing while failing to stop determined minors. Colorado and California would have operating systems report user age brackets, but rely on easily evaded self-attestation; a proposed New York bill would require adults to prove age for internet-enabled devices and leave verification methods to regulators. The company warns that Linux distributions omitting signals could be treated as serving the youngest users, restricting everyone. It favors guided digital education and gradual autonomy over centralized controls, while acknowledging it will comply with law.

### Comment pulse

- Privacy critics fear age signals normalize identity checks and surveillance → open-source systems may lose anonymity or face lowest-age defaults.
- A pragmatic counterpoint favored device-level self-attestation → it could expose less data than repeated face scans or identification uploads.
- Commenters split over child protection → some assigned responsibility to parents; others favored community guidance and gradually expanding autonomy.
