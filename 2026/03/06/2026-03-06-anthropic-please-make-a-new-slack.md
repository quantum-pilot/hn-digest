# Anthropic, please make a new Slack

- Score: 157 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47280200) | Link: https://www.fivetran.com/blog/anthropic-please-make-a-new-slack

### TL;DR

Fivetran’s CEO argues Anthropic should build a Slack competitor where Claude participates directly in group work and company chat becomes openly accessible institutional memory. He says Slack’s restrictive API, high Enterprise+ pricing, and limited Slack Connect network effects leave room for an interoperable Claude-plus-chat bundle sold to every employee. HN was much less convinced: building reliable collaboration software is outside Anthropic’s demonstrated strengths, AI integration may not overcome cross-company lock-in, and replacing one proprietary platform with another risks repeating the same closed-data cycle.

### Comment pulse

- Openness alone rarely motivates migration; a materially better collaboration model must beat Slack’s partner connections and familiar workflows.
- Matrix, Signal, Keybase, Mattermost, Rocket.Chat, and Zulip offer alternatives — counterpoint: usability and reliability remain uneven.
- Anthropic could bundle AI convincingly, but poor uptime and no messaging pedigree make it an uncertain platform operator.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The strongest product idea is multiuser-first prompting, not another chronological chat client with a bot attached.
- **Impact:** Employees gain shared AI context; security, retention, legal holds, identity, and interoperability become Anthropic’s burden.
- **Watch next:** Open export guarantees, protocol support, admin controls, enterprise pricing, uptime, and credible migration tooling.
