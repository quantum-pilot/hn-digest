# Global warming has accelerated significantly

- Score: 920 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47275088) | Link: https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-6079807/v1

### TL;DR

A preprint by climate scientists Stefan Rahmstorf and Grant Foster adjusts global-temperature data for El Niño, volcanism, and solar variation. It concludes that warming after 2015 was significantly faster than during any earlier ten-year period since 1945, where prior analyses could not separate acceleration from natural variability. HN readers stressed that the finding has not passed journal peer review, then turned to policy: carbon clubs and border tariffs could deter emissions leakage, but sovereignty, energy prices, political reversals, affordability, and fatalism complicate collective rules and individual decarbonization.

### Comment pulse

- Carbon clubs could align domestic taxes with import tariffs — counterpoint: governments may reject outside inspection and perceived loss of sovereignty.
- Cheap renewables increasingly support energy security, yet retail price ratios and upfront costs still make household electrification uneven.
- Some readers prioritized adaptation amid policy reversals; others rejected fatalism because emissions cuts remain technically and economically possible.
