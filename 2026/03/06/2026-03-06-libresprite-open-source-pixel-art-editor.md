# LibreSprite – open-source pixel art editor

- Score: 246 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47272799) | Link: https://libresprite.github.io/

### TL;DR

LibreSprite is a free GPLv2 program for drawing and animating pixel-art sprites, now also available on macOS. Its resemblance to Aseprite prompted a licensing dispute in HN, but commenters clarified the lineage: LibreSprite forked the final GPL-covered Aseprite commit before that project moved to a proprietary, source-available license in 2016, then continued independently. Discussion offered little direct comparison of current features; instead, users overwhelmingly praised paid Aseprite as an approachable, joyful animation tool and worthwhile purchase, while defending the legitimacy of parallel projects and mentioning DPaint JS as a fallback.

### Comment pulse

- Source availability is not open source; Aseprite can be compiled personally, but its current license limits redistribution.
- LibreSprite’s fork was lawful under the last GPL release — counterpoint: current Aseprite users still find its polish worth paying for.
- Pixel art and frame animation feel unusually approachable, especially for game jams and artists poorly served by broader image editors.
