# Chuck Norris has died

- Score: 676 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47454782) | Link: https://variety.com/2026/film/news/chuck-norris-dead-walker-texas-ranger-dies-1236694953/

### TL;DR

Chuck Norris died at 86 after being hospitalized in Hawaii, according to his family, who said he was surrounded by loved ones and kept the circumstances private. A genuine karate champion before becoming an actor, he fought Bruce Lee onscreen in Way of the Dragon, led decades of action films, and anchored Walker, Texas Ranger from 1993 to 2001. His later conservative advocacy and improbable-toughness memes broadened his public identity. HN responded mainly with affectionate jokes, career memories, and surprise at his recent fitness.
