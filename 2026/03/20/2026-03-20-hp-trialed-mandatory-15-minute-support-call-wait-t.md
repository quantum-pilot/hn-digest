# HP trialed mandatory 15-minute support call wait times (2025)

- Score: 303 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47454164) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2025/02/misguided-hp-customer-support-approach-included-forced-15-minute-call-wait-times/

### TL;DR

HP tested a policy in five European markets that made callers wait at least 15 minutes before reaching phone support, even when agents were free. Hold messages cited high volume while nudging customers toward “digital self-solve”; reminders arrived at five, ten, and thirteen minutes. After internal and customer criticism, HP ended the trial, saying timely access to live agents remained paramount. HN readers mostly saw disrespectful short-term cost cutting, though one view held that brief delays can filter easily solved problems.
