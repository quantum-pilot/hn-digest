# I'm OK being left behind, thanks

- Score: 789 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47454341) | Link: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/03/im-ok-being-left-behind-thanks/

### TL;DR

The essay argues that fear of being “left behind” is a poor reason to adopt every new technology early. Useful tools generally become cheaper, simpler, and more reliable; dead ends consume time without lasting advantage. The author’s path through WordStar, late adoption of Git, an unused VR degree, and choosing HTML over Flash illustrates both outcomes. Waiting is not blanket technophobia—high-benefit cases such as vaccines differ—and HN discussion split between “joy of missing out” and claims that early practice compounds.
