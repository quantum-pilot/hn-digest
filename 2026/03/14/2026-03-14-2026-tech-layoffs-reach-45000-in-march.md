# 2026 tech layoffs reach 45,000 in March

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47380405) | Link: https://technode.global/2026/03/09/2026-tech-layoffs-reach-45000-in-march-more-than-9200-due-to-ai-and-automation-rationalfx/

### TL;DR

RationalFX says 45,363 tech jobs were cut worldwide in 2026 through early March, attributing 9,238—about 20%—to AI adoption or related restructuring. Block accounts for 4,000, followed by WiseTech’s 2,000 and Livspace’s 1,000; other announced reductions span support, operations, engineering, and senior roles. The article projects 264,730 cuts if the pace persists. Commenters dispute the framing and completeness, noting earlier layoff spikes and arguing executives may invoke AI to justify ordinary cost cutting, organizational delayering, or GPU investment.

### Comment pulse

- Some veterans describe big-tech bloat and misaligned hiring incentives — counterpoint: removing staff without repairing broken processes can worsen operations.
- Readers say tracker omissions and earlier spikes undermine claims of a sharp new trend.
- Layoffs may fund compute: Google’s projected $170–180 billion AI capital spending makes the employee-versus-GPU tradeoff concrete.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Corporate attribution is the weak link; announced AI rationales do not isolate automation from cyclical or managerial causes.
- **Impact:** Workers face broader role risk while investors may reward headcount reduction regardless of operational quality.
- **Watch next:** Compare disclosed job functions, rehiring, productivity, revenue, and AI spending against consistent historical layoff data.
