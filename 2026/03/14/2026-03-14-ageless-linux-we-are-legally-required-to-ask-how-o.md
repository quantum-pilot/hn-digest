# Ageless Linux. We are legally required to ask how old you are. We won't

- Score: 127 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47381791) | Link: https://agelesslinux.org/

### TL;DR

Ageless Linux is a Debian conversion script and protest project designed to violate California’s AB 1043 age-signal rules openly. It changes system identity files, installs documentation, and exposes a stub API that returns no age. The creators argue the statute’s broad definitions cover volunteer distributions, package repositories, and even individual users, creating a compliance moat for Apple, Google, and Microsoft while teaching children to lie to age gates. Planned sub-$15 devices would document violations; commenters admire the challenge but dispute its legal reading, practical risk, and litigation strategy.

### Comment pulse

- Supporters prefer regulating addictive platforms and educating families over expanding device-level surveillance.
- Critics expect authorities to ignore the stunt or reject its interpretation — counterpoint: organizers seek enforcement precisely to create a court record.
- One alternative proposes first-party forks that avoid covered app-store status instead of inviting penalties.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The project’s value is forcing statutory ambiguity into public scrutiny, not offering a materially different Debian system.
- **Impact:** Volunteer distributions may self-censor when potential defense costs exceed their budgets, even without fines.
