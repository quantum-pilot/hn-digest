# Sunsetting Jazzband

- Score: 130 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47379067) | Link: https://jazzband.co/news/2026/03/14/sunsetting-jazzband

### TL;DR

Jazzband is winding down after a decade of cooperative Python maintenance. Its open-membership, shared-push model became unsafe amid AI-generated issue and pull-request spam, but founder Jannis Leidel says the deeper failure was governance: one unpaid “roadie” remained the bottleneck for transfers, permissions, releases, and infrastructure despite years of sustainability warnings. New signups are closed; leads will arrange transfers before PyCon US 2026, and infrastructure remains through year-end. HN mourned enormous extracted value and debated funding, foundations, licensing, and whether free software implies reciprocal obligation.
