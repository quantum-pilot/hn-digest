### TL;DR

German philosopher and sociologist Jürgen Habermas died in Starnberg at 96. Beginning at Frankfurt’s Institute for Social Research under Theodor W. Adorno, he later held Max Horkheimer’s chair and produced major works including *The Structural Transformation of the Public Sphere*, *Knowledge and Human Interests*, and *The Theory of Communicative Action*. He supported the 1960s student movement but opposed its radicalization, then remained politically engaged after retirement. HN remembered an intellectual giant while debating whether networked media undermined—or made more urgent—his account of rational public communication.
