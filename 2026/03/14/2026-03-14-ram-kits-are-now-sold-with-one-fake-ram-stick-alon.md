# RAM kits are now sold with one fake RAM stick alongside a real one

- Score: 252 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47375085) | Link: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/ram/fake-ram-bundled-with-real-ram-to-create-a-performance-illusion-for-amd-users-1-1-value-pack-offers-desperate-psychological-relief-as-the-memory-shortage-worsens

### TL;DR

V-Color’s “1+1” DDR5 value pack pairs one functional memory module with one deliberately nonfunctional RGB filler, letting budget builders populate two slots visually during a severe RAM shortage. The dummy adds no capacity, bandwidth, or dual-channel performance; its only job is matching illumination. HN criticized the headline’s “fake RAM” framing because the product is openly labeled rather than a scam. Discussion instead focused on mandatory-looking RGB aesthetics, rising memory prices, return fraud, and jokes about “no-access” or write-only memory.

### Comment pulse

- RGB has become the new default → buyers sometimes accept unwanted lighting to get the best price-performance components — counterpoint: enthusiasts still enjoy it.
- Dummy modules invite return abuse → swapped packages could hurt retailers and unsuspecting resale buyers despite visible labeling.
- The shortage remains the real story → commenters expect years of tight supply unless AI demand falls or production expands sharply.
