# Claude March 2026 usage promotion

- Score: 166 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47380647) | Link: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14063676-claude-march-2026-usage-promotion

### TL;DR

Anthropic is automatically doubling five-hour usage allowances outside 8 AM–2 PM Eastern from March 13 through March 27, 2026. Free, Pro, Max, and Team users qualify across Claude apps, Cowork, Claude Code, Excel, and PowerPoint; Enterprise does not. Bonus usage leaves weekly limits untouched, and plans and billing revert unchanged afterward. Commenters interpret the offer as a load-shifting experiment, ecosystem-growth tactic, or habit-forming promotion, while asking for explicit time-window quotas and a cheap off-peak subscription for occasional side projects.
