# Chest Fridge (2009)

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47473279) | Link: https://mtbest.net/chest-fridge/

### TL;DR

A 2009 experiment converted a well-insulated chest freezer into a refrigerator using an external thermostat, achieving a reported 0.1 kWh per day. Because cold air remains pooled when the lid opens, the design limits air exchange and temperature swings; the custom relay-and-battery controller also consumes no mains power while idle. The author reports quiet operation, good food preservation, and occasional condensation removal. HN discussion focused less on feasibility than ergonomics: floor space, unusable top area, and digging through stacked food may outweigh modest household electricity savings.

### Comment pulse

- Efficiency impressed, convenience did not → upright refrigerators fit standard kitchens and expose every item without rearranging layers.
- Storage redesign could narrow the gap → sliding baskets, cantilevered shelves, or individually sealed drawers preserve access and cold air.
- Air-loss mechanism was questioned → food dominates thermal mass, so practical savings may depend more on insulation and compressor design.
