# Reports of code's death are greatly exaggerated

- Score: 209 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47476315) | Link: https://stevekrouse.com/precision

### TL;DR

Steve Krouse argues that natural-language specifications feel precise only until software must embody them. Programming is an iterative process for discovering intent, and code remains the exact artifact that captures the result. AI makes early prototypes and conventional integrations dramatically faster, but its abstractions leak as systems grow complex; the collaborative-editor example collapsed under the hidden semantics of “live collaboration.” Rather than eliminating code, stronger models should help people create, inspect, and improve better abstractions—much as printing expanded writing instead of making authorship obsolete.
