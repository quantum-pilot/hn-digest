# Bored of eating your own dogfood? Try smelling your own farts

- Score: 288 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47477158) | Link: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/03/bored-of-eating-your-own-dogfood-try-smelling-your-own-farts/

### TL;DR

Using your own product catches product defects, but not the pain surrounding it. After failing to find information, cancel online, or quickly reach a human through a poor synthetic phone system, the author argues executives should personally traverse support, billing, and cancellation—and spend time beside call-center staff. HN readers supplied similar bureaucracy stories, where live product demonstrations punctured flattering dashboards. Some debated the deliberately crude metaphor, but broadly agreed that customer-service failures stay invisible when leaders rely on KPIs instead of direct experience.
