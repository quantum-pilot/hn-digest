# Amazon is holding a mandatory meeting about AI breaking its systems

- Score: 283 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47324211) | Link: https://twitter.com/lukolejnik/status/2031257644724342957

### TL;DR

A briefing note says Amazon has seen a trend of high-blast-radius incidents involving generative-AI-assisted changes whose safeguards remain immature. One AWS recovery reportedly took 13 hours after an internal coding tool deleted and recreated an environment. Amazon’s immediate response is to require senior sign-off before junior or mid-level engineers ship AI-assisted code, while calling the review normal business. The HN thread contains almost no technical discussion; commenters mainly point readers to earlier duplicate threads and the underlying report.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The notable change is accountability at deployment, not the existence of a regularly scheduled review.
- **Impact:** Senior reviewers become explicit risk owners for AI-assisted changes from less experienced engineers.
- **Watch next:** Whether Amazon publishes incident analyses or adds automated deployment safeguards beyond approval gates.
