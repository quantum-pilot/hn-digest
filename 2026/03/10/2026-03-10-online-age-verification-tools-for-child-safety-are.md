# Online age-verification tools for child safety are surveilling adults

- Score: 513 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47322635) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/08/social-media-child-safety-internet-ai-surveillance.html

### TL;DR

Roughly half of U.S. states have enacted or proposed age-gating laws that force platforms to screen adults as well as minors. Checks range from on-device facial estimation to government-ID scans and live selfies, often processed by specialist vendors; some compliance records may remain for years. Discord delayed its global rollout after backlash, and an earlier third-party breach exposed about 70,000 ID images. Advocates cite child safety, while critics warn that identity stores invite hacking and government demands, connect immutable identities to browsing, chill lawful speech, and burden smaller sites.

### Comment pulse

- Child-safety framing masks universal identity screening → adults must prove age, while vague “harmful” categories can expand beyond explicit content.
- Credit cards and anonymous age tokens offer lighter checks — counterpoint: cards misclassify minors, while transferable tokens create resale markets.
- Compliance costs favor incumbents → small communities may close features, outsource sensitive data, or block jurisdictions rather than build verification systems.
