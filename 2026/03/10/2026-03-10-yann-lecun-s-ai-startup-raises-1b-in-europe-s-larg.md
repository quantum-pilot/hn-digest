# Yann LeCun's AI startup raises $1B in Europe's largest ever seed round

- Score: 406 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47321533) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/e5245ec3-1a58-4eff-ab58-480b6259aaf1

### TL;DR

AMI Labs raised $1.03 billion in Europe’s largest seed round and the world’s second-largest, behind Thinking Machines Lab’s $2 billion, according to Dealroom. The financing values Yann LeCun’s world-model startup at $3.5 billion before the investment, with backers including Cathay Innovation, Bezos Expeditions, Temasek, SBVA, and Nvidia. Alexandre LeBrun is CEO, LeCun executive chair, and the 12-person company spans four cities. It expects at least a year of research before applications; Nabla is the first partner, while Meta is negotiating access and commercialization rights.
