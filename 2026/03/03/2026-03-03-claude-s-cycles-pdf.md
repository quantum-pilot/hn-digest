# Claude's Cycles [pdf]

- Score: 413 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47230710) | Link: https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/papers/claude-cycles.pdf

### TL;DR

Don Knuth recounts Claude Opus 4.6 discovering a construction that decomposes a three-dimensional modular digraph into three Hamiltonian cycles for all odd m above one. Guided by Filip Stappers, Claude ran 31 documented explorations in about an hour, moving through brute force, serpentine patterns, fiber decomposition, and simulated annealing before extracting a rule tested through m=101. Knuth then supplied rigorous proofs and classified 760 related decompositions. Even m remains open. HN praised the human-model partnership but debated whether finding a construction without proving it qualifies as solving the problem.
