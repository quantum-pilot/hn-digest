# India's top court angry after junior judge cites fake AI-generated orders

- Score: 334 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47231261) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c178zzw780xo

### TL;DR

An Andhra Pradesh junior civil judge used four nonexistent AI-generated precedents in an August property-dispute order. The state high court acknowledged the citations but upheld the outcome, accepting her explanation that first-time AI use caused a good-faith mistake because the underlying legal principles were correct. India’s Supreme Court disagreed, stayed the order, called the conduct misconduct and an institutional threat to adjudicatory integrity, and notified legal authorities for further examination. Commenters placed responsibility on licensed professionals, while warning that plausible automation predictably erodes vigilance and requires process safeguards beyond punishment.
