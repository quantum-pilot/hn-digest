# Ars Technica fires reporter after AI controversy involving fabricated quotes

- Score: 561 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47226608) | Link: https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/ars-technica-fires-reporter-ai-quotes

### TL;DR

Ars Technica reportedly dismissed reporter Benj Edwards after retracting a February 13 story containing fabricated quotations attributed to Scott Shambaugh. Edwards accepted responsibility, saying that while feverish and sleep-deprived he used a Claude Code-based extraction tool, then ChatGPT for troubleshooting, and mistakenly inserted a paraphrase as verbatim text; he cleared coauthor Kyle Orland. Ars called it an isolated standards failure and promised an AI-use guide. HN split between viewing dismissal as accountability and faulting Ars for opaque communication, insufficient editorial verification, and punishing one operator for a systemic control failure.
