# I'm losing the SEO battle for my own open source project

- Score: 424 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47232158) | Link: https://twitter.com/Gavriel_Cohen/status/2028821432759717930

### TL;DR

NanoClaw’s creator says an auto-generated impostor site, registered six days after the project’s February 2 launch, ranks directly below its GitHub repository while the official site is absent through page five. The fake copies documentation, publishes inaccuracies and false dates, attracts mistaken support requests, and could later distribute phishing or malicious code. Google has not corrected rankings despite the repository’s explicit domain field, coverage, and takedown requests. HN blamed first-mover and backlink weighting, noted other search engines also failed, and offered outreach, schema, social profiles, and patience as pragmatic repairs.

### Comment pulse
