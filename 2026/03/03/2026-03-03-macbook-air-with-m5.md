# MacBook Air with M5

- Score: 348 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47232502) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/03/apple-introduces-the-new-macbook-air-with-m5/

### TL;DR

Apple’s refreshed 13- and 15-inch Air pairs a 10-core M5 CPU and up-to-10-core GPU with 153GB/s unified-memory bandwidth. Starting storage doubles to 512GB with claimed twice-faster SSD performance and configurations up to 4TB; Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 also arrive. The fanless design, 18-hour battery claim, two Thunderbolt 4 ports, display, and camera remain familiar. Prices start at $1,099 and $1,299, with March 11 availability. Commenters saw it as the default roughly-$1,000 consumer laptop while criticizing repairability, macOS lock-in, weight, and same-side ports.

### Comment pulse
