# Iran War Cost Tracker

- Score: 266 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47237080) | Link: https://iran-cost-ticker.com

### TL;DR

The tracker estimates direct U.S. spending for Operation Epic Fury with a three-phase model: about $380 million daily for initial strikes, $220 million during sustained operations, then $155 million for air-dominance and surveillance. It separately adds major munitions and equipment losses, while excluding veteran care, energy disruption, allied spending, and environmental cleanup. Commenters challenged whether salaries and existing assets are truly incremental, noted omitted interceptors and uncertain damage, and argued the displayed total could therefore be simultaneously overcounted in some categories and far below full long-term costs.

### Comment pulse

- Personnel and ships incur baseline costs anyway — counterpoint: extended combat deployment adds operating expense and exists because the capability was procured.
- Classified interceptor use, unknown ordnance consumption, and disputed radar damage make precise real-time accounting impossible.
- School-lunch comparisons expose political priorities, though state finances are not constrained like household budgets.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** This is a transparent scenario model, not an audited ledger; omissions and allocation choices dominate apparent precision.
- **Impact:** Its strongest function is making opportunity costs discussable, while headline totals risk becoming false certainty.
- **Watch next:** Updated assumptions, disclosed interceptor usage, verified equipment losses, supplemental appropriations, and later government accounting.
