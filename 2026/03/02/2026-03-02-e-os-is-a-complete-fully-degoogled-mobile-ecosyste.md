# /e/OS is a complete, fully “deGoogled” mobile ecosystem

- Score: 605 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47215489) | Link: https://e.foundation/e-os/

### TL;DR

/e/OS packages a de-Googled Android fork with microG, non-Google network services, tracker controls, privacy-rated Android apps, and optional Murena email, storage, office tools, and encrypted files. Users can buy a preinstalled phone, use a WebUSB installer, flash manually, or self-host parts of the cloud. HN valued an approachable privacy-focused system across more hardware than Pixel-only GrapheneOS, but disputed “fully deGoogled,” criticized privileged microG and the Chromium-dependent installer, and warned that banking attestation, security tradeoffs, upstream Google dependence, and Murena centralization limit independence.

### Comment pulse

- Leaving Android entirely promises sovereignty — counterpoint: no alternative currently matches its security, hardware support, app catalog, and long-term maintenance.
- Banks and utilities can reject custom ROMs, turning platform attestation into a regulatory and collective-action problem beyond implementation quality.
- Firefox rejects WebUSB as excessive browser scope; a local installer and plain compatibility list would better fit the privacy audience.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** /e/OS optimizes practical privacy and compatibility, not complete technical independence from Google’s Android ecosystem.
- **Impact:** Mainstream users gain a gentler exit from Google services; high-risk users still need stronger security guarantees.
- **Watch next:** Update longevity, verified boot, microG privileges, app-attestation compatibility, self-hosting usability, and non-WebUSB installation.
