# New iPad Air, powered by M4

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47218175) | Link: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/03/apple-introduces-the-new-ipad-air-powered-by-m4/

### TL;DR

Apple’s updated 11- and 13-inch tablets move to an eight-core M4 CPU, nine-core GPU, 12GB unified memory, and 120GB/s bandwidth. Apple claims 30% higher performance than M3 and 2.3 times M1, while adding Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and a more efficient C1X modem on cellular versions. Storage starts at 128GB and prices remain $599 and $799, with March 11 availability. Commenters questioned power that iPadOS cannot fully exploit, prioritizing multiuser profiles and laptop-class flexibility, though artists, musicians, gamers, and pilots cited specialized value.
