# The workers behind Meta's smart glasses can see everything

- Score: 293 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47225130) | Link: https://www.svd.se/a/K8nrV4/metas-ai-smart-glasses-and-data-privacy-concerns-workers-say-we-see-everything

### TL;DR

An investigation based on interviews with more than 30 workers at Meta subcontractor Sama says Kenyan annotators encounter intimate smart-glasses footage, including nudity, sex, bathrooms, bank cards, and sensitive conversations. The glasses record only after button or voice activation, but AI functions require server processing; essential voice, text, image, and video handling cannot be disabled, and Meta’s terms allow human review. Reporters found Swedish retailers incorrectly describing processing as local. Meta referred them to policies without answering where clips originated, retention periods, subcontractor safeguards, or whether Swedish footage reached Kenya.
