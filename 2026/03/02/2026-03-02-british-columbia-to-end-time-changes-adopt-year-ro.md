# British Columbia to end time changes, adopt year-round daylight time

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47223620) | Link: https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/b-c-adopting-year-round-daylight-time-9.7111657

### TL;DR

Most British Columbians will move clocks forward on March 8, 2026, then remain on year-round daylight time, ending the twice-yearly switch. The province stopped waiting for California, Oregon, and Washington, whose changes require U.S. federal approval; eastern communities already using mountain-time variants will be handled differently. Vancouver’s winter sunrise could arrive as late as 9:08 a.m., with sunset near 5:14 p.m. HN broadly welcomed stability but split over the offset: standard-time advocates emphasized circadian health and safer mornings, while daylight-time supporters valued usable evening sun.

### Comment pulse

- Permanent-standard supporters prioritized school commutes and morning light — counterpoint: daylight supporters called post-work sunlight more valuable than early dawn.
- Commenters disputed whether clock time should track solar noon closely, noting geography and time-zone borders already create large variation.
- Many considered either fixed choice healthier and easier than recurring sleep disruption, scheduling errors, and seasonal clock maintenance.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Ending clock changes solves the shared problem; choosing daylight time merely relocates the seasonal inconvenience.
- **Impact:** Evening-oriented households gain leisure light, while winter commuters and students absorb darker mornings.
- **Watch next:** Accident, sleep, school, energy, and public-satisfaction data after the first winter without a fallback.
