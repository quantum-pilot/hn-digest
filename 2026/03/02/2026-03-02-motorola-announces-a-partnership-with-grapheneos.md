# Motorola announces a partnership with GrapheneOS

- Score: 2018 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47214645) | Link: https://motorolanews.com/motorola-three-new-b2b-solutions-at-mwc-2026/

### TL;DR

Motorola and the GrapheneOS Foundation announced a long-term collaboration covering joint research, software improvements, security capabilities, and future phones engineered for GrapheneOS compatibility. No models, dates, preinstallation plans, or technical requirements were specified. Motorola separately introduced Moto Analytics for enterprise fleet health and Private Image Data, which will strip sensitive metadata from new photos on signature devices. Commenters welcomed an official route beyond Pixels and hoped cooperation would improve verified boot, documentation, and updates, while questioning ease of use, MDM support, Lenovo-related supply-chain concerns, and Motorola’s uneven update history.

### Comment pulse

- Direct OEM cooperation could replace reverse-engineering with supported hardware documentation, verified boot, and reliable alternative-OS installation.
- Motorola’s security-update history worried buyers — counterpoint: some ThinkPhone owners reported monthly patches and nearly stock Android.
- Enterprise adoption depends on first-class MDM; consumer adoption requires secure defaults that do not turn phones into hobbies.
