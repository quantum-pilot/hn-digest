# 3D-Knitting: The Ultimate Guide

- Score: 209 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47347945) | Link: https://www.oliver-charles.com/pages/3d-knitting

### TL;DR

A commercial guide explains Wholegarment machines, introduced by Shima Seiki in 1995, which use four needle beds and a digital garment model to knit seamless sweaters. Oliver Charles claims 99% material use, lighter and stronger garments, on-demand customization, lower electricity use, and 150 daily pieces with six workers, contrasting the process with cut-and-sew, flatbed, and circular knitting. HN admired the manufacturing and inventory model but noted the page is advertising, disputed its fast-fashion cure, and asked why promised custom sizing is not already available.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The strongest advance is flexible, low-inventory manufacturing, not a complete sustainability solution.
- **Impact:** Short-run apparel brands can localize production and test demand without stocking every variation.
- **Watch next:** Verified lifecycle data, custom-sizing rollout, repairability, and unit economics against two-dimensional knitting.
