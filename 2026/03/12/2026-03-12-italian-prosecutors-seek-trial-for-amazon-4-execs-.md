# Italian prosecutors seek trial for Amazon, 4 execs in alleged $1.4B tax evasion

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47352297) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/world/italian-prosecutors-seek-trial-amazon-four-execs-over-alleged-14-bln-tax-evasion-2026-03-12/

### TL;DR

Milan prosecutors have asked a judge to send Amazon EU Sarl and four managers to trial over an alleged €1.2 billion in VAT evasion from 2019–2021, despite Amazon’s separate €527 million tax settlement. They say Amazon’s algorithms and operating model let tens of thousands of mostly Chinese sellers trade in Italy without disclosing their identities or paying VAT, for which platforms can share liability. A preliminary hearing will decide indictment or dismissal. Commenters emphasize competitive fairness, executive accountability, and skepticism that the case will survive settlement or political pressure.
