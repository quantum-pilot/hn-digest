# US private credit defaults hit record 9.2% in 2025, Fitch says

- Score: 214 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47349806) | Link: https://www.marketscreener.com/news/us-private-credit-defaults-hit-record-9-2-in-2025-fitch-says-ce7e5fd8df8fff2d

### TL;DR

Fitch says 9.2% of the 302 U.S. companies in its private-credit monitor defaulted in 2025, a record above 2024’s 8.1%. It counted 38 events across 28 borrowers, including bankruptcies and distressed debt exchanges rather than only missed cash payments. Most affected issuers earned $25 million or less; the monitored universe is largely middle-market companies, and defaults spanned sectors. Fitch identifies floating-rate debt with little hedging and three years of elevated rates as a catalyst. Commenters stress that private credit means privately negotiated corporate loans, not consumer debt.

### Comment pulse

- Default does not equal total lender loss → restructurings can exchange impaired debt while companies continue operating and recovery values remain unresolved.
- Software fears were not visible in Fitch’s count → issuers may be classified by customer industry, complicating sector interpretation.
- Some see a systemic warning → counterpoint: private credit is ordinary debt with collateral and seniority, not inherently a synthetic-crisis instrument.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** A record rate is concerning, but this selected 302-company monitor is not the entire private-credit market.
- **Impact:** Smaller floating-rate borrowers and their lenders face refinancing, restructuring, and valuation pressure first.
- **Watch next:** Recoveries after default, repeat restructurings, rate changes, lender write-downs, and portfolio redemption limits.
