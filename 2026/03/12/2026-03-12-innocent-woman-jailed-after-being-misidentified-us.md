# Innocent woman jailed after being misidentified using AI facial recognition

- Score: 293 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47356968) | Link: https://www.grandforksherald.com/news/north-dakota/ai-error-jails-innocent-grandmother-for-months-in-north-dakota-fraud-case

### TL;DR

Fargo police used facial recognition on bank-fraud footage, then a detective judged Angela Lipps similar by face, build, hair, and social-media photos. US Marshals arrested her in Tennessee; she spent 108 days awaiting extradition and nearly six months jailed before police interviewed her. Bank records placed her 1,200 miles away during the crimes, and charges were dismissed five days later. She lost her home, car, and dog. HN blamed both automation bias and cascading human failures across investigation, prosecution, and detention.
