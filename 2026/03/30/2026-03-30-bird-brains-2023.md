# Bird brains (2023)

- Score: 284 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47573887) | Link: https://www.dhanishsemar.com/writing/bird-brains

### TL;DR

From New Zealand kea moving traffic cones to stop cars for food, the survey builds a case that avian intelligence rivals primates in surprising ways. It reviews mirror recognition, water-displacement puzzles, delayed gratification, tool planning, vocabulary, and exceptional spatial memory across corvids and parrots. Dense forebrains may pack roughly twice the neurons of equal-mass primate brains. HN agreed birds are cognitively underestimated but warned that laboratory tasks can mistake species-specific ecology for a universal intelligence ladder, and debated what that means for keeping pets.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Neuron density explains capacity better than brain size alone, but behavior still depends on ecology and experimental design.
- **Impact:** Recognizing richer cognition raises the welfare standard for captive birds and how humans manage wild populations.
- **Watch next:** Cross-species replications, task controls, neural architecture, long-term planning evidence, and welfare guidance tied to cognitive needs.
