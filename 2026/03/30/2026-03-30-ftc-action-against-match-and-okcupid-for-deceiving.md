# FTC action against Match and OkCupid for deceiving users, sharing personal data

- Score: 232 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47575616) | Link: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-takes-action-against-match-okcupid-deceiving-users-sharing-personal-data-third-party

### TL;DR

The FTC says Match Group and OkCupid deceived users by sharing OkCupid data with a third party despite contrary privacy promises. According to allegations quoted in the HN discussion, the 2014 transfer gave Clarifai access to nearly three million user photos plus demographic and location information, without formal contractual limits, while OkCupid’s founders were investors in the recipient. The settlement prohibits future misrepresentations. Commenters focused on the twelve-year delay, questioned whether users could pursue copyright claims, and criticized an outcome that appeared to impose no monetary penalty.

### Comment pulse
