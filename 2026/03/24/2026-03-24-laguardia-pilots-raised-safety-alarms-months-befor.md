# LaGuardia pilots raised safety alarms months before deadly runway crash

- Score: 325 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47503965) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/mar/24/laguardia-airplane-pilots-safety-concerns-crash

### TL;DR

Months before an Air Canada Express jet struck a fire truck at LaGuardia, anonymous NASA safety reports described runway-crossing close calls, ambiguous separation guidance, heavy operational pace, and apparently disabled runway-status lights. Sunday’s crash killed pilots MacKenzie Gunther and Antoine Forest and hospitalized 41 people. Audio indicates a controller cleared the truck, then tried to stop it while also handling another aircraft’s emergency. The NTSB is analyzing intact recorders, so causation and staffing facts remain unsettled. HN urges systemic workload and staffing fixes rather than blaming one controller.
