# Epic Games to cut more than 1k jobs as Fortnite usage falls

- Score: 233 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47503810) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/epic-games-said-tuesday-that-it-will-lay-off-more-than-1000-employees-2026-03-24/

### TL;DR

Epic Games will eliminate more than 1,000 jobs after Fortnite engagement and average playtime declined, its second major layoff since cutting 830 roles in 2023. CEO Tim Sweeney says the company spends substantially more than it earns and will save another $500 million by reducing contractors, marketing, and open roles; he explicitly says AI is not the cause. Fortnite still led US monthly active players on PlayStation and Xbox last month. HN blames overexpansion, expensive platform bets, and quadrupled headcount against broadly flat revenue more than one declining title.
