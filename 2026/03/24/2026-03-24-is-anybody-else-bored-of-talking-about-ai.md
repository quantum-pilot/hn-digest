# Is anybody else bored of talking about AI?

- Score: 434 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47508745) | Link: https://blog.jakesaunders.dev/is-anybody-else-bored-of-talking-about-ai/

### TL;DR

A developer who uses AI daily and credits it with accelerating a difficult new role is nevertheless tired of AI dominating technical discourse. Repetitive workflow posts focus on the hammer instead of what people build, while management measures token consumption rather than product outcomes—recreating lines-of-code incentives. The request is not to abandon AI, but to restore discussion of projects, problems, and user value. HN agrees the conversation lacks diversity, yet argues that effective AI use exposes systems thinking, rewards experienced engineers, and still offers substantial unexplored technical depth.
