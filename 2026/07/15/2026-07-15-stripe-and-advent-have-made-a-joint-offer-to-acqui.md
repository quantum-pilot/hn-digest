# Stripe and Advent have made a joint offer to acquire PayPal – sources

- Score: 318 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48915953) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/stripe-advent-offer-buy-paypal-more-than-53-billion-sources-say-2026-07-15/

### TL;DR

Stripe and Advent International reportedly offered $60.50 per share—over $53 billion—to acquire PayPal, a 28% premium to its prior close. The equal partners have about $50 billion of committed bank financing, would keep PayPal intact, and seek agreement by month-end after an April approach; PayPal has not responded, and no transaction is assured. HN focused less on price than consolidation: Stripe would control PayPal, Venmo, Braintree, and Xoom, raising antitrust, fee, merchant-account resilience, and content-policy concerns, though commenters noted other processors and card networks remain influential.

### Comment pulse

- Antitrust scrutiny looked unavoidable → commenters predicted extreme checkout concentration and possible Venmo or Braintree divestitures.
