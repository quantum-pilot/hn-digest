# The Three-Second Theft: Why AI Voice Fraud Outruns Every Defence

- Score: 164 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48920432) | Link: https://smarterarticles.co.uk/the-three-second-theft-why-ai-voice-fraud-outruns-every-defence

### TL;DR

Voice cloning can turn three seconds of public audio into a convincing family-emergency scam, while detection, caller-ID authentication, and watermarks fail during live calls. The FBI counted over 22,000 AI-linked complaints and $893 million in adjusted 2025 losses, including $352 million among people 60-plus, likely underreported. The article argues protection must shift from panicked families to institutions: verified cloning consent, carrier authentication, bank friction, and mandatory reimbursement. HN agreed AI industrializes an old scam, but debated codewords, authenticated calling, elder autonomy, and whether the AI-assisted article itself felt trustworthy.
