# Jurassic Park computers in excruciating detail

- Score: 865 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48915709) | Link: https://fabiensanglard.net/jurrasic_park_computers/index.html

### TL;DR

A meticulous rewatch inventories Jurassic Park’s on-screen technology: PowerBooks and Quadra 700s, SGI Indigo and Crimson workstations, CM-5 supercomputers, PLI storage arrays, a Motorola Envoy prototype, and period CRTs. Production assembled roughly $1.7 million in 1993 equipment and fed prebuilt graphics to real displays, while recognizable software ranged from IRIX tools and fsn to QuickTime and Macintosh code. HN readers supplied missing provenance, identifying the Envoy as a frogdesign mockup, the visible code as MPW examples, and the CM-5’s path into the film.
