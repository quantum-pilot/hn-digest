# Collection of Digital Clock Designs

- Score: 164 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48923380) | Link: https://clocks.dev

### TL;DR

Clocks.dev is an open-source gallery of user-created digital clock designs, sortable by all-time popularity, weekly activity, or newest additions, with a built-in creation path. The collection treats time as a visual-design prompt: entries use geometric hands, words, numbered fields, binary-coded digits, radial layouts, dates, and playful reminders rather than conventional displays. HN readers shared their own SVG watchfaces and experimental clocks, floated a webring for clock enthusiasts, and praised the rabbit hole. Detailed critiques focused on ambiguous encoding, visual polish, and whether unusual representations remain readable.
