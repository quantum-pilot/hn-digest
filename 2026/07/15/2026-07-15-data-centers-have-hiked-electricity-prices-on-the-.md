# Data centers have hiked electricity prices on the public by $23B

- Score: 194 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48914683) | Link: https://fortune.com/2026/07/14/data-centers-23-billion-electricity-bills/

### TL;DR

An analysis of U.S. utility rate-setting argues that data-center demand helped raise PJM capacity-market costs through 2028 and could shift grid-expansion risks onto households. Regulators allocate shared plants, substations, and transmission costs across customer classes; flexible data centers may reduce usage during coincident peaks, lowering their assigned share, while abandoned or overestimated projects can leave stranded costs. HN commenters challenged the headline: the cited $23.1 billion is total added market revenue, including data-center payments, with one estimate putting other customers’ burden nearer $16 billion.

### Comment pulse

- Cost causation is a policy choice → commenters compared grid hookups with developers funding water and sewer upgrades, demanding proportionate upfront charges.

- Data centers might finance broader capacity → supporters called them anchor tenants — counterpoint: benefits depend on upgrades materializing and costs being allocated fairly.
