# Microsoft has released software updates to plug at least 570 security holes

- Score: 197 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48913190) | Link: https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/07/microsoft-patches-a-record-570-security-flaws/

### TL;DR

Microsoft’s July 2026 release fixes at least 570 vulnerabilities, nearly 60 critical, roughly 250 privilege-escalation flaws, and three zero-days; two are reportedly exploited. Notable issues affect ADFS, SharePoint, BitLocker, and Copilot, whose 9.6-rated flaw could accept crafted prompts from a malicious site through Edge on Android. Microsoft attributes rising counts to AI-assisted discovery, while researchers warn AI also accelerates exploit creation and weakens human-centered exploitability ratings. HN welcomed automated bug hunting but questioned reporting incentives, whether generated fixes introduce regressions, and why ordinary feedback rarely reaches engineers.
