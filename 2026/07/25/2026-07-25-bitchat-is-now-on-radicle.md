# Bitchat is now on Radicle

- Score: 195 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49047365) | Link: https://radicle.network/nodes/rosa.radicle.network/rad%3Az2v9tRJz1oknFAqCSY5W5c76nVvm6

### TL;DR
BitChat is a public-domain, decentralized messaging app for iOS/macOS that combines a Bluetooth LE mesh with Nostr-based internet relays. Locally, it forms multi-hop encrypted meshes with no accounts, phone numbers, or central servers; globally, it uses ephemeral keys and geohash-based channels on Nostr relays. It supports IRC-style commands, intelligent routing (Bluetooth first, Nostr fallback), and an emergency wipe. HN commenters see it as promising for protests/disasters, but note limited real-world density and practical distribution issues.
