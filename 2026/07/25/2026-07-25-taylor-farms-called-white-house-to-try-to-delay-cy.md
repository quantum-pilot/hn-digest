# Taylor Farms Called White House to Try to Delay Cyclospora Recall

- Score: 313 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49044074) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/health/taylor-farms-cyclospora-recall-delay-call-41fef0bc

### TL;DR

Taylor Farms asked the White House to delay announcing a cyclospora recall linked to one of the largest U.S. foodborne-illness outbreaks, seeking more evidence and time before public attribution. Officials ultimately accepted the FDA’s findings and allowed disclosure. The company faced a costly recall and reputational damage; critics faulted both corporate and government handling. HN discussion noted that one false-positive sample did not overturn converging epidemiological and traceback evidence involving shredded iceberg lettuce, questioned opaque branding of affected products, and broadened into distrust of food-safety institutions.
