# Taylor Farms Called White House to Try to Delay Cyclospora Recall

- Score: 313 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49044074) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/health/taylor-farms-cyclospora-recall-delay-call-41fef0bc

- TL;DR  
  Taylor Farms, a major U.S. produce supplier, reportedly called the White House seeking to delay the public recall announcement tied to a large Cyclospora foodborne illness outbreak. The company argued it needed more proof and time before being formally linked, highlighting the tension between brand protection and rapid public-health action. FDA officials presented evidence; administration officials ultimately backed immediate disclosure. Some current and former officials now criticize both the company’s lobbying and the government’s handling of the outbreak timeline.
