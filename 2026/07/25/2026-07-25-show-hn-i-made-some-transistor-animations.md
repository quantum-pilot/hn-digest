# Show HN: I made some transistor animations

- Score: 113 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49039868) | Link: https://brandonli.net/semisim/animations

### TL;DR
Author built a semiconductor simulator that animates the motion of electrons and holes inside three key transistor types: NPN BJTs, n‑channel MOSFETs, and n‑channel JFETs. One animation set shows average carrier motion from drift plus diffusion, another shows full random diffusion plus drift (more realistic, more visually noisy), and a third overlays terminal currents and voltages. HN readers, from educators to practicing EEs and total novices, report the visuals finally making transistor internals intuitive and teachable.
