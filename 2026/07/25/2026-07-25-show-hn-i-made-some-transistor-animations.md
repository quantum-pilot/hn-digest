# Show HN: I made some transistor animations

- Score: 113 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49039868) | Link: https://brandonli.net/semisim/animations

### TL;DR

A semiconductor simulator visualizes charge-carrier behavior in NPN bipolar transistors, n-channel MOSFETs, and n-channel JFETs. Blue dots represent electrons, red dots holes, and white flashes recombination. Three animation sets trade clarity for detail: simplified carrier motion, more accurate but busier drift-and-diffusion motion, and terminal-current/voltage probes. HN readers found the visual model unusually effective at turning formula-level transistor knowledge into physical intuition, including for experienced electrical engineers. Discussion asks about simulation fidelity and a permissive license so educators can reuse the material.

### Comment pulse
