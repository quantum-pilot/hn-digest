# A 77-year-old Republican man is staging a solo protest against Flock cameras

- Score: 119 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49051816) | Link: https://www.cltampa.com/news/a-77-year-old-republican-man-is-staging-a-solo-protest-against-st-petes-flock-cameras/

### TL;DR

Carl Gunn, 77, twice staged a legal, nondestructive protest by sitting beneath St. Petersburg Flock cameras and blocking their view with a sign on a pool-skimmer pole. Drivers brought drinks, honked support, and police questioned him before leaving. The city operates about 50 privately supplied cameras that record plates, vehicle features, locations, and searchable AI-generated profiles; police cite crime-solving value, while critics cite nationwide sharing, misuse, wrongful arrests, and routine searches. Commenters largely call surveillance a bipartisan concern, debating whether identifying Gunn as Republican clarifies cross-party opposition or manufactures division.
