# A 77-year-old Republican man is staging a solo protest against Flock cameras

- Score: 119 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49051816) | Link: https://www.cltampa.com/news/a-77-year-old-republican-man-is-staging-a-solo-protest-against-st-petes-flock-cameras/

## TL;DR
A 77-year-old Republican, Carl Gunn, is staging lone, low-tech protests against Flock license-plate cameras in St. Petersburg, Florida, literally blocking a camera with a pool skimmer and “DOWN WITH FLOCK CAMERAS” sign. He argues that always-on, privately run, AI-enhanced plate readers create unconstitutional mass surveillance of law-abiding citizens. The article details Flock’s nationwide searchable database, trivial and immigration-related uses, and rapid revenue growth. HN discussion centers on bipartisan privacy concerns and media emphasis on Gunn’s party affiliation.
