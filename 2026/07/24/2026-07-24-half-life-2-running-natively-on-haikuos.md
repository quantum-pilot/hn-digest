# Half-Life 2 running natively on HaikuOS

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49034868) | Link: https://discuss.haiku-os.org/t/haiku-nvidia-porting-nvidia-driver-for-turing-gpus/16520?page=18

- TL;DR  
Community work on HaikuOS’s new Nvidia driver for Turing/Ampere GPUs now runs Half‑Life 2 “natively” on an RTX 2080 at 4K60, using nillerusr’s Source engine fork derived from a 2020 Source leak. The Haiku thread digs into display modes, HDMI/DisplayPort audio, and which affordable cards work, with some users suddenly treating Haiku as daily‑driver material. Hacker News praises powerhouse developer X512, worries about the leak‑based engine, reminisces about BeOS, and compares this to HL2 on low‑power ARM and Steam Decks.

- Comment pulse
