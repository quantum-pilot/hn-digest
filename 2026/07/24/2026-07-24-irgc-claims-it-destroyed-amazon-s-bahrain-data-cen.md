# IRGC claims it destroyed Amazon's Bahrain data center

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49033240) | Link: https://houseofsaud.com/irgc-claims-destroyed-amazon-bahrain-data-center/

### TL;DR
Iran’s Revolutionary Guard claims it destroyed Amazon’s AWS Bahrain (me-south-1) data center with cruise missiles, in retaliation for a US strike on Iran’s Darkhovin nuclear plant. The article frames this as the third major attack on the same AWS region in 2026 and a clear doctrinal shift: US-branded commercial cloud is now treated as legitimate military infrastructure. AWS has not confirmed the latest strike, but earlier hits already caused multi-AZ outages, forced large-scale customer migrations, and exposed the fragility of centralized cloud in wartime.
