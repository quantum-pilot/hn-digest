# IRGC claims it destroyed Amazon's Bahrain data center

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49033240) | Link: https://houseofsaud.com/irgc-claims-destroyed-amazon-bahrain-data-center/

### TL;DR

The IRGC said cruise missiles destroyed AWS’s ME-South-1 facility in Bahrain, framing it as retaliation for a US strike on Iran’s unfinished Darkhovin nuclear plant. Amazon, Bahrain, and CENTCOM had not confirmed the claim, and the IRGC supplied no imagery. The article says the region had already suffered repeated drone and missile damage since March, disrupting services and prompting migrations. Hacker News users cited outage status, satellite-damage reports, and recovery from backups while debating whether centralized cloud campuses remain viable without hardened construction, geographic redundancy, and air defenses.

### Comment pulse

- The regional outage was not sudden → commenters said April damage already left ME-South-1 largely offline, with July imagery suggesting further physical destruction.
- Backups limited data loss but not user impact → one operator restored elsewhere, trading roughly 60 ms latency for 110 ms.
- Physical placement now joins software architecture → rebuilding behind permanent defenses or inside hardened shells may help — counterpoint: ballistic missiles can exceed either protection.
