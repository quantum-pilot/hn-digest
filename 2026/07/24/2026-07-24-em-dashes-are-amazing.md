# Em dashes are amazing

- Score: 323 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49034747) | Link: https://psychotechnology.substack.com/p/em-dashes-are-fucking-amazing

### TL;DR

The essay defends liberal use of spaced em dashes for clarifications, parenthetical asides, and dramatic pauses, arguing they keep related thoughts in one energetic sentence and should not be abandoned merely because readers associate them with AI. It ranks em dashes above colons, semicolons, brackets, and ellipses, while acknowledging their differing moods. HN responded with affectionate typographic pedantry: commenters distinguished hyphens, en dashes, em dashes, and multi-em Unicode characters; debated spacing conventions and overuse; and tested whether commas or parentheses communicate the same structure.

### Comment pulse

- Dash taxonomy matters → hyphens join ordinary compounds, en dashes express ranges or balanced compounds, and em dashes mark stronger sentence breaks.
- Spacing is style-dependent → some traditions use closed em dashes, others spaced en dashes — counterpoint: the essay intentionally chose spaced em dashes.
- Alternative punctuation changes emphasis → commas can become ambiguous inside lists, parentheses soften asides, ellipses trail away, and semicolons connect independent clauses.
