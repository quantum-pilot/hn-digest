# Flux 3

- Score: 547 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49031796) | Link: https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3

### TL;DR
FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs’ new multimodal “world model” backbone that jointly learns from images, video, audio, and actions using a unified flow-based architecture (Self-Flow). It targets three main domains: text/image/video+audio generation, advanced image editing, and action prediction for robotics, with an open-weight “Dev” backbone promised. Early internal benchmarks show strong video quality versus major competitors, especially on faces and audiovisual coherence, but HN discussion questions the marketing, missing full-length demos, and how real or open the “world model” and weights will be.
