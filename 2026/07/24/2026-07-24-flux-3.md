# Flux 3

- Score: 547 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49031796) | Link: https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3

### TL;DR

Black Forest Labs’ early-access FLUX 3 is a unified flow model trained jointly on images, video, and audio, aiming to learn shared physical structure rather than separate modalities. It generates 20-second video with native audio, supports multimodal references and multilingual dialogue, improves image text, and supplies a backbone for robot action prediction. Video is available first; image, action, private weights, and an open-weight Dev backbone are planned. HN reactions mix enthusiasm with doubts about sparse demos, preliminary benchmarks, world-model branding, missing touch data, and the open release’s completeness.
