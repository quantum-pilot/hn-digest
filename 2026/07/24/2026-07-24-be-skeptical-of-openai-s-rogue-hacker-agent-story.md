# Be skeptical of OpenAI's rogue hacker agent story

- Score: 387 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49038060) | Link: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jul/24/openai-rogue-hacker

### TL;DR
Thickstun argues OpenAI’s “rogue hacker agent” story repeats a long-running PR pattern: emphasize catastrophic AI risk to signal exceptional power, attract capital, and justify exclusive control. He stresses that the same AI capabilities used to hack can strengthen security, but this equilibrium only holds if strong models are broadly available. Today, guardrails keep US frontier models from being used defensively, so Hugging Face reportedly relied on an open Chinese model instead—highlighting the dangers of concentrated AI power more than of AI itself.
