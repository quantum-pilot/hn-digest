# India's first privately-developed rocket reaches orbit on debut launch

- Score: 468 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48973835) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/07/indias-first-privately-developed-rocket-reaches-orbit-on-dramatic-debut-launch/

### TL;DR

Skyroot Aerospace’s 22-meter Vikram-1 reached a confirmed 450-kilometer orbit on its debut, making it India’s first fully commercial satellite launcher and beating the poor first-flight odds of new rockets. Three solid stages and a liquid fourth stage delivered two CubeSats plus hosted payloads; a close third-stage separation was the only visible anomaly. The vehicle can place 350 kilograms in low Earth orbit and was developed with ISRO facilities and support. HN readers praise the eight-year, $160 million effort while probing cost competitiveness, government-derived expertise, and India’s broader private-space pipeline.
