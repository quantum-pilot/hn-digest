# SpaceX stock erases all its gains and slides below IPO price in intraday trading

- Score: 286 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48933344) | Link: https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-07-16/spacex-stock-erases-gains-slides-below-ipo-price-in-intraday-trading

### TL;DR

SpaceX briefly fell below its $135 IPO price, then closed at $135.27, erasing a post-listing surge above 50%. Its record offering raised $86 billion at a $2.2 trillion valuation, and the company entered the Nasdaq-100 after a rule change shortened eligibility. Investors are weighing AI spending, debt, the xAI merger, a planned $60 billion Cursor acquisition, and employee-share unlocks. HN largely viewed the valuation as detached from fundamentals, though others noted early IPO reversals are common and a return near issue price can indicate reasonable underwriting rather than fraud.

- Identity shift split readers → xAI made AI central despite lacking a frontier model — counterpoint: compute infrastructure could still be viable at lower margins.
