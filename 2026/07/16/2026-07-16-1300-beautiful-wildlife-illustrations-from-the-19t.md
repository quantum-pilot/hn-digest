# 1,300 Beautiful Wildlife Illustrations from the 19th Century Now Restored

- Score: 237 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48929945) | Link: https://www.openculture.com/2026/07/explore-1300-beautiful-wildlife-illustrations-from-the-19th-century.html

### TL;DR

Designer Nicholas Rougeux has digitized and restored the complete *Naturalist’s Library*, a Victorian series of more than 40 affordable natural-history volumes containing over 1,300 color wildlife plates. The free website recreates the collection online; a $295.11 large-format book and themed posters provide physical editions. Rougeux says AI helped discover the project, locate sources, fill visual gaps, and explore cover concepts. HN discussion centered on authenticity: skeptics disliked AI-assisted restoration, while others said its role was largely stitching constrained two-page scans and repairing binding seams, with original scans still available.
