# Governments, companies, nonprofits should invest in free, open source AI [pdf]

- Score: 288 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48927095) | Link: https://www.siegelendowment.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/fortune-david-siegel-open-source-ai.pdf

### TL;DR

David Siegel argues AI is becoming foundational knowledge and should retain the inspectability, modification, and shared learning that made open software indispensable. Open weights are insufficient: without training code and data, users receive runnable parameters but cannot understand their provenance or reproduce them. He accepts that released capabilities create risk, yet says secrecy neither guarantees safety nor prevents concentrated control. Rather than matching frontier scale, he proposes public compute, university and nonprofit support, corporate philanthropy, and open-by-default rules for publicly financed AI so credible nonproprietary alternatives survive.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** True openness is a reproducibility spectrum spanning weights, inference code, training recipes, data provenance, evaluation, and meaningful modification rights.
- **Impact:** Public investment can preserve scientific optionality without duplicating frontier spending, especially through efficient models and shared compute infrastructure.
- **Watch next:** Tie funding to reproducible artifacts, resource-bounded evaluations, safety testing, durable licenses, independent governance, and long-term maintenance.
