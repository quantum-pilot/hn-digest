# The LLM Critics Are Right. I Use LLMs Anyway

- Score: 180 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48933310) | Link: https://www.theocharis.dev/blog/llm-critics-are-right-i-use-llms-anyway/

### TL;DR

The author accepts LLM critiques—copyright concerns, vendor dependence, ideological bias, open-source spam, weakened junior development, and fluent low-quality output—yet uses them heavily because they amplify existing expertise. His rule is to retain human judgment and credibility: define a concise problem and scope, force agents to ask questions, use fresh critics to attack plans, verify important outputs, and avoid unfamiliar domains without experts or objective checks. HN shared the ambivalence, questioning whether productivity gains survive measurement, daily delegation erodes skills, and reduced friction lets bad ideas consume maintainer attention.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The key is epistemic ownership: who frames the problem, detects failure, and accepts responsibility for the result.
- **Impact:** As generation becomes cheap, scarce attention moves upstream to judgment and downstream to verification, increasing pressure on trusted reviewers.
