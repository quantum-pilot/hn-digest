# Guerrilla London bus ads mock Kylie Jenner’s Meta glasses campaign

- Score: 197 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48930011) | Link: https://hyperallergic.com/guerrilla-london-bus-ads-mock-kylie-jenners-meta-glasses-campaign/

### TL;DR

UK activist group Everyone Hates Elon installed a lenticular spoof near Meta’s London headquarters that flips Kylie Jenner’s smart-glasses campaign into a skeletal image reading “We’re always watching,” invoking the film They Live. The intervention targets camera-enabled glasses whose latest version records three-minute clips, amid concerns about nonconsensual filming, harassment, biometric identification, and lagging privacy law. Meta says an update disables recording when the indicator light is blocked or damaged, but campaigners argue technical safeguards do not resolve consent. The stunt turns wearable marketing into a warning about normalized surveillance.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The campaign succeeds by making bystanders, not buyers, the product’s stakeholders; wearable-camera utility imposes privacy costs on everyone nearby.
- **Impact:** Indicator lights deter abuse but cannot establish consent, constrain downstream recognition, or repair trust once covert-recording expectations spread.
