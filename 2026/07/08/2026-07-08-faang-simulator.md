# FAANG Simulator

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48836778) | Link: https://www.abeyk.com/escape-the-rat-race/

### TL;DR

FAANG Simulator is a satirical career game where each tap advances one calendar quarter in a 22-year-old engineer’s life at fictional “Pear,” earning $190,000. Players can pursue financial independence, turn a side project into a startup exit, climb to L10, or lose through burnout, layoffs, and AI replacement. A daily seeded run gives everyone the same timeline for comparison; free play randomizes it. HN found the caricature painfully recognizable but challenged its assumptions about geography, job security, startup odds, immigration status, ageism, and family costs, while requesting custom starting conditions.

### Comment pulse

- The simulation understates constraint diversity → visa holders face employment clocks, geographically tied workers cannot simply relocate, and families reshape acceptable risk.
- Startup escape looks too available → commenters argued even modest acquisitions are rare, making the founder route closer to fantasy than planning.
- High salary does not eliminate precarity → lower-cost living can extend runway — counterpoint: layoffs, healthcare, retirement, travel, and dependents remain expensive anywhere.
