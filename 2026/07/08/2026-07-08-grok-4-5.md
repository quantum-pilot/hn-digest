# Grok 4.5

- Score: 433 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48835111) | Link: https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5

### TL;DR

SpaceXAI released Grok 4.5 for coding, agents, and knowledge work after training alongside Cursor across tens of thousands of NVIDIA GB300 GPUs. Company-reported results place it near, but usually behind, the strongest models on DeepSWE, Terminal Bench 2.1, and SWE Bench Pro. It claims 80 tokens per second and 15,954 output tokens per SWE Bench Pro task, 4.2 times fewer than Opus 4.8. API pricing starts at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. Availability includes Grok Build, Cursor, and API; EU access is expected mid-July.
