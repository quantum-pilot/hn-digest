# US seeks cheaper hunter-killer drones after Iran destroys $1B worth of Reapers

- Score: 235 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48845514) | Link: https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/07/us-seeks-cheaper-hunter-killer-drones-after-iran-destroys-1b-worth-of-reapers/

### TL;DR

After losing nearly 30 Reapers worth about $1 billion from a fleet reduced to 135, the Pentagon wants cheaper drones that can absorb heavy losses while penetrating layered Iranian defenses. Its solicitation still demands Reaper-like flexibility: payloads up to 2,800 pounds, a 2,300-nautical-mile combat radius or 8,000-nautical-mile one-way reach, and 20 mission-ready aircraft by 2031. That creates the contradiction: attritable economics paired with exquisite range, sensors, weapons, and survivability. HN contrasted Ukraine’s rapid, disposable systems with US testing bureaucracy and requirement inflation, while noting American expeditionary missions need larger platforms.
