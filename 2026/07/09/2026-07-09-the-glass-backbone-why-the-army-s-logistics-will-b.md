# The glass backbone: Why the Army's logistics will break in the next war

- Score: 265 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48845442) | Link: https://mwi.westpoint.edu/the-glass-backbone-why-the-armys-logistics-will-break-in-the-next-war/

### TL;DR

Using Barbarossa and Ukraine, the essay argues that the US Army’s efficient, centralized logistics model, built for uncontested wars, is dangerously fragile against peer forces with drones, persistent sensing, and precision fires. Fuel and ammunition throughput—not platform sophistication—will set operational reach. It calls for dispersed mobile caches, signature discipline, organic air defense, armored and autonomous resupply, fault-heavy training, and institutional status for sustainment. HN agreed logistics determines combat power, citing Fabian strategy, decentralized Ukrainian drone procurement, and industrial replacement capacity; commenters called orbital delivery costly, escalatory, and vulnerable.
