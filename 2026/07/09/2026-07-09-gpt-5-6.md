# GPT-5.6

- Score: 982 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48849066) | Link: https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/

### TL;DR

OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 family pairs flagship Sol with balanced Terra and low-cost Luna, emphasizing more completed work per token alongside benchmark gains. Sol improves coding, browsing, computer use, artifact design, cybersecurity, and scientific workflows; Programmatic Tool Calling lets it filter intermediate data in code, while max increases deliberation and ultra coordinates four agents in parallel. API prices are $5/$30, $2.50/$15, and $1/$6 per million input/output tokens respectively. Safety adds reasoning-based monitoring, stricter cyber controls, and verified access. HN praised task economics but questioned benchmark selection, ARC significance, and shorter-prompt guidance.
