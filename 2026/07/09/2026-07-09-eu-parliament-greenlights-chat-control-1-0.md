# EU Parliament greenlights Chat Control 1.0

- Score: 941 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48843923) | Link: https://www.patrick-breyer.de/en/eu-parliament-greenlights-chat-control-1-0-breyer-our-children-lose-out/

### TL;DR

An EU Parliament motion to reject the interim Chat Control 1.0 rules received 314 votes against the regulation versus 276 in favor, but missed the 361-member absolute majority required at second reading. Voluntary, suspicionless scanning of unencrypted private messages is therefore permitted through 2028; end-to-end encrypted chats remain exempt while permanent rules await September negotiations. The advocacy article argues scanning produces false alarms without proven gains for convictions or rescues. HN focused on the last-session urgency vote, democratic legitimacy, procedural confusion, and fears that Chat Control 2.0 could target encryption.

### Comment pulse

- The outcome felt procedurally inverted → a voting majority opposed the regulation, yet absences counted against the absolute majority needed to reject it.

- Current encryption remains unaffected → concern centers on permanent rules and lobbying against E2EE, while providers may already analyze unencrypted data.
