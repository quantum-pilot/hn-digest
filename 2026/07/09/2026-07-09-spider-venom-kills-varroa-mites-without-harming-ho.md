# Spider venom kills varroa mites without harming honeybees

- Score: 288 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48841259) | Link: https://connectsci.au/news/news-parent/9703/Spider-venom-kills-varroa-mites-without-harming

### TL;DR

Researchers screened 50 mostly spider and scorpion venoms against Varroa destructor; more than 75% killed exposed mites within 24 hours. They isolated Ht1a from a Tasmanian cave spider and Gg1a from a giant Japanese funnel-web spider: externally applied peptides killed mites while honeybees survived, and the compounds are biodegradable. The result is an early candidate, not a hive treatment; testing on infested bees and real colonies remains. HN beekeepers stressed integrated control—screened bottoms, brood breaks, splits, hygienic genetics, and rotating treatments—while describing resistance, toxicity, labor, and viral disease as constraints.
