# Bonnie Tyler has died

- Score: 326 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48843438) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cg5pd9z2487o

### TL;DR

Welsh singer Bonnie Tyler has died aged 75 after emergency intestinal surgery in Portugal. Born Gaynor Hopkins in Skewen, she progressed from working-men’s clubs to a five-decade career defined by her husky voice. “Lost in France” broke through in 1976; Jim Steinman’s “Total Eclipse of the Heart” later topped UK and US charts, making her the only Welsh artist with a US number one. She represented Britain at Eurovision and received an MBE. HN shared affectionate memories, celebrated extended versions and parodies of her hits, and praised her live performances.
