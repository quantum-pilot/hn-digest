# How to hide from killer drones

- Score: 91 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48874357) | Link: https://www.economist.com/science-and-technology/2026/07/08/how-to-hide-from-killer-drones

### TL;DR

The excerpt reports that Russian military trucks in Ukraine are being painted with vivid black-and-white stripes intended to confuse machine-vision targeting on Ukrainian drones, despite making poor camouflage for human observers. It presents the pattern as anti-AI rather than conventional concealment. HN was broadly skeptical: commenters argued dedicated detectors can recognize vehicle shape, motion, and road context, and that conspicuous stripes may help acquisition. They compared the tactic with historical dazzle paint, disputed what that camouflage actually accomplished, and suggested active radio emissions or multi-sensor detection make hiding increasingly difficult.

### Comment pulse

- Stripes may attack the wrong feature → object detectors can use silhouette, movement, and context, potentially making high-contrast vehicles easier to follow.
- Historical analogy is contested → dazzle paint complicated range or heading estimates, not recognition — counterpoint: operators later questioned its practical effect.
- Sensor fusion narrows concealment → active radio systems can reveal positions through obstacles, though transmitting itself creates another detectable signature.
