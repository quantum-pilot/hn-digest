# The vintage beauty of Soviet control rooms (2018)

- Score: 190 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48868996) | Link: https://designyoutrust.com/2018/01/vintage-beauty-soviet-control-rooms/

### TL;DR

A 2018 photo collection celebrates Soviet-era control rooms built before computer displays, emphasizing their dense arrays of physical buttons, indicator lamps, analog dials, schematic panels, and deliberately functional layouts; one identified image shows Chernobyl Reactor 4’s control room. The page offers little historical or technical context beyond the gallery. HN commenters noted that comparable aesthetics appeared in Western nuclear plants, broadcast studios, transport networks, and other pre-digital infrastructure, while arguing that the panels sometimes conveyed system state, causality, and first-failure signals more directly than modern observability dashboards.
