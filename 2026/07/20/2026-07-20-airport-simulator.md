# Airport Simulator

- Score: 672 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48976846) | Link: https://airport.apunen.com/

### TL;DR

Airport Simulator is a minimalist browser game where players draw routes for aircraft, guiding arrivals to color-matched runway thresholds while tracking landings, departures, pace, and session time. HN readers found the loop immediately reminiscent of Flight Control and traded recommendations for related mobile, vintage, open-source, and programmable ATC games. Enjoyment was tempered by unclear landing instructions, paths that become hard to edit in congestion, an obstructive statistics panel, and highly forgiving flight physics. Commenters proposed better controls, runway approaches, animations, collision behavior, and departure clearance.

### Comment pulse

- Simple route-drawing has durable appeal → readers compared it fondly with Flight Control and shared mobile, vintage, open-source, and programmable alternatives.
- Congestion exposes interface friction → overlapping paths steal clicks, the statistics panel blocks airspace, and absent zoom or panning limits recovery.
- Arcade physics undermined the simulation label → instant turns, constant-speed landings, surprise collisions, and automatic departures prompted calls for more control and realism.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Immediate route drawing and short feedback recreate the appeal commenters miss from early mobile games.
- **Impact:** Small interaction flaws compound as traffic grows, turning intended difficulty into accidental difficulty rather than strategic airspace management.
- **Watch next:** Add an onboarding cue, path-selection priority, movable HUD, zoom, controlled departures, and runway physics before expanding content.
