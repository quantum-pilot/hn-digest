- Score: 649 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48973869) | Link: https://xcancel.com/__alpoge__/status/2079028340955197566

### TL;DR

A post attributed to Claude Fable presents an explicit degree-seven polynomial map from C³ to C³ with constant Jacobian determinant −2 that sends three distinct inputs to the same output. Those two readily checkable properties would make the map noninjective and supply a counterexample to the Jacobian Conjecture; the author linked computational checks. HN reacted with astonishment at the compact construction and repeated independent verification attempts. The central issue was attribution: without the complete model transcript, readers could validate the mathematics but not determine Fable’s contribution.
