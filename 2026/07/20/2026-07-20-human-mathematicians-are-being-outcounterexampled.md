# Human mathematicians are being outcounterexampled

- Score: 162 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48983382) | Link: https://xenaproject.wordpress.com/2026/07/20/human-mathematicians-are-being-outcounterexampled/

### TL;DR

Kevin Buzzard reports that AI systems paired with Lean have rapidly found and machine-checked counterexamples to major conjectures: Erdős’s unit-distance problem, Grothendieck’s group-scheme question, and reportedly the century-old Jacobian Conjecture. One Sol project generated 1.2 million Lean lines in three weeks; another counterexample required 1,076. Compilation verifies formal statements and proofs, but not safe code execution, faithful theorem encoding, or human understanding. Commenters welcomed faster error detection and theorem refinement while debating explanatory value, academic power, environmental cost, and unequal access to $200 monthly tools.

### Comment pulse

- Access could reshape academic inequality → $200 tools promise large productivity gains — counterpoint: cost, subsidized pricing, energy use, and company ethics remain concerns.
