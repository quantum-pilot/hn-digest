# The EU is about to sell our most sensitive data to the US for visa-free travel

- Score: 468 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48977711) | Link: https://edri.org/our-work/the-eu-is-about-to-sell-our-most-sensitive-data-to-the-us-for-visa-free-travel/

### TL;DR

EDRi warns that a leaked draft EU–US Enhanced Border Security Partnership would make biometric and risk-data exchange a condition of continued US visa-waiver access. The framework would authorize automated traveller screening and identity verification, followed by bilateral agreements with EU states. EDRi argues the draft exceeds the Council’s mandate, lacks safeguards against political discrimination, and may violate EU requirements for international data transfers. HN’s debate centered less on whether travellers already surrender biometrics than on query scope, non-traveller exposure, abuse, breaches, automated exclusion, and whether convenience justifies expanded access.

### Comment pulse

- Scope is the pivotal unknown → border-crossers already provide biometrics — counterpoint: database access may expose associated records or people who never travel.
- Automation promises easier, intelligence-led screening → counterpoint: centralized access can be hacked, repurposed, or deny whole groups without meaningful human appeal.
- Physical-presence gating offered a concrete safeguard → verify passport validity remotely, but release biometrics only when the document and traveller reach an entry facility.
