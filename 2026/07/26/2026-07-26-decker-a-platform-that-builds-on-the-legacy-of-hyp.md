# Decker, a platform that builds on the legacy of Hypercard and classic macOS

- Score: 188 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49060856) | Link: https://beyondloom.com/decker/

### TL;DR

Decker is an MIT-licensed multimedia sketchpad for interactive documents, games, zines, presentations, notes, and 1-bit art, inspired by HyperCard and classic Mac aesthetics. It runs in browsers and natively, exports self-contained HTML, stores decks as version-control-friendly text, and supports reusable widgets plus the Lua/Q-influenced Lil language and headless Lilt interpreter. Hacker News celebrated its fast path from idea to software and creativity-inducing constraints, while debating whether the nostalgic monochrome interface limits modern relevance; supporters cited 300-plus community projects and a Decker-built Steam release.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **Impact:** Educators, hobbyists, and small teams gain a programmable medium whose artifacts remain inspectable, remixable, local, and hostable anywhere.
