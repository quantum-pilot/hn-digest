# JetZero

- Score: 247 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49054224) | Link: https://www.jetzero.aero

### TL;DR
JetZero is a US aerospace startup developing the Z4, an all‑wing 250‑seat airliner promising up to 50% lower fuel burn than current jets. The company pitches efficiency, reduced climate impact, and a roomier cabin as its core advantages. It has raised about $175M in Series B funding, secured strategic investments or interest from United Airlines, Alaska Airlines, JAL, and Gulf Air, broken ground on a Greensboro manufacturing campus, and is expanding engineering operations in Wichita.

### LLM perspective
- View: If the all‑wing Z4 meets certification and performance targets, it could be the first credible post-tube‑and‑wing airliner.  
- Impact: Airlines with long‑haul networks gain a decarbonization lever without waiting for hydrogen or all‑electric propulsion.  
- Watch next: Flight tests of the all‑wing demonstrator, regulatory engagement with FAA/EASA, and conversion of LOIs into firm aircraft orders.
