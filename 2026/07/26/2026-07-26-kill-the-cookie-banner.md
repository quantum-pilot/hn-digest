# Kill The Cookie Banner

- Score: 772 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49057175) | Link: https://killthecookiebanner.eu/

### TL;DR
- A coalition of EU civil society groups (including EFF and noyb) campaigns to eliminate deceptive cookie banners by backing an EU proposal for browser-level privacy signals. Instead of constant pop-ups, users would set a single tracking preference (accept, refuse, or limit) that websites must honor, like language settings. Tracking companies, led by Google and ad-tech lobbies, are pressuring EU governments and Parliament to block this change, so the campaign urges citizens to contact representatives.

### LLM perspective
- View: Browser-level consent signals align law with user behavior; most people don’t genuinely want pervasive tracking.
- Impact: If adopted and enforced, ad-tech, analytics platforms, and consent-management vendors would lose current “dark pattern” leverage.
- Watch next: Which browsers implement standardized signals by default, and whether EU enforcement bodies penalize sites that ignore them.
