# The relay market powering token resellers and fraud

- Score: 144 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49058993) | Link: https://vectoral.com/blog/token-relay-market

### TL;DR

The article maps a four-layer market that turns bulk accounts, virtual cards, stolen or pooled credentials, and abused free credits into Chinese-language relay APIs selling access to US models at discounts reaching 97.8%. Account pools rotate keys and rate limits; consumer relays use neutral open-source gateways for billing, while buyers seek cheap inference, geo-bypass, or distillation. Ten leading relays reportedly draw 3.6 million monthly visits. Commenters recognize an old arbitrage pattern from ads, tickets, and cloud credits, but dispute whether paid subscription resale is fraud or merely loss-leader abuse.
