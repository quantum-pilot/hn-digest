# The Strongest El Niño Ever

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49060978) | Link: https://www.theclimatebrink.com/p/the-strongest-el-nino-ever

### TL;DR
Forecasts from 14 seasonal climate models (667 ensemble members) now give roughly a 90%+ chance that the 2026–27 El Niño will be the strongest on record. The expected Niño 3.4 peak is about 3.6°C above the detrended average—far beyond the 2015–16 and 1877–78 events—and daily sea surface temperatures are already unprecedented for mid‑July. Because global temperature lags El Niño by 3–5 months, 2027 is very likely to become the hottest year ever, though models have never been tested at such extremes and outcomes remain uncertain.
