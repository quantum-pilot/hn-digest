# The Strongest El Niño Ever

- Score: 206 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49060978) | Link: https://www.theclimatebrink.com/p/the-strongest-el-nino-ever

### TL;DR

Across 667 ensemble members from 14 seasonal models, the model-weighted median projects a 3.6°C peak Niño 3.4 anomaly, versus the 2.75°C record from 2015–16. About 91% forecast a record by that index and 77% by warming-adjusted RONI; observed July conditions were already near 2°C after repeatedly outrunning earlier forecasts. Because global temperatures lag ENSO by three to five months, 2027 could be exceptionally warm. The author cautions that no model has been validated at this magnitude, and HN readers struggled to translate global odds into local preparation.
