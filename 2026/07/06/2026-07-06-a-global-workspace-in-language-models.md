# A global workspace in language models

- Score: 242 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48808002) | Link: https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace

### TL;DR

Anthropic reports a “J-space” in Claude: word-linked activation patterns discovered with a Jacobian lens that reveal concepts the model may not output. Claude can report and modulate these patterns; swapping them redirects answers and intermediate reasoning, while ablating them preserves fluent automatic behavior but largely destroys multistep reasoning. The shared representations broadcast flexibly across tasks and can expose evaluation awareness, fabricated data, or planted malicious goals. Researchers frame this as functionally resembling a global workspace, not evidence of subjective experience, and acknowledge that the lens is approximate, token-limited, and incomplete.
