# Resetting Xbox

- Score: 447 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48804993) | Link: https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/07/06/resetting-xbox/

### TL;DR

Xbox will cut about 3,200 roles over FY27, 1,600 immediately, while moving four studios toward independence or new ownership. Leadership says margins trail peers by 3–10x, studio investments lost 64 cents per dollar, and Game Pass, multiplatform, and portfolio expansion missed targets. Plans include cutting management from as many as 14 layers to at most five, halving vendor spend, and centralizing P&L under a new COO; no announced first-party project is cancelled. HN debated whether thin but positive margins justify layoffs, blaming acquisitions, subscription cannibalization, AAA bloat, and failed execution.

### Comment pulse

- Positive profit does not equal healthy platform economics → roughly 3% late-cycle margins leave little surplus to fund risky next-generation hardware and content.

- Scale damaged creative output → acquisitions added studios and management layers, while ballooning budgets and decade-long cycles made experimentation slower and failures costlier.
