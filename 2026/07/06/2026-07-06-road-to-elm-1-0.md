# Road to Elm 1.0

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48803364) | Link: https://elm-lang.org/news/faster-builds

### TL;DR

Elm 0.19.2 begins a planned sequence of small, non-breaking releases toward 1.0, incorporating compiler lessons from Evan Czaplicki’s Acadia project. Reduced parser allocation cuts garbage-collection copying 20%, peak memory 10%, and total compile time 7% on an 850,000-line test; one 351-module project improved from 4.981 to 2.595 seconds. Incremental builds stayed below 350 ms. Future releases may add equatable and hashable types. HN welcomed renewed movement and Elm’s enduring elegance, but treated the seven-year release gap, single-maintainer governance, shrinking community, forks, and restricted JavaScript interoperability as unresolved adoption risks.
