# CoMaps – FOSS Offline Maps

- Score: 272 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48808928) | Link: https://www.comaps.app/

### TL;DR

CoMaps is a free, community-run navigation app offering offline maps, waypoint search, and routing for driving, cycling, and hiking using GPS alone. It uses OpenStreetMap data, collects no identifying or tracking information, claims efficient battery use, and has undergone an Exodus privacy audit. Development occurs openly on Codeberg; the project descends from Organic Maps and Maps.Me. Users praised offline operation and map updates but reported weak OSM search relevance and routing estimates that lack live-traffic precision. The fork reflects concerns about Organic Maps governance, proprietary components, and contributor influence.

### Comment pulse

- Offline reliability earned praise → selected regions update regularly and work without cellular data, making CoMaps useful for walking and travel.
- Search remained the largest blocker → nearby exact-name places can be missed — counterpoint: some users pair CoMaps with HERE or Waze.
- Fork politics mattered → commenters cited centralized financial and partnership decisions plus proprietary components in Organic Maps as reasons for CoMaps.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** CoMaps optimizes for offline privacy and community control, accepting weaker discovery and traffic intelligence than centralized services.
- **Impact:** It offers resilient navigation where connectivity or trust is limited, while depending heavily on local OSM completeness.
- **Watch next:** Search ranking, live-traffic options, contributor governance, update cadence, desktop support, and transparent privacy-audit findings.
