# Qwen-Image-3.0: Rich Content, Authentic Details, Deep Knowledge

- Score: 537 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48989701) | Link: https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen-image-3.0

### TL;DR

Qwen positions Image 3.0 as a productivity-focused generator built around rich content, authentic detail, and deep knowledge. It accepts prompts up to 4.5k tokens, claims legible 10-pixel text, supports 12 languages and more than 100 styles, and can produce dense multi-panel layouts, nested interfaces, realistic textures, or edits in one pass. The launch showcases academic pages, infographics, restoration, and UI simulation. HN readers were impressed but questioned demo reproducibility, broken Arabic, aesthetic tinting, dubious SEO metadata, and deceptive commercial imagery.
