# ICE to Pay Thomson Reuters $125M to Find Voter Fraud

- Score: 222 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48993448) | Link: https://www.404media.co/ice-to-pay-thomson-reuters-125-million-to-find-voter-fraud/

### TL;DR

Procurement records reviewed by 404 Media say DHS plans to pay Thomson Reuters $125 million for database access supporting ICE investigations into what the document calls voter fraud and immigration fraud. The data reportedly includes names, addresses, Social Security numbers, ethnicity, social posts, and geolocation, with continuous monitoring of millions of people and entities. HN commenters questioned ICE’s authority, the evidence base, and government purchases that bypass warrant expectations. A minority argued an impartial review could settle fraud claims, while others feared false matches or politically useful allegations.

### Comment pulse

- Mission creep alarmed readers → election policing appears far outside ICE’s immigration mandate, raising fears of federal enforcement influencing voter eligibility or public narratives.
- Buying data may sidestep constitutional friction → commenters cited the third-party doctrine, arguing commercial availability lets agencies obtain sensitive records without individualized warrants.
- Independent review could settle claims → one commenter trusted Thomson Reuters’ impartiality — counterpoint: others feared political pressure would inflate routine registration errors.
