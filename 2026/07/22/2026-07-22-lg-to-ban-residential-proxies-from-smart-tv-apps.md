# LG to ban residential proxies from smart TV apps

- Score: 392 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49000864) | Link: https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/07/lg-to-ban-residential-proxies-from-smart-tv-apps/

### TL;DR

Spur found residential-proxy SDKs in over 42% of LG webOS apps and more than a quarter of Samsung Tizen apps, letting third parties route traffic through TVs, sometimes indefinitely. LG says developers must remove the feature or face suspension and promises stronger app review; Bright Data argues users knowingly opt in and customers are vetted. Commenters largely viewed consent as inadequate on shared, opaque devices, blamed LG’s governance, and questioned whether existing installations will actually stop.

### Comment pulse

- Residential proxies enable harder-to-filter abuse → blocking them risks legitimate customers — counterpoint: ISP traffic monitoring raises privacy, authority, and incentive concerns.
- LG’s broader software reputation magnified distrust → users cited forced accounts, degraded interfaces, intrusive AI, and monitor-delivered adware.
- Air-gapping remains the preferred defense → external streaming boxes restore control, though average households still face comparable networked-device risks.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Platform-enforced networking permissions are more durable than developer promises or one-time opt-ins.
- **Impact:** App-store operators may need continuous disclosure, revocation, and per-domain egress controls for appliances shared across households.
- **Watch next:** LG’s remediation details: installed-app shutdowns, user notifications, SDK detection coverage, and equivalent enforcement across competing TV stores.
