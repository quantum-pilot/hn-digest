# John C. Dvorak has died

- Score: 470 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49012070) | Link: https://twitter.com/na_announce/status/2079952538040672302

### TL;DR

John C. Dvorak, technology columnist and No Agenda host, has died at 74, according to the show’s announcement. Hacker News readers remembered decades of columns in PC Magazine, InfoWorld, and MacUser, plus appearances on TechTV, Cranky Geeks, and TWiT. Tributes portrayed him as a funny, provocative curmudgeon whose confident predictions could miss but whose personality enlivened technology coverage. Many described him as a formative influence and linked his passing to the loss of computing’s more personal, experimental media era.

### Comment pulse

- His voice mattered beyond accuracy → readers valued bold takes, humor, and sparring with Leo Laporte even when predictions failed.
- Print created unusually durable relationships → readers recalled reading, transcribing, and religiously following columns decades before blogs and social media.
- Early computing media feels irreplaceable → commenters miss its identifiable personalities and playful immaturity while accepting that industry maturation changed the culture.
