# Terrence Tao's ChatGPT Conversation about the Jacobian Conjecture Counterexample

- Score: 545 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49010345) | Link: https://chatgpt.com/share/6a5fdc7a-d6f8-83e8-bbea-8deb42cfed56

### TL;DR

The recovered exchange shows Tao steering ChatGPT through an algebraic comparison rather than asking for a one-shot proof. The model claims an explicitly parameterized variety is polynomially isomorphic to C³, composes that parameterization with a four-coordinate map, drops an identically constant coordinate, and obtains a three-variable polynomial map G with Jacobian determinant −1. It then matches G to the announced counterexample via linear input substitution, output reversal, and rescaling. Discussion treated it less as autonomous discovery than a demonstration of domain expertise steering computation.
