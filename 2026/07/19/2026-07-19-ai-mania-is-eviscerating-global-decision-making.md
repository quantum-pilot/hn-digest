# AI Mania Is Eviscerating Global Decision-Making

- Score: 379 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48964185) | Link: https://ludic.mataroa.blog/blog/ai-mania-is-eviscerating-global-decision-making/#fnref:3

### TL;DR

Drawing on consulting and sales conversations, the essay argues large organizations have replaced evidence with an AI coordination game: executives overstate gains to protect status and contracts, employees fake usage to satisfy quotas, flashy demos override caveats, and ordinary projects receive AI labels to win funding. The author claims every observed project failed and recommends private dissent, anonymous polling, frontline evidence, and tactical self-preservation. HN recognized mandate dysfunction but challenged the 0% claim as selection bias, distinguishing failed corporate programs from useful individual tools.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The central failure is incentive design: measuring tool usage converts uncertain technology into performative compliance and suppresses corrective information.
- **Impact:** Boards lose accurate project signals; frontline staff inherit review toil, reputational exposure, and pressure to conceal professional judgment.
- **Watch next:** Require adoption, outcome, error, downstream-cost, and counterfactual baselines; compare mandated programs with voluntary task-level use.
