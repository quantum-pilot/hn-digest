# Qwen 3.8

- Score: 750 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48966120) | Link: https://twitter.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2078759124914098291

### TL;DR

Alibaba announced Qwen3.8, a 2.4-trillion-parameter model it says will become open-weight soon and compete near the frontier, behind only Fable 5 by its own characterization. A Max Preview is already available through Alibaba’s Token Plan, Qoder, and QoderWork, but the announcement supplies no release date, architecture details, license, benchmarks, or hardware requirements. HN read the timing as part of intensifying Chinese model competition and the World AI Conference. Excitement centered less on the enormous flagship than on hoped-for smaller variants suitable for private local inference.
