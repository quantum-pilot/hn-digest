# Situational Awareness down 67% in July in AI stock rout

- Score: 140 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49122994) | Link: https://www.wsj.com/finance/investing/situational-awareness-down-67-in-july-in-ai-stock-rout-cd19901f

### TL;DR

Situational Awareness fell about 67% in July after leveraged AI-stock bets triggered margin calls, prompting it to sell the bulk of its stockholdings to Citadel. It agreed to sell $3.5 billion of Anthropic shares, then withdrew, and says leverage is now gone. Despite the rout, the fund reports an 80% year-to-date gain after reaching 270% through May. Commenters split between calling this an inexperienced manager’s leverage blowup and emphasizing a liquidity crisis rather than negative returns, while questioning private-asset marks and investor basis.

### Comment pulse
