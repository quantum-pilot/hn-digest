# qm

- Score: 394 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49126604) | Link: https://github.com/yc-software/qm

### TL;DR

An open-source, self-hosted multiplayer agent platform gives each employee and shared room isolated memory, files, keychain access, permissions, automations, apps, and a durable sandbox, while Slack and web share identity and configuration. Its generic core supports Pi, OpenCode, Codex, and Claude Code through common interfaces, with Postgres persistence and organization-controlled security postures. HN commenters praised its organizational architecture but debated whether a crowded market, unclear product messaging, and increasingly easy custom builds weaken its differentiation.

### Comment pulse

- Practitioners favor scope-first design → per-person workspaces preserve autonomy, while shared rooms let teams collaborate without collapsing permissions or context.
- New AI interfaces remain hard to discover → emphasizing familiar capabilities can conceal the novel workflow users need explained.
