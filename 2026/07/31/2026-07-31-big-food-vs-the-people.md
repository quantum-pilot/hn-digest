# Big Food vs. the People

- Score: 177 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49124858) | Link: https://www.lighthousereports.com/investigation/big-food-vs-the-people/

- TL;DR  
An investigation argues multinational food and beverage companies systematically sue governments to block sugar taxes, warning labels, and child-targeted advertising rules, with Mexico as a key battleground. Commenters split: some dismiss the piece as thinly sourced NGO propaganda that hides the firms’ legal arguments; others emphasize Mexico’s unusually tough labeling, ad bans, and broader anti-obesity push, likening Big Food’s tactics to tobacco. Side debates cover class-action incentives and politicians’ reliance on, or rejection of, scientific expertise.
