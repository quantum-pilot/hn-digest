# The Religion of Speed

- Score: 275 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49117284) | Link: https://graybeard.ing/the-religion-of-speed/

### TL;DR
Speed in engineering and startups often becomes a “religion”: leaders demand visible motion and urgency, even when it produces rework, burnout, and failures. Commenters argue that real speed comes from understanding the work, planning, and making deep, measured improvements—“slow is smooth, smooth is fast.” Yet speed is also a genuine feature: customers, founders, and investors face real time pressure. The core tension is balancing urgency with quality so you move fast without thrashing or breaking trust.
