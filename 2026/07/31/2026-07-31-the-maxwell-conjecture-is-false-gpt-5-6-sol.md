### TL;DR

A paper gives a five-point-charge configuration whose electrostatic potential has at least 24 nondegenerate critical points, exceeding the conjectured maximum of (n−1)², which is 16 for five charges. That counterexample falsifies the bound rather than proving a replacement. HN discussion treated the reported AI role as both meaningful out-of-domain progress and potentially overhyped because the problem may be niche.
