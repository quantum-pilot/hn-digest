# The Maxwell Conjecture Is False (GPT 5.6 Sol)

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49121868) | Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27197

### TL;DR
An AI system (dubbed GPT 5.6) has produced a counterexample showing the Maxwell Conjecture—modern formalization of an 1873 claim by James Clerk Maxwell—is false. Mathematicians reportedly hand‑checked the argument, making it another credible AI-assisted result in a niche but nontrivial area. Hacker News discussion centers on how such “small” wins still accelerate harder work, what they imply for math and physics careers, messy machine proofs versus human elegance, and the growing need for independent formal verification.
