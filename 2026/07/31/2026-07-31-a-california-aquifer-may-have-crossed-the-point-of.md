# A California aquifer may have crossed the point of no return

- Score: 164 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49118663) | Link: https://www.science.org/content/article/california-aquifer-may-have-crossed-point-no-return

## TL;DR
California’s major aquifers, especially in the Central Valley, have been pumped so hard that underground clay layers are collapsing, causing land subsidence and permanently reducing storage capacity. Even if surface water returns, much of the “lost” space can never be refilled; the only partial fix is costly artificial recharge, which can also alter fault stresses and potentially trigger earthquakes. Hacker News discussion centers on how agriculture, water rights, and short‑term profit have driven this, and who should bear the costs.
