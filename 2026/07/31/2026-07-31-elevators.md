# Elevators

### TL;DR
Elevator control is a classic real‑world scheduling problem: simple-looking, but shaped heavily by traffic patterns and human expectations. Commenters recall simulating algorithms like SCAN/LOOK in school and via games, noting how disks and elevators share similar “elevator algorithms.” Discussion centers on “destination dispatch” systems: some report big improvements in modern offices and cruise ships where people move in batched groups, while others argue it’s worse for hotels or mixed flows. UX expectations (toggleable buttons, predictable motion) and game design further highlight how people mentally model elevators.
