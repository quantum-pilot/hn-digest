# The bond market isn’t buying what Fed Chair Warsh is selling

- Score: 136 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49118696) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/bond-market-isnt-buying-what-fed-chair-warsh-is-selling-2026-07-30/

### TL;DR
Reuters frames the story as the bond market rejecting new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh’s attempt to jawbone yields lower. HN commenters argue that 10-year Treasury yields, which anchor US mortgage rates, are rising because of structural forces: large US deficits, persistent inflation, geopolitical shocks, tariffs, and energy constraints. Debate centers on how much is Warsh’s weaker communication/credibility relative to Powell or Yellen versus how much is simply fiscal and political reality that no Fed chair can talk away.
