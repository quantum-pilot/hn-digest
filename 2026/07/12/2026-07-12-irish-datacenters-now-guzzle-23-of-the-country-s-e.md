# Irish datacenters now guzzle 23% of the country's electricity

- Score: 190 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48884322) | Link: https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2026/07/11/irish-datacenters-now-guzzle-23-of-the-countrys-electricity/5270013

### TL;DR

Irish data centers consumed 7,663 GWh in 2025, up 10% and equal to 23% of metered electricity—more than urban households’ 18% and over twice rural households’ 9%. Their share has risen from 5% in 2015 despite Dublin-area grid-connection restrictions covering most of 2025. After lifting the moratorium in December, Ireland requires projects above 10 MW to provide matching generation or batteries and return power when needed. The debate centers less on whether data centers create value than who receives it and pays for grid, price, and environmental costs.
