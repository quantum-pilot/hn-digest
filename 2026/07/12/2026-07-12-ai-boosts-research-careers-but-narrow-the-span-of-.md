# AI boosts research careers but narrow the span of ideas explored: study

- Score: 135 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48881043) | Link: https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-science-research-flattens-discovery

### TL;DR

An analysis of 41.3 million papers across six natural-science fields associates AI use with three times more publications, nearly five times more citations, and earlier team leadership. Yet AI-assisted work occupies a narrower topic space, concentrates on popular data-rich problems, and draws weaker follow-on engagement, suggesting tools accelerate tractable research while collective exploration contracts. The authors blame academic incentives more than model architecture. HN largely agreed that AI amplifies publication-count and citation games, while raising selection-bias concerns and countering that automating writing or paperwork could give discovery-focused scientists more time.
