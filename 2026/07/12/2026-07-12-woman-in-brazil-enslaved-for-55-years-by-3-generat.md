# Woman in Brazil enslaved for 55 years by 3 generations of the same family

- Score: 148 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48880209) | Link: https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-07-10/woman-rescued-in-brazil-after-being-enslaved-for-55-years-by-three-generations-of-the-same-family.html

### TL;DR

Brazilian authorities rescued a 62-year-old domestic worker who entered a household at seven and allegedly spent 55 years serving three generations without wages, schooling, money, or outside relationships. Prosecutors suspect the family also appropriated her welfare payments; it denies enslavement and cites care and affection. An agreement provides a $30,000 apartment plus $10,000 without barring further claims. She temporarily remains with the family while officials build support—a controversial response to extreme dependency. HN debated prevalence, structural racism, distinctions from paid live-in work, and whether compensation and punishment were adequate.
