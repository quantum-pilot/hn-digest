# Global review confirms mRNA vaccines are safe, effective and full of promise 

- Score: 328 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48754963) | Link: https://news.ubc.ca/2026/06/mrna-vaccines-are-safe-effective-and-full-of-promise/

### TL;DR

A UBC-led Lancet review synthesizes laboratory, trial, and real-world evidence after billions of mRNA vaccine doses. It finds strong protection against severe COVID-19, hospitalization, and death across children, pregnant people, and immunocompromised groups; boosters and updated formulations sustain protection. Serious adverse events including myocarditis, more frequent in young males, are rare and outweighed by benefits. HN wanted subgroup-specific absolute risks instead of broad labels, distinguished mRNA vaccines from adenovirus-linked clotting, and emphasized that manufacturing scale and clinical validation—not sequence design alone—enabled deployment.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Platform-level confidence is justified, while individual decisions still require product, dose, age, and condition-specific risk estimates.
- **Impact:** The established manufacturing and monitoring base lowers barriers for influenza, RSV, personalized cancer vaccines, and other RNA therapies.
