# Exapunks (2018)

- Score: 208 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48765663) | Link: https://www.zachtronics.com/exapunks/

### TL;DR

EXAPUNKS is a 1997-styled programming puzzle game in which a sick former hacker writes mobile execution agents to traverse networks, copy or destroy files, fight other agents, and hack targets ranging from banks to their own body. Its world is taught through underground zines and extended by JavaScript-authored custom networks and side games. HN readers praised it and Shenzhen I/O for capturing programming’s iterative pleasure: make a solution work before optimizing it, compare designs with friends, and use a simplified instruction set to build confidence with low-level computing.
