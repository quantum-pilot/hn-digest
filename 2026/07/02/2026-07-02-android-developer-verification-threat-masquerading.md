# Android Developer Verification: Threat masquerading as protection

- Score: 1564 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48755965) | Link: https://f-droid.org/2026/07/01/adv-malware.html

### TL;DR

F-Droid argues Google’s Android Developer Verifier, delivered through Play Protect to Android 8+ devices, will let Google block software from unregistered developers. Registration requires fees, identity documents, and app signing-key identifiers, while terms leave malware undefined; initial enforcement is scheduled for September 30 in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand, with broader rollout from 2027. The post calls this ineffective against first-time malware and dangerous centralized gatekeeping. HN shared ownership concerns but criticized the virus rhetoric, while noting GrapheneOS and mobile Linux alternatives face device, banking-app, compatibility, and security tradeoffs.
