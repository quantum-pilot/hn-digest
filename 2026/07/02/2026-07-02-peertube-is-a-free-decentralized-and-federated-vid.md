# PeerTube is a free, decentralized and federated video platform

- Score: 489 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48759634) | Link: https://github.com/Chocobozzz/PeerTube

### TL;DR

PeerTube is AGPL video-hosting software for a federation of community-run, ad-free instances. It supports uploads, livestreams, ActivityPub/RSS follows, embeddable players, customization, and WebRTC sharing plus inter-instance caching to spread delivery load. HN agreed the technology works, especially for project tutorials, institutions, conferences, and externally discovered niche videos. The dispute was strategic: professional creators need reliable monetization and mass audiences, while supporters see independence, ownership, and smaller communities as the point. Discovery, hosting cost, and the creator-viewer network effect remain its toughest barriers.

### Comment pulse

- Monetization limits professional adoption → a 20-minute video can require 40 skilled hours; occasional donations cannot sustain staff-intensive channels.
- PeerTube need not replace YouTube → institutions, FOSS projects, tutorials, and tiny audiences benefit from controlled hosting and external discovery.
- Network effects dominate adoption → mainstream topics lack viewers and creators — counterpoint: smaller, focused communities can be a feature, not a failure.
