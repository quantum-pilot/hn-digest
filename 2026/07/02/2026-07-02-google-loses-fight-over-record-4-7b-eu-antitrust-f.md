# Google loses fight over record $4.7B EU antitrust fine

- Score: 283 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48758309) | Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/alphabet-google-android-eu-antitrust-fine-4-1-billion-euro-appeal.html

### TL;DR

Europe’s top court upheld Google’s €4.1 billion ($4.67 billion) fine, ending appeals over 2018 findings that Android’s dominance enabled preferential pre-installation deals for Google apps. The penalty was reduced from €4.34 billion in 2022, and Google says it already changed agreements and preserves Android’s openness. HN focused less on the verdict than its eight-year lag: platform bottlenecks evolve faster than litigation, potentially weakening deterrence. Commenters also challenged “alleged” after a final ruling and disputed whether a one-time fine meaningfully changes conduct.
