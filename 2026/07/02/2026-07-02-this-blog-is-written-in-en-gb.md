# This blog is written in en-GB

- Score: 340 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48760289) | Link: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/07/this-blog-is-written-in-en-gb/

### TL;DR

The author rejects a request to replace British cultural references with globally familiar ones, arguing that `lang=en-GB` identifies not merely spelling but a lived culture, voice, and interpretive context. Readers can infer unfamiliar terms just as Britons have long inferred Twinkies and American media references; localization should not erase difference. HN strongly favored linguistic variety and questioned the entitlement behind the request, while expatriates described how constant self-editing flattens speech. Others noted real ambiguity from false friends and that locale settings also affect dates, weeks, paper, units, and time.
