# Virginia bans sale of geolocation data

- Score: 331 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48767347) | Link: https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/virginia-bans-sale-of-geolocation-data

### TL;DR

Virginia’s S.B. 338 amends the VCDPA to prohibit selling geolocation data beginning July 1, 2026. Its reach is narrower than Maryland’s and Oregon’s bans because Virginia defines a sale only as a controller exchanging personal data with a third party for money, not other valuable consideration. HN welcomed limits after examples involving reproductive-health visits and insurance profiling, but questioned enforcement across state lines, consent standards, and loopholes. Commenters favored targeting data brokers and undisclosed third-party transfers while preserving clearly disclosed first-party uses such as map personalization.
