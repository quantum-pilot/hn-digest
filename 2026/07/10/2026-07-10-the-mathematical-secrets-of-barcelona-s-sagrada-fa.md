# The mathematical secrets of Barcelona's Sagrada Familia

- Score: 121 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48772528) | Link: https://mappingignorance.org/2026/06/30/sagrada-familia/

### TL;DR

The Sagrada Família’s dimensions reportedly follow a 7.5-metre module and ratios among divisors of 12: its 90-metre length, 60-metre width, nave and vault heights, and many tower dimensions are module multiples. Geometry also serves structure and light through catenary arches, branching double-helix columns, ruled hyperboloid skylights, and polyhedral tower ornaments. Religious symbolism layers on 12-pointed forms, a seven-sided altar canopy, and a Passion-façade magic square summing to 33. HN admired Gaudí’s hanging-chain physical models but disputed whether the article conflates engineering mathematics, common proportional design, and numerology.
