# Punk, or why I don't stream anymore

- Score: 135 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48859671) | Link: https://geohot.github.io//blog/jekyll/update/2026/05/03/punk-or-why-i-dont-stream.html

### TL;DR

The essay argues algorithmic streams produce felt completion without real-world action, turn identity into marketing, and convert subcultures into saleable spectacle. AI deepens this by making prompting feel like agency while corporate systems increasingly predict and shape users’ inner lives. The author sees no easy return to an older internet because digital dependence displaced it. HN challenged the pessimism and messenger: hacker mythology can itself become performance, bland dating profiles may protect against scraping, and independent blogs, hobby groups, libraries, boredom, and offline activity remain accessible through deliberate withdrawal.
