# Mayor Mamdani announces "Click-to-Cancel" rules

- Score: 178 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48864158) | Link: https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/07/mayor-mamdani-announces-landmark--click-to-cancel--consumer-prot

### TL;DR

New York City’s final Click-to-Cancel rule takes effect October 1, 2026, requiring automatic-renewal and continuous-service subscriptions to disclose terms clearly and offer cancellation as straightforward as enrollment. Violators face consumer restitution and civil penalties starting at $525 per violation; projected annual savings range from $21.5 million to $162.5 million. A companion proposal would require advertised prices to include every mandatory charge, prohibit misleading fee descriptions, and document what service or processing charges cover. That all-in-pricing rule entered public comment ahead of an August hearing. HN supplied no substantive discussion.
