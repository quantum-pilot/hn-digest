# GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra produces proof of the Cycle Double Cover Conjecture [pdf]

### TL;DR

An OpenAI-authored note claims every finite bridgeless undirected graph has a cycle double cover. It reduces to loopless cubic graphs, starts from a nowhere-zero F₂³ flow, converts edge labels into two-element sets whose local incidences occur zero or twice, and resolves endpoint compatibility through linear algebra and duality. The note attributes the proof to GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra, with Codex assisting the write-up; the packet contains no independent verification. HN treated the construction as potentially major while probing extensive prompt scaffolding, survivorship bias, and whether recombining known tools constitutes autonomous discovery.
