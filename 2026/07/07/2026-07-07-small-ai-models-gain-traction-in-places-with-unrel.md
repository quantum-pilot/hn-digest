# Small AI Models Gain Traction In places with unreliable networks

- Score: 261 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48812055) | Link: https://spectrum.ieee.org/small-language-models-ai-pharmaceuticals

### TL;DR

Edge-deployed “small AI” is gaining value where cloud latency, connectivity, power, and cost make frontier models impractical. RxAll moved counterfeit-drug identification from a distant server to an Android phone after one scan took five minutes; other examples detect crop disease, mosquitoes, ants, and cardiac patterns directly on drones or microcontrollers. These systems use compact task-specific models created through pruning, distillation, quantization, or training from scratch, sometimes consuming a few watts. Better hardware and open-weight models broaden deployment, but data updates, reliable power, supply chains, education, and larger-model research remain necessary.
