# 30papers.com – Ilya's 30 essential ML papers, in a beginner friendly format

- Score: 313 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48819608) | Link: https://30papers.com/

### TL;DR

30papers.com turns a rumored reading list that Ilya Sutskever allegedly gave John Carmack into a beginner-oriented catalog of foundational machine-learning papers. Only 27 entries are currently known, spanning convolutional and recurrent networks, attention and Transformers, memory and relational models, scaling, distributed training, compression, complexity, and machine intelligence. Each entry offers a short plain-language description rather than a full tutorial or personal annotation. The student author calls it a work in progress and added background and motion toggles after usability complaints. The list’s provenance and canonical ordering remain unverified.
