# Europe's company websites are mostly served by US vendors

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48816612) | Link: https://ciphercue.com/blog/european-web-hosting-vendor-share-2026

### TL;DR

A study mapped apex and www DNS records for 19,450 European companies to the announcing network operator. US-headquartered vendors fronted 67.5% of UK sites, 53.6% in the Netherlands, 44–49% in France, Italy, and Spain, but only 31% in Germany and 18.8% in Poland. Cloudflare ranked first everywhere. This measures the public-facing CDN or proxy, not origin hosting, data location, or control arrangements. HN therefore treated it as a concentration signal, not sovereignty proof: alternative API-origin research favored OVH and Hetzner, and commenters distinguished easily switched CDNs from locked-in cloud services.

### Comment pulse

- The metric captures perimeter dependence → Cloudflare attribution says nothing about origin location, internal APIs, subprocessors, regional controls, or application portability.
- US incorporation is not physical geography → European data centers and entities complicate exposure — counterpoint: conflicting US authority can still reach the parent company.
- Concentration does not equal lock-in → CDN migration may be mostly DNS and configuration — counterpoint: managed cloud ecosystems are much harder to unwind.
