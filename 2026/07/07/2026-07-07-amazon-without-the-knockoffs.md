# Amazon without the knockoffs

- Score: 295 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48818963) | Link: https://knockoff.shopping/

### TL;DR

Knockoff is a free browser extension that flags, dims, or hides Amazon listings from trademark-squat pseudo-brands and can identify Etsy “handmade” items matching mass-market catalogs. It supports Chrome, Safari, Firefox, all 23 Amazon marketplaces, and optional Etsy filtering without accounts. Its promise is to leave sellers with reputations to protect. HN challenged implementation and premise: it uses AmazonBrandFilter’s community-maintained list, prompting complaints about absent upstream contributions and a more restrictive license. Readers also argued that unfamiliar brands may offer value and that brand recognition is an imperfect proxy for quality.

### Comment pulse

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The extension is a reputation filter, not a reliable counterfeit detector; its name promises more certainty than its method.
- **Impact:** It can reduce search noise, but false positives may hide competitive products and reinforce incumbent brands.
- **Watch next:** Upstream contributions, license compatibility, transparent classification criteria, appeals, privacy disclosures, and evidence that Etsy matching is accurate.
