# Dua Lipa opens library for banned and censored books in Portugal

- Score: 276 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48817017) | Link: https://www.euronews.com/culture/2026/06/29/dua-lipa-opens-library-for-banned-and-censored-books-in-portugal

### TL;DR

Dua Lipa and Porto bookstore Livraria Lello have opened the permanent Manifesto Library, a collection of nearly 100 works challenged, restricted, or censored for confronting power, exclusion, race, sexuality, and dominant narratives. Organized around power, control, voice, and memory, it includes books by Margaret Atwood, Reginald Dwayne Betts, Salman Rushdie, and Olga Tokarczuk, extending Lipa’s Service95 reading advocacy. HN largely debated framing: whether “banned” misleadingly equates school-library removals with state prohibition, and whether the venue is truly a library or an exhibition inside a tourist-oriented bookstore.
