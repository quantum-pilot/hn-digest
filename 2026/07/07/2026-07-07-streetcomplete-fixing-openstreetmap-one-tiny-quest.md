# StreetComplete: Fixing OpenStreetMap, one tiny quest at a time

- Score: 666 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48816883) | Link: https://streetcomplete.app/

### TL;DR

StreetComplete turns nearby OpenStreetMap gaps into location-based quests: visit a place, answer a concrete question, and submit the result under your OSM account without learning a full editor. HN users praised its beginner-friendly, game-like UX and described mapping sidewalks, crossings, benches, businesses, and overlooked trails. The main friction is OSM’s flexible schema: contributors may represent sidewalks or crossings several valid ways and fear duplicating data. Replies advised prioritizing accurate observations, following local conventions, consulting the wiki, or leaving geotagged notes and photos for experienced mappers; geometry remains easier on desktop.

### Comment pulse

- Accurate observation matters more than perfect tagging → future editors can normalize schemas more easily than they can rediscover missing physical facts.
- Flexible representation intimidates newcomers → separately drawn sidewalks and road tags can look duplicative — counterpoint: StreetComplete can mark mapped geometry as separate.
- Mobile tools divide by complexity → StreetComplete favors guided attributes, Every Door handles POIs, while paths may need GPS recording and later desktop editing.
