# Chat Control passed first round in EU Parliament

- Score: 522 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48819008) | Link: https://www.heise.de/en/news/Showdown-in-Strasbourg-The-unexpected-return-of-Chat-Control-1-0-11356680.html

### TL;DR

By 331–304, the European Parliament approved urgent handling—not Chat Control itself—for a Thursday vote. The proposal would revive an expired transitional rule that permits but does not require platforms to scan private communications for child-sexual-abuse material; it differs from “Chat Control 2.0.” Because this is a second reading, amendments or rejection require 361 MEPs, while approval needs only a simple majority present. Critics call the timing procedurally manipulative and warn of false positives and mass-surveillance normalization; supporters cite an enforcement gap. HN corrected confusion over what passed.
