# Microsoft fire idTech team at Id software

- Score: 500 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48819244) | Link: https://gamefromscratch.com/microsoft-fire-idtech-team-at-id-software/

### TL;DR

The article says Microsoft’s 3,200-role Xbox restructuring may have eliminated most or all idTech developers, based on social posts and one veteran’s LinkedIn update. HN immediately challenged the evidence: confirmed information showed significant id staffing cuts, but commenters had not seen clear engine-team or Frankfurt-specific departures. The broader debate concerned custom-engine economics. Standardizing on Unreal could lower staffing costs and increase contractor availability, yet risks technical sameness, poorer optimization, lost institutional expertise, and dependence on Epic. Open-sourcing idTech attracted interest, though critics noted its FPS specialization and missing general-purpose tooling.
