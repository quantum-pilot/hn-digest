### TL;DR

Donald Knuth’s project currently comprises published Volumes 1–3 and 4A–4B, covering foundational, seminumerical, sorting/searching, and combinatorial algorithms. Volume 4C is emerging through fascicles, beginning with 2025’s Constraint Satisfaction, while Volume 5 on lexical scanning and parsing remains in preparation; Volumes 6 and 7 are conditional future ambitions. The site catalogs editions, translations, draft material, emulators, indexes, and extensive errata. Knuth strongly recommends the authorized searchable PDF over Kindle or EPUB conversions, whose mathematical formatting he considers unreliable, and rewards first reports of genuine book errors with hexadecimal-denominated rewards.
