# Japan develops a method to recover up to 90% of lithium from used EV batteries

- Score: 717 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48901569) | Link: https://tech.supercarblondie.com/japan-recovers-up-to-90-of-lithium-from-used-ev-batteries/

### TL;DR

A lightly sourced report says a Japanese facility recovers about 90% of lithium from EV-battery black mass by substituting recovered lithium hydroxide for sodium hydroxide, producing reusable material with claimed 40% lower carbon emissions. Japan imports nearly all battery minerals, yet only 14% of spent batteries enter official recycling, making collection a major constraint. HN challenged the breakthrough framing: no facility or researchers were named, competitors reportedly achieve 95–96%, and commenters argued feedstock availability, economics, second-life storage, and recovering other materials matter more than headline yield.

### Comment pulse
