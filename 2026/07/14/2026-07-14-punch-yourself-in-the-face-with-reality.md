# Punch yourself in the face with reality

- Score: 203 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48905118) | Link: https://adi.bio/reality

### TL;DR

The essay argues that AI makes coding and prototypes faster without making the decisive parts of a startup easier: choosing a real problem, speaking with users, tolerating rejection, taking public risk, and abandoning weak ideas. Rapid generation can become sophisticated procrastination, producing unused software while an agreeable agent reinforces the founder’s bubble. HN commenters accepted the warning but added nuance: AI can compress experimentation and remove tedious blockers, provided developers regain technical understanding, impose structure, seek external feedback, and recognize that avoidance may reflect mental-health constraints rather than cowardice.
