# British runner Josh Kerr breaks world record for mile which stood for 27 years

- Score: 105 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48958814) | Link: https://news.sky.com/story/british-runner-josh-kerr-breaks-world-record-for-mile-which-had-stood-for-27-years-13564688

### TL;DR

Josh Kerr ran 3:42.66 at the London Diamond League, cutting roughly half a second from Hicham El Guerrouj’s 1999 mile world record before about 60,000 spectators. The Scottish Olympic 1500-metre silver medallist entered with a 3:45.34 best and framed the attempt as Project 222. Detailed splits showed striking consistency: 1:51.1 through 800 metres, 2:46.5 through 1,200, and a 54.9-second final 400. HN celebrated the performance while debating how much modern shoes, tracks, pacing lights, pacers, altitude simulation, nutrition, and training science contributed.
