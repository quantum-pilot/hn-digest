# KOReader

- Score: 637 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49095865) | Link: https://koreader.rocks/

### TL;DR

An open-source document viewer brings EPUB, PDF, DjVu, comics, MOBI, and many other formats to Kindle, Kobo, PocketBook, Android, and Linux. Users praise native format support, reflow, dictionaries, custom fonts, plugins, OPDS libraries, and cross-device progress syncing; on older Kindles it can restore sub-second responsiveness and extend useful hardware life. The cost is complexity: menus, gestures, organization, formatting, and initial setup frustrate many, with some preferring stock viewers. Community workarounds include sync servers, self-hosted catalogs, SSH, file-browser plugins, and alternate interfaces.

### Comment pulse

- Freedom changes purchasing → users choose jailbreakable readers or supported tablets, decoupling hardware from software and avoiding vendor stores.
- Customization trades against usability → exhaustive controls solve niche workflows — counterpoint: discoverability is poor enough that Zen UI feels mandatory.
- Library workflows remain modular → users combine Calibre alternatives, OPDS, rsync, Syncthing, or BookFusion rather than rely on one integrated ecosystem.
