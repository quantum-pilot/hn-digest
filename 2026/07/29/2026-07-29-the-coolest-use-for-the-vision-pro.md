# The coolest use for the Vision Pro

- Score: 227 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49102774) | Link: https://christianselig.com/2026/07/vision-pro-house/

### TL;DR

Christian Selig modeled his planned home in Fusion 360, added textures and downloadable furniture for scale, exported it as USDZ, and explored it immersively on Vision Pro. A morning of Claude-and-Codex coding produced Prospector, a rough viewer with controller navigation, terrain following, flight, speed controls, a skybox, and a real-world safety toggle. The experience made room proportions and sightlines legible before irreversible construction decisions. Commenters confirm similar VR workflows improve client understanding and real-time design feedback, while questioning Vision Pro’s longevity as a costly niche product.
