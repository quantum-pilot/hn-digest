# The coolest use for the Vision Pro

- Score: 227 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49102774) | Link: https://christianselig.com/2026/07/vision-pro-house/

### TL;DR  
An iOS developer used Apple’s Vision Pro as a house-design tool: modeling floor plans in Fusion 360, importing IKEA/3D Warehouse furniture, converting formats to USDZ, then building a custom Vision Pro app (“Prospector”) with controller movement, skybox, terrain following, flight, and passthrough toggling to freely walk through their future home. It made room proportions and layouts intuitively clear, long before construction. HN readers note architects already do similar VR walkthroughs with cheaper headsets, propose advanced sun/lighting simulations, and debate Vision Pro’s niche future.
