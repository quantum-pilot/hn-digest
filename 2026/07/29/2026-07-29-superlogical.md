# Superlogical

- Score: 450 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49098965) | Link: https://www.superlogical.com/

### TL;DR
Superlogical, a new company led by HashiCorp cofounder Mitchell Hashimoto and other veteran tooling designers, wants to unify “all work” (local dev, remote hosts, CI, agents, production) into a single durable session layer. They’re starting with a modern terminal multiplexer built on the open-source libghostty, with long‑lived sessions, web/macOS/iOS access, and built‑in collaboration. HN discussion focuses on the strong OSS posture, comparisons to existing agent/terminal orchestrators, UX/title choices, and a geeky SSH‑based careers page.
