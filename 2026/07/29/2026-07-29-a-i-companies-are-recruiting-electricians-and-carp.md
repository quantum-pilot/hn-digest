# A.I. companies are recruiting electricians and carpenters by the thousands

- Score: 192 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49098198) | Link: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/29/business/economy/data-center-electricians-training.html

### TL;DR

Michigan’s data-center expansion is pulling electricians from existing work with unusually high pay, bonuses, and overtime. OpenAI’s Saline Township project, described by the state as Michigan’s largest single investment, needs hundreds of electricians working 10-hour days, seven days weekly; Detroit’s training center has about 850 apprentices. Commercial construction hiring has risen since ChatGPT’s release while residential hiring dipped. Commenters welcome stronger trade wages but warn against career decisions based on a boom-bust buildout, noting apprenticeships take years and labor diversion raises costs for housing and other projects.
