# Darktable

- Score: 273 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49096654) | Link: https://www.darktable.org/

- TL;DR  
Darktable is a free, open‑source RAW photo editor and workflow tool that offers a Lightroom‑style non‑destructive pipeline, strong color management, GPU acceleration, and unusually deep documentation. HN users praise its power, local-only AI denoising/selection, and scriptable CLI, but report a steep learning curve, clunky organization UX, and painful breaking changes between processing versions. Some color‑science decisions (e.g., new grading/white balance, DJI drone support) are contentious, spawning forks like Ansel and vkdt and prompting some users to move to Lightroom or RapidRAW.
