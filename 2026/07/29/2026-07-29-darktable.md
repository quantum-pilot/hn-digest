# Darktable

- Score: 273 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49096654) | Link: https://www.darktable.org/

### TL;DR

This GPL-licensed photography application combines a database-backed lighttable with nondestructive RAW development, professional color management, and GPU acceleration while preserving originals. Enthusiasts praise its deep feature set, presets, color-space controls, documentation, CLI, and opt-in local AI denoising and subject selection, often replacing Lightroom. Critics report a steep learning curve, sluggish performance, clumsy masking and organization, workflow migrations that changed old renders, and persistent camera-color problems. The discussion points to Ansel for UX changes and vkdt for Vulkan graph editing, though vkdt remains less mature.

### Comment pulse

- Distribution affects capability → Debian users can obtain current Flatpaks, but one package removes local AI features; AppImage integration is weaker.
