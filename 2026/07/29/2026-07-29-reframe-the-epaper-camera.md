# ReFrame – The EPaper Camera

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49091379) | Link: https://reframe.camera/

### TL;DR

An open-source experimental camera pairs a Raspberry Pi Zero and Camera 3 with a 4-inch, six-pigment Spectra display. Each shutter press takes 15 seconds to reveal a dithered image; the screen then holds it without power until the next shot, doubling as a photo frame. Originals and processed versions remain downloadable through a web dashboard, and off-the-shelf parts support DIY builds. Commenters enjoy the film-like delay and deliberate constraint, while requesting an optical viewfinder and better dithering algorithms; others question treating reduced capability as intrinsically valuable.

### Comment pulse
