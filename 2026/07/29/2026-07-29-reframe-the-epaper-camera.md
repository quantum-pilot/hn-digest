# ReFrame – The EPaper Camera

- Score: 207 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49091379) | Link: https://reframe.camera/

### TL;DR
An open-source “slow” digital camera uses a 4" six-pigment color ePaper display and dithering to show exactly one photo at a time, which takes ~15 seconds to appear and then stays until you shoot again. Built from a Raspberry Pi Zero, Pi Camera 3, and off‑the‑shelf parts, it acts as both camera and always-on desk frame. The HN discussion centers on dithering quality, viewfinder ergonomics, and whether deliberate technical limitations meaningfully enhance creativity.
