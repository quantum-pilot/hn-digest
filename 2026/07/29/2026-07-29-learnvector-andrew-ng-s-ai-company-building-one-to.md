# LearnVector – Andrew Ng's AI company building one‑to‑one learning experiences

- Score: 257 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49092499) | Link: https://learnvector.ai/

### TL;DR

Andrew Ng founded LearnVector with a $100 million Coursera investment to build AI-guided, one-to-one learning experiences, with products promised by early 2027 and planned collaboration with Coursera and Udemy. The system aims to plan a trusted path, adapt instruction, and persist until mastery while avoiding chatbot answer-giving that causes cognitive offloading. HN readers saw promise in Socratic tutoring, frequent feedback, and personalized sequencing, but questioned differentiation from general models, whether motivation and credentials matter more than teaching, and why Coursera needs a separate company with unclear boundaries.
