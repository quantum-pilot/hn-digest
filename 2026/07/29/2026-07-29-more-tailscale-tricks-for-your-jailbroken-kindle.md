# More Tailscale tricks for your jailbroken Kindle

- Score: 385 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49093569) | Link: https://tailscale.com/blog/jailbroken-kindle-proxy-tun-modes

### TL;DR

An updated community Tailscale client expands what jailbroken Kindles can do: Tailscale SSH is enabled by default, proxy mode lets KOReader reach private Calibre, Wallabag, RSS, sync, and audiobook services, and experimental TUN mode provides device-wide routing on supported models. A separate KOReader plugin brings outbound tailnet access to some Kindles, Kobos, and PocketBooks, though not inbound reachability. Commenters praise the resulting self-hosted reading setups and revived older hardware, while warning that KOReader’s deep customization produces confusing menus and substantial setup friction.

### Comment pulse
