# More Tailscale tricks for your jailbroken Kindle

- Score: 385 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49093569) | Link: https://tailscale.com/blog/jailbroken-kindle-proxy-tun-modes

### TL;DR
An updated community Tailscale client for jailbroken Kindles adds three big capabilities: Tailscale SSH enabled by default, a local SOCKS5/HTTP proxy so apps like KOReader can reach Calibre/Wallabag and other tailnet services, and (on some models) full TUN mode for device-wide VPN routing. A separate KOReader plugin brings similar proxying to Kobo and PocketBook without jailbreaking the whole system. HN commenters dive into KOReader’s powerful but dense UX, jailbreaks to escape Amazon limitations, and pushing Tailscale onto weak hardware.
