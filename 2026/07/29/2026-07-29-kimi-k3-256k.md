# Kimi K3-256k

- Score: 289 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49101852) | Link: https://www.kimi.com/code/docs/en/kimi-code/models

### TL;DR
Kimi has introduced `k3-256k`, a cheaper 256k-context variant of its flagship 2.8T-parameter K3 coding model (1M context). Within 256k tokens, it should perform identically to the 1M model but uses about half the quota, making it the recommended everyday choice for coding, Q&A, and small-to-medium edits. You can later switch from `k3-256k` to full `k3` (1M) without losing cache, while switching the other way may require “compacting” context and dropping video, prompting workflow considerations and cost optimizations.
