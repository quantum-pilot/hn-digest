# Apple targets dozens of OpenAI employees with legal letters

- Score: 370 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48946303) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/1b8c9d52-88a9-426b-ba47-f1811f859166

### TL;DR

Apple reportedly sent document-preservation and interview requests to roughly 40 former employees now at OpenAI after suing the startup in federal court. Apple alleges OpenAI recruited current and former staff to obtain confidential hardware designs and information about unreleased technologies, with leadership orchestrating the conduct; these remain unproven claims. About 400 former Apple employees reportedly work at OpenAI. The dispute lands as OpenAI prepares hardware that could compete with Apple and has filed IPO paperwork. HN cautioned that preservation letters are routine litigation procedure, not evidence Apple has proved theft.
