# AWS: Inaccurate Estimated Billing Data – $1.7 billion

- Score: 1004 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48945241) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48945241

### TL;DR

An AWS customer whose normal monthly usage costs under $5 saw an estimated bill of $1.7 billion and opened an urgent support case. HN users reported similarly impossible alerts ranging from $78 million to hundreds of billions and even trillions, with console estimates matching the emails. Former AWS staff described a plausible failure mode: metering values are joined to pricing plans, and confusing gigabytes with bytes can multiply charges by roughly 2³⁰. The discussion emphasized that obviously false estimates still trigger phishing fears, compromise investigations, stress, and wasted response time.
