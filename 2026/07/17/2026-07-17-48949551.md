# Thanks HN for 15 years of support and helping me find my life's work

- Score: 234 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48949551) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48949551

### TL;DR

Fifteen years after Recurse Center opened, a cofounder credits Hacker News with turning failed startup pivots into enduring work. The YC team abandoned an OkCupid-for-jobs concept and built the self-directed programming retreat they wanted: participants pursue projects, contribute to open source, and learn together. Its HN debut supplied most early attendees and remains the second-largest applicant source after word of mouth. The retreat is free, funded by employer recruiting fees, and has served more than 3,000 people without fitting a billion-dollar startup model.
