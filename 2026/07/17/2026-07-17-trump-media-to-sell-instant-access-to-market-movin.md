# Trump Media to sell instant access to 'market-moving' social posts

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48944487) | Link: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c79gw4lj89eo

### TL;DR

Trump Media says its Truth API will begin selling institutional clients millisecond-speed delivery of posts from high-ranking Truth Social accounts on 1 August, while restricting unofficial data collection. The loss-making company has not disclosed pricing or confirmed whether President Trump’s posts are included. Traders value seconds because his tariff and trade announcements can move markets; his family’s majority ownership means he could benefit. A lawyer told Reuters tiered platform distribution can be legal under federal securities laws, but HN commenters described it as corruption and worried it rewards volatility.

- Volatility incentives worried readers → revenue from fast alerts could reward more market-moving posts, creating a feedback loop between attention and trading demand.
