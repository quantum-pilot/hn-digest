# Camera Chase Vehicle

- Score: 204 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48854988) | Link: https://transistor-man.com/gimbal_camera_rover.html

### TL;DR

A $50 surplus 1/5-scale RC chassis became a stabilized camera rover built from used cinema hardware, DeWalt batteries, a VESC, long-range control and HD video links, machined mounts, and 3D-printed parts. The write-up preserves failures: obsolete firmware blocked pairing, poor gimbal balance ruined footage, cables snagged, PLA mounts sheared, and suspension sagged. Iteration brought offline firmware recovery, flexible TPU parts, stronger springs, better balance, and smoother control. The final rig ran nearly 30 minutes on ice, captured stable zoomed footage, and survived an 18-mph curb tumble.
