# Short sellers notch $8.7B profit as SpaceX shares dip to IPO price

- Score: 150 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48948435) | Link: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/short-sellers-rack-up-87-bln-profit-spacex-slips-below-ipo-price-ortex-2026-07-16/

### TL;DR

Short sellers held an estimated $8.7 billion in paper gains after SpaceX shares retreated from a $225.64 post-IPO peak toward the $135 offer price. Ortex says roughly 49% of tradable shares are on loan, mostly for shorting, and bears kept adding instead of closing. The stock recovered to $136.28, underscoring that gains are unrealized. A constrained float, strong investor interest, Musk’s battles with shorts, and concern over debt-funded AI spending create squeeze risk: Ortex estimates every dollar move changes short-side value by more than $300 million.

### Comment pulse

- Valuation debate separated engineering from equity → commenters praised rockets and Starlink but argued AI expectations, not launch capability, support the market price.
- Orbital data centers drew skepticism → repairability, radiation, heat rejection, launch expense, and terminal write-offs challenge the promised economics.
- Shorting mechanics confused readers → profit comes from selling borrowed shares then repurchasing lower — counterpoint: rising prices, recalls, and borrow fees can reverse gains.

- **View:** Crowded positioning is not vindication: high short interest reflects skepticism while mechanically increasing reversal risk.
