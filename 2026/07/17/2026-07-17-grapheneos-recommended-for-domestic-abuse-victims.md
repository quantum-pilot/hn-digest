# GrapheneOS recommended for domestic abuse victims

- Score: 174 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48942454) | Link: https://privacypros.com.au/privacy-hub/articles/dv-safe-phone-australia/

### TL;DR

A commercial Australian seller recommends GrapheneOS on recent Pixels for people facing technology-facilitated domestic abuse, citing isolated profiles, Verified Boot, granular permissions, and a duress PIN. It pairs hardened phones with encrypted communications, tracker detection, metadata removal, and professional safety support. HN challenged the framing: GrapheneOS representatives said they do not specifically recommend their OS for stalkerware victims, and that a fresh phone of any kind, configured without old accounts or restored backups, is often the priority. Commenters also questioned reseller markups and warned that configuration mistakes can disrupt communication.
