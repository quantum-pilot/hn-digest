# Ask HN: Any AWS billing issues known? Amazon forecast of 3 billion dollars

- Score: 203 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48945681) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48945681

### TL;DR

An AWS customer received a budget alert against a $5 threshold showing a forecast of $3,005,575,870.47 despite having barely used the account during the past year. The console reportedly displayed perfectly uniform daily costs beginning July 1. AWS’s support chatbot itself characterized that pattern as strongly suggestive of a billing or metering error and said it opened a ticket, but no human response had arrived. The customer disabled every known IAM role and resource and found no evidence of account compromise, leaving the anomaly unresolved.
