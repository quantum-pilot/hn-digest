# DARPA, U.S. Air Force fly AI-controlled F-16

- Score: 163 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49021597) | Link: https://www.darpa.mil/news/2026/darpa-us-air-force-fly-ai-controlled-f-16

### TL;DR

DARPA and the U.S. Air Force are testing AI agents aboard F-16s modified with the VENOM Autonomy Kit. The interface connects to controls, sensors, and mission systems without altering core aircraft software; an onboard test pilot can switch between human and AI control. Building on X-62A autonomous dogfight trials, VENOM creates a reusable fleet testbed for multiple agents, beyond-visual-range multi-ship combat, and future uncrewed teammates. HN discussion mixes Skynet jokes with serious questions about takeover safety, whether the switch is novel, and differences from target drones.
