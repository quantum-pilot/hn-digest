# DARPA, U.S. Air Force fly AI-controlled F-16

- Score: 163 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49021597) | Link: https://www.darpa.mil/news/2026/darpa-us-air-force-fly-ai-controlled-f-16

### TL;DR

DARPA and the U.S. Air Force flew an F‑16 modified with the VENOM Autonomy Kit, where an onboard AI agent controlled the jet while a pilot supervised and could take back control. The VENOM fleet is meant as a reusable testbed under the AIR program to rapidly iterate combat AI, moving from earlier dogfight demos toward beyond‑visual‑range and multi‑ship autonomy, plus teaming with uncrewed aircraft. Hacker News discussion centers on safety, real tactical value, and accelerating AI militarization.
