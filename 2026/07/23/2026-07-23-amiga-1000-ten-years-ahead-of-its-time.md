# Amiga 1000: Ten years ahead of its time

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49017265) | Link: https://dfarq.homeip.net/amiga-1000-ten-years-ahead-of-its-time/

### TL;DR

Released in 1985, the Amiga 1000 paired a 7.14 MHz Motorola 68000 and 256 KB RAM with custom graphics, stereo audio, a GUI, and preemptive multitasking. A practical setup approached $2,000, lacked memory protection, and could crash when software mishandled resources, yet it enabled smooth concurrent work years before mainstream PCs. The author blames Commodore’s weak marketing and management for squandering that lead before bankruptcy. Hacker News celebrated its formative creativity, low-latency interface, extensibility, and video features, while disputing whether ten years ahead survives faster late-1980s hardware.

### Comment pulse

- Its deepest lead was architectural, not raw speed → preemptive multitasking, ARexx-style automation, integrated GUI/CLI tools, screens, and native genlock created unusual workflows.
