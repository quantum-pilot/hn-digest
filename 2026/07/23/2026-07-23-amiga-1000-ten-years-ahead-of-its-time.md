# Amiga 1000: Ten years ahead of its time

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49017265) | Link: https://dfarq.homeip.net/amiga-1000-ten-years-ahead-of-its-time/

- TL;DR  
  Introduced in 1985, the Amiga 1000 paired a custom graphics/sound chipset with a TRIPOS‑derived, preemptive‑multitasking OS, delivering Windows‑95‑class capabilities on a 7 MHz 68000 with 256–512 KB RAM. The author recalls true multitasking—background BBS downloads while gaming or doing homework—years before PCs and Macs felt comparable. Commodore skipped memory protection to keep costs down, then squandered its lead through inept, loot‑driven leadership and weak niche targeting, leaving a small but passionate user base watching the mainstream slowly catch up.
