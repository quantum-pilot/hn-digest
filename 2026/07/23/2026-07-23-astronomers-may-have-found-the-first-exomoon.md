# Astronomers may have found the first exomoon

- Score: 188 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49021783) | Link: https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2610/

### TL;DR
ESO’s Very Large Telescope has found strong radial-velocity evidence of a Jupiter-mass “exosatellite” orbiting a brown dwarf that itself orbits star CD-35 2722. Because the object is planetary-mass yet doesn’t orbit a star directly, astronomers struggle to name it—moon, planet, or something in between—making it the first plausible exomoon-like detection and a challenge to Solar-System-based labels. HN readers dive into classification semantics, gas-giant physics, and the Chilean observatories enabling such delicate measurements.
