# AI Companies Are Trying to Hide a Staggering Amount of Debt

- Score: 579 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49020999) | Link: https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-companies-hide-debt-off-balance-sheet

- TL;DR  
    - A Nikkei-led analysis says Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Oracle have about $1.65T in off‑balance‑sheet obligations—more than their reported $1.35T debt—largely tied to AI-driven data center buildouts via leases and special entities. The article warns this Enron-style opacity could worsen an AI bubble already supported by thin profits. HN discussion questions whether the debt size is actually alarming, debates if it’s really “hidden,” and worries about knock-on risks to pensions, insurers, and index investors.
