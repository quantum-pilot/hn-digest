# AI Companies Are Trying to Hide a Staggering Amount of Debt

- Score: 579 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49020999) | Link: https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-companies-hide-debt-off-balance-sheet

### TL;DR

Futurism, citing Nikkei Asia, says five US tech giants carry an estimated $1.65 trillion through off-balance-sheet arrangements—more than their reported $1.35 trillion of debt—with Meta attributed roughly $420 billion. The financing supports AI data centers but could amplify losses or dilution if demand disappoints; the article invokes Enron-style special-purpose vehicles. Hacker News disputed the framing, noting legal structures may be manageable relative to cash flow, while warning that leverage, insurer exposure, index concentration, and slow hardware depreciation could spread losses.

### Comment pulse

- Off-balance-sheet does not necessarily mean concealed → the arrangements are legal and publicly discoverable — counterpoint: extra disclosure effort can still distort headline valuation.
- Systemic exposure extends beyond lenders → insurers, pensions, 401(k)s, and index funds can transmit equity or credit losses to ordinary households.
- Asset lives may flatter earnings separately → slower GPU and data-center depreciation pulls profits forward until eventual write-downs force accounting to catch up.
