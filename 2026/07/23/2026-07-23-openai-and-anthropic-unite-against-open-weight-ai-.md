# OpenAI and Anthropic unite against open-weight AI risks to their bottom line

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49020868) | Link: https://www.axios.com/2026/07/22/openai-anthropic-open-models-trump-china

### TL;DR
OpenAI and Anthropic, normally rivals, are aligned in D.C. against “open‑weight” AI models, arguing that releasing weights makes powerful systems impossible to secure or revoke. They’re also backing a tougher stance on “distillation,” especially by Chinese labs, framing it as potential IP theft and a national‑security issue. Open‑model advocates counter that this is regulatory capture: it would entrench closed US incumbents, restrict vital research models (including from China), and weaken US competitiveness and scientific progress.
