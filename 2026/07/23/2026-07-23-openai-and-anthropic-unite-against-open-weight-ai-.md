# OpenAI and Anthropic unite against open-weight AI risks to their bottom line

- Score: 274 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49020868) | Link: https://www.axios.com/2026/07/22/openai-anthropic-open-models-trump-china

### TL;DR

OpenAI and Anthropic, despite competing commercially, are urging Washington to scrutinize powerful open-weight models. Anthropic stresses that released weights cannot be revoked, patched, or reliably constrained; OpenAI favors openness within an unspecified national framework and warns Chinese distillation may constitute IP theft. Advocates counter that accessible weights enable competition, diversification, and research, while restrictions could entrench incumbents or sweep in independent systems. HN commenters largely interpret the safety case as regulatory capture, though some say the labs may sincerely perceive existential risks and distillation can erode frontier-funding margins.

### Comment pulse

- Safety rhetoric has a credibility problem → critics see incumbents seeking protection immediately after open competitors narrow capability and price gaps.
- Protectionism may sacrifice national competitiveness → commenters compare AI restrictions with insulating lagging chipmakers and automakers — counterpoint: distillation could compress margins funding frontier progress.
- Open weights can be defensive infrastructure → Hugging Face reportedly needed an unrestricted model after guarded systems refused security analysis, challenging one-sided risk narratives.
