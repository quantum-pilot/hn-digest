# Show HN: Echo – Fable-level results at 1/3 the cost using open-weight models

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49026810) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49026810

### TL;DR
Echo is pitched as an “AI gateway” that routes each request to whichever open-weight model should give the best quality-per-dollar, claiming Fable-level outputs at one-third the cost. Hacker News largely treats it as another meta-router like OpenRouter or Fireworks: the architecture is sensible (mixture-of-models, abstraction over many providers), but commenters question the marketing, lack of deeply convincing benchmarks, unclear model list, and practical challenges of conversation-aware routing, caching, and whether cost savings matter with today’s heavily subsidized proprietary APIs.
