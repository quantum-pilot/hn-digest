# The arguments against open source AI are bad

- Score: 186 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49024643) | Link: https://tombedor.dev/arguments-against-open-source-ai-are-very-bad/

### TL;DR
The article argues that fears about open-source (really, open-weights) AI—especially Chinese models—are self‑serving and misguided. It notes that all modern proprietary software depends on open source, suppression attempts historically fail (encryption export controls), and many Western actors (Nvidia, startups, enterprises, Big Tech) are strongly incentivized to back open models. Claims of “AI dumping,” propaganda, or backdoors are portrayed as manageable in an open ecosystem. HN replies mostly agree on inevitability but push harder on safety risks and on what “open source AI” should mean.

### Comment pulse

- AI race anxiety → Some see a real “superweapon” or singularity goal; others argue secure hardware/software plus powerful open models (e.g., Hugging Face using GLM) are vital for defense.  
- Critique of the article → It downplays safety and leans on “you can’t stop open AI, therefore embrace it,” which several readers see as logically weak and ethically evasive.
