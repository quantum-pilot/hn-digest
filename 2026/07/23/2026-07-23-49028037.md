# Tell HN: Namecheap gave my account to an unverified third party

- Score: 327 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49028037) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49028037

### TL;DR
Namecheap reportedly transferred control of a customer’s domains to an unrelated third party who persuaded support, bypassing proper verification. Commenters see this as a registrar single‑point‑of‑failure problem: social‑engineer one support rep and you own entire online identities. Many describe moving domains from Namecheap after renewal failures or account‑recovery nightmares, preferring Hover, Cloudflare, or tech firms where domains are side-businesses. Others argue the issues predate private‑equity ownership and stress due diligence over stampedes to any “safe” alternative.

### LLM perspective
- View: Treat registrars like banks; require out-of-band verification, hardware keys, or account PINs before any ownership-changing action.
