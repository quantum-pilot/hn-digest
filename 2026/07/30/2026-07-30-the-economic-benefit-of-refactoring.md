# The Economic Benefit of Refactoring

- Score: 183 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49111176) | Link: https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/refactoring-economic-benefit.html

### TL;DR
Martin Fowler argues that classic refactoring has a new, measurable payoff in the age of AI coding tools. Cleaner, better-factored code reduces token usage and context size, making LLM assistance cheaper, faster, and more accurate. Comments note that long-standing best practices—good abstractions, in-code documentation, clear structure—now have quantifiable economic impact when AIs consume the code. Discussion centers on how far agents can autonomously refactor, and how much human architectural judgment remains essential.
