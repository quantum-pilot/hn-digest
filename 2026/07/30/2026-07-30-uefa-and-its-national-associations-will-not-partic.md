# UEFA and its national associations will not participate in FIFA competitions

- Score: 698 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49113929) | Link: https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/02a7-213a92896eb0-54dfbf454e3b-1000--statement-on-behalf-of-uefa-and-its-55-national-associations/

### TL;DR
UEFA has announced it and its national associations will not take part in future FIFA competitions if FIFA pushes ahead with a plan to sell stakes in World Cup and other tournament revenues to private investors. Commenters see this as a historic power struggle over football’s governance: Infantino’s FIFA seeking to financialize a nominally non‑profit body, versus UEFA and others warning that investor control would permanently prioritize shareholder returns over the sport, echoing broader tech and institutional debates.
