# UEFA and its national associations will not participate in FIFA competitions

- Score: 698 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49113929) | Link: https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/02a7-213a92896eb0-54dfbf454e3b-1000--statement-on-behalf-of-uefa-and-its-55-national-associations/

### TL;DR

UEFA and all 55 member associations unanimously rejected FIFA’s proposal to sell ownership interests in the World Cup and other competitions to private investors. European national teams will boycott FIFA events while the plan remains, ending only after full abandonment and binding assurances against future private ownership. UEFA condemns both the secret, coercive process and the permanent pressure shareholder returns would place on calendars, formats, players, and supporters. Commenters largely support the stand, framing the deal as corruption or bad finance, while debating whether investor influence is inherently destructive.

### Comment pulse

- Privatization changes institutional purpose → owners require returns, potentially subordinating sporting decisions to revenue and enriching insiders.
- Investor capture is not inevitable → founder control, benefit corporations, and nonprofits can constrain influence — counterpoint: legal structure alone offers no guarantee.
- Commercial expansion divides fans → critics cite injuries and tradition, while supporters say 48 teams created competitive underdog stories.
