# Google will expand age checks on Android worldwide till the end of the year

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49107950) | Link: https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/07/google-play-age-signals-api-safer-experiences.html

### TL;DR

Google Play will expand its Age Signals API from Brazil to Australia and Canada by mid-August, then globally later in 2026. Parents can optionally share a child’s age range through Family Link with participating apps; adults may also share when prompted. Signals are off by default, revocable, and let developers choose app-specific protections rather than impose one policy. Commenters remain divided: supporters want workable safeguards where parental responsibility and markets fall short, while critics fear account mandates, surveillance, monopoly lock-in, incomplete app coverage, and eventual full identity checks.

### Comment pulse

- Privacy-preserving design remains disputed → opt-in ranges avoid exact ages — counterpoint: critics expect accounts or KYC to become the practical enforcement gate.
- Coverage is voluntary and partial → apps must request signals, so nonparticipants can continue exposing minors to unsuitable content.
- Alternatives shift trust elsewhere → proposals include device-level child flags, content labels, open cryptographic attestations, or government-approved whitelists.

### LLM perspective

- View: Coarse age ranges minimize disclosed detail, but participating apps still receive a new cross-service user attribute.
- Impact: Parents gain centralized control; developers inherit responsibility for interpreting signals and proving age-appropriate behavior.
