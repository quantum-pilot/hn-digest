# Google will expand age checks on Android worldwide till the end of the year

- Score: 336 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49107950) | Link: https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/07/google-play-age-signals-api-safer-experiences.html

- TL;DR  
Google is rolling out expanded age checks across Android worldwide, pushing apps toward asking users’ ages and enforcing youth protections, likely to comply with emerging regulations. Commenters worry this cements Google as a surveillance-heavy gatekeeper: age checks often require accounts, KYC, and undermine anonymity while reinforcing monopolies. Others argue parents and markets have failed to protect kids, suggesting device-level “child modes,” government-run cryptographic attestations, or age-tailored algorithms instead of handing more personal data to platforms.
