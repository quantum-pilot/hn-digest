# The Productivity Mirage

- Score: 345 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49104335) | Link: https://frantic.im/mirage/

### TL;DR
Many modern teams chase a “productivity” mirage: metrics dashboards, AI helpers and elaborate setups that signal busyness but rarely improve shipped outcomes. The article contrasts VC-backed app factories, obsessed with productivity tooling, with game studios that survive only by releasing products players buy. HN commenters argue that good tools should be tuned once then disappear, that thinking and reading matter far more than typing, and that productivity rituals often mask procrastination, fear of ambiguity, or corporate status-theater.
