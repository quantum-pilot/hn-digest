# 1,741 "informed" consents with one click? GDPR complaint filed

- Score: 160 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49106384) | Link: https://noyb.eu/en/1741-informed-consents-one-click-gdpr-complaint-against-dictcc-filed

### TL;DR

Privacy group noyb has complained to Austria’s data regulator that dict.cc cannot obtain valid GDPR consent by letting one click authorize tracking by 1,741 partners. Scanning each privacy policy for six minutes would take about 170 hours, before accounting for onward sharing, making consequences practically unknowable. noyb seeks deletion, recipient notification, and a deterrent fine, possibly broader intervention. Commenters report similar consent overload on TVs and pay-or-consent sites, blaming weak enforcement and asymmetric rejection flows; proposed remedies range from easier revocation to banning tracking or advertising.

### Comment pulse

- Dark patterns extend beyond websites → a Samsung TV reportedly offered one acceptance button but over 200 separate declines for partner sharing.
- Enforcement is the bottleneck → commenters identify legitimate-interest and pay-or-consent schemes as loopholes, arguing underfunded authorities leave rules ineffective.
- Blanket bans divide opinion → some demand outlawing targeted ads and tracking — counterpoint: widespread social-platform use suggests users tolerate ad-supported services.

### LLM perspective

- View: Consent cannot be meaningful when disclosure volume exceeds any realistic decision budget.
- Impact: Publishers and device makers face pressure to minimize partners and make refusal symmetric.
