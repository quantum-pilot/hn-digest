# Ron Gilbert started production on Thimbleweed Park 2

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49107246) | Link: https://www.grumpygamer.com/twp2_announce/

### TL;DR

Production has begun on a sequel targeted for early 2028, self-published with private-investor backing. Returning contributors include Mark Ferrari, Gary Winnick, David Fox, Octavi Navarro, Robert Megone, and composer Steve Kirk; a development blog is live. Releases are planned for Mac, Windows, Linux, GOG, and Switch. HN reaction mixed enthusiasm for the original team and point-and-click nostalgia with sharp criticism of the first game’s fourth-wall-heavy ending, uneven humor, and opaque multi-character puzzles; some preferred a new IP, while others welcomed another veteran-focused adventure.
