# Ron Gilbert started production on Thimbleweed Park 2

- Score: 230 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49107246) | Link: https://www.grumpygamer.com/twp2_announce/

- TL;DR  
Ron Gilbert has begun production on Thimbleweed Park 2, reviving his retro point-and-click series. Commenters are split on the first game: some loved its art, nostalgia, and playing it with kids; others bounced off its punishing, “moon logic” puzzles, multi-character complexity, and relentless fourth-wall-breaking. The ending is especially divisive, seen either as clever Monkey Island–style metafiction or a nihilistic cop‑out. Many would have preferred a fresh IP, though fans welcome a GOG release for long‑term preservation.
