# Azulejo

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49089502) | Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azulejo

### TL;DR

Iberian painted tin-glazed tiles evolved from 13th-century Hispano-Moresque geometric mosaics into molded, polychrome, narrative, blue-and-white, industrial, and modern architectural art. Seville first led production; Portugal adopted the craft after 1503, developed a distinct tradition, and spread it through former colonies. Tiles also cover everyday interiors and may moderate heat. Preservation is urgent: Lisbon lost an estimated 25% of its artistic tiles from 1980–2000, though SOS Azulejo reduced theft and vandalism around 80% by 2013. Commenters urge tourists to avoid antique tiles and support living makers.
