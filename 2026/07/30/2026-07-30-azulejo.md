# Azulejo

- Score: 173 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49089502) | Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azulejo

## TL;DR
Azulejos are decorative ceramic tiles central to Portuguese, Spanish, Moroccan and Brazilian architecture, often forming large artworks or geometric wall coverings. Commenters warn that many “antique” tiles sold to tourists in Iberia are literally stolen from historic buildings, urging visitors to buy from contemporary artisans instead. Discussion ranges over the Arabic origin of the word (distinct from “azul”), how tiled walls can help keep interiors cooler, and cultural offshoots like the board game Azul.
