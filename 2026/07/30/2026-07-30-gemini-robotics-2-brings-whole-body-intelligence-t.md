# Gemini Robotics 2 brings whole body intelligence to robots

- Score: 455 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49111237) | Link: https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/

### TL;DR

Google DeepMind introduced three robotics models: a vision-language-action controller for full humanoids and bi-arm systems, an embodied-reasoning agent for multi-step and multi-robot work, and an on-device model that adapts to new bodies with under 200 examples. Capabilities include walking, object manipulation, knot tying, packing, self-correction, and safety stops, but Google acknowledges slow motion and difficult multifinger dexterity. Commenters praise the breadth yet question demo realism, sensing, actuators, benchmark quality, household safety, and whether robotics can improve as quickly as language models.
