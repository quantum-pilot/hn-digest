# Gemini Robotics 2 brings whole body intelligence to robots

- Score: 455 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49111237) | Link: https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/

### TL;DR
Gemini Robotics 2 is Google DeepMind’s second-gen system for “whole-body” robot control driven by large models, aiming to generalize across many manipulation and mobility tasks. From comments, people see solid but incremental progress compared to the leap from GPT‑2 to GPT‑3: motion is slow, heavily safety-constrained, and still brittle outside curated demos. Practitioners emphasize missing benchmarks, dexterity and sensing limits, and real safety risks, while acknowledging large investment and rapid improvement that could yield a “GPT‑2 moment” for robotics within a few years.
