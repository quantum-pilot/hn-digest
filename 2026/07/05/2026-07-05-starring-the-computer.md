# Starring the Computer

- Score: 153 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48796093) | Link: https://www.starringthecomputer.com/computers.html

### TL;DR

The page catalogs more than 100 films and television episodes, from 1960s science fiction through 2020, that feature the same recognizable computer-room prop. HN identified the slanted control panels as IBM AN/FSQ-7 equipment from the 1950s SAGE air-defense system—specifically modem panels, not the computer itself—kept in circulation by a Los Angeles rental company. Commenters enjoyed correcting remembered screen code and explaining why productions sometimes substituted paper displays: mismatched CRT refresh rates, camera frame rates, and exposure timing made monitors difficult to film.
