# Small Penis Rule

- Score: 122 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48797015) | Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_penis_rule

### TL;DR

The “rule” is a literary intimidation tactic, not a dependable libel defense: an author gives a recognizable fictional man a small penis, betting he will avoid suing because identification would be embarrassing. Legal scholar Michael Conklin argues the detail may itself be defamatory, could imply deliberate defamation, and need not be admitted to claim damages. Examples include Michael Crichton’s apparent caricature of critic Michael Crowley and Peter James’s Amis Smallbone. HN treated it as game theory but disputed the premise that filing suit concedes the humiliating trait.
