# EU Council forces Chat Control via fast-track

- Score: 381 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48793393) | Link: https://www.heise.de/en/news/Chat-Control-1-0-EU-Council-forces-messenger-scans-via-fast-track-11353659.html

### TL;DR

The EU Council is fast-tracking a replacement for expired Chat Control 1.0, restoring the e-privacy exemption that lets communications providers voluntarily scan content and metadata for child-abuse material or grooming. Because the old rule cannot formally be extended, governments advanced a near-identical “new” regulation for urgent second-reading action just before Parliament’s summer break, where blocking it requires an absolute majority. Critics call the timing procedural circumvention. HN stressed this is not Chat Control 2.0’s mandatory scanning of encrypted services; Parliament resistance and protest appear to have stopped that broader plan.

### Comment pulse

- Scope matters → this restores voluntary provider scanning under 1.0, not 2.0’s mandatory weakening of end-to-end encryption.
- Institutional blame is often inverted → national governments drive the Council proposal, while the directly elected Parliament blocked the broader mandate.
- Expiry should carry democratic weight → governments cite child protection and legal uniformity — counterpoint: recreating an expired exception under holiday pressure undermines scrutiny.
