# I Accidentally Started a Small Business Three Weeks Ago

- Score: 115 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48791048) | Link: https://extelligence.substack.com/p/i-accidentally-started-a-small-business

### TL;DR

A father built a personalized communication app after standard AAC tools proved too abstract for his minimally verbal son. Instead of generic symbols, it uses images of the child’s people, foods, toys, and routines in an animation style, spoken in his father’s cloned voice. The child engaged immediately, communicated specific needs, doubled symbol recognition through teaching mode, and spoke five times more within three weeks. Unsolicited interest from families, therapists, and school turned the prototype into a planned $9.99–$19.99 monthly product. HN readers saw overdue, affordable personalization enabled by AI.
