# Programmers need to start meditating

- Score: 126 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48792080) | Link: https://jacob.gold/posts/programmers-need-to-start-meditating-now/

### TL;DR

After eight months orchestrating several coding agents at once, the author feels more productive but spends far less time in the deep programming flow that previously quieted rumination and restored mental balance. The recommendation is to replace that lost contemplative function deliberately through meditation or another absorbing hobby. HN participants recognized the new pattern as sustained medium stress: prompting can continue all day without natural stopping points. They disputed constant switching’s productivity and any universal prescription; walks, sport, single-tasking, or leaving agent-heavy roles may suit different people better.

### Comment pulse

- Agent supervision removes natural recovery cycles → code once forced pauses after intense focus; prompting can sustain unresolved threads and moderate stress indefinitely.
- Multi-agent work alternates managerial and developer cognition → delegation feels cheap until one agent needs detailed steering, making the human the bottleneck.
- Meditation is an option, not an obligation → attention training helps some — counterpoint: ADHD, boredom, or anxiety may make walking or sport healthier.
