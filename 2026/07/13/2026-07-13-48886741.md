# Ask HN: Add flag for AI-generated articles

- Score: 999 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48886741) | Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48886741

### TL;DR

HN commenters debated whether linked articles should carry an AI-generated indicator. A moderator clarified that generated text is barred on HN itself, while linked content has no equivalent rule; a future flag-reason menu may include suspected generation. Supporters want disclosure because synthetic prose raises verification costs, obscures authorial understanding, and weakens human connection. Opponents warn that detection is speculative, accusations become low-effort weapons, and quality should remain the voting criterion. The unresolved divide is whether provenance matters independently of accuracy and interest.

### Comment pulse

- Trust requires an accountable author → readers cannot tell whether gaps reflect misunderstanding, weak proofreading, or hallucination, making follow-up discussion brittle.
- Quality-only evaluation has appeal → accurate, interesting prose may deserve attention — counterpoint: undisclosed generation still feels deceptive and removes human connection.
- Detection creates governance risk → false positives can stigmatize human writers, while repeated AI accusations offer cheap karma and demand unreasonable proof.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** Separate provenance from quality; they answer different reader questions and should not share one binary vote.
- **Impact:** A reasoned flag workflow could reduce meta-comments, but public AI labels require review and appeal.
- **Watch next:** Track false-positive rates, disclosure behavior, moderator workload, and whether labels improve choices without chilling submissions.
