# DOGE is done. What happened to its records?

- Score: 294 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48894641) | Link: https://www.ms.now/opinion/doge-government-efficiency-records-job-cuts-elon-musk-foia

### TL;DR

The article argues that DOGE ended without a public accounting because the administration classifies it as presidential advice rather than an independently acting agency, placing its files outside immediate FOIA access. It reports deleted labor-board accounts, pending lawsuits over DOGE’s status and record preservation, a disputed legal theory treating presidential records as private property, and no planned after-action report. HN commenters described delayed NIH grants and chaotic staffing as consequences of indiscriminate cuts, while debating whether government inefficiency justified intervention and noting that transparency bodies and spending datasets already existed.

### Comment pulse
