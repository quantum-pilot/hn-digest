# Samsung Health app threatens data deletion if users opt out AI training

- Score: 223 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48897991) | Link: https://neow.in/cWsyMTV3

### TL;DR

Samsung Health reportedly ties cloud backup and synchronization to consent for using sensitive health data in AI training: disabling the setting warns that Samsung-held data will be deleted after legally required retention. Covered categories include sleep, medications, medical records, and cycle tracking, with possible human review, while new features analyze biometrics and fitness metrics. HN readers called the coupling coercive for hardware buyers, reported unreliable export and sync, and argued deletion is privacy-friendly only if users can first obtain a portable copy and trust Samsung to honor the choice.

### Comment pulse

- Paid hardware loses practical utility → commenters rejected making backup contingent on broad medical-data training consent and suggested consumer complaints where protections exist.
- Deletion can protect privacy → refusing consent removes Samsung’s copy — counterpoint: without reliable export, users lose continuity alongside vendor access.
- Consent is nominal when paired with degradation → readers compared it with services that disable valuable history or synchronization unless training remains enabled.
