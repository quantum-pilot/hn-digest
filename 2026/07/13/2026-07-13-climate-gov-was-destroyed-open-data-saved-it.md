# Climate.gov was destroyed. Open data saved it

- Score: 384 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48897945) | Link: https://werd.io/climate-gov-was-destroyed-open-data-saved-it/

### TL;DR

After NOAA cuts took Climate.gov offline, former employees Rebecca Lindsey, Mary Lindsey, and Anna Eshelman rebuilt its public-facing archive as Climate.us. They preserved more than 15 years of maps, teaching material, climate indicators, oral histories, and the deleted Fifth National Climate Assessment. US-government public-domain rules made lawful copying possible; new Climate.us work uses CC BY-SA. HN celebrated the rescue but stressed that mirrors preserve neither ongoing measurement nor expert context automatically: underlying data still exists elsewhere, while fresh interpretation and maintenance now depend on donations rather than stable public funding.

### Comment pulse
