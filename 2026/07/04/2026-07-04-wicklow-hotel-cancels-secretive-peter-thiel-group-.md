# Wicklow hotel cancels 'secretive' Peter Thiel group conference

- Score: 107 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48787987) | Link: https://www.irishtimes.com/ireland/2026/07/03/wicklow-hotel-cancels-secretive-peter-thiel-conference/

### TL;DR

Powerscourt Hotel in County Wicklow cancelled an August retreat for Dialog, an invitation-only network cofounded by Peter Thiel and Auren Hoffman. Expected attendees included a senior NATO commander and Trump officials; a leaked schedule covered world-war preparation, battlefield technology, nuclear energy, and cult-building. The venue gave no reason, while its estate owner expressed relief. Pro-Palestinian campaigners, opposing Palantir’s partnership with the IDF, claimed victory and promised to follow any relocation. HN suspected organizers would move privately and debated whether secretive unfairly describes an ordinary private hotel event.
