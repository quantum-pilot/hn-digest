# Mir Books – Books from the Soviet Era

- Score: 170 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48739018) | Link: https://mirtitles.org

### TL;DR

Mir Books presents a 1978 English translation of Alexander Kompaneyets’s two-volume theoretical-physics course. Volume 1 unifies mechanics, electrodynamics, and quantum mechanics; Volume 2 covers statistical physics, fluid and gas dynamics, continuous-media electrodynamics, and physical kinetics, with exercises aimed at advanced students. HN discussion broadened into appreciation for inexpensive Soviet scientific texts and translated folklore, especially their circulation in India. Readers praised the archive’s preservation work and requested searchable catalogs, bulk downloads, quality reprints, and ways to place inherited collections.
