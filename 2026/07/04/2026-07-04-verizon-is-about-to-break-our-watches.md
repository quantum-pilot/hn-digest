# Verizon is About to Break our Watches

- Score: 121 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48787329) | Link: https://www.jefftk.com/p/verizon-is-about-to-break-our-watches

### TL;DR

Verizon planned to retire its Gizmohub app on July 6 while the replacement Verizon Family app still rejected customers whose only Verizon lines were Gizmo watches. Shutdown would remove texting, location viewing, and contact management, potentially blocking calls outside the existing allowlist. Three support contacts acknowledged a known compatibility problem but produced no fix or escalation before the deadline. HN suggested Google Fi-based verification may compound migration failures, while one watch-only customer succeeded after repeated resets but lost contacts. The broader complaint was deprecating a safety-relevant service before feature parity.
