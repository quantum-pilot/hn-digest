# The Vespa at 80

- Score: 138 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48746327) | Link: https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/vespa-italy-postwar-design-9.7252641

### TL;DR

Piaggio turned wartime aircraft expertise and shattered postwar infrastructure into an inexpensive new vehicle in 1946. Engineer Corradino D’Ascanio’s enclosed engine, aerodynamic body, and step-through frame made riding cleaner and skirt-compatible, giving newly enfranchised Italian women unusual mobility. Roman Holiday helped turn the scooter into a global emblem of freedom and style; more than 19 million have been produced in over 100 countries. HN owners argued modern 300cc models remain comfortable, quiet, quick, and practical, while others want electric performance paired with similarly enduring design.
