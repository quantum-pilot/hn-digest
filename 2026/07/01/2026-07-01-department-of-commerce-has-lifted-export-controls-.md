- Score: 918 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48740771) | Link: https://twitter.com/AnthropicAI/status/2072106151890809341

### TL;DR

Anthropic says the U.S. Commerce Department lifted export controls on Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5, allowing access restoration to begin the next day. A letter reproduced in HN attributes the reversal to Anthropic’s commitments on proactive security detection, government coordination, and malicious-activity reporting, while reserving Commerce’s right to reimpose licensing. Commenters welcomed availability but emphasized unpredictability: stricter classifiers may wrongly divert routine coding requests to Opus 4.8, and abrupt policy shifts make commercial models risky dependencies. Others argued unprecedented systems inevitably require adaptive regulation.
