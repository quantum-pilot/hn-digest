# Internal Combustion Engine (2021)

- Score: 271 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48746076) | Link: https://ciechanow.ski/internal-combustion-engine/

### TL;DR

An animated, first-principles tour builds a gasoline engine from crank and piston to a modern inline-four. It explains the intake, compression, power, and exhaust strokes; crankshaft geometry; bearings floating on pressurized oil; piston rings; cam-driven valve timing; direct injection and ignition; uneven combustion and inertial torque; and flywheel smoothing. HN praised the visual explanation and mechanical elegance while stressing what the simplified model omits: modern emissions hardware and decades of electronic control advances in fuel mixture, ignition, and variable valve timing. Commenters also clarified startup noises and lubrication failures.

### Comment pulse

- Core mechanics endured; control evolved → electronic injection, ignition, and variable valve timing improved power, efficiency, and emissions far beyond carburetors.
- Emissions systems complete the real engine → catalytic converters, DPF, EGR, and AdBlue reduce pollutants — counterpoint: added complexity can hurt reliability.
- Lubrication diagnosis needs precision → startup cam rattle may involve chain tensioners or phasers, not the crankshaft’s separate hydrodynamic bearings.
