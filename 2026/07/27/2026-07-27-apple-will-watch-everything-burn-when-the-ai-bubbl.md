# Apple Will 'Watch Everything Burn' When the AI Bubble Bursts

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49070427) | Link: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/ed-zitron-apple-watch-it-burn-ai-bubble-bursts/

### TL;DR

Ed Zitron argues LLM businesses break software economics: inference carries per-use costs, subscriptions hide subsidies, enterprise ROI is unclear, and debt-financed data centers require implausible profits. A collapse could hit private credit, pensions, chip suppliers, and investors, while Apple’s comparatively small infrastructure spend leaves it positioned to wait, buy distressed assets, and emphasize on-device silicon—though expensive memory already hurts its hardware business. Hacker News debated whether this is strategic restraint or spin on Apple’s AI failures, and whether painful enterprise token bills reveal unsustainable subsidies or strong pricing power.

### Comment pulse

- Skepticism needs better adjudication → commenters valued an anti-hype narrative but disputed Zitron’s reliability and demanded claim-level counterarguments.
