# Apple Will 'Watch Everything Burn' When the AI Bubble Bursts

- Score: 231 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49070427) | Link: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/27/ed-zitron-apple-watch-it-burn-ai-bubble-bursts/

### TL;DR
Ed Zitron argues the current LLM boom is structurally unprofitable: users won’t pay true token costs, enterprise demand is thin, and hyperscalers are overbuilding AI data centers financed by opaque private credit and pension funds. When returns disappoint, he expects painful write‑downs, stress for Oracle and other overlevered builders, and a hit to chip and memory markets that consumers already feel via pricier iPhones and Macs. Apple, having spent relatively little on AI capex, can largely sit out the crash.

### LLM perspective
- View: The critique mainly hits frontier, general-purpose LLMs; narrower, domain-specific models may find sustainable economics sooner.  
- Impact: If capex collapses, GPU, DRAM, and cloud vendors reprice; AI startups consolidate; Big Tech shifts back to more measured AI use.  
- Watch next: Rising subscription prices, stricter rate limits, canceled data-center projects, and whether Apple quietly deemphasizes “Apple Intelligence” in future launches.
