# AI companies are shredding rare books

- Score: 730 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49068738) | Link: https://twitter.com/HedgieMarkets/status/2081534588485296565

### TL;DR
AI labs are reportedly buying “rare” or out‑of‑print books, cutting off their spines, scanning them, then shredding the pages to build training datasets while dodging publisher licensing fights. Commenters argue this is a predictable outcome of long, rigid copyrights, publisher lawsuits against shadow libraries and Archive.org, and the economics of destructive scanning. The thread splits between outrage over cultural loss and IP theft, claims that most such books aren’t truly rare, and calls for copyright and heritage-law reform.

### Comment pulse

- Some archives also cut bindings but preserve pages and files; many argue digital preservation beats fetishizing luxe physical editions, while others see LLM training as simple IP theft.
