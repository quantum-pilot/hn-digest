# AI companies spend record sums on Washington lobbying

- Score: 249 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49069939) | Link: https://www.ft.com/content/d8a5f95e-3b6d-463a-a848-c9ef8e2394db

- TL;DR  
AI firms are dramatically increasing lobbying in Washington, seeking to shape AI regulation, secure federal contracts, influence safety rules, and affect issues like export controls and immigration. This raises classic regulatory capture concerns: well-funded incumbents may lock in rules that favor large players, define “safety” on their terms, and sideline open-source and smaller competitors. Transparency, lobbying disclosures, and diversified expert input will matter for preventing policy from being written primarily by the biggest AI vendors.
