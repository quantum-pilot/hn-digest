# Why I Left Google DeepMind

- Score: 177 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49067285) | Link: https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/iKm2FhpWkuuBojm82/why-i-left-google-deepmind

## TL;DR
Alexander Matt Turner (TurnTrout) left Google DeepMind after failing to stop Google from supplying AI and cloud services to U.S. immigration enforcement and signing a Pentagon “all lawful use” military AI deal with weaker safeguards than OpenAI’s. He documents a months-long internal and external campaign: pushing Jeff Dean, Sundar Pichai, and Demis Hassabis; organizing employees; drafting a 25‑page red‑line/oversight framework; and lobbying ethics leaders like Stuart Russell, Yoshua Bengio, and IASEAI. Under pressure, nearly all institutions and prominent individuals he appealed to backed down or went silent, so he resigned rather than help enable lethal autonomous weapons and mass AI surveillance.
