# Why I Left Google DeepMind

- Score: 177 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49067285) | Link: https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/iKm2FhpWkuuBojm82/why-i-left-google-deepmind

### TL;DR

A Google DeepMind research scientist resigned after Google signed a Pentagon AI agreement without explicit prohibitions on autonomous lethal targeting or mass surveillance. He had also objected to Google Cloud’s role in the DHS and ICE supply chain. Over several months, he organized roughly 250 employee signatures, persuaded Jeff Dean to join an amicus brief supporting Anthropic, and drafted a 25-page contract-and-oversight framework, but says senior leaders let it die. Hacker News praised the personal sacrifice and public accountability, while attributing institutional inaction to money, career incentives, and concentrated power.
