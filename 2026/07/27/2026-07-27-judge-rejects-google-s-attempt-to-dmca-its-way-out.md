# Judge Rejects Google's Attempt to DMCA Its Way Out of Being Scraped

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49073513) | Link: https://www.techdirt.com/2026/07/27/judge-rejects-googles-attempt-to-dmca-its-way-out-of-being-scraped/

### TL;DR

A judge dismissed Google’s DMCA Section 1201 claims against SerpAPI, finding that SearchGuard’s CAPTCHA-like controls block search results broadly, including pages without copyrighted material, and were not alleged to operate with copyright owners’ authority. Google may amend with narrower claims covering content it owns or licenses, such as portions of Knowledge Panels. Hacker News emphasized the irony of a web crawler attacking scraping, argued Google’s retired search API created the market for workarounds, and debated whether ranked result compilations contain enough creative selection to merit protection.

### Comment pulse

- API withdrawal made scraping predictable → developers still need general search results, while Google’s Gemini grounding is too narrow.
- Copyright status remains contested → ranking reflects investment and selection — counterpoint: snippets mostly reproduce public pages rather than expressing Google’s authorship.
- Scrapeable results enable accountability → outsiders can detect promoted scam sites and other harmful rankings users might otherwise mistake for official services.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The ruling separates access control from copyright: a barrier alone does not transform public information into a protected work.
- **Impact:** Search providers may need targeted protections or usable licensing APIs instead of blanket anti-bot systems backed by DMCA claims.
- **Watch next:** Watch Google’s amended complaint, treatment of Knowledge Panels, and whether related Reddit litigation adopts the same reasoning.
