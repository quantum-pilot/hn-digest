# Judge Rejects Google's Attempt to DMCA Its Way Out of Being Scraped

- Score: 236 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49073513) | Link: https://www.techdirt.com/2026/07/27/judge-rejects-googles-attempt-to-dmca-its-way-out-of-being-scraped/

### TL;DR
A U.S. judge tossed Google’s DMCA 1201 lawsuit against SerpAPI, which scrapes Google search results to offer an unofficial API. The court held that Google search pages mostly compile public facts, so Google can’t use DMCA anti-circumvention—which only protects access to copyrighted works—to block scraping of those pages. Google also failed to show its “SearchGuard” CAPTCHA-like system operated with copyright owners’ authorization. Google may refile narrow claims over copyrighted elements (like some Knowledge Panels), but not over generic search results.
