# Holes

- Score: 176 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48777832) | Link: https://xkcd.com/3266/large/

### TL;DR

An xkcd cross-section puts famous real and fictional excavations on one vertical elevation scale, from bunkers, tunnels, mines, caves, and laboratories to trenches and boreholes. Horizontal geography and landforms are schematic, but depth comparisons are scaled: infrastructure occupies a shallow band, while the Kola Superdeep and Deepwater Horizon boreholes reach roughly 12 km below sea level, deeper than Challenger Deep near 11 km. HN readers followed the chart into side investigations of Mexico’s giant crystal cave, a hand-dug well, Lake Baikal’s sediment-filled rift, and drilling-triggered floods or collapses marked “oops.”

### Comment pulse
