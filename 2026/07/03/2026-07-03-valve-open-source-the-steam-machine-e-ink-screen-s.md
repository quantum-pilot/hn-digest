# Valve open-source the Steam Machine e-ink screen so you can make your own

- Score: 525 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48774518) | Link: https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/07/valve-open-source-the-steam-machine-e-ink-screen-so-you-can-make-your-own/

### TL;DR

Valve released MIT-licensed firmware and mounting files for Inkterface, an optional Steam Machine front panel, instead of selling a finished accessory. The build specifies an Adafruit ESP32 Feather, eInk breakout, 5.83-inch monochrome panel, magnets, and screws, plus an assembly video; a Linux app sends metrics over Bluetooth. It is a modding example, not a standard screen included with the computer. HN praised the off-the-shelf design, imagined status dashboards and third-party kits, and debated airflow, refresh speed, illumination, and whether openness mainly expands Valve’s Steam-centered ecosystem.

### Comment pulse

- Standard parts lower the barrier → the 5.83-inch Adafruit panel and ESP32 make repair, remixing, and vendor-built kits plausible.
- Case mods have thermal constraints → front inserts may obstruct airflow on other desktops — counterpoint: spacing or smaller status tiles could preserve intake.
- Openness may be strategic rather than charitable → commenters see hardware as ecosystem seeding that reduces Valve’s dependence on Microsoft while strengthening Steam.
