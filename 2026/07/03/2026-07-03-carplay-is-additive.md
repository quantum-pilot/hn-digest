# CarPlay Is Additive

- Score: 540 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48769397) | Link: https://www.caseyliss.com/2026/7/2/carplay-is-additive-you-dolts

### TL;DR

The author argues Rivian’s screen-control objection confuses standard CarPlay with CarPlay Ultra: ordinary CarPlay can occupy only part of a display, preserve manufacturer controls, and remain entirely optional. Its real value is access to a phone’s app ecosystem, with future route sharing addressing vehicle-integration concerns; excluding it therefore rejects willing buyers without improving native software. HN emphasized consistency across rentals, drivers, and decade-long vehicle lifetimes, but Rivian owners countered that strong built-in navigation, media, and messaging can make projection unnecessary.

### Comment pulse

- Longevity favors phone projection → cars outlast infotainment hardware, while updated phones keep maps, media, and performance current.
- Portability is a major benefit → rentals and shared cars instantly inherit each driver’s apps, settings, language direction, and itinerary.
- Demand is not universal → some prefer HUDs or mounted phones — counterpoint: limited native app catalogs still constrain owner choice.
