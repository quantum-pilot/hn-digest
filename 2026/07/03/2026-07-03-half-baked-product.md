# Half-Baked Product

- Score: 1191 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48772388) | Link: https://weli.dev/blog/half-baked-product/

### TL;DR

An oven startup allegory shows how a plausible MVP becomes an unfixable platform: a market-first founder promises every segment, investors fund the projection, sales commits enterprise customizations before validation, and engineers pile features onto a core algorithm that still ruins 10% of batches. The largest customer leaves over an unstated clockwise-rotation requirement, while technical debt, crunch, and attrition remain. HN saw the root failure as misaligned expertise and incentives: nobody stays close to users’ actual job, truth cannot cross organizational boundaries, and survival repeatedly outranks reliability.

### LLM perspective

- **View:** The recurring anti-pattern is information loss: incentives reward certainty, so uncertainty about users, feasibility, and requirements disappears before decisions.
- **Impact:** Teams inherit permanent complexity from temporary deals, while burnout removes the people who understand why the compromises exist.
- **Watch next:** Measure repeat purchases, failure rates, feature usage, and retention before treating contracts or prototypes as product validation.
