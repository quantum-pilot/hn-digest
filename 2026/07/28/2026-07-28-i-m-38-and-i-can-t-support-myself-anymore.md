# I'm 38 and I Can't Support Myself Anymore

- Score: 187 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49082857) | Link: https://itsmevox.substack.com/p/im-38-and-i-cant-support-myself-anymore

### TL;DR
A chronically ill writer at 38 can no longer reliably work, must borrow from family, and describes the shame of losing “economic usefulness” in a culture that equates productivity with moral worth. They trace this to the Protestant work ethic and modern hustle/wellness culture, and detail how disability, poverty-level benefits, and the “benefits cliff” make survival both expensive and precarious. The essay argues that care work and fragile bodies expose capitalism’s myths, and asks what remains of a person when productivity is gone.
