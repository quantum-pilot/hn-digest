# Deflock Casa Grande

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49088148) | Link: https://deflockcg.com/

## TL;DR
Casa Grande, Arizona signed a 10‑year, $10M deal with Flock for 220 cameras, gunshot microphones, drones, and a unified AI platform. Deflock Casa Grande argues that Flock’s patents, product materials, and internal city emails show a people‑tracking, protest‑monitoring network, not just license‑plate readers. They warn about searchable movement histories, perpetual data licensing, always‑listening microphones, school camera integration, and a growing national record of ALPR‑enabled romantic stalking by police. The campaign backs law enforcement but demands warrants, strong contracts, audits, and public control before mass surveillance is normalized.
