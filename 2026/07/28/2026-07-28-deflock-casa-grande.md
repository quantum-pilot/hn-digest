# Deflock Casa Grande

- Score: 244 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49088148) | Link: https://deflockcg.com/

### TL;DR

Deflock Casa Grande argues Arizona’s $10 million, decade-long Flock deployment is becoming a searchable surveillance network, not merely license-plate readers. Planned systems include 100 plate readers, 100 pan-tilt-zoom cameras, 31 gunshot detectors, drones, school-camera integrations, and AI analysis. Citing protest monitoring and 20 reported ALPR stalking cases nationwide, the campaign seeks public hearings, audits, warrant-like search rules, data-sharing limits, misuse penalties, and a public vote. Commenters largely agree that databases invite abuse, while some acknowledge surveillance can improve public safety.

### Comment pulse
