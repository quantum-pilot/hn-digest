# Donate to GrapheneOS

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49088451) | Link: https://grapheneos.org/donate

### TL;DR

GrapheneOS asks individuals and organizations to fund its open-source operating-system project, with donations covering developers, test hardware, infrastructure, legal fees, and other maintenance costs. Contributions can be one-time or recurring through GitHub Sponsors, PayPal, Wise, bank transfers, Interac, or several cryptocurrencies. Commenters generally support donating amid growing government pressure on privacy tools, but their practical discussion centers on adoption tradeoffs: unsupported tap-to-pay and some apps, workarounds using physical cards or wearables, and the irony of needing Google Pixel hardware pending broader device support.

### Comment pulse

- Funding privacy infrastructure feels urgent → supporters cite growing governmental pressure and a federal case involving an alleged GrapheneOS duress password.
- Daily use is mostly smooth → users report strong Pixel experiences, but tap-to-pay and Strava compatibility remain gaps with physical-card and wearable workarounds.
- Pixel dependence divides users → critics reject buying Google hardware to escape its ecosystem — counterpoint: expected Motorola support could broaden adoption.
