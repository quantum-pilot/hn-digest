# Donate to GrapheneOS

- Score: 221 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49088451) | Link: https://grapheneos.org/donate

- TL;DR  
    - GrapheneOS’s donation page details how its non-profit foundation funds development, infrastructure, hardware, and legal needs for the hardened Android privacy OS, accepting GitHub Sponsors, several cryptocurrencies, Wise/local bank transfers, PayPal, and Interac. Hacker News discussion broadens this to supporting digital privacy as a whole, referencing a protester case involving a GrapheneOS duress password, and debating tradeoffs: loss of tap‑to‑pay and some apps, the irony of needing a Pixel to “escape” Google, and hopes for non‑Google device support.
