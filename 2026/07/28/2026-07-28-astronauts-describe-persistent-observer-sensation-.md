# Astronauts describe persistent 'observer' sensation after 6 month missions

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49076900) | Link: https://spacedaily.com/sd-v-astronauts-returning-from-six-month-missions-describe-a-persistent-observer-sensation-the-feeling-of-watching-their-own-lives-from-a-half-step-outside-the-frame-weeks-after-theyr/

### TL;DR

An AI-assisted article claims astronauts returning from six-month ISS missions often feel detached, as if observing their lives, for weeks or months. It attributes this alleged pattern to sensory readaptation, sustained self-monitoring, isolation, and the overview effect, while admitting it is not a diagnosis and samples are small. HN readers found no primary sources and said cited literature may contradict it, making confabulation the central concern. Others shared similar dissociation after submarine duty, VR immersion, stress, travel, or neurodivergent experience—but those anecdotes do not validate the astronaut claim.
