# Astronauts describe persistent 'observer' sensation after 6 month missions

- Score: 181 | [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49076900) | Link: https://spacedaily.com/sd-v-astronauts-returning-from-six-month-missions-describe-a-persistent-observer-sensation-the-feeling-of-watching-their-own-lives-from-a-half-step-outside-the-frame-weeks-after-theyr/

### TL;DR
Astronauts who spend about six months on the ISS reportedly often feel like detached observers of their own lives after returning: conversations lag, homes feel staged, gravity and sensory input seem theatrical. The article links this to brain rewiring in microgravity, constant self‑monitoring, isolation, and the “overview effect,” and notes NASA’s structured physical/psychological reconditioning and worries for Mars missions. Hacker News commenters, however, highlight weak sourcing and AI‑mediated authorship, treating the phenomenon as plausible but currently anecdotal and not well evidenced.
